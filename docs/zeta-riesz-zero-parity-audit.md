With `a=s/r`, `b=d/r`, and `s>d>0`, the candidate is

\[
G_r(s,d)=\frac b a\rho(a-1)
 +\int_1^b\frac{b-v}{a-v}\rho(a-v-1)\omega(v)\,dv.

recurrence and transform are discussed in Hildebrand and Tenenbaum,
[Integers without large prime factors, Section 2](https://tenenb.perso.math.cnrs.fr/PPP/Survey_friables.pdf).

**not formalized or imported as a theorem**.
