# A different completion boundary cancels the same prime head

The local test of global squarefree completion retains all prime counts
before taking real parts. Its ordinary-prime, semiprime and raw high-owner
boundaries are explicit. This does not solve the independent joint floor.

For every original native head pair `p,q`, write `T=log p+log q`, let `L`
be the unchanged moving length, and retain the exact owner allocation `A`.
The new leaf `ZetaRieszGlobalCompletionBoundary` proves, for
`1/2<=u<=10001/20000` and `N>=65536`,

```text
R_L(pq) = T-L,
(T-L) - (L-log p) A >= 17N/100.
```

The original head amplitude is `w (L-log p) A`, where `w` contains its
source normalization, factorial radial weight, actual prime sieve and
physical support. The subtracted global semiprime atom is `w (T-L)`.
Both keep the same full character `exp(-iy T)`. Hence their difference
has norm exactly

```text
w [(T-L)-(L-log p) A],
```

instead of the separate price `w [(T-L)+(L-log p) A]`. The improvement is
exactly twice the old head amplitude on every matching label.
`boundary_re_eq_neg_literal` checks that this semiprime boundary really
is the negative original `coefficient * zetaPrimeLogKernel` atom, including
its sieve. `matched_boundary_norm_bound` and `matched_boundary_real_floor`
join the entire original head population, including its zero sieve slots.
The resulting bound is still an unevaluated arithmetic allowance.

This does not contradict `ZetaRieszOwnerCompletionAudit`. Completion at
the smaller marked prime produced `R_(L-log q)(p)=L-log q`, reinforcing
the head. Global squarefree completion instead subtracts the full
semiprime response `R_L(pq)=T-L`. These are different literal coefficients.
Neither completion licenses deleting an ordinary-prime source.

The optional integer-lattice probe exhausts the toy windows at orders
6, 7 and 8. All 18 original complex-main/head regressions pass. A second
probe matches every original toy head pair and checks 18 head totals.
For example, at order 8 the matched separate atom-norm price is about
`0.0099637`; joining before norms reduces it to `0.0047962`. This is a
local auxiliary-boundary diagnostic, **not** a percentage of the native
floor deficit or a cofinal bound. The toy length, schedule and deletion
masks are not native, and no eventual budget is applied there.

`P1` the ordinary-prime part, `P2` the semiprime part, `Hraw` the raw

```text
central main - same full head
```

Stop global completion as an automatic floor proof at this point.
