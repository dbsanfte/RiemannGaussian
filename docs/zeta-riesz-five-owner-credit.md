# Additional owner credit in the same literal five-prime sum

The new terminal theorems in
[`ZetaRieszFiveOwnerBoxes.lean`](../RiemannGaussian/ZetaRieszFiveOwnerBoxes.lean)
strengthen both one-sided comparisons for the **existing** five-prime
population. Its additional angular credit is exactly **`1/800`**, with
the explicit relative calibration factor on the upper side. This is a
component improvement, not the whole joint floor or ceiling.

## What the numerical audit found

The optional [probe](riesz-five-owner-probe.json) keeps every subset sign
in the five-prime Riesz coefficient. At `L/log n = 0.693`, it estimates
negative coefficient mass of about `0.02049` in the existing saturated
chamber with largest share between `1/2` and `9/16`. The reflected-linear
balanced chamber contributes only about `0.00106` in the same diagnostic.
These numbers have no arithmetic, phase or source-scale certification.

The literal `interiorFamily` already permits largest shares up to `9/16`.
Its previous credited `orderedRegion` integral stops at `1/2`. Thus it
would be wrong to add another copy of the larger-share prime population.
The proof instead adds **disjoint angular mass inside the same family**.

## Exact boxes and their numerical lower bound

There are 246 half-open cofactor cubes. Their lower endpoints are
`i/100 + 1/1000000` and their common width is `9998/1000000`.
The kernel checks each cube's ordering, positive denominators, saturation,
owner geometry, and a rational lower score. Distinct integer grid
coordinates prove disjointness. Every owner share exceeds `1/2`, separating
these cubes from the previously credited integral.

The rational scores sum to

```math
\frac{1397410}{10^9}=0.00139741>\frac1{750}.
```

`owner_integral_lower` proves the latter lower bound uniformly for
`69/100 <= L/t <= 7/10`. `ownerRegion_subset_cells` places all the cubes in
the **original** fine interior grid; all ordering and saturation boundaries
have a fixed rational margin. The exact table is regenerated by
[`generate_riesz_owner_boxes.py`](../scripts/generate_riesz_owner_boxes.py).
Python supplies candidates only. Kernel reduction checks the table, its
uniqueness and its total; it adds no trusted computation or axiom.

## Transfer to the signed arithmetic sum

`ZetaRieszFiveOwnerCredit.ordered_plus_extra_le_cells` retains the old
boundary budget once and proves

```math
\int_{\mathrm{orderedRegion}}D_5+\frac1{750}-\frac1{100000}
\le\int_{\mathrm{interior\ grid}}D_5.
```

The existing literal prime-count, allocation, physical-mask and
total-log-window transfer then gives `eventually_owner_core_floor`.
Writing

```math
C_5=\frac{996}{1000}\int_{\mathrm{orderedRegion}}D_5-\frac1{50000},
\qquad
V_-=\frac{e^{-(t+h)/2}t^N}{N!},
```

its independent lower comparison is

```math
\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{coreResponse}
\ge\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{rest}
 +(C_5+1/800)V_-h\max(0,-\cos(yt)-|y|h).
```

The original radius range `1/2<u<=10001/20000`, dyadic schedule, moving
length, core window and finite masks remain. The cutoff bin may be any
`[lo,hi]` inside `[69/100,7/10]`; in particular it can be the existing
central certificate's bin. The phase height is arbitrary.

`eventually_owner_core_ceiling` gives the corresponding upper comparison
on a positive-cosine window:

```math
\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{coreResponse}
\le\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{rest}
 -(C_5+1/800)(1-|\pi/t|h)V_-h(\cos(yt)-|y|h).
```

Calibration observes the same actual coefficients and allocations; it
does not change the original height in the resulting inequality. These
are alternative comparisons with their own exact signed rests.

## Limits of the result

The extra `1/800` is an angular coefficient multiplying the displayed
radial/phase factor, not the value of its source-normalized contribution.
No extra credit is obtained by adding it to another already-spent
copy of `interiorFamily`. The central certificate has now passed all 447
chunks and assembly, and its literal combined transfer is checked. The
[central period payment](zeta-riesz-capacity-phase-payment.md#checked-central-payment-for-the-broader-triple-band)
uses this owner credit to pay the broad triple band and selected four-prime
population in one signed ledger, in both lower and upper directions.
The [source-scale refinement](zeta-riesz-central-reserve.md) now proves an
explicit growing credit for that combined central comparison, with its
opposing signed complement retained.

The all-multiplicity endgame still needs the whole-sum lower bound
`-79/1000-o(1)` for simple zeros and the whole-sum upper bound `3/2+o(1)`
for multiple zeros. Other counts, unselected signs and the entire remaining
joint complement are still open. This slice proves neither a zero
exclusion nor RH.
