# Masked Selberg, ceiling and semiprime checkpoint

This checkpoint publishes all accumulated local work: **39 Riesz proof
modules and 59 semiprime proof modules**, together with their proof audits,
optional probes and independent replays. The ordinary root includes every
module. Exhaustive numerical-certificate verification remains optional.

The independent whole-carrier floor and fixed-strip, all-height **42/25
ceiling remain open**. Neither a new zero exclusion nor a proof of RH is
claimed. The numerical contradiction margins are not estimates of the
remaining arithmetic error.

The [adjacent-order identity](zeta-riesz-selberg-adjacent-orders.md) preserves
the actual balanced pair mask and full phase. It joins orders
`N-1`, `N+1`, `N+2`, expands the imbalance into individual prime orders, and
retains both literal factorial prefixes. Moving to the next native mask
deletes the old box and incurs a divergent absolute price. The
[signed boundary audit](zeta-riesz-selberg-signed-boundary.md) strengthens
this diagnosis: at the test height `19*pi` the isolated balanced real
contribution diverges.

The [joint mask theorem](zeta-riesz-selberg-mask-cancellation.md) shows why
the complement must stay signed. At that same test height the original
complement diverges with the opposite sign, while their exact sum decays
geometrically. This height is already zero-free; it is not asserted to
be a zero ordinate. A nonzero selected prime source prevents transplanting
that geometric radius bound to the hypothetical source.

The whole-support pair completion, full factorial convolution and source
comparison preserve every analytic multiplicity. The
[boundary simplicity theorem](zeta-riesz-ceiling-sharp-multiplicity.md)
covers a shrinking boundary layer at all heights. The
[whole height bound](zeta-riesz-ceiling-whole-height-bound.md) and
[sublinear improvement](zeta-riesz-ceiling-sublinear-height.md) bound the
same signed carrier by height-dependent prices. The latter eventually
reduces the previous price by at least three quarters, but its checked
all-degree obstruction prevents closing the constant target by scalar
degree optimization.

The synthetic density, integer, finite-mode and finite-Euler-jet audits
remain explicitly separated from actual prime and zero data. They test
proposed sufficient mechanisms; they do not disprove an actual-zeta
arithmetic bound. Optional numerical probes do not enter ordinary builds.

The [semiprime investigation](semiprime-factorisation-investigation.md)
publishes the exact affine, row-area, index, seed, bit and Fermat identities,
their formal obstruction theorems, and the optional coverage experiments.
It does not establish a generic improved factoring exponent. Its theorem
family and complete source index remain separate from the RH campaign.

All prior positive results and no-go audits are retained. The next RH
estimate must bound the selected signed prime correlations with the whole
complement joined, rather than separately pricing the balanced box or its
rest.
