# The central signed credit has a growing source scale

The [checked central comparison](zeta-riesz-capacity-phase-payment.md#checked-central-payment-for-the-broader-triple-band)
now retains its radial scale. It gives an independent, explicit
source-normalized credit for the same literal triple/four/five-prime
selection, in both the lower and upper inequalities. It does not bound
the whole carrier: the exact opposing complement remains essential.

## Explicit arithmetic comparison

For fixed `1/2<u<=10001/20000` and `|y|>=54`, put

```math
G_N(u,y)=\frac{\pi u e^{-1}}{24000|y|}\frac{(2u)^N}{N+1}.
```

The optional, compiled
[`CheckRieszCentralCapacityTransfer.lean`](../scripts/CheckRieszCentralCapacityTransfer.lean)
proves `eventually_exists_central_bin_lower_source_credit` and
`eventually_exists_central_bin_upper_source_credit`. On every sufficiently
large original dyadic order they construct an actual complete phase period
and prove

```math
u^{N+1}\operatorname{Re} W_N^-+G_N
\le u^{N+1}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{coreResponse}_N,
\qquad
u^{N+1}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{coreResponse}_N
\le u^{N+1}\operatorname{Re} W_N^+-G_N.
```

Here `W_N^-` and `W_N^+` are the exact finite signed sums over the two
respective complementary populations already present in the central
transfer. They are generally different. Each population uses the original
prime factors, squarefreeness, physical and count masks, moving length,
allocation and complex phase. The triple share band is `31/100..7/20`;
the four-prime selection retains its original positive-coefficient and
`log(p)>delta*N` restrictions. The same five-prime supply is spent once in
each alternative comparison. There is no zero or simplicity hypothesis.

Equivalently, the selected literal real sum in the lower comparison has
source-normalized value at least `G_N`, and the selected sum in the upper
comparison has value at most `-G_N`. Neither statement discards its exact
complement when comparing with the whole carrier.

## Information retained from the radial proof

`eventually_exists_central_bin_period_at_saddle` preserves

```math
2N\le v_N\le2N+\frac12.
```

The two strengthened period budgets also preserve the quantitative witness

```math
\frac{e^{-v_N/2}v_N^N}{N!}\le\frac{501}{500}V_0,
```

along with the previous margin `g*V0*h/500`. Thus they give at least
`g*h/1000` times the original saddle weight, where
`h=pi/(4*g*abs(y))`. No different moment or frozen prime phase is used.

[`ZetaRieszCentralReserve.saddle_radial_lower`](../RiemannGaussian/ZetaRieszCentralReserve.lean)
proves, for `N>=1` and `2N<=v<=2N+1`,

```math
\frac{e^{-1}2^N}{6(N+1)}
\le\frac{e^{-v/2}v^N}{N!}.
```

`sourceCredit_le_scaled_margin` then gives the exact constant in `G_N`.
`sourceCredit_tendsto_atTop` proves `G_N -> +infinity` for every fixed
nonzero height and `u>1/2`, including along the original dyadic orders.
The earlier statements are retained as corollaries.

## What this requires of the remaining cancellation

The same module proves two conditional audits. If the whole normalized
carrier has a finite source and the lower comparison holds along a chosen
sequence of periods, its normalized rest tends to **minus infinity**.
For the upper comparison the corresponding rest tends to **plus infinity**.
These are `lower_rest_tendsto_atBot_of_finite_source` and
`upper_rest_tendsto_atTop_of_finite_source`.

Every multiplicity in the exposed-zero source
`-m+m^2*c_ret(u)` is finite, so this issue is not removed by allowing
multiple zeros. A growing selected credit is not a completed whole-carrier
floor. Keep the actual selected response, its unused surplus beyond the
lower credit, and its signed complement together. Do not replace the
complement by a small error or add the alternative lower/upper credits.
The minimal whole-sum `-79/1000-o(1)` floor and `3/2+o(1)` ceiling remain
open; a divergent whole-sum bound is not added as a prerequisite.
