`ZetaRieszPrimeHeadPeriods` puts the correction from the rough-owner
comparison on its exact integer cutoff axis. Its owner coefficient contains
the entire actual prime cofactor sum, smooth factorial allocation, sieve
and full product phase. `sum_headIncrement` proves its total is exactly
`nativeHead`; `headIncrement_eq_zero` retains the one-sided hinge boundary.
Adding a physical endpoint only extends the old native main by zeros.

For the complete remaining period totals `r_b` and head totals `h_b`, the
checked `cost_eq` says

```text
joined cost = previous remaining cost + signed head
              − same-sign overlap + negative head period mass.

overlap = sum_b [ min((r_b)+,(h_b)+) + min((-r_b)+,(-h_b)+) ].
```

This is an exact signed relation after **all counts and cutoffs are
assembled**. Favorable overlap alone is insufficient when some head
periods are negative. `price_le_signed_overlap` keeps the better old/new
floor and deducts precisely the positive part of the net overlap. The
actual `joinedPhysical` inequality spends the independently checked rough
and previous small-tag totals once, with the same source-small budgets
and `nativeError`. It does not bound the size of the native credit.
