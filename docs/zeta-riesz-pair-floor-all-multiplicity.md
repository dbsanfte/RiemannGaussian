## Why the older ceiling need not be a separate target here

The older whole-carrier source is

\[
-m+m^2c_{\rm ret}(u).
\]

Adding the retained-pair source gives exactly \(m^2-m\). For `m=1` this
is zero; at higher multiplicity it is positive. That explains why the
older whole-carrier floor only contradicted a simple source and used a
separate ceiling for multiple zeros.

The **current retained-pair upper bound** is stronger than that older
whole-carrier floor at higher multiplicity. Its own source is positive
and grows with `m^2`, so it excludes all multiplicities directly. The
older ceiling remains unproved and available; it is no longer an
additional required estimate **if this particular pair bound lands**.
There is no change to the arithmetic target or any double spending of
packet credit.
