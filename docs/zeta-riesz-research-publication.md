# Whole-floor research and semiprime side-investigation publication

This batch publishes the accumulated local work since `f761660`.
The [publication audit](research-publication-audit.json) records the complete
ordinary build, declaration lint and standard-axiom checks for all 54 new
proof modules (1,512 theorems including generated helpers).

The independent numerical floor, higher-multiplicity ceiling and RH remain
**open**. No new zero-free region or numerical zero bound is claimed.

The existing `joinedPhysical` carrier and exact exposed-zero source are
unchanged. All original phases, count/owner/physical/radial masks, allocation
and funding remain. The [source-slack audit](zeta-riesz-endgame-slack.md) proves
that a cofinal `-399/5000-o(1)` floor suffices for simple exposed zeros on
`1/2<u<=10001/20000`, while `42/25+o(1)` suffices for every higher multiplicity.
These are sufficient criteria, not independently established inequalities.

The batch includes geometric payments for the [actual diagonal](zeta-riesz-diagonal-payment.md),
[large shared factors](zeta-riesz-shared-factor-payment.md),
[nearby labels](zeta-riesz-near-label-payment.md),
[phase alignment](zeta-riesz-phase-orbit-payment.md) and
[additional phase sectors](zeta-riesz-wide-phase-payment.md).
Their stated hypotheses and paid sets stay explicit. The
[joint-credit ledger](zeta-riesz-joint-credit-floor.md) retains the remaining
signed energy; none of those credits can be spent twice.

The [complex divisor-null floor](zeta-riesz-complex-null-floor.md) and
[exact cubic credit](zeta-riesz-signed-null-gain.md) improve the same native
price by signed identities. In particular, the compiled theorem
[`native_floor_with_credit`](../RiemannGaussian/ZetaRieszSignedNullGain.lean)
proves

```text
Re(sourceScaled joinedPhysical)
  >= -nativeCost + nativeCredit - nativeError.
```

The error tends to zero under the existing exposed-zero hypotheses.
`nativeCredit>=0` is capped by the same baseline price. The cofinal estimate
`nativeCost-nativeCredit<=399/5000` has not been proved. No numerical size for
the native credit is inferred from finite model probes.

The optional unpaid-only probes now detect generic signed subset structure,
unsigned hinge boundaries, high-dimensional incidence null directions and
joint signed-cost improvements. Exact rational primal/dual replays check
supplied finite block problems. Unresolved regions stay unresolved, paid
examples are rejected before discovery, and unit/model regressions are not
native population certificates. See the
[generic-pattern](zeta-riesz-generic-patterns.md),
[hinge-geometry](zeta-riesz-hinge-geometry.md) and
[signed-null](zeta-riesz-signed-null-gain.md) audits.

The [insertion-capacity audit](zeta-riesz-insertion-capacity.md) joins every
finite count before comparing weights. The new
[genuine-prime rate theorem](zeta-riesz-insertion-rate-audit.md) proves that
independent actual weights `1/p` retain polynomial empty-channel mass, even
with every prime in the permitted pool and every insertion count. Its source
price regrows for fixed `u>1/2`, despite any fixed polynomial discount. This
rules out that independent matching mechanism, not cancellation in the
literal correlated carrier.

The [semiprime factorisation investigation](semiprime-factorisation-investigation.md)
is also published. Its ten proof modules cover modular/colour identities,
coverage conditions, order separation, root-preserving batches and explicit
limits. They are grouped in a separate theorem family and checked by the
ordinary Lean library. Their numerical probes remain optional. Prototype
benchmarks and partial algorithms retain their stated scope; there is no
claim of a generic faster factoring exponent or contribution to the RH floor.

The public default frontier remains the independently proved whole-core
estimate. The explorer adds a supporting view of the newer signed-null
inequality. Published status does not turn its open numerical premise into a
proof. Exhaustive numerical-certificate verification is unchanged and remains
optional/cached.
