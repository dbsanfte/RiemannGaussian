The following review qualifications are incorporated into the final reports:

* The epsilon core has degree at most J; cancellations of its leading
  coefficient are allowed. Fixed generalized eigenvalues describe only a
  generic regular pencil. At a native collision the determinant polynomial
  can be identically zero.

  desired product. The formally canceled endpoint ratio is evaluated only
  if F(z_1) is a unit. Confluent determinant rows have derivatives, while
  their final quotient identity extends polynomially with multiplicity.
* The Fourier diagnostic uses a=7,b=1 for N=1 modulo 3 and a=5,b=1 for
  N=2 modulo 3. These avoid both diagonals and survive nonaxis integer-zero
  deletion. The proof concerns the cost of a full Fourier expansion only.
* Auxiliary axis cleaning labels may be included even if their query rows
  were pruned. No deleted physical leaf is restored. After k-roughening,
  any smaller-side primary loss leaves order below k². Factor return is
  through the paid short-label schedule or paid localization, not through
  an arbitrary large annihilator.

  shared-exponent construction uses actual complement-tree provenance;
  a bare list of unrelated nodes would not have that cost. Expanded
  boundary lists for the full interval still cost soft m.
* All auxiliary proper gcds can help. Saturated additive moment/core zeros,
  universal epsilon zeros, and the extra endpoint F(z_(m+1)) do not inherit
