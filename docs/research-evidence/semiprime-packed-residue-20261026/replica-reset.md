## 1. Exact original sources and replica projection

On one ORIGINAL singleton block let a_e=1-T_j rho^t and let w_e be a canonical row mark: u_j=[j²]_n for one useful squared tag, or L+u_j for the packed observable. Define

    D=diag(a_e), P(delta,epsilon)=product_e(a_e+delta+epsilon*w_e).

The replica-selected trace is

    CT_delta sum_e w_e*delta²/(delta²-a_e²).

It equals zero if the block is empty and equals w_hit at a good singleton. It is not itself an acquired polynomial in the numeric masses.

For a known unit kappa define the squared-mass reset B=D²+kappa*1*1^t. At a good singleton B is invertible and

    B^{-1}*(kappa*1)=e_hit,
    kappa*1^t*W*B^{-1}1=w_hit.                  (1)

This gives an ordinary selected inverse-action target without a full inverse OUTPUT. It does not license numerical inversion before its actual determinant/first-variation guard passes. An empty block generally has a different, meaningless value and must be removed by the original P(0,0) gate.

## 2. Execute the determinant/replica cancellation before specialization

Write P=P(0,0), P1=partial_delta P, Pw=partial_epsilon P, P11=partial_delta²P, P1w=partial_delta partial_epsilon P, with derivatives evaluated at zero AFTER their polynomial identities are established. For invertible formal masses, elementary finite log derivatives give

    P1²-P*P11=P²*sum_e a_e^{-2},
    P1*Pw-P*P1w=P²*sum_e w_e*a_e^{-2}.

Both clear to polynomial identities over the actual coefficient ring. Rank-one determinant/adjugate formulas therefore give the exact finite identities

    det B=P²+kappa*(P1²-P*P11),
    kappa*1^t*W*adj(B)*1
       =kappa*(P1*Pw-P*P1w).                   (2)

These formulas do not differentiate an identity valid only away from the native collision. They are cleared first. At the ACTUAL global-zero gate P=0 they simplify to

    det B=kappa*P1²,
    selected numerator=kappa*P1*Pw.            (3)

All second variations disappear. If P1 is a global unit, the selected scalar (1) is exactly Pw/P1. On the good singleton this cancels the unknown common physical leaf cofactor and yields the tag.

This is a concrete DJ-type two-replica shortcut: there is no remaining regular self-energy in (3), and no growing Hasse depth. But P1,Pw themselves remain unknown FIRST variations of the long physical product. Claiming (3) has evaluated them would be circular. A two-fermion source name or rank-one update does not supply their numeric values.

## 3. Ring guards and fully initialized implementation

Classify P by norm and coordinates. Proper gcds factor, a unit skips an empty candidate block, and a saturated norm must pass coordinate tests before P is called globally zero. On that branch a proper norm gcd of P1 factors and a unit licenses (3); a saturated derivative may be declined. At a correct hitting block P1 is nonzero at the good field, so that decline cannot lose it. On a no-factor continuation, P=0 and P1 unit imply EXACTLY one zero mass in every component; hence B is actually invertible over B, with the displayed unit determinant. No additive determinant zero is sent to a multiplicative annihilator consumer.

To execute these inputs today, jointly acquire G_j=G_h(T_j) and gamma_j=hG_j-T_jG_h'(T_j), the first physical regulator derivative. Products of (G_j,gamma_j,w_j gamma_j) in the fixed three-coordinate jet ring give P,P1,Pw. Cost is soft(K+sqrt(Kh)) over the whole bank, followed by soft-K block processing and the bounded validator/HH consumer. Ordinary versus Hasse second-derivative factors are irrelevant to this implementation: no second derivative is acquired at all.

The newer ROW-VALUE gate is simpler still: multiply (G_j,1,u_j) and take its guarded ratio. It is output-equivalent at a good singleton, but generally not equal to the physical P1/Pw nor to the two determinant values in (3). Replacing those numbers silently would misidentify the finite source identity. For one useful block, arbitrary-unit amplitude is allowed; for exact M1 or packed C, every hitting block's contribution and empty-block gate must be retained.

Thus this hybrid removes second jets/cofactor products from a replica-reset implementation, but its initialized acquisition remains the retained K^(5/4). The explicit remaining objects are P1,Pw (or the simpler still-unacquired row-value triples), not a full matrix or unspecified regular part. A new method for those selected first variations could still improve the bill; none is proved here.
