## 5. Positive polar-square follow-up: finite Appell averaging

A more appropriate primary candidate is the meromorphic-Jacobi polar part, rather than the weak holomorphic seed. Dabholkar–Murthy–Zagier, [Quantum Black Holes, Wall Crossing, and Mock Modular Forms](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/arxiv/1208.4074/1208.4074v1.pdf), §8.2, equation (8.9), defines the elliptic averaging operator

    Av^(m)[F(Y)]=sum_(s in Z) X^(m s²)Y^(2ms)F(X^s Y).

Their polar decomposition uses rational pole inputs and Appell–Lerch sums, with modular completion treated separately. Only this explicit summand is used below; no convergent infinite specialization over Z/N is asserted.

For the simple-pole input F(T)=(1+T)/(1-T), expansion in the chamber |T|<1 gives positive k with coefficient 2, while expansion in |T|>1 gives negative k with coefficient -2. These are distinct Laurent chambers. In either applicable chamber the nonconstant monomials have

    n=m s²+s k, l=2ms+k,
    4mn-l²=-k².                                (5)

Thus this candidate really DOES have negative-square polar support. It is not rejected by the Igusa cutoff argument. Selecting k divisible by K is a compact rational operation: formally average F(omega^r T) over r mod K, or prove by finite geometric partial fractions, obtaining

    (1/K)sum_r F(omega^r T)=(1+T^K)/(1-T^K).    (6)

The right side is acquired by binary powering, with no auxiliary extension required in an implementation. Equation (6) is used only as a cleared rational identity, not evaluated at an unclassified denominator zero.

For positive a, choose m=a and Fourier coordinate n=bN. At a genuine physical hit l=-(aR+bS), take

Then (5) gives n=bN and the correct l exactly. This is a constructive algebraic polar parametrization of EVERY such native hit. Its k may be positive or negative, so it is not an assertion that all hits appear in one positive-power Fourier expansion or one fixed analytic chamber. Unlike the standard weak seed it has the right discriminant and Fourier congruences; actual chamber membership is an additional condition for coefficient interpretations. Signed sectors need their own positive-index treatment; this positive sector already exposes the actual winding cost.

## 6. Alternative winding and its exact scope
