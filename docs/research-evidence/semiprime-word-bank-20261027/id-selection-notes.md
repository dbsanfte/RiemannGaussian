## Exact semantics and the useful shortcut

SourceWordMenuOrder proves that the actual canonical head-major word list is exactly the indexed zero-first/filter-tail order. SourceWordIdMenu and SourceWordBankIndexed connect that ordered list to the acquired IDs. This is stronger than a set or permutation statement: every original tie decision is preserved. It applies to the actual PaidFiniteEnumerations.indexed constructor, rather than an arbitrary permutation supplied through the more general Enumerations interface.

SourceWordIdControl strengthens the existing controller's proof interface to require score correctness and widths only for acquired candidate IDs and prefixes composed of those IDs. The executed controller is unchanged. It still performs every candidate score call, physical comparison, selected-ID copy and complete prefix copy. InMenu is an invariant proved along the executed branch; it is not an unproved assumption at the original endpoint.

The physical environment stores the default ID, reverse-prefix IDs, and newest-first physical key/ID overrides. It never installs a decoded Word in the score runtime. Incidence truth now needs only two physical ID equalities. This works because the acquired bank's decoder is injective on its actual menu and every read preserves membership in that menu. Consequently an incidence test does not have to fetch and compare complete coordinate rows. Head truth still reads the actual bank rows and requested coordinates through the paid physical readers. Both kinds of truth are supplied by concrete execution, with no truth table or hit oracle.

SourceWordIdCounts executes acquired physical keys, copied override records, Boolean branch additions, acquired normalization padding, physical lane accumulation, and the cached integer-weight combination. The old completion counts and decoded environments appear only in correctness theorems. The common positive score factor and offset already proved in the score-count packet continue to cancel; rational scales and offsets are absent from this new search runtime.

SourceWordIdSearch.run_refines identifies the decoded result with the exact original indexed scalar search, for any common proof-side offset. Write M=sum_e arity(e), L=r^Q, U=r^(2Q), T=M^2+Q*n^2, and V=n+F. SourceWordIdSearchBudget.run_work retains the actual bank and weight initializers, concrete callback reports and the complete controller report. indexed_work also retains metadata and descriptor acquisition. Descriptor-support bounds follow from the completeness certificate of the index list actually acquired from the original inputs.

The displayed indexedBudget has ten independent numeric resources n,F,r,Q,lambda,M,L,U,T,V. SourceWordIdResources.indexedBudget_mono is componentwise monotonicity of that actual budget. indexed_report_actual substitutes the original M,L,U,T,V and bounds the executed caller by envelope(S) whenever these ten resources are at most S. envelope_monomial proves fixed C,k, chosen before S or the original family, with envelope(S)<=C*(S+1)^k. The sole subtraction L-1 in the controller tail factor is replaced by L only in a proved upper majorant; no executed work is dropped.

The alphabet cardinality L and normalization cardinality U are materialized resources. These theorems do not say they are polynomial in the binary descriptions of r,Q. All candidate branches up to fixed support four remain in the report; this packet does not prove a new near-linear long-query bound.

## Boundary of the achieved result

This closes the acquired physical word/ID bank and concrete ID-based score-selection adapter that the previous checkpoint left open. Native Word functions, cacheWord construction and decoded prefixes are removed from the executed source-selection data path. The new endpoint returns acquired IDs with exact original indexed-label semantics and a uniform original-resource report.

Physical reads of the selected incidence labels, packing, universal coefficient-cache construction, pullback and public counter composition are the next integration steps. The existing N-only public caller is unchanged. The earlier Hermite retained-report rate is preserved; a full primitive-bit or Turing-machine sixth-rate theorem does not follow from this packet alone.
