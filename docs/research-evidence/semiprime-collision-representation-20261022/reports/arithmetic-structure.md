# Ranked arithmetic bridges for the native row family

22 October 2026. Broadened constructive research and primary-theorem checks. No new near-linear collision query is proved. No examples, backend or frozen-file edits. The compact-cover and lifted-norm/Pell routes already audited are not counted as new candidates. Rankings below concern a concrete next lemma, not a claimed likelihood of success.

## 1. Shared lattice reduction with two simultaneous local/real cancellations

This is the strongest arithmetic fit. The native choice a_j*j²=N mod m cancels the first m-adic derivative of aP+N/P at P=j. It does NOT make its real derivative small throughout a public factor window. The useful additional property would be a common lattice basis simultaneously organizing real stationary directions and modular stationary directions for all tags, rather than independently enumerating a start for each tag/window.

A precise historical tool exists. Harvey--Hittmeir, arXiv:2105.11105v1, Proposition 3.3, takes PUBLIC N,m,m0,sigma0,sigma with sigma unit modulo m. It constructs coefficients in polynomial logarithmic time. Its guarantees are conditional on the hidden p lying in the supplied relative window and supplied residue class; the output linear form has both a real approximation bound and a prescribed residue modulo m². It is not a hidden-residue oracle. The full proof and algorithm loops were inspected at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11105 .

Concrete bridge: prove a restriction-stable shared reduction/continued-fraction diagram whose TOTAL number of retained window/tag cells and TOTAL useful index widths are soft O(m), with the covered own-tag point always in one cell. Alternatively the cells' anchors could form an acquired low-complexity family on which the existing scalar group collision can be contracted. The native input supplies exact modular tangency and public composite-modulus factorization, so the required lattice congruence is already affordable. The missing lemma is sharing/coverage of real stationary windows with its total charge, not lattice reduction of one supplied cell. Independent calls to Proposition 3.3 merely recover the known larger covering search and are not progress. This candidate is especially compatible with the new ell-first composite selector because Proposition 3.3 does not require prime m.

## 2. A carry-corrected SL2 transfer, replacing small-doubling hopes by an explicit action

On unit tags, j->N/j is the modular hyperbola involution. In inverse coordinates t=j^-1 the native low exponent is N(t-1)². Translations, inversion and scaling generate explicit fractional-linear maps, while the full row carries record their departure from a finite-field phase. Some actual maps are already paid: antipodal reflection, inverse-reflection low fibers, and the no-wrap conic scaling map. This is stronger structure than an arbitrary target set, but it is not a small-doubling theorem for its full integer exponent lifts.

The primary incidence source checked is Shkredov, *Modular hyperbolas and bilinear forms of Kloosterman sums*, arXiv:1905.00291v1, Theorem 1 and Corollary 2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.00291 . It works over a supplied PRIME field and bounds hyperbola incidences; Corollary 2 assumes a small-sumset set and a size restriction. It does not prove that our lifted rows have a small sumset, nor compute a multiplicative collision product. Its finite-field upper bounds could control exceptional action fibers only after a valid algorithmic action is constructed. For composite native m, CRT prime-power actions need a separate proof.

Concrete bridge: acquire a small generating set of exact operators on row/seed functions for t->t+b and t->-1/t, with a public multiplicative cocycle carrying EVERY gamma^(m² k) correction. Prove that the product of original differences over a complete pair group is a matrix coefficient or determinant of a product of these operators with total circuit work soft m. Zero-support must remain a same-source scalar property, with unit-cocycle checks or fraction-free formulas. A proved short group word is not enough; the action on the query must contract the carried origins rather than materialize them. The native low phase and exact rational maps provide the starting relations. The missing lemma is a small acquired representation/aggregation of the carry cocycle, not equidistribution or a bound on Fourier sums. This route permits additive/nonlinear cancellations that old prescribed q-chain or endpoint arguments did not exclude.

## 3. Native involutions as a genuine butterfly tower, with an exact finite-fiber norm

The geometric property worth testing is successive quotienting by source-preserving involutions, rather than a generic elliptic embedding. Native pair reflection already identifies a quadratic trace/norm, with its center varying explicitly with a. A useful tower would make those varying centers the parameters of the next quotient and preserve all covered scalar zeros. It would thereby turn the actual origin tree into fibers of a finite map; complete root-of-unity twist fibers alone do not do that.

A recent primary positive algorithm is Couveignes--Lercier, *Elliptic butterflies*, arXiv:2510.27375v2 (18 March 2026), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.27375 . Introduction and structural scope were inspected: evaluation/interpolation use a SPECIFIED elliptic-function basis, supplied point/divisor data and compatible degree-two isogeny structure. The arithmetic circuits have d log d scale for the corresponding dimension-d input/output problem. It does not accept an arbitrary degree-m² polynomial by a compact root descriptor, and it does not construct an unknown-factor elliptic field or its Frobenius data. Its near-linear transforms therefore are not immediately an H evaluator.

Concrete bridge: construct over Z/N a paid rational/elliptic cover of dimension soft m whose fibers encode the ACTUAL antipodal-group collision query and whose degree-two tower carries the native centers. Supply basis coordinates or an acquired norm recursion with soft m total data, verify all needed inverses by gcd, and prove that an interior original scalar collision survives the norm/localization (including mixed-zero fibers). This is substantially more specific than invoking fast elliptic periods: the desired map must identify the carried nodes and seed divisor, not merely provide fast arithmetic in an unrelated extension. The missing lemma is this exact source-preserving fiber/tower construction and coordinate acquisition. Without it, the cited algorithm improves only a transform already near-linear for explicit degree-m input and does not shorten the long query.

## Useful proven property and checkpoint

The strongest immediately usable mechanism remains modular tangency plus exact integer recovery: every true unit tag has an integer index in the paid public interval, the discriminant mask is an acquired composite-square image, and pair-group restriction suffices for localization. None of the literature above improves that covered index's acquisition by itself. Historical lattice results actually consume public residue/window advice; incidence results control counts over known prime fields; recent butterfly algorithms consume a compatible geometric basis and fibers. These precise inputs suggest the three missing lemmas above and prevent treating cancellation estimates, small descriptors or supplied fast arithmetic as the collision query.

Priority recommendation: attempt the shared real-and-m-adic lattice diagram first, while retaining the carry-corrected action as an alternate contraction. The butterfly route is more structurally ambitious and should be pursued only with an explicit native fiber map. No random-distribution or hidden P input is assumed in any proposed bridge; none is yet a guaranteed solution.
