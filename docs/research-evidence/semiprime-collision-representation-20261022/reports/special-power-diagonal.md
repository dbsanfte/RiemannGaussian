# The special-power varying diagonal: a concrete low-degree cocycle recurrence

Let A=(Z/NZ)[X]/(X^m−1), m odd prime with gcd(m,N)=1. Let W=t(1+gamma X)/(X+gamma), t²=gamma, R=W/X, B=W^N and b=N mod m. Preparation and the fixed-weight trace primitive are paid soft O(m). This report retains the exact cyclic algebra and integer powers. It does not prove the full native diagonal or collision query in soft O(m).

We seek H_a=CT(T_a), T_a=rho_(a/b)(B)R^a, for the canonical unit integers 1<=a<m (or the native quadratic-image subset). The distinction between a cyclic substitution exponent and the INTEGER power a is essential.

## 1. A new exact doubling recurrence with one acquired correction

For a unit r modulo m represented by a positive integer, set

 D_r=R^r/(rho_r R)=W^r/(rho_r W).

For canonical a'=ra−m*kappa, exact algebra gives

 T_(a')=rho_r(T_a) D_r^a R^(−m*kappa).                  (1)

This holds for an arbitrary fixed B; the last factor is the literal integer carry. For r=2, kappa is 0 or 1, and the correction is a fixed LOW-DEGREE rational unit:

 D_2=t(1+gamma X)^2(X²+gamma)/[(X+gamma)^2(1+gamma X²)]. (2)

All denominators are units: gamma has odd local order and X↦X² permutes the cyclic algebra. D_2 bar(D_2)=gamma. Both D_2 and R^m are actually acquired in soft O(m), using a constant number of cyclic operations and O(log m) powers. Equation (1) is therefore a real low-degree Mahler-type recurrence, not a formal root-of-unity replacement for gamma.

For the SPECIAL B=W^N, acquire the additional packed unit

 C=D_2^N

in soft O(m), including O(log N) cyclic operations. Since rho_2(B)=B²/C, equation (1) also becomes

 T_(2a−m*kappa)=T_a²/[rho_(a/b)(C)] * R^(−m*kappa).     (3)

Thus the varying special power is governed by an explicit quadratic recurrence with ONE publicly acquired correction vector C and its automorphism orbit. This is stronger structure than an unrelated arbitrary dense weight for each a. Norms check the exact carry: the norm of the right side is gamma^(N+2a−m*kappa), as required.

## 2. Why scalar traces do not yet close, and the specific repair required

Taking CT in (1) yields

 H_(a')=CT[T_a * (rho_(r^-1)D_r)^a * (rho_(r^-1)R)^(−m*kappa)]. (4)

Taking CT in (3) requires CT[T_a² rho_(a/b)(C)^(-1) R^(−m*kappa)]. Neither is determined by H_a alone. Even the square of an element of an m-dimensional etale algebra is not determined by its trace. The involution norm T_a bar(T_a)=gamma^(N+a) supplies a quadratic relation for EACH conjugate pair, not a common degree-two characteristic polynomial for the entire algebra.

The fixed-weight resolvent theorem does not directly evaluate (4): the weight contains T_a, whose B automorphism already varies. Repeatedly invoking the theorem for each a would again use m rank-m inputs. Conversely, writing all powers of C or all rho_a(C) explicitly is m² data and is not paid merely because C itself is acquired.

A precise constructive next theorem would be a batched CONTRACTION of the recurrence (3): given the paid vectors W, C=D_2^N, and R^m, return CT(T_a) for all requested canonical a while accounting for the varying rho_(a/b)(C), squares, and carry branches, without materializing their vectors. It would suffice to construct a closed family of scalar contractions of polylogarithmic size whose updates compute the right side of (3), with shared preprocessing soft O(m). This is a much more specific representation target than generic fast power projection: its only correction vector is the Nth power of the fixed degree-four rational unit (2).

There are two genuine additional issues even if one contracts a doubling orbit cheaply. Doubling covers only the subgroup generated by 2 in U_m, and can leave many independent orbits. A primitive generator r might be as large as m, in which case D_r is not bounded-degree. Also a carried branch uses R^(-m), which must remain inside every later contraction. Neither all-orbit startup nor boundary propagation can be dropped.

## 3. Test of the simplest scalar-correction repair

The degree-four correction is not already a scalar identity. Direct subtraction gives

 D_2−t = t*gamma*(gamma−1)*(X−1)^3*(X+1)
          /[(X+gamma)^2(1+gamma X²)].                 (5)

The denominator is a unit, gamma−1 is a unit on the retained prepared input, and X+1 is a unit because m is odd. Therefore D_2=t on the trivial X=1 component, while it differs from t at EVERY nontrivial mth root in each hidden field (m is prime and coprime to the field characteristic). This is an exact support calculation, not experimental evidence or a generic impossibility statement.

Its Nth power C could acquire further relations; equation (5) does not exclude that. But replacing C by t^N is an additional algebraic condition, not justified by odd order or by B=W^N. That condition is publicly CHECKABLE from the acquired vector C, with coefficient gcds, but is not a universal existence theorem. Even under such a condition, powers of the unknown T_a still require contracted higher moments; scalar correction alone is not an automatic full algorithm.

## 4. Special-power reciprocity retained, not used as a hidden oracle

The adjacent varying-weight-diagonal report's useful identity remains

 H_a=CT[X^(−aN) W(X^N)^a W(X^a)^N].                 (6)

It is symmetric in the INTEGER parameters a,N. Modulo a hidden P with N=PQ, Frobenius and a cyclic automorphism convert (6) to the analogous expression with Q replacing N. This is a source-exact cofactor interpretation, not a computation using known Q. Equations (1)–(3) offer an explicit transport mechanism for this special family, but do not remove the unknown cofactor or the native residual W_j^ell factor.

## 5. Cost and scope

W, R, R^m, D_2, C, B, and their inverses require only a constant number of length-m vectors and soft O(m) bit work; O(log N) exponentiation factors are suppressed. Individual automorphism permutations cost O(m). Generating all such permutations is not charged as free. Scalar norm checks and formula (5) have the same budget. No extension field, eigencharacter decomposition, local order oracle, or root extraction is used.

I found no justified near-linear contraction of (3). The concrete candidate now is the shared low-degree-correction recurrence, with exact wrap and all-orbit requirements stated above. This is not claimed as a collision algorithm or factoring-rate advance. It narrows the missing representation to one explicit special-power correction C rather than assuming m unrelated weights have been acquired.
