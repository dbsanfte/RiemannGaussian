|aP+bQ+lambda|>=Q-kP-H. If Q-kP>H(M+1), |I|>M at every stationary
certificate. From D=ceil sqrt(kN) and M=ceil(4D/H)+2,

These inequalities establish the stated scoped obstruction to deleting
axes from the OLD stationary proof. They say nothing against accidental
modular coverage by a new family or earlier termination of a controller.

The gamma-family products lie strictly between k^6/512 and k^6/128;
1/512>1/729 proves that the previous native scale k/3 is too small.
The ratio Q/(kP)>k^(1-2gamma)/4194304 diverges for gamma<1/2, and
P/H diverges for gamma>0. Both factors exceed 16H eventually. For a prime
p=11 mod12 the odd-part order (p-1)/2 is odd and prime to 3, hence prime
to H=4*3^(2e). A local beta with this order exists; CRT combines the two.
Thus q=beta^H keeps both orders, eventually >16H. This is compatibility
of the promises, not a construction forcing the actual controller branch
or cross-prime order coprimality.
