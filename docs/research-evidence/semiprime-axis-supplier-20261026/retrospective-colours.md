# Retrospective algebraic/colour information audit against the weaker HH input

26 October 2026. Read-only paper audit; no new tests, repository changes or literature claims. The new consumer is the one proved in [README §§1–2](README.md) and [joint-information-review](joint-information-review.md). This report searches old acquired data, rather than treating a newly affordable decoder as an information supplier.

**Result.** I found no previously acquired factor-provenanced residue palette or joint rectangle menu that already meets the new threshold. The strongest useful handoff is more specific than the old original-root locator: a content-stratified first collision slice needs only two *tagged* coefficients. Existing cheap clock/colour endpoint data do not acquire their ratio: the entire tagged factor cancels in the exact boundary identity. This is a scoped failed composition, not a lower bound for acquiring that ratio by another algorithm.

## 1. New acceptance criterion and provenance

For n|k, a menu of S unit residues must contain the actual smaller factor P mod n, with S/n at most order k^(-1/3). A menu for either factor can be complemented using N/sigma mod n. Alternatively A=soft O(k) joint rectangles (sigma mod n,[L,U]), U<=2L<=2sqrt(N), must jointly cover P, with Delta=sum(U-L)/L and Delta/n<=k^(-1/3). Interval and residue promises must hold on the **same** rectangle. Acquiring the list and its correlation are extra work.

These are weaker outputs than a complete native collision leaf. They are not supplied by a scalar group phase merely because that phase was computed using N. The distinction below is about exact output specifications, not a proved complexity separation.

## 3. Execute the strongest new pairing: integer-colour first slice versus the old boundary

Use the newly supplied provenance theorem only in its stated scope: correct-bin signed weighted **nonaxis** rows, partitioned by content g=gcd(a,k)=gcd(b,k)<=sqrt(k), n_g=k/g>=sqrt(k), and a nonsplit local factor R. Every hit in that stratum has j=R mod n_g. This is stronger provenance than an accidental axis alias. The following algebra does not independently re-prove that coverage theorem.

Write r_j=[j]_(n_g) as its actual integer residue, x_i=x_0 q^i, and mark a leaf by

    f_{j,i}(delta,Y)=1-y_j/x_i + delta*(1-Y*r_j).

At a local hit, its first factor is exactly delta*(1-Y*r_j). If there are d hits and all have r_j=r=R mod n_g, the first nonzero slice of the complete product is

    A*(1-Y*r)^d.

Consequently, for this local slice,

    U=-[Y]slice=d*A*r,   V=d*[Y^0]slice=d*A,   U/V=r.

Here A is a product of nonhit factors and therefore nonzero in that field. The inversion additionally needs d nonzero modulo R. The final native provenance audit supplies the exact bound d<=J_sector<=K<R, since a row has at most one hit in its short q-clock and there are at most K actual rows per bin. No claim that J=soft k alone proves J<R is made. Across the composite ring unequal first-slice orders may already give a proper gcd; matching orders can give a unit amplitude after the usual guarded coefficient handling. See the companion retrospective-provenance.md for the full quadratic-algebra and content/axis bookkeeping.

Now test the old acquired endpoint recurrence, first for one row with its fixed integer tag r. Set

    P_y(delta,Y)=product_(i=0)^(h-1) f(y,x_i;delta,Y,r).

There is an exact covariance, without any rescaling of Y:

    f(y/q,x;delta,Y,r)=f(y,q*x;delta,Y,r).

It gives the cleared polynomial identity

    f(y,x_h;delta,Y,r)*P_y(delta,Y)
      = f(y,x_0;delta,Y,r)*P_(y/q)(delta,Y).       (1)

All endpoint inputs are cheap and all factors are polynomial, so (1) holds at singularities over Z/N. An interior hit y=x_i, 1<=i<h, is accompanied in the transported product by y/q=x_(i-1). Both factors are the **same marked factor** delta*(1-Y*r); they cancel coefficientwise in the multiplicative difference. Thus even replacing a root-position derivative by the newly useful integer-tag derivative does not rescue the bulk mark from this boundary.

For the complete coloured support, further lattice differences give the old eight-shift identity. Carries simply move x_0 and x_h by known powers; literal deletion terms are added explicitly. They do not reintroduce the cancelled interior factor. Moving a row to an auxiliary boundary state also does not automatically preserve the nonaxis/content/correct-bin provenance theorem; an out-of-family boundary zero needs its own legal label recovery and is not automatically a factor-residue observation.

In first-slice language, the endpoint relation determines changes in tagged hit counts and their leading factors between shifted queries. It does not initialize one bulk tagged slice. In a run with no endpoint hits, the same unknown bulk factor occurs on both sides. The desired aggregate U/V could therefore be easier than a full amplitude, but the acquired endpoint identity does not compute it. The precise proposed new terminal primitive is: acquire the two first-slice coefficients U,V for one content stratum (with controlled composite-ring branching), without first acquiring the whole long product. This is an updated old first-variation/subresultant target with a simpler factor-provenanced tag, not an acquired scalar result.

Charging execution: ordinary arithmetic or automatic differentiation through the existing complete query still pays its whole query cost, and jets add precision-dependent overhead. Cancelling known endpoint automorphy factors does not pay for U,V. I found no old initialized character/twist recurrence whose output equals these coefficients. A new algorithm for their ratio would genuinely bolt onto the HH consumer: n_g>=sqrt(k) permits a palette of up to order k^(1/6) at that modulus, or suitable combination across strata, subject to the actual coverage and cost bookkeeping.

## 4. Smaller conductor and interval correlation: two tempting retunings

**Cyclotomic retuning.** The old streaming cost soft m² becomes at most soft k if m is reduced to about k^(1/3) (indeed it is then soft k^(2/3)). This is a real budget observation, not a supplier theorem. The cited translated-row theorem assumes an odd prime m, N<=m^6 and a bounded own index. Those promises fail at m~N^(1/18), and that m is not automatically a divisor of native k. Replacing it by a 3-power divisor requires a new composite-modulus construction/coverage proof. More importantly, even the full acquired coefficient list has no proved true-tag readout; the missing raw jet is not supplied by writing more coefficients modulo X^m-1. Hence lower cost alone does not establish the required factor residue. This is a precise possible retuning task, not grounds for discarding all small-conductor representations.

**All-colour rectangles.** The old public dyadic/Gauss menus can be restricted to any supplied colour, but their all-colour coverage is existential over that colour. Merely projecting all full colours to n, or attaching the same dyadic factor range to each projected colour, retains essentially n possible residue/range pairs per scale: Delta/n remains of constant or polylogarithmic order, rather than k^(-1/3). Splitting every interval into T parts reduces each width by T and increases their count by T, leaving the summed Delta unchanged. This calculation concerns that explicit menu, not a bound on all arithmetic rectangle suppliers. To exploit HH, some **measured selection or proven joint correlation** must remove enough pairs; separately existing colour coverage and interval coverage cannot be multiplied into such a claim.

## Disposition

Old representations contain genuine paid inputs—characters, all native phases, small boundary polynomials, deformed twist coefficients and some recurrence initial states. The reviewed interfaces do not already output the new factor-residue/rectangle information. The most promising newly narrowed handoff is the content-stratified integer-tag first-slice ratio, since all nonsplit hits share one actual factor residue and uniqueness of the original root is unnecessary. The exact old endpoint transform was tested against that target above; it loses the entire mark and leaves its bulk initialization unpaid. No general impossibility or sixth-exponent solution is claimed.
