# Adaptive Euclidean row choices still need large geometric carriers

2026-10-16. New classical arithmetic scope, independently reviewed. The
preceding channel-acquisition turn was no acquisition progress. This pass
first found no norm/determinant/lattice extractor, then closed a concrete
escape from the existing short-row carrier obstruction: choosing the
Euclidean row after seeing the higher input digits or even the factors.

The result excludes a few nearlinear-size geometric carriers for the full
tag family, including choices from all literal Euclidean intermediates.
It is not an arithmetic-operation lower bound. The long-query bound
remains soft O(m^(3/2)); a soft O(m) sufficient factor query and the full
Lean sixth-rate factorizer remain unproved.

## What the new proof establishes

[Primitive hyperbolic catalog](reports/primitive-hyperbolic-catalog.md).
For each tag, ALL primitive pairs with 0<|a|,t<m, |a|t<m and
a*j^2=N*t mod m number at most 81*ceil(log_2 m). A public square table
and harmonic-size enumeration construct the entire catalog nearlinearly,
without a modular square-root oracle. Given N,m,gamma, the complete
lifted-target setup costs soft O(m*ell^2) bits, ell=ceil(log_2(N+1));
this excludes long-product evaluation and charges gamma setup separately.

Coloring this catalog into caps on u^2=n*a*t, retaining repeated field
points across colors as WEIGHTED occurrences, gives its cone-energy
bound. Averaging the exact centered phases over the next input digit
then bounds the entire raw catalog before ANY row policy is chosen.
Thus the bound holds simultaneously for higher-digit-dependent and
factor-advised choices, with no union over policies.

[Complete Euclidean family](reports/full-ray-tag-trimming.md). Some
intermediate rays lie outside that hyperbolic region. The existing
compiled O(m log^2 m) TOTAL packet bound instead allows deterministic
low-digit tag trimming: tags with more than 64*(floor(log_2 m)+1)^4
distinct rows account for o(m) tags. Every full-tag row selection still
contains a large selected subset of the remaining tags. The same energy
argument applies there, including all native intermediate rays.

On the SAME original balanced prime-pair supply, the selector telescope
removes O(K_*M^6/lambda) pairs. At every surviving current m, every such
advised full-tag selection has a subset of at least m/3 distinct GLOBAL
targets with energy at most lambda*polylog(m)*m^(5/2). Consequently every
cover of the COMPLETE selected target family by k geometric progressions,
with total listed length T, satisfies

    k^2*T >= m^(3/2)/(lambda*polylog(m)).

For polylogarithmic menus, lambda=K_*log^4 M retains an original majority
and gives k^2*T=soft Omega(m^(3/2)). For the larger frozen K_*=O(M^.020),
lambda_m=m^.04 gives the explicit m^1.46/polylog(m) obstruction with
O(M^5.980) pair loss. Starts, ratios, holes, overlap and chosen covers
may all be privately advised.

## Why the scope matters

The earlier theorem fixed one low-digit-dependent short row at each tag.
The new argument dominates all candidates first and only then quantifies
over advised row choices. The full-ray corollary uses a low-energy
SUBSET as a witness against a cover of the entire family. It does not
claim an energy bound for the untrimmed full source, and does not assume
that trimming preserves a useful collision. The complete native source
budget and its generation are inherited; their mere existence is not
counted as the new advance.

One chosen origin per source entry is allowed when its relative span is
polylogarithmic, under the reports' explicit no-wrap inequalities. A huge
common origin cancels. The actual publicShift/reflectedShift centers can
have a larger relative span and are not automatically included. Tiny
privately chosen tag subsets, unrestricted origins, high-productset
carriers, many carriers sharing operations and implicit scalar methods
remain open. No general acquisition impossibility is asserted.

The next acquisition investigation should therefore avoid relying on
adapting Euclidean rays to fit a few small progressions. A successful
method must exploit another form of shared computation, or justify a
different source/origin regime and its collision coverage. It must still
pay a restriction-stable sufficient factor query and the complete
bit/controller costs before the Lean sixth-rate composition can close.
