## Node guards and saturated localization

After exact INTEGER E deduplication, F'(y_j)=prod_(l!=j)(y_j-y_l). A unit derivative value certifies every factor is a unit at both primes. A proper gcd factors. A saturated value guarantees some nonunit difference at that node; one O(J) scan either finds a proper gcd or a saturated equality beta^(E_j-E_l)=1. The exponent is nonzero by exact-E deduplication and has O(log N) bits. The licensed large-odd-order annihilator consumer is applicable. Only one such scan can occur before termination, so the stated softly linear preparation accounting holds.

This guard genuinely excludes p-local coincidences among the ORIGINAL nodes on a continued branch. It does not infer separation from a large local-order lower bound or from integer inequality alone.

## Fixed shift, source orientation, and complete local certificate

For supplied h of the stated short-bit size, x_j=q^h*y_j is acquired in soft J total. Monic multipoint evaluation is valid over R and does not invert a collision or require point separation. The gcd cases are exhaustive:

* A unit F(x_j) excludes every local target-node match at either prime.
* A proper gcd factors immediately.
* If F(x_j)=0 globally and x_j equals an acquired global node y_l, the known separation certificate makes l the unique local target at BOTH primes. Its literal gap is E_l-E_j-Hh. Nonzero gap gives a licensed global short annihilator; zero gap gives the literal edge.
* If the saturated value has no global matching node, any nonunit difference y_l-x_j is proper. A saturated difference would contradict the global lookup. Since each field product is zero, such a nonunit difference exists; one scan or tree descent returns a factor.

Hence no mixed CRT target indices hide behind a globally zero polynomial value. On the continued branch the iff statement holds at both primes for EVERY ordered pair of nodes, including p-local aliases.

Thus the gcd is the product over source nodes with E_j+Hh in the list, not the product over their target nodes. Certificates also prove this is the full field gcd at both primes. Because each noncommon node has unit evaluation, the split CRT description modulo F also justifies the corresponding monic common-divisor/ideal interpretation over R, without a generic ring Euclidean oracle.

    P²-((N+1)P-N)=-P(P-1)(Q-1)

and its Q analogue annihilate the exponent of beta at both primes. Expanding gives exactly

    beta^((aP-bQ)²)
      =beta^[-N(a+b)²]

The right side is a paid FORMAL element of the guarded rank-two algebra, with short-bit exponents and soft J total work.
