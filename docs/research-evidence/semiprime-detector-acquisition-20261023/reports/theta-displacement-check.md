# Focused finite-theta / displacement check

23 October 2026. Scratch only; all 234 frozen entries are untouched.

**Verdict: no new detector.** The finite theta endpoint products below really are acquired in soft-O(r), but their corrections remain inside the transformed leaves. Standard Frobenius specialization degenerates, and the acquired low displacement rank concerns an ordinary matrix determinant rather than the product of its entries. This stops the tested avenue; no missing evaluation is assigned zero cost.

## 1. Exact finite theta correction, with legal units

Write beta for the common curvature base beta0, and put p=beta^2. At row v let V_v=rho_v^(B_v), beta=rho_v^(a_v), and

    x_v=-beta V_v.

For any positive L define the finite Laurent polynomial

    Theta_L(x;p)=(x;p)_L (p/x;p)_L
      =product_(j=0)^(L-1)(1-x p^j) product_(j=1)^L(1-p^j/x).

Direct cancellation of finite products, without invoking an infinite theta value, gives

    Theta_L(p x;p)/Theta_L(x;p)
      =-x^(-1)(1-x p^L)/(1-p^L/x),

and, for c>=0,

    Theta_L(p^c x;p)/Theta_L(x;p)
      =(-x)^(-c) p^(-c(c-1)/2) B_c(x),

    B_c(x)=product_(j=0)^(c-1)
              (1-x p^(L+j))/(1-p^(L-j)/x).                    (1)

Consequently the exact finite replacement for the proposed chirp identity is

    beta^(c^2) V_v^c
      =Theta_L(x_v;p) B_c(x_v)/Theta_L(p^c x_v;p).              (2)

Every displayed theta and boundary factor is a unit modulo both hidden primes. Indeed beta and V_v lie in the odd-order subgroup generated by rho_v; x_v is its negative. All factors in question therefore have the form 1+rho_v^J. Such a factor cannot vanish in an odd prime field, because -1 does not belong to an odd-order subgroup. The inversions in (1)-(2) are legal, including negative exponents.

For a source leaf, (2) gives the exact numerator

    Theta_L(p^c x_v;p)
      -W_v Q_v^(-sigma) Theta_L(x_v;p) B_c(x_v).                (3)

It is divided by the unit Theta_L(p^c x_v;p). Removing B_c from inside (3) changes the leaf. Unit status does not justify that removal. Already B_1=(1-xp^L)/(1-p^L/x) is generally not 1; equality would require x^2=1. No supplied source congruence establishes that condition for its rows.

## 2. The aggregate finite endpoint units are actually paid

Take c=1,...,n, n=r-1, L=O(r), and let G=soft-O(r) be the number of row descriptors. Build the two scalar row-root polynomials

    F_+(Z)=product_v(1-x_v Z),
    F_-(Z)=product_v(1-Z/x_v).

Rearranging the finite product in (1) gives

    product_v product_(c=1)^n B_c(x_v)
      =product_(j=0)^(n-1)
          [F_+(p^(L+j))/F_-(p^(L-j))]^(n-j).                  (4)

Thus one ordinary multipoint evaluation of each degree-G polynomial at O(n) supplied GP points, followed by legal unit inversion and integer powering, acquires (4) in soft-O((G+n)(log N)^2) bit work and soft-O((G+n)log N) workspace. Repeated evaluation nodes are allowed; monic product/remainder trees require no inverses of their differences.

The aggregate product of Theta_L(p^c x_v;p) is likewise paid in soft-O(G+n+L), with the same logarithmic bit factors. Its first factors use F_+(p^t) for 1<=t<=n+L-1, with multiplicity equal to the number of pairs c+j=t, 1<=c<=n, 0<=j<L. Its second factors use F_-(p^t) for 1-n<=t<=L-1, with the analogous multiplicity j-c=t, 1<=j<=L. All multiplicities are <=min(n,L). These are O(n+L) points and explicitly computed triangular weights.

The product over an additional common sigma interval merely raises these aggregate units to its known length. Hence global endpoint-unit acquisition is not the remaining obstacle. It does not evaluate the product of the mixed differences (3): their B_c factors cannot be factored out without replacing the other term by the inverse chirp, which restores the original problem.

## 3. Why standard Frobenius does not perform this evaluation

[Rosengren–Schlosser, Corollary 4.7](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~schlosse/pdf/determinants-new.pdf) gives the Frobenius elliptic Cauchy determinant, whose entries contain theta(t a_j x_i)/theta(a_j x_i) and whose product side includes theta(a_i/a_j). Its theta function is the genuine infinite/formal-nome theta satisfying exact quasiperiodicity, not Theta_L without the correction (1).

Even granting such theta values, taking the proposed column parameters a_c=p^c degenerates the identity. Exact quasiperiodicity gives

    theta(t p^c x)/theta(p^c x)
      =t^(-c) theta(t x)/theta(x).

The matrix is then rank one wherever these ratios are defined, and the product side has theta(p^(c-d))=0 for distinct columns. Thus this direct Frobenius substitution does not supply the desired entry product. A nondegenerate finite or confluent identity incorporating (3) would be an additional theorem; none was obtained.

Over a field with p!=0, there is also no nonzero rational function of a free variable x satisfying the exact theta equation f(px)=-x^(-1)f(x): scaling x leaves the degree at infinity unchanged, whereas multiplying by x^(-1) changes it. This only rules out that literal rational replacement; it is not a lower bound on evaluations on the supplied finite source fiber.

## 4. The true low displacement rank, and the different missing quantity

For one fixed sigma, reindex a consecutive multiplier interval to start at zero, absorbing its known initial shift into W_v and V_v, and put

    M_(v,c)=W_v Q_v^(-sigma) beta^(c^2) V_v^c,
    L_(v,c)=1-M_(v,c),  0<=c<n.

Let D_V=diag(V_v), and let Z be the weighted nilpotent shift with

    Z_(c+1,c)=beta^(-2c-1),  0<=c<n-1.

Since M_(v,c+1)=beta^(2c+1)V_v M_(v,c), D_V M-M Z has only its last column nonzero. Its displacement rank is at most one. For the leaf matrix L, D_V L-L Z is the sum of that last-column matrix and two rank-one matrices arising from the all-ones matrix. Its rank is therefore at most three. The generators are acquired in soft-O(G+n), including the actual cubic-carry V values.

These are legitimate linear-algebra structures. [Bostan–Jeannerod–Mouilleron–Schost](https://mathexp.eu/bostan/publications/BoJeMoSc17.pdf) study fast matrix products, ordinary inversion and linear solving from such displacement generators, with their stated field and regularization hypotheses. Neither ordinary determinant nor ordinary matrix inversion detects a zero **entry** of L. The required quantity is product_(v,c) L_(v,c), and additionally its sigma product.

The Cauchy-determinant rescue would need an appropriate structure for the entrywise reciprocal R_(v,c)=1/(1-M_(v,c)). It does not follow from the displayed generator for L. In the interior columns, writing d=beta^(2c+1), v=V_v and m=M_(v,c), its same-operator displacement is exactly

    v/(1-m)-d^(-1)/(1-d v m)
      =[(v-d^(-1))-m(d v^2-d^(-1))]/[(1-m)(1-d v m)].            (5)

Formula (5) retains paired leaf denominators. No bounded generator for this reciprocal matrix, Cauchy determinant factorization preserving the leaf-zero support, or paid finite transfer evaluation was found. The fixed-a W GP and canonical cubic V clock do not by themselves remove those denominators. Computing the entry product explicitly still expands the multiplier dimension; the acquired rank-three generator alone does not improve its cost.
