Status: **proved cancellation and executed original-label selection; full original-input primitive-bit scaling remains open**.

The original source objective can be evaluated through two actual collision counts. If L is the candidate alphabet length, each term has at most four active positions. Its true completions are counted in their original order, then padded by L^(4-m). Summing incidence and head lanes gives I,H and the exact original score

\[
  s=(y^2I+x^2H)/L^4-\mathrm{offset}.
\]

The original bank U=r^(2Q) satisfies L^4=U^2. With scheduled x=M/(r lambda U), y=n/(2r), the positive factor gives

\[
  4r^2\lambda^2U^4(s+\mathrm{offset})
    =n^2\lambda^2U^2I+4M^2H.
\]

Thus comparisons use the integer on the right. The shared offset and denominators cancel, preserving equality and the exact original tie rule. The runtime source search no longer constructs rational scales or an offset, and it performs no GCD-backed rational score arithmetic.
