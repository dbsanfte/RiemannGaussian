## Public algebra and paid normalization

Let ell be a public auxiliary prime, k|ell−1, gcd(N,ell k)=1, A=(Z/N)[X]/(X^k−1), and proof-side B=A[Z]/Phi_ell(Z). Let g_a=G_a be the reviewed Gauss family and bar=rho_−1 acting on X and FIXING Z. This bar is coefficient-character inversion, not complex conjugation of both root systems. Let t be a PUBLIC unit with t²=gamma; it is supplied by initially preparing/rebasing the scalar base, not by a hidden-order root oracle. Define

    h_a=t g_a/g_−a.

Every Gauss factor is a unit by the reviewed exceptional-character formulas. In a component where the character indexed by a is trivial, g_a=g_−a=−1 and h_a=t. In all components,

    h_a bar(h_a)=gamma.                            (1)

Thus a WHOLE local-algebra equality h_1^K=1 implies gamma^K=1 at that factor by applying bar and multiplying. No claim is made that a SINGLE component equality implies the same scalar conclusion.

Write C_E=g_1^E/g_E. The acquired packed element

    A_E=t^(E−1) C_E/bar(C_E)

satisfies h_1^E=A_E h_E and

    A_E bar(A_E)=gamma^(E−1).                     (2)

C_E is paid in soft O(ell+k) for polynomial-size supplied E. bar is a coefficient permutation. Its inverse is legitimate and can be acquired with guarded extended polynomial gcd against X^k−1; a nonunit scalar pivot factors N, otherwise the fast field-algorithm specialization gives soft O(k) bit work. Since C_E is proved a unit, successful inversion exists. This is a paid ONE-element operation, not k² conjugate outputs. Formula(2) includes E=0, where C_0=−1 and A_0=t^(−1), and E=1 where A_1=1.

Actual h_a generally remains in the JOINT B carrier of rank k(ell−1); the paid ratio A_E alone does not acquire the full h_a vector at that lower cost. Its homogeneous identities can reduce particular grade-zero expressions to A, as below. A source representation in B is not charged as rank k.

## Hidden Frobenius and full native corrected coverage

For P|N let b=P mod ell and j=P modk. True Frobenius is phi=sigma_b rho_j. Locally t^P=t because t is a scalar residue. The exact Gauss formula gives

    h_1^P=chi(P)^(−2P) h_P modP.                  (3)

This retains twice the original character grade. It does not erase the unknown coefficient action rho_j. Trivial character components satisfy it as well.

For native cancellation take k=m, native a_j j²=N modm, ell_j=m b_j−2a_jj and s=j^(−1) modm. Put u=h_1 and E_j=N+a_j+ell_j. The PUBLIC corrected expression is

    T_j=u^(ell_j) h_j^(a_j) rho_s(A_N) h_(Ns).     (4)

Under phi, h_j picks up phase chi(b)^(−2j), and phi^(−1)(u^N) picks up phase chi(b)^(2Ns) multiplying rho_s(A_N)h_(Ns). The total phase2(Ns−a_jj) is0 modm. Therefore at the OWN true tag,

    T_j=u^(ell_j) (phi u)^(a_j) phi^(−1)(u^N)
       =u^(ell_j+a_jP+Q)
       =u^(m² I)

in the ENTIRE local algebra. The last equality is the exact native integer quotient relation. This is a genuine repaired full-algebra source coverage theorem, not merely a trivial-component collision or a scalar-Fermat argument. It does not require u to be an F_P scalar.

Every factor in(4) has a known bar norm. Since rho_s commutes with bar and fixes rational scalars, equations(1),(2) give the unconditional public identity

    T_j bar(T_j)=gamma^(ell_j+a_j+N)=gamma^E_j,
    q_h=u^(m²), q_h bar(q_h)=gamma^(m²).          (5)

This holds for EVERY tag, not only the true one.

The additive-root grade of h_a is2a, and the grade of(4) is2(ell_j+a_jj+Ns)=0 modm. Thus T_j and q_h have grade zero for odd prime m; their appropriate homogeneous reductions live in the smaller character algebra A. One requested row can be reduced using packed A-exponents/quotients in soft O(ell+m), analogously to the previously reviewed homogeneous Gauss construction. The whole m-row family of degree-m vectors is not acquired in softm: explicit output is quadratic. Supply of an auxiliary ell=1modm at affordable size also remains a separate requirement. A generic ell~m does not automatically have the prescribed order m.

## Same-leaf paired recovery is valid

For a leaf i define

    f_(j,i)=T_j−q_h^i,
    bar(f_(j,i))=bar(T_j)−bar(q_h)^i.

Then the exact identity is

    bar(T_j) f_(j,i)+q_h^i bar(f_(j,i))
       =gamma^E_j−gamma^(m²i).                   (6)

Hence if f and bar(f) vanish in the SAME local component, the original scalar difference vanishes at that hidden prime. The retained nonzero gap E_j−m²i and decoder apply. In particular a WHOLE local-algebra leaf equality satisfies this paired condition automatically.

The exact repair needed by a component-based controller is thus a SAME-LEAF paired witness, or whole-local-algebra leaf, not an arbitrary character-component root. Once supplied, equation(6) returns an original scalar factor/annihilator using only public quantities and the original integer ledger. One can simply compute gcd(gamma^E_j−gamma^(m²i),N). A proper value factors; saturation invokes the original decoder. No new unknown u-order contract is needed for this certified leaf.

## Why product norms and component gcds do not yet localize that witness

If a product U=product_(j,i)f_(j,i) is nonunit in A/P, some component of some leaf vanishes. This need NOT be paired with its bar component at that SAME leaf. Even if both U and bar(U) vanish at a chosen component, their zero factors can be DIFFERENT leaves. Applying(6) with two unrelated indices is invalid. Likewise a resultant/norm of U over all character components can be nonunit solely from one unpaired component hit. No gamma annihilator follows merely by multiplying that component phase by its conjugate elsewhere in the product.

Taking the coefficient content gcd of U tests the stronger statement “U is zero in the WHOLE local algebra.” True own-tag leaf coverage guarantees this for the complete query. But a whole-algebra ZERO PRODUCT can be assembled from complementary zero-divisor factors: the two child products may each vanish on different character components and neither be the zero element. Thus ordinary scalar one-path content descent is not automatically sound. A saturated child may represent distributed component coverage with no whole-algebra leaf. Choosing it can discard the genuine own-tag leaf in the other branch.

This is an exact failure of that proposed inference, not an impossibility theorem for a more sophisticated controller. Backtracking, carrying component support factors, or computing paired-leaf gcds can repair correctness, but their total number of branches/degree operations must be bounded. No such softm bound is proved here. A guarded polynomial gcd may expose N if the P,Q component patterns have differing degree/pivots, but equal patterns are a permitted remaining branch and are not guaranteed to factor.

Computing a SINGLE leaf resultant or its paired polynomial gcd is cheap after acquiring that leaf's degree-m coefficients. Enumerating all m-by-m² leaves, or all degree-m row targets, is not paid by the packed A_E primitive. A full B-carrier controller incurs rank m(ell−1) arithmetic and cannot be called softm merely because the proof writes one algebra element.
