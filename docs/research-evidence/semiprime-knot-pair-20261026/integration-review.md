# Integration review of the pair packet

26 October 2026. Read-only review of `twist-lift.md`, `scalar-audit.md`, `block-contraction.md`, and `nichols-rank-four.md`; no new research or tests.

**The positive result is accurately scoped.** `twist-lift.md` specifies an actual alternative native knot as a connected sum of negative binary-encoded twist knots. Its Alexander polynomial reduces to the acquired K, while Kohli's negative-twist formula and connected-sum multiplicativity acquire a degree-at-most-2L polynomial F(B) in soft O(L) bit cost. The concatenation recurrence initializes all large twist counts without division by u−1; hence this is a paid coefficient representation of the entire specified deformed scalar family at fixed u=q^-1, not an implicit diagram or a promise to evaluate an unacquired invariant. The source formula, rather than an identification of a sparse Seifert matrix with a particular knot, fixes its topology and convention.

This acquires F, not every orbit value F(B_i), their product, or a collision predicate. The original paired Alexander polynomial differs by the explicit correction (6). A unit constant coefficient of that correction proves it is not identically zero; it does not prove every evaluated correction is a unit. The report makes this distinction correctly. A deformed zero has no established original-label recovery or coverage implication. Pairing changes only a constant factor in the number of clock positions. There is no whole-query or coverage gain.

**The two failure/caution statements are logically distinct.** `scalar-audit.md` uses a primary normalized Links–Gould figure-eight formula and an invariant-level parameter dictionary. Its resultant25 certificate disproves the universal zero-preserving replacement for arbitrary knots. It expressly does not assert an attained complete-native-catalogue counterexample, nor that the selected twist-lift knot contains the requisite figure-eight summand. Its achiral example and separately inversion-invariant formula avoid the trefoil convention issue.

By contrast, `nichols-rank-four.md` checks an explicit raw local R, its rigidity, enhancement, and one cut-trefoil scalar. The appended caveat correctly records that its positive-trefoil scalar matches Kohli's displayed trefoil only after simultaneous variable inversion (equivalently an as-yet-unaudited mirror/convention change). This prevents claiming a universal same-variable identification of that raw operator with the canonical invariant. It does not undermine the independent primary-table scalar audit or the directly sourced twist-family formula. Conversely, the polynomial-level invariant identification does not automatically settle all raw matrix/gauge/orientation conventions.

`block-contraction.md` separately gives a lawful ordinary-exterior geometric block and initialized soft O(L+b) evaluator with an explicit unit normalization. Its Q_(2b)(Y)=Q_b(Y)Q_b(q^bY) composition retains shifted evaluations; it pays no stronger whole-prefix contraction. This remains compatible with the positive twist input: both remove specific acquisition gaps, neither removes the orbit-product or zero-recovery gap.

One chronological wording should be interpreted as superseded: the final paragraph of `scalar-audit.md` calls the twist acquisition refinement “open.” `twist-lift.md` now accomplishes that limited refinement. The remaining open part is the native collision-preserving bridge and full-family contraction, not acquisition of its chosen twist-family coefficient polynomial. Likewise a general arbitrary-knot Seifert-to-coupled-invariant algorithm remains outside the result; the positive result deliberately chooses its knot lift.

Overall the packet supports a paid soft-O(L) deformed-family representation, an independent arbitrary-knot zero-predicate failure, and a separate raw-R convention caveat. It supports no new semiprime factoring bound.
