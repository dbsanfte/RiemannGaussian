# Uniform shift boundary on the actual quadratic target catalog

2026-10-16. New classical arithmetic result: every nonidentity multiplicative shift of the actual carrier has at least ceil((m-1)/4) dangling nodes in each applicable hidden field. The shift may be chosen with full knowledge of the input and hidden local order. No new exceptional-input deletion is needed. Consequently regular lossless fixed-carrier weighted descent cannot be repaired merely by choosing a different stride. This is not an operation lower bound or a whole-query algorithm.

## 1. Exact contract

Let m be an odd prime, m not divide N, d=m^2. For original tags j=1,...,m-1 use the literal integer offsets delta_j, satisfying

 delta_j in [-3d,d],
 delta_j mod m = N(j^(-2)-2j^(-1)) mod m.          (1)

Let Delta be their set of distinct INTEGER values, J=|Delta|. Let K be a field containing gamma of finite order R>8d. Put z_delta=gamma^(-N-delta). Distinct offsets give distinct nodes because their difference has absolute value at most 4d<R. Denote the node set by Z and its monic polynomial by H(t).

For any rho in K^*, rho!=1, define

 Dang(rho)={z in Z: rho*z not in Z}.

Then

 |Dang(rho)| >= ceil((m-1)/4).                    (2)

If rho lies outside the cyclic subgroup generated by gamma, all J nodes are dangling. If rho belongs to that subgroup, let v be the unique integer in [-4d,4d] with gamma^v=rho, if one exists. If no such v exists, again all J nodes are dangling. If v exists and m divides v, the stronger bound |Dang(rho)|>=ceil(J/2) holds. If v exists and c=v mod m is nonzero, then

 |Dang(rho)| >= (m+1-chi(c/N)-chi(-c/N))/4,        (3)

where chi is the quadratic character of F_m extended by chi(0)=0. Division by N occurs only in F_m, where N is nonzero. These are bounds on the deduplicated carrier, not counts of repeated original tags.

## 2. The actual residue image is exactly a translated square set

As j runs over F_m^*, t=j^(-1) runs over F_m^*. Completing the square in (1) gives

 N(t^2-2t)=N((t-1)^2-1).

The variable u=t-1 runs over F_m excluding -1. This omission removes no square value: the only affected value is 1, and it is also attained by u=+1, which is allowed because m is odd. Therefore the residue image of the literal catalog is EXACTLY

 A=N(S-1),  S={u^2:u in F_m}.                    (4)

In particular |A|=(m+1)/2. Every residue in A has at least one node in the deduplicated integer carrier and at most two, since a quadratic equation has at most two original solutions t. At residues 0 and -N there is exactly one original tag (respectively t=2 and t=1); the other represented residues have two. Deduplication can merge tags only within their residue class and cannot remove a class from A. Hence (m+1)/2<=J<=m-1.

## 3. Exact additive boundary of the square set

For h!=0 in F_m let I_h=|S intersect (S+h)|. The square-set indicator is

 1_S(x)=(1+chi(x)+1_(x=0))/2.

The correlation is sum_x chi(x)chi(x-h)=-1. Here is an elementary proof, so the result does not require importing a character-sum theorem. The number of pairs (x,y) satisfying y^2=x(x-h) equals m+sum_x chi(x(x-h)). Equivalently

 (2x-h-2y)(2x-h+2y)=h^2.

Since h!=0 and 2 is invertible, choosing either nonzero factor determines the other and uniquely determines x,y. There are m-1 choices. Thus the sum is -1.

Expanding the two indicators now gives

 4 I_h=m-1+2+chi(h)+chi(-h)
       =m+1+chi(h)+chi(-h).

Consequently

 |S minus (S+h)|=(m+1-chi(h)-chi(-h))/4
                 >=ceil((m-1)/4).               (5)

The ceiling follows because the left side is an integer. By the affine change in (4), |A minus (A+c)| is (5) with h=c/N. The constant in this exact formula is m+1, not m-1; the latter would mishandle the two zero-indicator contributions.

## 4. Lift the additive boundary to every multiplicative stride

If rho*z_delta=z_epsilon, then rho=gamma^(delta-epsilon). Thus rho must lie in the gamma subgroup and must have a representative v=delta-epsilon in [-4d,4d]. Two such representatives differ by a multiple of R and by at most 8d<R, so v is unique. Also v!=0 because rho!=1.

If c=v mod m is nonzero, every residue a in A minus (A+c) has no successor residue a-c in A. Every node above a is therefore dangling. Selecting one node for each such residue proves (3) and (2).

If m divides v, every edge delta -> delta-v stays inside a single low residue class. That class has at most two distinct offsets. Integer translation by the nonzero v has no directed cycle; within a two-element class there is at most one edge. Hence each component is an isolated node or a single directed pair, and at least ceil(J/2) nodes have no successor. This proves the remaining cases.

The proof holds for ALL rho simultaneously on each qualifying actual input. There is no union bound over strides, and the stride need not be prescribed independently of N or of the hidden order. In particular it covers every nonidentity q^s when q=gamma^d. The stronger actual promise R>16d implies the order hypothesis used here.

## 5. Consequences for weighted descent and correction rank

For a fixed rho, let w=A_0/B_0 be regular at all nodes, with B_0 invertible modulo H. The representative-independent rule

 f(t) mod H -> w(t)f(rho*t) mod H

exists iff H divides A_0(t)H(rho*t). Indeed changing f by Hg changes the output by w H(rho*t)g(rho*t), and taking g=1 gives necessity. Sufficiency is immediate. At every dangling node H(rho*z)!=0, so any descending A_0 must vanish there. If A_0 is nonzero as a polynomial over K, its degree is at least (2), with the sharper case bounds above. Any descending regular weight vanishes at all these nodes.

On the inherited retained actual family, all but o(m) row products F(z) are nonzero in each hidden field. Thus every such descending weight kills at least ceil((m-1)/4)-o(m) nonzero row payloads. A regular weight which preserves those nonzero values cannot descend for ANY nonidentity stride. For H^s the same argument gives H^s dividing A_0 H(rho*t)^s, forcing numerator vanishing order at least s at each dangling node and killing the corresponding regular s-jet. These statements concern the supplied carrier and the stated regular homogeneous descent contract.

For arbitrary representative changes Hg, deg g<J, the defect map is

 g -> (w(z)H(rho*z)g(rho*z))_(z in Z).

Since rho*z are J distinct nodes, their evaluation matrix is invertible over K. Its rank is exactly the number of nonzero diagonal factors w(z)H(rho*z). If w stays nonzero on the original nonzero rows, that rank is at least ceil((m-1)/4)-o(m). This is an algebraic rank, not an arithmetic-operation bound.

All conclusions above are FIELDWISE. For rho over Z/N, nonidentity modulo N alone need not mean nonidentity in both fields. A proper gcd(rho-1,N) already gives a factor; gcd=1 makes rho nonidentity in both fields, whereas gcd=N is the identity. If a descending weight creates proper nonunits at nodes, factor-aware processing may exploit them; this theorem does not dismiss that possibility. No assumption of cheap access to the hidden order or its discrete logarithms is made or provided.

## 6. Exact scope and costs

The new result extends the previous actual q-stride weighted-descent obstruction to every nonidentity scalar stride. It requires no new input deletion and no generic specialization. The character calculation is a proof about finite sets, not an instruction to enumerate m values in each hidden field. No oracle, algorithm or uncharged parameter acquisition is inferred from it.

It does not prohibit canonical interpolated matrices, fixed-F identities, gauges with shifted poles, inhomogeneous boundary corrections, finite-grid methods, or a different carrier. In particular it neither acquires the actual quotient-tail forcing nor improves the general soft O(J+sqrt(JL)) bound. An Omega(m) defect rank is compatible with a soft O(m) structured correction algorithm. All ten prior evidence bundles are unchanged. This report is a classical proof; no native declaration, build or numerical experiment is claimed.

## 7. Uniform powered-node compatibility defect

There is a further exact consequence for the proposed powered-node generator conversion. Put Z=diag(z_i), V_(i,s)=z_i^s for 0<=s<J, and Q_rho=V diag(1,rho,...,rho^(J-1)) V^(-1). This is the canonical interpolation operator on degree<J representatives; no set closure is assumed. For every 1<=b<=J,

 rank(Q_rho Z^b-rho^b Z^b Q_rho)=min(b,|Dang(rho)|).           (6)

Indeed Lagrange interpolation, with H_j=H/(t-z_j), gives (Q_rho)_(i,j)=H_j(rho*z_i)/H'(z_j). The defect entry is exactly

 -H(rho*z_i)/H'(z_j) * sum_(s=0)^(b-1)(rho*z_i)^s z_j^(b-1-s).

This is a polynomial identity even when rho*z_i=z_j; that difference is never inverted. Factor it into the diagonal H(rho*z_i), a J-by-b Vandermonde at rho*z_i, the reversed b-by-J Vandermonde at z_j, and the invertible derivative diagonal. The right rectangular factor is surjective, while the left has rank min(b,|Dang(rho)|). This proves (6).

Choose b as the largest power of two at most J. Since b>J/2 and J>=(m+1)/2, both b and |Dang(rho)| are at least ceil((m-1)/4). Thus (6) is at least ceil((m-1)/4)=Omega(m), for EVERY fieldwise nonidentity scalar rho, including input-dependent or privately advised strides. An explicit polylogarithmic-rank powered-node compatibility factorization is impossible under this contract.

This extends the previous q-only defect calculation; its factorization is reproduced above rather than assuming a new matrix identity. Fast actions of Q_rho or its defect remain possible. Cancellation with other operators, a different generator, fixed-F syzygies, and implicit diagonal/norm acquisition are not excluded. Formula (6) is not a rank theorem for the mixed matrix's ordinary commutator and is not an operation lower bound.
