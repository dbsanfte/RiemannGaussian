RESULT
No constructor for sigma_1(N) mod r in soft O(r^3) was obtained.
Fixed depth 18 makes the required data finite, but neither of the primary
sources checked gives the needed size, construction, or evaluation bound.
This is an open computation, not a lower bound or a no-go for this route.

EXACT FINITE-DEPTH COMPUTATION
Let r>=5 be prime, r not dividing N, and N<r^18. Write
N=sum_{j=0}^{17} d_j r^j. Put f(q)=sum_{n>=1} sigma_1(n)q^n mod r.
It is enough to use (1-E_{r+1})/24 for f: Kummer congruences give
E_{r+1}=E_2 mod r, so their nonconstant coefficients agree.

On integral q-expansions define B_j by
  B_j(q^n)=binomial(n,r^j) q^n.
Lucas's theorem says that B_j acts modulo r by the j-th base-r digit of n.
Thus the commuting projectors
  Pi_{d,j}=1-(B_j-d)^(r-1)
give the exact finite expression
  g=product_{j=0}^{17} Pi_{d_j,j} f
   =sigma_1(N)q^N + O(q^(N+r^18)).
If t=q+O(q^2) is any normalized local parameter, the required scalar is
  sigma_1(N) = (g/t^N)(cusp).
The expression remains valid when that scalar is zero.

This is constructive only after two computations have been supplied:
  (1) build/apply these canonical higher q-operators in compact divided-
      congruence data, including the divisions hidden in B_j;
  (2) evaluate the displayed normalized cusp value in soft O(r^3) total.
No such implementation or bound was found. Expanding the operators is
not the desired algorithm. Formally B_j=binomial(theta,r^j), with
theta=q d/dq, so its degree is r^j, and the factorial denominator has
r-adic valuation (r^j-1)/(r-1). Merely taking 18 digit positions therefore
does not turn this expansion into an r^3 computation. A recurrence or
compact arithmetic circuit could conceivably do better; the sources
checked do not provide the requisite recurrence and evaluation theorem.

WHAT THE SMALL RAMANUJAN MODEL DOES SUPPLY
Ordinary theta gives the first digit projector Pi_{d,0} inside the
mod-r Ramanujan function field, since theta^r=theta on q-expansions.
Movasati constructs the invariant curve and a local eigenparameter for
this derivation. The statement is about the ordinary derivation and
integrality; it does not implement B_1,...,B_17 on canonical q-expansions.
Hornbostel--Naumann review the geometric divided-congruence/Igusa-tower
description and give explicit Artin--Schreier generators in examples.
They do not give a uniform r^3 algorithm for the higher operators above.
In particular, an explicit finite Igusa level is not by itself a proof
that the required functions and normalized cusp values fit that budget.

DOWNSTREAM USE, IF THE COMPUTATION IS SOLVED
Set s=sigma_1(N)-1-N mod r. For distinct-prime semiprime N=PQ, the roots
of X^2-sX+N over F_r are precisely P and Q mod r. Enumerate the roots
and all their r lifts modulo r^2. This is an explicit guaranteed menu of
at most 2r residues, acquired from the one trace in O(r) further work.
With r about N^(1/18), the existing sparse Gauss engine then has soft
O(N^(1/6)) cost. The missing work remains acquisition of the trace.
