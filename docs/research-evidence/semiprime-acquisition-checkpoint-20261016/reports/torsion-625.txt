Torsion acquisition audit, 2026-10-16

Scope: classical deterministic soft-O(N^(1/6)) factorization, N=P Q,
m ~ N^(1/6), with paid preprocessing giving P,Q>16m^2 or a factor.
This audit is scratch research. No Lean theorem was compiled and no repository
file was edited. The findings do not prove impossibility of a new algorithm.

RESULT
No constructive acquisition of the required large rational torsion subgroup,
useful base point, or annihilator K within soft-O(m) was found. The tempting
X_0 versus X_1 degree saving loses the required Frobenius eigenvalue. Known
prescribed-subgroup constructions and the new prime-field factorization
manuscript do not fill this missing step under the allowed assumptions.

1. EXACT X_0 GAP

Let ell != p be prime. An F_p-rational cyclic ell-isogeny kernel C is a
Frobenius-stable line in E[ell]. Frobenius restricts to multiplication by
lambda in F_ell^*. This is insufficient for a subgroup of E(F_p):

  C(F_p) = ker([lambda-1]: C -> C).

For prime ell it has size ell exactly if lambda=1, and size 1 otherwise.
For cyclic composite t with scalar lambda, its size is gcd(t,lambda-1).
Thus merely knowing a rational degree-t isogeny does not imply t divides
#E(F_p), and the rational image need not have index t.

A generic quadratic twist changes Frobenius lambda to -lambda. It supplies
rational ell-torsion only when lambda is +/-1. Isogeny changes within the
same F_p-isogeny class preserve #E(F_p), so ordinary isogeny walking cannot
turn ell nondivisibility into ell divisibility.

Concrete diagnostic: p=421, ell=5, E:y^2=x^3+x+3. Here p>16 ell^2,
#E(F_421)=412, trace=10. The mod-5 Frobenius polynomial is

  T^2 - 10T + 421 = T^2+1 = (T-2)(T-3).

The two eigenspaces give two rational cyclic 5-isogenies, but neither gives
rational nonzero 5-torsion. The nonsquare d=2 quadratic twist has equation
y^2=x^3+4x+24 and order 432, also not divisible by 5. This is independently
counted by check_x0_gap.py. Point counting is an exact diagnostic, not a
kernel-checked proof or a cofinal lower bound.

X_0(ell) has genus O(ell), so its coarse Weil existence scale is compatible
with p >> ell^2. But the map X_1(ell)->X_0(ell) has generic degree
(ell-1)/2: restoring a rational generator restores the lost data and degree.
For p-independent characteristic-zero modular presentations, Abramovich's
gonality bound gives

  gon_C(X_1(ell)) >= 7(ell^2-1)/1600.

This obstructs a generic plane-model projection of degree O(ell) over Q.
It is not a universal complexity lower bound, and this characteristic-zero
bound must not silently be asserted uniformly after reduction modulo p.

2. SMOOTH 2-POWER CHAINS / UNIVERSAL FAMILIES

Replacing ell by 2^k does not let a rational generic construction acquire
unbounded torsion for free. A Q-defined curve or rational one-parameter
family cannot have rational torsion orders growing like m: Mazur's bound
allows point orders only 1..10 or 12 over Q. Any modular chain construction
that actually produces a rational generator still lifts through X_1(2^k).
Its characteristic-zero congruence index is 3*(2^k)^2/8, hence the generic
gonality scale remains quadratic. A chain of rational 2-isogenies only
keeps a stable kernel; it need not keep every point in the kernel rational.

One can instead branch through quadratic root choices in finite fields,
but local roots modulo unknown P,Q are not available as free modular
square roots. Passing to a degree-growing etale algebra only supplies
torsion over extension components. That does not imply t | #E(F_p), and
the extension's group order has the wrong scale for the desired BSGS bound.

3. CLOSEST DETERMINISTIC CONSTRUCTION

Shparlinski--Sutherland, arXiv:1403.7887v3, Theorem 2: given a known prime p
and an integer t=o(sqrt(p)/log^4(p)), full GRH gives a deterministic curve
with t | #E(F_p) in t p^(1/2+o(1)) time. There is an unconditional almost-all
primes variant, not an all-input guarantee. Their tilde convention hides
p^o(1), not just powers of log p.

For balanced p~m^3 and t~m, this is m^(5/2+o(1)), not soft-O(m). Even if
p is only just above 16m^2 it costs at least m^(2+o(1)) at this scale.
The construction explicitly uses p to choose a trace congruent to p+1 mod t,
solve a norm/discriminant selection problem, and reduce Hilbert class
polynomials modulo p. It cannot be invoked on N instead of p.

Sutherland, arXiv:0811.0296v4, constructs optimized models of X_1(t) and
samples points by selecting random x and finding roots of f_t(x,Y) in a
known finite field. The practical fixed-t constructions are valuable ECM
methods; they contain no deterministic short hitting-set guarantee for
growing t~m over an unknown local prime.

4. NEW PRIME-FIELD POLYNOMIAL FACTORIZATION RESULT

OpenAI, Deterministic Polynomial Factorization over Prime Fields,
October 4, 2026, Theorem 1.1, takes an explicitly given prime p and a dense
degree-n polynomial over F_p. Its displayed upper bound is

  O( ((n+1)*ceil(log_2 p))^(10^12) ) bit operations.

It depends on the companion's uniform finite-order Hecke zero-free theorem
for cyclotomic fields, not merely the pinned Dirichlet QRH >7/8 input.
Thus it neither supplies a soft-linear growing-degree subroutine nor stays
within the accepted analytic input. Its dynamic evaluation is over product
algebras over a known finite field. In particular, Section 2.4's even-degree
separator writes p-1=2^s t and uses Delta^(p-1) as an off-diagonal idempotent.
Substituting N for p does not preserve that local identity: p-1 need not
divide N-1. Similar issues arise for the Frobenius fixed algebra. Ordinary
zero-divisor checks can be lifted to Z/N, but the prime-dependent identities
cannot be lifted by that device.

The paper also cites Altman's many-prime deterministic factorization:
input is a monic irreducible integral polynomial, and all prime reductions
below X are processed in total pi(X)*(D log H)^O(1)*log^5 X, where D is
the splitting-field degree. The prime inputs and dependence on D (possibly
factorial in polynomial degree) prevent this amortized statement from giving
the required unknown-local-prime acquisition.

5. ACQUISITION INTERFACE NEEDS MORE THAN TORSION

Suppose the desired explicit order-t subgroup C <= E(F_p) has been supplied.
Vélu produces phi:E->E/C in soft-O(t) field operations, but

  #((E/C)(F_p)) = #E(F_p),
  |phi(E(F_p))| = #E(F_p)/t.

Only the image has the smaller order. A useful supplied R in E(F_p) gives
phi(R) in that image. The distinguished t-torsion point supplied by X_1
maps to zero. Therefore an acquisition theorem for the suggested BSGS
consumer should jointly supply an appropriate base point, or prove a
point-generation procedure within budget. Choosing an arbitrary point on
the quotient does not justify the order bound. This caveat is independent
of the unresolved eventual factor-separation guarantee.

CONCLUSION
The surviving missing result is still constructive acquisition: produce,
over Z/N without knowing either local characteristic, a suitable curve,
point, and locally large pointwise-rational subgroup (or another useful K),
with all root finding, covers, and separation included in soft-O(m). The
audited standard constructions do not establish that result. This report
does not rename an m-smooth projection branch as new progress.
