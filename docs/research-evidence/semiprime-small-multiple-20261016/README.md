# Low-digit carrier plans fail; bounded covers can directly certify a factor

2026-10-16. **New classical arithmetic obstruction**, with a conditional
positive extraction algorithm. The preceding direct-frontier pass was
no acquisition progress. This pass goes beyond its unresolved identities;
it still does not acquire a soft O(m) whole-family query or prove the
guaranteed sixth-rate factorizer in Lean.

The [low-digit-plan theorem](reports/low-digit-plan.md) excludes a prescribed
library of up to M carrier plans per (m,N mod m^2), on a retained majority
of the SAME original balanced, order-qualified prime-pair supply. A plan
fixes origins, grouping and positions from those low digits. Its integers
may have polylogarithmic bit length and arbitrarily large numerical span.
Seeds and ratios may depend on the full input or even the hidden factors;
selection among the prescribed plans may also be fully advised.

If k^2*T is nearlinear, pair/triple elimination within a successful cover
forces a nonzero integer annihilating gamma. With fixed low-digit metadata there
are only O(m) prescribed annihilators, each with few large prime divisors.
The fixed product residue determines one small cofactor after the other
is fixed. Summing ALL comparable moduli gives at most

    soft O(B*M^4.969) successful original pairs.

For B<=M this is soft O(M^5.969), negligible against the original
Omega(M^6/log^2 M) supply. The energy guarantee applies at CURRENT
candidates; the enumeration of all comparable moduli is only a counting
overestimate. This is not an operation lower bound. Metadata using more
digits of N and different shared-computation routes remain open.

The [certificate bridge](reports/carrier-certificate.md) separately allows
full-input-dependent metadata: a supplied sufficiently compressed cover
with T*(H+4)<=8m^4 gives a small nonzero multiple K of the global order.
The [factorial-stripping proof](reports/small-order-multiple.md) recovers
that order and the factors in soft O(m) bits, using composite-safe monic
multipoint evaluation. Acquiring the cover or K is still unpaid. Very
large origin encodings incur their own cost.
