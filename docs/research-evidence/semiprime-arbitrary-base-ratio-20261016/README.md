# Changing the scalar geometric clock does not give a small recurrence

This packet closes the arbitrary-base loophole in the chirped-stream recurrence route. After the already paid setup and alias screen, every scalar geometric clock is covered, including a privately advised field-specific choice. These are finite-segment representation obstructions, not a query algorithm or arithmetic-operation lower bound.

Let d=m², q=g^d and L=2m²+2. Write F(T)=product_(i<L)(1-q^i T)=sum c_k T^k and A_k=c_k g^(-binom(k,2)). The previous proofs treated the g or q clock, or constant-coefficient recurrences. The present results allow an arbitrary nonzero h.

For FIRST ORDER, no rational U(h^k)/V(h^k) of numerator and denominator degree at most m can describe all ratios A_(k+1)/A_k on the certified branch, with denominators nonzero on the sample range. Grouping the four frequency blocks gives a q-directed cancellation graph. Its first occupied vertex and successor force g=h^a and q=h^b with0<|a|,|b|<=m. The paid alias certificate rules out the resulting g^b=q^a. Repeated h-powers and finite composite orders cause no exception.

A stronger companion treats polynomial-coefficient recurrences

 sum_(t=0)^r P_t(h^k) A_(k+t)=0, 0<=k<=L-r, degP_t<=D.

No nonvacuous such recurrence exists when

 r<=m,  2rD<=m,  (r+1)²(D+1)<=L-r-D+1.

Nonvacuous means that at least one coefficient FUNCTION on the h-orbit is not identically zero. This distinction handles finite-order h honestly. The proof allows initially singular coefficients: a first nonzero leading value occurs within D steps, and that sample loss is charged. Lowering the effective shift never extends the original sample range. All fixed or polylogarithmic r,D satisfy these bounds eventually.

After clearing denominators, short frequency aliases must lie on one rational line; otherwise eliminating h gives a forbidden short relation between g and q. Formal two-variable coarsening then exposes a highest-shift pole that cannot cancel. The proof retains numerical field constants separately from the running geometric clocks and is valid in arbitrary characteristic.

The setup and monic alias test together cost soft O(m) bits and either factor the semiprime or certify the needed relation box in both fields. The latest unbalanced setup supplies their hypotheses for every semiprime, on its surviving branch. No additional exceptional input deletion or enumeration of h is involved. The old literal source is not silently replaced or presumed to satisfy an untested alias certificate.
