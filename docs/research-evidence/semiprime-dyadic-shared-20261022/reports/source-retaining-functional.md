# Source-retaining functional attempt: reciprocal pair and scalar projections

22 October 2026. Scratch-only constructive investigation. This note does not establish the requested 1/6 factorization algorithm.

Let m=2^k, N=PQ odd, gamma=t^2 a prepared public unit of odd local orders greater than 16m^2, W=t(1+gamma X)/(X+gamma), Q=W^(m^2), in A=(Z/NZ)[X]/(X^m-1). Native targets are

 Z_j=W^ell_j (rho_j W)^a_j rho_(j^-1)(W^N).

For the true tag j=P mod m, Z_j=Q^I in the WHOLE local algebra modulo P, for the original bounded integer I. The native gap E_j-m^2 I is nonzero and has the original scalar decoder bounds, where E_j=N+a_j+ell_j. Antipodal pairing gives the acquired shared product

 K_j=Z_j Z_-j=gamma^(N+a_j) Q^(-s_j),
 ell_j+ell_-j=-m^2 s_j.

Its shared descriptors are paid; the two individual target vectors are not.

## Exact collision-bearing reciprocal observable

For a supplied seed z=Q^I define V_j=Z_j/z. Then its ACTUAL inverse is

 V_j^-1 = z Z_-j/K_j.

Consequently the reciprocal defect

 D_j(I)=Z_j/z + z Z_-j/K_j -2
       =(V_j-1)^2/V_j

has precisely the original zero support at every field component. Since P,Q do not divide m, the local cyclic algebras are reduced (X^m-1 is squarefree); D_j(I)=0 in the WHOLE local algebra is equivalent to Z_j=z there. Thus this quotient does preserve same-leaf source and supports the original whole-equality recovery implication. It introduces no denominator issue: all its displayed denominators are units.

This is not generally the relative conjugation trace V+bar(V). The scalar gamma-gap in V bar(V) cannot be silently set to one before detecting the collision. Calling it a reciprocal defect avoids that circular normalization.

The product identity pays K_j, but does NOT pay either summand in D_j(I). For a fixed I the first summand still contains the varying rho_j W raised to a_j and the native ell_j; the second has the antipodal version. The fixed-weight rational-trace theorem evaluates Tr(B R^a) for one fixed B. Neither summand here is that interface for all j, and the unknown I contributes the full Q^I carrier. Applying a scalar trace to D preserves the forward source implication but can add cancellations; it is not an equivalent zero test without another recovery argument. Thus I have not turned this exact sufficient whole-algebra observable into a paid query.

## A fully acquired source projection, and what it actually costs

Evaluation at X=1 gives W=t; evaluation at X=-1 gives W=-t. In the dyadic native rows E_j is even and m^2 I is even. Both projections therefore send the native equality to

 t^(E_j-m^2 I)=1.

The gap d=E_j-m^2 I is even, so t^d=gamma^(d/2). Since gamma has odd local order, gamma^(d/2)=1 if and only if gamma^d=1. Thus this is equivalent to the original gamma annihilator for the same gap; no odd-order premise on the chosen square root t is needed. All projected targets t^E_j and seed ratio t^(m^2) are acquired in soft O(m) bit work, including O(log N)-bit powering costs. This is a genuine source-retaining acquired representation, but its remaining query is exactly J scalar targets against a length-L geometric interval, J~m and L~m^2. It has the existing soft O(J+sqrt(JL)) bound, rather than soft O(J+sqrt L). Projection has not made that last operation free.

There is a precise reason these two evaluation choices appear in the simplest invariant multiplicative gauge. An evaluation X->xi that is fixed by every odd-tag automorphism must satisfy xi^j=xi for all odd units j. For m>=4 take j=-1, giving xi^2=1; conversely xi=+/-1 is fixed. This only classifies this explicitly defined evaluation family. It is not a lower bound against linear functionals, noninvariant evaluations, extension-valued projections, or other algorithms. Over a base field the two choices are exactly +/-1; over Z/NZ extra square roots of one themselves yield a factor by gcd(xi-1,N). A public nontrivial root must therefore be charged, not presumed.

## A concrete sufficient next interface

Two source-retaining routes now have exact output contracts:

1. Acquire the reciprocal defect, or a restriction-stable whole-local zero detector for it, jointly over j and I in soft O(m), while retaining native ell_j and the original I interval. Its zeros recover the original source without a signed-trace cancellation premise.
2. Supply a soft O(J+sqrt L) scalar geometric collision controller for the paid projected targets t^E_j. This already suffices with the established original scalar decoder; no full varying trace diagonal is necessary.

The fixed-weight trace theorem and gcd-aware KMP do not currently instantiate either contract: the former lacks a fixed weight after the native automorphisms, and the latter lacks O(polylog m) acquired fixed array pairs whose matches encode this two-parameter source. I did not find a gauge making that representation while retaining the nonzero scalar gap. No uncharged target expansion, derivative of a cyclic quotient, hidden-order root, or cancellation of a signed trace is used as a proposed solution.

The constructive outcome of this pass is the exact unit-safe reciprocal defect and the already-acquired scalar projection with its full remaining cost. The near-linear query, and therefore the unconditional 1/6 total factorization claim, remain unproved in this branch. No generic impossibility statement is intended.
