## The actual finite-ring adjoint remainder cannot vanish

The 2026 polynomial-reduction route suggests expressing the formal
weighted q-binomial input as an adjoint image plus a remainder. For its
first-order operator, the adjoint sends x^e only to exponents e and
e+m^2, so it preserves residue sectors modulo m^2.

A self-contained finite-ring summation functional annihilates every
finite-Laurent adjoint image. At a source monomial x^(3m^2-delta_j),
its value is EXACTLY the complete row product P_L(z_j). The functional
uses the q-binomial stream extended by zero outside [0,L]; the boundary
equations at -1 and L hold without division.

Consequently every unit row gives a nonzero adjoint quotient class on
the ACTUAL ring Z/N. At most two source tags occupy any residue sector,
by the native quadratic low-digit relation. The row sparsity theorem
therefore leaves Omega(m) nonzero sectors in the formal independent
weights. Finite-order numerical coefficient zeros do not remove them.

This rigorously excludes an endpoint-only FINITE-LAURENT adjoint
certificate for this route. It does not exclude a new algorithm that

## What the positive transformations do achieve

Odd local order makes every complementary factor 1+z_j q^i a unit.
Thus the exact identity

    P_L(z;q)P_L(-z;q)=P_L(z^2;q^2)

preserves the gcd of the whole query with N, without acquiring or
inverting those complements. It works for target subsets and contiguous
interval restrictions. It leaves J and L unchanged.

Ordinary time splitting, with or without this squaring, exposes at least
JT/2 distinct global target nodes after T branches on the actual source.
Materializing the T~m packet therefore costs Omega(m^2) residue words.
This is an explicit-branch-array obstruction; compressed scalar methods
remain open. See [the norm analysis](reports/norm-doubling-audit.md).

A common weight-independent scalar recurrence, regular at its start,
has order at least ceil((m-1)/2) on each actual hidden field. There is an
exact order-J recurrence with a known-unit leading coefficient, but its
natural dense coefficient table has Theta(J^2) slots. Keeping that
operator implicit remains possible; this is not an implicit propagation
lower bound. Fresh q-holonomic closure and 2026 polynomial-reduction
papers were inspected. See [the recurrence investigation](reports/holonomic-interface.md).
