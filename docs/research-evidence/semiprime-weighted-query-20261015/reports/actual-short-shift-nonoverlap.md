# Short phase corrections have full degree on a retained actual semiprime family

2026-10-15. Classical arithmetic obstruction to factor cancellation in a
particular transport method. No fast evaluator, Lean endpoint, circuit lower
bound, or general scalar-query lower bound is claimed.

## What is new and what is inherited

The previous transform-interface report proved over Q(gamma) that
P_L(gamma Z)/P_L(Z) has reduced degree L in both numerator and denominator.
It explicitly left numerical hidden-field cancellations open. This note
removes those cancellations on a large retained family of the ACTUAL
projected semiprime source, simultaneously over the candidate-prime menu.

Use the same original order-qualified ordered prime-pair supply N=P Q,
P,Q=Theta(M^3), of size Omega(M^6/log^2 M). As in the frozen digit-folding
proof, its assumptions are:

* candidate primes m=Theta(M), menu envelope K_*=O(M^.020);
* in each hidden field, the supplied gamma has exact PRIME order
  R_P>P^.677, with P-1=v_P R_P and 1<=v_P<=V=O(M^.969);
* (m-G_m)^6<N<=m^6, G_m=o(m), and
  sum_m(G_m+1)=O(K_*M).

These premises concern the inherited order-qualified supply before the
further frozen observation deletions. They are not assigned to inputs
excluded when that supply was constructed. Orders and factors are only
proof-side counting parameters, not input to an algorithm.

Put d=m^2, q=gamma^d, and

    P_L(Z)=prod_(i=0)^(L-1)(1-Z q^i),  1<=L<=4m^2.

For every m prescribe an m-only set H_m of at most H_* nonzero integer
shifts, each |h|<=C m^2 and NOT divisible by d. C is fixed. The sets are
fixed as N varies at fixed m. The ordinary window
0<|h|<=floor(M^.004) is one possible choice (up to a constant in H_*).
Allow H_*=O(M^.004).

## The simultaneous actual-family theorem

At most

    O(K_* V H_* M^5) = O(M^5.993)

original admissible input pairs have, in EITHER hidden field, at ANY
candidate m, ANY h in H_m and ANY |k|<=4m^2, a relation

    gamma^(h+m^2 k)=1.                                  (1)

For polylogarithmic H_* this exception bound is soft O(M^5.989).
These are input-exception counts, NOT running times. Union with the
previous soft O(M^5.994) deletions and the digit-folding O(M^5.993)
deletions preserves Omega(M^6/log^2 M) original input pairs.

On every surviving input, in both hidden fields and at every allowed m,
the polynomials P_L(gamma^h Z) and P_L(Z) are coprime for EVERY
h in H_m and EVERY 1<=L<=4m^2. Both have degree L. Their rational quotient
therefore has reduced numerator degree L and denominator degree L.

The usual q-step h=d is deliberately excluded: it has the genuine
endpoint identity (1-Z)P_L(q Z)=(1-Z q^L)P_L(Z). More generally h divisible
by d can cause integer-exponent overlap before any reduction modulo P.

## Counting proof

Fix m, h in H_m and |k|<=4m^2. The integer

    n=h+m^2 k

is nonzero, since otherwise d divides h. Its absolute value is O(M^4).
It has O(1) distinct prime divisors exceeding c M^2.031, uniformly in all
parameters: the product of t such divisors is at least
(c M^2.031)^t and divides |n|. No primality test or factoring oracle is
invoked by this counting argument.

If (1) holds in the P field, its actual prime order R_P divides n.
Enumerate the O(1) possible large prime divisors R of n first. For each R,
the equation P=1+vR with 1<=v<=V permits only O(V) possible hidden primes P.
Counting nonprime or out-of-bin candidates as well is an upper bound.

For each fixed m and such P, the menu-selector envelope gives

    (m-G_m)^6/P < Q <= m^6/P.

Because P=Theta(M^3), m=Theta(M), and G_m=o(m), this contains at most

    O(M^2(G_m+1))

integer Q values. Prime/order/source restrictions only decrease the count.
This Q interval is wider than in the digit-folding proof; no high-digit
index decomposition is used or needed here.

There are O(M^2) choices of k and at most H_* choices of h for each m.
Consequently the P-field exception count is bounded by

    O(V H_* M^4 sum_m(G_m+1))
      = O(K_* V H_* M^5).

Interchanging P,Q costs a factor two. With exponents .020+.969+.004+5,
the count is O(M^5.993), negligible compared with M^6/log^2 M. All deleted
sets are united on the same original qualified supply; unrelated
counterfamilies are not intersected post hoc.

## Polynomial and finite-subgroup consequences

For large enough M, R_P>4m^2 and R_P does not divide d. Hence q has order
R_P, and the L roots of each polynomial above are distinct and nonzero.
They belong to the subgroup generated by gamma.

A common root would give

    gamma^(-h-d i)=gamma^(-d j)

for some 0<=i,j<L. This is precisely R_P | h+d(i-j), contrary to the
simultaneous exclusion (1). The same argument applies in the Q field.
All leading coefficients are units. Thus both reduced degrees are L.

This is also a finite-subgroup FUNCTIONAL obstruction when R_P>4L, which
holds eventually uniformly since R_P>=cM^2.031 and L<=4m^2. Suppose
A(Z)/B(Z), B nonzero, deg A,deg B<L, agreed with P_L(gamma^h Z)/P_L(Z)
at every point in <gamma> where both denominators are nonzero. At most
L+deg B<=2L-1 subgroup points are excluded. The polynomial

    B(Z)P_L(gamma^h Z)-A(Z)P_L(Z)

has degree at most 2L-1 and vanishes at more than 2L-1 subgroup points.
It must vanish identically. Coprimality then forces P_L to divide B,
contradicting deg B<L. Thus a low-degree correction cannot be rescued by
requiring equality merely as a function on the complete actual subgroup.

This statement does not apply to a correction required only at a small
chosen collection of canonical target points.

## Exact scope for the acquisition problem

The new count closes the previous generic-versus-numerical gap for a
prescribed sparse collection of non-q-stride phase shifts. It rules out
turning those entire interval quotients into a bounded number of endpoint
factors by hidden-field root overlap. In particular the shift h=1 really
retains both degree-L products on the actual family.

It does NOT imply that evaluating a degree-L rational function needs
Omega(L) operations. Its q-product representation remains succinct; a
new implicit evaluator could exploit it. No arithmetic circuit lower
bound is claimed. An identity using additions, a norm-only algorithm,
or a formula required only at the original J target values remains open.

The allowed shifts can be chosen using full N or earlier observations
WITHIN the simultaneously granted H_m sets. A shift set itself chosen
using N at fixed m is outside the theorem unless its entire possible
support is charged. In particular H_*=O(M^.004) does not grant all
Theta(m^2) possible differences among the source offsets, or all J^2
pairwise canonical shifts. No general actual-target disjointness theorem
is inferred.

The original whole seed rectangle still has its existing compiled forced
collision and global-leaf recovery. The general acquisition bound remains
soft O(m^(3/2)), and the soft O(m) evaluator and full bit-complexity/Lean
composition are still missing.
