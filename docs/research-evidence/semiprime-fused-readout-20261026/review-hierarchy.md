Here g is a power of 3 and P=j+nu is a 3-unit; the hierarchy keeps n divisible by 3. There is no inversion of n or g modulo g². The same identity and valuations justify the cancellation. One must not silently replace these rational modular statements by integer divisibility of I0.

Direct subtraction gives

    (I0(u+v)+d(u+v))−(I0(u)+du)
      =v[B+(a0+nd)v]/(P+nv).

This proves the claimed finite cancellation, including its quadratic term, not only an infinitesimal approximation.
