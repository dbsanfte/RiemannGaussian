# Native coverage condition for powered cyclic/Bloch menus

26 October2026. Read the authoritative scalar catalogue and swap erratum under `formal/docs/research-evidence/semiprime-recurrence-checkpoint-20261025/evidence/`, respectively `semiprime-paired-weighted-query-20261024/scalar-catalogue.md` and `semiprime-arithmetic-locator-20261024/swap-erratum.md`. This continues beyond the arbitrary-knot diagnostic: it derives the exact arithmetic obligation for a native-safe powered menu, pays one conditional branch, and gives its recovery. No complete soft-k menu is proved.

## 1. Work before folding, to keep the labels exact

For retained native row j let

    E_j=a+bN+lambda, Y_j=beta^E_j, q=beta^H, H=k².

Let d_P=ord_P(q), d_Q=ord_Q(q); both are odd and exceed16H. The virtual cover supplies a row and its swap with the SAME signed index I, where

    HI=aP+bQ+lambda, |I|<M,
    E_j-HI=(P-1)(bQ-a),
    E_swap-HI=(Q-1)(aP-b).                            (1)

Both rows survive public integer-zero deletion and the continuing diagonal/pair pruning. Thus Y_j=q^I moduloP and Y_swap=q^I moduloQ. This is stronger than unrelated local witnesses, but it does not supply I or the local orders.

A general monomial-boundary cyclic menu entry has public integer labels (r,C), r>0, and tests

    Y_j^r-beta^C  or  Y_j^r-beta^(-C).               (2)

These are exactly the elementary factors in the cheap cyclic/Bloch norm, apart from already-unit normalizations. Half-center roots v_j=beta^(E_j-H/2) change the labels to r(E_j-H/2)∓C; all calculations below are unchanged after this known translation. There is no need to infer half-integer exponents or recover a discrete logarithm.

Every tested integer K=rE_j∓C must be publicly nonzero before using its equality as a leaf. The previous deletion E_j/H outside[-M,M] alone does NOT ensure this for arbitrary new r,C. Delete a tautological menu entry individually; a new coverage proof must then certify survival of a genuine entry. If both beta powers agree globally at a surviving leaf, K is the acquired nonzero polylog-bit annihilator passed to the licensed extractor. If their gcd difference is proper, it factors immediately. Accidental local collisions are therefore safe and need not equal the original virtual witness.

## 2. Exact condition imposed by the actual witness

Let D_P=ord_P(beta). For the row furnished by (1), entry (2) hitsP exactly when

    D_P divides rHI ∓ C.                            (3)

This uses the native Fermat relation, not an arbitrary-root counterexample. For grid-aligned boundary C=rHs, it becomes

    d_P divides r(I∓s),
    equivalently d_P/gcd(d_P,r) divides I∓s.         (4)

If menus are symmetric in sign it suffices to state the minus sign. A common menu at j and its swap catches BOTH local witnesses at the same entry precisely when (3) holds for D_P and D_Q; for aligned boundaries this requires

    lcm(d_P/gcd(d_P,r), d_Q/gcd(d_Q,r)) divides I-s.  (5)

With the supplied cross-prime coprimality this lcm is the product. It cannot be replaced by either local condition when claiming a global-zero endpoint. A weak nonunit consumer only needs ONE local condition in (4), and can return a factor or extractor from that entry; hence demanding (5) is optional, not an obstruction to a valid weaker algorithm.

If gcd(r,d_P)=1, the short index window and d_P>16H show that a nearby public s in the same short window hits this witness only for s=I. A useful compressed menu therefore has to (i) acquire suitably correlated s, or (ii) make the reduced unknown order d_P/gcd(d_P,r) small enough that sparse s meet its residue class. This is an exact condition on a particular proposed menu, not a claim that all algorithms must acquire I.

Substituting the native factor relation gives the arithmetic form

    d_P/gcd(d_P,r) divides (aP+bQ+lambda)/H - s,      (6)

with the displayed quotient an integer. Equivalently the actual I lies on

    (HI-lambda)^2-4abN=(aP-bQ)^2.                   (7)

The public congruence lambda²=4abN modH proves integrality for appropriate factor residues; it does not alone provide the much stronger divisibility (6). Equation(7) exhibits exactly where PQ=N can enter a future boundary-selection theorem: it must cover the actual factor-correlated square point, not every point of the conic or every independent tag/scale. I have not proved that polylogarithmically many s or r do so.

## 3. A fully paid conditional menu, and its precise remaining hypothesis

Choose B=polylog N and acquire the public integer

    r_B=lcm(1,2,...,B).

Its bit length is O(B), so repeated-power evaluation of Y_j^r_B for all soft-k rows costs soft-k bits (allowing additional polylogarithmic factors). Test gcd(Y_j^r_B-1,N). K=r_B E_j is nonzero for every retained row: E_j=0 would already have been deleted at index0. Proper gcd and saturated-leaf recovery are therefore completely paid, with no collisions inverted.

At the genuine P witness this menu succeeds exactly when

    d_P/gcd(d_P,I) divides r_B.                     (8)

The swap gives the analogous Q condition. This is a familiar smooth-order/Pollard-type sufficient branch, not a new knot speedup or a uniform guarantee. The branch does preserve coverage whenever (8) holds; it does not require knowledge of I to run. Since |I|<M and d_P>16H, a nonzero witness has

    d_P/gcd(d_P,I)>16H/M=Omega(sqrt k).

Thus (8) asks for a potentially large reduced order whose prime powers all fit r_B. Oddness and cross-prime coprimality do not imply that smoothness. If I=0, the central screen would already expose the local collision; continuing witness indices are nonzero.

Using a product of menu entries with signs, or the cyclic norm normalization from `powered-tangle.md`, changes neither conditions (3),(4) nor these recovery rules. In particular, no exact-zero binary-search endpoint can be asserted merely because this conditional smooth branch finds neither a proper factor nor a saturated leaf. One must prove a covered entry exists, or retain the original long query as fallback.

## 4. Native task left after the norm evaluation is paid

The cheap norm's acquisition problem is solved. The remaining sufficient arithmetic lemma is explicit: construct, from N,k and the acquired rows, soft-k total nonzero labels (j,r,C), with polylog-bit r,C, such that some actual local virtual witness satisfies (3), or some other tested entry is a nonunit, and give the public survival proof. For common grid-aligned menus the actual-factor form is (6); for one common globally zero entry it is the stronger paired condition(5). An aggregate zero-only endpoint may instead have different covered menu entries at P and Q; its proof needs both local coverings but need not impose (5) on one entry. A lemma guaranteeing merely an auxiliary cyclic eigenvalue or an unrelated formal conic point would not meet this contract.

No complete such lemma follows here from the native discriminant or swap identities. This is not the earlier universal scalar diagnostic: it isolates the factor-correlated order divisibility that a coverage-safe cyclic replacement must actually exploit, and supplies a paid conditional branch with exact global-annihilator decoding. No repository edits or numerical experiments were made.
