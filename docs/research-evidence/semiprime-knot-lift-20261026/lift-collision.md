# Native twist-lift dynamics at an original collision

26 October 2026. Constructive algebra pass using the current archived twist-lift.md. Scratch only; no experiments or repository edits.

**Result.** All integer lifts of one native twist coefficient give an explicitly acquired affine multiplicative orbit of LG values. At an ORIGINAL row collision its zero condition is a precise exponential equation, with both exceptional coefficient branches retained. The semiprime Frobenius relation identifies the multiplier locally but does not initialize a solution or a polylogarithmic covering list. A conditional all-lifts failure branch is also explicit. This does not exclude another native-specific lift construction and does not improve the original prefix exponent.

## 1. Exact initialized native input

Use the archived native knot choice and its conventions: roots v with v and v-1 units, u=q^-1 a known unit, d=u-1 a certified unit, and a canonical positive integer s_0 in [1,N-1] satisfying

    s_0 = v/(v-1)^2 mod N.

Its negative twist is T_-s_0 and its Alexander factor is 1-s_0(T+T^-1-2). Every s_t=s_0+tN with t>=0 specifies another genuine negative twist knot and has exactly the same modular Alexander factor. Polylog-bit t keeps the knot description polylogarithmic; no expanded crossing diagram is required.

Kohli's Proposition 2.18 supplies the normalized scalar, as audited in twist-lift.md:

    F_s(B)=1+(u+1)A_s B/u+H_s B²/u,
    A_s=(u^s-1)/d, H_s=((u+1)A_s-2s)/d,
    B=(a-1)(b-1), ab=u.

All quotient displays here have certified scalar-unit denominators u,d. Division-free binary recurrences in the prior report independently provide the same coefficients, including any specialization of d. Primary source https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.08296 , Corollary 2.12 and Proposition 2.18; original publication Experimental Mathematics 27 (2018),251–264, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2016.1255860.

## 2. Full lift dynamics, before taking any collision

Put U_0=u^s_0, W=u^N. Since s_t=s_0 in R, only u^s changes. Define the acquired quadratic expressions

    alpha(B)=(u+1)B(d+B)/(u*d²),
    gamma(B)=1-alpha(B)-2s_0 B²/(u*d).

Then the exact finite-ring identity is

    F_(s_0+tN)(B)=gamma(B)+alpha(B)*U_0*W^t.          (1)

This is a value identity, not an inference from a constant coefficient. Both alpha and gamma depend on the actual argument B. The shared multiplier W costs O(log N) ring operations once, and U_0 costs O(log N) per native row. Updating t by one requires a single multiplication of U by W. The lift-induced family remains degree at most two in B per row.

For r explicitly chosen global lifts t_1,...,t_r, all L-row connected-sum scalar polynomials can be acquired in soft O(rL) work (including powering the chosen t's) and have degree at most 2L each. Alternatively multiplying them gives an acquired polynomial of degree at most 2rL. This is lawful acquisition. It does not evaluate the resulting family at all m physical indices in that cost; the current geometric-query work is still required.

## 3. Evaluate (1) at an original native collision

Work first in either scalar field component F_p. At an ORIGINAL row collision, a=v or a=v^-1. In either case

    s_0=a/(a-1)^2,
    b=u/a,
    B=(a-1)(u-a)/a.                                (2)

Substitute these equalities in (1), obtaining

    alpha_a=(u+1)(a-1)(u-a)(2ua-a²-u)/(u*a²*d²),
    gamma_a=1-alpha_a-2(u-a)²/(u*a*d),
    F_(s_0+tN)(B)=gamma_a+alpha_a U_0 W^t.           (3)

All denominators shown are units. At a searched positive odd clock a=eta^(2i+1), b=eta^(-2i-3), the factors a-1 and b-1 are certified units by the native short-order guard. Thus u-a is a unit at a genuine collision as well. Also u+1 is a unit because the native subgroup has odd order and contains no -1. The only uncontrolled factor of alpha_a in (3) is

    T_a=2ua-a²-u.

This factor cannot be declared nonzero from order margins alone. No collision expression or value of F has been inverted.

### Nondegenerate branch

If alpha_a is nonzero locally, zero preservation at this leaf is exactly

    W^t = -gamma_a/(alpha_a U_0),                    (4)

or equivalently u^(s_0+tN)=-gamma_a/alpha_a. The right side must first be nonzero: if gamma_a=0 and alpha_a!=0, every lifted scalar is nonzero because U_0,W are units. If gamma_a!=0, it must belong to the cyclic subgroup generated by W, and then a discrete-logarithm congruence for t remains. The fact that the right side is a rational expression in the common native beta powers does not make it a power with a supplied exponent; multiplicative subgroups need not contain arbitrary sums or rational expressions of their elements.

A proper gcd encountered while testing any acquired coefficient/value already factors N. A globally unit alpha can be inverted safely. A saturated alpha cannot be treated as a field inverse; equation (3) is the correct cleared equation for that branch.

### Degenerate branch, where no lift changes the value

If T_a=0 locally, then u=a²/(2a-1). The denominator 2a-1 is necessarily nonzero because a is a unit and T_a=0. This gives

    d=(a-1)²/(2a-1),
    alpha_a=0, gamma_a=1-2/a.                       (5)

Thus every integer lift has the SAME scalar 1-2/a at this original Alexander collision. If a!=2, no lift, even an unbounded list, makes this one block vanish. If a=2, the value is zero for every lift and T_a=0 forces u=4/3 (with characteristic 3 excluded by that equality). These are conditional exact component statements, not asserted attained catalogue instances.

For i=1 the degenerate relation on the native clock is eta^8-2eta^3+1=0. It is not the forbidden short multiplicative relation eta^r=1. This illustrates precisely the additional additive algebra that a native-specific success theorem would have to exclude or exploit. I have not proved it is attained by the full prepared semiprime catalogue, nor that a different row/block could not furnish the needed collision.

## 4. What Frobenius and semiprimality actually provide

For N=P Q and u in R, in the P-component

    W=u^N=u^Q,

and in the Q-component W=u^P. Hence if d_P,d_Q are the local u-orders,

    ord_P(W)=d_P/gcd(d_P,Q),
    ord_Q(W)=d_Q/gcd(d_Q,P).                         (6)

On ordering P<=Q, d_P divides P-1, so gcd(d_P,Q)=1 and W generates the SAME subgroup as u in the smaller field. The larger component can lose a P-primary factor. The prepared interface's coprime odd local orders do not exclude P dividing d_Q. In particular one must not assume N is invertible modulo both local u-orders.

Even in the favorable smaller component, (4) requires its right side to belong to <u>, followed by an unknown exponent congruence tN=s_target-s_0 modulo d_P. Frobenius supplies neither membership nor s_target nor d_P. Actual P-th-power Frobenius distributes through the rational target and is the identity in F_P. Raising to N instead gives the target's Q-th power in that component; the Q-th-power map is generally NOT additive and must not be replaced by evaluation of the same rational formula at u^Q,a^Q. Neither observation simplifies the integer exponent s_0=a/(a-1)^2, whose equality is a FIELD equality modulo P, into an exponent identity modulo d_P. Confusing those two moduli, or treating N-th powering as a ring homomorphism, would be an invalid lift-selection rule.

This explicitly uses semiprimality rather than treating the lift multiplier as an arbitrary unrelated clock. It still leaves a native rational-target discrete-log/membership equation, or a method avoiding it.

## 5. Conditional algorithm, coverage requirement, and recovery

An actual candidate list of r=polylog N lifts is cheap to initialize: acquire U_0 per row and W once, build each row's quadratics by (1), then build the r complete scalar polynomials in soft L work up to logarithms. One can safely multiply the r scalar values to implement a local OR of their zeros; over squarefree R this detects whichever component has a zero. But coverage would require a theorem that for at least one actual original witness row and searched index, either the degenerate all-zero branch applies or equation (4) has a solution in the explicitly acquired list. No such theorem follows from the twist-lift freedom. Even all integer lifts require subgroup membership, except on the special constant-zero branch.

The right side of (4), after imposing a=v or a=v^-1, depends only on the SUPPLIED row root and its chosen orientation. Thus a row-dependent method could initialize up to 2L explicit exponential equations without first locating any physical collision index. Solving or covering those equations cheaply is the real remaining task; no unknown-index search is intrinsically required at this stage. One may construct a constant number of whole-knot variants to accommodate the two orientations if rowwise lift choices for each orientation are acquired. Iterating all t through an unknown order or expanding all original physical indices does not meet the target budget. Row-dependent choices are therefore a legitimate constructive route, but need an acquired solution/coverage rule for these known rational targets.

A deformed scalar zero also needs a valid ORIGINAL-leaf recovery implication. Localizing it among lifted blocks and physical indices exposes an LG zero, not automatically a_j-q^i or beta^K-1. Equations (3),(4) were derived assuming an original collision; the reverse implication is not proved and cannot be silently used as the annihilator decoder. If the original row and original clock collision are independently certified, their existing signed label and nonzero O(log N)-bit gap supply the licensed short-annihilator recovery. Additional LG zeros without that certificate are not licensed leaves.

The narrow remaining constructive primitive is therefore to choose/aggregate lift indices so that the affine orbit (1) covers an actual original witness and admits original-leaf recovery, while acquiring only polylog-many degree-O(L) polynomials and contracting their physical index family at the desired soft(L+sqrt m) cost. Formula (4) and the coefficient branches (5) make the missing arithmetic explicit. This is not a lower bound against a different lift or invariant construction.

## Independent self-audit clarification

The s sign is correct: c=-v/(v-1)^2 and the negative twist coefficient is -s=c, so s=v/(v-1)^2. The P<=Q ordering in Section 4 is only a proof convention for naming the hidden smaller prime; no computation is given either prime or order. All costed exponentiations use known N,s_0,t only. Equation (6) explicitly retains the possible larger-component order loss; coprime local orders are not mistaken for coprimality with N. The rowwise orientation correction in Section 5 strengthens the remaining constructive interface: up to 2L explicit target equations can be acquired before any collision localization.

## Stronger continuing interface after the new Frobenius screen

The subsequently acquired prepass in frobenius-screen.md costs soft k and either factors N or certifies gcd(N,ord_P(q))=gcd(N,ord_Q(q))=1. It also certifies the same coprimality for beta and eta because their order quotients over q divide H, coprime to N. On this strengthened continuing branch W=u^N generates exactly the same subgroup as u in BOTH components, so the possible larger-component order loss discussed above no longer occurs. It remains relevant only before that prepass. On the prepared coprime-local-order branch, local membership in both component u-subgroups also combines to membership in the global cyclic subgroup by CRT on the coprime orders.

This removes an actual subgroup-shrinking obstruction. It does not acquire the unknown orders, the inverse exponent N^-1 modulo their product, discrete logarithms of the rational targets, or a short deterministic lift cover. N-th powering is now a multiplicative group automorphism on the relevant subgroup, not an additive/ring automorphism. The affine law (1), both coefficient-degeneration branches and original-leaf recovery obligations are unchanged.
