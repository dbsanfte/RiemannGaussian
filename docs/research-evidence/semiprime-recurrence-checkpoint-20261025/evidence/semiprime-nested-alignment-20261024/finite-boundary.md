## Actual finite operators and paid short data

Write f(T)=P_L(T)=prod_(0<=i<L)(1-q^i T). The native inverse roots c are supplied units, J=Theta(k), L=Theta(k²)<4H, H=k². The triple constraint c0*c2/c1²=theta^(2a), theta^9=q, does not make adjacent native roots a q-clock. Below every clock starts separately at its actual supplied c; native labels and q carries remain attached.

Choose 2<=d<=k (in particular d=k), let S be the lower nilpotent d-shift, and define

    X_c=c diag(1,q,...,q^(d-1)),
    Y=S+e0 e_last^t.

These are compact explicit operators: the q-clock and shift data cost soft d and a start costs one supplied c. We do not claim that materializing all J matrices or all their functional values is softly linear.

The NILPOTENT alternative is genuinely initialized. Write f(T)=sum b_s T^s. Then

    b_0=1,
    b_s=-q^(s-1) (1-q^(L-s+1))/(1-q^s) * b_(s-1).       (1)

All denominators for s<d are actual guarded units. Powers begin with q^L and q^-1; hence the first d coefficients cost soft(d+log L), not L. They give f(S) EXACTLY because S^d=0, and its Toeplitz action costs soft d. This is the already meaningful finite-germ primitive. It does not evaluate f(c) at a supplied unit c, nor initialize a native collision query.

## Exact cyclic boundary, without discarding its coefficient

Direct matrix multiplication gives

    X_c Y-q Y X_c=c(1-q^d)e0 e_last^t.                    (2)

For ANY polynomial f, not just a formal quantum exponential,

    f(X_c)Y-Y f(qX_c)=[f(c)-f(cq^d)]e0 e_last^t.          (3)

All interior entries cancel identically; the sole cyclic corner retains the indicated two endpoint values. Thus the boundary remains rank one even after applying the long polynomial. It is not legitimate to infer that its coefficient is acquired.

For the actual finite f, let

    H_d(c)=P_d(c), B_d(c)=P_d(cq^L).

The division-free identity f(cq^d) H_d(c)=f(c) B_d(c) yields, on the ACTUALLY CERTIFIED H_d(c)-unit branch,

    f(X_c)Y-Y f(qX_c)
      = f(c) [1-B_d(c)/H_d(c)] e0 e_last^t.               (4)

Both complete short-value vectors are paid: construct degree-d P_d by a product tree (or its guarded q-binomial coefficients), then monic MPE at c and cq^L. Cost soft(d+J), hence soft k for d~k. No separation of targets is required. These are short values, not long diagonal amplitudes.

IMPORTANT: P_d(c) need not be a unit at the good prime when d=k; an own hit can lie in that prefix. Norm-screen the acquired heads. Proper gcd returns a factor; a saturated head invokes the actual original short-prefix row/index locator with its stored native carry and full original beta consumer, or returns bounded failure on an unpromised orientation. Its indices i<d are a subset of the actual shifted native query. Only remaining globally certified unit heads are inverted. Tail values B_d(c) are merely acquired auxiliary values; no saturated tail is assigned an original consumer here. Formula (3), or the cross-multiplied identity, remains available without any inversion.

The multiplier in (4) is therefore known where used. The rank-one coefficient itself still contains precisely the unknown native amplitude f(c). Dividing that amplitude away would erase its zero support. At an interior hit the same issue persists for first variations: differentiating (4) leaves the unknown derivative of f(c), multiplied by the short multiplier, since terms containing f(c) vanish locally. No method for acquiring that derivative is supplied by the rank-one fact. This is a statement about the executed operator identity only.

For the cyclic shift Y, f(Y) instead requires the folded coefficients

    sum_(s congruent t mod d, 0<=s<=L) b_s,

that is f(T) mod(T^d-1). The nilpotent first-d array is not this array. The low-rank corner (2) does not license dropping the higher coefficients or setting this remainder equal to f(S). Acquiring the folded remainder, a native long amplitude, or a suitably normalized derivative remains an additional operation.
