The original primitive equation P I=a x²+b x+c follows from the pinned second-content identity. For the repair, d'=d-epsilon*j² and b'=b+epsilon*j-k*zeta give c'=c-j*zeta exactly. Multiplication of I'=I+epsilon*x-zeta by P=j+kx gives a'x²+b'x+c', including both linear correction terms. Completing the square with the actually supplied t therefore gives equation(4) with the stated unit lambda and public gamma; no inverse of2 modulo k is assumed. The character identity is correctly restricted to unit X.

The all-residue square-fiber bound is valid for the controlled modulus. Each of its at most two odd squarefree factors contributes at most two roots, and the 2-primary component of exponent1,3 or4 contributes at most four, including the zero-square case. The claimed at-most16 bound and at-leastk/16 image size follow. Enumerating the square-map buckets stores exactly k entries, rather than charging an uninitialized root oracle. This image statement does not say that every residue is attained by the actual hidden prime.

For any explicitly supplied palette A, the lookup and j+kx conversion genuinely acquire at most16k|A| labeled residues modulo H. The own prime is included if its repaired index residue belongs to A. Product-tree evaluation at -Hn, 0<=n<=k, tests v+Hn up to a harmless sign. These are positive and smaller than N on the prepared branch; a nonunit leaf therefore gives a proper factor. MPE is monic and needs no difference inverse. A saturated row can be descended until finding a nonunit integer leaf. The stated soft(k+k|A|) cost includes the complete catalog and all row outputs. It is a conditional-class theorem, not acquisition of a covering palette.

The independent scalar-terminal option uses only original roots, m=k and G=3k, so kg+r is the intended main index class. Its last two factors are separately paid. Omitting reciprocal augmentation here correctly avoids mixing the opposite reversed residue class. The safe scalar resultant and original-leaf consumer are inherited and charged once per palette entry.

For legal lifts I_s=I+s*x-z_s, the quadratic coefficient in x remains a unit. The second difference 2a*j^-1 is nonzero modulo the continuing k>16, so this particular family cannot produce a universally constant polynomial in x. This does not preclude a choice exploiting the actual prime divisor. Finally, a cyclic group whose order is coprime to k admits no nonzero homomorphism to Z/k, and exponent residues modulo k are not well-defined on that unrestricted group. The report carefully does not turn this into a lower bound for interval-promised exponent recovery.

The report has no missing consumer, but its small covering palette remains an explicit missing input. All stated unconditional work is initialization and a supplied-palette algorithm; no whole-family exponent improvement, numerical proof, or completion of the one-sixth objective is claimed.
