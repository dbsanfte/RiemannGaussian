# Independent audit of the initialized ideal/seed query

24 October 2026. Root review. PASS for `ideal-seed-query.md`, SHA256
`25b326ed8a0dd0c0b3f81a7b997c130c79baec13b0b68f077ec0643b8f20159a`.
No source corrections are required. The small-degree input is conditional
and its actual construction cost is retained; the completed algorithm is
not claimed to improve the full query.

The homogeneous linear substitution is invertible over B because 2 and
tau are units. Its degree-e coefficient transform is a binomial/Taylor
shift with the stated small factorial units; summing through d costs
soft d^2. All triangular spectral nodes are actually acquired. Weighted
product trees give Q and both numerators by exact polynomial products,
with no evaluation of a potentially singular quotient. Conjugation swaps
the two spectral exponents, so the acquired polynomials have scalar
coefficients. Q(0)=1 justifies all formal-series inversions, including
coincident spectral values and zero answers.

The first M values initialize the monic reversed-Q recurrence. The
spectral-pole formula is correctly limited to a proof-side distinct-node
field. It does not identify same-index zeros of the coefficient sequences.
Guarded polynomial gcd or simultaneous Pade cancellation of the supplied
numerators and denominator has exactly the weaker meaning stated. The
complete algorithm and its correctness do not depend on obtaining any
reduction from this optional cancellation.

The norm coordinate is t^i, not one. Its degree-O(d^2) elimination or
the actual target norm polynomial leads back to a degree-O(J) geometric
query. A displayed pair of equations alone is not a supplied finite-free
quotient or multiplication matrix. The report preserves these input and
extra-intersection qualifications.

The full coefficient expansion through L costs soft(M+L), and its
ordinary gcd(N,G_i,K_i) tests common local zeros at the SAME index. A
proper gcd factors immediately; a unit discards that index; saturated
coarse zeros are retained for the actual-target test. The guaranteed
own hit necessarily survives this step or already gives a factor.
Batched monic evaluation of the original F_B at all retained seed points
charges their actual acquisition and permits repeats. Only after its norm
is nonunit does localization use the original row/index leaf consumer.
Consequently extra ideal points and positive-dimensional components do
not become false factor certificates. The worst case of L retained
candidates is included in the cost.

For regular balanced fiber ideals, the coefficient and total-degree
bounds give the asserted soft-J startup. For general fiber profiles,
sum s*d=J does not bound sum(s+d)^2; the report correctly charges the
latter for this spectral transformation, as well as additional full
length expansions for distinct components. No actual native balanced
profile or generic point assumption is introduced.

Delivered cost is T_ideal+soft(J+L), with the original safe cleanup and
recovery. The earlier soft(J+sqrt(JL)) method remains better at the
native parameters. The report does not confuse a paid recurrence or
spectral cancellation with acquisition of the required common-index
zero query. The separate new torus-order supplier can strengthen its
input guards but does not alter this already checked cost conclusion.
