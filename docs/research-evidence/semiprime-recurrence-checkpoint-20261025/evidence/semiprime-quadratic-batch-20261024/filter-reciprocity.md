## 1. Execute reciprocity without inventing a unit tag

Let R(u)=1-u. Then a(Ru)=e(u) and e(Ru)=a(u) exactly as canonical residues. Since every native u is odd and k is even, Ru is even and NEVER a unit modulo H. In particular chi_D(Ru) is not a replacement native colour, and Ru cannot be relabelled as an original tag.

Nevertheless G=R(F) is acquired in soft k with its originating u,j. It is disjoint from F, and S=F union G has exactly 4 phi(k) points and is R-invariant. Its dual strip is exactly

 G={v: 1-v in U(H), 1<=e(v)<2k}.

Thus S is a small, actually acquired self-dual mask. Its new points are auxiliary labels, not new applications of native coverage.

For a fixed eta let K_eta=q^(c0+eta) and acquire both polynomials

 A_eta(X)=product_(u in F) (X-K_eta pi^e(u)),
 B_eta(X)=product_(u in F) (X-K_eta pi^a(u)).                 (1)

Powering, root product trees and coefficients of both cost soft k, with O(log N)-bit exponents. Reciprocity gives, by explicit reindexing,

 product_(v in G)(X-K_eta pi^e(v))=B_eta(X),
 product_(v in G)(X-K_eta pi^a(v))=A_eta(X).                (2)

Consequently the phase-e product on S is A_eta B_eta. The phase-a product on S is the SAME A_eta B_eta. Reindexing does not make A_eta=B_eta: it sends the hard F/e strip to the hard G/a strip, and the easy F/a strip to easy G/e. A quotient of these identical symmetric products cancels the desired zero-bearing factor as well; it is not a zero detector.

## 2. Fully initialize a covered, consumable enlargement

For every u in F and every eta in {-5,...,1}, include the two plus-type targets in (1). Retain every original colour-minus target with its original labels. This is an explicit O(k)-row family, acquired in soft k. Each original plus target occurs at its true eta, and every original minus target is retained. Therefore the original same-row hit, for either unknown own colour, survives at the original index i<L. Duplicates may be retained; deleting identical duplicates preserves support. No nonunit product is divided away.

Every auxiliary target has the actual integer exponent

 E=H(c0+eta)+z, z=e(u) or a(u), 0<=z<H.

It is pi^E, so its norm is beta^(2E). With r0=N-Hc0, its delta=E-N=H eta+z-r0 lies strictly between -6H and 2H. These near-N labels are essential: replacing this with raw N(u-1)² would introduce large row-dependent q carries and would not have this consumer.

At any auxiliary norm leaf Norm(pi^E-q^i), a proper integer norm gcd immediately factors N. If it is saturated, the nonsplit field side has pi^E=q^i; taking its field norm gives G_beta=beta^(2E)-beta^(2Hi)=0 there. Its integer gcd is proper or N. In the latter case odd beta orders give beta^(E-Hi)=1 at both primes. Their coprime product dP*dQ>C² divides E-Hi and (P-1)(Q-1). Hence it divides

 R-(P+Q), R=1+Hi-delta.

For i<L and -6H<delta<2H, the source bounds Q<H²/16 and P<=k³ give |R-(P+Q)|<4H² on the continuing branch. This is below C²=256H², so R=P+Q. Checking the quadratic discriminant recovers P,Q. This proof uses the small factor-sum difference, not a false bound on E-Hi, which contains N. Thus new strip leaves are valid factor-or-label overcover; they are not passed to an unproved original-tag decoder.

All extra targets are units. Distinct exponent deltas span less than 8H, so twice any nonzero difference is <16H=C. The prepared beta orders therefore separate their norms. Target separation, including each split component, follows separately from the actual q screen: for a nonzero exponent gap d with |d|<8H<C, pi^d-1 divides q^d-1 (use the inverse-unit version for negative d). The latter is a certified unit, so pi^d-1 and the target difference are units in B. Distinct norms alone would not prove this split-component statement. Exact duplicate exponents can be deduplicated with retained labels. Original minus targets can be kept as a separate constant number of queries; no unsupported separation across unlike descriptions is needed.

## 3. Pay the easy strip and identify the surviving query

For any attained a in [1,2k), its fiber in F has size exactly mu=|U(H)[2]|<=16: the square map on U(H) has equal fibers. Thus there are 2 phi(k)/mu=Theta(k) attained curvature values. This count uses the paid controlled-modulus phi(k)=Theta(k) contract.

Inside one such fiber e(u1)=e(u2) iff 2N(u1-u2)=0 modulo H, iff u2=u1 or u1+H/2. Those are the known identical representatives. In particular u and -u have distinct phases when H>4, since their phase difference is -4Nu modulo H. One curvature value does not specify one phase with a harmless multiplicity. After removing the exact duplicated representatives, the remaining phase factors still have their explicit labels.

The B_eta strip has only low exponents a<2k. Its distinct root differences are units: pi^d=1 in any component for 0<|d|<2k would imply q^d=1 there, contradicting the actual q screen. Its distinct degree is Theta(k), not constant. Its coefficients are acquired in soft k. To certify that its entire interval product

is a unit, or find and consume a nonunit leaf, the available initialized geometric product algorithm costs soft(k+sqrt(kL))=soft k^(3/2). If it returns a nonunit the preceding consumer factors N. If it returns a unit, cancellation of THIS boundary product is legal, but the hard A_eta interval product in (1) remains. No cheap certificate for the boundary interval has been inferred from cheap polynomial acquisition.

Explicit execution of the enlarged family uses the acquired A_eta,B_eta and original minus polynomial(s), of total degree O(k), with the guarded geometric block method and original restriction/localization. It completes in soft k^(3/2) time, soft k-scale input data, and O(log N)-bit scalar arithmetic/exponents up to logarithmic factors. The constant seven carries do not affect the exponent. Keeping the full F square does not reduce the number of distinct curvature values or acquire any missing interval product.

## 4. The additional sign symmetry and scope

The other involution u->-u preserves F, flips the native character, and exchanges the two canonical reciprocal phases with signs. Its integer centres and carries are already charged in the source. Literal closure under BOTH u->-u and u->1-u contains translation u->u+1, hence any nonempty closed subset of Z/H is the whole H-point set. Thus materializing that particular full closure emits H labels, rather than O(k). This is an exact ledger for this closure attempt, not a lower bound against implicit representations, other pairings or scalar algorithms.

The executed self-dual O(k)-point construction is a valid covered family with a proved consumer for all auxiliary leaves. Its reflection identity leaves the hard phase strip intact. Completing a Gauss sum would drop the correlated low-a filter, and treating pi as an H-th root of unity would drop actual carries. Neither operation computes this query. No improvement below the existing soft k^(3/2) whole-query bound has been obtained here.
