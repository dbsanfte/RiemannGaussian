## 1. The actual reciprocal quotient, without new separation assumptions

Put L=2n, a=q^(1-L), c_L=q^(n(L-1)), and

    P_L(Y)=prod_(i<L)(1-q^iY).

Reversing the factors proves, without division by an output,

    P_L(Y)=c_L Y^L P_L(a/Y).                         (1)

Acquire all a/y_j, at soft J cost. They need not be mutually separated from the original targets. In particular no new saturated auxiliary difference is assigned the original decoder. Instead use the always-valid monic algebra

    A(Z)=prod_j[Z-(y_j+a/y_j)],
    F_aug(T)=prod_j(T-y_j)(T-a/y_j)
            =T^J A(T+a/T),
    E=B[Z,T]/(A(Z),T²-ZT+a).                         (2)

The coefficients of A and F_aug are acquired in soft J. E is free of rank two over E_+=B[Z]/A, and rank 2J over B, even if a new discriminant or target difference is nonunit. Its actual involution is sigma(T)=Z-T=a/T, sigma(Z)=Z. No etale CRT for the augmented points is assumed. Since 2 is a unit, coefficient comparison in the basis 1,T shows E^sigma=E_+.

Pairing the ORIGINAL factors i and L-1-i gives

    s_i=q^(-i)+a q^i,
    Q_n(Z)=prod_(i<n)(Z-s_i),
    P_L(T)=c_L T^n Q_n(Z).                           (3)

Thus T^(-n)P_L(T) really lies in the acquired rank-J invariant algebra. Its unknown coordinate is exactly Q_n mod A, not a supplied boundary constant. If Q_n mod A is acquired, evaluate it at the actual Z_j and multiply with the known units c_L y_j^n. This recovers every original value and its product, including zeros. Nonseparation of folded Z_j causes no difficulty for monic MPE.

This is a constant pair fold, not an exponent improvement. Literal application of the acquired balanced C action over E still costs soft J*sqrt(L), with all rank-2J arithmetic charged. Projecting to E_+ afterwards does not undo that cost.

## 2. Execute bidirectional initialization, rather than invent a midpoint

Let H_n(T)=P_n(T) in E. Exact reversal of the upper half gives

    P_L(T)=kappa T^n H_n(T)*sigma(H_n(T)),
    kappa=(-1)^n q^((3n²-n)/2).                     (4)

If H_n=h_0(Z)+T h_1(Z), its relative norm is explicitly

    H_n*sigma(H_n)=h_0²+Z h_0 h_1+a h_1².           (5)

All multipliers in (4)--(5) are acquired units or ordinary ring operations. They remain lawful at a collision. This executes the proposed two-boundary merge once the two actual h polynomials have been acquired; it does not invert H_n or the final product.

The reciprocal endpoint condition only supplies the relation between the two halves. It does not acquire h_0,h_1. One initialized completion evaluates the ONE acquired short polynomial P_n at the 2J actual arguments y_j,a/y_j, using the ordinary blocked method. Its cost is soft(J+sqrt(2Jn))=soft(J+sqrt(JL)), and (4) then supplies the original product. This is an exact paid bidirectional algorithm, with the old clock.

## 3. Successive involutions and actual sharing across levels

The involution for length m is r_m(y)=q^(1-m)/y. Consequently

    r_m r_(2m)(y)=q^m y.                            (6)

The second recursive fold therefore needs a DIFFERENT involution. Combining successive levels introduces the very q-shifted starts of ordinary blocking. For an executed depth t with m=L/2^t integral, retain the original labelled starts

    y_(j,g)=q^(mg)y_j, 0<=g<G=2^t,
    P_L(y_j)=prod_(g<G) P_m(y_(j,g)).                (7)

If L is not divisible by a requested larger dyadic depth, keep the final clipped interval explicitly; this changes none of the following upper bounds. A midpoint is supplied by these actual starts, not by a guessed solution of a boundary-value equation. Within any one j, distinct starts in (7) have unit differences, since their q-exponent gaps are <L<C. Across j, no extra LOCAL separation is assumed. For the original globally deduplicated native family, however, the following argument proves GLOBAL distinctness when m>=6; it does not apply automatically to reciprocal-augmented rows.

Acquire P_m, every labelled start and all its values by monic MPE. Shared short coefficients are computed ONCE, rather than once per row or level. The actual completion costs

    soft(m+JG+J)=soft(m+JL/m+J).                    (8)

There is a source-specific strengthening of this literal ledger. Write y_j=V_j^(-1), Norm(V_j)=beta^[2(N+delta_j)], with the native delta span <5H (the original envelope has span at most4H; the parity-repaired envelope is proved in native-pairing.md, SHA256 `06548c974c61d9922a4ec6c2f41ba1c95d7dfcbb92442b3b2e5ceff984cd8016`). The acquired global beta order is the product D_beta of its odd coprime local orders, hence D_beta>C²=256H². GLOBAL equality q^(mg)y_j=q^(mh)y_l implies, by norms and oddness,

    D_beta divides delta_j-delta_l-Hm(g-h).

Its absolute value is <5H+HL<=5H+4H²<D_beta. It must therefore be zero. For m>=6 the delta span forces g=h; equality of the actual points then forces V_j=V_l, hence j=l in the globally deduplicated family. Thus all JG original grid points really are globally distinct on every continuing input. This does not assert unit differences across distinct rows: local coincidences can remain and are processed by the retained norm/leaf consumer.

Consequently an implementation that explicitly outputs every original grid point has an unconditional Omega(JG) ring-output cost, and one that also explicitly acquires all coefficients of P_m has an Omega(m) input/output cost. Their optimized literal pipeline cannot beat the sqrt(JL) scale, up to logs. This scoped statement concerns that explicit pipeline only: the implicit prefixes in Section 4 are not required to materialize those points, and no scalar-algorithm lower bound follows. For m<6 the already-explicit JG count is Theta(JL), so that bounded-width branch supplies no improvement either.

At each deeper level the tagged boundary count doubles while the length halves. Optimizing the fully shared completion over m gives soft(J+sqrt(JL)), not J*sqrt L. This is the ordinary optimized block algorithm, now derived from the exact changing involutions; no new gain is credited. Binary product trees can share all intermediate products, but their scalar inputs in (7) must first be acquired. Retaining only the terminal norm instead still requires the equivalent resultant in Section 4.

## 4. Stronger cross-level test: both coefficient ends are actually cheap

The BALANCED native instance must use an exactly divisible main prefix, not an assumed balanced divisor of L=2H+2. Set

    L0=2H=k*(2k), m=k, G=2k, a0=q^(1-L0).

Acquire the two remaining ORIGINAL factors at q^(2H)y_j and q^(2H+1)y_j for every row, in soft J work; their scalar product T_tail is actual data. Then Delta_full=Delta_main*T_tail. Any nonunit tail norm is already localized to one of the original indices 2H,2H+1 and uses the retained original consumer. Otherwise the tail is simply multiplied, never silently omitted. This provides an actual balanced main instance with m,G=Theta(k). For the parity-repaired horizon 3H+2 the same optional construction uses L0=3H=k*(3k) and its last two original factors; no change of seed or decoder is needed.

The following equations hold for ANY exact length ell=mG. In their augmented application use a_ell=q^(1-ell) and F_aug,ell from (2) with that reciprocal parameter. The native balanced instantiation is ell=L0, a_ell=a0, NOT the full-horizon parameter q^(1-L). The full-length identities in earlier sections remain valid separately.

Try to avoid materializing the starts and retain their monic root polynomial. Let F(T)=prod_j(T-y_j), M=JG and

    R_G(T)=prod_(j,g<G)(T-q^(mg)y_j).

Its complete degree-M vector is NOT acquired initially. Nevertheless it has the exact low-degree q-difference equation

    F(q^(-m(G-1))T) R_G(q^m T)
       =F(q^m T) R_G(T).                            (9)

All degree-J coefficient generators in (9) are acquired in soft J. The q powers have O(log N)-bit exponents; no polynomial leading pivot is inverted.

More than a formal generator is available: acquire the top s coefficients of R_G for any s<=m by actual power sums. If p_r=sum_j y_j^r, then the grid power sum is

    p_r * S_G(q^(mr)), S_G(X)=1+X+...+X^(G-1).       (10)

The first s native power sums cost soft(J+s) from the logarithmic derivative of reversed F. Acquire S_G and evaluate it at the s actual geometric arguments by monic MPE, cost soft(G+s). This avoids assuming that 1-q^(mr) is a unit when mr>C. Formal logarithm/exponential reconstructs the actual s monic coefficients, with only integer divisions 1,...,s; these are units because s<=m<=L<P,Q. Thus the acquired prefix costs soft(J+m+G), including starts, with no unguarded q inverse.

Now execute this test on the corresponding AUGMENTED input F_aug,ell, replacing J by 2J, still constant scale. Its grid polynomial has reciprocal root centre

    b=a_ell*q^(m(G-1))=q^(1-m),                          (11)

because mG=ell. It is therefore reciprocal with a known unit scalar, computable from its constant coefficient. The same acquired top prefix supplies its bottom prefix. Thus BOTH length-m boundary coefficient vectors, the true normalization and the degree-2J equation (9) are paid in soft(J+m+G). This is a real two-ended initialization, not a promise that an arbitrary middle vector is known.

The terminal scalar still needed is exactly

    Res_T(P_m(T), R_G(T)),                           (12)

up to the explicit leading coefficient/unit phases converting factors to P_m(q^(mg)y_j). For the original F this is the length-ell main scalar up to those units. For F_aug,ell it is a known unit times the SQUARE of that MAIN scalar, by (1) at length ell; it preserves prime zero support, not the original numerical value. In the balanced native instance multiply the acquired T_tail² (and track that known unit) to obtain the full-query square. A reciprocal-image leaf maps back to the original MAIN row and index ell-1-i; it never maps a main leaf into either of the last two tail indices. No new collision source or recovery oracle is substituted. To compute (12) by monic reduction requires R_G mod P_m, degree<m. The two acquired length-m coefficient ends of the degree-2JG polynomial do not implement that reduction. The proposed forward/backward recurrence (9) has not supplied its interior terminal vector. In particular the given coefficient ends are not reinterpreted as an already-solved approximant-basis problem with a free inhomogeneous term.

Both polynomials in (12) now share the reciprocal centre q^(1-m). They can be folded once more into the variable T+q^(1-m)/T; this halves the appropriate polynomial degrees and tracks known unit phases (and any unpaired factors when m is odd). It still leaves the folded high-degree remainder. Its roots form a curved q-quadratic grid, not an acquired common scalar q-orbit quotient. No q-shift closure of the new folded algebra is assumed. This is precisely the previously credited constant-fold phenomenon, not an initialization of (12).

The complete safe fallback for (12) is to acquire the degree-2JG grid polynomial or its 2JG roots and evaluate the short P_m. It has cost soft(m+JG), as in (8). No claim is made that all scalar algorithms must acquire that vector; this identifies the unacquired operation in THIS boundary-value attempt.

## 5. What the C/C^t circuit does and does not initialize

The balanced q-binomial coefficient matrix and both actions remain available. Reciprocity changes its reflected coefficient entries by the known geometric twist

    c_(L-r)=c_L*q^((1-L)r)*c_r.                      (13)

Thus opposite coefficient ends are shared exactly, consistently with Sections 1 and 4. On actual outputs, however, (1) supplies P_L(a/y_j) as a known unit times P_L(y_j); it supplies no amplitude. The paired vectors u(y_j),v(y_j) remain paired after reflection. A fast action at one supplied vector does not become simultaneous evaluation at all those reflected pairs.

The kernel equation in the pinned Cauchy report has forcing (Y^D-X)g(X). It vanishes on the characteristic diagonal X=Y^D; reciprocity sends a characteristic pair to another characteristic pair and does not provide a nonzero forcing there. A transverse derivative still needs its own terminal state. We do not divide by a covered output, by 1-y_j, or by a new target difference in an attempted reverse sweep.

The exact additional primitive that would complete this route is a zero-safe scalar norm/remainder contraction for (12) using the acquired degree-J equation (9), its two actual coefficient prefixes and the q-binomial C/C^t circuit, at soft(J+m+G), with restrictions and original labels. On the actually initialized prefix ell=L0, m=k and G=2k, this would meet the desired clock after multiplying the acquired original tail. A balanced exact factorization of the full horizon L is not assumed. That primitive has not been implemented in this report. It is a native explicitly initialized boundary problem, not a generic lower-bound statement.
