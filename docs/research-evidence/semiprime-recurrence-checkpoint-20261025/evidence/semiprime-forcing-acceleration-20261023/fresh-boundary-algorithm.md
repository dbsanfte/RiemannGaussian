# Initialized q-grid boundary feedback attempt

23 October 2026. Scratch only; no repository edits. This derives an exact
joint correction kernel and a small initialization. It does **not** improve
the existing soft(J+sqrt(JL)) clock. The one remaining operation is stated
below without a lower-bound claim.

Write P_k(X)=product_(0<=i<k)(1-q^i X), a_k=(-1)^k q^(k(k-1)/2), and
F=sum_(k=0)^J f_k P_k, with f_J=a_J^(-1). Work in the P_0,...,P_(J-1)
basis of R[X]/F. Let B be multiplication by X modulo G=P_J/a_J,
represented in the same basis. Then

    B e_k=q^(-k)(e_k-e_(k+1))  (k<J-1),
    B e_(J-1)=q^(-(J-1))e_(J-1),
    C=B+a b^t,
    a=q^(-(J-1))*f_J^(-1)*(f_0,...,f_(J-1))^t,
    b=e_(J-1).

Thus the multiplication matrix is an EXACT rank-one perturbation of the
known q-grid matrix. No coefficient of a is inverted. All basis changes
and the displayed vectors cost soft J by the supplied q-Newton conversion.

## Diagonal q-grid gauge and actual initialization

Put lambda_r=q^(-r), 0<=r<J. Their differences are units. Evaluation at
these J points diagonalizes B. In this gauge

    C=Lambda+alpha beta^t,
    alpha_r=a(lambda_r),
    beta_r=1/(a_(J-1)*G'(lambda_r)).

The evaluations and G' values cost soft J, and every displayed denominator
is certified a unit. Let y_n be the values, at lambda_r, of the actual
remainder P_n mod F. Its initialized recurrence is

    y_(n+1,r)=(1-q^(n-r))*y_(n,r)-q^n*alpha_r*tau_n,
    tau_n=beta^t y_n,
    y_(0,r)=1.                                      (1)

The first J scalar feedback values are acquired in soft J, without
materializing all intermediate states: P_n has degree n<J and equals
the nth basis vector. Thus tau_n=0 for n<J-1 and tau_(J-1)=1. This
checks the actual startup rather than imposing a zero homogeneous
solution at time zero.

## A single common moment contraction for the entire correction

Variation of constants gives, for every n>=J,

    y_(n,r)=-alpha_r*(q;q)_(n-1-r)
                    *sum_(k=r)^(n-1) q^k*tau_k/(q;q)_(k-r).       (2)

Indeed P_n(lambda_r)=0, all forcing terms k<r have an actual zero factor,
and the remaining propagator is the indicated q-factorial ratio. This
uses no inverse of a zero factor.

The J sums in (2) share one scalar moment sequence. Define

    H_s(n)=sum_(k=0)^(n-1) q^((s+1)*k)*tau_k/(q;q)_k,
              0<=s<J,
    A_r(Z)=product_(j=0)^(r-1)(1-q^(-j)*Z)
          =sum_(s=0)^r A_(r,s)*Z^s.

Because (q;q)_k/(q;q)_(k-r)=A_r(q^k) for k>=r, and A_r(q^k)=0
for 0<=k<r, formula (2) becomes the joint triangular transform

    y_(n,r)=-alpha_r*(q;q)_(n-1-r)
                         *sum_(s=0)^r A_(r,s)*H_s(n).             (3)

Explicitly, with Q_i=(q;q)_i,

    A_(r,s)=(Q_r*q^(-binom(r,2))) * ((-1)^s/Q_s)
                                      * (q^(binom(r-s,2))/Q_(r-s)).

Thus (3) is a diagonal/convolution/diagonal transform, hence soft J.
For n=L, all J terminal q-factorials
are acquired from one scalar q-factorial plus J adjacent ratios in
soft(sqrt L+J), with only the promised units inverted. Once H(L) is
supplied, the entire native remainder therefore follows in
soft(sqrt L+J), including the inverse q-grid interpolation.

This is a joint correction formula, not J independent projection calls.
It also does not discard the correction: all nonzero terminal output in
(3) is carried by the initialized boundary moments.

## Exact remaining initialized subproblem

Acquire H_0(L),...,H_(J-1)(L), where tau is generated by (1), jointly in
soft(J+sqrt L). Equivalently, if

    T_<L(Z)=sum_(k=0)^(L-1) tau_k Z^k/(q;q)_k,

the missing operation is its J geometric evaluations

    H_s(L)=T_<L(q^(s+1)).                            (4)

The coefficients of T are NOT supplied; tau depends on the same feedback.
For n>=J, combining (1) and (3) gives the exact closed moment update

    tau_n=-sum_(r<J) beta_r*alpha_r*(q;q)_(n-1-r)
                                      *sum_(s<=r) A_(r,s)H_s(n),
    H_s(n+1)=H_s(n)+q^((s+1)*n)*tau_n/(q;q)_n.        (5)

Its startup through n=J is acquired as above. A naive update is not a
fast algorithm; naming (4) does not acquire those coefficients. In
particular the zero free q-grid solution after J is NOT a zero state:
tau_(J-1)=1 has already forced H_s(J)=q^((s+1)*(J-1))/(q;q)_(J-1).

No new exponent follows from (1)--(5). The benefit of this attempt is an
exact common-moment boundary representation with paid terminal decoding
and paid nonzero initialization. A fast solve of this finite rank-one
q-geometric feedback, rather than ordinary geometric MPE on supplied
coefficients, remains to be constructed. No generic impossibility or
claim about all other recurrences is made.
