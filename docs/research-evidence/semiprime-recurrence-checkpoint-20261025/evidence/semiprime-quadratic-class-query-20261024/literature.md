# A finite q-Casoratian normalization of the quadratic classes

24 October 2026. Scratch primary-source audit. A finite rational specialization of Warnaar's determinant gives an exact class-product normalization. Its short determinant value and label-direction derivatives are acquired across all classes in soft k. The remaining determinant still contains the unknown long q-Pochhammer values; no whole-query acceleration is proved.

## Actual convention and dimensions

Use k=d r³, m=d r², H=k², d=2c0 even and r an odd public prime. The supplied shifted native roots are Vhat_h=q^B C0 D0^h E0^(h²)=q^(e_h)V_h, with B=r²+r+12 and 0<=e_h<=2B. For the actual Pochhammer arguments write

 z_h=Vhat_h^-1=C D^h E^(h²),
 P_L(z)=(z;q)_L=prod_(i<L)(1-q^i z),
 L=3H+2+2B<4H.

Thus C,D,E here are the actual INVERSES of the supplied root-phase coefficients, including the common q^(-B) and mixed barpi^(-2m) factor. No D=q or E=q substitution is made. Every factor has the same zero as Vhat_h-q^i up to a known unit, with stored original signed index i-e_h. There are at most m classes, at most r points per class and at most k points altogether. Only r<=k^(1/3) and r²<=k are used; r is not assumed Theta(k^(1/3)) for variable conductor.

All bases, phases, carries and q differences through 16H are actually acquired. On the good smaller-nonsplit label the extended minus proof has exactly one row/index hit. Its new own index is I+e_h>B/2>r, using the source's stricter |d_h|<(r²+r+10)/2 and I>0. Therefore no first-r-1 new seed hits any native minus row at that field. Short head-unit screens below are consequently available without a new outside-index decoder.

## Primary determinant and exact finite specialization

S. O. Warnaar, *Summation and transformation formulas for elliptic hypergeometric series*, Constructive Approximation 18 (2002), 479–502:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00365-002-0501-6
https://people.smp.uq.edu.au/OleWarnaar/pubs/Elliptic-HS.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0001006

Lemma 5.2 is the finite Laurent-polynomial determinant, Lemma 5.3 its elliptic version, and Corollary 5.4 gives ratios of finite elliptic shifted factorials. Set the elliptic nome p=0 FIRST, so theta(x;0)=1-x. Then set the corollary's AUXILIARY reciprocal-pair parameter to zero and its A=q^L,B=1, and reverse the column order. This auxiliary zero is unrelated to native C. The finite result is

 R_(i,j)=(q^L z_i;q)_j/(z_i;q)_j, 0<=i,j<n,
 detR=K_n Delta(z)/prod_i(z_i;q)_(n-1),              (1)
 Delta(z)=prod_(i<j)(z_j-z_i),
 K_n=q^[2 binom(n,3)]
       prod_(t=0)^(n-2)(1-q^(L-t))^(n-1-t).

No infinite theta product, analytic tail or convergence assumption remains. After the displayed denominators are certified units this rational identity specializes to the actual commutative ring, including at long-product zeros.

Here is a direct independent check of every sign and q power. Multiply row i by (z_i;q)_(n-1). Column j is the degree-(n-1) polynomial

 Q_j(z)=(q^L z;q)_j (q^j z;q)_(n-1-j).

Its coefficient determinant is K_n. Evaluate at z=q^(-i), 0<=i<n: entries j<=i vanish when i<n-1, and the last row supplies column zero. The cyclic column sign cancels the sign in (q^(-(n-1));q)_(n-1). Division by the explicit q-grid Vandermonde gives exactly the constant in (1). In particular K_1=1. Equivalently, the corollary's reversed-column sign cancels the signs in prod_t(1-q^(-L+t))^(n-1-t), leaving q^[2 binom(n,3)].

## Execute the finite transformation through collisions

For the actual long q-Casoratian M_(i,j)=P_L(q^j z_i), the finite identity

 (z;q)_j P_L(q^j z)=(q^L z;q)_j P_L(z)

proves, on the SHORT-head unit branch,

 M=diag(P_L(z_i)) R,
 detM=U_class prod_i P_L(z_i), U_class=detR.         (2)

No P_L(z_i) is inverted. A native hit makes the entire corresponding M row zero while R is still invertible. This is an exact finite product identity, not an algorithm evaluating detM for free.

For the literal full-r product take n=r. Its shifted roots are not automatically separated. For a globally unit-normalized WEAK coarse-label variant, sort/deduplicate exact global coefficient pairs WITHIN each class, retaining original labels and shifts. Product-tree derivative evaluation plus one localized distinct nonunit pair gives a proper factor or certifies all differences unit in soft k, using the Jacobi-minus norm/coordinate classifier. No quadratic pair enumeration is needed. A duplicate involving the good own hit is impossible by extended unique-minus coverage. Removed global duplicates are therefore nonhit factors at that field. Dropping them preserves the coarse-label projective readout up to a nonzero LOCAL common multiplier, but is NOT equality of the original full-r scalar products. Identity (2) itself remains exact for either retained distinct menu or full multiset; a zero Vandermonde in the latter case is not called a unit. This optional deduplication does not reuse the old uniform class-size/leading-coefficient claims after sizes change.

Let the retained sizes be n_c<=r and J=sum_c n_c<=k. Every denominator (z_i;q)_(n_c-1) is a product of short head factors, nonzero at good P by the index bound above. Build all prefix polynomials P_s for 0<=s<r by successive linear multiplication in O(r²)<=O(k). Group points by n_c and evaluate P_(n_c-1) and its derivative by monic MPE. Total cost is soft(J+sum_(n<=r)n)=soft k. Norm screens give a proper factor or actual GLOBAL units on every continuing good label. Wrong labels may decline on a global-zero denominator. No long value or shifted tail numerator is inverted.

Acquire every K_n in soft r from prefix products and products of prefixes of 1-q^(L-t). Each such q-difference has 1<=L-t<=L<16H. Ordered class Vandermonde products cost soft n_c by recursive left/right polynomial products and MPE; this uses no square root of a discriminant or dense n_c² matrix. Hence all U_class, their inverses and U=prod_c U_class are genuinely acquired in soft k.

Their direction data are cheap too. With F_c(z)=prod_i(z-z_i), unit separation gives

 partial_w log U_class
 =sum_i w_i [F_c''(z_i)/(2F_c'(z_i))
              -P_(n_c-1)'(z_i)/P_(n_c-1)(z_i)].     (3)

The first term is the logarithmic derivative of the ORDERED Vandermonde. All derivative arrays and unit inverses cost soft k, with 2 invertible since N is odd. For coarse-label directions the w_i are 1 or the class's known label, constant within each class, so the Vandermonde term sums to zero; formula (3) does not need this simplification. Constant K_n has no root derivative.

Thus the transformed product of class determinants differs from the retained original product by the acquired GLOBAL unit U. At the good local zero, the derivative-of-U correction is a multiple of that product, so the two useful directional outputs receive the SAME U factor. Removed nonhit duplicates add another nonzero LOCAL common multiplier. This is a valid weak coarse-label transformation, not acquisition of the long determinant gradients.

Each short R also has a supplied-vector action in soft n: a rational-Newton prefix tree computes sum_j v_j prod_(t<j)(1-q^(L+t)z)/(1-q^t z), followed by MPE and the certified common head denominator. A final dummy transition retains exactly n terms. Transposition of that fixed linear circuit gives its transpose. These are the SHORT R matrices, not the long M matrices. Their complete determinant values were acquired above without materializing n² entries.

## Remaining amplitude, and the zero start

The remaining M entries are P_L(q^j C D^h E^(h²)). Their determinant in (2) still contains the unknown row amplitudes P_L(z_h). The earlier full-support Christoffel/alternant audit already warns against calling such long entries acquired from a determinant descriptor. The present finite specialization proves and pays a particular short normalization; it does not eliminate that terminal operation.

The actual class ratio is D E^(2h+1), with the mixed D factor retained. Even if E^(r²) is a known q-power, no finite transport identity above changes all those intermediate ratios to one short q-shift.

C=0 does give A(0)=1 for A(C)=prod_h P_L(C D^h E^(h²)). But zero is not a start on a native NONZERO q-orbit, since q*0=0. The exact scalar equation is

 prod_h(1-C D^h E^(h²)) A(qC)
  =prod_h(1-q^L C D^h E^(h²)) A(C).

Its product solution has degree nL. It has not evaluated a native amplitude from that constant value. Direct full coefficient execution pays that degree; a coefficient recurrence would additionally require its actual q-denominators when nL exceeds the original 16H screen. The known value at zero does not license analytic continuation or deletion of infinite theta tails modulo N. At C=0 the class Vandermonde also vanishes to a known power; removing that formal power does not supply the native determinant value.

The positive completed object is therefore an exact finite q-Casoratian normalizer, its lawful units and two coarse-label directions, with whole-family soft-k preprocessing. It is not a new evaluation of the class product or its weak readout. The earlier soft k^(3/2) complete query, or a separately proved faster shared evaluator, is still required. No D=q/E=q specialization, hidden field/order, randomized oracle, root-of-unity norm or generic lower bound is assumed.
