## 2. Test a corank-one kernel representation with actual acquisition

Write h=G_L mod F, degree h<J. The correct small Bezout matrix K is defined by

    sum_(r,s<J) K_(r,s) X^r Y^s

The divided difference is polynomial. With the actual Vandermonde V_(j,r)=V_j^r,

    V K V^t=diag(F'(V_j)h(V_j)).                    (2)

    adj(K)=c v v^t, c nonzero at P.

In particular C_0=adj(K)_(0,0) is P-side nonzero and C_1=adj(K)_(0,1)=alpha C_0 there. No random preconditioner or unknown local basis is needed. ## 3. Execute Bostan–Mori parity on the long q numerator

## 4. Track every later filter and the q-norm alternative

At stride B=2^s define D_B(U)=prod_j(1-V_j^B U), actual data acquired in soft J per level. For r=N mod B, exact repeated parity gives

    [T^N] A(T)/D(T)
       =[U^floor(N/B)] A_B(U)/D_B(U),
    A_B(U)=Section_r[A(T)K_B(T)],
    K_B(T)=prod_j sum_(a=0)^(B-1)(V_j T)^a
           =D_B(T^B)/D(T).                         (5)

Here Section_r selects exponents congruent to r modulo B. K_B is a POLYNOMIAL of degree J(B-1), with constant one and unit leading coefficient. The displayed quotient is exact; obtaining its coefficient array by constant-one series division is lawful and costs soft(JB). No scalar or formal inverse of a collision is used.

For any requested width-w window of A_B, w=O(J), the needed A coefficient band has width at most B(w-1)+J(B-1)+1=O(JB). Acquire it in soft(sqrt L+JB), acquire K_B in soft JB, convolve the actual arrays and extract the section. Thus the tested window operation costs

    soft(sqrt L+JB),                               (6)

not soft(sqrt L+J) independently of the level. At B~L/J~k its explicit filter/output band reaches the soft-L scale. This counts coefficient slots of THIS dense-window implementation; it does not assert that the filter has no other compact action or that every scalar algorithm must output it.

A window of fixed width does not magically suffice for the next level: producing a new width-w window requires a previous width about 2w+J. Running that dependency backward expands the demanded initial windows. Alternatively using (5) keeps short denominator root powers but retains the growing filter. Neither representation has supplied the late numerator to the proper-numerator Bostan–Mori interface at soft k.

The tempting q-Pochhammer identity is exact but different:

    A(T)A(-T)=prod_(i<L)(1-q^(2i)U)=A_(q²,L)(U),
    A_e(U)²-U A_o(U)²=A_(q²,L)(U).                 (7)

Its degree in U is L, NOT L/2, and it gives a norm of the parity pair, not either cross numerator N_e,N_o in (4). It cannot replace those two selected combinations without extra data.

There is even a valid zero-support version of that norm on the actual odd rough branch: A(-1/V_j) is a unit in every local component, because q^i/V_j has odd order and cannot equal -1. Squaring q and the targets therefore preserves original index support; component power injectivity maps a new leaf back to the same i. But (7) leaves the horizon L unchanged. Iterating it changes the base/targets and retains the same-size query, rather than performing the missing coefficient-index decimation. This safe power normalization is old rough-power credit, not a new rate gain.

Equivalently (5)'s numerator can be written D_B(U)*Section_r(A/D). Its far coefficients are therefore combinations of the VERY rational coefficients sought, at B-spaced indices. This exact identity explains why replacing the growing filter by a short rational descriptor without initialization would be circular. It does not rule out a different initialized scalar contraction using C/C^t.
