# Root review of the multiscale study and synthesis

26 October 2026. PASS as an applicability study. Checked the primary PDF
directly, especially the proof chain in Sections 2–7, Appendix A, the
polar-kernel and low-pass arguments in Appendix B, the planar calculation
in Appendix D, and the symmetric-pencil construction in Appendix E.
The parallel reader covered the applications and endpoint constructions.
This review does not certify all of the preprint's PDE theorems.

The scalar comparison is accurately scoped. Lemma 6.1 propagates a
nonnegative supersolution twice through annuli, and its displayed radial
harmonic fractions explain the two logarithms. Remark 6.2 gives the
matching scalar example, not a lower bound for a collision-query algorithm.
The source's positivity and maximum principles are never imported as an
ordering on the modular coefficient ring.

Appendix B's largest-admissible cutoff depends on both frequency and
observation radius. It aggregates the selected angular bands once and
keeps the others as exceptions. The report correctly preserves the final
reassembly needed before applying spatial frequency synthesis. Its finite
proposal asks for an acquired aggregate and a total exceptional cost; it
does not turn a norm estimate into a count of ring operations. This
proposal remains unexecuted and is labelled accordingly.

The finite random-sign equations follow by expanding and averaging:
cross terms vanish, and a genuine collision idempotent squares to itself.
Replacing it by an arbitrary weighted amplitude changes the diagonal and
can permit modular cancellation. The report therefore does not infer an
exact deterministic supplier from positive real packet estimates.

The main synthesis distinguishes the original packed residue from a
sufficient unique-hit weak pair. The row and clock boundary ratios are
only licensed on that unique-hit continuation. The rank-one segment
formula is ordinary commutative determinant algebra, not a transfer of
quaternionic positivity to the native ring. Its integration denominators
are certified by the original prime bounds.

The study makes no new whole-family rate claim, and no completion or
resumption of the paused goal is inferred. Preservation and source-hash
checks are documentation checks rather than evidence of the factoring
complexity theorem.
