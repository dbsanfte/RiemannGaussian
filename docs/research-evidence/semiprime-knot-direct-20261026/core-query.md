# Direct zero query from the epsilon core: exact factorial-coefficient test

26 October 2026. Bounded constructive pass, using the authoritative joint packet and actual native reciprocal root polynomial F. Scratch only; no numerical campaign, repository edits or new preparation theorem counted as a query gain.

**Outcome.** Executed the proposed factorial-basis/coefficient-singularity test exactly. In the long-prefix regime, bulk zero coefficients of the cheap core's universal-factor product are precisely evaluations of an explicitly converted degree-J polynomial H_T; that polynomial is EXACTLY a known unit times the original native F at a scaled argument. The top J-1 clock positions are cheaply acquired. Thus this particular core test does not shorten the remaining clock. For the moment proposal there is a further ACTUAL-input fact: the full weighted moment sequence has rank at least m+1-J, even when an original collision occurs, because F can erase at most J distinct clock nodes. A collapse to J states is therefore not the collision signal. This does not exclude a nonlinear conic-specific algorithm using the core or moments. No improved whole-query bound was found.

## 1. Actual initialized data and short-prefix branch

Use the supplied recentered monic F(z)=sum_(j=0)^J f_j z^j, J=2L=soft O(k), with native reciprocal roots and original integer exponent metadata. Set z_1=eta³ and z_i=z_1 q^(i-1), i=1,...,m. Known Laurent normalization units relate product F(z_i) to the original odd-weight prefix; actual opposite-row transport relates these to the legal negative-only original indices. Full-horizon native coverage remains the prior theorem, not an assumption that every prefix has a hit.

The joint packet acquires

    S(epsilon)=sum_(r=0)^m w_r F(z_1 q^r) epsilon^r,
    w_r=(-1)^r q^[r(r+1)/2-2mr] [m choose r]_q,

as the factored description

    S(epsilon)=C(epsilon) T(epsilon),
    C(epsilon)=(c epsilon;q)_M,
    M=m-J, c=q^(J+1-2m), deg T<=J.

The entire T coefficient list costs soft J; no nonzero leading coefficient is presumed. Its physical scalar values and 2J+1 moments have the separately proved soft(J+sqrt m) acquisition. These are valid input algorithms, not the original predicate yet.

If m<=2J, the old whole-product evaluator already costs soft(J+sqrt(Jm))=soft J. Hence the only regime needing a new idea is m>2J. Assume this below, together with the native short-order guard through2m. Then M>J and all q-powers and small q-factorials used below are certified units. Exceptional finite guard scales retain the inherited evaluator.

## 2. Root-on-clock becomes one coefficient zero, not one core root

Since each w_r is a unit, for 0<=r<m,

    F(z_1 q^r)=0 at a local prime
      iff [epsilon^r](C*T)=0 at that prime.           (1)

The index r=m belongs to the additional endpoint F(z_(m+1)), not the requested prefix. It must be omitted or explicitly subtracted in a moment construction. Formula (1) is exact on the ACTUAL native F, including its guaranteed final-horizon hit and any accidental earlier hits.

Let T(epsilon)=sum_(l=0)^J t_l epsilon^l and C(epsilon)=sum C_r epsilon^r. For 0<=r<=M the coefficient C_r is a unit. At x=q^r, a finite coefficient ratio is

    C_(r-l)/C_r
      =(-1)^l c^-l q^[l(l+1)/2]
         *(x;q^-1)_l / product_(h=1)^l(x-q^(M+h)),  (2)

with C_(r-l)=0 when l>r. In that case (x;q^-1)_l contains the exact factor1-q^0, so (2) still holds; its displayed denominator is a unit on this entire bulk range.

Define the explicit degree-at-most-J polynomial

    D_J(X)=product_(h=1)^J(X-q^(M+h)),
    H_T(X)=sum_(l=0)^J t_l (-1)^l c^-l q^[l(l+1)/2]
              *(X;q^-1)_l
              *product_(h=l+1)^J(X-q^(M+h)).        (3)

Then for every bulk index0<=r<=M,

    [epsilon^r](C*T)=C_r * H_T(q^r)/D_J(q^r).       (4)

No target difference is inverted: C_r and D_J(q^r) are certified clock units. The long coefficient-zero test is therefore a geometric-grid zero test for H_T. This already locates the exact physical readout instead of vaguely saying the core is invertible.

## 3. Execute that conversion: it is precisely the native polynomial

Put the acquired unit

    K_J=(q;q)_m/(q;q)_M
       =product_(h=1)^J(1-q^(M+h)).

The q-binomial ratios give, for0<=r<=M,

    w_r/C_r=K_J/D_J(q^r).

Combine this with the defining S coefficient and (4):

    H_T(q^r)=K_J F(z_1 q^r).

Both sides have degree at most J in x=q^r. The first J+1 such nodes have unit-separated differences under the guard, and belong to the bulk because M>J. Monic remainder/evaluation uniqueness over R therefore proves the POLYNOMIAL identity

    H_T(X)=K_J F(z_1 X).                            (5)

This proof is valid over the actual composite ring; it neither takes a generic-field limit nor assumes native coefficient independence. K_J is a product of only J certified unit differences and costs soft J. Formula (3) can also be acquired in soft J by the same division-free balanced product/basis-conversion strategy as the core: it is a sum of products of nested linear factors. All factor coefficients and scalar weights are known in soft J. But (5) shows that doing so recovers the already acquired native F, with exactly its original arithmetic root labels.

The coefficient positions M+1,...,m-1, at most J-1 requested boundary positions, can be handled separately: acquire those q-points and monically evaluate F at all of them in soft J. This boundary acquisition is real and cheap. The bulk still has M+1=m-J+1 positions, which is Theta(m) when m>2J. Applying the old evaluator to (5) retains soft(J+sqrt(Jm)); no horizon or degree power has decreased.

In particular a claim that gcd(T,C), or a rank defect depending only on their shared roots, detects the physical collision does not follow from the known identity: physical collisions are zeros of the coefficient functionals (4). C's universal roots instead make S vanish for EVERY F. As a narrow algebraic diagnostic, at J=1 and F(z)=z-z_1, a genuine first-clock root yields T(epsilon)=z_1 epsilon*(q^(1-2m)-q^(1-m)); T has only the root0, disjoint from every nonzero universal root of C. This is not asserted to be an attained full native reciprocal catalogue (J there is even); it simply falsifies the universal inference from a clock root to gcd(T,C). The ACTUAL-input result is the exact equation (5), which leaves any additional conic-specific theorem explicitly unproved rather than ruled out.

## 4. What the cheap moment rank can and cannot signal on native input

The acquired moments have the exact finite measure representation

    mu_t=S_m(z^t F)
        =sum_(r=0)^m w_r F(z_1 q^r)*(z_1 q^r)^t.

At either local field, the m+1 clock nodes are distinct and every w_r is nonzero. Since the ACTUAL F is monic degree J, at most J of these nodes can have F value zero, regardless of the conic catalogue constraints. Hence its nonzero support count R satisfies

    R>=m+1-J>J+1.

The full infinite Hankel sequence has rank EXACTLY R: factor it as the Vandermonde evaluation matrices times the diagonal nonzero support weights; the leading R-by-R factorization on those R support nodes has nonzero Vandermonde determinant. In particular an original collision does not turn this entire moment sequence into a rank-J recurrence. The loss of one or several clock nodes is a small deficiency relative to m+1, not a collapse to the cheap J-state size.

This statement is specifically about that moment-rank criterion. It does not claim all leading small Hankel minors are units: cancellation in the weighted sums can make them zero, especially in finite characteristic. A proper gcd of a cheaply computed minor factors N, but a saturated additive determinant zero is not an original beta annihilator. Nor is this an information lower bound: the small moment/core data already determine F, so some nonlinear arithmetic algorithm could in principle extract the original predicate from them.

The exact rank detector that does correspond to the physical zero predicate is the LARGE clock Hankel determinant. Remove the extra endpoint explicitly:

    nu_t=mu_t-w_m F(z_(m+1))*z_(m+1)^t
        =sum_(r=0)^(m-1) w_r F(z_1 q^r)*(z_1 q^r)^t.

Then the m-by-m Hankel determinant is

    det[nu_(i+j)]_(0<=i,j<m)
      =Vandermonde(z_1,...,z_m)^2
          *product_(r=0)^(m-1) w_r F(z_1 q^r).       (6)

Its prefactor is a unit, so it has EXACTLY the original zero/nonunit predicate over R. The single endpoint F(z_(m+1)) costs soft J and its scalar powers for any supplied t cost polylog work, but acquiring all2m-1 required moments or the full matrix costs at least the corresponding explicit length. The cheap2J+1 moments do not acquire (6). Calling its known low-displacement representation an evaluated determinant would reintroduce the same unpaid norm. Equation (6) is not counted as a new solution.
