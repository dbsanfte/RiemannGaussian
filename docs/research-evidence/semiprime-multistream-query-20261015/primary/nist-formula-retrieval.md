# NIST finite q-Chu source check

On 2026-10-15 the root and independent reviewer freshly opened
https://dlmf.nist.gov/17.6.E2 in the browser tool. The page records

    2phi1(a,q^-n;c;q,c q^n/a)=(c/a;q)_n/(c;q)_n.

The finite substitution used in the two-stream report is n=L,
a=q^-K, c=z q^-K. Its argument becomes z q^L and its ratio becomes
P_L(z)/P_L(z q^-K). Clearing the long denominator symbolically yields
the report's polynomial identity. The report and independent review
check its allowed unit denominators separately.

The second version https://dlmf.nist.gov/17.6.E3 was also browser-opened.
This note is an investigator's formula and retrieval record, not a saved
original NIST page or encoding. An attempted direct urllib download of
https://dlmf.nist.gov/17.6.E2.tex returned HTTP403 and the loop stopped
before fetching E3.tex. No original NIST encoding was saved.
