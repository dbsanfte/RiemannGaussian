# Independent algebraic/cost review of Alexander realization

2026-10-25. Read-only review of `alexander-realization.md`; no new source search or topological-source re-verification. **PASS scoped to the displayed algebra, acquisition costs, unit-normalization claims, and the explicit distinction between a genuine realizable knot and its compressed encoding.** No genuine algebraic or cost gap found in those claims.

- The displayed V has at most 4n-1 nonzeros and V-V^T=[[0,I],[-I,0]]. Interleaving gives the asserted bandwidth 3. For M_0=[[A,B],[C,0]], block-column exchange gives det M_0=T^n, and its inverse's lower-right block is -B^-1 A C^-1. The rank-one update therefore gives the stated sign. More explicitly, e_1^T B^-1 E C^-1 e_1=-sum_i f_i(1-T)^(2i-2)/T^i, yielding exactly 1+sum_i f_i((1-T)²/T)^i. No inversion of 1-T or f_i enters.
- The alternative lift f_Z, with f_0=1 and individually bounded lifts f_i, is legitimate and preserves the same modular Laurent polynomial. Its expanded coefficients may be large, but the sparse Seifert encoding never requires expanding them. The direct symmetric lift and this Conway-coordinate lift need not agree integrally; the report correctly separates them.
- Balanced Taylor shift G(w+2), with precomputed powers (w+2)^(2^r), uses only additions/multiplications and satisfies the stated soft-linear bound. This works over the composite ring without factorial inversions. The 4J-1 matrix entries have O(log N) bits; writing them costs soft O(J log N) bits. In the sixth-scale regime log N=O(log k), this is within the stated soft-k budget.
- The unit checks are sufficient: 2-z_j=-(x_j-1)²/x_j gives unit G(2). For each i below both local q-orders, 1-q^i is a unit modulo N. Directly, prod_j(z_j-u_i)/(2-u_i)^J=q^(Ji)G(2)Delta_R(q^i)/(1-q^i)^(2J), so the full prefix normalization and its signs/exponents are correct. The equivalence is for the *scalar aggregate product* across j; it does not recover its individual labels for free.
- An integral matrix with the displayed standard unimodular skew part is eligible for the quoted classical Seifert realization theorem. This gives existence of an actual knot with that matrix. The acquired output remains a sparse weighted band/Seifert description; ordinary crossing expansion can be much larger. The report explicitly charges that distinction and correctly avoids deducing that setting F_11 to zero geometrically unknots its particular realization merely because the resulting Alexander polynomial is 1.
- A block sum over m evaluation indices carries m separate rank-one updates. Their correction matrix is diagonal of size m, not a single shared rank-one correction. The report correctly preserves this unresolved partial-orbit product and makes no exponent claim.

The primary-source attribution and Kondo construction are accepted only at the report's stated inspected-source level; this review independently checks the resulting algebra and cost deduction, not every assertion in the original papers. In particular the second-difference recurrence quoted for Kondo has polynomial parameter growth O(J³N), so its encoded-parameter bit bound follows if that quoted recurrence is used.
