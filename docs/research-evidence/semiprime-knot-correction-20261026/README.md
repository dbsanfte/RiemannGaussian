## Paid nonaxis character filter

[The character-sieve derivation](conic-character-filter.md) starts from
the original complete signed scalar catalogue, with its known coverage.
For each row it uses the integer quadratic

    f_j(I)=H*I^2-2*lambda*I+(lambda^2-4abN)/H.

At a genuine factor witness this is an integer square. At an auxiliary
prime ell>3 coprime to abN, exactly `(ell+chi_ell(abN))/2` residues pass
the square-or-zero test. Degenerate primes dividing ab retain every
residue and are charged explicitly. Swapped rows inherit identical masks.

The catalogue divisor count proves an aggregate density bound; there is
no random-character premise. The algorithm builds a single common CRT
modulus, expands and pays for every allowed residue, and converts the
retained indices to arithmetic progressions with a common base. If B is
that modulus and t its number of prime factors, its nonaxis cost is

    soft(k + k*B*2^(-t) + sqrt(k*(M+B)*2^(-t))).

At the native sixth-root scale, an explicit choice of t yields a saving
of `exp(-(log(2)/8+o(1))*log k/loglog k)` on the `k^(5/4)` term.
This is a subpower saving, not a change to the exponent or a soft-k bound.
Here B is the modulus called L in the detailed report; it is unrelated
to the half-knot degree L used in the knot reports.

[The axis continuation](axis-continuation.md) supplies a precise completion.
The sufficient condition `3k^2*P^4>4N^2` guarantees a nonaxis witness in
the original horizon. A public bounded-factor search can certify this
condition or finish factoring, but currently costs soft(k^(5/4)) itself.
Axis conics are identically square and gain no half-density sieve.
Thus the whole-instance bound is unchanged. This is a proved sufficient
threshold, not a sharp characterization of all possible nonaxis coverage.
[Independent root review](character-review.md).

[The complete-family wheel](full-family-wheel.md) avoids that separate
factor-search prepass. It uses one primorial modulus for both kinds of
rows: quadratic-square masks on nonaxes and a prime-candidate wheel on
axes. A vertical axis receives the mask of its virtual horizontal partner,
so both primes retain the same physical witness index. Every mask and
progression is paid. The resulting square-root kernel is
`k^(5/4)/sqrt(loglog k)`, with the inherited polylogarithmic arithmetic
overhead. This familiar wheel effect is credited to prior literature;
it is not a new soft exponent or factoring record. Filtered prefix
values need not match all old accidental zeros. Retained coverage,
monotonicity and original-label recovery are the proved properties.
[Independent derivation and review](full-family-wheel-review.md).

## Executed knot corrections

[Twisted free-fermion TQ closure](fusion-correction.md) acquires a degree-d
vacuum polynomial directly from the native monic S, with d=2L:

    F_j=S_j/(q^j-eta^(d+1)),
    F(qz)-eta^(d+1)*F(z)=S(z).

All coefficient pivots are certified short odd eta-differences, so the
seed costs soft(L). Taking the full Bethe polynomial Q=S gives the
polynomially cleared transfer `T_full(z)=eta^d*S(z/q)`. Its geometric
product returns the original prefix up to known units. This pays for the
seed and removes an implicit input assumption, but the retained amplitude
still costs soft(L+sqrt(Lm)). Roots of F, physical Bethe eigenvectors and
singular rational eigenvalues are not asserted to be acquired.
[Independent review](fusion-review.md).

[The multi-hook calculation](multihook-correction.md) chooses the color
`((m+1)^m,m)`, whose diagonal hooks match the desired odd evaluations.
Annular Giambelli is exact, but the knot satellite evaluation is linear
and need not preserve products. At two hooks the report gives the exact
joint correction, including its quantum-dimension normalization. The
determinant of separately known hook values can miss a zero of the
desired diagonal product. That is a conditional algebraic diagnostic,
not an attained native-catalogue counterexample or a general no-go result.
[Independent review](multihook-review.md).

The review corrected the quantum variable: dimension brackets use

The finite annular presentation needs only short quantum-factorial
