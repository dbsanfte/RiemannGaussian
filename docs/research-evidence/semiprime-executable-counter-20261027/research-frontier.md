# What this slice closes and what remains

The goal remains a uniform guaranteed exponent at most 1/6, including the whole
input family and bit costs. This slice advances the alternative shared-counter
factoring route. It does not improve the native long-residue query's existing
soft-K^(5/4) bound or prove soft-K acquisition of its scalar C.

The public typed macro graph now has a complete semantic reduction: its literal
local Boolean tables, including equality subdivision, count exactly ordered
nontrivial integer factor pairs. Completeness uses actual computed transforms,
no-wrap integer coefficients, derived bounded carries, and constructed unique
flags; public source width and target-digit coverage follow from N. This is a
whole-family theorem, not evidence from a finite set of semiprimes.

The public graph has Boolean edge count E between L and 6400L and scope bound
at most 512L^(3/4), where L is the bit length. Its occurrence two and total
incidence 2E are attached to the actual constructed counter. The summary bound
and eventual acceptance of the exact integer guards are proved for this graph.
Do not confuse E with the macro transform word dimension.

The executable source constructor and factor adapter now match the intended
complete-bucket route at the implementation level, with independent structural
checks. Actual cached Hermite arrays and streamed ternary support enumeration
have value and operation-count proofs. Native rational rows and their integer
Taylor shifts have constructor-derived common-denominator/numerator bounds.
The cardinal-factor bounds close a further input-height obligation; their
separate proof report gives the exact final theorem and limitations.

The concrete operation ledger charges support pairs, summary boxes, representative
and fibre scans, local Boolean expansion, row setup and prefix calls. It gives
`poly(E)*2^(O(E*(loglog E)^2/sqrt(log E))+w)` rational operations, hence a
`2^o(L)` envelope with the public w=O(L^(3/4)). This ledger is not yet the full
executed primitive-bit theorem. Its interpretation and constants are documented
in `rational-operation-composition.md`; the finite Lean inequalities supporting
it are distinguished from the remaining numerical and execution composition.

Next required work:

1. Attach the actual row and cardinal input-height theorems to every corrected
   local input jet, streamed product, measurement/fibre accumulator, signed
   support prefix, convolution prefix and final Hermite sum. A bound only for
   the final mathematical count does not bound every intermediate fraction.
2. Refine finite choices, row selection, binary word decoding, normalization,
   local table evaluation and the whole cached/streamed pipeline into explicit
   primitive algorithms. Charge integer/rational arithmetic, data movement,
   indexing, allocation and retained state. The existing algebraic definitions
   and Python diagnostics alone do not supply that refinement.
3. Join the public factor-count reduction, executable counter, exact guard,
   finite fallback and prefix recovery into one all-input bit-time theorem.
   Then specialize the proved asymptotic bound to exponent 1/6 (or better).

The external published fixed-k SAT proof is already separately checked at its
exact expected theorem and original pinned source closure. That theorem does
not by itself provide this growing-arity tensor or factoring runtime result.
Our progress supplies missing ingredients for that extension; the final claim
remains open.
