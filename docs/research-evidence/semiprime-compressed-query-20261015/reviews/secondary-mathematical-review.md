and equal row products. Other targets cannot coincide in either field
because the offset range is shorter than the local order. Each exact
offset has one or two tags, and both multiplicities are nonzero in the
odd hidden fields. Unit-row poles therefore do not cancel. The reduced
denominator and Vandermonde/Hankel factorization give rank at least
ceil(U/2) when U rows are units. This refutes an h-rank bound for those
UNFILTERED moments, not sparse recovery from a different projector.
The p-1 projector is conditional on unavailable field/row information.

automatic saturated-leaf recovery or restriction inside an original
block. Every actual q-binomial coefficient is a unit for b below both

or the specialized actual q-binomial family. The resultant-paper bounds
