There is no missing root interpolation, but acquiring all long-clock execution remains charged. This clock-block expression is consistent with the row-block/no-G' projective alternative in the companion report; they are different groupings, not separate speedups.

The rational q-coboundary test is also correct in its stated scope. For a nonzero finite q-orbit over the algebraic closure of the good field, residues of R(qX)dX/X are the residues of R(U)dU/U at the permuted orbit. Thus every orbit residue sum of [R(qX)-R(X)]dX/X is zero. For f=XF'/F, f dX/X=F'/F dX has positive root multiplicities. Any orbit meeting the unit roots of F has total multiplicity between 1 and J, less than the good characteristic, hence a nonzero sum. This rules out a **global rational** R with that exact coboundary; it covers finite q-order as well as infinite order. Zero/infinity orbits are not used. It does not exclude telescoping only on a finite sampled clock, a corrected/inhomogeneous identity, other domains or a different projective selector. The report preserves these limitations.

For completely explicit moment initialization one may fix L=2J, as in structured-expressions; then both the short-q-unit guard and factorial-unit bounds hold directly from J<=K and the prepared 16H bounds. The existing finite-size guard for a larger chosen constant in L=O(J) should also cover the q-unit range. This is a parameter-explicitness note, not an acquisition improvement.
