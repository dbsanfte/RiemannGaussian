## Wilson: smallness is proved, but so is signal loss

For every actual integer n>4, the factorial residue is n-1 at primes and
zero at composites. Thus for any complex code F,

    V_F(n)=F(((n-1)! mod n)/n)-F(0)
          =1_Prime(n)[F(1-1/n)-F(0)].

If F(1)=F(0) and its slope from 1 is bounded by K, then ||V_F(n)||<=K/n.
For X<=n<=2X and arbitrary complex weights bounded by W, the WHOLE sum is
bounded by 2KW. The strict terminal theorem permits moving F_X with one
uniform K and weights bounded by log n; after division by any X^beta,
beta>0, its norm tends to zero. It cannot detect a nonzero matched source.

Dividing by the nonzero prime amplitude recovers 1_Prime(n) exactly,
and the arbitrary-weight finite sum becomes the original prime packet.
This is an exact source-restoring collapse, not a new prime estimate.
Growing slopes, cross-row correlations and Wilson quotients are outside
the theorem. The n=4 exception and other small heads are explicit.
