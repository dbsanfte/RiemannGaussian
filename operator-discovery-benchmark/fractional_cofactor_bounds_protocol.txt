Stage036: adaptive rational exponents with priced arithmetic recovery

Motivation and scope
The user requested exploration beyond square/cubic/sixth roots. Existing
rational_power_grammar.py already defines arbitrary exact rational powers;
some recent search defaults and proof providers used narrower finite lists.
The new reusable exponent scheduler has no semantic denominator ceiling.
It supports exact signed intervals and resumable reduced-rational height
enumeration. A finite work/candidate budget limits each batch. Explicit exact
requests pay their bit work and do not become duplicate later shell candidates.
This stage does not rerun the old fractional-moment or recursive studies.

Actual new construction
For each035machine-generated arithmetic state h and rational gamma>=0,
 h_gamma(n)=n^(-gamma)h(n).
This changes the arithmetic operator; replacing q=p^-1/6 by p^-1/12 alone
does not. Generalized prime powers are stored as exact rational exponents,
not expanded into an increasingly large least-common-multiple root polynomial.
The inherited valuation-e transfer multiplies by p^(-gamma*e), including
e=0 and the complete infinite valuation tail. The unit is unchanged.
An independent checker replays the035source certificate before admitting
any weighted estimate. Fractional branch choices on negative values are not
needed here because the weight's base n is positive. The scheduler also
constructs explicitly signed-power ASTs; those do not receive bounds from
this coefficient-weight proof provider merely by being syntactically valid.

Bound and recovery rules
Let the unweighted prime polynomial have leading power p^-r, magnitude
coefficient ceiling D and unit ceiling J. The weighted exponent is r+gamma.
On EVERY real partial prefix through2X, at the SAME y=ceil(sqrt(2X)), the
rough support is just unit and primes. Integer power summation gives
 |A_gamma(t;y)|<=K t^a (1+log t)^ell:
 r+gamma<1: a=1-r-gamma,ell=0,K=J+D/(1-r-gamma);
 r+gamma=1: a=0,ell=1,K=J+D;
 r+gamma>1: a=0,ell=0,K=J+D*(1+1/(r+gamma-1)).
For a zero prime polynomial use a=ell=0,K=J.
Complete-block energy is at most
 K^2*2^(2ceil(a)+2ell) X^(2a-1)(1+log X)^(2ell).
The035constant-sign and elementary prime-count lower rule also applies with
r replaced by r+gamma, retaining its sign threshold and updated unit head.

Undoing the weight is part of admission:
 A_0(n;y)=n^gamma A_gamma(n;y)
           -gamma integral_1^n A_gamma(t;y)t^(gamma-1)dt.
It uses every partial prefix, not merely an energy estimate at a few endpoints.
For gamma>0 the resulting upper bound costs at most2K, exponenta+gamma,
and the same log power. For gamma=0 the factor is one. Dyadic conversion
then gives the recovered UNWEIGHTED component energy exponent.
For r+gamma<1 this elementary route has exactly its original power1-2r;
for r+gamma>=1 it gives no better power. This is a scope/result check, not
an imposed expected winner. The checker recomputes all powers and constants.
No root-amplitude or fractional-weight shrinkage alone pays the joined core.

Frozen pilot
Four resumable batches of up to32exponents on[0,1], work budget100000per
batch; first batch explicitly requests1/257 to test a larger denominator.
This is a finite pilot budget, not a mathematical cutoff or exhaustive search
of all rational exponents. Freeze new code/tests/receipts before these batches.
Apply each emitted exponent to every035generated state. Check every resulting
candidate and its source/recovery separately. No arithmetic archive is reread;
this is exact symbolic proof search on the verified source-state certificate.
Record complete cursors, admitted requests, coverage and actual work. Peak RSS,
wall time and declared proxy work accompany the result. Memory16GiB,900seconds.
No hashes, old studies, old suites or model-generated winning exponents.

Interpretation
The scheduler can support richer fractional constructions in the joined-bound
search. This pilot covers coefficient weights plus exact Abel recovery only.
It does not refute nonlinear mixed potentials, fractional moments or other
operators. Accepted033 and035 obligations remain authoritative and unpaid.
