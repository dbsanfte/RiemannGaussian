1. What the new search must change
----------------------------------
PRIME_COFACTOR_BOUND_DISCOVERY_HANDOFF.txt records universally checked
arithmetic state closure, separately obstructed rough auxiliaries, and a search
of instantaneous quadratic forms. FRACTIONAL_COFACTOR_BOUND_HANDOFF.txt proves
that isolated coefficient twists lose their apparent gain on signed Abel
recovery. CORE_SIGNED_PRIMITIVE_SEARCH_HANDOFF.txt records an unrestricted
energy antiderivative whose endpoint is the original open target. The older
CORE_PREFIX_PROOF_SEARCH_HANDOFF.txt paid one convolution component but kept
its compensating drift and the complete signed remainder.

The useful new capability would be generation and checking of a recurrence
whose forcing and recovery are genuinely priced. Neither an exact source
identity nor an enlarged unpriced quadratic endpoint is such a recurrence.
The following interface keeps those distinctions machine checkable.

Use the stage035 verified source basis, with weighted modes
w=(beta nu0,-beta nu1,-R_u nu2,-beta mu), beta=9/2. The complete source is
f=sum w, f(1)=-beta-R_u. All units and repeated prime powers survive.
The accepted033 full source/drift/transport obligations are unchanged; this is
a direct complete-f alternative, not a numerical replacement of their join.

2. Primitive exact prefix and cutoff relations
----------------------------------------------
Let v(n) be the vector of verified discovered states, F(n)=sum_(k<=n)v(k).
For x<1 every prefix is zero. Let H(n;y) restrict every prime factor of k
to be greater than y, retaining the unit. Use the COMPLETE valuation matrix
T_e(p), derived from the rational Euler transfer, not the fresh multiplier^e.
Unique factorization by the LEAST prime gives the exact finite identity

 F(n)=H(n;y)+sum_(p<=y,p prime) sum_(e>=1,p^e<=n)
                  T_e(p) H(floor(n/p^e);p).                 (2.1)

The cofactor in the right side has all primes>p, hence is coprime to p.
Its unit is retained. No common-prime or repeated valuation is excluded from
the full coefficient: the least prime's exact valuation is simply selected
once. Every integer contributes once, and no global analytic moment or pole
identity is inferred from this finite equality.

For y<=z the exact crossing rule is

 H(n;y)-H(n;z)=sum_(y<p<=z) sum_(e>=1,p^e<=n)
                    T_e(p) H(floor(n/p^e);p).               (2.2)

Scale changes are also exact but have a real boundary:
 F(2n)=F(n)+sum_(n<k<=2n)v(k),
 F(2n+1)=F(2n)+v(2n+1).                                    (2.3)
The analogous H identity includes its cutoff predicate on every coefficient.
Do not assign signed cancellation to these new interval sums by naming them
an auxiliary. Their absolute divisor bounds remain available, with their
positive power cost. Full prefixes are not restarted at interval boundaries.

For a fixed p, F=sum_e T_e H_p(x/p^e) remains another exact relation.
The basis/unit/recovery and coprimality conditions are inherited checked
premises. If a program changes the cutoff with the scale, (2.2) supplies its
crossing term; this term does not disappear under a quadratic projection.
Parent and child use one source horizon N=4X-1. Any source truncation, finite
factor or support rewrite must be valid on that WHOLE horizon. Ceil/floor
root cutoffs and n=1 are part of the instance, not asymptotic conventions.

3. Concrete coupled recurrence certificate interface
-----------------------------------------------------
A generated state AST may contain full prefix, rough prefix, scale/floor
composition, verified prime-section states and signed linear combinations.
All scale/cutoff maps, parameters and source rows are declared. Their values
are not free trajectories. The certificate records exact expression DAGs,
units, domains and target recovery, not merely fitted covariance matrices.

Generate a real quadratic storage A(X) from these expressions, normalized by
X^-2 over X<=n<2X. Include every mixed term. A PSD form with an exact target
domination witness proves E_f(X)<=c A(X)+paid(X). Any variable weights remain
inside the sums; any additional recovery power/log is stated explicitly.
An indefinite storage requires its own proved recovery inequality and finite
lower/head bound. Calling signed work a positive energy is not admissible.

Align the child block with the parent by m=2n and m=2n+1. Child normalization
is 1/4 times the parent's. For each common n form a vector xi of ALL parent,
child, cutoff-crossing, valuation/cofactor and boundary expressions. Let M be
the exact symmetric form for A(2X)-rho A(X) in this common vector. Exact
arithmetic identities produce L xi=0. Inhomogeneous units can be included
by a constant-one coordinate, with its cost kept. Relations with varying
coefficients retain their n,X,p dependence and exact domain branches.

A sufficient certificate has

 D-M=P+L^t Y+Y^t L,        P positive semidefinite.          (3.1)

Here D contains ONLY forcing forms with independently checked all-scale
prices. Then xi^t M xi<=xi^t D xi on the actual source, for every admitted
n. Sum over the complete block. The detector may enumerate storages, exact
cutoff/scale stencils, rational rho, multiplier matrices Y and prices D;
an exact rational or polynomial PSD verifier checks the witness. No desired
source-bound value is supplied as an input matrix.

An aggregate version may use exact SUMMED relations and an explicitly stated
inner-product inequality rather than pointwise (3.1). Its measure, all index
domains and every residual boundary must be verified. A numerical covariance
or sample nullspace can propose a witness, never supply its universal relation.

This is a genuine proof rule if D is paid without using the requested root
bound. If D includes the original A, its complete endpoint, an unbounded rough
prefix or an unpaid cross, the result is an OPEN implication/restatement.
A positive term cannot be silently discarded; a negative term may be dropped
only after an exact sign or PSD certificate on its full domain. Arithmetic
relations must be proved before imposing them as zero constraints.

Finite infeasibility/dual witnesses exclude only the searched certificate
dictionary. The source may obey extra relations absent from L. A static
dictionary that merely restates the target does not become stronger because
more scales were added. In particular the search must expose which actual
arithmetic constraints, rather than PSD alone, supplied a signed gain.

4. Induction and exact all-scale recovery costs
----------------------------------------------
Suppose the proof DAG certifies, on every dyadic X>=X0,
 A(2X)<=rho A(X)+C X^gamma (1+log2 X)^k,
with A nonnegative, rho>=0, C finite independent of every auxiliary cutoff,
and a proved finite head A(X0). Standard geometric induction gives power
max(log2 rho,gamma,0), with the appropriate logarithm at resonance; rho=0
is interpreted separately, not through log0. For the cofinal objective:
rho<1 and gamma=0 give O(log^k X); rho=1 gives O(log^(k+1) X).
Nonpositive forcing power is also paid with the finite head. Positive forcing
power or positive recovery power is not paid merely because a finite curve
looks flat. If the target is all integer X, dyadic-to-integer comparison must
be proved for the relevant storage; full-prefix energy can use covering
dyadic blocks with an explicit constant, whereas a changing auxiliary cutoff
may require its separate crossing term (2.2).

A family depending on epsilon can recover a cofinal estimate only when its
construction, constants, resource/source domain and inverse bounds are proved
uniform in X for EACH fixed epsilon. Constants may depend on epsilon and
declared finite parameters. They may not depend on the current prime frontier
unless that dependence is priced. Base and cutoff induction have two distinct
well-founded rankings: smaller argument, or fewer remaining primes<=N.
The e=0 cutoff step does not count as a smaller-scale induction.

5. A rigorous no-go for the tempting lagged local norm variant
--------------------------------------------------------------
The complete fixed-p Euler transfer in original mode coordinates is diagonal.
For nu0 its symbol is
 F0(t)=(1-t)^2/(1-a t), a=p^-1/3.
In weighted logarithmic L2 at exponent sigma>0, dilation by p has Fourier
symbol t=r exp(-i omega log p), r=p^-sigma. At phase minus one,
 |F0(-r)|=(1+r)^2/(1+ar)>1.                            (5.1)
The strict inequality is elementary: (1+r)^2-(1+ar)=r(2-a+r)>0.

Now form ANY finite bank of fixed scalar dilations/linear filters of the
source states, and a positive storage on that bank. The filters commute with
the fixed-p dilation operator. Even with constant mode mixing, on a pure nu0
input the full bank after adjunction equals F0(t) times the same bank before.
Its Fourier energy weight is consequently multiplied by |F0(t)|^2.
If target recovery is uniform, that weight is nonzero at every neighborhood
of this target-visible mode/phase. Hence a strictly contractive full-phase
certificate is impossible. Adding a lag dimension or a recoverable polynomial
filter does not change this fact. A zero at the bad phase removes recovery;
an unbounded inverse is a debt, not a solution. The phase is not a loophole
of measure zero: continuity gives an open neighborhood with multiplier>1.

At sigma=1/2 the squared ratio is 1+4p^-1/2+O(p^-1). Thus even the proposed
per-prime envelope 1+C/p with a finite constant cannot hold in this uniform
all-phase operator grammar. This is a statement about formal operator norms,
NOT a lower bound on the actual cofactor source. Its arithmetic prefixes may
occupy a much smaller class; proving and using that restriction is precisely
an additional arithmetic inference required by a useful coupled construction.

The obstruction does not cover n-dependent cutoff projections, signed summed
constraints, nonlinear states or restricted-phase estimates with paid
complements. Those constructions generally do not commute with dilation and
need all their new boundary/metric/kernel costs. They are not automatically
successful. The old occupancy phase certificate alone does not prove that
their arithmetic domain is frequency closed.

6. Moving-cutoff forcing makes a new coupling genuinely necessary
----------------------------------------------------------------
The already proved prime-only rough lower bound excludes independently
bounding every H_f at critical scale. On the same child horizon, retaining
the parent's cutoff y=ceil(sqrt(2X)) has a further concrete change: for large
X and n<=4X-1 its rough coefficients include semiprimes p q with p,q>y
(and squares), as well as unit and primes. A third rough prime factor is
impossible once y^3>4X-1. Raising the cutoff to ceil(sqrt(4X)) removes those
semiprimes through EXACT (2.2), with their full valuations and source weights.
This crossing is not simply another copy of the old negative prime profile.

The source has opposite eventual signs in these sectors. Writing
a=p^-1/3,b=q^-1/3 for distinct p,q, the local formulas show
 f(pq)>=2beta-4beta epsilon-(beta+R_u)epsilon^2
whenever a,b<=epsilon. With epsilon=1/8 and R_u<=5 this is at least beta.
For a square,
 f(p^2)=(1-a)[beta-(beta+R_u)a^2]>=beta/2 for a<=1/8.
In contrast f(p) is eventually negative, as stage035 proves. These are exact
source sign envelopes; no all-scale short-prime count or saving is inferred.
The number and placement of admissible pairs stay in the forcing kernel.

This explains a concrete need for coupled arithmetic: a cutoff change can
expose positive composite mass against negative prime mass, and the full
small-prime complement must remain joined. Independently bounding each
crossing by its absolute mass may recreate the old positive power cost.
Exact signed relation (2.2) plus a generated quotient certificate (3.1) is a
way to TRY to retain that compensation. Declaring either sector zero or paid
from an observed cancellation would fail source binding.

7. A supplied all-scale arithmetic provider, with its real limitation
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Complete Mobius divisor inversion gives |c(U)|<=2 for
c(U)=sum_(n<=U)mu(n)/n. For finite P,
 C_P(U)=sum_(n<=U,gcd(n,prod P)=1)mu(n)/n
       =sum_(d<=U,d P-smooth)c(U/d)/d,
 |C_P(U)|<=2 product_(p in P)(1-1/p)^-1.                (7.1)
It is a genuine nonlocal actual-source provider that can be bound after a
discovered row is identified with the Mobius mode. Its exact prime-removal
identity, full positive geometric tail and growing-P product are checked.
The source weights/basis must be inverted with their full cost; a bounded
harmonic observable is not identified with a critical unweighted prefix.

One may add (7.1) as a bounded auxiliary in the new proof dictionary, retaining
its exact transitions/crosses. It supplies endpoint control for some harmonic
primitives, but no bound on the full nu0 mode or the joined prefix energy.
All-scale closure needs an additional coercive/recovery identity, not just
a bounded companion. This avoids repeating the signed-primitive search's
unpriced degree-ten energy endpoint in a differently named state.

8. Recommended machine-checkable result classification
-------------------------------------------------------
Emit: generated state/cutoff/scale programs; source-law proof; exact common-n
relations and domain partitions; storage/recovery witness; recurrence matrix
or summed-work certificate; independently priced forcing DAG; finite heads;
cutoff/metric/weight conversion costs; induction exponent/log result; residual
OPEN leaves; and restatement/commuting-obstruction witnesses where applicable.

The detector may discover the construction and certificate inside this grammar.
The identity, PSD, induction, source and cost primitives are supplied theorem
rules. Finite numerical validation can falsify proposed recurrences; it does
not prove them. Success must report the actual arithmetic premise responsible
for the saving, not only a larger state dimension or finer fractional lattice.
No such complete saving is established in this analytical review. Edits stop
with the reviewed interface and the precise remaining signed forcing.
