2. A tractable finite-template proof search

Search a conic identity over SYMBOLIC FINITE-SUM ASTs, instead of solving a
new matrix at each concrete N. The template dimensions/work limits are finite;
their summation variables and N domain are universal. Generate columns from:

 (a) complete038 zero-relations L_j(N)=0, multiplied by degree-one full-prefix,
     coefficient-difference, constant/RHS, or complete sum-atom templates;
 (b) rank-one square templates with nonnegative rational multipliers;
 (c) positive-kernel Cauchy Gram templates whose kernels come from038;
 (d) declared exact floor/block/telescope relations retaining every boundary.

Unknown rational coefficients are chosen by exact linear matching. For a
target defect M and independently priced forcing D, seek
 D-M = sum_g lambda_g*Gram_g + sum_j T_j*L_j,
 lambda_g>=0. The right side is nonnegative on the actual source. A more general
L^tY+Y^tL form is equivalent when every relation is exactly admitted and all
inhomogeneous constants have been included in an augmented coordinate.
The producer does real multiplier selection; the checker reconstructs every
column, verifies the rational solution/positive cone, and replays each universal
rewrite. No producer rule-application function is a verification oracle.

The basic positive-kernel identity, valid for every finite index set, is
 A*sum_d k_d*u_d^2-(sum_d k_d*u_d)^2
 = (1/2)*sum_(d,e)k_d*k_e*(u_d-u_e)^2 >=0,
 A=sum_d k_d, k_d>=0.
All diagonal/off-diagonal terms occur. If a normalization divides by A, prove
A>0 (for these complete kernels, d=1 gives A>=1) and keep its cost. This
allows a finite Gram TEMPLATE with an arbitrary-size summation domain. It is
not a finite numerical Gram standing for all N. Additional Young/Fubini bounds
must state their actual kernel masses, floor multiplicities and inverse costs.

AST atoms should include weighted_channel_prefix, generated_full_state_prefix,
coefficient_difference, positive_kernel, positive_rhs_prefix, full_finite_sum,
floor_argument, prime_selector, cutoff_selector, and constant. Every binder
declares integer endpoints and positivity/domain predicates. Sum products are
double sums, not one sum with its cross deleted. Match after reviewed exact
Fubini/Dirichlet-floor rewrites; preserve N-dependent domains in the normal form.
Kernel evaluation at a fixed finite list of d is not a complete all-N identity.

Represent a stage037 helper through its coefficient selector if relating it to
full states: its unit plus sum_(k<=t)1_prime(k)*1_(k>y)*h_i(k), where h_i(k)
is the corresponding difference of full state prefixes. The035 local prime
polynomial is an alternative exact representation on that prime domain.
Never assert helper=full state merely because both carry the same state id.

3. What a finite-template failure can prove

Rational primal solutions are sound sufficient certificates when all columns
have universal semantics and independent forcing prices. An exact separating
dual or coefficient-unmatched monomial proves infeasibility only of the declared
finite cone. The actual source can satisfy additional relations not represented
by its normal form; syntactic independence is not arithmetic independence.
Finite-horizon PSD tests may disprove the chosen universal certificate template
at an admissible formal assignment, but do not refute the actual source bound.
Do not call a changing sequence of solved finite-N matrices an all-scale proof.

Every new paid forcing/endpoint node needs its own admitted all-scale theorem.
The desired source energy, an equivalent changed-cutoff target or an OPEN
correlation cannot be used as that theorem. Changing metrics and rational power
weights retain complete target recovery. First solve/prices actual signed
expressions; taking absolute values separately would revert to the old cost
grammar which already leaves positive powers.

4. Decisive obstruction to an all-R proof grammar

Let f_R denote the actual four arithmetic modes with a FREE scalar R:
 f_R=beta*K*(mu*mu)-beta*K*(mu*mu_(2/3))
     -R*K*mu_(2/3)-beta*mu, beta=9/2.
The exact038 identities, prime-valuation laws, generated basis and divisor
coefficient envelopes hold for every such R. In particular they hold at R=2,
which belongs to[1,5]. The source-defined stage037 helpers keep their correct
units and prime coefficients at that R; the037 helper upper bounds remain valid.

On the initial ABSOLUTE half-plane Re(s)>1 the complete Euler identity is
 D_R(s)=zeta(s+1/3)*[beta/zeta(s)^2
       -beta/(zeta(s)*zeta(s+2/3))-R/zeta(s+2/3)]-beta/zeta(s).
This follows from the source Dirichlet-convolution AST. Coefficient divisor
envelopes justify the initial absolute identity. It does not assert convergence
of the defining Dirichlet series at s=2/3.

Its analytic continuation near theta=2/3 has simple-pole residue
 rho(R)=beta/zeta(theta)^2-beta/[zeta(theta)*zeta(4/3)]-R/zeta(4/3)
       =(R_u-R)/zeta(4/3),
 R_u=beta*(zeta(4/3)/zeta(theta)^2-1/zeta(theta)).
Both displayed zeta denominators are nonzero; for the actual rational arguments
this is independently certifiable by rigorous Arb enclosures. Correct R_u
cancels the pole by exact algebra. R=2 does not, as a rigorous nonzero enclosure
for (R_u-2)/zeta(4/3) can certify. No PNT or RH assumption is involved.

Why this is an energy obstruction, rather than just an analytic analogy:
Assume for every epsilon>0 the block energy of F_R is <=C_epsilon X^epsilon
on every dyadic block. The step prefix has integral of |F_R|^2 on[X,2X) at most
C_epsilon X^(2+epsilon). Cauchy gives, for real sigma>1/2,
 integral_X^(2X)|F_R(x)|x^(-sigma-1)dx
 <= const_sigma*sqrt(C_epsilon)*X^(1/2-sigma+epsilon/2).
Choose epsilon<2sigma-1 and sum dyadic blocks. The integral is absolutely and
locally uniformly convergent for Re(s)>1/2. Consequently
 s*integral_1^infinity F_R(x)x^(-s-1)dx
is holomorphic there. Abel equality on Re(s)>1 identifies it with D_R; identity
theorem/continuation excludes a pole at theta>1/2. The nonzero residue gives
a contradiction. A log^4 bound is an even stronger sufficient hypothesis.

Thus any all-R certificate of the proposed037 recurrence is impossible:
the paid helper/recovery and divisor forcing would give that log^4 bound for
f_2 as well. This is an actual arithmetic SOURCE-FAMILY counterexample to
uniform-R inference, respecting038's all-N identities, unlike the earlier
constant-sequence countermodel. It is NOT a counterexample to the correctly
normalized original source, and gives no finite violating X without further
work. Do not label it an RH refutation or a fixed-R impossibility theorem.

5. Immediate executable source-pole guard

The detector can extract each channel's zeta-product exponents from its factor
AST: mu_shift contributes a denominator, power_shift contributes a numerator.
Candidate positive poles are generated from numerator shifts, theta=1-shift.
At a SIMPLE pole, remove the zeta(1) factor and compile its residue into a
sparse Laurent polynomial/rational expression in named zeta constants and R.
Collect the exact affine R expression, solve its cancellation condition, and
compare it with the original supplied source-scalar definition. This discovers
the required normalization from the source rather than importing a nominated
winning auxiliary. Initial absolute domain and continuation scope are explicit.

Independent checker requirements:
 - reconstruct every zeta-product exponent, all signed weights and the unit;
 - use only the initial absolute Euler identity, never ordinary convergence at
   theta or an unproved critical coefficient moment;
 - certify all inverse zeta constants nonzero before rational simplification;
 - prove actual fixed-R residue zero by exact symbolic substitution, not by an
   Arb interval that merely contains zero;
 - prove a family witness nonzero by a strict rigorous enclosure;
 - higher-order poles need the COMPLETE Laurent principal part; canceling only
   its top coefficient does not prove removability;
 - denominators and net zeta exponent cancellations occur before deciding pole
   order; zeros introduced by inverse zeta(1) may remove a putative pole;
 - negative/complex/zero arguments or unknown inverse zeros are separate
   unsupported cases, never implicitly regular;
 - root/actual-source flags stay false; holomorphy is a NECESSARY implication,
   not a sufficient bound theorem when all visible poles cancel.

Fixed-R specialization supplies a real missing source constraint. It still
does not price finite remainder: analytic cancellation is not convergence of
ordinary sums of mu(n)n^(-theta), and it is not a floor-prefix error estimate.
The next multiplier engine must either represent and pay an admissible finite
version of this normalization/cancellation, or report that bridge OPEN.

7. Meaningful controls for a first finite-template implementation

Positive control: an exact source-generated convolution prefix has relation
Y=b with independently bounded RHS. Allow complete degree-one multiplier
templates in Y and b; discovery of -(Y+b)*(Y-b)=b^2-Y^2 should establish the
joined bound without separately pricing Y's summands. Hide rational state
coordinates by independently generated invertible changes after generator
freeze; do not supply the successful multiplier or storage basis. The actual
mu*One=delta channel can test this signed-cancellation mechanism, while its
convolved prefix is explicitly a different target from the full f prefix.
Report implementation capability only, not a new core theorem.

Positive kernel control: independently check the universal Cauchy Gram for
random positive exact weights/domains with full d,e cross terms and all unit
values. Random rational basis changes test actual multiplier coefficient
selection, not a hardcoded square pattern.

Negative controls: omit inhomogeneous RHS; truncate a complete d sum; change
full state into prime profile; substitute a moving cutoff; drop child odd
endpoint; change one signed mode/unit; count PSD alone as an upper bound; divide
by an unproved kernel mass; use an OPEN price/cycle; fit one finite-N solution
as universal; ignore a pole's lower Laurent terms; infer zero from ball overlap;
classify R=2 family witness as actual-R counterexample; accept actual residue
cancellation as sufficient full energy control.

These controls do not provide an answer bank of successful actual-source
auxiliaries. The actual target remains the complete original signed f and its
preserved parent recovery, even when every finite-template trial fails.
