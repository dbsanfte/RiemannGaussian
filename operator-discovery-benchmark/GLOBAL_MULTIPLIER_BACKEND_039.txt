1. Exact global operator language, not individual-N row fitting
--------------------------------------------------------------
For any prefix V with V(0)=0 define T_d V(N)=V(floor(N/d)). For every integer
N>=0 and positive integers d,e,

 T_d T_e=T_(de).

This includes floor-zero endpoints. A finite signed rational stencil
U=sum_d u_d T_d therefore composes by Dirichlet convolution of its coefficients.
Its domain is every N, not the handful of displayed038 finite rows.

Let the source-generated complete relation be
 D_j(T) W_j=P_j, where D_j(T)V(N)=sum_(k<=N)D_j(k)V(floor(N/k)).
W_j is the WEIGHTED channel prefix; P_j includes its complete source weight,
power convolution or delta RHS. Apply a finite generated U:

 sum_d u_d sum_(k<=floor(N/d))D_j(k)W_j(floor(N/(dk)))
        =sum_d u_d P_j(floor(N/d)).                         (1)

The prototype generates (1) directly from the source certificate. For any fixed
positive K it splits the inner sum at min(K,floor(N/d)); the COMPLETE remaining
domain is K<k<=floor(N/d). When the upper endpoint is smaller than K this tail
is empty. The unit is never removed. A changing cutoff is possible as an
additional typed production, but needs a checked exact domain/transition rule.

Thus a finite number of expression nodes can encode genuinely all-N identities
even though their arithmetic sums have unbounded length. Naming the tail as
one expression does not make its norm or covariance bounded.

5. Two reusable rejection gates
--------------------------------
Fixed finite inverse. Let D be a nonempty convolution of positive power atoms.
If a fixed finite Dirichlet stencil U were its exact inverse, its unit would
require U(1)=1. For every prime p beyond the support of U,

 (U*D)(p)=D(p)=sum_(power factors a)p^(-a)>0,

whereas delta(p)=0. This is an exact fresh-prime obstruction. It does not
exclude moving-support inverses, approximation with a paid complete remainder,
or jointly signed multipliers across different source channels. The prototype
emits the positive formal prime polynomial and support-domain witness. For
D=delta it separately requires the ENTIRE stencil U=delta; a unit coefficient
alone is insufficient when additional stencil coefficients survive.

Critical coercivity. A full operator inverse bound for kernels with zeta(s)
factors cannot be inferred from positivity of coefficients. On a weighted
logarithmic L2 line the Mellin multiplier is the full Dirichlet series, when
such an operator is defined. A zero/near-zero multiplier defeats a uniform
coercive inverse. At sigma1/2 the zeta zeros make this a direct obstruction
to a generic-input lower bound. Actual-source inversion remains exact, but
recovering its prefix costs the actual cofactor input or new signed constraint.
There is no unconditional evaluation of sum mu(n)n^(-2/3) here, and no absolute
inverse-kernel claim on that line. A truncated finite-domain multiplier cannot
silently cross into this global operator assertion.

6. Actual scalar normalization is essential, not its coarse enclosure
---------------------------------------------------------------------

For Re s>1 its Dirichlet series is

 -beta zeta(s+1/3)/(zeta(s)zeta(s+2/3))

Zeta(theta) is nonzero (the alternating-series continuation is negative for
0<theta<1), and zeta(4/3)>0 by its convergent series. The residue has nonzero
slope in R, so it vanishes at exactly the actual specified normalization

Consequently a claimed uniform-R cofinal critical energy bound using only

does NOT refute the actual fixed-R_u target. Its exact analytic normalization
must enter a source-specific proof beyond its numerical enclosure. A symbolic
