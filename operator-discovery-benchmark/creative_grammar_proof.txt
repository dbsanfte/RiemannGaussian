7 October 2026. Universal mathematical controls and finite discovery leads.

1. REPRESENTATIONS ARE NOW SEARCH VARIABLES

creative_grammar.py extends the rational Dirichlet grammar with typed
arithmetic sequences, residue vectors, residue maps and scalar values.
An explicit complete coefficient prefix may be embedded into F_p by

 E_(p,N)(a)(r)=sum_(1<=n<=N, n=r mod p) a(n).

The cutoff is mandatory. Missing coefficients cannot be supplied as zeros.
Residue operations declare a prime p. They include unitary Fourier F,
F^*, residue inversion, the nonzero support projection, conjugation,
translation, additive phases, and quadratic/cubic/quartic phases.
Composition, sums, differences, adjoints, pointwise powers and weighted
cyclic products allow changes of representation and higher-degree laws.
Complex-linear and anti-linear maps are tracked separately. Their sum and
complex Hilbert adjoint are only accepted when the declared types permit.

For f in C^(F_p), use
 (Ff)(a)=p^(-1/2)sum_x exp(-2*pi*i*a*x/p)f(x).
Finite character orthogonality proves F^*F=FF^*=I, and F^2 is residue
reversal, so F^4=I. Let I_inv f(x)=f(x^-1) on units, fixing the index 0.
Then I_inv^2=I and I_inv is a self-adjoint permutation. P kills coordinate
0 and retains every nonzero coordinate. Thus P^*=P, P^2=P, and PI_inv=I_inv P.

All phase multipliers have modulus one; translations are permutations.
Diagonal multipliers commute. Conjugation C obeys C^2=I and CF=F^*C.
It commutes with the real permutation and mask maps. These identities
justify every rewrite in word_normal_form. The rewriting system is
incomplete: numerical coincidences without a rewrite remain unproved.

The source enumerates all words of length at most four in ten primitives,
including the empty word: 1+10+100+1000+10000=11,111 programs. Training
evaluates every basis vector for p=7,13. Aliases not covered by the proof
rules receive two generic complex-vector probes at each fresh prime.
Those probes are empirical validation, not universal proof certificates.

2. AN INVERSION/FOURIER OPERATOR WITH A PROJECTION SQUARE

The search includes K=F^* I_inv P F. Its kernel is

 K(a,b)=p^(-1)sum_(x!=0)exp(2*pi*i*(a*x-b*x^-1)/p).

These are normalized classical Kloosterman sums. This is a recovered
Fourier/permutation construction, not a claim of historical originality.
The operator square is recovered numerically as a projection, then proved
for EVERY prime by the generic identities in section 1:

 K^*=K,
 K^2=F^*I_inv P I_inv P F=F^*P F=Q,
 Q=I-J/p,
 K^3=K.

J is the matrix all of whose entries are 1. The identity F^*(I-P)F=J/p
follows because F^*e_0 is the normalized constant vector. Thus K has
kernel the constant vectors, rank p-1, and exact l2 operator norm 1.
The latter follows from ||Kf||^2=<f,Qf>, with equality on a nonzero
vector perpendicular to constants. The proof includes p=2,3,5.

For the finite zeta-inverse coefficient embedding v=E_(p,N)(mu),

 ||Kv||_2^2=sum_r |v(r)|^2-|sum_r v(r)|^2/p.                 (1)

Every integer in the source prefix comes from inversion of u(n)=1 by
the divisor recurrence, not from an input prime indicator. The full
unwindowed series sum mu(n)n^-s=1/zeta(s) initially holds on Re(s)>1.
Embedding and projection have nonzero kernels. Equation (1) supplies no
ordinary-zeta zero-preservation theorem and no new arithmetic cancellation
bound as N or p tends to infinity. These missing bridges remain gates.

3. DEFECTS CAN BE MORE INFORMATIVE THAN ANOTHER PRODUCT

For two maps A,B the grammar can generate [A,B]=AB-BA and its Gram
operator [A,B]^*[A,B]. Positivity of that Gram operator is automatic;
it is not ranked as a new arithmetic positivity theorem.

The calibration [F,P] has rank exactly two for every prime, since

 [F,P]=(e_0 1^T-1 e_0^T)/sqrt(p)

and e_0 and 1 are independent. This also gives its exact norm
sqrt(1-1/p). It illustrates a support defect with a universal law.

A less trivial discovery is [F,I_inv]. The observed rank is p-3
at all sampled odd primes, across several residue classes. First, the
uniform upper bound is elementary. Inversion fixes 0,1,-1 and pairs all
other residues, so its +1 and -1 eigenspaces have dimensions (p+3)/2
and (p-3)/2. Relative to these eigenspaces, the commutator has zero
diagonal blocks and two off-diagonal blocks of rank at most (p-3)/2.
Therefore rank[F,I_inv]<=p-3 for every odd prime p.

The sampled equality can also be proved. Row and column 0 vanish.
For a,b!=0 its entries are p^-1/2[e_p(-a/b)-e_p(-b/a)], a convolution
kernel on the multiplicative group F_p^*. Its character eigenvectors
are chi(a), with eigenvalues

 lambda_chi=p^-1/2 sum_(x!=0)e_p(-x)[conjugate(chi(x))-chi(x)].

The trivial and quadratic characters give zero. No other character does.
Indeed the bracket coefficients lie in K=Q(zeta_(p-1)). Since p and p-1
are coprime, the compositum K(zeta_p)=Q(zeta_(p*(p-1))) has degree p-1
over K, by the standard cyclotomic degree formula. Thus the minimal
polynomial of zeta_p over K is Phi_p=1+X+...+X^(p-1). A polynomial with
the displayed coefficients, zero constant term, and degree at most p-1
can vanish there only if every coefficient is zero. That would require
chi=conjugate(chi), so chi has order one or two. These are the two
already identified characters. Therefore rank[F,I_inv]=p-3 for EVERY
odd prime, including p=3. Native Gauss eigenvalues independently confirm
the spectral formula and nonvanishing at the sampled primes.

This uses classical character and cyclotomic algebra. Historical priority
for the exact stated rank application is unresolved, so it is not called
a historically new operator. A useful uniform bound for the nonzero
singular values and a consequence for ordinary zeta or prime distribution
remain unproved. Algebraic nonvanishing alone supplies no scale-uniform
lower bound.

4. NONASSOCIATIVE OPERATIONS WITH EXACT HIGHER LAWS

The grammar no longer requires every candidate to be an associative
binary product. For prime p and integers a,b>=1, let

 w_(a,b)(x,y)=exp(2*pi*i*x^a*y^b/p),
 (f star g)(r)=sum_(x+y=r mod p)w_(a,b)(x,y)f(x)g(y).

On a basis triple x,y,z, the ratio of the two nonzero bracketing
coefficients is exp(2*pi*i*A_(a,b)(x,y,z)/p), where

 A_(a,b)=x^a*y^b+(x+y)^a*z^b-y^a*z^b-x^a*(y+z)^b.          (2)

An exact polynomial expansion certifies this for all integer x,y,z and
every prime. Characteristic-dependent cancellations are retained: a
coefficient may vanish modulo one prime without vanishing universally.
For (a,b)=(1,2), (2) reduces to -2xyz. This sparse associator is a
proved higher law despite failure of ordinary associativity at odd primes.
It is a phase-twisted graded algebra construction, not verified novelty.

There is also an ALL-INTEGER Dirichlet lift. Let Omega(i) be total prime
multiplicity, and define

 (f diamond g)(n)=sum_(ij=n)
       exp(2*pi*i*Omega(i)*Omega(j)^2/p)f(i)g(j).

Since Omega(ij)=Omega(i)+Omega(j), homogeneous basis triples have
associator exp(-4*pi*i*Omega(i)*Omega(j)*Omega(k)/p). The unit is delta_1.
The law applies to homogeneous components; it is not one scalar
associator for arbitrary inhomogeneous arrays. This distinction matters.
For the zeta coefficient input, |(u diamond u)(n)|<=d_2(n), so its
ordinary Dirichlet series converges absolutely on Re(s)>1.
No critical-strip continuation, ordinary-root law, or prime-distribution
estimate is inferred from that initial convergence statement.

5A. SYNTHESIZING A KERNEL FROM UNKNOWN COEFFICIENTS

Instead of supplying a named product, parameterize
P(x,y)=sum_(i,j>=1,i+j<=5)c_(i,j)x^i y^j with TEN unknown coefficients.
The source fits associativity from 27 grade triples by rational linear
algebra and finds a four-dimensional kernel. Its primitive basis is

 xy,
 xy^2+x^2y,
 2xy^3+3x^2y^2+2x^3y,
 xy^4+2x^2y^3+2x^3y^2+x^4y.

Each has identically zero polynomial associator in (2), so the finite
fit is lifted to a universal law by symbolic verification. An independent
symbolic constraint matrix has nullity four. The fit is exhaustive only
within this declared degree-five polynomial space. These are recovered
polynomial cocycles, not a novelty claim. Learned coefficient vectors
can be executed directly by the learned_phase_product production.
Over Q these four polynomials are respectively one-half delta(x^2),
one-third delta(x^3), one-half delta(x^4), and one-fifth delta(x^5),
where delta(h)=h(x+y)-h(x)-h(y). Thus they reduce to known scalar-potential
differences. The integer polynomial certificates cover small primes
without dividing by 2,3,5 in their residue fields.

Training the functional equations only in characteristic three instead
has nullity eight. Six basis representatives fail at a fresh prime seven,
including x*y^3. Counterexample grades are stored. The search therefore
retains characteristic-specific laws without mistaking them for laws over
all integer indices or all prime fields. Search vocabulary and feature
degree still introduce biases; empirical discovery does not remove them.

6. HOW THE SEARCH PRIORITIZES CREATIVE RESULTS

Operators and representations are generated before choosing one favored
target identity. Defects, anti-linear maps, nonlinear phase products,
singular masks and compositions are allowed with their actual domains.
Missing inverse or associativity laws do not automatically discard them;
the candidate must instead supply its true projection, module, defect,
graded, norm, or support law.

Acceptance remains separate from discovery. Required gates are universal
mathematical law, analytic domain, ordinary-zeta root/zero bridge, actual
prime observable across scales, information gain and historical priority.
Generic transport and automatic Gram positivity receive a penalty.
Finite coincidences, sampled ranks and new AST spellings cannot populate
the verified-historically-new list. The next primary target is a bound or
cancellation law for arithmetic families, with uniformly controlled scale
loss, leading to new information about actual primes or ordinary zeta.
The global_moment_grammar.py extension now searches mixed energy transfer
coefficients and positive functionals, with uniform induction, support and
forcing-cost certificates. Its unrelated calibration families and exact
scope are documented in global_moment_proof.txt.
