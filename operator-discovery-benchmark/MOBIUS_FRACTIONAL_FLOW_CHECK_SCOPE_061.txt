Exact fractional transport and the signed source
------------------------------------------------
Sort actual nonzero ordinary-Mobius source tokens by integer index d_j.
For fixed t>0 put b_j(t)=mu(d_j)*kernel_sign_j*h_profile_j(t), x_j=d_j^-t,
and S_j=sum_(l<=j)b_l. Append terminal carrier x_(last+1)=0. Finite Abel gives
 sum b_j*x_j =sum S_j*(x_j-x_(j+1)); hence absolute source <=
 V_t=sum |S_j|*(x_j-x_(j+1)).
V_t is exactly the one-dimensional fractional transport optimum between the
positive and negative source masses, after adding a balancing dummy of signed
mass -sum b_j at carrier0. For each interval between carrier locations, the
net mass crossing the cut must equal S_j, so any coupling has cost at least
|S_j| times that interval's length. The monotone quantile coupling, equivalently
complete ascending fractional cancellation with the balancing dummy, has no

Masses need not be integral and may depend on t. This is not an equality with
the060 fixed integer-copy cost or with a single transport plan independent of t.

Paid discrete exit remainder (the +1 is essential)
--------------------------------------------------
The source tail after real y has at most D-floor(y) token indices. Thus
 |S_N(y,t)-S_D(t)|<=2*(D-floor(y)).
For D>=2, K>=1 and b>=D-K>=D/2. Therefore

   <=(4t/D)*integral_(D-K)^D(D-floor(y))dy
   =2t*K*(K+1)/D <=4t*K^2/D.
The full integer-cell integral is K(K+1)/2, not K^2/2; a continuous D-y
replacement would underprice the final cell. Since D=floor(4N/p)>=2 implies
D>=2N/p, K<=N^exit_rho, the integrated residual is bounded by

D1 has K0 and no exit remainder, so no small-horizon division gate is lost.

are independently reconstructed and compared exactly with the certificate.
