2. Exact local obstruction, including finite lag filters

Let q=p^(-1/6), a=p^(-1/3), r=p^(-sigma), sigma>0. The first source local
Euler symbol is F0(t)=(1-t)^2/(1-a*t). At phase xi=-1,
 F0(r*xi)=(1+r)^2/(1+a*r)>1,
because (1+r)^2-(1+a*r)=r*(2-a+r)>0. It exceeds one on an open phase arc.
This statement is exact and uses no source observations or PNT.

For any Hermitian nonnegative metric M(xi) on the full formal input space,
testing its visible first-mode vector v in T* M T <= kappa M gives
 |F0(r*xi)|^2 v*Mv <= kappa v*Mv.
If root recovery ell*ell <= c M holds uniformly at every phase and ell v!=0,
then v*Mv>0 there. Consequently kappa cannot be below one on that arc.
The conclusion also applies to a fixed finite polynomial lag filter G(xi)
with metric M=G*QG and complete recovery. A filter vanishing only at xi=-1
does not remove the neighboring open arc. A polynomial filter annihilating
that mode throughout the arc annihilates it identically and loses recovery.

For sigma=1/2, the necessary squared gain is
 1+4p^(-1/2)+O(p^(-1)), so 1+C/p cannot dominate it for one fixed finite C.
Different metrics on adjacent cutoffs must price their conversion and final
normalization. Shrinking a metric can hide gain in the endpoint recovery cost;
it does not erase the cost.

Scope of this obstruction is essential. It rejects a generic-input, full-phase,
same-norm contraction with target recovery. It does NOT reject every finite
quadratic lag construction, all actual-source inequalities, or a recurrence
using independently proved restrictions on the input. Formal eigenvectors need
not be realized by arithmetic cofactor prefixes. Record a formal-input failure
as a rule/grammar failure, never an actual-source lower bound.

3. A minimally different executable proof attempt

Use two variables: prefix horizon x and prime cutoff y. Define each state from
a source-law program, not from an observed fit. A primitive state is an exact
linear combination of the035 mode coefficients restricted by declared prime
support, valuation and coefficient-weight predicates. Generate a bounded
dictionary bottom-up by complete prime sections, rational coefficient twists,
causal dilation lags and optional harmonic accumulations. Every program must
have a unique coefficient semantics and a proved unit.

The exact next-prime recurrence is
 H(x;y_prev)=H(x;p)+sum_(e>=1)T_e(p) H(x/p^e;p),
where p is the NEXT prime, p>y_prev, and there is no prime between them.
For real x, only p^e<=x contribute; prefixes below one are exactly zero.
The e=0 term has the SAME x and an increased cutoff. Every e>=1 branch has a
smaller x. A well-founded proof must decrease a declared lexicographic or
finite-horizon rank using both variables, not claim all branches shrink x.
At y>=x, the rough prefix is the full signed unit, not zero.

Lift a finite collection of these prefixes and their dilated versions into a
coupled storage. Infer rational storage coefficients by solving the complete
signed recurrence before taking absolute values. On squaring, preserve every
e,j cross, including shared-prime/repeated-valuation terms and all moving floor
boundaries. An all-valuation rational generating law licenses the infinite
symbol; a finite-x proof additionally records the exact finite summation range.

Actual arithmetic restrictions, rather than a new unrestricted metric alone,
are the minimal change that can evade Section2. Candidate restriction providers:

 (i) exact Dirichlet identities, notably mu*One=delta, proved from the source
     Euler factors or full divisor inversion at every integer;
 (ii) exact cutoff transition identities with all support boundaries;
 (iii) bounded harmonic states derived from complete floor inversion, such as
     |sum_(n<=N)mu(n)/n|<=2; a prime-free harmonic state needs its complete
     prime-dilation formula and its uniform bound, not sample boundedness;
 (iv) signed paired-kernel identities whose full boundary remainder has an
     independent norm provider, rather than a missing correlation renamed zero.

These are available proof primitives or candidate lemma types, not evidence
that they suffice. Harmonic coordinates can be bounded while the target is not
coercively recoverable from them. Adding a growing family of prime-free states
requires explicit dimension, prime-product, metric and recovery costs. No fixed
prime-set constant is silently uniform in a growing set.

The producer should output the attempted joint inequality plus its exact
unpaid residual, endpoint cost and dependency frontier. A new arithmetic
correlation lemma is allowed as an OPEN requested input. It becomes a premise
only after a separate proof is admitted. The search must not assume the desired
full-energy bound to certify its storage or that new lemma.

4. Concrete certificate schema and validator requirements

Suggested top-level fields:
 source_binding, inherited_parent_binding, state_programs, domain,
 normalization, recurrence, valuation_tail, boundary_partition,
 storage_candidates, selected_proof, dependency_graph, failure_feedback,
 search_grammar, search_limits, whole_signed_bound_proved, RH_proved.
Producer choices and checker rules must be distinct modules.

Each state_program contains: earlier dependencies; coefficient expression;
exact rational exponents; restrictions (next-prime/coprimality/valuation);
unit; valid horizon/cutoff domain; and explicit recovery row. A q-root change
is a representation alias. A coefficient twist is a new operator and must
carry its signed Abel inverse and EVERY partial-prefix requirement.

Each recurrence node contains: input/output goals and exact norm convention;
typed variables with domains; source-law certificate; all branches and units;
normalization factors; boundary terms; actual arithmetic restrictions used;
their proved dependency ids; and decreasing rank. Generic and actual-source
premises cannot share an untyped label.

Each storage candidate contains: complete monomial/lag basis; rational unknown
solution; Hermitian form; exact PSD/target-domination witness; scale/cutoff
dependence; coefficient-change terms; initialized endpoint; forcing terms;
complete signed expansion; and cost tuple (power,log,constant,quantifier).
If indefinite storage is allowed, its signed endpoint and telescoping proof
need their own price. Positivity is not manufactured by dropping cross terms.
PSD alone bounds nothing without domination and a recurrence estimate.

The validator independently reconstructs source algebra and every attempted
column. It checks primal identities and dual infeasibility witnesses against
ALL declared affordable columns. Failure means failure of that finite grammar.
If the producer claims finite-search optimality, reconstruct full candidate
coverage or verify an independent global lower certificate for that search.
An accepted single sound proof need not claim exhaustive search optimality.

For polynomial parameter inequalities, display the parameter domain, all
nonzero denominators, substitutions and exact nonnegative certificate (PSD,
SOS, interval Bernstein partition, or another reviewed rule). Rational
samples are proposals, not positivity certificates. Rational exponent parsing
is canonical and bounded by explicit resource guards; arbitrary semantic
denominators must not become a mathematical obstruction.

All proof dependencies are topological. A paid node has only admitted premises.
OPEN arithmetic leaves cannot be counted as proved by replaying conditional
algebra. A cycle through the full target, through its endpoint price, or through
an equivalent changed-cutoff target is rejected. Conditional feasibility and
unconditional payment are separate fields. Every epsilon>0 quantifier must
carry one uniform finite head and constants independent of every searched
cutoff; an estimate at one chosen epsilon is not the entire root goal.

For logarithmic Plancherel rules, zero-extend the input below one and above the
declared finite T, retain all phases, and restrict the output only afterward.
Discrete whole-prefix energy uses complete step-function cells and the exact
conversion cost (T=N+1 for prefixes through N). L2 permits no unchecked
point sampling. A low-phase certificate leaves the complement explicitly open.

5. Counterexample-guided expansion without answer leakage

Separate counterexamples into three types:
 A. algebraic source-law failure: an exact formal polynomial identity fails;
 B. generic norm-rule failure: an admissible formal vector/phase violates the
    proposed universal inequality;
 C. arithmetic finite witness: a separately licensed exact source instance
    violates a finite claim (usable after an explicitly frozen observation run).
Only A/C reject a claimed actual identity; B rejects its generic justification.
No absence of finite witnesses establishes an infinite arithmetic inequality.

A finite-grammar dual can identify a missing transition monomial. Add only
states generated by documented operations that can produce that monomial,
and regenerate its complete transition and endpoint costs. Do not inject the
desired cancelling coefficient or a known final invariant into the grammar.
If a generic phase obstruction persists, redirect to proving an actual-input
restriction or price the complement; do not merely refine a fitted phase mesh.
If a storage is exactly the target energy, request an independent endpoint
provider or mark endpoint_restatement. Do not publish it as progress in payment.

6. Discovery-capability calibration design

Freeze one general producer before generating private synthetic inputs. Inputs
provide exact coefficient/Euler or recurrence definitions and the requested
bound, but no successful states, invariant, cancellation coefficients, selected
prime, lag length or target normalization. The checker has those source
definitions and independently verifies the final proof. Benchmark identity
success, new affordable endpoint/forcing success and full target success are
reported separately. Do not credit a supplied state basis as a discovery.

Useful calibration families:
 - arithmetic polynomial primitive families with randomized exact affine
   coordinate changes; test that a cheaper endpoint is found and recovered;
 - finite coupled scale recurrences with hidden target-visible stable quotient
   and unstable unobserved modes; checker proves all-scale identities and the
   quotient norm, producer must infer the quotient from the source;
 - paired signed transition families requiring a nonzero cross term and a
   separately controlled boundary, including deterministic zero-event updates;
 - fractional coefficient families where a real gain survives a proved joint
   inverse, mixed with pure-twist controls where recovery consumes the gain;
 - random exact bases hiding a bounded harmonic coordinate and instances where
   that coordinate cannot recover the target; success cannot mean projection
   onto the cheap coordinate alone.

Negative controls: identical finite training prefix with a delayed unstable
source transition; wrong fresh-prime/copimality domain; omitted repeated
valuation; omitted e=0 branch; unit zeroed; wrong next-prime transition; missing
one cross; moving cutoff substituted into a fixed-cutoff theorem; singular form
that drops the target-visible direction; shrinking metric with unpriced
inverse; paid OPEN lemma; target-energy endpoint; alpha-dependent constant
masquerading as uniform; good endpoints but uncontrolled partial exits; a
low-phase filter whose complementary phases carry the expanding direction.

Synthetic success proves reusable certificate capability only. It neither
proves that these calibrations cover the actual arithmetic difficulty nor
contributes a full-core payment. The actual adapter must expose the same exact
source/recovery checks and honestly report unresolved closure or bound leaves.

7. Recommendation to the implementer

Do not spend the next study only finding a smaller local multiplier on the
same unrestricted state space: Section2 already gives a structural obstruction.
Implement a coupled recurrence compiler with source-derived restrictions and
explicit initialized endpoints first. Permit automatic conditional lemma
generation from its remaining signed residual. The immediate meaningful result
is a complete attempted uniform proof, independently replayable, with a new
arithmetic premise or independently paid endpoint clearly identified. There is
no guarantee this finite language closes the present full signed goal.
