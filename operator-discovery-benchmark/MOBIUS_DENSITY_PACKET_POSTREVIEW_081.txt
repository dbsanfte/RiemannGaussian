What remains and how to make next search meaningful
The exact paid packet control by itself cannot close the recurrence.
Conditional normalized drift has multiplier at most3/4, but still requires
U(u)<=K*u uniformly with a complete source-bound base. The output exports
that leaf explicitly. For these actual kernels E(1)=0, so the all-positive-
integer conditional leaf would handle the base; no uniform U proof exists.
Original source/head/core/parameter transfer and an epsilon family remain
separate. A fixed q1 slice is not RH, and a failed K is not an RH refutation.

The next detector should consume the exact dense residual kernel, not simply
increase K. It can seek an exact decomposition of the joint frontier into
several paid balanced packets, paid diagonal coefficient prices and
nonpositive forms, with every coefficient reconstructed symbolically.
Alternatively a cofactor recurrence must isolate its g=1 current-radius
primitive coprime-pair frontier; g>=2 shorter radius alone is not closure.
Sparse subpower packet coverage and eligible retention cannot stand in
for that dense arithmetic obligation. The balanced packet theorem is useful
proof progress, but its current conservative constants and extracted finite
size do not supply an effective source bound.
