COMPLETE PRIME-ORBIT REDUCTIONS OF JOINED CUTOFF WORK

Status: exact arithmetic grouping and some unconditional component payments;
the complete uniform signed-work estimate remains OPEN. This new memo reads
the current cutoff-work and arithmetic-grouping handoffs, the authoritative
obligation, and divisor_dual_proof.txt. No source observation, test, numerical

1. Target and domain

For each integer q>=2 let w_1(q),w_2(q) be the COMPLETE old-height integrals
W_1,W_2 in candidate10_cutoff_work_obligation.json, with the exact actual old
state at cutoff q-1, its analytic residues, and all scalar and mode crosses.
Write
 R(q)=mu(q)w_1(q)+mu(q)^2 w_2(q),
 S(V)=sum_(2<=q<=V)R(q).
The required leaf is S(V)<=C V^beta, EVERY integer V>=1, beta=12/5.
Set w_1(1)=w_2(1)=R(1)=0 only to extend this finite event sum. This does not
erase the original cutoff-energy base or the arithmetic unit. Review024 has
already paid the zero-sign work and the complete CHILD height shells, and
proves recovery from this one-sided all-prefix bound. Neither w_j is positive
or independent of mu(q). In particular mu(q)^2 w_2(q) is not an unsigned square.

All prime statements below use actual mu. Repeated-prime valuations have
mu(q)=mu(q)^2=0 and therefore zero EVENT weight, but their previous-state,
finite-head, prime-power, and source-mode contributions inside w_j remain.
Replacing that previous state by a squarefree-only source is forbidden.

2. Exact one-prime identity, with the whole exit

Fix a prime p. Every squarefree q is uniquely r or pr with p not dividing r.
The local identities mu(pr)=-mu(r), mu(pr)^2=mu(r)^2 then give
 S(V)=sum_(p not dividing r, r<=V/p)
       {mu(r)[w_1(r)-w_1(pr)]+mu(r)^2[w_2(r)+w_2(pr)]}
      +sum_(p not dividing r, V/p<r<=V)R(r).             (2.1)
This includes r=1 with its zero event weight. Non-squarefree r contributes zero.
There is no contribution from p^2r events, because their two source factors
are exactly zero. The floor V/p is used in integer implementations. The last
sum is a FULL signed exit; it is not a negligible count or a paid energy shell.

Thus an odd difference is accompanied by an even SUM. Neither prime insertion
nor absolute values turn the latter into a difference. An exact or estimated
coupling between the two sectors must be proved separately. Pairing primes
with the unit yields R(p) itself, not a cancellation with a missing unit event.

3. Squarefree cubes and incomplete faces

For a finite distinct prime set P let Q=product_(p in P)p and T_p w(r)=w(pr).
The unique decomposition q=rd, gcd(r,Q)=1, d dividing Q, gives
 S(V)=sum_(r<=V,gcd(r,Q)=1)
  {mu(r) sum_(d|Q,rd<=V)mu(d)w_1(rd)
   +mu(r)^2 sum_(d|Q,rd<=V)w_2(rd)}.                    (3.1)
For r<=V/Q the cube is complete and its two inner operators are
 product_(p in P)(I-T_p)w_1(r),
 product_(p in P)(I+T_p)w_2(r).                         (3.2)
For r>V/Q the truncated face in (3.1) is retained literally. Equivalently
apply (3.2) to w_j(q)1_(q<=V) extended by zero; its cutoff discontinuities are
then part of the differences. Do not evaluate unrequested q>V and subtract
an estimated outside tail in place of those exact faces.

Every composite event is represented once. Iterating separate prime pairings
without this unique decomposition may overcount it. If P=P(V), Q and the
number of faces enter the certificate. When Q>V there are no complete cubes:
ALL of (3.1) is truncated-face work. Calling it a boundary does not make it
small. A growing set can be legitimate, but the uniform cost must be proved;
a constant depending on a growing P is not a uniform C.

4. General fractional orbit covers: capacity is part of the theorem

A detector need not use complete cubes. Consider edges (r,pr) within [1,V],
with p prime, p not dividing r, and nonnegative rational weights lambda_e.
If each nonunit vertex has total incident weight at most one, then exactly
 S(V)=sum_edges lambda_e [R(r)+R(pr)]
      +sum_(n=2)^V [1-sum_(e incident n)lambda_e]R(n).    (4.1)
An edge touching the unit uses R(1)=0. Unit multiplicity can be omitted or
carried explicitly because this EVENT value is zero, but supplies no free
signed cancellation. Every other multiplicity and unmatched residual remains.
Each paired edge has precisely the odd/even form in (2.1). This rule permits
bounded-congestion growing-prime matchings or scale-dependent orbit covers
without requiring each complete cube. It does not prove their edge sums or
the residual. Taking absolute values only after the pairing is an additional,
usually stronger, majorant premise with its own analytic gates.

5. Freeze kernel versus ACTUAL state variation

The work is not a fixed function of q alone. Write its compiled polynomial
integral as Phi_j(q;Z;h), where Z=(A,c,E,D) is the entire old cutoff state,
D is the deterministic finite head, and h is the integration-height endpoint.
For q=r, Z=Z_(r-1), h=h_r=H_(r-1). For q=pr these are Z_(pr-1),h_(pr).
For r>=2 the exact difference is
 w_j(pr)-w_j(r)=K_j(r,p)+T_j(r,p)+B_j(r,p),              (5.1)
 K_j=Phi_j(pr;Z_(r-1);h_r)-Phi_j(r;Z_(r-1);h_r),
 T_j=Phi_j(pr;Z_(pr-1);h_r)-Phi_j(pr;Z_(r-1);h_r),
 B_j=Phi_j(pr;Z_(pr-1);h_(pr))-Phi_j(pr;Z_(pr-1);h_r).
Frozen states at a virtual q are algebraic inputs to the SAME full polynomial;
they are not falsely declared to be an actual source prefix. The r=1 edge is
treated by its exact event formula, not by an undefined H_0.

The paired interior work becomes
 -mu(r)[K_1+T_1+B_1]
 +mu(r)^2[2w_2(r)+K_2+T_2+B_2].                        (5.2)
This explicitly exposes three distinct tasks: finite kernel differences;
signed actual cutoff-state variations; and height variations. The even base
2w_2(r) survives. A smooth-kernel calculation pays neither that base nor the
cutoff-state term by naming them variations.

The complete state variation is itself a compilable arithmetic polynomial.
For r>=2 define
 D_shell(s)=sum_(r<=n<pr)mu(n)n^-s, dc=D_shell(1),
 Q_shell=dc delta-D_shell,
 A_(pr-1)=A_(r-1)+D_shell,
 c_(pr-1)=c_(r-1)+dc,
 E_(pr-1)=E_(r-1)+zeta(s)Q_shell,
 D_(pr-1)=D_(r-1)+sum_(r<=n<pr)n^-s.                   (5.3)
The notation D_shell(1) here means series evaluation, not the unit coefficient.
Q_shell(1)=0 removes the pole at 1 before any expansion is estimated. The shell
INCLUDES n=r and EXCLUDES n=pr. It is a multiplicative annulus of length
(p-1)r, not automatically a short additive interval. Its deterministic head
sum includes every integer, including repeated-prime numbers. Expand (5.3)
into T_j with all mixed shell/old-state terms; do not center its factors
separately and drop the opposite drifts.

6. A further UNCONDITIONAL payment: height variation of work

B_j is an indefinite work shell, not the positive energy shell of Review024.
Nevertheless it can be paid without the target, by exact virtual polarization.
For the full corrected transition at cutoff q let F_0,F_+,F_- be the three
series obtained by assigning the new coefficient mu(q)=0,+1,-1, with the OLD
state fixed and EVERY head and analytic-residue update retained. Pointwise,
 W_1=(|F_+|^2-|F_-|^2)/2,
 W_2=(|F_+|^2+|F_-|^2)/2-|F_0|^2.                     (6.1)
The common measure is dt/(2pi |s|^2). Therefore the absolute integral of each
W_j over any interval is bounded by a fixed positive sum of these branch
energies. The virtual coefficients still satisfy |a_n|<=1, have finite support
through q, and their c=A(1) has |c|<=2+1/q<=5/2. Thus the raw moment provider's
coefficient majorants change by fixed constants only, and its pole-removal
hypothesis is still satisfied. Their finite corrected heads have full norm
O(q^11/10), irrespective of the actual head identity at that virtual coefficient.

For fixed p, the constant-factor length extension from Review022 applied at
h_r=2(r-1)^(45/2) proves
 |B_1(r,p)|+|B_2(r,p)|<=C_p r^11/10.                    (6.2)
The raw tails are O_p(r^-1/16); the larger displayed cost pays the complete
finite heads. All small r are covered by uniform fixed constants; the r=1
term is a separate finite base. Summation gives O_p(V^21/10), below beta=12/5.
This is a genuine paid component of (5.2), not an arithmetic sign estimate.

There is a UNIFORM growing-prime version for 0<=lambda<1/8 and p<=r^lambda,
r>=2. Choose kappa' strictly between 5(1+lambda)/(45/2) and 1/4. The maximum
virtual polynomial length q^5<=r^[5(1+lambda)] then meets the inherited
short-polynomial provider at h_r, with a fixed-factor finite-head extension.
For branch index j, its raw tail has exponent
 (2+j)(1+lambda)(1-2sigma)+(45/2)(d_j+epsilon),
with sigma=9/20, epsilon=1/120 and the d_j of Review020. The maximum is
 -1/16+lambda/2<0, from j=3; j=0 and j=1 are retained. The complete virtual
head bound instead gives
 |B_1|+|B_2|<=C_lambda r^[11(1+lambda)/10].               (6.3)
For edge covers of bounded weighted multiplicity at each parent, its total
cost is O(V^[21/10+11lambda/10]), still strictly below 12/5. This applies to
the weighted edges in (4.1), or a certified comparable cover; it is not a
free estimate for every edge of a many-prime cube. All p outside the length
condition, unit edges, unmatched vertices and truncated faces stay in the
remaining exact obligation. lambda=0 allows no prime p<=1 but states the
continuous cost boundary; fixed p is separately covered by (6.2).

7. Exact kernel signatures and why smoothness is not an exponent gain

With a fixed old state and fixed height, expand the scalar work integrand in
 a=q^-1, b=q^-s, conjugate(b). A monomial a^j b^k conjugate(b)^l is
 q^-Omega exp(-i nu t log q),
 Omega=j+(k+l)sigma, nu=k-l.                             (7.1)
The compiler can derive these signatures exactly; coefficients retain their
zeta factors and old-state dependence. A prime difference multiplies this
pure kernel by
 1-p^-Omega exp(-i nu t log p),                          (7.2)
and a cube gives the product of those factors. The even-sum kernel instead
has plus signs. A fixed finite P changes a constant, not the q exponent.
Only Omega=nu=0 is identically annihilated by an odd difference. Any actual
annihilation must be checked in the complete polynomial, not in one term
whose old-state coefficient changes with q.

For a pure phase on a cone cos(nu t log p)>=delta>0,
 |1-p^-Omega exp(-i nu t log p)|^2
 <=1+p^-2Omega-2delta p^-Omega.
If p^-Omega<=delta this is <=1-delta p^-Omega, a genuine pure-kernel
contraction on that domain. Outside the cone it need not be <=1. For example
the fundamental difference 1-p^-s has squared modulus
 1+p^-2sigma-2p^-sigma cos(t log p),
which exceeds one on many heights. The complete low integral includes all
heights through H_V, not just a common zero-frequency rectangle.

A phase-restricted certificate must recover its complement, the even sector,
state drift and exits. A weighted difference I-c_p T_p that cancels a chosen
monomial changes the target: the exact residual (c_p-1)T_p must be restored.
There is no automatic arithmetic L2 credit from a pure-dilation multiplier
while the p-free projections and changing states are ignored.

8. An actual-source scalar primitive for eligible frozen sectors

There is a small but rigorous arithmetic rule beyond generic signs. Define
 c_P(V)=sum_(r<=V,gcd(r,Q)=1)mu(r)/r.
For one prime, the exact local inverse gives
 c_{p-free}(V)=sum_(k>=0,p^k<=V)p^-k c_floor(V/p^k).
Iterating gives the analogous P-smooth sum, hence
 |c_P(V)|<=B_P=2 product_(p in P)(1-1/p)^-1.             (8.1)
Repeated valuations in this inversion are all retained; this is not a
squarefree truncation of the smoothing inverse. For fixed P the constant
is uniform in V. For growing P its product is an explicit additional cost.

By Abel, for Omega real and frequency omega,
 |sum_(r<=V,gcd(r,Q)=1)mu(r)r^-Omega exp(-i omega log r)|
 <=B_P[V^(1-Omega)+|1-Omega-i omega| integral_1^V x^-Omega dx]. (8.2)
For omega=0 and Omega>=1 this is <=B_P; for Omega<1 it is <=2B_P V^(1-Omega).
For Omega=1 and nonzero omega the safe bound is B_P[1+|omega|log V].
The corresponding even sum is bounded absolutely by sum_(r<=V)r^-Omega:
bounded for Omega>1, logarithmic at 1, and a power below 1.

These rules pay eligible scalar, state-independent frozen sectors. They do
not apply unchanged to a coefficient depending on Z_(r-1). Its variation
must enter Abel explicitly, and omega=nu t can grow to H_V. A finite coefficient
polynomial or a phase cone cannot silently remove that growth or the zeta
weight. This is a useful typed compiler primitive, not a bound for all T_j.

9. Rejection gates, carefully scoped

(a) Generic even cancellation is false, even for actual mu. Take arbitrary
smooth weights w_1(q)=0,w_2(q)=q^2. For V>=1024 the number of squarefree integers
in (V/2,V] is at least V/16: nonsquarefree ones are at most
 (V/2)sum_(k>=2)k^-2+sqrt V <=3V/8+sqrt V,
and allow one extra integer endpoint. Here sum_(k>=2)k^-2<=3/4 follows from
1/4+integral_2^infinity x^-2 dx. Thus S(V)>=V^3/64. No complete prime cube or
smooth finite difference yields O(V^12/5) for this class. This refutes a generic
orbit theorem with no coupled-work premise; these are NOT the actual w_j.

(b) Finite-prime identities alone are insufficient arithmetic information.
For fixed P define h_P(n)=mu(n_P) if n is squarefree, and zero otherwise,
where n_P is its part supported in P. This normalized three-valued source
satisfies h_P(pr)=-h_P(r), h_P(pr)^2=h_P(r)^2 for every p in P, p not dividing r,
and every repeated valuation is zero. It also satisfies every complete divisor
zero row whose index is divisible by a prime in P. Yet an elementary squarefree
sieve gives
 sum_(n<=x)h_P(n)=c_P^* x+O_P(sqrt x),
 c_P^*=(1/zeta(2)) product_(p in P)(p-1)/(p+1)>0.        (9.1)
Proof: uniquely write n=dr, d|Q, gcd(r,Q)=1, and count coprime squarefree r by
sum_(k^2|r)mu(k), using inclusion-exclusion for gcd. Their density is
(1/zeta(2))product p/(p+1); summing mu(d)/d supplies product(1-1/p).
The floor and tail errors are O_P(sqrt x).
This extends the fixed-prime diagnostic in divisor_dual_proof.txt while
retaining squarefree support. It does NOT satisfy the actual bounded-harmonic-
sum hypothesis: its harmonic sum grows like c_P^* log x. Therefore (9.1) is
only an obstruction to rules using those finite-prime identities ALONE, not
a countermodel to their conjunction with every established source premise.
Review021/022 supply a different bounded-harmonic generic obstruction; one
must not pretend that two separate counterexamples prove their intersection.

(c) Old fresh-prime/semiprime grouping refutations cannot be copied here.
Unlike the coefficient f(p), w_j(p) depends on A_(p-1) and the whole height
integral. No proved fixed-sign fresh-prime asymptote for R(p) has been supplied.
A planner must derive such an asymptote from the COMPLETE work before using
a frontier positivity gate. The exit is nevertheless mandatory.

(d) A divisor-zero Gram injection is an exact equality only for COMPLETE
divisor rows. A shell [r,pr) picks partial rows; its missing divisors must be
restored. As divisor_dual_proof.txt shows, full nonunit Gram domination can
re-express the same signed bound on its null vector, rather than create it.
Finite selected rows, phase gains or a fitted PSD matrix are not global proof.

10. Concrete remaining arithmetic interface and recovery

For a fixed prime, or an admitted weighted growing-prime cover, (5.2) and
sections 2-4 turn the original leaf into an exact JOIN of:
 frozen kernel differences K_j;
 actual shell-state variations T_j from (5.3), including every old/shell cross;
 the even base 2w_2(r);
 all unmatched vertices, prime-free exits, unit cases, and incomplete faces.
The height terms B_j are uniformly paid under section 6's explicit gates.
It is sufficient to prove a ONE-SIDED O(V^12/5) bound for this remaining JOIN,
with all-prefix coverage and one constant independent of V. The known height,
zero-sign and full-head payments then compose through Review024 to the low
goal. Taking absolute values of every remaining term is optional and stronger.

This is executable beyond a bare relabeling: expand each T_j in the finite
shell sums D_shell,dc,Q_shell and the deterministic head shell; retain actual
mu(r) and every tuple mu(n_1)...mu(n_k), with r<=n_i<pr and all previous-prefix
indices explicit. Reduce repeated source factors by mu^3=mu, but keep their
gcd, repeated-prime and composite sectors. The resulting intermediate leaf is
a weighted multiplicative-annulus correlation, coupled to the even base and
exit, with fixed polynomial/range semantics. A detector can enumerate prime
covers, freeze choices, complete-divisor zero insertions, and polynomial
potentials, then compare their theorem costs or emit these concrete signed
correlations. No unproved orthogonality, independence or current-scale target
is used to pay the height sector.

One can also enumerate the unique largest-prime exposure
 S(V)=sum_(r squarefree)mu(r)^2
       sum_(p>Pplus(r),p<=V/r)[-mu(r)w_1(pr)+w_2(pr)],
Pplus(1)=1. This preserves every event once, but is not itself a gain. Pairing
such children with r requires an explicit capacity/compensation rule as in
(4.1); otherwise r is used repeatedly. Any proposed new source-specific
correlation estimate must account for that recovery cost.

No bound for the remaining joined multiplicative-annulus correlation has been
proved here. The scalar free-harmonic rule and work-height payment are genuine
unconditional components; the even base, state variation and frontier remain
the actual forcing. Generic prime identities, a pure kernel contraction, or
the failure of an unsigned template neither proves nor disproves that bound.
The full original signed core stays priority zero, and global/RH flags remain
false. This memo is final and stable; edits have stopped.
