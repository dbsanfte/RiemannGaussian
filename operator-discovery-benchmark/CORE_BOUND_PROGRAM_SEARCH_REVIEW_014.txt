1. Complete signals, polynomial syntax and exact expansion
---------------------------------------------------------
Let g be real, |g(n)|<=K d2(n), and G(n)=sum_(k<=n)g(k), including its
actual unit. In the current transported source K=37/2. All logarithms
without a subscript are natural; log2 X denotes the base2 logarithm,
whereas log 2 denotes the natural logarithm of2. Define
 L(n)=log(2n), H_j(n)=sum_(k<=n)g(k)log(k)^j,
 H_0(n)=G(n).
Thus log(1)^0 in H_0 means1, and the unit contribution to H_j, j>0,
is0. There is no division by log1. Fix a rational polynomial
 p(t)=sum_(j=0)^d c_j t^j,
 U_p(n)=sum_j c_j H_j(n)/L(n)^j.
All polynomial and channel parameters are fixed globally, independently
of an observed scale. Trim zero leading coefficients and use exact
rational coefficient comparisons; no approximate nullspace is a proof.

The exact change of basis is
 p(1-z)=sum_q b_q z^q,
 b_q=(-1)^q sum_(j>=q)c_j binom(j,q).
Consequently
 U_p=sum_q b_q U_q,
 U_q=L^(-q) W_q,
 W_q(n)=sum_(k<=n)g(k)log(2n/k)^q.                       (1.1)
Here q is a nonnegative INTEGER, even when channel weights are rational.
In particular U_q(1)=g(1) for every q, since W_q(1)=g(1)(log 2)^q.
Hence U_p(1)=p(0)g(1), as the original polynomial definition requires.
The exact identity retains all prime/composite signs and valuations; it
does not replace g by a nonnegative coefficient model.

2. A self-contained weighted prefix bound
-----------------------------------------
The elementary complete divisor summatory bound is
 sum_(k<=x)d2(k)<=x(1+log x), x>=1.
Write this summatory function as D(x). For q>=1, integration by parts
against the nonnegative decreasing kernel gives
 sum_(k<=x)d2(k)log(2x/k)^q
 =D(x)(log 2)^q+q int_1^x D(t)log(2x/t)^(q-1)dt/t.
The unit mass is included in D; its left limit before1 is0. Thus the
right side is at most x(1+log x) times
 (log 2)^q+q int_0^infinity e^(-v)(log 2+v)^(q-1)dv
 =q! sum_(i=0)^q (log 2)^i/i!
 <=C_q, C_q=q! sum_(i=0)^q 1/i!.
For q0 use the original summatory bound. Therefore
 |W_q(x)|<=K x(1+log x) C_q, x>=1.                      (2.1)
This is a finite-prefix inequality, not an analytically continued tail
claim. C_0=1 and C_q=q C_(q-1)+1 give the exact integers1,2,5,16,... .

3. Increment certificates, including changing rational log weights
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Fix eta>=0 rational and set
 V_(q,eta)(n)=L(n)^(-eta)U_q(n)=L(n)^(-t)W_q(n),
 t=q+eta, B_(q,eta)=q C_(q-1)+(q+eta)C_q,
 C_(-1)=0. The first term in B is0 when q0.

On each real interval[n-1,n), the coefficient set in W is fixed. Its
ordinary derivative, followed by the exact new coefficient at n, gives
 Delta V_(q,eta)(n)
 =g(n)(log 2)^q/L(n)^t
  +int_(n-1)^n [q W_(q-1)(x)-t W_q(x)/L(x)]
                                      /(x L(x)^t) dx.   (3.1)
For q0 the W_(-1) term is absent. This formula includes the change of
the denominator and the entire accumulated bank. It is not the false
rule Delta V=L^(-t)Delta W with the old denominator frozen.

Take a dyadic X>=2, h=1+log2 X, and only internal increments
 n in[X+1,2X-1]. On the integration domains x>=X,
 L(x)>=(log 2)*h>h/2, L(x)>1,
 1+log x<=1+log(2X)<=2h, (log 2)^q<=1.
Using(2.1), the new coefficient and integral in(3.1) are bounded by
 K L(X)^(-t)[d2(n)+B_(q,eta)(1+log(2X))].
Also d2(n)^2<=d4(n), and
 sum_(n<=2X)d4(n)<=2X(1+log(2X))^3.
Squaring with (a+b)^2<=2a^2+2b^2 and counting at most X increments
therefore proves
 sum_internal(Delta V_(q,eta))^2
 <=K^2 2^(2t)[32+8 B_(q,eta)^2] X h^(3-2t)
 <=K^2 D_(q,eta) X h^(3-2q-2eta),                       (3.2)
 D_(q,eta)=2^ceil(2(q+eta))*[32+8 B_(q,eta)^2].
The last constant is rational and involves no unverified real power.
For eta0 this is exactly the proposed D_q; D_0=32 is safe. The
coefficients32 and8 are conservative: no improvement is needed for
soundness. This proof works for every fixed rational eta>=0, not only
sixth fractions or the common-alignment choice eta=R-q>=0.

For a polynomial channel V_p=L^(-eta)U_p, let T={q:b_q!=0}.
If T is empty, V_p=0 and its increment constant is0. Otherwise put
 Babs=sum_(q in T)|b_q|,
 D_p=Babs sum_(q in T)|b_q|D_(q,eta),
 d_p=3-2 min(T)-2eta.
Weighted Cauchy gives
 sum_internal(Delta V_p)^2<=K^2 D_p X h^d_p.            (3.3)
All constants are rational. A coefficient-count Cauchy alternative is
also valid, but the weighted form does not introduce an unnecessary
factor for a singleton channel. Exact cancellations must be performed
BEFORE extracting min(T); min(T) is the order of vanishing of p at1.
Finite degrees and rational bit/resource limits remain implementation
limits, not mathematical permission to expand an unbounded polynomial.

4. Orthogonal detail payment with full channels
----------------------------------------------
Let the detector generate a finite list of channels
 V_i=L^(-eta_i)U_(p_i), eta_i>=0,
 A(X)=X^(-2)sum_(n=X)^(2X-1) sum_i V_i(n)^2.
On the complete annulus use cells of length Lcell=2^floor(r log2X),
0<=r<=1, and apply the mean projection to each ACTUAL V_i. Orthogonality
gives A=S+Vdetail exactly, S and Vdetail both nonnegative. The unchanged
scalar inequality
 sum_cell(y-mean y)^2<=Lcell^2/8 sum_internal(Delta y)^2
therefore proves
 Vdetail(X)<=K^2/8 X^(2r-1) sum_i D_i h^d_i
 <=B_D X^(2r-1)h^d,
 B_D=K^2 sum_i D_i/8, d=max_(D_i>0)d_i<=3.             (4.1)
Zero channels may be dropped. X1 has a single unit cell and zero detail,
so the special finite head needs no extension of the X>=2 increment
proof. Means are not coefficient sums restarted at cell boundaries.

For r<1/2, delta=1-2r in(0,1] and
 (1+j)^3 2^(-delta*j)<=96/delta^3, j>=0,
give the rational uniform payment 96 B_D/delta^3. For r1/2 the payment
is B_D h^d, or B_D h^max(0,d) when reporting nonnegative log degrees.
For r>1/2 this certificate is power-unpaid, regardless of a finite
negative log exponent; it does not refute the channel itself.

For rho>=0, the exact signed increment decomposition implies
 A(2X)-rho A(X)
 =S(2X)-rho S(X)+Vdetail(2X)-rho Vdetail(X)
 <=S(2X)-rho S(X)+Vdetail(2X).                          (4.2)
No parent-detail absolute payment is necessary. At r1/2 the child
payment is at most B_D 2^ceil(max(0,d)) h^max(0,d).
At r<1/2 its uniform payment is unchanged. These are paid addenda,
not a proof of a proposed small constant for the coarse increment.

5. Exact nullspace recovery and its finite uniform constants
-----------------------------------------------------------
The recovery test is the polynomial coefficient identity
                  1=sum_i a_i p_i(t), a_i rational.    (5.1)
It is equivalent to a finite exact rational linear system. Failure to
solve it means this stated recovery rule is unavailable; a numerical
near-identity is not admitted. If it holds, every complete prefix obeys
 G(n)=sum_i a_i U_(p_i)(n)=sum_i a_i L(n)^eta_i V_i(n).
Thus Cauchy yields
 E_G(X)<=sum_i a_i^2 L(2X)^(2eta_i) A(X)
 <=Crec h^(2e) A(X),
 e=max_(a_i!=0)eta_i,
 Crec=2^ceil(2e) sum_i a_i^2.                           (5.2)
The rational upper constant uses L(n)<=2h on[X,2X), and2h>=1, so it
is also safe at X1 even though L(1)=log 2<1. Unused recovery coefficients
need not inflate e. A positive storage with all a_i=0 cannot satisfy
(5.1). Full signs/crosses in each channel are retained before squares.

With the paid transport theta<=5/6, the known source rule is
 E_F(X)<=2 E_G(X)+50X^(2theta-5/3).
Combining this with(5.2) is valid. One must NOT instead write
 PG=sum_i a_i L^eta_i PV_i: multiplication by a varying weight does
not commute with taking a cell mean. Ordinary energy recovery uses
the WHOLE A=S+Vdetail and avoids that false step.

6. Generated scale recurrences and explicit goal feedback
-------------------------------------------------------
Let B>=S(1), C>=0, k>=0 rational, and 0<=rho<=1. A generated uniform
premise must say for every dyadic X>=1, using the same global program,
             S(2X)<=rho S(X)+C(1+log2X)^k.             (6.1)
For rho<1 it gives S(X)<=K_S h^pS with
 K_S=B+C/(1-rho), pS=k.
For rho1 it gives K_S=B+C, pS=k+1.
If C0 the sharper pS0 works for either rho. These formulas use base2
exactly: iterating on j sums (1+j)^k, never silently substitutes a
natural-log bound with different constants. A finite initial cutoff
can be handled by a declared finite head; it cannot be inferred from
a fitted training constant. A recurrence on finitely many scales is
finite evidence only, not(6.1).

For r<1/2 set dpaid=0 and K_D=96 B_D/(1-2r)^3.
For r1/2 set dpaid=max(0,d) and K_D=B_D.
Then a complete proved composition is
 pF=max(pS,dpaid)+2e,
 E_F(X)<=[2 Crec(K_S+K_D)+50] h^pF.                    (6.2)
The finite unit/detail head is included as above. This is the target
log degree actually paid by these rules, not merely the degree of S.
For a desired degree pgoal, the detector must require
 pS<=pgoal-2e, dpaid<=pgoal-2e,
and compile the corresponding allowed recurrence k and rho. A
verified pole of order m>=2 of the original full source requires
pF>=2m-2; the current double-pole witness gives pF>=2. Pole rejection
is conditional on the independently source-bound verified witness and
paid transport; it is not manufactured from an observed slope.

There is a useful structural limit at r1/2. Evaluating(5.1) at t1
forces at least one active a_i with p_i(1)!=0. For this channel min(T_i)=0
and its detail exponent is3-2eta_i. Since e>=eta_i,

Consequently the present absolute derivative/payment rules cannot

7. A separate weighted-mean Mellin rule; no illegal commuting
-----------------------------------------------------------
For possible stronger proof feedback define a DIFFERENT lifted signal
 Y(n)=sum_i a_i L(n)^eta_i (PV_i)(n),
 H(n)=sum_i a_i L(n)^eta_i [V_i(n)-(PV_i)(n)].
Then G=Y+H EXACTLY, while Y is generally not PG. Direct Cauchy proves
 E_Y(X)<=Crec h^(2e) S(X).                              (7.1)
This costs the same log weight; it does not assert a scalar mean
recovery identity with weights evaluated at a cell endpoint.

Let h_i=V_i-PV_i. Its exact discrete cell sum is0. The increment/detail
rules give an annular L1 bound
 sum_(n=X)^(2X-1)|h_i(n)|
 <=K sqrt(D_i/8) X^(r+1)h^(d_i/2).
For the unit-cell step extension the weighted Mellin integral is
 sum_(n in cell) h_i(n) phi_(s,eta_i)(n),
 phi_(s,eta)(t)=log(2t)^eta int_0^1(t+v)^(-s-1)dv.
This smooth function of real t>=2 has derivative bounded, uniformly
on compact s sets, by O_(s,eta)(X^(-Re(s)-2) h^eta) on an annulus.
Subtract phi at the cell start using sum h_i=0. The complete annular
weighted residual is consequently
 O_(s,program)(X^(2r-Re(s)-1) h^(eta_i+d_i/2)).
Dyadic summation converges normally on compact subsets of
                         Re(s)>2r-1.                  (7.2)
The finite X1 contribution is entire. This proof treats the exact
integer log weight through the averaged Mellin kernel phi; replacing
log(2n) by log(2x) without accounting for unit cells is unnecessary.
No infinite coefficient series is invoked outside its initial domain.

Hence for r<3/4 this residual is analytic near every critical zero.
The transported error has radial Mellin bound O(1/epsilon) even at
theta5/6, and is analytic beyond critical when theta<5/6. An original
pole of order m>=2 cannot be hidden in either residual. If a cofinal
coarse hypothesis is S=O(log^p),(7.1) gives radial Mellin growth of Y
at most O(epsilon^(-1-(p+2e)/2)). Therefore a stronger COARSE gate is
                         p+2e>=2m-2.                  (7.3)
For the verified double pole this means p+2e>=2, even when the loose
energy payment in(6.2) would otherwise allow detail degree3 to obscure
an impossible bounded coarse hypothesis. This is a proved separate
rule, not ordinary A=S+V energy recovery with detail discarded.

The same exact lifted residual also supports the separately flagged

8. Exact arithmetic mean kernels and why universal closure fails
---------------------------------------------------------------
For each actual channel and complete cell I define
 T_i(I,k)=Lcell^(-1) sum_(n in I,n>=k)
                    L(n)^(-eta_i)p_i(log k/L(n)).
Then PV_i on I is sum_k g(k)T_i(I,k), including every k before I.
The full storage is the joined signed quadratic
 S(X)=sum_(h,k)g(h)g(k) J_X(h,k),
 J_X(h,k)=Lcell/X^2 sum_I sum_i T_i(I,h)T_i(I,k).        (8.1)
Thus the exact open increment is the same quadratic with kernel
J_(2X)-rho J_X. J_X is positive semidefinite, but this difference need
not be positive, negative or contracting. Positivity of storage does
not supply the sign of the arithmetic increment. Applying absolute
values replaces it with a stronger majorant obligation and must be
declared as such, with all boundary/full-prefix complements intact.

For the source of013 one can substitute the COMPLETE convolution
 f=n^(-1/3)*chi-beta mu,
 chi=beta(mu*mu)-beta[mu*(mu n^(-2/3))]-R_u mu n^(-2/3),
and g=f-(f-g), retaining the actual transported correction. This
produces finite reciprocal/divisor pair kernels for detector generation,
not a tail estimate for chi at2/3. The analytically canceled bracket at
that point is not ordinary convergence of its coefficient sum there.
The polynomial channel grammar may expose different signed pair weights,
but no coefficient envelope proves their required global contraction.

A precise countermodel to closure from ONLY the local rules is the
fixed negative unit together with g(n)=1 on even composites n>=4,
and0 at every prime and every other n. It obeys the divisor envelope
and has no large prime coefficients, but G(n)=n/2+O(1). For any fixed
polynomial p with p(1)!=0, elementary summation of logarithmic moments
gives U_p(n)=p(1)n/2+O_p(n/log n). Therefore at least one active recovery
channel, forced by(5.1), has V_i(n) of size n/log(n)^eta_i, with a
nonzero fixed sign eventually. Its coarse mean storage is of positive
power size X/log(X)^(2eta_i), not any fixed polylog. This countermodel
does not satisfy the actual four-mode Euler AST. It disproves only the
claim that local derivative, projection, prime deletion and recovery
rules alone establish the missing source-specific recurrence.
