---------------------

candidate10_occupancy_joint_forcing_obligation.json and

finite product-band stencil language. The source remains actual candidate10

were used. No companion RH bound or prime independence is assumed.

There are two constructive facts, with different scopes.
  * The exact fixed-prime centered recurrence has a uniformly contracting
    delayed part for q>=3. Its floor and unit pole forcing is paid, even
    uniformly in q. The unpaid input includes a CURRENT-scale q-free
    source difference; removing that term or assuming its bound would
    make the argument circular. A single-prime q-free norm is recoverably

  * The new centered product-band family has a rigorous sufficient full

    partial-band Abel summation removes the relative product weight at

The uncentered squarefree-product source also has a provably nonzero
limiting bivariate pole at (s,t)=(2/3,2/3). Its residue component is

This warns against an uncentered squarefree moment premise. It does NOT

for the full scalar prefixes and F_U^0,F_V^0,F_B^0 for the q-free

COEFFICIENT index, not that its prefix argument is restricted.

  V_q(z)=(1-z)/(1-t^2z): 1, t^2-1, t^4-t^2, ...,

Exactly A=P-D_same, with D_same the full d prefix; A^0=P^0-D_same^0.
The local same-prime mass is beta, so

2. Exact pole deletion and paid floor forcing
---------------------------------------------
Set kappa_u=2/3, kappa_v=1/3. The full U,V pole-prefix coefficients are
  c_u=1/[kappa_u*zeta(kappa_u)],
  c_v=1/[kappa_v*zeta(kappa_v)].
The B coefficients are
  c_Bu=zeta(4/3)/[kappa_u*zeta(kappa_u)^2],
  c_Bv=zeta(2/3)/[kappa_v*zeta(kappa_v)^2].
Thus the complete p pole profiles use
  m_u=beta*(c_Bu-c_u), m_v=beta*(c_Bv-c_v).
The coefficient formulas are exact analytic constants, not fitted data.

Removing q deletes its exact Euler factor. At a pole exponent kappa,
put z_k=q^(-kappa). For U or V with its appropriate t_i=t or t^2,
  c_i^0=c_i*(1-t_i*z_k)/(1-z_k)
       =c_i*(1-q^(-1))/(1-q^(-kappa)).               (2.1)
For B at either pole,
  c_Bk^0=c_Bk*(1-t*z_k)*(1-t^2*z_k)/(1-z_k)^2.       (2.2)
The q-free p coefficients are beta*(c_Bu^0-c_u^0) and
beta*(c_Bv^0-c_v^0), respectively. It is WRONG to divide the whole
nonmultiplicative p center by one common B Euler factor.

All centers below are defined for integer n>=1, zero at n=0. Dilated
centers use the actual floor n/q^j. Put
  R_i=F_i-C_i, R_i^0=F_i^0-C_i^0,
where U,V each have their single pole and B has both poles. Exact
prefix decomposition gives
  (I-tD)R_U=(I-D)R_U^0+Gamma_U,
  (I-t^2D)R_V=(I-D)R_V^0+Gamma_V,
  (I-s_qD+v_qD^2)R_B=(I-D)^2 R_B^0+Gamma_B.         (2.3)

For a single U or V pole, m=floor(n/q),
  Gamma_i=(c_i^0-c_i)*n^kappa+(t_i*c_i-c_i^0)*m^kappa
          =(c_i^0-c_i)*[n^kappa-q^kappa*m^kappa].   (2.4)
The continuous leading power cancels exactly because of (2.1).
Since 0<kappa<1, concavity gives
  0<=n^kappa-(q*m)^kappa<=q^kappa,
and hence the UNIFORM bound
  |Gamma_i|<=G_i:=|c_i|/(1-2^(-kappa)).              (2.5)
This includes n<q and the n=1 unit; no unit strip is omitted.

For one B pole let c=c_Bk,c0=c_Bk^0, m_j=floor(n/q^j), and
  a1=-2c0+s_q*c, a2=c0-v_q*c.
Its exact forcing is
  Gamma_B,k=(c0-c)*n^kappa+a1*m_1^kappa+a2*m_2^kappa.
Equation (2.2) implies
  (c0-c)+a1*q^(-kappa)+a2*q^(-2kappa)=0.
Let epsilon_j=n^kappa-q^(j*kappa)*m_j^kappa. Then
  Gamma_B,k=-a1*q^(-kappa)*epsilon_1
                         -a2*q^(-2kappa)*epsilon_2.           (2.6)
Using |epsilon_j|<=q^(j*kappa), s_q<=2,v_q<=1, and
|c0|<=|c|/(1-2^(-kappa))^2 gives the uniform constant
  |Gamma_B|<=G_B:=sum_{k=u,v}3|c_Bk|
                                *[(1-2^(-kappa_k))^(-2)+1].   (2.7)
These forcing terms have dyadic cost <=G_i^2/X or G_B^2/X, and finite
critical integral cost. Their payment is real progress, but it only
removes the explicit pole/floor error, not the q-free arithmetic frontier.

3. A constructive delayed contraction, with its exact unpaid input
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Use the finite-horizon norm
  ||F||_(sigma,T)^2=int_1^T |F(x)|^2*x^(-2sigma-1) dx,
with the actual step extension, sigma>=1/2. D has norm <=q^(-sigma).
The U,V delayed feedbacks in (2.3) have norms at most q^(-u-sigma)
and q^(-v-sigma). The B delayed part has norm at most
  rho(q,sigma)=q^(-u-sigma)+q^(-v-sigma)+q^(-1-2sigma).
For EVERY q>=3 and sigma>=1/2,
  rho(q,sigma)<=3^(-5/6)+3^(-7/6)+1/9<17/18<1.       (3.1)
Indeed 3^(5/6)>2 and 3^(7/6)>3. Thus there is a genuine uniform
delayed contraction for a fixed q>=3, with smaller arguments n/q,n/q^2.
At q=2 this particular triangle bound need not contract, although the
factorized causal inverse in (2.3) still exists and is bounded.

The indispensable B forcing is
  G_q,B(n)=R_B^0(n)-2R_B^0(floor(n/q))
                                  +R_B^0(floor(n/q^2)).       (3.2)
It includes CURRENT-scale R_B^0(n). A recurrence which calls all of
(3.2) smaller scale is false. Its value at n can contain the entire
fresh q-free annulus; a prime-free coefficient restriction is not an
earlier prefix argument. The analogous U,V first differences have
the same issue. Paying Gamma does not pay these source differences.

For fixed q the difference I-D is uniformly invertible at sigma>=1/2:
  ||(I-D)^(-1)||<=1/(1-q^(-sigma)).
The rational transfer and its inverse are also bounded uniformly over
q>=2, because q^(-sigma)<=2^(-sigma)<1. Consequently, after the paid
floor forcing, cofinal bounds for G_q,B and for the q-free B state are
recoverably equivalent. Likewise a single-prime q-free/full U,V,B norm
is comparable through their exact centered Euler operators. Selecting
one q does not automatically create a weaker arithmetic obligation.
The new lemma would have to estimate a genuinely restricted or coupled
fresh forcing, not simply rename (3.2).

The Mellin characteristic factor is
  1-s_q*q^(-s)+v_q*q^(-2s)
       =(1-q^(-u-s))*(1-q^(-v-s)).                 (3.3)
Its roots have real parts -u,-v, with imaginary periods 2pi/log q.
Thus local stability is not an off-line-zero or arithmetic source theorem.
The numerators 1-q^(-s) vanish on real part zero, not at the real poles
2/3 or 1/3. These nonzero local factors preserve the pole channels and
require the center deletion formulas (2.1)-(2.2).
At the opposite Mellin phase, the complete U transfer has modulus
(1+r)/(1+t*r)>1, r=q^(-sigma), and B has modulus
(1+r)^2/[(1+t*r)(1+t^2*r)]>1. Therefore the full arithmetic transfer
is not a contraction on every frequency merely because its delayed
feedback contracts. Fractional weights shift r to a smaller radius,
but unweighting the original source carries its recorded inverse-power
cost; phase cones need an actual arithmetic projection/closure argument.

5. Uncentered squarefree-product pole: a rigorous scoped warning
---------------------------------------------------------------
Let Q_sf(n)=sum_{h,k<=n,h*k squarefree}a(h)a(k). On this domain a=p.
Put channel labels i in (delta,B,U,V), coefficients c=(1,1,-1,-1),
and prime rows lambda_delta=0, lambda_B=-2+q^(-u)+q^(-v),
lambda_U=-1+q^(-u), lambda_V=-1+q^(-v).
For Re(s),Re(t)>1 the full bivariate coefficient series is exactly
  Z_sf(s,t)=beta^2*sum_{i,j}c_i*c_j
       *prod_q[1+lambda_i(q)q^(-s)+lambda_j(q)q^(-t)].          (5.1)
Each q is absent or belongs to just one leg, as squarefree PRODUCT
requires. Unit/prime-source cancellations are in the complete c sum.

For each pair factor the product as
  Z_ij(s,t)=D_i(s)D_j(t)*C_ij(s,t),
where D_delta=1, D_U=zeta(s+u)/zeta(s),
D_V=zeta(s+v)/zeta(s), D_B=D_U*D_V. The correction Euler factor is
  [1+lambda_i(q)q^(-s)+lambda_j(q)q^(-t)]
                             /[F_i,q(q^(-s))*F_j,q(q^(-t))].  (5.2)
Its error from one is O(q^(-2Re(s))+q^(-2Re(t))+q^(-Re(s)-Re(t))).
Thus C_ij converges absolutely in a neighborhood of (kappa,kappa),
kappa=2/3, and provides a legitimate meromorphic continuation there.

At s=t=kappa all C_ij are strictly positive and nonzero. Indeed the
smallest numerator is the B,B one. With x=q^(-1/3), it is
  1-4x^2+2x^3+2x^4
       =(1-2x^2)^2+2x^3*(1-x)>0.                  (5.3)
All other rows are >=lambda_B, and all denominator local factors
F_i,q(q^(-kappa)) are positive. Absolute tail convergence makes their
positive infinite product nonzero.
Only B and U have a pole at kappa. Their residues are
  r_B=zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2>0,
  r_U=1/zeta(2/3)<0.
The latter sign follows from the alternating eta representation on
0<s<1. Hence the joint Laurent coefficient is strictly positive:
  R_sf=beta^2*[r_B^2*C_BB-2r_B*r_U*C_BU+r_U^2*C_UU]>0.         (5.4)
The uncentered squarefree-product projection has NOT canceled this
simultaneous (2/3,2/3) pole merely by removing repeated-prime fibers.

A valid infinite-series Perron residue decomposition crossing these
poles contains R_sf/kappa^2 times the endpoint to power 2kappa.
Its normalized dyadic component has power 2kappa-1=1/3. For
0<=gamma<kappa, weighting the product by m^(-gamma) shifts BOTH poles
to kappa-gamma; the corresponding power is 1/3-2gamma, critical at
gamma=1/6. At gamma=kappa the shifted poles meet the Perron origin;
the simple residue formula needs separate head/logarithmic treatment
and is not used here. This component is a reason to retain all
normalizers and complements in attempted recovery.

There are two necessary qualifications. First Q_sf is indefinite, not
positive energy. A nonzero local Laurent coefficient does not alone
prove an unconditional unsmoothed asymptotic or a lower bound for its
TOTAL diagonal moment: other contour/remainder contributions must be
controlled to make that inference. No RH/zero-free companion estimate
is assumed here. Second every finite cutoff Euler product is a finite
Dirichlet polynomial and has NO literal pole. The poles in (5.4) belong
to the limiting meromorphic source. They cannot be inserted into a
finite low-rectangle integral as though finite-product continuation
already supplied a uniform asymptotic theorem.

Accordingly the saved O(1/X) low-contour component does not justify a
cofinal unweighted whole squarefree projection. Its high complement
still has to account for the limiting pole mechanism and all other
frequencies. With gamma=1/6 the pole component is not itself a
supercritical falsifier, but it can be order one and the unsolved high
frequency/restoration obligations remain. For the NEW centered b source
below, (5.1) cannot be reused unchanged: subtracting increments of the
center destroys this simple multiplicative color source. Its full
AA, main-cross, main-square and nonsquarefree terms must be restored.

6. Exact centered product bands, head and tail
----------------------------------------------
Now define the actual centered coefficient sequence
  b(n)=a(n)-[M(n)-M(n-1)], M(0)=0,
with ALL desired complete fixed center profiles in M. Its inclusive
prefix is exactly A(n)-M(n). In particular b(1) need NOT be zero even
though a(1)=0. Reusing the old a unit mask for b would lose the center.
With N=2X-1 the WHOLE centered moment is exactly
  E_R(X)=X^(-2)*sum_{h,k<=N}b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k).         (6.1)
This automatically includes AA, both center crosses and the center
square, on BOTH squarefree and nonsquarefree product sectors. A separate
SF/NS diagnostic is not a license to require each indefinite component
to be positive or cofinal on its own.

Fix gamma>=0 and J=ceil(log2(N^2)). For j=0,...,J and 1/2<=t<=1,
define exactly
  D_j(t)=2^(j*gamma)/X^2
       *sum_{h,k<=N,2^(j-1)<hk<=t*2^j}
                    b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k)*(hk)^(-gamma).           (6.2)
Set negative-index D_j identically zero. The j=0 band contains the
unit product m=1 at t=1; for t<1 it is zero. X=1,N=1,J=0 is covered.
The final band retains every product through N^2 and is a plateau
for t>=N^2/2^J. Empty upper portions are not new source terms and the
truncated final band must not be omitted. Every product 1<=hk<=N^2
belongs to exactly one band, with its lower endpoint strict.

For each j let S_j be the corresponding UNWEIGHTED band total. Treat
D_j as the cumulative signed atomic measure on relative rho=m/2^j
in (1/2,1]. Signed Abel summation is exactly
  S_j=D_j(1)-gamma*int_(1/2)^1 t^(gamma-1)D_j(t) dt.           (6.3)
The lower boundary term is zero. Consequently
  |S_j|<=(2-2^(-gamma))*sup_t|D_j(t)|<=2sup_t|D_j(t)|.         (6.4)
For gamma=0 the cost is exactly one and the endpoint alone suffices.
For gamma>0 a uniform partial-band bound is essential; an endpoint-only
weighted signed sum can cancel while its unweighted sum is large.
This is a finite signed-measure issue, not an assumed counterfeit mu
source. The j=0 atom also has exact cost one for every gamma.

The relative weight is bounded on each band and is undone BEFORE
summing the bands. There is no global N^(2gamma) recovery loss.
All formulas apply at the SAME t for all j in the filtered sequence.
Allowing a different chosen t at each shifted band would destroy the
causal filter identity needed next.

7. Quadratic band filters and sufficient full-energy recovery
-------------------------------------------------------------
Take C(z)=1+c1*z+c2*z^2 and
  E_j(t)=D_j(t)+c1*D_(j-1)(t)+c2*D_(j-2)(t).
The real coefficients may be the fixed proposed integers, but the
following proof is generic. The RECURRENCE characteristic polynomial
is lambda^2+c1*lambda+c2. Its roots lie in the CLOSED unit disc iff
  |c2|<=1 and |c1|<=1+c2.                            (7.1)
These are not the zeros of C(z), which reciprocate the nonzero roots.
For real roots, necessity follows from their product and the nonnegative
values at lambda=+1,-1; sufficiency follows because any root outside
[-1,1] with those two values would force both roots past the same
endpoint and product>1. For complex conjugate roots their common
modulus is sqrt(c2), giving the remaining case. Zero roots, constant
C, first-order degeneracies and repeated unit-circle roots are included.

Let g_l be the causal inverse coefficients of 1/C(z). For roots
lambda1,lambda2 satisfying (7.1),
  g_l=sum_{r=0}^l lambda1^r*lambda2^(l-r),
  |g_l|<=l+1.                                       (7.2)
The formula covers a repeated root by the same finite sum, with
g_0=1 and the usual zeroth-power convention, including zero roots. Then
  D_j(t)=sum_{l=0}^j g_l E_(j-l)(t),
  sup_t|D_j(t)|<=binom(j+2,2)*U_X,
  U_X=max_{0<=j<=J}sup_{1/2<=t<=1}|E_j(t)|.          (7.3)
All states at negative indices are exactly zero; no missing head
condition or unknown prehistory is assumed.

Summing (6.4), using sum_j binom(j+2,2)=binom(J+3,3), gives the FULL
centered energy recovery
  0<=E_R(X)<= (2-2^(-gamma))*binom(J+3,3)*U_X.        (7.4)
With the exact unit-head factor the slightly sharper bound is
  [1+(2-2^(-gamma))*(binom(J+3,3)-1)]*U_X.           (7.5)
Both hold with the final truncated plateau, and (7.5) has cost one
at X=1. The dimensionless recovery cost is O((1+log X)^3), with
no hidden X power or independent center/source premise.

Thus a uniform cofinal bound on U_X for every positive exponent is a
SUFFICIENT new full-target lemma. Absorb the displayed polynomial in
log X by using a smaller exponent in that auxiliary bound. The stencil
search can certify recovery without fitting arithmetic drift constants.
It cannot certify the needed signed band bound merely by root stability.

Every admitted finite C is itself equivalent, up to polynomial-in-J
cost, to the SAME complete uniform band/partial auxiliary:
  max_{j,t}|E_j(t)|<=(1+|c1|+|c2|)*max_{j,t}|D_j(t)|,
and the reverse follows from (7.3). Accordingly stable stencils change
the exact operator/argument presentation, not the cofinal equivalence
class of that auxiliary. The full centered moment has only the proved
ONE-WAY implication (7.4) from these uniform signed partial-band data.
No reverse implication from the whole energy, nor a cofinal bound for
the actual auxiliary, has been established here. Large signed band
cancellations are not bounded just because their complete sum is small.

Final audit and proof boundary
------------------------------
The fixed-prime recurrence is constructive with a true delayed
contraction and fully paid floor/unit/d forcing. Its remaining current
q-free frontier is the missing arithmetic information; the local
characteristic roots, fractional symbols and phase cone do not provide it.
The squarefree-source pole analysis identifies a retained limiting main
mechanism, with explicitly stated finite-product/indefinite-projection
limitations. The complete centered band language has a separate sound
polynomial recovery theorem, retaining head, tail, all valuations and
all center/SF/NS terms. It creates a precise stronger sufficient signed
auxiliary goal, not an already proved moment or a cancellation estimate.

No complete bound, closed all-source forcing recurrence or RH result
is claimed. All earlier/frozen files remain unchanged.
