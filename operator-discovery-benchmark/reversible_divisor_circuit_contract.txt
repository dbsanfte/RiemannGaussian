Reversible divisor circuit discovery v1 -- NEW study, 2026-10-09

Purpose
Search for complete transformed Mobius-prefix moments, with a uniform inverse
proved for the operator grammar. This does not assert those moments are bounded.
It replaces neither the frozen potential studies nor their missing evaluations.
Growing normalized prefix averages were rejected as the main next search family
after an analytical tail-norm obstruction, before any new arithmetic experiment.

Program schema: {"stages": [stage, ...]}, at most five stages, at most three
convolutions and two signs. Each stage is exactly one of:
  {"op":"convolve", "alpha":canonical rational, "prime_phase":phase}
  {"op":"sign", "phase":phase}
An empty program is the baseline. Alpha is nonzero, |alpha|<=2, denominator<=6.
All generated nonempty programs finish with a convolution: a terminal sign has
no effect on their moments. Validation may accept terminal signs for inversion.

A phase takes a positive integer n, and returns exactly +1 or -1:
  ["constant", +1 or -1]
  ["parity", index]                         (-1)^index
  ["source_sign", index]                    -1 iff a(index)<0; otherwise +1
  ["residue_sign", index, m, residues]       -1 iff index mod m in residues
  ["multiply", phase, phase]
Index forms:
  ["n"]
  ["root", numerator, denominator]          floor(n^(numerator/denominator))
  ["subtract_root", numerator, denominator]  n minus that root
  ["quotient", integer] or ["remainder", integer]
Index root exponent is reduced, positive and <=1, denominator<=6. Integer
quotient/remainder modulus is 2..16. Residue modulus is 2..16; residues are a
strictly increasing list in 0..m-1, possibly empty. Phase syntax has at most31
nodes and depth6. Every queried source index is in0..n; a(0)=0. No intermediate
state, fitted response, or block X can change a phase. The SAME fixed source
coefficient sequence supplies phases in every stage and in its inverse.

For chi(p)=prime_phase(p), the multiplicative convolution kernel is specified
by a(1)=1 and a(p^k)=chi(p)^k * alpha(alpha+1)...(alpha+k-1)/k!.
At integer n: (T_alpha,chi f)(n)=sum_{d|n} a(d)f(n/d), ALL positive divisors.
A sign stage multiplies by phase(n). Input f(n)=sum_{j<=n} source(j), f(0)=0.
The evaluation is exact rational. Each finite convolution is invertible by
the same prime phase with -alpha; sign stages are self-inverse. Circuit inverse
reverses stages. Neither n normalization nor an omitted complement is allowed.

Uniform recovery certificate schema (exact keys):
 {"schema":"reversible-divisor-circuit-v1", "program":program,
  "convolution_orders":[ceil(abs(alpha)) for convolve stages],
  "recovery_power":sum(convolution_orders),
  "domain":"discrete_weighted_l2_sigma_gt_half",
  "bound":"(1+1/epsilon)^recovery_power",
  "coefficient_binding":"same_fixed_source_in_all_stages_and_inverse"}
Independent checker API: verify(certificate, meter=None) -> True, raises on
malformed or false data. A checked certificate proves the grammar's uniform
recovery theorem, not a source-specific moment estimate. Proof is ordinary
mathematical reasoning plus a restricted checker, not a proof assistant.

Let H_sigma,T(f)=sum_{1<=n<=T}|f(n)|^2/n^(2sigma+1), sigma=1/2+epsilon.
For r=ceil(|alpha|), each kernel and its inverse have coefficient majorant d_r.
Divisor-supported dilation has norm d^(-sigma-1/2), so each convolution and
inverse has norm <=zeta(1+epsilon)^r <=(1+1/epsilon)^r, uniformly in T.
Signs are isometries. The circuit has the stated UNSQUARED norm bound; recovery
of squared energy uses its square. No integrability assumption on infinite M
is used to prove these finite-T inequalities.

Numerical observable: D(X)=X^-2 sum_{X<=n<2X} output(n)^2, full output.
For one fixed circuit, the OPEN gate is: for cofinally many positive integers
q, there exists finite C_q independent of dyadic X>=1 with
 D(X)<=C_q*ceil(X^(1/q)). These estimates imply H_sigma(M)<infinity for every
sigma>1/2 by the inverse theorem. The floor-step Mellin integral then provides
holomorphic ordinary 1/zeta on Re(s)>1/2. Finite fits do not establish this gate.

Selection's conditional cost retains explicit theorem constants. For a fixed q,
sigma=1/2+1/q, a proved dyadic estimate from X>=32 would imply
 H_sigma(output)<=sum_{n<32}output(n)^2/n^2+4q*C_q.
Indeed each dyadic block contributes at most2C_q*X^(-1/q), and grouping q
successive powers of two gives sum_{j>=0}2^(-j/q)<=2q. Applying the inverse
squares its norm factor, giving (1+q)^(2R) times that displayed quantity.
The search ranks these CONDITIONAL quantities. A fitted C_q is not a proved
global constant, and only four tested q do not establish the cofinal gate.

Finite evaluator interface agreed with independent checker:
 producer module reversible_divisor_circuits.py:
   source(name,H,meter) -> list a[0..H], a0=0, ai in{-1,0,1};
   evaluate(program, values, meter) -> output list[0..H], output0=0;
   moments(output, scales, meter) -> [{X:int, energy:canonical rational}].
 checker module check_reversible_divisor_circuits.py: independent versions of
   verify, source, evaluate, moments; evaluator must NOT import producer.
 Sources: mu; one; partial_prime_13. The last is zero on nonsquarefree n and
 (-1)^(number of prime factors in {2,3,5,7,11,13}) otherwise. It agrees with mu
 through16 but has a positive mean asymptotically; it is a diagnostic control,
 not a counterexample to actual Mobius. Each control binds its OWN source
 phases consistently. Primality/factorization are explicit evaluator oracles.
