CENTERED BANDS: PAID HEAD, DIAGONAL, AND RECIPROCAL-INTERVAL FORCING

Scope and outcome
-----------------
This new analytical memo reads centered_product_fibers.py, its protocol,
the fixed pilot-002 selection, and the prior pole/recovery review. The
selected stencil is C(z)=(1+z)^2 at gamma=0,1/6,1/3. No new numerical
source evaluations, experiments, tests, hashes, subagents or prior/frozen
edits are used. No uniform complete moment or RH theorem is proved.

There are three useful source-uniform reductions. First, all three fractional
presentations of this same relative-band filter are equivalent by constants,
not merely logarithms. Second, the exact increment center differs from a
pure multiplicative power by a positive l1 sequence; restoring this change
costs only a logarithm at EVERY sharp product cutoff. Third, the complete
low-product head and every diagonal stripe are paid polylogarithmically.
The remaining forcing is an explicit averaged OFF-DIAGONAL reciprocal-
interval correlation, with a full Mobius/divisor convolution presentation.

A canonical gamma-zero identity presentation removes the selected stencil's
cubic-log inverse overhead. Better still, joining ALL high product bands
before taking an absolute value removes even the band-count loss: a
one-sided bound for the full signed high-product off-diagonal correlation
recovers the target with explicit polylog padding. This latter residual
is equivalent to the original moment up to paid pieces, not an independent
gain. The pole bracket which appears in its convolution vanishes
analytically, not as a proved convergent coefficient tail.

1. Exact band and stencil conventions
------------------------------------
Use u=1/3, v=2/3, kappa=2/3, beta=9/2, the old exact positive m=m_u,
and R_u=kappa*m. Let a=p-d be the complete actual-source coefficient;
the entire same-prime-power d is retained. Define
  c(h)=m*[h^kappa-(h-1)^kappa], b(h)=a(h)-c(h),
  b(1)=-m, A(n)=sum_(h<=n)a(h), B(n)=A(n)-m*n^kappa.
Every index zero is zero, prefixes below one vanish. Coefficients are real.
For integer dyadic X>=1 put N=2X-1, J=ceil(log2(N^2)) and
  W_X(h,k)=max(0,2X-max(X,h,k)).
Thus W counts the integers n in X..N with h,k<=n.

For j>=0 let Y=2^j and, for t in [1/2,1], set
  D_(j,gamma)(t)=Y^gamma/X^2
      *sum_(Y/2<hk<=tY, h,k<=N) b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k)(hk)^(-gamma).  (1.1)
Negative bands are zero. At j=0 the unit product appears at t=1, not
earlier. At j=J the upper Y may exceed N^2: the actual state then has
a terminal plateau. All formulas retain that truncated last band.
The selected filter is
  E_(j,gamma)(t)=D_j(t)+2D_(j-1)(t)+D_(j-2)(t),
  U_(C,gamma)(X)=max_(0<=j<=J) sup_(1/2<=t<=1)|E_j(t)|.         (1.2)
The SAME relative t is used on all three product bands.

Every coefficient product in (1.1) is
  aa-ac-ca+cc,
each class further split by squarefree/nonsquarefree PRODUCT. All six
sectors, repeated valuations, main crosses and the unit remain. Neither
an SF sector nor a restricted product kernel is a positive complete energy.

2. Why the chosen filter and gamma menu do not give an exponent gain
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Writing a backward band shift as z, C=(1+z)^2 has inverse
  g_l=(-1)^l*(l+1), l>=0.
The complete absolute recovery cost is
  sum_(l=0)^J (J-l+1)|g_l|=binom(J+3,3).                       (2.1)
Thus its cubic-log cost is real. It annihilates a perfectly alternating
band mode to second order. For a real growth mode D_j=lambda^j with
lambda>0 it multiplies the mode by (1+lambda^(-1))^2, which is nonzero.
It does not remove any positive real-power obstruction. Mobius sign
(-1)^omega(product) is not an identity equating it to (-1)^j.
Any finite band cancellation, or a favorable fitted slope, cannot supply
the missing actual arithmetic identity.

The gamma variants have an even stronger exact equivalence. For fixed
j let D_(j,0)(t) be the signed cumulative measure on relative product
v=hk/Y in (1/2,1]. Stieltjes Abel gives
  D_(j,gamma)(t)=t^(-gamma)*D_(j,0)(t)
           +gamma*integral_(1/2)^t v^(-gamma-1)*D_(j,0)(v)dv.   (2.2)
This includes all atoms, the unit at relative one, and terminal plateaus.
The SAME causal relative-t operator acts on every j, so it commutes with
ANY fixed constant-coefficient band stencil. Consequently (2.2) also holds
with D replaced by E. The inverse formula is
  E_(j,0)(t)=t^gamma*E_(j,gamma)(t)
           -gamma*integral_(1/2)^t v^(gamma-1)*E_(j,gamma)(v)dv.
Taking absolute values gives, uniformly in X and J,
  U_(C,gamma)<=2^gamma*U_(C,0),
  U_(C,0)<=(2-2^(-gamma))*U_(C,gamma).                         (2.3)
At gamma zero both are equality. The three selected presentations are
therefore constant-equivalent, with no distinct cofinal exponent mechanism.
This is relative-band normalization; it is not the global weighted-prefix
Abel operator of the earlier memo. Uniform partial control is essential.

3. Actual-source divisor bounds, including prime-power removal and unit
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
For alpha>0, a_alpha=mu*n^(-alpha) is multiplicative with
  a_alpha(q^e)=-(1-q^(-alpha))*q^(-(e-1)*alpha), e>=1.
Hence |a_alpha(n)|<=1. The zero-unit factor h_alpha=(a_alpha-delta)/alpha
has h_alpha(1)=0 and |h_alpha(n)|<=1/alpha otherwise. Complete convolution
therefore gives |p(n)|<=beta*d2(n). At prime powers p=d exactly, because
all its nonunit factor splits use that same prime. At every other integer
d=0. Also a(1)=0. It follows, WITHOUT adding a second absolute d bound,
  |a(n)|<=beta*d2(n), all n>=1.                               (3.1)
Concavity of x^kappa gives 0<c(n)<=m, so with K=beta+m,
  |b(n)|<=K*d2(n).                                           (3.2)
In particular the retained b(1)=-m obeys this bound.

The elementary divisor estimates used below are
  d2*d2=d4, d2(n)^2<=d4(n),
  sum_(n<=H)d4(n)<=H*(1+log H)^3, H>=1.                       (3.3)
The pointwise square inequality follows primewise from
(e+1)^2<=binom(e+3,3). The summatory estimate follows by summing the
three free divisor indices harmonically and bounding the fourth floor.
No prime distribution or RH estimate enters these inequalities.

4. Pure-power centering with exact logarithmic restoration
----------------------------------------------------------
Define
  e(n)=c(n)-R_u*n^(-u),
  tilde_b(n)=a(n)-R_u*n^(-u)=b(n)+e(n).                        (4.1)
For n>=2, the integral of the decreasing derivative over [n-1,n]
is at least its endpoint value; at n=1, m>=R_u. Thus e(n)>=0, with
  e(1)=m-R_u, b(1)=-m, tilde_b(1)=-R_u.
Euler summation with all integer endpoints retained gives
  sum_(n<=H)e(n)=m*H^kappa-R_u*sum_(n<=H)n^(-u),
  E0:=sum_(n>=1)e(n)=-R_u*zeta(u)>0.                          (4.2)
Here zeta(u)<0, and the Euler remainder tends to zero. This is an exact
finite l1 mass including the unit, not a fitted or conditional tail.
Also |tilde_b(n)|<=(beta+R_u)d2(n)<=K*d2(n).

For ANY band and ANY partial t, its normalized kernel weight satisfies
  Y^gamma*(hk)^(-gamma)<=2^gamma,
while W<=X. Expanding tilde_b(h)tilde_b(k)-b(h)b(k) gives both b/e
crosses and e/e. Equations (3.2),(3.3),(4.2) yield
  |D_j(tilde_b)-D_j(b)|
    <=2^gamma/X *[2E0*sum_(h<=N)|b(h)|+E0^2]
    <=2^gamma*[4E0*K*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X].                     (4.3)
For stencil (1+z)^2 the cost is at most FOUR times (4.3). This is
uniform in j,t,X and gamma, including truncated bands. It proves
cofinal-equivalence up to logarithmic padding for the entire sharp-band
auxiliary after pure-power centering. The original six-sector values and
program are unchanged; all e cross/unit terms are restored analytically.

This paid change simplifies the arithmetic obstruction, not its exponent.
By contrast, a component with a paid PREFIX norm, such as the entire
same-prime d, is not automatically removable at all sharp product cuts.
Its prefix bound alone cannot replace the l1 argument in (4.3).

5. Uniform low-product head and every diagonal stripe
-----------------------------------------------------
These estimates apply to b or tilde_b using the SAME K. Define L_N=1+log N.
For every Y=2^j>=1 and partial t, absolute values and (3.3) give
  |D_(j,gamma)|<=2^gamma*K^2*(Y/X)*(1+log Y)^3.                (5.1)
Indeed the product kernel W/X^2 is at most 1/X, and all pairs hk<=Y
are bounded by the complete divisor convolution d2*d2=d4. Therefore
ALL product bands with Y<=X are paid polylogarithmically. For the selected
filter with Y<=X, the finer explicit bound is
  |E_(j,gamma)|<= (9/4)*2^gamma*K^2*(Y/X)*(1+log Y)^3.          (5.2)
The factor 9/4 comes from 1+2/2+1/4; negative subbands are simply absent.

At ANY Y, a diagonal pair has h=k and relative product in (1/2,1]. Its
nonnegative contribution is at most
  2^gamma/X *sum_(h<=N)|b(h)|^2
     <=2^gamma*K^2*(N/X)*L_N^3
     <=2^(gamma+1)*K^2*L_N^3.                                (5.3)
Replace b by tilde_b with the same result. The selected stencil's complete
diagonal cost is at most 2^(gamma+3)*K^2*L_N^3. For h>1 these diagonal
products are nonsquarefree; h=1 and its precise center are still included.

Partition each selected-filter subband ONCE: if its Y_r<=X pay the entire
band by (5.1); if Y_r>X pay only its diagonal by (5.3). The low-piece total
is <=4*2^gamma*K^2*L_N^3 and the high-diagonal total is
<=8*2^gamma*K^2*L_N^3. Thus a uniform paid envelope is
  12*2^gamma*K^2*L_N^3,                                     (5.4)
before adding four times (4.3). These are conservative constants, not
optimized constants. They keep the log power explicit and avoid subtracting
overlapping diagonal/head pieces twice.

6. Exact averaged reciprocal-interval correlation: the hard frontier
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Use tilde_b for the simplified auxiliary and write x_h=tilde_b(h)h^(-gamma),
  I_gamma(A,B)=sum_(A<k<=B)x_k,
with integer endpoints, zero if B<=A, and prefixes below one zero.
For fixed n in X..N and real y>=0 define
  Q_gamma(n,y)=sum_(h,k<=n,hk<=y)x_h*x_k.
The COMPLETE truncated hyperbola identity is
  Q_gamma(n,y)=2*sum_(h<=a)x_h*sum_(k<=min(n,floor(y/h)))x_k
                 -[sum_(h<=a)x_h]^2,
  a=min(n,floor(sqrt(y))).                                   (6.1)
At y<1 both sides vanish. For y>=n^2 it equals the full prefix square.

For a band put L=Y/2, V=tY, a0=min(n,floor(sqrt L)),
a1=min(n,floor(sqrt V)), and k_z(h)=min(n,floor(z/h)). Then exactly
  Q_gamma(n,V)-Q_gamma(n,L)
    =2*sum_(h<=a0)x_h*I_gamma(k_L(h),k_V(h))
     +2*sum_(a0<h<=a1)x_h*I_gamma(h,k_V(h))
     +sum_(a0<h<=a1)x_h^2.                                  (6.2)
The first two sums are disjoint off-diagonal reciprocal intervals; the last
is the full diagonal stripe. The second sum is the root/frontier correction,
not an optional short-side error. Derive it by expanding the difference of
the two prefix squares in (6.1). It retains every floor and equality edge.

Let the OFF-DIAGONAL band be
  O_gamma(X,Y,t)=2*Y^gamma/X^2 *sum_(n=X)^N
      [sum_(h<=a0)x_h*I_gamma(k_L(h),k_V(h))
        +sum_(a0<h<=a1)x_h*I_gamma(h,k_V(h))].                 (6.3)
This is a genuinely specified arithmetic bilinear correlation, not a new
name for the full prefix energy. It asks whether the coefficient on the
short leg cancels against an actual-source interval on the reciprocal leg,
averaged over n with the exact prefix geometry. The h=k part is already paid.
No gcd, valuation, SF/NS, main or unit contribution is omitted from x_h.

The selected hard forcing is
  sum_(r=0)^2 c_r*1_(Y_r>X)*O_gamma(X,Y_r,t),
  (c0,c1,c2)=(1,2,1), Y_r=2^(j-r), with negative bands absent. (6.4)
Its cofinal uniform bound is equivalent to the selected auxiliary after
the explicit polylog padding (4.3),(5.4). It remains strictly stronger
than the original whole moment for generic sources. Its domain is
  X<Y_r<=2^J, t in [1/2,1],
NOT merely Y_r<=N^2. The last Y may exceed N^2; clamps in (6.2) make its
intervals empty or stationary exactly where required. At t=1/2 all bands
are empty; at t=1 their lower-open/upper-closed dyadic edges remain exact.

7. Full signed arithmetic in each reciprocal interval
------------------------------------------------------
Let delta be the Dirichlet unit, mu_gamma(n)=mu(n)n^(-gamma), and
k_alpha(n)=n^(-alpha). The complete source, initially in Re(s)>1, has
  tilde_b=beta*(mu*mu*k_u*k_v-mu*k_u-mu*k_v+delta)
                  -d-R_u*k_u.                               (7.1)
The delta term has coefficient beta, and the entire d is present. In
particular (7.1) gives tilde_b(1)=-R_u, not zero. Weighting n^(-gamma)
shifts every factor: convolution still preserves the product weight.

An explicit interval version is as follows. Let
  S_alpha(y)=sum_(k<=y)k^(-alpha),
  K_gamma(y)=sum_(rs<=y)r^(-u-gamma)*s^(-v-gamma),
zero below one, and Delta f(A,B;r)=f(B/r)-f(A/r). Then
  I_gamma(A,B)
    = beta*sum_(rs<=B) mu(r)mu(s)(rs)^(-gamma)
                            *Delta K_gamma(A,B;rs)
      -beta*sum_(r<=B)mu(r)r^(-gamma)
            *[Delta S_(u+gamma)(A,B;r)+Delta S_(v+gamma)(A,B;r)]
      +beta*1_(A<1<=B)
      -sum_(A<k<=B)d(k)k^(-gamma)
      -R_u*[S_(u+gamma)(B)-S_(u+gamma)(A)].                    (7.2)
All sums are finite. No factor endpoint, prime-power sector, or rectangle
has been truncated. The signed forcing (6.3) therefore includes, explicitly,
one outer centered coefficient times a TWO-MOBIUS weighted divisor interval,
both one-Mobius complements, the exact pole power, delta and the entire d.
Bounding just one branch or converting every sign to an absolute value
does not prove (6.3). The new lemma must control these terms jointly or
pay a proved error for every removed term.

There is a compact factorization exposing the pole cancellation. Put
  g=beta*(mu*mu*k_v)-beta*mu-R_u*delta, G(s)=sum g(n)n^(-s).
Then, for Re(s)>1,
  tilde A(s)=zeta(s+u)*[beta*zeta(s+v)/zeta(s)^2
                              -beta/zeta(s)-R_u]
                 -beta*zeta(s+v)/zeta(s)+beta-mathcal D(s),
  tilde_b=k_u*g-beta*(mu*k_v)+beta*delta-d.                   (7.3)
By the exact definition R_u=beta*[zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2
                                             -1/zeta(2/3)],
the bracket G(s) has an ANALYTIC zero at real s=kappa. This cancels the
local pole of zeta(s+u). It does NOT prove ordinary convergence of
sum g(n)n^(-kappa), or that any finite tail is small, or convergence to
zero. Treating the analytic zero as a convergent cancellation tail would
insert precisely the missing source-specific signed estimate.

For example, a complete outer hyperbola exposes a well-defined forcing.
For real z>=1 choose U=z^(1/3), V=z/U, and let
  G_gamma(y)=sum_(d<=y)g(d)d^(-gamma),
  G_kappa(U)=sum_(d<=U)g(d)d^(-kappa),
  A_(v,gamma)(z)=sum_(n<=z)(mu*k_v)(n)n^(-gamma),
  D_gamma(z)=sum_(n<=z)d(n)n^(-gamma).
Set alpha=u+gamma<1 and lambda=1-alpha=kappa-gamma. Exact hyperbola and
Euler summation give
  sum_(n<=z)tilde_b(n)n^(-gamma)
    =z^lambda/lambda*G_kappa(U)+zeta(alpha)*G_gamma(U)
      +sum_(m<=V)m^(-alpha)*G_gamma(z/m)
      -G_gamma(U)*S_alpha(V)
      -beta*A_(v,gamma)(z)+beta-D_gamma(z)+epsilon_(g,gamma)(z).
The intersection rectangle is explicitly retained. The finite G_kappa(U)
is NOT replaced by the analytic value G(kappa)=0.

One component of this recurrence is uniformly subcritical. Euler's exact
S_alpha remainder obeys |e_alpha(y)|<=y^(-alpha). Since
  |g(d)|<=(2beta+R_u)*d3(d),
the complete short-side error satisfies
  |epsilon_(g,gamma)(z)|
     <=(2beta+R_u)*z^(1/9-gamma)*(1+log(z)/3)^2.               (7.4)
This retains all real floor endpoints. It has finite critical norm, even
at gamma zero. HOWEVER a paid prefix error is not automatically a paid
sharp-product error after another unrestricted signed convolution. The
outer factor in (6.3) and its reciprocal geometry still require a bound.
The remaining finite bracket tail, long-side G_gamma quotient sum, rectangle,
single-source A_v and d cannot be independently assumed RH-strength. This
is a structured smaller-scale forcing presentation, not a closed induction.

8. Optional stronger partial-band recovery with off-diagonal forcing
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This is an analytically justified simplification for the NEXT construction,
not a reranking or alteration of the frozen numerical pilot. Drop the
equivalent fractional presentations and use the identity band stencil.
Let
  U_off(X)=max_(j: X<2^j<=2^J) sup_(t in [1/2,1])
                       |O_0(X,2^j,t)|,
with empty maximum zero. Head bands cost at most K^2*L_N^3. High diagonal
stripes cost at most 2K^2*L_N^3. The center restoration costs (4.3) at gamma
zero. At t=1 the bands partition EVERY product, including the unit and last
plateau, so the complete positive moment obeys
  E_R(X)=X^(-2)*sum_(n=X)^N |B(n)|^2
    <=(J+1)*[U_off(X)+2K^2*(1+log N)^3
                  +4E0*K*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X].               (8.1)
All constants are fixed old source constants; no fit occurs. In fact only
the t=1 endpoints are needed for this particular inequality. The uniform
partial supremum is an additional stronger premise retained for future
sharp-band operators. Thus (8.1) is sufficient, not an equivalence of that
premise with the original target.

The ONLY open quantity in (8.1) is the explicit complete correlation (6.3),
at gamma zero and high product scales. Low products, diagonals, and exact-
to-pure-power centering have source-independent proved costs. The actual
R_new=K-L0+Lu-main is still linked to R by R=R_new+Lv with the prior paid
comparison and its full finite cross; no branch is silently deleted.

This is an OPTIONAL stronger construction lemma. The next primary target
should preserve cancellation between different high product bands, as follows.

9. Full joint high-product frontier: one-sided recovery with NO band loss
------------------------------------------------------------------------
At gamma zero join all high products BEFORE abs and define
  O_X=2/X^2 *sum_(n=X)^N
                sum_(1<=h<k<=n, hk>X)tilde_b(h)*tilde_b(k).    (9.1)
The low full support hk<=X is bounded in absolute value by
  K^2*(1+log X)^3,
since W=X on this support and d2*d2=d4. The remaining diagonal h=k,
h^2>X, has cost <=2K^2*L_N^3. The full kernel b-to-tilde correction has
the SAME bound (4.3), with gamma zero: its proof used only W<=X and
the complete e l1 mass, not a relative-band premise. Thus EXACTLY
  E_R(X)=O_X+P_X,
  |P_X|<=3K^2*(1+log N)^3
                +4E0*K*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X=:Paid_X.            (9.2)
All product edges, repeated valuations, main crosses and units remain.
In particular
  E_R(X)<=O_X+Paid_X, O_X>=-Paid_X.                            (9.3)
Only a ONE-SIDED upper bound O_X<=C_epsilon*X^epsilon is needed. Because
E_R is nonnegative and (9.2) is exact, such cofinal upper bounds and the
original cofinal moment are equivalent up to these paid polylog terms.
This is an equivalent residual obligation, not an independently proved
prime-cancellation mechanism. It does NOT inherit the strict strengthening
of the uniform sharp-partial auxiliary in Section 8. There is no J loss
and no demand to pay separate high bands, SF/NS classes or arithmetic legs.

The full-joint reciprocal-interval expression is also exact. Put
  H=floor(sqrt X), T(y)=sum_(h<=y)tilde_b(h).
Since n>=X and floor(X/h)<=X for h>=1,
  O_X=2/X^2 *sum_(n=X)^N
       [sum_(h<=H)tilde_b(h)*I_0(floor(X/h),n)
         +sum_(H<h<=n)tilde_b(h)*I_0(h,n)].                   (9.4)
The lower endpoints exclude equality hk=X and h=k. Here I_0 is the
COMPLETE actual-source interval in (7.2), equivalently (7.3), not a
prime-only or uncentered interval. The second term has the alternative
exact form
  2*sum_(H<h<=n)tilde_b(h)*I_0(h,n)
    =[T(n)-T(H)]^2-sum_(H<h<=n)tilde_b(h)^2.                  (9.5)
Thus an attempt to bound the large-leg triangular piece independently
would meet a long-prefix square again. It cannot be declared stable or
small merely because the other leg h<=sqrt X is at a smaller scale.
The small-leg reciprocal intervals and large-leg triangular term must
remain coupled, unless an independently proved recovery estimate is supplied.

For comparison the low-product scalar is exactly
  Q_low(X)=2*sum_(h<=H)tilde_b(h)*T(floor(X/h))-T(H)^2,
and its complete kernel contribution is Q_low(X)/X. This quantity is
already absolutely polylogarithmic by the divisor bound. Its negative
credit cannot alone absorb a macroscopic long-prefix square by a generic
positivity argument; that would assume the target. The genuine missing
information is signed correlation in the joined high-product expression,
not a scalar filter or a separately assumed companion moment.

The concrete next search input can therefore use (9.1)/(9.4) as its PRIMARY
forcing, with (7.2)/(7.3) specifying every signed arithmetic term and
(9.2) supplying exact recovery. Partial-band tests such as (6.3) remain
optional stronger auxiliaries. Any proposed joint construction must either
bound (9.1) from above with uniform constants, or reduce it to a new
well-founded correlation system without assuming the long-prefix square
in (9.5). The analytic bracket zero at kappa is not such a bound.
