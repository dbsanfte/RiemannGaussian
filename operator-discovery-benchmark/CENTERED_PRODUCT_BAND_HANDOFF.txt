Primary next target: combine high products before taking any bound
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Define the single signed correlation
  O_X = 2/X^2 * sum_(n=X)^(2X-1)
                   sum_(1<=h<k<=n, hk>X) tilde_b(h)*tilde_b(k).
This retains cancellation across ALL high product bands. It includes every
prime/composite contribution, repeated valuation and gcd overlap.

There is an exact decomposition
  E_R(X)=O_X+H_X+D_X+C_X,
where H_X is the complete tilde_b kernel on hk<=X, D_X its diagonal on
h^2>X, and C_X the difference between the b and tilde_b complete energies.
The independently proved remainder bound is
  |H_X+D_X+C_X| <= B(X),
  B(X)=3K^2*(1+log N)^3+B_center(X).
Consequently E_R(X)<=O_X+B(X), and O_X>=-B(X). The primary missing lemma is
ONLY the one-sided bound O_X<=C_epsilon*X^epsilon for every epsilon>0,
uniformly over dyadic X. There is no band-count recovery loss in this route.

This is an equivalent residual form of the original complete moment, with
the prior paid R=R_new+Lv comparison still required. It is NOT an independent
signed gain or a closed proof. Its value is that the remaining source and
geometry are explicit and the removable terms are now proved bounded.
Uniform separate sharp-band estimates remain optional stronger auxiliaries;
they are not mandatory prerequisites for the primary target.
