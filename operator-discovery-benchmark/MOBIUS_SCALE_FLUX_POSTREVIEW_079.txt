the removed prefix p<=ceil(H^gamma) costs O(H^gamma)=o(u/logu).
The finite fixed endpoints each contribute their coefficient timesu/d.

For1/2<gamma<3/4, put alpha=2gamma/3, so1/3<alpha<1/2. The last
least-prime bin contains at most two distinct prime factors, since three

Its leading term follows from ordinary PNT and partial summation:
