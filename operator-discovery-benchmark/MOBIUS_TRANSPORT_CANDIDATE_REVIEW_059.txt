2. Exact rescue savings and irreversible work
--------------------------------------------
For ANY complete first-pass pairing let E be its sum of edge costs and L
its remaining token list. Let M_L=sum_(i in L)m_i. Its cost with those
tokens unmatched is B_first=E+M_L. Every second-pass opposite-sign pair
(i,j) replaces m_i+m_j by |m_i-m_j|. Consequently

 B_final=B_first-2 sum_(rescue pairs)min(m_i,m_j)
        =E+B_rescue(L).                                    (2)

This is an exact signed-plan reduction with no missing leftover term.
It proves |J_N|<=B_final<=B_first. Crucially B_final>=E: rescue savings
come only from first-pass leftover mass. They cannot compensate an
expensive edge already committed in E.

All first and second passes that scan opposite signs greedily exhaust
the smaller sign count in their group. A global rescue therefore leaves
exactly |sum_(i in L)epsilon_i| tokens, independent of their magnitudes.
This count equality does not determine their residual mass or the cost
of already paired paths.

For an arbitrary signed residual list define

 T_L(y)=sum_(i in L)epsilon_i 1_(m_i>y),
 V_L=integral_0^infinity |T_L(y)|dy.

V_L is its optimal unit matching cost, with zero-valued dummy tokens
representing all unmatched charges. If Omega_L=B_rescue(L)-V_L, then

 B_final=E+V_L+Omega_L,       Omega_L>=0.                    (3)

At threshold y, let C_+(y),C_-(y) be numbers of oppositely directed
crossings from the chosen rescue edges and zero-dummy paths. Then
T_L=C_+-C_- and Omega_L=2 integral min(C_+,C_-)dy. This explicitly
identifies the cost lost through incompatible path directions.

An auxiliary can certify Omega_L=0 by proving one-direction threshold
flow (for example, a correctly sorted common-kernel matching). Merely
sorting by cofactor d when profiles differ does not prove it.

A further reusable SUFFICIENT inequality, valid for any maximal-count
global matching and any chosen m_0>=0, is

 B_rescue(L)<=m_0*|sum_L epsilon_i|+sum_L |m_i-m_0|.          (4)

For pairs apply |m_i-m_j|<=|m_i-m_0|+|m_j-m_0|; for leftovers
apply m_i<=m_0+|m_i-m_0| and use the exact leftover count. This exposes
a residual-count-plus-spread construction without assuming the original
bound. Its arithmetic count and spread premises remain unpaid. A small
net count alone does not suffice. Unit matching cannot freely cancel a
magnitude near 2m_0 against two opposite tokens near m_0; that would be
a different, explicitly licensed three-term grouping grammar.

3. What0013 controls: first-pass variation minus actual rescued mass
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Within profile i, list d_1<...<d_s and m_1>=...>=m_s. Put
A_i(y)=sum_(m_j>y)epsilon_j. Ascending stack realizes its exact optimum

 V_i=integral |A_i(y)|dy
    =sum_j |sum_(l<=j)epsilon_l|*(m_j-m_(j+1)), m_(s+1)=0.

Its leftovers all have the sign of C_i=sum_j epsilon_j and number |C_i|.
Let L_i(y) be their signed threshold count. The first committed paths
and leftover zero paths realize the optimum pointwise, so

 E_i=integral [|A_i(y)|-|L_i(y)|]dy >=0,
 sum_(leftover i)m_j=integral |L_i(y)|dy.                   (5)

Thus0013 has the concrete all-N identity

 B_0013=sum_i E_i + integral |sum_i L_i(y)|dy + Omega_L.    (6)

The saved improvement over the profile-only control can arise from
cancellation of the residual L_i across profiles. Bounding each complete
V_i separately would lose precisely that cancellation. Conversely all
balanced-profile variation (C_i=0) is already committed and cannot be
rescued. Equation(6) is more diagnostic than a single fitted cost.

A concrete sufficient next lemma is a uniform bound on the committed
masked-prefix variation sum E_i together with the joint residual
threshold integral and a structural bound on Omega_L. This is NOT a
proved arithmetic estimate. It does identify independently meaningful
objects that a detector can falsify, including the balanced-profile
sector and wrong-way residual crossings. A claim bounding the sum of
these objects without another identity is equivalent to the selected
plan bound, not a new argument.

There is also a stronger, simpler prefix-discrepancy route: prove for
each i and every prefix y that the actual N-dependent masked mu count
is <=D_N in absolute value. Then V_i<=D_N*m_i(d_min). Since
G_M(t)<=t^k product_(ell|M)log ell, each m_i(d_min) has a finite uniform
upper bound (S costs at most twice the one-factor bound). This proves
B_0013<=sum V_i<=constant*D_N. An independently proved D_N=O(sqrt(N)
polylog N) would be useful, but is a strong unproved source lemma; it is
not supplied by an ordinary unmasked endpoint Mertens value.

The floor/profile mask can be expanded exactly into ordinary-mu interval
counts. Its breakpoints are N/[3(j+r/4)] and N/[5(j+r/4)] with integer j
and r=0,1,2,3. Intersections give the complete profile cells. On a cofactor
shell d in[D,2D] there are O(1+N/D) such cells. This provides a compiler
to short-interval coprime mu discrepancies, including every boundary.
Applying triangle to each interval can be costly; no square-root control
of those discrepancies is assumed. A useful new lemma must exploit the
joint intervals or pay their number, not hide that loss in a constant.

4.0372 and0402: changes of pairing geometry, not new source laws
---------------------------------------------------------------
For d in[N,4N/3], N/(3d) lies[1/4,1/3] and N/(5d) lies[3/20,1/5].
Thus these tokens, when squarefree and coprime, are all P with kernel
sign +1. Width-N blocking therefore splits ONLY the P profile at N;
all other profiles occur below N. At the point d=N itself the assigned
block is the upper one, exactly as floor(d/N) requires.

Let A_0(y),A_1(y) be signed threshold counts in the lower and upper P
blocks. The first-pass cost with leftovers priced is

 V_P,split=integral (|A_0(y)|+|A_1(y)|)dy.

Compared with the complete P optimum its exact excess is

 V_P,split-V_P=2 integral min(|A_0|,|A_1|)
                                  1_(A_0*A_1<0)dy.        (7)

Above Phi(3N), A_1=0. Below that value the lower block has constant
threshold count equal to its total signed count. Hence the difference
comes from a precisely delimited leftover frontier. The global pass
can trade some of this split excess for different cross-profile rescue.
There is no universal dominance between the TWO-PASS0013 and0372 merely
from profile optimum; that dominance applied only before cross-profile
rescue. Nor are the two programs universal aliases.0372 changes which
P paths are committed before that rescue, without changing the arithmetic
source or introducing an additional Mobius identity.

0402 identifies P and Q as the SAME kernel Phi at scaled arguments3d
and5d. D and S remain the separate kernels Phi(z)-Phi(5z/3) and
Phi(z)+Phi(5z/3). This exact normalization removes a genuine artificial
P/Q separation. Ascending scaled arguments would optimize each common
kernel group. The SELECTED word scans them descending, and with leftover
paths ending at zero it is not generally optimal. If a group has balanced
sign counts, both directions attain its optimum, but that exception
does not establish generic optimality or uniform equivalence.

The common P/Q signed prefix has an exact arithmetic interpretation. At
scaled index a, P terms have a=3d and sign -mu(a)W(N/a), while Q terms
have a=5d and sign +mu(a)W(N/a), because gcd(d,30)=1. Each also retains
its exclusive-profile mask: the other floor coefficient must be zero.
These are disjoint exact-one-prime-valuation sectors, not independently
varying signs and not the whole unrestricted mu prefix. This is a useful
shared-kernel discrepancy leaf, but no prime-exchange estimate pays it.

5.0390 is asymptotically refuted despite its finite passes
-------------------------------------------------------
This result depends on the ACTUAL fixed source and the ACTUAL first
ascending-prime pass. Let a satisfy

 4N/125<a<4N/123,       mu(a)^2=1,       gcd(a,210)=1.       (8)

Then v=N/a lies(123/4,125/4). The four exact floor arguments give

 v/3 in(10+1/4,10+5/12),  v/5 in(6+3/20,6+1/4),
 v/21 in(41/28,125/84),  v/35 in(123/140,25/28).

W is +1 on fractional part[1/4,1/2), -1 on[1/2,3/4), and zero
otherwise. Therefore BOTH a and7a have profile P and kernel sign +1.
Both supports are inside4N/3. Since7 does not divide a,
mu(7a)=-mu(a), so the edge(a,7a) is an eligible opposite-sign edge.

Every source token is coprime to30, so no edges with prime2,3,5 exist.
Prime7 is first. All eligible7-edges are disjoint: squarefree a with
7 not dividing a has its unique partner7a, while7^2a is not a token.
Thus every edge in(8) is necessarily taken by0390 before any later
prime or global rescue. An edge cannot be prevented by another7-edge.

Squarefree coprime integers have positive density. For Q=210,

 rho_Q=(phi(Q)/Q) product_(ell prime,ell not dividing Q)(1-ell^-2)>0.

Proof: expand mu(n)^2=sum_(d^2|n)mu(d), impose gcd(n,Q)=1, and count
the coprime multiples on the rational interval. Each inner count is
(phi(Q)/Q)*(v-u)N/d^2 plus an O_Q(1) endpoint error. Summing d up to
sqrt(vN) and extending the absolutely convergent main series leaves
O_(Q,u,v)(sqrt N). This proves

 number of a in(8)=rho_210*(8/15375)N+O(sqrt N).            (9)

It uses neither PNT nor RH. Even positivity can be certified elementary:
all primes outside210 are >=11, sum_(n>=11)n^-2<=1/10, and finite
product(1-x_i)>=1-sum x_i. Hence rho_210>=36/175. A conservative
interval error is66sqrt N for N>=123, so an explicit sufficiently large
threshold follows by demanding66sqrt N<=half the positive main lower
bound. No assertion about a practical or observed threshold is needed.

Every such edge has the irreversibly charged positive cost

 Delta_a=Phi_N(3a)-Phi_N(21a)
 =1/Gamma(1-alpha) integral_eps^tau
       G_M(t)(3a)^-t(1-7^-t)t^(-alpha-1)dt.                (10)

Put L=log(4N). For all sufficiently large N the interval[1/L,2/L]

1-ell^-t>=t log ell/2 for ell=2,3,5, and
1-7^-t>=t log7/2. Restricting the integral to that interval gives

 Delta_a>=c_1 L^(-(k-alpha+1)),       c_1>0 fixed.

Here c_1 can be displayed as
 exp(-2)*(log2 log3 log5 log7)/(16 Gamma(1-alpha))
 times [2^(k-alpha+1)-1]/(k-alpha+1).

Combining(9),(10) with B_final>=E proves

 B_0390(N)>=c_2 N/(log(4N))^(19/5)                         (11)

for every sufficiently large integer N, with c_2>0. Therefore no
fixed finite C can make B_0390<=C N^beta at ANY beta<1. In particular
its saved C=1/256,beta=1/2 claim is false all-scale. Its five finite
passes remain correct, and no new finite counterexample is claimed.

Generic detector rule: a first ascending-prime same-profile pass, whose
smallest allowed prime r has a positive-length rational cofactor cell
with equal profile AND equal nonzero kernel sign, incurs a positive-
density family of disjoint r-edges. For P,Q,S each edge has logarithmic
power k-alpha+1. D has an additional small-t zero in p^-t-q^-t, giving
k-alpha+2. Both powers rule out every beta<1. A generic density proof
can use rho_Q>=phi(Q)/(4Q), since sum_(n>=2)n^-2<=3/4.

Important limits: an earlier scan, an earlier ALLOWED prime, a changed
source, coefficient zeros, a nonexistent cell, or opposite kernel signs
invalidate automatic edge-taking unless separately checked. A later
global pass does NOT invalidate the result because it cannot remove
charged edges. This does not rule out prime-ratio mechanisms that select
only a sparse family or use their identities inside larger signed groups
before absolute values. It rules out this greedy positive-density route.

the exact common-kernel geometry;0390 uses an actual mu prime-removal
identity but pays too many expensive edges. Finite cost improvement may

 * balanced-profile committed variation and joint residual thresholds(6);
 * shared-kernel actual prime-valuation prefix discrepancy;
 * a proof of one-direction residual threshold flow or a bound on Omega;

Uniform bounds for the first three are new signed arithmetic premises,
not paid by the matching identity, finite C calibration or finite ablation.

necessarily loses, but must rebind the complete source and recovery.

a proved square-root/polylog cost bound restores the complete RH-sensitive
exchange; the scientific parameters satisfy these conditions at equality.
Those endpoint bounds cannot turn the surviving finite passes into proofs.
No ordinary-mu uniform estimate for0013,0372 or0402 was established here.
