Anchored multiscale energy diagnostic

complete-gram-storage-probe-001 passed three tests and froze 42 sources. It binds the independently admitted full14 separator and preregisters the entire anchored PSD state family R=e_P e_P^T+K, K PSD. For V_t equal to the full fourteen-atom vector, define S_R(X)=sum_{X<=t<2X} V_t^T R V_t. Coercivity S_R(X)>=E(X) is exact, where E(X)=sum P_t^2. This changes the proposed target to an innovation bound for S_R; it does not treat a signed increment as a nonnegative square.

The correct rearrangement is D-Delta(E)=Q+Delta(S_K), where Delta(S_K)=S_K(2X)-4 S_K(X). Its dual matrix is H_child-4 H_parent. Exact replay finds a negative direction consisting solely of the half coordinate. Consequently the full14 separator is invalid for this anchored-storage extension. This is a same-implementation exact diagnostic replay, not an independent uniform proof or a feasible primal certificate.

Here half=P_floor(t/2). The selected diagnostic energy is therefore E(X)+sum_{X<=t<2X} P_floor(t/2)^2. For even integer X, the second sum is exactly 2 E(X/2); at X=1 it is zero because P_0=0. For dyadic X>=2 its innovation is Delta(E)(X)+2 Delta(E)(X/2). Thus this particular direction adds a previous-scale energy, not new prime-sign information. Invalidating one witness does not exclude a different separator of the enlarged cone.

Conditional recovery and unresolved estimate

Suppose, separately, a fixed-in-X R_q=e_P e_P^T+K_q with K_q PSD and a finite nonnegative C_q satisfy

 S_Rq(2X)-4 S_Rq(X) <= C_q X^2 ceil(X^(1/q))

at EVERY dyadic X>=1, for q>0. Set W(X)=S_Rq(X)/X^2. Then W(2X)<=W(X)+(C_q/4)ceil(X^(1/q)) <= W(X)+(C_q/2)X^(1/q). Summing the geometric series gives

 E(X) <= [S_Rq(1)+(C_q/2)/(2^(1/q)-1)] X^(2+1/q)

for dyadic X>=1. This algebraic recovery is conditional: no uniform innovation estimate has been supplied. To invoke the project's RH bridge, the required estimates must hold for a cofinal family of q with constants independent of X, together with the recorded energy/Mellin hypotheses. A q=6 finite certificate would not suffice. All initial, floor, cutoff, unit, prime-power, noncoprime and complement contributions must survive a uniform proof.
