COFINAL ENDPOINT COUNTEREXAMPLES FOR FROZEN ACTUAL SIGNED-GAP CLAIMS

The source is the already frozen, independently verified profile pilot.
Its original 48 templates and 704 available fresh passes are unchanged.
This analytical extension uses the original actual arithmetic cases; they
are not fresh holdout data and are not used to retune any constant.
Actual Mobius counterexamples refute particular fitted inequalities, not RH.

1. EXACT ROOT PLATEAU, NOT A NUMERICAL LIMIT.
Fix an integer X>=2. For every integer d>=bit_length(X),
1<X^(1/d)<2, since X<2^d. Thus ceil(X^(1/d))=2 EXACTLY.
Every declared cofinal exponent schedule has epsilon(q)=1/d(q),
with d(q) monotone and tending to infinity. For all sufficiently large q,
the root ceiling at this fixed X is therefore two.

A finite explicit threshold can be certified without evaluating an enormous
factorial or root denominator. If d(q)=ceil(q^(p/r)), p,r positive integers,
q>=d0^r guarantees d(q)>=d0. If d(q)=2^ceil(q^(p/r)), the same threshold
is sufficient. If d(q)=q!, q>=d0 is sufficient. Here d0=bit_length(X).
For a fixed rational epsilon there is no cofinal conclusion; those ordinary
calibrations are excluded. Resource exhaustion in evaluating a huge exact
integer is not a semantic failure of this threshold theorem.

2. BOUNDED CONSTANT ENDPOINTS.
For C(q)=A, and log factor L_X=ceil(ell(X)^L), the exact eventual diagonal
is D=2*A*X*L_X. The source half-gap claim requires
Delta(X)<=A*X*L_X*Q(4X).
Any strict violation at ONE fixed dyadic X persists for EVERY q beyond
the certified threshold. Therefore it refutes
exists q0, every q>=q0, every dyadic X: Delta<=D*Q/2.
This is stronger than finding a failure at a single sampled q, which an
unknown starting q0 could potentially exclude.

The audit also distinguishes two weaker quantities:
the necessary complete-nonunit-Gram PSD entry is D*Q-Delta, and
the fully charged defect majorant is 4X*D-Delta.
A negative half-gap margin alone does not refute either weaker claim.
They are checked individually and reported separately. If D*Q-Delta<0,
the actual mu vector is a negative direction for the full dual matrix,
regardless of its Gram weight. If 4X*D-Delta<0, the proposed fully charged
RH-sufficient bound is itself false at that X for all large q.

The positive-power constant law with power zero has eventual C=2*A and
the same logic. An unrestricted per-level constant family has no supplied
endpoint value; equal numerical fits at finitely many levels do not establish
one. Growing positive-power, exponential, factorial and positive denominator
power laws are likewise not declared globally successful by this endpoint
test. Their uniform all-scale claims remain unresolved.

3. INDEPENDENT ACTUAL ARITHMETIC AND PROOF SCOPE.
Every original training, validation and final signed energy is recomputed
with an independent prime sieve. Q is its exact coefficient square sum.
All endpoint margins are rational; the negative witnesses are exact.
Three cofinal integer levels beyond each threshold are illustrative native
checks of the plateau. The universal quantifier comes from the monotone
schedule and elementary root theorem, not those finitely sampled levels.
No formula or coefficient is changed. Successful old finite tests remain
valid finite tests while the global dispositions are superseded.

This audit leaves the research objective open. A larger fitted constant may
remove these particular counterexamples, but that would be a new candidate
and would still require an all-scale signed arithmetic proof.
