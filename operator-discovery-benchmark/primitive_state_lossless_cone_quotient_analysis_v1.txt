Exact quotient proof

Write the regional quadratic coefficient contribution abstractly as
    A_child(Gc+K) + A_parent(Gp-4K),
where Gc,Gp,K are PSD and A_region is the linear polynomial map of that
region. Set
    Gc' = 0, K' = K+Gc, Gp' = Gp+4Gc.
These matrices remain PSD, because nonnegative sums of PSD matrices are PSD.
Their contribution is
    A_child(K+Gc) + A_parent(Gp+4Gc-4*(K+Gc)),
which is the identical polynomial. All scalar weights, targets, shared
coefficient contracts, five slacks and gamma-zero equations stay unchanged.
This proof holds before sampling, with the actual source polynomials at each
scale. Conversely, every feasible candidate with Gc=0 is already a member
of the original cone. Thus restricting Gc to zero AFTER applying this
canonical lift loses no feasible polynomial identities.

For every signed relation coefficient beta, the original nonnegative split
can be recovered exactly as beta_plus=max(beta,0), beta_minus=max(-beta,0).
Conversely, beta=x_plus-x_minus for any original nonnegative split. Hence
613 split pairs can be replaced by613 unrestricted rational coordinates
without losing any effective relation polynomial.

The resulting variable count is

Existing projected coordinates and their CORRECT cone

Let C=Gc+K and P=Gp-4K. These are exactly the effective coefficients against
the retained child and parent columns in the existing equation projection.
The projected PSD cone is
    C PSD, H=P+4C PSD.
It is NOT C PSD and P PSD. The canonical lift is
    K=C, Gp=H=P+4C, Gc=0.
Necessity follows because C=Gc+K PSD and H=Gp+4Gc PSD for every original
feasible triple. Sufficiency follows from the displayed lift. The map between
(C,P) and independent PSD coordinates (K,H) is invertible, rational, and
entrywise triangular with determinant1. No rank decision or matrix direction
is discarded. The old projection receipt licensed equation-rank computation
only; a NEW cone-quotient receipt/check is needed before using this cone in
optimization or certificate reconstruction.

The corresponding dual simplification is also exact. Original region moments
satisfy Hc PSD, Hp PSD, Hk=Hc-4Hp PSD. The child PSD requirement follows from
Hc=Hk+4Hp and is therefore redundant when Hp and Hk are PSD. This is a
structural implication, not evidence that any moment must vanish.

Objective, boundary and all-scale qualifications

The original trace objective is tr(Gc)+tr(Gp)+tr(K). The canonical trace
objective is tr(Gp+4Gc)+tr(K+Gc), which exceeds it by4*tr(Gc).
Thus objective values and optimizers are not preserved. Replacing relation
splits by free beta preserves their MINIMUM split L1 cost |beta|, but an
absolute-value objective needs an explicit convex representation or a
separately declared new objective. A feasibility objective can avoid keeping
redundant split variables merely to reproduce an L1 objective.

The canonical anchored initial cost increases by v^T*Gc*v >= 0. It is finite
for fixed rational matrices, and R'=e_prefix*e_prefix^T+K+Gc remains coercive
on the prefix. This is acceptable for a fixed-template asymptotic argument
which requires a finite initial constant and retains the complete identity.
It is not lossless for a prescribed upper bound on initial cost, a fixed
boundary matrix, an objective including that cost, or an argument requiring
identical quantitative constants. Such extra restrictions need explicit
checking. The current finite admission checker verifies finite initial cost,
not an upper cap that would invalidate this lift.

The full formal scalar ledger is preserved:
    2*z^T Gc z + z^T Gp z -2*z^T K z
    = z^T(Gp+4Gc)z -2*z^T(K+Gc)z.
The unchanged gamma-zero coefficients also remain unchanged. This quotient
cannot convert a finite witness at X=1,2,3,5 into a proof for every X, establish
the all-scale scalar growth premises, or prove RH. One must still establish
the unchanged complete all-scale identity and its required bound. In
particular, a fixed uniform recurrence depending on S_R must be reevaluated
with the new R if it relied on the previous boundary value.
