COMPLETE PRIME-LOGARITHMIC PAIR FORCING: IDENTITIES AND PREFIX COSTS

This is a supplied proof family for complete actual mu and c=mu*lambda. It
proves exact identities and a primitive-prefix component envelope. It proves
NO complete all-scale signed bound, prime correlation estimate, or RH result.
Finite intervals and tests check computations, not the missing uniform premise.

1. Actual local forcing and every endpoint.
For mu, prime local series is 1-z; for c it is (1-z)/(1+z), so
mu(p)=-1, mu(p^e)=0(e>=2), c(p^e)=2(-1)^e. Differentiate each local
series formally: mu(n)log n=-(mu*Lambda)(n), where Lambda(p^e)=log p
for ALL positive e; and c(n)log n=-2(c*Lambda_odd)(n), where
Lambda_odd(p^e)=log p for ODD positive e, zero otherwise. These formal
identities are coefficientwise finite, not dependent on analytic convergence.
Let f denote mu or c, kappa respectively1 or2, and L_f the corresponding
prime-power log sequence. Thus f log=-kappa f*L_f exactly, including n=1.

K_X(t)=max(0,2X-max(X,t)) counts integers n in [X,2X) with n>=t.
J_X=K_2X-4K_X. For N=4X-1, complete step prefix energy has
Delta=sum_(i,j<=N) f(i)f(j)J_X(max(i,j)). The zero-weight endpoint4X
may be included but is not enumerated. The pair(1,1) contributes J_X(1),
since f(1)=1. For every other pair log(ij)>0. Apply logarithmic forcing
to each coordinate, divide by log(ij), and substitute i=ka or j=kb.
Symmetry of the complete pair kernel gives
 S_X=sum_(ka,b<=N,k>=2) L_f(k) f(a)f(b) J_X(max(ka,b))/log(kab),
 Delta=J_X(1)-2*kappa*S_X.
ALL permitted prime powers k, ALL a,b and all actual zeros are retained.
This is ordered-pair forcing: no symmetry factor is inserted into S itself.
At X=1, J1=-2, and the identity still holds. Positive Lambda weights do
not make S positive: J changes sign and f(a)f(b) changes sign.

2. Exact primitive partition, unchanged thresholds.
For every triple write a=g*u,b=g*v, gcd(u,v)=1. Define its core height

3. Uniform primitive-prefix envelopes.
Since k=p^e>=p and a,b>=1, 0<=log p/log(kab)<=1. Also |J_X|<=2X.
For fixed coprime u,v, g>=1 and kgu<=N. The number of permitted prime
powers k for that g is at most N/(gu), even for the restricted odd family.
Actual mu gives |mu(gu)mu(gv)|<=1. Actual c gives
 |c(gu)c(gv)|<=|c(u)c(v)|4^omega(g)<=|c(u)c(v)|d_4(g).
The first inequality remains true when g shares primes with uv: the union
of prime supports has no larger size than their sum. For T>=1,
 sum_(g<=T)1/g<=1+log T,

by the complete four-divisor expansion and enlarging its product domain.
Therefore EVERY X>=1 and fixed positive coprime u,v satisfy
 |S_(u,v)|<=8 X^2/u*(1+log(4X))                         [mu],
 |S_(u,v)|<=8 |c(u)c(v)| X^2/u*(1+log(4X))^4           [c].
If their domain is empty, the inequality is automatic. No kappa is charged
inside these S bounds. Sum over all u,v<=B, enlarging away coprimality:
 |S_prefix|<=8 X^2 B(1+log B)(1+log(4X))                 [mu],

prefix is exactly zero; no expression log0 is evaluated.
