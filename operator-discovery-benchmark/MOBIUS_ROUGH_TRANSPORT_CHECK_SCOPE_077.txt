verify_transport(intervals,payload) takes exact rough-prime-transport-v1 keys
schema,lower_cutoff,upper_cutoff,excluded_primes,children. It independently sieves
all primes through min(upper,H), compares the COMPLETE ascending nonexcluded
roster, and verifies c(p*m) via floor-scaled coefficient interval FUNCTIONS.
Normalization/splitting/merging of equal adjacent pieces is permitted. The exact
identity is T(c,y1)-T(c,y2)=-sum_(y1<p<=y2,p not excluded) T(c(p*),p). Child p
remains the child's strict least-prime cutoff. The unit survives every rough
state and cancels in the difference. No parent/child mu source is queried.

verify_terminal(intervals,payload) takes exact rough-factor-terminal-v1 keys
schema,cutoff,degree,excluded_primes,layer_polynomials,layer_counts,signed_polynomial.
It requires degree0..4 and exact cutoff^(degree+1)>=H, then independently
sieves the prime roster and enumerates DISTINCT increasing-prime products<=H,
excluding P and requiring each prime>cutoff. Repeated factors/p-squares do not
enter. Layer counts include ALL such products regardless whether c(product)=0;
layer0 is the unit and count1 whenever H>=1, even when c(1)=0. Complete formal
polynomial coefficients are summed by each factor degree, and ALL graded signs
are retained in signed_polynomial=sum_r(-1)^r layer[r]. This equals T(c,y).
No cross-term removal or semiprime sign approximation is licensed.
