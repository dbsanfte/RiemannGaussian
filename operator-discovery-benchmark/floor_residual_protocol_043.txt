043 FROZEN ARITHMETIC FLOOR-FAMILY / REFINEMENT DISCOVERY

Purpose
Search fixed arithmetic formulas across increasing support, together with a
bound-producing conditional refinement rule. The basis is globally sensitive to
ordinary zeta zeros via its Mellin transform. This is a known representation
framework, not a claimed new criterion or uniform bound. The original F and
centered-Mobius041 estimates remain OPEN, separate inputs.

Representation
f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(n*x)),0<x<=1, zero beyond1.
g_N=sum c_n(N)f_n; e_N=1-g_N. Target families have no indicator steps.
For q2/3,b in{-1,-1/2,0,1/2,1}, h_N=e_(qN)-bD_q(e_N).
D_q f_n=f_(qn)-f_q/n. Compile h_N with its full signed cross and exact step b.
|b|/sqrt(q)<1. Proved h_N->0 along a geometric chain would imply e_N->0.
The primary bound language is ||h_N||²<=A/L(N)^eta, L(N)=N.bit_length().
eta1/5,1/3,1/2,2/3,1; A1/16,1/4,1,4. All constants/formulas are fixed before
observations. The compiler evaluates each final RHS through exact integer root
brackets at128dyadic bits. Along N=N0*q^j,L(N)>=1+j, so the conditional Young
recurrence with rho<1 has decaying polynomial-in-j forcing and gives e_N->0.
These logarithmic-bound leaves remain OPEN pending independent all-scale proof.
Uniform relative forcing ||h_N||²<=tau||e_N||² with fixed contraction would
imply polynomial decay in N, which is impossible in this representation.
The tau1/64,1/16,1/4,1/2 probes remain negative calibrations, not viable goals.
b0 is an endpoint restatement, retained as a declared control.

Imported rate obstruction, added before ANY candidate performance observations
Burnol, arXiv math/0103058, Theorem1.3, gives a positive liminf for D(1/N)²logN.
f_n=rho(1/(n*x))-rho(1/x)/n belongs to the allowed span with theta>=1/N.
Its zero extension is exactly this difference on(0,infinity). Thus any target
with support<=N has error at least D(1/N). A fixed energy contraction along
geometrically growing support, or a logarithmic exponent eta>1, contradicts
this theorem. No bound constant from that asymptotic is used at finite N.
This imported theorem is not a detector discovery or a refutation of RH.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0103058

Candidate language
434 fixed recipes,4340 q/b refinements. Arithmetic atoms: actual mu,Liouville,
mu*mu,(-1)^omega,constant1,mu+Liouville/2,mu-Liouville/2, all on ordinary integers.
Untapered c_n=-a(n), or c_n=-a(n)*n^-beta*(1-(n/N)^alpha)^gamma with exact dyadic
rounding at EACH power. alpha1/5,2/7,1/2,1,2;gamma1/3,1,5/4;beta0,1/7.
Each recipe optionally applies the exact cell1 correction
c_2 +=2*(1-sum c_n/n). This is a defined formula, not a fitted coefficient.
The API accepts arbitrary rational positive exponents under resource limits;
the finite pilot bank is not a permanent root-denominator whitelist.
Root rounding is exactly floor(2^p*x^(u/v))/2^p, p=4*ceil(log2N), checked by
integer powers, never float approximations. Rounded families are the exact
objects tested; no convergence claim for an ideal unrounded family is imported.
No per-N free optimization, coefficient fitting, seed, expected winning formula,
model call, literature call, or source-label concealment is used in selection.

Whole-domain certificates
Each program's global norm is an exact-integer-residue Arb192 head sum plus the
analytic entire tail, H1048576. An independent heap event replay at Arb256 checks
the full head,exact lattice,tail,dyadic bounds,and theorem-scope metadata.
Two processes certify distinct complete programs; no signed square is split.
Duplicate programs share the same certificate through exact tuple equality.
No precautionary hashes. Source byte snapshots freeze the run.
Producer per-program work cap500m;checker750m;1200s producer timeout;memory16GiB
per process plus an aggregate conservative two-worker preflight below15GiB.
The compiler uses a2b work cap,1200seconds,400000-bit prospective power guard.
Fixed finite stencil forcing is obstructed by an independent three-cell positive
norm lower bound; this does not obstruct the growing-support recipes universally.

Admission and feedback
An interval certifies one finite program over ALLx, not all N. A small norm,
downward trend or finite relative-forcing success cannot become a convergence
theorem. Finite signed cancellation is reported separately from contraction.
Final counterexamples, unresolved intervals and OPEN forcing-growth leaves become
the next exact search input, with recipe,parameters,quantifiers and circularity
guard. Existing041/042/F obligations are retained, not overwritten or rerun.
For each successful finite logarithmic bound, also replace its signed cross by
its absolute value and by zero. Record whether cancellation is needed for that
specific bound, or whether the bound survives the replacement. This diagnostic
does not change selection or its cost after final observations.

Development
Only synthetic exact programs and elementary individual arithmetic values are
used by focused tests. Receipts record each invocation; candidate performance is
first observed after protocol/source snapshot. No unchanged old suites rerun.
