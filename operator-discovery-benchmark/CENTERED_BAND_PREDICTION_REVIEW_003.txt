CENTERED BAND FILTERS, HYPERBOLA PREDICTION AND THE UNPAID COVARIANCE

Scope
-----
This is an independent analytical review of the centered-product pilot-002
operator C(z)=(1+z)^2 and of constructive next auxiliary languages. Only
this new file is written. There are no tests, arithmetic source sweeps,
parameter searches, hashes or changes to frozen files. The finite observations
quoted below are already supplied by the root agent; they are not recomputed.
No asymptotic inference is made from their slopes. No RH-strength companion
bound or prime independence is assumed.

The conclusions are specific. The selected filter has no newly established
arithmetic cancellation mechanism. All admitted band filters and all three
relative fractional weights belong to the same cofinal auxiliary class.
Nevertheless, a useful next reduction is available: the ENTIRE full-centered
low-product region hk<=X is paid absolutely with an explicit logarithmic
bound. An exact hyperbola identity then exposes the remaining high-product
region as a current-scale high/high square and a signed complementary-
quotient covariance, with all units and moving endpoints retained. This
identity supplies a concrete correlation language, not a proved bound for
that correlation. A positive smaller-scale prediction theorem and a bounded
main/floor remainder are proved below. They also distinguish useful recovery
from an unproved predictor premise or an unstable pole-removal trick.
The final primary frontier is the INTEGRATED high-product off-diagonal sum
in section 3a. It preserves cancellation across all bands and needs only
one one-sided signed bound. Partial-band maxima remain optional stronger
diagnostics; they are not imposed on the new primary argument.

1. The actual complete center and source envelope
------------------------------------------------
Use u=1/3, v=2/3, beta=9/2,
  U=mu*n^(-u), V=mu*n^(-v), B=U*V,
  p=beta*(U-delta)*(V-delta), a=p-d,
where * is Dirichlet convolution and d is the COMPLETE same-prime part.
Thus a(1)=a(q^e)=0, and a(n)=p(n) at every other n. All repeated prime
valuations and gcd overlaps remain in these coefficients.

The running pilot uses the LEADING center
  M(n)=m_u*n^(2/3), M(0)=0,
  m_u=(3/2)*beta*[zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2-1/zeta(2/3)],
not a fitted constant. Put
  m(n)=M(n)-M(n-1), b(n)=a(n)-m(n),
  R(n)=sum_(h<=n)b(h)=A(n)-M(n), R(0)=0.             (1.1)
In particular b(1)=-m_u. The source producer's main array and the pilot
protocol agree with this definition. No lower pole is silently subtracted.
Adding the known n^(1/3) profile would change the finite b coefficients;
its separate dyadic cost is O(X^(-1/3)), allowing a paid norm comparison,
not silent reuse of the current finite responses.

For every n, |U(n)|,|V(n)|<=1: their prime-power coefficients are the
negative geometric differences p^(-e*u)-p^(-(e-1)*u) and the v analogue,
and their multiplicative products retain that bound. Consequently
  |p(n)|<=beta*d_2(n), |a(n)|<=beta*d_2(n).
For p this follows by counting its proper two-factor divisor allocations;
for a, the prime-power coefficients are removed exactly, rather than
bounded by adding an extra absolute d term. Concavity gives
  |m(n)|<=|m_u|, |b(n)|<=C_b*d_2(n), C_b=beta+|m_u|.          (1.2)
The unit is included in (1.2). If both fixed pole profiles are explicitly
used instead, the same proof replaces C_b by beta+|m_u|+|m_v|.

With N=2X-1 and integer dyadic X>=1, the complete moment is
  E_R(X)=X^(-2)*sum_(X<=n<2X)R(n)^2
        =X^(-2)*sum_(h,k<=N)b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k),
  W_X(h,k)=max(0,2X-max(X,h,k)).                      (1.3)
It contains AA, both main crosses and MM, each on both squarefree and
nonsquarefree PRODUCT. Only the whole moment is positive. Centering is
performed BEFORE any product masks or fractional weights.

2. What (1+z)^2 does, and what it does not establish
---------------------------------------------------
For J=ceil(log2 N^2), the existing exact band states are
  D_(j,gamma)(t)=X^(-2)*sum_(h,k<=N,2^(j-1)<hk<=t*2^j)
                         b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k)*(hk/2^j)^(-gamma),
  1/2<=t<=1, j=0,...,J, D_j=0 for j<0.               (2.1)
The selected output is
  E_j(t)=D_j(t)+2D_(j-1)(t)+D_(j-2)(t).             (2.2)
All shifted terms use the SAME relative t. Its causal inverse is exactly
  g_l=(-1)^l*(l+1).
The repeated characteristic root is -1. Thus an alternating affine band
sequence (-1)^j*(a+b*j) is annihilated in the interior. The retained head
prevents a global nullspace, but reconstructing such a mode amplifies
head/interior forcing by a polynomial in J. This algebra is not an identity
for actual Mobius or for actual arithmetic/center sector cancellation.

The complete sufficient recovery bound is
  E_R(X)<=K_gamma*binom(J+3,3)*U_C,gamma(X),
  K_gamma=2-2^(-gamma),
  U_C,gamma=max_j sup_t |E_j(t)|.                   (2.3)
For the identity filter the corresponding bound is
  E_R(X)<=K_gamma*(J+1)*U_I,gamma(X).
The exact ratio of these inverse-cost envelopes, before comparing their
different U values, is (J+2)*(J+3)/6. A smaller finite slope of U_C alone
does not remove this larger recovery cost.

All admitted fixed filters are equivalent, up to powers of J, to the same
uniform signed partial-band auxiliary. The chosen filter has coefficient
l1 equal to four, so U_C,gamma<=4 U_I,gamma, while its inverse gives
U_I,gamma<=binom(J+2,2)U_C,gamma. These are operator statements, with no
source-specific improvement in the exponent.

There is also exact constant-equivalence across relative weights, for
EVERY fixed causal band filter C. Signed partial-band Abel summation gives
  D_gamma(t)=t^(-gamma)D_0(t)
                +gamma*int_(1/2)^t rho^(-gamma-1)D_0(rho)d rho,
  D_0(t)=t^gamma D_gamma(t)
                -gamma*int_(1/2)^t rho^(gamma-1)D_gamma(rho)d rho.
There is no atom at the strict lower boundary. These transforms are the
same on every j and commute with C. Therefore, for gamma>=0,
  U_C,gamma<=2^gamma U_C,0,
  U_C,0<=(2-2^(-gamma))U_C,gamma.                   (2.4)
In particular gamma=0,1/6,1/3 cannot produce distinct asymptotic auxiliary
classes in this protocol. No global product-power gain survives relative-
band normalization. A new arithmetic proof might use one presentation
more conveniently; numerical weighting itself proves no new cancellation.

The already supplied pilot summary is consistent with this warning. At
gamma=0, the identity U decreases from about 15.398 to 8.718 between X=64
and X=1024, while the selected U decreases from about 60.795 to 26.860.
The sufficient reconstructed bounds are about 231 to 200.5 for the identity
and 41,340 to 61,778 for the selected filter; actual full moments are about
118.24 to 67.19. Those are finite observations, not growth theorems. The
supplied witness audit reports same-sign inter-band contributions at all
15 selected witnesses. Thus even the displayed finite improvements do not
exhibit cancellation between the three selected band weights. The signed
cancellation at these witnesses occurs across AA/main-cross/MM and SF/NS
sectors. Those joint sectors must remain in the next mathematical operator.

3. A new paid region: full centered products below X
----------------------------------------------------
Define the complete normalized product prefix
  F_X(T)=X^(-2)*sum_(h,k<=N,hk<=T)b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k),
with F_X(T)=0 for T<1. No positivity is assumed for F_X.
The divisor identity d_2*d_2=d_4 and elementary harmonic summation give
  sum_(m<=T)d_4(m)<=T*(1+log T)^3, T>=1.            (3.1)
Indeed enumerate four ordered positive factors, eliminate the fourth by
floor(T/(abc))<=T/(abc), then enlarge each of the other harmonic sums to
its unrestricted range <=T. This needs no prime distribution theorem.

Since 0<=W_X<=X, (1.2) and (3.1) prove uniformly over every real T>=1
  |F_X(T)|<=C_b^2*(T/X)*(1+log T)^3.               (3.2)
In particular the ENTIRE complete centered region hk<=X is paid by
  |F_X(X)|<=C_b^2*(1+log X)^3.                     (3.3)
This includes its large individual center sectors through the joint b
envelope, and every unit/repeated valuation/gcd overlap. There is no
companion moment premise and no cancellation assumption. The same estimate
holds for every partial product prefix below X.

For a band with 2^j<=X, its relative weight is at most 2^gamma. Thus
  sup_t|D_(j,gamma)(t)|
      <=2^gamma*C_b^2*(2^j/X)*(1+log(2^j))^3.      (3.4)
Summing all such bands gives an absolute upper bound
  2^(gamma+1)*C_b^2*(1+log X)^3.
If C has fixed coefficient l1=L_C, its outputs entirely below that
frontier are bounded by the corresponding L_C multiple. The j=0 unit is
covered. Boundary terms from a finite causal stencil crossing the frontier
remain explicitly paid, rather than discarded as an unknown head.

Thus one can reduce the full moment to
  E_R(X)=F_X(X)+H_X,
  H_X=X^(-2)*sum_(h,k<=N,hk>X)b(h)b(k)W_X(h,k),
with the first term paid independently. H_X is signed and includes low/high
as well as high/high pairs; it is NOT just the sector h,k>sqrt(X).
A bound on H_X itself is now cofinal-equivalent to the original full moment
up to the paid term. Simply renaming H_X an auxiliary would not be progress.
The useful next construction must expose and estimate its actual covariance.

Fixed cofactor heads are also paid. The union h<=H or k<=H has absolute
normalized cost at most
  4*C_b^2*H*(1+log H)*(1+log(2X)),                  (3.5)
for fixed integer H>=1, by sum_(k<=N)|b(k)|<=C_b*N*(1+log N).
This includes the nonzero centered unit. If H grows as a power of X, (3.5)
has an unpaid power: a fixed-head theorem cannot silently cover a growing
cofactor bank.

3a. Primary integrated off-diagonal frontier, without a J cost
-------------------------------------------------------------
The subsequent root/backend proposal admits a useful exact simplification.
Set kappa=2/3 and R_u=kappa*m_u. Here m_u>0 since zeta(4/3)>0 and
zeta(2/3)<0, so R_u>0. Define the SMOOTH coefficient center
  tilde_b(n)=a(n)-R_u*n^(-1/3),
  e(n)=m_u*[n^kappa-(n-1)^kappa]-R_u*n^(kappa-1).
Concavity, or integrating the decreasing derivative on [n-1,n], gives
  e(n)>=0, b(n)=tilde_b(n)-e(n).                   (3a.1)
For n>=2, another integration of that derivative gives
  e(n)<=m_u*kappa*(1-kappa)*(n-1)^(kappa-2)/2.
The unit e(1)=m_u-R_u is retained. Therefore e is summable. Its exact mass
is
  E0=sum_n e(n)=-R_u*zeta(1/3)>0.                 (3a.2)
To see the constant without an unproved asymptotic premise, summability
first proves convergence of H_u(N)-N^(1-u)/(1-u), with u=1/3. If its limit
is L, the finite identity for alternating sums
  eta_u(2N)=H_u(2N)-2^(1-u)*H_u(N)
and the convergent alternating series imply L=eta(u)/(1-2^(1-u))=zeta(u).
Telescoping (3a.1) then proves (3a.2). No source-sign cancellation is used.

Put tilde_R(n)=sum_(h<=n)tilde_b(h). The actual centered prefix satisfies
  R(n)=tilde_R(n)-E(n), 0<=E(n)=sum_(h<=n)e(h)<=E0.
With K=beta+m_u, |tilde_b(n)|<=K*d_2(n), since R_u<=m_u.
Consequently
  |E_R(X)-E_tilde_R(X)|
        <=4*E0*K*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X.             (3a.3)
Indeed |tilde_R(n)|<=K*n*(1+log n); expand the complete square and sum
the linear and constant error terms. This is a PAID norm correction, not
permission to replace already computed finite b responses by tilde_b data.

Now define precisely
  O_X=(2/X^2)*sum_(n=X)^N
             sum_(h<k<=n,hk>X)tilde_b(h)tilde_b(k).           (3a.4)
This is a single signed full-union sum; no band is independently absolutized.
Its complement in E_tilde_R consists of all low products hk<=X and the
high-product diagonal h=k. Low products cost at most K^2*(1+log N)^3
by (3.2). For the entire diagonal,
  d_2(n)^2<=d_4(n),
since at p^e, (e+1)^2<=binom(e+3,3), and both are multiplicative. Thus
  X^(-2)*sum_(h<=N)tilde_b(h)^2 W_X(h,h)
        <=2*K^2*(1+log N)^3.                     (3a.5)
Using the full diagonal overbounds the high diagonal harmlessly; low
diagonals are NOT subtracted twice in the exact partition.

Combining the exact partition and (3a.3)-(3a.5) proves
  |E_R(X)-O_X|<=B_X,
  B_X=3*K^2*(1+log N)^3
             +4*E0*K*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X.         (3a.6)
All constants and cutoffs agree with the proposed primary frontier. X=1,
N=1 is included; its O_X is empty. Every cofactor, prime-power allocation,
gcd overlap and both centered source legs are retained.

This yields a useful positive recovery fact: because E_R>=0,
  O_X>=-B_X,
unconditionally. Only the ONE-SIDED remaining lemma
  O_X<=C_epsilon*X^epsilon for every epsilon>0     (3a.7)
is needed. It recovers E_R with constant one and a paid logarithmic
remainder, with no J or inverse-stencil factor. Conversely a full moment
bound gives (3a.7) by (3a.6); this is an equivalent residual frontier,
NOT an independently proved signed gain. Its advantage is that it retains
all cross-band cancellation while removing provably paid low/diagonal/
center-rounding pieces. Uniform bounds on every high band or on its every
partial cutoff are stronger optional lemmas, not requirements of (3a.7).

4. Exact hyperbola prediction and complete cutoff complements
--------------------------------------------------------------
For any n>=1 and real T>=1 set
  Q_n(T)=sum_(h,k<=n,hk<=T)b(h)b(k),
  c=min(n,floor(sqrt(T))), R_n=R(n), R_c=R(c).
Then the complete Dirichlet hyperbola identity is
  Q_n(T)=2*sum_(h<=c)b(h)R(min(n,floor(T/h)))-R_c^2. (4.1)
Every admitted pair has at least one leg <=c. Pairs with both legs <=c
are counted twice and corrected once. The cap n, unit and strict product
exit are part of the identity. It is valid at T>=n^2 with c=n, where it
becomes Q_n(T)=R_n^2, and at T<1 with empty c=0 and Q=0.

Write
  V_(n,c)=R_n-R_c,
  Gamma_n(T)=sum_(h<=c)b(h)
                    [R(min(n,floor(T/h)))-R_c].
Equation (4.1) yields exactly
  Q_n(T)=R_c^2+2 Gamma_n(T),
  R_n^2-Q_n(T)=V_(n,c)^2+2R_c*V_(n,c)-2 Gamma_n(T). (4.2)
The full 2R_c*V term is essential. Equivalently put
  Gamma_hat_n(T)=Gamma_n(T)-R_c*V_(n,c)
      =sum_(h<=c)b(h)[R(min(n,floor(T/h)))-R_n].
Then the complement is V_(n,c)^2-2 Gamma_hat_n(T). Gamma_hat is the
negative of the EXCLUDED low/high covariance, not the inside covariance
Gamma. Interchanging the two drops exactly the required low/high cross.
Consequently
  E_R(X)-F_X(T)=X^(-2)*sum_(X<=n<2X)
                  [V_(n,c)^2+2R_c*V_(n,c)-2 Gamma_n(T)].     (4.3)
The moving c is used separately at every n. This retains the full high/high
complement and the low/high credit. V still contains CURRENT-scale R_n:
neither its square nor Gamma is independently paid by saying c is small.
Dropping the square, or bounding only the negative part of Gamma, loses
the original target. At T=X the subtraction F_X(T) is paid by (3.3), but
the right side of (4.3) remains the unsolved joint energy.

For integer/rational cutoffs, the correlation has an explicit complementary
quotient-interval form. Put
  w_m=R(min(c,floor(T/m)))-R(min(c,floor(T/(m+1)))), c<=m<n,
  w_n=R(min(c,floor(T/n))).
These are the exact sums of b(h) on the fibers
min(n,floor(T/h))=m, h<=c. Thus
  Gamma_n(T)=sum_(m=c+1)^(n-1)w_m*[R(m)-R_c]
                         +w_n*[R_n-R_c].           (4.4)
The final capped fiber is a birth/frontier term involving R_n; it is not
the difference w_n-w_(n+1) and must not be omitted. The m=c fiber vanishes
against its increment. If n=c all increments vanish. Formula (4.4) retains
unit fibers and all integer endpoints, with no assumption about squarefree
products or independent source signs. Finite Abel/summation-by-parts can
rearrange it, but does not pay it without a new signed source estimate.
If Gamma_hat is used instead, subtract the COMPLETE R_c*V. The capped
fiber then cancels against its own piece of that subtraction because its
endpoint equals n; every other fiber retains R(m)-R_n. This is exact
outside-domain accounting, not permission to delete a frontier in Gamma.

For gamma>0, the same hyperbola identity applies to the EXACT coefficients
b(h)h^(-gamma) and their weighted prefixes. One must not substitute an
uncentered multiplicative a channel and then reuse the old center. The
relative-band Abel theorem (2.4) allows the new grammar to use gamma=0 as
its default correlation representation without forfeiting recovery.

5. A positive predictor theorem with a real coefficient gap
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let d>=2 be fixed, alpha real and source-free, and define
  Z(n)=R(n)-alpha R(floor(n/d)), Z(0)=0.            (5.1)
On the actual step extension and finite weighted norm
  ||R||_(sigma,T)^2=int_1^T |R(x)|^2*x^(-2sigma-1)dx,
the dilation has norm <=d^(-sigma). If |alpha|<sqrt(d), then for
sigma>=1/2,
  ||R||_(sigma,T)<=[1-|alpha|/sqrt(d)]^(-1)
                                       *||Z||_(sigma,T).    (5.2)
The inverse is the causal sum of alpha^j R/floor-dilations on successively
smaller arguments, with exact zero below one. This is a positive recovery
theorem and its constant has a genuine gap independent of T.

There is also a completely discrete cumulative proof. Let
  S_R(T)=sum_(n<=T)R(n)^2, S_Z(T)=sum_(n<=T)Z(n)^2.
Each lower argument occurs at most d times, giving for any delta>0
  S_R(T)<=(1+delta)*alpha^2*d*S_R(floor(T/d))
                                 +(1+1/delta)*S_Z(T).       (5.3)
If S_Z(T)<=A*T^(2+epsilon), choose delta with
(1+delta)*alpha^2/d<1. Then induction gives
  S_R(T)<= [(1+1/delta)*A /
                  (1-(1+delta)*alpha^2*d^(-1-epsilon))]
                                                    *T^(2+epsilon). (5.4)
The missing lower indices are zero; T<d is included. The denominator can
be replaced by its smaller epsilon=0 value for a simpler uniform constant.
The dyadic-block/cumulative comparison costs only its usual geometric sum.

Equation (5.2) also implies the reverse norm comparison by the triangle
bound 1+|alpha|/sqrt(d). Therefore a bare cofinal lemma about Z is in the
same fixed-d norm class as R. Declaring it a new source estimate is not
progress. A useful predictor program needs an independently derived
arithmetic decomposition of Z or a signed covariance argument for its
forcing, with full current-scale terms exposed.

For the exact dyadic split d=2 and X>=2, put
  L(n)=R(floor(n/2)), V(n)=R(n)-L(n),
  L_X=X^(-2)*sum_(X<=n<2X)L(n)^2=E_R(X/2)/2,
  V_X=X^(-2)*sum V(n)^2, C_X=X^(-2)*sum L(n)V(n).
Then
  E_R(X)=L_X+V_X+2C_X.                             (5.5)
A concrete sufficient SIGNED forcing lemma is, for fixed -1<=tau<1, delta>=0,
  2C_X<=-(1+delta)V_X+tau L_X+F_X^paid.            (5.6)
It gives a strict recurrence
  E_R(X)<=(1+tau)E_R(X/2)/2+F_X^paid,              (5.7)
with the finite X=1 head explicit. Cofinal F^paid then closes a coupled
induction. Unlike a norm-only proposal, (5.6) identifies the exact negative
low/high credit needed to absorb the fresh square. But no such credit has
been proved for this actual source. Unless its proof uses a new source
identity or independently paid correlation, merely asserting (5.6) is an
unresolved energy-drift restatement. All AA/cross/MM and gcd sectors must
be present when expanding its C_X and V_X.

6. Bounded mean/floor remainder, and the unstable pole predictor
--------------------------------------------------------------
There is a useful fully proved remainder for testing proposed predictors.
For any fixed integer d>=2, let alpha=d^(2/3). The raw increment
  Z_A(n)=A(n)-alpha*A(floor(n/d))
and centered increment Z_R from (5.1) obey
  Z_A(n)-Z_R(n)=m_u*[n^(2/3)-(d*floor(n/d))^(2/3)]. (6.1)
Concavity gives the UNIFORM bound
  |Z_A-Z_R|<=|m_u|*(d-1)^(2/3).                    (6.2)
Its dyadic squared cost is at most |m_u|^2*(d-1)^(4/3)/X. For d=2,
the difference is zero at even n and at most |m_u| at odd n. This is a
real paid floor remainder with the unit included, not an unknown companion.

However the mean-canceling gain d^(2/3) EXCEEDS sqrt(d). At the critical
weighted norm its delayed multiplier is d^(1/6)>1. The associated causal
inverse is not uniformly bounded on the whole critical norm class. Thus
a cheap mean-canceling increment does not by itself recover the target.
For an explicit norm-class witness, F(n)=d^(kappa*floor(log_d n)), kappa=2/3,
has F(n)-d^kappa F(floor(n/d))=0 for n>=d, while its critical finite norm
diverges as the horizon grows. Its forcing is supported only on the
retained finite head. This is a generic recovery witness, not a claim
about actual source values or the admissibility of this F as an a prefix.
Conversely |alpha|<sqrt(d) leaves the nonzero continuous raw main forcing
  m_u*(1-alpha*d^(-2/3))*n^(2/3),
unless the complete center is retained. One cannot simultaneously remove
that raw real pole and invoke the forward critical coefficient gap.

A backward inverse for alpha=d^(2/3) formally uses future scales. On
exact dilation orbits it would require the boundary term
  lim_(j->infinity)alpha^(-j)R(d^j*n)=0.
No such boundary condition has been established here. Subtracting the
known displayed center does not prove it: off-line source singularities
and log-periodic dilation modes remain possible. The Mellin characteristic
zeros occur on Re(s)=2/3 with imaginary periods 2pi/log d; removing a
single real main term is not removal of that entire characteristic line.
Any analytic argument restoring a future inverse must declare and prove
its boundary/pole-mode hypothesis and all associated source remainders.

The full fixed-prime U,V,B transfer from the earlier review supplies
bounded rational local operators and paid floor forcing, but its current-
scale q-free input remains. It cannot be inserted as an independently
bounded predictor in (5.1). Actual source laws, rather than the coefficient
gap alone, must pay the new forcing.

7. A small next grammar with an honest proof gate
------------------------------------------------
The constructive next language should manipulate the full centered b and
the actual prefix R, or the PAID tilde_b replacement in section 3a, not
choose another C or tune gamma. The primary objective is the joint O_X,
preserving cancellation across all product bands. A minimal set of
exact primitives is:

  * Complete product-prefix and complementary-product masks, with the
    low-product certificate (3.2) as a paid leaf, and the complete
    low/diagonal/center certificate (3a.6) for the primary O_X.
  * Hyperbola split (4.1), quotient-interval grouping (4.4), and exact
    subtraction of two cutoffs. These generate joint signed low/high
    correlations and retain the high/high complement.
  * A fixed rational-coefficient lower-prefix predictor with its declared
    gap and positive recovery certificate (5.2)/(5.3), or a coupled matrix
    version only when its actual weighted norm is proved below one.
  * Exact original-source prime/divisor transfer expansions of the
    correlation coefficients. Every prime-power allocation, q-free
    restriction, unit, capped frontier and center profile is an obligation,
    not a removable annotation.
  * Joint square completion, retaining both modes and cross terms. A
    canceled projection cannot replace the full reconstruction identity.

For example, a generated program can split a high product band at a
declared T, group its low/high term by the explicit w_m in (4.4), then
apply actual prime-power row identities to those SHORT COFACTOR INTERVAL
increments. Its next unresolved bound is precisely the retained weighted
correlation with R(m)-R(c), including the capped R_n frontier. This gives
the search an arithmetic correlation whose support and complementary costs
are known. Whether the expansion yields a paid forcing or smaller-scale
closure is an analytical question, not guaranteed by the syntax.

The proof gate must distinguish:
  (i) exact identity and full-target reconstruction;
  (ii) independently paid components such as (3.3), fixed heads and (6.2);
  (iii) an actual coefficient gap in an induction;
  (iv) the remaining signed source lemma and its dependencies.
A program containing its own target under a new name, a q-free companion
of equivalent norm, or a future boundary assumption without proof has not
closed (iv). The stable-filter and relative-weight aliases in section 2
should be recognized before allocating another search to their constants.

Preserving the full signed cross is essential. Absolute separation of AA,
main-cross and MM can retain supercritical deterministic components even
when the complete centered expression cancels them. Conversely evidence
that those sectors cancel at saved witnesses supplies no all-scale signed
credit. A rigorous new argument must estimate their complete joint kernel
or a source-identity forcing with all complements paid.

Final proof boundary
--------------------
The new unconditional results are the complete centered low-product bound,
the constant-one integrated off-diagonal recovery with explicit paid
low/diagonal/center remainder and a one-sided frontier, the exact full-cross
hyperbola/capped-quotient covariance identities, the positive
prediction recovery theorem with constants, and the bounded pole/floor
remainder together with its critical-recovery obstruction. They identify
a narrower source-correlation frontier and a plausible operator-plus-
argument language. No bound for that frontier, strict signed credit (5.6),
new all-scale moment result or RH theorem is claimed. No finite slope or
selected numerical witness is used as an asymptotic premise.
