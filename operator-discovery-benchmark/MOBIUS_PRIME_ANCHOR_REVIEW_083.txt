3. Exact known divisor functional, with no unknown signs

For integer0<=K<=N define

 a(K)=0 forK0,
 a(K)=K-1-sum_(p<=K,prime)floor(K/p) forK>=1.

This equals sum_(k inknown)b_k C_K(k), including the unit contribution.
The prime floor sum can be computed either directly or as
sum_(l>=1)pi(floor(K/l)); both are exact finite prime geometry.
ForN>=2 the known functional of a unit-balanced row is

 h_N(K)=a(K)-max(K-1,0)/(N-1)*a(N).

ForN<=1 allD rows vanish. Exact h values may be zero; do not divide without
a proof/admission. At early horizons with no squarefree composites, the
entire known functional must be zero, because known mu is already the full
mu vector. This provides a useful independent integration test.

Suppose a coupled identity has geometry weight supported ONLY on composites.
Its known-known effective block is zero and its anchor-composite entry atj

Then that anchor-linear coefficient is canceled exactly by a valid zero
