Universal identity backend for primitive/full15 grammar: design v1
2026-10-09. New analysis only; existing/frozen/published sources unchanged.
No research solver, source instance rebuild, heavy elimination or archive run.

Conclusion and smallest implementable fragment

Add a bounded, independently replayed proof DAG for PARAMETRIC guarded finite
sums, with exact commutative-ring rules and complementary-domain partitions.
Its first useful fragment can universally prove complete inside+outside and
rowmass all=head+tail identities, plus weighted inverse-row/square closures.
It can reject malformed source transformations and exactly refute a false
universal identity with a legal finite counterexample. It cannot infer a
universal identity from a matching grid or prove the whole full15 target by
sampling. A certificate for the remaining quartic coefficient cancellations
would still have to be supplied; the proposed fragment is a real proof layer,
not a claim that such a full certificate is currently known.

Existing pieces and what is new

cancellation_arithmetic_language.py:247 compile_expression expands at a GIVEN
integerX. Its partition(:255) inserts predicate/not-predicate under leading
sums; polarization(:271) generates an exact ring identity. It has no universal
proof DAG connecting generated source expressions to the target at everyX.
autonomous_grammar.py:285 polynomial_certificate proves its restricted closed
commutative polynomial sort, explicitly leaving unsupported sums/maps unknown.
Reuse its mathematical ring principle, not its unsupported scope.
cancellation_factor_blocks.py:79-103 enumerates pairs and verifies finite
inside/complement and coprime rewrites; check_cancellation_factor_blocks.py:57
and :85 independently reconstruct those finite instances.
cancellation_divisor_coupling.py:43-68 and its independent checker verify the
weighted inverse-row and difference-of-squares closure at supplied finiteH.
A parametric proof kernel can certify the SAME algebra at arbitraryH and weights,
without duplicating finite arithmetic generation or inventing an estimate.

Three judgments must remain distinct

1. FORMAL_EQ: forall declared integer parameters and forall formal coefficient
   variables x_n, P=Q as polynomials. This is the judgment required to establish
   universal nonconstant coefficient matching for the existing certificate cone.
2. MU_EQ: P(mu)=Q(mu) for actual mu=Dirichlet inverse ofOne, from valid arithmetic
   premises. A primitive rho(mu)=0 is a MU_EQ fact, not FORMAL_EQ(rho,0).
3. INEQUALITY: arithmetic equality plus exact nonnegative square/PSD/coercivity
   and a separate scalar bound yields an all-scale operator inequality.

There is NO rule converting MU_EQ into FORMAL_EQ. For example x1-1 vanishes
at actualmu but is a nonzero formal polynomial. An arithmetic proof can support
a separately typed new inequality backend, but cannot masquerade as admission
of the current formal coefficient identity. The new scalar-readiness gate in
the preceding note likewise is not the missing FORMAL_EQ certificate.

Proposed certificate envelope

Version: primitive-state-parametric-sum-proof-v1.
Context: X is an integer>=1; optionally dyadicX=2^m,m integer>=0. All bound
index variables are integers with explicit finite inclusive intervals. State
formal indeterminates x_n, 1<=n<=4X-1. Explicit coefficients are canonical
bounded rationals. Candidate weights/matrices and every descriptor/source
recipe have IDs and exact values/hashes; no coefficient may depend onX unless
that dependence is already an internal source weight in the frozen grammar.

Envelope fields: version, context, grammar_definition_hashes, candidate_hash,
source_recipe_ids, exact_coefficient_table, theorem_judgment, lhs_root_id,
rhs_root_id, AST_DAG, proof_DAG, conclusion_id, limits, charged_counts.
Each proof node specifies rule ID/version, earlier premise IDs, operand IDs,
explicit substitutions/side-condition certificate IDs, and output IDs.
No executable callbacks, unconstrained lemma text, user-supplied rewrite code,
implicit coordinates, or producer-computed normal form is trusted. The checker
rebuilds each conclusion from its premises under a small whitelisted rulebook.
A cyclic DAG, missing source term, wrong scope or unsupported rule rejects
that certificate; it does not establish mathematical inequality falsity.

Universal source binding is stronger than a source hash. A separately checked
adapter must produce a symbolic recipe for EACH of the366 square and613 relation
groups, both full15 regional sources, storage child-4parent, and the full target.
It must compare descriptor IDs/order, atom formulas, index endpoints, masks,
factors, cut/root conventions and complements against the existing definitions.
Hashing an arbitrary truncated AST is not a semantic binding to those sources.
The first fragment need not adapt every group: partial proof status must list
its exact covered recipes and never claim the whole target was proved.

Typed symbolic syntax

Index terms: integer literals/variables, bounded integer addition/subtraction/
product, gcd, exact division under a divisibility side condition, min/max,
integer floor/ceiling root nodes with rational parameter records.
Predicates: equality/order/divides/coprime/prime/squarefree, and Boolean
and/or/not. Arithmetic predicates are opaque exact Boolean functions unless a
specific admitted rule proves a relation between them. No density approximation
or sampled truth value is used in a symbolic guard.
Scalar coefficients: rationals and specified index-dependent internal weights;
opaque exact arithmetic constants sf(n)=1_(n squarefree), prime-power local values, indicator
functions, and weight kernels retain their definitions. Formal polynomials:
x(index), constant, addition, product, rational scaling, square, guard, finite
sum, and named primitive-polynomial definitions. Bound variables use canonical
IDs or deBruijn indices to prevent capture. Free-variable sets, finiteness,
index horizon and formal degree annotations are checked independently.

A sum normal form retains a COMMON domain/predicate and an exact polynomial
body. It may normalize ring expressions locally or combine identical guarded
summands, but does not enumerate a parameter-sized interval. It does not equate
two different arithmetic guards merely because sampled values agree. Opaque
sums can be treated as ring atoms only after their definitions are bound; this
proves algebraic combinations of sums without asserting identities between
unrelated sum atoms.

Minimal rulebook with independently checked side conditions

ALPHA: rename a bound variable capture-freely.
RING: exact polynomial normalization in the local commuting expression atoms;
      validates distributivity, associativity, square expansion and factorization.
SUM_LINEAR: split/add/scaling inside sums; pull a factor outside only if it
      is independent of that bound variable. No hidden dependence through a cut.
GUARD_BOOL: complement, conjunction and identical-predicate Boolean identities.
PARTITION: Sum_D F = Sum_D [phi]F + Sum_D [not phi]F, sameD/F/phi and multiplicity.
      Works even when phi is false everywhere or true everywhere.
RANGE_SPLIT: replace a finite range by guards i<=k and i>k; no assumption that
      k lies within the interval is needed when both guarded pieces are retained.
RECT_FUBINI: swap sums over a COMMON finite Cartesian box, retaining the same
      guard. Dependent bounds first require a licensed guarded-box conversion.
BOUND_ROW_TRIANGLE: for X>=1, t in[2X,4X-1], n in[1,t] is exactly n in[1,4X-1],
      t in[2X,4X-1], n<=t; equivalently t in[max(2X,n),4X-1]. This specific
      linear-order lemma has a short independent checker, not a generic oracle.
REINDEX: only registered explicit bijections with inverse and domain proofs.
      First include swapping independent indices and d*k=n under d|n,k>=1.
      Do not admit a transformation merely because a producer supplies a map.
DEF_EXPAND: expand an approved named source/primitive definition exactly.
MU_PRIMITIVE: imported proved arithmetic primitive vanishing, output onlyMU_EQ.
MU_CONGRUENCE: multiply/sum arithmetic equalities over a complete finite domain.

Every rule charges actual DAG/node/local ring operations and checks output size,
coefficient bits, scope and degree. Local distributive expansion can blow up;
stop UNKNOWN at the bound rather than deleting inconvenient terms. Independent
verification must not import the certificate producer's rewriting implementation.

Rational-root divisor cuts and boundaries

For a nonnegative rational coefficient c/d, powerp/q>=0 and integer t>=1,
normalize the definition
    k=floor((c/d)*t^(p/q))
to the unique integerk>=0 satisfying
    d^q*k^q <= c^q*t^p < d^q*(k+1)^q,
with c>=0,d>0,p>=0,q>=1 and reduced rational parameter records. The cases c=0
and p=0 remain explicit. For c>0, monotonicity/unboundedness of integer powers
provides existence/uniqueness; a supplied root-definition lemma establishes
this, not evaluation at selectedt. Ceiling-root nodes are DIFFERENT definitions
with their own inequality/equality endpoint conventions.

The crucial first fragment does not need root comparison solving. Partition
at d_index<=k versus d_index>k is an identity for ANY integerk. It only needs
both occurrences to reference the SAME well-typed cutoff AST. This immediately
handles theta1/3,1/2,2/3 divisor cuts and rational factor-cell cuts. The equality
case belongs to the inside<=piece. Replacing floor by ceiling, changing t toX,
using a different rational coefficient, or losing the equality boundary changes
that AST and must fail source binding. Proving a reindexing that crosses a
root threshold requires additional exact side-condition rules and remains
unsupported in the initial fragment.

Primitive relations and semantic imports

Formal definitions retain all index arguments and arithmetic constants:
unit x1-1; norm xn^2-sf(n), sf(n)=1_(n squarefree); prime power x_(p^e)+1 for e1 and x_(p^e) for e>=2;
squarefree-zero xn on a proved nonsquarefree domain; inverse row Sum_(d|n)xd
forn>=2; coprime xa*xb-x_(ab) with gcd(a,b)=1 and ab<=4X-1; complete mu gcd
product with g=gcd(a,b),u=a/g,v=b/g, squarefree(g) and coprime(g,uv) indicators.
See arithmetic_cycle_language.py:relation and grouped_arithmetic_language.py
:_relation. The symbolic adapter must keep every prime power, diagonal/gcd
case, index horizon and family source domain. The seven primitive-state
families retain their actual complete sum domains, not selected primitive rows.

MU_PRIMITIVE can import the elementary global proofs from the defining actual
Dirichlet-inverse/Euler-factor law and factor algebra. These supplied lemmas
must have fixed IDs and independently reviewed proofs; finite factor witnesses
at a fewn are not a proof for everyn. This is still a written mathematical
kernel, not a formal proof-assistant claim. Formal primitive EXPANSION and
arithmetic primitive VANISHING are separate rule IDs and judgments.

Hand-worked universal proof A: complete divisor partition

For anyt>=1 and any integercutk(t), with arbitrary specified weightw(a,t), let
    F(t)=Sum_(1<=a<=t) Sum_(1<=d<=t) [d|a] w(a,t)*xa*xd,
    I(t)=same sum with the additional guard d<=k(t),
    O(t)=same sum with the additional guard d>k(t).
PARTITION with phi=(d<=k(t)), followed by GUARD_BOOL, proves FORMAL_EQ(F,I+O)
for everyt, hence for every required parent/child sum over t. No Mobius premise,
prime estimate or expansion horizon is used. This preserves ordered source
multiplicity; dividing out symmetric pairs or discarding d=a would change it.
It implies the complete normalized contrast identity
    D=(I-O-1)/2, Z=I+O-1=F-1
by RING. It does NOT imply that D^2 is small or that its negative source
contribution can be replaced by an absolute majorant without cost.

An exact obstruction example: theta1/3,t2,power0 gives k=1 since1^3<=2<2^3.
Then F=x1^2+x1*x2+x2^2, I=x1^2+x1*x2 and O=x2^2. The false transformation
F=I has coefficient mismatch1 at x2^2, or evaluation0 vs1 at formalx1=0,x2=1.
This single legal counterexample DISPROVES that universal formal identity.
It is not sampling used to prove an identity, and is not a counterexample to
the project's actual signed bound. A checker can independently validate such
small typed counterexamples under a strict work bound.

Hand-worked universal proof B: rowmass full primitive partition

ForX>=1, letH=4X-1, r_X(n)=Sum_(t=max(2X,n))^H t, and let rho_f(n) be any
specified primitive polynomial at index/anchor n. Define
    Rall=Sum_(n=1)^H r_X(n)*rho_f(n),
    Rhead=same sum guarded n<=2X,
    Rtail=same sum guarded n>2X.
PARTITION proves FORMAL_EQ(Rall,Rhead+Rtail), including n=2X exactly inhead.
Thus b_all=t,b_head=b_tail=-t cancels the FULL polynomial, not just its formal
constant, at everyX. For prime-power families replace n by the anchorp^e and
keep the complete guarded(p,e) domain; the same partition proof applies.
This is the universal backend form of the cheap scalar-gate identities.

DEF_EXPAND, BOUND_ROW_TRIANGLE and RECT_FUBINI also prove
    Sum_(n=1)^H r_X(n)*rho(n)
    =Sum_(t=2X)^H t*Sum_(n=1)^t rho(n).
Both sides traverse exactly the same guarded finite(n,t) box. This rule can
connect rowmass templates with regional primitive-prefix localizers. It does
not identify a weighted norm or prime source with a continuous density; sf(n)
and prime guards remain exact, so scalar error estimates remain separate.

Additional immediately useful universal closure

For arbitraryH>=1 and specified weightsw(a), put
    B=Sum_(a=1)^H w(a)*xa*Sum_(d|a)xd, c=w(1),
    rho1=x1-1, rhoinv(a)=Sum_(d|a)xd for a>=2.
Split a=1 from a>=2, use the divisor-of1 definition, then RING/SUM_LINEAR:
    B-c = w(1)*(x1+1)*rho1 + Sum_(a=2)^H w(a)*xa*rhoinv(a).
Multiplying by B+c and using RING proves
    B^2-c^2=(B+c)*[the displayed complete relation sum].
Both are FORMAL_EQ for arbitrary weights/H, including empty a>=2 atH1.
MU_PRIMITIVE yields B(mu)=c separately. This exact closure is already tested
at finiteH by cancellation_divisor_coupling; the parametric certificate removes
its horizon dependence. It supplies arithmetic normalization, not a critical
prefix-energy estimate. The a1 multiplier MUST be x1+1: using x1 alone leaves
a formal remainderx1-1 and fails RING.

What remains unsupported for the full candidate

The first fragment can certify source completeness and reusable closures, but
is not a generic universal-equivalence decision procedure for this grammar.
It cannot automatically solve arbitrary divisibility/gcd/prime/root guarded
sum identities or perform unbounded monomial collection. To prove the full
candidate it must combine certified source ASTs into a theorem that every
nonconstant coefficient equals the signed defect at all requiredX; degrees3/4
and unwanted linear/quadratic terms must cancel universally. An expanded
sum of independent opaque atoms with a residual only gives UNSUPPORTED, not
proof that those atoms can never cancel arithmetically. A true global obstruction
needs a valid exact counterexample or a proved nonzero symbolic coefficient
on an explicitly nonempty infinite/finite domain.

Once FORMAL_EQ(F_X,B0(X)-DeltaE) is certified, existing exact PSD/coercivity,
primitive MU_EQ, explicit scalarM and actual boundary cost can form a separate
inequality certificate. Promotion must bind the source context, fixed coefficients
and every q/scale quantifier; it must not claim a universe larger than the proof.
Dyadic-only certificates are enough for the stated recurrence bridge but must
be labeled dyadic-only. Extending to all integers needs its own bracketing
argument. None of this machinery supplies the currently unknown full target
certificate or makes finite numerical feasibility a theorem.

Implementation scope and bounded resources

Implement first: a hash-consed typed AST, capture-free scope checker, FORMAL_EQ
DAG replay forALPHA/RING/SUM_LINEAR/GUARD_BOOL/PARTITION/RANGE_SPLIT, and the two
hand-worked identities plus the exactt2 counterexample. Next add approved
primitive definitions, the one triangular-domain Fubini lemma and inverse-row
closure. Keep arbitrary reindexing and global quartic cancellation unsupported
until accompanied by additional individually reviewed rules and actual proofs.

Example strict per-certificate caps:4096 proof nodes,50000 AST nodes,
20000 local ring monomial cells, degree4 where licensed by a source annotation,
4096 coefficient bits, depth128,16MiB receipt,25m charged replay operations,
cooperative/process time and memory caps. These are engineering limits on proof
representation, not mathematical theorems about complexity. Inputs are parsed
and bounded before expansion; DAG references cannot evade expanded-output/fill
limits. A complete conclusion requires every node and side condition verified.
Cap exhaustion is UNKNOWN. Unit tests must mutate cut conventions, complements,
endpoints, capture substitutions, omitted multiplicities, primitive judgments,
source IDs and proof references; no tautology-only tests should license the
whole detector grammar.

Analysis accounting: bounded local source/function reads and hand algebra only,
with a conservative5m operation/read reservation (below the requested25m).
No parameterized source compilation or solver; no changes to completed notes,
frozen files, published evidence, or candidate coefficients.
