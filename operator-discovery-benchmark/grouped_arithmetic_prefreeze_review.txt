Mathematical checks.
1. Norm and gcd identities are correct at zeros, units, equal arguments and
coprime arguments. The mu gcd coefficient is the squarefree(g) compatibility
mask, not a generic c mask. The c cleared coefficient counts common primes with
unequal exponents; its quartic right side x_g^2*x_u*x_v is correct. Exact prime
factor verification and horizon checks prevent user supplied factor constants
from becoming arbitrary arithmetic axioms. Old relations remain whitelisted.
2. A computed scalar slack is sound precisely when lhs-RHS has no nonconstant
coefficient and its constant is nonnegative. Appending that constant times1^2
preserves the prescribed lhs. Direction is NOT RHS-lhs. Shared structural
weights can be preserved at a new scale while this scalar is recomputed; doing
so is finite template transfer, not a uniform bound. Exact coefficient matching
and scalar derivation must independently verify every transferred instance.
3. The unsigned calibration lhs is U_X-Delta, distinct from criticalD-Delta.
Its exact weights4,1,-1 and full prefix/pair/norm groups reproduce the identity
in grouped_arithmetic_proof.txt section7. U has unconditional cubic/polylog
charge; a calibration success does not certify criticalD. Metadata must state
certified_bound=U or D according to claim, even if both diagnostic numbers appear.
4. Shared dimensions alone do not prove matching descriptor semantics. The runner
must bind unchanged descriptor ordering/IDs across training and transfer and
reconstruct groups independently. Flattened certificate terms must have the
same shared weights as every declared group; atomic substitutions are rejected.
5. Scalar slack adds one independent nonnegative constant column PER instance to
the stacked finite cone. Independent dual verification must include those columns
and require each corresponding constant-coordinate functional value>=0. Omitting
these columns could falsely separate the broader slack cone. Independent primal
verification must reconstruct the exact constant slack, not trust an LP weight.

Source/resource scope.
The solver proposes float support, reconstructs exact rational nonnegative
coefficients, checks full polynomial equality, and compiles an exact certificate.
A failed reconstruction or LP is no_certificate_found/missing, not infeasibility.
A dual admission needs exact full monomial coverage and exact sign/equality
checks. Group generation admits empty stable positional groups and retains every
original pair target term. The pair/coprime relation subset is intentional and
cannot be described as a complete product-horizon cycle bank.
The template preflight N^4 work proxy, synthesis coefficient/degree/monomial/time
limits, relation factor predicate limits, and compiler expansion limits are
explicit computation guards. They are not total physical memory, bit-complexity,
serialization or walltime proofs. Runtime test/source-seal hashes and failure
retention are operational provenance, not arithmetic cancellation.

Representation gap, separate future proposal.
For c, a gcd column uses R=2^overlap*x_a*x_b-x_g^2*x_u*x_v. The norm relation
rho_g=x_g^2-h_g yields the lower-degree exact combination
 R+rho_g*x_u*x_v=2^overlap*x_a*x_b-h_g*x_u*x_v.
The current norm multiplier dictionary contains constants/anchor/prefix linear
forms, not the degree-two multiplier x_u*x_v, so this cancellation may be absent
from its cone. That is a declared representation gap, not an obstruction to
actual c arithmetic or a proof of the desired inequality. A future grouped
production could sum this COMPLETE relation combination over exact gcd classes
with shared rational weights, preserving all boundaries, factor constants and
norm sources. It should not be injected into the present frozen dictionary.

Uniform scope.
A finite shared certificate is stronger compression than unrestricted rowwise
weights, but still lacks a proved all-scale coefficient identity and critical
scalar budget. Norm/gcd/cycle premises with finitely normalized prime signs admit
the positive-mean counterfeit. Sufficient arithmetic information must exclude
that model uniformly; not every individual prime sign is logically necessary.
No finite source check, exact dual, transfer case or unsigned calibration supplies
the cofinal actual signed inequality. Global/RH flags must remain false.
