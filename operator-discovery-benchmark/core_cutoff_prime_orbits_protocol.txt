Cutoff prime/cofactor orbit search, pilot 001

Question and scope
The exact input is candidate10_cutoff_work_obligation.json, byte-bound to
core-cutoff-work-results/pilot-001/publication-001/next_search_input.json.
The unresolved input is the complete joined work R_q=mu(q)W1(q)+mu(q)^2 W2(q)
for P_U=A_U^2 E_U^3-c_U^3 D_U, at sigma=9/20, theta=2/3.
The original continuous old-window integral has dt/(2*pi), H(U)=2 U^(45/2).
Its uniform target is cumulative O(V^(12/5)). Full signed core stays priority0.
This auxiliary component alone is insufficient for RH.

Search grammar, sealed before observations
18 programs: fixed prime 2,3,5, followed by every ordered pair of distinct
increasing exponents in [0,1/6,1/3,1/2,2/3,1]. A pair (a,b) selects primes
in (B^a,B^b]; a=0 denotes lower endpoint zero. Integer roots are exact.
Squarefree q in (B,2B] has a unique cofactor after selected primes are removed.
All q with the same cofactor form a truncated orbit, with all faces retained.
Non-squarefree q contribute exactly zero to joined R_q; their W0/head change
and child shell remain in the numerical energy-transition record.
The unit is the complete initial state at U=1, P_1=0, not a recurrence event.
Fixed-single-prime programs necessarily have only singleton groups in a strict
dyadic block. They are retained as a declared negative structural calibration.

Finite experiment
Seven heights [0,1/2,1,2,4,8,16], positive paired-height weights [1,2,2,2,2,2,2]
times abs(zeta(s+1/3)/s)^2. No quadrature widths or 2*pi factors are assigned.
This finite discrete functional is NOT the continuous integral, a lower/upper
enclosure of it, or an all-scale estimate. Finite improvement cannot prove RH.
All polynomial crosses, current c and A coupling, complete prefix states,
strict old/child height masks and first child shell are retained.
Producer precision192: three increment sectors then complete energy crosses.
Checker precision256: three counterfactual child norms and independent Abel
prefix reconstruction, exact harmonic c and Mertens sum. Finite intervals are
serialized with sufficient digits, compared by overlap and relative width.
Each role reads its own preserved Mobius archive from the signed-work study.
Producer checks every inverse-divisor equation; checker factors every integer.
All input primes were historically observed. This is new sampled work evidence,
not a fresh arithmetic holdout.

Selection and extension
Discovery measures q2..1024 once, with every grammar program on B64,128,256,512.
B64,128,256 are discovery, B512 validation; all four are used in selection.
For EVERY integer V in (B,2B], compute sum_h sum_groups abs(sum_(q<=V) R_q(h)).
Retain the entire partial curve, its peak, endpoint, singleton cost, signed
odd/even/joined totals, and the ungrouped full-block absolute work.
Score is the largest integer b in [0,20] for which 2^b*peak<=ungrouped is
certified by intervals. b=0 follows triangle inequality even if intervals
overlap at equality. Zero/uncertain-zero denominator has no score.
Any all-singleton or one-bucket partition at any of the four blocks is
ineligible. Missing cases and zero baselines also exclude a program.
In each lane fixed,prefix,window choose the largest minimum score across the
four blocks, then the smallest program id. No fitted constants or model calls.
Seal selection before extending each owner's OWN saved state to q1025..2048.
Evaluate selected programs only on B1024. Never reselect using this evaluation.
No previous numeric prefix is recomputed in phase2. Source prefixes may be
reread for exact validation and continuation binding. No precautionary hashes.

Verification, resources, and feedback
One owned development suite per owner, affected checks only after changes.
No passing unchanged suites are rerun in study. Independently compare every
actual sample, every case/partial curve and the selected identifiers. Preserve
source bytes, complete observations, all cases, exclusions and terminal errors.
Two numeric owner subprocesses run in parallel: 4GiB each, main process4GiB,
under the authorized 16GiB aggregate memory limit. Each worker phase has
300000000 accounted operations/900 seconds; total including development and
main verification is capped at1500000000 operations/1800 seconds wall time.
Operation accounting is a conservative budget estimate, not a CPU-instruction
counter. No source fitting, no prime regeneration, no numerical uniform C.
The continuous counterpart integrates the sum of absolute group integrands
over all real heights with dt/(2*pi), each event masked by its old window.
The universal partition identity gives a conditional route: this complete
grouped continuous work O(B^(12/5)), uniformly for all partial blocks, implies the
inherited signed cumulative estimate by dyadic summation. This is a stronger
sufficient target, and remains OPEN. Exact orbit identities do not pay their
state variation, even-sector interaction, or truncated boundary contributions.
Review025 records analytically supplied rules and their limits; it does not
make numerical candidate selection. The numerical detector generates/ranks
grouping programs; analytical reviewers supply and assess proof lemmas.
Every original source binding, paid correction, drift, recovery, full-source
priority0 and ungrouped target is retained in published feedback. No new
