FACTOR-REGION SEARCH: COMPLETE SIGNED CORRELATIONS AND GLOBAL REJECTIONS

Objective and current status

The goal remains a verified actual signed arithmetic estimate at all scales
with cofinal exponents, sufficient for ordinary RH. No complete estimate of
that strength is proved. This turn implemented the proposed prime-factor
language, ran a frozen search, reviewed its candidates in parallel, and proved
that two selected inequalities are false despite passing every available final
test. These refutations concern their full potential-innovation claims; the
direct target energies and RH remain unresolved.

Implemented representation and conditional bridge

At each blockX, set z=floor(X^theta),u=floor(X^phi), theta,phi positive rational.
Each source integer m>=2 is p^e*b with p its SMALLEST prime, e its exact
positive valuation, and P^-(b)>p or b=1. The nine components are unit, then
prime-small/large × core-small/large × odd/even valuation. The source indexm
is classified; filter eventd*m is not reclassified. Every event and boundary
is retained. Cutoffs are recomputed at each child scale.

The complete Gram records sum_[X,2X)S_i(n)S_j(n)/X^2. Its sum over all81
entries is the original filtered energyE_G. The search keeps signed cross
terms inside each square and uses W=E_G+V with V a finite nonnegative sum
of those full square forms divided byceil(X^eta). Every sufficiently large
q admitting a uniform dyadic bound W(2X)-W(X)<=Cq ceil(X^(1/q)) would give
critical E_G by telescoping. The existing fixed-filter pole-budget theorem
then implies ordinary RH for these actual targets. The uniform inequality
remains the missing premise.

Also implemented: complete least-prime recursion for rough prefixes and
coefficientwise symbolic log-prime forcing. Their identities are checked using
actual arithmetic, not floating logarithms or random signs. They are calibration
and proof coordinates, not new distribution estimates by themselves.

Analytical result that changes the search

Let F_X(y) be the actual c sum in component2: leastprime<=X^theta, even
valuation, core<=sqrtX, with theta>=1/3 fixed. A separately reviewed proof gives

 F_X(vX)=(-64 sqrt(v)+o(1))*X^(3/4)/(logX)^2,

uniformly for v in any fixed compact subinterval of(0,infinity). Its main
contribution is c(p^2*q)=-4 with bothp,q prime, p~X^(1/4), q~sqrtX. ALL other
classes are bounded absolutely, including positive two-prime cores, smallp,
high even exponents, unit cores, and the largep tail. The proof uses the
unconditional classical prime number theorem, not an RH assumption or a
prime-pair conjecture. It is not a claim of new independent prime information.

For fixed filterP(s)=sum_d w_d d^(-s), the source cutoffs remain at the parentX.
Consequently the full filtered component square satisfies

 X^-2 sum_[X,2X) S2(n)^2
   =(6144 P(1/2)^2+o(1))*X^(1/2)/(logX)^4.

The multiplier isP(1/2), notP(3/4); this follows from the held parent cutoff.
If P(1/2)!=0, any positive fixed weight on this singleton with eta<1/2 forces
W to grow at least a positive power ofX divided bylog^4X. This contradicts
the cofinal innovation telescope, even with arbitrary unknown Cq and q0.
The asymptotic threshold is existential, not explicitly computed.

factor_region_asymptotic_backend.py checks this theorem's domain for arbitrary
matching programs in the current actual-c one/two-term fixed-filter class.
It checks P(1/2)!=0 using exact rational squared magnitudes. Vanishing leading
multiplier, signed component contrasts, different cutoffs and eta>=1/2 are
unsupported by this rejection rule. All other potential weights must be
nonnegative. Five domain tests cover these boundaries. Numerical constants
never enter the rejection. The theorem and its independent review are frozen
separately in asymptotic-adjudication-001, with exact source/queue bindings.
This is reviewed mathematics plus program-domain checks, not a formal machine
verification of the proof or PNT.

Surviving auxiliary progress and remaining hard term

For selected mu program679662, all even-valuation components vanish. Its
global parity and all-nonunit vectors both yield M(n)-1. Its thin odd region
has p<=X^(1/3), core<=X^(1/6), so every contributing m is<=sqrtX and its
normalized squareH is<=1. The exact potential simplifies to

 V=14T/R+16H(1+1/R)+(8/X)(1+1/R),
 T=X^-2 sum_[X,2X)(M(n)-1)^2, R=ceil(X^(1/3)).

Hence E<=W<=29E+76 at all integer scales. This proves auxiliary control and
an equivalence with critical M energy, not that critical energy itself.
The selected unit potentials are likewise bounded corrections. The remaining
c even-core contrast ededbf6c31c4 failed its storedq12 constant atX32768;
its existential global claim is not thereby refuted.

Next work

Prioritize cancellation INSIDE complete signed region combinations. The new
theorem is a general exclusion rule for growing positive corrections; it must
not be evaded by silently deleting a component, changing a normalization, or
retuning the frozen candidates. Larger normalizers can create bounded
auxiliaries, but do not supply the missing critical bound for E_G.

Useful further expressivity is learned rational region vectors and exact
prime/core counting counterterms, with full reconstruction and residuals.
Any subtraction of a leading asymptotic leaves an explicit error whose rate
must be proved: an o(X^(3/4)/log^2X) remainder is not a square-root bound.
The next proof attempt should target a full signed cross-region inequality,
using the rejected candidates to identify the necessary cancellation. Preserve
the current positive/negative results as development evidence in a new protocol.
