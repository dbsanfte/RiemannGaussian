The complete divisor domain includes zero-weight sources and the unit endpoint. B-1 is exactly a sum of the unit relation with multiplier x1+1 and inverse rows a>=2 with multiplier a^j xa. D=(I-O-1)/2 equals I-1=-O only modulo those complete relations; it retains an unknown partial arithmetic channel. Squaring the whole B alone would be tautological calibration.

All primitive multipliers and both complements remain explicit. The three squares have formal constant 1/4 per t. The four complete relation groups have formal constants -1,0,1/2,1 per t. Their summed costs are O(X), so they introduce no new cubic scalar term: old mu gamma=0 remains necessary and is not sufficient. Normalization weights a^j are an explicitly new program; no subpower estimate for its partial channels is assumed.

There are 366 square groups and 403 relation groups, preserving the full target, the preceding archives and every dual column. Shared finite coefficients do not prove either all-scale polynomial matching or a critical scalar budget. Sparse primal verification may omit only exactly-zero products after admission; a dual must evaluate all columns.
