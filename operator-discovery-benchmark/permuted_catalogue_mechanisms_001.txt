Fixed permuted catalogue: exact pair mechanisms and analytical attempt

Scope and outcome
Read only the fixed source catalogue in permuted_circuit_discovery.py. Its
generator, arithmetic providers and tests were not executed; no numerical
response artifacts, source tables, hashes or frozen files were inspected or
modified. Ordinals below follow the source rule: baseline0, then12 words in
each family. This new memo alone is edited. No cofinal full moment bound for
actual M is proved.

Useful proved results are exact conjugated pairings, interval-source and
prime-complement decompositions, forced all-scale source-branch regimes,
unconditionally bounded cyclic-translation padding, and bounded smoothness
remainders for the direct adjacent-shear subfamily. Opposite prime phases
have a square-supported core, but their unequal orders and the intervening
permutation prevent a pure whole-word square fold. Every main prefix channel,
source-domain complement and complete mixed energy remains.

1. Catalogue indexing and operation order
Let j=0,...,11 index MAPS as written in the source. Family IDs are
 1+j permutation_input; 13+j permutation_interleaving;
 25+j conjugated_convolution; 37+j shear_interleaving.
Let U_pi f(n)=f(pi(n)), A_j be the shear, T_j the first convolution and V_j
the final convolution defined by the catalogue's cyclic alpha/phase lists.
For even j the shear word is exactly
 rho=V_j U_pi^-1 A_j U_pi M.
For odd j it is
 rho=V_j U_pi^-1 A_j U_pi T_j M.
The shear-family j0 explicitly REPLACES its listed translation map by the
identity for calibration. j2's listed polynomial is already identity. There
is no direction swap in the shear family's permutation conjugation.
In conjugated_convolution only, j congruent1 mod3 swaps the forward/inverse
permutation stages. These conventions matter for exact integer pairings.

2. General conjugated pair and complete energy
Fix block[m,2m), m=2^k, and original even n=m+r, r even. Put
 a=pi(n), b=pi(n+1), t=c*phi(n).
For even_from_odd, U_pi^-1 A U_pi changes f(a) to f(a)+t*f(b) and leaves
f(b) unchanged. For odd_from_even it leaves f(a) and changes f(b) to
f(b)+t*f(a). The phase is evaluated at the ORIGINAL even n, not at a or b
after relabeling. The exact pair energy is respectively
 f(a)^2+f(b)^2+c^2*f(b)^2+2c*phi(n)*f(a)*f(b),
 f(a)^2+f(b)^2+c^2*f(a)^2+2c*phi(n)*f(a)*f(b).
The positive c^2 term and untouched coordinate cannot be discarded.

For f=M define the full signed interval I(a,b)=M(b)-M(a). If b>a it is
sum_{a<l<=b}mu(l); if b<a it is minus sum_{b<l<=a}mu(l). Then the changed
coordinate is (1+t)M(a)+t*I(a,b) in even_from_odd, or
(1+t)M(a)+I(a,b) in odd_from_even. For arbitrary t, the former pair energy
is exactly
 [1+(1+t)^2]M(a)^2+2[1+t+t^2]M(a)I(a,b)
 +(1+t^2)I(a,b)^2.
The latter is
 [1+(1+t)^2]M(a)^2+2(1+t)M(a)I(a,b)+I(a,b)^2.
These formulas expose the actual complete interval correlation, rather than
assuming anti-invariance under a reversible map.

If f=T_j M, its difference has the exact additional lower-block complement
 f(b)-f(a)=I(a,b)
   +sum_{d|b,d>=2}u_j(d)M(b/d)
   -sum_{d|a,d>=2}u_j(d)M(a/d).
Every proper-divisor prefix here is below m. A short gap between a and b does
not bound these complements by the gap; divisibility may change completely.

The final V_j creates further lower-block terms. For the complete catalogue
word the valid block identity is rho_block=Q_block M_block+G_block(M_<m),
with Q the actual invertible conjugated pair matrix and G including ALL
proper-divisor terms from both convolutions. The full target is
 m^-2[||Q M_block||^2+||G||^2+2<Q M_block,G>].
Neither a bounded interval component nor the raw pair's negative correlation
supplies a bound for this complete output without paying these terms.

3. Exact MAPS pair geometry
All formulas below are for complete blocks, with k large enough for the
specified bit positions. Smaller blocks use the same definitions with their
declared identity/modulo conventions and are finite head exceptions.

j0 / shear ID37: the calibration uses pi=identity, so (a,b)=(n,n+1).
Its c=-1, phi=+1, direction even_from_odd is an adjacent difference plus
an unchanged prefix coordinate. The translation listed in MAPS is used in
the other families, not in this shear word.

j1 / ID38: F(r)=3r+2r^2. Let s=F(r) mod m. The ordered endpoints are
 a=m+s, b=m+(F(r+1) mod m),
 b-a=4r+5-m*w,
 w=floor(F(r+1)/m)-floor(F(r)/m).
Retain the entire wrap term w. The map preserves parity of r, so a is even
and b odd; their separation need not be1. This is an odd-j word with an
inner T_{-1/2,constant(-1)}. Its shear is odd_from_even, c=1, phi=-1.
The changed channel is the exact f(b)-f(a), including the displayed lower
divisor complement; the retained channel is f(a).

j2 / ID39: identity map. Adjacent pair, c=-1/2, even_from_odd, phase parity
of floor(sqrt(n)). No inner T. Its cancellation is a variable-amplitude
bounded-increment mechanism, with the full unchanged coordinate retained.

j3 / ID40: xor all bits, pi(n)=3m-1-n. It maps an original even/odd pair
to adjacent odd/even endpoints with a=b+1. The shear direction therefore
flips in arithmetic even/odd coordinates. It has an inner T (alpha=-1/6,
prime phase parity of cube root), c=1/2 and the same cube-root parity phase.
The adjacent-M smoothness theorem cannot be applied to this inner T output.

j4 / ID41: xor top bit, mask m/2. It takes an even/odd pair to an adjacent
even/odd pair in the other half of the block, b=a+1. No inner T. Its phase
at the original even n is the residue3 sign, c=-1/3. After conjugation this
is still an adjacent-prefix shear, with phase relabeled by the half exchange.

j5 / ID42: xor alternating bits includes bit0. It maps an even/odd pair to
adjacent odd/even endpoints, a=b+1. Inner T has alpha=1/3 and residue5 prime
phase. Its shear is odd_from_even, c=1/3, residue5 phase at original even n.
The orientation flips, but no large separation is created by this conjugation.
In general xor by mask A maps r,r+1 with r even to a pair differing by+1
if mask bit0=0, and by-1 if mask bit0=1; every other bit is identical.

j6 / ID43: bit reversal. If r=2s, then
 a=m+rev_{k-1}(s), b=a+m/2.
As s varies these pair the entire lower half with the upper half, at exact
distance m/2. No inner T. The shear is even_from_odd, c=-1, and
 phi(n)=source_sign(mu(n-floor(sqrt(n)))).
The full pair is M(a)-phi(n)M(a+m/2), with unchanged M(a+m/2).
Its cross term samples source sign at a bit-reversed PREIMAGE, not an endpoint
or a multiplicative multiple of the interval. Both zero/squareful and signed
source sectors remain: phi(n)=1-mu(h(n))^2+mu(h(n)).

j7 / ID44: rotation LEFT1. Rotating bit0 moves it to bit1, so
 b=a+2, with bit1 of a-m equal0. Pairs are the two length2 gaps inside
each four-point group. This is short-gap, not a far-half mechanism. There
IS an inner T (alpha=1, prime phase source sign of floor cube root). The
shear is odd_from_even, c=1, phase source sign of floor cube root at even n.
On phi=-1 it produces f(b)-f(a); its fresh component is the two-source sum
mu(a+1)+mu(a+2), plus the complete lower-divisor difference. On phi=+1 it
adds the two prefix values instead. No phase sector may be omitted.

j8 / ID45: rotation LEFT(-2), equivalently right2. For k>=3, bit0 moves
to bit k-2 and
 b=a+m/4, with bit k-2 of a-m equal0.
The quarter pairs connect first to second and third to fourth quarter.
No inner T. Its c=-1/2, phi=+1, direction even_from_odd exposes the actual
signed quarter interval I(a,a+m/4), retaining the full other prefix.

j9 / ID46: compose F(r)=1+3r+2r^2 then xor alternating mask A. Put
 s0=F(r) mod m, s1=F(r+1) mod m.
Here s1-s0=4r+5-m*w with the same exact wrap formula as above. The actual
pair separation is
 b-a=(s1-s0)-2[(s1 bitand A)-(s0 bitand A)].
This follows from z xor A=z+A-2(z bitand A). No fixed short separation
follows. The polynomial flips parity and the xor flips it back, so a is
even and b odd. Inner T has alpha=-1/2, prime phase constant(-1). The shear
is odd_from_even, c=1/2, phi=-1, hence effective coefficient -1/2.

j10 / ID47: choose bit reversal for positive block-base phase and xor all
for negative phase. The branch phase is source sign of
 h(m)=m-floor(sqrt(m)). For every EVEN k=2l with l>=2,
 h(m)=2^l(2^l-1), divisible by4, so mu(h(m))=0 and the sign is+1.
Thus the bit-reversal, half-block pair branch is FORCED on every even k>=4.
On a negative branch it instead makes adjacent orientation-reversed pairs.
Other odd k cannot be classified from this square-factor identity alone.
The shear is c=-1/3, even_from_odd, phase parity of floor sqrt at original
even n; there is no inner T. Adjacent-branch smoothing does not control the
far branch, which occurs on infinitely many mandatory scales.

j11 / ID48: choose rotation LEFT1 for positive phase and F(r)=1-r+2r^2
for negative phase. The block-base phase is source sign of floor(m^(1/3)).
If k=3l with l>=2, that argument is exactly2^l, divisible by4. Its Mobius
value is0, so the rotation branch is FORCED for every k multiple3>=6.
There its conjugated pairs are distance2. In the polynomial branch,
 b-a=4r+1-m*w,
 w=floor(F(r+1)/m)-floor(F(r)/m),
and parity is reversed. No full-block short-gap bound follows. This is an
odd-j word with inner T alpha=-1/6, prime cube-root parity; shear c=1/3,
odd_from_even and cube-root parity phase. The forced short branch still
does not remove its lower-divisor increment complement.

Both source choices read indices BELOW m and are constant throughout the
block. The square-factor deductions are symbolic source laws, not numerical
source evaluations. Branch behavior on a finite set of scales cannot replace
these cofinal effective-regime obligations.

4. Proved direct-prefix smoothness components and their limitations
For adjacent actual values x=M(e), y=M(e+1), w=mu(e+1), |w|<=1, a full shear
with t=c*psi(e) has pair energy
 kappa(t)*(x^2+y^2)+c^2*y*w-(t+c^2/2)*w^2
in even_from_odd, or
 kappa(t)*(x^2+y^2)-c^2*x*w-(t+c^2/2)*w^2
in odd_from_even, where kappa(t)=1+t+t^2/2>=1/2. Summing the remainder and
dividing by X^2 gives O_c(1), uniformly over scales, using only |M(n)|<=n.
Orientation reversal simply swaps these two cases and relabels the unit phase.

IDs37,39,41, and ID47 only on its negative xor branch, therefore have an
exact direct-prefix amplitude-plus-bounded-channel decomposition. On a
changed endpoint a paired with adjacent b, write
 M(a)+t M(b)=(1+t)M(a)+t[M(b)-M(a)].
The second term has magnitude at most |c| and the untouched endpoint remains.
The main multiplier is positive and bounded away from0 for c=-1/2 or-1/3.
For37, c=-1 annihilates the changed endpoint's common-prefix part but leaves
ALL odd prefix coordinates. In particular its raw full shear energy is
 E_M(X)/2+O(1), a relation valid for any bounded-increment prefix, not a
special Mobius cancellation. IDs39/41 obey the analogous complete kappa
weighting of prefix energy and its positive retained complement.

There is a useful unconditional estimate even AFTER the final V_j. Each
catalogue final convolution has |alpha|<=1, hence |a_alpha,chi(d)|<=1.
For a bounded component b(n),
 |V_j b(n)|<=||b||_infinity*tau(n).
For every eta>0, tau(n)<=C_eta n^eta: for sufficiently large primes,
l+1<=2^l<=p^(eta*l), and the finitely many smaller primes contribute a finite
product of sup_l (l+1)/p^(eta*l). Consequently
 E_{V_j b}(X)<=C_eta'||b||_infinity^2 X^(-1+2eta).
This is a proved power-decaying component bound. It is NOT a bound on the
main V_j[d(n)M(n)] channel or its mixed square with V_j b. For37 specifically,
 rho=V_0[1_{odd}M]+V_0[-1_{even}mu(n+1)].
The second channel has the just-proved bound. The first retains original
prefix energy up to a bounded adjacent-source complement, and is unresolved.
Replacing the whole output by the bounded difference channel would discard
its recoverable target.

Inner T words40/42/44/46/48 do not inherit this bounded-increment conclusion.
Their fresh short differences, when short, are bounded source sums plus
unbounded-as-yet lower-prefix complements. The exact difference formula in
section2 is the honest analytical obligation. Far direct words43/45 and
the far branch47 instead require long-interval/prefix covariance estimates.

5. Full source-complement identity for every far interval
For any prime p define C_p(t)=sum_{j<=t,p does not divide j}mu(j), with floor
extension. The actual source law gives exactly
 M(t)=C_p(t)-C_p(floor(t/p)).
Hence for arbitrary integer endpoints a,b in the same dyadic block,
 I(a,b)=[C_p(b)-C_p(a)]
        -[C_p(floor(b/p))-C_p(floor(a/p))].
For p>=2 the latter endpoints are below the block. The former is a complete
FRESH p-nonmultiple interval. For p=2 it is an odd-source interval. This is
a useful signed decomposition of bit-reversal and quarter-rotation intervals,
but supplies no uniform small bound for the fresh nonmultiple component.

Substituting it into the pair formulas yields every fresh/fresh, fresh/past
and past/past term, as well as its mixed products with the retained endpoint
M(a) or M(b). No nonmultiple source block is discarded, and no assumption
of independent signs or an RH-strength bound for C_p is introduced. Polynomial
wraps merely change the signed endpoints in this same identity; their wrap
intervals are full original-source complements, not removable residues.

There is also a useful exact source-sign refinement for ID43. Its original
phase index is even n=2s. Write q=floor(sqrt(2s)). If q=2l is even, then
 h(n)=2(s-l),
 mu(h(n))=-mu(s-l)1_{s-l odd},
 phi(n)=1-1_{s-l odd}mu(s-l)^2-1_{s-l odd}mu(s-l).
The queried source on this sector is strictly below the current block, since
s<=m-1. If q=2l+1 is odd, the complete complementary sector instead has
h(n)=2(s-l)-1, an odd source index near n, which can lie in the fresh block.
Those nonmultiple signs are not reduced by the prime2 law. This exact split may be useful
for a signed proof, but neither its latter source correlation nor its products
with the bit-reversed endpoints are estimated here. Counting root transitions
or retaining only the reducible sector would omit the required complement.

6. A separate proved cyclic-translation padding family
In the other families map j0 really is r->r+1 mod m. Acting DIRECTLY on M,
 delta(n)=M(pi(n))-M(n)
is mu(n+1) at the m-1 nonwrap coordinates. At n=2m-1 it is the complete
negative interval sum from m+1 through2m-1, of magnitude at most m-1.
Therefore E_delta(m)<=(m-1+(m-1)^2)/m^2=1-1/m.
This retains the one long wrap exactly; maximum-displacement bounds alone
would miss this useful uniform estimate. More generally a fixed block shift
b, 0<=b<m, has
 E_delta(m)<=b(m-b)/m<=b,
by summing the squared b-distance and (m-b)-distance source bounds over
their respective m-b and b coordinates. Fixed finitely many smaller blocks
are harmless retained head cases.

Thus delta has finite H_sigma for every sigma>1/2 unconditionally. Any fixed
admitted circuit subsequently acting on delta preserves that property. In
particular permutation_input ID1 is
 T_{-1,1}M+T_{-1,1}delta,
with an unconditionally critical-class-finite padding term. This proves a
real component estimate, but gives no bound for the first term, and no
prime-specific mechanism for the translation. This conclusion does NOT apply
to cyclic translation acting on T M in an interleaving or conjugation: the
divisor-transformed sequence's increments are not bounded by1.

7. Opposite-prime-phase cores and square support
The interleaving family sets the second prime phase to -chi for j=0,3,6,9,
namely IDs13,16,19,22. Their (alpha,beta) pairs are respectively
 (-1,-1/6), (-1/6,1/2), (1/2,-1/2), (-1/2,1/6).
None has alpha=beta. The local exact identity without a permutation is
 (1-chi*z)^(-alpha)(1+chi*z)^(-beta)
 =(1-chi*z)^(-(alpha-beta))(1-chi^2*z^2)^(-beta).
All catalogue prime phases are unit signs, so chi(p)^2=1. The second factor
defines a SOURCE-FREE square-supported convolution Q_beta, with coefficient
a_beta,1(e) at denominator e^2 and zero at nonsquares. Thus
 T_beta,-chi T_alpha,chi=T_alpha-beta,chi Q_beta.
It is a pure source-erasing square kernel only when alpha=beta, which does
not occur in these catalogue words. Here the remaining orders alpha-beta
are -5/6,-2/3,1,-2/3.

The square channel has a useful unconditional critical norm: its forward
and inverse norm is <=zeta(2sigma+1)^|beta|<=zeta(2)^|beta| for sigma>=1/2.
This uses genuine square-supported density, not a floor-quotient substitution.
It controls that component operator uniformly at the critical endpoint but
does not bound its input's unknown moment.

The actual catalogue contains an intervening U_pi. Its exact split is
 rho=T_beta,-chi U_pi T_alpha,chi M
     =U_pi C+D,
 C=T_alpha-beta,chi Q_beta M,
 D=[T_beta,-chi,U_pi]T_alpha,chi M.
The complete moment is E_C+E_D+2X^-2<U_pi C,D> on complete dyadic blocks.
The commutator has both unmatched divisor domains
 sum_{d|n}v(d)f(pi(n/d))-sum_{e|pi(n)}v(e)f(pi(n)/e).
It can restore nonsquare path support. No square-fold identity through pi,
vanishing defect, favorable cross sign, or full moment estimate follows.
Identity map j2 is not among the opposite-phase cases.

8. Conjugated-convolution aliases and the fixed full gate
For every even j in the conjugated_convolution family, the final u is exactly
the inverse of t at the same prime source. The word is a reversible group
commutator between T_j and U_pi (with orientation changed at j congruent1
mod3), rather than an adjacent inverse pair. Its diagonal dyadic block is
identity, with the full lower-block defect retained. At j2/ID27 pi=identity,
so normalization removes both permutation stages and the exact T inverse
pair: this is a baseline alias. No corresponding identity is asserted for
the other maps merely from their bijectivity or finite small-block coincidences.

The source catalogue's normalizer removes only authorized exact inverse/merge
aliases and terminal complete-dyadic energy aliases. Irregular diagnostic
intervals need not agree with raw terminal-permutation words. These exact
algebraic distinctions do not alter a selected formula or selection protocol.

For one fixed complete normalized observer the actual proof gate remains:
cofinally many q>=2, finite Cq independent of every dyadic X>=32, and
 E_rho(X)<=Cq ceil(X^(1/q)).
All the pair, wrap, prime-nonmultiple, proper-divisor and mixed-square terms
above remain in this rho. Smoothness, unconditional bounded padding, critical
square support and unit inverses do not prove that gate. Its reviewed weighted
recovery/Mellin bridge would imply ordinary RH if it were established.

Final mathematical conclusion
The strongest proved new simplifications are the direct adjacent shear's
amplitude-plus-power-decaying channel, translation's full-wrap bounded energy,
forced source-choice regimes, and exact square-supported cores. The genuine
new unresolved signed mechanisms are far-interval/prefix correlations in43/45
and the far branch47, and proper-divisor increment complements in the odd-j
shear words. Exact prime laws decompose those intervals into fresh nonmultiple
source and past compensation, but do not bound the former. This identifies
specific complete analytical obligations without an assumed cancellation law,
an optimizer, a favorite expected outcome or a new numerical experiment.
Reviewer numerical/test/source-probe work is zero; frozen files unchanged.
