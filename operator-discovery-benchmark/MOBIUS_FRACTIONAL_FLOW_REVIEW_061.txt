MOBIUS FRACTIONAL FLOW REVIEW 061
================================
Scope
-----
This is a source-only mathematical review of transport BEFORE the t
integral. The060 handoff was read;060 and all earlier files remain
unchanged. No scientific arrays, costs, integrals, signatures, nullspaces,
tests or scores were evaluated. The formulas below are exact on the stated
domains. They establish uniform component bounds and an unconditional
logarithmic gain, not a uniform RH-strength estimate of the remaining core.

Fix distinct primes p<q in the fixed registered set M, R=product(M),
k=|M|>=2, alpha in(0,1), gamma>0, tau in(0,1). All parameters remain
fixed as N varies. Let eps=min(tau,N^-gamma),

 W(x)=floor(4x)-3floor(2x)+2floor(x),
 mu_M(d)=mu(d)*1_(gcd(d,R)=1),
 G_M(t)=product_(ell in M)(1-ell^-t), c_alpha=1/Gamma(1-alpha).

The complete joined middle is

 J(N)=c_alpha integral_eps^tau G_M(t)t^(-alpha-1)
       sum_d mu_M(d)[p^-t W(N/(pd))-q^-t W(N/(qd))]d^-t dt. (1)

The actual ordinary Mobius source, all outside prime factors, cofactor
d=1, both floor signs and the q-range exit are retained. All floors are
right-continuous and inclusive. The source horizon is D=floor(4N/p).
If D=0 there are no terms. Missing squareful terms are exact mu zeros;
they are not discarded complements. Empty integration band gives J=0.

1. Fractional conservation and exact optimal transport at fixed t
----------------------------------------------------------------
At each t>0 the positive profile capacities are

 h(t)=(p^-t,q^-t,p^-t-q^-t,p^-t+q^-t).

For each source token take actual sign epsilon_d=mu(d)*kernel_sigma and
capacity h_profile(d)(t), with COMMON carrier a_d(t)=d^-t. This retains
the pointwise signed source in(1): in every P,Q,D,S case,

 kernel_sigma*h_profile(t)
       =p^-t W(N/(pd))-q^-t W(N/(qd)).                    (2)

In particular h(t) is a positive element of the060 conservation space,
but the statement follows directly from the four profile cases; it does
not require trusting a numerical nullspace or a supplied copy winner.
For every t>0 all capacities are positive, even for D. At t=0 the D
capacity vanishes; that endpoint is not a licensed positive-capacity
token and causes no error in the integrable limiting estimates below.

Define for real y>=1 the actual signed prefix masses

 A_N(y)=sum_(d<=y)mu_M(d)W(N/(pd)),
 B_N(y)=sum_(d<=y)mu_M(d)W(N/(qd)),
 S_N(y,t)=p^-t A_N(y)-q^-t B_N(y).                        (3)

After D the prefix is constant, since the joined source vanishes.
Zero-capacity locations may be inserted into the ordered carrier grid.
Then the exact optimal fractional transport cost, permitting fractional
splits and a zero-carrier dummy to absorb the final excess mass, is

 V_t(N)=sum_(j=1)^(D-1)|S_N(j,t)|[j^-t-(j+1)^-t]
                       +D^-t|S_N(D,t)|.                 (4)

When D=1 only the terminal term remains. D=0 returns zero, rather than
evaluate a fictitious negative or zero carrier. Equivalently, for D>=1,

 V_t(N)=t integral_1^D |S_N(y,t)|y^(-t-1)dy
                         +D^-t|S_N(D,t)|.               (5)

Proof of optimality: scan d ascending, so carriers descend. The waiting
stock always has one sign. For an opposite incoming mass, cancel the
smaller capacity, price that capacity times the carrier distance, and
retain any excess. At each carrier cut the net signed capacity that must
cross is S_N(j,t), so every legal flow has cost at least the corresponding
absolute flux in(4). The one-sign-stock algorithm has no oppositely
directed excess flux, and attains this lower bound. The dummy capacity
is exactly the absolute final signed mass and its carrier is zero. Stack
or FIFO choices within the stock give the same optimal total cost in
this common-carrier ordering.

This is unequal-mass one-dimensional transport, not integer-copy
matching with an unpriced fractional residual. Without the terminal
charge the theorem is false whenever the total signed capacity is not
zero. For the same reason, the full source must not be truncated before
the unequal q exit or the cofactor unit is counted.

2. t-dependent flows, integration and the paid total mass
---------------------------------------------------------
The plan may change with t. This is mathematically harmless: (4) is a
finite sum of continuous absolute values of analytic functions of t,
and is measurable. Each prefix is p^-t A-q^-t B with exact integer A,B.
Unless A=B=0, it has at most one zero for t>0; a possible crossing solves
(q/p)^t=B/A with a positive ratio. Thus no arbitrary or nonmeasurable
selection is needed. Direct evaluation of(4) also avoids committing to
a finite list of plans valid simultaneously for all t.

The pointwise signed carrier sum has absolute value <=V_t by signed
Abel summation, or by the transport triangle. Finite summation and the
nonnegative integral therefore give the SAME target bound

 |J(N)|<=B_flow(N):=c_alpha integral_eps^tau
                               G_M(t)t^(-alpha-1)V_t(N)dt. (6)

This is a stronger auxiliary than the absolute joined integral: it loses
cancellation in t and across differently directed prefix fluxes. Fractional
capacities remove integer-copy restrictions, but there is no automatic
uniform dominance over060's costs, which take some absolute values after
integration. No finite observation is used to compare them.

Let Psi_M(Y) count M-smooth positive integers <=Y, including1, and be
zero for Y<1. The complete elementary convolution mu_M*One gives

 A_N(D)=Psi_M(4N/p)-3Psi_M(2N/p)+2Psi_M(N/p),
 B_N(D)=Psi_M(4N/q)-3Psi_M(2N/q)+2Psi_M(N/q).               (7)

Every individual floor convolution is evaluated to its full horizon,
or the outside-source cancellation is proved before splitting it. The
partial prefixes in(3) do NOT equal the expressions in(7).
For N>=2 let L_N=1+log_2(4N). Elementary exponent counting gives
Psi_M(Y)<=L_N^k for the arguments in(7), so

 |S_N(D,t)|<=12L_N^k.                                    (8)

One integration by parts gives
Gamma(1-alpha)=integral_0^infinity e^-u u^-alpha du
             >=1-e^-1>1/2.
Consequently c_alpha<2 and in particular c_alpha<=3. Also
G_M(t)<=R t^k. Hence

 c_alpha integral_eps^tau G_M(t)t^(-alpha-1)
                           |S_N(D,t)|dt
 <=36R/(k-alpha)*L_N^k.                                  (9)

This bounds the terminal charge in(4). It also bounds the ENTIRE actual
greedy leftover cost: the leftover fractional capacity is |S_N(D,t)|,
each original carrier is <=1, and its zero-dummy charge is at most that
mass. The terminal term alone is not the entire leftover cost. Portions
of earlier leftover-to-zero paths also appear in the prefix terms of(4).
Conflating those two decompositions would falsely pay part of the core.

3. Paid short-cofactor head for general rational orders
------------------------------------------------------
For every y>=1 and t>0,

 |S_N(y,t)|<=sum_(d<=y)h_profile(d)(t)<=2floor(y)<=2y.      (10)

Choose any fixed rational theta in[0,1/2], with no special denominator
restriction, and put Y=N^theta, a=min(Y,D). For D>=1 the head in(5) is

 H_head=c_alpha integral_eps^tau G_M(t)t^(-alpha)
                              integral_1^a |S_N(y,t)|y^(-t-1)dy dt.

Since y^-t<=1 on[1,Y], (10) yields

 H_head<=6R/(k+1-alpha)*N^theta.                          (11)

This is a genuine unconditional square-root-or-smaller component bound,
valid for every integer N>=2. It does not use complete convolution at a
partial endpoint, a Mertens assumption, or finite data. At theta=0 the
exact head is zero; the displayed positive bound is merely coarse.

There is an optional sharper logarithmic head estimate. For a<=Y,
0<t<=tau<1, the same proof gives
H_head<=[6R/(1-tau)]Y*Gamma(k+1-alpha)/(log Y)^(k+1-alpha)
when Y>=2. The Gamma factor is <=(k-1)!+k!, by splitting its elementary
integral at1 and dominating the power by the adjacent integer powers.
Small Y uses(11). This refinement is not needed to admit the head rule.

4. Paid integer exit strip, with the floor correction retained
--------------------------------------------------------------
For D>=2 choose any fixed rational rho in[0,3/4] and let

 K=min(floor(D/2),floor(N^rho)), b=max(a,D-K).

Here K>=1, b>=D/2>=1. Decompose(5) into the exact head[1,a], core[a,b]
and exit[b,D], followed by its terminal term. For y<=D,

 |S_N(y,t)|<=|S_N(D,t)|+2(D-floor y).                    (12)

The integer floor in(12) is essential: replacing it by D-y is false
inside the last cofactor cell. The exact exit plus terminal is bounded by

 |S_N(D,t)|b^-t
       +t integral_b^D 2(D-floor y)y^(-t-1)dy.            (13)

Thus ONE smooth-count bound(9) covers both its conservation term and
the terminal. For the remaining strip, enlarging to[D-K,D] and using
y^(-t-1)<=2/D gives

 strip<=2t*K(K+1)/D<=4t*K^2/D,

because integral_(D-K)^D(D-floor y)dy=K(K+1)/2. Therefore

 H_exit,forcing<=12R/(k+1-alpha)*K^2/D
                <=6pR/(k+1-alpha)*N^(2rho-1).             (14)

The second step uses D=floor(4N/p)>=2N/p when D>=2. All fixed rational
rho<=3/4 give a square-root-or-smaller forcing. The domain includes
rho=0; no zero-width division or exception is needed. If a>D-K, the
actual exit is narrower and the same upper bound holds. D=1 has only
the already-paid terminal term. D=0 is empty. Thus the rule covers the
small source horizons without a missing base range.

This exit rule corrects the naive real-width estimate that omitted the
one-cofactor floor jump. No uncorrected D-y estimate is used here.

5. Exact reduced core and an unconditional logarithmic gain
-----------------------------------------------------------
Define the joined remaining core by

 C_core(N)=c_alpha integral_eps^tau G_M(t)t^(-alpha)
             integral_a^b |p^-t A_N(y)-q^-t B_N(y)|y^(-t-1)dy dt. (15)

For D>=2, (9),(11),(14) prove the explicit all-integer recovery

 |J(N)|<=B_flow(N)
 <=C_core(N)+6R/(k+1-alpha)*N^theta
              +6pR/(k+1-alpha)*N^(2rho-1)
              +36R/(k-alpha)*L_N^k.                     (16)

For D=0/1 omit empty head/core/exit pieces and retain the applicable
terminal bound. In particular theta=1/2 and rho=3/4 leave only an
interior joint prefix-variation leaf unpaid. These endpoint choices can
be derived by searching the exact power inequalities theta<=1/2 and
2rho-1<=1/2; they need not be supplied as favorite scientific formulas.

There is also a useful unconditional baseline, proving more than an
absolute-source restatement. For D>=2 use(10) on the ENTIRE prefix
interval, then complete smooth-count control only at its end. One gets

 B_flow(N)<=[6R*Gamma(k+1-alpha)/(1-tau)]
                *D/(log D)^(k+1-alpha)
                +36R/(k-alpha)*L_N^k.                   (17)

Extending the positive t integral to(0,infinity) proves the first term.
It is finite because k+1-alpha>0. If desired replace Gamma by the
adjacent-factorial bound above, and log D by log(2D) using the explicit
factor2^(k+1-alpha)<=2^(k+1). Equation(17) supplies an all-scale
logarithmic improvement: the carrier variation contributes one extra
small-t factor, while its nonvarying terminal mass is paid by conservation.
It retains power1, so it is NOT an RH-strength estimate. At the scientific
k=3,alpha=1/5, its denominator power is19/5. No asymptotic score or source
observation was used in this theorem.

6. Constructive arithmetic frontier and limits of recovery
----------------------------------------------------------
The remaining (15) preserves both source channels inside each absolute
value. Separating A and B with triangle discards this possible signed
control. An exact decomposition is

 S_N(y,t)=p^-t[A_N(y)-B_N(y)]
                       +(p^-t-q^-t)B_N(y),
 0<p^-t-q^-t<=t log(q/p).                                (18)

The second channel has an extra small-t factor. This is a paid coefficient
gain, not an estimate of its actual partial sum. The first channel is a
joint prime-scaled floor difference, not a complete smooth count.

Another concrete compiler route exposes the missing boundary divisor
correlations. Define M_M(z)=sum_(d<=z)mu_M(d), zero below1, and

 T(Z;y)=sum_(y<d<=Z)mu_M(d)floor(Z/d)
       =sum_(m<=floor(Z/y))[M_M(Z/m)-M_M(y)], y>=1.        (19)

Each summand is zero if its upper endpoint equals y, so the inclusive
floor convention is harmless at equality. Finite hyperbola interchange
proves(19) exactly; it assumes no estimate of M_M. Put

 Q_p(y)=T(4N/p;y)-3T(2N/p;y)+2T(N/p;y),
 Q_q(y)=T(4N/q;y)-3T(2N/q;y)+2T(N/q;y).

Then the exact signed prefix transport identity is

 S_N(y,t)=S_N(D,t)-[p^-t Q_p(y)-q^-t Q_q(y)].              (20)

It retains ALL cutoff entries, unit terms and different p/q ranges.
For y>=sqrt N its hyperbola sum uses only O(sqrt N) small cofactor
indices m, but the interval endpoints M_M(Z/m) can remain at the
current source horizon. Thus this is not a free smaller-scale recurrence.
A proof must pay the joint reciprocal-interval forcing, including its
largest endpoints and every subtraction of M_M(y). Assuming square-root
Mertens bounds on the companion simply imports the missing arithmetic.

Equations(18)-(20) give a detector source-bound grammar distinct from
renaming the middle integral: exact joint prefix/tail transforms, a paid
small-t channel factor, finite reciprocal interval correlations, and
explicit cofactor head/exit power rules. A uniform estimate of the
remaining joined weighted correlations is still a conjectural leaf.
It cannot be supplied by the complete smooth-count identity or by
calibration on saved source arrays.

There is no algebraic recovery of all variation from total mass alone.
Even two generic opposite fractional masses of the same size at distinct
carriers have zero total mass but positive optimal transport cost.
Scaling their capacities preserves the zero endpoint while increasing
variation arbitrarily. This is a scope-limited counterexample to a free
conservation-to-core rule, not a replacement source or a disproof for
actual Mobius. No obstruction to every actual-Mobius fractional-flow
square-root bound was established here.

General gamma>0 and tau<1 suffice for all flow, conservation and component
rules above. To recover the FULL058 RH-sensitive source also require
gamma*(k-alpha)>=1/2 and tau>=1/2, or separately paid endpoints. Finite
flow checks or the unconditional logarithmic bound do not meet that
missing middle premise. Source-free controls replacing mu by |mu| are
different convolutions and cannot inherit(7).

Disposition: review complete; edits stopped. Optimal fractional flow,
polylog terminal/leftover control, square-root head/exit control and the
logarithmic baseline are proved on their stated domains. The remaining
joined core variation, full RH-strength estimate and RH remain OPEN.
