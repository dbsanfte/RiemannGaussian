ANALYTICAL ATTEMPTS ON THE ALREADY FROZEN DIVISOR-DUAL PROOF QUEUE

This extension begins after pilot-001's detector selection, proof queue and
fresh evaluation have been frozen and independently audited. It does not
change those formulas or the original search sources. A rejected uniform
matrix claim is not a counterexample to the actual Mobius bound or RH.
These arguments are ordinary written proofs with independent computational
certificate checks, not machine-formalized proofs.

1. A COVERAGE OBSTRUCTION THAT IS INDEPENDENT OF THE GRAM WEIGHT.
Let N=4X and take any vector supported on 2X<n<=4X. Each such index has
only its own multiple below N, so C_N*z=z. If z_n is one precisely on
unselected rows in this interval and zero elsewhere, then G_w*z=0.
Also z_1=0: there is no unit forcing cost on this witness.
Write J=number of unselected rows in (2X,4X], and J_3=number in (2X,3X].
The parent energy is zero and the child energy is
z^T H_X z = sum_(t=2X)^(4X-1) #(unselected rows in (2X,t])^2
           >= X*J_3^2.
Consequently
z^T S_(X,q) z <= D(X,q)*J-X*J_3^2 <= 2X*D(X,q)-X*J_3^2.
Neither Y nor its size enters this inequality.

For every FIXED integer q>=2,
D<=2*c_D*(1+ceil(q^r))*X^(1+1/q)=o_q(X^2).
If J_3>=kappa*X eventually for some kappa>0, the quadratic form is
strictly negative at all sufficiently large dyadic X. This proves failure
for EVERY fixed q>=2. The existential q0 in the frozen candidate claim
cannot avoid this obstruction. Three exact witness levels are illustrative
checks of the universal argument, not a substitute for its quantifiers.

An unselected residue class of a fixed period m gives J_3>=X/m-1, hence
J_3>=X/(2m) for X>=2m. Every residue mask misses such a class. A finite
union of divisibility classes with bases >=2 misses the class 1 modulo
their least common multiple. A constant zero mask misses every row.
For prefix rows <=floor((4X)^beta), beta<1, all tail rows are eventually
unselected, since (4X)^beta/(2X) tends to zero. For square rows,
J_3>=X-sqrt(3X)>=X/2 when X>=12. Thus these pruned-row matrix families
fail regardless of their Gram powers or coefficients.

The barrier is about this universal PSD certificate. It does not say that
the selected arithmetic identities are false or useless in other proofs.
It does show why merely increasing their weights cannot repair this matrix.

2. A GRAM-COEFFICIENT OBSTRUCTION EVEN WITH EVERY TAIL ROW.
Take z_n=1 on (2X,4X] and zero otherwise. Again C_N*z=z and
z^T G_w z<=2X for every boolean mask. Its exact child energy is
T(X)=sum_(j=0)^(2X-1) j^2=(8/3)X^3-2X^2+X/3.
Therefore
z^T S z <= 2X*(D+Y)-T(X).
For Y=c_Y*X^2, c_Y<4/3, and any fixed q>=2, this is eventually negative:
the cubic leading coefficient is 2*c_Y-8/3<0 and 2X*D=o_q(X^3).
This independent argument refutes those exact templates even when every
divisor row is available. The threshold is a sufficient obstruction, not
an assertion that larger coefficients suffice for positivity.

3. WHAT REMAINS FOR THE FULL NONUNIT DIVISOR GRAM.
For w(k)=1_(k>1), G_0=C^T diag(0,1,...,1) C and ker(G_0)=span(mu).
Congruence by C^(-1) gives
C^(-T) S C^(-1) = D*C^(-T)*C^(-1)-C^(-T)*H*C^(-1)
                   +Y*diag(0,1,...,1).
Its (1,1) entry is EXACTLY a_11=D*Q(4X)-Delta(X).
Every global PSD proof must bound this actual signed arithmetic entry.
Deleting it, fitting it on finite prefixes, or hiding it behind a large Y
does not prove the necessary inequality. It is independent of Y.

There is also an explicit conditional sufficient comparison. Normalize
u=mu/sqrt(Q), put a=D-Delta/Q>0, h=||H||<=8X^2, and let g be the least
positive eigenvalue of G_0 on u's orthogonal complement. The inverse
divisor matrix has row sum <=2sqrt(N) and column sum <=N, hence
g>=1/(2N^(3/2)). The orthogonal block is >=D-h+Y*g and its coupling
norm is <=h. Thus
Y*g > h+h^2/a
is sufficient by the block Schur complement. If the arithmetic gap
Delta<=D*Q/2 were known, a>=D/2 and the elementary D>=2*c_D*X gives
the sufficient buffer
Y > 128*X^(7/2)+(1024/c_D)*X^(9/2).
The named gap remains UNPROVED for the selected templates at every scale.
This conditional computation identifies the missing signed lemma; it does
not supply it. High Gram powers can pay the orthogonal comparison while
leaving the nullspace arithmetic unchanged.

4. CANDIDATE DISPOSITIONS AND FEEDBACK.
Each queue entry keeps the exact program, its sealed hash and its fresh
finite outcome counts. Coverage or insufficient-coefficient obstructions
produce 'disproved_uniform_matrix_claim'; full nonunit cases are
'unresolved_uniform_matrix_claim', with the exact missing signed entry and
conditional Schur obligations. The proved all-scale inverse identity and
ordinary q=1 power-three calibration are recorded as subclaims, not as
proofs of an unresolved candidate's critical family.

The original D-family imposes polynomial growth on the constant as q grows.
The generic RH-sufficient energy criterion allows an arbitrary finite
constant for each positive epsilon. Therefore this detector family is a
sufficient route to RH, not a search of every RH-compatible constant law.
Fresh future grammars should separate exponent decay from constant growth,
and include other finite growth programs or existential per-level constants.
The r=0 template additionally forces a bounded critical constant and the
stronger simple-critical-zero consequence; it is retained but not promoted
over the positive-r candidates.

Analytical feedback must be applied only to a NEW frozen search. In a new
run, the universal coverage obstruction can prune this particular PSD
family without deleting the arithmetic operator from other representations.
The constant-law limitation is explicit, and the unresolved full nonunit
leads must not be described as newly proved signed arithmetic control.
