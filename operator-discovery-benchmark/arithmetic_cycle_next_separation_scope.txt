EXACT SEPARATION AND ADAPTIVE FINITE CERTIFICATE EXPANSION

Posthoc scope note; no frozen pilot source or outcome is changed. The pilot
reported8 finite certificates (all mu at X1),48 true bounds with no certificate
found,8 false bounds (all c at X1),0 missing. These finite arithmetic labels do
not turn solver non-success into mathematical infeasibility. This note describes

It supplies no novel arithmetic estimate, global inequality or RH proof.

1. The finite cone from first principles.
Let V be the finite rational monomial space containing EVERY monomial in the
fixed lhs L=D-Delta_X, in every declared square column S_j=P_j^2, and in every
declared relation column R_i=Q_i*rho_i or exact grouped sum thereof. The search
cone is
 C={sum_j w_j S_j+sum_i v_i R_i: w_j>=0, v_i arbitrary real}.
A rational certificate additionally requires rational weights. Any rational
linear functional y on V with
 y(S_j)>=0 for ALL declared squares,
 y(R_i)=0 for ALL declared free signed relations,
 y(L)<0
proves L is NOT in C: applying y to any representation yields a nonnegative
value, contradicting y(L)<0. In a plus/minus nonnegative-column encoding,
y must annihilate both signs of every relation column; imposing only one sign
is insufficient. All equalities and signs must be checked exactly with
Fractions, after any floating LP merely proposes a dual vector.

The underlying cone is finitely generated plus a subspace, hence a polyhedral
closed cone. Finite-dimensional separating-hyperplane/Farkas reasoning gives
such a real functional when L is outside this cone. With rational column/lhs
data, a feasible strict rational separator can be obtained by scaling y(L)<=-1
and solving the resulting rational linear inequalities; existence does not
justify using an approximate floating separator without exact reconstruction.
The direct contradiction above is sufficient for every admitted finite witness
and does not depend on trusting an LP infeasibility status.

2. Certificate obstruction is not an arithmetic counterexample.
The separator is a formal monomial functional, not necessarily evaluation at
an arithmetic coefficient assignment, nor a positive functional on ALL squares.
It is positive only on the declared basis squares and zero only on the declared
multiplier relations. Therefore y(L)<0 does NOT mean actual L(mu) or L(c)<0.
It rules out only the current finite cone. In particular, an actual true bound
among the48 solver non-successes may have an exact cone separator. Conversely,
solver failure without such a witness establishes neither cone separation nor
false actual arithmetic. Actual false bound labels must come from independent
complete coefficient evaluation or another valid arithmetic proof.

3. How a witness guides expansion.
For a candidate missing relation multiplier T*rho already supported in V,
if y(T*rho)!=0, its freely signed column is incompatible with the separator;
adding both signs invalidates this PARTICULAR witness. A grouped relation
extension must evaluate its COMPLETE sum; a violated atomic term need not make
the grouped sum nonzero. For a proposed new square P^2 supported in V,
if y(P^2)<0, adding that nonnegative square invalidates this witness.
A new relation for which y=0 or square with y>=0 leaves the witness valid,
assuming every original constraint and target remains unchanged.

If a proposed column contains a monomial absent from V, y has NO declared
value on it. Do not silently insert0 and claim a determined violation or an
unrestricted extended separator. One must either restrict the test to supported
columns, explicitly search for a full extension y' agreeing on V and satisfying
ALL new constraints, or solve a new complete extended dual from scratch.
An explicitly chosen zero extension is just one proposed extended functional;
its validity must be tested on every new column, and its failure says nothing
about existence of another extension. Missing monomials are therefore unknown
for the original witness-guided evaluation, not arithmetic zeros.

Invalidating one separator does NOT imply certificate feasibility: another
separator may remain. Every expansion must resynthesize and check the complete
primal coefficient identity and nonnegative weights exactly. If unsuccessful,
a new exact separator can certify the larger declared cone's obstruction.
All previous candidates, dual failures, primal failures, resources and alias
expansions must be retained. This is an adaptive proof-basis audit, not a
posthoc change of a frozen pilot's success/failure status.

4. General enumeration without a nominated winning identity.
A new search may enumerate exact signed rational multiplier coefficients in a
fixed height/denominator ordering, monomial degree, index horizon and arithmetic
predicate family; bounds must precede enumeration and evaluation. Enumerate
square templates and relation multipliers jointly, including missing unit,
prime-power, coprime-product, squarefree-zero and inverse-row relations ONLY
under their existing exact domain guards. Cost limits must charge polynomial
expansion, primal/dual monomial dimensions, coefficient bits, relation predicates,
complete source/product horizons and exact solving. Resource exhaustion is
missing, not mathematical separation or a false bound.
A reusable motif must specify its variable-index map, arithmetic premises,
complete product boundaries, coefficient norms and initializer. Similar-looking
finite weights are not evidence of a proved scale law. Motif extraction can
propose grouped templates, then independent symbolic verification must establish
the claimed family before any all-scale mathematical admission.

This is finite proof expressivity, not newly supplied cancellation information.

shows that squarefree support, coprime cycles and finitely normalized prime

partial-normalization obligation remains explicit after a dual-guided expansion.

The constructive next objective is therefore JOINTLY grouped square AND relation
templates with a proved uniform scale law, rather than unrestricted per-index
fitting. For full critical signed control, the template must ultimately prove:
