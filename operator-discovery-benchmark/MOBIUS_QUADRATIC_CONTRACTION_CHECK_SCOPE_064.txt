EXACT LOCAL ADMISSION RULE

The lift uses the exact legacy063 field set. Operational local inputs are
parameters p/q, N,y,H,K, the two complete linear_terms arrays Pf, and the
two complete long_kernels_after_fill arrays PG. Metadata is not promoted
to independent evidence. The strict certificate contains only
 schema,N,y,H,K,conditioned_primes,endpoints,budget,
with schema mobius-quadratic-contraction-v1. Each endpoint contains only
 ratio,rows,l1; each complete j=1..H row only j,terms; each term only
 index,coefficient. The endpoint ratios are exactly p/q and1. Primes are
actual, distinct, ascending and at most B=max(H,K), or the list is empty.
Rational text is canonical Fraction text, at most4096 numerator/denominator
bits and2500 characters. Zero terms are omitted. Claimed term indices are
positive, distinct, ascending squarefree integers. Booleans are rejected
where integers or exact rational text are required.

For each endpoint and every j, the checker expands the COMPLETE expression
 c_j=sum_(m<=H/j)mu(m)*Pf[jm]
     -sum_(m<=H/j,ell<=K)mu(m)*mu(ell)*PG[jm,ell]
     -sum_(m<=H/(j+1))mu(m)*Pf[(j+1)m]
     +sum_(m<=H/(j+1),ell<=K)mu(m)*mu(ell)*PG[(j+1)m,ell].
The omitted H+1 branch is zero because its multiples range is empty.
Squarefulness is independently sieved, including squareful m and ell.
For squarefree m,ell it uses key m*ell/gcd(m,ell)^2. Thus a shared prime
occurs twice and cancels from the sign monomial; mu(2)^2=1 rather than
mu(4)=0. The unit is the constant monomial with index1.

Every linear and quadratic contribution enters the SAME exact dictionary
before any absolute value. Coefficients cancel there, including the unit.
For every conditioned prime dividing a surviving squarefree key, that
prime is removed once and its coefficient receives the factor-1. These
terms coalesce again, with exact zero removal. Every claimed coefficient
is compared to this complete independently expanded dictionary. Cardinality
checks plus ascending uniqueness prevent missing, extra or repeated terms.
The exact endpoint price is sum_(j,key)|coefficient|, checked against l1;
budget must equal the maximum of BOTH endpoint prices, with neither
underpricing nor arbitrary overpricing accepted.

FINITE THEOREM AND ITS LIMIT

Assign arbitrary +/-1 signs to unconditioned primes, fix each conditioned
actual-prime sign at-1, set the unit sign1 and squareful inputs zero, and
extend multiplicatively on squarefree integers. Each surviving monomial
then has magnitude1. The triangle inequality bounds the whole endpoint
variation by its checked price. The SAME shared prime-sign assignment is
used in both arithmetic factors; independent arbitrary index signs would
not justify the overlap reduction.

Conditional source admission establishes affine dependence of Pf/PG on r.
For r=(1-t)*p/q+t, 0<=t<=1, the same assignment yields
 sum_j|c_j(r)| <= (1-t)*sum_j|c_j(p/q)|+t*sum_j|c_j(1)|
               <= max(endpoint_l1).
The proved bound therefore covers every real r in[p/q,1], at the supplied
fixed N,y,H, and even all such assignments to the remaining prime signs.
The ordinary Mobius sequence is one permitted assignment. Conditioning
every actual prime<=B leaves constants and gives exact finite arithmetic
evaluation at the endpoints. This alone supplies no new growth theorem.
Products/symmetric-difference keys can reach scale H*K; smaller input
queries and the reindexing do not prove exponent contraction.
