EXACT SCALE-DEPENDENT TWISTS AND MOMENT MIXTURES 045

Purpose and scope
-----------------
This new coefficient language implements the moving rational twist proposed
in COMPATIBLE_FLOOR_BOUND_HANDOFF.txt. Its parent continuation is
floor-residual-results/pilot-002/analytical_followup_001/next_search_input.json.
The implementation does not import frozen043/044 compilers, read source
arrays, evaluate floor norms, apply actual searches, or change frozen files.
The certificates prove scalar coefficient facts only. Whole signed norm,
uniform forcing, convergence, original-core bounds and RH remain OPEN.

Grammar and APIs
----------------
The exact weight specification is
  {kind: "drift_mixture", a: canonical rational, theta: canonical rational,
   terms: [{rate: canonical rational, weight: canonical rational}, ...]}.
Here a>0, 0<theta<1; rates are strictly increasing, distinct and positive;
signed weights are nonzero and sum exactly to 1. There are 1..8 terms.
No denominator bank or coefficient sign restriction is imposed.

beta(a,theta,N,meter=None) returns the exact Fraction
  beta_N=ceil(a*L(N)^theta)/L(N), L(N)=N.bit_length(), N>=2.
beta_certificate returns floor-drift-beta-v1 with N,L,ceiling,beta, the exact
power-comparison data and prospective power-bit estimate. If theta=u/v and
a=A/B in lowest terms, the ceiling z is licensed by
  (z-1)^v B^v < A^v L^u <= z^v B^v.
The compiler obtains z by integer bisection with upper bound ceil(a*L).
There is no numerical approximation to this ceiling.

synthesize_mixture(rates,a="1",theta="1/2",meter=None) returns the grammar
specification. It sorts any admitted distinct positive rational input set
and solves the full square moment system by exact Gaussian elimination:
  sum w_j=1, sum w_j*r_j^k=0, k=1..s-1, s=number of rates.
The solve is not a hard-coded binomial construction. Exact replay then
checks its order. Distinct rates give a nonsingular Vandermonde matrix.

mixture_certificate(spec,meter=None) accepts general normalized signed
mixtures, not just synthesized ones. It returns
floor-drift-mixture-certificate-v1 with full moments k=0..s, first nonzero
order m, leading_moment, vanishing_orders, sum_abs_weights,
weighted_abs_order_moment, signed leading_error_coefficient,
coefficient_rate, energy_rate, and kernel_certificate. The certificate
also explicitly denies whole signed norm, forcing, convergence and RH claims.

compile_mixture(spec,n,N,meter=None), also called compilation_certificate,
returns floor-drift-compilation-v1. It includes the exact beta certificate,
precision_bits, every signed rate/weight/exponent and rounded_value, their
exact rational total value, and rounding_error_bound. mixture_value returns
only the Fraction total. Any integer n>=1 is allowed under resource guards,
whether or not n<=N. Compilation retains every sign.

CompilationContext(spec,N,meter=None) snapshots and parses the spec once,
certifies beta once, and caches compile(n)/value(n). profile() returns values
for n=2..N, with an explicit allocation cap. DriftCompiler(meter=None)
owns contexts keyed by exact (a,theta,rate-weight pairs,N) tuples, and a
shared raw-atom cache keyed by (n,precision_bits,exponent numerator,
exponent denominator). Different finite drift recipes with equal exponents
reuse the exact roots while retaining their own signed mixture sums. There
are no file hashes or external coefficient observations in these caches.

coefficient_error_bound(spec,n,N,meter=None) returns
floor-drift-coefficient-bound-v1 with Taylor, optional simplex, raw,
rounding and total rational bounds. It is valid for every admitted positive
integer n, and explicitly remains coefficient control only.

Moment order and local asymptotics
---------------------------------
Write M_k=sum w_j*r_j^k. The evaluator finds the first nonzero M_m exactly.
Some m<=s must exist: if all M_1..M_s were zero, applying the annihilating
polynomial product(x-r_j) would give its nonzero constant coefficient equal
to zero. A synthesized s-term mixture has m=s and
  M_s=(-1)^(s+1)*product(r_j),
which is also checked by exact moment arithmetic in synthetic tests.

Since 0<=ceil(a*L^theta)-a*L^theta<1 and theta>0,
  beta_N/(a*L^(theta-1))->1,
  beta_N->0,
  beta_N*log(N)->infinity.
For fixed n>=2, Taylor expansion of P(u)=sum w_j exp(-r_j*u),
u=beta_N*log(n), gives
  1-P(n,N)=C*log(n)^m*L^(-m*(1-theta))
            +o(L^(-m*(1-theta))),
  C=(-1)^(m+1)*M_m*a^m/m! !=0.
All quantities except the positive log(n)^m are exact rationals. Thus the
local coefficient rate is m*(1-theta), and the corresponding necessary
fixed-cell energy rate is 2*m*(1-theta). This module does not itself bind a
complete floor recipe or forcing witness. In a pure actual-Mobius wrapper,
the coefficient gap is mu(n)*(1-P(n,N)); it vanishes at indices with mu(n)=0.
Products with old weights may change or cancel the first local error, so
the wrapper must analyze the combined formula and its actual bound exponent.

Taylor remainder and rounding
-----------------------------
For x>=0 the integral remainder of exp(-x), truncated at degree m-1, has
absolute value at most x^m/m!. Unit normalization and exact M_1..M_(m-1)=0
therefore imply, for every u>=0,
  |1-P(u)| <= u^m/m! * sum |w_j|*r_j^m.
This is valid for general signed mixtures even when P leaves [0,1].

The rounding policy is p=4*ceil(log2(N)), computed as
4*(N-1).bit_length(). Each n^(-rate*beta_N) is independently rounded down
to the dyadic lattice 2^-p. For exponent A/B>0, the returned numerator k
satisfies
  k^B*n^A <= 2^(pB) < (k+1)^B*n^A.
Integer bisection proves the comparison directly. Exact boundary roots and
the unit n=1 are retained. The signed mixture itself is not rounded again.
Triangle inequality pays
  |P_exact-P_rounded| <= 2^-p * sum |w_j|.
This error is flat relative to every inverse log power at fixed n, so it
does not change the local leading rate. Signed weights can make the rounded
mixture slightly leave a raw certified range; no unqualified positivity
claim is made for the rounded profile.

For a completely rational instantiated error bound, the evaluator uses
log(n)<=ceil(log2(n)), with value0 at n=1, and u_upper=beta_N*that integer.
It pays the Taylor bound above and the rounding term. At n=1 all roots are
exactly1 and unit normalization gives an exactly zero error, so its rounding
allowance is zero. This elementary logarithmic upper bound follows from
log(2)<1 and n<=2^ceil(log2(n)).

Separately verified exponential-convolution range certificate
------------------------------------------------------------
kernel_certificate(spec,meter=None) expands exact rational polynomials
with coefficients in ascending order. It admits the extra theorem only if
  sum_j w_j*r_j*product_(i!=j)(s+r_i) = product_i r_i
coefficient by coefficient. It exposes every numerator summand, full
numerator, denominator product(s+r_i), and expected constant numerator.
Unsupported general mixtures return NOT_CERTIFIED and no raw range claim.

Here is an elementary proof of the admitted representation. Let
K_j(t)=r_j exp(-r_j*t) for t>=0. Its convolution h=K_1*...*K_s is nonnegative
and has integral1. For distinct rates, repeated elementary integration
  integral_0^u exp(-r*t) exp(-R*(u-t)) dt
    =(exp(-r*u)-exp(-R*u))/(R-r)
shows that h is a finite sum of distinct exponentials. Its coefficients
are the partial-fraction coefficients of product_j r_j/(z+r_j).
The certified polynomial identity identifies those coefficients as w_j*r_j.
Thus h(u)=sum w_j*r_j exp(-r_j*u)=-P'(u). Since P(0)=1 and P tends0,
  P(u)=integral_u^infinity h(t) dt, 0<=P(u)<=1.
This proof can equivalently be written with elementary Laplace integrals;
it requires no unverified numerical transform or zero information.

The positive convolution has the simplex-integral bound
  h(u) <= product(r_j)*u^(s-1)/(s-1)!,
because the exponential integrand is at most1 on the nonnegative simplex.
Integrating from0 to u gives
  0<=1-P(u)<=product(r_j)*u^s/s!.
The exact polynomial identity forces all lower moments to vanish and m=s.
The instantiated raw bound is the minimum of Taylor, simplex, and1; the
same explicit absolute rounding allowance is then added. This improved
scalar theorem still does not bound a signed floor residual norm.

Why the frozen cutoff guard does not automatically apply
-------------------------------------------------------
This profile depends on the scale through beta_N. Its beta_N*log(N)
diverges while beta_N tends0. It is not a fixed function w(n/N), nor a
static convex mixture of cutoff powers. The frozen044 fixed-cutoff Mellin
obstruction cannot be attached solely because the raw profile happens to
lie in [0,1]. Neither failure of that old guard nor the present scalar
certificate closes any global all-prime bound.

Resources and metering
----------------------
Implementation rational input/intermediate/output bits are capped at4096;
integer arguments are capped at4096 bits; terms at8. Exact power operations
have a prospective400,000-bit cap, based on the numerator/denominator powers
actually needed, not a rational-denominator whitelist. Huge denominators
may honestly fail this cost guard. Explicit profile allocation is capped
at1,048,576. These finite compilation limits do not truncate the mathematical
formula's definition for all N. Default Meter permits200,000,000 charged
operations and600 seconds, and may be replaced by a larger shared Meter.
It records integer root/ceil comparisons, rational operations, atom hits,
maximum and summed prospective power bits, and charged work. Charged work
is a reproducible conservative proxy, not measured CPU instructions.
