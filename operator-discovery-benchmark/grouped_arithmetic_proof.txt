FACTOR-CERTIFIED NORMS AND GCD PRODUCTS; FINITE GROUPED PROOF SCOPE

This supplied arithmetic language extends the frozen polynomial language. It
proves exact actual relations and conditional finite SOS positivity. It proves
no full critical energy bound, uniform scale law, formal global mathematical
certificate or RH result. No actual coefficient evaluation is a language axiom.

1. Factor witnesses and source horizons.
A witness for n>=1 is a sorted unique list[p,e] of prime p and positive integer
e, with exact product n. For n1 the list is empty. Strict integer types exclude
booleans; composite, unordered, duplicate, incomplete and overlarge factors are
rejected. Prime validation uses exact trial divisibility. The declared horizon
bounds n,a,b and all polynomial variable indices, and a separate resource cap
precedes generation. Before exponentiation the exponent and power bit/work proxy
are bounded. Exact product is checked without silently ignoring surplus factors.
The generator factor_witness uses only integer factorization, not any actual
signed sequence input. Witnesses are independently checkable. Scalar work caps
are explicit computation limits, not total memory/bit complexity guarantees or
mathematical restrictions on factorization.

2. Norm relations at ALL admitted indices.
Actual mu(p)=-1 and mu(p^e)=0 for e>=2; actual c=mu*lambda has
c(p^e)=2(-1)^e. Both multiply over coprime prime-power factors. Therefore
 mu(n)^2=1_(n squarefree), c(n)^2=4^omega(n), including n1.
The norm polynomial is x_n^2 minus the certified corresponding integer.
It contains no signed coefficient value axiom. Determining the norm requires
complete factor witnesses for the declared indices; it is not an observed
finite norm reused as an unproved all-scale fact. The old prime_power relation
still supplies actual signs only at its certified prime powers.

3. Gcd-product relations, all zero and degenerate cases.
Let g=gcd(a,b), u=a/g, v=b/g, so gcd(u,v)=1. Actual mu obeys
 mu(a)mu(b)=1_(g squarefree)*1_((g,uv)=1)*mu(u)mu(v).
Proof by each prime exponent: a shared prime whose smaller exponent>=2 makes
both products zero. A shared prime with unequal exponents makes one left
coefficient zero and is excluded by the compatibility mask. A shared prime
with equal exponent1 contributes (-1)^2=1. Unshared primes are exactly the
remaining u/v factors, including their own squarefree zeros. Thus the identity
also holds when any coefficient vanishes. At a=b it is the norm identity;
at g1 it is ordinary coprime multiplicativity; a=b=1 is still valid.
The admitted polynomial is xa*xb minus that exact constant mask*xu*xv.

For c, complete multiplicative lambda and the number of distinct prime supports
give, for all positive g,u,
 c(gu)=c(g)c(u)/2^omega(gcd(g,u)).
Indeed parity of total prime exponents is additive, while a shared prime's
absolute local factor2 is counted twice on the right and once on the left.
Since u,v are coprime, their shared-prime sets with g are disjoint. Hence
 2^omega(gcd(g,uv))*c(a)c(b)=c(g)^2*c(u)c(v).
This is polynomial after clearing the positive integer denominator. The overlap
count is exactly the number of common primes whose certified exponents in a,b
are UNEQUAL. The gcd exponents are their minima. The norm factor in the mu law
and denominator in the c law are therefore derived from factor_a/factor_b,
not a coefficient table. All variable indices g,u,v<=max(a,b) remain within
the input horizon. No product index ab is introduced by this gcd relation.
Formal polynomial multiplication xa*xb remains two variables, not xab unless a
separate admitted coprime_product relation supplies it and charges its horizon.

4. Finite compiler soundness.
The extended compiler admits only the above factor-certified relations and the
unchanged frozen unit/coprime-product/squarefree-zero/prime-power/inverse-row
whitelist. Unknown shapes or custom per-index signed values are rejected.
The canonical serialized sparse polynomial format and exact rational algebra
are unchanged. It checks coefficientwise

On complete actual arithmetic each rho_i vanishes, so lhs>=0. No coefficient
response is needed to verify this polynomial identity. Negative square weights,

it. Both lhs and all terms require the declared variable horizon. Grouped
proofs must expand every term before matching; positivity belongs to individual
squares with nonnegative weights, not to an unsigned subset of cross terms.
This establishes conditional finite nonnegativity only. Limits on scalar work,
terms and factor predicates return explicit failure rather than partial proof.

Uniformly justified further arithmetic information must exclude such models;
