NORMALIZED ROOT ARITHMETIC AND SIGNED PROFILE CANCELLATION

Scope. The stage supports positive rational arithmetic roots and searches
actual inverse-coefficient operators. It can prove rapid signed cancellation
when an exact arithmetic profile collapses. That case erases ordinary zeta
zeros and cannot prove RH. Noncollapsing critical estimates remain unproved.
Numerical successes never supply an all-scale premise.

1. Semantics and honest ordinary arithmetic scale
Take rational r>0, ell>0, K>max(1,1/r), R=K/ell, and signed rational c_m
on nonnegative integer moments. Let
 g(u)=exp(R)*sum_m c_m*u^(K+ell*m)*exp(-R*u^ell),
 D_r(X)=sum_{j,d>=1}mu(d)*g(X/(j*d^r)),
 V_r(M)=D_r(M^r)-A*M^r.
The unsigned reference replaces mu(d) by 1. All sums converge absolutely
since K>1 and r*K>1. The peak of the moment-zero kernel is at u=1, so
changing ell or K does not silently move the scale most sensitive to the
arithmetic. The primary coefficient basis is u^(ell*m), not (R*u^ell)^m.
Using X=M^r compares arithmetic index coverage at comparable d scales.
M is exact positive rational and X is its exact positive real rational
power; rounding X to an integer is not part of the operator definition.

2. A complete global moment series, not a truncated lattice
For w=K+ell*h>max(1,1/r), the full moments are
 inverse: zeta(w)/zeta(r*w); unit: zeta(w)*zeta(r*w).
Expanding exp(-R*(X/u)^ell), each atom at M has
 exp(R)*M^(r*(K+ell*m))*sum_{h>=0}(-R*M^(r*ell))^h/h!
                 *moment(K+ell*(m+h)).
Taylor's remainder for exp(-y), y>=0, is at most y^L/L!. Therefore the
complete error is at most
 exp(R)*M^(r*(K+ell*m))*(R*M^(r*ell))^L/L!
                 *zeta(K+ell*(m+L))*zeta(r*(K+ell*(m+L))).
All zeta arguments in this bound exceed 1; no RH or prime-distribution
estimate is used. Dynamic precision pays for the exp(z) cancellation
in the alternating series as well as exp(R) normalization and scale powers.
Numerical resource exhaustion is a missing evaluation, not a counterexample.

The independent evaluator subtracts an explicitly factorized rectangular
prefix j<=J, d<=D from the complete moments, evaluates that prefix by
exponentials, and evaluates only its remaining tail by reverse Horner sums.
Its Taylor length and precision differ. The prefix is genuine actual mu
from an independent prime-factor sieve; both evaluators retain every tail.

3. Mellin transform and exact centering
With v=(K-s)/ell, P(v)=sum_m c_m*R^(-m)*(v)_m,
 W(s)=exp(R)*Gamma(v)*P(v)*R^(-v)/ell.
The ordinary-index Mellin transform of the raw observable is
 (1/r)*W(z/r)*zeta(z/r)/zeta(z), initially Re(z)>max(1,r).
Its known main residue at z=r is A=W(1)/zeta(r) for r!=1.
When r=1 the arithmetic ratio cancels exactly and A=0; substituting an
infinite value for zeta(1) is not a numerical centering operation.
Thus subtract A*M^r. The local Mellin part at M<1 has an absolute bound
sum_m exp(R)*|c_m|*zeta(w)*zeta(r*w)*M^(r*w).

4. A true weak bound, normalized exponent one, for 0<r<1
Write p=1/r>1. Summing unsigned positions over j gives
 #{(j,d): j*d^r<=t} <= sum_j(t/j)^p <= zeta(p)*t^p.
Partial summation and an absolute derivative envelope for each g_m yield
 |D_{r,m}(X)|<=B_m*X^p for every X>0,
 B_m=zeta(p)*exp(R)*R^(-beta_m)*Gamma(beta_m)
             *((K+ell*m)+ell*beta_m)/ell,
 beta_m=(K-p)/ell+m>0.
Consequently |V_r(M)|<=(sum_m|c_m|B_m+|A|)*M, M>=1.
The ordinary-zeta exponent remains 1 regardless of r; this is not RH strength.
The exact derivative X*D'_{r,m}(X)=(K+ell*m)D_{r,m}(X)
                         -R*ell*D_{r,m+1}(X)
implies a bounded derivative in M:
 |V'_r(M)|<=r*(sum_m|c_m|*((K+ell*m)B_m+R*ell*B_{m+1})+|A|).
The factor M^(r-1) from differentiation is exactly balanced by X^(p-1).
Integer-M estimates therefore extend to real M with this charged increment.

5. Single-operator RH bridge for roots below one
The critical target is, for every q, some free finite C_q, for every real
M>=1, |V_r(M)|<=C_q*M^(1/2+epsilon/q^nu)*log(e+M)^d,
where nu>0 is arbitrary rational. There is no growth restriction on C_q.
Assume also W(rho/r)!=0 for every right-half ordinary zero rho, all
heights and multiplicities. This finite-operator condition is certified
by the preceding frozen Hermite/Rouche backend with the converted P above;
the independent checker uses its separately coded Sturm and disk checks.

Cofinality makes the centered Mellin integral holomorphic in Re(z)>1/2.
At a hypothetical ordinary zero rho=beta+i*T with beta>1/2, denominator
holomorphy forces zeta(rho/r) to vanish with at least the same multiplicity.
For 0<r<1 its real part beta/r is larger than beta. Applying the same
argument repeatedly, after finitely many divisions it reaches Re>=1,
where zeta has no zeros. An exact power r^J<=1/2 gives a worst-case
certificate for every beta>1/2: beta/r^J>1. No zero height is sampled.
Heights increase; no accumulation argument or critical-line exception is
needed. Kernel preservation at every right-half zero remains mandatory.
For r extremely close to 1, a finite certificate budget may be exhausted;
that is not evidence against the estimate. At r>1 the preceding stage's
reflection/exception-set argument applies. At r=1 it does not apply.

Under RH the converse follows by shifting the same Mellin contour to
every fixed ordinary line sigma>1/2, using polynomial reciprocal-zeta
growth there and exponential Gamma decay. The pole at r is subtracted
exactly; a pole to the left of the new line need not be crossed. The usual
local/main-term split proves the identity in both cases. Thus the critical
uniform premise for a recovering root operator is RH-equivalent, not proved.

6. A supplied signed-cancellation proof grammar and behavioral recovery
Arithmetic profiles are finite ledgers of zeta(slope*w)^integer powers,
with exact positive rational slopes. Add exponents at equal slopes and
remove zero exponents. An empty ledger gives moment 1 at every argument
in the absolute-convergence halfplane. Generalized Dirichlet-series
uniqueness gives a unit point mass at position 1. Here it is the familiar
mu*1=delta_1 identity, proved also by the defining divisor inverse.
This cancellation happens BEFORE taking an absolute envelope and retains
the actual mu signs. The collapsed actual response is exactly g(M), r=1.

For any rational a>0, every positive real M has
 |g(M)|<=C_a*M^(-a),
 C_a=sum_m exp(R)*|c_m|*R^(-alpha_m)*alpha_m^alpha_m*exp(-alpha_m),
 alpha_m=(K+a)/ell+m>0.
This is a genuine signed actual-arithmetic all-scale theorem. It does not
control ordinary zeta: the arithmetic profile cancels its zeros identically,
even when the kernel preserves them. A kernel-only RH gate would be wrong.
The detector records strong cancellation and absent ordinary-zero recovery
as separate facts. It never counts this as achieved RH-strength information.

The anonymous learner receives moment values only, not r or arithmetic
names. It infers short linear recurrences by rational least squares on
Hankel rows, rounds coefficients under a declared resource budget, and
checks independent held-out moments. A universal recurrence is admitted
only when a supplied exact profile certificate proves it. Otherwise it
remains a numerical lead. The learner is not required to return a nominated
recurrence, shape or dilation to make the discovery run succeed.
Actual-response least squares separately proposes signed shapes with small
training centered response. It receives no pole locations or named answers.
Automatic Euler derivatives and an equal-slot random-shape ablation are
frozen before data. The exact profile grammar is supplied, not invented.

7. False tails and zero-erasure controls
Replace mu(d) by mu(d)+1_{d>H}, H=2^B. At each finite M the signed shape
change is enclosed by
 sum_m |c_m|*exp(R)*M^(r*w)*zeta(w)*H^(1-r*w)/(r*w-1), w=K+ell*m.
This comes from the full positive tail integral, not a finite fake prefix.
The counterfeit Dirichlet profile adds
 (1/r)*W(z/r)*zeta(z/r)*(zeta(z)-sum_{d<=H}d^(-z)).
At ordinary z=1 it has an uncancelled simple pole for r!=1, or double
pole for r=1, whenever P((K-1/r)/ell)!=0. The top coefficient is
 W(1/r)*zeta(1/r)/r, or W(1) at r=1. The finite polynomial term cannot
remove that top pole. A cofinal half-power uniform bound is therefore false.
If the kernel exactly masks the pole, this particular control is inapplicable.
No counterfeit arithmetic theorem is attributed to the actual coefficients.

Separately, true r=1 rapid certificates deliberately fail an RH inference
that uses kernel preservation alone. This control has a TRUE arithmetic
bound and a FALSE proposed zero-recovery inference. Arithmetic-type aliases
are likewise rejected; neither is mislabelled a growth counterexample.

8. Independent scope and status
Certificates charge the normalized Mellin factor 1/r, Gamma, R normalization,
kernel polynomial, local integrals, the exact pole at r, unit increments,
numerator zeta(z/r), and every positive approach-to-line gap. All point costs
are conditional on the signed uniform estimate. A finite q grid is not proof.
All frozen and fresh cases, failures, promotions and equal-slot ablations
are retained. Fresh-q constants are trained before final scales are created.
The known signed collapse is reported as calibration; no historical novelty,
new prime-distribution estimate, or proof of RH is claimed.
