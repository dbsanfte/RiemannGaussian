Coordinate mathematics/protocol:
  r=A*x-b, x>=0, initially x=0. Norm_j=dot(A_j,A_j)>0.
  lambda_ref=max(0,max_j dot(A_j,b)). Penalties lambda_ref*2^-e
  for e=1,3,...,31, followed by0 (17 phases). New objective is unit L1
  in scaled variables. Positive scaling preserves support and feasibility;
  no original L1 or convergence theorem is claimed.
  Warm start successive phases. Visit j in ascending retained position:
    new=max(0,x_j-(dot(A_j,r)+lambda)/Norm_j), delta=new-x_j;
    x_j=new; r+=delta*A_j, using only nonzero entries of A_j.
  Charge the whole declared sweep proxy BEFORE visiting coordinates; check
  deadlines within the loop. Stop a phase early if a complete sweep changes
  no stored coordinate. Refresh r from the matrix after every16 completed
  sweeps and at EVERY phase end. Charge refresh BEFORE computation. Use
  math.fsum of each row's -b and all sparse x_j*a_ij terms, with nnz+m
  temporary cells accounted, to reduce cancellation error during refresh.
  Norms/initial setup are charged as the setup proxy. No opaque LP/Gram solve.
  All arrays and diagnostics must remain finite. Norms use stable L2 e.g.
  math.hypot; reject zero target norm, nonpositive/nonfinite column norm.
  Save phase-end checkpoints with phase index, penalty, completed sweeps,
  residual_norm, relative_residual=residual_norm/target_norm, support_size.
  Among checkpoints with1..443 strictlypositive weights choose minimum
  (relative_residual,support_size,phase). Keep its FULL n-vector including
  all zeros. Do not threshold or trim coefficients into support. Numerical
  statistics are proposal diagnostics, never proof of feasibility/infeasibility.
  If resource or nonfinite failure interrupts the prescribed trajectory,
  complete=False and do not attempt exact restoration from a partial result.
