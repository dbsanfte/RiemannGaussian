Language change
adaptive_rational_exponents.next_batch enumerates reduced rationals by height
|numerator|+denominator within any exact closed finite rational interval. Each
batch has work/candidate limits and a transparent resumable cursor; there is
no semantic denominator ceiling. Explicit requested rationals are separately
metered and deduplicated against later ordinary enumeration. Larger inputs
remain queued if their parse/bit work exceeds the current call's budget.
Signed intervals, normalization, pause/resume and large requested denominators
were tested. No hashes or old fractional/search imports are used.

Factories distinguish coefficient_twist from signed_power; the latter also
has an adapter to the existing rational-power AST. Signed powers are not
ordinary even roots of negative values or Dirichlet powers. Their construction
alone carries no estimate. The existing rational-power language was already
semantically general; the new module broadens actual search scheduling.

The proved pilot uses h_gamma(n)=n^-gamma*h(n), gamma>=0, on every035generated
state. This is a genuinely changed coefficient operator. Merely expressing
the same source in q=p^-1/12 instead of p^-1/6 would not add candidates.
Prime exponents are stored as exact Fractions, avoiding a large common-root
polynomial lattice. All valuation-e transfers acquire p^(-gamma*e), with
the unit, coprime-cofactor domain and complete valuation tail retained.

What the search proved and what it rejected
Generic power summation bounds every partial weighted rough prefix, and the
035prime-only support/sign/count proof gives cofinal lower bounds where valid.
Undoing the weight is checked through the complete signed Abel identity at
the same cutoff. It requires every partial prefix; a block endpoint estimate
cannot silently supply that input. Recovery restores the missing X^gamma cost.

89 of the128weighted versions of the original rough component have a proved
subpower-or-better energy estimate. ZERO improve its unweighted energy power
after recovery; no state among all512constructions improves that power either.
Example: gamma=1/5 gives weighted energy O(X^-1/15), yet signed Abel recovery
returns exponent1/3 for the original rough component. Gamma=1/257 yields
weighted exponent251/771 and again recovered exponent1/3. No numerical slope
or manually guessed winning exponent is involved.

This limitation applies to the current integer-sum/Abel proof family. For a
nonzero prime profile with leading exponent r>=0, its weighted prefix exponent
is max(1-r-gamma,0). Recovery has exponent
 max(1-r-gamma,0)+gamma=max(1-r,gamma)>=max(1-r,0).
Thus more pure weights cannot improve its power within this provider. Mixed
fractional potentials, correlations and other proof mechanisms are not refuted.
In particular this is not an obstruction to a full joined arithmetic bound.
