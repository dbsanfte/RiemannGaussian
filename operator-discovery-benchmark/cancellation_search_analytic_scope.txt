ANALYTICAL GATES FOR AST-BACKED PRIME TRANSPORT AND CORRELATION SEARCH

New pre-score scope note. No frozen source edits or score queries. The proposed
AST can express complete signed pair transformations and common-weight groups,
but the original full signed defect remains the target. This note derives a
necessary scalar-charge condition for a broad forthcoming dictionary, extending
the preceding three-rowmass coefficient test without asserting a new bound.

1. Universal constant extraction survives arbitrary AST grouping.
Expand every declared square and relation multiplier EXACTLY into formal
polynomials before matching. For a shared square weight a_j>=0 and relation
weight b_j, the scalar budget is
 B(X)=sum_j a_j sum_(terms in square groupj)w_term*[P_term(0)]^2
      +sum_j b_j sum_(terms in relation groupj)[Q_term*rho_term](0).
Here0 means all formal variables zero, NOT actual arithmetic; constants in an
AST linear form must be retained. For polynomial relations, constant extraction
is just multiplication of constant coefficients. Coprime/gcd products and zero
relations have zero constant; norms contribute minus their known h_n; local
prime-power/unit relations have their supplied actual constants. Complete
transport equality certificates may have nonconstant multipliers and then no
scalar at all. Adding such zero-scalar columns can change representation and
nonconstant cancellation, but cannot directly cancel an existing scalar charge.
If all nonconstant coefficients are exactly-Delta, B-Delta equals the grouped
RHS. Optional scalar slack is D-B, computed only after exact cancellation.
No AST compression, sign pairing or grouping alters this extraction formula.

2. A broad mu density-mass test: any fixed BV kernel.
Suppose the collected norm scalar at scaleX can be written
 -sum_(n>=1)mu(n)^2 X^2 psi(n/X),
with compact support[0,L], fixed bounded-variation psi, and source horizon>=LX.
Continuity is sufficient for the simplest stated integration-by-parts formula;
for discontinuous kernels use declared one-sided atom conventions and explicit
jump costs. Fixed rational piecewise-polynomial kernels have computable integral
and variation certificates, encompassing prime-dilated shell-row and block
incidence charges, not only the old head/tail rowmass groups.
Let Q(t)=delta*t+E(t),delta=1/zeta(2), |E(t)|<=3sqrt t. Stieltjes partial
summation for continuous BVpsi gives EXACTLY
 sum mu(n)^2 X^2 psi(n/X)
 =delta X^3 integral_0^L psi(u)du
  +X^2 psi(L)E(LX)-X^2 integral_0^(LX)E(t)d[psi(t/X)].
Thus its error is at most
 3 X^(5/2)*(sqrt(L)*|psi(L)|+integral_0^L sqrt(u)|dpsi|(u)).
No PNT, RH or signed-Mobius cancellation is used. For source weights differing
from X²psi by a uniformO(X) complete endpoint correction atO(X)indices, the
added cost isO(X²), so the same cubic leading coefficient holds. A different
moving kernel needs its own uniform bounds; it is not silently fixed.

If the dictionary has several such norm groups, combine their SIGNED shared
weights FIRST: gamma=-sum_j b_j integral psi_j. For fixedq>1, constant squares
with critical normalization and unit termsO(X²), and prime-local contributions
o(X³), one obtains B(X)=delta*gamma*X³+o(X³). Therefore gamma>0 rigorously
rejects THIS fixed template's critical all-scale budget; gamma<=0 is a necessary
filter, not a sufficient proof. Unlike an unsignedl1score, this test retains
signed mass cancellation between norm groups. It can be implemented from exact
AST kernel integrals without large target scores, provided each group's
rescaled-weight and endpoint certificate is proved.

3. Fixed divisor classes: density charges are not interchangeable.
For a fixed squarefree d, source mask d|n gives
 sum_(n<=y,d|n)mu(n)^2=Q_d(y/d)
 =delta_d*y+O((2^omega(d)+2)*sqrt(y/d)),
 delta_d=(1/zeta(2))*product_(p|d)p/(p+1)/d.
Apply the same BV proof with delta_d in place ofdelta and its explicit error.
For nonsquarefree d the masked norm sum is identically0. Fixed coprime masks
useQ_d(y) and its density directly. A finite rational mixture of these masks
therefore has a precise density-weighted norm mass, not a universaldelta copied
to every source class. Overlapping masks/groups and all shared coefficients
must be retained. If d or the prime set grows withX, the uniform modulus errors
and density variation are additional obligations; this fixed-divisor test does
not discharge them. This generalizes the gate to fixed prime-dilation source
classes while remaining elementary and exact.

4. Why zero cubic mass still does not prove a critical budget.
Actual mu local-prime scalar sums with complete weights bounded byO(X²) have
absolute costO(X²*pi(LX))=o(X³) by elementary Chebyshev, but this can still be
as large asX³/logX. Likewise the norm-discrepancy error above isX^(5/2), which
is supercritical for sufficiently largeq. Therefore gamma0 is NOT an RH-scale
admission. A useful candidate must either cancel these scalar pieces EXACTLY,
prove a substantially sharper signed combination bound, or have a strictly
negative dominant cubic scalar that bounds all remaining subcubic terms above
for largeX, with initial finite scales rigorously charged. This last scalar
possibility alone still does not prove a nonconstant template identity.
A strictly negative cubic budget paired with an all-scale SOS identity would
force Delta to be eventually negative cubic; nonnegative original energy
recurrence makes that impossible for any nonzero initial finite energy bound.
Indeed A(2X)<=4A(X)-cX³ along all sufficiently large dyadics implies
A(2^j)/4^j decreases byc2^j/4 without bound, contradictingA>=0. Thus if a
proposed actual uniform identity yields such a negative dominant scalar, its
nonconstant matching cannot persist at ALL scales. Negativegamma is a scalar
upper-bound filter pass but also a structural uniform-identity obstruction.
For these hypotheses, gamma=0 is therefore necessary for a sound uniform
critical template; it leaves the exact next-order scalar estimate unresolved.

5. Actionable column design and guards.
Search paired prime-dilation/correlation columns whose nonconstant kernels can
change the required norm charge while keeping originalDelta complete. Pure
zero-scalar transport relations offer representational freedom, not automatic
budget saving. Norm-gcd combinations with degree-two multipliers can lower
quartic c relations to signed primitive pair columns; this was absent from the
old dictionary and is a representation opportunity, not an assumed bound.
Jointly group square AND relation coefficients by exact source/kernel regions;
extract formal constants and exact density masses before declaring a candidate
as potentially uniform. Enumerate broad rational coefficients without feeding
a winning kernel, and retain witnesses against unsigned/cubic templates.
For every surviving finite certificate the two separate missing theorems remain:
ALL-scale nonconstant coefficient matching/nonnegative squares, and an actual
cofinal critical scalar budget with every boundary/source/prime charge. Neither
may be inserted as an axiom or inferred from a finite sequence of transfers.
The fixed-BV density test concerns mu; for c norm4^omega requires a separately
proved weighted-density asymptotic and cannot be assigned the mu coefficient.
No such c density theorem or global signed estimate is supplied here.

6. Exact generated-family applicability without a descriptor whitelist.
For each generated square atom inspect its expanded formal constant, and for
each relation atom inspect BOTH multiplier and relation constants. This is a
coefficient-level algorithm independent of family names. If all added square
atoms haveP(0)=0 and all added relation atoms haveQ(0)=0 orrho(0)=0, their
scalar contribution is EXACTLYzero. Prime transport multipliers of typeP+B,
signed block-correlation linear forms without offsets, paired-dilation forms,
and degree-two gcd/norm closure multipliers satisfy this condition. For example,
(x_g²-h_g)*x_u*x_v has zero formal scalar despiteh_g!=0; no new scalar
arithmetic appears by reducing the gcd quartic term. This must be checked on
expanded AST instances rather than presumed from a family label.
In that case the enlarged dictionary's scalar MODULE is the old one: shared
weights may change, including the old rowmass-norm coefficientgamma, but every
new column directly has zero scalar. For the existing mu rowmass groups the
exact cubic coefficient remains
 gamma=-[(56/3)b_all+12b_head+(20/3)b_tail].
All new zero-scalar columns can help find nonconstant matching withgamma0;

proof rather than silently assignedzero. This exact audit is the smallest
pre-score analytic guard for the forthcoming AST dictionary.
