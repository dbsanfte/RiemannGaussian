SEARCHED INTERACTIONS OF THE TWO CARRY TRANSFERS

Status: universal formal identities and a proved analytic bridge for one
selected sector. The full commutator has no proved continuation to
Re(s)>1/2 in this investigation. Historical originality is unresolved.
These are mathematical proofs, supported by exact and Arb checks; they
are not theorem-prover certificates and do not prove RH.

1. DEFINITIONS AND THE LABELLED SEARCH DOMAIN

Work over C with arithmetic functions f satisfying f(1)=1. Write D for
ordinary Dirichlet convolution, delta for its unit, and LOG_D and EXP_D
for the coefficient-local formal logarithm and exponential. Define

 (a A b)(n)=sum_(ij=n) [v_p(i)&v_p(j)=0 for every p] a(i)b(j),
 (a G b)(n)=sum_(ij=n) [Omega(i)&Omega(j)=0] a(i)b(j).

The ampersand denotes binary bitwise AND. Both products are associative:
for a triple product, every intermediate no-carry condition holds exactly
when the three relevant bit sets are pairwise disjoint. They are
commutative and have unit delta. LOG and EXP under either product are
coefficient-local and mutually inverse, by the usual formal series laws
in a commutative associative algebra over a characteristic-zero field.

 T(f)=EXP_A(LOG_D(f)), S(f)=EXP_G(LOG_D(f)),
 Dcomm(f)=T(S(f))-S(T(f)).

Dcomm uses subtraction of coefficient functions, rather than the group
commutator defined using inverse operators. T and S are established
compositions of logarithm and exponential with carry products. Their
definitions alone are not counted as new arithmetic primitives.

For distinct primes p,q_1,...,q_k a tensor entry (e,k) represents the
coefficient at p^e*q_1*...*q_k. When the coefficient depends only on e,k,
products sum over a split of e and a subset of the k labels. A split
having j labels has multiplicity binom(k,j). For A the weight is
[a&(e-a)=0]; for G it is [(a+j)&(e-a+k-j)=0]. Equivalently, dividing entry
(e,k) by k! gives an exponential generating series in the label count.
There is no truncation at the potentially huge integer p^e*prod q_i.

Because convolution only uses divisors, tensor truncation by e and k is
an exact quotient algebra. Terms outside its declared bounds cannot
feed back into a retained coefficient. A zero at a missing, larger
integer is therefore never assumed. The ordinary arithmetic power
f_alpha=u^{*alpha}=EXP_D(alpha LOG_D u), u(n)=1, is defined formally for
every complex alpha. Its Dirichlet series is zeta(s)^alpha on Re(s)>1,
with the Euler logarithm normalized at positive real infinity.

2. A UNIVERSAL FIRST-OTHER-PRIME MODULE

Restrict an arbitrary f to the divisor chains p^e and p^e*q, for distinct
p,q, and set the square of the q-variable to zero. Its formal series is

 F(x,y)=F_0(x)+y F_1(x), y^2=0, F_0(0)=1.

Ordinary logarithm gives h=log F_0 and k=F_1/F_0, so
 LOG_D F=h+y k. Let t=EXP_A h in the local p-variable. The square-zero
q-component and the exponential addition law imply

 T(F)=t+y (t A k),
 S(F)=t+y B(t,k),
 B(t,k)_e=sum_(a+b=e) [a&(b+1)=0] t_a k_b.

In the second formula a local p^a term has total degree a, while p^b*q
has total degree b+1. This explains the searched offset rather than
postulating a shift in an untyped binary product. Equivalently
 B(t,k)_e=coefficient_(e+1) of t A (x*k).

B is a unital left A-module action. Its associativity is the basis
identity
 [a&b=0][(a+b)&(c+1)=0]
   =[b&(c+1)=0][a&(b+c+1)=0].
Both sides equal one exactly when the bit sets of a,b,c+1 are pairwise
disjoint. Thus B(t A r,k)=B(t,B(r,k)). It is not a commutative product:
B(x,1)=0 whereas B(1,x)=x. The grammar fitted two offset pairs on 0..3;
(0,5) fails at a=4,b=0, leaving the correct pair (0,1) on fresh exponents.
The written identity is universal; the finite offset search by itself is
not its proof.

At prime-power indices A=G, so Dcomm vanishes there for every f. On the
squarefree divisor quotient A=D and T is the identity, so Dcomm also
vanishes at squarefree indices. The generic cubic law proved in
projection_proof.txt section 6 is Dcomm(f)(p^2*q)=f(p)^2*f(q).

3. THE FACTORIAL FAMILY LEARNED FROM ZETA POWERS

For f_alpha and k labelled external primes, let y_i^2=0 and Y=sum y_i.
Retain p-exponents at most 2. LOG_D f_alpha is
 alpha*(x+x^2/2+Y) modulo higher p-exponents and repeated y_i.
Under G, the odd total-degree ideal has square zero: a product of two
odd-degree homogeneous terms has a carry in bit zero. Consequently

 S(f_alpha)=F_0+x F_1+x^2 F_2 modulo x^3,
 F_0=1+alpha Y, F_1=alpha, F_2=(alpha/2)(1+alpha Y).

On the external squarefree quotient A equals the ordinary product.
However, x A x=0, so no two x-degree-one terms can contribute to degree
two in EXP_A. Applying the ordinary logarithm then EXP_A therefore gives

 [x^2] T(S(f_alpha))
   =F_0*(F_2/F_0-F_1^2/(2 F_0^2))
   =(alpha/2)(1+alpha Y)-alpha^2/(2(1+alpha Y)).

T(f_alpha) is multiplicative. Its local coefficient at x^2 is alpha/2,
and the local coefficient of its ordinary logarithm at x^2 is
(alpha-alpha^2)/2. Applying G once more gives

 [x^2] S(T(f_alpha))=(alpha-alpha^2)/2*(1+alpha Y).

Subtracting yields the exact rational formal identity

 [x^2] Dcomm(f_alpha)
   =alpha^2/2*((1+alpha Y)-(1+alpha Y)^(-1))
   =alpha^3 Y-alpha^4 Y^2/(2*(1+alpha Y)).

The coefficient of y_1*...*y_k in Y^k is k!, since all permutations of
the distinct labels occur once. The universal laws are accordingly

 Dcomm(f_alpha)(p^2)=0,
 Dcomm(f_alpha)(p^2*q)=alpha^3,
 Dcomm(f_alpha)(p^2*q_1*...*q_k)
   =(-1)^(k+1)*k!/2*alpha^(k+2), k>=2.

The last formula would give alpha^3/2 at k=1, and is false there. This
negative control prevents extending a good asymptotic-looking fit to
the wrong arity. The two surviving grammar expressions k! and k*(k-1)!
are the same law, not two operators. A 1,920-program search had 30
training fits at alpha=1,k=2..5, with only these two equivalent fits
surviving k up to 16 and six other parameters.

For fixed alpha this selected p^2-sector converges absolutely on
Re(s)>1. To see this despite k!, put a_j=q_j^(-sigma). Their sum is
finite. Choose a finite head of m primes whose remaining sum L obeys
|alpha|L<1. Then
 e_k(a)<=sum_(j=0)^m e_j(head)*L^(k-j)/(k-j)!.
Multiplication by |alpha|^k*k! leaves a polynomial in k times a
convergent geometric factor. The outer sum over p is bounded by
sum p^(-2 sigma). For alpha!=0, absolute convergence fails when
sigma<=1 already in the k=1 subseries at any fixed p, by divergence of
the sum of reciprocal primes. This is a statement about absolute
convergence, and does not rule out analytic continuation. No such
continuation of the full commutator is inferred from the next section.

4. THE COMPLETE FIRST-OTHER-PRIME LOCAL PROFILE

For alpha=1 let H=EXP_A(sum_(e>=1) x^e/e), let
 t_2=EXP_A(LOG_D H), and g=H_even/H, where
 H_even(x)=(H(x)+H(-x))/2.

Apply section 2 twice to u. S(u) has local p-series H. Its p^e*q
coefficient is h_e when e is even, and zero when e is odd: the only
q-term in LOG_D u has total degree one. Thus T(S(u)) has p^e*q series
t_2 A g. In contrast T(u) is multiplicative, so its ordinary logarithm
again has no cross-prime terms and its q-term is one. S(T(u)) has
p^e*q series (t_2)_even. Hence

 d(x)=sum_(e>=0) d_e x^e=t_2 A (H_even/H)-(t_2)_even,
 d_e=Dcomm(u)(p^e*q).

In particular d_0=d_1=0 and d_2=1; the subsequent values begin
d_3=1/3,d_4=5/3,d_5=1/5. Section 3 concerns e=2 and arbitrary k;
this section instead concerns arbitrary e and exactly one other prime.

All local series through degree 256 were checked by an independent
weighted bit-partition evaluator, ordinary reciprocal recursion, and
the identity LOG_D(H)'=H'/H. It does not import the production evaluator.
The implementation explicitly raises FLINT's global series capacity and
asserts the returned precision: an unavailable higher coefficient is
not replaced by zero. The full integer-index calculation through 512
agrees with the selected tensor/local coefficients at three parameters.

5. A UNIFORM LOCAL NORM AND ITS INFINITE TAIL

For radius r use the norm ||a||_r=sum |a_e| r^e. Local A is
submultiplicative since its kernel is bounded by one. From the Euler
recurrence
 e h_e=sum_(j proper bit-submask of e) h_j
and the fact that there are 2^popcount(e)-1<=e proper bit submasks,
induction proves 0<=h_e<=1 for every e. Set R=(1-x)H. Then
R(0)=1 and |[x^e](R-1)|=|h_e-h_(e-1)|<=1.

For r=39/50 and head degree 128 the exact rational certificate is

 c=sum_(e=1)^128 |h_e-h_(e-1)| r^e+r^129/(1-r)
   <0.964071<1.

This proves H has no zero in |x|<=r and bounds ordinary formal log H:
 ||LOG_D H||_r<=-log(1-r)-log(1-c).
Therefore ||t_2||_r<=K=1/((1-r)(1-c)). Also
 ||H_even||_r<=1/(1-r^2),
 ||H^(-1)||_r<= (1+r)/(1-c),
so ||g||_r<=K and
 ||d||_r<=M=K*(K+1).
The exact rational values c,K,M, not rounded decimals, are retained in
coupling_search.json and independently recomputed. Since d_0=d_1=0,
 |d(x)|<=M*(|x|/r)^2 for |x|<=r.

6. ANALYTIC BRIDGE AND AN RH EQUIVALENCE FOR THE SELECTED SECTOR

Let P(s)=sum_p p^(-s) initially on Re(s)>1. Define

 A(s)=sum_p d(p^(-s))=sum_(e>=2) d_e P(e*s),
 B(s)=sum_p p^(-s)*d(p^(-s))=sum_(e>=2) d_e P((e+1)*s).

Because 2^(-1/2)<r, section 5 gives normally convergent prime sums on
Hhalf={Re(s)>1/2}. Thus A and B are holomorphic there. A is not
identically zero: along positive real infinity A(s)~4^(-s), by d_2=1.
Writing Q(s)=sum_(k>=2) P(k*s)/k, a normally convergent holomorphic sum
on Hhalf, the Euler logarithm gives P(s)=log zeta(s)-Q(s) for Re(s)>1.

Select just the commutator coefficients at indices p^e*q, e>=2,p!=q:

 C_1(s)=sum_(p!=q,e>=2) d_e*(p^e*q)^(-s)
       =A(s)*P(s)-B(s)
       =A(s)*log zeta(s)-A(s)*Q(s)-B(s), Re(s)>1.

Uniqueness of the repeated prime ensures that an integer in this sector
is counted once. Section 5 and sigma>1 justify all rearrangements.

Define the germ Phi_1=C_1+A*log(s-1) on Re(s)>1, with normalized real
branches. RH holds if and only if Phi_1 admits a single-valued
holomorphic continuation to Hhalf. Indeed Z=(s-1)*zeta(s) is holomorphic
on Hhalf with its removable value at 1. If RH holds it is nonzero there,
so the simply connected half-plane admits log Z and
 Phi_1=A*log Z-A*Q-B is holomorphic.

Conversely, a zero rho in Hhalf of multiplicity m produces local
monodromy 2*pi*i*m*A(s) in this expression. A is holomorphic and not
identically zero, so no isolated zero of A, of any finite order,
eliminates that nonzero analytic germ. It contradicts single-valued
continuation. The standard reflection of nontrivial zeta zeros then
makes absence of zeros in Hhalf equivalent to RH.

This equivalence supplies a criterion, not a zero-free estimate. It
does not prove Phi_1 holomorphic throughout the half-plane and does not
assert the same domain for the unselected commutator coefficients.

7. ACTUAL-ZETA VALIDATION, TAILS, AND THE ZERO CONTROL

For a>1 put D(a)=2^(-a)+2^(1-a)/(a-1), which bounds
sum_(n>=2) n^(-a). A local cutoff E has infinite tails

 |A-A_E|<=M/r^(E+1)*D((E+1)*sigma),
 |B-B_E|<=M/r^(E+1)*D((E+2)*sigma).

This follows by writing |d_e|p^(-e*sigma)=|d_e|r^e
times (p^(-sigma)/r)^e, applying the weighted norm, and summing primes.
For arithmetic cutoff N and sigma>2, the omitted C_1-series is bounded
by N^(2-sigma) times an absolute Dirichlet norm at 2. That norm is at
most P(2)*sum_(e>=2)|d_e|P(2e); its local tail obeys the preceding bound.

The analytic evaluator obtains P(z) from actual Arb zeta evaluations,
using sum_(m<=L) mu(m)/m*log zeta(m*z) plus an explicit infinite tail.
For Re(z)>1 it certifies log zeta(Re(z))<pi, connecting the principal
logarithm to the normalized Euler branch. The separate audit instead
uses |log zeta(m*z)|<=D(m*Re(z)) and tail
 D((L+1)*Re(z))/((L+1)*(1-2^(-Re(z)))).

Six new points use real parts 3,4,6 and heights 61.19,-67.43,139.71,
-151.23,257.89,-269.31. Production arithmetic cutoff 4096/local degree
64 and independent cutoff 8192/local degree 48 both pass their proved
infinite bounds. Changing the coefficient at 12 by 1/7, or independently
by 1/11, fails all six comparisons. Neither route fits this coefficient
from the analytic values used for validation.

At c=1/2+14.13472514173469379046*i and zero-disk radius 1/10000, actual
Arb zeta and derivative values certify exactly one simple zero by
Rouche: the constant plus quadratic remainder is smaller than
|zeta'(c)|/10000. Reflection s->1-conjugate(s) preserves the disk, so
uniqueness places this zero on Re(s)=1/2. No claim of its global rank is
needed. A separate audit replaces the quadratic interval remainder by
a Cauchy estimate from radius 1/100 and obtains the same zero count.

To continue P(2s) across the entire disk, the principal log zeta(2s)
is explicitly connected to the Euler branch. Ten Arb rectangles cover
Re(2s) from 0.9998 to 1.5 with imaginary centre twice Im(c) and radius
0.0002. Each has Re(zeta)>0, and the right anchor satisfies
log zeta(1.5)<pi. Higher Mobius terms have real part greater than one.
The audit recomputes this connection with eight rectangles. The sums
P(e*s),e>=3, already lie in their absolutely convergent domain on this
disk. Local norm tails remain summable on its full crossing rectangle.

Production local degree 256 and independent degree 224 both certify
A is holomorphic and nonzero there. Production has |A|>0.587775 and
local tail below 1.869e-7. Thus Phi_1 has the actual-zero monodromy
2*pi*i*A at each basepoint, with modulus greater than 3.69310 throughout
the disk. This probes the true zeta function and the genuine selected
arithmetic coefficients. The boundary zero is consistent with RH;
the control is not an off-critical-line counterexample.

8. WHAT THE SEARCH DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The factorial and module identities are proved for every declared
parameter and arity, rather than inferred from finite validation.
The first-other-prime analytic criterion uses a complete local series
and its infinite tails, rather than a finite prime or integer fit.
A new analytic estimate sufficient to prove RH is still absent.

The formal coefficients depend only on the free prime monoid's exponent
data. Replacing prime labels by arbitrary generators preserves every
exact law above. This is an explicit limitation of the arithmetic
specificity supplied by the present grammar. Further, the selected
sector collapses analytically to A*P-B. Its zero test is therefore a
test of the familiar zeta logarithm with holomorphic prefactors, rather
than a new mechanism excluding off-line zeros.

These facts do not make the detector uninformative: it learned an
unbounded-arity law, rejected an incorrect extension at k=1 and an
incorrect kernel shift, and established a precise continuation domain
with a real-zero control. They do mean that naming this operator word
or sector should not increase the verified-new operator count.
