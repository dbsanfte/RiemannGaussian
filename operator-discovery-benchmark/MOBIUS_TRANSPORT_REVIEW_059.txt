1. Complete source, profiles, and recovery domain
================================================
Let N>=2 be an integer, 0<alpha<1, gamma>0 and 0<tau<1 be fixed rationals.
Let M be a fixed product of DISTINCT actual primes containing p<q. Put
k=omega(M), epsilon=min(tau,N^(-gamma)), and

 G_M(t)=product_(l|M)(1-l^(-t)),
 Phi_N(a)=1/Gamma(1-alpha) integral_epsilon^tau
                              G_M(t)a^(-t)t^(-alpha-1)dt,
 W(y)=floor(4y)-3floor(2y)+2floor(y), W in {-1,0,1}.

The normalizer follows from one integration by parts:
alpha I_alpha=Gamma(1-alpha)>0. The complete finite middle is

 J(N)=sum_(1<=d<=floor(4N/p),gcd(d,M)=1)mu(d)
           [W(N/(pd))Phi_N(pd)-W(N/(qd))Phi_N(qd)].           (1)

This is the weighted exchange source from058, with all valuation sectors
already incorporated. Non-squarefree d have mu=0; they contribute zero,
not a discarded nonzero source. The actual cofactor unit d=1 is retained.
It maps to source indices p and q. It is not the original source n=1,
whose weighted-exchange coefficient is exactly zero. The q term vanishes
when qd>4N by its COMPLETE floor weight; that exit is not another arbitrary
cofactor cutoff or an omitted complement.

For (a,b)=(W(N/(pd)),W(N/(qd))) nonzero, the profiles and kernel signs are

 P: b=0, magnitude Phi(pd), sigma=a;
 Q: a=0, magnitude Phi(qd), sigma=-b;
 D: a=b=+/-1, magnitude Phi(pd)-Phi(qd), sigma=a;
 S: a=-b=+/-1, magnitude Phi(pd)+Phi(qd), sigma=a.

The ACTUAL token sign is mu(d)*sigma, not mu(d) alone. When epsilon<tau
all four magnitudes are strictly positive and nonincreasing in d; D is
positive because p<q. They share a positive Laplace representation

 m_i(d)=c_alpha integral_epsilon^tau
                G_M(t)f_i(t)d^(-t)t^(-alpha-1)dt,            (2)
 f_P=p^(-t), f_Q=q^(-t), f_D=p^(-t)-q^(-t),
 f_S=p^(-t)+q^(-t), c_alpha=1/Gamma(1-alpha).

If epsilon=tau all magnitudes and J are zero exactly. This is a vacuous
finite middle, not evidence of a uniform estimate. The current source
implementation has an exact powered empty-band gate and labels quadrature
magnitudes as FLOAT SCREENING, without certified error. The independent
checker rebuilds ordinary mu, profiles and all units/exits, then encloses
only the needed magnitudes and plan charges. Its finite proof scope must
not be enlarged to uniform growth.

A uniform square-root/polylog estimate for B_plan restores the COMPLETE
RH-sensitive exchange using058 endpoints only when

 gamma*(k-alpha)>=1/2 AND tau>=1/2,                          (3)

or when separate stronger endpoint premises are paid. General positive
gamma and tau alone do not guarantee this recovery. The scientific choice
alpha=1/5, M=30, gamma=5/28, tau=1/2 satisfies (3) at equality. No actual
candidate values or outcomes are used here. The target is the full signed
middle; the cofactor units and inter-leg cancellation stay joined.

2. Every complete opposite-sign matching is a valid positive bound
=================================================================
A plan pairs distinct opposite-sign tokens, uses every token at most once,
and retains every remaining token. If signs are epsilon_i and magnitudes
m_i>=0, its exact cost is

 B_plan=sum_pairs |m_i-m_j|+sum_unmatched m_i.               (4)

Each pair contributes either m_i-m_j or its negative to J. Triangle
inequality therefore proves |J|<=B_plan. Also B_plan<=sum_i m_i for every
legal plan. Repeated tokens, missing leftovers, same-sign cancellation,
or pairings based on a sign without its kernel sigma invalidate this rule.
The read producer and independent checker retain these conditions.

A source-dependent algorithm is allowed to read actual mu signs, indices,
quotients, residues, profiles and exact rational-power block thresholds.
The algebraic bound holds for every output matching, regardless of how
those choices are made. To make it a uniform theorem, however, the policy
must be a TOTAL fixed program on all N in its claimed domain and its COST
must satisfy a separately proved uniform estimate. A program defined only
through the scientific resource ceiling is not itself a proof for larger N.
Frozen finite calibration of C and independent fresh finite comparison
can reject a proposed estimate; neither supplies its missing quantifier.

3. Exact one-dimensional optimum, including all leftover mass
=============================================================
For any finite signed list of nonnegative magnitudes, augment the smaller
sign class by zero-magnitude dummy tokens to balance counts. A pair with a
dummy has exactly the unmatched charge. Zero is only an accounting endpoint:
it is not a new source index, not Phi(0), and not permission to drop a tail.

The minimum cost over ALL pairings and leftovers is

 B_* = integral_0^infinity |sum_i epsilon_i 1_(m_i>y)|dy.     (5)

Proof: each pair is a unit path between its two magnitudes, and its length
is the integral of its threshold crossings. Every threshold must have at
least the absolute signed count difference of crossing paths. Sorting the
two balanced lists and matching in order attains that lower bound, by the
usual uncrossing inequality for absolute distance on a line. Extra equal
numbers of zero dummies do not change (5), so plans leaving both signs
unmatched are also bounded below by it.

For ONE fixed profile, order d_1<...<d_n, hence m_1>=...>=m_n, and put
S_j=sum_(i<=j)epsilon_i, m_(n+1)=0. Then

 B_profile^*=sum_(j=1)^n |S_j|(m_j-m_(j+1)).                 (6)

Canonical ascending-d STACK and FIFO both attain (6) without inspecting
magnitudes. Cancel every incoming opposite sign against any waiting token;
otherwise enqueue it. The waiting list has only one sign and exactly |S_j|
entries after every prefix. Each waiting token eventually goes to a lower
magnitude or to zero. Thus exactly |S_j| paths cross each magnitude gap,
which proves (6) directly. Choice of FIFO versus stack changes edges, not
this optimal cost. Weak monotonicity and zero/tied magnitudes cause no gap.

Scanning descending d need not be optimal with leftovers sent to zero
at the end. For example ascending magnitudes 1-,2+,3+,4-,5- gives greedy
cost 1+1+5=7, while the optimum is 5. If counts are balanced, reflection
also preserves the optimum; that special case is not a generic guarantee.
Arbitrary adjacent cancellation is likewise not the proof: the one-sign
waiting invariant and the correct location of the zero endpoint matter.

UNIVERSAL DOMINANCE RULE: any policy whose EVERY pair joins equal-profile
tokens has cost >=sum_profiles B_profile^*(N), at every N. Arbitrary
partitions, residues, quotient bands, block powers, reverse orders, partial
passes, profile-restricted prime ratios and their compositions cannot beat
the canonical ascending-d profile stack. All unmatched tokens count in
this comparison. Hence a syntactic PRE-score gate may archive such policies
as dominated, preserving explicit controls. It does not prove a growth
bound for the reference. In the read grammar, every non-ALL group includes
profile; prime_ratio/profile explicitly enforces it. ALL/cross-profile
policies are outside this gate and can beat, equal or exceed that reference.

4. The optimum is a concrete arithmetic-prefix variation
========================================================
For fixed N and a profile i, define the ACTUAL masked prefix

 A_(N,i)(y)=sum_(d<=y,gcd(d,M)=1)
                  mu(d)*sigma_N(d)*1_(profile_N(d)=i).

With D=floor(4N/p), (6) is equivalently

 B_profile^*=integral_1^D |A_(N,i)(y)|[-m_i'(y)]dy
                                  +|A_(N,i)(D)|m_i(D),    (7)

with zero cost for an empty profile. Differentiating (2) is legitimate on
the finite parameter interval. The terminal term is the complete leftover
mass; it cannot be deleted because there is a cofactor cutoff. Equivalently
it is the integral of the constant final discrepancy against -m_i' from
D to infinity, since m_i tends to zero.

The signed profile sum has the SAME formula with A instead of |A|. Thus
(7) keeps cancellation among mu signs inside each arithmetic prefix, but
loses cancellation BETWEEN prefix discrepancies with different signs.
It is a stronger auxiliary than |J_profile| and is equivalent to that
signed quantity only when the relevant threshold discrepancies have one
sign almost everywhere. A single global residual mass or an endpoint
Mertens value does not bound this weighted variation.

If m_min is the smallest positive magnitude in a complete nonempty list,
the threshold formula also proves B_plan>=m_min*|sum_i epsilon_i|: below
m_min every positive token is present. Equal-profile-only plans obey the
sum of these separate profile lower bounds; unrestricted plans obey only
the global version. Thus a uniform cost theorem constrains actual signed
token counts as well as weighted prefix variation. A small final count
alone is insufficient: intermediate prefix discrepancies can remain large
even when that count is zero.

A precise reusable sufficient leaf is a uniform bound on (7), summed
across profiles, for all N and all prefix y with the displayed weights.
It is not the norm of h renamed: it is an actual ordinary-mu discrepancy
under N-dependent floor/profile masks. The mask depends on N/d, and no
prime-independence or random-sign assumption is licensed. A claimed bound
on an unrelated unmasked Mertens prefix cannot be substituted without
proving the full interval/mask transfer and its cost.

A further generic proof-language construction is possible without numeric
magnitude choice: P and Q magnitudes share the SAME Phi function, with
arguments pd and qd. Their merged group can be sorted by those exact integer
arguments, and canonical cancellation is optimal for that shared-kernel
class. This can be inferred from canonical kernel AST equality, rather
than nominate a favorite formula or read floating magnitudes. D and S are
positive sums/differences of two kernels and cannot be silently flattened:
flattening changes transport constraints and can either raise or lower a
bound. All cross-profile or multi-kernel comparisons need their own proof.

5. Prime-ratio pairs add a paid local factor, but not coverage
===========================================================
For squarefree ordinary-mu d and an actual prime r|d, mu(d/r)=-mu(d).
The pair is opposite in the ORIGINAL tokens exactly when their kernel
sigmas agree. Same profile alone is insufficient to infer that sign; the
implemented policy also checks the actual signs. Coprimality with M implies
r is outside M, and at M=30 necessarily r>=7.

Within a common profile and a legal edge (d/r,d), its cost is exactly

 m_i(d/r)-m_i(d)
 =c_alpha integral_epsilon^tau G_M(t)f_i(t)
                  (d/r)^(-t)(1-r^(-t))t^(-alpha-1)dt>=0.   (8)

This is a real elementary Möbius cancellation with one EXTRA Laplace
factor. It is useful local structure, not an assumed sign law for unrelated
integers. All unmatched vertices remain, and a vertex can be used once.
Coverage or the cumulative sum of these positive edge costs is not paid
by the identity.

A route-specific unconditional obstruction can be proved. Any plan using
ONLY same-profile prime-ratio pairs and retained leftovers has, for fixed
M,p,q,alpha,gamma,tau and sufficiently large N,

 B_plan >= c N/[log N]^(k-alpha+2), c>0.                    (9)

Consequently this restricted class cannot satisfy a square-root times
any fixed polylog estimate. This does NOT reject plans adding global or
cross-profile matching of untouched tokens.

Here is a complete non-RH proof. Put D=floor(4N/p), L=log(2qD). For large N
the parameter interval [1/L,2/L] lies inside [epsilon,tau]. On it,
G_M(t)>=c_M t^k, d^(-t)>=exp(-2) for d<=D,
f_P,f_Q,f_S>=c, and f_D>=c t because p<q. All constants are positive and
fixed; 1-exp(-z)>=z/2 for 0<=z<=1 proves the first bound. Thus every
unmatched magnitude is >=c L^(-(k-alpha+1)). For every same-profile
prime-ratio pair, r^t-1>=t log r>=t log2; (8), expressed with d^(-t),
gives cost >=c L^(-(k-alpha+2)). The weaker exponent covers all profiles.

There are at least c_0 N actual nonzero tokens. Indeed the P+ exit interval

 max(2N/p,4N/q)<d<=4N/p

has Wp=+1,Wq=0 and positive length. Squarefree d coprime to M have positive
density rho_M=(phi(M)/M)*product_(r prime,r not dividing M)(1-r^(-2)).
This density is elementary: expand mu(d)^2=sum_(a^2|d)mu(a), count the
finite coprime arithmetic intervals, and pass to the limit using the
absolutely summable a^(-2) tail; the O(sqrt(N)) endpoint count disappears.
The Euler product is positive because sum_r r^(-2) converges. No prime gap,
PNT, RH, or source table is used. With T>=c_0 N tokens, every plan has at
least T/2 paired-or-unmatched charges. Their lower bounds prove (9).

A same-profile ratio edge is irreversible even if followed by other
passes. If E_N such edges were already charged, any final plan has
B>=c E_N/log^(k-alpha+2)N. A proposed square-root/polylog bound therefore
requires E_N=O(sqrt(N)*a fixed polylog), or an independently justified
avoidance of a positive-density ratio pairing pass. Finite pair counts
can diagnose this issue; they do not prove its uniform coverage rate.
This obstruction is on a restricted proof route, not on the full grammar
or the actual RH conjecture.

6. All-positive controls require distinct scope
==============================================
Discarding actual token signs and setting every token sign positive makes
B_plan=sum magnitudes: no opposite pairing remains. Replacing ordinary mu
by |mu| while keeping the kernel sigma is a DIFFERENT control, since floor
signs still oppose each other. It must not be mislabeled as the ordinary
source or used as its upper provider. The latter control has a rigorous
all-scale obstruction even after its real linear mean is removed.

For this squarefree-positive cofactor control,

 Q_M(s)=sum_(gcd(d,M)=1)|mu(d)|d^(-s)
       =zeta(s)/zeta(2s)*product_(l|M)(1+l^(-s))^(-1).

It has a simple positive-residue pole at s=1. Its complete normalized
exchange Dirichlet symbol on Re(s)>1 is

 F_abs(s)=c_alpha integral_0^infinity G_M(t)zeta(s)Q_M(s+t)
                   [p^(-s-t)-q^(-s-t)]t^(-alpha-1)dt.       (10)

All cofactor units and both exits are retained. The elementary Euler
product proves this formula; standard zeta continuation/pole background
is available at https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2 . This source is not actual mu.

Near s=1+delta, a simple scale-zero P(q0)=0 cancels the pole of zeta(s),
but Q_M(1+delta+t) still contributes a nonzero multiple of 1/(delta+t).
Since G_M(t) starts at order k, the Taylor-subtracted parameter integral
contains the nonzero noninteger term delta^(k-alpha-1). The coefficient
is a nonzero multiple of the regularized beta integral
Gamma(k-alpha)*Gamma(1-k+alpha). Analytic amplitudes produce only higher
noninteger orders and analytic Taylor terms. Any finite extra multiplier
zero merely increases this noninteger exponent; it does not remove the
branch. Thus the complete scale-filtered control is NOT holomorphic near
s=1 and cannot have an all-integer square-root/polylog bound. This uses
no off-line-zero hypothesis or finite observation.

Under endpoint conditions (3), the positive-cofactor microscopic piece
is O(sqrt N) directly from G_M<=t^k product log l and the finite absolute
d sum. Its tail is O(sqrt N), retaining the p,q attenuated d=1 terms.
Therefore a square-root/polylog bound on ANY middle matching cost for
this |mu| control would imply the forbidden complete bound. It is
impossible in that scope. Without paid endpoints this particular transfer
must not be asserted. The actual ordinary-mu symbol has 1/zeta(s+t),
not Q_M(s+t), and this real-pole-continuum obstruction is not an obstruction
to its admissible square-root middle estimate.

7. New arithmetic frontier, code scope and noncircular claims
============================================================
The grammar can in principle produce a valid RH-strength auxiliary: a
proved uniform cost bound, under (3), gives |J| control, complete endpoint
restoration, and the058 conditional RH bridge. No algebraic impossibility
has been shown for all actual-mu cross-profile/global policies. Their hard
leaf is the uniform ordinary-mu signed-prefix variation or an independently
proved pairing/coverage-cost theorem. The profile optimum is not a proof
of that leaf, and the extra factor in a prime-ratio edge is not a proof
that enough cheaply paired vertices cover the source.

The code reviewed has the correct P/Q/D/S signs, stable positive D
integrand, ordinary squarefree-zero mu handling, d=1, complete q exit,
once-only opposite matching and explicit leftover pricing. Producer float
magnitudes are screening values; independent rigorous intervals and
coverage replay are required before a finite bound is admitted. Even that
admission proves only the listed finite cases. There is no universal
normalization hidden in the fitted C, and no finite plan comparison pays
an all-N bound. The floor-power resource precharge issue was reported to
the parent before freeze; no owned code or suite was changed here.

Detector feedback can now distinguish:
 * universal token triangle and profile-dominance certificates: PAID;
 * exact prime-ratio cancellation and source-free endpoint rates: PAID;
 * square-root cost of an all-positive control: REFUTED in the stated scope;
 * prime-ratio-only same-profile square-root route: REFUTED asymptotically;

The constructive next proof object is a bound on the weighted masked
