Exposure theorem
If h_g=0, then g*x=0 for every affine point. If h_b=-c*h_a with c>0, then
(c*g_a+g_b)*x=0 for every affine point. Since each functional is nonnegative
on the primal cone, each must individually vanish at every FEASIBLE point.
All members of an exact line class containing both signs are exposed: each
can pair with a member of opposite sign using exact positive coefficients.
A class having only one sign gives no exposure merely from proportionality.
This theorem remains conditional when the feasible set is empty and proves
no existence or infeasibility by itself.

For scalar unit g, exposure forces its scalar0. For matrix ray vv^T,
exposure forces v^T C v=0 or v^T H v=0. PSD then implies C v=0 or H v=0:
for any y, PSD of (v+t*y)^T M(v+t*y) for both signs of smallt forces y^T Mv=0.
Thus the span of exposed vectors lies in the matrix kernel. Numerical zero
eigenvalues, nearly proportional columns, negative search coefficients or
low penalties do not license any such face. For the original lift,
C=Gc+K and H=Gp+4Gc, with allthree PSD, so a C-kernel vector lies in both
Gc/K kernels; an H-kernel vector lies in Gp/Gc kernels. No whole child block
is forcedzero just because the canonical lift chooses itzero.

Counterexample to residual-only exposure: the affine equation s1+s2=1 has
s1=f,s2=1-f. Their residuals1 and-1 oppose, but full responses(1,0),(-1,1)
do not. Feasible s1=s2=.5 violates either alleged forcedzero. Constants must
be included in every line classification and pair certificate.

Column compression
Drop exact zero response columns. For h_j=c_j*h_rep with c_j>0, replace the
class by one ORIGINAL representative column. Original alpha>=0 maps to
alpha_rep'=sum_j c_j*alpha_j>=0. A compressed solution lifts by setting only
the representative alpha to alpha_rep' and all other memberszero. Thus the
feasible conic target representation is exactly preserved, although an L1
cost on representatives changes the objective. A representative normalized
by a positive scale needs its inverse scale in the lift; normalizing by a
negative first coefficient is not a positive column scaling.

Opposite-sign columns MUST remain distinct in the LP. Identifying them by
signed line changes the nonnegative generated cone. Opposite classes can be
used for exposure while positive-oriented classes separately define lossless
compression. Zero-response rays may matter for faces but not the target-1 LP.
No near-zero cutoff, numerical rank truncation or support dropping is exact.

Row compression
Operate on the COMPLETE AUGMENTED row(coefficients,RHS) of the compressed
column system. Drop only exact allzero augmented rows. Arbitrary nonzero
proportional augmented rows may merge using signed rational factors because
these are equations, not inequalities. A row with zerocoefficients but
nonzero RHS must remain as a contradiction or explicit inconsistency branch.
Equal coefficient rows with opposite/incompatible RHS are not proportional
augmented rows and cannot merge. Check every original row under the returned
representative/scales before declaring compression verified.

An inconsistent compressed LP means the selected851-ray system cannot
represent the target; it is NOT a primal-cone infeasibility certificate.
Conversely a floating successful LP is not a witness. One exact support
restoration and complete851 alpha/782 lambda Farkas recombination must verify
all1224 original quotient coordinates and pairing-1. Geometry may be fully
verified and archived as reusable even if this later step isUNKNOWN.
