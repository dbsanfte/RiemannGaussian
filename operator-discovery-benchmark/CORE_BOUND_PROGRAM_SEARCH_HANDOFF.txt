Why this changed
----------------
The user asked whether the detector is finding core bounds itself or
whether analysts/subagents are selecting the constructions and attempting
all the proofs. The previous workflow was a hybrid: executable searches
generated transports, storage choices and recurrence conjectures, while
analysts supplied the main analytical constructions and proof methods.
In particular the projection argument was analyst-supplied. That should
not be described as autonomous discovery of the missing signed bound.

This new executable takes the unresolved core target, generates complete
weighted-prefix combinations, solves exact recovery identities, derives
all-scale local controls from reusable rules, and works backwards to the
specific global inequality that would close the target. The generator
and selector make no model calls and read no numerical source values.
Analysts still supply the primitive language and reusable proof rules.
This is a bounded search of proof compositions, not a general analytic
theorem prover or a claim of independence from every design assumption.

Concrete generated example and exact outstanding work
----------------------------------------------------
Let L(n)=log(2n), G(n)=sum_(k<=n)g(k), B(n)=sum_(k<=n)g(k)log k.
Program51 generated the channels
 R(n)=B(n)/L(n)^2,
 U(n)=G(n)-B(n)/L(n).
The program was generated by polynomial enumeration and exact recovery;
its first-order form is also a known calibration from analytical review013.
No historical novelty or analyst-blind invention is claimed for this form.
The same general enumeration also generates higher-degree combinations.

For A(X)=X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X)[R(n)^2+U(n)^2], the compiler proves
 E_G(X)<=8(1+log2X)^2 A(X).
Project the actual R and U separately onto complete cells of length
2^floor((1/3)log2X). With S the energy of these complete cell means,
 A=S+V exactly,
 V(X)<=1020 K^2 X^(-1/3)(1+log2X), K=37/2.
Recovery pays the full varying log cost; weights are never pulled through
cell means. The recovered detail's X^-1/3 log^3 factor is uniformly paid
by the explicit constant2592. All units and earlier prefixes remain.

The new sufficient lemma emitted automatically is
 S(2X)<=S(X)/2+C for EVERY dyadic X>=1, one fixed finite C.
If this genuinely open lemma were proved, dyadic induction and the paid
detail/recovery would give the requested original E_F=O(log^2 X).
The detector has NOT proved this lemma or the old fixed-C recurrence.
It emits the exact signed pair kernel J_(2X)-rho J_X for each construction,
including every prime/composite cross, prime power and complete history.

Automatic proof feedback
------------------------
Recovery at polynomial argument1 forces a nonvanishing channel. For
square-root-sized cells, these derivative/payment rules always incur at
least log^3 in the whole target after recovery, however weights move
between channels. The checker detects missing costs and rejects the
corresponding log^2 proof routes. This is not an actual-core lower bound.

A negative unit with positive even-composite coefficients satisfies the
divisor envelope, prime-zero and local-variation rules, but its coarse
energy eventually exceeds every fixed log power. The countermodel does
not satisfy the actual four-mode source AST and refutes no actual-core
conjecture. It prevents a proof using only those weak facts from being
presented as closure of the signed leaf.
