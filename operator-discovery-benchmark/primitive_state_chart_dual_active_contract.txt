Face PCGLS
Charge face setup. Start unconstrained z_P=current x_P, r=b-A_P*z,
s=A_P^T*r,h_j=s_j/D_j,p=h,gamma=sum(s_j*h_j). All products/sums finite;
gamma>=0. Fresh sparse products use math.fsum; norms use math.hypot and
finite squares. A gamma==0 yields cg_stop zero_normal_residual, no steps.
For at most cg_limit iterations, charge one cg block then:
  q=A_P*p,delta=||q||^2.
  If delta==0 while gamma>0: stop zero_curvature without updating z.
  Otherwise a=gamma/delta,z+=a*p (unrestricted), with finite guards.
  Recompute fresh r=b-A_P*z,s=A_P^T*r,h=s/D,gamma_new=sum(s*h).
  If gamma_new==0, count completed step and stop zero_normal_residual.
  Otherwise beta=gamma_new/gamma,p=h+beta*p,gamma=gamma_new.
  At limit use iteration_limit. No numerical convergence theorem is claimed.
cg_blocks counts charged attempts; cg_steps_completed counts full z/residual
updates. A zero_curvature stop has blocks=completed+1. Other normal stops
have blocks=completed; iteration_limit equals cg_limit. Deadline/nonfinite
or memory failure is incomplete with consumed blocks retained.
