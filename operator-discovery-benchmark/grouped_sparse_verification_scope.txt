Sparse primal verification: soundness and cost scope

The old check_grouped_arithmetic.verify_joint_result first calls verify_joint, expanding every group via columns; it later calls columns again for each instance and checks the expanded certificate. Thus a sparse accepted witness pays for all unused quartic factor columns twice. The producer's grouped_arithmetic_synthesis.instantiate_certificate likewise invokes _limits on the whole instance before expanding its accepted coefficients. Full-cone preparation remains appropriate for searching arbitrary weights or validating a separator, but is unnecessary for verifying a fixed primal witness.

The new independent check_grouped_arithmetic_sparse verifier checks canonical exact weights and all group counts, hashes each COMPLETE instance, parses every source polynomial with its original horizon, and validates every primitive relation through the independent checker with memoization. Only exactly zero-weight group PRODUCTS and polynomial aggregation are skipped. Negative square weights, malformed inactive inputs, false inactive arithmetic premises, changed source hashes and incomplete/noncanonical polynomials are rejected. All nonzero arithmetic premises are validated and expanded, and the FULL original lhs minus their weighted sum must have no nonconstant coefficient and only the explicitly permitted nonnegative scalar slack. The exact expected certificate expansion must match the archived certificate, including every square, relation multiplier and scalar slack. There is no coefficient evaluation of mu/c and no producer arithmetic import.

Source binding is explicit through expected_instance_sha256. When absent, the returned source_binding_checked flag is false; a caller must bind dictionary/source/protocol hashes independently before claiming a sealed workflow audit. The whole instance hash includes the skipped groups. This checker proves only the finite primal identity and conditional positivity on established arithmetic relations. It does not check a dual: a separator must still test EVERY generator, regardless of any primal weights. It cannot conclude global arithmetic bounds or LP/SDP optimality.

Posthoc accepted unsigned fixture: X7 verifies against its archived full-checker result. At X11 the frozen transfer retained missing_resource, so a separate active-weight certificate was formed without refitting and independently checked; the frozen failure was not overwritten. Timings are recorded with exact artifact/source hashes. Source construction still costs about11s/32s at X7/X11, while sparse audits take about4s/12s respectively on this session. The full-checker baseline was bounded by a30s subprocess timeout INCLUDING source construction; it supplies no completed old-checker timing and hence no numerical speedup ratio. The optimization removes inactive product expansion, not the cost of materializing the current instance grammar. Four focused tests and actual X7 tamper checks cover equivalence and rejection behavior. These are verification benchmarks, not new fitted scores or all-scale evidence.

Resource accounting caveat: charged_operations is explicitly a proxy counting source-atom visits, parsed monomials, and active multiplication pair counts. It does not count JSON hash serialization, dictionary copying/aggregation inside P.add, or factor-validation internals. The time limit is checked at operation boundaries; it is not a hard interrupt within hashing or a primitive relation check. Existing independent primitive caps bound horizon, degree, polynomial size, coefficient bits, and factor loops. Wall-clock observations are therefore the performance measurement; the proxy must not be advertised as a full computational cost certificate. The independent reviewer found no mathematical admission problem and additionally rejected dimension mismatch, a tiny exact nonzero formerly inactive weight, wrong target, unsupported active axiom, and boolean relation weight.
