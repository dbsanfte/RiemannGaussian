COMPLETE conditional moment m_theta(h) times J_theta/theta from K*h,
where K(n)=n^(theta-1), J_theta(n)=n^theta-(n-1)^theta. The supplied

resulting bound is 6/[theta(1-theta)]*L*C^(1-r)*n^r. The conditional
moment includes the whole infinite tail; no N-dependent finite sum is
silently substituted. Cutoffs are fixed within each identity.
