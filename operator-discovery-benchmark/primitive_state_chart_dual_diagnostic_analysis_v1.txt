Three restricted searches

requires w_C=w_P=0 as well as w_beta=0; ignoring matrix moments is NOT sound.
There are853 zero equations and371 nonnegative scalar moments. A strict exact
witness separates the original cone; failure says nothing.
Diagonal dual: require both S and T offdiagonal zero and their30 diagonals

Diagonally dominant dual is the more useful next restriction. For each block,

Scope and limitations
A strict witness proves infeasibility of this FULL augmented finite cone,
not merely the current endpoint. Learned formal equations are additional
restrictions; the witness need not refute the unlearned original5641-equation
cone. It does not refute actual Mobius arithmetic, RH, all possible grammars,
or every uniform certificate. Conversely, failure of scalar/diagonal/DD
search or exact reconstruction proves nothing about feasibility. Even a full
PSD dual may lack a strict separating witness in weakly infeasible conic
systems; no Slater/strong-duality assumption has been established here.
The method advances finite grammar diagnosis, not an all-scale theorem.
