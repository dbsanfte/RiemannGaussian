===============================================

Scope and settled proposed grammar
---------------------------------
This review reads the authoritative077 handoff and preceding mathematical

No new scientific source query, candidate-score inspection, hashes,

The proposed study selects three surviving076 residuals: q1_p16 zero
baseline, q1_p04 direct3 and q1_p00 prime operator, with their frozen077
fold schedules. At each parent u its FULL folded coefficient function
c_u has horizon H and original parent count tokens. Choose y=ceil(H^gamma),
with generated rational gamma in[1/7,2/3]. Seven coordinates are signed

ceil(H^(2gamma/3)),y. Their exact projection is R=sum_j V_j.

1. Exact state admission precedes every fitted energy
---------------------------------------------------
For squarefree n retained by gcd(n,Q)=1, either all factors>y or its
least prime belongs to exactly one small-prime bin. The terminal signed
coordinate of factor count r is(-1)^r times its weighted coefficient
sum. In a least-prime child n=pm, the child is restricted to factors
STRICTLY>p, inherits gcd(m,Q)=1 and the SAME parent count tokens, and
mu(pm)=-mu(m). Thus NEGATIVE child aggregates and signed terminal layers
have projection d=(1,...,1). Units, empty bins and upper endpoints are
included; prime squares are excluded by strict factor order. Do not
introduce independent mu copies in different bins or layers.

With gamma>=1/7, y^7>=H. Strict factors>y imply no product of seven
factors can lie<=H, so the terminal degree<=6 is rigorously paid,
including equality y^7=H. Arbitrary smaller rational gamma can remain
an API option, but failure of the degree/resource gate is an unresolved
construction, not an arithmetic counterexample. At H=0 the function
and all terminal/bin coordinates are zero; a nonexistent c(1) must not
be inserted as a unit. At small positive H, prime bins may be empty
or cutoffs may coincide; those coordinates are exactly zero.

Recombination must independently equal the saved076 G-L on the SAME
parent. Prime groups/count roots, folded source binding and original
G/B/Q arithmetic admission remain separate dependencies. Reusing known
076 arithmetic for new features is legitimate; it is not a fresh holdout.
No missing dyadic parent may be zero-filled or called admitted. The
complete squarefree partition matters even if omitted pieces appear
numerically small. Changing normalization at a rough child changes the
kernel and invalidates this exact state projection.

2. A universally paid projection with genuine auxiliary coordinates
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For rational rays a_r, nonnegative lambda_r and rational eta_r>=0 define

 E(u)=R(u)^2+sum_r lambda_r u^(-2eta_r)(a_r dot V(u))^2.

Every term is a complete SIGNED square, retaining all prime/composite,
terminal/bin and same-source cross terms. Therefore E>=R^2 universally.
The exponent weights are evaluated at the actual parent u; at child
v=floor(u/2), its own v^(-2eta_r) is used. Fixed eta does not make the
parent and child state equal or remove token/count changes. Universal
positive-square domination is a real certificate, but bounds no E growth.

Equivalent matrix notation is W(u)=dd^T+sum lambda_r u^(-2eta_r)a_r a_r^T.
It is PSD and dominates dd^T by construction. This avoids energy shrinking
or an arbitrary matrix declared to control R without a projection proof.
More general rational PSD matrices can be admitted by exact LDL/Gram
certificates for W-dd^T, with complete dependencies and positivity domains.

One/two-coordinate rays, block sums and contrasts form a useful finite
dictionary. Exclude a ray proportional to d if claiming a new auxiliary
mechanism; that square merely rescales R. Reduce zero rays implied by
EXACT transport/degree identities. A ray zero only on discovery is not
automatically an identity and should be tagged as finite degeneracy.
Evaluate rank modulo declared exact state relations to distinguish real
auxiliary directions from many names for the same energy. Rank1 domination
of R is a valid baseline, never by itself a new cancellation theorem.

3. Uniform recurrence theorem and tau=1 distinction
-------------------------------------------------
Fix rational1<=q<2, complete base U0>=2, and rational0<=tau<=1. Write
F(u)=E(u)/u^q, v=floor(u/2). The searched arithmetic statement is

 F(u)<=tau F(v)+C for EVERY integer u>U0, C>=0.             (A)

If0<=tau<1 and EVERY base integer1<=u<=U0 obeys F(u)<=B0,
strong induction gives

 F(u)<=B=max(B0,C/(1-tau)), E(u)<=B u^q, R(u)^2<=B u^q.

At each step v<u, v>=1 and either its invariant or its complete base
row is available. This proof concerns the RESIDUAL energy's OWN base,
not merely the older G base. The residual bound can then feed the
075/076 G transfer with its separately paid original operator correlation,
G base and contraction budget. Changing q requires corresponding new
normalizations/base bounds; old q1 C is not a certificate at a new q.

If tau=1, induction instead gives
F(u)<=B0+C ceil(log2(u/U0)) for u>U0 (a safe dyadic step bound).
It gives logarithmic normalized growth, not constant B. This is a valid
different search objective if explicitly labelled. E=O(u^q logu) can
be converted to u^(q+epsilon) only with a separately explicit logarithm
price for each fixed epsilon; it is not the tau<1 theorem. A proved
q1 drift would supply a residual O(sqrt(u logu)) for this slice, while
the full original family/head/core/RH transfers remain unpaid.

Observed smallest-scale energy in discovery is NOT a complete finite
base. Until all base integers are independently paid, B0 is an explicit
unpaid obligation and any max(observedbase,C/(1-tau)) is a finite score.
For tau1, C+observedbase is likewise a numerical ranking preference,
not an extrapolation theorem. Uniform(A) remains unpaid for every fit.

4. Useful finite LP and exact freeze/validation
---------------------------------------------
For fixed q,tau,ray dictionary and eta, each observed recurrence is
linear in lambda_r and C:

 b_i+sum_r D_ir lambda_r<=C,
 b_i=R(u_i)^2/u_i^q-tau R(v_i)^2/v_i^q,
 D_ir=(a_r V(u_i))^2/u_i^(q+2eta_r)
       -tau(a_r V(v_i))^2/v_i^(q+2eta_r).

Nonnegative lambdas with a declared physical coefficient budget make
a real finite energy search. Minimizing a variable representing observed
base energy and C/(1-tau), rather than C alone, exposes the base cost
of added storage. Use the SAME tau,q,domain and normalization for the
zero-auxiliary baseline. A different energy can lower observed drift
without lowering the full eventual G transfer; report both distinctions.

Scale normalization of ray coordinates affects a trace/weighted budget.
Record the physical rays/weights so a large convenient feature scaling
does not appear as a cheap new theorem. Fixing a finite budget defines
the searched grammar, not the space of all possible energies. Rational
rounding of lambda,C and any budget repair must be followed by exact
admission of the FINAL formula and all signed recurrence terms. No
pre-rounding LP score is a mathematical certificate.

For interval admission, the UPPER normalized drift is
E_upper(u)/power_lower(u)-tau E_lower(v)/power_upper(v).
The LOWER normalized drift uses E_lower(u)/power_upper(u)
-tau E_upper(v)/power_lower(v). Thus upper<=C verifies a finite row,
lower>C refutes it, and a straddle is unresolved rounding. Each ray
square must be bounded as a whole signed linear form before squaring;
componentwise square sums would delete cross terms. Positive energy
term weights use certified inverse power endpoints in the correct
direction. The same signed comparison applies to observed base tests.

Freezing before known-data validation prevents adaptation from silently
rewriting the formula. Exact failure history is monotone: a later pass
cannot erase an earlier witness for that same frozen energy. New gamma,
ray or recurrence constant is a NEW candidate. Finite infeasibility of
one grammar does not refute all energies, q values or signed residuals.

5. A finite Farkas diagnostic that says more than refitting constants
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For the simpler fixed-tau LP minimizeC subject to the above rows,
lambda>=0 and sum_r t_r lambda_r<=T, with positive declared costs t_r.
Rational dual y_i>=0,z>=0 satisfying

 sum_i y_i=1,
 sum_i y_i D_ir+z t_r>=0 for every ray r

proves C>=sum_i y_i b_i-zT. This follows by multiplying each row by
y_i, using the budget and nonnegative lambdas; it requires no claim
about unseen rows. To verify a robust certificate from exact intervals,
use LOWER enclosures of b_i and D_ir in those inequalities. Such a dual
can identify a finite set of signed rows/rays blocking improvement over
the baseline. It is a useful actionable grammar diagnostic, not an
all-scale impossibility theorem. The observed-base epigraph LP has a
corresponding ordinary linear dual with all its constraints retained.

6. Generic all-scale Farkas/SOS proof rule, with honest arithmetic leaves
---------------------------------------------------------------------
An eventual uniform certificate can express the drift margin
C-F(u)+tau F(v) as a nonnegative rational combination of independently
PAID uniform quadratic inequalities, plus a rational PSD square form,
plus exact transport identities multiplied by linear forms. On the
declared arithmetic/domain family, every identity vanishes and every
paid inequality is nonnegative. Exact matrix/Gram verification then
proves(A), preserving signed cross terms.

This is a sound proof rule only if each imported inequality has its
own all-integer theorem, complete cutoff/token domain and noncircular
dependency. An inequality naming the desired parent energy bound is
not an admissible paid primitive. Finite source rows or a finite dual
do not establish those primitives. Algebraic count/root tokens can be
handled by declared positive-root relations and exact enclosures; a
pointwise numeric SOS is not a parameter-uniform certificate. Changing
prime roster size requires a genuine symbolic summation/domain rule,
not one fixed-dimensional matrix checked at selected parents.

If the only primitives are PSD and |mu|<=1 with unsigned coefficient
prices, the077 fixedP outer-band obstruction may still block the
desired sqrt-strength price. A meaningful new primitive is an actual
signed rough-terminal/small-prime-child correlation, a uniformly priced
transport innovation, or a closed smaller-rank rough-state energy theorem.
Its precise arithmetic claim must remain an explicit leaf. Smaller
support, factor cap6 and exact partition conservation do not prove it.

8. Independent static producer/checker audit after pre-run corrections
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Reviewed mobius_rough_energy_078.py, check_mobius_rough_energy_078.py
and relevant frozen077 helpers before science, with no arithmetic source
queries or test reruns. Early issues were sent to root and the producer
was re-read after fixes. This section describes that corrected snapshot;
the eventual runner's admission chain still needs integration validation.

Arithmetic partition: producer uses a compact Euler sieve for least prime,
mu and distinct-factor count. Repeated-prime products leave mu zero;
only nonzero-mu positions enter category queries, so their factor counts
are genuine squarefree omega. Checker independently flips signs over
prime multiples, zeroes prime-square multiples, records least prime and
distinct omega with an Eratosthenes construction. Its source arrays are
readonly, protecting cached admission from caller mutation.

Both compute three exact CEILING power cutoffs at gamma/3,2gamma/3,gamma.
For retained squarefree n, terminal category is its omega when least
prime>y; otherwise it enters the unique bin with open lower/closed upper
least-prime endpoints. Producer searchsorted side='left' places equality
at a cutoff in the lower bin; checker explicitly uses least>lo,least<=hi.
Thus both implement the same endpoint signatures and exclude foldedP
primes. Unit category is explicitly terminal0. An early producer finite
sentinel bug for gamma>1 was corrected by forcing pos==1 category0,
which implements P^-(1)=infinity independently of cutoff size. Generated
gamma<=2/3 was already safe, but this fix preserves the broader API.

The degree6 hypothesis is checked by y^7>=H. Checker uses bounded
power comparison, avoiding oversized seventh powers. Failure is tagged
domain_unpaid rather than an arithmetic operator refutation. Producer
positions may come from a larger cached source, but prefix queries stop
at the actual interval horizon. Checker dense category prefixes likewise
can use a larger N; higher-index data do not enter any queried endpoint.
Its force-unit branch and degree gate are sufficient to partition every
nonzero-mu row at or belowH. H0 has empty intervals and zero features.

Feature polynomials: producer separately sums signed mu weights in each
category against the complete interval coefficients. Checker independently
uses dense integer prefixes with right-inclusive interval subtraction.
Both retain parent tokens symbolically, merge shared tokens before
evaluation and preserve each terminal/bin sign. The bin coordinate is
the signed original-n aggregate, hence equals the NEGATIVE rough-child
aggregate by the least-prime identity; no additional sign should be
inserted during d=allones recombination. residual_polynomial constructs
the literal saved076 G-L using the same atom/count token names. The
independent verify_features can compare its complete feature sum to that
payload. Source/compiler/fold binding is still separately mandatory.

Projection and signed squares: ray_dictionary canonically normalizes
first nonzero coefficient, removes constant all-coordinate rays (zero
and proportionald), and emits axis, signed pair, block and contrast
directions. Equal rays deduplicate. Zero-on-discovery atoms are filtered
only as a numerical proposal decision; they must not be described as
new all-scale identities. Program admission rejects zero/projection-only
active rays and nonpositive lambda. Every admitted auxiliary square is
nonnegative, so E>=R^2 is universal, retaining ALL signed cross terms.
This proof requires no growth assumption and supplies no drift estimate.

Exact energy routines first build the whole residual polynomial and the
whole polynomial of each ray, then form their interval square. This
preserves shared-token cancellation and the intended signed interactions.
Each positive lambda term divides its lower square by the upper
u^(2eta) endpoint, and upper square by the lower endpoint. Normalization
by u^q uses the same safe directions. Independent drift comparison is
parent normalized LOWER minus tau child UPPER for refutation, and parent
UPPER minus tau child LOWER for verification. The checker enforces literal
child u//2 and positive index. Its three statuses CERTIFIED_FINITE,
REFUTED_FINITE,UNRESOLVED_INTERVAL correctly distinguish a threshold
straddle. Complete base, uniform drift/growth and source proof flags
remain false even after projection or finite drift admission.

LP scope and corrected rounding: for fixed q,tau the dictionary's ray
squares and baseline residual squares generate a linear finite minimax
problem. The observed-base epigraph is charged alongside C/(1-tau),
or C+observedbase for tau1. Column scaling is undone when serializing
physical lambda; the coefficient costs use squared l1 physical ray norms.
The corrected producer rounds nonnegative lambdas down to denominator
2^24, computes the EXACT weighted coefficient cost, and uniformly
rescales if that cost exceeds the budget. It then recalibrates the FINAL
formula, rather than retaining the floating LP C. Calibration rounds C
UPWARD to denominator2^40, preventing a valid large common-denominator
fraction from exceeding the checker's4096-bit input format. The exact
budget receipt is outside the small program schema; runner/integration
checks should preserve it if claiming an admitted budget-constrained search.

Numerical LP failure, terminal resource failure and an inactive/zero
dictionary are construction/search statuses, not all-scale residual
refutations. Only an independently admitted LOWER drift>C is a true
finite counterexample to that frozen recurrence. Changes to rays,C,
gamma or token normalization define a different candidate. A tau1
program remains a logarithmic-mode finite hypothesis, never a contractive
bound disguised by its score.

Resources: producer now accepts a supplied search meter, caps nonempty
