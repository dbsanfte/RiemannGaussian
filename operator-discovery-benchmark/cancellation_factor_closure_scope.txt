FACTOR-BLOCK SQUARE CLOSURE AND THE ACTUAL REPRESENTATION GAIN

Pre-score analytical advice for the new factor language. Existing sources and
calibrations remain fixed. No numerical scores, expected winning operator or
critical signed estimate is supplied.

1. Exact factor decomposition in formal polynomial space.
For a complete declared ordered product domain and exact numeric kernel weights,
let B=sum_(a,b)w_ab*x_a*x_b. Let C=sum_(coprime a,b)w_ab*x_(ab), with EVERY
product multiplicity retained, and R=sum_(noncoprime a,b)w_ab*x_a*x_b.
Define T=B-C-R=sum_(coprime a,b)w_ab*(x_a*x_b-x_(ab)).
This is a COMPLETE relation sum; every inside/complement part has its own exact
B,C,R,T and their full sums reconstruct the original domain. In the actual
arithmetic quotient T=0. Formal B andR are quadratic, C is linear, andT has
both degrees2 and1. The supplied compiler proves this identity, not a bound.
A nonconstant external multiplier is applied to EVERY relation and to the
full difference, not only its favorable terms.

2. Minimal degree-four closure for new squares.
The exact identity
 B²-(C+R)²=T*(B+C+R)
is the essential square transport. Its RHS is a sum of admitted coprime
relations with COMPLETE multiplier w_ab*(B+C+R). This closes the degree4
part of B² while retaining C²+2CR+R², including signed mixed terms. Using C²
alone drops R²+2CR and is false unless R is identically absent by the declared
source domain. A coprime-only domain has R=0, simplifying the identity to
B²-C²=T*(B+C). Empty/zero-kernel domains are harmless exact zero cases.
No actual signed coefficients are needed for these polynomial identities.

For any declared polynomial U, polarization closure is
 B*U-(C+R)*U=T*U.
Choose U from COMPLETE B,C,R, original prefix/block linear forms and fixed
rational mixtures, with shared coefficients. These generate degree3/4 relation
columns that the unmultiplied coprime bank lacks. Relation multipliers require
all source/product horizons and every polynomial term; budgets cannot silently
truncate the multiplier. T² itself is a relation multiple, sinceT=Σwρ:
T²=Σwρ*T. It is a nonnegative square that is identically0 on actual arithmetic,
so it may aid certificate conditioning/closure but supplies no arithmetic bound.
Similarly (B-C-R)² is a null square, not an observed cancellation estimate.

3. Quotient equivalence and the genuine opportunity.
With full square closure present, B² and(C+R)² represent the same quadratic
observable on actual arithmetic. This does NOT mean they are redundant in a
restricted finite cone: adding a grouped multiplier may express a relation that
was inaccessible to the previous shared basis. The gain is exact compressed
proof representation and new sign-bearing product-index interactions, not
new arithmetic information. If closure is unrestricted per index, it can simply
encode known finite coefficients; grouping must be specified before fitting.
For coprime-only domains, C is a weighted product-index linear form. Its norm
square can be placed in a general Gram atom bank, with exact collision weights.
This is more varied than the old five prefix/residue atoms, but its ordinary
energy estimate remains unknown. For full domains, R remains a signed bilinear
polynomial, so(C+R)² contains quartics and complete dense cross correlations;
it is not automatically an old linear-atom Gram square.

4. Minimal generic dictionary advice.
Declare factor kernels/cutoffs by a broad complexity-ordered rational grammar,
not one chosen from observed response values. For each declared part retain
B,C,R andT, with complete source complement. Start with shared square groups
B² and(C+R)² and optional nullT². Add closure relation columns T*U with U from
{B,C,R,B+C+R} and complete parent/child prefix or contrast linear forms, and
allow rational sparse mixtures. B,C,R individually span the square-transport
multiplier; a single supplied combined B+C+R column is the smallest calibration,
while independent shared coefficients leave room to discover other exact matches.
Multipliers need degree up to2, permitting degree4 expansion; linear multipliers
alone cannot generically cancel a bilinear-square degree4 term.
Keep a highest-degree exact nullspace/matching guard before target fitting,
and formalconstant extraction for every added column. These objects have zero
formalconstant, so their squares and relation products do not directly alter
the existing scalar budget module. They can only enable different matching
weights on old scalar groups. Norm-density gamma0 and sharper scalar-budget
obligations therefore still apply when their stated hypotheses hold.

5. Counterexample-driven boundaries and the uniform gate.
A generic coprime multiplicative positive sequence satisfies B=C+R while having
noncritical prefix growth; relation closure alone supplies no sign estimate.
The squarefree partially normalized counterfeit further satisfies actual mu
norm/gcd relations and a fixed prime bank. Any finite bound-search claim must
retain sufficient actual arithmetic information to exclude those models;
no theorem requires every individual prime sign in every possible proof.
A dual witness may be invalidated by a new factor column, but feasibility still
needs resynthesis and exact shared certificate verification. Unexpected monomials
require a complete extended dual rather than implicit zeros.
The full original signed target remains unchanged. A useful finite factor
certificate merely nominates a symbolic template; uniform admission requires
ALL-scale nonconstant identity, nonnegative shared squares and a proved critical
scalar budget with every initial/source/product/boundary charge. The factor
closure identities themselves prove none of those missing signed estimates.
