SIGNED ARITHMETIC LANGUAGE: FIXED FILTERS AND PRIME-CORE CANCELLATION

Status

The objective is an actual all-scale signed arithmetic bound sufficient for
ordinary RH. That bound remains unproved. This stage adds a verified finite
search, conditional RH bridges and an obstruction to one proof strategy. It
does not promote a global inequality or change the legacy theorem registry.
Historical novelty is not an acceptance criterion.

Implemented language and verified experiment

The immutable run is fixed-pole-filter-results/pilot-001. Its 17 frozen
source/test/proof files implement fixed rational dilation filters on all nine
actual targets a=mu^{*r}*lambda^{*l}, 1<=r+l<=3. Complete ordinary Dirichlet
convolution and actual prime coefficients define each source. Writing
A(x)=sum_(n<=x)a(n), programs have G(x)=sum_d w_d A(floor(x/d)), with unit
leading weight, merged distinct divisors and all signed cross terms retained.

The generator enumerates sparse filters and compositions and proposes exact
box-constrained quadratic fits, followed by rational quantization and exact
refitting. The supplied divisor grid, arities, fitting objective and selection
rules are explicit design assumptions. This is bounded automatic discovery
within those rules, not an assumption-free language inventor.

There were 3,959 proposals, 3,935 distinct programs, 24 aliases, no missing
proposals and 11 selections. Thirty tests and 17 independent audit groups
passed. An independent factor-local coefficient implementation and arbitrary-
precision full covariance calculation checked the producer. Three sealed
worker outputs replayed byte for byte. Training base scales run through8192;
validation bases are12288,16384,24576; final bases are32768,42435,65536, with
complete coefficients through262143. All numerical work uses declared finite
budgets. The Python file-access guard is not OS isolation. Historical actual
coefficient prefixes overlap; this is not an unseen-prime holdout.

Direct tests:53 passes,13 failures,66 missing. Innovation tests:62 passes,
4 failures,66 missing. The missing constants are at unseen q=17,29; they are
never counted as passes. Nine direct and two innovation failures are dyadic;
the other failures are odd-scale diagnostics. A stored-constant failure does
not refute an existential bound that permits different constants. Three
parallel Sol6.1 review batches cover all11 programs in review-collection-001;
the collector checks provenance/coverage, not mathematical validity, and does
not independently authenticate the runtime model. Every global claim remains
unresolved after mathematical review.

A concrete numerical lead

For c=mu*lambda and C(x)=sum_(n<=x)c(n), the learned program50ea9e46f5e6 is

  G(x)=C(x)-(11/4)C(floor(x/6)).

It passed all12 available final observations, six direct and six innovation;
six observations in each mode remain missing at untrained exponent levels.
At q=12 its fitted direct constant is179/128. At X=65536 its normalized
energy is9642391273/4294967296 (about2.245), against about25.074 for C itself.
The unfiltered C baseline failed three direct tests of its fitted constants.
The mu and lambda baselines also passed their available tests, so passing
alone is not evidence of a new uniform arithmetic estimate.

The conditional bridge permits this aggressive filter. For k=r+l and
P(s)=sum_d w_d d^(-s), the actual Dirichlet series is

  P(s) zeta(2s)^l / zeta(s)^k.

A nonzero m-term fixed multiplier has zero multiplicity at most m-1 by the
Vandermonde matrix of distinct log divisors. Thus m<=k preserves a pole at
every possible ordinary zeta zero with real part greater than1/2. An all-dyadic
bound sum_(X<=n<2X)|G(n)|^2 <= Kq X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)), for every sufficiently
large integer q with Kq independent of X, would imply RH by Mellin continuation.
This proves the implication, not its premise. A bounded inverse recovering C
is unnecessary here; the usual absolute inverse condition fails for11/4>sqrt6.
Moving filters do not inherit this fixed-multiplier theorem.

Post-selection diagnostic, separate from scoring

low-zero-response-audit.json/txt evaluates the already frozen multiplier at
the first20 returned critical-line zeta zeros, using mpmath1.3.0 at30 and60
decimal digits. At the first two zeros, |P| is about0.23846 and0.12747. Across
20 zeros it ranges from0.12280 to2.09537, with RMS1.44633 (baseline1). Therefore
the attenuation is selective. Agreement across precisions is numerical
stability, not a certified error bound or multiplicity certificate.

For a simple zeta zero rho, the leading double-pole Laurent coefficient is
multiplied by P(rho); the simple-pole coefficient also receives a P'(rho)
term. No actual residue, modal cross term, contour remainder or infinite tail
was bounded. This diagnostic cannot attribute the measured energy reduction
to those zeros or supply a uniform estimate. It changed no selection or review.

What the pairing proof rules out

prime_power_pairing.py certifies complete partial matchings using actual
c(p^e m)=2(-1)^e c(m), p not dividing m, e>=1. An odd exponent and the next
even exponent cancel exactly if both endpoints fit inside the cutoff. The
implementation retains every unmatched core, boundary and blocked edge.

Every squarefree integer in(X/2,X] is isolated in this entire same-core
matching class. The proof gives unsigned residual>=X/8 for every integer
X>=256, even if all primes and adaptive matching orders are allowed. Thus
triangle inequality on these leftovers cannot yield a sublinear bound.
This is not a lower bound for the signed C(X) and does not refute RH. It
identifies the next task: cancellation between different cores or within the
complete signed boundary, not simply a larger prime dictionary.

Next language extension: exact prime-conditioned factor regions

The following productions are PROPOSED, not implemented by this pilot.
Existing grammars already contain signed Gram observables, coprime masks,
fixed-filter composition and nonnegative potentials. The missing extension
is a composable arithmetic partition that feeds its COMPLETE signed Gram
to the detector and proof backend.

1. RoughPrefix(a,x,z) = sum_(n<=x, P^-(n)>z) a(n), with P^-(1)=infinity.
For an actual multiplicative a, integer x>=1 and y>=z>=1, an exact least-prime
partition gives

  R_a(x,z)=R_a(x,y)
    +sum_(z<p<=y, p prime) sum_(e>=1, p^e<=x)
       a(p^e) R_a(floor(x/p^e),p).

The exponent is the exact valuation, so each n appears once. The rough
remainder and n=1 persist. For mu, a(p)=-1 and higher prime powers vanish:

  M(x)=1-sum_(p<=x, p prime) R_mu(floor(x/p),p).

This exposes signed relations between prime-conditioned cores while giving
exact recovery of the ordinary prefix. Allow cutoffs floor(x^theta) for
positive rational theta, with exact floors and child-scale recomputation.
Count all support, boundary and lookahead costs.

2. PrimePowerForcing(a,n), preserving the exact actual identity

  a(n) log n = sum_(p^e|n) [-r+l*(-1)^e] log p * a(n/p^e)

for a=mu^{*r}*lambda^{*l}. For c only odd prime powers survive, with coefficient
-2 log p. This follows from the Euler logarithmic derivative, or coefficientwise
from the local rational Euler factor. Exact symbolic log-prime coefficients
should be retained; finite numerical evaluation needs controlled intervals.
Partial summation, its initial term and its integral must be charged before
using a log-weighted bound to control an ordinary prefix. The identity alone
does not add a prime-distribution estimate.

3. FactorRegionSum and CompleteRegionGram. Partition the prime/core domain
using product cutoffs, valuation, coprimality, quotient intervals and rational
root scales. Search weights and compositions under frozen complexity limits.
Emit an exact reconstruction such as M(n)=1-sum_i S_i(n)-R(n). Squaring must
retain the constant, every region pair, and every residual interaction.

For integer X, the exact full prefix-energy kernel is
  K_X(v)=max(0,2X-max(X,v)).
Pairs of events at products pm and p'm' carry K_X(max(pm,p'm')). A normalized
dyadic innovation carries [K_(2X)(v)-4K_X(v)]/(4X^2), including its signs.
Learned partitions may expose cancellation here; bounding every region
separately can destroy the effect. Direct prime-error versions must also
retain the deterministic main term and every prime-power contribution.

Proof and search acceptance for that extension

Certify identities for all inputs in their declared domain; use finite
examples only for implementation checks and candidate ranking. Freeze new
productions, resource rules and selection before a new evaluation. The current
final scales and low-zero responses become development evidence if reused.
Preserve failures and missing constants. Let the search vary partitions,
fractional cutoffs and signed weights without supplying a desired winner.

The needed output is a complete signed correlation or potential-innovation
estimate, uniform in scale and with cofinal exponents, together with ordinary
prefix recovery or a checked ordinary-zeta pole bridge. Keep exact identities,
conditional implications, finite observations and global estimates separate.
No single correlation family is assumed to be the eventual solution; retain
unsupported but reproducible numerical leads for analytical review.
