Independent analytical scope for the typed anchored full14 storage extension

This note is an algebraic and conditional recovery audit. It supplies neither
an all-scale innovation estimate nor primal feasibility. Published and frozen
sources are unchanged. The new checker imports the independent full14 source
builder and independent polynomial/PSD checker, never the new producer backend.

The complete state and the signed identity

Let V_t contain prefix, half, even, mod3one, prime and every one of the nine
unit-normalized divisor contrasts in the recorded order. For a fixed q choose
one finite exact matrix K_q PSD, independent of t and X. Write

  R_q = e_P e_P^T + K_q,
  E(X) = sum_(X <= t < 2X) P_t^2,
  S_K(X) = sum_(X <= t < 2X) V_t^T K_q V_t,
  S_R(X) = E(X) + S_K(X),
  Delta(A)(X) = A(2X) - 4 A(X).

PSD gives S_R(X)>=E(X)>=0 at every scale, including X=1. The new target
identity is exactly

  D(X)-Delta(E)(X) = Q(X) + Delta(S_K)(X),
  D(X)-Delta(S_R)(X) = Q(X).

Here Q consists of the unchanged nonnegative scalar squares, the two unchanged
regional full14 PSD Gram polynomials, valid signed arithmetic relations and
any nonnegative scalar slack. Arithmetic relations vanish only after their
actual premises have been admitted. The negative parent coefficient belongs
to Delta(S_K). It is not a nonnegative Gram coefficient or an SOS term.

The independent checker expands both complete regional sources, validates the
typed child coefficient +1 and parent coefficient -4, and checks the transformed
target D-Delta(S_R) with the ordinary independent SOS checker. K is one shared
matrix across all instances. A PSD R alone is insufficient: the remainder
R-e_P e_P^T=K must be PSD. The finite checker requires exact PSD of this
remainder; it also reconstructs the initial unit state below. Independent
source rebuilding remains mandatory in the enclosing run audit: rehashing a
truncated caller-supplied source is not an independent source-completeness proof.

Complete dual admission requires positivity of

  H_K = H_child - 4 H_parent,

in addition to the two regional moment matrices. Matrix entries include every
atom cross polynomial and every formal constant. Upper-triangle compiler
columns have coefficient two off the diagonal; moment entries do not. The
entire scalar/Gram monomial domain is reconstructed independently. Missing
coordinates are unsupported, never assigned zero. The current source geometry
introduces no new monomials beyond the complete regional crosses, but it adds
a signed PSD parameter cone and its additional dual condition. A negative
direction of H_K invalidates that particular separator. It proves neither
feasibility of this cone nor a uniform arithmetic inequality. Conversely a
separator admitted only at finite scales excludes only the declared finite
shared-template cone with its explicit auxiliary restriction.

Exact constants, X1, and the old cubic gate

The formal zero-variable constant vector is

  z=(0,0,0,0,0,-1/2,...,-1/2).

Every Gram constant is z^T G z, including all divisor-divisor cross terms.
There are 2X child and X parent blocks, hence the storage RHS constant is

  (2X-4X) z^T K_q z = -2X z^T K_q z.

It is nonpositive and O_q(X) for fixed K_q, not a nonnegative SOS charge. This
formal constant is distinct from the actual initial state. At X=1 the complete
parent source uses t=1 and the actual unit x_1=1. Its state is exactly

  v_1=(1,0,0,1,0,0,...,0),
  S_R(1)=1+v_1^T K_q v_1.

The half coordinate is P_0=0; all nine divisor contrasts vanish on the unit
boundary. All mask and cross contributions survive in v_1^T K_q v_1. This is
finite for every fixed finite rational K_q and is at least one. A zero formal
storage charge does not permit dropping this initial cost.

The previous scalar asymptotic for the unchanged mu dictionary, fixed finite
shared weights and fixed integer q>1 is

  B_old,q(X) = delta*gamma_q*X^3 + o_q(X^3), delta=1/zeta(2)>0,
  gamma_q=-(56/3)b_all-12 b_head-(20/3)b_tail.

It uses the complete child-rowmass norm sums, squarefree density, subcubic
prime sums, fixed-kernel/modulus assumptions and the recorded normalization
and constant extraction. Unit-normalized added fixed Gram terms are O_q(X).
The new storage adds exactly -2X z^T K_q z, so

  B_new,q(X)=delta*gamma_q*X^3+o_q(X^3).

The old gamma=0 auxiliary can therefore be retained as a necessary gate for
the following specific uniform certificate objective: (i) the fixed-template
polynomial identity holds at every required dyadic scale, with PSD and actual
relations; (ii) its extracted scalar budget is critical/subcubic, specifically
O_q(X^(2+1/q)); (iii) the old scalar asymptotic hypotheses hold unchanged.

Proof of both signs: gamma>0 contradicts requirement (ii), since 2+1/q<3.
If gamma<0, the all-scale identity and nonnegative proof terms imply eventually

  Delta(S_R)(X) <= B_new,q(X) <= -a_q X^3,

for some a_q>0. This implication follows by matching nonconstant coefficients
and replacing D by the exact extracted scalar B; any allowed nonnegative slack
can only reduce the upper bound. Put W(X)=S_R(X)/X^2>=0. Then for all sufficiently
large dyadic X,

  W(2X) <= W(X) - (a_q/4)X.

Starting at one such finite scale, iteration makes W negative because the sum
of its dyadic X terms diverges. This is a contradiction. Only a finite starting
state and nonnegative S_R are used; no upper growth assumption on the raw
divisor channels or on S_R is required. Thus gamma<0 is also impossible under
(i). This proves gamma=0 under (i)-(iii), but does not prove that gamma=0 makes
the next-order scalar remainder critical. The recorded o(X^3) prime/density
terms can still exceed X^(2+1/q).

This necessity is NOT an unconstrained theorem about all possible innovation
bounds or all finite certificates. An arbitrary uniform bound on the actual
innovation need not supply this fixed-template critical scalar budget. A
positive-gamma template with a cubic extracted budget might be accompanied by
a sharper argument outside the present language. Finite identities alone also
do not imply either asymptotic sign contradiction. At q=1 the subcubic premise
fails and gamma=0 is an explicitly imposed restriction. The new descriptor
therefore names the gate's conditional scope; any finite separation result must
retain that qualification. Allowing K to grow with X, changing source weights,
or normalizing only part of a centered channel invalidates this scalar proof
and requires a fresh complete charge/growth calculation.

Conditional uniform recovery, including the coercivity constant

Suppose independently that for fixed q>0, finite C_q>=0 and fixed PSD K_q,

  Delta(S_R)(X) <= C_q X^2 ceil(X^(1/q))

holds at EVERY dyadic integer X>=1. Set a=1/q, W(X)=S_R(X)/X^2. Then

  W(2X)<=W(X)+(C_q/4)ceil(X^a)
        <=W(X)+(C_q/2)X^a,

because X>=1. At X=2^m, exact summation gives the sharper conditional bound

  W(X)<=S_R(1)+(C_q/2)*(X^a-1)/(2^a-1),
  E(X)<= [S_R(1)+(C_q/2)/(2^a-1)] X^(2+a).

The formula includes X=1 (an empty geometric sum). Its denominator is positive
for q>0. The fixed anchor here is c_q=1. For the more general preregistered
condition R_q-c_q e_P e_P^T PSD, c_q>0 independent of X, divide the recovered
energy constant by c_q. An anchor that vanishes or depends on X cannot inherit
this recovery; an alternative scale law would need its own proof.

For the project's RH bridge, a single q=6 or any finite grid is insufficient.
The recorded structural objective asks for some q0 and every integer q>=q0,
with K_q, c_q and finite constants permitted to depend on q but not on X. A
cofinal unbounded sequence of such q also yields energy exponents 2+epsilon
for every epsilon>0, by choosing 1/q<epsilon and keeping that q fixed; this
weaker schedule must be stated if used instead of the recorded every-q goal.
The ordinary prefix-energy/Mellin bridge still requires its actual arithmetic,
analytic continuation and growth hypotheses. The present algebra proves no
pointwise square-root estimate and supplies no missing bridge hypotheses.

The half-coordinate boundary and scale redundancy

Let H(X)=sum_(X<=t<2X)P_floor(t/2)^2. For EVERY even positive integer X,
each k in [X/2,X) has the two exact fibers t=2k,2k+1. Therefore

  H(X)=2E(X/2).

At X=1, H(1)=P_0^2=0. In particular H(2)=2E(1), so

  Delta(H)(1)=2E(1),
  Delta(H)(X)=2 Delta(E)(X/2) for dyadic X>=2.

For K=e_half e_half^T the full state is consequently

  S_R(1)=E(1),
  S_R(X)=E(X)+2E(X/2) for dyadic X>=2,
  Delta(S_R)(1)=Delta(E)(1)+2E(1),
  Delta(S_R)(X)=Delta(E)(X)+2Delta(E)(X/2) for dyadic X>=2.

The X=1 term must not be replaced by a nonexistent E(1/2) target. If the
original Delta(E) critical inequality is already available uniformly, the
last expression is bounded by at most (3/2)C_q X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)) at X>=2,
with the separate finite X1 contribution charged. Conversely a uniform
critical innovation estimate for this half-state recovers E by the telescope
above. A uniform critical E bound in turn bounds Delta(E) by E(2X). Thus the
two uniform critical targets are equivalent up to q-dependent constants and
the starting scale. The half direction adds a previous-scale energy and no
new signed prime information. It may still enlarge a finite shared certificate
cone because the corresponding previous-scale inequality was absent there;
that finite algebraic enlargement is not evidence of a new arithmetic estimate.
The entire full14 K family is retained, rather than replacing it by this one
diagnostic direction. No redundancy claim is made for every general K.

Outstanding promotion obligations

A finite accepted template must still be an exact structural formula valid at
all required scales; numerical transfer checks cannot prove that identity.
Every unit, floor, cutoff, prime-power, noncoprime and complementary source term
must remain. PSD, valid arithmetic premises, shared matrix/coefficient laws,
the complete scalar budget and any denominator signs require uniform proofs.
Initial cost, fixed positive coercivity, q quantifiers and the actual RH bridge
must be explicitly retained. These obligations are unresolved. No optimizer
failure, resource failure, missing moment or witness invalidity is promoted to
primal feasibility, mathematical infeasibility, an all-scale bound or RH.
