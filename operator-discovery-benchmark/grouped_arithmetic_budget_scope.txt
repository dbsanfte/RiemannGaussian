THE EXACT SCALAR BUDGET OF THE FIXED GROUPED DICTIONARY

Analytical follow-up before scores; no outcomes read or frozen sources edited.
This note concerns the declared52 square/195 relation descriptor dictionary in
grouped_arithmetic_templates.py. It extracts its exact scalar budget. No global
coefficient identity, critical budget estimate, prime cancellation theorem or
RH conclusion is established.

1. Formal coefficient extraction, not substitution of x1=1.
Let a_d>=0 denote the fixed shared square weights, and b_d the fixed signed
relation-group weights. Expand the COMPLETE grouped polynomial RHS_X with these
weights and every declared term. Let [1] denote its formal constant coefficient,
not evaluation at actual arithmetic. If EVERY nonconstant coefficient equals
the corresponding coefficient of -Delta_X, then
 RHS_X=B_template(X)-Delta_X, B_template(X)=[1]RHS_X.
For a prescribed D=C X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)), the computed slack is exactly
 s_X=D-B_template(X).
A finite certificate accepts only nonnegative s_X and appends s_X*1^2.
This formula has no actual signed response input and cannot repair an unmatched
nonconstant coefficient. Formal x1 is still a variable: a polynomial containing
x1 has zero constant coefficient even though actual x1=1. Treating it as1 before
coefficient extraction would alter the fixed language and the budget.

2. Exactly which declared groups contribute.
The sole constant square group is descriptor
 {family:constant,region:all,weight:critical_normalizer}.
Its fixed internal coefficient is Z_q(X)=X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)). Write its shared
weight as a_0. Every coordinate, prefix, pair, divisor-row and product square
has a polynomial with zero constant term, so its square has zero constant term.
This includes product squares of degree4 and all empty positional groups.

For relation groups, multiplier one is a constant internal weight W_X(n).
The available weights are1,X,J_X(n), and
 R_X(n)=sum_(t=max(2X,n))^(4X-1)t,
with exact empty sums0. Regions are all, head n<=2X and tail n>2X.
Norm relation x_n^2-h_n contributes -W_X(n)*h_n, where
 h_n=1_(n squarefree) for mu, h_n=4^omega(n) for c.
Prime-power relation x_(p^e)-v_f(p,e) contributes -W_X(p^e)*v_f(p,e),
where v_mu=-1 at e1 and0 for e>=2; v_c=2(-1)^e.
The unit relation x1-1 with multiplier one contributes -1.
Every other declared multiplier (anchor, prefix, global_prefix) has zero formal
constant, and its product with any relation therefore has zero constant.
In particular, norm at n1 with anchor x1 contributes no formal scalar; the
same applies to prime-power prefix multipliers. Squarefree-zero relations have
no constant at any n. Inverse rows are declared only for n>=2 and have no
constant; the excluded inverse row n1 is represented separately by unit.
Coprime-product and gcd-product relations have no constants, including degenerate
arguments1 and equal arguments. Their signed coefficient and norm factors do
not create a constant monomial. No zero/empty group contributes a hidden scalar.

3. Exact extraction formula with descriptor multiplicities retained.
For each relation descriptor d with multiplier one, let I_d(X) be its complete
declared region of norm indices or prime powers, and W_d,X its exact internal
weight. Distinct all/head/tail descriptors may overlap: they are separate columns
and their shared weights must ALL appear. Then
 B_template(X)
 =a_0*Z_q(X)
  -sum_(d:family norm,multiplier one)b_d*sum_(n in I_d(X))W_d,X(n)*h_n
  -sum_(d:family prime_power,multiplier one)b_d*
                  sum_(p^e in I_d(X))W_d,X(p^e)*v_f(p,e)
  -b_(unit,all,one,multiplier one).
This formula is exact for EVERY finite X as formal algebra; it does not assume
that nonconstant matching holds there. The polynomial source horizon isN=4X-1.
For mu the prime-power inner sum reduces exactly to minus the weighted prime
sum, because every higher local value is0. For c ALL positive exponents occur
with their alternating2 signs. No prime powers or region endpoints may be
replaced by a continuous density without an independently justified error.

4. What the formula exposes, and what remains unknown.
The scalar budget contains no arbitrary ordinary signed prefix values. It is a
fixed linear combination of complete actual norm counts and signed local prime-
power sums, with elementary polynomial/kernel weights, rational coefficients
and critical normalizer. This is a useful way to state the missing all-scale
scalar theorem explicitly. But its fixed weights may have either sign on the
relation families, and different regions/weights have different growth. A bound
must retain their cancellation rather than separately bounding all magnitudes
and claiming a critical result.
The rowmass norm sum is exactly the unsigned calibration U_X. With shared norm
weight-1 it contributes +U_X; the companion square weights4 and1 cancel the
nonconstant kernel exactly. Its unconditional upper bound is power3 for mu and
power3 timeslog^3 for c. This is a complete uniform identity but fails the critical
budget objective. It demonstrates why algebraic matching by itself is inadequate.

Possible useful new arithmetic would be a proved fixed coefficient combination
in the extraction formula with B_template(X)=O_q(X^2 ceil(X^(1/q))) and ALL its
region/end-point/remainder charges, paired with a proved complete nonconstant
identity and nonnegative shared square weights. Weighted prime/norm main terms
might cancel, but no such cancellation or leading asymptotic is asserted here.
PNT alone cannot be credited with an RH-strength weighted error, and cancellation
of one leading power can still leave a supercritical next term. Any norm-density
or prime-power asymptotic used must state its uniform errors and exact cutoff
changes; an attractive finite scalar profile proves none of these.

5. The two independent uniform obligations.
First prove for ALL dyadicX that the complete symbolic grouped RHS has
nonconstant coefficients exactly -Delta_X, with the fixed square weights
nonnegative and all factor-certified arithmetic relations valid on the whole
domain. Finite shared matching or successful transfer does not prove this.
Second prove the extracted B_template(X)<=Cq X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)) for EVERY
integerq>=q0 with finiteCq independentX, including finite initialization and all
rational ceilings. A sampled nonnegative slack does not prove this either.
Both are necessary to admit this particular template as an all-scale certificate;
other proof languages may have different obligations. Neither unknown step may
be inserted as a new arithmetic axiom. The original full actual defect is then
bounded, energies telescope, and the ordinary-zeta bridge applies conditionally.
No outcomes, numerical re-fit, leading-term heuristic or solver completeness
claim is used in this extraction note.
