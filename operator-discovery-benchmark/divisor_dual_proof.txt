DIVISOR-ZERO GRAMS AND A DUAL CERTIFICATE FOR THE FULL SIGNED DEFECT

Scope: this stage targets the FULL signed energy inequality, including the
dense remainder of the preceding common-factor audit. The algebraic and
matrix-certificate methods are supplied. Learned kernels and masks must
keep their exact semantics. Native matrix positivity proves a finite-scale
inequality, never an unbounded-scale premise. No RH-strength global PSD
comparison is claimed. No prime-distribution theorem is imported.

1. FULL ACTUAL TARGET AND EXACT DIVISOR CONSTRAINTS.
For X>=1 integer let N=4X, v_n=mu(n), and H_X(n,m)=W_X(max(n,m)),
where W_X(r)=K_(2X)(r,r)-4*K_X(r,r), K_X(r,r)=(2X-max(X,r))_+.
Then v^T H_X v=Delta(X)=A_2(2X)-4*A_2(X), for the actual STEP Mertens
energy. Every pair is present. The cofinal positive-epsilon target and its
all-ordinary-height recovery are precisely those of critical_defect_proof.txt.

Let C_N(k,n)=1_(n divides k), k,n=1..N. Every divisor row is COMPLETE.
Dirichlet inversion of the constant-one sequence gives C_N*v=e_1 exactly.
The coefficient producer uses full divisor inversion, with no factor oracle.
For any unit-normalized generic sequence f, the analogous convolution matrix
C_f(k,n)=1_(n|k)*f(k/n) times the exact inverse of f also gives e_1.
That generic algebraic identity does NOT imply the actual bound |v_n|<=1,
or ordinary-zeta recovery. Large generic inputs refute that transfer.

2. A CREATIVE BUT EXACT ARITHMETIC GRAM.
For a nonnegative boolean row mask w(k), put
G_w=C_N^T*diag(w)*C_N.
It is PSD, and v^T G_w v=w(1). Its exact entry is
sum_(k<=N, lcm(n,m)|k) w(k).
For all rows this is floor(N/lcm(n,m)); for every nonunit row it is
G_0(n,m)=floor(N/lcm(n,m))-1_(n=m=1).
Thus G_0 is PSD and G_0*v=0. The kernel uses actual arithmetic constraints
at every row scale, including numbers divisible by primes up to N. Its
signed double sum vanishes at EVERY finite N, not just observed prefixes.
This is an exact arithmetic identity, not new prime-distribution control.

Anonymous matrix-entry fitting can recover the floor/lcm expression from
divisor-Gram response tables, against a broader declared index dictionary.
Anonymous zero-forcing response masks can infer a reusable row production.
Their admission backends count multiples and use the universal inverse
identity; an approximate fit is not substituted for an exact kernel.

3. THE DUAL OPERATOR AND EVERY COST.
For any D>=0 and Y>=0 define
S_(X,q)=D(X,q)*I-H_X+Y(X)*G_w.
If S is PSD, evaluating it on the ACTUAL vector v gives
Delta(X)<=D(X,q)*sum_(n<=N)mu(n)^2+Y(X)*w(1)
          <=N*D(X,q)+Y(X)*w(1).
All terms in H remain. Large Y is free only when its forcing cost w(1)
is exactly zero. The scalar cost is otherwise charged; calling it a zero
term when its unit row is present is a false certificate.

The rational family is
D(X,q)=c_D*(1+ceil(q^r))*X*ceil(X^(1/q)), q>=1 integer, r>=0 rational,
Y(X)=c_Y*X^floor(gamma)*ceil(X^(gamma-floor(gamma))), gamma>=0 rational,
with nonnegative rational coefficients and complete exact ceiling semantics.
Ceilings increase the bound; they do not alter its ordinary powers. At any
FIXED q, N*D=O_q(X^(2+1/q)). If w(1)=0, or gamma<=2, the total cost has
this cofinal critical power. Constants may depend on q arbitrarily.

A GLOBAL proof that the proposed S_(X,q) is PSD for every dyadic X and
every sufficiently large q would prove the full inequality and hence RH.
If r=0 and c_D is fixed across q, its charged diagonal constant is bounded
in q. Taking q to infinity at each finite X then gives a fixed critical
Delta bound. That stronger candidate would also imply simple critical
zeros as explained in the preceding proof. It is labelled separately;
the positive-r family does not require that extra bounded-constant premise.
Finite sampled q or X does not supply either universal statement.

4. AN UNCONDITIONAL GLOBAL CALIBRATION, NOT THE RH ESTIMATE.
All entries of H have absolute value at most 2X, N=4X, so
||H||_2<=8X^2 by the maximum absolute row sum. Consequently D>=8X^2
makes D*I-H PSD at every integer X, and adding Y*G_w preserves PSD.
For q=1 the family can meet that condition with c_D>=4. This is a
proved power-THREE energy bound, corresponding to ordinary unsigned
Mertens power one. It is not a cofinal-q critical certificate. The backend
keeps this global calibration separate from finite critical-matrix leads.

5. WHY LARGE GRAM WEIGHTS DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY PROVE RH.
For the full nonunit Gram, ker(G_0)=span(mu), since C_N is invertible and
only its first row was removed. Hence positivity necessarily requires
D*Q(N)-Delta(X)>=0. That is the actual signed inequality again.

Conversely, if D*Q(N)>Delta(X), some finite Y makes the matrix positive
definite. Write u=mu/sqrt(Q(N)), decompose into u and its orthogonal
complement, and set a=D-u^T H u>0. On that complement G_0 has a positive
least eigenvalue. Its block Schur complement is positive for sufficiently
large Y; explicitly Y*g_min>||H||+||H||^2/a suffices.
One elementary bound is g_min>=1/(2*N^(3/2)): for v orthogonal to mu,
v=P_perp*C_N^(-1)*(0,C_tail*v), while the inverse row sum is at most
2*sqrt(N) and its column sum at most N. Thus the finite dual certificate
can re-express an existing finite energy bound. It does not manufacture
the missing sign cancellation. A simple GLOBAL matrix comparison, or a
uniformly proved recursive decomposition of it, remains the needed new step.

6. UNIVERSAL BARRIERS FOR PRUNED ARITHMETIC CONSTRAINTS.
Prefix rows k<=L constrain no coefficient above L. A vector that agrees
with mu through L and is positive thereafter satisfies those complete
prefix rows. For fixed L its cumulative sum is x+O_L(1), giving energy
power three. An all-scale critical dual certificate from only fixed prefix
rows is impossible. A negative matrix witness refutes the certificate,
not automatically the actual Mobius inequality.

Even infinitely many row constraints from finitely many primes are
insufficient for a generic sign bound. For a finite prime set P define
b_P(n) multiplicatively: local values at p in P are 1,-1,0,0,... on
1,p,p^2,..., and at other primes every local value is one.
Then |b_P(n)|<=1, and C*b_P vanishes on EVERY row divisible by a prime
in P. Yet
sum_(n<=x)b_P(n)=c_P*x+O_P(1), c_P=product_(p in P)(1-1/p)^2>0.
Indeed b_P=1*a_P with finite support on divisors of product(P)^2;
a_P(p)=-2, a_P(p^2)=1. The floor remainder is at most 4^|P|.
Its full energy defect has leading (28/3)*c_P^2*X^3 and an O_P(X^2)
error. Thus any cofinal critical PSD comparison using ONLY such selected
rows is false, even though those rows supply exact arithmetic at all scales.

For P={2}, the explicit counter-vector is 1 on odd indices, -1 on indices
congruent to two modulo four, and zero on multiples of four. It satisfies
every even divisor row and its cumulative sum grows like x/4. These are
counterfeit arithmetic models, not evidence against RH. They show exactly
why selected-prime constraints cannot replace complete all-scale arithmetic.

7. CERTIFICATE SEARCH AND AUDIT SCOPE.
The source protocol precedes scoring. Its primitives are index divisibility,
floor, lcm/gcd features, nonnegative row masks, rational powers, diagonal
majorants and Gram sums. Seeds, response tables, fitting, rounding, balanced
profile sampling, native precision, selection and promotion budgets are
recorded. The learner receives numeric responses and feature columns, not
an expected winning formula. Known inverse and Gram recovery is calibration.
Model-supplied dictionaries and analytic methods remain explicit assumptions.

Each native LDL certificate is a proof of positivity at its declared finite
scale. Negative pivots are checked with exact rational negative directions
and reject that matrix candidate. Numerical failures of the enclosure are
missing, not counterexamples. The independent checker uses exact integer
Sylvester minors, independent direct divisor-row Gram sums, and prime
arithmetic; it imports no producer mathematics. Promotion and equal-slot
random-mask ablation are frozen before validation and replayed independently.
Fresh final matrices and generic inputs are protected from the learning worker.
Every full-defect bound charges N*D and the exact unit forcing cost Y*w(1).
No global critical PSD proof or RH conclusion follows from finite success.
