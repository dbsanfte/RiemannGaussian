the fixed-prime count proved from square-divisor inclusion-exclusion.
Let root_upper(x)=ceil(2^20*sqrt(x))/2^20, certified by integer squares.
baseline=sum_band abs(value)*(hi-lo).
leading_upper=baseline-objective_gain;
leading_lower=baseline-objective_upper (may be negative, retain it).
