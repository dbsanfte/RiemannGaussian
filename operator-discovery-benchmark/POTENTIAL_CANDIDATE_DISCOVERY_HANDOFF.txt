New implemented discovery loop
potential_candidate_language.py evaluates exact compositional arithmetic ASTs
on complete actual Mobius values. potential_candidate_discovery.py generates
and structurally mutates programs. run_potential_candidate_discovery.py freezes
the protocol, runs two generations, saves training constants before validation,
fixes the final batch before final responses, and conducts one independent audit.
check_potential_candidate_discovery.py uses independent AST semantics and actual
mu from trial factorization; producer mu uses complete proper-divisor inversion.
report_potential_candidate_discovery.py only summarizes already checked results.

There is no coefficient optimizer, gradient step or LP in this new search.
The supplied grammar/families and ranking rule remain design assumptions;
this is automatic structural selection within a declared language, not bias-free
mathematics. The exact identities/estimates are not nominated by expected-output
assertions in the research path. Ordinal IDs and complete payload equality replace
cryptographic fingerprints. No new hashes were created.

Complete target and proof obligation
M(t)=sum_{n<=t}mu(n), E(X)=X^-2 sum_{X<=t<2X}M(t)^2.
Every program specifies nonnegative rational weights and signed state ASTs U_j:
 V(X)=X^-2 sum_{X<=t<2X} sum_j w_j U_j(X,t)^2, W(X)=E(X)+V(X).
Evaluate the FULL W(2X)-W(X), with every child cutoff/mask/root recomputed.
W(1) is charged in selection. The original E survives every transformation.
State products, signed mixtures and moving prime sums are allowed. In particular,
square(sum_p state_p) retains its complete signed cross-prime terms.
The grammar supports exact rational-root syntax, including square/cube/sixth roots.

Each proof-queue entry explicitly requires: there exists integerq0>=1 such that
for every integerq>=q0 a finiteCq>=0 independent of dyadicX>=1 satisfies
 W(2X)-W(X)<=Cq*ceil(X^(1/q)).
Conditional telescoping bounds E through W>=E, W1 and the geometric sum; the
memo explains the subsequent ordinary-zeta implication. No universal signed
estimate, full universal source/compiler binding or RH theorem is admitted.

Next action
The next search should generate candidates through three concrete changes:
1. Recognize checked lag/mask/padding identities with explicit source and domain
   scope; leave unrecognized structure unknown. These annotations must not
   silently merge programs based only on equal finite responses.
2. Mutate factor placement within distinct summands before squaring, alongside
   other structural mutations. For example (A+mu(r)B)^2 retains 2mu(r)AB,
   while (mu(r)A)^2 only adds a squarefree mask. Keep the full mixed expression
   and its unresolved estimate; a surviving sign alone proves no cancellation.
3. Allocate selection slots by effective mechanism as well as family, retaining
   baseline/calibration and unknown exploration. Predeclare affordable tests
   around effective selector changes before new responses; mark other regimes
   unexposed. Recompute child selectors and retain every boundary term.
These are the reviewed next implementation requirements, not features already
added to the frozen pilot. Prioritize candidates with explicit complete signed
lemma obligations; preserve uncertainty and exploration outside recognized forms.
Do not replace them with more numerical solver tuning, claim that a moving
sixth-root expression was tested beyond its actual regimes, or promote passing
finite constants to a uniform bound. The full active goal remains unresolved.
