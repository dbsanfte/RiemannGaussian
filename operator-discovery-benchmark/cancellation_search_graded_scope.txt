No frozen source edits or score queries. This concerns the declared dictionary,
not all possible arithmetic operators or proof methods.

For c, the new gcd_pair relation is degree4 plus degree2; its product/difference
multipliers are quadratic. Hence these12 groups contain degree6 terms. No other
current group has degree6: norm_core tops at4, prime_transport at4, old product
squares at4, old gcd at4, and new signed-block squares at2. The quadratic target
has no degree6. Therefore the aggregated degree6 coefficient vector of the new
c gcd_pair groups must be EXACTLYzero. This is an unconditional formal matching
constraint, not an estimate about actual c values.

More specifically, for n>=2, the monomial x_n^4*x1^2 occurs only in the new
gcd_pair product multiplier at a=b=n: g=n,u=v=1, so the degree6 part is
 -x_n^2*x1^2*x_n^2. The difference multiplier vanishes at equal arguments.
No other pair has that monomial: to have its gcd-coordinate support and four
copies of n requires the equal pair; distinct coprime u,v cannot both supply n.
Consequently its effective product-gcd coefficient
 w_all,one+w_region,one + J_X(n)*(w_all,J+w_region,J)
must vanish for every n>=2, with region=head or tail according to n<=2X.
The dictionary uses overlapping all/head/tail groups, so this does not require
every named weight individually0; all=head+tail produces exact redundancies.
It does require the entire effective product-group combination to vanish on
its full anchor domain. Several distinctJ values in each region can separate
the constant andJ coefficients; at tiny scales some values may be absent.
Allscale shared weights must satisfy every such constraint at arbitrarily
largeX. This family therefore cannot contribute a genuinely nonzero product-
gcd effect after formal cancellation in the present c dictionary.

For difference-gcd groups the remaining degree6 block must also vanish, but
index collisions can couple several pairs. Do not assert all weights vanish
without exact analysis. A cheap generic compiler optimization is to build the
highest-degree coefficient matrix first, compute its exact rational nullspace,
and restrict the shared weights to that subspace. Then process degree4 and
finally degree2/constants. These formal constraints are independent of actual
response scores and can diagnose a representation gap before expensive fitting.
They cannot substitute for nonnegative square weights or complete coefficient
matching. For mu the gcd relation is quadratic, so these multipliers give
quartics that can interact with norm/product-square columns; the c degree6
argument does not transfer unchanged.
