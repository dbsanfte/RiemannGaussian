UNIFORM ARITHMETIC CERTIFICATE EXTENSIONS: NORMS, GCD, AND GROUPED TEMPLATES

Read-only analytical design after exact separation review. No frozen code,
adaptive algorithm, prior certificate or numerical score is changed. The independently verified adaptive probe completed7 of its allowed16
promotions and found finite certificates. Its promoted atomic relation columns
break the purely grouped-relation restriction; this is finite basis expansion,
not a grouped uniform theorem. This proposal does not alter that frozen audit.

1. Legitimate new norm relations and their limited information.
Actual mu satisfies x_n^2=s_n, s_n=1_(n squarefree). Actual c satisfies
x_n^2=h_n, h_n=4^omega(n), including s_1=h_1=1. Proof: complete prime local
mu values1,-1,0,... and c values1,2(-1)^e multiply over distinct primes.
The constants require a complete factorization or certified squarefree/prime-
count source through EVERY used index; their cost/horizon is explicit. They
are not generic free coefficients inferred from observed norms. A typed
norm relation could carry the factorization proof and source hash, then allow
polynomial multipliers; the polynomial compiler must expand each relation fully.

These norm constants expose coefficient size without explicitly listing signs,
but do not create cancellation. The squarefree counterfeit f_P has EXACTLY the
same mu norm at every integer and all coprime cycles, while a finite normalized
prime set still leaves cubic energy. For c, define epsilon_P completely
multiplicatively, epsilon_P(p)=-1 on P,+1 elsewhere, and
 f_P,c(n)=epsilon_P(n)*2^omega(n).
It has the actual c norm globally, coprime multiplicativity and actual local
c(p^e)=2(-1)^e on P. For P empty it is nonnegative and its prefix is at least
x, so critical energy control is impossible. This last simple case suffices to
show that the c norm axiom and multiplicative cycles alone are inadequate;
no unproved asymptotic for general P is needed here. The listed norm/cycle premises and a fixed finite normalized prime set remain
insufficient. A successful route needs uniformly justified arithmetic information
that excludes these positive-mean countermodels; this does not prove necessity
of every individual prime sign. Omitting a fixed finite prime set can preserve
critical bounds through harmless local factors, and other growing/global
arithmetic constraints may suffice.

2. Gcd compatibility with complete factors.
For arbitrary a,b, put g=gcd(a,b), u=a/g, v=b/g. For mu, the exact actual law is
 x_a*x_b=s_g*x_u*x_v*1_((g,uv)=1).
Its square follows from the norm constants but retains the same sign-bearing
primitive pair. Any proposed cancellation must keep the coprimality-zero cases.
For c, c is never zero and prime-support counting gives the exact rational law
 x_a*x_b=x_g^2*x_u*x_v / 2^omega(gcd(g,uv)).
Indeed c(gu)=c(g)c(u)/2^omega(gcd(g,u)), with parity signs matching because
Omega(gu)=Omega(g)+Omega(u); u,v are coprime, so the two shared-prime sets are
disjoint. This equation can be cleared of its positive integer denominator
before polynomial matching. It requires actual distinct-prime-count constants,
not the mu squarefree mask. Indices g,u,v<=max(a,b); a complete relation bank
must classify ALL pairs and not silently omit incompatible factors.

These laws can reduce pair coordinates and compress certificates. The known
coprime-squarefree density estimate already controls small primitive mu cores;
the complete large primitive signed residual is still unknown. Gcd rewrites
alone do not improve its uniform arithmetic estimate.

3. What the exact dual witness actually suggests.
The reviewed separation probe at mu X2 found y(L)<0 for each original declared
cone even though the actual bound is true (Delta6,D8). Its selected extension
was x1 times the prime-power relation x4=0, with nonzero exact dual evaluation.
That extension invalidated the PARTICULAR separator, but one-column resynthesis
still found no certificate. This points to a missing interaction between
arithmetic zero relations and the pair monomials, not evidence of a global
bound. A natural reusable relation family is aggregated prime-square-zero
constraints, multiplied by grouped kernel/prefix linear forms, while square
families are grouped on the SAME arithmetic blocks. The grouping must retain
all signs, endpoints, multiplicities and index collisions.

A candidate template could specify rational shell endpoints, valuation classes,
fixed coprime/gcd maps and polygon kernel coefficients, producing
 sum_alpha w_alpha sum_(indices in declared region)P_alpha,index(x)^2
 +sum_beta v_beta sum_(indices in declared region)Q_beta,index(x)*rho_beta,index(x).
Both square AND relation weights should be shared by template class, or governed
by an independently proved bounded-complexity formula. Otherwise free per-index
squares and free rowwise multipliers can encode the known finite sequence.
Complete coefficient matching must include every monomial that appears after
expansion, even those absent in the old dual support. Absent dual coordinates
are unknown; a new full dual or valid extension must be solved, never filled
with implicit zeros. A violated witness suggests an expansion but does not
certify primal feasibility; exact resynthesis remains mandatory.

4. Nomination versus uniform theorem.
Learned finite certificates can nominate reusable motifs by canonicalizing index
maps and grouping repeated coefficients. A protocol may automatically enumerate
fixed rational coefficient/degree/region complexity, rank exact witness violation
and description cost, then test additional declared scales. Novelty of indices,
finite compression or repeated motifs is a proposal, not a proof of transfer.
The proof obligation is a coefficientwise symbolic identity or an induction valid
at every X in a declared scale domain, with shared square weights nonnegative,
all arithmetic premises proved, and all norms, exceptional small scales, product
horizons and changing-cutoff boundaries charged. A claimed identity can be checked
by exact lattice-region partition, not by matching a few sampled expansions.

Cross-scale recurrence templates should retain ordinary original energy, or a
nonnegative potential with a proved recovery charge. The exact target is
 A_f(2X)-4A_f(X)<=Cq X^2 ceil(X^(1/q))
for every dyadicX and every integerq>=q0, finiteCq independentX. A recurrence
certificate must prove this signed step or an admitted stronger potential step;
it may not insert an axiom stating the same unknown estimate, a square-root
bound for primitive prefixes, or an RH-strength prime discrepancy estimate.
Critical additive correctors already have a proved recovery equivalence and do
not supply the missing original energy control. Their finite fitting is not the
next source of new information.

5. Concrete next objective and barriers.
Following the verified7-promotion adaptive audit, the useful controlled extension is:
add FACTOR-CERTIFIED norm and gcd relations; enumerate jointly grouped square/
relation templates using complete arithmetic regions; retain exact dual/primal
certificates, description costs and all failure/missing records. Compare against
the squarefree counterfeit with identical norms, so a template depending only on
norms and partial prime normalization is rejected as a route to critical control.
Sufficient actual arithmetic information must exclude the positive-mean
countermodels and be justified uniformly. Complete prime normalization and
complete inverse rows are available sufficient specifications, not proved
logically necessary ingredients of every successful proof. No per-index
coefficient table should be advertised as fresh arithmetic.
The unknown uniform step is precisely a bounded-cost symbolic certificate for
the complete dense signed kernel after those relations are applied. No such
certificate, new all-scale signed estimate, formal mathematical proof or RH
conclusion is asserted by this design.
