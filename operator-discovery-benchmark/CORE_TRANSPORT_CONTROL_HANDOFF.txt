Current result and limitation
-----------------------------
The detector now jointly searches prime/composite transports, complete
auxiliary energies, and their proposed uniform scale recurrences. It does
not merely score a transport whose bound was separately selected by an
analyst. The grammar and available proof rules are still supplied by us;
this is bounded construction/inequality synthesis, not unrestricted
autonomous proof invention.

The completed pilot generated90 transport programs.28 have both a paid

short-interval-prime import. For each it generated55 storage programs and
16 recurrence shapes:24,640 joint hypotheses. An independent compiler
checked all24,640 proof compositions before arithmetic responses were read.
9,240 shapes were excluded using original-core pole growth and paid
recovery. The other15,400 passed the finite discovery-validation tests.

16 candidates were selected by a frozen rule, one per auxiliary mechanism
and admitted window exponent, using6 distinct transports. All16 passed
the larger-scale interval tests. Two independent implementations agreed
on1,398,157 scalar comparisons, including every transformed coefficient,
full prefix/log bank, complete energy/work block, selected storage, and
recurrence verdict. The combined frozen suite passed23 tests.

No complete core bound over all scales was proved. The zero numerical
rejection count is an important limitation: the finite tests did not
discriminate the15,400 remaining uniform conjectures. They must not be
advertised as15,400 plausible RH mechanisms. The next search should target
identities or estimates for the explicit signed increment, not tune C or
extend an unchanged fit. Every selected uniform increment remains OPEN.

The arithmetic operator
------------------------
The complete original source is unchanged:
 f=beta*nu0-beta*nu1-Ru*nu2-beta*mu, beta9/2,0<Ru<5,
 nu0=k_(1/3)*(mu*mu), nu1=k_(1/3)*(mu*mu_(2/3)),
 nu2=k_(1/3)*mu_(2/3).
For actual prime p, f(p)=-Ru*p^(-1/3)+(beta+Ru)*p^(-2/3).

For each cofactor m2..16 and theta in1/2,7/12,2/3,3/4,5/6,1, partition
each dyadic[A,2A) into complete windows of length2^floor(theta*log2A).
Move negative prime mass to positive coefficients at m*q, qprime>m,
using sorted round-robin assignment. Each load is at most4m and at most
half the receiving coefficient. ANY failed/uncertain sign, count or
capacity check retains the ENTIRE original window. All other terms and
the complete unit remain. The new coefficient g is nonmultiplicative.

Every accepted window preserves ordinary mass. With complete prefixes
F,G and D=F-G, the current-window bound gives unconditionally
 E_D(X)<=25X^(2theta-5/3),
 E_F(X)<=2E_G(X)+50X^(2theta-5/3),
 E_G(X)<=2E_F(X)+50X^(2theta-5/3),
for every dyadicX>=1, including all failed/head windows. Here
E_V(X)=X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X)V(n)^2. Theta<=5/6 pays the correction cofinally.
The theorem does NOT bound E_G. Completed windows generally retain
nonzero log moments: B_G is the complete direct sum g(n)logn. The old
multiplicative Euler log generator is not inherited by g.

The complete cofactor asymptote is positive precisely when the number
of prime factors of m with valuation exactly1 is odd. This admits
m2,3,5,7,11,12,13, including repeated-valued cofactor12. Eventual prime
deletion additionally uses Guth--Maynard, New large value estimates for
Dirichlet polynomials, Corollary1.3:

Its short-interval theorem covers fixed theta>17/30 below.99. It is an
import for eventual acceptance, not a detector discovery or a premise of
the unconditional recovery payment. Theta1/2 remains outside that import;

Fresh semiprimes whose two factors exceed16 are untouched. The earlier

The generated bound search
----------------------------
The generator offers the baseline A=E_G and54 mixed storages

Full signed crosses are retained. The language accepts other canonical
rational exponents; the menu is finite for this declared run. The D
auxiliary is a paid-perturbation calibration, not a new missing-bound
mechanism. The null auxiliary is explicitly a baseline.

For every storage the generator conjectures

training drift ratio, with minimum1. It is never refitted. The basic
identity certifies E_G<=(1+a²/w)A. Conditional induction yields log
degree k forrho1/2 and k+1 forrho1. Paid recovery transfers that same
degree to ORIGINAL E_F. Its previously verified critical double pole
therefore excludes degree<2 even at theta5/6; no meromorphic double pole
of g is asserted. This is a checked composition of reusable proof rules.
