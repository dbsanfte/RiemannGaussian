1. Exact states, prime transfer, and all exits
--------------------------------------------

of these bins. Its occupied mask is EXACTLY M subset I. A u-bin contributes
r_q=1-q^(-1/3), a v-bin s_q=1-q^(-2/3). Products of the h bins form h and
of the k bins form k. Put beta=9/2 and
  W_X(h,k)=max(0,2X-max(X,h,k)), N=2X-1.
For every squarefree m let Psi_M,X(m) be the sum of the positive assignment
weights times W_X(h,k), over assignments with exactly occupied mask M.
The empty assignment is m=1,M=empty,h=k=1, with weight W_X(1,1)=X.
Bins may be empty in these states; the FULL target row requires all four.
The squarefree-product part of the original quadratic coefficient term is
  S_0(X)=beta^2*X^(-2)*sum_(m squarefree)mu(m)*Psi_I,X(m).         (1.1)

energy. The full a=p-d square, pole/main cross, all gcd and nonsquarefree
sectors, and the old D/Lv/smaller-pole corrections remain complementary
obligations. Their removal is not inferred from this occupancy calculation.

Write e_M for the 16-state column basis and A_i e_M=e_(M union {i}).
The A_i commute and A_i^2=A_i. For q not dividing squarefree m, appending
q to bin i multiplies that bin's leg by q and sends M to M union {i}.
Let E_i(q,m) be the vector of the same positive assignments, now in mask

Every lost prefix point, including outside-domain lifts, is retained.
Then exactly

  sum_sf mu(m)Psi_X(m)

2. Simultaneous spectrum and constant-weight recovery
-----------------------------------------------------
For J subset I define the left cumulative row and its dual right vector
  ell_J(e_M)=1_(M subset J),
  v_J=sum_(M superset J)(-1)^|M\J| e_M.
They satisfy ell_J(v_K)=delta_(J,K), and
  A_i v_J=1_(i in J)v_J, ell_J A_i=1_(i in J)ell_J.              (2.1)
Let a_J,b_J count u,v bins in J, and t=q^(-1/3). Then
  ell_J T_q=lambda_J(q)ell_J,
  lambda_J=1-a_J*r_q-b_J*s_q
           =1-a_J-b_J+a_J*t+b_J*t^2.           (2.2)
The joint eigenspaces with counts (a,b) have dimension binom(2,a)binom(2,b).
All common constant-weight invariant subspaces split among these spaces:
three distinct prime parameters recover the commuting u/v union sums by
polynomial interpolation, and their joint spectral projectors give the
split. Arbitrary subspaces inside a degenerate count space are invariant.

The empty mode has eigenvalue one. One-bin modes have t or t^2. Two-bin
modes have -1+2t, -1+t+t^2 or -1+2t^2, lying strictly between -1 and one
for actual q>=2. Three-bin and four-bin eigenvalues tend to -2 and -3.
They cannot be uniformly contracted by a fixed positive coercive quadratic
storage on the complete state space; eigenvalues cannot be altered by
changing that norm. A noncoercive storage may ignore them but then loses
target recovery.

The full-occupancy row is exactly
  e_I^T=sum_(J subset I)(-1)^(4-|J|)ell_J.        (2.3)
Every simultaneous mode occurs. In particular v_I=e_I is annihilated by
EVERY proper cumulative row but e_I^T v_I=1. Thus no combination of the
proper stable rows recovers the full row on the 16-state space, even with
prime-dependent scalar coefficients. This is a linear recovery obstruction,
not a claim that all source-dependent nonlinear recovery is impossible.

There is also an actual-source obstruction to a COEFFICIENTWISE constant-
weight identity using only <=2-bin rows. Fix a prime count L, let its distinct actual primes
tend to infinity, and choose X>=m so W_X is constant X on every divisor

3. Which stable rows really have unconditional envelopes
--------------------------------------------------------
Normalize a cumulative row by X^(-2)*sum_sf mu(m)ell_J Psi_X(m), without
beta^2. Its empty-row absolute value is 1/X. A one-bin row has the other
leg one, m<=N, and assignment weight<=1, giving absolute bound two. If
two bins are on the SAME leg, its assignment weight is at most 2^omega(m)
<=d2(m), again m<=N, so its absolute bound is
  2*(1+log(2X)).                               (3.1)
These are real paid modes, independently of their scalar eigenvalues.
They are not the unsolved constant mode of the earlier prime-exchange graph.

The four opposite-leg two-bin rows are different. A positive pointwise
domination through J={hv,kv} exists: since r_q<=s_q,
  Psi_I,X(m)<=2^omega(m)*ell_J Psi_X(m), squarefree m.             (3.2)
For any delta>0, 2^omega(m)<=C_delta*m^delta, e.g. bound small primes
below 2^(1/delta) by a finite constant and use 2<=q^delta on all others.
Thus the coefficient cost on m<=N^2 is subpower. But (3.2) controls TOTAL
VARIATION, not a comparison of the signed sums in (1.1). Its actual total
variation is macroscopic, not a paid companion.

Here is an elementary proof for the vv row. Let
  f(h,k)=1_(hk squarefree)*prod_(q divides hk)s_q.
Its bivariate local series is 1+s_q*z+s_q*w. Convolving separately with
mu in each coordinate gives local series

The sum of absolute nonconstant coefficients evaluated at z=w=1/q is
O(q^(-1-v)+q^(-2)), v=2/3, so the bivariate divisor inverse has absolutely
convergent harmonic mass. Exact floor counting and dominated convergence give
  sum_(h,k<=n)f(h,k)=C_vv*n^2+o(n^2),
  C_vv=prod_q(1-1/q)^2*(1+2*s_q/q)>0.           (3.3)
Every local factor is positive and its difference from one is summable;
no PNT or RH is used. Consequently
  X^(-2)*sum_sf |mu(m)|*ell_J Psi_X(m)
      =(7/3)*C_vv*X+o(X).                      (3.4)
The signed row can still cancel, but its absolute recovery bound cannot

6. Exact finite Euler/Perron compilation of the squarefree prefix projection
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix X>=2,N=2X-1, P_N={q prime:q<=N}, and Mellin variables z1,z2. Define
  T_q(z1,z2;gamma)=I
      -r_q*q^(-gamma-z1)*A_hu-s_q*q^(-gamma-z1)*A_hv
      -r_q*q^(-gamma-z2)*A_ku-s_q*q^(-gamma-z2)*A_kv,
  Z_N(z1,z2;gamma)=e_I^T*[prod_(q in P_N)T_q(z1,z2;gamma)]*e_empty.
The product is FINITE. Expanding it assigns each prime either absent or
to one bin. Its sign is mu(m), each prime occurs at most once, its product
weight is m^(-gamma), and its leg weights are h^(-z1)k^(-z2). The full row
selects exactly occupied mask I. Thus it compiles the COMPLETE squarefree
product restriction, including coprimality and prime-absent projections;
no independence approximation or growing-cutoff local filtering is used.
Any valid prefix pair h,k<=N has all its primes in P_N. Terms outside
the prefix are retained in the finite polynomial and removed by the exact
Perron indicators below, rather than treated as arbitrary coefficient zeros.

For c>0 and integer h>=1, use n+1/2 endpoints:
  1_(h<=n)=lim_(T->infinity)(1/(2pi i))
                *integral_(c-iT)^(c+iT)(n+1/2)^z*h^(-z)*dz/z.  (6.1)
The ratio (n+1/2)/h is never one, so there is no half-weight endpoint.
This is the elementary Perron step identity, obtained by closing the
contour left or right; only its pole at zero contributes. The finite Euler
expansion permits the ordinary limits term by term. With
  D_X(z1,z2)=sum_(X<=n<2X)(n+1/2)^(z1+z2)/(z1*z2),
the exact weighted squarefree projection is
  S_gamma(N^2)=beta^2*X^(-2)/(2pi i)^2
             *integral_(Re z1=c) integral_(Re z2=c)
                        D_X(z1,z2)*Z_N(z1,z2;gamma) dz2 dz1.   (6.2)
These full-contour integrals mean the ordinary separate truncated Perron
limits (or their simultaneous limit in this finite expansion), NOT an
assertion of absolute convergence uniformly in N. They equal the finite
signed sum. No estimate of the full high-frequency integral is assumed.

Now set gamma=1/6, c=1/log N, and take the bounded rectangle
  |Im z1|,|Im z2|<=c/4.
All individual A_i modes (2.1) remain diagonal. Their factor is
  Lambda_J=1-q^(-gamma-c)
        *[R_J(q)*exp(-i Im(z1)log q)+S_J(q)*exp(-i Im(z2)log q)].
For every q<=N, (4.2) and (5.2) give |Lambda_J|<=1. Also
  Z_N=sum_(J subset I)(-1)^(4-|J|)*prod_q Lambda_J,
so |Z_N|<=16 on the ENTIRE rectangle. This includes every occupancy state
and the arithmetic absent/present choices of the whole finite product.

Its integral is absolutely bounded. Indeed
  |D_X|<=X*(2X)^(2c)/c^2,
the rectangle area is c^2/4, and the Mellin factor is 1/(4pi^2). Therefore
  |S_gamma,low(X)|<=beta^2*(2X)^(2c)/(pi^2*X)
                   <=beta^2*exp(4)/(pi^2*X).                  (6.3)
For X>=2, N>=3 and 2X<=N^2, giving (2X)^(2/log N)<=exp(4). Every
endpoint/unit convention has been retained. X=1 is a finite head, not a
singular c=1/log1 construction. A general admitted delta rectangle yields

This is a proved cofinal bound for an ACTUAL arithmetic prefix component.

is an exact way to expose the
uniformity missing in (4.3); it also adds its entire contour and projection
recovery. A stable endpoint scalar cannot pay that third contour automatically.
