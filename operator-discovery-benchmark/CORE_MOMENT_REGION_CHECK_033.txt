2. Exact whole function, finite head and centering

For integer U>=1 let A(n)=mu(n)1_(n<=U), c=A_hat(1),
 E=delta+One*(c delta-A), D=One_[1,U], u=1/3, theta=2/3.
The actual current component is
 f=A^2*E^3-c^3 D,
 F_U(s)=zeta(s+u)[A_hat(s)^2 E_hat(s)^3-c^3 D_hat(s)],
 E_hat(s)=1+zeta(s)(c-A_hat(s)).
All coefficient products are Dirichlet convolutions. E_hat has a removable
pole at1 because c=A_hat(1) EXACTLY. It is not enough to say the integer unit
of c delta-A vanishes: that unit is c-1 and generally does not vanish.

On any fixed strip sigma<=Re s<=2 with 0<sigma<theta, the whole F_U has
only the possible simple pole at theta, with residue
 M_f=A_hat(theta)^2 E_hat(theta)^3-c^3 D_hat(theta).
The finite head and its unit are not dropped. No reciprocal zeta factor or
zero-free premise occurs. Its pole contribution in the Perron integral is
 M_f x^theta/theta, so the coefficient compensation is
 (M_f/theta)J_theta, J_theta(n)=n^theta-(n-1)^theta, including its unit.

Ordinary E_hat(theta) is justified by bounded prefixes of E. Ordinary
coefficient convergence for E^3 at theta is NOT justified by multiplying
that value. The018/019 ordinary-moment gate must not be silently changed to
proved: the new compiler needs an explicitly ANALYTIC-residue-centered rule.
The current source already defines this analytic moment in032. A theorem
about that exact source is allowed; a theorem about a differently centered
ordinary coefficient sum is not inferred.

where C_sigma is fixed independently of U,t. The lower-power term and
finite head are rounded up using U>=1; their source coefficients remain
in the exact function before this positive upper bound is applied.

For 1/3<sigma<7/19 the two effective lines after reflection are

4. Why analytic centering is sufficient for the global physical norm

Use the Gaussian Perron regulator exp(e s^2), e>0, beginning at Re s=2
where the original coefficient series is absolutely controlled. Fixed-U
polynomial growth and Gaussian decay send horizontal edges to zero. The
full removable pole at1 remains removed; crossing theta yields exactly
 P_e(x)-(M_f/theta)exp(e theta^2)x^theta
  =(1/(2pi i))integral_(sigma) F_U(s)exp(e s^2)x^s ds/s.
No ordinary cubic moment is used in this contour step.

Mellin Plancherel and dominated convergence on the proved vertical L2 line
give the global weighted norm of the COMPLETE staircase-centered function
 C(x)=P_(K*f)(x)-(M_f/theta)x^theta:
 integral_0^infinity |C(x)|^2 x^(-2sigma-1) dx
 <= C_sigma U^(10(1-sigma)).
The entire region0<x<1 is included: P=0 there and C is its full negative
continuous main. It is integrable because sigma<theta. Uniformity in U
comes from the vertical bound, not a uniform horizontal-edge limit.

Integer cells n<=x<n+1 retain the correction
 C_n=C(x)+(M_f/theta)(x^theta-n^theta).
The safe8/32 constants of018 apply below half; their correction is globally
summable since2+2sigma-2theta>0. One can bound |M_f|^2 by the explicit
finite/ordinary single-E estimates, or directly from the included small-x
part of the global norm. In either case it has no greater U exponent.
Therefore the SAME U-dependent bound holds for the global discrete norm
 sum_(n>=1)|C_n|^2 n^(-2sigma-1).

Restriction to [X,2X) yields normalized block energy
 O(U^(10(1-sigma))*X^(2sigma-1)).
Restriction to1..N yields energy_N=N^-2 sum_(n<=N)|C_n|^2 with the same
power2sigma-1. These are whole-prefix norms, not restarted block prefixes.
With U<=N^(1/24), the combined energy exponent is exactly
 e=2sigma-1+(10/24)(1-sigma)=(19sigma-7)/12.
The strict interval1/3<sigma<7/19 simultaneously pays height and physical
energy. Endpoints with an epsilon/log loss cannot be declared subcritical.
The cutoff conversion N=4X-1 costs4^((10/24)(1-sigma)) in a fixed symbolic
constant. Parent and child must use the same U and residue M_f.
