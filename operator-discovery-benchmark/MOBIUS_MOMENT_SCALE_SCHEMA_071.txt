UNIFORM CONDITIONAL PROOF-PROGRAM SEARCH071: IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

This stage searches symbolic cost/rank inequalities, not measured arithmetic
scores. The reciprocal-moment and interval-disjointness rules are supplied
mathematics. Exact rational primal/dual certificates prove optimality only
inside the declared affine language. Prefix premises and the joined signed
floor remainder stay unpaid. No RH or full head bound follows.

Generic system schema:
 {variables:[names], rows:[{name,a:[canonical rationals],b:rational}],
  objective:[canonical rationals]}
Each row means a dot x >= b, all variables otherwise free.
Result is {status:'optimal',primal:[rationals],dual:[rationals],
           objective:rational,solver:metadata}, or
 {status:'infeasible',dual:[rationals],solver:metadata}, or
 {status:'unresolved',reason:string,solver:metadata}.
For an optimum verify Ax>=b, y>=0, A^T y=c and c.x=b.y exactly.
For infeasibility verify y>=0, A^T y=0 and b.y=1 exactly.
Solver metadata is observational; it never licenses proof acceptance.

Program schema:
 schema='mobius-moment-scale-program-v1', sigma, rho, fixed_theta (null or
 canonical rational), primary={system,result}, secondary=null or
 {system,result}, short={system,result}, derivation=null or derived record,
 conditional_prefix_bounds_paid=false, uniform_head_bound_proved=false,
 RH_proved=false. 0<sigma<1, 0<rho<1, 0<fixed_theta<1 if present.
All rationals canonical, at most4096 bits; explicit power work caps apply.

Primary variables theta,gamma_u,gamma_v,kappa_u,kappa_v, in that order.
Rows in order (names below are literal):
 theta_lower: theta>=0; theta_upper: -theta>=-1;
 u_domain: gamma_u-theta>=0;
 v_domain: gamma_v+theta>=1;
 u_rank: -gamma_u>=-rho; v_rank: -gamma_v>=-rho;
 u_exposure: kappa_u-theta+gamma_u>=0;
 u_moment: kappa_u-(sigma-1)*theta>=0;
 v_exposure: kappa_v+theta+gamma_v>=1;
 v_moment: kappa_v+(sigma-1)*theta>=sigma-1;
 u_nonpositive: -kappa_u>=0; v_nonpositive: -kappa_v>=0.
Optional fixed_theta_lower/upper are theta>=fixed_theta and
 -theta>=-fixed_theta, appended. Objective kappa_u+kappa_v.
No optimum exponent formula or nominated balance is supplied to search.

Secondary variables append exposure to the primary variables. Extend all
primary rows by zero. Append optimum_lower/upper fixing the primary
objective to its exact optimum; exposure_u: exposure-gamma_u>=0;
exposure_v: exposure-gamma_v>=0; exposure_lower: exposure>=0;
exposure_upper: -exposure>=-rho. Minimize exposure.

Short variables split,delta. Rows split_lower: split>=0;
split_upper: -split>=-1;
short_prefix: delta-(1-sigma)*split>=sigma;
short_tail: delta+split>=1; delta_upper: -delta>=-1.
Minimize delta. This instantiates the two supplied interval cost laws
O(C T^(sigma+(1-sigma)*split)) and O(T^(1-split)); it does not
pretend that this absolute short bound controls the joined remainder.

If primary and secondary and short are exact optimal, derive from the
secondary primal theta,gamma_u,gamma_v,kappa_u,kappa_v,exposure;
alpha=1+kappa_u+kappa_v; reciprocal_A=(2-sigma)/(1-sigma);
head_C_polynomial=[8/alpha,16*reciprocal_A/alpha,
 8*reciprocal_A^2/alpha]; short_exponent=short primal delta;
short_split=short primal split; short_C_exponent=short_exponent;
short_per_branch_constant=1+4/(1-sigma);
sufficient_base_log2=ceil(1/(1-exposure)).
The head polynomial multiplies D^(alpha-1)*M1, where M1=sum|a_T|T.
The short constant multiplies C^short_exponent*M_short_exponent.
M_delta=sum|a_T|T^delta, with no reversed interpolation inequality.
Base condition is R>=2^sufficient_base_log2; finite exact rank may hold
earlier. Universal bases, prefix premise, joint remainder and induction
closure are explicit open dependencies, never paid by a sampled check.

Geometry certificate schema='mobius-moment-scale-source-v1', N,y,max_j,
parameters, programs (the complete program list), endpoints, and the three
false claim flags above. Same registered source as068/069/070.
Endpoint: ratio,denominator,steps [[T,a_scaled],...] ascending nonzero,
M1_scaled, rows. g(d)=-1[d>y]*(den Wp(d)-ratio_num Wq(d)),
Wp(d)=floor(4N/pd)-3floor(2N/pd)+2floor(N/pd), D=floor(4N/p).
Steps a_T=g(T)-g(T+1). Row: j,R=floor(D/j),direct_scaled,
prefix_steps [[A,h_scaled],...] ascending nonzero, b_scaled=sum h*A,
V_scaled=sum|h|, plans. h from a_T at floor(T/j), minus a_T at
floor(T/(j+1)); omit index0. direct_scaled is the coalesced six atoms
at floor(vN/p),floor(vN/q), v=4,2,1, weights1,-3,2, scaled by
den and -ratio_num respectively, evaluated at row index j.
Plan for every program with nonnull derivation: program_index,u,v,T_u,T_v,
strict_rank,universal_base_gate,short_zero_support. Here u=floor(R^theta),
v=floor(R^(1-theta)), T_u=ceil(R^gamma_u),T_v=ceil(R^gamma_v).
strict_rank means max(T_u,T_v)<R, including failure at unit/base rows;
universal_base_gate means R>=3 and R^(1-exposure)>=2 by integer powers;
short_zero_support means (j+1)*max(u,v)<=y (sufficient, not necessary).
The complete rectangle satisfies (u+1)(v+1)>R; never drop ab>R terms.

Independent checker API: Meter(limit=...,seconds=...), verify_program(cert,
meter=None), verify_source(cert,meter=None). No producer or optimizer imports.
verify_source independently regenerates geometry and returns verified,N,y,
max_j,endpoints [{ratio,rows:[{j,R,actual_coefficient,evaluations:[
 {u,v,H_u,H_v,L_u,L_v,E,J,separable,direct,actual_coefficient}]}]}].
All evaluation numbers are canonical unscaled rationals. One evaluation
per distinct (u,v), ordered by first appearance in plans. Only evaluator
queries actual mu. H_u=sum mu(a)/a; L_u=sum mu(a)*kappa_j(a);
E=sum_(a<=u,b<=v)mu(a)mu(b)*(K_j(ab)-b_j/(ab));
J=L_u+L_v-E; separable=-b_j*H_u*H_v;
actual=direct+separable+J. Independently compare to direct+sum mu*kappa.
Store all shared/repeated prime factors. No coprime or squarefree-product
restriction is allowed. Return no uniform proof claim based on finite data.
