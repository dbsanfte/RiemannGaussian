INDEPENDENT PRIME/COFACTOR CLOSURE AND BOUND-ADMISSION SCOPE — stage035

Disposition

Actual prime/cofactor states address a real mismatch exposed by034: digit
sections closed for recursive benchmarks but did not close for the complete
arithmetic source. A detector may generate scalar prime-power sections,
infer parameterized relations, and submit them to an exact Euler-factor
verifier. This is a meaningful new arithmetic identity capability.

It does not convert coefficient closure into prefix cancellation. An honest
implementation needs separate admission boundaries for exact source laws,
prefix recovery, local operator estimates, and a complete uniform bound.
No source-dependent successful state list, relation, metric, exponent or
winner is nominated in this memo. Source Euler primitives are definitions;
the detector must produce the useful construction and proof certificate.

OCCUPANCY_TRANSFER_HANDOFF.txt, OCCUPANCY_TRANSFER_REVIEW_001.txt;

This memo is the sole new file for this audit. No tests, source scoring,

1. Exact original source, unit and distinct meanings of multiplication

The source is the COMPLETE positive-integer coefficient sequence
 f=(9/2)K*(mu*mu)-(9/2)K*(mu*mu_(2/3))
                       -R_u*K*mu_(2/3)-(9/2)mu,
 K(n)=n^(-1/3), mu_(2/3)(n)=mu(n)n^(-2/3).
Every star above is DIRICHLET convolution. Pointwise multiplying a channel
by K(n), or taking a pointwise cube, changes the source.

Each of its four primitive channels is multiplicative. Their signed sum
is not generally multiplicative. A cofactor proof must retain all four
channel contributions and the exact inherited constant R_u. If observations
use x(n)=f(n+1), the actual source index is one greater: the cofactor unit
is f(1)=-(9/2)-R_u. It is not zero and must not be discarded.

Older exchange/occupancy reviews use a decomposed coefficient a with its
same-prime contribution removed. Statements a(1)=a(p^e)=0 and their fresh-
prime transfer multipliers do NOT become laws for the current full f.
The corresponding signed centers, removed component, exits and recovery
would have to be restored explicitly before such an alternative is admitted.

The original source, g=f-e, the inherited paid components and opposite
analytic drifts, all144 channel/transport bindings and their constants/log
budgets remain authoritative. A direct f bound is an alternative route,
not a silently changed residual Z033 or a free transfer to every old norm.

2. Neutral exact source compiler; no supplied successful state basis

Let p be an actual prime and q=p^(-1/6). The verifier can independently
compile the source AST into local formal power series in z, with q and
R_u treated symbolically. The neutral atom definitions are
 mu: 1-z;
 mu_(2/3): 1-q^4 z;
 K: 1/(1-q^2 z);
 delta: 1.
Dirichlet convolution multiplies local series; rational scalar addition
and the inherited signed R_u term remain separate source operations.
This compiler belongs to proof checking. It need not expose a successful
scalar-state decomposition to the discovery routine.

For p not dividing m, a multiplicative atom/channel satisfies its exact
Euler cofactor decomposition. Thus any detector-generated scalar expression
f(p^e m) can be compiled to a row acting on complete p-free cofactor data.
An identity whose FULL row difference is identically zero is sufficient
for every allowed m and every actual prime. It is stronger than equality
only on the actual cofactor values; failure of this strong row test means
not proved in this verifier, not automatically an arithmetic counterexample.

All-prime identity admission must be a polynomial/rational identity, or a
proved recurrence for the entire valuation series. Checking finitely many
primes, finitely many e, or a long numerical training range is insufficient.
Generating-function clearing of denominators must preserve the formal
series' unit and all low-valuation coefficients. A rational local factor
with denominator constant coefficient1 defines its complete formal series;
the checker must not truncate its nonzero valuation tail as if mu itself
were the whole channel.

If additional relations between actual cofactor rows are needed, provide
their own complete source proof. They cannot be assumed from sample rank.
An exact generic-row rejection or coefficient mismatch is a formal witness.
Only a certified admissible prime/cofactor/valuation with unequal actual
values is an arithmetic counterexample. R_u remains the exact source
constant; a fitted rational approximation cannot supply either conclusion.

3. Parameter and valuation domains

Every law states p prime, p>=2, m>=1 and gcd(p,m)=1. If a source index is
already divisible by p, its ACTUAL valuation must be folded into e.
Fresh-prime, valuation-one and arbitrary-valuation laws are different types.
When another prime is introduced, retain distinctness and every coprime
guard that the local law requires. Cofactors may be squareful and may share
other primes; this cannot be replaced by an undocumented squarefree model.

q is positive with q^6=1/p and q<=2^(-1/6). The rational outer interval
0<q<=9/10 safely contains all actual primes, since (9/10)^6>1/2.
The endpoint q=0 is only a limiting parameter, not an actual prime. It is
useful for uniform polynomial certification, but cannot make q^(-a) bounded.

Every normalization/division needs a licensed nonzero denominator on the
entire domain. Source formal-series denominators are distinct from numerical
denominators used in a norm estimate. For the latter, check positivity or
nonvanishing explicitly and pay any deterioration as p or the cutoff grows.
Do not divide by R_u or a candidate state value without an inherited proof
that it cannot vanish. Avoid replacing the exact parameter relation by an
arbitrary polynomial interpolation convention.

The search may propose rational/polynomial laws from observations, but each
coefficient and each inferred zero must be admitted exactly. Interval overlap
is an observation check, not equality. Approximate least squares, rational
denominator caps and finite failures establish only discovery limits.

4. Prefix recovery is a separate complete identity

For a fixed prime, decompose the complete prefix into every valuation with
the cofactor boundary floor(N/p^e), including e=0 and the m=1 unit. The
p-free prefix state is part of the identity, not a proved smaller remainder.
Its smooth/rough restriction and every floor remain explicit.

To sum across primes, give an exact allocation of every integer coefficient:
a canonical prime choice, or a complete multiplicity correction and any
unallocated complement. A coefficient with several allowable labels must
not be counted repeatedly. Restricting to valuation-one labels does not
cover powerful numbers. Multiple prime valuations in convolution channels
can survive even when the underlying mu at that index is zero.

For energy, expand the entire prefix square or licensed equivalent norm.
Keep equal-prime and distinct-prime pairs, both orientations, all higher
valuations, all cofactor overlaps, all center crosses and the deterministic
center square. A local cofactor identity does not preserve prefix membership.
The transformed index has its own indicator; differences create actual
moving faces and exits, including terms outside the old orientation.

For a largest-prime allocation, the cofactor's largest prime must be smaller
than the label, not merely coprime. For a free-label allocation the changed
support/multiplicity must be carried. Neither convention gives independent
prime/cofactor samples or an unconditional cancellation estimate.

Retain EVERY partial integer cutoff required by the target. An endpoint
orbit, a fixed prime-set Euler product, a weighted signed sum or an aligned
prime-power chain does not pay its unweighted or arbitrary-prefix target.
Undoing variable weights requires the exact signed Abel identity and all
partial cutoffs, with its complete amplification and head costs.

5. Viable local norm interface, with a precise remaining input

One sound supplied norm rule is a fixed-prime dilation operator in weighted
logarithmic L2. Let H(x) be the complete p-free vector prefix, zero below1,
and let T_e(p) be the admitted valuation matrices in a discovered basis.
The exact full vector prefix is
 F(x)=sum_(e>=0) T_e(p) H(x/p^e).
For fixed sigma>0 and fixed Hermitian Q>=0, a complete circle certificate
 T(p,p^(-sigma)*xi)^* Q T(p,p^(-sigma)*xi)<=kappa(p) Q,
 EVERY |xi|=1,
admits the associated operator bound in the measure x^(-2sigma) dx/x.
All coefficients and tails of T are included. A zero-phase scalar test is
not this circle inequality.

For a finite T0, first extend H*1_(x<=T0) by zero above T0. Its causal
dilation convolution agrees with the original F on x<=T0. Global Fourier/
Plancherel gives the bound for this full convolution, and restricting the
output to x<=T0 only decreases its positive norm. This justifies finite
prefix use without incorrectly asserting the truncated input identity at
x>T0. The ENTIRE p-free input norm remains a proof dependency.

For discrete whole-prefix energy through N, take T0=N+1 so the last integer
cell is retained. Since the prefix is constant on [n,n+1),
 sum_(1<=n<=N)|F(n)|^2
 <=(N+1)^(2sigma+1)*integral_1^(N+1)|F(x)|^2 x^(-2sigma)dx/x.
The original N^-2 normalization and all subsequent Hardy/Abel channels
then have their explicit powers and constants. A continuous norm on the
wrong horizon cannot silently become a discrete endpoint estimate.

Target recovery is separate: prove row domination r^*r<=C Q on the proved
state domain. A singular Q can be sound when it still dominates the target;
it cannot ignore a target-visible mode for free. Positive definiteness alone
does not bound the unproved input norm or its base value.

This is a component operator theorem, not a full-source payment. It can be
useful output even when the p-free input, its uniformity or multi-prime
recovery remains OPEN. Report the missing dependency precisely.

6. Important norm obstructions and changing-state gates

The current primitive channels have divisor-growth envelopes, not the
row-contractive recursive-source bound used by034. New scalar sections do
not inherit |x|<=one fixed constant merely because their laws are exact.
A coefficient envelope and every weight/divisor cost must be proved first.

Review028/029's endpoint debt also remains: an identity yielding the desired
energy as its own potential endpoint is an exact restatement. The endpoint,
unit height integral and residual all require independent paid providers.
Cheap bounded harmonic coordinates do not dominate an unrelated critical
Dirichlet-polynomial state without an actual recovery/coercivity proof.

A per-prime zero-phase contraction need not hold for all logarithmic
frequencies. The rational local operators possess target-visible modes
whose opposing phase has amplification rather than contraction. The backend
is separately certifying that local operator obstruction. It excludes the
specified full formal-state contraction shape, not the actual arithmetic
correlation or every coupled signed argument.

If the metric Q(p), basis B(p), or observable quotient R(p) changes with
the prime, retain the exact OLD/NEW metric and coordinate conversion.
Individual inequalities in separately normalized coordinates do not chain
without paying their conversion. A changing potential coefficient also
has a drift; it is not fixed storage merely because each isolated prime
admits a nice formula.

A fixed common observable quotient can be generated from the target row
under all coefficient matrices of the parameterized family. The verifier
may accept it by exact simultaneous invariance. This controls algebraic
visibility; it still does not prove prefix cancellation. The034 single
matrix phase argument must not be copied onto noncommuting or changing
operators without its full product proof.

Removing a growing prime set pays EVERY local factor and every remaining
input norm. Fixed-prime boundedness is not a uniform growing-set constant.
Products of inverse factors, branch counts, path/congestion weights and
cutoff faces stay in the final exponent/log cost. A PNT estimate, if used,
is an explicit theorem premise, not implied by the Euler identities.

7. Proposed exact checker schema and status separation

Input source_binding:
 immutable parent reference; exact original signed AST; four source scalars;
 K and shifted-mu definitions; positive-integer/unit convention; R_u source
 provenance; original target norm/quantifiers; all inherited recovery routes.

Candidate state_program:
 detector-generated state ASTs; source index maps; parameter ring and domain;
 fresh/valuation/cofactor guards; complete valuation initialization and tails;
 observations and rejection trace stored separately from proof evidence.

Coefficient-law certificate:
 declared lhs/rhs ASTs and all domains; compiled source rows or full rational
 local series; exact cleared numerator identities; denominator/series gates;
 low-valuation/unit recovery; topological premises with no open dependencies.

Prefix-recovery certificate:
 exact every-index allocation; valuation sums and all floors; all prime-set
 restrictions/multiplicities; every transported mask/exit and complement;
 explicit complete signed center/error identity; original target recovery.

Uniform-bound certificate:
 typed observable (coefficient, prefix, positive norm, signed work or energy);
 exact metric/target domination; circle/interval polynomial witnesses;
 endpoint/state/source providers; all parameter changes and product costs;
 complete uniform quantifiers, constants, exponents/logs and proof DAG.

Possible APIs, independent of producer search:
 verify_laws(source_binding,state_program,law_certificate,meter);
 verify_recovery(parent,laws,prefix_certificate,meter);
 verify_bound(parent,recovery,proof_DAG,meter);
 verify_feedback(parent,result,output,artifact,meter).
The root may combine these APIs, but their statuses must stay distinct.

Law success: all_prime_coefficient_closure_proved=true, and no automatic
prefix/energy/core payment. A valid local norm transfer has a separately
typed component_operator_bound_proved flag and explicit unpaid input.
Uniform target success requires a complete original-source proof DAG with
no open premise and no circular use of the target, its equivalent endpoint
or its requested correlation. Failed finite search is OPEN/UNSUPPORTED,
not a proof of impossibility. Formal norm/linear-language exclusions are
explicitly scoped to their domains.

8. Meaningful adversarial tests for the later implementation

Source mutations: dropped signed channel, fitted R_u, wrong K multiplication,
erased unit, prime-power tail truncated, old decomposed-a law relabeled as f.
Domain mutations: composite label, already-divisible cofactor, repeated prime
miscalled fresh, omitted lower valuation, unlicensed parameter division.
Identity controls: finite-prime interpolation aliases and long valuation
aliases; false generic-row claim without an admissible actual counterexample.
Recovery mutations: duplicate prime labels, missing powerful complement,
missing equal-label pair or reverse orientation, omitted moving face,
endpoint-only partial recovery, weight inversion without signed Abel cost.
Norm mutations: zero-phase in place of the complete circle, singular metric
losing target recovery, ignored basis change, unpaid p-free input, dropped
unit integral or final discrete cell, growing prime-set constant declared fixed.
Dependency mutations: target-as-endpoint, cyclic graph, paid node depending
on an open norm, and synthetic closure relabeled as actual core/RH proof.

These tests should use tiny synthetic arithmetic definitions and symbolic
laws. Actual observations begin only in the frozen runner; withheld numerical
functionals are still observations, not universal proof premises.

Conclusion

The next detector can genuinely propose and certify all-prime coefficient
laws, with their successful states and relations produced by execution.
It should then attempt explicitly licensed prefix/norm arguments on those
states and save complete unsuccessful costs and missing signed controls.
Learning local arithmetic laws is a worthwhile mechanism extension, but
calling that closure a cancellation bound would repeat the exact gap that
the034 arithmetic attempt exposed. The complete signed core remains open
until its global correlations and recovery are independently paid.
