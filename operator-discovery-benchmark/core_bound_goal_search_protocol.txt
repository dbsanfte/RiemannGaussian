For EVERY eligible m generate the entire binomial identity
 E^m=sum_(j=0..m) binom(m,j) One^j*T^j.
Both j0 and j1 remain. The finite harmonic moment of T vanishes. Product
jets therefore have order at least j, so every moment of T^j at1 involving
(log d)^r, 0<=r<j, vanishes. Expand every arbitrary main monomial
x*(log x)^r under the finite convolution and verify this cancellation.
No special coefficients for a divisor asymptotic polynomial are supplied.

Define D_j(x)=sum_(n<=x)One^j(n), zero for0<x<1. For an ARBITRARY fixed
polynomial P of degree<=j-1 define the COMPLETE remainder
Delta_j(x)=D_j(x)-x*P(log x), for ALL real x>0. The exact moment identity
replaces a finite T^j convolution with this remainder. Every d in the
finite support remains, including d>n and hence x=n/d<1. The j1 branch
uses P=1 and the proved |floor x-x|<=1, giving prefix<=3U. The j0 branch
is delta. For j>=2, a uniform remainder estimate and its polynomial
coefficients are OPEN. They are not silently supplied by divisor theory.

The conditional schema |Delta_j(x)|<=C_j*x^kappa_j*(1+|log x|)^ell_j
requires one finite constant, polynomial and log degree independent of x,U,
EVERY x>0, 0<=kappa_j<theta. Complete finite weighted mass gives
sum_all_d |T^j(d)|/d^kappa <=(2+1/(1-kappa))^j U^(j*(1-kappa)).
Thus branch b=j*(1-kappa), rho=kappa. Costs in U and all logarithms remain.
For these branches the mass premise is also paid: positive divisor
convolution has mass<=t*(1+log t)^(j-1), and the finite harmonic mass
of T is <=2+H(U)<=3H(U). Thus mass_(One^j*T^j)(t) is at most
3^j*H(U)^j*t*H(t)^(j-1). The j0 delta branch has mass<=t.
The compiler explicitly checks the known j0 and j1 HEAD branches through
both kernel routes before calling a conditional auxiliary route feasible.
The conditions are sufficient per-branch conditions only; their failure
is NOT a counterexample to the joint signed arithmetic estimate.
