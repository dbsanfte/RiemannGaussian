1. General signed vectors and the target zero response

A program is exactly {id,constant,coefficients,steps}, defining, with zero
extension outside(0,1],
 e(x)=C-sum c_n f_n(x)-sum d_q I_q(x),
 f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)), I_q=1_(x<=1/q).
On cell k=floor(1/x), f_n=(k mod n)/n and I_q=1_(k>=q).
Indices are sorted distinct integers2..4096; combined support<=2048.
Coefficients and C are canonical exact rational strings, including arbitrary
rational denominators within the stated bit/work caps. Zero coefficients are
omitted. A signed auxiliary difference can have C=0. It is a diagnostic
vector, not automatically an RH-bearing target. Target admission requires
C=1 and steps=[]; a caller cannot replace this source/role guard by a flag.

The initial absolutely integrated Mellin identity for Re(s)>1 is
 M f_n(s)=(n^-1-n^-s)zeta(s)/s.
Boundedness extends the left side holomorphically to Re(s)>0, giving analytic
continuation of that formula; the singularity at1 is removable. An indicator
has Mellin response q^-s/s. At an actual zeta zero rho to the right of1/2,
the general vector response is(C-sum d_q q^-rho)/rho, whereas the admitted
target response is1/rho. Hilbert Cauchy-Schwarz therefore gives
 ||e||^2 >=(2Re(rho)-1)/|rho|^2
for every finite target. A sequence of its whole norms tending to0 excludes
all right-of-half zeros. No convergence or RH conclusion is obtained merely
from one finite global norm, an auxiliary difference, or finite observations.

2. Exact periodic covariance, including signed pairs and constant component

After every step threshold has activated, e(k) is a periodic function of k.
The period can be the enormous lcm of the floor indices; it need not be
enumerated. Its exact period mean is
 mu=C-sum d_q-sum c_n(n-1)/(2n).
For g=gcd(m,n), covariance of(k mod m)/m and(k mod n)/n is
 (g^2-1)/(12mn).
One elementary proof conditions on k mod g: the two remaining coordinates
are independent and uniform over a complete joint period. Their shared
coordinate has variance(g^2-1)/12. Consequently
 V=mu^2+sum_(m,n)c_m c_n(gcd(m,n)^2-1)/(12mn)
is the FULL period mean-square, not just its variance. Signed cross terms
can cancel; deleting them, taking absolute coefficients before the Gram,
or omitting mu^2 gives a different quantity and cannot price this certificate.

Choose an exact lattice D making C*D, c_n*D/n and d_q*D integers. Write
 w_n=c_n*D/n and T=2D(C-sum d_q)-sum w_n(n-1).
The Jordan totient identity gives a positive form while retaining all signs:
 gcd(m,n)^2-1=sum_(d|m,n,d>1)J2(d),
 S=sum_(d>1)J2(d)*(sum_(d|n)w_n)^2,
 V=(3T^2+S)/(12D^2), variance=S/(12D^2).
J2(p^a)=(p^2-1)p^(2a-2). Each SIGNED inner sum is collected before its
square. The certificate records every d dividing at least one occupied
index, including exact-zero inner sums. The checker independently factors
occupied indices and enumerates their divisors; the producer uses a dense
multiple-sum/Jordan sieve. Exact small-period and ordered-gcd calibrations
check the mathematical identity independently of both implementations.

3. Imported large sieve and a rigorous unobserved weighted tail

Primary provider: H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, 'The large sieve',
Mathematika20(1973), Theorem1(1.4) and its matrix duality Lemma1:
 https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/large_sieve.pdf
The original bound is length L plus inverse frequency spacing. Its '-1'
sharpening is not used or attributed to this primary theorem.

Each periodic f_n has a finite discrete Fourier expansion with rational
frequencies of reduced denominator<=n. Equal frequencies are merged,
including the zero frequency. If K is the largest floor index(default1),
distinct frequencies are separated on the circle by at least1/K^2. Parseval
over the complete period gives sum|Fourier coefficients|^2=V. The dual large
sieve thus bounds any consecutive L integer cells by
 sum e(k)^2 <=(L+K^2)V.
This is a rule for the periodic state AFTER every indicator has activated.
It is not asserted for an earlier interval crossing a step threshold.

Let a=H+1, require H>=all step q, and w(k)=1/[k(k+1)]. Set
 A_j=sum_(k=a..a+j-1)e(k)^2 <=(j+K^2)V.
Discrete partial summation, with the terminal product tending to0, yields
 sum_(k>=a)e(k)^2 w(k)
 <=V sum_(j>=1)(j+K^2)[w(a+j-1)-w(a+j)]
 =V[1/a+K^2/(a(a+1))].
The equality uses sum j*Delta w=1/a and sum Delta w=w(a).
The independent pointwise alternative is M^2/a, where
 M=|C-sum d_q|+sum|c_n|(n-1)/n.
Both cover every remaining cell to infinity. The selected upper bound is
their exact rational minimum. No observed tail sign, empirical period,
fitted constant or finite L stopping point is used to infer the infinite tail.

The exact head is sum_(k=1..H)e(k)^2/[k(k+1)], with integer recurrence
 R(0)=C*D,
 R(k)-R(k-1)=-sum w_n+sum_(n|k)c_n*D-sum_(q=k)d_q*D.
The checker scans a heap of the next wrap events and one-time steps, then
accumulates at least256-bit Arb. The producer uses a dense event array and
192-bit Arb. Head limits are0..8388608. Input/intermediate bit caps and
explicit meter charges are enforced before expensive replay. Exact dyadic
head/whole endpoints must contain the independent head interval and the
FULL selected tail range[0,upper]. Decimal display balls are finite,
overlapping diagnostics only; exact endpoints drive all admission/ranking.
Cache/work counters are descriptive and cannot license a proof step.

4. Dilation and signed recovery

D_q f_n=f_(qn)-f_q/n and D_q I_s=I_(qs), exactly at jumps as well.
D_q e has C=0 and a constant indicator C*I_q, so residual dilation must
retain that unit term. The API compile_dilation lifts the subtracted floor/
step combination; compile_residual_dilation additionally inserts-C in its
step coefficient at q. These two meanings are explicitly separate.
For target parent/child and rational b, compile
 h=1-g_child-b I_q+b D_q g_parent,
 e_child=h+b D_q e_parent.
The whole norm identity retains the signed cross:
 ||e_child||^2=||h||^2+(b^2/q)||e_parent||^2+2b<h,D_q e_parent>.
The norm operator price is1/q. b=0 is an endpoint restatement. An auxiliary
vector norm is useful only with its exact role/recovery identity; smallness
alone cannot erase the main source, unit, forcing or required signed cross.

5. Global convergence leaves and imported geometric-rate exclusion

For t=b^2/q<1, an OPEN leaf||h_N||^2<=A/L(N)^eta at EVERY N=N0*q^j,
integer N0>=1, fixed recipe and parameters, L(N)=N.bit_length(),
0<eta<=1,A>=0 gives a legitimate CONDITIONAL convergence implication.
If t>0 choose Young y=(1-t)/(2t), rho=(1+t)/2<1,B=(1+1/y)A.
For b=0 use rho=0,B=A. Since L(N0*q^i)>=1+i,
 E_j<=rho^j E0+B sum_(i=0..j-1)rho^(j-1-i)/(1+i)^eta ->0.
No finite records discharge this all-scale leaf. All convergence/RH proof
flags remain false; b=0 is labeled endpoint-equivalent.

Burnol's unconditional Theorem1.3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0103058 ,
gives a positive lower limit for log(N)*E_N for pure targets supported<=N.
The inclusion follows from f_n={1/(nx)}-{1/x}/n, whose fractional-part
parameters are1/n and1. Along N=N0*q^j this excludes uniform geometric norm
decay. Thus a strict relative forcing contraction is a negative calibration,
not a plausible OPEN convergence provider in this linear-support family.
This does not exclude slower logarithmic convergence or arbitrary growing
support. The zero-forcing A=0 limit is also geometrically excluded here.

A separate exact finite-dictionary gate remains: for constant-one forcing,
take a common multiple M of all floor indices beyond all indicator indices.
The three cells1,M,M+1 satisfy h(1)+h(M)-h(M+1)=1 and hence
 ||h||^2>=1/[2(1+(M+1)^2)].
Constant-zero diagnostic vectors do not have this gap. A growing dictionary
has a moving M and a potentially vanishing gap; it is not refuted by this
fixed-stencil rule. This gate cannot be repurposed as an RH impossibility.
