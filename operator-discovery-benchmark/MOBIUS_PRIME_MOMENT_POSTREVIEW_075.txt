2. Merge all equal children before choosing a correlation grammar
---------------------------------------------------------------
For fixed parent u and its exact prime blocks let

 m_ij(u)=#{p in block i: u/(j+1)<p<=u/j}.
 w_j(u)=sum_i a_i m_ij(u),
 h_j(u)=sum_d a_d 1[j=u//d],
 v_j(u)=w_j(u)+h_j(u).

Every count is literal, with open lower and closed upper endpoints.
Include j=0 if desired: G(0)=0; in these prime blocks it is absent.
Direct collisions and prime/direct collisions add their coefficients,
rather than creating independent copies of G(j). All nonzero children
are strictly smaller than u. The exact identity is

 L(u)=sum_j v_j(u)G(j).

Its complete square is

 sum_j v_j^2 G(j)^2 + 2 sum_{j<k}v_jv_k G(j)G(k).

Even the diagonal now contains prime-prime multiplicity cross terms,
cross-block same-child products and any direct collision. Equivalently
put P=sum w_jG(j), D=sum h_jG(j); then L^2=P^2+2PD+D^2.
Dropping2PD or off-diagonal child products changes the target energy.
The exact paid original diagonal moment is

 E(u)=sum_i beta_i Q_i +sum_d beta_d G(u//d)^2
     =sum_j t_j(u)G(j)^2,
 t_j=sum_i beta_i m_ij+sum_d beta_d 1[j=u//d]>=0.

This t has linear multiplicity, whereas the target diagonal has v^2.
Grouping does not allow replacing m by1 or by m^2 in E.

3. A universal support/coherence certificate and its full price
-------------------------------------------------------------
If t_j=0 implies v_j=0, weighted Cauchy proves

 L^2<=K(u) E(u),  K(u)=sum_{j:t_j>0}v_j^2/t_j.

Thus K<=1 certifies the original correlation universally, without any
arithmetic assumptions about G. This merged-child criterion can exploit
coefficient cancellation before squaring and is stronger than treating
duplicate children as separate vectors. If v_j!=0 where t_j=0, no
universal inequality L^2<=K E can hold for arbitrary child vectors:
put a vector value1 at that unsupported j and0 elsewhere. This does
NOT refute an actual arithmetic inequality for G; it identifies the
additional arithmetic relationship such a claim must prove.

For K(u)<=Kstar uniformly, the075 induction bound E<=B rho u^q
gives L^2<=B Kstar rho u^q. The full contraction price is Kstar*rho,
not rho alone. Require Kstar*rho<=r^2<1 and the same residual budget.
Alternatively a potentially sharper purely structural price is

 T_q(u)=sum_{j>=1}|v_j(u)|(j/u)^(q/2).

The child invariant implies |L|<=sqrt(B)u^(q/2)T_q. Uniform T_q<=r
would close the transfer with the residual budget, but coherent prime
weights need not have a bounded price. Neither condition follows from
smaller rank, from the finite observed ratios, or from arbitrary beta
inflation; any inflation must repay contraction simultaneously.

4. Exact discrete Abel reduction with the boundary paid
------------------------------------------------------
Discard j=0, whose G value is zero. Let1<=J<u encompass every
remaining supported child (the zero operator can be handled separately),
and put
V_j=sum_{h=1}^j v_h. Direct subtraction proves

 L=V_J G(J)-sum_{j=1}^{J-1}V_j [G(j+1)-G(j)].

Indeed G(h)'s coefficient is V_h-V_(h-1)=v_h, including both ends.
The endpoint V_J=sum v_h is essential. For002 the prime coefficients
are positive, so sum w_h is the total151 primes at the displayed row;
it is not a zero-mass pairing. For136 all positive prime coefficients
likewise preclude a zero-mass weight identity. Mixed139 has positive
and negative coefficients, but no centering constraint forces total
quotient mass zero. Centering of smooth coefficients in074 does not
imply sum_i a_i*#block_i=0. Removing the anchor would be a new error.

The exact increment has a completely specified arithmetic stencil:

 Delta G(j)=1[8 divides j+1]mu((j+1)/8)
           -1[10 divides j+1]mu((j+1)/10)
           -1[4 divides j+1]mu((j+1)/4)
           +1[5 divides j+1]mu((j+1)/5).

Terms at nondisible arguments are absent. This follows from the jump
of M(floor(j/h)), includes mu(1), and costs no asymptotic boundary
approximation. Its absolute value is at most4, but exploiting signed
increments in the V-weighted sum requires further control; taking
absolute values can lose exactly the hoped-for cancellation.

5. Payable difference-energy or pairing leaves
---------------------------------------------
Choose positive weights eta0 and eta_j for retained Abel terms. Set

 F_eta=eta0 G(J)^2+sum_{j=1}^{J-1}eta_j(Delta G(j))^2,
 K_eta=V_J^2/eta0+sum_{j=1}^{J-1}V_j^2/eta_j.

Weighted Cauchy universally proves L^2<=K_eta F_eta. Zero weights
are allowed only when the corresponding Abel coefficient is zero.
One independently payable arithmetic leaf is
K_eta(u)F_eta(u)<=B r^2 u^q under the smaller-child invariant,
proved without assuming the parent target. A more modular version
requires uniform K_eta<=Kstar and F_eta<=B Dstar u^q, with
Kstar*Dstar<=r^2<1. F_eta's expansion retains ALL products among
the four signed mu increments; replacing it by individual squares
requires a separately charged Cauchy factor. Its anchor is likewise
paid explicitly. These are possible new search leaves, not proved here.

An unconditional fallback is F_eta<=eta0 B J^q+16 sum eta_j
under the child invariant; if B>0 its transfer cost is
K_eta[eta0(J/u)^q+(16/B)sum eta_j/u^q]. This safe price can be poor.
Writing down a difference norm does not establish a small uniform cost.

More generally any exact finite signed flow representation

 v=sum_e f_e(e_(j_e)-e_(k_e))+b e_anchor,
 b=sum_j v_j,

gives L=sum_e f_e[G(j_e)-G(k_e)]+bG(anchor). Such a representation
always exists using an anchor within1..J and edges to every other
supported child. Pairing coefficients can be chosen rationally and
verified by exact conservation at each child. With positive edge
weights eta_e and anchor weight eta0, Cauchy pays

 L^2<=[b^2/eta0+sum_e f_e^2/eta_e]
       [eta0 G(anchor)^2+sum_e eta_e(G(j_e)-G(k_e))^2].

Long-edge differences telescope into the exact Delta G stencil above,
with every intervening term retained. Each difference square is
G(j)^2+G(k)^2-2G(j)G(k). Generic induction gives only
(G(j)-G(k))^2<=B(j^(q/2)+k^(q/2))^2; any better sign/nearby-child
correlation is a distinct arithmetic lemma. All endpoints must remain
strictly below parent u, and a pure pairing is permissible only if b=0.
