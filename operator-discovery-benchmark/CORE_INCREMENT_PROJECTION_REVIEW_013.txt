----------------------------------------------------

4A. Source-specific analytic recovery at r2/3: a separate conditional rule
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
An unpaid ENERGY detail is not necessarily an analytically false operator.
At r2/3, (4.3) makes M_h holomorphic for Re(s)>1/3, despite(3.1)'s
positive-power energy envelope. Suppose the genuinely OPEN full coarse
leaf is established: S(X)=O_epsilon(X^epsilon) for EVERY epsilon>0.
Then (1.2) and shell Cauchy make M_PG holomorphic for Re(s)>1/2. For a
cofinally paid transport, M_D is also holomorphic in that open half-plane,
including theta5/6. Consequently M_F and its complete source Dirichlet
series D_f(s)=s M_F(s) are holomorphic there. Initial equality holds in
Re(s)>1; this is ordinary analytic continuation, not a claimed coefficient
tail convergence at s2/3.

For THIS complete source AST,
 D_f(s)=zeta(s+1/3)*[beta/zeta(s)^2
             -beta/(zeta(s)zeta(s+2/3))-R_u/zeta(s+2/3)]
             -beta/zeta(s).                              (4A.1)
The real s2/3 pole is exactly canceled by the already verified R_u.
Holomorphy in Re(s)>1/2 forces RH by a self-contained rightward-extremal
zero argument. If any offcritical zeros existed, let B>1/2 be the supremum
of their real parts, bounded by1. Choose one rho with
 Re(rho)>max(1/2,B-1/3). Then zeta(rho+1/3) is nonzero because its real
part exceeds B; it is finite since a nontrivial zero is not real, so this
argument is not the zeta pole1. Also zeta(rho+2/3) is finite and nonzero
by the absolutely convergent Euler product in Re(s)>1. If rho has
multiplicity m>=1, the first term in(4A.1) has an uncanceled pole of
order2m with nonzero leading coefficient. All other terms have order
at most m or are regular. This contradicts the established holomorphy.
Thus no zeros lie right of1/2; the functional equation supplies the
corresponding left-side exclusion. No rightmost zero need be attained.

This proves a CONDITIONAL qualitative RH bridge for the open coarse leaf
at r2/3 (and any r<3/4), with its complete source binding. It does not
prove that leaf, prove RH unconditionally or quantitatively pay V's energy.
The numerical energy backend's r<=1/2 admission remains unchanged.

For an automated analytic-lane certificate the sufficient hypotheses are:
the complete actual f AST agrees with its coefficient Dirichlet series in
Re(s)>1; its unique highest inverse power of zeta(s) has nonzero certified
scalar coefficient; all its other zeta arguments are fixed STRICTLY positive
real shifts; the projected G storage has the coercivity(1.2); 2r-1<1/2;

choose a hypothetical zero within that smallest shift of the real-part

multiplicity. Exact merging/cancellation of source rows and nonzero leading
coefficient verification are required before granting that uniqueness flag.
A rational noninteger zeta power or several tied leading rows require a
different local argument and are not covered by this integer-order rule.
The lane is ADMISSIBLE CONDITIONALLY, not an established coarse bound or
unconditional RH claim. The original full target remains open.

Returning from that conditional RH conclusion to original moments needs
an explicit growth theorem, not mere holomorphy. A suitable external
input is the classical RH bound |log zeta(s)|=o(log|t|), uniformly on each
fixed strip Re(s)>=1/2+eta; it yields both zeta(s) and1/zeta(s)=O_eta,epsilon
((1+|t|)^epsilon), away from the bounded-height pole neighborhood. A primary
modern proof is Aleksander Simonic, "Estimates for L-functions in the
Critical Strip Under GRH with Effective Applications" (2023), Theorem1,
especially its log-modulus bound; the introduction also states Littlewood's
fixed-strip result. Source:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00009-023-02289-2
The absolute bound on complex log controls its real part with BOTH signs,
so the reciprocal bound is not silently inferred from an upper bound for
|zeta| alone. Above the1-line the Euler product gives the needed bounds.

For clarity the remaining standard Perron step can be made explicit.
Under that RH growth input, (4A.1) is O_eta,epsilon(T^epsilon) on fixed

This outlines the explicit growth/recovery bridge; its classical external

Higher-order projection is a future component extension, not an implemented

8. Rigorous insufficiency and the remaining proof obligation
-----------------------------------------------------------
No cofinal mean theorem follows from the divisor envelope, absence of
large prime coefficients and orthogonal detail payment alone. For a
countermodel to THOSE RULES, keep the same fixed negative unit and take
g(n)=1 on even composites n>=4, zero at every prime and every other n.
Put D=0. This is not actual f and does not satisfy its complete Euler AST;
it is used only to audit a purported source-independent implication from
the above envelope/prime-removal/local-jump rules. It obeys their coefficient
majorants and unit convention, has G(n)=n/2+O(1), and hence S_G(X) is
of order X for every r<=1. Its detail is paid at r<=1/2 while its coarse
mean recurrence cannot have a fixed-polylog forcing with rho<=1.

Thus a proof using only local jump squares, prime singleton deletion or
positive-semidefinite projection would be missing genuine signed source
information. The actual four-mode identities can supply that information
only through a proved joined correlation estimate, with complete complements.
An absolute prefix estimate yields merely the valid weak reference
 S(X)<=A(X)<=16K^2[(1+|a|)^2+w]X(1+log2X)^2,
not a critical cofinal bound. The proper mean kernels expose exactly where
this positive power must be replaced by signed arithmetic.

The next open lemma can be stated precisely as an all-scale one-sided
bound for S(2X)-rho S(X) with declared finite head, log forcing and SAME
global program, using(5.2)-(5.4) or the joint parent covariance(6.4).
The detector may enumerate that signed ramp/cofactor or parent-pair grammar,
compile the paid detail/refinement/recovery rules, and reject impossible
coarse log degrees via(4.5). Its generated uniform coarse leaf remains
OPEN until actually proved. Finite passes do not establish it.
