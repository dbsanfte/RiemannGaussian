1. Exact least-prime decomposition of the signed remainder
--------------------------------------------------------
Let c=f_P be the complete folded coefficient function supported on
1..E, with the distinct folded prime setP and Q=productP kept as a
separate excluded domain. Its residual is

 R=sum_{n<=E,gcd(n,Q)=1}c(n)mu(n).

For n>1 with nonzero mu, let p be its LEAST prime factor and n=pm.
Then p not in P, all prime factors of m are STRICTLY greater than p,
gcd(m,Q)=1, and mu(pm)=-mu(m). Conversely these conditions give a
unique squarefree n when m is squarefree. Squareful m contributes zero,
and the strict inequality excludes p^2 and all other repeated-p rows.
Define P^-(1)=infinity. The EXACT identity is

 R=c(1)-sum_{p<=E, p prime,p not in P} A_p,
 A_p=sum_{m<=E/p,P^-(m)>p,gcd(m,Q)=1}c(pm)mu(m).

Units m=1 and c(1) are retained separately. Upper support is literal;
when c(pm) is an interval stencil its endpoints divide by p with exact
floors. No independent copies of mu occur. Bounding sum|A_p| would
return to an unsigned price, so this identity alone proves no power gain.

2. Executable new grammar: rough factor-count polynomials
-------------------------------------------------------
Introduce source-bound weighted states

with omega(1)=0 and P^-(1)=infinity. z is a FORMAL variable. At z=-1

partition between thresholds0<=y1<=y2 gives

          +z sum_{y1<p<=y2,p prime,p not in P}F(c(p dot),p,z).

At z=-1, T(c,y1)-T(c,y2)=-sum_{y1<p<=y2}T(c(p dot),p).
Every prime endpoint is open at y1, closed at y2; child threshold is
STRICT P^->p, and Q exclusions persist. This is a new exact transport
grammar with parity information, rather than another coefficient-l1 fit.
It can be independently tested using synthetic finite squarefree prime
factor models, interval/token functions and complete least-prime partitions.
Arbitrary unconstrained mu vectors do not satisfy its arithmetic premise.

All parent count tokens in c remain bound to THEIR ORIGINAL parent as
c is dilated. Recomputing the count normalization at a child would change
the kernel. The new states are NOT automatically ordinary G at child

------------------------------------------------------------------
For a chosen threshold y, transport from0 gives
