Stage035: machine-generated prime/cofactor states and bound feedback

Purpose
The034 detector proved bounds for recursive benchmark sources but its fixed
base arithmetic attempts stopped after coefficients1..71. This stage supplies
the original arithmetic expression and a generic prime-section language. It
generates companion states, full valuation laws, and power-bound certificates.
It then uses a proved obstruction as an input to a joined-storage search.
Neither recursive benchmark success nor coefficient closure establishes RH.

Frozen experiment
The runner saves the new source files, test receipts and this protocol before
calling discover on the complete source or reading production archive entries.
Earlier target knowledge and source reuse are explicitly disclosed. This is
not a blinded or held-out mathematical discovery exercise. No hashes are used.
The accepted033 full obligation is not replaced or marked paid.
Observer development uses tiny direct arithmetic fixtures through31; their
three invocations and exact work are recorded in its development receipt.

Source
f=(9/2)K*(mu*mu)-(9/2)K*(mu*mu_(2/3))-R_u*K*mu_(2/3)-(9/2)mu,
K(n)=n^-1/3, R_u=(9/2)[zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2-1/zeta(2/3)].
Dirichlet convolution and positive integer indexing retain the unit.
The independent checker establishes 1<R_u<5 with Arb at256bits. Proof

The route targets complete f directly; it does not isolate residual Z033.

Discovery language and limits
q=p^-1/6; source atoms mu_shift(k) have local1-q^k t and power_shift(k)
have local1/(1-q^k t). Source weights are affine in R_u. Starting from f,
apply coefficient jets [q^d] f(p^e m), with p prime and p coprime to m.
Generate only new rationally independent rows. Valuations1,2,3; degrees0..24;
12states; ten million symbolic proxy operations;300seconds. State laws are
admitted by complete rational functions, never extrapolated from finite jets.
All prime valuations including squares, cubes and units remain represented.
The grammar is human designed; discovered rows/exponents are not nominated.

Bound language
For each generated state derive its prime polynomial. Select its first exact
nonzero power and interval bounds on the affine coefficients. A sum over all
integers gives a uniform upper bound for the raw rough prefix at one shared
cutoff y_X=ceil(sqrt(2X)). An elementary binomial prime count plus an eventual
constant-sign certificate gives a cofinal lower bound when the exponent is
less than1. The complete block has unit and primes only. An unresolved earlier
coefficient cannot be bypassed by selecting a later degree. Sign thresholds,
unit costs and head conditions are kept by the verifier's theorem scope.
If the resulting energy exponent is positive, the detector rejects separate
subpower payment of this raw state. This says nothing adverse about the full
joined source, which can have a cancelling composite contribution.

Joined-storage search
Feed that positive lower exponent into enumeration of Q on(A_X,B_X), with
F=A_X+B_X, diagonal dictionary[1/2,1,2,3,4], cross dictionary
[-4,-3,-2,-1,-1/2,0,1/2,1,2,3,4]. All275candidates are considered.
Require Q-J positive semidefinite, J_ij=1, to dominate the target square.
A positive Schur complement gives an actual arithmetic lower bound from A
and refutes a subpower endpoint price. A singular coercive survivor must be
Q=kJ and has the original energy as its endpoint: an unresolved restatement.
This restricted instantaneous grammar is not the proposed richer scale/cutoff
potential search. Its verified failure drives the next input to that search.
No fitted numerical matrix can be promoted to a global inequality.

Finite arithmetic observations
Reuse the independently verified12-vector archive through262143; decode the
required entries through131071. Scales256,1024,4096,16384,65536 are fixed here.
Compute every integer prefix in each whole block and the joined identity
E_F=E_A+E_B+2C_AB. Keep the unit, all prime powers in B, and the signed cross.
Check generated prime polynomials and sampled cofactor valuation laws against
the archived intervals. Observations cover the actual original source and are
finite evidence, not new data, unseen data, independence, or an all-scale bound.
