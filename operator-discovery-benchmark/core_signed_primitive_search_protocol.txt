Source and generated primitive grammar
Canonicalize the complete joined polynomial x*W1+x^2*W2 with v=1 and
E=1+z(c-A), retaining every conjugate and exact signed coefficient. Use
x^3=x. The state increments are A:+x*b, Abar:+x*bbar, c:+x*a, D:+b,
Dbar:+bbar; z,zbar are fixed. D changes even when mu=0. State at cutoff1
is A=Abar=c=D=Dbar=1; invariant zeta factors are not set to1.

Infer potential monomials by reversing one declared increment inside each
source monomial, admitting the result only when all remaining factors are
state or invariant symbols. Close under conjugation. The basis is m+mbar
for a nonreal monomial, m for a real monomial, with rational coefficients.
No target-energy potential or its coefficients are inserted by hand. This
finite source-inferred dictionary is a declared limitation of the search.

Compute every full DeltaF under the SAME state transition and ternary
relation, including all higher increment terms. Never treat current a,b
as fixed potential coefficients. An exact result is W=DeltaF+R. At fixed
height and measure, its signed cumulative sum telescopes to
F(state_V)-F(state_1)+sumR. The complete common window H=2(2B)^(45/2) is
held fixed for every q; varying original masks are recovered separately.

Uniform proof and rational feasibility search
Use the independently verified parent height library without recomputing
its passed proof searches. Every used zeta power retains its own cost, with
outer zeta weight and |s|^-2 once, compact heights, epsilon and logs. The
source reciprocal bound |c_N|<=2 follows for actual mu from
N*c_N=1+sum_(d<=N)mu(d)*{N/d}; it is not a sampled or prime-truncated bound.
Other state majorants are |A|,|D|<=U^(1-sigma)/(1-sigma), 0<sigma<1.

For each potential basis element, calculate the endpoint cost of F-F(unit),
including the moment cost of the unit polynomial on the growing window.
For each residual monomial, calculate its complete q-sum and height cost.
Sum_q q^r costs B^max(r+1,0), with a log at r=-1. Endpoints have no q-sum
factor. Costs retain exact rational exponents and logarithmic degrees.

Enumerate every distinct endpoint/residual cost threshold, the requested
threshold and zero. At a threshold admit only basis columns whose endpoint
cost fits, and require all residual monomials with larger costs to vanish.
Solve these coefficient equations over rationals. A feasible certificate
contains its potential coefficients; an infeasible certificate is a complete
left-null combination y with y*M=0 and y*source nonzero. The independent
checker reconstructs every column and verifies these witnesses directly.
Select the smallest feasible cost and price its full endpoint and remainder.

Separately attempt to cancel every row containing x using all columns. This
shows exact telescoping possibilities even when their endpoints are too large.
After discovery compare the endpoint to the original energy; mark an exact
restatement explicitly. Neither this comparison nor an unknown energy bound
participates in the coefficient solve or an available payment provider.

Infeasibility concerns ONLY this finite dictionary with these endpoint and
residual absolute majorants. It does not lower-bound actual signed work,
refute its requested bound, exclude other primitives, or establish RH.
Negative residual signs are retained but this initial backend uses absolute
residual costs; a future one-sided sign rule must be independently proved.
The prime-filtered bounded-state extension in Review029 is not implemented
in this study. It remains a concrete possible grammar extension.
