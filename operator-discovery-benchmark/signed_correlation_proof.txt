EXPLICIT SIGNED CORRELATIONS: THE REMAINING ARITHMETIC TASK

Let phi_n(u)=max(0,min(1,u-n+1)). Then for u>=0,
 M_tilde(u)=sum_n mu(n) phi_n(u).
The sum is finite at every u, and interpolation agrees with the cumulative
sum. For a rational polynomial P(z)=sum c_j z^j, c_0=1, and integer b>=2,
 R(t)=P(S_b)M_tilde(t)=sum_n mu(n) psi_n(t),
 psi_n(t)=sum_j c_j phi_n(t/b^j).
For integer X>=1 define the explicit Gram kernel
 K_X(n,m)=integral_X^(2X) psi_n(t) psi_m(t)dt.
Each integral is of piecewise rational affine functions, with breakpoints
b^j(n-1),b^j n,b^j(m-1),b^j m. It is exactly computable in rationals.
Only n,m<=2X contribute. Expanding the square gives the UNIVERSAL identity
 A_2(X)=D(X)+C_off(X),
 D(X)=sum_n mu(n)^2 K_X(n,n)>=0,
 C_off(X)=sum_(n!=m)mu(n)mu(m) K_X(n,m).
This is a signed arithmetic sum, not an absolute-value estimate.

Put S=sum_j |c_j|. Since |mu(n)|<=1 and |psi_n(t)|<=S,
 D(X)<=2*S^2*X^2
at EVERY integer scale, with no prime cutoff. Thus the unknown premise
 C_off(X)<=K*X^2 for EVERY dyadic X>=1, for some finite K>=0,
would prove A_2(X)<=(2*S^2+K)*X^2. With the independently certified
all-height ordinary-zeta multiplier bridge, the direct Holder-Mellin theorem
in signed_mellin_proof.txt proves RH. The constant need not equal the fitted
constant from a finite search. Any valid finite uniform K suffices.
The diagonal estimate is proved. The required signed off-diagonal estimate
is unproved. Expanding a square does not supply it.

For comparison, discarding all signs gives a generic O(X^3) energy bound.
Positive coefficients indeed have energy of order X^3 whenever P(1/b)!=0.
The required improvement therefore concerns real arithmetic signs across
all scales. A smaller residual, a finite kernel identity or a diagonal bound
does not establish that improvement.

EXACT FAST DIAGONAL FORMULA

For n>=2, the ramps for consecutive scales d=b^j and e=b^(j+1) do not
overlap: d*n<=e*(n-1). Thus for j<k,
 phi_n(t/b^j)*phi_n(t/b^k)=phi_n(t/b^k).
Writing s_k=sum_(j<k)c_j gives
 psi_n(t)^2=sum_k c_k^2 phi_n(t/b^k)^2
             +2*sum_k c_k*s_k*phi_n(t/b^k), n>=2.
The exceptional n=1 term is integrated directly, including boundary scales.
No disjoint-ramp claim is made for arbitrary unrelated integer dilations.

Let Q(N)=sum_(n<=N)mu(n)^2 and W(N)=sum_(n<=N)n*mu(n)^2.
For integer t=d*N+r, 0<=r<d, and h=1 or 2,
 sum_n mu(n)^2 integral_0^t phi_n(u/d)^h du
 = t*Q(N)-d*W(N)+d*Q(N)/(h+1)
   +mu(N+1)^2*r^(h+1)/[(h+1)*d^h].
The last term is zero when r=0 and needs no out-of-prefix coefficient.
Subtract primitives at X and 2X, then apply the preceding identity and
the direct n=1 correction. This computes D(X) exactly from complete arithmetic
prefixes. Independent small-domain tests integrate each psi_n squared on its
breakpoints without using Q, W or the disjoint-ramp formula.

It does not select new operators or claim fresh finite evaluation. Subsequent
