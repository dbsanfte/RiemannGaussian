1. Global storage and exact constants
------------------------------------
Fix real r and w>r^2, independently of scale. On the SAME block/domain
and positive weight define the bilinear pairing
  <G,H>_X=X^(-2)*sum_(n=X)^(2X-1)G(n)H(n),
  A(X)=E_G(X)+2r*<G,H>_X+w*E_H(X).
The complete pointwise matrix is M=[[1,r],[r,w]]. Completion gives
  G^2+2rGH+wH^2
     =w*(H+rG/w)^2+(1-r^2/w)*G^2
     =(G+rH)^2+(w-r^2)*H^2.                            (1.1)
Consequently, with no sign assumptions on either source,
  E_G<=w/(w-r^2)*A,  E_H<=A/(w-r^2),  A>=0.            (1.2)
The smallest and largest eigenvalues
  lambda_+-=(1+w +/- sqrt((1-w)^2+4r^2))/2
also give
  lambda_-*(E_G+E_H)<=A<=lambda_+*(E_G+E_H).             (1.3)
These rules hold pointwise, on every finite truncation, and in every
positive weighted integral. They retain every signed cross term.
They do not hold if the cross uses a different support/weight from its
two energies, unless the missing complements are explicitly restored.
A delayed H is allowed: it is still evaluated as the declared state at
the same n. Any source cutoffs must be recomputed at the actual child
scale; a retained parent cutoff is a different program with extra flux.

At w=r^2 the recovery fails: H=-G/r makes storage zero for arbitrary G
(when r!=0). This singular boundary is not a coercive candidate. Small
positive w-r^2 is legal but has the large exact cost in (1.2). Constants
fixed for each epsilon are allowed in a cofinal theorem; parameters
changing with X require a separate nonuniform recovery proof. A slope
ranking must not silently disregard this conditioning cost.

2. Real and complex poles survive a positive storage matrix
----------------------------------------------------------
If A has a cofinal or polylog bound, (1.2) supplies that bound to BOTH
G and H. Therefore any proved supercritical pole in either prefix
transform forbids a cofinal A, even if the scalar leading combination
G+rH cancels. Its complementary square in (1.1) restores the pole.
The gate must inspect both components, not only one completed square.

For H(n)=n^(-alpha)J(n), J a declared mode-prefix combination or delay,
the continuous Mellin identity is M_(x^-alpha J)(s)=M_J(s+alpha).
The integer-floor version differs by a harmless local analytic term:
from |J(n)|<=C_J*n*(1+log n), the error on [n,n+1) is
O(C_J*alpha*n^(-alpha)*(1+log n)) for alpha>=0.
Its transform is holomorphic in Re(s)>-alpha. Thus the gate's real pole
at 2/3-alpha is genuine unless the exact row residue or exact delayed
multiplier cancels it. At alpha=0 there is no interpolation error.
Alpha1/6 is a critical, not supercritical, location; this distinction
must remain explicit. All units, real-pole compensators and delays are
part of the declared row, not assumed independently paid.

Review006 proves the necessary block-energy degree
  p>=2ell-2                                             (2.1)
for a retained critical Mellin singularity of order ell. Applying (1.2)
gives the SAME guard for A. The actual core has ell2 at the locally
certified simple zero once the explicit shifted-nonvanishing witness is
discharged. The identity, selected nonresonant delays and log-Abel target
preserve that order. Cross storage cannot admit degrees zero or one for
them. Under the new recurrence domains, rho1/k>=1 and rho<1/k>=2 permit
degree at least two, so this particular pole obstruction is removed;
no arithmetic bound thereby follows. Higher zero multiplicities or
additional uncancelled component poles can require larger degrees.

3. A complete unconditional weak reference
-------------------------------------------
The previously proved coefficient envelopes imply for every integer n
  |G(n)|<=C_t*n*(1+log n), |H(n)|<=C_h*n*(1+log n).
One may use C_f=27/2+ceil(R_u), C_t=2C_f for the existing delays,
C_t=C_f for complete log-Abel, and C_h=2*sum_i|v_i| for the existing
auxiliary delays/rows with nonnegative alpha. The factor two in these
PREFIX estimates uses the half-sized argument, not an erroneous claim
that the delayed coefficient envelope is unchanged. Finite log-Abel
head values also obey the displayed prefix estimate.

The elementary inequality 2|GH|<=G^2+H^2 gives
  A<=(1+|r|)E_G+(w+|r|)E_H.
On the child block [2X,4X), summing n^2 and using
1+log(4X)<=3*(1+log2 X) yields the safe theorem
  A(2X)-rho*A(X)
    <=72*[(1+|r|)C_t^2+(w+|r|)C_h^2]
                           *X*(1+log2 X)^2             (3.1)
for every integer X>=1 and every rho>=0. Nonnegative A permits subtracting
rho*A(X). An alternative safe constant is
  72*[C_t^2+2|r|C_t*C_h+w*C_h^2].
This is a weak gamma1/logdegree2 reference, not a cofinal bound. The
absolute proof cannot justify the conjectural gamma0 branch.

          <=A(1)+C*j*(1+j)^k.                           (4.1)
Multiply by w/(w-r^2), then apply the exact source/filter inverse cost.

Changing sign/r/w after looking at a new scale creates a new program.

5. A generated child-block prediction certificate with all errors
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Here is an exact enumerable schema that preserves signed transport.
Let z(n)=(G(n),H(n))^T and fix the positive matrix M in section1. For
n in [X,2X), define the actual children
  U(n)=[z(2n)+z(2n+1)]/2,
  V(n)=[z(2n+1)-z(2n)]/2.
The full child identity is
  A(2X)=(1/(2X^2))*sum_n[U(n)^T M U(n)+V(n)^T M V(n)]. (5.1)
Both child endpoints are exact integers; no odd child or diagonal term
is dropped. For any fixed candidate matrix B define eta(n)=U(n)-Bz(n).
Then
  A(2X)-rho*A(X)
   =(1/(2X^2))*sum_n{
       z^T[B^T M B-2rho M]z
       +2z^T B^T M eta+eta^T M eta+V^T M V}.             (5.2)
This identity is independent of arithmetic and readily certifiable by
exact coefficient expansion. A rational/algebraic matrix inequality
  B^T M B<=(2rho-kappa)M, kappa>0,
gives a true coefficient gap, PROVIDED the complete joint forcing
  Phi_X=(1/(2X^2))*sum_n[2z^T B^T M eta+eta^T M eta+V^T M V]
has an independently established bound. It suffices to prove
  Phi_X<=C*(1+log2 X)^k
to obtain the desired recurrence (even retaining a negative storage term).
The signed mixed term should be retained rather than separately bounded
by a current-scale target norm that consumes the claimed gap.

This is not yet a smaller-scale recurrence: eta contains the actual
current children unless a SOURCE identity transforms it into concrete
lower-argument forcing plus fully paid frontier terms. Defining eta and
conjecturing Phi_X is otherwise just an exact reexpression of the drift.
The matrix gap is useful algebra; it does not prove Phi_X. Nullspace
guards must check both state components and all omitted error channels.
The detector can enumerate B and M, certify the matrix inequality, derive
the full Phi AST, then attempt arithmetic rewrites. A finite holdout can
falsify that AST's bound. Only an all-scale arithmetic proof closes it.

6. Source-specific log-work expansion and an additional degree guard
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For a vector step prefix z define its EXACT coefficient increments
q(n)=z(n)-z(n-1), its bank B_z(x)=sum_(n<=x)q(n)log n, and
  P_z(x)=B_z(x)/log x=z(x)-H_z(x)/log x,
  H_z(x)=int_1^x z(t)dt/t.
The Hilbert norm induced by M has the same finite-horizon Hardy bound
as review005. On [x0,T], x0=64, sigma>=1/2, let
  b0=1/(sigma*log x0)<1/2,
  c0=x0^(-sigma)/(sqrt(2sigma)*log x0),
  h0=||H_z(x0)||_M.
For the FULL signed work
  W_M(T)=int_(x0)^T z(x)^T M P_z(x)*x^(-2sigma-1)dx,
the exact coercivity proof gives
  ||z||_(M,sigma,x0..T)^2
       <=2*W_M(T)/(1-b0)+h0^2*c0^2/(1-b0)^2.            (6.1)
It follows by Cauchy/Hardy in the positive M-inner product and Young;
the finite head is retained. This supplies a signed auxiliary with a
proved recovery map, but its complete current-scale work is still open.

The core log bank has the exact all-prime-power expansion
  B_F(x)=sum_(d<=x,d>=2)Lambda(d)
                  *sum_i c_i lambda_i(d) A_i(x/d),
in evaluator order (nu0,nu1,nu2,mu),
  c=(beta,-beta,-R_u,-beta),
  lambda=(d^-u-2, d^-u-1-d^-v, d^-u-d^-v, -1).          (6.2)
Every cofactor argument is <=x/2; units and every power of each actual
prime survive. A delay G=F-aF(x/q) has the essential bank correction
  B_G(x)=B_F(x)-aB_F(x/q)-a*log(q)*F(x/q).               (6.3)
This term cannot be removed by calling it a lower-scale contribution.

For weighted H(n)=n^-alpha J(n), its exact discrete increment is
  h(1)=j(1),
  h(n)=n^-alpha*j(n)
          +[n^-alpha-(n-1)^-alpha]*J(n-1), n>=2.         (6.4)
Thus a certificate using h(n)=n^-alpha j(n) is false: it omits the
changing-weight transport. For a delayed J, j(n) itself includes the
divisibility mask and the source at n/q. Equation(6.4), or its complete
equivalent Abel formula, must appear before applying the source log law.

An additional reusable obstruction concerns cumulative norms. If
  N_Q(T)=int_1^T Q(x)^2*x^-2 dx=O((1+log T)^p),
write g(t)=exp(-t/2)Q(exp t). Stieltjes integration gives
  int_0^infinity |g(t)|^2*exp(-delta t)dt=O(delta^-p)
for p>=0. Weighted Cauchy then gives
  |M_Q(1/2+delta+i*tau)|=O(delta^(-(p+1)/2)).
A retained pole of order ell therefore requires
  p>=2ell-1.                                           (6.5)
This differs from the BLOCK degree2ell-2. For the double-pole core,
cumulative coercive work/norm must allow degree at least three. In
particular a bounded increment of W_M would imply cumulative degree
one by telescoping and is impossible under the same local certificate
conditions as review006. A work forcing of quadratic-log degree may
allow cubic cumulative growth; proving it remains a signed arithmetic
problem, not a consequence of these identities.

7. Independent audit of the prioritized SIGNED SHELL-WORK family
---------------------------------------------------------------
The main proposed experiment now prioritizes thirteen full delay filters
and signed work parameters |eta|<=2, rather than a static cross-storage
rerun. The exact continuous shell quantities are
  N_G(X)=int_X^(2X) G(x)^2/x^2 dx,
  J_G(x)=int_1^x G(y)/y dy,
  B_G(x)=sum_(n<=x) g(n)log n=G(x)log x-J_G(x),
  W_eta,G(X)=int_X^(2X){(1-eta)G(x)^2/x^2
                       +eta*G(x)B_G(x)/(x^2 log x)}dx
            =N_G(X)-eta*int_X^(2X)G(x)J_G(x)/(x^2 log x)dx. (7.1)
This uses the actual zero extension below one and full coefficient g,
including the delayed unit/divisibility mask. In particular (6.3) is the
EXACT arithmetic bank for G. Both terms in (7.1) are joined before any
bound; B_G or the delay correction is not separately assumed paid.

W is SIGNED and need not be nonnegative on a shell. Nevertheless an
upper recurrence with rho>=0 can be iterated from a valid signed base
upper bound: multiplying an upper inequality by rho preserves order.
Using max(0,ceil(W(X0))) as the base upper bound and a nonnegative fitted
forcing constant does not modify W. Replacing W by max(W,0) inside the
recurrence, or treating its exact signed drift as an absolute drift,
would change the conjecture and must be rejected by the AST checker.

Here is an independent complete causal recovery proof. Split J_G into
its boundary J_G(X) and local integral from X to x. On the shell, the
local map on h(x)=G(x)/x is
  T h(x)=x^(-1)*int_X^x h(y)dy.
The Schur test with positive weight x^(-1/2) bounds BOTH row and column
integrals by lambda=2-sqrt(2), so ||T||<=lambda. Explicitly they are
2[1-sqrt(X/x)] and 2[1-sqrt(y/(2X))], respectively. Therefore the local
work error is at most lambda*N_G(X)/log X. The constant boundary's
weighted norm is |J_G(X)|/sqrt(2X). Put
  g_X=1-|eta|lambda/log X,
  b_X=|J_G(X)|/[sqrt(2X)*log X].
When g_X>0, (7.1) and Cauchy imply
  g_X*N_G(X)<=W_eta,G(X)+|eta|*b_X*sqrt(N_G(X)).
Young's inequality gives the exact signed recovery inequality
  N_G(X)<=2*W_eta,G(X)/g_X+eta^2*b_X^2/g_X^2.            (7.2)
It is valid even when W is negative. If its right side were negative,
the preceding exact inequality could not hold; no positivity of W has
been presumed.

For dyadic X=2^j, the entire history starts at one. Cauchy on each
previous dyadic shell gives
  |J_G(X)|/sqrt X<=sum_(k=1)^j 2^(-k/2)*sqrt(N_G(X/2^k)).
A second weighted Cauchy, with A0=sum_(k>=1)2^(-k/2)=1+sqrt(2), gives
  J_G(X)^2/X<=A0*sum_(k=1)^j2^(-k/2)*N_G(X/2^k).
Substitution into (7.2) yields the complete memory convolution
  N_G(X)<=2W(X)/g_X
       +eta^2*A0/[2*g_X^2*(log X)^2]
                       *sum_(k=1)^j2^(-k/2)*N_G(X/2^k). (7.3)
All earlier shells, including the unit shell and pre-cutoff head, appear.
There is no untracked prime-entry or earlier-scale source remainder.

For |eta|<=2 and X>=X0=1024, elementary bounds lambda<3/5,
log X>69/10 and A0^2<6 give g_X>4/5. The TOTAL memory mass in (7.3) is
  eta^2*A0^2/[2*g_X^2*(log X)^2]
       <12/[(4/5)^2*(69/10)^2]<2/5.                    (7.4)
Define the complete finite head
  H0=max{E_G(Y): Y dyadic, 1<=Y<1024}.
The step-prefix comparison is N_G(Y)<=E_G(Y)<=4N_G(Y).
If a signed upper bound W(X)<=K*(1+log2 X)^p holds at all dyadic
X>=1024, K>=0,p>=0, strong dyadic induction in (7.3) proves
  N_G(X)<=max(H0,5K)*(1+log2 X)^p,
  E_G(X)<=4*max(H0,5K)*(1+log2 X)^p.                   (7.5)
Indeed 2/g_X<5/2, earlier polynomial weights are no larger than the

One first replaces W by its NONNEGATIVE proposed upper bound before
enlarging 2/g_X to5/2; enlarging that coefficient on a negative W would

No extra summation log is needed: this is local causal recovery, not
conversion through a cumulative norm. The head is finite and can be

  W(2X)-rho W(X)<=9*C_shell*X*(1+log2 X)^2.              (7.6)
The child costs at most eight times the parent-scale normalizer and the

8. What can genuinely change the proof search
---------------------------------------------
The new r parameter is not an arithmetic operator by itself. For fixed
coercive M, storage has the same joint asymptotic energy class as (G,H).
It can make a negative covariance visible and improve an inequality
presentation, but minimizing a measured slope does not explain that
covariance or pay (5.2)/(6.2). Moving toward w=r^2 to obtain a small
measurement merely sends the recovery constant to infinity.

A substantive detector output can instead be a triple:
  (i) a fixed operator/state/storage and exact coercivity/inverse costs;
 (ii) an independently verified child identity or full log-divisor
      rewrite with every unit, repeated prime, weight/delay flux and
      product/prefix boundary retained;
(iii) a proposed all-scale bound on its explicit JOINT forcing, with
      an arithmetic proof certificate or a stated unresolved lemma.
Schemas(5.2) and(6.1)-(6.4) make this enumerable. A compiler may search
mixed source rows with vanishing leading prime-power differences, but
must retain the unsuppressed symmetric mode and its boundary work;
review005 already proves why angular statistics alone cannot recover it.

An argument advances the present frontier only if it pays some retained
forcing by a proved lower-exponent estimate, proves a strict recurrence
gap with independently bounded forcing, or establishes a new signed
source correlation. Merely replacing the open drift by Phi_X or W_M is
an exact identity/recovery improvement, not a cancellation theorem.
No such actual-source uniform covariance estimate is proved here.
The global proofs, degree guards and full identity ASTs are available
for the next detector protocol; all older frozen conclusions remain
unchanged.
