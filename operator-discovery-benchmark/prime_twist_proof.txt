FRACTIONAL PRIME TWISTS, SIGNED HARMONIC CONTROL, AND RIGHT-SHIFT RECOVERY

Scope
-----
The input is actual lambda(p^k)=1/k, zero off prime powers, generated by the
factor-free Dirichlet logarithm. Lambda(n)=lambda(n)log(n). The source stage
proved psi(x)<=C_psi*x for every real x>=1, using a factorial trace and a
universal periodic-floor certificate. No prime-distribution estimate is
imported here. Historical novelty is not a mathematical acceptance gate.
This is a new general shift grammar and a supplied mathematical proof backend.
It is not claimed that the detector autonomously invented these proofs.
The goal remains an RH-strength uniform signed estimate for ordinary zeta.
Weak signed bounds and shifted output exponents do not complete that goal.

1. Programs and fractional roots
--------------------------------
For any positive rational delta and finite rational vectors c,b of equal
length, let nu be the smallest j*delta with c_j!=0. Normalize c at that
leading index to one. Define tau_a(f)(n)=n^(-a)f(n), n>=1. A real fractional
twist is evaluated as (positive real qth root of n)^(-p) when a=p/q.
There is no branch ambiguity and no semantic denominator ceiling.
The coefficient polynomial is P(z)=sum c_j z^j with z=n^(-delta).
It may have either sign and may vanish at arithmetic indices.

There are two channels, with independent normalizer coefficients b:
  weighted: E(x)=sum b_j J_(1-jdelta)(x)
                 -sum_(2<=n<x)lambda(n)P(n^(-delta));
  log:      E(x)=sum b_j I_(1-jdelta)(x)
                 -sum_(2<=n<x)Lambda(n)P(n^(-delta)).
Both vanish below two. For x>=2,
  J_a(x)=integral_2^x t^(a-1)/log(t) dt,
  I_a(x)=integral_2^x t^(a-1) dt.
These have regular values at a=0: J_0=log(log(x)/log(2)), I_0=log(x/2).
For a!=0, J_a=Ei(a log x)-Ei(a log 2), I_a=(x^a-2^a)/a.
Thus fractional arithmetic shifts and independently chosen smooth kernels
are represented with explicit singular-parameter conventions.

On Re(s)>1, finite sum exchange gives the exact Mellin formula
  weighted: s H_E(s)=sum b_j E1((s-1+jdelta)log 2)
                         -sum c_j log zeta(s+jdelta);
  log:      s H_E(s)=sum b_j 2^(1-jdelta-s)/(s-1+jdelta)
                         +sum c_j zeta'(s+jdelta)/zeta(s+jdelta).
E1(z)=Gamma(0,z). Its sum with log(s-kappa) is an entire function:
  B_kappa(s)=E1((s-kappa)log 2)+log(s-kappa)
    =-gamma-log(log 2)+integral_0^(log 2)(1-exp(-(s-kappa)u))/u du.
The exponential-integral and incomplete-gamma definitions are documented in
NIST DLMF 8.19: https://dlmf.nist.gov/8.19 . Ordinary zeta's simple pole and
Laurent finite part are documented in DLMF 25.2: https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2 .

2. Signed actual-prime harmonic control from the factorial trace
---------------------------------------------------------------
For every integer N>=1,
  log(N!)=sum_(n<=N)Lambda(n)floor(N/n)
          =N A(N)-R(N),
  A(N)=sum_(n<=N)Lambda(n)/n,
  R(N)=sum_(n<=N)Lambda(n){N/n},
where {u}=u-floor(u). Positivity and the proved prime bound give
  0<=R(N)<=psi(N)<=C_psi N.
The elementary log-factorial integral comparison gives
  log(N!)/N>=log N-1+1/N,
  log(N!)/N<=log N-1+(1+log N)/N.
For real x>=2 choose N=ceil(x)-1, so N<x<=N+1 and the strict prime sum
A_<(x)=A(N). The inequalities log(1+1/N)<=1/N and log N<=N-1 imply
  -1 <= Q(x):=A_<(x)-log x <= C_psi, EVERY real x>=2.       (1)
This is signed arithmetic information about actual primes over all scales.
It uses the exact trace to center the arithmetic sum, not an absolute-sum
bound or an imported prime theorem. It remains below RH strength.
At integer N, the centered fractional-part trace has the identity
  Q(N)+gamma = [R(N)-(1-gamma)N]/N
                 +log(N!)/N-log N+1-Lambda(N)/N.
The last two terms are O(log N/N) with explicit elementary bounds. Thus an
all-scale square-root bound on the centered fractional-part trace would
supply an all-scale inverse-square-root harmonic residual. Finite evaluation
of either quantity does not prove that estimate.

For the log-channel monomial nu=1, E(x)=log(x/2)-A_<(x).
Its compiled Mellin finite part at zero is gamma-log 2, since
  2^(-s)/s+zeta'(1+s)/zeta(1+s)
    =(1/s-log 2)+(-1/s+gamma)+O(s).
Therefore |E(x)-(gamma-log 2)|=|Q(x)+gamma| is uniformly bounded using (1).
The stronger target
  |Q(x)+gamma| <= C*x^(-1/2)*log(e+x)^ell, every x>=2,
would be RH-sufficient. That decay estimate remains unproved.
The finite part determines the exact necessary centering constant; (1)
alone is not claimed to prove Q(x) tends to -gamma.

3. A proved signed reciprocal-prime tail, uniform in fractional order
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For nu>=1 put phi_nu(t)=t^(1-nu)/log t, which is decreasing on t>=2.
Stieltjes partial summation of (1), with strict endpoints, gives
  E_nu(x):=J_(1-nu)(x)-sum_(n<x)lambda(n)n^(-nu)
    =-phi_nu(x)Q(x)-phi_nu(2)log 2
        +integral_2^x Q(t)phi_nu'(t)dt.
Because Q is bounded and phi tends to zero, E_nu has a finite limit K_nu,
and the asymmetric interval [-1,C_psi] in (1) gives
  |E_nu(x)-K_nu| <= (C_psi+1)*x^(1-nu)/log x,
                       EVERY real x>=2 and EVERY real nu>=1.            (2)
This is an actual signed cancellation theorem, uniform in the fractional
order. For nu>1, absolute convergence identifies
  K_nu=E1((nu-1)log 2)-log zeta(nu).
At nu=1, existence of the limit already follows from the above integral.
The elementary Abelian limit s H_E(s)->K_1 for a bounded function tending
to K_1, together with the exact Mellin formula, identifies
  K_1=-gamma-log(log 2).
No prime number theorem is needed to prove this identification.
In particular (2) states
  |sum_(p^k<x)1/(k p^k)-log(log x)-gamma|
       <= (C_psi+1)/log x, every real x>=2.
For a signed polynomial whose nonzero shifts all satisfy jdelta>=1 and whose
normalizers match b=c, summing these paired cancellation estimates proves
  |E(x)-sum c_j K_(jdelta)|
     <= (C_psi+1)(sum|c_j|)*x^(1-nu)/log x, x>=2.
The inverse shift costs nu in the ordinary-zeta exponent. Here
  (1-nu)+nu=1.
Thus these verified signed tail bounds are NOT RH-strength after recovery.

4. Necessary pole compensation and exact centering
--------------------------------------------------
Let F(s)=(s-1)zeta(s), regular at one. It is positive at every real s>=0:
for 0<s<1 the paired alternating series for eta(s) has positive terms, so
zeta(s)=eta(s)/(1-2^(1-s))<0; at zero zeta(0)=-1/2; above one zeta>0;
and F(1)=1. There are no positive-real ordinary zeta zeros.

An uncancelled real pole at kappa_j=1-jdelta inside the output half-plane
has residue c_j-b_j in the derivative of the weighted-channel numerator,
or residue b_j-c_j in the log-channel numerator. It rules out a uniform
growth claim whose output Mellin exponent is below kappa_j. Extra shifted
poles must be checked even when arithmetic weights are nonnegative and
prime-density bounds hold unchanged.

For negative output exponents, a constant offset must cancel the pole of
H_E at s=0. The exact required offset is the compiled numerator's finite
part at zero, not a rounded empirical mean. The grammar stores this as an
exact special-function expression with a certified numerical enclosure.
At kappa=0 it uses the regularized constants above. At other real shifts,
native zeta values and derivatives and the elementary kernel finite parts
give the exact expression. Arbitrary nearby rational offsets cannot be
treated as the same exact centering constant.

5. Integer-to-real transfer and recovery of every ordinary zero
---------------------------------------------------------------
The shift grammar has nonnegative shifts. Let a be the smallest shift
appearing in either coefficients or normalizers. On every whole unit
interval, the weighted-channel increment is bounded by
  (sum|c_j|+sum|b_j|/log 2)*n^(-a),
and the log-channel increment by
  (sum|c_j| log n+sum|b_j|)*n^(-a).
The prime-power jump at the left endpoint is included. Therefore an integer
bound |E(n)-A|<=C n^theta log(e+n)^ell extends with the factor
2^max(0,-theta) and a declared decaying increment cost. Its Mellin integral
is holomorphic for Re(s)>eta_out=max(theta,-a). This permits negative rational
growth exponents without silently discarding prime jumps.

At an ordinary zero rho of multiplicity m(rho), either channel's meromorphic
residue rule, after compensation of real poles, gives the constraint
  sum_j c_j m(rho+(j-r)delta)=0,
where r is the leading nonzero coefficient index. If m(rho)>0 and all the
right-shifted multiplicities were zero, this would be impossible since c_r=1.
Thus cancellation at one zero requires a zero farther to the right by at
least delta. Iterating must reach Re(s)>1 in finitely many steps, where the
Euler product gives no zeros. Hence a holomorphic output excludes EVERY
ordinary zero with Re(rho)>eta_out+nu, even if shifted zeros coincide and
cancel locally. No all-height nonvanishing condition on a scalar multiplier
is imposed. There is no finite dilation filter in this proof template.
It follows that eta_out+nu<=1/2 is RH-sufficient if the signed premise is
uniform. A stricter value is ruled out by the independently enclosed actual
critical-line zero. The written proof is not machine-formalized.

6. Full quantitative recovery costs
-----------------------------------
For the weighted channel use L(s)=log F(s). For the log channel use
B(s)=F'(s)/F(s). The compiled centered Mellin expressions give
  sum c_j L(s+jdelta)
     =-s H_center(s)-A+sum b_j B_(kappa_j)(s)
                              +sum(c_j-b_j)log(s-kappa_j),
  sum c_j B(s+jdelta)
     = s H_center(s)+A
         +sum b_j[1-2^(-(s-kappa_j))]/(s-kappa_j)
         +sum(c_j-b_j)/(s-kappa_j).
The displayed normalizer quotients and B_kappa are entire at their apparent
singularities. Isolate the leading coefficient and recurse rightward. Stop
each path only in a declared Euler half-plane Re(s)>=9/8, where absolute
Dirichlet sums bound log zeta or zeta'/zeta. All paths and coefficients are
charged, not merely the output amplitude. For the log channel the bound is
uniform over a horizontal segment, then integrated to reconstruct log F
from its Euler endpoint. Reciprocal zeta reconstruction costs an exponential:
  log|1/zeta(s)| <= log|s-1|+|log F(s)|.
The resulting number is a conditional fixed-point cost. It does not prove
the signed arithmetic premise or a new all-height norm bound.

7. Density-matched controls and finite evidence
-----------------------------------------------
The grammar's exact Bernstein backend can prove 0<=P(z)<=1 on [0,1].
For such a nonzero polynomial, positivity of the Bernstein basis on (0,1)
also proves P>0 there. Thus lambda(n)P(n^(-delta)) retains EXACTLY the actual
prime-power locations and obeys the same prime-density upper bound.
In particular a declared delayed attenuation equals actual lambda through
a finite cutoff B, and equals (1-n^(-delta))lambda(n) afterwards. It obeys
0<=lambda_control<=lambda and the same all-scale density bound. Through the
source coefficient-density finite patch it is identical to actual lambda.
The source logarithmic-saving square/Gram bound still follows: the tail
proof requires only nonnegative weights<=lambda, and the finite patch is
unchanged. It is never labelled as the unmodified coefficient sequence.

With the unmodified li normalizer, this delayed control has coefficient
Dirichlet logarithm log zeta(s)-log zeta(s+delta) plus an entire finite-prefix
correction. For 0<delta<1/2 the extra pole logarithm at 1-delta rules out
all RH-strength uniform point or quadratic-moment bounds for that control,
regardless of finite success. This demonstrates that density and diagonal
information alone cannot establish the signed pair premise. Exact prime
weights and signed correlations carry information that those bounds omit.
Matched shifted normalizers are different programs and are audited by the
right-shift recovery rule, so potentially useful repaired operators remain
eligible for the search.

Every search candidate is frozen before discovery-validation; fresh final
points and moment windows are drawn after archive sealing. Native scalar
shifts, complete prime prefixes, exact fractional-root identities, generic
positive-input identities, kernel quadrature, ordinary-zero dependencies,
real shifted-pole obstructions, finite shift-path costs, and signed harmonic
certificates require independent checking. Successful finite estimates remain
open proof obligations. The verified signed results (1),(2) remain below RH
strength; all RH-strength decay and pair premises remain unproved.
