Joint divisor Gram scope and scalar accounting

Each regional atom vector is (P,D_1,...,D_9), ordered by theta
1/3,1/2,2/3 and power -1,0,1. All weights have w_1=1. Thus its
formal constant vector is z=(0,-1/2,...,-1/2), with no arithmetic
values substituted. For a fixed exact PSD matrix G the scalar charge
of one block is z^T G z = (1/4) sum_{i,j=1}^9 G_ij >= 0.
All DD offdiagonal entries are included twice in this sum. The parent
has X blocks and the child 2X blocks. Therefore the combined charge is
X/4 sum DD(parent) + X/2 sum DD(child). With fixed matrices this is
O(X), and it can be zero for nonzero PSD matrices: the direction
D_i-D_j has zero constant. A D_i+D_j direction has positive charge.

The old separate pair cone embeds exactly into this joint cone:
embed each 2x2 [P,D_j] matrix on coordinates (0,j) and sum the nine
embedded matrices. Congruence embeddings and sums preserve PSD;
all P diagonals add, each P-D_j entry retains its coefficient, and
all previously absent D_i-D_j entries are zero. Legacy 5x5 Grams are
copied. This preserves every full formal polynomial and scalar charge
at every complete source block. Joint mixing adds genuine channels.

Under the previously proved fixed-template mu scalar asymptotic
assumptions at q>1, these nonnegative O(X) charges cannot cancel the
old cubic scalar term. Hence the old gamma=0 necessary gate is still
necessary. The auxiliary row encodes only that old gate; its four
empty Gram block lists introduce no unrecorded Gram constants. Full
original polynomial identities, constant slack, and finite budgets
still require separate exact checking. Neither the gate, the cone
embedding, a finite separator, nor a failed separator for a larger
cone establishes a uniform signed bound or RH.
