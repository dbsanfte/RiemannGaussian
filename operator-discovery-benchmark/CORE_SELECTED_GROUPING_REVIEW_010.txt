1. Common exact matrix and support convention
----------------------------------------------
Use H=4X-1, f the unchanged complete four-state core, and
 Phi_X(h,k)=K_1,2X(h,k)-K_1,X(h,k)/2,
 K_1,Y(h,k)=log(h*k)/2*integral_max(Y,h,k)^(2Y)
                                      dx/(x^2 log x),
zero for max(h,k)>=2Y. For the selected label map L_X,
 Q_rs(X)=sum_(h,k<=H,L_X(h)=r,L_X(k)=s)f(h)f(k)Phi_X(h,k),
 M_L(X)=sum_(r,s)|Q_rs(X)|,
                    Delta W(X)=sum_(r,s)Q_rs(X).          (1.1)
The label vector is computed with THIS_X cutoff throughout the parent
and child kernel. It is not replaced by L_(2X) in the child.
All ordered crosses and the diagonal are present. Exact full-cell weights
and the old-head product are the frozen ones; no pointwise approximation
or sign of that head is presumed.

For h,k in[2X,5X/2], the parent kernel is zero and, as009 proves,
                        Phi_X(h,k)>=3/(40X).             (1.2)
For ANY h,k>2X inside H, Phi_X is nonnegative. That broader sign plus
complete class membership, rather than a favorable subset alone, is
what makes the117 lower bound valid.

2. Candidate117: complete positive fresh-semiprime classes
----------------------------------------------------------
Put S=floor(sqrt X). Strip all powers of primes<=S, leaving r whose
prime factors are strictly greater than sqrt X. For sufficiently large
X, r<=4X-1 has at most TWO prime factors counted with multiplicity:
three such factors would give r>X^(3/2)>4X.

Choose a prime key q in[(41/20)sqrt X,(21/10)sqrt X]. A residual equal
to one prime maps to key1, so a residual with selected key q must be
r=pq with p<=q and p>sqrt X. The original n=d*pq, with d S-smooth.
But pq>(41/20)X>2X. Any d>=2 gives n>(41/10)X>H, so d=1. Also q^2
>4X, so p=q is IMPOSSIBLE. Finally p<=H/q<q. This completely describes
every member of these classes; there are no small-smooth multiples,
hidden unit branches, three-rough-factor terms or prime squares.

For distinct primes p,q both>sqrt X, let t_p=p^-1/3,t_q=q^-1/3. Exact
multiplicativity in the complete four-mode source gives
 f(pq)=beta*(t_p-2)*(t_q-2)
       -beta*(t_p-1-t_p^2)*(t_q-1-t_q^2)
       -R_u*(t_p-t_p^2)*(t_q-t_q^2)-beta.
Thus uniformly over ALL members of these complete classes,
                   f(pq)=2beta+O(X^-1/6)>beta>0          (2.1)
for sufficiently large X. Since every member is>2X, their entire
class-pair matrices Q_qr are nonnegative. No adverse terms elsewhere
inside those same Q_qr blocks can undo a favorable sub-sector.

Now restrict to primes
 p in[(101/100)sqrt X,(21/20)sqrt X],
 q in[(41/20)sqrt X,(21/10)sqrt X].
Their products lie strictly between2X and5X/2. The proved PNT gives
Omega(X/log^2 X) such distinct indices; the two prime intervals are
disjoint, so their representation and labels are unique. By(1.2),(2.1),
 summing the COMPLETE positive class-pair blocks dominates
 (3/(40X))*[sum_(restricted p,q) f(pq)]^2
                             >=c*X/log^4 X, c>0.         (2.2)
Hence M_117(X) has a positive-power lower bound and cannot prove ANY
fixed-polylog or all-epsilon cofinal bound. This holds at every sufficiently
large dyadic X, not just a sampled sequence. It does not refute Delta W,
which can cancel these blocks against different label pairs.

The imported theorem is only the classical PNT on fixed-ratio intervals;
a primary formalization/proof reference is Carneiro(2016),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02029. No RH remainder, prime table or numerical
coefficient fit is needed. Without this import, the lower bound is a
generic conditional guard with the explicit interval prime-count premise.

A reusable largest-delete analogue is also valid. Choose key p in
[(29/20)sqrt X,(3/2)sqrt X]. Every class member is n=pq with q>=p,
d=1 and n>2X; no third rough prime is possible. Squares p^2 ARE now
present and must remain. Their exact coefficients satisfy
 f(p^2)=beta*(1-t_p)^2+beta*t_p*(1-t_p)^2
                                   -R_u*(1-t_p)*t_p^2
                         =beta+O(X^-1/6)>0.
Distinct-prime coefficients obey(2.1). Taking q in[(31/20)sqrt X,
(8/5)sqrt X] puts products in[2X,5X/2]. The same whole-class positivity
and PNT lower bound apply. For largest-all deletion, squares go to key1
instead; they are excluded by that exact map, not silently omitted.
The reusable rule must certify complete class support and source sign
before using a favorable semiprime rectangle to lower-bound a block.

3. Candidate37: a fixed masked sequence has an uncanceled real pole
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Removing powers of2 preserves divisibility by3. Thus label0 is EXACTLY
the set3 divides n. Let b(n)=f(n)*1_(3 divides n), with its complete
prefix F_b and logarithmic bank B_b. Q_00(X) is precisely the signed
eta1 half-scale work increment of this SINGLE FIXED sequence b. Its
unit is zero; all other finite-head terms remain.

The nonzero pole can be proved algebraically, without an assumed
asymptotic for its prefix. Put kappa=2/3, t=3^-1/3, z=t^2 and
 A0=1/zeta(kappa)^2>0,
 A1=1/[zeta(kappa)*zeta(4/3)]<0,
 A2=1/zeta(4/3)>0.
The full core pole cancels exactly:
                   R_u*A2=beta*(A0-A1).                  (3.1)
At prime3 its local factors for the first three modes are
 L0(z)=(1-z)^2/(1-tz),
 L1(z)=(1-z)*(1-t^2 z)/(1-tz),
 L2(z)=(1-t^2 z)/(1-tz).
The fourth mu mode is analytic at kappa. Removing the local factor gives
the3-free source series a residue
 R_no3=beta*A0/L0-beta*A1/L1-R_u*A2/L2
 =beta*t^2*(1-t^3)*(2A0-A1)/[(1-t^2)^2*(1+t^2)]>0.        (3.2)
All denominators are nonzero. Since the full series has canceled that
real pole, the mask3 divides n has residue -R_no3!=0 at kappa.
This is a genuine analytic simple pole; no cofactor coefficient series
is claimed to converge there. Real zeta signs used above follow from
the positive alternating eta series and the absolutely convergent
zeta(4/3) series, as009 explains.

Suppose M_37(X) had a uniform fixed-polylog upper bound. Then
 Q_00(X)<=|Q_00(X)|<=M_37(X) gives a universal signed recurrence for
the masked sequence with rho1/2. Signed upper iteration and the generic
007 causal Volterra recovery give fixed-polylog complete E_b(X), with
its own unit/head retained. Mellin/Cauchy then makes its Dirichlet series
holomorphic for Re(s)>1/2, contradicting(3.2). The same argument rejects
all-epsilon subpower upper bounds: choose an exponent below1/3 to put
the resulting Mellin half-plane to the left of kappa.

Therefore37's grouped absolute proof route is FALSE at every cofinal
class of this type, irrespective of its finite curve. There is no
contradiction for the original f: its main pole cancels only in the
complete cross-class sum. Taking absolute values of Q blocks reinstates
an unpayable masked channel by forcing each diagonal block controlled.

This compiles to a generic residue-prime mask rule. If stripping does
not contain q, zero modulo q is exactly q divides n, and(3.2) holds
with t=q^-1/3. If step1 stripping contains q, the zero class is empty,
so that rule does NOT apply. If step2 stripping contains q, zero modulo
q is the FIXED odd-v_q mask. With z=t^2, its exact kappa residue is
 -beta*[(1-t^3)/(1+t^3)]*z
       *[A0*(2+z+z^2)-A1*(1+z^2)]/[(1-z)^2*(1+z)]<0.    (3.3)
Derivation: use the odd local factor(L_i(z)-L_i(-z))/2, divide by
L_i(z), and invoke(3.1). Thus it also yields the masked-pole guard once
q is included. Multi-prime residue masks require their actual local
product computation; no generic nonzero claim is inferred without it.

4. Candidate110 exact square-lift cells and repeated-prime complement
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let Y=floor(X^(1/3)). Each n has a UNIQUE decomposition
 n=d^2*r,
 d supported on primes<=Y,
 v_p(r) in{0,1} for primes<=Y,
with arbitrary valuations of primes>Y retained in r. Label j means
                   j^6<=r<(j+1)^6, j>=1.                 (4.1)
The lowest cell contains residuals1 through63, not only r1. Large-prime
squares and higher valuations are NOT deleted from the residual.

A semiprime sub-sector does not prove positivity of a complete110 cell:
its sixth-root interval mixes many other residuals and signs. Nor can
the dynamically Y-dependent cells be treated as one fixed masked
Dirichlet series when applying the37 pole argument. No complete class
sign or fixed-pole countercertificate for110 is established here.

The unremoved-square set can be bounded explicitly but is not thereby
critically paid. With H=4X-1 and |f(n)|<=K0*d2(n),
 sum_(n<=H,exists prime p>Y with p^2 divides n)|f(n)|
 <=3K0*sum_(p>Y)sum_(m<=H/p^2)d2(m)
 <=3K0*H*(1+log H)/(Y-1), Y>=2.                          (4.2)
Here d2(p^2 m)<=3d2(m), including p dividing m; union overcounting is
an upper bound and excludes no valuations. It is O(X^(2/3)log X), above
the critical prefix scale. It is neither an actual lower obstruction
nor a signed tail bound; further joint correlation is needed before
discarding or separately paying this complement.

5. Uniform paid low-cell theorem, including a polylog-growing head
------------------------------------------------------------------
There is a useful complete-source payment which DOES hold. For integers
J>=1, sum all label cells1 through J, and set R=(J+1)^6. Since
 d2(r d^2)<=d2(r)*d2(d^2)<=d2(r)*d3(d),
 sum_(d<=y)d3(d)<=y*(1+log y)^2,
their absolute coefficient prefix is bounded for every real x>=1 by
 sum_(j<=J)|F_(j,X)(x)|
 <=K0*sqrt x*(1+log x)^2*sum_(r<R)d2(r)/sqrt r
 <=A_J*sqrt x*(1+log x)^2,
 A_J=2K0*(J+1)^3*[1+6log(J+1)].                           (5.1)
The first bound allows ALL d and r, so is uniform in Y and includes
common primes between d and r. The second follows from
sum_(r<=R)d2(r)/sqrt r<=2sqrt R*(1+log R), by expanding r=ab and
the elementary sum of b^-1/2. Every original n belongs to just one
canonical cell; allowing extra representations only increases this
absolute majorant.

The source constant can be any K0>=3beta+R_u. The frozen residue
enclosures certify R_u<5, so K0=37/2 is a valid rational instance.
This is a previously verified constant, not a new source calculation.
The unit and all fixed low residuals are present in(5.1).

Let B_(j,X)(x) be the logarithmic bank of the SAME_X masked coefficient
sequence. Since log n<=log x for n<=x,
 sum_(j<=J)|B_(j,X)(x)|/log x
                           <=A_J*sqrt x*(1+log x)^2       (5.2)
for x>1. For the exact eta1 matrix each ordered block is the symmetrized
integral of(F_j B_k+B_j F_k)/(2x^2 log x), with child minus half parent.
Consequently the ENTIRE low-by-low sector obeys
 sum_(j,k<=J)|Q_jk(X)|
 <=(3/2)*A_J^2*log2*(1+log(4X))^4.                       (5.3)
This pays all its internal units, valuations, source-mode crosses and
both parent/child intervals. It is stronger than paying each fixed cell
separately, and has no current-scale source-bound premise.

The theorem is pointwise in arbitrary J=J(X). In particular any declared
polylog-growing J, for example J<=C*(1+log X)^tau with fixed C,tau,
gives an explicit cofinal head envelope of order
 (1+log X)^(4+6tau)*(1+loglog(4X))^2,
with fixed constants from(5.3). Its loglog factor is retained, not hidden
as an exponent gain. Changing X changes the partition and J, but BOTH
parts of each Q_jk step still use its original SAME_X partition.

For a generic floor kth-root post-map with the same square-lift base,
replace R by(J+1)^k and A_J by
 2K0*(J+1)^(k/2)*[1+k log(J+1)].
The same proof and(5.3) compile mechanically. It is a genuine reusable
paid-sector rule, not a hand-selected bound for the remaining target.
Step1 stripping is not a square lift and cannot use this critical rule
by changing a name: its coefficient-lift exponent and norm cost differ.

6. Exact signed remainder for110 and required proof feedback
-------------------------------------------------------------
Define, with this SAME_X matrix,
 L_J(X)=sum_(j,k<=J(X))Q_jk(X),
 R_J(X)=sum_(max(j,k)>J(X))Q_jk(X).
Then exactly
 Delta W(X)=L_J(X)+R_J(X)
 <=(3/2)*A_J^2*log2*(1+log(4X))^4+R_J(X).                 (6.1)
Both ordered low/high crosses and high/high are present ONCE in R_J.
They are not covered by(5.3). The signed remainder may be negative; only
a ONE-SIDED cofinal upper bound is needed for the broader cofinal goal.
If a fixed-polylog R_J upper estimate is independently proved at every
dyadic scale, (6.1), signed work recurrence iteration and007 recovery
give the complete cofinal f target with its existing original recovery.
No separately assumed RH-strength companion bound is used in the head.

This does not preserve the old fitted degree2 constant1: the paid head
may have a higher fixed log degree, which is still sufficient for the
cofinal objective. A stronger degree2 proof would need the exact L_J
and additional signed compensation; it cannot substitute(5.3) for that
budget and silently discard its larger log degree.

The new detector may enumerate rational log cutoff powers tau, generic
root degree k and exact square-lift label ASTs, compile(5.1)-(5.3), then
emit the complete signed R_J as its explicit residual obligation. It
must not call that obligation solved because the head is paid. Additional
prime/cofactor/valuation transports apply to R_J only with complete exit
and assignment certificates. Repartitioning child labels or assuming
fixed masks at varying X adds new differences that must be accounted for.
