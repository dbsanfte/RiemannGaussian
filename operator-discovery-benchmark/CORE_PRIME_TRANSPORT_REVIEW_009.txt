1. Exact orbit grouping and what absolute values can certify
----------------------------------------------------------
Let the complete step kernel be
  L_X(h,k)=K_eta,2X(h,k)-rho K_eta,X(h,k), h,k<=4X-1.
Any total label map l_X on this ENTIRE support defines
  Q_rs=sum_(l_X(h)=r,l_X(k)=s) f(h)f(k)L_X(h,k).
The exact identities/rule are
  Delta W=sum_rs Q_rs, Delta W<=sum_rs |Q_rs|.
Units, diagonal and old-head pairs are included. Grouping is legitimate
only when every pair is represented exactly once, or an overlapping
weighted cover has a verified reproduction identity. All four modes
must be combined before evaluating each group; separate triangle bounds
for their sixteen cross channels can destroy actual prime cancellations.

The streamed grouping implementation reads each index in ascending order.
Its new-index update uses the complete max-index kernel
a(n)+b(n)(log h+log k), adds both orientations, and then the coefficient
diagonal. The displayed formulas agree with the full grouped identity.
The diagonal of the GROUP matrix includes within-group off-diagonal
pairs and must not be confused with the coefficient diagonal of008.
Float discovery explicitly lacks a certified rounding bound; no universal
grouped arithmetic inequality follows from those numerical expressions.

2. Actual first-prime signature and the growing-cutoff obstruction
----------------------------------------------------------------
Combining ALL source channels at an actual prime gives exactly
  f(p)=-R_u p^-u+(beta+R_u)p^-2u.                       (2.1)
The constant and part of the p^-u terms cancel between channels. Thus
the leading exponent is u1/3, not zero; the AST compiler's combined
prime-row extraction correctly records this cancellation. At odd primes
any fixed delay g(n)=f(n)-a*1_(2|n)f(n/2) has g(p)=f(p).

Consider any prime-stripping set P_X contained in primes<=X. Stripping
any whole valuation or even-valuation chunk leaves each p in[2X,5X/2]
unchanged. Moreover its complete orbit inside the support is a singleton:
every nonunit stripped dilation d>=2 has dp>=4X. The parent kernel is
zero on these pairs, while008 proves the child lower bound
  L_X(p,q)=K_eta,2X(p,q)>=3/(40X), |eta|<=2.
For all sufficiently large primes (2.1) has one sign and
|f(p)|>=R_u p^-u/2.

The only external arithmetic theorem used for this obstruction is the
classical prime number theorem. A primary reference is Atle Selberg,
"An Elementary Proof of the Prime-Number Theorem", Annals of Mathematics
50 (1949),305-313, https://doi.org/10.2307/1969455 ; accessible original:
https://www.math.lsu.edu/~mahlburg/teaching/handouts/2014-7230/Selberg-ElemPNT1949.pdf .
Partial summation gives for fixed 0<A<B and 0<u<1
  sum_(AX<p<=BX)p^-u
    ~ [(B^(1-u)-A^(1-u))/(1-u)] X^(1-u)/log X.
Therefore the absolute sum of these singleton pair groups is
  Omega(X^(1-2u)/log^2 X)=Omega(X^(1/3)/log^2 X).        (2.2)
This is uniform over arbitrary growing choices P_X<=X. No prime gaps,
RH, short-interval estimate or source randomness is assumed. It forbids
a cofinal grouped-absolute proof for the BASE stripping partitions;
it does not refute the original signed step.

For a FIXED finite prime set there is a stronger elementary guard.
Removing its finitely many Euler factors from008's absolute mass series
leaves a positive constant times x log x for the P-free |g| mass. On
[2X,5X/2] each such cofactor is again a singleton orbit. The same kernel
lower bound gives Omega_P(Xlog^2 X). Hence exact rational local tails
or infinite local sums cannot repair a fixed-prime fresh boundary.

3. Two sound automatic postcomposition guards
---------------------------------------------
For valuation_step1, let Y be the actual integer stripping cutoff.
If (Y+1)^2>4X-1, the residual of every n<=4X-1 is either1 or a single
prime: two remaining factors, WITH multiplicity, would exceed support.
Thus removing largest once, smallest once or largest all maps every
residual to1. There is ONE group and sumabs=|whole Delta W|. This is
an exact degenerate presentation, not an analytically false candidate.
For cutoff floor(X^theta),theta>1/2 this holds at all sufficiently large
X; finite scales require the displayed integer inequality. Step2 does
not satisfy this premise because leftover odd small-prime valuations
remain. Its removal posts must not inherit this collapse certificate.

Now suppose step1, (Y+1)^2>4X-1, Y<=X, and a monotone root/quotient
post has label preimage width o(X) on residuals<=4X. A complete interior
fresh band, for example[9X/4,19X/8], then has labels whose ENTIRE
preimages eventually lie inside[2X,5X/2]. Residuals there are primes;
any nonunit dilation exits support. The full classes therefore contain
only actual fresh primes. Their coefficients all have the same sign
and L_X is positive, so merging several such primes cannot reduce the
lower bound(2.2). Root orders2,3,6 have width O(X^(1-1/k)); quotient
by floor(X^a),0<a<1 has width O(X^a). Both meet the width premise.
Boundary clusters straddling2X cannot be called prime-only.

This generalizes the cutoffX guard to cutoffs much larger than sqrt X
with the explicit residual1/prime condition. A positive subsector cannot
be used to lower-bound |Q_rs| if its class also contains oppositely signed
composites. In particular coarse residues can mix units/composites and
prime sectors; they remain OPEN. Step2 or smaller cutoffs also require
new sign/remainder certificates before inheriting the prime-only guard.

4. Boundary sqrt-cutoff extension: REVIEW ONLY
-----------------------------------------------
There is an additional proof at Y=floor(sqrt X),step1, for root/quotient
posts of widtho(X). This is not claimed as an implemented gate.
Interior residual classes above2X have no nonunit stripped dilation.
Their actual indices are rough primes or semiprimes: every remaining
prime is>sqrt X, so three factors exceed4X at large X. For two such
primes, including a repeated prime, f(pq) is eventually positive; for
distinct primes it tends to2beta, while f(p^2) tends tobeta. Large rough
prime coefficients are negative by(2.1).

PNT supplies Omega(sqrt X/log X) primes in any fixed interval just
above1.5sqrt X. Taking both factors in, say,
[1.5sqrt X,1.54sqrt X] supplies Omega(X/log^2 X) distinct positive
semiprimes with products in the interior band[2.25X,2.375X]. Their
total positive source mass has that order, whereas all negative primes
in the selected classes have mass O(X^(2/3)/log X). On this complete
fresh support the kernel is between positive constants times1/X.
The positive-positive contribution therefore dominates all negative
prime cross terms and gives aggregate grouped absolute lower bound
Omega(X/log^4 X). No uniformly short prime-interval estimate is used.
This proof does not extend automatically to theta<1/2 (more rough
prime-factor counts with alternating signs) or to step2. It is kept
separate from the frozen implemented guard scope.

5. Complete rational valuation transfers and boundaries
-------------------------------------------------------
For p-free H, t=p^-u, the four exact generating rows are
  R0(z)=(1-z)^2/(1-tz),
  R1(z)=(1-z)(1-t^2 z)/(1-tz),
  R2(z)=(1-t^2 z)/(1-tz),
  Rmu(z)=1-z.
Consequently at every e>=3
  f(p^e H)=t f(p^(e-1)H).
The complete first rows are
  f(pH)=A0(H)+t A1(H)+t^2 A2(H),
  A0=beta(-2nu0+nu1+mu),
  A1=beta(nu0-nu1)-R_u nu2,
  A2=beta nu1+R_u nu2,
and
  f(p^2H)-t f(pH)=beta nu0(H)-beta t^2 nu1(H)-beta t mu(H).
These retain every mode and unit; H can share primes with the OTHER
pair leg. For a delayed row at2 the common tail starts at valuation3;
all other primes keep the displayed tail. Common-prime valuation pairs
(e,f), including both positive, remain in the tensor product transfer.

For any finitely supported kernel sequence Phi(e), exact numerator
transfer gives, for example,
  sum_e nu0(p^e)Phi(e)=sum_(j>=0)t^j
                             [Phi(j)-2Phi(j+1)+Phi(j+2)].
The other rows use (1-E)(1-t^2 E),1-t^2 E and1-E, respectively, with
the appropriate geometric denominator. Kernel values outside support
are ZERO, so these formulas INCLUDE every cutoff jump. On a fresh
singleton Phi0!=0,Phi1=Phi2=0 the second difference is Phi0, with no
small-prime suppression. Dropping those exits defeats the exact identity.

At a completely constant UNTRUNCATED local weight the signed sums are
R0(1)=R1(1)=Rmu(1)=0,R2(1)=1+t. Thus the full local f sum is
-R_u(1+t)nu2(H), not zero. The geometric series justify this statement
for each fixed prime, but the actual prefix kernel is not constant.
An infinite-prime or infinite-cutoff limit requires its own convergence
and boundary theorem; it does not pay the fresh support.

Fractional SHIFTS with integral Euler exponents retain rational rows.
Fractional Euler EXPONENTS generally do not: (1-tz)^(-alpha) has
coefficients (alpha)_e*t^e/e!, and its exact recurrence is
(e+1)c_(e+1)=t(e+alpha)c_e. A pure geometric/rational-tail certificate
for noninteger alpha is false. Exact finite-support truncation and a
proved generalized-binomial tail bound are possible. Fractional finite
differences still retain their cutoff exits and unit coefficient one.
If used as a genuine invertible prefix operator rather than a partition,
each fixed-prime inverse has its declared dilation-norm cost; products
over growing prime sets cannot be assigned a scale-free cost for free.

6. Constructive cofactor-changing cancellation with honest recovery
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Prime-label exchanges at fixed cofactor1 cannot cancel(2.2): all fresh
f(p) have one sign. A detector can instead enumerate small cofactors H
with an exact certificate A0(H)!=0, and source-free prime matches r
comparable to q/H, r>Pplus(H). The generic AST identity is
  P(q,r;H)=f(q)+(R_u/A0(H))*q^-u f(rH)
    =(beta+R_u)q^-2u
       +(R_u/A0(H))*q^-u[r^-u A1(H)+r^-2u A2(H)].
For r>=c q with fixed c>0, this proves |P|<=C_H,c q^-2u. It is an
actual coefficient power gain generated from the complete source AST,
not a fitted weight. For example the cofactor2 has
A0(2)=beta(2-2^-u-2^-2u)>0; this example only proves the nonempty
scope of the generic enumeration, not a preferred search candidate.

No prime-gap estimate is needed for this coefficient identity. A
bounded-multiplicity prime matching between fixed comparable intervals
can be certified by PNT counts, or by exact combinatorial coverage at
finite scales. However the FULL kernel pullback has a compulsory flux:
  f(q)K(q,k)+c_q f(rH)K(rH,k)
   =P(q,r;H)K(q,k)+c_q f(rH)[K(rH,k)-K(q,k)].
Applying it to both legs gives both mixed fluxes and their product.
When both prime sets lie in fixed comparable X-intervals and a common
reference kernel is O(1/X), the doubly canceled block has an absolute
envelope O(X^(1-4u))=O(X^-1/3), with a prime-count saving if desired; the flux
has NOT been paid by this estimate. Prefix indicators similarly retain
1_(rH<=x)-1_(q<=x). Coarse comparability alone does not make these
frontiers small enough. Unmatched primes, reused composite partners and
the remaining coefficient (1-sum c_q)f(n) must be represented exactly.
Crosses between the canceled bank and the retained full source are not
bounded by the paid doubly canceled block alone.

This is a useful enumerable source-specific primitive and a precise
new forcing obligation, but not a complete core bound. A compiler can
certify its polynomial cancellation, exact matching multiplicities,
pair-kernel pullback and coefficient coverage; an all-scale signed
estimate for the joint flux/complement remains open. Assuming an
RH-strength q-free/cofactor bound to pay it would be circular. The
known old-head product must also remain in the recovered step.
