EXACT ORDINARY SOURCE

The strict request is {N,alpha,gamma,tau,primes,p,q}: integerN>=2, canonical
rationals0<alpha<1,gamma>0,0<tau<1, sorted distinct checked actualprimes M
containingp<q. LetR=productM andG_M(t)=product_(l inM)(1-l^(-t)).
The complete source is

 J=sum_(d<=floor(4N/p),gcd(d,R)=1) mu(d)
                 [W_p(d)Phi(p*d)-W_q(d)Phi(q*d)],
 W_l(d)=floor(4N/(l*d))-3floor(2N/(l*d))+2floor(N/(l*d)),
 Phi(a)=1/Gamma(1-alpha)*integral_epsilon^tau
                         G_M(t)*a^(-t)*t^(-alpha-1)dt,
 epsilon=min(tau,N^(-gamma)).

mu is independently rebuilt by trial prime factorization: one distinct prime
factor flips the sign and any square factor setsmu=0. The d=1 unit is kept
whenever its W pair is nonzero. The full horizonfloor(4N/p) is retained,
including terms where q*d>4N and hence W_q=0. Only noncoprime d, zero mu,
and an exact W pair(0,0) are omitted. W_p,W_q are each in{-1,0,1}.

Every nonzero term is exactly one positive-magnitude token:
 P: (a,b)=(+/-1,0), magnitudePhi(pd), sigma=a;
 Q: (a,b)=(0,+/-1), magnitudePhi(qd), sigma=-b;
 D: a=b=+/-1, magnitudePhi(pd)-Phi(qd), sigma=a;
 S: a=-b=+/-1, magnitudePhi(pd)+Phi(qd), sigma=a.
Its kernel_sign is sigma and source sign ismu*sigma. These signs are distinct
fields and neither is inferred from an approximate numerical value.

All Phi are positive on a nonempty band, and Phi(pd)>Phi(qd), sincep<q and
the pointwise integrand factor p^(-t)-q^(-t)>0. They vanish on an exact empty
band. This independently proves token-magnitude nonnegativity, includingD.
Intersecting a numerical enclosure with this proved nonnegative half-line is
valid; interpreting an overlapping interval as evidence of cancellation isnot.

PLAN CERTIFICATE

A plan is exactly {pairs:[[d_i,d_j],...],unmatched:[d,...]}. Every source
token must appear EXACTLY ONCE; no supplied token table or source flag is
trusted. Each pair has oppositeSOURCE signs. Pair order is irrelevant and
cross-profile pairs are allowed. For any such complete plan,

 |J|<=B_plan=sum_pairs |magnitude_i-magnitude_j|
                         +sum_unmatched magnitude_i.

This is the ordinary triangle inequality after exact opposite-sign grouping.
It is not a uniform power bound, nor a proof that the pairing can be cheaply
implemented or its residual cost is small at every N. In particular a source
unit, range exit or unmatched residual cannot disappear from the bound.

INDEPENDENT FINITE INTEGRATION

The empty-band comparison is exact. For gamma=u/v andtau=A/B>0,
 N^u*A^v<=B^v iffN^(-gamma)>=tau.
It is executed only after a prospective powered-bit guard. Exact equality
returns epsilon=tau and all kernel magnitudes are exactlyzero, retaining
the structural token/plan coverage checks.

Otherwiseepsilon>0. ExpandG_M(t)=sum_(e|R)mu(e)e^(-t) with all signs. Each
basea*e>=2, so lambda=log(a*e)>0, and its exact antiderivative is

 integral_epsilon^tau exp(-lambda*t)t^(-alpha-1)dt
 =lambda^alpha*[Gamma(-alpha,lambda*epsilon)
                         -Gamma(-alpha,lambda*tau)].

The upper incomplete gamma formula has negative shape but strictlypositive
real arguments, so it is a legitimate finite integral. Its derivative with
respect to t is-exp(-lambda*t)t^(-alpha-1). The checker evaluates the signed
finite divisor expansion using rigorous Arb arithmetic and multiplies the
complete sum byrgamma(1-alpha). It does not reuse the producer's FLOAT
Gauss-Legendre quadrature or archived kernel values. Outward rational160-bit
dyadic endpoints, not decimal display radii, are authoritative.

Phi andD enclosures may be wide under severe near-zero cancellation; width
never establishes a sign or a bound. The integral sign gates are analytical.
If the chosen precision/capacity cannot give a finite adequate enclosure,
admission may fail or a claim may remain UNRESOLVED; no theorem is inferred
from partial arithmetic. The finite tests also compare against independent
integrated exponentialTaylor remainders and the alpha1/2 erfc formula.

Each private context is a live checker-created object. Functional cache keys
retainN,alpha,gamma,tau,M,a,precision forPhi; ordinarymu and token caches
contain the exact corresponding source arguments. Context dictionaries or
persisted source flags cannot be supplied as verified authority. Across plans
at oneN every neededPhi is constructed once. The full signedJ interval and
L1 magnitude sum are shared. Each opposite-sign pair cost is enclosed by
the complete difference interval followed by its exact interval absolute
value, preserving every component and unmatched magnitude.

PROVED FINITE PROFILE-TRANSPORT DOMINANCE

Each ofP,Q,D,S has magnitude m(d)=integral_positive_band K_profile(t)d^(-t)dt,
with nonnegative K_profile independent ofd. ForD the factor is
G_M(t)*(p^(-t)-q^(-t)); forS it isG_M(t)*(p^(-t)+q^(-t)). Therefore every
profile magnitude is nonnegative and decreases asd increases. This remains
true on an emptyband, where every magnitude iszero.

Fix one profile and sort its tokens by ascendingd, so m_1>=...>=m_l>=0.
Let s_i be their actual source signs and S_j=sum_(i<=j)s_i, m_(l+1)=0.
Every complete opposite-sign pairing with unmatched tokens transported to
the dummy at0 must cross the gap(m_(j+1),m_j) at least|S_j| times. Integrating
that required flux gives the lower cost
 B_opt_profile=sum_j |S_j|*(m_j-m_(j+1)).

The chronological ascending-d stack achieves exactly that flux: its waiting
tokens all have one sign, whose count is|S_j| at the gap. Matching an incoming
opposite sign removesone, otherwiseone isadded. At the end the remaining
waiting tokens go to the zero dummy. A chronological FIFO also attains it;
arbitrary same-profile order neednot. For example signs+,-,+ with magnitudes
4,3,2 have optimum3, whereas reverse processing can pair3with2 and leave4,
cost5. Hence the immutable reference in the certificate is specifically an
ascending-d profile-stack policy, not a reverse scan.

Each recognized subject policy step stays within one profile: scan grouping
byprofile, by(profile,kernel_sign), by(profile,quotient), by(profile,residue),
or by(profile,block); or prime-ratio edges restricted to equalprofiles.
Any complete plan so generated costs at least the per-profile optimum,
regardless of its within-group order, FIFO/stack choice or preliminary
matching. Thus the canonical reference dominates that finite policy cost
for every admittedsourceN, without a sourcevalue or fitted constant. Actual
plan coverage and opposite signs must still be checked independently. The
typed policy admits atmosttwo steps and exact declared group parameters;
group'all' is rejected by this theorem. Cross-profile plans may be cheaper
and are not excluded. This is proof-language pruning, not a growth bound.

evaluate_many additionally encloses profile_optimum using the SAME finite
kernel table and exact source prefix signs. Each successive magnitude gap
is intersected with its analytically proved nonnegative half-line. No extra
Phi values are needed. This is a floor on same-profile-only plan costs,
not a lowerbound on actual|J| and not a floor on cross-profile plans.
