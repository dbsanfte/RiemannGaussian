Root review addendum: one fractional majorant for all interleavings
2026-10-09. Mathematical deduction only; frozen code/certificates/selection
and every numerical response remain unchanged. No new arithmetic evaluation.

The generic certificate's sum of individual ceilings is conservative. A simpler
stronger theorem subsumes the special two-stage majorant in the interleaved
review and also applies to both double-interleaved candidates.

Let d_b be the multiplicative generalized divisor function with local
coefficients (b)_k/k!, for b>=0, taking d_0 to be the convolution identity.
For any real alpha and fixed prime coefficients |chi(p)|<=1,
 |a_alpha,chi(p^k)|<= (|alpha|)_k/k!,
because |alpha+j|<=|alpha|+j for every integer j>=0. Thus
 |a_alpha,chi(n)|<=d_|alpha|(n), with no rounding to an integer order needed.
The positive coefficient identity d_a*d_b=d_(a+b) follows by multiplying
(1-z)^(-a) and (1-z)^(-b) at every prime.

Expand a fixed circuit with arbitrary intervening unit phases into its complete
ordered divisor paths. Taking absolute values removes those phases and bounds
the coefficient of f(n/d) by
 (d_|alpha1|*...*d_|alpham|)(d)=d_B(d), B=sum_i|alpha_i|.
The reverse circuit with every alpha negated has exactly the same majorant.
Divisor-supported dilation therefore proves, uniformly over every finite T,
 ||Circuit f||_(1/2+epsilon,T), ||Circuit^-1 f||_(1/2+epsilon,T)
 <=zeta(1+epsilon)^B ||f||_(1/2+epsilon,T)
 <=(1+1/epsilon)^B ||f||_(1/2+epsilon,T).
Squaring gives exponent2B for squared energy. This argument includes complex
inputs and arbitrary fixed unit phases; it assumes no Mobius cancellation.

For a wholly rational/integer certificate bound one can use ceil(B), instead
of sum_i ceil(|alpha_i|). If B<=1, every coefficient d_B(d)<=1 and the
unsquared norm/inverse bound is simply1+1/epsilon.

This does not retrospectively replace the selected programs, fit constants,

It gives no signed correlation estimate and no advantage over baseline by
