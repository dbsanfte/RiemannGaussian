Next research action
Do not restart033 or continue its vector under a reset ledger. Its residual
does not justify changing the arithmetic grammar by an infeasibility claim.
The global scalar step used here differs from032's per-column curvature
updates. Uneven coordinate curvature is a plausible reason for slower
progress, but it has not been isolated as the cause by this experiment.

A concrete next method is diagonal-metric accelerated projected gradient.
For positive D_j=||A_j||^2, use z_j=max(0,y_j-g_j/(L*D_j)) and test
||A*(z-y)||^2<=L*sum_j D_j*(z_j-y_j)^2. Start L=1 and preserve finite
guards, direct sparse products, the frozen restart/support rules and the
original exact witness admission. This is a general coordinate-balancing
rule, not a choice fitted to a particular selected support. Its weighted
projected-gradient diagnostic has different units and must not be compared
silently with033's unweighted mapping norm. Freeze its policy before any
new source metric or trajectory is computed.

Future allocation can also move work from substantially unused model/audit
reserves into proposal iterations, while retaining one complete450m ledger,
all consumed preparation/testing work and enough exact-verification capacity.
That would require an explicit new allocation and matching helper limits,
not silently reusing the old190m cap or spending an old unused allowance.

If numerical refinement keeps failing, search should address the fixed851
ray restriction through a declared dynamic PSD-direction method rather than
infer anything about the entire cone. Even a full exact separator would
exclude only this augmented finite proof template. The actual objective
still requires a universal operator identity and signed control at all
scales, uniform scalar and initial-storage bounds, a cofinal parameter
family and the ordinary-zeta bridge. Those requirements remain unresolved.
