Main result

There is a cheap EXACT candidate diagnostic that can distinguish an elementary
all-scale scalar route from an unresolved weighted-density route. Require the
multiplier-one child_rowmass norm and prime-power families to cancel POINTWISE
between their all/head/tail descriptor coefficients. Under this optional
stronger gate, every remaining formal scalar except the removable constant
square has an explicit O(X^2) elementary majorant. If the candidate's complete
NONCONSTANT polynomial identity is separately proved for every required scale,
then dropping that nonnegative constant square yields a q-independent innovation
bound Delta(S_R)<=M X^2, and hence E(X)<=X^2[S_R(1)+(M/4)log_2 X] on dyadics.
No finite endpoint, gamma-zero constraint, numerical precision setting, or
closed finite polynomial compiler supplies the missing global identity.
This is a conditional exact deduction, not an all-scale certificate found here.

1. Gradient, curvature and precision audit

Reviewed current primitive_state_free_cone_search.py functions forward(:66),
adjoint(:86), coordinate_gradient(:111), power_operator(:119), merit_gradient
(:123), calibrate(:148), propose(:160), exact_endpoint(:197). Line references
identify functions as read; the active implementation may evolve before freeze.
The sparse forward/adjoint signs and descending/ascending pivot traversal are
correct for x=c+Nf. Upper-triangle matrix gradients correctly double offdiagonal
entries; C receives C_-+4H_-, P receives H_-, with H=P+4C. The exact convex merit
is half squared distance of the square/slack coordinates and C,H to their cones.
It is differentiable even at zero or repeated eigenvalues. Its gradient is
1-Lipschitz in the stacked cone coordinates, so N^T B^T B N is an exact positive
semidefinite curvature MAJORANT operator. It is not the local Hessian, and eight
power iterations estimate its norm without certifying an upper bound.
Armijo backtracking is therefore justified as a proposal heuristic, not as an
exact convergence or infeasibility argument.

The implementation is MIXED precision. Sparse affine/adjoint arithmetic uses
80 decimal digits/269 binary bits, but merit_gradient converts C,H to binary64
and uses numpy.linalg.eigh(:132-141). The computed negative parts are then
converted back to80-digit numbers. Thus the gradient and Armijo merit have
binary64 spectral errors; the whole search is not an80-digit PSD projection.
A small negative eigenvalue relative to a large matrix norm can be hidden by
conversion/eigensolve error. Symmetrizing the numerical negative part is fine
for proposals and does not license an exact PSD claim. Exact endpoint checks
remain the only admissible sign/PSD decisions.

The actual-map calibration checks one known exact parent point and one forward/
adjoint dot pairing at relative1e-40. This is useful, but does not bound the
whole operator norm, all later cancellation, or binary64 PSD-gradient error.
The historical roughly1e32 absolute multiplier propagation is a caution, not
a proven lower condition number. Eighty digits may leave substantial accuracy
at some tested points; no such accuracy can be assumed for every search point.

Useful fixed tests before a costly candidate search: an exact tiny forward/
adjoint dot identity; a2x2 spectral negative-part/offdiagonal derivative test;
a smooth-point directional finite difference; numerical eig reconstruction
and negative/positive-part orthogonality diagnostics. For
C=[[1,a],[a,1]], a>1, its negative eigenvalue is1-a, merit=(a-1)^2/2 and the
upper-triangle offdiagonal derivative is a-1. At a=2 the negative part is
[[-1/2,1/2],[1/2,-1/2]], giving derivative2*(1/2)=1. Test H composition as
well so a missing factor4 cannot pass. For finite differences avoid declaring
failure at nonsmooth eigenvector choices: squared cone distance is smooth,
but naive derivative eigenvectors are not; fixed perturbations and error
scales must be stated. Calibration failures are UNKNOWN, not grammar refutations.

2. Cheap exact scalar gate: descriptor selectors and indices

Use EFFECTIVE signed beta=x_plus-x_minus, not individual nonnegative split
coordinates. Within the unchanged613-relation descriptor list select
family in{'norm','prime_power'}, multiplier='one', weight='child_rowmass',
region in{'all','head','tail'}. Exact zero-based positions are:
    norm:       all9, head21, tail33;
    prime_power:all45,head57,tail69.
Let b_f,a,b_f,h,b_f,t be the corresponding rational weights. Define
    alpha_f=b_f,a+b_f,h, tau_f=b_f,a+b_f,t.
The sufficient pointwise-cancellation gate is
    alpha_norm=tau_norm=alpha_prime_power=tau_prime_power=0.
This requires FOUR rational checks, no new arithmetic source generation.
It removes the entire multiplier-one child_rowmass polynomial combination
for each selected family, not just its formal scalar. Other norm/prime relation
multipliers are retained. This is an OPTIONAL sufficient route and may exclude
valid uniform candidates using more delicate signed density cancellation; it
is not a lossless reduction of all RH routes and is not imposed on the search.
It is stronger than gamma=0, optional and explicitly not a necessity theorem
for every legitimate all-scale proof. If it fails, preserve the candidate and
label its weighted-density budget obligation unresolved, rather than declaring
infeasibility. The detector can report this gate without imposing it on the
current finite search.

Proof: the complete all-region source partitions EXACTLY into head n<=2X and
tail n>2X, using the same rowmass r_X(n)=sum_{t=max(2X,n)}^{4X-1}t and primitive
polynomial. Therefore the effective kernel is alpha_f*r_X(n) on the head and
tau_f*r_X(n) on the tail. The gate cancels the ENTIRE relation polynomials,
including constants, at every X. It is not only asymptotic main-term matching.
The vector(b_all,b_head,b_tail)=(t,-t,-t) is a universally zero column combination.

By comparison gamma=-(12*alpha_norm+(20/3)*tau_norm). The exact pair
(alpha_norm,tau_norm)=(5,-9) has gamma0 but a nonzero head/tail kernel.
The existing elementary squarefree-density error then only gives O(X^(5/2))
for this rowmass remainder, which does not furnish the desired X^(13/6) q6
bound. Prime rowmass magnitudes can be O(X^3/log X), likewise too large.
Neither statement disproves a favorable one-sided cancellation; they identify
why gamma0 alone does not close the scalar proof.

4. Why the q schedule and constant square can be removed conditionally

Source inspection: grouped_arithmetic_templates.py:58 sets D=C*Z_q; :67 makes
the constant-square coefficient Z_q. These are the only occurrences of the
PROGRAM parameter q in the polynomial template body. The growing-prime square
cutoffs in cancellation_arithmetic_templates.py:31 have descriptor-fixed root
orders2,3, not the program q. Factor/divisor/cell/full15/primitive source maps
are likewise independent of program q. Their signed/group/matrix coefficients
must stay fixed acrossX; changing them in response to each scale invalidates
this argument.

Precisely the missing hypothesis is: for ONE fixed exact set of nonnegative
square weights (excluding a0), signed relation weights and PSD C,H, the complete
formal polynomial F_X formed from those source groups and signed storage has
EVERY nonconstant coefficient equal to -Delta(E)(X) for every required dyadicX.
Equivalently
    F_X=B0(X)-Delta(E)(X)
in the polynomial ring of all formal x_n through4X-1. All full source terms,
endpoints, factors, prime powers, complementary cells and floor-cut changes
must be present. This hypothesis is independent of q. Matching at1,2,3,5
establishes it only there. A finite endpoint with exactly zero residual still
supplies no induction or infinite-domain coefficient proof.

At actual arithmetic, every primitive/signed relation vanishes at every scale,
PSD regional squares and nonnegative scalar squares remain nonnegative, and
F_X=Q0(X)+Delta(A_C)(X). Thus, with S_R=E+A_C and R=e_prefix e_prefix^T+C,
    Delta(S_R)(X)=B0(X)-Q0(X)<=B0(X)<=M X^2.
The pure constant square a0*Z_q is nonnegative and can be dropped from this
bound. Keeping it would only give the weaker(a0+M)*Z_q bound. It is not a
required coercivity atom or storage boundary term.

On dyadicX=2^m, W=S_R/X^2 obeys
    W(2X)<=W(X)+M/4,
    E(X)<=S_R(X)<=X^2[S_R(1)+(M/4)*log_2 X].
Here S_R(1)=1+u^T C u, u=(1,1,0,0,1,0,...,0), is finite and >=1.
The canonical quotient increases the original initial cost by u^T Gchild u;
that increase must remain explicit. The logarithm conclusion may be stronger than what RH alone supplies for this
energy, so its hypotheses must not be treated as necessary RH conditions.
The logarithm bound yields every
X^(2+epsilon) energy exponent with finite epsilon-dependent constant, so
cofinal q is automatic under this stronger uniform hypothesis. A single q6
FINITE endpoint does not meet it. The project's ordinary-zeta/energy bridge
still requires its separately recorded analytic premises.

This derivation is compatible with the target contract as a NEW conditional
operator statement. It does not relabel the archived C32,q6 candidate or its
slacks. At finite scales one may algebraically set a0=0 and increase each
ordinary slack by a0*Z_q, preserving the original target and positivity;
the auxiliary square group is empty. For a newly proved uniform critical
certificate choose C_q=M, allq, and slack M*Z_q-B0(X)>=0. For the sharper
innovationMX^2 statement use slack MX^2-B0(X)>=0 instead. Existing snapshots
and admission receipts must remain unchanged, and a newly instantiated proof
must have new coefficient/slack/target bindings.

5. All-scale operator identity remains the decisive open gap

Every signed primitive relation is sound globally: unit, complete factor-
certified norm, actual prime power, squarefree zero, complete inverse divisor
row, coprime product and gcd product vanish for mu at every integer. See
arithmetic_cycle_language.py:relation and grouped_arithmetic_language.py:_relation,
and primitive_state_localizers_uniform_scope.txt. Multiplying/summing them
with the fixed full15 state is legitimate for everyt. This validates each
relation premise; it does NOT prove the chosen sum cancels the target's
nonconstant coefficients universally.

An actionable structural gate before expensive source expansion should return
three separate statuses: exact finite admission; elementary scalar-readiness
with the four checks and explicitM above; global nonconstant identity UNPROVED
unless accompanied by a new independent structural certificate. A global
certificate could normalize source sums into universal monomial coefficient
functions ofX and up to four indices, then certify finite predicate/domain
partitions and sum-reindexing identities, including divisibility/gcd/prime and
changing root/floor boundaries. Higher-degree, linear and unused coefficient
functions must vanish exactly; quadratic coefficients must equal the complete
signed defect kernel. A fixed-X compiler or a handful of transfer scales
cannot certify these universally quantified functions.

Existing closed backends do not fill this gap. cancellation_arithmetic_language
:247 compile_expression expands a given integerX under resource limits.
autonomous_grammar.py:285 polynomial_certificate handles a closed commutative
polynomial sort and explicitly returns unsupported for sums/maps outside it.
signed_arithmetic_certificates.py:146 component_certificate proves one
zero-moment weighted squarefree component and labels full_signed_bound_proved
False; :292 positive_energy_transform_certificate supplies a CONDITIONAL
recovery bridge, not the signed forcing premise. estimate_grammar.py:301-306
lists restricted supplied backends and no new RH-cancellation backend.
The actual weighted inverse-row square closures in cancellation_divisor_coupling
are exact identities, but explicitly not critical-bound certificates.
Unsupported global proof syntax is an open method gap, not a mathematical
refutation of the current grammar. No theorem here excludes all fixed
primitive/full15 templates.

An elementary scalar-ready, globally matched candidate would be a substantive
uniform operator proof rather than a tiny-grid fit. Until both parts are
independently established, finite feasibility remains finite evidence and
all uniform/RH proof flags must stay false.
