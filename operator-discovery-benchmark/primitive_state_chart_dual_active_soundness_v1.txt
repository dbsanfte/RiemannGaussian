Entry and approximate stationarity
With r=b-A*x,w=A^T*r and fixed positive D_j=||A_j||_2^2, a positive inactive
w_j is an exact descent direction along its nonnegative coordinate, since
the derivative of f there is -w_j. Maximizing w_j/sqrt(D_j) chooses the

ties. This is a heuristic choice, not a support-completeness theorem.

Coupled face solve and PCGLS recurrence
For a fixed working face P, solve the unrestricted least-squares problem
in z_P starting at current x_P. Negative internal z values are permitted;
nonnegativity is restored only in the outer commit. Set
  r=b-A_P*z, s=A_P^T*r, h=D_P^-1*s, p=h, gamma=s^T*h.
The normal-equation gradient has sign -s. For q=A_P*p,delta=||q||_2^2,
the declared iteration is
  a=gamma/delta, z_new=z+a*p,
  r_new=b-A_P*z_new, s_new=A_P^T*r_new,
  h_new=D_P^-1*s_new, gamma_new=s_new^T*h_new,
  beta=gamma_new/gamma, p_new=h_new+beta*p.
This is the standard diagonally preconditioned CGLS/normal-equation CG
recurrence in exact arithmetic. Fixed positive D is a valid positive
preconditioner even when the active normal matrix is singular; no dense
Gram matrix, rank cutoff or exact normal-equation solve is required.

Fresh compensated residual recomputation limits incremental drift, but
floating directions need not retain exact conjugacy or orthogonality. The
cap min(128,2*|P|) licenses no exact face optimum or convergence theorem.
gamma0 is a floating zero-normal-residual diagnostic. delta0 with gamma>0
is a diagnostic Krylov breakdown; it is not a certificate about rank or
infeasibility. A finite current internal z can still be feasibly committed
after breakdown, followed by normal termination and full final diagnostics.

Gamma/products, division ratios, p/z updates, matrix/transpose products,
norms/squares and beta must be finite. gamma is nonnegative; a and beta
use strictlypositive denominators whenever computed. Stable math.fsum and
math.hypot are not interval enclosures. Underflowed gamma/delta or rounded
zero values remain diagnostics, never arithmetic conclusions. A nonfinite,
resource or deadline failure interrupts the trajectory and clears selection.

Nonnegative boundary commit
If every unconstrained z_j on P is strictlypositive, accept it directly.
Otherwise for each z_j<=0 form x_j/(x_j-z_j), with bothzero interpreted
aszero. For x_j>=0 and z_j<=0, every exact ratio lies in[0,1]. The minimum
alpha therefore makes x+alpha*(z-x) nonnegative on the whole face and hits
at least one boundary coordinate. Explicitly zero every ratio-minimizer,
including ties, then keep every remaining strictlypositive coordinate.
Outside-face coordinates stayzero. This is the usual boundary interpolation
argument in exact arithmetic; floating interpolation/clamping is heuristic.

Check denominators, ratios, differences, products and candidate values before
clipping. Any finite negative rounded candidate can be clamped tozero to
preserve stored nonnegativity, without proving exact interpolation or an
objective decrease. Additional rounded zeros may disappear, but positive
stored entries must never be thresholded. A boundary event with alpha=1
still removes exact-zero candidate positions and sets dampedTrue; survivor
refinement depends on removal/damping, not merely on alpha<1. A newly
entered zero coordinate with nonpositive candidate can cause alpha0 and
drop immediately. Empty surviving support is a valid nonnegative state.

One boundary correction is one outer commit, not an authorization for an
unmetered inner face solve. Compute fresh full residual, support, metrics
and state before appending a complete checkpoint and marking the face
complete. A partial commit is not an accepted face. Approximate face solves
and finite clamping may stall or revisit a coordinate; the1000-face cap is
a bounded search rule, not a finite-termination theorem.

Full-source guards and face-specific charges
For full rows m,columns n,nonzeros z, the frozen proxies are
  setup=12z+64(m+n)+65536,
  gradient=8z+32(m+n)+512,
  face_setup_full=8z+48(m+n)+1024,
  cg_step_full=12z+64(m+n)+1024,
  commit=8z+48(m+n)+1024,
  final=12z+64(m+n)+2048,
  cg_limit_full=min(128,2n).
iteration_reserve is gradient+face_setup_full+cg_limit_full*cg_step_full+
commit; minimum_attempt adds setup and final. A working face with k columns
and z_P nonzeros instead charges setup_P=8z_P+48(m+k)+1024 and
cg_step_P=12z_P+64(m+k)+1024, at most min(128,2k) times.
These are declared sparse work proxies, not hardware instruction counts.

Before any setup require the complete minimum_attempt to fit. Before every
global gradient reserve one full-source worst-case face solve, commit and
the still-unspent final diagnostic. Charge only actual blocks before work,
including charged attempts interrupted by deadline or nonfinite failures.
Live/deadline checks use charge(0). Exact reported work is

  +sum_faces(setupbool_P*setup_P+cg_blocks_P*cg_step_P+commitbool_P*commit)
  +finalbool*final.
