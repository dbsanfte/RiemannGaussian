SIGNED ENERGY DEFECT: THE RH TARGET AND A CONTROLLED COMMON-FACTOR PART

This is a targeted bound-decomposition audit. The common-factor construction
and squarefree density argument are supplied analytic methods, not an operator
selected without mathematical assumptions. All numerical outcomes stay finite.
The FULL RH inequality remains unproved; no power saving is credited to it.
The frozen earlier sources are unchanged. Only ordinary-zeta continuation,
its Euler identity on Re(s)>1 and functional symmetry are needed for recovery.
The arithmetic density proof below is elementary, with no PNT, sieve estimate,
zero-density theorem, prime oracle or imported uniform sign-cancellation bound.

1. THE TARGET, INCLUDING ITS QUANTIFIERS.
Let M(x)=sum_(n<=x)mu(n), x>=1, with its step-function convention, and
A(X)=Integral_X^(2X) M(t)^2 dt. For integer X, A is an exact integer
sum of M(n)^2, X<=n<2X. Put Delta(X)=A(2X)-4*A(X).

The RH-sufficient target is:
for EVERY epsilon>0 there exists finite B_epsilon>=0 such that
for EVERY dyadic X>=1, Delta(X)<=B_epsilon*X^(2+epsilon).
A cofinal sequence epsilon_j>0 tending to zero suffices. Constants may
depend arbitrarily on j, but NOT on X. Only an UPPER bound on the signed
defect is required; negative defects do not have to be small.

Indeed, with X=2^j, induction gives
A(2^j)<=4^j*A(1)+B_epsilon*4^(j-1)*
                 (2^(epsilon*j)-1)/(2^epsilon-1).
Consequently A(X)=O_epsilon(X^(2+epsilon)). Holder on each dyadic block
implies absolute Mellin convergence for Re(s)>1/2+epsilon/2. The identity
Integral_1^infinity M(x)*x^(-s-1)dx=1/(s*zeta(s)), initially Re(s)>1,
then rules out ordinary zeros in that half-plane. Letting epsilon tend to
zero and using ordinary functional symmetry proves RH. This covers every
off-critical multiplicity and height. A finite list of epsilons is insufficient.

At epsilon=0 the same recurrence yields A(X)=O(X^2*log(eX)). For the
UNFILTERED M this is stronger than necessary: Holder and a dyadic sum give
1/zeta(1/2+h+i*t)=O_t(h^(-3/2)) as h->0+, so a critical-line zero of
integer multiplicity >=2 would be impossible. Thus the fixed critical
recurrence would also imply simple critical zeros. This is NOT an assumed
fact about actual zeros and NOT an obstruction to its truth. The cofinal
positive-epsilon target avoids requiring that extra statement. The earlier
fixed-critical conditional recurrence theorem remains valid and unchanged.

2. THE EXACT KERNEL, DIAGONAL AND OFF-DIAGONAL.
K_X(n,m)=max(0,2X-max(X,n,m)). This equals the integral on [X,2X]
of 1_(n<=t)*1_(m<=t). Therefore
A(X)=D(X)+C(X),
D(X)=sum_n mu(n)^2*K_X(n,n),
C(X)=2*sum_(n<m)mu(n)*mu(m)*K_X(n,m).
Let W_X(m)=K_(2X)(m,m)-4*K_X(m,m). The exact defect is
Delta(X)=sum_n mu(n)^2*W_X(n)
             +2*sum_(n<m)mu(n)*mu(m)*W_X(m).
All contributing indices are at most 4X, with the final endpoint weight zero.
No positivity of W or of the off-diagonal sum is asserted.

3. A DENSITY BOUND AT EVERY SCALE AND MODULUS.
For k>=1 and y>=0 let Q_k(y)=sum_(g<=y, gcd(g,k)=1)mu(g)^2.
Write tau_rad(k)=2^omega(k), including tau_rad(1)=1. Then
c_k=(1/zeta(2))*product_(p|k) p/(p+1), and
|Q_k(y)-c_k*y| <=(tau_rad(k)+2)*sqrt(y).
For y<1, Q_k=0 and c_k<=1, giving the same bound. For y>=1,
mu(g)^2=sum_(d^2|g)mu(d), with gcd(d,k)=1 when g is coprime to k.
The inner coprime count is phi(k)*Y/k plus an error at most tau_rad(k),
by finite divisor inclusion-exclusion. There are at most sqrt(y) outer d.
Replacing the main d sum by its infinite absolutely convergent value has
error at most 2*sqrt(y); its Euler product is exactly c_k. This proves
the bound uniformly in k and y without any prime-distribution assumption.
The producer can evaluate c_k from divisor inverse coefficients alone:
c_k=[sum_(d|k)mu(d)/d]/[zeta(2)*sum_(d|k)mu(d)/d^2].

In particular, Q_1(t)=t/zeta(2)+O(3*sqrt(t)). Put
J0=(2/3)*(2^(3/2)-1). The full diagonal obeys
|D(X)-(3/(2*zeta(2)))*X^2|<=3*J0*X^(3/2),
and hence
|D(2X)-4*D(X)|<=3*J0*(2^(3/2)+4)*X^(3/2)
for EVERY real X>=1. The leading X^2 term cancels exactly. This is a
proved component bound, not a proof of the full energy defect.

4. PRIMITIVE COMMON-FACTOR PROJECTION.
For every n<m write n=g*a, m=g*r with gcd(a,r)=1 and a<r. Actual
arithmetic satisfies
mu(g*a)*mu(g*r)=mu(a)*mu(r)*mu(g)^2*1_(gcd(g,a*r)=1).
Zero factors are retained. A divisor or coprimality relation is not a new
independent distribution theorem. For an integer B>=1, let C_B(X) retain
exactly the pairs with r=max(n,m)/gcd(n,m)<=B. Then
C_B(X)=2*sum_(1<=a<r<=B, gcd(a,r)=1)mu(a)*mu(r)*
                                      Integral_X^(2X)Q_(a*r)(t/r)dt.
Define its continuous leading coefficient
L_B=3*sum_(a<r<=B, gcd(a,r)=1)mu(a)*mu(r)*c_(a*r)/r,
and the explicitly positive cost
S_B=sum_(a<r<=B, gcd(a,r)=1)|mu(a)*mu(r)|*
                                      (tau_rad(a*r)+2)/sqrt(r).
The every-scale density estimate proves
|C_B(X)-L_B*X^2|<=2*J0*S_B*X^(3/2),
|C_B(2X)-4*C_B(X)|<=2*J0*(2^(3/2)+4)*S_B*X^(3/2).

Since tau_rad(a*r)<=tau(a)*tau(r), and sum_(n<=B)tau(n)<=B*(1+log B),
S_B<=3*B^(3/2)*(1+log B)^2. Thus with
C0=6*J0*(2^(3/2)+4),
|C_B(2X)-4*C_B(X)|<=C0*X^(3/2)*B^(3/2)*(1+log B)^2.
This remains true when B is chosen separately at each X, provided the
SAME B is used in BOTH terms of that scale's defect. Using B(2X) for
the first term and B(X) for the second adds uncancelled leading terms.
Changing the cutoff silently is not an admitted rewrite.

For any rational 0<=beta<=1, B=floor(X^beta), the component power is
gamma(beta)=3/2+3*beta/2, with a log^2(eX) loss. Any beta with gamma<=2
therefore controls that component at every cofinal positive-epsilon RH
scale. A frozen rational grammar may select the largest such coverage
automatically; no expected cutoff is passed to the selector. For example,
the power balance permits cube-root-growing primitive cutoffs. This is
squarefree-density information applied to actual signed pairs; it does
not establish a new prime-sign-cancellation estimate.

5. THE REMAINING SUM IS NOT DROPPED.
Define R_B(X)=C(X)-C_B(X), retaining EVERY primitive pair with r>B.
For each scale and chosen shared B,
Delta(X)=[D(2X)-4D(X)]+[C_B(2X)-4C_B(X)]
                              +[R_B(2X)-4R_B(X)].
For an RH-admissible cutoff power, the first two brackets have proved
O_epsilon(X^(2+epsilon)) bounds. The EXACT remaining proof obligation is
for every cofinal epsilon>0, a finite K_epsilon independent of X bounds
R_(B(X))(2X)-4*R_(B(X))(X) from ABOVE by K_epsilon*X^(2+epsilon)
at EVERY dyadic X. The shared parameter B(X) stays fixed within each pair.
Proving this would prove the full target. It has NOT been proved here.

Full expansion has about X^2 pair terms, each kernel weight O(X).
Taking their absolute values gives power THREE, not the required power two.
Common-factor subfamilies are sparse, so counting them alone gives better
powers; it does not control the dense residual. A positive-coefficient
control has full Delta of order X^3 while its fixed-B projection still has
power two and a cancelled leading X^2 term. It explicitly disproves any
inference from a controlled projection to a controlled full signed sum.

6. EXPERIMENTAL HONESTY.
The protocol, proof, code, cutoff grammar, training and validation scales,
resource ceilings and selection rule are frozen before outcomes. The target
is unfiltered actual Mertens energy: this is a recovering calibration, not
a historically new operator. Every common-factor cutoff is retained, with
resource exhaustion recorded as missing. Final dyadic scales and generic
geometry cases are generated after selection is sealed and withheld from
the worker. Independent prime arithmetic checks the producer's full divisor
inverse prefix, then primitive factor identities, exact whole energies,
diagonal defects, signed projections, density costs and the residual.
Selected finite residual fits are conjectural, never global certificates.
No source outcome is mutated and no failed pilot is reused as fresh data.
