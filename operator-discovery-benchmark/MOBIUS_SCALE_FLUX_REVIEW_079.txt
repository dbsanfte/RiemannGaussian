INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL/DESIGN REVIEW079

Review scope
============
This new file reviews the agreed079 shared-index flux/covariance design.
It preserves frozen075-078 files, performs no scientific source query or
candidate scoring, and is not a report of a completed079 run. Static code
and actual-result review can be added only when root supplies that schema
and requests those checks. The main agreed design keeps preregistered
power cutoffs and searches localized covariance leaves plus conditional
PSD reductions, rather than another unrestricted auxiliary-energy fit.

1. What the new experiment must construct exactly
================================================
Let h=floor(u/2). Work on the shared PHYSICAL integer n index domain,
not on a rescaled real index and not by pairing n at u with2n at h.
Refine the parent and half folded coefficient intervals at every endpoint,
rough cutoff, least-prime cutoff and terminal category boundary. A canonical
cell records its integer half-open interval, gcd(n,Q)=1 exclusion, squarefree
domain, parent category, half category or outside-half-support, and BOTH
coefficient/token polynomials. Unit status must be explicit. Empty cells
and repeated cutoffs have zero contribution. Boundaries use the exact
ceil/floor rules of the source; never discard a short endpoint cell.

The cell's signed sum is sum mu(n) over its exact domain, with the two
coefficient polynomials attached separately. From these shared cells derive
V(u),V(h),D=V(u)-V(h) by exact linear maps. A parent coefficient token is
never rebound to the half's count. Nevertheless the half state uses its
own independently admitted counts/tokens. Recombination must match both
saved076 residual polynomials and both independent078 feature partitions.
The exact transport identity is valuable even if every subsequent growth
leaf fails. It must receive a distinct verified-identity status.

Bare Young on D is an honest conditional reduction, but d.D=R(u)-R(h)
contains the difficult source residual. A leaf that bounds the entire
normalized D energy may merely repackage the original missing theorem.
The proposed no-total-D/no-original-drift leaf gate is therefore sensible.
It is a syntactic nontriviality gate, not a proof that arbitrary combinations
of local leaves are logically weaker than the target. Export their exact
combined reduction so reviewers can see whether a new arithmetic fact
has actually been supplied.

2. A concrete local covariance grammar
=====================================
Use normalized shared cells and child channels as a vector x. Generate
small-support rational quadratic forms A_j: one-cell squares, two-cell
signed products/contrasts, and a cell paired with one explicitly named
half channel. Preserve signs; A_j need not be PSD when it is a signed
upper-correlation leaf. Exclude full all-ones source totals, full D totals,
and the original drift polynomial as leaf definitions. Coefficient/ray
budgets and support-size limits must be part of the frozen grammar.

For each leaf state its own proposed price c_j and scale normalization,
 x^T A_j x<=c_j.
Finite discovery can genuinely search which LOCAL correlations persist;
exact validation can refute a particular signed leaf without rejecting
the residual. Price selection is still finite arithmetic discovery. A
constant fitted to the same full drift is not a uniform primitive.

For a transport z_u=T z_h+W, define the exact drift matrix on
x=(z_h,W), with z=V/u^(q/2), by
 J=[T^T M_u T-tau M_h, T^T M_u;
    M_u T,            M_u],
 M_u=dd^T+sum lambda*u^(-2eta)*aa^T.
If nonnegative rational multipliers alpha_j obey
 sum alpha_j A_j-J >=0 in PSD order,
then the local leaves imply F(u)-tau F(h)<=sum alpha_j c_j.
This is a generic independently verifiable proof rule. PSD checks may use
exact rational matrices for a finite row, or a parameterized symbolic
certificate valid throughout a stated uniform domain. Floating eigenvalues
and PSD on finitely sampled u are not a uniform certificate. Fractional
powers and inherited count tokens require valid enclosures or exact
symbolic parameter inequalities, including every allowed count jump.

An equivalent Schur form is
 [tau M_h-T^T M_u T, -T^T M_u;
  -M_u T,           K-M_u] >=0,
which yields drift<=W^T K W. The remaining leaf must be a separately
specified arithmetic estimate, not drift<=C under another name.

Useful finite feedback separates: identity failure; local leaf failure;
conditional PSD failure; interval ambiguity; resource/grammar failure;
and success on a known finite set. A prospective resource rejection is
not a mathematical refutation. Every failed leaf should name its physical
cells/tokens and return an exact lower bound above its own fixed price.
An exact Farkas dual for a finite restricted multiplier grammar can prove
that grammar infeasible on those rows; it cannot prove no arithmetic
certificate exists outside the grammar.

3. Analytic signatures must constrain the auxiliary grammar
=========================================================
Frozen078 established that terminal prime V1 carries a nonzero u/logu
main term for fixed constant-coefficient kernels with nonzero integrated
mass and fixed finite P. A positive square with exponent gap2-2eta-q>0
is an impossible normalized-energy direction unless its full signed ray
annihilates the growing nuisance mode. This is a method obstruction,
not a source residual refutation.

For the usual selected gamma>1/2, terminal r>=2 vanish, but least-prime
bins retain composites. Hence prime/composite cancellation lives in a
direction N with d.N=0. A semidefinite energy dominating dd^T CAN vanish
on such a nuisance direction; a positive isolated prime square cannot.
Searching transport in this quotient is meaningful. Empirically fitting
N does not prove it uniform, and a fixed-width rational approximation to
an irrational nuisance coefficient does not exactly annihilate a growing
mode. Either derive the signature, represent it with honest exact/error
tokens, or normalize unresolved growing modes sufficiently. The threshold
eta>=(2-q)/2 escapes this particular power-growth gate but proves no drift.

There is a particularly concrete bounded-factor primitive. Set
alpha=2gamma/3, with gamma in(1/2,3/4), so alpha in(1/3,1/2).
The last least-prime bin has p in(H^alpha,H^gamma], with exact ceils.
A squarefree n in that bin has at most TWO factors: three primes each
above H^alpha would exceed H. Thus the bin is exactly its negative
one-prime portion plus a positive DISTINCT-semiprime sum p<q,pq<=H.
Parent interval coefficients and tokens remain attached to pq. Prime-square
rows contribute zero; p=q must not be counted. This is a new inspectable
arithmetic primitive rather than the total residual under a new label.

For a fixed constant prefix kernel, folded mass m_P, PNT gives the last
bin leading signature
 V9(u)=m_P log((1-alpha)/alpha)*u/logu+o(u/logu),
whereas V1(u)=-m_P*u/logu+o(u/logu).
For completeness, the semiprime sum at endpoint X~c u is
 sum_{H^alpha<p<=sqrt(X)} [pi(X/p)-pi(p)],
up to exact endpoint/unit exclusions. Its leading term follows by PNT
and partial summation from
 X/logX * integral_alpha^(1/2) dt/[t(1-t)]
 =X/logX * log((1-alpha)/alpha).
PNT is uniform on the compact power ranges because all arguments tend
to infinity; the subtractive sum pi(p) is O(X/log^2X), and the upper
H^gamma cutoff exceeds sqrt(X) eventually. Removing the one-prime bin
costs O(H^gamma), lower order. Finite fixed kernel endpoints then combine
with mass m_P. This derivation imports only ordinary PNT, cited in078
postreview from Zagier's primary author-hosted paper.

The contrast V9+kappa V1 with kappa=log((1-alpha)/alpha) cancels the
leading term. It does NOT imply square-root scale: its remainder may
still be u/log^2u or larger than every desired subpower. This exact
prime/semiprime decomposition is useful for generating local signed flux
leaves and for rejecting inappropriate growing rays. It is not an
automatic source bound. Variable prime-count-token kernels require a
separate moving-coefficient analysis; the fixed-kernel mass theorem cannot
silently be applied to them.

4. Genuine multiplicative cross-term primitives and their limit
==============================================================
For any prime p and p not dividing m,
 mu(pm)=-mu(m), including nonsquarefree m where both sides vanish.
Therefore for any nonnegative interval weight w,
 sum_{m in I,p not dividing m} w(m)[mu(pm)+mu(m)]^2=0.
Expansion gives an EXACT negative prime/composite cross moment paired
with diagonal squarefree terms. This identity preserves all signs and
can be admitted independently of any observed signed sum. Domain
intersection with both physical cells, p-coprimality, transformed floors
and any inherited tokens must be retained. If coefficient products change
sign, their contributions change sign too; do not select only helpful
cross terms and drop the others.

A conditional SOS experiment can multiply such zero identities by
nonnegative rational cell weights and search a PSD remainder dominating
the dyadic drift kernel. Diagonal squarefree counts have separate exact
or conservative prices. A uniform advancement must produce a generated
prime-range/multiplier rule with all count and PSD obligations paid.
Enumerating enough factor identities to hard-code every finite mu(n)
is just a finite evaluation, not a growth theorem.

With ONLY fixed finite P pairings, squareful-zero constraints, bounded
signs and prime/unit values, a robust subquadratic certificate is blocked.
After folding, P-coprime squarefree COMPOSITE core variables remain free
in the relaxation. A nonzero outer coefficient band contains linearly
many such cores, whereas prime values contribute only o(u). Their relaxed
signs can align with the coefficient. Positive linear squarefree density
follows elementarily by finite prime inclusion-exclusion and the convergent
tail sum1/p^2; the primitive constraints do not give cancellation there.
Thus fixed-P universal SOS/box domination can disguise the old077 l1
obstruction. The useful next ingredient is a parameterized prime-factor
pairing rule or an explicitly unpaid signed rough-composite covariance,
not simply another fixed small prime.

5. Moving cutoff recommendation
===============================
Keep079's preregistered fixed-power cutoff set while testing the NEW
shared-cell/local-covariance grammar. This isolates whether the proof
reduction improves, instead of changing both basis and asymptotic regime.
A later constructive variation can allow exact
 y=ceil(H^alpha*ell(H)^beta), ell(H)=bit_length(H),
with rational alpha,beta under explicit positive-power/reciprocal and bit
gates. Its dyadic bit-length jumps are additional physical boundary cells.
Degree-free terminal parity aggregation (unit; even omega>=2; odd omega)
is exact and removes an artificial factor-count resource gate. It does
not pay a uniform cancellation leaf.

For y~log^A H, v=logH/logy grows without bound. A generalized signed
Buchstab/Dickman main-term expression suggested by root cannot be used
outside its proved uniform v/error regime. A small main term can coexist
with an unacceptably large error. Moving y alone also does not remove the
pure-prime u/logu obstruction whenever the removed prefix is lower order;
only signed parity/composite coupling can do that. This review does not
import or certify a uniform generalized Buchstab theorem.

7. Audit of the concrete proper-ray cover pilot
==============================================
Root's concrete pilot uses only the constant fixed-dilation sources
q1_p16 andq1_p04,17 old rational gammas, and base6300. Its local leaf is
 S_a(u)^2 <= kappa_a*u^(q-1)*Q_a(u),
 S_a=a.D,
 Q_a=sum_{n squarefree,gcd(n,Q)=1}
       [a_parentcategory*f_u(n)-a_halfcategory*f_h(n)]^2.
Outside a horizon the corresponding coefficient is zero. This is the
shared-index DIAGONAL Gram, not S_a^2, not a product of independent
cofactor sums, and not an observed signed residual squared disguised as
Q_a. Its exact mixed-token expansion must use the union of token bindings.

The unconditional coefficient price is valid. For the fixed folded step
kernel f, put I=integral f(t)^2 dt and E=sum f(t)^2 over its rational
breakpoint bands. Each band's integer count differs from u times its
width by less than1. Hence sum_n f_u(n)^2 <=Iu+E for EVERY integer u>0.
With K=I+E/6300 this is <=Ku when u>=6300. Exclusions/squarefree domain
only reduce the nonnegative sum. For h=floor(u/2)>=6300,
 Q_a <=2 max_i|a_i|^2 (||f_u||_2^2+||f_h||_2^2)
     <=3K max_i|a_i|^2 u.
No Mobius sign estimate is used in this coefficient norm theorem.

The folded unit coordinate vanishes for every v>=6300: all retained
prefix divisors are <=6300, so all unit indicators are1 and their total
weight is0. The terminal r coordinate vanishes if r*gamma>=1, since r
primes strictly above ceil(H^gamma) have product>H. Thus the advertised
active-coordinate target is exact, subject to the independent y^7>=H
degree gate and h>=6300. The proposed discovery/validation scales satisfy
the half-base domain; these are distinct from a complete all-integer base.

If proper rays of support1or2 admit the EXACT rational cover
 d_active=sum_a t_a a,
then |d.D|<=sum|t_a||S_a| and the uniform leaves would imply
 (R(u)-R(h))^2
 <=[sum_a |t_a|sqrt(kappa_a*3K*max_i|a_i|^2)]^2*u^q.
This reduction is sound. All square-root prices need positive rational
upper enclosures, exact cover reconstruction must survive rationalization,
and all local leaves remain independent UNPAID uniform obligations.
Float LP feasibility or small float price is not an exact cover theorem.

The two-actual-cell determinant is a useful proper-leaf admission:
proper(cell1)*total(cell2)-proper(cell2)*total(cell1) excludes0 under
the admitted tokens. It proves the leaf kernel is not a scalar copy of
the total flux on that physical row. A support1or2 label alone cannot
prove this, since source degeneracy can collapse a ray. The determinant
does not prove a uniform cancellation estimate or independence of the
entire collection from the final target. Source/cell/token admissions must
precede this check; fabricated positive cells cannot serve as witnesses.

8. Strong structural feedback for failed proper flux leaves
===========================================================
For these fixed kernels with nonzero folded mass m_P and fixed0<gamma<1,
the primary PNT lemma yields
 D1(u)=V1(u)-V1(h)=-m_P*u/(2logu)+o(u/logu).
Since Q1<=3Ku, any fixed kappa in the pure-prime leaf would require
positive const*u^2/log^2u <=kappa*3K*u^q. This is impossible for EVERY
q<2. It is an all-scale local-leaf obstruction independent of its finite
calibration, and rejects that leaf, not the arithmetic residual.

For gamma>1/2 and alpha=2gamma/3 in(1/3,1/2), a ray supported on terminal
prime and last bin has flux main coefficient
 (m_P/2)*[-a1+a9 log((1-alpha)/alpha)]*u/logu.
If an exact log enclosure proves the bracket nonzero, that leaf also
cannot hold with any fixed kappa,q<2. Generated slopes +/-1,+/-2,+/-1/2
can be checked by rational log enclosures, without asserting a general
transcendence theorem. A zero or unresolved main coefficient supplies no
uniform leaf admission. Rays involving the other bins require their own
factor-count/correlation analysis and must not inherit this two-factor
formula outside its domain.

If all selected covers fail, the strongest finite feedback is a sparse
leaf-refutation set: identify which proper rays actually fail, preserve
their exact S/Q and physical-cell witnesses, and show which exact cover
constraints cannot be met after removing those frozen failed leaves.
An exact separating dual can certify that particular surviving-ray cover
grammar infeasible. It cannot exclude new signed multi-bin leaves, a
different cutoff, or a different proof rule. Do not merely recalibrate
kappa upward, and do not claim all-scale impossibility from finite failure.
Analytic pure-prime/main-coefficient obstructions can additionally prune
entire leaf families uniformly when their stated fixed-kernel gates pass.
This exposes the actual next target: a prime-bearing MULTI-bin signed
flux correlation that cancels the growing prime/composite mode and has
a separately payable remainder, rather than a larger constant on a
provably impossible isolated prime flux.

11. Complete residual base paid without new arithmetic
=====================================================
Root proposed a useful unconditional improvement for these fixed kernels.
For the already folded prefix coefficients b_d put A=sum|b_d|/d. Exact
folding and the coprime domain give for EVERY positive integer v
 |R(v)|<=sum_d |b_d|floor(v/d)<=A v.
Hence F(v)=R(v)^2/v^q <=A^2*v^(2-q)<=A^2*(2base)
for1<=v<2base and1<=q<2. This is a complete integer base price, not a
sparse observed-source base and not a new arithmetic query. It includes
small scales where the unit coordinate need not vanish.

If all proper leaves are proved uniformly and their exact cover flux
price is J, Young gives C<=3J for tau3/4 and C<=2J for tau1. Together
with this paid base, the contractive case yields
 F(u)<=max(A^2*(2base),12J) for every positive integer u.
The tau1 case yields the paid base plus2J times the finite dyadic descent
depth, thus O(logu) normalized growth. No finite proper-leaf calibration
proves either conclusion. This closes the complete R-base obligation
for the specific constant-kernel pilot; it does not prove its uniform
covariances, its original source transfer, a full parameter family, or RH.
