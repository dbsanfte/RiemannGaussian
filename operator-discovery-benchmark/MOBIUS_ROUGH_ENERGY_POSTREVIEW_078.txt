The floating LP proposes formulas only. Exact lambda rounding is downward;
the rational weighted coefficient budget is restored by rescaling if
needed. Since changing lambda can change drift in either direction, the
subsequent exact upward recalibration of C is essential and is performed.
Each selected formula and its C is written before validation features are
evaluated; its bytes are checked afterward. A separately calibrated
zero-auxiliary baseline uses the same gamma,q,tau and source. Interval drift
certification uses parent upper minus tau times child lower; refutation
uses parent lower minus tau times child upper. Thus the failed inequalities
below have the correct enclosure direction, including fractional q/eta.

Selection uses a floating score across gamma, so it need not select the
best exact-calibrated score. This limits search optimality; it does not
invalidate the selected formula's exact finite checks. The report is honest
about complete base, uniform drift, residual/source/head/core and RH being

energy bound.

Two pre-freeze accounting cautions were sent to root. The displayed operation

3. A uniform obstruction to terminal-prime auxiliary squares
===========================================================
Unlike a finite witness, the following is an all-scale obstruction to a
specified auxiliary-energy grammar. Its only imported arithmetic theorem
is the unconditional prime number theorem pi(x)~x/log x. Primary reference:
Don Zagier, Newman's Short Proof of the Prime Number Theorem, American
Mathematical Monthly104(1997),705-708; the theorem is stated on705 and
deduced on707. Author-hosted primary PDF:
https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.2307/2975232/fulltext.pdf
All kernel/folding conclusions below are deductions from that theorem.

Let a fixed finite constant rational kernel be
 c_u(n)=sum_d b_d 1_{1<=n<=floor(u/d)},
with fixed positive integer divisors after merging duplicates. More
generally fixed rational endpoints work identically. Define its mass
 m=sum_d b_d/d.
For fixed finite distinct primes P, Q=product(P), let
 f_P,u(n)=sum_{e|Q} mu(e)c_u(en).
The exact floor identity floor(floor(u/d)/e)=floor(u/(de)) shows this is
again a fixed prefix kernel. Its mass is
 m_P=m sum_{e|Q}mu(e)/e=m delta_P,
 delta_P=product_{p in P}(1-1/p)>0.
No observed Mobius value enters this mass calculation.

For each fixed positive endpoint v, PNT gives
 pi(vu)=vu/log u+o(u/log u).
Finite linear combination therefore gives
 sum_{prime p} f_P,u(p)=m_P u/log u+o(u/log u).
Using < versus <= at a prime endpoint changes only a bounded number of
terms. Removing the finitely many primes in P changes only O(1).
If H(u)=h u+O(1), h>0, and y=ceil(H(u)^gamma) with FIXED0<gamma<1,
then the removed prime sum at p<=y is O(y)=o(u/log u), since coefficients
are uniformly bounded. Consequently the actual terminal-prime coordinate is
 V1(u)=-m_P u/log u+o(u/log u).
This conclusion holds for every0<gamma<1; gamma>1/2 is not needed for the
pure-prime coordinate. The fixed gamma, fixed P, fixed coefficient kernel
and nonzero mass assumptions matter.

For lambda>0 and a pure prime ray with eta>=0,
 E(u)/u^q >=lambda*u^(-2eta-q)*V1(u)^2
 ~lambda*m_P^2*u^(2-2eta-q)/(log u)^2.
If2-2eta-q>0, this lower bound grows faster than log u. Any eventual
all-integer normalized recurrence F(u)<=tau F(floor(u/2))+C, with a finite
complete base and fixed C, implies bounded F for tau<1 and O(log u) for
tau1. Hence such a recurrence is impossible for this energy, for EVERY C
and eventual threshold. This is a proved energy-method obstruction, not
an assumption of small R or an RH implication.

For the source G, m=1/8-1/10-1/4+1/5=-1/40. For direct3 residual above,
 m=(-1/40)*(1+3547/(8192*3))=-28123/983040.
For P={2,3,5,7}, delta_P=8/35 and m_P=-28123/4300800.
The strongest discovery energy contains the positive pure-prime eta0 ray,
so q1 gives growth at least a positive multiple of u/log^2u. The selected
direct3 tau1/2 energy likewise contains a pure-prime eta1/4 ray with
lambda314487429/16777216; q1 gives u^(1/2)/log^2u. Both are impossible
uniform normalized recurrences irrespective of their finite calibrations.
For arbitrary q the exact scanner condition remains2-2eta>q.

The executable certificate can contain: merged rational prefix kernel,
fixed P, exact mass and delta, fixed gamma domain, positive ray weight,
exact rational exponent gap, and identity of the pure-prime coordinate.
An independent checker can verify all algebraic gates; PNT remains the
named imported theorem. Gate failure means UNRESOLVED by this obstruction,
never VERIFIED_DRIFT. Mass0, exponent gap<=0, moving kernels/coefficients,
moving P, or gamma1 need different reasoning. In particular076 prime-block
count-token predictors cannot automatically be treated as constant kernels.

4. A stronger cancellation-aware ray obstruction
===============================================
For fixed gamma>=1/2 and gamma<1, y>=sqrt(H) eventually (indeed exactly
when H>=1 in the equal case). Products of two primes strictly greater than
y exceed H. Thus terminal layers r>=2 vanish. V0=f_P,u(1)=O(1), and the
three least-prime bins sum R-V0-V1.

Suppose a ray has equal coefficients b on all three bins and coefficient
a1 on the terminal prime channel. Its other terminal coefficients r>=2
are immaterial. Exactly,
 a.V=bR+(a1-b)V1+(a0-b)V0=bR+J.
For t=lambda*u^(-2eta)>0, completing the square yields
 R^2+t(bR+J)^2
 =(1+t b^2)[R+t b J/(1+t b^2)]^2+t J^2/(1+t b^2)
 >=t J^2/(1+t b^2).
If a1!=b and m_P!=0, J has a nonzero u/log u main term, while
1+t b^2<=1+lambda b^2 for u>=1,eta>=0. The same exponent-gap obstruction
therefore applies WITHOUT assuming any bound on R. This extends the
pure-prime detector to many terminal/bin block contrasts. Equal a1=b
escapes this gate because prime/composite transport cancels in that ray's
R projection. Unequal bin weights cannot be collapsed by this identity;
their individual arithmetic asymptotics remain unpaid.

This makes the lesson more precise than forbidding primes: a positive
auxiliary norm must respect the large prime/composite cancellation mode.
Normalizing a pure-prime coordinate enough to escape this gate does not
prove the remaining drift; it only removes one necessary-condition failure.
Zero mass also is not safety. Higher inverse-log terms may obstruct a
nonzero mass-zero fixed kernel, but that stronger theorem needs an imported
prime-count expansion with its remainder, not just ordinary PNT. It is
left unpaid here, as agreed with root.

5. Exact flux construction and a payable next bound grammar
=========================================================
Let h=floor(u/2), d=(1,...,1), and write
 M_u=dd^T+sum lambda*u^(-2eta)*aa^T,
 F(u)=V(u)^T M_u V(u)/u^q.
Because eta>=0 and h<=u, M_u<=M_h in PSD order. Define the exact signed
flux D=V(u)-V(h). It must be compiled from the union of the two admitted
token sets, not evaluated after rebinding parent tokens to child tokens.

Put s=(h/u)^q. For tau>s, matrix Young and M_u<=M_h give
 F(u)-tau F(h)
 <= [tau/(tau-s)] * D^T M_u D/u^q.
Proof: use (x+y)^TM(x+y)<= (1+epsilon)x^TMx+
(1+1/epsilon)y^TMy with epsilon=tau/s-1, x=V(h), y=D.
The child term is then at most tau F(h). The remaining exact scalar
price is1+1/epsilon=tau/(tau-s).
At even u,q1,tau1/2, tau=s: no finite universal Young price for an
arbitrary flux exists. Tau3/4,q1 has price3; tau1,q1 has price2.
At q just above1 and tau1/2 the price is large. These facts explain a
specific contraction-margin limitation, rather than diagnosing LP failure
as an arithmetic impossibility.

An executable next detector should construct D symbolically by splitting
the shared integer index domain at BOTH support and cutoff breakpoints.
Cells record stable category, changed rough/least-prime category, new outer
support, excluded-square/prime status, and exact coefficient/token changes.
All boundary cells and signed terms remain. Search small signed quadratic
forms on these flux cells and their interactions with V(h), preserving
prime/composite cancellation. The separately payable leaf could be
 D^T M_u D/u^q <= C*(tau-s)/tau,
or a sharper signed correlation inequality retaining the cross term.
Neither is certified by its finite fit; the outer support generally still
contains the difficult signed Mobius arithmetic. Taking coefficient l1
again returns the already proved077 obstruction.

For a richer generic rule set z_u=V(u)/u^(q/2), z_h=V(h)/h^(q/2), and
z_u=T z_h+W, with T specified independently and W exactly compiled from
the signed transport cells. If an independently checked block matrix
 [ tau M_h-T^T M_u T,  -T^T M_u ]
 [ -M_u T,             K-M_u    ]
is PSD, then F(u)-tau F(h)<=W^T K W. A genuine arithmetic leaf
W^T K W<=C closes drift. Rational interval enclosures or symbolic scalar
inequalities must pay every fractional power in the PSD certificate; a
floating eigenvalue check is not a uniform proof. Defining W as the
transport difference is exact, but does not itself pay its energy. Choosing
K or a leaf by simply renaming the original drift is circular and supplies
no progress. The useful search restricts flux cells/ranks and derives
their identities first, then tests genuinely separate signed correlation
leaves and records their own witnesses.

6. What078 advances and what remains
===================================
The experiment successfully constructed and independently admitted a new
signed rough state, searched actual auxiliary recurrences, froze formulas,
and obtained exact failed witnesses. The prime-main-term detector gives
an additional rigorous pruning rule for a class of auxiliary energies.
None of this proves a uniform exponent for the residual. Its useful next
target is a cancellation-respecting signed flux/correlation estimate with
an exact transport certificate and an independent arithmetic leaf.
Complete energy base, all-integer drift including count/cutoff jumps,
original residual/correlation/source transfer, an epsilon family, and
head/core/RH remain open. The original signed candidate is not rejected
merely because its chosen positive-square auxiliary energy is impossible.
