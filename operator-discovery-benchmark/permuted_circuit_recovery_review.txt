1. Polynomial bijections and exact digit inverses
For n>=1 write n=m+r, m=2^k, 0<=r<m. Fix integer coefficients and let
 F(r)=b+a*r+sum_{j=2}^J c_j*r^j,
with a odd and every c_j even. Then
 F(x)-F(y)=(x-y)[a+sum_{j>=2}c_j sum_{l=0}^{j-1}x^{j-1-l}y^l].
The bracket is odd. Therefore divisibility of F(x)-F(y) by2^k is equivalent
to divisibility of x-y by2^k. The map r->F(r) mod2^k is injective, hence a
bijection of the finite ring, for every k. The constant b does not affect
this argument. These are sufficient conditions, not a classification of all
permutation polynomials modulo powers of2.

Given a target y mod2^k, start with r_0=0 modulo1. If r_j solves F(r_j)=y
mod2^j, its two possible lifts are r_j and r_j+2^j. Their images differ by
2^j modulo2^(j+1), because the displayed difference factor is odd. Exactly
one is the next solution. Repeating through j=k-1 gives the unique inverse
residue in[0,2^k). This is an exact finite algorithm, including the first
binary digit; it needs no floating roots or enumeration of the whole block.
For k=0 the sole residue is0 and the block map fixes1.

2. Other maps, block branches and composition
Xor by a k-bit mask is self-inverse. The mask must be in[0,2^k), or explicitly
reduced to those k bits. Bit reversal on exactly k bits is self-inverse.
Cyclic rotation by s positions has inverse rotation by -s modk, with the
k=0 case explicitly the identity. These are finite bijections of each block.
Any finite composition is bijective; undo its factors in reverse order.
If U_pi f(n)=f(pi(n)), then U_pi^-1=U_{pi^-1}; function-composition order
inside a pullback must be handled consistently by an implementation.

Choose between two such maps using a fixed original-source phase evaluated
at the BLOCK BASE m, with a specified unit-sign convention for a zero source.
The chosen branch is then constant throughout that block, and the inverse
uses the same choice and inverse branch map. Recompute that choice from the
same original source and same base, never from current intermediate values.
Choosing a branch separately at each n has no automatic bijection theorem:
on a two-point block, choosing identity at its first point and swap at its
second sends both points to the first. The constant-block branch condition
prevents precisely this defect. A branch may vary between blocks; no source
cancellation is required for this algebraic admission rule.

3. Closed horizons and exact restriction
H=2^K-1 contains complete blocks[2^k,2^(k+1)), k<K. Every admitted permutation
and inverse preserves this set. Full divisor transforms and inverses use only
coordinates n/d<=n and therefore also preserve it. Restricting a global fixed
circuit and its inverse to this closed horizon is exact: every intermediate
coordinate required for n<=H is retained. Coefficients and arithmetic phases
still denote one fixed original source, not a horizon-dependent substitute.

An arbitrary prefix cutoff is not closed under a block permutation. Clipping
the mapped index, dropping out-of-range terms, or inserting a zero extension
does not give the inverse of the intended restricted circuit. Load the complete
enclosing block or reject the unclosed horizon. Closed horizons exhaust the
positive integers and are sufficient for all monotone global H limits below.

4. Weighted norm recovery without a fictitious divisor-path majorant
Use H_{sigma,H}(f)=sum_{n<=H}|f(n)|^2/n^(2sigma+1), with its square-root norm.
Within one block, n and pi(n) have ratio between1/2 and2. Changing variables
in the finite sum shows
 H_{sigma,H}(U_pi f)<=2^(2sigma+1)H_{sigma,H}(f),
and the same holds for U_pi^-1. Thus the unsquared norm cost is
2^(sigma+1/2). For sigma=1/2+epsilon, 0<epsilon<=1/2, it is <=sqrt(8)<3.
Every complete dyadic unweighted moment is preserved EXACTLY by U_pi and its
inverse. Arbitrary intervals and the reciprocal-power weights are not exact
invariants of a generic permutation.

For one divisor stage, the local coefficient bound
 |a_{alpha,chi}(p^l)|<=(|alpha|)_l/l!
gives |a(d)|<=d_|alpha|(d), including fractional negative alpha. The
divisor-supported dilation has norm d^(-sigma-1/2), so independently
 ||T_{alpha,chi}||, ||T_{-alpha,chi}||<=zeta(1+epsilon)^|alpha|.
Unit signs have norm1 and are their own inverses. Multiply these SEPARATE
stage norms for a circuit with P permutation occurrences and B=sum|alpha|:
 ||O||, ||O^-1||<=3^P zeta(1+epsilon)^B
                <=3^P(1+1/epsilon)^R, R=ceil(B).
R=0 when B=0. Every inverse uses the same fixed coefficient source, with
alpha negated, and reverses the entire stage order.

The bound is sound even though a single d_B kernel on inputs n/d generally
does NOT survive intervening permutations. A permutation changes the next
integer to be divided and its divisor set. The old path identity that combines
all divisors into one product denominator is unavailable. Separate operator
norm multiplication needs no such identity. Squared H costs are
9^P(1+1/epsilon)^(2R), not the unsquared factors.

For a shear with coefficient c and fixed original-source phase, the additional
stage norm and inverse cost is <=1+2|c|, as proved in section9. Define
 V=product_over_shears(1+2|c|), with empty product1.
The amended full bound is 3^P V(1+1/epsilon)^R, and the squared H bound is
9^P V^2(1+1/epsilon)^(2R). No changed divisor-path argument is needed.

5. Exact conditional cost and cofinal bridge
Let rho=O M for ONE fixed circuit. Suppose for a given integer q>=2 a finite
Cq>=0 satisfies, for EVERY dyadic X>=32,
 E_rho(X)=X^-2 sum_{X<=n<2X}rho(n)^2<=Cq ceil(X^(1/q)).
At sigma=1/2+1/q, a block contributes at most
 X^(-2/q)E_rho(X)<=2Cq X^(-1/q)
to H_sigma(rho). For t=1/q in(0,1], convexity of2^-t gives
2^-t<=1-t/2, so
 sum_{k>=5}2^(-k/q)<=1/(1-2^(-1/q))<=2q.
Define the retained head h=sum_{1<=n<32}rho(n)^2/n^2, or use an explicitly
larger nonnegative head. Its H_sigma contribution is <=h. Consequently
 H_{1/2+1/q}(rho)<=h+4qCq,
 H_{1/2+1/q}(M)<=9^P V^2(1+q)^(2R)(h+4qCq).
For no shears V=1, giving the originally proposed cost exactly.
The head belongs to this same fixed output and must not be omitted or silently
replaced by a cheaper candidate's head. The displayed cost is conditional on
the universal moment premise; a finite fitted constant is not such a Cq.

If this premise holds for a cofinal/unbounded set of q, for every sigma>1/2
choose q with1/q<sigma-1/2. Monotonicity in the weights then gives finite
H_sigma(M). The reviewed step-extension/Mellin argument continues ordinary
1/zeta holomorphically to Re(s)>1/2; the standard ordinary-zeta continuation
and functional equation give RH. No ordinary Mobius-series convergence in
that whole half-plane is assumed in this argument. Finite tested q and a
scale-varying circuit do not establish the cofinal fixed-circuit premise.

6. Permutation/sign/divisor subgrammar: complete signed kernels and obstruction
For one stage the exact matrix kernels are
 T(n,u)=1_{u|n}a(n/u),
 U_pi(n,u)=1_{u=pi(n)}, S(n,u)=S(n)1_{u=n}.
The full K_O(n,u) is the sum of products over all intermediate integer paths.
Every coordinate stays inside the closed horizon. The complete moment is
 X^-2 sum_{n in block}sum_{u,v}K_O(n,u)K_O(n,v)M(u)M(v).
This is the valid full signed interaction formula after permutations; it
includes their changed divisibility sets and all mixed input pairs.

If n lies in[ m,2m), every PROPER divisor input n/d lies below m. Thus T is
identity on its diagonal dyadic block plus a strictly lower-block matrix.
Permutations and signs are block-diagonal monomial orthogonal matrices.
Their arbitrary interleaving remains block lower triangular, with a signed
permutation Q_m as its diagonal block. Exactly,
 rho_[m,2m)=Q_m M_[m,2m)+G_m(M_<m),
where G_m is linear in the lower-block PREFIX COORDINATES. Its coefficients
may query the fixed original arithmetic source, including fresh source data;
this is not a claim that G_m is computable from past source information alone.
Once a path goes to a lower block it never returns to the original block.
The unique same-block path uses the divisor1 at every T stage, which proves
the signed-permutation diagonal assertion without approximation.

Therefore the complete dyadic energy is exactly
 E_rho(m)=E_M(m)+m^-2||G_m||^2+2m^-2<Q_m M_block,G_m>.
Equivalently rho=Q_m[M_block+Q_m^-1 G_m] on this block. Nontrivial new prime
and within-block coordinate pairings are available, but the full fresh-block
energy remains with coefficient1. For any unrestricted input supported in
this block and zero below it, the output on the block is just Q_m f_block
and has precisely its original energy. No source-independent lower-block
norm argument can turn that energy into a subpower estimate.

On actual M the requisite arithmetic mechanism is a sufficiently negative
FULL correlation. Specifically the moment gate would require
 2<Q_m M_block,G_m>
 <=-||M_block||^2-||G_m||^2+m^2 Cq ceil(m^(1/q)).
Merely proving that the cross term vanishes, or that G_m is small, provides
no universal cancellation of E_M. This is an obstruction to an argument
based only on reversibility/norms, not a refutation of an arithmetic proof
using the actual source-dependent correlations.

7. Exact commutators, conjugation and analytic aliases
In the permutation/sign/divisor subgrammar, the basic interaction has the
exact two-domain formula
 [T,U_pi]f(n)
 =sum_{d|n}a(d)f(pi(n/d))-sum_{e|pi(n)}a(e)f(pi(n)/e).
The domains differ and neither unmatched divisor complement may be dropped.
Conjugation gives
 (U_pi^-1 T U_pi f)(n)
 =sum_{d|pi^-1(n)}a(d)f(pi(pi^-1(n)/d)).
This does change coordinate-level proper-divisor arithmetic, while every
nonidentity term remains in a strictly lower DYADIC block. The commutator
[T,U_pi] has zero diagonal block. A reversible group commutator, for example
T^-1 U_pi^-1 T U_pi, has identity diagonal block and lower-block correction.
These are genuine ways to alter the required correlation, not removal of
the fresh-prefix term. A block permutation cannot anticommute with T as
whole operators: their diagonal blocks would require Q=-Q, impossible for
a nonzero unit permutation block.

Adjacent inverse stages cancel exactly using the same fixed branch/source.
Adjacent compatible permutation stages compose exactly; adjacent same-source
T stages merge their exponents. Multiplicative phase folding retains its
usual fixed-source qualifications. A TERMINAL permutation, or any terminal
composition of permutations and unit signs, preserves every COMPLETE DYADIC
moment. Such an output is an energy alias for that target. This is not a
claim about non-dyadic diagnostic intervals or exact weighted H/head equality.
Removing a terminal permutation may also change a conservative recovery cost;
that change is not new signed arithmetic cancellation.

8. Involutions, interval complements and a proved padding rule
For a block involution pi, U_pi is a self-adjoint orthogonal involution on
the unweighted block. Its projections
 P_plus=(I+U_pi)/2, P_minus=(I-U_pi)/2
satisfy ||f||^2=||P_plus f||^2+||P_minus f||^2. The antisymmetric channel
may cancel invariant data, but it has a kernel. Retaining only it loses the
target; retaining the complementary symmetric channel preserves the entire
energy and its unresolved cost. The algebra alone supplies no anti-invariance
identity for actual M. A conjugated divisor cancellation rule must retain
these complements if it uses such a projection inside a proposed proof.

For every permutation acting DIRECTLY on actual M there is the exact interval
identity
 delta_pi(n)=M(pi(n))-M(n)
            =signed sum of mu(j) between n and pi(n),
with both interval endpoints specified by the usual prefix difference.
Thus |delta_pi(n)|<=|pi(n)-n|. If
 L(X)=max_{X<=n<2X}|pi(n)-n|,
then E_delta_pi(X)<=L(X)^2/X, unconditionally. In particular a directly
applied fixed low-bit xor, or a near-identity family with
L(X)<=C_eta X^(1/2+eta) for every eta>0, adds an unconditionally subpower
padding channel. This is a useful mechanism classifier, not an estimate for
the full prefix M. If such a channel is followed by a fixed admitted circuit,
its H_sigma finiteness survives by the proved stage bounds.
The rule cannot be transferred blindly to interior permutations acting on
T M or S M: those sequences need not have increments bounded by1.

Conversely, large polynomial/bit displacements replace the prefix coordinate
by a potentially large signed interval sum. A residue polynomial identity
is not an identity for mu or M. Even the affine map r->p*r mod m sends the
original integer to
 m+(p*r mod m)=p(m+r)-m[p-1+floor(p*r/m)],
so the prime-multiple law mu(pu)=-mu(u)1_{p does not divide u} does not apply
without its entire wrap/translation complement. A polynomial permutation
does not preserve squarefreeness just because it is a ring bijection.
No unconditional cancellation for these signed interval complements or the
full lower-block correlation is proved here.

9. Amended shear primitive: inverse, full cross terms and smoothness classifier
For each even n>=2 let the pair be(n,n+1), let t=c*phi(n), and let
phi(n) be a unit sign evaluated from the SAME original source at the even
index. Every such pair lies inside one complete dyadic block; n=1 is unchanged.
The even_from_odd matrix on(u,v) is [[1,t],[0,1]], and odd_from_even is
[[1,0],[t,1]]. In either case the off-diagonal part N has N^2=0. Hence
(I+N)^-1=I-N: negate c, keep direction and the same fixed phase. Both original
coordinates remain; there is no discarded difference complement. Computing
the target from current pair values is safe only if the untouched source
coordinate and the original-source phase remain unchanged during the stage.

On H_sigma, the even_from_odd off-diagonal norm is at most
 |c|*((n+1)/n)^(sigma+1/2)
 <=|c|*(3/2)^(1+epsilon)<2|c|,
uniformly for0<epsilon<=1/2. The other direction costs at most |c|.
Triangle inequality gives <=1+2|c| for each shear and inverse. The sign of
c and its rational denominator do not affect the theorem. Its specified
finite rational bounds belong to the grammar, not to a needed cancellation
assumption. Adjacent same-direction/same-phase shears merge by adding c,
because their shared N squares to zero. An adjacent inverse pair cancels.

The exact unweighted pair energies, with both coordinates retained, are
 even_from_odd: (u+t*v)^2+v^2
              =u^2+v^2+c^2*v^2+2c*phi(n)*u*v;
 odd_from_even: u^2+(v+t*u)^2
              =u^2+v^2+c^2*u^2+2c*phi(n)*u*v.
This is a genuine fresh/fresh signed cross term, paid for by the positive
c^2 complement. Negative correlation cannot be counted without that cost.
Conjugating a shear by a block permutation U_pi^-1 A U_pi makes its pair
coordinates pi(n),pi(n+1), with the phase still evaluated at the ORIGINAL
even n as dictated by the conjugated stages. Those coordinates need not be
adjacent in arithmetic order. This can expose different within-block signed
interval correlations. It is not an identity identifying their source values.

There is an especially important unconditional classifier for a shear acting
DIRECTLY on adjacent actual M. Put x=M(n), y=M(n+1), w=mu(n+1), so y=x+w
and |w|<=1. With t=c*phi(n), define kappa(t)=1+t+t^2/2.
The even_from_odd pair has the EXACT identity
 (x+t*y)^2+y^2
 =kappa(t)*(x^2+y^2)+c^2*y*w-(t+c^2/2)*w^2.
The odd_from_even pair similarly equals
 kappa(t)*(x^2+y^2)-c^2*x*w-(t+c^2/2)*w^2.
No probabilistic source premise is used. Since |x|,|y|<2X on the dyadic
block and there are X/2 pairs, the total remainder divided by X^2 is bounded
in absolute value by
 c^2+(|c|+c^2/2)/(2X).
Thus it is O_c(1), uniformly over every scale and every unit-phase choice.
Also kappa(t)=((t+1)^2+1)/2>=1/2, so
 E_shear(X)>=E_M(X)/2-O_c(1).
The retained full pair energy cannot become a subpower observer here without
the original E_M itself becoming subpower.

If phi is a constant sign, more precisely
 E_shear(X)=kappa(c*phi)E_M(X)+O_c(1).
For c=-1, phi=+1, the changed coordinate is M(n)-M(n+1)=-mu(n+1), but the
unchanged coordinate is M(n+1). The full energy is E_M/2+O(1), rather than
just the bounded difference energy. This same leading cancellation holds
for ANY prefix with bounded source increments, including coherent controls.
It is smoothness cancellation, not evidence of an actual-prime mechanism.
For a nonconstant phase, the leading term is the complete positive pair
energy weighted by kappa(c*phi); it still has the uniform lower bound1/2.
The phase-dependent improvement requires correlation with prefix energy,
and is not supplied by the bounded-increment identity alone.

These classifiers do not automatically apply to interior shears on T M or
S M, which need not have bounded adjacent increments. Nor does their adjacent
version apply after a permutation has separated the two arithmetic indices.
There the exact complementary pair energy and interval-source relation must
be analyzed anew, retaining every translated/wrapped source complement.

With shears included, the whole circuit is still dyadic-block lower triangular,
but its diagonal Q_m is now an INVERTIBLE same-block matrix, not necessarily
a signed permutation. Thus section6's coefficient1 fresh-energy identity is
specific to its stated subgrammar and must not be applied to shear words.
The general exact identity is instead
 rho_block=Q_m M_block+G_m(M_<m),
 E_rho=m^-2[||Q_m M_block||^2+||G_m||^2
             +2<Q_m M_block,G_m>].
All fresh/fresh cross terms reside in ||Q_m M_block||^2 and must remain.
On the unweighted block, every shear and its inverse has norm<=1+|c|;
signs and permutations have norm1. If V0=product(1+|c|), then
 V0^-1||f_block||<=||Q_m f_block||<=V0||f_block||.
Consequently for inputs zero in lower blocks, the entire fresh-block energy
remains at least V0^-2 E_f. Shears remove the monomial-diagonal restriction,
but a fixed finite uniformly invertible word still has no input-independent
subpower estimate for arbitrary coherent fresh blocks. An actual-source
proof would need negative fresh/fresh correlations plus their positive
complements, and any additional lower-block cross credit, or a complete
moment bound using other source-specific information.

Each shear has determinant1; signs and permutations have determinant of
absolute value1. Hence |det Q_m|=1. A uniform strict contraction of every
fresh-block direction is impossible; any contracted directions must have
compensating expansion elsewhere. A source-specific alignment with those
directions could still matter, but the adjacent-prefix identity shows one
large alignment shared by all bounded-increment controls. Repeating lifting
or differencing through an unbounded number of scale-dependent stages is
not covered by the FIXED finite-word cost. Replacing all M coordinates by
their bounded adjacent differences without their coarse complement would
need an unbounded prefix-sum inverse, rather than the stated certificate.

10. Consequences for the proposed structural study
The block inverse/circuit recovery certificate is sound with the domain,
source-binding, shear-factor and head qualifications above. A different
prime-coordinate obstruction has been removed. The permutation-only dyadic
fresh-energy identity and the amended invertible-block/shear cross identities
supply exact accounting rules for candidate proof attempts. State which signed
lower-block or fresh/fresh correlation a structural rearrangement exposes.
Classify terminal energy aliases, exact inverse/merge aliases, directly
applied bounded-displacement padding and adjacent-prefix smoothness shears
before interpreting finite improvements. The amended contract's normalizer
uses exact permitted inverse/merge rules; terminal-permutation removal is
explicitly an alias of complete dyadic moments and not irregular diagnostics.

A meaningful all-scale advance would prove a complete correlation/compensation
identity, or a source-specific estimate strong enough to pay all squared
complements and retained fresh energy. An involution's antisymmetric channel
must not be used without its symmetric complement. Nontriangular coordinate
access and reversible fresh/fresh mixing are real new grammar abilities,
but do not by themselves supply a signed moment bound. No favorite
permutation, numerical result, optimization backend or unproved Mertens/RH
premise is used in this review.
