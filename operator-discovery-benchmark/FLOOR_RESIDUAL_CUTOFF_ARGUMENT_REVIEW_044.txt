044 INDEPENDENT REVIEW: CONVEX MOBIUS POWER CUTOFFS
New post-analysis proof and executable guard; no frozen source or result edits.

1. Result and limits

For a fixed convex mixture
    w(t)=sum_i lambda_i(1-t^alpha_i), 0<=t<=1,
    alpha_i>0, lambda_i>=0, sum_i lambda_i=1,
consider the actual coefficients c_n(N)=-mu(n) times the dyadically rounded
version of w(n/N), for 2<=n<=N. The precision is p=4 ceil(log_2 N), each
inner power is rounded down, and the outer taper exponent is exactly one.
Let f_n be the original floor basis, g_N=sum c_n f_n, e_N=chi-g_N,
and h_N=e_(qN)-b D_q e_N, D_q f(x)=f(qx). For fixed integer q>=2 and
fixed real b with |b|<sqrt(q),

    ||h_N||_2 does not tend to zero as N tends to infinity through ALL integers.

Consequently, for every finite A>=0 and eta>0, the uniform assertion
    ||h_N||_2^2 <= A/(1+floor(log_2 N))^eta
for every integer N>=N0 is false, for every finite starting threshold N0.
This conclusion concerns the exact functions and rounded coefficients. It does
not depend on finite numerical slopes, observed norms, RH, or simple zeros.
It yields no specific finite violating N and no positive numerical lower bound.

The supported mixed recipe has c_n=-mu(n)(T_(2/7)+T_1)/2, obtained by generic
AST coefficient algebra; the baseline has c_n=-mu(n)T_(2/7). Thus the theorem
applies to both analytic formulas in the task. No executable application to
either actual selected payload was run during this review. Their already-saved
finite norm certificates are untouched and may all hold while the stated
all-integer uniform extension fails.

The conclusion does NOT refute any individual fixed geometric subsequence or
every possible later tapping. Original019, original041 core, and RH remain OPEN.
Full-sequence convergence e_N->0 for this supported shape is also impossible:
it would imply h_N->0 by the triangle inequality and ||D_q||=q^(-1/2).
That is a statement about this particular family, not about all approximations.

2. Imported facts and exact transformed basis

Use the known L2 unitary U commuting with positive dilations and satisfying
    U rho(1/x)=rho(x)/x,
    U chi(x)=S(x)=sin(2*pi*x)/(pi*x).
These are imported facts, displayed in Baez-Duarte, Arithmetical aspects of
Beurling's real variable reformulation of the Riemann hypothesis, section4,
equations4.1-4.4: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0011254 .
No unitary is numerically constructed or newly discovered here. The other
imported fact is that ordinary zeta has at least one zero rho=1/2+i*tau on the
critical line. Neither its simplicity nor the absence of other zeros is needed.

Writing rho for fractional part only in the following basis identities,
    f_n(x)=rho(1/(n*x))-rho(1/x)/n,
which equals floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(n*x)) on 0<x<=1 and is zero for x>1.
Commutation gives
    U f_n(x)=[rho(n*x)-rho(x)]/(n*x).
On 0<x<1/N, every n<=N therefore has U f_n(x)=1-1/n. This interval
identity is exact; endpoint values at single points do not affect the norm.
Define the signed constant
    T_N=sum_(2<=n<=N) c_n(N)(1-1/n).
Then on 0<x<1/(qN), all terms in both g_(qN) and D_q g_N are constant,
and the FULL refinement forcing satisfies
    U h_N(x)=S(x)-b*S(q*x)-[T_(qN)-b*T_N].                    (2.1)
In particular, |S(x)|<=2 and the L2 triangle inequality on this interval give
    |T_(qN)-b*T_N|/sqrt(N)
       <=sqrt(q)||h_N||_2+2(1+|b|)/sqrt(N).                  (2.2)
The indicator forcing chi-b D_q chi is present in (2.1); it is never discarded.
All signed contributions of g_(qN) and the dilated parent remain in T.

3. Rounding, harmonic complement, and unit

Let the unrounded sums be
    F_w(x)=sum_(n<=x) mu(n)w(n/x),
    m_w(N)=sum_(n<=N) mu(n)w(n/N)/n.
The actual taper is 1-floor_(2^-p)((n/N)^alpha). Its outer power-one floor is
exact on that dyadic. Thus its difference from 1-(n/N)^alpha lies in
[0,2^-p], including exact zero error at n=N. Since 2^-p<=N^-4 and the
merged weights are convex, the effective weight error is at most N^-4.
Identical powers in the literal AST have identical floors, so their coefficients
can be combined before this error is bounded, even if the raw AST has negative
terms which cancel. Therefore
    T_N=-F_w(N)+m_w(N)+delta_N, |delta_N|<=N^-3.             (3.1)
Indeed |1-1/n|<=1 and there are fewer than N summands.

There is no missing n=1 basis term. The n=1 contributions -mu(1)w(1/N) and
+mu(1)w(1/N)/1 cancel IDENTICALLY in (3.1). This is why the useful scalar
sum can include the unit while the original g_N starts at n=2. As 0<=w<=1,
    |m_w(N)|<=sum_(n<=N)1/n<=1+log N.                      (3.2)
No arithmetic cancellation is assumed in this paid harmonic complement.

Set G(x)=F_w(qx)-b F_w(x). Combining (2.2)-(3.2) gives the explicit bound
    |G(N)|/sqrt(N) <=sqrt(q)||h_N||_2
      +[2(1+|b|)+(1+log(qN))+|b|(1+log N)
         +(qN)^(-3)+|b|N^(-3)]/sqrt(N).                    (3.3)
Consequently, vanishing forcing on all integers implies G(N)=o(sqrt(N)).
In particular, no full-energy convergence/interpolation argument is necessary.

4. Exact integer-to-real transfer

For a single power alpha, the real sum F_alpha(x) is continuous at every
integer: the newly entered term has weight 1-(n/x)^alpha=0 at x=n. Between
integers its derivative obeys the elementary estimate
    |F_alpha'(x)|
      <=alpha*x^(-alpha-1)*sum_(n<=x)n^alpha<=alpha.
Thus F_w is globally Lipschitz on [1,infinity) with constant
    C_w=sum_i lambda_i alpha_i.
G is Lipschitz with constant (q+|b|)C_w. For integer N<=x<N+1,
    |G(x)-G(N)|<=(q+|b|)C_w.
The all-integer little-o implication from (3.3) therefore extends to
    G(x)=o(sqrt(x)) for ALL real x->infinity.               (4.1)
This uses fixed positive powers and zero endpoint weight, not a claim about
arbitrary moving/logarithmic cutoff ASTs. The dyadic rounding is removed with
the explicit error in section3 before this Lipschitz argument is used.

5. Mellin identity with the entire endpoint term

For Re(s)>1, absolute convergence justifies exchanging the scalar sum and
integral. For each n and alpha,
    integral_n^infinity [1-(n/x)^alpha]x^(-s-1)dx
      =n^(-s)*alpha/[s(s+alpha)].
Since sum mu(n)n^(-s)=1/zeta(s), define the EXACT rational function
    K_w(s)=sum_i lambda_i alpha_i/[s(s+alpha_i)]
and obtain Mellin(F_w)(s)=K_w(s)/zeta(s). Scaling the LOWER ENDPOINT as well
as the integrand gives
    Mellin(G)(s)=(q^s-b)K_w(s)/zeta(s)-C_q(s),             (5.1)
    C_q(s)=q^s*integral_1^q F_w(t)t^(-s-1)dt.
C_q is entire because its integral is over a fixed compact positive interval.
This term cannot be omitted; the whole transformed function includes it.

For Re(s)>0, the factor s is nonzero and
    Re[s K_w(s)]
      =sum_i lambda_i alpha_i (Re(s)+alpha_i)/|s+alpha_i|^2>0.   (5.2)
At least one weight is positive, so K_w has no zero in that half-plane.
This is a positive Stieltjes proof, not the incorrect general claim that an
arbitrary polynomial with positive coefficients has no right-half-plane zeros.
At a known critical zero rho,
    |q^rho|=sqrt(q)>|b|,
so (q^rho-b)K_w(rho) is nonzero.

6. Boundary-pole contradiction, including multiple zeros

Suppose (4.1) holds. Mellin(G) then converges absolutely and is holomorphic in
Re(s)>1/2. For delta>0, splitting its integral at an arbitrary fixed A and
using |G(x)|<=epsilon sqrt(x) on the tail proves
    delta*Mellin(G)(rho+delta)->0.                         (6.1)
The finite head contributes O_A(delta), and the absolute tail is bounded by
epsilon*delta*integral_A^infinity x^(-delta-1)dx<=epsilon.

Multiply (5.1) by zeta before taking the limit. The resulting meromorphic
identity continues from Re(s)>1 to Re(s)>1/2; any pole at s=1 is handled as a
meromorphic identity and is irrelevant near the nonreal critical zero.
As zeta is analytic and vanishes at rho, even for a multiple zero,
    zeta(rho+delta)=O(delta).
Equation (6.1) implies zeta(rho+delta)*Mellin(G)(rho+delta)->0, whereas the
right side tends to
    (q^rho-b)K_w(rho)-zeta(rho)*C_q(rho)
      =(q^rho-b)K_w(rho) != 0.
This contradiction proves section1. No assertion of RH, simple zeros,
zero-free continuation, prime independence, or sign randomness occurs here.
The conclusion is limsup_N ||h_N||_2>0 (possibly infinite); it is not a
pointwise lower bound at every large N and not a subsequence theorem.

7. Selected analytic formulas, without actual guard applications

For the baseline alpha=2/7,
    K_0(s)=2/[s(7s+2)].
Ascending rational polynomial coefficients can be given as numerator [2]
and denominator [0,2,7]. This is a scaled representation of the denominator
factorization used by the executable guard, which preserves monic factors.
For the half-and-half mixed formula,
    K_mix(s)=(9s+4)/[2s(7s+2)(s+1)].
One scaled coefficient representation is numerator [4,9] and denominator
[0,4,18,14]; its only numerator zero is -4/9. Both formulas also satisfy the
generic Stieltjes argument, so no bespoke identifier/zero check is required.
Their Lipschitz constants are 2/7 and 9/14 respectively.

The frozen auxiliary convention c=-mu*base+lambda*mu*(left-right) makes
the effective weight base-lambda*left+lambda*right. For the mixed formula,
left=base=T_(2/7), right=T_1, lambda=1/2: the weights become 1/2,1/2.
Recognizing this signed AST algebra, rather than naming compatible0149, is
essential to reusing the argument on genuinely generated recipes.

8. Executable certificate API and verifier scope

NEW files:
    floor_residual_cutoff_guard_044.py
    test_floor_residual_cutoff_guard_044.py
    floor_residual_cutoff_guard_044_development_receipt.json

API:
    build_guard(request, meter=None)
    verify_guard(request, certificate, meter=None)
    Meter(...).operations

Request schema, with exact canonical rational strings:
    {recipe:{id:ordinal_label,base:taper_AST,auxiliary:null_or_aux_AST},
     q:integer, b:rational_string,
     domain:{kind:"all_integer",minimum:integer_N0},
     rounding:{kind:"dyadic_power_floor",bits_multiplier:4,
               scale:"ceil_log2_N"}}
The existing recipe AST is used directly, with no recipe-ID whitelist. A taper
has kind taper, coordinate cutoff, positive rational alpha, and gamma "1".
A nonnull auxiliary must use actual mu and a literal difference envelope
whose two children have the same supported taper shape. The guard normalizes
base-coefficient*left+coefficient*right and admits only a convex mixture after
merging equal powers. Labels do not affect admission but are retained in full
payload identity. No data-dependent signs, coefficient fitting, or numerical
Mellin evaluations enter this decision.

Output status REFUTED_ALL_INTEGER_VANISHING_FORCING binds the full request,
exact rational mixture, ascending numerator/denominator coefficients of K,
the positive-Stieltjes argument, q-b^2>0, exact rounding/harmonic/unit costs,
Lipschitz transfer, entire endpoint term, imported dependencies, and scope.
The method returns UNSUPPORTED for other ASTs or domains; this never means
those recipes are refuted. Resource failures propagate instead of producing
an obstruction certificate. Geometric-subsequence status and original019/041
core/RH status are always OPEN in a valid scoped certificate.

The verifier does NOT call the producer build_guard or its kernel constructor.
It independently normalizes coefficients with a list-based algorithm, checks
the denominator factorization s*product(s+alpha_i), and verifies K by exact
cross multiplication against the literal Stieltjes fractions. Strict JSON
identity binds every proof field and prevents booleans masquerading as numeric
flags. Shared syntax/rational parsers and static theorem-schema constants are
explicit trusted code; this is an executable algebraic guard, not a new formal
proof of the imported analytic facts. The verifier returns a scoped dictionary
with verified True, status REFUTED, and the OPEN/false flags.

Finite input/certificate sizes, parameter/intermediate rational bit caps, a
deadline, and a metered exact-algebra operation cap keep this small schema
bounded. These are proxy counts rather than machine-instruction counts.
No source sieve, floor-residual compiler, norm evaluator, old suite or saved
score module is imported by either producer or verifier.
