1. General constant and exact integer head

The exact input schema is
 {id:str,constant:canonical rational str,
  coefficients:[{n:int,value:canonical nonzero rational str}],
  steps:[{q:int,value:canonical nonzero rational str}]}.
The floor and step lists are strictly sorted, distinct and always present.
Constant may be positive,negative or0. Combined support is at most2048 and
indices n,q lie in2..4096. Empty supports are allowed.

On each complete cell(1/(k+1),1/k], k>=1, the vector is
 e(k)=C-sum_n c_n*(k mod n)/n-sum_q d_q*1_(k>=q).
Its continuous norm squared is sum_(k>=1)e(k)^2/[k(k+1)].
Use D=lcm(denominators(C),denominators(c_n/n),denominators(d_q)),
 w_n=c_n D/n, A=sum w_n, R(0)=C D.
The exact event update is
 R(k)-R(k-1)=-A+sum_(n|k)c_n D-sum_(q=k)d_q D.
Periodic events occur at ALL multiples; step events occur exactly once.
Every R(k) is an exact integer. The complete head1..H is summed at default
Arb192bits as D^-2 sum R(k)^2/[k(k+1)], with all signs/cross terms retained.
H must cover every step q. The universal EARLY bound used to check the prefix
is |C|+sum|d_q|+sum|c_n|(n-1)/n, allowing nonzero early cells even if the
tighter eventual-tail bound vanishes.

2. Exact complete periodic energy beyond all steps

After the steps activate, write C_tail=C-sum d_q. The floor residues are
periodic. Their exact means are (n-1)/(2n), and their exact covariance is
 G(m,n)=(gcd(m,n)^2-1)/(12mn).
One elementary derivation conditions on r=k mod g, g=gcd(m,n). Over the
common period, the remaining residues modulo m/g and n/g are independent by
the Chinese remainder theorem. The conditional centered means are
 (r-(g-1)/2)/m and (r-(g-1)/2)/n.
The uniform variance of r=0,...,g-1 is(g^2-1)/12, proving G, including its
diagonal and coprime-zero cases. No covariance sign is inferred numerically.

The complete periodic mean square is exactly
 V=(C_tail-sum c_n*(n-1)/(2n))^2+sum_(m,n)c_m*c_n*G(m,n).
Every signed covariance is present, including identical reduced frequencies
shared by different floors. Expanding each individual floor and separately
summing its Fourier norms would incorrectly omit those shared-mode crosses.
V instead represents the Parseval energy AFTER grouping identical frequencies.

3. Jordan2 representation: signed sums before squares

The exact divisor identity sum_(d|g)J2(d)=g^2 gives
 gcd(m,n)^2-1=sum_(d|m,n,d>1)J2(d),
 J2(d)=d^2*product_(p|d)(1-p^-2).
Put t=2D(C-sum d_q)-sum_n w_n(n-1). Then
 S=sum_(d=2..K)J2(d)*(sum_(n multiple of d)w_n)^2,
 V=(3t^2+S)/(12D^2), K=max occupied floor index(default1 if empty).
This is exactly the signed GCD quadratic form; cancellations occur inside
each divisor sum BEFORE squaring. Neither absolute coefficients nor a sum of
separate floor squares replaces those signed sums.

JordanKernel caches one coefficient-free Jordan2 sieve through the largest
requested K, at most4096, and reuses it for smaller K. The integer divisor
sums and squares take O(K log K) arithmetic, with no quadratic pair table and
no Fourier-mode expansion. Certificate rows include every d>=2 dividing at
least one occupied floor index, in ascending order, INCLUDING a row when its
signed weighted_sum cancels to0. Each row exposes exact J2 and weighted_sum.
The mathematical pair_count is floor-support squared, not a claimed pair loop.
An optional bounded GCDKernel helper exists only for independent small toy
kernel checks; certify always uses the Jordan provider.

4. Imported large sieve and its exact applicable constant

Imported provider: H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, "The large sieve",
Mathematika20(1973),119-134, Theorem1(1.4) and matrix duality Lemma1.
Primary author-hosted paper:
 https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/large_sieve.pdf
DOI https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300004708
The original theorem gives L+delta^-1 for L consecutive integer indices.
Its dual form bounds the squared exponential sum over those indices by that
constant times the coefficient energy. This is an imported classical theorem,
not a detector discovery. The source was read directly before implementation.

All grouped floor frequencies are rational with reduced denominator<=K,
including the constant frequency0. Distinct circular frequencies therefore
have separation>=1/K^2. Parseval identifies their coefficient energy with V.
The dual theorem consequently gives, for ANY integer start and L>=1,
 sum over that L-cell interval of e_tail(k)^2 <=(L+K^2)*V.
For zero or a single occupied frequency, the elementary constant-sequence
bound also implies this displayed bound, without needing a distinct-pair
spacing definition. No frequency is omitted when it is present.
The original1973 provider does NOT include the later minus-one sharpening;
the certificate explicitly records that it is unused.

5. Exact discrete weighted tail and the complete integral

Let a=H+1, w(k)=1/[k(k+1)] and
 T(L)=sum_(k=a..a+L-1)e(k)^2 <=(L+K^2)V.
Summation by parts gives the finite weighted tail in terms of T(L) and positive
differences w(a+L-1)-w(a+L). The endpoint tends0 because T(L)=O(L) and
w(a+L)=O(L^-2). In the infinite limit,
 sum_(L>=1)L*(w(a+L-1)-w(a+L))=sum_(k>=a)w(k)=1/a,
 sum_(L>=1)(w(a+L-1)-w(a+L))=w(a)=1/[a(a+1)].
Therefore the FULL unobserved continuous tail is bounded by
 0<=tail<=V*(1/a+K^2/[a(a+1)]).
This is the exact primary-safe weighted formula, not the proposed sharper
formula requiring L-1+K^2. It retains the complete infinite tail.

The separately proved pointwise tail has
 M=|C-sum d_q|+sum|c_n|*(n-1)/n,
 tail<=M^2/a.
The implementation computes both rational bounds EXACTLY and uses their
minimum. Neither bound is inferred from an observed finite tail pattern.
Arb encloses head+[0,min(pointwise bound,large-sieve bound)] for the entire
continuous domain0<x<=1. A negative/zero/positive C is treated identically;
no float norm or sign admission occurs.
