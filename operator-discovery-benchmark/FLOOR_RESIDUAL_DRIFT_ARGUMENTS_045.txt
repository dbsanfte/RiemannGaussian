045 INDEPENDENT WHOLE-FORCING ARGUMENT LANGUAGE
Source-free derivations, executable conditional proof packets, and paid scalar
compensation. Older sources/results are unchanged. No arithmetic norms or
actual selected applications were performed in this review.

1. Scope and conclusion

The moving profile is
    P_N(n)=sum_j w_j n^(-r_j beta_N),
    beta_N=ceil(a L(N)^theta)/L(N), L(N)=1+floor(log_2 N),
where a>0, 0<theta<1, r_j are sorted distinct positive rationals, signed
rational weights sum to one, and their first nonzero power moment has order m.
The generator accepts 1..8 terms. The detector's maximal-order Vandermonde
profiles are included; the argument generator also admits nonmaximal signed
profiles without inventing a positive-range theorem for them.
Actual coefficients are c_n(N)=-mu(n)V_N(n), 2<=n<=N, with each exponential
rounded separately at p(N)=4 ceil(log_2 N) dyadic bits before signed addition.
V_N may additionally multiply one frozen044 cutoff or log taper, using the
exact product of rounded children. Neither rounding nor optional taper is
discarded. Child qN recomputes its level, beta, precision and taper endpoints.

The new executable language supplies:
 - the exact finite-Dirichlet/Mellin identity for the COMPLETE h_N;
 - a meromorphic comparison with a signed moving reciprocal-zeta mixture;
 - distinct joint bias, truncation/taper/rounding, and harmonic-compensation
   energies, with their entire parent/child and mixture crosses inside each;
 - a sufficient rational Young bound argument and its exact unpaid premises;
 - an unconditional, explicit scalar bound for the bare/cutoff harmonic leaf,
   including rational all-large-level power constants and threshold constraints.

It proves NO complete uniform forcing bound, target convergence, or RH.
The bias and truncation leaves remain OPEN, including their separate L2
integrability. A signed mixture's coefficient Taylor order cannot pay them.
The positive proof route can lose important cancellation between these leaves.
An optional joint route avoids that loss but is identified as endpoint-equivalent
when it becomes the complete current-scale energy, not a smaller-scale theorem.

2. Profile identities and actual rounding

Write u=beta_N log n, Cw=sum_j |w_j|, rmin=min r_j, rmax=max r_j.
Always |P(u)|<=Cw exp(-rmin u), and moment cancellation gives
    |1-P(u)|<=u^m/m! * sum_j |w_j|r_j^m.                 (2.1)
For maximal order m=number of rates, exact partial fractions give
    sum_j w_j r_j/(z+r_j)=product_j r_j/(z+r_j).          (2.2)
The left side is the Laplace transform of -P'(u). The right side is the
transform of the convolution of the nonnegative probability densities
r_j exp(-r_j u) on u>=0. Thus P is their survival function:
    0<=P(u)<=1,
    0<=1-P(u)<=product_j r_j * u^m/m!.
The last bound follows by bounding every exponential by one on the simplex
in the convolution integral. This randomness is only a positive integral
representation of the profile; actual Mobius signs are never randomized.
The verifier checks (2.2) by independent literal rational-kernel evaluation.

The raw survival range does NOT transfer automatically to signed rounded
profiles. Each exponential floor error is at most epsilon=2^(-p)<=N^-4,
so the bare coefficient discrepancy is at most Cw*epsilon. Rounded profiles
can be negative; the actual compiler may not clamp or substitute the raw range.
For optional taper parameters alpha,gamma, let C_t=max(1,t), d_t=min(1,t).
On [0,1], |x^t-y^t|<=C_t |x-y|^d_t. These elementary Holder/Lipschitz
inequalities price every stage of the frozen two-power taper construction.
For cutoff coordinates n/N, define
    d=d_gamma, Cround=Cw(2+C_gamma).
For rounded log coordinates log n/log N, define
    d=d_alpha*d_gamma, Cround=Cw[2+C_gamma(C_alpha+1)].
For the bare profile, d=1 and Cround=Cw. The actual rounded product differs
from its unrounded counterpart by at most
    Cround*N^(-4d).                                      (2.3)
The log rule includes coordinate rounding before the inner power floor.

Since 0<=f_n<=1 for x<=1/n and f_n(x)<=1/(nx) for x>1/n,
    ||f_n||_2^2<=2/n.
Also sum_(n<=N)n^(-1/2)<=2 sqrt N. The triangle inequality therefore pays
the rounding-only target error by 8 Cround^2 N^(1-8d), and the complete
rounding-only forcing error by
    8 Cround^2 (q+|b|)^2 N^(1-8d).                        (2.4)
The conservative q factor is essential when d<1/8: the child's target-error
envelope then grows with its support. A previously contemplated 1+|b|
factor was corrected BEFORE the sole suite; the small-d regression covers it.
If 8d<=1, this positive rounding-norm route does not prove decay. The finite
rounding discrepancy nevertheless remains exactly inside the joint remainder.
None of (2.1)-(2.4) proves a small full signed residual or forcing norm.

3. Exact complete finite-Dirichlet identity

Use the Mellin convention Mf(s)=integral_0^infinity f(x)x^(s-1)dx.
For 0<Re(s)<1,
    M[rho(1/x)](s)=-zeta(s)/s,
and consequently for the original floor basis
    M f_n(s)=-zeta(s)(n^(-s)-1/n)/s.                     (3.1)
This follows directly by splitting the fractional-part integral at one and
using the Euler floor integral for zeta; it does not use an infinite mu series.

Include a unit term in the FINITE polynomial
    A_N(s)=sum_(1<=n<=N) mu(n)V_N(n)n^(-s).              (3.2)
The difference A_N(s)-A_N(1) cancels n=1 identically. This remains true when
an optional taper changes V_N(1); no unit basis vector is inserted. Then
    M e_N(s)=[1-zeta(s)(A_N(s)-A_N(1))]/s.              (3.3)
The complete forcing h_N=e_(qN)-b D_q e_N has transform
    [1-b q^(-s)
     -zeta(s){A_(qN)(s)-A_(qN)(1)
              -b q^(-s)[A_N(s)-A_N(1)]}]/s.             (3.4)
In particular chi-b D_q chi is present through 1-b q^(-s). The zeta pole
at s=1 is removable in (3.3) because its finite difference vanishes there.

Mellin Plancherel on Re(s)=1/2 gives
    ||h_N||_2^2=(1/(2pi)) integral_R |M h_N(1/2+i t)|^2 dt. (3.5)
One can obtain this directly from ordinary Fourier Plancherel by x=exp(-y)
and the L2 isometry f(x)->exp(-y/2)f(exp(-y)). Every actual finite program
is in L2. No critical-line convergence of sum mu(n)n^(-s) is asserted.

There is also a finite partial-summation primitive, with M(x)=sum_(n<=x)mu(n):
    sum_(n<=N)mu(n)n^(-z)
      =M(N)N^(-z)+z integral_1^N M(x)x^(-z-1)dx.         (3.6)
For an unrounded weighted profile it becomes
    M(N)V_N(N)N^(-s)-integral_1^N M(x)d[V_N(x)x^(-s)].
The lower-end convention retains the unit; all cutoff endpoints remain.
Actual rounded jumps are accounted for by an additional finite discrepancy,
not silently removed from this identity. An assumed bound
M(x)=O_epsilon(x^(1/2+epsilon)) for every epsilon would itself imply RH via
the ordinary reciprocal-zeta Dirichlet integral. It is not an admissible paid
companion bound, and it supplies no uniform constants as epsilon->0 anyway.

4. A genuinely distinct comparison, with its dangers exposed

Define meromorphically, not as an assumed convergent source sum,
    S_N(s)=sum_j w_j/zeta(s+r_j beta_N),
    Delta_N(s)=A_N(s)-S_N(s).
Away from poles, define the spectral functions
    B_N(s)=[1-zeta(s)S_N(s)]/s,
    T_N(s)=-zeta(s)Delta_N(s)/s,
    H_N(s)=zeta(s)A_N(1)/s.
By exact algebra M e_N=B_N+T_N+H_N. Apply the SAME scale-difference operator
    R_q,b Z_N=Z_(qN)-b q^(-s)Z_N
to each, recomputing the child beta. Its three energies are E_B,E_T,E_H.
No monotonicity of the ceiling schedule beta_N is assumed. The earlier044
fixed-cutoff scalar obstruction has no automatic application to this moving
N-dependent profile; it cannot be used as a blanket rejection of drift.

Delta_N includes, without dropping any component,
    rounded finite - unrounded tapered finite
    + unrounded tapered finite - unrounded bare finite
    + unrounded bare finite - S_N(s).
This provides a target for structural future search: signed profile transfer
through reciprocal zeta, and a complete cutoff remainder including both
scale endpoints. It does not convert the third line into a freely convergent
critical-line tail. Only on an initially absolutely convergent half-plane may
one write the infinite Dirichlet tail directly; elsewhere its control is OPEN.

S_N can introduce poles absent from the finite target. Thus E_B and E_T may
fail to exist separately even though B+T=[1-zeta(s)A_N(s)]/s is L2. A valid
separate positive route MUST prove integrability/pole cancellation inside each
block. Replacing these leaves by RH or a uniform RH-strength companion moment
would be circular. The signed joint E_(B+T) retains their compensating poles
and crosses; with H paid at a matching rate this is an equivalent frontier,
not proof that arithmetic cancellation is already controlled.

Whenever the separate energies are finite, the exact full identity is
    E_h=E_B+E_T+E_H
       +2 Re(<B,T>+<B,H>+<T,H>),                         (4.1)
where B,T,H now denote their scale-difference spectral functions. Signed
mixture crosses and parent/child crosses are inside every term. Absolute
allocation only loses the three inter-block crosses displayed in (4.1).

5. Unconditional harmonic compensation: explicit constants

This part is paid without RH. First consider the bare unrounded profile.
For kappa_j=r_j beta_N>0 the harmonic Dirichlet series is absolutely convergent,
so its infinite comparison is sum_j w_j/zeta(1+kappa_j), with tail bounded by
    sum_j |w_j| N^(-kappa_j)/kappa_j.                    (5.1)
This is a Re(s)>1 harmonic calculation, not a critical-line source assumption.

The elementary Euler continuation formula
    zeta(1+z)=1/z+1-(1+z)integral_1^infinity {x}x^(-2-z)dx
gives, on |z|<=1/4,
    |z zeta(1+z)-1|
       <=1/4+(1/4)(5/4)/(3/4)=2/3.
Hence R(z)=1/zeta(1+z) is analytic in that disk, R(0)=0, and |R(z)|<=3/4.
For R(z)=sum d_k z^k, Cauchy gives |d_k|<=(3/4)4^k. The exact profile
moments cancel k=1,...,m-1. If rmax beta_N<=1/8, summing the geometric
Taylor tail gives
    |sum_j w_j R(r_j beta_N)|<=H beta_N^m,
    H=(3/2)4^m sum_j |w_j|r_j^m.                         (5.2)
The proof does not require maximal order or positive profile weights.

For a cutoff taper W=(1-(n/N)^alpha)^gamma, the elementary inequality
    0<=1-W<=max(1,gamma)(n/N)^alpha
and |P_N(n)|<=Cw n^(-rmin beta_N) give, once rmin beta_N<=alpha/2,
the additional harmonic discrepancy bound
    Ccut N^(-rmin beta_N),
    Ccut=max(1,gamma) Cw(1+2/alpha).                      (5.3)
Here sum_(n<=N)n^(p-1)<=N^p(1+1/p) for p>0: for p>=1 use N*N^(p-1),
and for 0<p<1 use the decreasing integral bound. This includes EVERY n,
not a selectively favorable source domain. Log tapers are not paid by (5.3).
Their full harmonic discrepancy remains an explicit open leaf in this API.

Finally add actual rounding through Cround N^(-4d)(1+log N). Thus the complete
bare/cutoff A_N(1) is bounded by (5.1)+(5.2), plus (5.3) when present, plus
this rounding compensation, under the stated beta constraints. Its norm
cost follows from rho(1/x)<=1 for x<=1 and rho(1/x)=1/x for x>1:
    ||zeta(s)/s||_critical^2=||rho(1/x)||_2^2<=2.
Dilation has norm q^(-1/2), so
    E_H(N)<=2[|A_(qN)(1)|+|b|q^(-1/2)|A_N(1)|]^2.       (5.4)
This pays a distinct scalar compensation; it is not an assumed full-h estimate.

6. Rational all-large-level certificate, avoiding enormous supports

Let kappa=1-theta and tau=m kappa. The schedule obeys
    a L^(-kappa)<=beta_N<=(a+1)L^(-kappa).
For integer L>=2, log N>=L/4 and 1+log N<=2L, using log 2>=1/2.
Thus each term of (5.1) satisfies
    N^(-r beta_N)/(r beta_N)
      <=(1/(ra)) L^kappa exp[-(ra/4)L^theta].
Choose k_T=ceil((m+1)kappa/theta), at least one. Since exp(-x)<=k!/x^k,
the complete harmonic tail is at most
    C_T L^(-tau),
    C_T=sum_j |w_j| k_T! 4^k_T/(r_j a)^(k_T+1).         (6.1)
For (5.3), k_C=max(1,ceil(tau/theta)) gives the bound C_C L^(-tau), where
    C_C=Ccut k_C! [4/(rmin a)]^k_C.
For rounding, k_R=max(1,ceil(tau+1)) gives
    Cround N^(-4d)(1+log N)
       <=2 Cround k_R! d^(-k_R) L^(-tau).                (6.2)
The local bias constant is H(a+1)^m. Adding these rational constants yields
    |A_N(1)|<=C_scalar L^(-tau)
whenever
    L>=2,
    L^kappa>=8 rmax(a+1),
and, for the cutoff taper, L^kappa>=2 rmin(a+1)/alpha.
These conditions also pay the child qN, since L(qN)>=L(N). Equation (5.4)
therefore gives the explicit conservative rational certificate
    E_H(N)<=C_energy L^(-2tau),
    C_energy=2 C_scalar^2(1+|b|)^2.                      (6.3)

The executable generator caps factorial depth at64. Larger requested depth
does not invalidate the mathematical scalar estimate; it returns an unsupported
explicit-level certificate while preserving the other conditional arguments.
No gigantic N is constructed. Level constraints remain rational-power predicates
which a later exact compiler can check at a symbolic/finite starting L0.

If E_H must fit A*pi_H^2 L^(-eta), the additional exact condition is
    L^(2tau-eta)>=C_energy/(A*pi_H^2) when 2tau>eta;
for equality the ratio must be <=1. A negative exponent gap is not paid by
this envelope. Earlier levels and all cases below the threshold remain unpaid.
In particular, an eventual O(L^(-2tau)) bound does NOT automatically establish
the frozen domain starting N>=607 or its chosen numerical A. The certificate
and bound instantiator make this distinction explicit.

7. Sufficient generated inequality and cancellation bookkeeping

Choose any rational pi_B,pi_T,pi_H>0 with sum one. Weighted Cauchy gives
    E_h<=E_B/pi_B+E_T/pi_T+E_H/pi_H.                     (7.1)
For requested A>0 and 0<eta<=1, the following are mathematically sufficient:
    E_i(N)<=A*pi_i^2 L(N)^(-eta), i=B,T,H,
for EVERY N in the requested domain, and separate L2 integrability of B,T.
The constants recombine exactly to A sum pi_i=A. The detector can enumerate
these proof words with rational denominators; no coefficient is response-fit.
The API returns each premise OPEN_ALL_DOMAIN_BUDGET and exact constants,
integrability gates, and the harmonic level reduction. Even the harmonic
leaf stays unpaid at a fixed domain until threshold and head obligations pass.

This is a positive sufficient argument distinct from simply asking for the
same complete norm bound, but may be substantially stronger: it discards
beneficial crosses between comparison bias and finite cutoff remainder.
If this split fails, the correct next production is a signed joint bias/tail
auxiliary with a proved recovery rule, not a new assumption that either fake
pole is harmless. Collapsing to B+T+H with a renamed endpoint-energy premise
does not count as progress. Neither survival positivity, local coefficient
rates nor finite norm success can license (7.1)'s open arithmetic leaves.

8. Additional source-specific logarithmic primitive for future grammar

A narrow log-taper subgrammar can be treated structurally rather than by an
absolute source envelope. For positive INTEGERS k and g,
    W=(1-(log n/log N)^k)^g
is a finite polynomial in log n. Let D_N(z)=sum_(n<=N)mu(n)n^(-z).
The unrounded harmonic sum has the exact identity
    sum_(ell=0)^g (-1)^ell binom(g,ell)(log N)^(-k ell)
       sum_j w_j (-1)^(k ell) D_N^(k ell)(1+r_j beta_N).   (8.1)
All signs, units and the upper cutoff are retained. Infinite harmonic
comparisons are absolutely convergent for every beta_N>0; their derivatives
are locally bounded by the same analytic disk around one. For ell=0 the
moments give beta_N^m; for ell>=1 the constant derivative term at one need
not cancel, leaving at worst powers (log N)^(-k). Finite derivative tails
are paid by the complete absolute sums sum_(n>N)(log n)^j/n^(1+r beta),
which a finite integration-by-parts expansion bounds; because beta log N
tends infinity, those tail terms decay faster than any fixed log power.
Thus this particular harmonic subgrammar has an unconditional asymptotic
rate min(m(1-theta),k) at the scalar level, plus rounding. It still pays no
critical-line bias/truncation energy. General fractional log tapers are not
covered by (8.1); the current executable language deliberately leaves their
harmonic discrepancy open rather than inventing an integer derivative identity.
This is a possible future typed proof primitive, not a selected or tested winner.

Request uses recipe{id,base,auxiliary:null}, q, b, domain{kind,minimum}, and
rounding{kind:dyadic_power_floor,bits_multiplier:4,scale:ceil_log2_N,
drift:round_each_exponential_before_signed_sum,
product:multiply_rounded_children_exactly}. Base is drift_mixture{a,theta,terms}
or an exact product of one such profile and one frozen044 taper. Product child
orientation is generic. Domains all_integer and geometric_chain are retained;
the latter means a chain starting at the declared minimum with the saved q.
The generator requires |b|<sqrt(q) for the RH-bearing recurrence scope and
canonical rational strings; it returns UNSUPPORTED for legacy/control ASTs.
Unsupported means no admitted argument, never false formula.

Certificates bind the full request, finite source, meromorphic comparison,
discrepancy stages, signed Mellin expressions, partial summation, leaf statuses,
actual rounding/Harmonic rules, profile polynomial proof and level thresholds.
There is no identifier whitelist. The verifier does not call build_arguments
or its polynomial/moment constructors. It independently accumulates simultaneous
powers and identifies numerator/denominator polynomials by enough distinct
literal rational samples, then checks the exact source/theorem-schema payload.
Shared parsers and reviewed static identity/inequality constructors are trusted
code; this is an algebraic proof-packet checker, not a proof-assistant kernel.
It returns verified True only with uniform_forcing_bound_proved False and
whole_joint_energy_status OPEN. Any changed numerical proof flag, moment,
harmonic constant, source payload or child-recomputation rule is rejected.
