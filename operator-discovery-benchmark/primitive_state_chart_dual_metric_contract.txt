Metric algorithm
Minimize half||A*x-b||^2 over x>=0, starting cold with x=y=0,t=1,L=1.
For every nonzero finite column compute sigma_j=math.hypot(column_j),
D_j=sigma_j^2, then root_j=sqrt(D_j). All three are finite and positive.
Save the full D vector as metric_diagonal, a numerical diagnostic. D remains
fixed during the trajectory. L0=1 is a starting value, not a global bound.
For each step compute fresh r_y=A*y-b,g=A^T*r_y with guarded sparse fsum.
For each of at most32 trials:
  denominator_j=L*D_j, finite and positive;
  z_j=max(0,y_j-g_j/denominator_j), with ratio/candidate guards before clamp;
  d=z-y; compute fresh A*d and A*z-b;
  accept if ||A*d||^2<=L*||sqrt(D)*d||^2, otherwise double L.
Use finite guarded sqrt(D_j)*d_j and math.hypot to compute the weighted norm,
and separately guard its square and the L product. Never accept an infinite
right side. Carry the accepted L. Momentum, restart every64 accepted steps,
max10000 steps, accepted checkpoints and zero-reported-residual stop match033.
Floating products/roots/norms do not establish an exact metric or line test.

Final diagnostic at the last accepted x recomputes r,g and p_j=max(0,
x_j-g_j/(L*D_j)), then reports L*||sqrt(D)*(x-p)||. Its mapping_metric label
is diagonal column curvature. These units differ from033's unweighted norm.
