1. Complete core, delay and logarithmic bank
-------------------------------------------
Let f,F be the complete four-mode core of reviews005/006, including
f(1)=-beta-R_u, beta=9/2, R_u>0. All prefixes are zero below1. Put
 B_F(x)=sum_(n<=x)f(n)log n,
 J_F(x)=integral_1^x F(y)dy/y.
For ALL real x>=1, exact summation by parts is
                   B_F(x)=log x*F(x)-J_F(x).               (1.1)
At1 this is0=0; the unit is not recovered by dividing by log1.

For a fixed real a, the delayed signal and its actual coefficient are
 G(x)=F(x)-a F(x/2),
 g(n)=f(n)-a*1_(2 divides n)*f(n/2).
Both prefixes are constant on every integer unit cell, with G(1)=f(1).
Its exact logarithmic bank is
 B_G(x)=B_F(x)-a B_F(x/2)-a*log2*F(x/2),                   (1.2)
not merely the difference of the two B_F values. The last term is the
logarithmic cost of multiplying an integer coefficient index by2.
Define J_G(x)=integral_1^x G(y)dy/y; then universally
                   B_G(x)=log x*G(x)-J_G(x).               (1.3)
No source term is deleted by this identity.

For explicit proper-prime-power work, use the exact four-mode formula
 B_F(x)=sum_(r<=x)Lambda(r)*sum_i c_i D_ii(r)A_i(x/r),
 c=(beta,-beta,-R_u,-beta),
 D=diag(r^(-1/3)-2,r^(-1/3)-1-r^(-2/3),
        r^(-1/3)-r^(-2/3),-1).
All r are prime powers, including higher valuations, and all arguments
are <=x/2. Substitution in (1.2) retains the four bank channels, the
additional delayed term and every signed covariance with G. Individual
channels must not be separately centered or squared away without paying
their missing deterministic main/pole forcing.

2. Exact work family, cells and product-pair kernel
---------------------------------------------------
For dyadic X>=1024 and rational eta in[-2,2], define
 N_G(X)=integral_X^(2X) |G(x)|^2*x^(-2)dx,
 W_eta,G(X)=integral_X^(2X)
   [(1-eta)|G(x)|^2+eta*Re(conjugate(G(x))*B_G(x))/log x]
                                                     *x^(-2)dx. (2.1)
For the actual real source the real part is just G*B_G. This matches the
proposed eta menu{-1,-1/2,0,1/3,1/2,2/3,1,3/2,2}; the theorem covers
the entire stated interval. W can be SIGNED. No clipping or termwise
absolute value is part of its definition.

With G_n=G(n), B_n=B_G(n), exact integer-cell weights are
 d_n=1/n-1/(n+1),
 ell_n=integral_n^(n+1) dx/(x^2 log x)
       =Ei(-log(n+1))-Ei(-log n)>0.
Thus the finite full-cell expression is
 W_eta,G(X)=sum_(n=X)^(2X-1)
            [(1-eta)d_n*|G_n|^2+eta*ell_n*Re(conj(G_n)*B_n)]. (2.2)
Ei denotes its real branch on negative arguments. No log1 singularity
occurs because X>=1024. Rigorous interval integration or a proved Ei
evaluation is valid; replacing ell_n by an endpoint sample is a different
observable and requires a separately proved error/recovery rule.

The ordinary complete dyadic moment E_G=X^(-2)sum G_n^2 satisfies
                 N_G(X)<=E_G(X)<=4N_G(X).                 (2.3)
These comparisons are exact and lose no power or log degree.

For real coefficients the full quadratic product-pair kernel is
 W_eta,G(X)=sum_(h,k<=2X-1)g(h)g(k) K_eta,X(h,k),
 K_eta,X(h,k)=(1-eta)*sum_(n=max(X,h,k))^(2X-1)d_n
       +(eta/2)*log(h*k)*sum_(n=max(X,h,k))^(2X-1)ell_n.    (2.4)
Empty sums are zero. The unit log1 is zero, while its other contribution
remains. The symmetrization in (2.4) preserves all ordered cross terms,
diagonal terms, prime/composite sectors and valuations. No product-band
absolute value or gcd support deletion is present. Some kernel values
are negative for admissible eta; that alone neither proves cancellation
nor defeats the causal recovery below.

3. Uniform causal recovery, including the exact past-block head
--------------------------------------------------------------
By (1.3),
 W_eta,G(X)=N_G(X)-eta*Re integral_X^(2X)
                      conj(G(x))*J_G(x)/(x^2 log x)dx.     (3.1)
Split J_G(x)=J_G(X)+integral_X^x G(y)dy/y. In logarithmic coordinates
t=log x, h(t)=exp(-t/2)G(exp t), the local integration operator becomes
convolution on an interval of length log2 with kernel exp(-(t-s)/2),
s<=t. Its L1 mass, hence its L2 operator norm, is at most
                       lambda=2-sqrt2.
Division by log x has norm at most1/log X. The past-head contribution has
block norm bounded by
                   b_X=|J_G(X)|/(sqrt(2X)*log X).
Therefore, for
             gap_X=1-|eta|lambda/log X>0,
 W_eta,G(X)>=gap_X*N_G(X)-|eta|*b_X*sqrt(N_G(X)).           (3.2)
Young gives the exact useful recovery inequality
 N_G(X)<=2W_eta,G(X)/gap_X+eta^2*b_X^2/gap_X^2.             (3.3)
This is valid even if W is negative. Replacing its first coefficient by
a larger constant times W is not valid when W<0; instead apply an upper
bound for W BEFORE increasing that coefficient.

For X=2^j, ALL previous dyadic blocks partition[1,X). Cauchy on each
block yields
 |integral_Y^(2Y) G(y)dy/y|<=sqrt(Y)*sqrt(N_G(Y)).
With A0=1+sqrt2=sum_(k>=1)2^(-k/2), weighted Cauchy then gives
 J_G(X)^2/X <=A0*sum_(k=1)^j 2^(-k/2)*N_G(X/2^k).         (3.4)
This includes the block[1,2), hence the nonzero unit; no head is omitted.
Combining (3.3),(3.4) gives a STRICTLY SMALLER-SCALE bound:
 N_G(X)<=2W_eta,G(X)/gap_X
    +[eta^2*A0/(2gap_X^2*(log X)^2)]
                    *sum_(k=1)^j 2^(-k/2)N_G(X/2^k).      (3.5)
No companion source bound or current-scale iteration appears on the right.

One FIXED cutoff handles the whole eta menu. At every X>=1024,
 log X>=10log2>69/10,
 lambda<3/5, |eta|<=2, gap_X>4/5, A0^2=3+2sqrt2<6.
The TOTAL smaller-scale mass in (3.5) is bounded by

       <12/[(4/5)^2*(69/10)^2]<2/5.                       (3.6)
The last inequality is an exact rational check: its denominator is
19044/625>30, so12 divided by it is less than2/5.

Suppose the ACTUAL-SOURCE one-sided work lemma is independently proved:

                   W_eta,G(X)<=K*(1+log X)^p, K>=0,p>=0. (3.7)
Let the full finite head be

4. Signed recurrences and full-target recovery
----------------------------------------------
The detector may enumerate eta, a, rho and integer log forcing degree k,
then conjecture, with C>=0 independent of scale,
 W_eta,G(2X)<=rho*W_eta,G(X)+C*(1+log(2X))^k,
                           EVERY dyadic X>=1024.          (4.1)
The repeated W in (4.1) is SIGNED. Upper iteration only uses rho>=0;
it does not require W>=0. The finite base is W_eta,G(1024), with
Wbase_plus=max(0,W_eta,G(1024)). For0<=rho<1,
 W_eta,G(X)<=[Wbase_plus+C/(1-rho)]*(1+log X)^k.            (4.2)
For rho=1,
 W_eta,G(X)<=[Wbase_plus+C/log2]*(1+log X)^(k+1).           (4.3)
The recurrence is only applied above cutoff; its full positive energy
head is recovered using section3. A negative observed base poses no
problem; deleting its finite-head recovery would pose a problem.
Rho>1 gives a generic power cost and has no automatic cofinal rule.
In (4.1)-(4.3), log(2X) is the natural logarithm of the product2X.
Unadorned log in the exact arithmetic/integration identities is always
natural. The implementation's BASE-TWO bound weights are handled next.

Exact primary implementation constants: let L2(X)=1+log_2 X and suppose
 W_eta,G(2X)<=rho*W_eta,G(X)+C*L2(X)^k,
            EVERY dyadic X>=1024, C>=0, k>=0.              (4.4)
Take B>=max(0,W_eta,G(1024)). For0<=rho<1 define
              K=B+C/(1-rho), p=k;
for rho=1 define
              K=B+C, p=k+1.
Then, with NO additional numerical or natural-log conversion factor,
            W_eta,G(X)<=K*L2(X)^p, X>=1024.               (4.5)
Indeed writing X=2^j, j>=10, geometric iteration sums at most C/(1-rho)
times(1+j)^k in the strict-gap case. For rho1 the number of steps is at
most j, so the sum is <=C*(1+j)^(k+1), and the base is bounded by B times
the same factor. If C=0, both cases sharpen to K=B,p=0, independently
of the recorded k. A pole gate should use this sharper actual consequence.

Section3 induction uses only monotonicity of its weight, not its logarithm
base. Apply it with L2(X)^p verbatim: with H0 from that section and
D=max(H0,5K), one gets
        N_G(X)<=D*L2(X)^p, E_G(X)<=4D*L2(X)^p
at EVERY dyadic X>=1. No constants are changed. Head weights are at least1,

5. Critical-pole degree gate and cumulative-work caution
--------------------------------------------------------
The current obligation links the independently saved witness

The full F and every13 delayed G have a surviving leading double pole at

for gamma0 signed recurrences, admissible necessary floors are

This is a necessary filter, not proof of any admitted recurrence. Signed
storage does not evade it once its proved recovery gives the target bound.
With a general surviving order d the floor is p>=2d-2.

Cumulative signed work can be coercive too, but pays a different degree.
For L>=1024 define Q_eta(H)=integral_L^H the same work integrand and
A(H)=integral_L^H G^2/x^2. The full Volterra tail norm is2, so
 g=1-2|eta|/log L>0,

 Q_eta(H)>=g A(H)-|eta| b sqrt(A(H)).
Thus S(H)=Q_eta(H)+eta^2*b^2/(2g)>=g A(H)/2>=0. This is proved aggregate
positivity despite signed local work, with an explicit finite head. It
does not grant arbitrary indefinite potential boundary terms for free.

If S(H)=O((1+log H)^p), then A has the same upper growth. Stieltjes
Laplace integration gives
 integral_L^infinity G^2/x^2*x^(-delta)dx=O(delta^(-p)),
and Mellin Cauchy gives |I_G(1/2+delta+i*t)|=O(delta^(-(p+1)/2)).
A surviving order-d leading singularity therefore requires p>=2d-1,
so the current double pole requires cumulative degree at least3.
This is why the shell language is primary: its induction does not pay
or conceal this extra cumulative log. Cumulative storage is a legitimate
optional language if its stronger degree gate and head are retained.
