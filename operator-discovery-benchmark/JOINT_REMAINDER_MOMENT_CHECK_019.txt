INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF JOINT REMAINDER MOMENT REVIEW 018

Disposition
-----------
The proposed below-half-line Mellin/L2 component rule is sound under the
explicit unconditional fourth-moment provider and the centering premises
listed below. The proof gives a uniform finite constant, without evaluating
that constant or any actual arithmetic coefficient. It supplies no estimate
for the complete signed core and no RH conclusion.

Only this new audit is written. No frozen source, checker, result or receipt
is changed. No tests, arithmetic experiments, source tables or hashes are run.
The next executable stage must distinguish supplied analytical rules from
generated parameter choices, bound certificates and exact source partitions.

1. Moment provider and independently checked exponent budget
-----------------------------------------------------------
The imported fact is the unconditional fourth-moment upper bound
 integral_0^Y |zeta(1/2+it)|^4 dt <= B4*Y*(1+log Y)^4,
with one fixed finite B4 and every Y>=2. The primary research source read
for this audit is Bettin, Bui, Li and Radziwill, A quadratic divisor problem
and moments of the Riemann zeta-function:
 https://arxiv.org/html/1609.02539v1
Its introduction records the classical fourth-moment result; Theorem 1.1
with constant Dirichlet polynomials and zero shifts also supplies a stronger
smoothed statement. Ingham's original bibliographic reference is
 https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-27.1.273
The original full proof was not accessible here. No twisted short-polynomial
length theorem, conjectural higher moment, RH or Lindelof premise is imported.
The functional equation reference used by the reviewed proof is
 https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4.E2

Off-critical fourth moments follow without an additional arithmetic theorem:
remove the entire principal part of zeta(s)^2 at1, multiply by a Gaussian
centered on the dyadic height, and apply the analytic L2 three-lines theorem
between real parts1/2 and2. The left boundary is controlled by the provider
and all Gaussian tails; the right by the absolutely convergent series. The
Gaussian is bounded below on the requested dyadic interval. Restoring the
principal part changes a fixed constant only. This is a strip-Hardy argument,
not pointwise interpolation of already integrated quantities.

Let m>=2,p>=0 be fixed integers, 0<u<1, theta=1-u and
 0<sigma<min(1/2,theta).
On a height interval[Y,2Y], the functional equation contributes
 Y^(m*(1-2sigma)) to |zeta(sigma+it)|^(2m). The off-critical
fourth moment contributes Y*(1+log Y)^4. Elementary convexity for
the extra2m-4 factors on real part1-sigma contributes
 Y^((m-2)*sigma+(m-2)*epsilon).
Convexity for |zeta(sigma+u+it)|^2 contributes
 Y^(1-sigma-u+epsilon), and division by |s|^2 costs Y^(-2).
The resulting DYADIC INTEGRAL exponent is therefore exactly
 d=m-u-(m+3)*sigma+(m-1)*epsilon.
The term with no zeta(sigma+it) factor has exponent
 d0=-sigma-u+epsilon and must also be checked. These are integral
exponents; an extra dyadic-length factor must not be added afterward.
Strict d<0 and d0<0 absorb the fixed height-log degree and pay every
positive and negative dyadic tail. Equality does not establish this rule.

For1/2<=sigma<theta, the separate direct-fourth-moment branch has
 d=(m-2)-u-(m-1)*sigma+(m-1)*epsilon.
The below-half formula must not be extrapolated into this domain. All
provider, convexity, functional-equation and compact-line constants depend
only on fixed parameters and the imported B4, never on U or X.

2. Full finite weights and removable-pole audit
---------------------------------------------
For A(n)=mu(n)1_(n<=U), c=sum_(d<=U)mu(d)/d and T=c*delta-A,
 |A(sigma+it)|<=U^(1-sigma)/(1-sigma),
 |T(sigma+it)|<=[2+1/(1-sigma)]U^(1-sigma).
These bounds include the actual unit. In particular T(1)=c-1 as a
coefficient need not vanish; it is the Dirichlet value T_hat(1)=0 that
removes the pole of E(s)=1+zeta(s)T(s) at1.

For the complete function
 F_U(s)=zeta(s+u)A(s)^p E(s)^m,
the vertical squared finite-weight cost is
 U^a, a=2*(p+m)*(1-sigma).
Using |1+z|^(2m)<=2^(2m-1)*(1+|z|^(2m)) and rounding the smaller
finite-weight power up to a is valid for every U>=1. This does not erase
any signed term in the operator: the pole is removed in the FULL E factor
before this upper bound. Expanding zeta powers and contour-shifting their
individual principal parts independently would require further compensators.

In sigma<=Re(s)<=2 the only possible remaining pole is the simple one
at theta from zeta(s+u), with residue
 M=A(theta)^p E(theta)^m.
It may be removable if that residue vanishes. Division by s introduces
no crossed pole because sigma>0. No reciprocal zeta factor, zero-free
region or claim about individual source-term independence is needed.

For m2 the earlier all-real-prefix hyperbola estimate
 |P_(E^2)(t)|<=30U(1+log U)*sqrt(t)
proves ordinary conditional convergence at theta2/3 after any fixed finite
A^p convolution. Abel continuation identifies the value with
A(theta)^p E(theta)^2; its whole tail is included. For m>=3 an analytic

An executable certificate must either prove that separate premise or label

3. Gaussian Perron and global Mellin norm
----------------------------------------
Start on Re(s)=2, where the original coefficient Dirichlet series and
Gaussian Perron integral are absolutely controlled. For fixed e>0 the
multiplier exp(e*s^2) decays as exp(-e*t^2) on every vertical strip.
Fixed-U polynomial growth makes both horizontal contour edges tend to zero.
Crossing theta yields exactly
 P_e(x)-(M/theta)*exp(e*theta^2)*x^theta
   =(1/(2pi*i))integral_(sigma) F_U(s)exp(e*s^2)x^s ds/s.
The point1 is removable;0 is not crossed. The multiplier is Gaussian
smoothing of the COMPLETE staircase prefix in log x, not a prefix begun
at the current block.

The proved vertical L2 bound allows Mellin Plancherel. The multiplier
converges to1 in that vertical L2 space by dominated convergence; inverse
Mellin functions therefore converge in the physical weighted L2 space.
Their almost-everywhere Gaussian limit identifies the limiting function
 C(x)=P(x)-(M/theta)x^theta.
Consequently the full integral over0<x<infinity with weight
x^(-2sigma-1) is bounded by a fixed constant times U^a.
Uniformity in U comes from the vertical norm, not from interchanging U
with the fixed-U contour-height limit.

For0<x<1 the function C(x) is explicitly -(M/theta)x^theta.
This entire lower tail is integrable precisely because sigma<theta.
It is already included in the global norm. The original coefficient
sequence still vanishes below its actual unit; no fictional coefficient
or deleted lower tail is introduced.

4. Integer staircase, history-preserving channels and recovery
-------------------------------------------------------------
The discrete centered prefix is
 C_n=P(n)-(M/theta)n^theta,
whose coefficient subtraction is exactly (M/theta)*J_theta,
 J_theta(n)=n^theta-(n-1)^theta, J_theta(1)=1.
On[n,n+1), its difference from C(x) is bounded by
2|M|x^(theta-1). Comparing the cell weights gives the safe constants8/32
in Review018 for sigma<1/2. For the generic above-half domain sigma<theta<1
use16/64 instead; this clarification has been incorporated into Review018.
The correction is globally summable, including n1, because
2+2sigma-2theta>0.

Also |M|^2 is bounded by a fixed multiple of
U^(2*(p+m)*(1-theta)), which is at most U^a because sigma<theta.
Thus the integer weighted norm has the same U exponent as the contour norm.
Restricting the GLOBAL sum to[X,2X) gives normalized block energy
 O(U^a*X^(2sigma-1)), EVERY integer U,X>=1.
The same fixed U applies to the child2X; its coefficient array and residue
must not be rebuilt using a different cutoff inside one recovery identity.

For a polynomial channel q(t)=sum_j q_j*t^j and eta>=0, discrete Abel
summation of the centered coefficient retains every earlier C_k. With
L_n=log(2n), the internal difference is bounded by
 P1/(k*L_n), P1=sum_j j*|q_j|, while the endpoint costs
 P0=sum_j |q_j|. In weighted l2(n^(-2sigma-1)), Schur's test bounds
the positive Hardy operator sum_(k<n)|C_k|/k by1+1/sigma.
The resulting valid rational channel norm factor is
 2^ceil(eta)*[P0+2*(1+1/sigma)*P1].
Its square must be summed over the full channel vector. Orthogonal cell
averaging contracts the block l2 norm, so arbitrary complete cell partitions
are covered. There is no restarted history and no uncharged log-degree loss.

The exact source still has its opposite J_theta drift, every other E/C/One
binomial companion, the inherited paid vector, all channel and transport
routes, and the exact -e. A newly paid centered component does not prove any
bound on those remaining signed terms or their correlations. Source scalar
and inverse coefficient multiply the component constant through their square.
