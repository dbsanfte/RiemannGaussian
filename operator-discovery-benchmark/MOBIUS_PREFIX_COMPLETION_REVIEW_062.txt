MOBIUS PREFIX FLOOR COMPLETION REVIEW 062
=======================================
Scope
-----
This source-only review reads the061 handoff and one archived061 feedback
JSON. No scientific arrays, LPs, candidate enumeration, integrals, source
calls, norms or tests were evaluated. All061 and earlier files remain
unchanged. The review establishes exact reconstruction/majorization rules
and a useful uniform coefficient-cost construction. It does NOT establish
the missing short-residual or full joint-interior bound.

The active source remains the actual ordinary Mobius prefix

 S_N(y,t)=sum_d mu_M(d)f_d(t),
 f_d(t)=1_(d<=y)[p^-t W(N/(pd))-q^-t W(N/(qd))],
 mu_M(d)=mu(d)1_(gcd(d,R)=1), W(x)=floor4x-3floor2x+2floorx.

Here p<q are registered primes, R=product(M), k=|M|>=2, alpha in(0,1),
eps=min(tau,N^-gamma), gamma>0 and tau in(0,1). All actual outside prime
factors, cofactor1, inclusive boundaries, p/q exits and signed cross terms
remain. The source f is zero after D0=floor(4N/p). Its prefix depends on
y only through the integer Y=floor y. This does not eliminate the real-y
weight in061's variation integral.

1. A reusable exact floor-completion certificate
------------------------------------------------
Choose a finite positive floor list X_j, integer short cutoff H>=0, and
exact rational coefficient columns a_j,b_j. Keep

 r=(p/q)^t, c_j(t)=p^-t(a_j-r b_j),
 C_d(t)=sum_j c_j(t)floor(X_j/d).

Define rational short residual columns u_d,v_d by

 1_(d<=Y)W(N/(pd))=sum_j a_j floor(X_j/d)+u_d,
 1_(d<=Y)W(N/(qd))=sum_j b_j floor(X_j/d)+v_d.             (1)

A complete exact certificate verifies(1) for ALL integer
1<=d<=D*=max(D0,max_j floor X_j), with u_d=v_d=0 for d>H.
For d>D* the target and every floor are zero, so this finite check proves
the identities on the whole positive source domain. If H>D*, the residual
is simply zero after D*, but a large H does not by itself give an
admissible residual-cost rate. H=0 means complete exact reconstruction.

No extra source horizon is silently truncated to D0 or y. An X_j>D0
requires verification of the newly created region D0<d<=floor X_j,
where the target is zero and completion columns must cancel. Individual
floors may not be convolved at a smaller horizon. A column with X_j<1
is exactly zero, including its smooth count. For integer d the floor
action depends only on floor X_j; rational X_j are still useful for
moment certificates, but cannot encode unverified fractional source rows.

The two exact rational column checks in(1) imply every t in the band:

 f_d(t)=C_d(t)+p^-t(u_d-r v_d), residual supported d<=H.   (2)

Checking finitely many numerical t values does not replace this algebra.
Checking only the old quarter-grid geometry in v=N/d also does not
verify the new cutoff at Y. A prefix can split one of those cells: if
two source rows d=Y,Y+1 have identical columns but different target
values, exact completion with H<Y is impossible for that basis. This
identical-row obstruction is exact and source-free; the basis needs a
new distinguishing floor or must keep that row in the priced residual.

2. Complete convolution and an exact joint price
------------------------------------------------
Let Psi_M(X) count M-smooth positive integers <=X, including1, and be
zero below1. The elementary mu_M*One law gives, with full horizons,

 S_N(y,t)=sum_j c_j(t)Psi_M(X_j)
                  +p^-t sum_(d<=H)mu_M(d)(u_d-r v_d).    (3)

This is exact even if added horizons exceed the original source. It is
not a new assumed Mobius Dirichlet-series bound. Its universally valid
positive route is

 |S_N(y,t)|<=p^-t [sum_j Psi_M(X_j)|a_j-r b_j|
                                  +sum_(d<=H)|u_d-r v_d|]. (4)

One may omit residual rows proved to have mu_M(d)=0, but must never
drop d=1 or unregistered primes merely because M is finite. Bounding
by all short rows is always safe. Formula(4) retains the p/q affine
dependence INSIDE each absolute value. Replacing it by separate channel
prices is a weaker optional rule and must disclose the lost cancellation.

For fixed N,y and fixed columns the bracket in(4) is convex in r. Thus
its maximum on any certified interval [r_lo,r_hi] occurs at an endpoint.
The actual interval is [(p/q)^tau,(p/q)^eps]. A safe rational enclosure
may be used; [0,1] is a generic conservative one. This supplies an exact
uniform-in-t price without trusting sampled t values. Alternatively
the finitely many rational roots of the affine factors partition r into
exact sign intervals. Measurability is then immediate. These finite
rules are NOT proofs that the coefficients or residuals have an
admissible rate as N varies.

There is also a valid endpoint-construction variant. Because 0<t<1,
r lies in [r_0,1], with rational r_0=p/q. At each endpoint s=r_0,1
one may independently find an exact completion of

 g_s(d)=1_(d<=Y)[W(N/(pd))-s W(N/(qd))]
       =sum_j c_(s,j)floor(X_j/d)+e_(s,d),

with e_(s,d)=0 for d>H. Take the UNION of the two floor lists, pad
missing columns by zero, and check every row through the largest
union horizon. If the endpoint residual cutoffs differ, use their
maximum and price every resulting short row. For
lambda=(1-r)/(1-r_0), exact affine interpolation gives

 g_r=lambda g_(r_0)+(1-lambda)g_1,
 c_j(r)=lambda c_(r_0,j)+(1-lambda)c_(1,j),
 e_d(r)=lambda e_(r_0,d)+(1-lambda)e_(1,d).

Multiplying by p^-t recovers the actual source. The full price is at
most lambda times the first endpoint price plus (1-lambda) times the
second, hence at most their maximum. Both floor identities, not merely
their objective values, must be verified exactly. Different endpoint
optimizers are allowed: this need not optimize W_p and W_q separately.
It is equivalent to a common affine coefficient a_j-r b_j, where
a_j=(c_(r_0,j)-r_0 c_(1,j))/(1-r_0) and
b_j=(c_(r_0,j)-c_(1,j))/(1-r_0). Balanced first moments at both
endpoints imply balance throughout the band. This is a uniform-in-t
algebraic certificate at a fixed N,y; its all-N coefficient and residual
rates remain separate source-bound obligations.

If all X_j<=C_X N^nu for fixed C_X,nu, then
Psi_M(X_j)<=[1+log_2(max(1,C_X N^nu))]^k. Consequently a uniform
coefficient-L1 rate O(N^delta polylog N) and residual-L1 rate
O(N^beta polylog N), with delta,beta<=1/2, pay an admissible
square-root/polylog PREFIX bound via(4). The statement must hold for
every integer N>=2 and every prefix in061's active interior, including
small-source/base exceptions, not merely the finite LP requests.

A uniform prefix bound C sqrtN*(1+log_2(4N))^A implies the061 core
bound with coefficient at most3R C/(k-alpha): integrate y^(-t-1)
to at most1/t and then G_M(t)<=R t^k. Thus this route has explicit
recovery with no hidden derivative or parameter loss. More general
weighted budgets may be integrated directly if their complete domains
and finite heads are proved. Existing058 endpoint requirements remain.

3. A concrete generic completion with paid coefficient mass
-----------------------------------------------------------
The following constructive rule does not use scientific source values.
For an integer threshold T>=1 and K>=0 define

 I_K(T,d)=sum_(ell<=K)mu(ell)floor(T/(ell d)).              (5)

The coefficient is ORDINARY mu(ell), not mu_M(ell). Complete ordinary
mu*One convolution proves

 I_K(T,d)=1_(d<=T) whenever floor(T/d)<=K.                 (6)

In particular for T>H and K=floor(T/(H+1)), (6) holds for every
integer d>H. Each added floor has horizon at most T. Replacing mu by
mu_M in(5) would produce a smooth-count function rather than the
indicator; it is not an interchangeable primitive.

Now expand a single truncated W channel. Let Y=floor y. For b in{4,2,1}
take coefficients A_b=1,-3,2 respectively and Z_b=bN/p. Since
floor(Z_b/d)=sum_(m>=1)1_(d<=floor(Z_b/m)),

 1_(d<=Y)W(N/(pd))
    =sum_b A_b sum_(m>=1)1_(d<=min(Y,floor(Z_b/m))).       (7)

For d>H retain only thresholds T=min(Y,floor(Z_b/m))>H.
There are at most floor(Z_b/(H+1)) such m. Replace each retained
indicator by(5), with its own K=floor(T/(H+1)); collect identical
floor columns before pricing if useful. This gives exact completion
for every d>H. All residuals are computed by(1) on EVERY d<=H.
The construction also works if Y<=H: then there are no completion
terms and the whole target is a short residual.

Using |mu|<=1, K<=Z_b/[m(H+1)], and the harmonic estimate
sum_(m<=L)1/m<=1+log(max(1,L)), the coefficient L1 of the p channel
is bounded by

 C_p <=12N/[p(H+1)]
              *[1+log(max(1,4N/[p(H+1)]))].              (8)

The factor12 is 4+3*2+2*1 from the three W floors. The analogous
q bound has q in place of p. For the joined coefficient at each t,
L1<=p^-t C_p+q^-t C_q. Coalescing columns can only improve this
conservative bound, and every new X_j is <=D0. No added horizon is
ignored or licensed by an approximate pattern.

More generally, fix any rational eta in [0,1] and choose H=floor(N^eta).
Then H+1>N^eta, and(8) proves coefficient mass
O(N^(1-eta)(1+log N)) and smooth-count charge
O(N^(1-eta)(1+log N)^(k+1)), uniformly in every prefix Y.
The constants depend only on the fixed primes and eta, not on a
scientific source table. Thus eta>=1/2 pays the coefficient part at
square-root/polylog scale. It does not pay the joined short defect.

For H=floor sqrtN, H+1>sqrtN, so(8) supplies a PROVED
O(sqrtN logN) coefficient-mass bound. Its smooth-count charge is
O(sqrtN log^(k+1)N), uniformly for every prefix Y and N>=2.
This is useful paid progress for a real completion family, independently
of the unresolved residual. It is not a full signed-core theorem.

4. Why a short support is not a paid short cost
----------------------------------------------
The indicator completion can be large on d<=H. The elementary bound

 |I_K(T,d)|<=T/d*(1+log(max(1,K)))                        (9)

does NOT imply O(H) residual L1. Already K=1 gives
I_1(T,d)=floor(T/d), and indicator residual at d=1 is1-T for T>=1.
The defect has no long support, but its unit can be large. For larger
K the coefficient cancellations require actual arithmetic estimates;
they may not be assumed merely from mu*One's complete identity.
The signed p/q combinations in(7) may cancel some defects, but those
combinations must be retained and priced, not presumed to cancel.

For a generic full interpolation there is no existence obstruction:
floors floor(j/d), 1<=j<=D*, form a triangular integer matrix in
descending j,d, with unit diagonal. They reconstruct any finite target
exactly, with H=0. Their coefficient mass can be expensive. Alternatively
matching only d>H leaves the short rows free; adding small-horizon
columns can repair them, but those columns have a price too. Existence
of a solution, zero long residual or a low finite LP objective is not
a uniform coefficient/residual-rate certificate.

The actual difficulty can be exposed without a norm restatement. For
one indicator, (5) gives the exact finite convolution

 sum_d mu_M(d)I_K(T,d)=sum_(ell<=K)mu(ell)Psi_M(T/ell).

When (6) holds outside H,

 sum_(d<=H)mu_M(d)[1_(d<=T)-I_K(T,d)]
 =M_M(T)-sum_(ell<=K)mu(ell)Psi_M(T/ell),                 (10)

where M_M(T)=sum_(d<=T)mu_M(d). Thus a SHORT signed kernel may encode
a CURRENT-SCALE Mertens remainder. Assuming it is small just because
its support is short would be circular. In the illustrative M-empty
case, Psi is the constant unit count on arguments>=1 and, for the
constructed K<=T, (10) is M(T)-M(K). This
example is not a change to the licensed k>=2 source; it makes the
hidden recovery loss explicit. For the actual joined W source all
corresponding p/q threshold terms stay signed together.

5. Cheap bounded bases, moment guards and the correct trade-off
--------------------------------------------------------------
A bounded elementary atom is

 B_X(d)=floor(X/d)-2floor(X/(2d)) in{0,1}.

It equals1_(d<=X) for d>X/2, has floor-coefficient mass3 and zero
linear first moment. It is a cheap indicator completion only with
short horizon at least X/2. For X of order N this does not yield a
sqrtN residual horizon. A multiscale Boolean OR of its digits could
represent an indicator, but products of floors are a NEW basis and
do not inherit the mu_M*One smooth-count rule without another proof.
No free linear completion is obtained by calling that OR a sum.

More generally, for any completion let
L_c(t)=sum_j|c_j(t)| and M_1(t)=sum_j c_j(t)X_j. Since
floor(X_j/d)=X_j/d-frac(X_j/d),

 |C_d(t)|<=|M_1(t)|/d+L_c(t).

As |f_d(t)|<=2, this yields the conservative EXACT residual rule

 sum_(d<=H)|f_d(t)-C_d(t)|
 <=H[2+L_c(t)]+|M_1(t)|[1+log H],                       (11)

for H>=1, with zero for H=0. In particular a balanced moment M_1=0
pays the residual by H[2+L_c]. To bind this for the whole t band it
suffices to check sum a_j X_j=sum b_j X_j=0. Joint affine price
may also certify an approximate moment without splitting p/q signs.

This is a reusable positive route, but its exponent must be priced:
if H=O(N^eta) and L_c=O(N^delta polylogN), the balanced-moment route
requires eta+delta<=1/2 for an RH-strength residual, in addition to
delta<=1/2 for the smooth part. With H near sqrtN and the generic
coefficient bound(8), this coarse rule gives power1, not power1/2.
For the entire generic indicator trade-off delta=1-eta, the same
coarse moment-balanced rule has eta+delta=1. Hence that bound alone
never closes the square-root residual requirement, for any eta in
[0,1]. This is a limitation of this particular positive estimate, not
an impossibility theorem for exact indicator combinations, better
short-defect control, or other floor completions.
A detector must improve the actual short defect or discover a different
completion trade-off; it cannot hide this product in an asymptotic constant.

6. Next source-bound leaf and noncircular proof gate
---------------------------------------------------
The062 construction genuinely changes the interior proof coordinates:
it can move the complete large-cofactor prefix into priced smooth counts
and an exact SHORT kernel, with a uniform coefficient bound available
from(7)-(8). The remaining source-bound leaf is the joined residual
variation in(4), or a separately proved signed residual estimate with
complete recovery. It is not the original norm renamed, but it can
still contain an equivalent current-scale correlation as(10) shows.

Useful detector certificates are: exact two-channel reconstruction on
all integer source/addition rows; origin/unit retention; complete floor
horizons; exact joint affine price; convex endpoint admission for t;
uniform coefficient mass; balanced/approximate first moment; every
short residual and its bound; and explicit all-N/all-prefix quantifiers.
Finite counterexamples can reject a recipe. Finite LP success or
numerical scaling can only propose its remaining rate, not pay it.

The061 reciprocal-interval tail identity is another exact presentation
of the same frontier. Floor completion can attempt to cancel those
tail intervals, but must either retain every induced short row as
above or establish a new signed divisor/reciprocal correlation lemma.
Assumed RH-strength bounds on M_M or a companion are not providers.

No generic impossibility for all completions with admissible total cost
was proved. The explicit generic completion pays its coefficient part,
but the short residual part remains OPEN. A uniform proof of both parts
would imply the061 interior bound, then the already-paid058 source/RH
bridge with its original endpoint scope. Those are conditional
implications, not a new RH assertion.
