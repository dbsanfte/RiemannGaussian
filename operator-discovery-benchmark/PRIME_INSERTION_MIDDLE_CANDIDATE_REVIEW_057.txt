2. Exact prime-exchange decomposition, without squarefree shortcuts
===================================================================
For every n>=1 and t>0,

 G_(6n)-G_(10n)
 =G_(2n)[5^(-t) 1_(5 does not divide n)
                         -3^(-t) 1_(3 does not divide n)].  (3)

This uses only the radical product. It retains n divisible by 3 or 5,
both, repeated prime powers, and arbitrary other shared factors.
If neither divides n the sign is negative; if only 3 divides n it is
positive; if only 5 divides n it is negative; if both divide n it is zero.
Consequently the exchange is not a single positive prime-gap factor over
the full source. Coprime-only reasoning would omit its positive sector.

For real x>0 define the INCLUSIVE unit filter

 h_R^+(x,t)=sum_{n>=1}v_x(n)G_(Rn)(t),
 v_x(n)=1_(2x<n<=4x)-2*1_(x<n<=2x).

Equivalently its prefix coefficients are (2,-3,1). Its relation to the
original n>=2 filter is

 h_R^+(x,t)=h_R(x,t)+G_R(t)v_x(1),
 v_x(1)=1_(2x<1<=4x)-2*1_(x<1<=2x), |v_x(1)|<=2.          (4)

This actual unit indicator is zero for every real x>=1. It is +1 on
1/4<=x<1/2 and -2 on 1/2<=x<1. It must not be confused with the full
d=1 DIVISOR contribution W(x)=floor(4x)-3floor(2x)+2floor(x), which can
be nonzero even when x>1 and |W(x)|<=1. Summing (3), and removing
multiples by n=pm, proves the exact
scale relation

 h_6(N,t)-h_10(N,t)
 =(5^(-t)-3^(-t))h_2^+(N,t)
       +3^(-t)h_6^+(N/3,t)-5^(-t)h_10^+(N/5,t).             (5)

No coprimality premise or simple-prime restriction is needed. Multiples
of all orders are handled by the complete lower real-argument prefix.
The inclusive unit convention is essential to (5). For integer N>=3
the N/3 argument has no actual unit correction. The N/5 correction
occurs only at N=3,4 (and at N=2 if its base is used); all N>=5 are
unit-free. Real arguments still retain their possibly nonzero d=1
divisor contribution W(N/p) in any factorized expansion.

More generally, for distinct primes p,q not already dividing a fixed R,

 h_(Rp)^+(x)-h_(Rq)^+(x)
 =(q^(-t)-p^(-t))h_R^+(x)
   +p^(-t)h_(Rp)^+(x/p)-q^(-t)h_(Rq)^+(x/q).                (6)

This is an enumerable source-specific transport rule. It trades explicit
q-free masks for smaller real arguments and actual Laplace attenuations.
It is not a scalar norm contraction. The current-scale channel h_R stays
and its multiplier does not vanish identically on the middle interval.

The exact selected B is the common-cutoff integral of (5). To compare a
lower argument x=N/p with a separately defined channel, extend its cutoff
as epsilon(x)=min(1/2,x^(-gamma)), and keep the attenuation p^(-t):

 integral_epsilon_N^tau p^(-t)h_(Rp)(x,t)t^(-6/5)dt
 = integral_epsilon(x)^tau p^(-t)h_(Rp)(x,t)t^(-6/5)dt
  +integral_epsilon_N^epsilon(x)
                         p^(-t)h_(Rp)(x,t)t^(-6/5)dt.       (7)

The weighted channel on the right is not an ordinary J_(Rp). Replacing
it by J_(Rp) introduces the additional signed integral with multiplier
p^(-t)-1. The units from (4) add
v_x(1) integral_epsilon_N^tau p^(-t)G_(Rp)(t)t^(-6/5)dt.
Its normalized absolute value is <=2K(Rp)/alpha<=2log(Rp)^alpha/alpha,
so this particular head is paid uniformly. Fractional x<2 is not an
ordinary licensed integer middle channel; it remains an explicit finite
head rather than an invented source value or an implicit basecase. No
uniform norm or recurrence for these real-argument weighted channels is
inherited from the frozen integer-N>=2 theorem. Their exact definitions
are new proof-language states, and any bounds used on them need their
own explicit domain or a proved transfer rule.

What remains unpaid in (5)-(7) is the JOINT weighted current-channel
forcing and the actual lower-argument correlation, with their cutoff

There is no extra universal Taylor order from this exchange. On an n with
