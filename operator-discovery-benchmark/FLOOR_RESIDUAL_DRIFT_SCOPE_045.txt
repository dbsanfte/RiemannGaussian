SIGNED LOGARITHMIC DRIFT: INDEPENDENT SCOPE045

Only new045 source files are owned here. Earlier numerical and analytic
artifacts remain unchanged. No source array or finite residual norm is a
premise of this proof admission.

1. Moving rational drift

Let L=1+floor(log2 N), a>0 rational, and 0<theta<1 rational.
Set k=ceil(a L^theta), beta=k/L. At every integer N the exponent is
rational, and exact ceiling gives
 a L^(theta-1)<=beta<a L^(theta-1)+1/L.
In particular beta=a L^(-(1-theta))(1+O(L^-theta)); beta->0 and
beta log N->infinity. The latter distinguishes this construction from a
fixed Mellin transfer, but is not a proof of a global norm bound. Resource
limits may refuse a finite compilation; the mathematical definition does
not stop at that resource threshold.

2. Signed mixtures and all-index Taylor bound

For fixed distinct positive rational rates r_j and rational signed weights
w_j let sum w_j=1. If M_k=sum w_j r_j^k vanishes for 1<=k<m while
M_m!=0, then with z=beta log n>=0
 P_beta(n)=sum w_j exp(-r_j z)
 =1+(-1)^m M_m z^m/m!+O(z^(m+1)) at every fixed n.
Taylor's integral remainder before order m obeys
 |P_beta(n)-1|<=z^m/m! *sum |w_j| r_j^m
 for EVERY n>=1. This uses r_j>0 so the mth derivative of exp(-r_j z)
is bounded in absolute value by r_j^m on z>=0. No positivity of the
signed mixture itself is claimed. Every weight and every signed moment is
retained; substituting unsigned moments would erase the construction.

If each twist is rounded down to p-bit dyadics, its error is between
0 and 2^-p in absolute value. The signed-sum error is bounded by
2^-p sum |w_j|. Rounding the mixture again is a different operator and
must not be substituted. All cross terms remain in any later norm pricing.

3. Actual source coefficient and first local obstruction

For c_n(N)=-mu(n)*P_beta(n) with exact ordinary Mobius arithmetic,
 c_n(N)+mu(n)=(-mu(n))*(-1)^m M_m*(a log n)^m/m!
                    *L^(-m(1-theta))+o(L^(-m(1-theta))).
The ceiling relative correction and dyadic rounding do not change this
leading term. For mu(n)=0 the entire coefficient is exactly zero; no
nonzero obstruction is asserted at that index. At n>=2 with mu(n)!=0,
all remaining factors are positive except the independently checked
rational coefficient, which licenses its sign without numerical fitting.

Exact finite-cell recovery witnesses annihilate every higher-support
floor column through sum k*alpha_k=0 and check every lower column. They
retain the constant-one residual and all indicators. For a licensed
leading term of order nu=m(1-theta), they give
 liminf L^(2nu)||e_N||^2>=leading_constant(n)^2/D_n>0.
Only requested energy rates eta>2nu are excluded for any fixed finite
constant. Equality and slower rates remain OPEN, and no whole norm upper
bound follows from coefficientwise compatibility or Taylor's bound.

4. Necessary admission gates

All rational input strings must be canonical, with positive rates, nonzero
weights, distinct rates and <=8 terms. Moment order is recomputed exactly;
no declared cancellation order or Boolean is a premise. Finite beta is
checked by powered inequalities for ceil(a L^theta), and every rounded
twist by integer-power inequalities at the exact dyadic boundary. Explicit
bit/work caps bound computations instead of restricting a denominator bank.

Every proof certificate is tied to its complete mixture, source coefficient
sign, mathematical scale and exact rounding policy. The verifier never
uses a finite norm table or a source archive to license a leading term,
and never promotes formal compatibility to convergence, RH or original
signed-core closure. A failed resource check is unsupported computation,
not an arithmetic counterexample.

5. Positive convolution kernel is an additional exact certificate

For the maximal-order mixture the detector's signed rational weights can
also satisfy the independently checked polynomial identity
 sum_j w_j r_j prod_(i!=j)(s+r_i)=prod_i r_i.
Divide by prod(s+r_i). Each r_i/(s+r_i) is the Laplace transform of a
positive exponential density. Direct convolution and distinct-rate partial
fractions give the density -P'(u), with P(0)=1 and P(infinity)=0.
Consequently raw P is its survival function, 0<=P(u)<=1. Integrating the
positive convolution density over the simplex sum u_i<=u gives
 0<=1-P(u)<=prod(r_i) u^s/s!.
The exact numerator identity, every signed summand, its denominator and
all positive rates are verified before this stronger rule is admitted.
General unit-normalized mixtures retain only the absolute Taylor bound.
The raw probability interpretation is never transferred to rounded signed
coefficients without the full sum-absolute-weights rounding allowance.

6. Public checker and source integration

verify_spec(spec) and verify_certificate(spec,proposal) derive the exact
moments/order/kernel and admit the coefficient-only certificate.
verify_compilation(spec,n,N,proposal) independently replays every exact
ceiling and dyadic twist, with either a full certificate or scalar string.
verify_coefficient_bound(spec,n,N,proposal) prices the all-index scalar
Taylor/simplex/rounding bound, including exact n=1.
verify_obstruction reports only a symbolic local order until the actual
source coefficient and a cell witness are admitted.

weight_series handles drift_mixture and its products with the earlier
weight AST. verify_rate_candidate(recipe,index,row,precision=192) derives
-mu(n)*(P-1), preserving all tied signed terms and unresolved cancellation
barriers. It uses frozen independent cell-column and logarithmic sign
primitives. Ordinary earlier recipes delegate their frozen checker. New
drift recipes require a null auxiliary; unsupported new source mixtures
are not silently substituted. At n=2 log(n)=log(2) is normalized before
licensing a constant. At a nonsquarefree n the pure actual-Mobius error is
exactly zero and provides no nonzero obstruction.

A convergence claim for all scales needs an independent norm argument
beyond this admission. Neither the probability-kernel identity nor an
arbitrarily high moment-cancellation order supplies that missing argument.
