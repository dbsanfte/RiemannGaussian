1. Typed source, exact periodic extension, mean and unit

Fix an INTEGER U>=1. Let w(d),1<=d<=U, be arbitrary real weights |w(d)|<=1.
For the actual source w(d)=mu(d), c_U=sum_(d<=U)mu(d)/d,
 E_U=delta-One*A_U+c_U One, A_U=mu restricted to[1,U].
Its COMPLETE integer prefix is, for n>=1,
 P_U(n)=1+sum_(d<=U)mu(d){n/d}.                        (1.1)
The same sawtooth expression is a generic target for arbitrary w. Define
its periodic extension on ALL integers by that displayed formula, including
P_U(0)=1. This is NOT the actual source prefix at0, which is0. Its period
divides lcm(1,...,U); never construct this enormous period to apply the rule.

The exact period mean and centered periodic function are
 C_U=1+sum_(d<=U)w(d)(d-1)/(2d), Q_U(n)=P_U(n)-C_U.
For actual mu this reads
 C_U=1+(M(U)-c_U)/2.                                  (1.2)
The full M(U) term is retained. Under actual |c_U|<=2, |C_U|<=2+U/2<=5U/2.
No critical Mertens estimate is being used. In the generic route |C_U|<=1+U
is enough for an O(U) bound. A raw uncentered target includes C_U explicitly;
variance is not a bound for that raw mean square.

For actual source put e_U=E_U-C_U delta. Its actual prefix is
 Q_U^+(n)=Q_U(n) for n>=1, Q_U^+(0)=0.                 (1.3)
Periodic Q_U(0)=1-C_U is different. Every finite-convolution or floor map
must choose Q^+, not accidentally request the periodic value at a zero
argument. Equivalently E_U=delta+Delta P_period on positive integers; the
extra delta is the exact unit boundary. All infinite E_U coefficients and
repeated-prime/source histories remain in subsequent convolution products.

2. Exact reduced-frequency compiler BEFORE magnitudes

Write e(t)=exp(2pi i t). A length-d discrete Fourier transform gives
 {n/d}=(d-1)/(2d)
       -sum_(j=1)^(d-1)e(jn/d)/[d(1-e(-j/d))].
At a reduced nonzero fraction a/r, (a,r)=1, 1<=a<r<=U, combine every divisor
d=kr contributing that SAME frequency. The exact coefficient is
 B_(r,a)=-K_r(U)/[r(1-e(-a/r))],
 K_r(U)=sum_(k<=floor(U/r))w(kr)/k.                   (2.1)
Thus Q_U(n)=sum_(r=2)^U sum_(a,r)=1 B_(r,a)e(an/r).
Grouping unreduced pairs separately and then taking absolute values loses
the intended rule. Cyclotomic/algebraic representations or formal root-of-
unity symbols can encode the exact coefficients without sampled sines.
Source zeros and all signs stay in K_r. Every prime/composite coefficient
is represented once. U=1 has no nonzero frequencies and Q_U identically0.

The exact full-period variance is
 V_U=sum_(r=2)^U K_r(U)^2 J_2(r)/(12r^2),
 J_2(r)=r^2 product_(p|r)(1-p^-2).                    (2.2)
Proof: orthogonality gives coefficient squares. Summing all nontrivial rth
roots gives sum_(a=1)^(r-1)|1-e(a/r)|^-2=(r^2-1)/12;
this follows by logarithmically differentiating (z^r-1)/(z-1) at1. Splitting
that sum by reduced denominator and applying divisor Möbius inversion gives
sum_(a,r)=1 |1-e(a/r)|^-2=J_2(r)/12 for r>1. The frequency coefficient phase
is still retained in the full AST; only this exact period diagonal is reduced.

3. Competing GENERIC and actual-inverse variance providers

The strongest simple generic provider retains the conductor cutoff:
 |K_r(U)|<=H_floor(U/r), H_t=sum_(k<=t)1/k.
Since J_2(r)/r^2<=1,
 V_U<= (1/12)sum_(r<=U)H_floor(U/r)^2
 <=(U/12)sum_(k,l>=1)1/[kl max(k,l)].                 (3.1)
Interchanging positive sums is justified by monotone convergence. The double
sum equals 2 sum_k H_k/k^2-sum_k1/k^3. The elementary H_k<=1+ln k and decreasing
(1+ln x)/x^2 give sum_k H_k/k^2<=1+integral_1^infinity(1+ln x)x^-2 dx=3.
The subtracted sum is>=1. Consequently
 V_U<=5U/12<U/2, ALL U>=1, ANY bounded weights.        (3.2)
No arithmetic distribution or source inversion is needed. Replacing every
H_floor(U/r) by H_U would unnecessarily lose two logs. The detector should
rediscover the cutoff-sensitive summation rather than crediting an inferior
actual-source bound as signed arithmetic improvement.

For comparison actual mu gives K_r=0 for nonsquarefree r and

The complete divisor-floor identity proves |c_T|<=2, every integer T.
For primes dividing r, invert the finite prime-dilation identity to obtain

All floor endpoints terminate at cutoff0; no partial-prime law is substituted
for the complete source bound. This gives

For the last inequality expand f(r)=(r/phi(r))^2=sum_(d|r)g(d), g squarefree
and g(p)=(2p-1)/(p-1)^2. Then

Here g(p)/p=1/(p-1)^2+1/[p(p-1)]. Extending to all integers n>=2 bounds
the exponent sum by7/4+1=11/4<3; hence the product is<exp(3)<27.
All products and tails converge absolutely and e<3 is elementary.

4. Uniform finite-window provider: an elementary proof, no import needed

Distinct reduced frequencies with denominator<=U have circular separation
>=U^-2. For ANY integer starting point M and integer length L>=1 the geometric
sum kernel satisfies |sum_(n=M+1)^(M+L)e(n t)|<=min(L,1/[2||t||]).
For one frequency, order neighbors on each side around the circle. The jth
neighbor is at distance>=j/U^2. Summing the two sides gives at most
U^2 H_R<=U^2(1+ln R)<=U^2(1+2ln U), since the number R of frequencies<=U^2.
The diagonal is L. Gram row-sum/Schur therefore proves
 sum_(n=M+1)^(M+L)|Q_U(n)|^2
 <=[L+U^2(1+2ln U)] V_U.                             (4.1)
For R=0 the expression is0; for R=1 there is no offdiagonal row sum. This
is uniform in M, U and L, with no period-length assumption. It handles all
short windows, rather than transferring an asymptotic period average by fiat.
The arithmetic coefficients were coalesced before the Gram magnitudes.

Combining (3.2),(4.1) gives the unconditional generic bound
 sum_window |Q_U|^2 <=(U/2)[L+U^2(1+2ln U)].           (4.2)
The rational-frequency large sieve can improve the logarithmic spacing term.
Its primary reference is Montgomery--Vaughan, The large sieve, Mathematika20
(1973),119--134, https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300004708 . I browsed the
primary bibliographic/abstract page, which did not expose its full theorem.
NO external theorem is needed or imported for (4.1); the self-contained row
sum proof is the provider proposed here. A sharper import needs its own exact
statement, provenance and independent application check.

Raw or centered is a searched proof choice. The raw route can add
2L|C_U|^2+2 times(4.2), or retain the exact cross with the mean. Centered
control alone never certifies deletion of that mean from the source.
