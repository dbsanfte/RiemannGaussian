Problem and equivalence notion
Write each saved generator response as h_j=(r_j,t_j), with
g_j*x(f)=r_j*f+t_j on the complete licensed affine chart. The separator
search is sum_j alpha_j*h_j=(0,-1), alpha_j>=0. Prior030's positive-only
column and complete augmented-row compression supplies an equivalent
317-row,695-column system E*z=d, z>=0. The new reduction acts only on
this already licensed system, retaining original compressed row/column ids.
The constant equation is a genuine equation throughout; it is never an
implicit zero or an optional coordinate.

The required equivalence is existence of a nonnegative target
representation, with a checked lift of any reduced solution. It need not
preserve every original solution or the minimum representative L1 cost.
In particular, discarded homogeneous components can carry nonzero feasible
weights. The archive must distinguish this choice from forced zeros.

Homogeneous same-sign propagation
If an active equality has RHS zero and every nonzero coefficient a_j has
one strict sign, then sum_j a_j*z_j=0 and z_j>=0 imply z_j=0 for every
member of its entire support. Multiplying a negative row by -1 gives the
same argument. Delete precisely that full support from every active row;
the equality system restricted to the remaining weights is equivalent.
An empty row forces nothing. A mixed-sign row licenses no such inference.
A nonzero RHS cannot be treated by this rule: z1+z2=1 allows positive
weights. The reasoning concerns nonnegative separator weights, not primal
scalar or PSD coordinates, and does not by itself expose a new primal face.

Closed zero-target components
Partition an active system into rows/columns I,J and retained rows/columns
I',J'. For safe discard require RHS_i=0 for all i in I, support(row_i)
contained in J, and no retained row coefficient in J. Then restriction of
an original solution solves the retained system, and extension of any
retained solution by z_J=0 solves every removed equality. Isolated columns
also admit zero extension. This proves equivalence of feasibility.
Closure must be checked on the CURRENT exact equations after previous
steps. A target-zero row alone does not suffice when its variables also
occur in a retained target-bearing row. A component containing any nonzero
RHS cannot be removed by this rule. Positive costs make zero extension a
convenient objective choice for a detached homogeneous component; this is
not the justification for equivalence or for calling its weights forcedzero.

Exact augmented row operations and repeated reduction
A pivot a!=0 permits dividing the complete augmented pivot row by a.
For each listed different active row, subtract its current pivot coefficient
times that normalized augmented row, including RHS. Each such operation
is invertible over the rationals and leaves variables and nonnegativity
unchanged. Unlisted rows remain unchanged; soundness does not require
eliminating the pivot from every row. Deleting a row requires its entire
augmented row to be exactly zero. A zero coefficient row with nonzero RHS
must instead be retained and reported as a contradiction.

Exact arithmetic, sparse nonzero canonicalization and result/temporary bit
caps are necessary. A floating rank decision, tolerance-based deletion or
coefficient-only elimination does not preserve this proof. Sparse Markowitz
pivot selection changes resource use, not the theorem. Recording every
step with stable original ids lets a separate checker replay equivalence
without trusting producer rank, component enumeration or final equations.

Every finite prefix of valid steps is sound. Stopping when active columns
are unchanged or after eight rounds therefore preserves equivalence, even
if later row combinations could reveal further zero weights or dependencies.
The fixed_point field is a policy stopping diagnostic. It proves neither a
maximal cone face, complete sign propagation, minimal row basis, rank nor
exhaustion of all useful exact reductions. A resource-incomplete artifact
does not receive admission merely because its recorded prefix looks valid.
