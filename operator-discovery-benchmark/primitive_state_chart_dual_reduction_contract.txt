Exact reduction steps have these canonical encodings:
  {kind:'force_zero', row_id:int, columns:[sorted distinct column ids]}
    Selected active row has RHSzero and nonempty strictly one-sign support;
    columns is its full current support. Remove these columns from ALL rows
    and active columns. Record them as forced_zero_columns, not merely chosen.
  {kind:'drop_zero_rows', rows:[sorted distinct active row ids]}
    Every removed row is the complete zero augmented row.
  {kind:'discard_homogeneous_components', columns:[sorted distinct ids],
    rows:[sorted distinct ids]}
    Removed rows have RHSzero and coefficients only in removed columns;
    retained rows have no coefficient in removed columns. Isolated columns
    may also be discarded. Zero extension proves equivalence. These columns
    are discarded_zero_target_columns, NOT inferred forcedzero.
  {kind:'pivot', row_id:int, column:int, eliminate:[sorted distinct row ids]}
    Nonzero pivot; divide entire augmented pivot row by its pivot value,
    then eliminate its column from every listed OTHER active row using that
    row's current pivot coefficient. Unlisted rows remain unchanged. Exact
    arithmetic throughout, including RHS. This is an invertible row transform.
