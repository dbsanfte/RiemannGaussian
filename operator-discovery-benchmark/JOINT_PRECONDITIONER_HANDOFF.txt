What the detector searched
--------------------------
The grammar enumerates the identity and 36 operators with Dirichlet series
zeta(s+v)^(-r), with sixth-lattice shifts and signed integer/half-integer
powers. Every program retains all four source terms and the complete
unit/same-prime-power correction. Twenty-five programs have a proved
bounded or polylogarithmic two-direction recovery; twelve retain an unpaid
power cost. Their full ASTs and costs are archived, not discarded.

The algebraic ranking was fixed before actual-source generation. It favors
fewer positive Euler factors with paid recovery, and then simpler exact
local rational functions. It selected program23, v=2/3,r=1. This is an
explicit grammar/ranking choice, not an exhaustive or bias-free search.
No numerical slope or validation value selected the operator.

With u=1/3,v=2/3,beta=9/2, k_w(n)=n^(-w), mu_w(n)=mu(n)n^(-w),
the selected operator is complete Dirichlet convolution by mu_v:
  w=tilde_b*mu_v=f+eta,
  f=beta*nu0-beta*nu1-beta*mu-R_u*nu2,
  nu0=k_u*(mu*mu), nu1=k_u*(mu*mu_v), nu2=k_u*mu_v,
  eta=beta*mu_v-d*mu_v.
The inverse is convolution by k_v. Both critical PREFIX norm costs are
at most zeta(7/6)<=7. This is not a pointwise block-energy inequality:
divisor convolution couples smaller scales. It preserves the required
cofinal class without proving that the source belongs to it.

The complete correction has a finite critical prefix norm. The analytical
review proves D(x)<=beta*pi(sqrt x), using complete mass beta at each prime,
then the elementary bound pi(y)<=10y/log y. Consequently
  ||D||_crit<=C_D=20beta/sqrt(log4),
  ||prefix eta||_crit<=beta*sqrt27+7*C_D.
This pays the correction for full-prefix recovery; it does not permit
deleting it at independent product cutoffs. The unit is explicit:
  f(1)=-beta-R_u, eta(1)=beta, w(1)=-R_u.

What is proved about primes at all scales
-----------------------------------------
The compiler factors exact local Euler numerators and sums the COMPLETE
geometric valuation tails. For every actual prime q, t=q^(-1/3), e>=2:
  nu0(q^e)=(1-t)^2*t^(e-2),
  nu1(q^e)=-t*nu0(q^e),
  nu2(q^e)=(1-t)*t^e,
  mu(q^e)=0.
The absolute tail masses are <=1,t,t^2,0 respectively. The independent
checker reconstructs these polynomial/recurrence certificates with exact
rationals and verifies the complete first-valuation bounds separately.

These identities give uniform absolute bounds for pairs sharing q^2 with
q>n^rho. For modes i,j in {0,1,2}, their envelope is
  C_a*2^max(2-(3+a/3)*rho,0)
      *X^(1-(3+a/3)*rho)*(1+log(2X))^2,
  a=i+j, C_a=1+1/(3+a/3), n in[X,2X).
It applies to the union counted once, includes every valuation, and remains
valid after the joined high-product/off-diagonal restriction. Actual source
scalar products |c_i*c_j| must multiply these unscaled-mode bounds.

The thresholds for polylogarithmic payment are:
  nu0/nu0:1/3; nu0/nu1:3/10; nu0/nu2 and nu1/nu1:3/11;
  nu1/nu2:1/4; nu2/nu2:3/13.
Both orientations are retained; any mu leg vanishes on a shared square.
There are9 nonzero envelope sectors and7 exact zero sectors.

This is partial progress on real arithmetic support. The unsuppressed
common mode, first valuations and remaining interactions are NOT paid.
In particular these results do not authorize separately bounding every
raw multiplicative mode: their common pole cancellation stays joint.

Next proof-driven search
------------------------
Keep the exact full core as the unresolved bound:
  F(x)=sum_(n<=x)f(n), ||F||_sigma<infinity for every sigma>1/2,
equivalently its complete cofinal dyadic energy. The paid original recovery,
center correction, bounded inverse and eta norm remain part of the route
back to the original target. A renamed core is an equivalent obstruction.

The reviews supply concrete next constructions, not completed bounds:
1. Valuation pairings with exact q-free cofactor defects. For example
   nu1(q^eH)+t*nu0(q^eH)=t*l0(e)*(nu0(H)-nu1(H)).
   The defect survives for general H; dividing by t loses the prime gain.
2. An all-prime logarithmic recurrence whose Volterra memory is absorbable
   on x>=64 and whose finite head is explicit. The remaining JOINT signed
   prime-power bank is open. Separate pole compensation creates an unpaid
   macroscopic forcing; local analytic pole cancellation is not a tail bound.
3. A mixed log-divisor identity with a true d^(-1/3)-d^(-2/3) gain in its
   symmetric kernel term. Its max/prefix boundary commutator is unsuppressed,
   and the original symmetric source mode must still be recovered.
4. All-valuation prime insertion and actual-prime divided differences, with
   their q-free forcing, outside-source complements and inverse gap costs.

The next implementation should search arguments controlling these concrete
defects while preserving the full target. Prioritize a proved exponent gain,
an acyclic scale recurrence with a strict gap, or a reduction to independent
paid lemmas. Smaller constants and more reversible rewrites do not close it.
No full signed contraction or RH proof is claimed. The research goal stays
active.
