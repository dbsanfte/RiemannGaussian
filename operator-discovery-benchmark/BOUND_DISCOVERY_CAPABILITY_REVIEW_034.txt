INDEPENDENT CAPABILITY REVIEW: CONSTRUCTION AND BOUND DISCOVERY

Scope and disposition

This review reads CORE_BOUND_SYNTHESIS_HANDOFF.txt,
CORE_BOUND_PROGRAM_SEARCH_HANDOFF.txt, the semantic potential grammar and
soundness contract, and the current complete-source033 handoff. It proposes
a prospective capability benchmark and full-core adapter requirements.
It does not implement a synthesizer, write successful answer formulas or
an answer bank, execute any test, score arithmetic, read numerical source
caches, calculate hashes, or modify any frozen source/artifact.

The proposed new mechanism is meaningful: infer auxiliary dilation/residue
states from observations, independently PROVE their all-index closure from
a recursive source definition, synthesize a quadratic invariant rather than
receive one, and prove an arbitrary-prefix summatory bound by induction.
That is a construction-and-bound capability beyond selecting a supplied
moment theorem or fitting a constant to a fixed state. Its scope is still
a finite language of recursive sequences, expressions and proof rules.
Passing it establishes neither unrestricted mathematical autonomy nor a
missing actual-Mobius/RH-strength estimate.

1. What earlier evidence does and does not establish

The bound-synthesis stage enumerated delays, an Abel filter and nonnegative
storage before scoring responses. It generated recurrence hypotheses and
exact recovery obligations. Finite passing hypotheses were subsequently
refuted by the surviving-pole rule. Thus executable conjecture generation
and proof feedback existed, but the key rejection theorem was supplied.

The bound-program stage generated polynomial channels, solved recovery and
priced local detail. Its signed coarse leaf remained open. The precise
rule language and the useful projection argument were analyst supplied.

Semantic potentials generated complete signed mixtures and changed factor
placement/arguments. Their annotations explicitly were NOT universal proofs.
A bounded component inside a square did not pay its mixed term, a mask did
not establish sign cancellation, and finite aliases were not identities.

The newer moment stage searched contour lines and Holder allocations and
proved a new component using imported rules. It also diagnosed why stronger
moments alone could not pay the remaining absolute envelopes. This is real
executable proof composition. It does not yet show the executable inventing
the central construction or new signed correlation argument.

Do not count variants of these same accepted forms as a withheld test of a
new discovery ability. Keep them as named calibrations and regression cases.

2. A feasible first implementation boundary

Use signed recursive sources with an exact finite linear definition over
integer residues, initially base2 and rational/integer coefficients. A
target is the complete summatory function, with a stated growth budget and
all integer N in its domain. The searcher is given the scalar source oracle,
target, primitive operators, resource limits and permitted proof vocabulary.
It is NOT given a successful auxiliary-state list, successful closure matrix,
invariant, cancellation exponent, special family name or witness certificate.

The verifier receives the source definition because checking a universal
claim against a black box alone is impossible. A source definition is a
premise, not an answer: it must be a neutral recursive program. If it exposes
a latent vector representation, document that assistance. Prefer a source
interface exposing only the scalar recursion and exact evaluation, with a
generic checker that proves candidate identities against the definition.

Bottom-up expression candidates start from the target/source atom, index
variables, rational constants and declared generic operations. Dilation and
residue expressions x(b^k*n+r) are generated by the grammar; their successful
depth, set and linear combinations are outputs. The search may infer a
linear closure from exact observations but must submit a separate all-index
certificate. Rank on the observed prefix is only a discovery heuristic.

Then derive complete prefix recurrences from the certified scalar identities.
Search quadratic forms and growth factors generically, using exact linear
algebra and rational PSD/SOS certificates. The successful quadratic form and
its normalization/domination constant must be found by the executable.
The verifier checks the induction base, all branches, every finite exception,
the target-domination inequality and the growth consequence. No fitted
constant, unproved invariant or finite verification is a theorem.

This initial implementation can be small and useful without adding general
nonlinear arithmetic, arbitrary special functions or a general theorem prover.
Products, other bases and weighted sources are held-out stress families when
the declared grammar supports them; otherwise report unsupported scope.

3. Certificate obligations for recursive cancellation

Closure: for every generated state and every residue, prove the exact
linear transition identity for ALL applicable n. Include source n=0 or1
convention, parity, floor/index maps, empty sums, unit and exceptional initial
rows. A proof that candidate sequences agree at sampled n is insufficient.
If an induction is used, every recursive call must decrease a well-founded
integer index and all required base cases must be checked.

Prefix lift: the complete summatory state must have its actual transition.
Do not confuse a transition of coefficient states with a transition of their
sum. For nonstandard initial rules, the lift may contain an explicit finite
head/correction. Every such correction must be retained and priced.

State-size control: a cancellation proof using block transforms may also
need a uniform bound on the local coefficient-state vector. Prove it from
branch transitions and initial states, rather than silently assuming that
all newly inferred auxiliary states have magnitude<=1.

Invariant: verify an exact PSD or SOS identity for every declared transition
and its correction. A nonnegative quadratic form alone need not dominate
the target; the zero form and singular forms are important rejection tests.
Require a separate exact domination certificate on the licensed state domain.
If a form is only PSD on a proved reachable subspace, that subspace and its
invariance need their own certificates.

Growth: search the permitted growth factors and derive the exponent from
the certified recurrence. Do not insert a favored square-root exponent as
the answer. A problem may ask whether a fixed exponent budget can be met;
then the result must display the constructed form, uniform constant and
complete proof, or the precise unpaid premise.

Arbitrary prefixes: controlling sums at b^k endpoints is insufficient.
Supply a checked digit-block decomposition, a running-prefix recursion, or
another induction controlling EVERY N, including partial final blocks.
Pay the geometric sum/log cost and all prefix history. This is a critical
test because endpoint-only cancellation can conceal large partial sums.

Coefficient domain: a quadratic failure at an arbitrary rational vector
may refute a universal proof shape without refuting the actual source, whose
reachable vectors may be restricted. Conversely an actual sequence failure
must supply an admissible index/trajectory, not an unreachable PSD witness.

4. Prospective benchmark cohorts; no answer bank supplied here

Calibration cohort, reported separately:
 zero, constant, alternating, finite support and single-coordinate recursive
 sources; already known telescope/channel cases from earlier stages; identity
 and constant potential traps. They validate syntax/units but do not count
 as withheld main-construction successes.

Withheld positive cohortA, finite-kernel signed recursion:
 generate new recursive scalar definitions with nontrivial auxiliary dilation
 closures. Vary seed signs, transition coefficients, required closure depth,
 latent dimension and observable projection. The engine must find its own
 state set and invariant. A familiar cancellation family may be a calibration;
 renamed copies alone are not an independent positive cohort.

Withheld positive cohortB, mixed or interleaved sources:
 compose independently generated recursive definitions, change residue
 placement, add bounded finite exceptions, and vary base where supported.
 These test whether discovery transfers beyond the original visible pattern.
 Distinguish a meaningful new state construction from a preloaded Kronecker
 or interleaving template. No successful basis/form is included in inputs.

Withheld positive cohortC, coupled observables:
 require a bound for a signed combination of recursive outputs for which
 coordinatewise unsigned estimates lose the desired exponent. Do not supply
 the cancelling combination as an auxiliary; the target itself specifies the
 observable, while the storage/invariant and proof must be synthesized.
 All cross terms remain. Vary the number of outputs and exact rational scales.

Withheld transfer cohort:
 integer dilations, finite head corrections, nonaligned endpoints and products
 test construction robustness. Fractional n-dependent weights generally
 BREAK constant-coefficient closure; do not promise that the same mechanism
 supports them. If a weighted Abel/induction rule is licensed, make it generic,
 record its full boundary/tail cost, and classify use of it as proof composition.
 Otherwise correct output is unsupported/open, not an invented closure.

Each positive instance needs an independently verified known-solvable status,
 established by a private generator or custodian after the synthesizer is
 frozen. This review deliberately supplies no successful forms or gold proofs.
If no independent solvability certificate exists, classify the item as an
open exploratory task; failure cannot be charged as a proved capability miss.

5. Meaningful negative and near-miss controls

Long-transient alias: a recursive/piecewise source agrees with a positive
source through all discovery observations but differs later. Its definition
and exception must be available to the verifier. A sampled-rank closure must
be rejected, or remain unproved if its proof exceeds the budget. The engine
may expand from a supplied exact later counterexample; it must not promote
the earlier fit while waiting for one.

Unstable or mean-bearing twin: a model shares the same observed prefix or
local alphabet but has an excited mode that defeats the requested exponent.
Require a verified admissible trajectory/index or a generic structural
growth certificate. A different sign or an inaccessible latent coordinate
is not enough to certify an actual lower bound.

Endpoint trap: dyadic/full-block totals are small while some partial prefix
is large. It tests arbitrary-prefix proof, not merely held-out endpoint data.

Degenerate invariant: zero, singular or ill-normalized quadratic forms pass
nonnegativity but do not dominate the requested observable. Reject missing
domination, missing initialization, zero denominators and scale-dependent
normalization disguised as one fixed constant.

Moving parameter trap: choose a different state set, growth factor, cutoff
or constant for each N. A universal certificate must use one fixed generated
construction and fixed allowed parameters, unless a separately proved uniform
parameterized theorem explicitly permits those changes.

Product sign trap: a pointwise square of a signed source can lose cancellation;
a Dirichlet convolution is a different operation. The source types and exact
domains must prevent transferring a proof across these operations.

Proof-status trap: cycles, proved nodes depending on open premises, posthoc
fitted constants, missing finite heads and an unresolved boundary must block
the theorem flag. Add each as a mutated-certificate test, independently of
whether the search finds the mutation itself.

Generic-fact insufficiency: retain a model satisfying only the declared weak
coefficient bounds and local syntax but violating the global target. This
tests that a weak-fact argument is not mistaken for an actual-source theorem.
The countermodel does not refute the true recursive/Mobius source unless it
also satisfies its complete definition and source identity.

6. Anti-answer-leakage protocol

Freeze the generic synthesizer, verifier, primitive grammar and budgets BEFORE
generating the withheld suite. Keep source generators and their solvability
certificates under a separate role from the search implementation. That role
does not hand successful state/form seeds to the searcher. The test objects
contain problem definitions, not labels naming a successful mechanism.

Development uses disjoint recursive definitions. Withheld variants must
change transition structure or construction depth, not just coefficient
names. Random relabeling or invertible coordinate changes are useful invariance
checks but not enough by themselves to establish structural generalization.

Record exact seeds/inputs by direct bytes; no hashes are necessary. Publish
them after the run so leakage can be inspected. Log every oracle observation,
candidate state, closure proposal, verifier rejection, structural expansion,
proof attempt, resource stop and final certificate. Do not delete failed
families, redefine a held-out source after viewing success, or rerun the same
suite and present it as a fresh holdout.

The generic verifier may return an exact counterexample or typed missing
premise. It may not return a desired auxiliary-state list or successful
invariant. Counterexample-guided expansion uses the same frozen operators
and schedule for every problem; new analyst-written constructors after seeing
a failure belong to a later version and a new holdout, not a repaired success.

Run matched ablations: observations without proof; closure without invariant
search; fixed-state invariant search; full state-construction plus proof;
counterexample-guided expansion disabled; and a named template baseline.
Attribute gains separately to state discovery, invariant discovery and proof
verification. A theorem oracle providing the entire missing form is not
equivalent to the complete pipeline.

7. Score construction and universal proof, not finite numerical survival

Report per instance:
 generated nontrivial auxiliary states; certified global closure;
 constructed invariant and exact domination; induction/all-prefix proof;
 requested exponent met or not; universal constant fixed or not;
 genuine source counterexample versus proof-language rejection;
 open/unsupported/resource outcome; operations, memory and elapsed work.

The main positive success count requires ALL certificate stages. A finite
bound pass without proof scores conjecture generation only. A proof on a
provided basis scores invariant/proof discovery but not state construction.
An exact identity without a useful bound scores identity discovery. More
equivalent expressions or certificate rows do not multiply successes.

For negatives, separately report no false theorem admission, exact detected
counterexamples, and honest resource/unknown outcomes. Do not reward timeout
as a proof of impossibility or require every open task to be solved.

Charge all failed proof searches, counterexamples, expansion steps and retries.
Freeze maximum depth/state dimension/coefficient bits and operations in
advance. A comparison of methods needs the same declared oracle and total
budget, not one handpicked easy solved instance versus an unrestricted run.

8. Original-core adapter: mandatory complete semantics

The real target is the complete signed four-mode source and its inherited
transport/correction, not a chosen small residual. The accepted033 input is
candidate10_moment_region_obligation.json. It keeps the original core open,
the two paid centered components, their opposite analytic J_theta drifts,
all other binomial companions, and the exact transport error-e.

The adapter must reconstruct the original AST, all source scalars and inverse
coefficients, all units/prime powers and all four modes. Every candidate is
interpreted on this exact target. Pointwise powers and Dirichlet convolutions
have distinct types. Auxiliary x(b^k n+r) observations cannot substitute a
different source, omit a transport companion or restart the prefix at X.

Supply the actual source definition to the exact verifier: complete Mobius
divisor inversion, all kernel shifts, finite truncations and analytic moment
scope. A finite-dimensional recursive closure for a toy signed-recursive
source does NOT certify such a closure for the full core. Literal Mobius
divisor inversion does not imply a fixed finite b-kernel. If no closure proof
can be constructed within the grammar, expose that precise gap as open.

All144 inherited channel/transport bindings remain. Reconstruct forward and
reverse recovery, original target log budget, local detail, initial energy,
full parent/child history and the SAME cutoffs on a common N=4X-1. Pay the
inferred-state envelope, domination constant, varying weights, finite heads,
cell/projection errors, every drift and every signed cross or licensed bound.
Payout of an auxiliary component is not payout of the current complete Z033.

The accepted033 finite cube is already controlled via its exact analytic
full-source proof and032 padding. Rediscovering its bound is a calibration,
not solving the next open signed join. The old fixedsigma9/20 low Gram remains
a separate open norm; a physical-block proof at another contour does not
pay it automatically.

If the target is augmented by nonnegative storage W=E_core+V, independently
prove W>=E_core, the full drift bound, W(1), and its uniform recurrence
consequence. Nonnegative storage inside each state does not make the signed
parent-child drift nonnegative. Fractional/cofinal exponent claims require
one fixed construction and the correct every-q quantifier; a finite q bank
or a fixed positive power is not control for every epsilon.

No synthetic closure/invariant result, finite actual-source test, learned
recurrence or generic unsigned majorant authorizes a full-core or RH flag.
Those flags require a complete original-source proof DAG with no open leaf.

9. Recommended executable next step and explicit limits

Implement the narrow recursive-source constructor/closure/invariant/induction
pipeline now, with separate producer and independent checker. Use calibrations
to validate exact source and all-prefix semantics. Freeze it, then let an
independent custodian generate a modest withheld mixed-positive/negative suite.
Record all construction/proof traces and matched ablations. In the same
frozen executable expose the actual full-core adapter and attempt its stated
mechanism; report the exact unproved closure/bound/recovery stage if it fails.

This answers a useful capability question: can the program create a useful
auxiliary construction and universal bound without being given their forms?
It is stronger than another optimized application of a supplied estimate.
It cannot establish general autonomous research competence or the actual
signed arithmetic bound from toy success. A missing main-bound proof stays
missing even when every calibration and held-out recursive problem succeeds.

Reviewer work: read-only source/contract review and this new memo. No answer
bank, tests, source observations, arithmetic scores, hashes or frozen edits.
