DIRECT UNIFORM SIGNED MOMENTS TO ORDINARY-ZETA CONTROL

This is an additional universal transfer theorem. It reclassifies existing
numerical leads; it does not reuse inspected final data as fresh evaluation,
retune an archive, or prove any new uniform arithmetic moment premise.

1. THE DIRECT HALF-PLANE

Let R be a signed residual whose ordinary-zeta Mellin identity is
 T_R(s)=F(s)/(s*zeta(s))+H(s), Re(s)>1,
where H is holomorphic on Re(s)>0. Suppose, for EVERY dyadic X>=1,
 A_q(X)=integral_X^(2X)|R(t)|^q dt <= C X^beta,
with even q>=2 and finite C>0. Define eta=(beta-1)/q.
For sigma>max(eta,0), Holder's inequality and t>=X give
 integral_X^(2X)|R(t)|t^(-sigma-1)dt
 <= X^(-sigma-1)*X^(1-1/q)*A_q(X)^(1/q)
 <= C^(1/q)*X^(eta-sigma).
Summing X=2^j proves
 integral_1^infinity |R(t)|t^(-sigma-1)dt
 <= C^(1/q)/[1-2^(eta-sigma)].
On any compact subset of Re(s)>max(eta,0), the same summable geometric
majorant, with polynomial j factors for log(t) derivatives, proves that
T_R(s) is holomorphic there. No Lipschitz-to-pointwise loss is required.

If F is nonzero everywhere on Re(s)>=1/2, this excludes ordinary-zeta zeros
Re(s)>max(eta,1/2). If eta<=1/2 it excludes EVERY zero right of 1/2, and
the classical functional-equation reflection gives RH. A SINGLE fixed q
therefore suffices if beta<=q/2+1 and the premise is actually proved.
This corrects the overly broad statement in the earlier pilot report that
a finite list of moment orders is always insufficient. A sequence approaching
the critical exponent is one route, not a mandatory form of an RH proof.

2. A SHARPER FALSIFICATION RULE

A known critical-line zero rho with F(rho)!=0 gives a nonremovable pole of
F(s)/(s*zeta(s)). Since H is holomorphic near rho, any uniform moment claim
with eta<1/2 is impossible. The necessary threshold is beta>=q/2+1, stronger
than the Lipschitz pointwise threshold beta>=(q+1)/2.
This obstruction applies even when no all-height inverse certificate is
available, provided F is proved nonzero on the certified actual-zero disk.
It does not require assuming RH. The numerical audits use a fresh nested
Rouche disk and classical reflection/uniqueness for its critical-line location.
Neither a finite fit nor passing fresh complete windows can defeat this pole.

3. QUANTITATIVE CONTROL OF THE RECIPROCAL OF ORDINARY ZETA

For R(t)=M_tilde(t)-sum a_d M_tilde(t/d), write F(s)=1-sum a_d d^(-s).
Let E(s)=integral_1^infinity [M_tilde(t)-M_step(t)]t^(-s-1)dt.
The interpolation error is bounded by one, so |E(s)|<=1/sigma for sigma>0.
Substitution in each dilation gives the exact identity
 T_R(s)=F(s)/(s*zeta(s))+F(s)E(s)+K(s),
 K(s)=-sum a_d*(d^(-s)-d^(-1))/(1-s).
The apparent singularity of each compact correction at s=1 is removable.
For Re(s)=sigma>0,
 |K(s)|<=K_sigma=sum |a_d|/d * integral_1^d t^(-sigma)dt.
If the independently certified multiplier lower bound is |F(s)|>=delta>0,
then at EVERY height, for sigma>max(eta,1/2),
 |1/(s*zeta(s))|
 <= [C^(1/q)/(1-2^(eta-sigma))+K_sigma]/delta + 1/sigma.
This retains all constants and the multiplier cost. Unlike pointwise recovery
of M, direct zero exclusion needs no weighted inverse operator bound.
The all-scale arithmetic premise remains explicit and unproved in this pilot.

4. INDEPENDENCE AND SCOPE

The input candidate batches, archives, final moments and their previous
independent audits retain their hashes. This later analytic audit makes no
candidate selection and no new claim of held-out numerical validation.
It applies the stronger universal implication and pole obstruction to every
generated program. Unsupported global multiplier certificates remain distinct
from an actual ordinary-zero obstruction. Exact coefficients and ball-arithmetic
multiplier checks cannot substitute for an all-scale signed moment proof.
