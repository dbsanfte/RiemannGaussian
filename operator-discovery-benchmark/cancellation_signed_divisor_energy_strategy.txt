Independent strategy for a signed divisor-energy grammar (post-publication design)

Current omission. The implemented complete gcd-inverse compiler establishes, for every complete rational kernel K on 1..H,
  sum_(gcd(a,b)=1) K(a,b)x_a x_b
  = sum_(d<=H) x_d sum_(d|a,d|b) K(a,b)x_a x_b
modulo actual mu inverse rows. Its difference is exactly the sum of inverse_row(gcd(a,b))*K(a,b)x_a*x_b, using unit x1−1 at gcd1. The source horizon is H, not H²: no x_(ab) occurs. All ordered pairs, diagonals, zero kernel entries, and the complete noncoprime complement are recorded. Existing inverse-row families in grouped_arithmetic_templates use constant/linear multipliers; existing quadratic multipliers belong to other arithmetic families. Thus grouped inverse rows with quadratic pair multipliers are genuinely absent from the bound-search dictionary, though this separately supplied compiler already knows the identity. Adding this production is a representation extension, not discovery of the identity.

Positive K does not make the right side positive: x_d remains signed. For a shell Gram K(a,b)=sum_t w_t 1_(a<=t,b<=t), w_t>=0, define L_(d,t)=sum_(a<=t,d|a)x_a. The complete projection is sum_(d,t) w_t x_d L_(d,t)^2. A signed divisor-square expression must never be passed to a PSD checker as if x_d were a nonnegative scalar. For the defect J, construct child and parent shell Grams separately; the parent subtraction is signed and remains explicit.

A bounded new closure production is actual mu sign-localization. Let h_d be the factor-certified squarefree indicator and q_d=x_d²−h_d. Exact polynomial algebra gives, for either sign s=+1 or−1,
  (1+s*x_d)L² = 1/2*((1+s*x_d)L)²
                 +(1−h_d)/2*L² −1/2*q_d*L².
Here L is any declared homogeneous linear source polynomial. Every square coefficient is nonnegative, including nonsquarefree d where h_d=0. The norm-relation multiplier is quadratic; it is needed to cancel the quartic leading term. This certifies the localizer's actual positivity from |mu(d)|<=1 without substituting a signed value. It does not supply a bound on the signed divisor sum. Its negative part can be retained through the two localizers or their difference instead of absolute values. For actual c an analogous valid localizer is (a_d+s*x_d)L²=((a_d+s*x_d)L)²/(2a_d)−(x_d²−a_d²)L²/(2a_d), a_d=2^omega(d). This uses no inverse-of-ones assertion for c; that assertion is mu-specific.

Small generic implementation. Use a typed catalog with (i) inverse_pair(kernel,source_cells), (ii) divisor_localizer(sign,linear_source_program,divisor_cells), and (iii) its matching norm_closure. Linear source programs may be prefix sums with the divisibility predicate d|a and rational moving cutoffs. Enumerate all reduced cutoff powers in a predeclared denominator/description-length budget, both signs, and every declared cell and its complement; no coefficient response or old-dual sign chooses which catalog entries exist. An initial resource-bounded catalog can use the two original parent/child shell kernels and a small cost-ordered rational-cut dictionary. The full original defect is always the lhs. A restricted feature is allowed, but a restricted projection cannot be advertised as the full target: retain the mixed cells and noncoprime/complement polynomials explicitly. Do not silently replace a complete J kernel by a PSD positive part.

All these new columns have zero formal scalar if L has zero formal constant. The squared localizer polynomial itself then has zero constant even at d=1; x1 is a variable until its actual unit relation is applied. Norm multipliers L² also have zero constant. Inverse pair multipliers x_a*x_b have zero constant, including the gcd1 unit row. Hence the old scalar-budget module and gamma=0 necessary constraint extend by zeros unchanged. The new cone may permit different old coefficients, but adding zero-scalar columns cannot by itself create new scalar arithmetic cancellation.

Costs and independent audit. Before materialization declare horizon, kernel/cut descriptor hashes, denominator/bit/degree limits, maximum catalog columns, and a conservative expansion budget. For one fixed-prefix complete divisor Gram the number of triples is sum_(d<=H)floor(H/d)²=O(H²); enumerating a separate square at every shell t can nevertheless cost O(H³) product work. Long square source lists and all relation records must be charged, not just their compressed monomial count. Start with small complete horizons under the budget, and return explicit missing_resource rather than omit a divisor, source pair, collision or boundary. The independent checker must rebuild all factor witnesses, both sign-localizer identities, complete kernel cells, the original lhs and scalar slack. Dual validation must test ALL old and new columns and ALL PSD directions claimed by the cone, not just the active primal support. Negative pairing with an old exact dual only invalidates that witness; it is not a feasible certificate or a new global estimate.

The arithmetic estimate still missing. The diagonal part of the actual mu defect is already harmless: |sum_(a<=4X−1)J_X(a)mu(a)²|<=8X² using |J|<=2X. What remains is the COMPLETE off-diagonal signed pair sum, or an exactly equivalent projected divisor sum plus all noncoprime/mixed residuals, bounded above by C_q X² ceil(X^(1/q)) uniformly on every dyadic X for cofinal integer q. This must be proved from actual arithmetic; it cannot be admitted as a new grammar axiom. Taking absolute values of localizers or all divisor squares recovers unsigned cubic shell costs, not that estimate.

Limitations. Norm bounds and sign-localizers hold for squarefree positive-mean counterfeits with only finitely many normalized prime signs, so they do not exclude unsigned degeneration by themselves. Complete inverse rows do determine actual mu through a triangular recurrence. Allowing arbitrary per-row multipliers and free per-index squares can therefore implement finite arithmetic interpolation even without a coefficient table. Keep shared descriptor weights and scale programs explicit, record all finite attempts, and require BOTH an all-scale nonconstant identity and a separately proved critical scalar budget before any RH bridge. The existing conditional bridge applies only to the actual full defect; no new all-scale control is proved here.
