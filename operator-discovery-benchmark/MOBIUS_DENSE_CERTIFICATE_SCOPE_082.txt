082 independent dense-certificate detector design and proof boundary

Disposition
A meaningful next detector is exact quadratic completion of the COMPLETE
joint drift using divisor identities, rather than extracting another thin
balanced ratio packet. The detector below either returns an independently
checkable finite upper certificate or an exact moment-relaxation obstruction.
Both are actionable mathematical outputs. Neither is an all-scale bound.
No historical scientific run, test, numerical mu query, or hash was made for
this design. Frozen files were read only. A source-reading command requested
an absent guessed066 review filename; the069 review itself contains the
needed066 correspondence and was successfully read. No result depended on

What must not be repeated

The new object must retain joint arithmetic constraints through pricing.

Complete dense target
Let f_i(n)=sum_k b_k 1[n<=floor(u/(2^i d_k))] for the ORIGINAL kernel.
Use fixed unnormalized T=embed(H)-r shift(H), as081. Compile the entire
quadratic F_u(x)=sum_ij T_ij (sum_n f_i(n)x_n)(sum_m f_j(m)x_m),
including unit, diagonal, every prime/composite pair, and both endpoints.
At ordinary mu, F_u=E(u)-r E(floor(u/2)). Use horizon equal to the maximum
actual endpoint, not an artificial ratio cutoff. Represent off-diagonals
with the exact factor2 convention and bind both the source kernel and all

Finite exact detector primitive: divisor-constrained quadratic completion
Coordinates z=(1,x_1,...,x_H). For n>1 use
 r_n(x)=sum_{d|n} x_d=0; use r_1=x_1-1=0.
Optionally use squareful-zero x_n=0 and known squarefree-diagonal
 x_n^2=s_n, where s_n is determined by factorization, not fitted signs.
Coprime multiplicativity x_ab-x_a*x_b=0 may be included if ab<=H;
it is a quadratic constraint, not a rule for noncoprime products.

Search for an identity
 B-F_u(x)=z^T S z + sum_n ell_n(x) r_n(x)
          +sum_n t_n(1-x_n^2)
          +sum_n c_n(x_n^2-s_n)
          +sum_(coprime a,b) h_ab(x_ab-x_a*x_b),
where S is rational PSD, ell_n is affine with rational coefficients,
t_n>=0, and c_n,h_ab are unrestricted rational constants.
Squareful-zero relations may also have affine multipliers. The last two
families are optional; all active relations must be listed and checked.
At ordinary mu this proves F_u<=B exactly. The certificate must compare
EVERY constant, linear, and quadratic coefficient, not evaluate the
identity only at actual mu. PSD must be certified by exact rational LDL
with zero-pivot compatibility, or an explicit rational Gram decomposition;
rounded floating eigenvalues are not an admission rule.

Do not label a finite verified coefficient identity a uniform theorem.

Practical LP-first search
Use a fixed PSD dictionary: squares of sparse affine forms, source-delay
forms, divisor-prefix forms, and pairs of shared cofactor forms. Their
nonnegative coefficients make the completion search a rational linear
program with free divisor multipliers. This is a finite restricted SOS
cone, not the full SDP. Admit a returned LP solution only by exact replay.
If necessary enlarge the dictionary using exact residual coefficient blocks;
record every dictionary expansion, prior infeasibility, and resource limit.
Do not silently treat restricted-cone infeasibility as true impossibility.

Useful exact obstruction
A dual witness is a rational moment matrix Y PSD with Y00=1, satisfying
all admitted equalities after multiplication by each allowed affine
coordinate, satisfying diagonal bounds and optional multiplicativity,
and giving <F,Y> greater than proposed B. Independently verify every
moment constraint and exact PSD. This rules out that specific certificate
cone/constraint roster at that u. It is not a counterexample to actual mu.
If only sparse multiplier slots are admitted, the dual must expose exactly
those slots; it cannot claim a stronger full-ideal obstruction.

Important uniqueness guard
All complete divisor relations through H already uniquely determine x_n
recursively. Adding all affine multipliers makes the finite quadratic
problem essentially an exact arithmetic evaluation in disguise. A perfect
finite certificate may therefore pay for all source signs through its
coefficients. This is permissible finite evidence but not a new uniform
cancellation mechanism. Log whether coefficients/solver objectives use
actual mu, whether the multiplier grammar is geometry-only, and how the
admitted divisor roster grows. A source-sign lookup encoded as free
multipliers must not be presented as an all-scale discovered recurrence.
Conversely, a deliberately partial roster has genuine freedom and can
produce a useful dual witness; record which prime constraints are absent.

Uniform proof obligation for a growing detector
A uniform certificate requires a SYMBOLIC rule for S_u, ell_n,u, and other
multipliers, complete coefficient equality for EVERY integer u, and a
uniform upper bound for B(u). A finite table of certificates or stable
rational fits proves none of these. An acceptable endpoint is B(u)<=C u^q
with q<2, a contracting normalized child coefficient, and complete base.
For081 r=3/2,q>=1, r*(floor(u/2)/u)^q<=3/4; this factor is paid arithmetic,
but the dense signed upper law and source transfer are still unpaid.

Avoid paying the PSD trace alone if diagonal constraints cancel signed
terms: first coalesce the COMPLETE identity, then price the remaining
nonnegative/known-diagonal part. The resulting trace/diagonal law needs a
proof uniform in H and all growing divisor rosters. Do not import the
square-root Mertens premise that the recurrence is intended to prove.

Stronger shared-cofactor/count-error averaging alternative
081 paid each pair's squarefree count error separately and summed absolute
prices. An improved transform should retain the same square-divisor index
across ALL pair labels. For N=ab and x=u/den,
 Q_N(x)=sum_(d<=sqrt(x),gcd(d,N)=1) mu(d)
           sum_(k|rad(N)) mu(k) floor(x/(d^2 k)).
This finite equality includes unit and every coprimality divisor. Combine
signed coefficients at a common (d,k,den) BEFORE taking absolute values.
The result is an exact shared-cofactor residual functional rather than a
sum of independent O(sqrt(x)) errors. A finite LP can choose balanced
weights or zero-identity multipliers to minimize the whole coalesced
floor-error norm while preserving the COMPLETE drift identity. Any
removed density contribution must have an exact cancellation certificate;
all complementary edges remain part of the target.

For a growing uniform rule, prove a bound on the coalesced jump/floor-error
budget, including the square-divisor tail. A reduced measured budget is
finite feedback only. Coalescing by (d,k,den) also cannot arbitrarily merge
mu(d) with label signs mu(a)mu(b): repeated/shared primes are constrained,
not independent signs. An all-scale averaging estimate must pay their
joint dependence. This is where divisor-multiplier completion can assist;
it should not be replaced by independent sign boxes after coalescing.

Resources and independence
Never allocate a dense H-by-H matrix at the known million-scale horizon.
Compile source low-rank forms and sparse constraint/multiplier incidences.
A proposed full SDP must be rejected prospectively; use bounded blocks,
streamed coefficient maps, or geometry-class templates with exact replay.
Charge divisor edges, active affine-multiplier slots, square dictionary
supports and all rational bit growth BEFORE allocation. A simple count of
pairs or fixed bytes per Fraction is inadequate for arbitrary denominators.
Any omitted block is an explicitly unpaid residual, not zero. Cap partial
searches and return UNRESOLVED_RESOURCE without truncating certificates.
The checker should reconstruct divisor incidences by its own factor logic
and source coefficients by direct floor kernels, independently of producer
quotient grouping. No arithmetic science is required to validate generic
small synthetic coefficient identities or exact dual witnesses.

Recommended082 deliverable
Implement the LP-first dense completion and exact dual checker on bounded
synthetic/fixed-source scales, preserving prime pairs; record its complete
unpaid residual if the resource horizon is smaller than the source.
Search geometry-only divisor/cofactor multiplier templates and retain
useful exact dual obstructions. Promotion requires the symbolic growing
coefficient identity and price law above, not another successful finite
calibration. This proposal is a concrete detector specification, not an
assertion that its uniform proof obligation can already be closed.
