Recovery was investigated, not assumed
--------------------------------------
PRIME_SLIDE_RECOVERY_REVIEW_001.txt derives the full kernel pullbacks under
k->q^e*k, preserving v_q(k_lift)=e, k>=2, the smooth mask on the inverse
image, every entry/exit endpoint and all cofactor gcd overlap.
The first right lift is inside the original coefficient prefix, in its
nonsmooth non-prime-power sector. Every higher lift lies outside that
prefix. In particular B(q^2)->1, so the outside complement is not all small.

There is also a valid exact elimination of explicit high powers. The local
cumulative rows satisfy
  C_m=(t+t^2)*C_(m-1)-t^3*C_(m-2), m>=2.
This follows from their common rational generating denominator and is valid
at every prime power, not inferred from a finite fit. The selected stencil is
  C6=(t^5+t^6-t^11)*I
           +(t^5+t^6+t^7+t^8+t^9+t^10)*T_q^right.
Thus its auxiliary can be written with weighted baseline and interior terms
only. Recovering the unweighted baseline still divides by a quantity of
order q^(-5/3), and leaves an unweighted interior/companion covariance.
A bound AFTER summing over q cannot be multiplied by this variable inverse
as if it were a uniform constant.

Additional proof hook: centered common-bank recurrence
-------------------------------------------------------
CENTERED_COMMON_BANK_CLOSURE_REVIEW_001.txt retains all poles, floors, unit
strips and repeated-prime terms in a simultaneous state F=(J,M_u,M_v).
It derives F(n)=H(n)+sum_q A_q*F(floor(n/q)), with q>n^theta and k>=2.
The explicit matrix and complete smooth compensator are in the next input.
This is a sufficient coupled route; equivalence to the original target is
not asserted. H contains unpaid arithmetic and is not a bounded forcing.

Conditional on the SAME closed cumulative induction at smaller arguments,
its shared-prime centered variance at theta3/4 is bounded by
  (93/2)*2^(2+epsilon)*(1+1/epsilon)*A_epsilon*X^(epsilon/4).
The horizon is T<=2X^(1/4), with a fixed finite head. This pays one component
after an induction closes; it does not provide the induction. The full
smooth, distinct-prime, pole and cross-term forcing remains explicit.

The review also proves a sharper exclusion: the isolated deterministic
pole compensator P_theta cannot have cofinal critical energy by itself.
Its nonzero cofactor profile yields a Mellin factor F_C(s)*P_prime(s)
with a logarithmic branch at s=1; finite-order harmonic zeros do not remove
it. Small-prime cutoff corrections, the weighted bank and the main term
are analytic near1. This source-independent argument excludes standalone
payment of P_theta, while permitting the required joint cancellation with K.
Do not separate that deterministic correction from its smooth/cross terms.
