Admission and authority
-----------------------

by the post059 protocol. It imports the immutable052 canonical rational/schema
helpers and charge-only Meter. It does not import the new miner, enumerate the

The existing059 policy source was read only to confirm its operational meaning:

The exact source and floor-cell gate
-----------------------------------
Write W(v)=floor(4v)-3floor(2v)+2floor(v). For d=a and d=ra the source shell
weights are the pairs (W(N/(pd)),W(N/(qd))). Every component weight is in
{-1,0,1}. P means (A,0), Q means (0,B), D means (A,A), S means (A,-A), with
nonzero A or B. Their kernel signs are A,-B,A,A respectively. Ordinary source
sign is mu(d) times this kernel sign.

On the positive OPEN cell v=N/a in (lo,hi), the checker verifies separately
each of the twelve component floors. For a positive multiplier c, the minimum
floor on the open interval is floor(c*lo), and the maximum is ceil(c*hi)-1.
Both must coincide. Thus an integer endpoint is allowed, but a change strictly
inside the cell is not. A midpoint sample or cancellation between changing
floors is insufficient. Both weight pairs must be nonzero and have identical
profile and nonzero kernel sign. Their nonzero weights also guarantee that
both indices are within the complete source horizon floor(4N/p).

The source includes d=1 and every range exit. The obstruction only selects a
subset of its nonnegative charged costs; it never discards terms from J.

Irrevocable first-pass edges
----------------------------
Take squarefree a coprime to r*product(M) in
      N/hi < a < N/lo.
Then mu(ra)=-mu(a), so the identical kernel signs produce opposite source signs.
All such r-edges are disjoint. A nonzero squarefree endpoint divisible by r
can only be the upper endpoint, while r^2*a has mu zero. Every smaller prime
is registered and hence absent from the coprime source. Consequently this
entire family is paired by the first ascending-prime pass, for either group
all or group profile. No prior scan, descending pass, other partition, undo,
or unsupported rescue is admitted. The optional canonical residual scan does
not remove already charged absolute edge differences.

Positive density, without prime distribution data
-------------------------------------------------
Let Q=r*product(M), u=1/hi, v=1/lo. The elementary identity
      1_squarefree(n)=sum_(d^2|n) mu(d)
and finite coprime counting give
      # {uN<a<vN: squarefree, gcd(a,Q)=1}
       =rho_Q*(v-u)*N+O_(Q,cell)(sqrt(N)),
      rho_Q=(phi(Q)/Q)*product_(ell prime,ell not dividing Q)(1-ell^-2).
Indeed only d coprime to Q contribute; the integer count for each d has bounded
periodic error O_Q(1), there are O_cell(sqrt(N)) possible d, and the omitted
absolute 1/d^2 tail contributes the same order. All constants here may depend
on the fixed cell. No restriction v<=1 or numerical error constant is assumed.
The Euler product is >=1/4: sum_primes ell^-2 <=sum_n>=2 n^-2 <=3/4, and finite
products (1-x_i) are >=1-sum x_i. Passing to the limit proves
rho_Q>=phi(Q)/(4Q)>0. Hence at least c_density*N edges exist eventually, for
some fixed positive constant. There is no PNT import, finite density premise,
or claim of a computable finite failure scale.

Local positive kernel cost
---------------------------
The normalized source magnitudes are
 rgamma(1-alpha)*integral_eps^tau G_M(t) f_profile(t) d^-t t^(-alpha-1) dt,
 eps=min(tau,N^-gamma), G_M=product_(ell in M)(1-ell^-t),
 f_P=p^-t, f_Q=q^-t, f_D=p^-t-q^-t, f_S=p^-t+q^-t.
This is the positive normalized Laplace representation of the actual source,
not a coefficient-envelope countermodel. Each f is positive; for D use p<q.
The cost of an r-edge is exactly the same integral with the additional factor
1-r^-t, since the upper index is ra and both have the same profile.

Put k=|M|, delta=1 for D and0 otherwise, D_N=floor(4N/p),
T=1/log(2qD_N). Eventually [T,2T] lies inside (eps,tau) and t<=1, since gamma
and tau are fixed positive. On that window a<=D_N gives a^-t>=exp(-2).
For0<t<=1, each registered factor is >=t/(2ell), the ratio factor is >=t/(2r),
P/Q/S are >=1/q, and D is >=t/q^2 (integrate exp(-yt) over log(p)..log(q)).
Also exp(-2)>=1/9 and rgamma(1-alpha)>=(1-alpha)/2, since Gamma(s)<=1/s+1
for0<s<1. Thus a rational integrand lower constant is
 (1-alpha)/(36*2^k*product(M)*r*q^(delta+1)).
Integrating t^(k+delta-alpha) on [T,2T] gives at least this constant times
T^(k+delta-alpha+1). Therefore the only admitted logarithmic exponent is
      A=k-alpha+delta+1.
Combining the density estimate and log(2qD_N) asymptotic to log(N) yields
      B_policy(N)>=c*N/(log(N))^A
for all sufficiently large integer N, with fixed c>0. The checker reports
these existence quantifiers honestly, without a numerical lower c or N0.
