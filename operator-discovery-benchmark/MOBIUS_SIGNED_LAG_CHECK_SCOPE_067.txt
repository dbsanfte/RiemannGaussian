INDEPENDENT SIGNED HEAD LAG CERTIFICATE CHECKER067

Scope

check_mobius_signed_lag_067.py checks finite certificates of the same RAW
short inverse represented in066. It does not import the producer, FLINT,
the066 checker, saved scientific results, repaired core inverses, prime-sign
surrogates or LP code. The runner must separately bind the SAME admitted066
certificate and its063 raw source admission; this checker explicitly reports
that original source binding is not proved within its own call.

The algebraic rules used here were supplied. Automatic generation/checking
of a finite lag decomposition is not automatic discovery of a uniform
arithmetic cancellation theorem. Numerical far-lag observations remain
finite observations. Uniform far-lag control, the uniform full head bound,
the whole signed-prefix target and RH remain OPEN.

Source and arithmetic independence

For each endpoint r=p/q or1, the checker evaluates the ORIGINAL source
pointwise, with W_b(d)=floor(4N/(bd))-3floor(2N/(bd))+2floor(N/(bd)).
It creates f_y(d)=1[d<=y]*(W_p(d)-rW_q(d)) and the tail correction
g(d)=-1[d>y]*(W_p(d)-rW_q(d)), all through D=floor(4N/p). It reconstructs
the coalesced step dictionary from jumps g(T)-g(T+1), bypassing the
producer's quotient enumeration and supplied sparse intervals. Full-floor
atoms are independently rebuilt from the six horizons floor(vN/b).

An independent Eratosthenes sign sieve constructs ordinary mu through D;
mu(1)=1 and square-divisor zeros are retained. For every checked head row
the checker obtains kappa_j(n)=g(jn)-g((j+1)n) pointwise and replays the
raw inverse directly as sum_n mu(n)*(f_y(jn)-f_y((j+1)n)). It verifies this
equals the independently rebuilt direct coefficient d_j plus the joined
tail row sum. Arithmetic outside the registered source prime mask remains
present: ordinary mu is not a multiplicative character on that mask.

Exact correlations and all finite fields

For each I=[L,min(2L-1,ceil(H/2)-1)], where L runs through powers of2,
the checker forms a_j(n)=den*mu(n)*kappa_j(n), n=1,...,floor(D/j).
Den is the reduced endpoint denominator. It uses NumPy's direct integer
correlation product sums, a different algorithm from the producer's exact
FLINT polynomial multiplication. Before each native call, the rigorous
bound length*maxabs(a)^2 <= 2^63-1 ensures every partial signed product
sum is representable in int64. This guards negative as well as positive
intermediate sums; no floating-point FFT or numerical tolerance is used.
Native row results are accumulated using Python integers.

Every integer correlation numerator R_I(h)*den^2 is compared, including
h=0 and trailing zero lags, over the COMPLETE domain0<=h<floor(D/L).
Missing, reordered or extra blocks/lags are rejected. A correlation
mutation preserving the total signed cross sum is also rejected.

The checker independently verifies all row sums, direct floor terms,
joined kernel L1, maximum jump, diagonal, signed cross, moment, actual
head variation, both diagonal upper bounds and rounded Cauchy bounds.
It verifies E_I=R_I(0)+2*sum_(h>0)R_I(h) exactly. The elementary identity
W_b(d) in{-1,0,1} proves |kappa_j(n)|<=4. Thus, with the coalesced
M1=sum_T|a_T|T, it verifies the supplied uniform algebraic inequalities

  0<=R_I(0)<=4*sum_(j in I,n)|kappa_j(n)|<=4*M1/L,
  |R_I(h)|<=4*M1/L for every h.

These uniform positive majorants do not pay the complete signed-lag sum
at a square-root arithmetic rate.

Every declared candidate is retained, including baseline cutoff0. For
each canonical rational delta in[0,1], requested B=floor(N^delta) is
checked by the integer inequality B^den<=N^num<(B+1)^den. This differs
from the producer's binary search. The denominator has no whitelist;
delta=1/257 is explicitly tested. B is clipped to the full available lag
domain before near/far splitting. All signed near/far fields, paid moment
(4+8B)M1/L, finite upper bound obtained by retaining positive actual far
mass, head bounds and unpaid-risk bounds are checked exactly. Every
square-root bound is the least point on the denominator2^24 grid whose
square exceeds the exact rational radicand. Noncanonical rationals and
false uniform claims are rejected, including false represented as0.

Positive/negative far values are observations for these finite blocks.
Endpoint convexity transfers endpoint bounds for the absolute raw head
variation. A signed far-lag sum is an indefinite quadratic in r; its
nonpositivity at both endpoints does not alone prove nonpositivity at
every interior ratio. The endpoint convexity flag grants no such inference.
No infinite extension bound is allowed in the strict certificate schema.
An infinite ordinary floor series or an attractive factor-transfer rate
cannot be substituted for these finite lag identities.

Evaluation flags distinguish what actually ran: ordinary-mu lag correlations
and unmasked original-source inverse replay are evaluated; a new masked
scientific prefix, integral or LP is not. Root's separate new pipeline test
checks those explicit flags and same066 source binding. It changes no
mathematical checker rule and reruns no prior suite.
