Parameter cancellation, logarithmic parameter jets, and adaptive Cauchy bounds

Scope. This stage preserves the actual Mobius arithmetic and full fractional
centers of the preceding stage. It strengthens the proved logarithmic losses
and removes a spurious growth of normalization costs with the parameter level.
The proved ordinary power remains ONE. Critical signed premises remain open.
Probability integrals, Gamma's product, Cauchy's formula, and ordinary-zeta
continuation and functional equation are foundational dependencies. No PNT,
zero-density estimate or new prime-distribution theorem is imported. The
proof is independently audited, not proof-assistant formalized.

1. Entire arithmetic parameter and its actual prime-factor representation.
For n>1 define L_a(n)=exp(a*log(log n)), with L_a(1)=0. The complete
finite convolution lambda_a=mu*L_a is ENTIRE in complex a at each n.
For n=product p_i^e_i, let h_i=log p_i, b=sum(e_i-1)h_i, r=omega(n).
Let independent exponential variables have rates h_i, and let Y be their
maximum. Its CDF is P(Y<=t)=product_i(1-exp(-h_i*t)). Actual arithmetic
equals the iterated finite difference of (b+sum h_i)^a.

If n is squarefree, integration by parts of the Bernstein integral gives
lambda_a(n)=(-1)^(r-1)*E[Y^(-a)]/Gamma(1-a).
Initially 0<Re(a)<1; both sides continue to Re(a)<1. The moment integral
at zero converges because the CDF is O(t^r), and the large-t tail is
exponential. If n is not squarefree, then b>=h_min and
lambda_a(n)=(-1)^(r-1)*a/Gamma(1-a) *
 integral_0^infinity exp(-b*t)*P(Y<=t)*t^(-a-1)dt,
again analytic for Re(a)<1, including zero. At a=0 the squarefree
coefficient is -mu(n), while every nonsquarefree coefficient is zero.
These are statements about ALL actual integers, not a finite factor oracle.
The producer still computes divisor inverses and convolutions without
factorization. Prime factors belong only to the independent evaluator.

2. A uniform complex-parameter bound, without divisor-count losses.
Gamma's product gives
1/Gamma(1-a)=exp(-gamma*a)*product_(j>=1)(1-a/j)*exp(a/j).
For |a|<=1/2, gamma<1 and zeta(2)<2, the modulus is below exp(1)<3:
the logarithm of its modulus is at most
|a| + |a|^2*zeta(2)/(2*(1-|a|)) <=1.
On 1/2<=t<=3/2, log convexity and Gamma(1/2)=sqrt(pi)<2 give
Gamma(t)<=2; on 1<=t<=3/2 its modulus is at most one.

Write L=log n. Each h_i lies between log 2 and L, r<=L/log 2,
E[Y]<=r/h_min<=4L, and Y is at least an exponential variable of rate
h_min. Thus for 0<=d<=1/2,
E[Y^d]<=(4L)^d and E[Y^(-d)]<=2*max(1,L^d).
For a center alpha in [0,A], radius delta>0 with A+delta<=1/2,
the squarefree probability formula proves
sup_(|a-alpha|<=delta)|lambda_a(n)| <=9*(4L)^(A+delta).
For nonsquarefree n, use P(Y<=t)<=1-exp(-h_min*t), b>=h_min,
and
Integral exp(-bt)(1-exp(-ht))*t^(-d-1)dt <=Gamma(1-d)*h*b^(d-1),
Integral exp(-bt)(1-exp(-ht))*t^(d-1)dt <=Gamma(1+d)*h*b^(-d-1).
Combining the extremes Re(a)<=A+delta and Re(a)>=-delta, with
|a|<=1/2 and 1/Gamma bound three, gives at most six times the same
(4L)^(A+delta), hence the common bound nine is valid. Cauchy gives
|partial_a^m lambda_alpha(n)| <=9*m!*delta^(-m)*(4L)^(A+delta).
The constants and domains hold for every integer n, prime rank and scale.

3. Actual parameter jets and full centers.
The NATURAL Taylor jet is
v_m(n)=mu * [1_(n>1)*(log(log n))^m/m!], m a nonnegative integer.
For m=0 the bracket is one on n>1 and zero at one, so v_0=delta-mu.
This known Mertens calibration is kept separate from inferred discoveries.
For m>=1 the raw weight at n=2 can be negative. No positivity or
multiplicativity of these jets is assumed. A generic unit-normalized input
f has an analogous exact finite jet f^(-1)*(f*weight), but neither the
actual-prime estimate nor the ordinary-zeta bridge transfers to generic f.

Let V_m(x)=sum_(n<=x)v_m(n) and B_m(x)=[a^m]A_a(x), the FULL
center coefficient. If 1/Gamma(1-a)=sum g_j*a^j, g_0=1, then
B_0=0 and for m>=1
B_m(x)=Integral_0^1 H_x(v)*sum_(j=1..m)
 g_(j-1)*(-log v)^(m-j)/(m-j)! dv,
where H_x(v)=(x^(1-v)-1)/[(1-v)*Z(1-v)] and Z=(s-1)zeta(s).
The full Taylor-in-v integral and its Cauchy remainder give native centers;
they are not merely leading x/log x asymptotics. A separate recurrence
j*g_j=-gamma*g_(j-1)-sum_(r=2..j)zeta(r)*g_(j-r)
provides an independent Gamma-coefficient evaluation.

For |a|<=1/2, |c_a|<=3/2, Z(u)>=1/(4 log 2), and Re(a)<=1/2,
the full center derivative has modulus below twelve for every real x>=1.
Consequently |B'_m(x)|<=12*2^m, B_m(1)=0, and |B_m(x)|<=12*2^m*x.
With V(x)=log(4*(1+log x)), ANY radius 0<delta(x)<=1/2 yields
|V_m(x)-B_m(x)| <= x*[9*delta(x)^(-m)*exp(delta(x)*V(x))+12*2^m]
for every real x>=1. At x<2 the coefficient sum is zero and the center
guard alone suffices. At larger x use log n<=1+log x inside the common
Cauchy circle. Variable radii are chosen separately at each scale; no
finite-scale estimate is used in this universal argument.

4. Cancellation BEFORE the parameter absolute envelope.
For distinct positive rational b_i<1 and nonzero rational weights w_i,
let p_j=sum w_i*b_i^j and m be their first nonzero moment. Put
h(q)=p_m/q^m and A_w=sum |w_i|*b_i^m/|p_m|.
The actual normalized family R_q=sum w_i(T_(b_i/q)-A_(b_i/q))/h(q)
has pointwise finite-prefix limit V_m-B_m, not m! times that jet.
This follows from the entire parameter Taylor series and moment cancellation.
The limit is uniform on compact arithmetic scales; it is NOT an exchange
of infinite-q and infinite-x limits and does not transfer a critical global
estimate without a separate uniform premise.

For q>=q0 with A0=b_max/q0<1/2, let rho0=1/2-A0>0. Taylor's integral
remainder, AFTER the low moments cancel, proves
|sum w_i lambda_(b_i/q)(n)/h(q)| <=
 9*A_w*delta^(-m)*(4 log n)^(b_max/q+delta)
for any 0<delta<=rho0. The factor m! cancels against the Taylor remainder.
There is NO q^m cost from taking each summand's absolute value first.
The same argument for the center derivative gives
|partial_x sum w_i A_(b_i/q)(x)/h(q)| <=K_w=12*A_w*rho0^(-m).
More specifically, apply Cauchy pointwise to c_a*v^(-a): its modulus on
the permitted circle is at most (3/2)*v^(-1/2). After moment cancellation,
integration against 1/Z gives total variation at most K_w for the FULL
center's signed cut density. Thus its Mellin modulus is at most
K_w/(|s|*|Im(s)|). This uses a density variation bound, not an inference
of variation from a bound on the center derivative alone. The jet density
likewise has variation at most 12*2^m, with its Taylor factorial retained.
For m=0 use a direct weighted envelope, giving the same formulas.
Therefore, for every q>=q0 and EVERY real x>=1,
|R_q(x)| <=A_w*x*[9*delta(V)^(-m)*exp((b_max/q+delta(V))*V)
                            +12*rho0^(-m)],
V=log(4*(1+log x)). A_w and rho0 do not grow with q. All signed
arithmetic, normalizations and the complete center remain present.

5. The detector chooses radii, rather than receiving a winning estimate.
The admissible language has constant radii, positive rational powers of
shifted V and integer m, and a cap by the permitted Cauchy radius. For ANY
positive radius program whose cap is rho0, paragraph 4 is a universal bound.
The numeric learner receives anonymous Cauchy-bound response tables and
grid minimizers, not expected coefficients. Generic sparse monomial fitting
can propose reusable capped inverse products; the admission backend checks
positivity, cap, domain and exact original semantics. Rejected or unsupported
fits are retained. Sampling an effective bound at huge V is explicitly
optimization of a PROVED envelope, not sampling actual primes at huge x.

For example, a positive reciprocal-linear radius yields a true envelope
of order x*(1+log x)^(b_max/q)*(1+log(1+log x))^m. This is a
smaller logarithmic loss than x*log x and is uniform in the parameter
normalization. Fractional radius exponents give other fully declared,
possibly stretched iterated-log envelopes. Every such bound still has
ordinary power one. No selected radius is asserted in the discovery path.
Promotion and equal-budget random-radius ablation use the same data and
number of extra slots. Exact fitted formulas are frozen before validation.

6. Whole continued jet observers by two different evaluations.
Let F_a(s) be the preceding stage's WHOLE continued numerator. Its Taylor
coefficient F_m^jet(s)=[a^m]F_a(s) is evaluated by a finite parameter
difference of complete Euler continuations, with a rigorous Cauchy error.
F_0(s)=zeta(s)-1 exactly. For a disk R=1/2, a native bound on zeta'
along s+[0,1] and exponential zeta tails prove
B_F=3*|zeta(s)-1|+(3/2)*[2*B_zeta_prime
              +(1+2/Re(s))*2^(-Re(s)-1)/log 2]
as an upper bound on |F_a(s)| throughout the disk. The regularized
fractional integral proves this bound for complex a, including zero.
For h>0, m*h<R, the m-th forward difference divided by m!*h^m differs
from the Taylor coefficient by at most
m*(m+1)*h*B_F/(R-m*h)^(m+1).
Native resources scale with m; exhaustion is missing, not a counterexample.

The independent checker integrates whole zeta with logarithmic kernels:
J_k=1/k!*[Integral_0^1 (zeta(s)-zeta(s+t))*(-log t)^k/t dt
             +Integral_1^infinity (1-zeta(s+t))*(-log t)^k/t dt].
Then F_m^jet=g_m*(zeta(s)-1)+sum_(r=0..m-1)g_r*J_(m-1-r).
The small-t Taylor endpoint and the exponential upper tail are enclosed
completely. This checker imports no producer mathematical module. A
nonzero jet numerator at one query is not an all-height nonvanishing proof.
Every reciprocal cost includes the full jet normalization and center
derivative guard; nonpositive Mellin gaps and uncertain numerators are missing.

7. Ordinary-zero relevance and the necessary bank quantifier.
Every finite-q parameter family retains the preceding stage's cofinal
all-height RH implication. Compiling its limit does not preserve that
implication for a single positive-order jet, whose numerator may mask zeros.
This unresolved recovery gate is recorded, rather than silently inherited.
The m=0 jet is the known recovering Mertens calibration.

A NEW all-height sufficient bank statement is: for ALL integers m>=M,
there exists finite C_m with, for EVERY real x>=1,
|V_m(x)-B_m(x)|<=C_m*x^(1/2+eps/(m+1)^nu)*log(e+x)^d,
where eps,nu>0 and d>=0 are fixed. No growth law for C_m is required.
At an ordinary zero rho with beta>1/2, all sufficiently large m have
Mellin integrals holomorphic at rho, forcing F_m^jet(rho)=0 for every
integer in a TAIL, not merely an unbounded sampled subsequence. The entire
function a->F_a(rho) would be a polynomial. Its positive integer values
are (-1)^k*zeta^(k)(rho), giving zeta infinite Taylor radius and
contradicting the actual pole at one. Functional symmetry then gives RH.
Finite banks, finite samples or a cofinal subset of m do not supply this
tail premise. The true proved bank envelopes are power one, not critical.

8. Experimental scope.
Search protocols, grammar, source hashes, arithmetic/validation splits,
seeds, fitting tolerances, promotion and ablation rules precede scoring.
Anonymous root responses can infer limiting weight features without prime
names or an expected formula. Exact limit admission follows the fit and
does not alter its original coefficients. Generic unrelated coefficient
sequences test the finite analytic identities separately; their large input
amplitudes explicitly refute transferring actual-prime norm certificates.
Fresh actual scales, parameter levels and envelope coordinates are generated
after sealing and are unavailable to the learning worker. Failed runs retain
their original source and data. No historical novelty, autonomous proof
invention, new prime-distribution estimate or RH proof is inferred.
