What the search derives
------------------------
For generic real drift variables d, solve the exact rational coefficient
equations for all vector polynomials v(d) of degree<=1 satisfying v.d=0.
No pairwise solution is seeded. RREF yields a complete10-dimensional
kernel of a rank20 map. The independent checker recomputes constraint

For s_j=sum_i v_(j,i), every nonempty basis subset T yields
  W_i=sum_(j in T) s_j*v_(j,i), S=sum_(j in T) s_j^2.
The checker expands all1023 constructions and proves
  sum_i W_i=S, sum_i W_i*d_i=0, S>=0.
Where S>0, lambda_i=W_i/S thus recovers
  g=sum_i lambda_i(R_i+P_i).
The weights are constant through the entire prefix/channel/cell map at
fixed N. Negative weights and all residual crosses remain. Cauchy gives
  E_g <= 2 E_(sum_i lambda_i R_i) + 2 K sum_i C_Pi,
  K=sum_i lambda_i^2.

This is a conditional exact recovery theorem. It does not establish
S>0 at all arithmetic scales, a uniform bound for K, or the signed
weighted residual bound. Equal nonzero abstract drifts give S=0 for
every construction, so separate drift magnitude upper bounds cannot
prove recovery. That countermodel concerns the weak proof route, not
the actual Mobius sequence or RH. A separately certified all-zero drift
would permit identity recovery; a zero-containing ball is insufficient.
