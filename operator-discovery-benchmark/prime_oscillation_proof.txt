General program class, not a nominated formula
------------------------------------------------
In the log channel let
  E(x)=sum b_j I_(1-jdelta)(x)-sum_(n<x)Lambda(n)P(n^(-delta)),
  P(z)=sum c_j z^j, c_r=1, nu=r delta, delta>0.
Let W(t)=1+sum_(j>r)c_j t^(-(j-r)delta), so P(t^(-delta))=t^(-nu)W(t).
This normalized polynomial is eventually positive even if it changes sign
at smaller indices. Put S=sum_(j>r)|c_j| and choose rational
  z0=min(1,1/(2S)) if S>0; z0=1 otherwise.
For EVERY real t>=T=max(2,z0^(-1/delta)), the elementary triangle bound gives
  |W(t)-1|<=S z0<=1/2.
Thus g(t)=1/P(t^(-delta)) exists there and satisfies
  |g(t)|<=2 t^nu,
  |g'(t)|<=(2nu+4delta sum_(j>r)(j-r)|c_j|)t^(nu-1).
The implementation chooses an integer power of two T meeting the exact
rational inequality T^(delta numerator)*z0^(delta denominator)>=1.
The program certificate supplies a universal eventual inverse, not an
all-height complex multiplier certificate.

Consider an alleged integer bound
  |E(n)-A|<=C n^(1/2-nu), every integer n>=1,
with any fixed real A. Suppose each normalizer mismatch obeys
  c_j!=b_j => (j-r)delta>=1/2.                           (1)
This implies no normalizer precedes the leading arithmetic shift. It is
exactly the real-pole compensation condition at the critical output exponent.
The previously proved whole-unit extension gives, at real x>=T,
  e(x)=E(x)-A = O(x^(1/2-nu)) + O(x^(-nu)log(e+x)).

Write B(t)=sum b_j t^(-jdelta). In the sense of Stieltjes measures,
  de=B(t)dt-P(t^(-delta))dpsi(t).
On [T,x] invert the weight and integrate by parts:
  psi(x)-x = constant + integral_T^x(B(t)/P(t^(-delta))-1)dt
                        -g(x)e(x)+integral_T^x g'(t)e(t)dt.
Strict versus inclusive endpoints alter this by at most Lambda(x)=O(log x).
Condition (1) bounds the first integral by O(sqrt(x)): each mismatch, after
division by the leading twist, decays at least as t^(-1/2).
The g and g' bounds give O(sqrt(x))+O(log(e+x)^2) for the remaining terms.
Consequently the alleged bound forces psi(x)-x=O(sqrt(x)), contradicting
the pinned oscillation theorem. ALL such pure critical-power log-channel
claims are false, including fractional monomials and signed polynomials.
Nearby real centers cannot repair this oscillation obstruction.

Weighted channel and slowly varying factors
---------------------------------------------
In the lambda channel inversion to psi introduces an additional log(t).
A critical pure-power lambda bound consequently gives a psi bound with a
logarithmic loss, so the above rejection cannot be applied to that channel.
Similarly a positive log-power in a log-channel bound is not rejected by
this theorem: log(e+x)^ell eventually dominates log log log x for every
fixed ell>0. Compatibility is not a proof that the estimate is true.

The audit retains the source programs, coefficients, learned constants,

log-power estimate already present in that batch for the same rejected

Repaired choices are sealed before fresh final cases. All earlier final
and exhaustive coverage data are development evidence for this extension.
