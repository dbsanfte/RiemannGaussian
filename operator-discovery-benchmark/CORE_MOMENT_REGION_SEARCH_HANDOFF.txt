Why the next search must change mechanism
The same raw component U power holds for arbitrary complex |a_n|<=1 with
c=sum a_n/n: |A(s)|<=U^(1-sigma)/(1-sigma) and
|c-A(s)|<=2U^(1-sigma)/(1-sigma). Thus this progress comes from a stronger
proof language, not a new Mobius-specific exponent gain. No generic
mu*One=delta support identity is inferred.

The detector also computes an optimistic limitation of this proof language.
Even if all moment orders at all effective lines>=1/2 were granted, the
reflected high branch requires sigma>(m-1)/(2m). With the current absolute
finite-factor cost U^(2(p+m)(1-sigma)), the quartic case p2,m4 has best
possible energy infimum1/16, already positive. All20 remaining m4..23
targets fail this optimistic gate. This is NOT an arithmetic lower bound
or impossibility theorem. It says that merely importing more standalone
moments cannot close those targets in this grammar.
