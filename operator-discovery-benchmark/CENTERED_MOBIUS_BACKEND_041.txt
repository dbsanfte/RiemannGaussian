1. Exact source and prime-power proof

Let r be any canonical rational with0<r<1/2, theta=1-r, and q any integer>=2.
All products in this memo are Dirichlet convolution. Define K_r(n)=n^-r,
b=K_r*mu, c=b-q delta_q*b, where delta_q is1 exactly at q.
The unit is b(1)=c(1)=1. The generating series at each actual prime p is
 (1-t)/(1-p^-r t).
Writing v=p^-r in(0,1), its coefficients are b(p^e)=(v-1)v^(e-1), e>=1.
Consequently |b(p^e)|<=1, and multiplicativity proves |b(n)|<=1 for ALL n.
No squarefree restriction is made: every repeated valuation is retained.
The exact all-prime adjunction law, including its non-coprime branch, is
 b(pn)=v b(n) if p divides n, and (v-1)b(n) otherwise.
In particular b(p^e)=v b(p^(e-1)) only for e>=2; the unit is separate.

For arbitrary composite q, c(n)=b(n)-q*1_(q divides n)*b(n/q) is used directly.
Thus |c(n)|<=q+1. No multiplicativity of c for composite q is asserted.
If q is prime, its local series is (1-t)(1-q t)/(1-q^-r t).
The exceptional valuation-one crossing must remain:
 c(qn)=q^-r c(n)-(q+1)1_(q does not divide n)b(n)
        +q1_(valuation_q(n)=1)b(n/q).
At other primes p!=q, c(pn)=p^-r c(n)-1_(p does not divide n)c(n).
Geometric recurrence at the q prime starts at e>=3, not e>=2.
The certificate includes these conditional laws without claiming q prime.

2. Analytic center, complete increment, real endpoints

Define the analytic scalar
 kappa=(1-q^r)/(theta*zeta(theta)).
This is analytic continuation of the normalization. It is NOT an assertion
that sum mu(n)/n^theta converges ordinarily. For real x>0 let
 J_q(x)=sum_(n<=x)c(n)-kappa*x^theta.
The equivalent expression J(x)-q J(x/q), with
 J(x)=sum_(n<=x)b(n)-x^theta/(theta*zeta(theta)), retains the center even when
 x/q<1. Replacing x/q by floor(x/q) in the power center changes the source.
At integer n>=1 its COMPLETE increment is
 a(n)=c(n)-kappa*(n^theta-(n-1)^theta),

Concavity of x^theta gives0< n^theta-(n-1)^theta<=1, including n1.
Given an INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED rational ceiling T>=|kappa|, therefore
 |a(n)|<=q+1+T, every integer n>=1.
This bounds coefficients/increments, not accumulated signed energy. In
particular it does not pay any generated energy recurrence or storage claim.
An externally supplied ceiling in symbolic_certificate is a premise; it
requires separate verification. normalization_certificate computes a finite
positive Arb enclosure and its upper absolute ceiling and binds the complete
parameter/certificate payload. Invalid/insufficient enclosures raise.
