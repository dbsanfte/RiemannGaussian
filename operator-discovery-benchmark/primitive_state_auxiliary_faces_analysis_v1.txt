Source semantics and exact coefficient derivation

cancellation_cubic_gate.build_constraint supplies zero target, empty square
groups and relation c_j*(mu_1-1). The only nonzero coefficients are at old
signed relation positions 9,21,33:
    c_9 = -56/3, c_21 = -12, c_33 = -20/3.
All remaining coefficients are zero. The primitive extension retains the old
403 relation groups and appends 210 empty groups, hence 613 total. All 366
auxiliary square groups are empty. cancellation_complete_gram_program and
cancellation_affine_storage keep both regional Gram-block lists empty;
both transported storage-block lists are empty too. The 15-dimensional
formal vector z does not become an auxiliary Gram row.

Let r_j denote the effective signed relation coefficient and s_aux >= 0 the
per-instance constant slack added by grouped_arithmetic_synthesis._add_slacks.
Define gamma = -(56/3)*r_9 - 12*r_21 - (20/3)*r_33.
The complete auxiliary polynomial equation is
    0 = gamma*(mu_1-1) + s_aux.
With five instances ordered ordinary X=1,2,3,5 followed by auxiliary,
the unchanged 1957-coordinate layout has squares 0..365, signed splits
366..1591, slacks 1592..1596 and Gram coordinates 1597..1956. The forced
auxiliary slack is coordinate 1596 (zero-based), or coordinate 1597 in
one-based notation. Its value is zero independently of other slack values.

The coefficient of mu_1 gives gamma=0. The constant coefficient gives
-gamma+s_aux=0, so s_aux=0. Every shared square and every regional/storage
matrix coordinate has coefficient zero in both auxiliary rows.

An exact exposing functional exists, but exposes only the auxiliary slack:
set y=1 on the auxiliary constant coefficient and y=1 on its mu_1
coefficient; set y=0 on every ordinary-instance coefficient. This evaluates
an auxiliary polynomial at mu_1=1. Every signed relation column has pairing
c_j*(-1+1)=0; every square and every PSD coordinate has pairing zero;
the auxiliary slack column has pairing 1; all other slack columns and the
complete target have pairing zero. Thus the cone-dual scalar entries are
nonnegative, every dual PSD block is the zero matrix, and target pairing
is exactly zero. This certifies s_aux=0 for every full feasible certificate.
It supplies no other vanishing nonnegative term.

Exact counterexample to any stronger auxiliary-only face claim

Assign every shared square weight 1, all three Gram/storage matrices I_15,
and all effective signed coefficients zero. Assign s_aux=0. These values
satisfy the complete auxiliary equation and every relevant positivity
constraint, while all shared square weights are strictly positive and all
three PSD blocks are positive definite. In the numerical nonnegative
positive/negative split for signed coefficients, take BOTH split coordinates
1 for every relation; their difference remains zero, so neither split is
forced to vanish. Ordinary per-instance slacks can also be positive because
they do not occur in the auxiliary equations.

This example is deliberately an auxiliary-only example. It makes no claim
that the ordinary-scale polynomial identities are satisfied. It proves that
no logical consequence of the auxiliary equations and cone constraints alone
can force any shared square, signed split, or PSD direction to zero. The
auxiliary-only cone slice has interior in those coordinates, after removing
the single zero auxiliary slack and imposing the signed linear gamma equality.

The formal vector and signed storage do not rescue the proposed inference

The actual formal state vector is
    z = (1,0,0,0,0,0,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2).
Its exact squared norm is 13/4. For ordinary scale X the formal matrix scalar
ledger is
    X*(2*z^T G_child z + z^T G_parent z - 2*z^T K z).
The negative storage term is essential: storage columns are child-4*parent,
so their formal block counts are 2X-4X=-2X. K is PSD, but its transported
polynomial is not a nonnegative square contribution.

Even if the entire displayed matrix ledger were separately set equal to zero,
this would not force any z-null direction. An exact strictly positive-definite
example is
    G_child = I_15, G_parent = I_15, K = (3/2)*I_15.
Then z^T G_child z = z^T G_parent z = 13/4,
z^T K z = 39/8, and 2*(13/4)+(13/4)-2*(39/8)=0.
All three matrices have full rank. The associated linear functional has dual
matrix blocks (2*zz^T, zz^T, -2*zz^T); its negative storage block prevents it
from belonging to the dual of the product PSD cone. It therefore cannot be
used as a nonnegative exposing identity. In the actual full problem, signed
relation contributions and ordinary target/slack terms must also be retained.

What an additional rigorous reduction would require

For the unchanged complete ordinary-plus-auxiliary equality system A*x=b,
a facial argument needs an exact functional y with y^T b=0, annihilating each
effective signed column, nonnegative pairing against every scalar nonnegative
column, and a PSD dual matrix H for every primal PSD block. Offdiagonal
upper-triangle coefficient pairings must be divided by 2 to obtain H entries
under the trace pairing. Exact equality and exact PSD verification are required.
Then positive scalar pairings force the corresponding coordinates to zero,
and trace(H*G)=0 for PSD H,G implies H*G=0, equivalently range(G) lies in
ker(H). The storage dual block must be formed from the complete signed
child-4*parent columns; it cannot be treated as a positive child block.
No such additional exposing witness is established in this analysis. Solver
errors, floating negative square weights/diagonals, and nonzero residuals are
not witnesses of a face. A consistent exact linear basis alone also does not
establish cone feasibility or forced faces.

All-scale and theorem scope

The gamma gate is a conditional necessary restriction for unchanged fixed
mu templates, q>1 and the proved scalar-growth assumptions. This finite
cone calculation is neither an all-scale identity nor an arithmetic bound,
and proves no RH statement. Additional facial constraints, if later derived
from the finite sample equations, must still be checked against the original
complete equations and separately interpreted under the all-scale grammar.
