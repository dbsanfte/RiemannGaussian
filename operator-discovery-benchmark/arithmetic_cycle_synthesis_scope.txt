EXACT FINITE ARITHMETIC SOS SYNTHESIS: SOUNDNESS AND LIMITS

Independent mathematical scope review of the producer language and synthesis
pipeline, before its first scored freeze. This text is not a formal machine-
checked proof and does not establish any full actual bound or RH conclusion.
The language and solver are reviewed as implementations of finite algebra.

1. Certificate semantics and positivity.
A polynomial is a finite rational linear combination of sorted monomials
 x_(i1)...x_(id), including repeated indices; constant monomial is empty.
For target actual mu or c, every whitelisted relation rho(x)=0 holds:
 unit x1-1;
 coprime_product xa*xb-xab, with EXACT gcd(a,b)=1;
 squarefree_zero xn for nonsquarefree n, ONLY actual mu;
 prime_power x_(p^e)-v, with proven prime p and positive e, v=-1 at e1
 and0 at higher e for mu, v=2(-1)^e for c;
 inverse_row sum_(d|n)xd-1_(n=1), ONLY actual mu.
Unknown shapes, individualized actual coefficient axioms, arbitrary custom
relations, missing product indices, false coprimality and nonprime prime-power
premises are rejected. At zero coefficients the coprime relation still holds;
no division by xab is permitted. Relation multipliers may have either sign.

The compiler checks the EXACT coefficient identity
 lhs=sum_j w_j P_j(x)^2+sum_i Q_i(x)*rho_i(x), w_j>=0 rational.
For actual arithmetic, the second sum vanishes and every square is nonnegative,
so lhs(actual)>=0. The identity is finite and polynomial, independent of actual
coefficient evaluation or approximate numerical residuals. For generic inputs
positivity is conditional on the same whitelisted relations, not unconditional.
A zero polynomial has a valid empty certificate. Omitting even one nonzero
coefficient invalidates matching; negative square weights are invalid.

2. Full defect and a rational bound.
For integer X>=1 and N=4X-1, the mandatory original complete defect polynomial is
 Delta_X(x)=sum_(i,j<=N)J_X(max(i,j))*xi*xj,
 J_X(t)=max(0,4X-max(2X,t))-4max(0,2X-max(X,t)).
The symmetric implementation merges ordered pairs into coefficient J on i=j
and2J on i<j. This retains ALL original coefficients, endpoints and cross terms.
To prove a finite proposed bound D, the certificate must match
 lhs=D-Delta_X exactly, not a projection, substituted response, or different
polynomial. D=C X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)), C>=0 exact rational and q positive integer,
is itself rational because the ceiling is an exact integer. The runner/checker
must independently bind X,q,C, the complete polynomial and exact root ceiling.
Floating powers may not define D. An unavailable exact ceiling is missing,
not a rounded bound, successful certificate or mathematical counterexample.

3. Product horizon, degree and explicit resource scope.
Complete coprime ordered a,b<=N needs xab through N^2; checking a,b alone is
insufficient. All source variables in lhs, squares and multipliers must lie in
the declared positive horizon. Products of polynomials multiply formal variables:
xa*xb is not automatically xab without the corresponding admitted relation.
The polynomial degree, monomial count, expanded term count, primality/squarefree
predicate work, divisor-row enumeration, coefficient-bit size and exact linear
algebra costs are distinct constraints. Budget failures must retain missing
outcomes, never truncate a complete domain or change a mathematical relation.
The language's scalar work cap is not a total physical-memory/bit-complexity
bound. The synthesis adds degree, monomial, denominator, coefficient-bit,
source-horizon and time limits. No finite computational cap is a global
semantic bound or proof of infeasibility.

4. Solver soundness and incompleteness.
The declared square basis generates columns P_j^2. Each grouped relation column
is an EXACT sum of declared multiplier*relation products. Both signs of every
relation column are available as nonnegative LP columns, so arbitrary signed
relation weights are expressible. Floating LP supplies only a support proposal.
Exact Fraction elimination solves coefficient equations on that support, with
free variables set to0; inconsistent or negative reconstructed weights are
rejected. It then reconstructs the entire polynomial and re-runs the strict
certificate compiler. Neither floating feasibility nor tiny residuals can
certify an identity. A float false positive is therefore harmless if the exact
matching/reconstruction gate remains mandatory.
Free-zero RREF can reject a support that has another nonnegative solution;
thresholded support can omit a needed column. Solver resource failure and
no_certificate_found are NOT rigorous nonexistence or infeasibility results.
No LP optimum is independently certified or required for finite soundness.
Grouping may compress a proof, but grouping does not change the relations or
remove unmatched monomials. Aggregate weights must expand back to every term
of every group in the final independent certificate check.

5. Atomic calibration versus reusable arithmetic proof.
Complete local normalization/multiplicativity determines the actual finite
sequence. A freely chosen relation multiplier for each index can encode those
known finite coefficients and re-express an already true finite inequality.
Such atomic_local synthesis is a calibration of exact finite proof plumbing,
not independent new cancellation or a uniformly compressed theorem. It can
succeed without explicitly receiving a coefficient table: sufficient local
relations already determine that table logically.
Grouped_cycles constrains the relation multiplier family and tests compression,
but STILL uses free per-index square weights; it is not a fully grouped or
uniform proof ansatz. The pilot intentionally uses only relation pairs ab<=N,
a subset of coprime a,b<=N. This is sound because the lhs retains the FULL
pair defect and every missing relation is simply unavailable to synthesis;
there is no claim that the pilot materializes the complete N^2 cycle domain.
The separate complete cycle production does require horizonN^2. A
successful group does not automatically give a scale-independent recursion or
control its cost at an unbounded horizon. A new genuine proof needs a symbolic
or recursive family valid for every declared scale, with all initializer,
coefficient-growth, prime-normalization, product-index and boundary charges.

6. Frozen pilot operational scope.
The proposed pilot declares development X1,2 and final X4 plus odd X5 or7
chosen from the training seal, C in{1,4}, q in{2,6}, and modes atomic_local and
grouped_cycles. The solver RECOMPUTES weights at each final case. Consequently
this is a post-seal finite feasibility audit, NOT out-of-sample transfer of a
learned operator or fixed certificate coefficients. Novel final indices and
sealed dataflow do not imply mathematical uniformity. OddX is a valid finite
arithmetic identity case but is not an all-dyadic quantifier witness. Numerical
scores, modes, failed certificates and resource failures must all be retained.
Each accepted final certificate must be checked independently coefficientwise;
no actual f evaluation is needed for that identity check.

7. Uniform RH bridge; all premises retained.
A sufficient actual premise is: there exists integer q0>=1 such that for EVERY
integer q>=q0 there is finite Cq independent of X, and for EVERY dyadic X>=1 a
sound complete certificate proves Delta_f(X)<=Cq X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)).
All coefficients may depend on q, but no unknown global validity of the template
is supplied by its finite instances. Since ceil(X^(1/q))<=2X^(1/q), original
nonnegative block energies telescope to A_f(X)=O_q(X^(2+1/q)), with initial A_f(1)
and the finite geometric factor1/(2^(1/q)-1) charged. Absolute Mellin convergence
then excludes ordinary zeta zeros in Re(s)>1/2: actual mu represents1/zeta(s),
and actual c represents zeta(2s)/zeta(s)^2 with numerator nonzero in that open
half-plane. Functional symmetry would imply RH. This is a conditional theorem,
not a conclusion of the finite pipeline. A finite q list does not give cofinality.
