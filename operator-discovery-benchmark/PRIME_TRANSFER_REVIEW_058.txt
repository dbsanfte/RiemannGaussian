GENERIC PRIME-VALUATION TRANSFER: ANALYTICAL REVIEW 058

Scope
=====
This NEW review preserves every frozen057 and earlier artifact. Producer
prime_transfer_algebra_058.py and independent check_prime_transfer_058.py
were read without execution. No scientific source values, tests, numerical
integrals, tables, candidate scores, or hashes were computed. Exact identities
and endpoint rules below are paid algebra. The whole signed middle bound
remains OPEN. A separately proved conditional RH implication is not an
upper-bound provider or an executable admission of a source estimate.

Fix 0<alpha<1, a finite universe P of distinct actual primes, and for a
subset S let R_S=product S and G_S(t)=product_(p in S)(1-p^(-t)). For n>=1
let G_n(t)=product_(p|n)(1-p^(-t)), including G_1=1. The inclusive kernel is

 H_S(x,t)=sum_(n>=1) v_x(n)G_(R_S n)(t),
 v_x(n)=1_(2x<n<=4x)-2*1_(x<n<=2x), x>0,t>0.                (1)

Its equivalent prefix coefficients are (2,-3,1). Actual K(1)=0 is NOT the
integral of G_1. Inclusive kernels are legitimate formal finite-t states;
normalized integration additionally requires convergence of the WHOLE
combination and correct units.

1. Exact signatures cover every valuation and outside-prime factor
=================================================================
An atom is a^(-t)H_S(x/d,t), with a,d positive integers supported on P.
Change n=d*m. Its coefficient at the common index n is zero unless d|n;
otherwise it is a^(-t)G_(R_S(n/d))(t). Put z_p=p^(-t), e_p=v_p(n), and
f_p=v_p(d). The registered polynomial is exactly

 1_(e_p>=f_p for all p) * product_p z_p^(v_p(a))
                    * product_(p in S or e_p>f_p)(1-z_p).   (2)

The factor for every prime OUTSIDE P is G_(n_outside)(t), common to all
atoms, because neither d nor S changes that outside cofactor. It is positive
for t>0 and bounded by one. Its arbitrary prime powers are retained.

For divisors d in {1} union P, the complete states are e_p=0,1,>=2. In the
middle state division by p removes its last occurrence; in the last it
leaves a p factor. Using only presence/absence for this grammar would be
wrong. For more general divisors, the sufficient states are
0,...,m_p,>=m_p+1, m_p=max_atom v_p(d). Both reviewed implementations use
this correct threshold and the residual test e_p>f_p.

A rational nullspace of all coefficients of (2), across all states,
proves an identity for EVERY real x>0 and t>0. Finite summation retains
all floor endpoints automatically. A polynomial identity is sufficient
without treating actual prime phases as independent random variables.
Conversely, a nonzero formal polynomial cannot vanish identically under
z_p=p^(-t): distinct integer prime monomials are distinct integers by
unique factorization, and their finitely many exponentials in t are
linearly independent. The finite nullspace is complete for this declared
valuation-polynomial signature. It is not a classification of all
arithmetic identities, all integral aliases, or all signed bounds.

The producer's signature and independent checker's literal polynomial
construction agree on these mathematical scopes. Exact RREF and independent
recomposition pay the identity, not a norm bound or RH. Current-only order
uses presence classes 0/1 because no division is then performed; that
smaller state set is correct in that restricted API.

2. Real units and divisor constants are different quantities
============================================================
Let h_S exclude n=1. Then

 H_S(x,t)=h_S(x,t)+G_S(t)v_x(1),
 v_x(1)=2*1_(x>=1)-3*1_(2x>=1)+1_(4x>=1).                  (3)

The unit is +1 on 1/4<=x<1/2, -2 on 1/2<=x<1, and zero elsewhere.
It is zero at ALL real x>=1, not just integer x. This is distinct from

 W(x)=floor(4x)-3floor(2x)+2floor(x), |W(x)|<=1,

which is the entire d=1 DIVISOR/cofactor contribution after factorization.
W(x) can be nonzero at a real x>1. For integer N, W(N)=0, so the common
tail rule below has no d=1 denominator. For lower atoms x=N/d neither
quantity can be deleted without checking its own condition.

An empty-S atom at a lower scale can have a nonzero constant t=0 source
unit and a divergent normalized integral there. Exact finite-t identities
do not allow integration of those atoms separately through zero. A whole
combination may cancel that row and become integrable; the cancellation
must precede integration. Individual geometric chains and new real-argument
norms are not licensed by old all-integer source statements.

3. Weighted exchange is exact complete lower-scale transport
===========================================================
Let p,q be distinct actual primes outside S, R=R_S. With a=p^(-t),b=q^(-t)
prime insertion gives

 H_(Rp)=(1-a)H_R+a H_(Rp)(x/p),
 H_(Rq)=(1-b)H_R+b H_(Rq)(x/q).

Inserting the other prime in the lower terms cancels the joint x/(pq)
state, and proves

 C_R,p,q(x,t)=(1-q^(-t))H_(Rp)(x,t)-(1-p^(-t))H_(Rq)(x,t)
             =p^(-t)H_(Rpq)(x/p,t)-q^(-t)H_(Rpq)(x/q,t).   (4)

This is valid for every real x>0,t>0, including all units and all repeated
valuations. It does not assume gcd(n,pq)=1. At the coefficient level C
vanishes on n avoiding p,q; if only p divides n its coefficient is
p^(-t)(1-q^(-t))G_(Rn); if only q divides n it is the negative of
q^(-t)(1-p^(-t))G_(Rn); if both divide n it is
(p^(-t)-q^(-t))G_(Rn). The full both-prime sector is mandatory.
The unit coefficient C(1,t) is exactly zero. The lower atom units in (4)
map to the ACTUAL source indices n=p and n=q, not to a removable n=1.

For complete normalization define

 f_p(k)=(alpha I_alpha)^(-1) integral_0^infinity
                         p^(-t)G_(Rpq k)(t)t^(-alpha-1)dt,
 f_q analogously, and A_C(x)=sum_(n<=x)C_normalized(n).

Both f_p,f_q are finite and nonnegative, including k=1. When p<q,
f_p>=f_q and (4) gives the exact positive prefix/complement decomposition

 A_C(x)=sum_(k<=x/q)(f_p(k)-f_q(k))
                      +sum_(x/q<k<=x/p)f_p(k)>=0.           (5)

This is a genuine paid signed transport property: common-cofactor
cancellation is performed before positivity, and the outside-domain
complement is retained. It does NOT give a small centered error. For k>=2,
f_p(k)<=K(Rpq*k)/alpha<=K(k)/alpha<=log(k)^alpha/alpha;
the k=1 term is at most K(Rpq)/alpha. Thus (5) has an unconditional
linear-times-logarithmic envelope. Its positive real linear main term
must be removed by the scale filter before a square-root premise is even
possible. Summatory positivity cannot bound that signed scale difference.

4. A conditional RH bridge for this complete exchange source
============================================================
This paragraph proves an implication, not a source bound or converse.
For Re(s)>1 put

 U_M(s,t)=product_(l|M) (1-l^(-t))/(1-l^(-s-t)), M=Rpq.

The exact Dirichlet symbol of C_normalized is

 F_C(s)=(alpha I_alpha)^(-1) integral_0^infinity
 U_M(s,t)*zeta(s)/zeta(s+t)
                 *[p^(-s-t)-q^(-s-t)]*t^(-alpha-1)dt.       (6)

To verify it, the Dirichlet series of G_(Mn) is
U_M(s,t)zeta(s)/zeta(s+t); multiply by (1-q^(-t)) or
(1-p^(-t)) in the two current terms and subtract. The numerator difference
is exactly p^(-s-t)-q^(-s-t). Equivalently reindex both lower states in
(4). C(1,t)=0, so no fictitious unit subtraction is inserted into (6).
For n>=2 its normalized coefficient is bounded in absolute value by
2K(n)/alpha. This pays absolute initial Dirichlet convergence and finite
Fubini on Re(s)>1. Near t=0 the registered order is ell=|S|+2; at infinity
the bracket decays. All integrals in (6) are consequently well defined.

The real pole at s=1 has strictly positive residue for p<q: every factor
of its integral, including p^(-1-t)-q^(-1-t), is positive. No claim of a
Tauberian asymptotic is required to see that an unfiltered square-root
bound for A_C would contradict this pole. Our intended prefix operator
already uses P(z)=z^2-3z+2 with P(2)=0, canceling this real linear mode.

For the nonreal obstruction, suppose RH is false and choose a zero rho
with Re(rho)>1/2, rightmost at its FIXED imaginary height. Standard zeta
continuation, zero location, isolation, and functional equation are the
imported background from NIST DLMF25.2,25.4,25.10:
https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2 ; https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4 ;
https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10 . They do not assert our exchange bridge.
The finite compact-ray and Euler-tail argument licenses continuation to
rho+delta along delta>0; it does not assert a zero-free half-plane.
Let the zero have any integer multiplicity d>=1. Locally
zeta(rho+z)=z^d g(z), g(0)!=0, and U_M(s,t)=t^ell u(s,t).
The smooth amplitude at (rho,0) contains

 u(rho,0)*[p^(-rho)-q^(-rho)] !=0.                           (7)

The first factor is nonzero because Re(rho)>0; the bracket cannot vanish
because |p^(-rho)| and |q^(-rho)| differ. Thus the same fully
Taylor-subtracted integral argument of review056 gives

 F_C(rho+delta)=Q_(ell-1)(delta)
                    +c_rho delta^(ell-alpha)+O(delta^ell),
 c_rho!=0.                                                 (8)

For d>=ell the coefficient uses the convergent positive beta integral.
For d<ell the finite part after subtracting powers through ell-d-1 has
coefficient Gamma(ell-alpha)*Gamma(d-ell+alpha)/Gamma(d),
which is finite and nonzero, with sign (-1)^(ell-d). Analytic amplitudes
shift subsequent noninteger powers above ell; the kth right-derivative
remainder is integrable because 1-alpha>0. Zero simplicity is unnecessary.

A fixed nonzero finite scale polynomial P with P(q0)=0 removes the real
linear mode. A hypothetical all-integer square-root times fixed-polylog
bound on its complete prefix filter extends to all real x by the bound
2log(n)^alpha/alpha on source jumps. Its Mellin transform is
P(q0^s)F_C(s)/s minus exact finite heads, which are entire. It would be
holomorphic on Re(s)>1/2. Even if P(q0^rho) has a finite zero of order j,
(8) leaves a noninteger power ell+j-alpha after analytic subtraction.
That contradicts holomorphy. Consequently this hypothetical COMPLETE
source-bound implies RH. No such bound has been proved; positivity (5)
or exact transfer (4) does not supply it. Moving-middle variants additionally
need paid endpoint restoration before this complete-source implication.

5. The unregistered sector is a local recovery obstruction
=========================================================
At a coefficient n avoiding ALL registered primes, every d>1 atom
vanishes. Current atoms have the polynomial

 P_free(z)=sum_(current atoms) c_atom z^(v(a))*G_S(z).        (9)

If this is nonzero, the current combination cannot have an exact
representation solely in lower-scale atoms in this finite grammar.
Conversely P_free=0 is only a necessary condition; all other valuation
rows must also vanish for a proposed identity. It is NOT target coercivity.
A family of operators annihilating this sector cannot algebraically
recover a target whose P_free is nonzero. This is a scoped finite-prime
linear recovery obstruction, not a theorem that global signed recovery
or a source-specific arithmetic proof is impossible.

There is also an adversarial guard against paying a bound from finite
valuation identities plus positivity alone. Replace the common outside
factor by any orbit-invariant psi(n_outside) in [0,1]. All identities (2)
remain true, and the row endpoint bounds remain true. Choose psi to be one
on registered-prime-free cofactors g in (2N,4N] and zero elsewhere. At this
N, no registered-prime multiple of such a g fits below 4N, and none lies
in the negative block. The target current H_S with nonempty S is then
G_S(t) times the number of those g. Finite inclusion-exclusion gives at
least 2N*product_(p in P)(1-1/p)-2^(|P|+1) such g. On any fixed parameter
interval inside (0,1/2), its positive normalized middle mass is linear
in N, exceeding a source-free square-root bound for large N.
This is not the ordinary-mu source and is not a counterexample to RH.
It proves that a universal upper provider using only this local information
is insufficient. It must use an additional property of the actual outside
arithmetic. A vanishing P_free certificate is an identity/support fact,
not that missing property.

6. Finite prime removal is conditional norm equivalence, not closure
===================================================================
For fixed t>0 define D_S(x,t)=H_S(x,t)/G_S(t). When p not in S,

 D_(Sp)(x,t)=D_S(x,t)+p^(-t)D_(Sp)(x/p,t).                   (10)

All real heads are included. Since H_S(x)=0 for x<1/4, iterating (10) is
an EXACT finite sum at every finite horizon:
D_(Sp)(x)=sum_(j>=0)p^(-jt)D_S(x/p^j), stopping when x/p^j<1/4.
No infinite mu series at the critical line is assumed.

For either weighted sup norm sup |f(x)|/x^beta or weighted L2 norm
(integral_0^T |f(x)|^2 x^(-2beta-1)dx)^(1/2), dilation by p has norm
at most p^(-beta) on a finite horizon. If t+beta>0 set c=p^(-t-beta)<1.
Then the universal operator bounds are

 ||D_(Sp)|| <=(1-c)^(-1)||D_S||,
 ||D_S|| <=(1+c)||D_(Sp)||.                                (11)

In critical beta=1/2 these constants are uniform as t decreases to zero;
this is useful conditional equivalence of fixed finite-prime removal.
At t+beta=0 the inverse costs horizon depth; below it no such uniform
contraction holds. Dividing by G_S itself loses its near-zero vanishing,
and replacing fixed-t states by moving-cutoff integrals requires complete
flux and unit descriptors. One cannot integrate (11) as if it paid an
unknown source norm or preserved signed parameter crosses. The unregistered
cofactor forcing remains; (10) is not an RH proof from a norm identity.

7. Current-only endpoint certificates are sound and deliberately limited
=======================================================================
For a CURRENT-ONLY combination sum c_atom a^(-t)H_S(N,t), substitute
z_p=1-y_p exactly in each valuation/presence row. Let k be the minimum
nonzero total y degree over ALL rows, and

 B=max_rows sum_y |c_y| product_p p^(y_exponent_p),
 L=sum_atoms |c_atom|.

For 0<t<=1, 0<=y_p=1-p^(-t)<=t log p<=tp. The common outside factor is
bounded by one. Hence each point coefficient is bounded by B t^k, and
integer-N filter absolute mass is 4N. Thus

 |h_combo(N,t)|<=4NB t^k, N integer>=2, 0<t<=1.              (12)

The unit row can make this conservative: an empty registered sector
could still have a nonempty outside radical at actual n>=2. The certificate
does not get to assume that extra gain without a separate proof.
If the combination is identically zero, it has its own zero-source
certificate. Otherwise k>alpha is required to integrate through zero.
For epsilon=min(1/2,N^(-gamma)), gamma>0,

 |normalized microscopic piece|
 <=12B/(k-alpha)*N^[1-gamma(k-alpha)].                       (13)

For current atoms at integer N, factoring the forced primes gives the
finite mu polynomial with d=1 contribution W(N)=0. Attenuations satisfy
a^(-t)<=1. The source-free tail is consequently

 |normalized t>=1/2 piece|<=128 L sqrt(N).                   (14)

More generally 0<tau<1 gives
16L/[tau^2(1-tau)]*N^(1-tau), matching the independent checker schema.
The normalizer bound (alpha I_alpha)^(-1)<2, log2>=1/2, and finite sum
sum_(d<=4N)d^(-tau)<=(4N)^(1-tau)/(1-tau) prove it. No source data enter.

These endpoint rules do not automatically apply to LOWER atoms, where
W(N/d) may be nonzero, or to individually divergent empty-S components.
The whole row cancellation and real-head treatment must be established.
At the weighted exchange (4), the exact current order is ell=|S|+2:
p-only and q-only classes attain it, the both-prime class has extra order,
and the avoiding class is zero. This is a paid endpoint gain with an
unpaid middle, not a guarantee that the complete source has small energy.

8. Concrete next arithmetic leaf and proof-language guard
=========================================================
The weighted exchange has a full ordinary-mu cofactor expression, with
M=Rpq, p<q:

 A_C(x)=c_alpha integral_0^infinity G_M(t)
  sum_(d<=x/p,gcd(d,M)=1)mu(d)d^(-t)
       [p^(-t)floor(x/(pd))-q^(-t)floor(x/(qd))]
                                     t^(-alpha-1)dt.       (15)

Both range exits and the d=1 cofactor are retained. At fixed t>0 its
exact linear mean uses the absolutely convergent density
G_M(t)/[zeta(1+t)*product_(l|M)(1-l^(-1-t))]
times x[p^(-1-t)-q^(-1-t)]. Subtracting it exposes a JOINED mu-weighted
fractional-floor term and the complete d>x/p Dirichlet tail. They may
be defined exactly at t>0; no critical mu-series convergence is assumed.
Splitting and bounding those pieces separately can lose cancellation.
The scale-filtered form keeps them joined automatically through its
complete floors. A new all-scale signed estimate on that joined cofactor
error, or a genuine arithmetic recurrence that controls it, is the leaf
needed to close the full bound. Finite-prime transport, positive complements,
local valuation nullspaces, and small endpoint powers do not pay it.

A detector can now infer universal transfers, synthesize complete weighted
exchange sources, learn exact vanishing orders, and isolate an explicit
signed middle with paid endpoints. It must keep its proof graph acyclic:
source identities and endpoint inequalities are paid; actual uniform
middle/source norms and target coercivity are not. No companion RH-strength
bound may be imported as an unconditional provider. The added conditional
RH bridge makes a proved bound useful, but does not make it proved.

Disposition: this source-only review is complete and edits are stopped.
The generic inference and endpoint rules are sound in their stated scopes;
the positive exchange transport and conditional bridge are genuine additional
structural deductions. Full signed arithmetic control remains OPEN.
