The remaining proposed uniform recurrence is

Fresh finite passing pairs and helpful auxiliary increments do not pay

2. Exact alias of whole predictor work

Use the full Hilbert inner product, equivalently the complete k>=1
floor-cell energy with weight 1/(k(k+1)). Let Q be positive definite,
B any constant real two-by-two matrix, and y=z_(2N+r), z=z_N. Define
  xi=y-Bz,
  W_B=2<Bz,Qxi>+<xi,Qxi>.
Expanding every term gives EXACTLY
  W_B=<y,Qy>-<z,B^T Q B z>.                                       (2)
All cross terms between y and Bz cancel in their SUM. Let R=B^T Q B.
Then the whole-work observable is determined by R, not by B. In
particular B and O B have identical work if O^T Q O=Q. Such a Q-orthogonal
gauge can change innovation energy and correlation separately without
changing their combined work. A large negative correlation by itself is
not evidence of a new bound: the positive innovation may exactly offset
it. Nonconstant B_N similarly gives a per-instance parent Gram R_N,
unless a further derivative/transport operator is introduced.

If the proposed contraction guard is R<=rho Q, then
  W_B=(P_child-rho P_parent)+<z,(rho Q-R)z>,
  W_B>=P_child-rho P_parent.                                      (3)
Thus proving W_B<=forcing is a sufficient but possibly stronger version
of the old recurrence. A standalone search of whole W_B cannot furnish
an easier arithmetic bound than that recurrence. Its additional parent
slack is nonnegative. In the limiting case R=rho Q it is the exact same
increment. General R merely searches a parent quadratic reweighting.
If R_1<=R_2, then W_(R_1)>=W_(R_2) for every source state. This dominance
is universal and can remove redundant observable candidates without
inspecting numerical norms.

For rational certificates, equality and Loewner order of two-by-two
matrices are exact entry/minor computations. Rational B with the same R
must not count as distinct empirical mechanism discoveries merely because
their coefficient words differ. The appropriate canonical observable is
the exact triple (R00,R01,R11), along with its source Q and recurrence
domain. This rule compares full work; it does not erase different proof
tactics associated with the same observable.

3. Scope-correct anti-restatement rule

The detector should label a candidate ALIASED_PARENT_GRAM_RECURRENCE when
its only construction is xi=y-Bz and its only bound leaf is W_B<=C/L^eta.
The certificate should show (2), the canonical R, and slack (3). It must
not label this a newly paid correlation bound or rank a Q-orthogonal gauge
as a new mechanism. B=0 is the endpoint control W=P_child.

A B word may still be kept as a PROOF_TACTIC if it has a genuinely new
downstream decomposition. The minimum acceptable proof record is:
  (i) actual source identity expressing xi in explicit source atoms or
      auxiliary operators, with complete support, units and cutoffs;
  (ii) exact algebra or source law producing a paid coefficient/kernel
       factor, smaller argument, telescoping boundary or compensator;
  (iii) separate unpaid leaves for whatever uniform arithmetic estimate
        remains, with quantifiers and recovery cost;
  (iv) a noncircular implication from those leaves to W, not a paid flag
       inferred from its numerical value.
Distinct B gauges can then represent different tactics for the same
observable if one aligns with an actual prime/divisor identity. They are
not distinct measured whole-work mechanisms. A favorable finite covariance
does not itself supply (ii) or (iii).

For example, if B^TQB<=sigma Q and delta>0 with (1+delta)sigma<=rho,
Young's inequality yields
  P_child<=rho P_parent+(1+1/delta)||xi||_Q^2.
A separately proved full innovation estimate is a legitimate stronger
source leaf. Alternatively an exact compensation inequality retaining
2<Bz,Qxi> can yield a smaller forcing. Neither is paid by defining xi.
Every source-bound innovation estimate must include the new source blocks
and full k>N truncated-divisor energy. A fake identity omitting those
components will misleadingly make the predictor appear accurate.

4. Constant-rho recurrences and what they actually add

With rho fixed below one and forcing C/L^eta, a recurrence plus finite
base provides an effective uniform constant via the 047 induction. Its
proof is meaningful and remains open. But, if constants are free, its
asymptotic class is not automatically a new cancellation mechanism:
P_N=O(L^-eta) implies a recurrence of this form with a sufficiently large
C, simply because P_child<=D/L_parent^eta and P_parent>=0. Conversely
the recurrent bound plus suitable base/induction gives P_N=O(L^-eta).
Consequently class-level existence of a constant-rho recurrence is another
presentation of the same energy order. A tight fixed C or a source-derived
forcing decomposition is stronger information; a fitted finite C is not
such a decomposition.

For any nonnegative added storage T_N=P_N-||h_N||^2, improvement of EVERY
increment at fixed rho would mean T_child<=rho T_parent. Along a chain
this forces geometric decay. The observed helpful increments at a few
supports must not be promoted to that universal claim. The actual
recurrence (1), with positive forcing and without a universally-helpful
assertion, does not require it.

Zero forcing C=0 with theta>1/2 contradicts the reviewed 046 eventual
whole-forcing lower bound along a chain, since it would give geometric
energy decay. This refutes that argument, NOT every slower logarithmic
target. The corrected 047 target_status=OPEN in the C0-only slower-rate
case is essential. Eta>1-theta, in contrast, refutes the target itself
within the licensed bare-profile scope. No frozen gate is changed here.

5. Exact ceiling and precision transport

Let m_l=ceil(a l^theta), beta_l=m_l/l. The exact adjacent-level drift is
  beta_(l+1)-beta_l
    =[l(m_(l+1)-m_l)-m_l]/[l(l+1)].                               (4)
It cannot be replaced by the negative smooth derivative of a l^(theta-1).
Eventually a theta l^(theta-1)<1, so adjacent ceiling increments are 0
or 1; there are infinitely many 1 increments because m_l tends to infinity.
At such a jump, eventually m_l<l, and (4) is POSITIVE. Between jumps it is
negative. A simple source-free model P_l=beta_l already refutes the claim
that smooth negative drift controls every exact level transition. This
is a proof-route counterexample, not a computation of actual Mobius norms.

The two-profile state needs separate exact index records:
  l_N=L(N), t_N=L(pN),
  a_N=ceil(log_2 N), c_N=ceil(log_2(pN)).
For children 2N+r, l_child=l_N+1 always. For r=0, t_child=t_N+1.
For r=1, t_child can be t_N+1 OR t_N+2: the odd prime child may cross an
additional power-of-two boundary. Similarly a_child and c_child can
increase by one or two. In particular, doubling-plus-one from an exact
power-of-two parent can change ceil(log_2 N) by two while L changes by one.
The actual floor precision therefore changes as well as beta. The
state source must recompute all four records; matching only l_N does
not bind the prime-child profile. The planned stress at the child-source
4096 boundary addresses a genuine source issue, but passing it cannot
certify all later jumps.

For the unrounded four-atom profile,
  P_beta(n)=1-(1-n^(-beta))^4.
Its exact difference is
  P_beta'(n)-P_beta(n)
    =(z'-z) sum_(i=0)^3 (1-z')^i(1-z)^(3-i),
  z=n^(-beta), z'=n^(-beta').                                    (5)
This is a useful coefficient factor before norms and absolute values.
The general signed mixture can instead use exact divided differences
of each exponential. But (5) is NOT an identity for the actual four
separately floored atoms. Their explicit finite discrepancy must be
retained and paid. Even a positive scalar factor in (5) is not a sign
claim about a Mobius covariance or about child/parent Hilbert states.

6. A valid derivative-scale induction, with endpoint guard

A second reusable all-integer proof rule uses rational gamma>eta>0,
minimum level l0>=gamma, a full paid base, and the uniform source lemma
  P_(2N+r)<=(1-gamma/l)P_N+C/l^(eta+1), l=L(N), both r.             (6)
If the desired base constant A satisfies A>=C/(gamma-eta), then
  (1-gamma/l)A+C/l <= A(1-eta/l)
                         <=A(l/(l+1))^eta,
where the last inequality is the convex tangent bound
(1+x)^(-eta)>=1-eta x for x>=0. This proves P_N<=A/L(N)^eta by the
same complete base-interval induction. Coefficients are nonnegative on
the domain l>=gamma. The statement is valid for arbitrary positive
rational eta; no numerical fractional-power comparison is required.

This requires a stronger, derivative-order forcing leaf than the
constant-rho production. It is not a free improvement. The gap gamma>eta
must be explicit. At gamma=eta, a positive forcing generically produces
an extra log L: the equality recurrence has homogeneous product of order
L^-eta, and its accumulated forcing in that integrating factor is a
harmonic sum. Thus the equality endpoint does not automatically pay a
constant-times L^-eta target. A detector must either preserve the log
loss, prove extra cancellation, or use a stricter gap.

Equation (6) is sensitive to ceiling jumps; a proof obtained only by
differentiating beta is invalid. An exact jump compensator, based on (4),
is a reasonable additional production. Its energy/correlation costs must
be source-bound and cannot be declared zero or smooth.

7. Beta-calibrated stationary forcing, without hidden normalization

A source-free positive weight w_N can be declared in advance, for example
beta_N, beta_(pN), or a fixed positive combination of both. It is computed
from the profile AST and ceiling rule, not fitted from norms. It must
remain defined through every jump and have a paid target-comparison bound.
For w_N=beta_N,
  a L^(-(1-theta))<=w_N<=(a+1)L^(-(1-theta)).                     (7)
Thus a bound P_N<=D w_N recovers a legitimate critical-order target with
constant (a+1)D; paying a smaller requested constant requires a sharper
explicit comparison, not treating ceil(aL^theta) as aL^theta.

Put S_N=P_N/w_N. A credible source lemma is the stationary normalized rule
  S_(2N+r)<=(1-gamma/l)S_N+C/l,
  l>=l0>=gamma>0, both parities.                                  (8)
It has the paid elementary recovery S_N<=max(full normalized base,C/gamma).
In unnormalized form the exact child multiplier is
  (w_child/w_parent)(1-gamma/l),
and forcing C w_child/l. Ratios must use the EXACT child profile, including
ceilings, and can exceed one at jumps; that does not invalidate normalized
induction. This production targets stationary normalized energy, rather
than pretending the ceiling trajectory is smooth. For a weight involving
beta_(pN), both source level records from section 5 remain necessary.

Equation (8) alone is another presentation of an energy bound until its
source forcing is reduced. It supplies useful bookkeeping and an explicit
stationary budget C/gamma; it is not a new arithmetic estimate by itself.
One must not overstrengthen it by declaring the normalized forcing
o(1/l) with gamma>0. That would force S_N->0 along a chain, by the usual
positive recurrence comparison: an arbitrary epsilon upper equilibrium
eventually attracts under product(1-gamma/l)->0. For w_N comparable to
beta_N and theta>1/2 this contradicts the reviewed 046 lower bound.
In particular C/l^(1+delta), delta>0, is an impossible uniform forcing
for such a strictly damped normalized rule on the unchanged full target.
This is an ARGUMENT refutation. It does not refute a critical-order target
or the stationary rule with positive C/l.

A different, permitted rule has no artificial damping:
  S_child<=S_parent+F_l,
with a genuine uniform positive-envelope sequence whose sum is paid.
For F_l=C/l^(1+delta), delta>0, the sum from l0 is at most
  C[l0^(-1-delta)+l0^(-delta)/delta].
It bounds S by its base plus a summable total positive flux and allows a
nonzero limiting energy. This can be a useful telescoping production if
an exact arithmetic source identity pays the flux. Merely setting
F_l=max(0,S_child-S_parent) and observing a finite small sum is circular.

8. Concrete source-bound grammar beyond constant predictor reweighting

The following constructions are meaningful proposed proof tactics because
they alter explicit coefficient/kernel structure rather than only R.

(a) Full prime-adic atom transport. Before norms, express H and A in the
four atom types chi, D_p chi, f_n and D_p f_n. The H coefficients are
1, V(p), mu(n)V(n) for n<=pN, and mu(n)V(pn) for n<=N; A coefficients
are 0, 1-V(p), 0, and mu(n)(U_N(n)-V(pn)). Then construct child minus
predictor coefficients EXACTLY, including common-profile changes and
new source intervals. The basis identity
  D_p f_n=f_(pn)-f_p/n
and ordinary mu(pm)=-mu(m) for p not dividing m allow actual valuation-one
pairings. When p divides m, mu(pm)=0 but mu(m)D_p f_m may survive through
the dilated parent and generate valuation-two basis terms. These cannot
be deleted. Group complete p-adic cofactor orbits BEFORE absolute values,
retain m=1 units and every profile cutoff, and generate their full kernel.
An all-cofactor signed kernel estimate is still open. Counting a paired
orbit or repeating its energy as a new bound does not pay it. A useful
certificate must exhibit a quantitative kernel factor, paid complement,
or well-founded smaller-scale source reduction.

(b) Drift divided differences with explicit jump/birth work. Generate
factor (5), or general fixed-rate exponential divided differences, for
common coefficients; retain exact rounding errors and the born blocks
pN<n<=p(2N+r) and N<n<=2N+r. The signed innovation energy includes all
common-common, common-birth and birth-birth pairs and counterterm crosses.
A source lemma may control their joint forcing. Independently bounding
every block by absolute values generally loses the sought cancellation.
Its dependency record must say which loss is incurred and keep every
tail. This is a concrete source-difference leaf, not a declaration that
profile Taylor order alone controls the whole norm.

(c) All-prime-power logarithmic removal with fixed parameter. For an
unrounded single rate, at a FIXED beta,
  mu(n)n^(-r beta) log n
    =-sum_(d|n) Lambda(d)d^(-r beta)mu(n/d)(n/d)^(-r beta).          (9)
This follows from the actual Euler product or ordinary local prime
factorization; Lambda includes all prime powers. For the truncated sum
C_(beta,K)(t)=sum_(n<=min(t,K))mu(n)n^(-r beta), it gives the exact identity
  sum_(n<=min(t,K))mu(n)n^(-r beta)log n
   =-sum_(d<=min(t,K))Lambda(d)d^(-r beta)
                         C_(beta,floor(K/d))(t/d).               (10)
All arguments n/d are smaller; the unit contributes zero log and must
remain in C. Identity (10) continues to bind a finite source even when
t>K. Replacing its truncated inner source by unrestricted M(t/d), or
dropping d that are higher prime powers, is invalid.

The genuine off-diagonal state in (10) holds beta fixed while K and t
shrink. It is NOT the old moving profile at floor(N/d): that would
recompute beta and rounding. A detector can generate the parameter-held
cofactor family and exact conversion auxiliaries to the moving family,
then search a uniform joint forcing/covariance lemma for it. If it uses
only the original moving family, every conversion difference and boundary
is an explicit new leaf. No companion RH-strength bound is assumed paid.

For the signed mixture, apply (9)-(10) atomwise and keep the fixed weights
before the norm. Separate floor rounding destroys exact multiplicativity;
its finite discrepancy must be emitted as a compensator. It is small in
the original basis without source cancellation: |f_n|<=1 and |f_n|<=1/(nx)
for x>1/n give ||f_n||^2<=2/n, hence total coefficient rounding at precision
4ceil(log_2 N) has norm at most 2 sqrt(2) C_w N^(-7/2). Dilation has its
exact p^(-1/2) norm factor. Log-weighted discrepancy introduces the
explicit finite log N factor. Those estimates pay rounding itself,
not the unrounded main source covariance or a premature kernel bound.

The log weights in (9)-(10) are symbolic arithmetic weights, not response-
fitted coefficients. Recovering a prefix through log division/Abel
summation needs its finite head and exact inverse cost; it is not free
coercivity. Simple log-weight prime removal has earlier failed to give a
strict entrywise norm contraction in two-prime sectors. The detector must
seek a joint signed gain or a paid source forcing, rather than infer a
gap from positive normalized divisor weights summing to one.

9. Noncircular proof graph and next admission requirements

Each proposed bound should carry:
  SOURCE: exact AST, ordinary mu, prime, all parameter-held or moving
          profiles, independent coefficient rounding and complete units;
  IDENTITY: exact transport/grouping/log-divisor or divided-difference
            expansion with its full support and every exit/birth term;
  ALGEBRA: PSD recovery, canonical observable R and any universal slack;
  UNPAID SOURCE LEAF: explicit uniform kernel/innovation/flux inequality
                     with all N, both parities, all K and all spatial cells;
  RECOVERY: paid induction/telescoping implication with constants and
            finite head/base admission;
  RESULT: conditional until every actual source leaf has a valid proof.
An edge back to the same unknown energy is legitimate only through a
proved coefficient gap or a well-founded smaller argument with paid
forcing. An assumed critical source/companion bound closing (10) is
circular. A finite proof of base energies is genuine progress but cannot
pay a uniform covariance. Unsupported tail/parameter transfers are OPEN,
not silently true. RH is never admitted as an upper-bound provider here.

The current promoted recurrence still deserves the planned exact jump
stress test, with no new fit. Whole constant-predictor work alone should
not be the next large numerical catalogue: canonicalization (2)-(3) proves
its limitation first. The productive next production is a source-bound
coefficient/orbit/log-divisor identity plus an explicit joint forcing,
with calibrated drift and all boundary costs visible. The rules above
give detector-reusable proof and scope constraints; none supplies that
unresolved arithmetic estimate yet.

10. Executable canonicalization guard and development receipt

At root's request the new lightweight universal guard is implemented in
floor_residual_prime_work_guard_050.py, with APIs
  build_guard({Q,B,rho},meter=None),
  verify_guard(request,certificate,meter=None), Meter.operations.
Q and B are exact two-by-two rational matrices; Q must be symmetric PSD.
The algebra allows singular Q, including zero; it NEVER infers target
recovery from this condition. R is computed by a matrix product. The
verifier separately reconstructs R from its two column quadratic forms
and polarization, then checks the exact request-bound metadata and scope.
This is distinct arithmetic replay, not proof-assistant formalization or
an independent source-norm calculation.

The certificate canonicalizes (Q,R), emits rho Q-R, and decides its PSD
condition. It labels the complete-work observable
ALIASED_PARENT_GRAM_RECURRENCE and admits the conditional implication
only when that algebra guard passes. Failure is explicitly
FAILED_ALGEBRA_GUARD; no weakening comparison is then asserted. The
comparison coefficient rho may be any nonnegative rational here: a
Loewner comparison with rho>=1 is not a strict dynamic contraction and
cannot by itself supply an induction gap. The consuming argument must
separately impose its own coefficient domain and recovery theorem.

Whole-work change alone is never certified as new signed arithmetic.
Source-bound downstream proof tactics remain permitted. The guard always
states target_recovery_proved=false and uniform_arithmetic_bound_proved=false;
it neither pays nor refutes a source inequality from its numerical value.
Changed matrices, canonical R, payment flags or weakening claims fail
verification. No arbitrary source/evidence fields can enter this algebra
request. Root binds the result to its source argument separately.
