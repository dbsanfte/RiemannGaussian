1. Why past storage alone cannot control a fresh coherent block
For t in the child block[2X,4X), every admitted prime quotient and the lag
index are <=floor(t/2)<=2X-1. The cubic root is also <=2X-1. Therefore BOTH
child potentials31/53 are functions solely of the old source indices1..2X-1.
Their parent potentials have even earlier source access. In contrast child E
contains the fresh source increments2X..4X-1. Write
 M(t)=M(2X-1)+h(t), h(t)=sum_{2X<=n<=t}mu(n).
Its square has the positive fresh-prefix term h(t)^2 and its old/fresh cross
term. No root transfer or mask-jump estimate determines either fresh sum.

This is a rigorous obstruction to deriving source-independent negative drift
from those lemmas. For comparison, a bounded source can have its old prefix
zero and fresh values+1, making V_parent=V_child=0 while DeltaE is of order X.
A stronger comparison preserves the exact even-prime recurrence and initial
values. For dyadic X>=8, set a(1)=1,a(2)=-1, all other old a(n)=0; fresh odd
a(n)=1 and fresh even a(n)=0. Extend odd values freely thereafter and define
a(2n)=-a(n) for odd n, a(2n)=0 for even n. It satisfies |a|<=1 and that exact
even law. All old prefixes queried by31/53 vanish, hence both V values vanish.
The child prefixes on its even/odd pairs are j,j+1,0<=j<X, giving exactly
 DeltaE=X/6+1/(12X).
This does NOT satisfy the complete actual-Mobius prime/squarefree axioms and
does NOT refute either selected candidate for Mobius. It proves that the stated
norm lemmas plus the even recurrence cannot supply the desired credit.

There is also one fixed bounded comparison source with the even recurrence:
a(n)=1 on odd n,-1 on n=2 mod4,0 on multiples of4. Its prefix is t/4+O(1),
so E is of order X. Any cofinal subpower drift of E plus nonnegative storage
would contradict telescoping for this source. Thus access to fresh point
queries alone cannot repair a source-independent proof: nonnegative child
storage cannot cancel arbitrary coherent fresh target input. Actual arithmetic
constraints on fresh odd/nonmultiple correlations are indispensable.

In quadratic-form language, on an unconstrained fresh source block the new/new
principal block of Delta(E+V) contains the positive prefix-Gram block of E.
For past-only storage its additional block is zero; for fresh nonnegative
square storage it is positive semidefinite. A universal negative quadratic
certificate cannot remove that block without source-specific relations.

2. Exact parity transport identifies the missing actual source component
Let O(t)=sum_{n<=t,n odd}mu(n). The actual law mu(2n)=-mu(n) for odd n and0
for even n gives M(t)=O(t)-O(floor(t/2)). Consequently
 M(2u)=sum_{u<n<=2u,n odd}mu(n)=J(u),
 M(2u+1)=J(u)+mu(2u+1).
Hence child E equals
 (4X^2)^-1 sum_{X<=u<2X}[2J(u)^2+2J(u)mu(2u+1)+mu(2u+1)^2].
The one-point residual has normalized norm at most1/(4X). The principal J
energy is an actual sliding odd-annulus correlation, not the smaller parent
annulus. This exact transport does not bound J by parent E. It exposes the
fresh component that a real contraction lemma would have to control.

An attempted telescoping of the mixed point term uses
 J(u+1)-J(u)=mu(2u+1)-1_{u+1 odd}mu(u+1).
Summation by parts retains both endpoint J energies and the correlation with
the lower odd-source point. In particular an endpoint at4X is outside the
saved child block and cannot be discarded. This rearrangement does not create
an unsigned forcing bound on the J energy.

4. No orthogonal-projection interpretation for the stored subtraction
The root subtraction KB is not the root-cell conditional mean of A_R. For it
to supply a projection credit one would need the exact orthogonality condition
<A_R-KB,KB>=0, which has not been established and is not an algebraic identity.
The stored coefficient K comes from counting prime rows, not that covariance.
Completing a square with a covariance-defined coefficient would require source
responses and would change the program; it is not a universal analytic proof.

The gate IS an orthogonal multiplication projection, giving exactly
 ||F_R-G_m L||^2=||G_m(F_R-L)||^2+||(1-G_m)F_R||^2.
Its credit relative to ||F_R||^2 is

Neither idempotence nor the mask-jump bound fixes the sign of this expression.
Adding the opposite square ||F_R+G_m L||^2 cancels the cross term but costs
2||F_R||^2+2||G_m L||^2. That completion diagonalizes the ambiguity; it does
not produce negative net transport. All old/new prime and changing-modulus
terms remain as in the bank memo, including new-prime/mask covariance.

5. Normalized quotient operators: a genuine finite-norm contraction
For any function f=0 below1 define the FINITE norm
 H_T(f)=integral_1^T |f(t)|^2 dt/t^2.
For D_p f(t)=f(t/p), exact substitution gives
 H_T(D_p f)=p^-1 H_{T/p}(f)<=p^-1 H_T(f).
No assumption that an infinite target-energy integral converges is made.
For fixed source-free nonnegative weights a_p summing to1, the frozen mixture
K_a=sum_p a_p D_p obeys
 ||K_a f||_{H_T}<=(sum_p a_p/sqrt(p))||f||_{H_T}
 <=2^-1/2 ||f||_{H_T}.
For step functions f(t)=M(floor(t)), the weighted integral on a dyadic block
lies between E(X)/4 and E(X). This is a real uniform operator estimate, but
it does not furnish a source identity M=controlled forcing+K_a M.

The raw bank's corresponding norm is sum_{p<=R}p^-1/2, not a uniform constant.
This upper bound is sharp as a norm uniformly over T: truncated square-root
test functions f(t)=sqrt(t) on[1,T] give that ratio on[R,T], and the relative
logarithmic endpoint loss tends to zero as T grows at fixed R. This is an
analytical test function, not an actual Mobius assertion or experiment. Raw
bank amplification is structural rather than merely an excessive inequality.

Moving weights a_p(X) require their own old-weight/rescaling/new-domain terms.
The estimate freezes coefficients; it cannot silently be applied to arbitrary
output-dependent mixtures a_p(t), because a_p(pu) no longer sums to1 after
the change of variables. All domain complements must stay in any such family.

6. Source-specific compensation stronger than near-point translations
For every prime p define complete nonmultiple prefixes
 C_p(t)=sum_{n<=t,p not dividing n}mu(n), D_p(t)=C_p(floor(t/p)).
The actual prime-multiple law proves M(t)=C_p(t)-D_p(t) exactly. Both parts of
the source partition are complete. C_p contains fresh source indices; replacing
it by a few latest residues loses the identity and requires the omitted prefix.

For a fixed source-free normalized prime mixture, let A=sum_p a_p C_p and
B=sum_p a_p D_p. Then M=A-B pointwise, and the nonnegative candidate storage
V=X^-2 sum_block(A+B)^2 preserves the full target while giving
 W=E+V=2X^-2 sum_block(A^2+B^2).
The fresh/past covariance cancels by the parallelogram identity, not a fitted
coefficient or an assumed sign estimate. Prime-pair correlations inside A^2
and B^2, moving coefficients, initial costs and selector boundaries remain.
For normalized weights on primes>=2 this family has A(1)=1,B(1)=0 and W(1)=2;
that initial storage cost is part of every conditional telescoping argument.
The related family V=sum_p a_p ||C_p+D_p||^2 cancels each per-prime covariance;
neither version may drop an unrepresented source or prime-tail complement.

For p=2 fixed, put F(X)=X^-2 sum_block O(t)^2. This completion has the exact
dyadic energy law, for X>=2,
 W(X)=2F(X)+F(X/2),
 DeltaW=2F(2X)-F(X)-F(X/2).
It exposes explicit parent credit and the fresh odd-prefix cost without an
unknown covariance. No contraction of F(2X) has been proved. This is a useful
mechanism-based storage family, not a reason to assert that odd prefixes are
small. More primes introduce their full residue-domain transports, not extra
unconditional cancellation.

Finite-norm analysis also prevents hiding an equivalent-energy premise:
 C_p=(I-D_p)^-1 M=sum_{j>=0}M(floor(t/p^j)), locally a finite exact sum,
 ||C_p||_{H_T}<=(1-p^-1/2)^-1||M||_{H_T}.
Thus the compensation energy is comparable to the original finite energy;
this identity supplies a new accounting mechanism, not a new regularity bound.
To close, one still needs a source-specific estimate on fresh C_p or on its
transport that consumes less than the explicit parent credit. No bound of that
strength is assumed here.

7. Implications for the proposed generic translate/remainder mutations
A bank of bare near-t point queries, with at most pi(R) terms and r>=2, has
normalized square<=pi(R)^2/X<=1. It is uniformly bounded padding and its
cofinal drift assertion is equivalent to that of E up to a constant. Coupling
those points to unresolved prefixes can expose genuine fresh/past correlations,
but the prefix factor and all mixed costs remain.

For the latest multiple n=p floor(t/p), the actual identity is
 mu(n)=-mu(floor(t/p))*1_{p not dividing floor(t/p)}.
Despite the near-t index, this query reduces to a past quotient plus a mask.
Other residues can access distinct fresh source values; being coprime to p
does not establish any sign law for them. Separate nominal index freshness,
arithmetically reduced dependencies, and an actual signed control lemma.

The complete C_p/D_p compensation family is stronger than translations in
one precise sense: it uses a valid prime-multiple law to eliminate a whole
fresh/past covariance while keeping E. Its unresolved fresh norm is explicit.
Translations can supply exploratory interactions, but a pure norm/past proof
still misses the coherent fresh-prefix mode identified above.

Final disposition
No negative frozen-transport credit sufficient for full selected31/53 drift
was proved. Reasonable source-independent contraction/projection claims fail
for the comparison modes above. Useful proved additions are the small parity
residual bound, finite normalized-dilation contraction, and exact prime-law
parallelogram compensation. They direct future structural generation toward
complete fresh nonmultiple-prefix/transport pairs and explicit complements,
rather than assuming that near-point access or normalization establishes RH.
