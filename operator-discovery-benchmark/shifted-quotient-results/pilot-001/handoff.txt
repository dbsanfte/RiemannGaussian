Program {'shifts': [...]} has1..3 distinct increasing canonical rationals,
each0<b<1 with denominator<=24. The fixed pilot used all subsets of sizes1,2,3
from {1/6,1/3,1/2,2/3,5/6}:5+10+10=25 programs. Products are Dirichlet-series
products, so their coefficient operation is Dirichlet convolution.

a_b=mu*n^(-b), h_b(1)=0, h_b(n)=a_b(n)/b for n>1. At prime powers,
 a_b(p^e)=-(1-p^(-b))*p^(-b(e-1)).
Other composites are present and essential. Fixed-cutoff b->0 recovers
-Lambda and degree-k products recover (-1)^k Lambda^{*k}; this is not uniform
in a growing cutoff. The factor1/b prevents a trivial vanishing-parameter win.

For B={b_i}, coefficient f_B=*h_bi, the exact centered prefix is
 J_B(x)=sum_(n<=x)f_B(n)-sum_i R_i*x^(1-b_i)/(1-b_i),
 R_i=1/[b_i*zeta(1-b_i)]
       *product_(j!=i)[(zeta(1-b_i+b_j)/zeta(1-b_i)-1)/b_j].
Every residue channel is retained, even if its exponent is below1/2.
The arithmetic input and inclusive endpoints are explicit. Unit coefficients
are removed before products; this is not a fitted Perron constant.

The matched degree-k baseline is (mu-delta_1)^{*k}. Both it and the candidate
only use factor indices<=x/2^(k-1). Comparing to mu^{*k}-delta_1 would falsely
reward omission of fresh coordinates. No finite-prefix inverse is claimed.

Whole block energy: E(X)=X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X)J_B(n)^2. Absolute envelopes retain
all coefficient and main-term channels, but never determine candidate rank.

Proved: an all-scale complete cofinal subpower moment for any of these fixed
centered products implies RH. Exact Mellin compensation removes the real
poles. Any surviving off-line zeta zero would force a farther-right zero
at the same height; positive shifts make that chain impossible past Re=1.
All multiplicities and cross-factor zero/pole cancellations are included.
The proof does not supply the moment premise or a finite-prefix inverse.

For candidate10, write its complete prefix S=D+T+C:
 D = factors that are powers of the SAME prime;
 T = prime-power factors from DISTINCT primes;
 C = all three branches with at least one non-prime-power composite factor.
Then J=S-m_u*x^(2/3)-m_v*x^(1/3), with both exact residue coefficients.

The whole per-prime overlap mass is9/2, giving D(x)<= (9/2)pi(sqrt x).
Elementary Chebyshev bounds imply finite critical weighted L2 norm for D.
The smaller pole term m_v*x^(1/3) also has finite critical norm. Therefore
the precise remaining RH-sufficient estimate is, for cofinally many q,

 X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X)|T(n)+C(n)-m_u*n^(2/3)|^2 <= K_q X^(1/q)

for EVERY dyadic X, with the finite head and all constants retained.
The full cross term is essential. T(x)>=c*pi(x/2)>>x/log x; all smooth
main terms are smaller. C must cancel this positive bulk very accurately.
This mixed/composite signed estimate remains UNPROVED.

A second exact reduction uses k(n)=n^(-2/3)sigma_(1/3)(n). Its prefix has
the complete expansion
 K(y)=(3/2)zeta(4/3)y^(2/3)+3zeta(2/3)y^(1/3)
       +zeta(1/3)zeta(2/3)+Delta_K(y), |Delta_K(y)|<=19/2.
In a complete outer hyperbola split at U=x^(1/3),V=x^(2/3), the short-side
mu*mu error is <=(19/2)x^(1/3)(1+log x/3), with finite critical norm.
The two single-quotient short errors are <=x^(1/9),x^(-1/9). The memo keeps
every weighted mu/mu*mu mismatch, long-side quotient prefix and rectangle;
none may be substituted with a conjectural RH bound. Section8 gives an
equivalent reduced full-square target with all explicit error costs.
