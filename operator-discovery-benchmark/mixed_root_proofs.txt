UNIVERSAL PROOFS AND DOMAIN AUDIT FOR MIXED ZETA-ROOT PROGRAMS

These proofs are mathematical arguments, not machine-checked certificates.
root_proofs.txt supplies the coefficient-local root construction. Let
b_q=P_(1/q)(u), where u(n)=1 and q is an integer >=2.

1. DIFFERENT PRODUCTS ARE OPERATORS, NOT JUST MATCHED PROFILES

An exponent kernel w(a,b) gives an arithmetic product
  (f *_w g)(n)=sum_{ij=n}f(i)g(j)*product_p w(v_p(i),v_p(j)).
All sums are finite. A normalized symmetric kernel satisfying
  w(a,b)w(a+b,c)=w(b,c)w(a,b+c), w(0,a)=1
gives a commutative associative product with identity delta. The proof is to
expand both products into ijk=n and use the kernel identity for each prime.

Ordinary w=1 obeys the law. Unitary w(a,b)=[a=0 or b=0] obeys it: both
triple weights are 1 precisely when at most one of a,b,c is nonzero. The
binomial kernel w(a,b)=(a+b)!/(a!*b!) obeys it by cancellation of factorials.
For carry kernels, w=t^(F(a+b)-F(a)-F(b)), F(a)=v_ell(a!), telescoping
proves the law, even at t=0 with 0^0=1, as shown in root_proofs.txt.
The programs therefore define operations on ALL rational arithmetic inputs.

Multiplicative functions are preserved: factorizations ij=n split independently
over prime powers, so their finite product formula equals the product of the
local exponent convolutions. This justifies the local interpreter in
mixed_root_search.py; direct arithmetic unitary folds audit it independently.

2. SHARED FIRST COEFFICIENT AND SQUAREFULL CORRECTION

For each of these products define H_(q,w)=b_q *_w ... *_w b_q (q factors).
The local root series starts 1+x/q+O(x^2). Because w(0,a)=1, the q-fold
local product starts 1+x+O(x^2): exactly one of q factors contributes x/q.
Call its local coefficients h_w(e). The ordinary Dirichlet quotient
  R_(q,w)=H_(q,w)*u^{-1}
is multiplicative, with local coefficients r(0)=1 and
  r(e)=h_w(e)-h_w(e-1), e>=1.
Thus r(1)=0. Every nonzero R_(q,w)(n) for n>1 has all positive prime
exponents at least 2: its support is squarefull. This is a universal law,
not an inference from the finite support observed at n<=2048.

The multiplicativity hypothesis matters. On arbitrary normalized f, the
same product of P_(1/q)(f) need not have a squarefull quotient by f.
The negative control f=u+delta_6 in mixed_root_search.py exhibits this
failure and prevents extending the zeta theorem to a larger false domain.

3. UNITARY ROOT LIFT: A CONCRETE ANALYTIC IDENTITY

For the unitary kernel, at a fixed prime a positive exponent must be placed
entirely in one of the q factors. Therefore
  H_q(n)=q^omega(n)*b_q(n),
and the local generating series is
  1+q*((1-x)^(-1/q)-1)=q*(1-x)^(-1/q)-(q-1).
Here omega counts DISTINCT prime factors. The coefficient-local program is
the q-fold unitary product; the closed formula interprets its output.

Since c_(1/q)(e) decreases from c(1)=1/q for e>=1, each nonconstant local
coefficient q*c(e) is in (0,1]. Hence 0<H_q(n)<=1 and its Dirichlet series
converges absolutely for Re(s)>1. Its exact Euler factorization is
  H_q(s)=zeta(s)*R_q(s),
  R_q(s)=product_p [q*(1-p^(-s))^(1-1/q)-(q-1)*(1-p^(-s))], Re(s)>1,
where all local powers use the analytic log on |x|<1 with log(1)=0.

The local correction is
  R_q(x)=1+q*sum_{e>=2}(c(e)-c(e-1))*x^e.
All nonconstant coefficients are negative and their absolute sum is
  q*sum_{e>=2}(c(e-1)-c(e))=q*c(1)=1,
because c(e)->0 for 0<1/q<1 (for example its logarithm is bounded above
by -(1-1/q)*sum_{j=1}^e 1/j plus a constant). Thus
  |R_q(x)-1| <= |x|^2, |x|<1.
For sigma>1/2 the sum over primes of p^(-2*sigma) converges. The product
therefore converges uniformly on compact subsets and defines a holomorphic
function on Re(s)>1/2. Each factor is nonzero there. Indeed, put
z=exp(Log(1-x)/q). The factor equals z^(q-1)*(q-(q-1)*z).
z is nonzero. If it were q/(q-1), then
  x=1-(q/(q-1))^q,
whose absolute value is >1, since the binomial expansion of
(1+1/(q-1))^q is already >1+q/(q-1)>2.
This contradicts |x|<1. An absolutely convergent product of nonzero factors
with summable deviations is nonzero. Consequently R_q is holomorphic AND
nonzero throughout Re(s)>1/2, without any assumption of RH.

Multiplying this R_q by the meromorphic continuation of zeta extends H_q
meromorphically to Re(s)>1/2. In this region its zeros have exactly the same
locations and multiplicities as zeta's zeros. This addresses a root's
arithmetic transformation, but DOES NOT make zeta's zeros disappear and
does not prove they lie on Re(s)=1/2. Positivity of coefficients gives no
zero-free theorem at complex s in that region.

4. RIGOROUS NUMERICAL TAILS FOR THE UNITARY CASE

The coefficient bound 0<H_q(n)<=1 gives a series tail after N bounded by
  N^(1-sigma)/(sigma-1), sigma>1.
For an Euler correction truncated to primes <=P, put
  T=P^(1-2*sigma)/(2*sigma-1), sigma>1/2.
The omitted deviations sum to at most T, using |R_q(p^(-s))-1|<=p^(-2*sigma)
and bounding primes by all integers >P. Expanding the omitted product gives
  |omitted_product-1|<=exp(T)-1.
Thus its absolute error is at most |finite_product|*(exp(T)-1).
The numerical test compares an Arb finite Dirichlet sum with Arb
zeta(s)*finite_product(s), allowing the series tail plus |zeta(s)| times
the product tail. These are ball arithmetic checks backed by explicit
mathematical bounds, rather than unqualified floating-point residuals.

5. OTHER KERNELS: WHAT IS PROVED, WHAT IS NOT

For a fixed q, prime ell and finite t, the carry family ALSO has a correction
product holomorphic on Re(s)>1/2. This growth argument alone does not establish
NONVANISHING. The subsequent carry_zero_proof.txt and exact disk certificates
resolve nonvanishing for selected instances and two whole parameter families.
In a q-fold local product, the total carry exponent is
  K=v_ell(e!)-sum_{j=1}^q v_ell(e_j!), sum e_j=e.
It is nonnegative and the digit-sum formula bounds it by
  q*(1+log_ell(e+1)).
Put M=max(1,|t|). The weight magnitude is at most
  M^q*(e+1)^(q*log_ell(M)).
There are binom(e+q-1,q-1) compositions and 0<c_(1/q)(e_j)<=1, so the
absolute local coefficients grow at most polynomially in e. The local
series has radius at least 1. Its correction starts 1+O(x^2), with a bound
uniform for |x|<=r<1. Summing over primes proves compactly uniform absolute
product convergence for sigma>1/2. Local zeros could add zeros beyond
zeta's for instances outside those certified families; they require a separate
audit. No unconditional nonvanishing assertion for arbitrary t is made.
The multiplicative Dirichlet series itself converges absolutely for sigma>1:
its local series converges at every p^(-sigma), and for sufficiently large p
its sum of nonconstant absolute terms is p^(-sigma)+O(p^(-2*sigma)).

The binomial kernel demands DIFFERENT growth analysis. Its multinomial
weights can grow exponentially with the exponent, so a formal squarefull
support law alone does not prove a product analytic on sigma>1/2. In the
implemented family the local root coefficients satisfy c(e)>=(1/q)/e for
e>=1: compare each product factor (j+1/q)/(j+1)>=j/(j+1).
For e>=q, the sum of multinomial weights over compositions in which every
part is positive is at least q^e-q*(q-1)^e. Each product of root coefficients
is at least q^(-q)*e^(-q). Therefore the local q-fold binomial product has
coefficients at least [q^e-q*(q-1)^e]/(q^q*e^q), and at most q^e. Its
radius of convergence is EXACTLY 1/q. The positive-coefficient local series
has a singularity at x=1/q. In particular the factor at prime 2 reaches this
boundary at real s=log_2(q). This invalidates an automatic analytic bridge
to Re(s)>1/2. The detector excludes it from that proposed bridge.

6. HISTORICAL NOVELTY IS A SEPARATE QUESTION

Unitary, binomial, infinitary and general weighted transport convolutions
have prior art. The programs above produce exact root applications and
useful corrections. Their correctness and analytic domains are proved here;
their definitions as compositions of known primitives do not by themselves
establish mathematical novelty. The registry therefore distinguishes known
operators, applications under literature review, proven laws, and genuinely
verified new operators. The last list remains empty unless a novelty audit
and independent verification warrant promotion.
