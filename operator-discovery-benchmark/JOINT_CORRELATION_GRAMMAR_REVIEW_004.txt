ALL-VALUATION PRIME DIFFERENCES AND JOINT LOG-DIVISOR FORCING

Scope and result
----------------
This independent analytical review takes
candidate10_centered_reciprocal_obligation.json as its open input. Its primary
target is the joined high-product off-diagonal covariance O_X, with a proved
paid recovery remainder. No independent sharp-band bound is imposed. Only
this new file is written; there are no source runs, numerical studies, tests,
hashes, old/frozen edits or subagents. No finite observation supplies an
asymptotic premise. The original Mobius source, all prime valuations, gcd
overlaps, complete center and unit are retained.

The useful constructions proved here are:
  (i) exact prime insertion annihilators at ALL valuations, with a uniformly
      paid same-prime/unit remainder;
 (ii) a distinct-prime divided difference exposing a joint composite/center
      state, with its actual prime-gap inverse cost;
(iii) an all-prime logarithmic source recurrence, genuinely using smaller
      coefficient arguments, lifted exactly to the WHOLE prefix-square
      kernel rather than a diagonal coefficient norm;
 (iv) an exact mixed-channel commutator splitting a true prime-power weight
      gain from an unattenuated prefix-boundary covariance.
These give an enumerable source-specific operator-plus-forcing language.
They do NOT prove the remaining signed forcing estimate, a strict joint
moment contraction, or RH. The missing symmetric mode and boundary costs
are explicit. No source identity is admitted as a bound merely because its
coefficient arguments are smaller.

1. Full target, center and paid recovery
----------------------------------------
Put u=1/3, v=2/3, beta=9/2,
  U=mu*k_u, V=mu*k_v, B=U*V, k_w(n)=n^(-w),
  p=beta*(U-delta)*(V-delta), a=p-d,
where * is Dirichlet convolution, delta(1)=1, and d is the COMPLETE original
same-prime-power contribution. Thus a vanishes at the unit and every prime
power. Neither p nor d is replaced by its squarefree restriction.

The fixed leading center is m*n^(2/3), where
  m=(3/2)*beta*[zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2-1/zeta(2/3)]>0,
  R_u=(2/3)*m,
  b(n)=a(n)-m*[n^(2/3)-(n-1)^(2/3)],
  tilde_b(n)=a(n)-R_u*k_u(n).
The unit tilde_b(1)=-R_u is retained. Write R(n)=sum_(h<=n)b(h) and
T(n)=sum_(h<=n)tilde_b(h), both zero at n=0. The positive summable correction
  e(n)=tilde_b(n)-b(n), sum_n e(n)=E0=-R_u*zeta(1/3)>0
and all its finite cross terms are part of recovery.

For integer dyadic X>=1, N=2X-1,
  O_X=(2/X^2)*sum_(n=X)^N sum_(h<k<=n,hk>X)tilde_b(h)tilde_b(k).
Equivalently let the symmetric kernel be
  K_X(h,k)=W_X(h,k)*1_(h!=k)*1_(hk>X),
  W_X(h,k)=max(0,2X-max(X,h,k));
then O_X=X^(-2)*sum_(h,k>=1)tilde_b(h)tilde_b(k)K_X(h,k).
Its support automatically has h,k<=N. No band or arithmetic/center sector
is required to be positive independently.

With K0=beta+m, the proved exact recovery is
  |E_R(X)-O_X|<=B0(X),
  B0(X)=3*K0^2*(1+log N)^3
                +4*E0*K0*(1+log(2X))+E0^2/X,
  E_R(X)=X^(-2)*sum_(n=X)^N R(n)^2.                (1.1)
Low products, the high-product diagonal and the complete center correction
are the paid complement. Hence O_X>=-B0(X) unconditionally, and only the
one-sided lemma O_X<=C_epsilon X^epsilon for every epsilon>0 remains.
This lemma is cofinal-equivalent to the full target up to (1.1). Rebranding
it a new auxiliary is not proof progress. The operator constructions below
are intended to expose an independently attackable arithmetic forcing.

The exact reciprocal split uses H=floor(sqrt X):
  O_X=(2/X^2)*sum_(n=X)^N [
       sum_(h<=H)tilde_b(h)*I(floor(X/h),n)
          +sum_(H<h<=n)tilde_b(h)*I(h,n)],
  I(A,B)=sum_(A<k<=B)tilde_b(k), zero for B<=A.     (1.2)
The second triangle equals [T(n)-T(H)]^2 minus its diagonal. It retains
current-scale T(n); it cannot be called a smaller-scale paid remainder.

2. Exact fixed-prime insertion, including every valuation
----------------------------------------------------------
For an actual prime q, t=q^(-u), define
  I_q f(n)=f(qn), E_q f(n)=1_(q does not divide n)*f(n).
These are coefficient insertion and coefficient restriction, not prefix
dilation. Their distinction matters when constructing the kernel pullback.
The actual local Euler series are
  U_q(z)=(1-z)/(1-tz), V_q(z)=(1-z)/(1-t^2z),
  B_q(z)=(1-z)^2/[(1-tz)(1-t^2z)].
The raw first U,V rows are t-1,t^2-1. The B row is t+t^2-2; its second
row is 1-2t-t^2+t^3+t^4. At every n, WITHOUT an assumed q-free n,
  (I_q-t)U=-E_q U,
  (I_q-t^2)V=-E_q V,
  (I_q-t)(I_q-t^2)B=E_q B.                        (2.1)
For U,V, verify the initial row and the subsequent geometric ratios. For
B, its second-order recurrence has a single residual 1 at valuation zero
and zero at all higher valuations. Multiplicativity on the remaining
cofactor proves (2.1). Thus gcd overlaps and repeated powers are explicit,
not a sector silently discarded by a first-row substitution.

Let L_q=(I_q-t)(I_q-t^2). Since I_q k_u=t*k_u, the smooth center is killed
EXACTLY. Also L_q delta=t^3 delta. Therefore
  L_q tilde_b=beta E_q(B-t^2 U-t V)+r_q,
  r_q=beta*t^3 delta-L_q d.                       (2.2)
The unit matters. At n=1, the q-free source part and r_q cancel consistently
with a(q^e)=0; neither term is independently the whole source.

There is a useful unconditional UNIFORM remainder. The same-prime d is
nonnegative, its complete mass at each prime is beta, and
D(x)=sum_(n<=x)d(n)<=beta*sqrt(x). The sequences d(qn) and d(q^2 n) are
supported on the q-power chain and each has total mass at most beta. Thus
  sum_(n<=x)|r_q(n)|
      <=beta*[1+(t+t^2)+t^3+t^3*sqrt(x)]
      <=beta*(4+sqrt(x)).                         (2.3)
The first bound separately includes the shifted chains, the unit, and the
unshifted d part; it does not remove other-prime d(n). For the prefix of
r_q the dyadic squared cost is at most
  beta^2*(4+sqrt(2))^2                            (2.4)
for every X>=1 and every actual prime q. This is a paid source remainder
with no companion norm hypothesis.

The remaining E_q(B-t^2 U-t V) is CURRENT-scale restricted source data.
It has not been bounded here. Furthermore L_q has a large generic kernel:
on each q-free chain H, any t^e F(H)+t^(2e)G(H) at n=q^e H is annihilated.
F,G may vary over all q-free H. Removing the one known global center does
not remove every such q-free mode. A full-target recovery from (2.2) must
retain these initial chain rows or use a separately proved joint forcing.
No uniformly bounded inverse on arbitrary full source prefixes is claimed.

3. Actual-prime divided differences and composite/center coupling
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Let q,r be distinct actual primes, t_q=q^(-u), t_r=r^(-u), and impose the
EXACT domain gcd(H,qr)=1. Then
  [tilde_b(qH)-tilde_b(rH)]/(t_q-t_r)
     =beta*[(1+t_q+t_r)B(H)-U(H)-(t_q+t_r)V(H)]
                                                -R_u*k_u(H). (3.1)
This holds at H=1, where both sides are -R_u. On H>=2, qH and rH have
at least two distinct prime factors, so d(qH)=d(rH)=0. If H itself is a
prime power, its ORIGINAL d(H) still has to be subtracted when recovering
tilde_b(H). No false inference about d(H) is made from d(qH)=0.

Proof: the first-row factors for B,U,V are t+t^2-2,t-1,t^2-1, respectively.
Subtract the q and r values, use t_q^2-t_r^2=(t_q-t_r)(t_q+t_r), and retain
the pure-power center. This is an actual signed/composite identity, not
selection of a coefficient to optimize observed data.

Define the joint states
  F=beta*(B-U)-R_u*k_u,
  G=beta*(B-V).
Equation (3.1) is F(H)+(t_q+t_r)G(H). It is affine in the actual-prime
parameter. A second divided difference in that parameter isolates G;
the intercept isolates F. The original source is exactly
  tilde_b=F-beta*V+beta*delta-d.                   (3.2)
All complements in (3.2) are retained. In particular V is not paid just
because its positive real pole is at 1/3.

The known analytic factorization of F is
  F(s)=zeta(s+u)*[beta*zeta(s+v)/zeta(s)^2
                                     -beta/zeta(s)-R_u].
The bracket has a local analytic zero at s=2/3 by the fixed residue choice.
It does not thereby have a convergent coefficient series at that line or
a uniform finite reciprocal-interval remainder bound. That missing tail is
exactly the warning in the open input, not a new hypothesis supplied here.

The divided-difference inverse cost must also be preserved. For q>r,
the mean-value theorem gives
  (q-r)/(3*q^(4/3))<=t_r-t_q<=(q-r)/(3*r^(4/3)),
so its reciprocal lies between
  3*r^(4/3)/(q-r) and 3*q^(4/3)/(q-r).             (3.3)
No prime-gap estimate is assumed. A small raw exchange numerator can be
mere attenuation by this denominator. Normalizing it is not a free gain.
Repeated q=r requires the all-valuation operator (2.1), not division by zero.
Terms with q|H or r|H must use their actual valuation rows; terms with qH
or rH outside the original prefix are separate pullback complements.

There is a particularly clear dependency guard for V. If M_mu is the
ordinary Mobius prefix, then
  V=mu*k_v, mu=V*M_v, M_v(n)=mu(n)n^(-v).
For the finite step-prefix norm
  ||F||_(sigma,T)^2=int_1^T |F(x)|^2*x^(-2sigma-1)dx,
each direction has norm at most
zeta(v+sigma), since dilation by d costs d^(-sigma), and v+sigma>=7/6>1
for sigma>=1/2. Thus the raw V critical prefix norm is uniformly comparable
to the ordinary Mobius prefix norm. Subtracting its n^(1/3) pole is paid
in that norm. Independently assuming the needed cofinal V bound would
therefore assume an RH-equivalent companion bound; it cannot close (3.2)
without a joint, independently justified arithmetic argument.

4. All-prime log-divisor coupling with complete units
-----------------------------------------------------
Let L f(n)=log(n)f(n), an exact derivation for Dirichlet convolution:
  L(f*g)=(Lf)*g+f*(Lg).
The finite coefficient identities Lmu=-mu*Lambda and
Lk_w=k_w*Lambda_w, where Lambda_w(d)=Lambda(d)d^(-w), give
  LU=U*J_u, LV=V*J_v, LB=B*(J_u+J_v),
  J_u(d)=Lambda(d)(d^(-u)-1),
  J_v(d)=Lambda(d)(d^(-v)-1).                     (4.1)
Lambda includes EVERY prime power; d=1 contributes zero. These can also
be proved directly from the local Euler factors, so no infinite-series
exchange or unproved analytic continuation is needed for finite n.

Use the source state
  Z=(p,beta U,beta V,R_u*k_u,beta delta),
  ell=(1,0,0,-1,0), f_p=ell Z=p-R_u*k_u.
For a prime power d let
  l_u=d^(-u)-1, l_v=d^(-v)-1, l_B=l_u+l_v.
Define the exact matrix
  A_d = [l_B l_v l_u  0       -l_B]
        [0   l_u 0    0        0  ]
        [0   0   l_v  0        0  ]
        [0   0   0    d^(-u)   0  ]
        [0   0   0    0        0  ].
Then at EVERY n,
  log(n) Z(n)=sum_(d|n,d>=2)Lambda(d) A_d Z(n/d).   (4.2)
The initial row is Z(1)=(0,beta,beta,R_u,beta). At n=1 both sides of
(4.2) are zero; the initial row is not inferred by dividing by log 1.
The -l_B unit entry in the first row retains the derivative of p's delta
correction. It cancels the spurious prime value of p. The fourth row is
the complete pure-power center, not a discarded deterministic forcing.

All coefficient arguments n/d in (4.2) are genuinely smaller, and there
is no current-scale q-free restriction. This is a real distinction from
(2.2). However, after summation, there are many branches and fresh/fresh
mixed terms. Smaller arguments alone do not prove a prefix-moment gap.
One must lift the identity to the actual whole kernel, as in section 5.

It is safer to use p and then its paid target bridge than to delete d
inside (4.2). If one writes log(n)*a(n) directly, the extra defect is
  (J_u+J_v)*d-Ld.
The fact that d itself has a paid prefix norm DOES NOT pay this convolution.
Indeed its Dirichlet factor is D(s)*[-zeta'(s+u)/zeta(s+u)
 -zeta'(s+v)/zeta(s+v)+2zeta'(s)/zeta(s)]. At s=1 it has residue -2D(1),
where 0<D(1)<infinity. D is analytic for Re(s)>1/2 by its prime-power
support and mass bounds. Ld has no pole there to cancel it. Thus the
isolated defect has a genuine linear-scale pole mechanism. It belongs
to joint cancellation, not a paid logarithmic d remainder.

For exact original recovery, define R_p(n)=sum_(h<=n)p(h)-m*n^(2/3).
Then R_p=R+D. Since E_D<=2beta^2,
  E_R<=2E_Rp+4beta^2, E_Rp<=2E_R+4beta^2.          (4.3)
All finite mixed terms are retained in these two-direction inequalities.
The smooth-center replacement and paid low/diagonal reduction apply to
f_p as well, because |p(n)|<=beta*d_2(n). Let O_p be its complete joined
high-product off-diagonal expression. A cofinal upper bound for O_p,
together with its same paid remainder, gives the original target by
(4.3). No bound on |O_p-O_X| independent of the unknown target is asserted.

5. Exact lift to the whole prefix-square kernel
------------------------------------------------
The tensor derivation is
  log(hk)[Z(h) tensor Z(k)]
     =sum_(d|h)Lambda(d)[A_d Z(h/d) tensor Z(k)]
       +sum_(d|k)Lambda(d)[Z(h) tensor A_d Z(k/d)]. (5.1)
On the high-product kernel hk>X>=1 the denominator log(hk) is strictly
positive. Contract (5.1) with ell on BOTH legs, retain K_X, and use its
symmetry to obtain exactly
  O_p=(2/X^2)*sum_(d>=2)Lambda(d)
       *sum_(H<=N/d,k<=N)
         [ell A_d Z(H)]*[ell Z(k)]
         *W_X(dH,k)*1_(dH!=k)*1_(dHk>X)/log(dHk). (5.2)
Only prime powers d<=N contribute. Unit cofactors H=1 or k=1 are retained.
There is no gcd or coprimality assumption; d may share its prime with H
and k. The inequality dHk>X is the exact new product birth, and dH!=k is
the exact diagonal exit. The max cutoff and prefix cap are not replaced
by a product-only kernel. No source above N is needed for this removal
formula. All source/center cross channels remain in ell A_d Z(H), namely
  l_B p(H)+l_v beta U(H)+l_u beta V(H)
                      -l_B beta delta(H)-d^(-u)R_u k_u(H).

Equation (5.2) is a concrete all-prime signed covariance rather than a
heuristic coefficient bound. One leg H is strictly smaller; the other leg
k is still current-scale. Summing its terms absolutely or replacing its
kernel by a diagonal coefficient energy changes the proof obligation.
Iterating removal on both legs is possible, but every common-prime and
repeated-power intersection, unit boundary and moved cutoff remains.

For comparison, coefficient-level Jensen with weights Lambda(d)/log(n)
can give a cheap weighted DIAGONAL norm. That is not a prefix-square norm.
The full prefix energy has all h!=k terms in (5.1); their signed covariance
is precisely the open input. A factor d^(-sigma-1/2) from a discrete
coefficient Hilbert norm cannot be silently substituted for the d^(-sigma)
cost of step-prefix dilation. Such a substitution would manufacture a
false critical gap.

There is also no uniform fixed-positive-metric contraction for the whole
matrix A_d based only on its entries. It has eigenvalues l_B,l_u,l_v,d^(-u),0;
along large actual primes their first three limits are -2,-1,-1. Any induced
positive-metric norm is at least its spectral radius, hence eventually
exceeds one. Normalizing by the exact identity sum_(d|n)Lambda(d)=log n
does not alter that pointwise obstruction. For two distinct primes,
U(qr)=l_u(q)l_u(r); the two removal terms have the SAME sign and reproduce
exactly log(qr)U(qr). The corresponding B factor behaves the same way.
A universal strict entrywise prime-removal credit is therefore not supplied
by the source identity. This is a limitation of a generic absolute/fixed-
metric argument, not a proof that actual joined covariance cannot contract.

6. Mixed log-divisor gain and its exact boundary commutator
-----------------------------------------------------------
The convolution derivation also yields the useful signed identity
  (LU)*V-U*(LV)=B*(J_u-J_v),
  J_u(d)-J_v(d)=Lambda(d)(d^(-u)-d^(-v))>=0.       (6.1)
For a full product fiber m,
  sum_(hk=m)U(h)V(k)log(h/k)=[B*(J_u-J_v)](m).
The antisymmetric determinant multiplied by log(h/k) has twice this value:
  sum_(hk=m)[U(h)V(k)-V(h)U(k)]log(h/k)
                                           =2[B*(J_u-J_v)](m). (6.2)
This exposes a genuine d^(-u) prime-power gain and an exact divisor-pair
correlation. It is not a statement that actual Mobius signs are random.

For an arbitrary finitely supported symmetric kernel K, define
  S_K=sum_(h,k)U(h)V(k)log(h/k)K(h,k),
  K_L=K(dH,Kc), K_R=K(H,dKc),
where Kc is a cofactor variable, distinguished from the kernel name.
Pulling back both terms of LU*V-U*LV gives exactly
  S_K=sum_(d>=2)Lambda(d)*sum_(H,Kc)U(H)V(Kc)
       *{(d^(-u)-d^(-v))*(K_L+K_R)/2
           +(d^(-u)+d^(-v)-2)*(K_L-K_R)/2}.        (6.3)
All repeated valuations and overlapping primes are included. There is no
q-free assumption or prime-independence approximation.

For a product-only kernel K(h,k)=phi(hk), K_L=K_R and the unattenuated
commutator vanishes. For the actual prefix kernel W_X and product masks,
K_L-K_R has max/prefix-exit and any diagonal-mask terms. Those are the
missing transport covariance. Their coefficient d^(-u)+d^(-v)-2 tends
to -2, so the positive d^(-u) gain of the first term does NOT pay them.

On the flat interior dH,dKc<=X, the W part has K_L=K_R=X; their product
mask also agrees because both products equal dH*Kc. Thus the max-kernel
commutator is confined to a genuine prefix frontier. If K includes the
off-diagonal mask, the unequal diagonal exclusions are retained separately.
For this angular statistic the original h=k contribution has log(h/k)=0,
so one may use the diagonal-inclusive kernel and then account for target
diagonals by the already paid bound. None of this pays the moving frontier
merely because it is called a boundary. Both orientation supports remain.

The paired angular identity is a useful detector of an actual arithmetic
mechanism. Its unattenuated commutator is a concrete next source-bound
lemma, and its symmetric source mode is another. The original O_p is a
joined symmetric square, not just this angular channel. Bounding (6.3)
alone does not recover that symmetric mode. A candidate must present the
complete reconstruction/correlation identities for both.

7. Kernel insertion pullbacks and outside-source complements
------------------------------------------------------------
The insertion operators in sections 2/3 require their own exact domain
descriptors. For any finite original kernel K_X,
  sum_(h,k)f(q^a h)g(q^b k)K_X(h,k)
   =sum_(q^a|H,q^b|Kc)f(H)g(Kc)K_X(H/q^a,Kc/q^b). (7.1)
In particular the pulled support has H<=q^a N and Kc<=q^b N,
  H*Kc>q^(a+b)X,
  H/q^a != Kc/q^b,
and the original max cutoff on the divided coordinates. The unit h=1
becomes H=q^a. Split inside H,Kc<=N and its outside-source complement
explicitly. Insertion cannot silently identify its new sampled horizon
with the old full prefix, even when it kills the smooth center.

By contrast removal/pushforward f(h/q)1_(q|h) gives K_X(qH,Kc), with
smaller cofactor support and no source beyond N. Its center is correspondingly
masked on q multiples; it is not the same operator as I_q, and cannot reuse
the unmasked eigenlaw I_q k_u=t k_u. The distinction is also the difference
between (7.1) and the exact all-prime lift (5.2).

For two-prime divided differences, gcd(H,qr)=1 belongs to the exact
primitive. The complementary q/r-divisible sector uses the rational local
valuation rows (2.1). If the cofactor legs share primes, both masks are
retained jointly. Distinct-prime exchange, repeated-prime insertion and
unit chains are separate branches of the same complete grammar, not
alternative definitions of the actual source.

8. A constructive enumerable grammar and its proof gate
--------------------------------------------------------
A small language can now enumerate STRUCTURAL words rather than optimize
drift constants. Its leaves are the actual channels p,U,V,k_u,delta,d, the
complete reciprocal intervals and the whole max/product/diagonal kernel.
Its exact operations can be:
  * actual-prime insertion, removal and valuation restriction, with (2.1);
  * distinct-prime divided difference (3.1), with the declared denominator,
    inverse cost, domain and all complementary sectors;
  * log-divisor derivation/removal (4.2)/(5.2), including every prime power;
  * tensor swap, symmetric/antisymmetric decomposition and mixed-log
    commutator (6.3), retaining both orientation kernels;
  * reciprocal-interval grouping, signed square completion and coupled
    lower-prefix prediction with an actually proved coefficient gap;
  * the paid low/diagonal/center leaves (1.1), fixed-prime remainder (2.3),
    and the two-direction p/a bridge (4.3).

Bound word length, rational coefficient complexity and prime-domain syntax
before evaluation. These operations alter source coupling and kernel
transport; they are not a coefficient search inside a fixed numerical LP.
Canonical equality must compare full formulas, masks, centers and recovery
descriptors, rather than only local eigenvalues or finite scores.

A useful generated candidate might compose all-prime removal with a mixed
log difference so that its bulk uses the d^(-u) part of (6.3), and keep its
max-kernel commutator as a separate JOINT forcing. An argument must then
either pay that forcing independently or derive a simultaneous induction
whose signed cross supplies a coefficient below one. It must also recover
the symmetric O_p mode. The fact that the suppressed angular statistic is
cheap does not imply domination of the joined target.

Concretely, the remaining complete forcing in (5.2) is a weighted sum of
source-state correlations
  sum_(H,k) Z_i(H)Z_j(k)
     *W_X(dH,k)*1_(dH!=k)*1_(dHk>X)/log(dHk).
Its input channel matrix A_d and its supports are explicit. A proof can
try to exploit actual source constraints between these correlations,
their swapped transports, and the symmetric/commutator split (6.3).
Simply assigning a cofinal bound to the entire displayed sum restates O_p.
Likewise paying U,V or q-free companions separately is not permitted
without a noncircular proof; section 3 gives an explicit RH-equivalent
dependency for V. A metric on unconstrained free source vectors cannot
manufacture the missing signed credit from section 5's matrix.

Admission requires a complete certificate with four separate parts:
  (a) exact source/tensor identity and all domain/center complements;
  (b) paid components with explicit uniform constants;
  (c) full original-target recovery, including any inversion power or
      prime-gap cost and any future boundary condition;
  (d) the next independently justified arithmetic forcing lemma, or an
      actual simultaneous recurrence with a proved induction coefficient
      gap, smaller arguments and acyclic dependencies.
Finite numerical evidence can prioritize an argument but supplies none
of (b)-(d). An unresolved target appearing in a q-free companion, a future
boundary, an inverse divided difference or a renamed joined sum remains
an unresolved target. A partial canceled projection is not the whole energy.

Final boundary
--------------
The grammar has exact source-specific primitives, a proved uniform
fixed-prime remainder, genuine all-prime smaller-cofactor equations, and
a precise attenuation-versus-prefix-commutator separation. These are useful
analytical reductions for an obligation-driven search. No strict complete
moment contraction or independent bound for its signed symmetric/frontier
forcing has been proved here. The all-scale bound remains open; no numerical
constant improvement is substituted for it.
