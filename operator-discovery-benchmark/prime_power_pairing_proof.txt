1. Exact one-prime sign-reversing edges.
Fix an ACTUAL prime p. For p not dividing m and an odd integer e>=1,
 c(p^e m)=-2c(m), c(p^(e+1)m)=2c(m).
Thus n=p^e m and pn are opposite signs with identical magnitude. A complete
edge exists in [1,X] iff pn<=X. Its certificate includes the actual p-free core,
odd exponent, both endpoints and both coefficients. At exponent0 the weight
changes1 to2: it is NOT an equal-weight cancellation edge and cannot be silently
matched. A positive odd top exponent with its even partner aboveX is unmatched.
For X=0 the domain and residual are empty; for X>=1 the coefficient at1 persists.

The complete single-prime residual is therefore exactly
 R_p(X)=C_p(X)-2sum_(j>=0)[C_p(floor(X/p^(2j+1)))
                                -C_p(floor(X/p^(2j+2)))],
 C_p(y)=sum_(n<=y,p not dividing n)c(n).
All sums terminate. Its unsigned charge is
 H_p(X)+2sum_(j>=0)[H_p(floor(X/p^(2j+1)))
                                -H_p(floor(X/p^(2j+2)))],

The intervals have lower endpoint floor(X/p^(2j+2)) EXCLUDED, upper endpoint
floor(X/p^(2j+1)) INCLUDED; this keeps all cutoff boundaries exactly. Signed
R_p(X)=C(X) because every removed pair sums0. This is an identity, not a bound
on the core or signed boundary intervals.

2. Disjoint multprime matching, exact selectors, resource semantics.
Choose a supplied finite ordered list of actual primes, with no duplicates.

endpoints define a fixed-point-free involution. Everything else stays in the
residual, including exponent0 cores, odd top boundaries and edges blocked by a
previous prime pass. One cannot assume the single-prime residual formula applies
after other passes without keeping those blocked terms.

Complete coverage is a partition of all1..X into edges and residual. The signed
prefix equals the complete signed residual; the original unsigned charge equals
matched cancelled mass plus residual charge. The implementation verifies these

For a FIXED finite set S, put P=product_(p inS)p (P=1 if S empty).
Every n<=X coprime toP has exponent0 at every selected prime and cannot be an

 unsigned_residual >= #{n<=X:gcd(n,P)=1}

Finite inclusion-exclusion proves the last inequality: replace each floor(X/d)

This concerns absolute charge, not the signed prefix; signs within the residual
could still cancel. Adaptive growing prime support requires its own cost and
still faces the universal obstruction below.

4. Even ALL primes cannot yield a power-saving absolute residual bound.
Every actual prime q with sqrtX<q<=X has exponent1, partnerq^2>X, and exponent0

 R_abs(X)>=2[pi(X)-pi(sqrtX)].
We now prove an explicit prime-count lower bound without using PNT or RH.

For integer n>=1, the largest binomial coefficient among the2n+1 entries of

For each prime p, the exponent in this binomial is
