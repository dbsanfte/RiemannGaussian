Strong limitations and noncircularity audit
The companion state by itself is a finite-filter repackaging of the target;
it is not a number-theoretic gain. The pair extraction is what adds paid
arithmetic structure. Even then the remainder is RH-strength for this
slice once enough algebra/base is paid, because proving its constant
bound would prove the displayed power recurrence. Physical nonproportionality
is necessary for a distinct leaf, but not sufficient to make it easier.

A finite dilation relation set plus arbitrary-sign pricing of all unlinked
pairs is intrinsically inadequate. In the outer parent band, every child
delay coefficient vanishes and K has a positive rank-one parent block.
There are many unlinked pairs there; arbitrary signs can align to make
their sum quadratic in the band size. Linked finite integer dilations
cannot absorb a dense set of pairs from a short multiplicative interval.
Thus the detector must seek an actual signed unlinked-correlation identity
or theorem, not apply absolute values after extracting a few easy edges.
Shared-cofactor ratios can link all primes through m=1, but their sign is
then positive and their large contribution still needs composite cancellation.
Expanding enough ratios to encode all pairs can reconstruct the entire
original target; that is exact but supplies no price or contraction.

The pilot therefore has two honest possible outcomes: a finite joint
recurrence hypothesis with explicit proper remainder and newly paid linked
coefficient control, or an exact dual/witness showing that this finite
memory/motif grammar cannot improve the remainder. It must not count a
fitted C_C as a proved control. A credible next analytic target is a
closed signed cofactor-pair summation rule for the residual kernel with a
uniform budget; that would supply real arithmetic progress. Current PNT,
Mertens zero-free bounds, finite first/second-mode centering and coefficient
norm estimates do not furnish it.

A machine-generatable density-balanced packet lemma

not just another fitted C. For squarefree g coprime to N=ab*product(P),

 Q_N(x)=c_N*x+O((2^omega(N)+2)*sqrt(x)),

An explicit absolute constant can be supplied without any PNT/RH input.
Proof: expand squarefreeness by sum_(d^2|g)mu(d), restrict gcd(d,N)=1,
and expand gcd(g/d^2,N)=1 by its squarefree-divisor inclusion/exclusion.
There are at most2^omega(N) floor errors per d<=sqrt x. The tail of
sum d^-2 costs at most2sqrt x. The Euler product gives c_N. This elementary
counting lemma is stronger and more explicit than assuming random mu signs.

For fixed delay depth and coefficient kernel, define the unnormalized
parent pair step kernel k_ab,H(t)=fdelay(a*t)^T H fdelay(b*t), t=g/u.

uniformly in a,b. Therefore
 sum_g mu(g)^2 1[gcd(g,N)=1] k_ab,H(g/u)
     =c_N*u*I_ab(H)+O(C_kernel,H*(2^omega(N)+2)*sqrt u).
The sum includes g=1 and its units exactly; the count error bound covers it.
