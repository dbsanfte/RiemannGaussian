The quarter-cell values of W are0,1,-1,0, so|W|<=1. For emptyS, W(N)=0 removes

2. Uniform normalizer and exponential integration.

4. Exact complete first derivative, not a prime-only source replacement.
For each finite N the integrand is a finite sum of exponentials and may be
differentiated at0. The p-free convolution identity

is exact, with the common zero-jet h_S(N,0)=0 from mu*One=delta and P(1)=0.
Thus the derivative of externalR^-t adds no surviving logR term and
 h_S'(N,0)=Psi_P(N)-sum_(p inS)b_p(N),
 Psi_P=Psi(4N)-3Psi(2N)+2Psi(N),
 b_p=logp*[floor(log_p4N)-3floor(log_p2N)+2floor(log_pN)].
Every ordinary prime contribution outside S remains in Psi_P. This identity
does not assert the full h_S equals its prime-only first derivative.

For a signed rational mixture a_i across canonical distinct S_i,
 sum_i a_i h_(S_i)'=(sum_i a_i)Psi_P+sum_p e_p b_p,
 e_p=-sum_(i:p inS_i)a_i.
Every generator prime is emitted even if e_p=0. A nonzero common coefficient
retains an OPEN ordinary common-prime term and licenses no uniform first-jet
constant. With zero common coefficient, each dyadic shell contains at most
one power of p>=2, hence |b_p|<=2logp<=2p. At p2, both shells have exactly one
power and b2=-log2, giving the sharper rational bound1.
Therefore sum_p|e_p|*(1 ifp2 else2p) is a proved uniform first-jet bound. If
every e_p is alsozero, the full first derivative is identicallyzero for allN.
This is an exact first-jet-only cancellation: no uniform higher derivative,
finite low-t integral, norm convergence, target bound or RH is inferred.
In particular cancelling only the outside prime class is weaker than
cancelling every prime class, and neither is full-kernel cancellation.
