Purpose

and have the detector certify both finite coefficient bounds and a generic
uniform obstruction to the fixed-prime absolute-coefficient method. These
are distinct tasks. A failed method does not refute a signed arithmetic
candidate. Preserve all076 residual/correlation counterexamples and leaves.

Representation and independent admission

Compile the exact076 G(u)-L(u) using actual prime quotient multiplicities,
direct children and arbitrary admitted rational inverse-count powers.
The producer regenerates prime membership only, without new mu queries.
The independent checker regenerates primes, cutoffs, counts, components
and count-root intervals itself. Original z/B/Q data require matching
stored independent075 source admission on the complete076 datasets.

Each coefficient function is a list of disjoint integer intervals(l,r]
carrying exact rational polynomials in named real count tokens. Combine
endpoint events; never allocate one coefficient object per integer. A fold
by p changes the FULL coefficient function f(m) to f(m)-f(pm), whose
scaled interval endpoints are floor(l/p),floor(r/p). The represented
Mobius sum separately retains gcd(m,product(P))=1. Do not zero function
values on excluded indices before a later fold. Retain units, prime-square
relations, every boundary and all signed prime/direct interactions.

On each coefficient interval the coprime multiplicity is EXACTLY
 sum_(d divides product(P))mu(d)(floor(r/d)-floor(l/d)).
Compute its full signed polynomial interval before taking its absolute
upper/lower envelope. Summing multiplicities times those envelopes gives
certified bounds on coefficient l1. Its upper value bounds the absolute
Mobius aggregate by |mu|<=1. Its lower value does NOT bound the absolute
arithmetic sum from below; it can only reject an overly small l1 price.

Uniform obstruction discovery

Give the detector the rational step kernel (8,+1),(10,-1),(4,-1),(5,+1),
the literal child rank at most u/2, selected fixed prime schedule and q.
Its generic kernel scanner searches breakpoint bands(alpha,beta] where
the kernel coefficient c is nonzero, alpha is beyond every child horizon,
and alpha>=kernel_horizon/2. Such a band survives all higher fold dilations.
This is a generic prefix-kernel rule, not a special-cased expected G result.

Let delta=product_(p in P)(p-1)/p. Exact inclusion-exclusion and floor
errors prove the coprime band count is at least
 delta*(beta-alpha)*u - 2^|P|.
Consequently coefficient l1 is at least A*u-E, with
A=|c|delta(beta-alpha)>0, E=|c|2^|P|, and at least A*u/2 for
u>=ceil(2E/A). The independent checker validates all kernel, band, prime,
coefficient and rational constant conditions. For fixed1<=q<2 this rules
out a uniform coefficient-l1-square bound C*u^q for any finite constant C.
It does NOT rule out cancellation in the represented signed Mobius sum.
The same obstruction applies to a fixed finite catalogue of such schedules.
Growing schedules need their own uniform analysis; pointwise maximization
or total factor elimination can simply encode the original target.
