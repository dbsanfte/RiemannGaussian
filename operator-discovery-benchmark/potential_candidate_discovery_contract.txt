Complete target and candidate
Actual mu on every positive integer is the target. M(t)=sum_{1<=n<=t}mu(n),
M(0)=0. E(X)=X^-2 sum_{t=X}^{2X-1}M(t)^2. A candidate is exactly
  {states:[{weight:canonical_nonnegative_rational_string, tree:AST}, ...]}.
Allow0..2 states,<=256 nodes total,depth<=24, rational/index bits<=4096.
V(X)=X^-2 sum_{t=X}^{2X-1} sum_j weight_j*U_j(X,t)^2.
W(X)=E(X)+V(X). Every child evaluates U with its own X=2X; the complete
innovation is W(2X)-W(X). Always retain W(1), E and both potentials.
An empty state list is the baseline V=0. No component or transformed energy
replaces E. Products and signed sums INSIDE U are allowed; V weights cannot
be negative. In particular square(sum_p state_p) retains all prime cross terms.

AST semantics: use the existing cancellation_arithmetic_language syntax
exactly for this supported subset (no digest-producing wrappers):
indices: int,v,iadd,isub,imul,idiv,imod,gcd,min,max,root(index,a,b).
root=floor(nonnegative_index^(a/b)),a>=0,b>=1;0th power1, including0^0.
booleans: eq,le,lt,divides,prime,squarefree,and,or,not.
scalars: q(canonical rational),x(index actual source),embed(index),ratio(i,j),
add,mul,scale(rational,scalar),guard(bool,scalar),
sum(fresh_name,inclusive_lo,inclusive_hi,scalar).
Initial free variables are X,t. Sum variables must be fresh, not shadowing.
N is reserved and cannot be referenced or bound, matching the legacy compiler.
No N-dependent program. x is defined only at1..source_horizon, never clipped;
guard short circuits scalar evaluation but does not skip syntax validation.
Integer division uses floor. Division byzero rejects. A sum lo>hi is zero.
False and/or short circuit like the existing compiler. Explicit endpoints,
zero-index guards and all residuals of any claimed transform are retained.
