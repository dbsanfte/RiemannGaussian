1. Exact storage, units and all signed crosses
---------------------------------------------
Let G(n) be the full prefix of the complete transported coefficient g, Bg
its FULL logarithmic bank, and D=F-G the complete transport-prefix error.
The global bank includes all completed-window logarithmic mismatches from
review011. Zero index is zero; g(1) and G(1) retain the original unit.

Choose either no auxiliary, with mix0/reserve1, or
 Q(n)=n^(-alpha) times one of
       Bg(n)/log(2n), G(floor(n/2)), D(n),
where alpha is a fixed canonical nonnegative rational. Mix m is any fixed
canonical rational and reserve r is a fixed positive canonical rational.
At n1 the log denominator is log2, not log1, while Bg1=0. Half delay uses
G0=0. No old head or bank prefix is deleted when n exceeds a cutoff.

Define for every dyadic X>=1
 A(X)=X^(-2) sum_(n=X)^(2X-1) [(G(n)+mQ(n))^2+rQ(n)^2].
This squares the COMPLETE mixed scalar before adding reserve; all its
internal signs and target/auxiliary crosses remain. It is not replaced
by G^2+m^2 Q^2+rQ^2. No absolute value or clipping is applied to Q.
The null program is exactly the baseline E_G, not a new arithmetic bound.

For EVERY real pair G,Q,
 c=1+m^2/r,
 c*[(G+mQ)^2+rQ^2]-G^2
                          =[mG+(m^2+r)Q]^2/r>=0.        (1.1)
Thus E_G(X)<=c A(X). This constant is sharp: equality occurs at
Q=-mG/(m^2+r), including m0/Q0. It is independent of alpha, of the
auxiliary's own size and of any unproved cancellation or companion bound.
Strict r>0 is essential; a zero reserve can conceal G entirely by Q=-G/m.

2. Full-source recovery and critical boundary
---------------------------------------------
For the fixed global transport program, review011 proves unconditionally
 E_D(X)<=25 X^e, e=2theta-5/3,
 E_F(X)<=2E_G(X)+2E_D(X)
                            <=2c A(X)+50 X^e.            (2.1)
Failed windows are kept whole, so this requires no prime-count threshold.
The original complete F and its original corrections/recovery chain are
retained. The storage domination is not a claim that A itself is bounded.

For theta<=5/6, e<=0, a fixed-polylog or all-epsilon cofinal A bound gives
the corresponding cofinal E_F bound. Theta5/6 is INCLUDED: its constant50
payment introduces no positive power or extra logarithmic degree. At
theta>5/6, (2.1) contains a positive power loss, so this certificate does
not supply critical cofinal recovery without an additional error estimate.
It does not analytically refute every larger-theta program.

The known critical witness belongs to ORIGINAL f, not to a newly assumed
meromorphic source g. If that independently verified witness has a surviving
critical double pole, the prior Mellin estimate excludes E_F=O(log^p X)
with p<2. Therefore for theta<=5/6 a proposed A=O(log^p X), p>=0, is also
excluded when p<2, by (2.1). This applies AT theta5/6 without asserting a
meromorphic double pole for g. It is a composition of two proved recovery
rules with the original source-bound witness. A supplied boolean is only
the API's external verified-witness token; this checker does not establish
that token's source/zero/noncancellation facts. Unverified tokens cannot
be promoted by finite storage measurements or a witness file's existence.

3. Universal recurrence premise and exact log-degree consequence
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let rho be1/2 or1, k a fixed nonnegative rational, and C a fixed integer>=1.
The proposed premise is
 A(2X)<=rho A(X)+C(1+log2 X)^k for EVERY dyadic X>=1.      (3.1)
Finite base A(1) is retained. Put X=2^j, so 1+log2 X=j+1 is an EXACT
integer, never log(2X) with natural logarithm. Finite sampled inequalities
are not the universal quantifier in(3.1).

If rho<1, nonnegativity and induction give
 A(2^j)<=A(1)+[C/(1-rho)](1+j)^k
                  <=[A(1)+C/(1-rho)](1+j)^k.             (3.2)
If rho1, summing the increments gives
 A(2^j)<=A(1)+C j(1+j)^k
                           <=[A(1)+C](1+j)^(k+1).        (3.3)
Hence conditional_log_degree is k for rho1/2, k+1 for rho1.
For cofinally paid transports the verified original pole witness rejects
the resulting degree below2. No C0 shortcut is used: the present grammar
fixes C>=1. A recurrence beginning after a declared finite head needs that
head and base to be included explicitly; this API's primary domain isX>=1.

These arguments are generic proof rules a detector can compose. They do
not require hand-selecting a storage vector or prove any generated full
core recurrence. A generated A hypothesis remains OPEN unless (3.1) is
actually established with its complete domain and source.

4. Independent implementation and interval decisions
----------------------------------------------------
Public APIs in check_core_control_synthesis.py:
 validate(program,meter=None)->True;
 measure(state,program,X,meter)->finite Arb storage;
 certificate(program,shape,transport_program,
             pole_witness_verified=False,meter=None)->JSON metadata;
 check_test(storage_by_scale,parents,shape,C,meter)->decision rows.
The optional metadata meter makes validation/certificate work accountable
when called in the shared stage; arithmetic measurement/testing requires
the supplied meter. Tests supply their own cumulative meter throughout.

Exact input schemas are program{auxiliary,mix,reserve}, nonnull auxiliary
{kind,alpha}, shape{rho,log_power}, and the independently specified fixed
global transport{cofactor,theta,capacity_fraction,load_multiple,
keep_failed_windows}. Scalar rationals must be canonical strings with
bounded4096-bit numerator/denominator. Boolean-as-integer inputs are
rejected. Independent arithmetic uses Arb192 precision, finite checks and
charged power/log blocks. It does not silently clamp nonfinite values.

State arrays g,G,Bg,D have matching complete zero-based shapes. Measure
checks the required finite scalar indices and support through2X-1; it does
not rebuild every prefix for every candidate. Complete source arithmetic,
g/G/Bg linkage, D=F-G, horizon ownership and transport validity belong to
the separate independent transport checker, not to a synthetic array's
shape alone. The record must bind to that verified state before recovery
is used. Alpha/reserve/mix are fixed program parameters, not fitted by a
different window or changed after viewing validation responses.

check_test requires a strict frozen C, increasing distinct dyadic parents
and complete parent/child storage. For each parent it forms the full Arb
drift A(2X)-rho A(X) and bound C(1+log2 X)^k. It reports
 pass if the entire inequality is certified;
 fail if its strict violation is certified;
 unresolved if the intervals overlap the decision boundary.
Invalid/missing/nonfinite domain is explicit failure, not a silently
omitted test or pass. Certified-negative storage intervals are rejected;
an interval containing nonnegative storage is not clipped to make the
recurrence pass. Source state must be verified independently as above.

Certificate metadata keeps the domination and full-target constants,
error power, conditional log degree, cofinal gate, externally verified
pole-token gate and explicit uniform premise separate. It never marks
uniform_premise_established, all_scale_storage_bound_proved,
all_scale_target_bound_proved or RH_proved true from finite tests.

5. Proof provenance and unresolved arithmetic
---------------------------------------------
The detector can now generate full control hypotheses rather than only
transport parameters: positive coercive storage with signed internal
coupling, proposed recurrence shape, frozen training constant, conditional
recovery and algebraic pole exclusions. This is genuine bound synthesis,
but finite validation remains evidence for an OPEN all-scale premise.
The recovery/recurrence theorem rules are supplied analytic inputs; the
generated storage and bound ASTs need their own provenance in records.

No closing inequality for the actual complete transported source is
proved here. In particular positivity of A, a favorable cross, removal
of large prime coefficients, a finite pass or satisfaction of necessary
pole log degree is not a sufficient arithmetic estimate. Full prefix,
composite/prime-power, center, unit and history interactions remain in the
universal recurrence. All original global/RH claims stay false/open.
