The pilot learns H_X(t)=G(t)+sum_j w_j T_(j,X)(t), with the leading coefficient of the complete actual target prefix G fixed at1. The twelve T coordinates are actual c=mu*lambda source sums over positive even valuations of the smallest prime p, p-free core m<=floor(sqrt X), p<=floor(X^theta), retaining core1 or Omega(m)>h. The same fixed target filter is applied, and each correction is divided by ceil(X^(1/[2(h+1)])). Here Omega counts prime factors with multiplicity. The proved uniform critical correction envelope is the admission premise; it is not fitted from finite correlations. Arbitrary fixed signed rational weights preserve critical-energy equivalence between H and G. This yields an RH-directed conditional bridge for admitted actual targets, not a proved energy estimate or an automatic converse from RH to every target's energy bound.

Exact theta saturation.
Since p is the SMALLEST prime factor, every prime factor of a nonunit core m is strictly greater than p. If Omega(m)>h, then m>p^(h+1). Together with m<=sqrt X, this implies p<X^(1/[2(h+1)]). Therefore every theta>=1/[2(h+1)] already includes ALL retained nonunit source terms. Integer floors introduce no exceptional boundary: an integer p strictly below X^a is included by floor(X^theta) whenever theta>=a.
For h1, the threshold is1/4: theta1/3 and1/2 have identical retained nonunit source, while theta1/6 may differ.
For h2, the threshold is1/6: all three declared theta values have identical retained nonunit source.
For h3 and h5, thresholds1/8 and1/12 are below every declared theta, so again all three theta values have identical retained nonunit source.
These are identities of the nonunit source portions, before the fixed filter. For equal h their normalized differences are PURE core1 prime-power source corrections. They are not necessarily identical whole numerical columns, because the core1 portion varies with theta. The twelve declared coordinates consequently contain at most FIVE nonunit source classes: two at h1 and one each at h2,h3,h5. This is not a claim that those five classes are statistically or arithmetically independent.

What core1 controls mean.
Core1 here means prime-power sources p^(2j), with actual coefficient c(p^(2j))=2; it is NOT the old factor-region unit-source delta at n1. These columns contain real prime-count/prime-power arithmetic, changing theta cutoffs and exact normalizers. Under the fixed filtered target, the supplied signed filter also acts on them. However their source sign is fixed before that filter; they do not probe signed Mobius/Liouville correlations across nonunit p-free cores. A finite improvement using only these controls is evidence about a known critical auxiliary prime-power response, not discovery of a new signed core cancellation inequality. Their growth and filter transitions must still be charged; they are not identically zero or bounded constants.

Recommended interpretation and reporting.
Keep the baseline H=G and every attempted/manual/fitted/quantized candidate. Report raw core1 versus retained nonunit source coverage and charge for each feature and each dataset, rather than presenting all twelve coordinates as independent cancellation directions. Record exact unridged Gram ranks and source-identity classes; varying normalizers can distinguish columns over several scales even when their underlying nonunit source is identical or absent. Ridge positive-definiteness certifies a finite training quadratic optimum only. It does not certify data identifiability or an all-scale law.
Selection should distinguish known-control-only programs from programs with actual activated nonunit corrections. State the fraction of finite gain attributable to core1 controls only when recomputed from the complete signed Gram, including target/control and control/control cross terms; no diagonal-only attribution. Compare direct energy, innovation, worst finite margin, coefficient costs and baseline on the same declared indices. Unknown q constants remain missing. An optimizer minimizing pooled direct energy can produce genuine finite target-corrector cancellation through its off-diagonal Gram entries, but a lower fitted constant alone is not a proof of cancellation at future scales.

Expressive limit and next grammar priorities.
The present experiment is a twelve-dimensional linear correction space with a quadratic same-index energy objective. It retains complete signed base-corrector covariance and has a strong recovery guarantee. It can change finite amplitudes and conditional proof representations; its unconditional correction theorem does not weaken the remaining unknown critical G estimate. It neither learns general two-index arithmetic correlations nor supplies an all-scale residue/tail calculation.
A broader follow-up should prioritize exact relational observables involving valuation and p-free core, core1/nonunit cross terms, multiple-prime or gcd/coprime interactions, and complete mixed signed covariance over factor regions. Source support, quotient/floor transitions and omitted-domain complements must remain explicit. Learned coefficients should come from full signed matrices and be normalized with an anchored ordinary target, rather than by rescaling a small observable. Direct banks need exact rational ordinary-energy recovery; nonlinear or bilinear operators without such recovery require a separate ordinary-zeta pole/reconstruction theorem. Previously proved region obstructions should reject only the precisely covered supports or certified dominant terms, not arbitrary mixtures whose signed cancellation remains unknown.
If the next objective is learning actual high-Omega core cancellation, choose new source scales that activate those cores in more than one training and validation block, or reduce the declared depth inventory with an explicit pre-data rule. Existing pilot data may become development evidence for that new language; newly sealed final scales should remain disjoint in their declared response domains. Historical prime coefficients remain historical overlap, not an unseen-prime holdout.

No full critical energy bound, ordinary-zeta zero exclusion, or RH result is established by this scope audit.
