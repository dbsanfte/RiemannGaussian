PERIODIC NORMALIZATION SEARCH 040

Frozen before the sole actual-source symbolic search. No arithmetic coefficient
archive, old finite study, fitted constant, or new source observation is used.
Inputs are the unchanged035 complete source certificate and exact scalar,
039 independently derived pole certificate, and039 complete continuation.
Inherited proof code is compared byte for byte, without hashes.

The supplied grammar is mean-zero periodic preconditioning, exact Dirichlet
convolution/inversion, analytic centering, all-real power and shifted-inverse
kernel bounds, and a complementary hyperbola argument. These theorem rules
are authored and reviewed, not automatically invented by the detector.
The detector solves the periodic coefficient from its mean equation, enumerates
q in[2,3,5,7,11,17] and convolution powers j in[0,1,2], compiles every signed
source channel, optimizes the rational split, prices the complete remainder,
and selects a program only if a reciprocal-zeta pole survives. Periods need
not be prime; this finite dictionary is a work choice.

The hyperbola split solves min_alpha max(alpha,theta*(1-alpha)),0<alpha<=theta.
When the optimum does not give v+sigma>1 but v>1/2, the exponent may be enlarged
to the exact rational midpoint of1-v and1/2. This has no denominator whitelist.
The large-x comparison and separate subunit estimate license that enlargement.
No choice of a tiny numerical constant or fit selects the split or candidate.

Independent checker check_periodic_normalization_040.py imports no current
producer. It reconstructs the full transformed source, all prices and selection;
replays the separately bound exact normalization using check_source_pole_guard;
and verifies the absolute scalar ceiling with256-bit Arb. The proof and its
classical analytic premises are in PERIODIC_NORMALIZATION_SCOPE_040.txt.
This is a checked implementation of the stated mathematical proof rules,
not a formal proof assistant certificate of all analytic premises.

Extra detail for the all-real shifted-kernel rule used in that proof:
 e_theta(x)=prefix(n^(-r))(x)-x^theta/theta lies in[-1/theta,0],x>0.
 For x>=1, sum_(d<=x)d^-v<=x^(1-v)/(1-v), and for w=theta+v>1,
 sum_(d>x)d^-w<=x^(1-w)*(1+1/(w-1)).
 Splitting the exact centered K_r*mu_v convolution into that finite sum and
 complete tail gives C_L=[1/(1-v)+1+1/(v-r)]/theta with power1-v.
 For0<x<1 its prefix vanishes and its main is at most x^theta/theta,
 hence the same envelope holds because theta>1-v. No ordinary critical
 mu moment is assumed. This supplies the generic actual-source C_L=21/2.

All-real prefix scope includes x/d<1 centered values. Every source unit,
dilation coefficient, conditional periodic moment tail, and shifted inverse
divisor is retained. Exact normalization cancels the theta main; an interval
for R alone cannot do so. The new signed target is always OPEN until its own
uniform bound is proved. A bounded inverse to the periodic operator is never
assumed. Two preconditioner copies erase the registered RH pole and therefore
cannot be promoted to an RH result through this bridge.

Success criteria for this experiment:

 - A machine-readable OPEN centered-Mobius target with its exact source binding,
   all scales and constants, and conditional RH implication.
 - Retain the old original-F obligations; no circular endpoint payment.
The full source bound and RH are not success claims of this partial experiment.
