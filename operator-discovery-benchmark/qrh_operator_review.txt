The source claims are not a local independent verification of the full proofs.

The release links to manuscripts proving fixed zero-free half-planes,
weaker than RH's critical-line assertion. The main September-30 manuscript
has a 7/8 conclusion; the alternate October-5 argument has an 11/12
conclusion. The supplied Lean scope documentation states a 7/8 region for
zeta, Dirichlet L-functions and finite-order Hecke L-functions over Q(sqrt(-3)).
We did not run or certify those formalizations. Challenge statements are
not substitutes for checking their referenced formal proofs.

In the main proof, completed cubic-theta indices have the form c*n^3.
Local masks must mark the whole index, since c and n can share primes.
Section 12 defines a prime operation that subtracts a rescaled probe
from a prime-marked probe. Composition expands over fixed, distinct prime
slots. Section 16 combines local factors without division by a raw factor
that may vanish. The paper describes its contribution through compatible
comparisons, controlled compensation and uniform moment/scale margins.
The elementary Gauss, Poisson and Mobius ingredients have cited prior art.
Detecting the finite algebra is only one part of reproducing that argument.

The alternate proof controls mean squares of twisted Mobius sums across
an arithmetic row family. Sixth-power prime rows agree with the untwisted
row except for a controlled divisibility error. An all-scale family bound
then implies cancellation of the original Mobius sum; Mellin inversion
links that cancellation to zero exclusion. Poisson transformation brings
sextic Gauss sums; conversion absorbs mu into cubic-theta coefficients,
and reflection produces quadratic twists. Cube completion is reversed by
Mobius inversion with explicit row/scale costs. Merely dropping the cube
terms does not preserve the needed inequality.

OUR DETECTOR'S CHECKED SCOPE

432 scalar conversion programs give a unique surviving Gauss identity
after norms 7,13 train and 19,31,37,43,61 are held out. Exact cyclotomic
arithmetic agrees with independent 384-bit native complex balls. All 816
finite Fourier covariance identities pass with the nonunit masks retained.
Conjugating the generator and chi(4) is essential in the source convention.
The same normalized conversion executes through the new scalar AST.

Generic divisor/cube inversion and fixed-slot prime mark/rescale composition
have written algebraic proofs and changed-table independent controls.
The local rational replacement control covers the principal-row case
W=Q^-w, including a vanishing raw factor. Finite coefficient tables are
integer proxies; they are not full number-field theta probes.

The detector does not implement the full cubic-theta reflection, prove the
global Hecke-family moment bounds, or independently verify either full QRH
proof. These finite external calibrations add nothing to the main ordinary
zeta root-comparison count or historically verified new-operator list.

TO DISCOVER GLOBAL BOUNDS WITHOUT FITTING THIS EXACT PROOF

The new global_moment_grammar.py targets quantified energies, unknown
mixed-moment transfers, positive functionals, frequency separation and
scale-cost recurrences. Additive Mobius families, signed recursive pairs
and lacunary products serve as unrelated calibration families. Operator
classes, generic coefficient inputs, scale ratios and row moduli are held
out. A fitted recurrence requires a universal support/operator certificate;
only then may induction promote it to a global bound. Persistent forcing
requires a strict contraction margin or a separately retained logarithmic
loss. QRH-specific exponents and theta primitives are not training labels.
The next substantive target is a certified transfer for actual arithmetic
families with a new prime/Mobius estimate and ordinary-zeta zero information.
