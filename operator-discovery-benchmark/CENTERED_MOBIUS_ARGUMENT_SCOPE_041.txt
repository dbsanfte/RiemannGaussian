1. The target, source and admitted unconditional inputs

Let 0<r<1/2, theta=1-r, q>=2 integer, b=power(-r)*mu,
c=b-q delta_q*b, and
 kappa=(1-q^r)/(theta*zeta(theta)),
 H(x)=sum_(n<=x)c(n)-kappa*x^theta, x>0; H(0)=0.
The actual040 target has r=1/3,q=2. Its bound is still OPEN:
 every epsilon>0 admits C_epsilon independent of every integer X>=1,
 E_H(X)=X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X) H(n)^2 <= C_epsilon X^epsilon.
Neither ordinary convergence of mu(theta) nor this estimate is assumed.
Kappa is an analytic normalization. Initial Dirichlet equality is in its
absolute convergence half-plane, followed by analytic continuation.

For every prime p, put v=p^-r in (0,1). The local b series is
 (1-t)/(1-v*t). Its coefficients are b(p^e)=v^(e-1)(v-1), e>=1,
so |b(n)|<=1 by multiplicativity and |c(n)|<=q+1 by the full dilation.
For a separately certified integer K>=|kappa|, set B=q+1+K. Concavity gives
 |a(n)|<=B, a(n)=H(n)-H(n-1)=c(n)-kappa[n^theta-(n-1)^theta].
This includes n=1, a(1)=1-kappa. No previous prefix can be restarted.
The independent checker computes kappa at256-bit Arb precision and rejects
an unproved ceiling; a supplied bound certificate is replayed exactly.
This scalar computation observes no arithmetic coefficient or cached norm.

for EVERY integer X>=1 with ONE fixed program and constants. Finite checks

3. The paid part is exact and small

Finite variance decomposition gives the identity
 E_H(mX)=E_A(X)/m+V_m(X),
 V_m(X)=(m^2 X^2)^-1 sum_n sum_j[H(mn+j)-A_m(n)]^2.
For any m real numbers z_j, sum_j(z_j-mean)^2=(1/m)sum_(j<k)(z_j-z_k)^2.
Here |z_j-z_k|<=B|j-k| and sum_(j<k)(j-k)^2=m^2(m^2-1)/12. Hence
 0<=V_m(X)<=v_m/X, v_m=B^2(m^2-1)/(12m).
The constant is sharp for a linear sequence with increments B. Counting
all increments, including the unit, gives D(X)<=2mB^2.

Consequently the OPEN covariance premise implies
 E_H(mX)+(delta/m)E_I(X)<=rho E_H(X)+2CB^2+v_m/X.
Dropping the nonnegative innovation is valid only because delta>=0.
The independent checker admits this conditional implication and its paid
forcing/error constants. It leaves the covariance and uniform target OPEN.

4. Complete scale recovery, including m>2 and C=0

An m-adic chain starting only at1 leaves gaps for m>2. Keep ALL m-1 chains
X=s*m^j, s=1,...,m-1. A unconditional seed bound is
 E_H(s)<=4B^2 s<=S=4B^2(m-1), since H(0)=0 and |a|<=B.
Put F=2CB^2, v=v_m. Conditional chain prices are:
 rho<1:       L=S+(F+v)/(1-rho), log_power0;
 rho=1,C=0:  L=S+v*m/(m-1),     log_power0;
 rho=1,C>0:  L=S+v*m/(m-1)+F*m/(m-1), log_power1.
The middle case is genuinely bounded: sum_j v/(s*m^j) is finite. It must
not be unnecessarily recorded as logarithmic growth.

For any integer X choose j with m^j<=X<m^(j+1), s=floor(X/m^j). Then
Y=s*m^j<=X<Y'=(s+1)*m^j, with Y' becoming the seed1 next chain if s=m-1.
The two energy windows [Y,2Y),[Y',2Y') cover [X,2X), because Y'<=2Y.
Their nonnegative energies give E_H(X)<=E_H(Y)+4 E_H(Y'). The factor5
is therefore a safe all-integer recovery, not merely an endpoint statement.
For logarithmic chains Y' can exceed X: do NOT use ln Y'<=ln X. The chain
at Y' takes at most j+1 steps, j<=ln X/ln m. Since ln m>=(m-1)/m,
 E_H(Y')<=S+vm/(m-1)+F+F*m/(m-1)*ln X
          <=L*(1+ln X).
The same L covers Y. Thus E_H(X)<=5L*(1+ln X)^log_power for all integers.
For every epsilon>0, 1+ln X<=(1+1/epsilon)X^epsilon, X>=1. The recovered
subpower bound is CONDITIONAL on the single all-integer covariance leaf.
There is no promotion from observations to a recurrence theorem.

5. Source-sensitive prime innovation identities

For p distinct from q prime, c has the same local b multiplier at p:
 c(pn)=p^-r c(n)-1_(p does not divide n)c(n).
Thus with O_p(x)=sum_(n<=x,p does not divide n)c(n),
 P_c(px)=p^-r P_c(x)+O_p(px)-O_p(x).
This is valid for composite q also when p does not divide q; 'distinct' alone
is insufficient for composite q. The backend certificate's other-prime law
is explicitly under its q-prime hypothesis. No generic q is claimed prime.

At the selected prime q=p the local c series is
 (1-t)(1-p*t)/(1-p^-r*t).
It has a valuation-one crossing:
 c(pn)=p^-r c(n)-(p+1)1_(p does not divide n)b(n)
                         +p 1_(valuation_p(n)=1)b(n/p).
The geometric recurrence starts only at coefficient e>=3, not e>=2.
With O(x)=sum_(n<=x,p does not divide n)b(n), the exact full-prefix identity is
 P_c(px)=p^-r P_c(x)+O(px)-(p+1)O(x)+p O(x/p).
Every fractional/subunit argument is retained. Let k_b=1/(theta*zeta(theta)),
 k_O=k_b*(p-1)/(p-p^r), and Otilde(x)=O(x)-k_O*x^theta.
Analytic local-factor algebra gives kappa=(1-p^r)k_b and
 H(px)=p^-r H(x)+Otilde(px)-(p+1)Otilde(x)+p Otilde(x/p).
No ordinary critical coefficient moment is needed for this algebra. No
uniform bound on Otilde is supplied or follows from the algebra alone.

This is a useful genuinely source-dependent second construction family:
infer valuations from the rational local series, generate p-free prefix
states and all scale crossings, and substitute them into I and the covariance
matrix. It is not vanishing prime-only padding. The free p-coprime innovation
and its parent correlation are actual arithmetic quantities. The signed
source identity does not automatically make their quadratic form nonpositive.

A subsequent finite storage search may use exact relations L z=0, select

A formal failure of this relation relaxation does not refute actual source

7. Fractional weights and degree costs cannot disappear

For a storage monomial of degree d in prefix quantities with only |H(n)|<=Bn,
X^-2 summation costs O(X^(d-1)). A coefficient X^-gamma changes that to
d-1-gamma; replacing X by mX changes it by the exact factor m^-gamma.
A squared short innovation of length O(X^eta), eta>=0, has trivial normalized
energy O(X^(2eta-1)). Paying that small local boundary says nothing about the
long parent-child innovation. Telescoping approximately X^(1-eta) such pieces
and using triangle/Cauchy restores the generic O(X) energy price. All counts,
weights, endpoint/head and mixed terms must be retained. Odd/coprime states
cannot be substituted for full-prefix states; a prime-only model does not
cover composite coefficients.
