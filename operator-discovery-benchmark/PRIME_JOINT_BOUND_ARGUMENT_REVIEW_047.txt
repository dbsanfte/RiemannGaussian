PRIME JOINT BOUND ARGUMENT REVIEW 047
Reusable conditional potential/recurrence proof production

1. What has been proved, and what has not

The new executable argument grammar searches a FULL signed-state potential
and an all-integer recurrence. Its mechanically checked conclusion is:
if the stated complete base and uniform recurrence are proved for the
actual rounded source, then the whole forcing has the target logarithmic
bound. Rational coercivity, source split, level arithmetic and induction
are PAID universal algebra. The actual BASE and RECURRENCE leaves remain
OPEN. A finite successful recurrence experiment, fitted rational C, or
holdout cannot pay those leaves. No uniform arithmetic bound or RH result
has been obtained by this review.

This production is different from merely optimizing a coefficient in h:
it enumerates a joint signed storage functional and a proof implication.
It can propose a genuine stronger arithmetic lemma. Nevertheless, storage
alone is reversible conditioning: a smaller constant or slope in a finite
potential is not a new all-scale cancellation theorem. The main next leaf
is a signed transport-work inequality, with predictable source transport,
complete innovation and both parities. Its forcing is neither assumed
small nor estimated from a numerical constant.

2. Exact source binding and prime split without division

Fix the actual ordinary Mobius source, a prime p, the full bare drift AST
with fixed rational a>0, 0<theta<1, positive distinct rates r_j and signed
weights w_j summing to one. Let
  beta_N=ceil(a L(N)^theta)/L(N), L(N)=1+floor(log_2 N),
  U_N(n)=sum_j w_j floor_dyadic(n^(-r_j beta_N)),
with each exponential separately rounded at 4 ceil(log_2 N) bits. Let
V=U_(pN), recomputed with the actual child level and precision. Every
recipe payload, rounding policy and prime is bound in the argument.

Using f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)) on (0,1], zero elsewhere, put
  e_N=chi+sum_(n=2)^N mu(n)U_N(n)f_n,
  h_N=e_(pN)+D_p e_N, D_p f(x)=f(px).
The direct conversion auxiliary is
  A_N=D_p[(1-V(p))chi
             +sum_(n=2)^N mu(n)(U_N(n)-V(pn))f_n].                 (1)
Define H_N=h_N-A_N. Then, equivalently,
  H_N=e_(pN)+D_p[V(p)chi+sum_(n=2)^N mu(n)V(pn)f_n],
  H_N+A_N=h_N                                                        (2)
for every integer N, with all prime powers and units present. This is
universal finite algebra; it needs no nonzero lambda or quotient. If
V(p)=0, (1)-(2) remain defined and exact. There is no assumption that the
profile at p is independent of N, or that child beta equals parent beta.
An independently computed finite source implementation still needs its
own replay; it cannot establish a uniform bound by verifying (2) once.

The source split is therefore a PAID definition/identity, not an unpaid
arithmetic estimate. The original full h is retained. Bounding only H,
only A or a quotient-normalized H does not supply the requested bound.
Because h is unchanged, the reviewed 046 bare-source global rate barrier
continues to apply when theta>1/2. Prime matching does not evade it by
renaming or conditioning the state.

3. Complete spatial energy and the truncated divisor tail

All basis breakpoints, including those of D_p, become integer points
under y=1/x. The whole state is constant almost everywhere on [k,k+1).
Consequently, with z_N(k)=(H_N(1/k),A_N(1/k)), the exact complete energy is
  P_N=sum_(k>=1) z_N(k)^T Q z_N(k)/(k(k+1)).                       (3)
It includes every k, not merely k<=N or k<=pN. Endpoint choices have no
effect on the integral. The displayed sum is exactly the L^2(dx)
potential; no fitted N normalization or change of source is hidden in it.

For clarity the complete floor/divisor target can also be written
explicitly. Define the finite harmonic scalar
  S_N=sum_(n<=N) mu(n)U_N(n)/n, U_N(1)=1.
Then for y>0
  e_N(1/y)=1_(y>=1)+S_N floor(y)
                         -sum_(n<=N) mu(n)U_N(n)floor(y/n).
For k>=1 the target's exact increment is
  h_N(1/k)-h_N(1/(k-1))
   =1_(k=1)+1_(k=p)+S_(pN)+S_N 1_(p|k)
       -sum_(n<=pN,n|k) mu(n)V(n)
       -sum_(n<=N,pn|k) mu(n)U_N(n),                              (4)
where the previous value at k=1 means the y<1 zero function. The harmonic
and unit terms in (4) are indispensable. For k>pN the source remains
truncated, but its eligible divisors still contribute. New k is not a
new unrestricted source coefficient, and finite source support does not
make this floor-energy tail zero.

There is an unconditional, explicit tail certificate available. Write
C_w=sum |w_j|. Since |U_N(n)|,|V(n)|<=C_w and |f_n|<=1,
  |h_N|<=2+C_w(p+1)N,
  |A_N|<=B_A=1+2 C_w N,
  |H_N|<=B_H=3+C_w(p+3)N.
For any PSD Q and any integer K>=0,
  tail_(k>K) P_N
    <=[|Q00|B_H^2+2|Q01|B_H B_A+|Q11|B_A^2]/(K+1).               (5)
The last factor is the exact telescoping sum of 1/(k(k+1)). Thus a finite
base proof can use an exact certified head plus (5), choosing K large
enough to pay its actual budget. The cost before division is quadratic in
N: (5) alone is not a uniform decaying logarithmic bound. Both base and
recurrence proofs must include this tail or a stronger genuine signed
tail certificate. In particular, k>N cannot be erased from an induction
argument merely because coefficient generation was finite.

4. Enumerated joint quadratic potentials and exact recovery

Let l=(1,1)^T. A rational symmetric matrix Q is admissible if
  Q-l l^T is PSD.                                                    (6)
For two dimensions this is checked by its two nonnegative diagonal
entries and nonnegative determinant, all in exact rational arithmetic.
It follows pointwise and after integration that
  ||h_N||^2<=P_N.                                                    (7)
Q itself is PSD as a consequence. No statistical or source-sign premise
is needed for recovery. An H-only Gram fails (6); the diagonal Gram I
also fails, because it would silently drop 2HA. The Gram 2I is a valid
but usually lossy majorant; its loss is explicit.

A concrete generated family is
  P_N=||h_N||^2+tau ||h_N-d A_N||^2, tau>=0,
  Q=[[1+tau,1+tau(1-d)],
     [1+tau(1-d),1+tau(1-d)^2]].                                    (8)
The full signed cross is retained before the square. Its difference from
l l^T is the exact rank-one PSD matrix tau(1,1-d)(1,1-d)^T. For tau>0 and
d!=0, det Q=tau d^2>0, so the entire two-state potential is coercive.
Tau=0 or d=0 is explicitly flagged as a target-only control. Such a
control helps compare experiments but is not a new source mechanism.
General rational majorants are also admitted; rational denominators and
exponents are bounded by explicit algebra/bit resource guards rather
than by a small handpicked analytic exponent list.

5. Exact all-integer induction with noninteger logarithmic exponents

Take M=2^(L0-1), L0>=2; positive rational target A_* and eta; fixed
rational 0<=rho<1 and C>=0. The required arithmetic premises are
  BASE: P_N<=A_*/L0^eta for EVERY N in [M,2M-1];
  STEP: P_(2N+r)<=rho P_N+C/L(N)^eta
                     for EVERY N>=M and BOTH r=0,1.                (9)
They concern the full energy (3), including the infinite floor tail.
The exact sufficient budget condition is
  rho+C/A_* <= (L0/(L0+1))^eta.                                   (10)

For every child, L(2N+r)=L(N)+1. Since (L/(L+1))^eta increases in L,
(10) and the inductive parent bound imply
  rho P_N+C/L^eta <= (rho A_*+C)/L^eta
                  <= A_*/(L+1)^eta.
Descending by floor(child/2) eventually reaches exactly the base interval
[M,2M-1], so (9)-(10) prove
  ||h_N||^2<=P_N<=A_*/L(N)^eta for EVERY integer N>=M.              (11)
No continuum interpolation, even-only shortcut or unproved odd-child
regularity is used. A recurrence proved only for N->2N would yield a
chain theorem, not the advertised all-integer theorem. A few probes such
as three base supports do not cover the complete base interval.

For eta=u/v>0, (10) is decided with positive rationals by
  (rho+C/A_*)^v <= (L0/(L0+1))^u.                                 (12)
This avoids floating-point fractional powers. Exponentiation is charged
and bit-preflighted before work. The condition is sufficient, not
necessary: failure returns INVALID_ARGUMENT for this particular induction
budget, without refuting the underlying arithmetic target or all other
possible recurrences. Different supplied A_*, base scale or proof schema
can be explored. Constants selected from finite data remain hypotheses
in (9); exact arithmetic checking of (10) does not make (9) true.

6. A constructive next arithmetic leaf: joint predictable transport work

The strongest useful extension found here is a reusable proof composition
that exposes signed covariance rather than separately bounding H and A.
Generate a fixed rational two-by-two predictor B. If exact matrix algebra
proves
  B^T Q B <= rho Q,                                                  (13)
put, at the same quotient index k,
  xi_(N,r)(k)=z_(2N+r)(k)-B z_N(k).
With the complete inner product from (3), the exact identity is
  P_child-rho P_parent
   =<z_parent,(B^T Q B-rho Q)z_parent>
       +2<B z_parent,Q xi>+<xi,Q xi>.                              (14)
Therefore the single source-specific signed-work lemma
  2<B z_parent,Q xi>+<xi,Q xi> <= C/L(N)^eta                         (15)
for all N, both parities and all spatial k implies STEP. The negative
covariance in (15) can compensate the innovation energy. It is not legal
to call the first term positive, to suppress it, or to assume its sign
from a finite witness. Rational B is enumerable, (13) is an exact PSD
test, and xi is the original full source difference including rounding,
cutoff births, divisor truncation and the tail. This is a concrete next
proof leaf for detector-generated source identities to attack.

There is also a sufficient positive route with a quantified loss. If
B^T Q B<=sigma Q and choose delta>0 with (1+delta)sigma<=rho, Young's
inequality gives
  P_child<=rho P_parent+(1+1/delta)||xi||_Q^2.
Thus a genuine uniform innovation bound
  ||xi||_Q^2<=C delta/(1+delta) L(N)^(-eta)
would prove STEP. This route loses negative signed work and is generally
stronger than (15), but is a valid proof schema with a distinct full
source-difference leaf. No such innovation bound is proved here. B=0
turns (15) into P_child<=C/L^eta and is explicitly an endpoint restatement.
Calling it a predictor would not add an arithmetic mechanism.

The current root pilot enumerates storage, not predictors; (13)-(15) are
an analytically proved proposed next production, NOT implemented predictor
search or an asserted source estimate. The executable certificate exposes
the identity and its open leaf so unresolved signed work can feed future
structural generation. A future all-scale proof admission must replay an
actual source-bound lemma or exact recurrence proof, not a paid boolean.

7. Counterexamples and proof-route gates

Universal linear algebra cannot supply the required source correlation.
For arbitrary Hilbert states let A_N=0 and H_N=v for a fixed nonzero v.
Then the completed-square storage is positive and constant, while a
decaying bound is false and a recurrence with rho<1, logarithmically
decaying forcing fails eventually. Even a positive quadratic majorant
and fixed source identity do not create a contraction. Taking unrelated
states at different N similarly defeats a predictor innovation estimate.
These examples refute an algebra-only proof route, not this actual source.

For the ACTUAL bare source and theta>1/2, the 046 reviewed theorem gives
eventual ||h_N||^2>=c beta_N. Since every admissible Q retains (7), an
argument claiming eta>1-theta is analytically refuted. Zero forcing C=0
with rho<1 is also refuted in this scope: (9) along a fixed dyadic chain
would give P_N<=rho^j times a finite base, faster than every logarithmic
power, contradicting the same lower bound. The executable rule therefore
returns REFUTED_ARGUMENT for those cases after validating the algebra.
For C=0 alone at eta<=1-theta, only the recurrence/storage argument is
refuted; target_status remains OPEN. A slower logarithmic target bound
does not require this impossible geometric recurrence. Only eta>1-theta
licenses refuting the advertised target rate itself.
This is a bound rejection, not a new RH assertion or a rejection of all
prime auxiliaries. For theta<=1/2, the 046 finite-tail rule supplies no
such gate; their arithmetic leaves remain OPEN.

There is no hidden workaround through tau,d or smaller apparent lambda.
The prime auxiliary changes the joint potential while (2) keeps h fixed.
The profile ceiling and separate rounding remain in its actual source at
every scale. Fixed-parameter primitive comparisons and finite cancellation
ratios cannot establish a uniform bound on (15), including tail sectors.

Exact request fields are source_recipe, rounding, prime,
original_forcing_b='-1', target={A,eta}, and argument={state,gram,recurrence,
base}. State is prime_split_H_A; recurrence binds rho,C,

complete bare drift AST and auxiliary=null. Unknown proof flags, changed

parities are UNSUPPORTED. Resource failures propagate.

OPEN means the algebraic implication is paid but at least BASE/RECURRENCE
is unpaid. INVALID_ARGUMENT identifies failure of the chosen majorant or
induction budget; it does not analytically refute the target. The scoped
046 cases are REFUTED_ARGUMENT. No supported return claims that an actual
uniform bound is proved. SOURCE_SPLIT is PAID_UNIVERSAL_ALGEBRA by the
direct definition (1)-(2), while finite compiler replay remains separate.

verify_argument strictly rebuilds the exact request-bound output and
rejects any changed dependency, interval, matrix or proof flag. This is
producer replay, explicitly not an independent arithmetic verifier or
proof-assistant formalization. Root independently checks source and matrix
rules and the finite numerical implementation. Verification of a numerical
source vector must not be conflated with verifying the global recurrence.
