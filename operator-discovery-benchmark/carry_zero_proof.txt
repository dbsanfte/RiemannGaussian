EXACT DISK CERTIFICATES FOR CARRY-FILTERED ZETA ROOT LIFTS

Fix q>=2, a prime ell, and rational t. Let G_e be the q-fold local product
of c_(1/q)(e) under the carry-weighted convolution. Every term has a
nonnegative root coefficient product times t^K, where
  K=v_ell(e!)-sum_{j=1}^q v_ell(e_j!), sum e_j=e.
The ordinary q-fold product of the root equals (1-x)^(-1). Therefore the
sum of the nonnegative coefficient products at EACH degree e is exactly 1.

If |t|<=1, this immediately proves |G_e|<=1 at all e, independently of q
and ell. The correction coefficient R_e=G_e-G_(e-1) has |R_e|<=2. Its
first-degree coefficient vanishes. Hence after degree m, on |x|<=r<1,
  sum_{e>m}|R_e|*r^e <= 2*r^(m+1)/(1-r).

For t=2 we use a different bound. In base-ell addition of q nonnegative
integers totalling e, let c_j be the carry into digit column j. The digit
addition equation gives c_(j+1)=floor((sum_i d_(i,j)+c_j)/ell), with c_0=0.
Induction gives 0<=c_j<=q-1. If e has L digits, every input has at most L
digits, and the carry beyond the final column must be zero: otherwise the
sum would have another digit. K=sum_j c_j by the digit-sum form of
Legendre's formula. Thus K<=(q-1)*L. Since ell>=2,
  2^K <=2^((q-1)*(1+log_2(e+1)))
        =2^(q-1)*(e+1)^(q-1).
The coefficient products average to 1, so the same bound holds for |G_e|.
Consequently |R_e|<=2^q*(e+1)^(q-1).

For k=q-1, the tail terms (e+1)^k*r^e beyond m have successive ratios at
most theta=r*((m+2)/(m+1))^k; this is a conservative bound on the actual
ratio r*((e+2)/(e+1))^k for e>=m+1. If theta<1, geometric summation gives
  sum_{e>m}|R_e|*r^e
    <=2^q*(m+2)^(q-1)*r^(m+1)/(1-theta).
All certificates use EXACT rational arithmetic for this inequality.

For each operator, compute its EXACT coefficients through degree m and
form the rational number
  S=sum_{e=2}^m |R_e|*r^e + the applicable proved tail bound.
If S<1, then |R(x)-1|<=S<1 on the whole CLOSED disk |x|<=r. This directly
proves R(x) cannot vanish there; no argument-principle approximation or
floating-point root finder is involved. A record with S>=1 is merely
unresolved by this method and is never counted as a zero counterexample.

The chosen r=71/100 satisfies r^2>1/2 by an exact rational comparison.
For every prime p and every s with Re(s)>1/2,
  |p^(-s)|<1/sqrt(p)<=1/sqrt(2)<r.
Thus a passing disk certificate proves every local correction factor is
nonzero throughout Re(s)>1/2. mixed_root_proofs.txt proves absolute compact
convergence of the correction product there, so the entire correction is
nonzero there as well. The corresponding root lift consequently has the
same zeros as zeta in this half-plane. This is a PROVED analytic law for
the passing parameter instances, not a proof of RH or historical novelty.

The coefficient computations rely on the exact recursive root and local
kernel implementations, which implement the definitions proved in the
earlier proof files. independently_verify checks the serialized rational
bound and theorem choice without trusting stored pass flags; it does not
constitute a separate implementation of the coefficient computations.
No certificate is described as machine-checked theorem-prover output.

PARAMETERIZED THEOREMS: NO FINITE-q EXTRAPOLATION REQUIRED

The same method yields TWO uniform statements covering infinitely many root
orders and parameter values. If e<ell then v_ell(e!)=0, hence every carry
weight in degree e equals 1. Therefore G_e=1 for 0<=e<ell, and R_e=0 for
1<=e<ell. These facts hold for EVERY integer q>=2.

If 0<=t<=1, all weights and root products are nonnegative and |G_e|<=1.
Consequently 0<=G_e<=1 and |R_e|<=1 at every e. On |x|<=r,
  |R(x)-1|<=r^ell/(1-r).
For r=71/100, r^5/(1-r)<1 by exact rational arithmetic. Thus for EVERY
integer q>=2, EVERY prime ell>=5 and EVERY real t in [0,1], the correction
Euler product is holomorphic AND NONZERO throughout Re(s)>1/2.

If -1<=t<=1, negative weights are allowed, but |G_e|<=1 still holds and
|R_e|<=2. Now |R(x)-1|<=2*r^ell/(1-r), and 2*r^7/(1-r)<1. Thus the same
holomorphy and nonvanishing statement holds for EVERY integer q>=2, EVERY
prime ell>=7 and EVERY real t in [-1,1].

These proofs use uniform coefficient bounds, not observations at q=2,...,7.
They certify the parameterized laws mathematically; only rational t have
exact rational implementations in the current grammar. The extension to
real t follows from the same finite-at-each-coefficient formulas and bounds.
Whether these specific consequences of known carry convolutions have prior
publication remains unresolved. They are not promoted to historically new
operators merely because the detector produced the identities and bounds.
