Resolved alternative: growing prime/composite folds
For a finite prime roster P_y, every squarefree n has a unique decomposition
n=d*m, d|product(P_y), gcd(m,product(P_y))=1. For a finitely supported real
coefficient f, define g_y(m)=sum_d mu(d) f(d*m). Then exactly
 sum_n mu(n)f(n)=sum_m mu(m)g_y(m).
Let L_y(m) count the occupied squarefree entries in this orbit. Cauchy gives
 sum_(L>0) g_y(m)^2/L_y(m) <= sum_n mu(n)^2 f(n)^2,
uniformly even when y grows. This is a useful coefficient-normalization lemma;
streaming squarefree n and removing selected prime factors avoids enumerating
the full divisor cube. It requires every smooth factor, not a degree-six
truncation. Parent and half must use the same roster, or an exact prime-entry
transport identity must be included.

This does not pay signed cancellation. Since sum_m L_y(m) equals the occupied
squarefree support size, weighted Cauchy only recovers the trivial O(u) price
when coefficient energy and support size are both O(u). A roster containing
all primes in support reduces the entire signed target to g_y(1); treating
that coefficient as bounded is circular unless a new arithmetic proof bounds it.

A separate disjoint-matching obstruction applies whenever f has a nonzero
macroscopic prime band inside (X/2,X], X=max physical support. A prime p there
cannot be the lower endpoint of p<->q*p inside support; its only downward
partner is 1. A matching can remove at most one such prime. Thus an absolute
terminal price retains Omega(u/log u) mass by PNT. G has outer band
(u/5,u/4], coefficient -1, with X=u/4; fixed prime folds preserve that outer
band. Allowing partners outside support with coefficient zero does not reduce
the resulting absolute difference price. This obstruction is specifically to
disjoint matching plus absolute terminal pricing, not every growing fold.

Conditional validity
For each leaf the proposed local inequality is S_a^2<=kappa*u^(q-1)*Q_a.
Exact reconstruction and Q_a<=3K*u*max_i|a_i|^2 give the existing conditional
cover price by triangle/Cauchy. This is legitimate also for symbolic a,b if
all coefficient/log enclosures and kappa rounding are directed outward.
The equality d=a+b is polynomial-exact and must not rely on interval overlap.
Intervals certify sign/nonzero/enclosure claims; zero-leading-mode claims
must be proven symbolically from the exact imported mode expression.
The arithmetic leaf inequalities are still conjectures. A finite selected
cover, or a log-annihilation certificate, is not a paid uniform bound.

The promotion a1=L,a9=1 exactly annihilates the proven first signature
(-1,L), since -a1+L*a9=0. For that same restricted signature the full target
has entries d1=d9=1 and value L-1. Its complement carries that entire
restricted contribution: (-1,L).b=L-1. This does not prove b has a nonzero
full leading mode: b contains other channels whose signatures have not been
derived. It proves that cancellation has moved into b and depends on those
other channels, rather than disappearing from the cover. Do not apply the
two-coordinate obstruction to b while silently ignoring its other channels.

Why the second obligation is real
Already the elementary smooth model Li(u)-2Li(u/2) cancels its u/log u term
but has expansion -log(2)*u/log^2 u+O(u/log^3 u). This follows directly by
expanding the logarithmic integral; it is an illustration, not an imported
second-order asymptotic for these arithmetic channels. Every fixed finite
inverse-log power remains larger than u^(q/2) for q<2. Endpoint logarithmic
moments, moving-cutoff offsets and parent/half shifts can create such terms.
The single equation -a1+L*a9=0 says nothing about their coefficients. Ordinary
PNT as imported in 079 supplies no quantified error suitable for this bound.
Deriving further channel signatures would be new mathematics and needs its
own hypotheses and proof; unsupported channels cannot be treated as zero.

Indefinite-cross-moment caution
An unrestricted quadratic majorant dd^T<=H forces H positive semidefinite.
An indefinite majorant needs independently proved restrictions on attainable
channel vectors, such as exact linear identities and a reduced-space PSD
certificate. Asymptotic cancellation and sampled relations are insufficient.
SEARCH080's explicit two-leaf cover avoids claiming such a missing theorem.

construction, noncircularity and conditional price obligations and produce
