Before any source responses are read, the generator enumerates:

drift. It is never refitted on validation or final observations. The code
also generates the exact next obligation, its uniform domain, domination,
conditional recurrence consequence and recovery cost. No model chooses
each expression, bound exponent, or fitted constant.

The grammar and admissible proof rules remain human-specified. This is not

Proof rules and the new discovery
---------------------------------
For all generated programs, the independent checker verifies an instance
of a universal absolute-prefix reference bound
  A(2X)-rho*A(X)<=C_ref*X*(1+log2 X)^2, rho>=0.
This follows from the complete arithmetic coefficient majorants. There are
10,766 INSTANCES of one rule, not10,766 independent new theorems. The power
X is too large to supply the required critical signed control.

The new critical-pole rule is core_pole_growth_obstruction.py. It has two
parts: a checked local analytic witness and a generic Mellin growth theorem.
The exact complete source Dirichlet series is
  D(s)=zeta(s+1/3)*[beta/zeta(s)^2
       -beta/(zeta(s)*zeta(s+2/3))-R_u/zeta(s+2/3)]-beta/zeta(s).
The unique term of order2 at a simple ordinary zero has nonzero coefficient
beta*zeta(rho+1/3)/zeta'(rho)^2. All other terms have smaller order.

Fresh rigorous enclosures use the rational disk centered at
  1/2+14.13472514173469379046*i, radius1/100000.
A256-bit Rouche criterion and a320-bit Newton contraction criterion each
certify one simple zero. Reflection symmetry places it on Re(s)=1/2.
Whole-rectangle evaluations bound zeta(s+1/3) away from zero by more than
0.2315, and zeta(s+2/3) by more than0.4087. All13 delay multipliers are also
bounded away from zero on the disk. These are geometric enclosures, not
sampled center values or estimates inferred from finite arithmetic data.

For log-Abel the exact identity M_G'(s)=D'(s)/s plus an entire finite head
preserves the leading pole order. The integer-cell discrepancy is bounded
and has a Mellin transform holomorphic for Re(s)>0. Dividing by log(n)
therefore does not remove the core's original double pole.

Generic theorem: a surviving leading order d at a critical-line point
forces any complete dyadic energy bound O((log X)^p) to satisfy p>=2d-2.
For this witness d=2, so p>=2. With gamma=0, the recurrence implies p=k
when rho<1 and p=k+1 when rho=1. Positive added storage cannot evade this
restriction because A>=E_G. The test concerns the actual filtered target;
extra logs in a conservative inverse bound do not rescue a false premise.

Every selected hypothesis had rho1/2,gamma0. Twelve had k0; the log-Abel
candidate had k1. Thus all13 are analytically false for EVERY fixed finite
C, despite their final finite passes. No failure scale is asserted. The
rule leaves rho1,k1 and rho<1,k>=2 open. Positive powers gamma>0 and filters
whose noncancellation is uncertified are not rejected by this rule.
This is fully consistent with RH and proves no bound at the log-squared
boundary. Fractional zeta powers require a separate branch theorem; the
integral-pole compiler explicitly refuses to assume one.
