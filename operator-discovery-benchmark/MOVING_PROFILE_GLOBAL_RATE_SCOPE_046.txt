GLOBAL MOVING-PROFILE RATE GUARD046
Independent mathematical admission; no source arrays or norm observations.

The source is ordinary Mobius, not a bounded-coefficient substitute. The
approximation is the pure floor target g_N=sum_(2<=n<=N)-mu(n)P_N(n)f_n,
f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)),0<x<=1. It is zero outside that domain.
P_N(n)=sum_j w_j n^(-r_j beta_N), with fixed positive rational rates, fixed
signed rational weights summing to1, and separate exact dyadic rounding.
beta_N=ceil(a L(N)^theta)/L(N),a>0,L(N)=N.bit_length().
Only bare profiles are admitted here; a cutoff taper, auxiliary, product,
N-dependent rates/weights, different source or unknown rounding is unsupported.

1. Exact finite Mellin identity and off-half-zero branch

Include the harmless virtual unit mu(1)P_N(1)=1 in
 A_N(s)=sum_(n<=N)mu(n)P_rounded,N(n)n^-s.
The finite floor identity, first obtained in an absolute-convergence
half-plane and then continued to Re(s)>0 by boundedness of the finite
floor vector, gives
 E_N(s)=[1-zeta(s)(A_N(s)-A_N(1))]/s.
No infinite series is asserted to converge at a zero or on the half-line.
For h_N=e_(qN)-b D_q e_N, D_q f(x)=f(qx) with zero extension,
 H_N(s)=[1-b q^-s-zeta(s){A_(qN)(s)-A_(qN)(1)
                     -b q^-s(A_N(s)-A_N(1))}]/s.
The harmonic compensation and both virtual units are retained exactly.

If RH is false, the functional equation supplies a nontrivial zero rho
with Re(rho)>1/2. The exact finite identity gives
 H_N(rho)=(1-b q^-rho)/rho for EVERY N. For fixed integer q>=2 and
rational b with b^2<q this is nonzero. Cauchy--Schwarz yields
 ||h_N||^2 >=(2Re(rho)-1)|1-bq^-rho|^2/|rho|^2>0.
This already excludes any forcing bound tending to0, on all integers or
any unbounded geometric chain. Equivalently a vanishing forcing premise
implies RH. This is a conditional implication, not a proof of RH.

2. Imported RH-dependent estimate and complete finite tails

Imported primary theorem: K.Soundararajan, Partial sums of the Mobius
function, Theorem1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.0723 . It assumes RH and
bounds |M(x)| by a fixed constant times
 sqrt(x)*exp(sqrt(log x)*(log log x)^14) for sufficiently large x.
This estimate is a reviewed supplied theorem, not detector-discovered.

Suppose RH for this branch. For T=log K, write
 E(T)/T=T^-1/2(log T)^14. This ratio decreases once log T>28.
For theta>1/2,
 r_min beta_K/[T^-1/2(log T)^14] -> infinity.
Consequently, for every sufficiently large K and EVERY t>=K,
 |M(t)|<=C t^(1/2+r_min beta_K/2).
The same fixed C applies; no constants depending on moving beta are
introduced by this majorization. For z with Re(z)=1/2+r beta_K,
r>=r_min, and bounded imaginary part, exact partial summation gives
 sum_(n>K)mu(n)n^-z=-M(K)K^-z+z integral_K^infinity M(t)t^-z-1 dt,
 and absolute value <=C[1+2|z|/(r_min beta_K)]
                         *exp[-r_min beta_K log K/2]=o(1).
The beta^-1 loss is explicit. This bound is uniform over the entire
infinite t-tail and bounded local frequency window, separately for K=N
and K=qN. Convergence here is justified to the RIGHT of1/2 under RH,
then identified with1/zeta(z) by analytic continuation from Re(z)>1.
It is never reciprocal-series convergence at the zero itself.

3. Harmonic, rounding and child drift terms

For the exact unrounded mixture,
 A_N(1)=sum_j w_j/zeta(1+r_j beta_N)+finite tail.
Absolute convergence and zeta(1+kappa)>=1/kappa give
 |A_N(1)|<=beta_N sum_j |w_j|r_j
          +sum_j |w_j|N^(-r_j beta_N)/(r_j beta_N)
          +C_w N^-4(1+log N)=O(beta_N), C_w=sum|w_j|.
The final term is rounding. Each separately rounded exponential has
error<=2^-p, p=4ceil(log2N), and its signed mixture retains C_w.
On Re(s)=1/2 the finite Dirichlet-polynomial rounding error is
 <=2 C_w N^-7/2, uniformly in imaginary part. No positivity of rounded
signed profiles is used, and no cross or unit is removed.

For fixed q, L(qN)-L(N) is bounded. The exact ceiling contributes relative
O(L^-theta), so xi_N=beta_(qN)/beta_N->1. This ratio is retained in the
child formula until the uniform limit; equality of the two finite drifts
is not assumed.

4. Shrinking-frequency lower bound and strict rate scope

Fix a nontrivial zero rho=1/2+i gamma under RH; existence of nontrivial
zeros and their finite positive integer multiplicity are standard analytic
zeta facts. Let d be its multiplicity and s=rho+i beta_N v. For each fixed
bounded real v and each positive rate r,
 zeta(s)/zeta(s+r beta_K)->(iv)^d/(r xi_N+iv)^d,
 uniformly on compact v, because the positive real shifts keep every
denominator away from the local zero. The complete finite-tail and
harmonic estimates above permit this substitution in H_N(s), giving
 H_N(rho+i beta_N v)->(1-b q^-rho)/rho
             *[1-(iv)^d sum_j w_j/(r_j+iv)^d].
The limit is continuous and nonzero at v=0. It follows that H_N is bounded
away from0 on a fixed sufficiently small v-interval for all large N.
Mellin Plancherel on Re(s)=1/2 therefore gives ||h_N||^2>=c beta_N.
Only this shrinking interval is used; no global critical-line tail norm
estimate is supplied or assumed.

Combining the RH and RH-false branches gives an eventual positive lower
bound c beta_N, with c and threshold EXISTENTIAL and unspecified. Since
beta_N~a L^(-(1-theta)), a fixed finite A cannot satisfy
 ||h_N||^2<=A L^-eta along any unbounded admitted domain when eta>1-theta.
Equality and slower rates remain OPEN. theta<=1/2 is not licensed by this
Mertens-tail provider, regardless of local moment cancellation order.
The global cap uses unit normalization sumw=1, not a supplied zero
multiplicity or an assumed successful cancellation order.

This refutes the specified uniform rate premise only. It does not refute
finite norm certificates, convergence of a different family, the floor
representation, the original signed core, or RH. Constants and thresholds
are not extracted from finite measurements. No taper or auxiliary is
silently stripped in order to apply this guard.

A useful exact nonzero gate, independent of the unknown zero multiplicity,
is delta_v=r_min/(2 C_w). Since C_w>=1 and rates>=r_min,
 |1-F_d(v)|<=C_w*(|v|/r_min)^d<=1/2
for every integer d>=1 and |v|<=delta_v. This is a formal boundary-layer
window only. It does not compute the actual zero's scale factor, an effective
uniform-convergence threshold, or a numerical lower c for the source norm.
