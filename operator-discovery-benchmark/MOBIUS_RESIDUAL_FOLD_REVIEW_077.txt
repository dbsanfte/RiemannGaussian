1. Interval/token representation and exact dilation
-------------------------------------------------
Let c(n),n>=1, be a finite coefficient function, represented by sorted
disjoint integer intervals(l,r] carrying rational token polynomials.
At a fixed parent, each token has a declared real value (for example a
count-power coefficient), and polynomial evaluation gives c(n). All
count variables, source binding, domains and token dependencies must
be exported. Equal formal token expressions should cancel symbolically
before interval evaluation; irrational values must never be replaced by
floating approximations in a certificate.

For integer prime p and integer endpoints l,r>=0,

 1[l<p m<=r]=1[floor(l/p)<m<=floor(r/p)].

Thus c(pm) is obtained by dilating interval endpoints with integer floor
division, retaining coefficient tokens unchanged. Floor collisions can
create empty intervals, which contribute zero. Sum overlapping original
and dilated intervals by an exact endpoint sweep; adjacent intervals
with identical canonical polynomial coefficients can merge. Sign changes
and token dependence are retained. A source prefix event at endpoint0
is zero, whereas a resulting interval containing m=1 retains mu(1)=1.

2. Prime folds with the excluded domain separate
-----------------------------------------------
For one prime p, squareful p-adic rows have mu(n)=0; rows n=pm with
p not dividing m have mu(pm)=-mu(m). Consequently

 sum_n c(n)mu(n)=sum_{m:p does not divide m}[c(m)-c(pm)]mu(m).

After a finite DISTINCT prime set P, put Q=productP and define a
coefficient function on ALL positive integers

 f_P(m)=sum_{d|Q}mu(d)c(dm).

The residual identity is R=sum_{m:gcd(m,Q)=1}f_P(m)mu(m).
For a NEW prime p not in P, transform the unrestricted function by
f_(P union{p})(m)=f_P(m)-f_P(pm), and update the excluded domain to
gcd(m,Qp)=1. Since p is coprime to Q, multiplying by p preserves the
old gcd gate on admissible m; the same splitting proof applies. Fold
order does not change the formal result. Repeating a prime should be
rejected or canonicalized away, not added as another subset factor.

The coefficient function and excluded domain must remain distinct.
Do not zero f_P at excluded m and then claim that the new transformed
interval function is the original unrestricted subset formula. Although
some such values never enter the final sum, preserving the full function
is what makes conservation, independent verification and the schedule
formula transparent. No prime-square term is silently omitted from an
arbitrary-vector identity: the omission is justified specifically by mu.

Primality of schedule entries, distinctness, all integer endpoints and
the ORIGINAL residual binding must be independently checked. Count-power
normalization is part of c and is fixed at this parent throughout folding.
If the parent changes, its count tokens must update; count jumps are not
annihilated by prime folding. Different source columns and direct terms
share ONE mu sequence and must be combined before interpreting costs.

3. Exact coprime interval counts and coefficient norm enclosures
--------------------------------------------------------------
For0<=l<=r and squarefree Q,

 N_Q(l,r)=#{l<m<=r:gcd(m,Q)=1}
        =sum_{d|Q}mu(d)[floor(r/d)-floor(l/d)].

This follows by expanding product_{p|Q}(1-1[p|m]) and counting each
divisibility interval. It includes both boundary conventions exactly.
For Q=1 the count is r-l. Every N_Q is a nonnegative integer; a
negative computed count indicates an arithmetic/certificate error.
An inclusion-exclusion implementation has2^|P| divisor terms unless
it employs another independently verified counting construction.

For disjoint coefficient intervals with evaluated constants k_r,
the literal coefficient-l1 price is

 T(u)=sum_r |k_r(u)| N_Q(l_r(u),r_r(u)).

The universal triangle certificate is |R(u)|<=T(u), since |mu(m)|<=1.
It counts coprime squareful m too, conservatively. A certified interval
[a,b] for k has absolute LOWER bound0 if it crosses zero, otherwise
min(|a|,|b|), and UPPER boundmax(|a|,|b|). Multiplying by exact counts
and summing gives certified T_lower<=T<=T_upper. To refute a proposed
price T^2<=C u^q use T_lower^2 divided by the UPPER enclosure of u^q;
to verify it use T_upper^2 divided by the LOWER enclosure. Threshold
straddling is unresolved rounding. A lower-price failure refutes that
coefficient-l1 certificate, NOT the signed R bound, which could benefit
from cancellations that the price discarded.

Token polynomial norm/interval arithmetic must use bounded exact rational
operations, including reciprocal signs and shared monomial cancellation.
The number of segments, divisor vocabulary, polynomial terms and rational
bit sizes need prospective resource gates. An interval representation
avoids source-sized dense coefficient expansion, but repeated folds can
still grow its segment/token vocabulary substantially.

4. Generic outer-support witness
--------------------------------
Suppose c has support within1..E. For ANY finite prime schedule,
if m>E/2 then dm>E for every integer d>=2. Therefore

 f_P(m)=c(m) on the whole outer shell(E/2,E].

This is independent of schedule order, signs, selected primes or lower
coefficients. It provides a generic certificate interface: an interval
(l,r] with l>=floor(E/2), exact coefficient k and r<=E produces the
lower witness |k|N_Q(l,r). More generally split every constant outer-
shell segment and sum these nonnegative lower witnesses. Zero coefficient
segments contribute zero. The detector can discover such witnesses from
support geometry, without a hardcoded anticipated source outcome.

For the exact current residual c_u=s_u-sum_j v_j s_j, every child
j<=u//2, and every child prefix endpoint is<=u//8. The parent source
has maximal horizon E=u//4 and coefficient -1 on

 (l,r]=(u//5,u//4].

All child coefficients vanish there. Since l>=floor(E/2) for integer
u>=1 (trivial small empty intervals included), the generic witness gives

 f_P,u(m)=-1 on this top band,
 T(u)>=N_Q(u//5,u//4).

Count-normalized coefficients, lower prime/direct interactions and their
signs cannot change this identity. Folding might reduce other coefficient
segments, but it cannot remove this outer-band coefficient on admissible
m. This is NOT an arithmetic lower bound |R|>=N_Q: the signed mu sum
on this band can cancel internally and against the lower bands.

5. Fixed-schedule all-scale obstruction, with exact period price
--------------------------------------------------------------
For fixed Q define phi(Q)=#{1<=a<=Q:gcd(a,Q)=1} and
delta=phi(Q)/Q=product_{p|Q}(1-1/p)>0. Both are elementary exact
integers/rationals; Q=1 gives phi=delta=1. Any Q consecutive integers
contain exactly phi(Q) coprime integers, because their residues moduloQ
are complete. An interval of integer length L therefore satisfies

 N_Q(l,r)>=phi(Q)floor(L/Q)>=delta L-phi(Q), L=r-l.

This lower bound uses complete periods plus a nonnegative remaining
partial period; it includes the cost of endpoints without assuming the
interval is aligned to a period. Inclusion-exclusion alternatively gives
|N_Q(l,r)-delta L|<2^|P|, since each floor-difference error has absolute
value<1. Either bound can be independently certified; use whichever is
sharper for a declared input rather than dropping its error term.

Here L=floor(u/4)-floor(u/5)>=u/20-1, hence

 T(u)>=delta u/20-delta-phi(Q).

For every integer u>=40(delta+phi(Q))/delta,
T(u)>=delta u/40. Consequently for fixed rationalq<2,

 T(u)^2/u^q >=(delta^2/1600)u^(2-q) -> infinity.

Thus NO fixed finite prime schedule admits a finite constant C with
the uniform coefficient-l1 proof price T(u)^2<=C u^q for all largeu.
The conclusion includes q near1 and every fixedq<2. It does not depend
on finite fitted scores, observed mu values or actual residual failures.
It refutes this proof rule's uniform price, not the possibility of a
signed residual power bound or RH. Selecting among a FIXED finite
catalogue of schedules cannot evade the theorem: take the minimum
positive delta and maximum threshold over the finite catalogue.

6. Growing schedules, maximal cancellation and remaining mechanism
-----------------------------------------------------------------
For a parent-dependent P(u), the exact outer-band witness still holds
with its actual Q(u). The fixed-Q asymptotic cannot be substituted without
retaining delta(u), period/divisor error and schedule complexity. A
necessary condition for the l1 certificate is

 N_(Q(u))(u//5,u//4)<=sqrt(C)u^(q/2).

When a uniform density estimate is available with controlled error,
this forces its density contribution to shrink suitably. A huge period
error can instead make that particular lower estimate vacuous; it does
not prove the actual count small. Exact coprime counts or another paid
sieve bound must establish the price. Merely appending primes until a
finite price improves is not a uniform compression theorem.

At the extreme, folding all primes<=E leaves only the coprime support
m=1, with coefficient f_P(1)=sum_{d<=E}mu(d)c(d)=R itself. The l1
price is then |R|. This is exact but simply embeds the original signed
target into its folded coefficient, with enormous prime/product/symbolic
cost. It shows why arbitrary maximal folding is no automatic analytic
shortcut and why schedule/compression resources and proof leaves must
be explicit. Folding l1 is nonincreasing, but coefficient quadratic
energy need not be: c(1)=1,c(p)=-1 folds to coefficient2, changing
energy2 to4. Any energy shortcut must pay collision/cross terms.

Recommended executable uniform-certificate search:

  coefficients, coprime-count formulas and rational affine lower envelopes
  A u-D with their declared parent domain. Prove the associated q<2
  uniform-price obstruction automatically when A>0.
* Keep surviving symbolic fold conservation as a representation of the

  R^2=sum_m f(m)^2 mu(m)^2
      +2sum_{m<n}f(m)f(n)mu(m)mu(n), restricted to gcd(mn,Q)=1.
  A useful new leaf prices this COMPLETE kernel uniformly, or a rational

* If proposing recursive/growing fold compression, specify its exact

  A uniform compression recurrence must be proved from already paid

These interfaces let the detector prove a genuine obstruction to its

7. Stronger detector-generated rational kernel barrier
-----------------------------------------------------
A general parent source can export a fixed rational prefix kernel
G_kernel(u)=sum_d w_d M(u//d), where d are positive integers and
w_d rational. Put h=max_{w_d!=0}(1/d). Its normalized coefficient
function is sum_d w_d 1[x<=1/d]; exact breakpoints1/d determine
constant coefficients on rational open-left/closed-right bands. Combine
duplicate divisors and zero weights first. Suppose an exact scan finds
a band(alpha,beta] with constant c!=0 satisfying

 0<=alpha<beta<=h,
 alpha>=h/Dmin and alpha>=h/2,

and every operator child index is<=u//Dmin, Dmin>=2. For integer n in
(floor(alpha*u),floor(beta*u)], parent kernel coefficient is precisely c,
including a breakpoint at beta. Every child endpoint is<=floor(h*u/Dmin)
<=floor(alpha*u), so no child modifies this band. For every folded
d>=2, dn>2alpha*u>=h*u and the complete residual coefficient at dn
vanishes. Therefore its folded coefficient remains c on the band for
ANY finite prime schedule. Empty integer bands at smallu cause no error.

The generic checker must verify the actual kernel coefficients and band,
all barrier inequalities and the source's declared child-rank bound;
it must not trust an anticipated coefficient or source-specific name.
The normalized breakpoint scan has no floating power or floor rounding.

For rational alpha,beta and every integer divisor d,
floor(floor(alpha*u)/d)=floor(alpha*u/d), and likewise for beta.
Consequently inclusion-exclusion directly gives

 N_Q(floor(alpha*u),floor(beta*u))
 =sum_{d|Q}mu(d)[floor(beta*u/d)-floor(alpha*u/d)]
 >=delta(beta-alpha)u-2^|P|.

Each individual floor-difference error relative to(beta-alpha)u/d has
absolute value<1, since it is the difference of two fractional parts.
The2^|P| error bound is independent of denominator sizes, period alignment
and Q itself; it needs no EXTRA outer floor error. This is often stronger
than the period bound in section5 and proves the requested interface.

Thus the interval-l1 certificate has the affine lower envelope

 T(u)>=A u-E,
 A=|c|delta(beta-alpha)>0, E=|c|2^|P|,
 T(u)>=A u/2 whenever u>=2E/A.

For fixedP and fixedq<2, T(u)^2/u^q>=A^2 u^(2-q)/4 is unbounded.
All numbers except u^q are exact rationals/integers. The detector can
export its discovered kernel band and these constants as a independently
checkable UNIFORM_PRICE_OBSTRUCTION, pruning a fixed-schedule l1 proof
branch before fitting any large C. The disposition must explicitly say
coefficient_l1_certificate_refuted=true and arithmetic_residual_refuted=false.
Finite transform checks can validate conservation and leftover exposure,
but do not establish or refute the signed arithmetic theorem.

After this obstruction, a viable next proof-search object retains the
signed coprime remainder rather than fitting its inevitably linear l1
price. An explicit leaf may be a complete signed pair-kernel estimate,
or a conserved anchor plus difference energy with a proved uniform
price; section6 describes the required cross terms and dependencies.
An exact fold schedule is an arithmetic representation, not a free
cancellation theorem. This generic obstruction permits the detector
to recognize that distinction autonomously without an expected-result
special case or a claim of RH progress.
