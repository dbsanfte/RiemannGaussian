1. Why this representation is RH-relevant

Define f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)) on0<x<=1, zero beyond1. Its value on
cell k, (1/(k+1),1/k], is(k mod n)/n. For pure finite programs g=sum c_n f_n,
e=1-g has Mellin response1/rho at every ordinary zeta zero rho. Therefore
 ||e||² >= (2Re(rho)-1)/|rho|² when Re(rho)>1/2.
An actual sequence of these norms tending0 excludes off-half zeros. This is
known Nyman-Beurling-related territory, not a newly discovered criterion.
The proof uses finite bounded functions and analytic continuation, not
unproved ordinary convergence of a reciprocal-zeta series.
Primary context: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202141

For D_q g(x)=g(qx), D_q f_n=f_(qn)-f_q/n and ||D_q||=1/sqrt(q). Thus
 e_(qN)=bD_q e_N+h_N,
 h_N=1-g_(qN)-b I_q+b D_qg_N, I_q=1_(x<=1/q).
Indicators are admitted ONLY for forcing norms, not freely for RH-bearing
targets. Their Mellin response does not vanish at zeta zeros. Exact identities
retain all signed cross terms; their validity does not pay the forcing bound.

5. Detector-executable coefficient recovery and analytic rejection

The followup solves a rational cell system for weights alpha_k isolating a
coefficient c_n. It independently verifies EVERY finite basis column and
sum k*alpha_k=0, which annihilates every higher-support column at once.
This is not a finite-support assumption or a free-vector countermodel.
The resulting identity is
 c_n+mu(n)-sum d_q*sum_(k>=q)alpha_k = sum alpha_k e(cell k).
Weighted Cauchy gives the whole-norm lower bound by the square of that exact
left side divided by sum alpha_k²*k*(k+1).

Examples generated by the solver:
 c2-1=-2e1+e2, hence E>=(c2-1)²/14;
 c3-1=-e1-e2+e3, hence E>=(c3-1)²/20;
 c4=e1-e2-e3+e4, hence E>=c4²/40.
Consequently convergence forces c_n(N)->-mu(n) at EVERY fixed n. This is
necessary, not sufficient. Exact dyadic rounding errors tend0, the fixed
tapers tend1, and each unanchored coefficient tends-a(n)n^-beta. Rational
power inequalities certify intervals for irrational limits. Anchored c2 is
left unresolved when its limit would need an unproved full growing sum.
The direct forcing witness includes the indicator and parent-dilation terms.

6. Feed the obstruction into the next grammar

Do not repeat this norm study or optimize its constants. Do not blacklist
Liouville or mixed arithmetic generally: fixed coefficient limits cause the
failure. Future productions should preserve c_n(N)->-mu(n) while allowing
nontrivial signed interaction at moving indices. Proposed, NOT implemented:
 - auxiliary a(n)*(W1(n/N)-W2(n/N)) with both weights tending1 at fixed n;
 - scale-vanishing amplitude N^-delta times a signed arithmetic correction;
 - exact head repair through n<=floor(N^theta), with a growing cutoff;
 - scale-dependent rational exponents tending0, rather than fixed beta>0.
Each only repairs a necessary local condition. It does not bound the growing
tail or the joint forcing. Search still needs an independently proved global
upper estimate, with signed cross terms doing necessary work. Prefer new
constructions/arguments to smaller finite constants. All final supports in
this run are now development evidence; any new run needs a new frozen split.
