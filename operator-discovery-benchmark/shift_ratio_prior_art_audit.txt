The older ThaiScience domain now serves a landing redirect via direct
HTTP; its indexed PDF text was useful for the initial comparison, but
the live journal PDF is the preferred source. Its SHA256 is recorded in
literature/shift-ratio/reference_access.json.

Page 200 defines f_Dinv*(f log n), proves its convolution-to-addition
law, triangular inverse, and prime-power support for multiplicative input.
It credits Rearick's 1968 work. Our v=1 reduces, after division by log n,
to this known arithmetic logarithm. The triangular-inverse method is
therefore established. For 0<v<1 our operator has nonzero coefficients
on mixed squarefree pq and fails the convolution/root homomorphism;
it is not the arithmetic logarithm or its root operator from that paper.
This distinguishes a formula, not all possible known compositions.

3. KNOWN HIGHER LOGARITHMS AND THE ACTUAL CLOSURE

Generalized integer von Mangoldt functions mu*(log n)^j are established;
the prior fractional audit records Pratt's 2020 paper, section 2:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40687-019-0199-8

For v>1/2, the full arithmetic family is the convergent geometric
combination (1/v) sum_(j>=1)(-(1-v)/v)^(j-1) R_j(f).
The independent checker verifies fourteen changed integer-coefficient

compound unequal-shift definition must not be called a new primitive.
Its rational resummation, global bound and RH-equivalent regularizer still
require a direct priority comparison; this audit has not completed one.

The scalar kernel family also belongs to established complete-Bernstein
functional calculus. Jacob and Schilling, "Function Spaces as Dirichlet

https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/35466
With lambda=v/(1-v), normalization at t=1 is precisely our

mixtures are a known scalar function class. Applying them to this
inverse-weighted arithmetic kernel and comparing the exact compound
estimates still requires a separate priority review. No novelty is
inferred from selecting an unfamiliar member of that known class.
