RATIONAL POWER PRODUCTIONS AND FRACTIONAL SIGNED-MOMENT TRANSFER

1. The exponent a=p/d is an exact reduced rational, d>0. A finite run may
bound p and d for cost; the semantics impose no denominator catalogue.

For x>=0 and a>0, x^a is the nonnegative algebraic value y with y^d=x^p.
For x<0, the ordinary real production requires odd d and its sign is (-1)^p.
The signed production S_a(x)=sgn(x)|x|^a is distinct. For a,b>0 it satisfies
S_a(S_b(x))=S_(ab)(x), S_(1/a)(S_a(x))=x, and |S_a(x)|^k=|x|^(ak).
These are universal elementary identities, not statistical discoveries.
The absolute production loses sign. Nonpositive exponents exclude zero;
0^0 is rejected. Numerical balls crossing zero are bounded by endpoints
and zero where appropriate; branch-ambiguous inputs are rejected.

2. On C\(-infinity,0], the declared principal complex power is
exp(a Log z). It is holomorphic there. Fractional power composition across
different branch domains is NOT a universal law. At a zero of multiplicity
m a local fractional power has monodromy exp(2*pi*i*a*m). No finite branch
evaluation proves a global ordinary-zeta continuation or excludes a zero.

3. Normalized Dirichlet powers already existed in exact_arithmetic.py.
The new language exposes them as a distinct typed production for any Q
exponent. If f(1)=1 and h=f-delta_1, define f^a=sum_k binomial(a,k) h^{*k}.
At index n the terms vanish for 2^k>n. The ordinary binomial formal-series
identities imply (f^a)^b=f^(ab) and f^a*f^b=f^(a+b) coefficientwise for all
n. No finite prefix is used to infer convergence. The independent audit
uses a formal logarithm and exponential with its own convolution routine.

For f(n)=1 the coefficients are those of zeta(s)^a on Re(s)>1, with the
Euler-product logarithm branch. At prime powers they are (a)_k/k!; their
absolute values are <= (|a|)_k/k!, so the absolute series is bounded by
zeta(sigma)^|a| for sigma>1. For 0<u<sigma-1 the omitted absolute tail
above N is <=N^(-u)*zeta(sigma-u)^|a|. This gives a genuine all-index
coefficient/convergence theorem, but NOT RH-strength signed cancellation.

4. Let R=P(S_b) M_tilde, as in the independently checked signed Mellin
bridge. It is real, |R(t)|<=L*t, and has Lipschitz constant at most L.
On an integer unit interval its endpoints are rational u,v. For every
real q>0 (not only even integers), set G_q(x)=sgn(x)|x|^(q+1).
Then integral_0^1 |u+t(v-u)|^q dt equals |u|^q if u=v, otherwise
[G_q(v)-G_q(u)]/[(q+1)(v-u)]. The formula also handles sign crossings.
All intervals in a declared window are included. Native real balls
enclose these algebraic values; fractional sums are NOT exact rationals.

5. Suppose A_q(X)<=C X^beta at every dyadic X>=1. For q>=1, Holder
(or the elementary L1 endpoint) gives
integral_X^(2X)|R(t)|t^(-sigma-1)dt
 <= C^(1/q) X^((beta-1)/q-sigma).
Summing dyadic blocks proves absolute Mellin convergence for
sigma>max((beta-1)/q,0), with bound
C^(1/q)/(1-2^((beta-1)/q-sigma)).
The proof therefore accepts fractional q>=1 without an integer assumption.

6. For 0<q<1, Holder with exponent q is unavailable. At a maximum of
|R| in [X,2X] let H be its height. H<=2LX. One side has available length
at least X/2. The interval of length H/(4L) on that side stays inside
the block and has |R|>=H/2. Thus
A_q(X)>=H^(q+1)/(2^(q+2)L),
and H<=K X^(beta/(q+1)), K=(2^(q+2)LC)^(1/(q+1)).
Then integral_X^(2X)|R| <=H^(1-q) A_q(X)
 <= C K^(1-q) X^(2beta/(q+1)).
Its Mellin transform converges for sigma>max(2beta/(q+1)-1,0),
with bound C K^(1-q)/(1-2^(2beta/(q+1)-1-sigma)).
This is a sufficient transfer with an explicit loss, not a claim that
the q>=1 formula continues unchanged below one.

7. If the ordinary-zeta multiplier F=P(b^(-s)) is nonzero throughout
Re(s)>=1/2 and the resulting eta<=1/2, a PROVED uniform moment premise
would exclude ordinary zeros right of 1/2 via the existing meromorphic
Mellin identity, hence RH by symmetry. If an already certified actual
critical zero has F!=0 and eta<1/2, that uniform premise is impossible.
Neither the root's smaller amplitude nor finite window survival supplies
the missing signed arithmetic premise. A moment of S_a(R) of order k
must be audited at effective order a*k of R. Nonlinear sums of roots
have no automatic linear Mellin bridge and remain unsupported leads.

Proof dependencies: elementary real/complex algebra, formal binomial
series in the locally nilpotent Dirichlet algebra, Holder for q>=1,
the Lipschitz estimate above, and signed_mellin_proof.txt plus the
independently checked all-height multiplier certificate. Historical
priority does not enter acceptance. RH remains unproved.
