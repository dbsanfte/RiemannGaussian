066 independent sparse prefix checker: admitted scope and proof obligations

Checker: check_mobius_sparse_prefix_066.py
Synthetic suite: test_check_mobius_sparse_prefix_066.py
API: verify_sparse(cert, meter=None)
Certificate: mobius-sparse-prefix-inverse-v1

This checker admits an exact raw short inverse of the original ordinary-Mobius
prefix source, at one finite N,y,H and the two ratios r=p/q and r=1. It does not
import the discovery producer, scientific arrays, frozen studies, numerical LP
outputs, or an earlier scientific certificate. Repaired core_inverse objects
and free-sign or conditioned-prime objects do not belong to its exact schema.
The SAME 063 raw source admission must separately bind this prefix to the
scientific source. That external binding is explicitly not proved here.

Source and decomposition

For positive integer d, the source is
 f_y(d)=1[d<=y]*(W(N/(p*d))-r*W(N/(q*d))),
 W(x)=floor(4*x)-3*floor(2*x)+2*floor(x).
There are six original floor atoms (b,v,a), with b=p,q and
(v,a)=(4,1),(2,-3),(1,2). Write Z=floor(v*N/b) and lambda=a for
b=p, lambda=-r*a for b=q. The checker independently enumerates
 i=1,...,floor(Z/(y+1)), T=floor(Z/i)>y,
and retains lambda*floor(Z/d), adding
 -lambda*1[d<=T]+lambda*1[d<=y].
These additions exactly remove lambda*floor(Z/d) for d>y and vanish for
d<=y. All six channels are signed-coalesced; zero atoms are omitted. No
head correction, ratio endpoint, threshold boundary, or unit is discarded.
The original six atoms can share horizons, so the recorded coalesced floor
list need not contain six entries. Its horizons may be inside the short H.

Raw actual-Mobius inverse

Ordinary mu is independently built by an Eratosthenes sign sieve through
D=floor(4*N/p). mu(1)=1; prime values are -1; squareful values are zero.
For an indicator atom with threshold T, its inverse at row j is
 M(floor(T/j))-M(floor(T/(j+1))). For a full floor atom of horizon Z,
mu*One=delta_1 contracts its inverse to delta_(j=Z). Thus each raw row is
 c_j=sum_floor lambda*1[Z=j]
     +sum_step a_T*sum_(T//(j+1)<n<=T//j) mu(n).
The checker independently constructs signed integer-interval events and
sweeps them into maximal disjoint nonzero intervals, checking every recorded
endpoint and coefficient. It then evaluates the joined intervals using its
own ordinary Mertens prefix table.

A second, independent computation checks each row directly against
 sum_n mu(n)*(f_y(j*n)-f_y((j+1)*n)).
The p/q channel arrays are evaluated with exact integer floor divisions,
and the sum is evaluated without the certificate's floor/step/interval
representation. This replay is O(D log H), not an O(D^2) source-forward
reconstruction. Tiny synthetic tests additionally invert all D rows forward
and check sum_j c_j*floor(j/d)=f_y(d) at every d.

Every row j=1,...,H must be supplied. There is no repaired row substitution.
Every tail J=1,...,H must be supplied, including the head and terminal row.
The finite data support all real r between the endpoint ratios by affine
source/inverse interpolation and convexity of absolute value; no all-N
variation growth rate follows from finite endpoint certificates.

Joined and uniform tail rules

The row interval cost is abs(direct_j)+sum_I abs(a_I)*length(I), after signed
coalescing. It bounds abs(c_j) because abs(mu(n))<=1. For each J<=H, the
checker verifies exactly the joined cost, actual coefficient variation, and
coarse telescoping cost
 sum_(J<=Z<=H) abs(lambda_Z)
 +sum_T abs(a_T)*(floor(T/J)-floor(T/(H+1))).
The step inverse windows are disjoint in n as j varies, so their sizes
telescope. Signed joining cannot increase the separate-atom triangle cost.
The coarse cost is at most floor_l1+M1/J, where
 M1=sum_T abs(a_T)*T
is computed AFTER signed atom coalescing. It is not the signed moment
sum_T a_T*T. The checker verifies these inequalities for every recorded J.

The schema's all-scale first-moment rule is independently reviewed:
 M1 <= 12*(1/p+r/q)*N*(2+log(max(1,D/(y+1)))).
For an original atom the pre-coalescing moment is at most
abs(lambda)*Z*(H_K+1), K=floor(Z/(y+1)); K=0 contributes zero. For K>=1,
H_K<=1+log(K), Z<=v*N/b, and K<=D/(y+1). Sum abs(a)*v=12 across the three
atoms in a channel. Coalescing only decreases the moment. This is an exact
reviewed schema theorem for every eligible integer scale and prefix; its
logarithmic right side is not fabricated as an exact rational certificate.

The uniform tail rule is proved for the represented short tail. It does not
pay the unresolved head uniformly, prove a uniform full-variation rate, bound
the whole signed scientific target, or prove RH. Return flags preserve all
those limits. The new arithmetic interval queries may reach n=D, including
the unit and squareful integers; the old sqrt-sized arithmetic query radius
is not preserved. This enlarged range is explicit, not treated as free work.

Resource and input gates

N<=65536, H<=min(D,4096), and 1<=y<=D; p<q are actual registered primes.
The source parameter list permits alpha,gamma,primes,p,q and optional tau,
with exact fractional gates. Integers reject booleans. Rationals must be
canonical strings and every checked/intermediate rational is capped at 4096
bits. Unknown fields, zero/duplicate/unsorted atoms, nonmaximal/overlapping
intervals, missing rows/tails, and changed exact aggregates are rejected.

The checker uses the established Meter but additionally enforces its own
500,000,000-unit cap even when the caller supplies a charge-only meter.
Prospective storage is conservatively reserved before allocations; the
reported upper bound counts transient reservations cumulatively, with a
512 MiB cap and 900,000-entry cap. Sorting events, integer source divisions,
sieve operations, interval construction, raw source sums, rational sizes,
and multiplication bit work receive charges. A caller's stricter Meter cap
or deadline remains effective. The tests record wall time, charged units,
and actual peak RSS; only new synthetic tests are run.
