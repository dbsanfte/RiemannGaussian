1. Exact masks, grid, boundaries and the inference certificate
-------------------------------------------------------------
Fix distinct actual primes p<q in a fixed registered-prime set M. Let
R=product_(ell in M)ell, k=|M|>=2, and

 W(v)=floor(4v)-3floor(2v)+2floor(v).

W has period1 and takes +1 when frac(v) is in[1/4,1/2), -1 on
[1/2,3/4), and0 elsewhere. All intervals are left-closed/right-open.
Put u=W(v/p), z=W(v/q). The profiles and kernel signs are:
 P:u!=0,z=0, sigma=u;
 Q:u=0,z!=0, sigma=-z;
 D:u=z!=0, sigma=u;
 S:u=-z!=0, sigma=u.
If u=z=0 there is no token. Define f_i(v)=sigma*1_(profile=i).

In coordinate x=4v, each f_i(x/4) is a right-continuous step function
constant on[j,j+1), j integer, with period L=4pq. For v>=0 its value
at v=0 is zero. For v<p/4 every profile is zero; this supplies the exact
source horizon d<=floor(4N/p). No inclusive endpoint may be changed to
an open one in a machine reconstruction.

For proposed integer weights w_i>0 let F(v)=sum_i w_i f_i(v). A sufficient
exact certificate consists of rational/integer c_b for b dividing L with

 F(v)=sum_(b|L)c_b floor(4v/b).                            (1)

The following finite verification proves(1) on ALL real v>=0, not merely
on the sampled grid. Set a_j=F(j/4), using its periodic extension, and
check for j=1,...,L

 a_j-a_(j-1)=sum_(b|L,b|j)c_b,       a_0=0.               (2)

The RHS is the exact jump of the floor expression at x=j. Summing the
rows gives sum_b (L/b)c_b=0. Hence the floor expression has zero period
drift and is itself periodic. Its value at x=0 is zero. Equal jumps and
the equal origin value imply equality on each cell and every boundary.
Jump equality without an origin check leaves an unproved additive
constant. Period checking without all cell jumps leaves unproved cells.

There is a generic necessary-and-sufficient algebraic test, useful for
an independent miner. The jump sequence h_j=a_j-a_(j-1) must depend only
on gcd(j,L). For each b|L reconstruct

 c_b=sum_(e|b)mu(b/e)h_e.                                 (3)

Divisor inversion proves sum_(b|g)c_b=h_g for every g|L. Thus the
gcd-class condition, plus the origin convention, reconstructs(1).
Conversely every divisor-floor representation satisfies that condition.
This characterizes a rational linear subspace of weights. Its intersection
with the strict positive cone is a genuine finite feasibility question;
positive weights are not guaranteed merely by the existence of some
signed conservation. Rational positive solutions can be cleared to
integer weights. For integer weights the c_b in(3) are automatically
integers. Common scaling is an alias, so primitive weight normalization
is appropriate.

A nonzero bounded floor combination must have both positive and negative
c_b: sum c_b/b=0 excludes a nonzero all-nonnegative coefficient list.
Positive copy weights therefore do NOT imply positive smooth-count
coefficients. Their signs must remain in the exact conservation.

2. Ordinary-Mobius convolution gives a paid smooth-count invariant
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Let mu_M(d)=mu(d)*1_(gcd(d,R)=1), using the FULL actual Mobius source.
For every integer n>=1,

 sum_(d|n)mu_M(d)=1_(every prime factor of n belongs to M).

The Euler factors prove this directly: a prime outside M contributes
1-1=0; an M-prime contributes only the unit factor. All prime valuations
and the integer1 are included. Define Psi_M(Y)=0 for Y<1, and for Y>=1
let it count the M-smooth positive integers <=Y, INCLUDING1. Then

 sum_(d>=1)mu_M(d)floor(Y/d)=Psi_M(Y).                     (4)

All sums are finite. No infinite Mobius series, RH premise or probabilistic
sign model is used. For any real N>0,(1) and(4) give

 T_w(N):=sum_i w_i C_i(N)
       =sum_(d>=1)mu_M(d)F(N/d)
       =sum_(b|L)c_b Psi_M(4N/b),                         (5)

where C_i(N)=sum_d mu_M(d)f_i(N/d) is the actual signed token count.
The cofactor unit d=1 remains. It can correspond to actual source primes
p or q; it is not the separate inclusive-source unit v_x(1).

HORIZON GUARD: the complete original F sum vanishes for d>4N/p, but an
individual floor term may persist up to4N/b. To obtain a Psi term one
must use its FULL individual floor horizon. Equivalently prove that all
outside-original-horizon terms cancel jointly by(1). Truncating each
floor at4N/p and calling it Psi is generally invalid. This matters when
some admitted b<p. Formula(5) avoids that error by extending the complete
sum before splitting it. Ordinary real/integer boundary coincidences are
handled by the inclusive floor convention.

For a fixed registered set, unique prime factorization gives the elementary
uniform bound

 Psi_M(Y)<=product_(ell in M)(1+floor(log Y/log ell))
          <=(1+log_2 Y)^k,       Y>=1.                    (6)

Thus, for N>=2 and C_c=sum_b|c_b|,

 |T_w(N)|<=C_c*(1+log_2(4N))^k.                          (7)

This is a paid source-specific polylogarithmic invariant. It concerns the
weighted SIGNED copy count, not the number of squarefree terms, an absolute
coefficient mass, or the unweighted original counts individually. If M,
w or c depend on N without further bounds, the constants/degree in(7)
are not fixed; the all-scale rule requires those objects to be fixed.

3. Copy splitting retains the whole signed middle and pays leftovers
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For 0<alpha<1, gamma>0, 0<tau<1 set eps=min(tau,N^-gamma) and

 Phi_N(a)=1/Gamma(1-alpha)
          integral_eps^tau G_M(t)a^-t t^(-alpha-1)dt,
 G_M(t)=product_(ell in M)(1-ell^-t).

The P,Q,D,S magnitudes are Phi(pd), Phi(qd), Phi(pd)-Phi(qd),
Phi(pd)+Phi(qd). They are nonnegative, and positive when the band is
nonempty. The actual signs are mu(d)*sigma. Replace a profile-i token
with w_i identical copies, each having magnitude m_i/w_i and the SAME
actual sign. Their signed sum is exactly the original token. Consequently
the FULL joined middle, both signs, unequal range exits and all source
coefficients are retained.

A global greedy scan must exhaust opposite signs; then it leaves exactly
|T_w(N)| copies. No magnitude ordering assumption is required for this
count statement. A policy that restricts pairs by groups must either
perform a complete final global scan or price its additional leftovers;
the global count formula does not apply to an unexhausted grouped list.

The maximum magnitude bound proposed by the parent is safe uniformly.
For t>0, 1-ell^-t<=t log ell<=t ell, so G_M(t)<=R t^k.
All arguments pd,qd>=2 give a^-t<=1, and S costs at most twice one
factor. Elementary integration by parts gives
alpha*I_alpha=Gamma(1-alpha)=integral_0^infinity e^-u u^-alpha du.
The latter is >=integral_0^1 e^-u du=1-e^-1>1/2. Therefore

 max_(i,d)m_i <=4R/(k-alpha) <=6R/(k-alpha).               (8)

Here tau^(k-alpha)<=1 was used. The argument even works for k=1,
though the licensed two-prime source has k>=2. If eps=tau every
magnitude is exactly zero and every norm/cost statement holds trivially.
There is no fixed-N asymptotic approximation or dyadic rounding in(8).

Let w_min=min_i w_i>=1. The final residual charge satisfies

 B_left(N)<=6R*C_c/[(k-alpha)w_min]
                  *(1+log_2(4N))^k.                     (9)

The slightly sharper4R constant is optional;6R needs no correction.
Only exact positive integer weights license the stated identical-copy
counting. Rational weights require clearing denominators and rebinding the
copy list. Common rescaling cancels between T_w and the maximum copy
magnitude; it cannot improve the theorem by choosing enormous weights.

4. Exact paired-cost frontier and the limits of conservation
------------------------------------------------------------
For a complete copied plan define

 E_copy(N)=sum_(paired copies)|m_i/w_i-m_j/w_j|.

Triangle on every pair and explicit pricing of every remaining copy prove

 |J_N|<=B_copy(N)=E_copy(N)+B_left(N).                     (10)

Thus the new sufficient source-bound leaf is E_copy=O(sqrt(N)polylog N)
or its cofinal-power version. Under the058 paid endpoint scope, that leaf
would restore the complete conditional RH-sensitive bound. The source-free
conservation pays(9), not this leaf. No fixed finite C, finite training pass
or signed count identity supplies(10)'s paired-work growth.

This is a real operational improvement over059's two unpaid terms: the
unknown cross-profile residual charge becomes uniformly polylogarithmic.
It is not a new identity beyond the established mu_M*One law. The new
content is that a detector-inferred mask conservation converts that law
into a count invariant for a nonlinear complete matching construction.
The source coefficients/kernel themselves were not attenuated or replaced.
This is a new use of a proved elementary law, not a claim of prior-art
novelty or a new prime-distribution theorem.

The exact optimal copied cost, including zero dummy endpoints, is

 B_copy^*=integral_0^infinity
   |sum_i w_i sum_(d of profile i)epsilon_d
                   1_(m_i(d)/w_i>y)|dy.                  (11)

Conservation controls the threshold count at y=0 only. It does not
control the remaining threshold discrepancies. For arbitrary signed data,
positive magnitudes1,3 and negative magnitudes2,2 have zero signed sum
and zero signed count, but optimal pair cost2. Multiplying all magnitudes
by a factor makes the cost arbitrarily large while both invariants remain
zero. This is an algebraic counterexample to free paired-cost recovery,
not a counterfeit replacing the actual Mobius source.

Likewise(5) pays no truncated prefix for free. A prefix in d has the exact
formula

 sum_(d<=y)mu_M(d)F(N/d)
 =sum_b c_b[Psi_M(4N/b)
       -sum_(y<d<=4N/b)mu_M(d)floor(4N/(bd))].             (12)

The boundary divisor tails on the right remain joined and unpaid. Using
only the smooth count in(12) would discard the very signed forcing
needed to control copy paths or magnitude-threshold variation.

An exact residual-count conservation also cannot reverse a bad first pass.
If a same-profile prime-ratio stage pairs all copies belonging to an edge
(a,ra), its w_i equal-copy costs sum to |m_i(a)-m_i(ra)|, the original
edge charge. The059 positive-density obstruction survives that lift.
The new rule must not rehabilitate the rejected0390 first-pass mechanism
merely because its copied leftovers are now paid.

5. Cost comparison and reusable proof-route guards
--------------------------------------------------
Splitting preserves the complete absolute mass, and every legal copied
plan still has B_copy<=sum_original m_i. It can never exceed that absolute
control. It does NOT automatically improve an old signed pairing cost.
For example original tokens +1 and -1 can pair at cost0. Weights1 and2
make copies +1,-1/2,-1/2; every optimal complete binary plan costs1.
Unequal profile weights can therefore worsen even the optimal cost.
This does not contradict unchanged signed sum or the leftover theorem.

Uniform replication by the SAME integer multiplies every copy count and
divides every magnitude. Its exact optimal cost is unchanged, by a change
of variable in(11). The same holds for a contiguous-copy greedy scan under
uniform further replication. This alias should not be ranked as a new
mechanism. Different profile weights genuinely alter admissible binary
transport; old profile-dominance results must be rebound to its new kernels.

The next detector can correctly certify the following without scientific
tables: complete step-mask and divisor-floor equality; strict integer
copy weights; exact source/rounding/kernel binding; smooth-count identity;
global opposite-count exhaustion; maximum magnitude and polylog leftover.
It must leave paired-copy growth as an explicit source-bound obligation.
An auxiliary proof may seek exact joint interval/divisor-tail cancellations
in(12), a one-direction threshold-flow certificate for(11), or larger
signed groups whose internal sums are taken before absolute values. None
is admitted merely by a finite matching score.

The rule's scope is the whole actual Mobius source with fixed M,p,q and
all integer N>=2; its mask/convolution identities also hold for real N>0.
No independent-prime assumption, random-sign model, assumed RH-strength
Mertens estimate or source-dependent fitted normalization is used. General
gamma>0,tau<1 suffice for leftover control but not for endpoint restoration:
the complete058 bridge additionally needs gamma*(k-alpha)>=1/2 and
tau>=1/2, or separately paid endpoint bounds. These scopes must be explicit
in any final argument certificate.

Disposition: mathematical review complete; edits stopped. No conservation
vector was selected or enumerated here. Polylog leftover control is proved
conditionally on an exact positive mask certificate. Paired-copy signed
arithmetic growth, full middle control and RH remain OPEN.
