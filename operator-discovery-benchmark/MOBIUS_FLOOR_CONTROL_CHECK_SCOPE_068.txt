INDEPENDENT FULL-FLOOR AUXILIARY AUDIT 068

The checker imports neither the 068 producer nor an optimizer, dense matrix
package, historical checker, source compiler, or prime-transfer learner.
It evaluates the original joined truncated W source pointwise at each
required rational endpoint. Coalesced tail jumps are reconstructed as
g(T)-g(T+1), including the final jump at the true arithmetic radius.
Row endpoints are independently reconstructed from actual pointwise kappa
jumps, so cancellations of row indicator coefficients are removed before
the grammar. Endpoint1 is retained as source data but creates no floor
horizon. For each declared rational theta in[0,1], depth=floor(N^theta)
is replayed exactly. The complete distinct union of floor(A/k)>=2 for
1<=k<=depth is formed using quotient blocks before any scores are read.
When it exceeds the declared limit, sorted equally spaced integer ranks
are selected, including both extremes; limit1 selects the maximum. The
truncation flag reports the full-set comparison. The complete-quotient
flag compares realized coverage against all quotients through A//2.
Identical realized dictionaries share exactly one model in first-use order.
Every plan, model, row, endpoint, selection, and schema field is checked.
Unsigned squarefree support is reconstructed by removing multiples of
every integer square. Ordinary Mobius signs are reconstructed separately
for the post-selection actual coefficient audit; they are not inputs to
the auxiliary grammar or to the unsigned upper-bound proof.

For every raw head row let den be the reduced endpoint denominator,
k_n=den*(g(jn)-g((j+1)n)), and d=den*d_j. All these are integers.
The coefficient numerators x_X, unit witness eps, and squarefree-coordinate
witness z_n use the fixed common rational grid G=2^20. The declared box
is |x_X/G|<=C, C=4*den*max(1,number of admitted horizons). All eps/G
and z_n/G are in [-1,1]. The complete ordinary identity
sum_n mu(n)*floor(X/n)=1, together with mu(1)=1, gives the center

 a=d+k_1+sum_X(x_X/G)*(1-X).

The independently replayed upper cost is

 (|a|+sum_(squarefree n>=2)|k_n-sum_X(x_X/G)*floor(X/n)|)/den.

The checker reconstructs the floor-column sum using quotient-block
interval start/end events and prefix accumulation, not the producer's
dense dot product. Every squarefree coordinate through the full row
radius is retained, including zero source coordinates within that radius.

The boxed dual lower witness is independently replayed as

 [ (eps/G)*(d+k_1)+sum_(squarefree n>=2)(z_n/G)*k_n
   -C*sum_X|(eps/G)*(1-X)
               -sum_(squarefree n>=2)(z_n/G)*floor(X/n)| ]/den.

Weighted floor sums for the penalty use a distinct quotient-block
partition and witness prefix sums. This formula bounds the optimum in
the declared coefficient box even when equality dual constraints are
inexact. Negative lower bounds are valid and retained. All source,
center, residual, primal, lower, and gap arithmetic is exact integer or
rational arithmetic. Solver objective tolerances do not certify a cost.
Solver status, iteration, and message fields are bounded proposal metadata;
their accuracy is outside the mathematical certificate. The checker's
learner-access flag verifies the declared scope, not software isolation.

These certificates establish finite bounds for the same actual raw head.
Independent admission/source binding to the preceding source experiment
is an external obligation. No all-scale estimate, full signed target,
uniform sparse auxiliary rule, RH conclusion, or fresh holdout follows.
A complete current-scale indicator/quotient span may encode the exact
target itself; its flag is a scope warning rather than an admission of
an independent uniform proof.
The fixed rounding grid is also finite: no all-scale accumulation bound
for rounding errors is supplied by the checker.

Development uses fresh tiny synthetic sources, scalar floor evaluation,
trial-factorization signs, direct full-source inversion, and meaningful
certificate mutations. All new invocations append their complete result
to mobius_floor_control_checker_068_development.json. No historical suite,
scientific result, frozen snapshot, hash, or optimizer is rerun by these
tests.

Finite resource gates include N<=65536, H<=4096, 512 admitted horizons,
100000 bits for rational-power intermediates, 4096 bits per rational,
300 billion independently charged work units, a 7200 second deadline,
four million accounted entries, and 512 MiB prospective checker storage.
The experiment's larger aggregate process budget is a separate runner
