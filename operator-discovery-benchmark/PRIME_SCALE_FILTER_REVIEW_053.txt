1. Fixed exact source and filter word
=====================================
Fix rational 0<alpha<1. On every ordinary integer define

 K_alpha(1)=0,
 K_alpha(n)=-sum_{d|n} mu(d)*(log(d))^alpha, n>=2,
 A(x)=sum_{2<=n<=x} K_alpha(n), every real x>=1.

This is the b=0, a=1, sign=-1 branch of 051. Its logarithms and powers are
unrounded, and 0^alpha=0. It has 0<K_alpha(n)<=(log(n))^alpha for n>=2,
retaining every prime-power valuation. With

 I_alpha=int_0^infinity (1-exp(-u))*u^(-alpha-1) du>0,

the absolutely convergent initial Dirichlet identity is

 F(s)=sum_{n>=2} K_alpha(n)n^-s
     =I_alpha^-1 int_0^infinity
            [zeta(s)/zeta(s+t)-1]*t^(-alpha-1) dt, Re(s)>1.       (1)

Fix an integer q>=2 (the proposed bank uses {2,3,5}), and a NONZERO finite
polynomial

 P(z)=sum_{j=0}^m c_j z^j

with fixed rational coefficients, trimmed to its actual degree. No c_j,
degree, q, or alpha may vary with the observed scale. Define

 T_P(x)=sum_{j=0}^m c_j A(q^j x).                                (2)

The proposed grammar requires P(q)=0. P(1)=0 is optional. The zero polynomial
must be excluded: its identically zero statistic has no RH implication.

2. Exact finite arithmetic expression and every boundary
========================================================
Counting multiples after reversing a FINITE divisor sum gives, for every
positive integer N,

 T_P(N)=-sum_{d<=q^m N} mu(d)*(log(d))^alpha
                        *sum_{j=0}^m c_j floor(q^j N/d).         (3)

No prime-power, source complement, or unit is suppressed. The d=1 contribution
is zero because log(1)^alpha=0; it may be retained explicitly. For a real x,
the same formula holds with upper limit floor(q^m x). Formula (3) is an exact
finite-source evaluator, not an appeal to an infinite mu series.

Write C_d(N)=sum_j c_j floor(q^j N/d). Since P(q)=sum_j c_j q^j=0,

 C_d(N)=-sum_j c_j {q^j N/d},
 T_P(N)=sum_{d<=q^m N} mu(d)*(log(d))^alpha
                          *sum_j c_j {q^j N/d}.                 (4)

For d>q^m N all floors vanish, and the fractional-part sum in (4) also vanishes
by P(q)=0. Thus the actual support in (3)-(4) is complete and finite. Applying
absolute values termwise gives only a linear-size bound; the required signed
mu/fractional-part correlation remains unpaid. A finite floor cancellation
inside C_d is a useful primitive, not already the missing square-root estimate.

3. Integer-to-real transfer with explicit jump cost
==================================================
Let x>=1 and N=floor(x). Each interval (q^j N,q^j x] contains at most q^j
integers. The 051 pointwise bound therefore gives

 |T_P(x)-T_P(N)|
 <=sum_j |c_j| q^j *[log(q^j(N+1))]^alpha
 <=B_P*[log(q^m(N+1))]^alpha,
 B_P=sum_j |c_j| q^j.                                           (5)

This uses q integral and the original full source, including all jumps. It
costs only O_(P,q,alpha)((1+log(x))^alpha), and hence can be absorbed into
x^(1/2+epsilon) for every epsilon>0. Consequently the precise OPEN premise

 for every epsilon>0 there exists finite C_(epsilon,P,q,alpha)
 such that |T_P(N)|<=C_(epsilon,P,q,alpha)*N^(1/2+epsilon)
 for every integer N>=N0                                        (6)

with fixed finite N0 implies the same all-real estimate. Any finite omitted
head enlarges the constant only. A single geometric subsequence does NOT
supply (5) across its proportional gaps and is not licensed by this bridge.
Nor are infinitely many selected scales or finite holdouts sufficient for (6).

The definitive 053 request uses the stronger explicit OPEN inequality

 |T_P(N)|/alpha <= C*sqrt(N)*(1+log(N))^eta
 for EVERY integer N>=2, with fixed rational C>0 and eta>=0.     (6a)

For any epsilon>0 a fixed logarithmic power is bounded by a constant times
N^epsilon, so (6a) implies (6). Division by the fixed positive alpha is a
declared normalization and does not change the qualitative implication. It
must remain in the finite comparisons and source request; it cannot vary by
scale. Neither C nor eta is paid merely by being supplied in a conjecture.

4. Complete Mellin identity and finite-head entire terms
=======================================================
In Re(s)>1, partial summation gives

 int_1^infinity A(x)*x^(-s-1) dx=F(s)/s.

Substitution u=q^j x in each term of (2) consequently yields

 int_1^infinity T_P(x)*x^(-s-1) dx
   =P(q^s)*F(s)/s-E_P(s),
 E_P(s)=sum_j c_j q^(js)*int_1^(q^j) A(u)*u^(-s-1) du.           (7)

EVERY finite head is retained. It is entire because its integration interval
is compact in the positive real axis. More explicitly, writing Q=q^j,

 q^(js)*int_1^Q A(u)*u^(-s-1) du
   =sum_{2<=n<=Q} K_alpha(n)*[(Q/n)^s-1]/s.                     (8)

The apparent denominator at s=0 in EACH term of (8) is removable, with value
log(Q/n). For j=0 the integral is zero; n=Q likewise contributes zero. This
assertion concerns the finite head; it does not claim that F(s)/s itself is
holomorphic at zero. The RH argument concerns Re(s)>1/2, so s=0 is immaterial.

By (5)-(6), the left side of (7) is holomorphic on Re(s)>1/2. To justify this
on any compact subset, choose epsilon smaller than its real-part gap from
1/2. The same domination with logarithmic factors permits complex derivatives.
Adding the ENTIRE E_P proves the conditional continuation

 G_P(s)=P(q^s)*F(s)/s is holomorphic on Re(s)>1/2.                (9)

All remaining implications below follow from this precise holomorphy. They
do not invoke a Tauberian mean theorem or assume a centered-error converse.

5. Real pole and what P(q)=0 does
================================
The reviewed b=0 density is the constant

 kappa=I_alpha^-1 int_0^infinity
                    t^(-alpha-1)/zeta(1+t) dt>0,

and its EXACT primitive is kappa*(x-1). It follows directly from (1) that
delta*F(1+delta) tends to kappa for positive delta tending to zero.
In fact F(s)-kappa/(s-1) is holomorphic in a neighborhood of 1. To see this
without a Tauberian claim, put f(z)=1/zeta(z) and g0(z)=zeta(z)-1/(z-1).
The integrand after subtracting the pole is

 [f(s+t)-f(1+t)]/(s-1)+g0(s)*f(s+t)-1.                         (10)

The divided difference is removable at s=1. A small disk about 1 and the ray
to its right contain no denominator zero: use compact nonvanishing on bounded
t intervals, the removable zero of f at 1, and Euler-product separation at
large t. The complete bracket in (10) vanishes at t=0, so is uniformly O(t)
there; the tail is bounded. This pays holomorphy after integration for alpha<1.

Thus P(q)=0 cancels the genuine real pole in G_P. Indeed if P(q)!=0, the
simple real-pole divergence contradicts (9) at 1, so no premise (6) could
hold. The requirement is a real-main-term compatibility condition, not a
fitted value of kappa.

For R(x)=A(x)-kappa*(x-1), a purely exact substitution gives

 T_P(x)=sum_j c_j R(q^j x)-kappa*P(1), when P(q)=0.              (11)

P(1)=0 removes this constant as well. When P(1)!=0 the constant is retained
and harmless for the all-epsilon square-root estimate. No asymptotic for R
is assumed in (11), and no numerical kappa is needed to evaluate (3).

6. Initially proposed annulus guard: sufficient, not necessary
=============================================================
If ordinary RH is false, reflection supplies a zero rho=beta+i gamma with
1/2<beta<1 and gamma!=0. Choose a rightmost zero at that FIXED height, as in
052. Then

 sqrt(q)<|q^rho|=q^beta<q.

Therefore the suggested condition that P have no zero in the open annulus
sqrt(q)<|z|<q guarantees P(q^rho)!=0, and the fractional singularity of F
directly contradicts (9). Annulus boundary roots are immaterial to this
particular off-line argument. This condition is valid as a sufficient simple
diagnostic, but rejecting every annulus-root word is unnecessarily restrictive
for the conditional RH implication. The next section removes that condition.

7. Stronger bridge: finite polynomial zeros cannot erase a fractional branch
===========================================================================
The frozen 052 proof gives, for ANY multiplicity d>=1 of the chosen zero,
along the permitted horizontal approach s=rho+delta, delta positive real,

 F(rho+delta)=C_d*delta^(-alpha)+O(1),
 C_d=I_alpha^-1 int_0^infinity
                 [(1+u)^(-d)-1]*u^(-alpha-1) du<0.              (12)

The fixed-height rightmost choice ensures the denominator zeta(rho+t) is
nonzero for every t>0. Compact nonvanishing and the Euler tail give the needed
path tubes. It does not assert a zero-free half-plane at other heights.
Factoring zeta(rho+z)=z^d*g(z) gives the local ratio error O(t), so the
integrated remainder in (12) is O(1). This includes repeated zeros and the
bounded, rather than decaying, b=0 large-t numerator. These are exactly the
conditions established in the 052 review.

Since P is a NONZERO finite polynomial, P(q^s)/s is analytic at nonreal rho
and is not identically zero there. Its order k is finite, with expansion

 P(q^(rho+delta))/(rho+delta)=a_k*delta^k+O(delta^(k+1)), a_k!=0.

The map q^s has derivative log(q)*q^s!=0, so k is the ordinary finite
multiplicity of a possible P root at q^rho. Multiplication of (12) gives

 G_P(rho+delta)=a_k*C_d*delta^(k-alpha)+O(delta^k).               (13)

The cross error delta^(k+1-alpha) is absorbed in O(delta^k) because alpha<1.
If k=0, (13) diverges, contradicting holomorphy. If k>=1, then

 k-1 < k-alpha < k.

A function holomorphic at rho with the growth in (13) must have Taylor
coefficients of degrees 0,...,k-1 all zero: otherwise its first nonzero such
coefficient would dominate delta^(k-alpha) along the positive real approach.
It would therefore be O(delta^k), contradicting the nonzero leading term in
(13). Being finite or even tending to zero does NOT make the fractional-power
expression holomorphic.

It follows that EVERY nonzero finite P, not merely annulus-free P, preserves
the conditional RH sensitivity. Under the grammar's necessary real-pole
condition P(q)=0, premise (6) implies RH. No simple-zero hypothesis, actual
zero computation, prime-independence assumption, or critical-line mu-series
convergence enters the proof. The strict noninteger range 0<alpha<1 is
essential to this argument; at an integer endpoint a finite polynomial can
erase an ordinary integer-order pole, so this branch proof cannot be reused.

8. What the bridge does not recover or prove
============================================
There is no proved converse RH=>the specific estimate (6), no bound for its
constants, and no uniformity over changing alpha, q, or P. There is also no
automatic quantitative inverse norm or recovery of R(x) from T_P(x).

The distinction between arithmetic fractional sensitivity and generic filter
invertibility matters. If P has an annulus root z0, an abstract function
x^(lambda+i tau), with lambda=log|z0|/log q and
tau=(arg(z0)+2*pi*l)/log q, is an EXACT null mode of (2), with lambda>1/2.
It could obstruct recovery for arbitrary source functions. The ordinary
fractional source cannot hide its zero singularity in that way, by (13).
Thus annulus exclusion can remain useful for a separate generic inverse
argument, but it must not be asserted necessary for this conditional bridge.

The filter also annihilates linear counterfeit means. For the artificial
source K(n)=1 for n>=2, A(N)=N-1 at integer scales, and P(q)=0 gives
T_P(N)=-P(1), identically zero if P(1)=0. Such a control may satisfy (6), yet
is not ordinary mu and has no connection to (1) or (12). Exact arithmetic
source binding is indispensable; a successful filter-control amplitude test
cannot certify ordinary RH. Likewise multiplication of P by a small nonzero
scalar changes finite amplitudes without changing the implication. Numerical
comparison needs its declared normalization and cannot manufacture a proof.
The actual 053 source grammar prevents that scalar freedom by taking primitive
integer coefficients with positive leading coefficient, rather than allowing
an arbitrary rescaling. Its divide_by_alpha normalization is an additional
explicit comparison guard, not a source-bound proof.

At fixed n, the limit alpha down to zero of the b=0 kernel is 1 for n>=2;
this follows from the Boolean sum with its d=1 power equal to zero. Thus a
small-alpha finite-word behavior can resemble the linear counterfeit above.
That fixed-n limit is not a uniform all-scale limit and does not pay (6).
