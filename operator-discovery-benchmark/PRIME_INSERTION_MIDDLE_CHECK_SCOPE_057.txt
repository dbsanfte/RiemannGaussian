INDEPENDENT 057 FINITE SOURCE AND CONDITIONAL TRANSPORT ADMISSION

This checker creates new finite source-specific enclosures. It reads no saved
source arrays, previous candidate values, archived VerifiedSource objects or
Boolean verification flags as arithmetic authority. It imports only the frozen
052 charge-only Meter, canonical rational parser and exact typed comparison.
It does not import the 057 producer. The synthetic tests import that producer
solely to construct fixtures, and count that work separately.

The public interfaces are prepare_context(meter=None),
verify_middle_interval(request,{lower,upper},meter=None,context=None), and
verify_parent_recovery(request,certificate,meter=None). verify_recovery aliases
the last interface. A context is a live checker-created object with private
functional caches. Persisted dictionaries and user-supplied source tables are
not contexts and cannot establish arithmetic authority.

FINITE OBJECT AND COMPLETE SOURCE

For integer N>=2, R the product of distinct checked primes, and 0<alpha<1,
the source is the ordinary unrounded kernel E_R K(n)=K(R*n). It is not the
masked Boolean difference D_S K(n). For every n>=2,

 K(R*n) = I_alpha^(-1) integral_0^infinity
              product_(p|R*n)(1-p^(-t)) t^(-alpha-1) dt.

The q=2 polynomial [2,-3,1] has cumulative weights zero for n<=N,
-2 for N<n<=2N, and +1 for 2N<n<=4N. The n=1 contribution is zero.
The integral object admitted here is

 J_band(N) = 1/Gamma(1-alpha) integral_a^b h(N,t)t^(-alpha-1)dt,
 h(N,t) = sum_(N<n<=4N) w(n,N)
                         product_(p|R*n)(1-p^(-t)).

There is no extra alpha in this normalization: alpha*I_alpha=Gamma(1-alpha).
The source compiler trial-factors each n, unions its radical with the registered
primes, then expands every subset of that full radical with its signed Mobius
coefficient. Repeated powers are retained through radical invariance and shared
registered primes are unioned once; sharing a prime does not delete n. This
differs from the producer's p-free cofactor/sieve compiler and matches it by
the exact finite identity

 h(N,t)=product_(p|R)(1-p^(-t))
    sum_(d<=4N,gcd(d,R)=1) mu(d) W(N/d)d^(-t),
 W(y)=floor(4y)-3floor(2y)+2floor(y).

All equal exponential arguments are merged before integration. The exact
coefficient sum and the d=1 coefficient must both be zero. Every original
product has at least |S| simple zeros at t=0; even for empty S it has at least
one, because n>=2. Therefore derivatives of orders below max(1,|S|) vanish
exactly, independently of cancellation-prone numerical evaluation.

MOVING BAND AND INDEPENDENT NUMERICS

epsilon=min(1/2,N^(-gamma)), gamma a positive canonical rational, is decided
exactly at the half boundary: N^(gamma numerator)<=2^(gamma denominator).
Affine fractions 0<=l<=u<=1 define
 a=epsilon+(1/2-epsilon)*l and b=epsilon+(1/2-epsilon)*u.
An exact half cutoff or l=u has zero band and needs no source reconstruction.
Otherwise a,b>0, so this is a finite nonsingular integral.

For each exponential argument lambda=log(d), the checker uses

 exp(-lambda*t)=sum_(k=0)^M (-lambda*t)^k/k! + remainder,
 |remainder|<=(lambda*t)^(M+1)/(M+1)!, t>=0.

This bound follows from the Taylor Lagrange remainder, since the absolute
derivative of exp(-x) on x>=0 is at most 1. Exact signed source moments are
computed as Arb enclosures after retaining all source coefficients; the
remainder sums absolute coefficients only after the signed polynomial has
been evaluated. Its integrated absolute enclosure is

 [sum_d |c_d| log(d)^(M+1)]/(M+1)!
       * (b^(M+1-alpha)-a^(M+1-alpha))/(M+1-alpha).

Every polynomial term integrates by
 integral_a^b t^(k-alpha-1)dt=(b^(k-alpha)-a^(k-alpha))/(k-alpha),
where k>=1 and alpha<1. The implementation uses an expm1 form to avoid a
near-zero denominator/near-equal-powers loss. Both the signed polynomial and
the absolute remainder are multiplied by the positive rgamma(1-alpha).

Initial replay uses 256-bit Arb and degree 128, with outward 160-bit dyadic
conversion. If its full interval is not contained in the supplied interval,
one 384-bit/degree-160 replay is permitted. Failure after that is a failure
to admit the interval at these resource limits, not an arithmetic refutation.
The producer uses upper incomplete gamma and an outward 80-bit output grid;
the checker does not reuse that integration routine. Exact rational outward
endpoints are authoritative, not rounded decimal display radii.

Private caches keyed by the full mathematical arguments share factorization,
source coefficients, log atoms and signed Taylor moments. In particular
moments do not depend on alpha, gamma or the affine band fractions. Precision
and Taylor degree are part of their cache keys. Fresh source-specific work,
including overlap with earlier source primitives if any, is charged here.

CONDITIONAL NEIGHBORING-PARENT RECOVERY

Each feature must have a nonempty registered prime set of size k. The complete
positive-product envelope gives |h(N,t)|<=4*N*R*t^k for 0<t<=1. The bound
1/Gamma(1-alpha)<=3 follows from Gamma(s)>=integral_0^1 exp(-t)dt>1/3 for
0<s<1. Integrating the envelope over 0..1, hence over every admitted band,
gives |J_i(N)|<=b_i*N, b_i=12*R_i/(k_i-alpha).

For a fixed integer vector a and rational lambda>=0,
 F(N)=[J_0(N)^2+lambda*(sum_i a_i J_i(N))^2]/N
is nonnegative and dominates the literal feature-0 energy. The certificate
replays the complete exact matrix, including doubled signed off-diagonal
terms. The source-free base B=5*(b_0^2+lambda*(sum|a_i|b_i)^2) pays EVERY
integer seed N=2,3,4,5. No finite fitted values are used to reduce this base.

The recurrence leaf remains OPEN:
 F(2N+r)<=sum_(s=-1,0,1) w_s F(N+s)+C/N^delta,
for every integer N>=3 and BOTH r=0,1; fixed nonnegative weights sum to one.
All three offset domains and both children are retained even when some
weights are zero. If this uniform leaf is supplied, convexity reduces it
to the largest previous envelope. With child M=2N+r and shifted index M-2,
the largest parent obeys
 (N+1)-2=N-1<=floor((M-2)/2).
Thus five seeds, not two, are necessary, and the relevant halving index is
M-2, not M. Each path is finite and the positive forcing can be bounded by
the dyadic ancestor sum. For delta>0 it is at most
 C/(2^delta-1)<=2*C/delta, using log(2)>=1/2 and exp(x)-1>=x.
This yields F(M)<=B+2*C/delta for every M>=2.

For delta=0, seeds2..5 have F<=B. Every subsequent child can add at most one
C and at most max(0,floor(log2(M-2))-1) such additions are needed. Hence
 F(M)<=B+C*max(0,floor(log2(M-2))-1).
The certificate records logarithm base2, argument shift2 and base maximum5.
It licenses only this conditional implication: no finite recurrence samples
discharge the all-integer leaf, and no dependency makes it already PROVED.

LIMITS AND CLAIMS

Request capacities: N<=32768, <=8 distinct actual primes, prime product<=1m,
canonical rational inputs<=4096bits, exact cutoff powered comparison<=400kbits,
at most2m generated radical-subset terms per source. Private live contexts<=16;
their aggregate prospective cache capacity is4GiB. Endpoint rationals<=16384bits,
conditional rational intermediate results<=32768bits. Runtime and work caps
are enforced through charge-only metering; the default finite verifier is
200bn operations/7200seconds. These are explicit admission capacities, not
mathematical denominator restrictions. No module-level process limits affect
other owners' suites. A caller may impose additional process memory limits.

The admitted finite output is one source-specific integral enclosure. Conditional
recovery targets the literal feature0 middle band, not original K, the full
original signed source, or all unpaid end regions. Uniform recurrence, uniform
middle bound, RH and RH refutation flags remain false. The analytic rule replay
is independently reviewed mathematics, not a proof-assistant formalization.
