Analytical strategy beyond completion of same-block Gram crosses

The complete fourteen-atom probe is a useful diagnosis of one admitted witness, not an estimate. It closes the previously absent legacy/divisor cross entries without changing the source atoms, their degree, the relation dictionary, or the shared coefficient law. If a complete moment matrix is indefinite, the old witness is invalid for the larger cone; primal feasibility is still unknown. If both regional matrices are PSD, that particular completion cannot defeat the functional. Neither outcome identifies an all-scale arithmetic obstruction.

The present hierarchy is approaching the full quadratic mixing space of its fixed atom bank. The five legacy channels are linear in sequence variables, while the nine divisor contrasts are quadratic with formal constant -1/2. Completing their Gram matrix introduces all products of these atoms, of degree at most four. Adding more matrices on the same span changes parameterization rather than information once the full block is present. New information must then come from additional source observables, additional valid ideal multipliers/localizing inequalities, or a different uniformly recoverable state/scale law. Enlarging the bank indefinitely without choosing one of those mechanisms is not an analytical strategy.

What the existing separator does and does not diagnose

The stacked constructor in grouped_arithmetic_synthesis._limits labels each monomial by its instance. Thus the witness has separate functionals y_X on the scale copies. Shared scalar-group coefficients impose summed dual constraints over X; shared Gram coefficients impose PSD on the aggregated regional moment matrix. They do not impose positivity or relation annihilation separately at every scale, or compatibility of moments of the same index across different scale copies. This is the correct dual of the particular stacked shared-template problem. It is not a moment model for a single actual Möbius sequence.

Consequently, a separator of the shared problem does not establish that every single-scale certificate problem is infeasible. The functional could exploit incompatibility of one fixed coefficient vector across scales, missing within-scale constraints, or both. Determining which requires a separately frozen diagnostic: compare individual-scale admission and shared admission, and inspect overlaps of identical formal observables. No such comparison or arithmetic score is asserted here. Merely identifying instance copies or summing their targets would not fix the issue: an aggregate inequality can hold while an individual scale fails. Any common-sequence or scale-coupled formulation must retain an exact recovery of every required target inequality.

The zero ordinary and auxiliary constant moments explain why increasing C_q cannot rescue the admitted fixed cone: constant budget shifts pair to zero. They do not make the functional a probability measure. A positive normalized arithmetic evaluation has positive constant mass; the admitted functional is positive only on the declared grouped squares/Gram blocks. It need not be positive on every square, every valid arithmetic localizer, or every individual norm premise. It is therefore a sparse proof-language witness, not a counterexample sequence. Full individual norm relations plus positivity on the appropriate individual variable squares would severely restrict a zero-mass functional; the current grouped relations and aggregated Gram positivity are weaker.

Fixed shared coefficients are a deliberate restriction, not a demonstrated universal barrier. All-scale proofs often do use fixed coefficients or a fixed q-dependent matrix. The current dictionary already contains explicit scale factors, cutoffs and source weights, but it does not include every rational function of t/X or every q-dependent scale law. Per-scale fitted weights would remove one restriction while sacrificing transfer and uniformity. A useful relaxation is a finite, preregistered rational coefficient grammar in dimensionless variables, with exact denominator sign, floor-boundary and growth proofs. Fitting unrelated coefficients at X=1,2,3,5 is not that relaxation.

Low-degree arithmetic closure is another credible limitation. Complete source enumeration is not the same as closure under all ideal multipliers. Present rows aggregate inverse, norm, gcd/coprime and unit premises with a fixed small multiplier bank. Full fourteen-atom mixing still tests only a finite degree-four moment family. The available facts do not prove that degree is the decisive obstruction, but this is a concrete place to add valid signed information rather than another redundant same-span cross.

A generic expansion with a clear mechanism: sign-conditioned localizers

For the mu target, let c_n be the exact squarefree indicator, so x_n^2-c_n=0 and c_n is 0 or 1. The inequality 1+s*x_n>=0 is valid for s=+1,-1 without supplying any actual sign response to the learner. For the complete fourteen-atom vector V_t, preregister all n=1,...,t and both signs, with nonnegative rational weight w(n,t)=1/t, and one shared PSD matrix G_s per fixed source-context group. The proposed localizing form is

 sum_t sum_{n<=t} w(n,t)*(1+s*x_n)*V_t^T G_s V_t.

All source terms and both signs must be present. There is an important simplification: since V_t and the shared G_s do not depend on n, the complete uniform-weight sum collapses EXACTLY to

 sum_{n<=t}(1/t)*(1+s*x_n)*V_t^T G_s V_t
 = (1+s*P_t/t)*V_t^T G_s V_t.

It is therefore mean-prefix conditioning, not independent per-prime or per-index sign conditioning. The elementary fact |P_t/t|<=1 validates this localizer but does not supply square-root cancellation. Its potentially new requirement is positivity of a covariance polynomial after multiplication by the signed mean prefix; unconditional Gram positivity alone need not impose this degree-five constraint. Its novelty within the present algebra must still be checked: existing sufficiently rich ideal/SOS closure could make it redundant.

A more generic preregistered grammar uses nonnegative rational weights w(n,t) with sum_n w(n,t)=1. With an n-independent V_t and matrix, this always collapses to (1+s*L_w(t))*V_t^T G_s V_t, where L_w(t)=sum_n w(n,t)*x_n. Complete n/t cells and their complements give weighted signed block sums. A cell normalized by its exact count must specify the empty-cell case without dividing by zero; alternatively use weights bounded by 1/t, record their total mass m_w(t), and retain the exact factor m_w(t)+s*L_w(t). All cells, both signs, all boundaries and complete complements must be fixed before inspecting a witness. Prime masks, if admitted, condition only their complete weighted signed prime sum; a shared matrix still does not give separate prime-wise information.

Genuinely index-dependent covariance conditioning would require a structurally defined V_{n,t} or a PSD matrix law varying with n/t, together with a uniformly proved source and coefficient rule. Assigning a free matrix to each observed n would be a different finite fitting problem. The immediate controlled expansion is the weighted-signed-sum localizer grammar, not a claim that the uniform 1/t example supplies many independent conditional constraints.

There is an exact rational SOS-plus-norm identity for every term:

 (1+s*x_n)*V^T G V
 = (1/2)*[(1+s*x_n)V]^T G[(1+s*x_n)V]
   + (1/2)*(1-c_n)*V^T G V
   - (1/2)*(x_n^2-c_n)*V^T G V.

The first two terms are nonnegative when G is PSD; the last is an admitted norm relation with a COMPLETE polynomial multiplier. This gives a concrete compiler and independent-checker contract, without a new arithmetic axiom. The lifted atoms have degree at most three, and the expanded SOS and norm multiplier terms have degree at most six. Their high-degree cancellations must be exact; no omitted monomial may be set to zero. A probe can evaluate the corresponding complete localizing moments of an old witness only when its domain supports them; otherwise the result is unknown and a new exact search domain is required.

Normalization matters. Since sum_{n<=t}(1/t)=1, evaluating the formal constant functional gives only the old V constant charge per t: O(X) for fixed matrices. An unweighted full n sum would introduce O(X^2) charges, and growing source weights could produce larger powers. The exact compiler must extract constants from all three terms of the displayed identity, including the norm-relation constant, rather than counting only the SOS terms. Here the constant vector is zero on the five legacy atoms and -1/2 on the nine divisor atoms, so all DD cross terms remain in c^T G c. The old cubic gamma gate can be retained only after a fresh scalar-degree proof for the entire extended dictionary. The existing proof applies to its recorded normalized fixed-template assumptions; a descriptor label cannot inherit it.

These are candidate finite expressivity expansions, not proven signed bounds; the uniform 1/t case should be described and implemented through its exact mean-prefix collapse when useful. Conditional localizers can also become redundant after complete ideal reduction. Before spending optimizer resources, compare their exact reduced columns with the existing cone and keep the transport identity as a calibration. If the necessary degree-six algebra is too large, fail under a declared budget rather than select a few favorable n or moments after seeing the witness.

A complementary analytical direction: a recoverable multiscale storage energy

Let E(X)=sum_{X<=t<2X} P_t^2. The existing target is Delta(X)=E(2X)-4E(X). Define a complete channel state energy S_R(X)=sum_{X<=t<2X} V_t^T R_q V_t, with one exact q-dependent PSD matrix and an anchored coercivity condition R_q-c_q*e_P e_P^T PSD for an explicit c_q>0. Then S_R(X)>=c_q E(X) is a universal exact recovery inequality, even though the divisor channels themselves have no known critical envelope. Set W_q(X)=S_R(X)/X^2.

Competitive state normalization is an additional obligation. The present unit-normalized source weights are a^j, not dimensionless (a/t)^j: c=1 removes the unit scalar growth but does not make the j=1 divisor channel comparable in magnitude to the prefix. A fixed matrix on raw a-weighted channels may be dominated by those channels rather than capture a critical energy. One can instead preregister a known diagonal state map A(t,X) with prefix coordinate identically 1 and use A(t,X)V_t. For example multiplying j=1 channels by 1/t is an exact bounded rational normalization worth analyzing; it is not a proved cancellation estimate. The coefficient rule, denominator positivity and source growth must be proved uniformly. With R_q-c_q e_P e_P^T PSD and A(t,X)e_P=e_P, congruence preserves the stated prefix coercivity.

Every scaled channel must be transported as an entire expression: h*D_j=(h*I_j-h*O_j-h)/2, with complete normalization h*B_j-h=0. Keeping the old -1/2 centering after scaling only the source coefficients would change the channel and its unit boundary. The new constant vector is A(t,X)z and every block pays z^T A(t,X)^T R_q A(t,X)z. Mixed constants, ideal multipliers, initial costs and parent/child normalization differences must all be re-extracted from exact polynomials. If A depends on X, the child innovation uses its own scale 2X; borrowing the parent's map would change the identity.

Bounded downscalings can keep new charges at most O(X), but that must be verified for the complete grammar rather than inherited by name. Amplifying the j=-1 source by t, as in the (a/t)^-1 normalization, changes its unit scalar from 1 to t; centered-square scalar sums can become cubic. In that case the previous gamma gate is incomplete and all additional leading scalar coefficients, including mixed terms and relation constants, need fresh cancellation gates and a uniform remainder estimate. Competitive normalization and an admissible critical scalar budget must be designed together.

A meaningful new analytical target is a uniform innovation inequality

 S_R(2X)-4S_R(X) <= C_q*X^2*ceil(X^(1/q))

for every dyadic X, together with the coercivity and all initial-scale costs. This is equivalent to a bound on W_q(2X)-W_q(X). Dyadic telescoping bounds W_q by O_q(X^(1/q)); coercivity then recovers the original prefix energy. The signed storage increment can include channel increments and cross-scale transport that are unavailable to an independent same-block nonnegative square dictionary. It does not assume a separate bound for D_j, but proving this stronger state inequality is itself a substantial unresolved estimate.

This mode must be declared as a new representation with an explicit recovery bridge. It must emit the exact difference between its innovation and the original Delta; every channel increment, product boundary and remainder survives. One cannot rename a filtered target as the original defect, discard a positive storage term from the wrong side, or infer a bound from PSD alone. Complete prefix transport P_t=P_floor(t/2)+(P_t-P_floor(t/2)), exact divisor split/transport and complete arithmetic ideal rows provide identity generators, not estimates. Coercivity and the uniform innovation are the two separate admission obligations.

Uniform promotion obligations

For either expansion, a finite accepted matrix or coefficient vector must become an explicit structural formula valid at every required integer scale, with all unit, floor, cutoff, prime-power, noncoprime and complementary-source contributions retained. Nonnegative weights, PSD, denominator signs, exact ideal multipliers and polynomial recovery must be proved uniformly, not checked on a grid. The scalar budget must include every signed relation constant and full Gram cross constant, with explicit growth rates and constants. For the critical objective, the quantifiers remain: some q0; every integer q>=q0; finite C_q independent of X; every dyadic X, with all starting scales charged. The corresponding ordinary prefix-energy/Mellin recovery bridge must be invoked with its actual hypotheses. Average energy control should not be casually described as a pointwise square-root bound.

Scale normalization, covariance signs, symbolic recovery and scalar costs should be settled before a larger numerical campaign. The next informative finite result would be either an independently verified old-witness violation of one complete new conditional localizer, or an exact recoverable storage template with a genuinely subcubic scalar law. Neither, by itself, supplies the missing all-scale inequality. All optimizer failures, unsupported moments and failed transfers remain separate from mathematical infeasibility.
