1. New coefficient and auxiliary language

Use f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(n*x)) on0<x<=1, zero beyond1. The target is
  e_N=1-g_N, g_N=sum_(2<=n<=N)c_n(N)f_n,
  c_n(N)=-mu(n)W0(n,N)+lambda*a(n)V(n,N).
Here mu and every arithmetic channel refer to actual ordinary primes.
W0 is bounded0..1 and tends1 at every fixed n; V tends0 there. This addresses
the exact coefficient-limit obstruction from043, without declaring global
convergence. The auxiliary can contain mu, Liouville, mu*mu, radical sign,1,
or mu plus/minus half Liouville, with their signed contributions retained.

Weights include cutoff and logarithmic tapers
  T_(alpha,gamma)(z)=(1-z^alpha)^gamma,
unit, vanishing twists n^(-a/L^k), and products. L(N)=1+floor(log2 N).
Envelopes include differences of limit-one weights, N^-delta or L^-kappa
amplitudes, and moving lower bands n>floor(N^theta). Arbitrary positive
rational taper/amplitude exponents are accepted under work and bit caps.
The581-formula finite bank is not a denominator whitelist. Existing twist k
is still integer1..8; the proposed fractional-log-scale twist below is a
further extension, not something already implemented.

All coefficient powers are rounded by an exact dyadic policy with
p=4ceil(log2 N). Log-coordinate rounding uses exact integer-power detection
at rational boundaries and Arb interval exclusion elsewhere. Each fixed
formula defines all integer N mathematically even though the evaluator may
refuse a finite compilation on resource guards.

Refinement is
  h_N=e_(qN)-b D_q e_N, D_q v(x)=v(qx), ||D_q||=q^-1/2.
The desired forcing bound is ||h_N||^2<=A/L(N)^eta. A checked conditional
Young/contraction argument would imply convergence on a geometric chain
if its forcing leaf were established uniformly there. That leaf remains OPEN.
The full-pure-floor target has constant1 and no indicators. Auxiliary norm
diagnostics may have constant0; they are not substituted for the RH target.

floor_residual_rate_review_044.py derives a leading fixed-index error from
the typed formula. check_floor_residual_rates_044.py independently replays
the AST algebra, actual arithmetic at the index, nonzero constants and exact
cell witness. No numerical norms or source arrays are premises.

If c_n(N)+mu(n)=C L^-nu+o(L^-nu), C!=0, an exact cell functional gives
  liminf L^(2nu)||e_N||^2 >=C^2/D_n>0.
All growing higher-support floor columns are annihilated exactly. Forcing
witnesses with q not dividing n annihilate the parent and indicator terms;
b0 can use every index. Thus eta>2nu is impossible for any fixed A. Equality
and slower rates remain OPEN. Cancellation of the compiled leading term
stays unresolved until a next term is proved. No heuristic sign is licensed.

4. Second new proof gate: persistent cutoff transfer

floor_residual_cutoff_guard_044.py recognizes convex actual-Mobius mixtures
of fixed power cutoffs with outer power1. It constructs the exact Mellin
kernel and separately replays polynomial cross multiplication. No candidate
identifier is encoded in the decision. Unsupported ASTs are kept OPEN.

Imported unitary U commutes with dilation and has
  U chi=sin(2pi x)/(pi x), U f_n=(rho(nx)-rho(x))/(nx).
Its action on0<x<1/(qN) converts vanishing h_N to a bound on the signed
coefficient sum. Explicit rounding O(N^-3), harmonic O(log N), unit

parts prove K_w nonzero for Re(s)>0. At a known critical-line zero rho,
|q^rho|=sqrt(q)>|b|. An elementary Abel limit contradicts the little-o bound.
No RH assumption or zero simplicity is used.

6. What should change in the next search

Apply the rate and cutoff proof gates BEFORE expensive numerical ranking,
with each candidate's actual bound exponent and domain. Bind every rejection
to its exact formula; keep unsupported rules, slower exponents and distinct
subsequence claims available. Passing a necessary gate never proves a bound.

Search for auxiliary cancellations that improve the first nonzero local
rate, and compile the next term rather than rewarding a vanished leading
coefficient alone. Search also needs genuinely scale-dependent constructions
whose global Mellin transfer can suppress critical-line contributions.
Static convex power cutoffs now have a proved all-integer obstruction.

One proposed production is
  beta_N=ceil(a L(N)^theta)/L(N), rational a>0,0<theta<1,
  W(n,N)=n^(-beta_N), possibly in a joint auxiliary/profile.
It has beta_N->0 while beta_N log N->infinity. The exponent remains rational
at every finite N. This is a proposal, not an implemented production, proven
global bound, or preferred RH solution. Its local rate, rounding and global
control would each need their own argument. Keep other moving profiles,
pairings, correlated terms and new intermediate lemmas eligible.

The detector generated the operators/auxiliaries and finite bound proposals.
These two general analytic obstruction templates were developed by analysis
and integrated for automatic reuse. The tool still lacks an autonomous
argument that closes the joint signed remainder uniformly over all scales.
This limitation remains the central research task; additional numerical
success or a weaker necessary-condition filter must not be reported as closure.
