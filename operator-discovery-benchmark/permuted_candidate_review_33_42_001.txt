Selected permuted circuits33 and42: exact reductions and a uniform attempt
2026-10-09. Source-only mathematical review; new memo, frozen files unchanged.

Scope
Read the selected raw/normalized programs and certificates in final_batch.json,
the new circuit contract and permuted_circuit_recovery_review.txt. No response
file, source evaluation, numerical probe, test, rescore or hash was used. Finite
constants and reported ranks are not premises below. The input throughout is
the COMPLETE actual prefix M(n)=sum_{j<=n}mu(j), M(0)=0, not a convolution of
mu followed by a prefix. All inverse coefficients use the same original source.

Main proved advances
(a)33 has an explicit four-quarter/residue commutator and exact inactive
power-of-two chains; its prime activation criterion depends on the COMPLETE
divisors of an inverse-rotated coordinate, not its syntactic prime appearance.
(b)42's conjugated shear is still an adjacent shear. Its full fresh energy is
F42(X)=(101/90)E_M(X)+O(log X), uniformly and unconditionally, with explicit
constants below. This is amplification plus subpower padding, not a fresh
energy contraction. Its value at every odd prime is M(p) plus a bounded offset.
(c) A block-sup norm lemma supplies unconditional FULL E=O(X), and transfers
a genuinely proved full X^eta moment bound through either circuit AND inverse
with explicit constants. It does not supply the missing small eta premise.
No complete cofinal signed moment bound for33 or42 is proved.

1.33: exact four-way interleaving and complete path
Write A=T_(1,1), B=T_(-1,1). These are divisor sum and genuine Mobius inversion:
 Af(n)=sum_{d|n}f(n/d), Bf(n)=sum_{d|n}mu(d)f(n/d), AB=BA=I.
Let pi rotate the k low bits right by2 within [m,2m), m=2^k, and
 Uf(n)=f(pi(n)). The selected word acts as
 rho33=A U^-1 B U M.
For k>=2 and r=4a+b, 0<=b<4,0<=a<m/4,
 pi(m+r)=m+a+b*m/4.
For t=j*m/4+s,0<=j<4,0<=s<m/4,
 pi^-1(m+t)=m+4s+j.
The singleton and k=1 block use the stated rotation modulo k; k=1 is identity.
The four quarters and all translated/wrapped residue classes remain present.
An index permutation polynomial is not an identity for mu or M.

The exact full path formula is
 rho33(n)=sum_{d|n}sum_{e|pi^-1(n/d)}mu(e)
                M(pi(pi^-1(n/d)/e)).                         (1)
Since B U=U B+[B,U], it also gives
 rho33=M+A U^-1[B,U]M,
 [B,U]M(t)=sum_{e|t}mu(e)M(pi(t/e))
                    -sum_{e|pi(t)}mu(e)M(pi(t)/e).           (2)
Both divisor domains in (2) are essential. Pairing a convenient subset and
discarding its unmatched complement would destroy the identity. The e=1
terms cancel in this commutator. Once a proper divisor is used, the path
enters a strictly lower dyadic block and cannot return to the original block.
Consequently the COMPLETE fresh diagonal is I:
 rho33(n)=M(n)+g33(n),
 E33(X)=E_M(X)+G33(X)+C33(X),
 G33=X^-2 sum g33^2, C33=2X^-2 sum M*g33.                 (3)

Two useful exact activation facts, without a density assertion:
* pi and pi^-1 fix each power of2. Their entire divisor chains are also
  powers of2. Thus rho33(2^k)=M(2^k), including the low blocks, by AB=I.
* At an odd prime p, set q=pi^-1(p)>1. The outer divisor sum has only its
  q contribution and the singleton contribution. Since M(1)=1,
  rho33(p)-M(p)=sum_{d|q,d>1}mu(d)[M(pi(q/d))-1].         (4)
  If q is prime this is exactly0. The d=q term, when present, is exactly0
  in this displayed form, not an omitted numerical complement. If q=l^a,
  a>=2, (4) is -[M(pi(l^(a-1)))-1]. For general composite q all squarefree
  proper divisor combinations remain. For k>=2, q=m+4s+j has parity j;
  even quarters j=0,2 force q composite. Odd quarters merely ALLOW q prime.
  Nothing here proves infinitely many simultaneous prime p and q.

There is a bounded fixed-dilation defect worth distinguishing from the full
divisor commutator. If n=m+j*m/4+s, then for a>=0,k>=2,
 pi^-1(2^a*n)=2^a*pi^-1(n)-(2^a-1)j.
Therefore |M(pi^-1(2^a*n))-M(2^a*pi^-1(n))|<=3(2^a-1).
This is genuine bounded padding for a FIXED dilation. It does not identify
the divisor operators: their supports include different parity/divisibility
predicates, and pi^-1(n) need not share n's divisor set. Dropping those
predicate complements would falsely turn (2) into a small displacement rule.

2.42: the conjugated pair is exactly adjacent, with reversed direction
Let phi5(t)=-1 for t=1,4 modulo5 and +1 otherwise. Write
 A5=T_(1/3,phi5), D=T_(-1,xi),
 xi(p)=source_sign(mu(floor(p^(1/3)))), extended completely multiplicatively.
The zero-sign convention is +1. Thus for j=floor(p^(1/3)),
 xi(p)=1+mu(j)-mu(j)^2.
The outer kernel is exactly b(d)=mu(d)xi(d); it vanishes on nonsquarefree d.
The dependence on actual mu occurs in both M and these fixed prime-phase
bands. It is not an independently varying sign oracle or a character theorem.

Let pi be alternating-bit xor and U its self-inverse pullback. On a block
X=2^k,k>=1 its mask a_k has bit0=1. For every even n in[X,2X),
 pi(n+1)=pi(n)-1.
Let H be odd_from_even with coefficient1/3 and phase phi5. Hence
 Q=U^-1 H U acts EXACTLY as
 Qf(n)=f(n)+(psi_X(n)/3)f(n+1) for even n,
 Qf(n+1)=f(n+1),
 psi_X(n)=phi5(pi(n+1)).                               (5)
The singleton1 is unchanged. No long-distance pair remains: only its phase
was rearranged. However the actual input to Q is A5 M, whose adjacent
increments are not known bounded. Applying bounded-M-increment arguments to
the ENTIRE interior state would be unjustified. They apply below ONLY to the
fresh diagonal Q M after complete proper-divisor terms have been separated.

Put f=A5 M, q=Q M. The exact entire circuit is rho42=D Q f, or explicitly
 rho42(n)=sum_{d|n}mu(d)xi(d) [f(n/d)
       +(1/3)1_{n/d even>=2}psi(n/d)f(n/d+1)],
 f(t)=sum_{e|t}a_(1/3,phi5)(e)M(t/e).                 (6)
The shifted branch has domains d|n and e|(n/d+1), NOT e|(n/d).
Its two divisors cannot be combined into a single divisor of n. For a proper
outer divisor, even n/d is at most X-2 on an output block[X,2X), so even
its shifted partner is still below X. Proper inner divisors at n+1 are also
below X. Thus the complete g=rho42-Q M is a lower-block linear action,
although its fixed arithmetic coefficients need not represent independent
past randomness. All lower and shifted/wrap contributions remain in
 rho42=q+g, E42=F42+G42+C42, C42=2X^-2 sum q*g.        (7)

3. A proved uniform fresh-energy reduction for42
Let N=X/2 and n_j=X+2j,0<=j<N. Put
 w_j=M(n_j)^2+M(n_j+1)^2.
Write b_k=a_k>>1. Then
 pi(n_j+1)=X+2(j xor b_k),
 psi_j=phi5(X+2(j xor b_k)).                          (8)
The function h(t)=phi5(X+2t)-1/5 is periodic modulo5 with mean0 and
|h|<=6/5. A sum over ANY consecutive interval has absolute value<=24/5:
remove its complete periods and bound the at most4 remaining terms.

Every prefix[0,J) of pair indices is a disjoint union of at most k aligned
binary intervals. Xor by a fixed (k-1)-bit mask sends each aligned interval
BIJECTIVELY onto another consecutive aligned interval of the same length.
It need not preserve the order inside it. Applying the preceding interval
bound to the whole image, and keeping every interval complement, proves
 |sum_{j<J}(psi_j-1/5)| <=(24/5)k, 0<=J<=N.           (9)
This is an unconditional discrepancy statement; no distribution conjecture
about actual prefix values is used. Complete pair counts also have bounded
residue discrepancy, but weighted prefix energy needs (9), not counts alone.

Use |M(t)|<=t and |M(t+2)-M(t)|<=2. Then
 w_(N-1)<=8X^2,
 sum_{j<N-1}|w_(j+1)-w_j|<=(N-1)(16X+8)<=8X^2+4X.
Discrete Abel summation with the EXACT endpoints in (9) yields
 |X^-2 sum_j(psi_j-1/5)w_j|<=(432/5)k, X>=2.         (10)
The loose constant uses 8+8+4/X<=18. This is a bound on the complete
weighted correlation, not a claim that the individual signs vary freely.

For each pair let x=M(n),y=M(n+1),v=mu(n+1),t=psi/3, so y=x+v.
The full retained pair square satisfies the exact identity
 (x+t*y)^2+y^2
  =(1+t+t^2/2)(x^2+y^2)+(1/9)y*v-(t+1/18)v^2.       (11)
Here 1+t+t^2/2=19/18+psi/3. Since there are X/2 pairs,
 the normalized remainder in (11) is bounded by1/9+7/(36X).
Combining (10) and (11), and retaining the unmodified singleton in the head,
 |F42(X)-(101/90)E_M(X)|
       <=(144/5)log_2 X+1/9+7/(36X), dyadic X>=2.    (12)
Indeed 19/18+(1/3)(1/5)=101/90. This result holds for ANY prefix with source
increments bounded by1. It is a smoothness/residue transport identity, not
additional actual-prime information. In particular F42 is subpower for all
small exponents if and only if the original E_M is, up to logarithmic padding.
The full candidate may still use g to change the needed arithmetic correlation;
(12) does not identify g or declare its signed cross harmless.

4. Actual arithmetic content of42 and a prime-coordinate obstruction
For any odd prime p the shear does not touch p and does not touch singleton1.
Only outer divisors1,p and inner divisors1,p occur. Therefore EXACTLY
 rho42(p)=M(p)+phi5(p)/3-xi(p),
 |rho42(p)-M(p)|<=4/3.                               (13)
Thus the circuit has no large cancellation of a prime endpoint's prefix value.
This is not a lower bound on its complete moment: prime endpoints form a
restricted set, and no unsupported inference about their energy density is
made. It is a concrete necessary source-specific prime-prefix obligation.

The inner prime phase is a fixed arithmetic character twist: for p!=5,
 phi5(p)=-chi_5(p), where chi_5 is the quadratic character modulo5, while
 phi5(5)=+1. On the absolutely convergent Euler domain Re(s)>1 the kernel has
 sum_d a_(1/3,phi5)(d)d^-s
       =zeta(2s)^(1/3)L(s,chi_5)^(-1/3)(1+5^-s)^(1/3). (14)
This follows locally from (1+chi*x)^-1=(1-chi*x)/(1-x^2);
the displayed5-factor is retained. Fractional powers are defined by their
convergent Euler logarithms. Formula (14) is about a kernel acting on prefix
coordinates, not a transformed coefficient source. The cuberoot prime phase
is constant on each prime band[j^3,(j+1)^3), with sign given above; there is
no proved global Dirichlet-character replacement for it.

One may split the complete operator as D A5+(1/3)D N_psi A5. The first term
is a pure divisor kernel with local factor
 (1-xi(p)z)(1-phi5(p)z)^(-1/3).
Its prime coefficient is phi5(p)/3-xi(p), never0; its magnitude is2/3 or4/3.
The second term has the shifted divisor domains in (6). It cannot be dropped
or folded into that Euler factor. The source-specific estimates required for
these two terms and their mixed square are not supplied by (14).

5. General uniform block-norm lemma: what can actually be closed
For eta>0 define
 B_eta(f)^2=sup_{dyadic X>=1} X^(-2-eta)sum_{X<=n<2X}|f(n)|^2.
Use the same finite definition over complete blocks of any closed horizon.
Set beta=2+eta, s=1+eta/2 and C_eta=sqrt(1+2^beta).
For a single divisor dilation V_d f(n)=1_{d|n}f(n/d),
 sum_{n in[X,2X),d|n}|f(n/d)|^2
    =sum_{ceil(X/d)<=j<=floor((2X-1)/d)}|f(j)|^2.
If X/d>=1, choose dyadic m<=X/d<2m. The interval is contained in the union
of the COMPLETE blocks[m,2m) and[2m,4m). Their bound is
 (1+2^beta)m^beta B_eta(f)^2 <= C_eta^2(X/d)^beta B_eta(f)^2.
If X/d<1 and d<=2X-1, its only possible quotient is1. The same inequality
holds since X/d>1/2 and C_eta^2>2^beta. If d>2X-1 the contribution is0.
For d=1 only the original target block is needed. For d>=2 all required
covering blocks lie within the closed horizon. No arithmetic zero-padding
or omitted low quotient is a premise of this finite uniform estimate.

Triangle inequality and the positive Euler coefficient majorant prove
 B_eta(T_(alpha,chi)f)
       <= C_eta*zeta(1+eta/2)^abs(alpha)*B_eta(f).     (15)
The same holds for its inverse, with the same source. A dyadic permutation
is an EXACT isometry of B_eta. A shear and inverse cost <=1+abs(c), using
the full two-coordinate unweighted block norm. Thus these circuits and their
inverses are bounded on this norm, without any9^P factor:
 K33(eta)=C_eta^2*zeta(s)^2,
 K42(eta)=(4/3)C_eta^2*zeta(s)^(4/3).                 (16)
These are unsquared costs; the moment uses K^2. No frozen recovery certificate
or finite ranking is changed by this separate analytical norm lemma.
For33, both forward and inverse bounds can additionally divide (16) by
zeta(2s), since |mu(d)| has Dirichlet sum zeta(s)/zeta(2s). For42 the same
improvement is valid FORWARD for its outer negative-integer stage; its inverse
positive stage has no such squarefree gain. The symmetric bounds (16) suffice.

Since |M(n)|<=n, the exact integer square sum gives B_1(M)^2<=7/3.
C_1=3, so a genuinely unconditional COMPLETE bound is
 E33(X)<=189*zeta(3/2)^4*X,
 E42(X)<=336*zeta(3/2)^(8/3)*X.                      (17)
These include every mixed path, shifted branch and quarter complement.
They close a uniform FIRST-power moment estimate, not the required subpower
estimate. The very generous constants can be made elementary with
zeta(3/2)<=3. No numerical measurements enter them.

Conversely, if a full candidate moment theorem supplied E_rho(X)<=C_q
ceil(X^(1/q)) for all dyadic X>=32, then eta=1/q and the complete head
h=sum_{1<=n<32}rho(n)^2/n^2 give
 B_eta(rho)^2<=max(4h,2C_q).
The head bound uses n<2X, hence sum_block rho^2/X^(2+eta)<=4h for X<32.
Therefore (16) applied to the full inverse proves the SAME-EXPONENT bound
 E_M(X)<=K_candidate(1/q)^2*max(4h,2C_q)*X^(1/q).
This strengthens block-by-block recovery accounting, but its functional
hypothesis is exactly the as-yet-unproved full moment theorem. A tiny eta
bound on only a discarded difference, fresh term, kernel envelope or control
does not meet it. Both directions show that this fixed circuit cannot obtain
a source-independent subpower theorem from bounded increments alone.

For clarity, a generic obstruction is rigorous: f(n)=n has bounded increments,
yet B_eta(f)=infinity for every eta<1. Keep ANY fixed actual-mu phases and
apply these uniformly invertible circuits to that arbitrary input f. If their
full output had finite B_eta, the proved inverse would force finite B_eta(f),
a contradiction. This is an arbitrary-input countermodel to a generic proof
rule; it is NOT a counterexample for actual M and NOT a refutation of RH.

6. The full remaining signed gate, now candidate-specific
For33, (3) requires a negative COMPLETE correlation sufficient for
 C33(X)<=-E_M(X)-G33(X)+C_q ceil(X^(1/q)).             (18)
For42 define R_X=F42(X)-(101/90)E_M(X), with the explicit bound (12).
Then (7) gives the equally exact requirement
 C42(X)<=-(101/90)E_M(X)-G42(X)-R_X
                              +C_q ceil(X^(1/q)).    (19)
Logarithmic R_X is harmless as subpower padding, but cannot substitute for
the much larger signed credit in (19). Cauchy-Schwarz supplies a magnitude
bound, not the required negative direction or payment of G. Making g small
does not remove the fresh E term. The all-scale arithmetic question is the
actual joint correlation of complete prefix values with the rotated divisor
combinations (1)-(4), or the cuberoot-twisted shifted divisor combinations
(6), including their squared lower block and every mixed product. No proved
identity above signs these correlations strongly enough.
