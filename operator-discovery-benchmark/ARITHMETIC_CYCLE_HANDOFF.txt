1. New finite certificate language and independent implementation.

coefficient variables x_n, for targets mu and c=mu*lambda. Its only arithmetic
relations are unit normalization, coprime products, actual prime-power local

negative square weights and altered polynomial terms are rejected.

A_f(2X)-4A_f(X), A_f(X)=sum_(X<=n<2X)(sum_(m<=n)f(m))^2, through N=4X-1.
Finite positivity follows by evaluating the coefficient identity on actual f.
The independent checker reconstructs Delta via direct prefix-square expansion;
the producer uses the signed max kernel. Every original pair remains present.

The language permits arbitrary finite rational polynomial multipliers, including
nonlinear ones. Its complete ordered coprime-cycle production charges product
horizon N^2. The pilot uses only coprime relations ab<=N, a declared subset;
omitting relations does not omit any original target terms. Resources have
explicit finite caps, distinct from invalid mathematics and global semantic laws.

arithmetic_cycle_synthesis.py matches polynomial coefficients using a floating LP
only to propose support. Exact rational elimination, nonnegative reconstructed

An unsuccessful solver is not an infeasibility proof. Time/resource failures are

The supplied arithmetic relations nevertheless logically encode actual arithmetic;
this is not discovery without assumptions or new independent prime information.
