NEXT RELATIONAL PRODUCTIONS BEYOND FULL GRAM MIXING

Design note before new scores. Existing exact prime transport, primitive gcd
partition, prime-log forcing and full atom Grams remain supplied methods. The
following are proposals for representing and testing complete signed relations,
not discovered arithmetic estimates or promises of progress toward RH.

1. Bilinear factor blocks, with a complete factor boundary.
Candidate schema:
 {kind:factor_bilinear,left:actual_mu,right:actual_mu,
  outer:[floor(X^alpha),floor(X^beta)],
  inner_domain:{product_le:floor(c*X),coprime:true},
  kernel:rational_piecewise_polynomial,scale_program:exact_rational}.
Its semantic value is sum over the declared complete integer(a,b) domain of
mu(a)mu(b)K_X(a,b), NOT a product of independently truncated sums. A factor
split must produce the exact complement and account for collisions when mapping
(a,b) toab. On coprime pairs mu(a)mu(b)=mu(ab); product multiplicity is retained,
not replaced by coefficient1. A particularly useful grouped multiplier family
is Q_(a,b)=a fixed rational kernel times a complete prefix/factor-block linear
form, multiplied by the coprime relation xa*xb-xab. This creates signed cross
columns that atom Grams alone cannot express. Product indices require the full
source horizon. A signed bilinear estimate is still a separate unknown premise.

2. Moving prime transport banks with exact overlap.
Candidate schema:
 {kind:prime_transport_bank,prime_range:[floor(X^theta1),floor(X^theta2)],
  prime_weight:rational_kernel,source:mu,block:parent_or_child,
  grouping:shared_coefficients,normalizer:exact_ceiled_power}.
For EVERY prime individually M(n)=B_p(n)-B_p(floor(n/p)) is exact. Combining
several such identities requires all cross-prime overlaps and source costs;
it is not an average of independent random signs. Do not divide by a possibly
zero prime count: an empty bank is explicit missing/empty, with a separate
boundary rule. Changing the prime range at child2X leaves an exact bank-change
residual. The prime-square complement and p-free masks remain complete.
Fixed-P counterfeits satisfy each transport inP while having positive mean,
so a fixed finite bank plus norms cannot give a generic critical theorem.
Growing banks must charge their own uniform density/modulus/normalization costs.

3. Two-axis coboundaries and paired forcing.
Candidate schema:
 {kind:paired_coboundary,maps:[integer_dilation_or_affine_maps],
  coefficients:exact_rationals,domain:complete_pair_region,
  arithmetic_witnesses:[whitelisted_relations],boundary:explicit_complement}.
Mixed dilation K(a,b)-K(pa,b)-K(a,pb)+K(pa,pb) is an exact prime transport
kernel, with signed source factors retained. Search simultaneous parent/child
kernels, rather than favorable pieces. Prime-log forcing may reindex one or
both axes, but log denominators, all permitted prime powers and unit pair
exception remain. A zero unweighted kernel moment does not erase denominator
drift or variation cost. No square-root prime error is assumed.

4. Relation multipliers chosen by formal obstructions.
A new column can be a complete sum of multiplier*norm/gcd/coprime/prime-power
relations, grouped on exact factor/valuation/prime regions. Enumerate rational
multiplier templates by degree and description cost. First inspect highest-
degree matching constraints, then exact scalar contributions, then the remaining
signed quadratic form. For example, (x_g^2-h_g)*x_u*x_v closes a gcd quartic
relation to a lower-degree primitive pair without adding a scalar constant.
This is representation closure, not new arithmetic information. Exact dual
negative directions nominate columns; missing dual monomials require an extended
dual. Invalidating one witness never proves feasibility. Jointly shared square
and relation weights must replace free per-index encoding for a uniform proposal.

5. Adverse cases and the uniform proof gate.
Reject dropped factor complements, changed child cutoffs, uncharged product
multiplicities, missing prime-square sources, division by empty banks, implicit
zeros for unsupported monomials and wrong signed cross coefficients. Compare
with squarefree partially normalized counterfeits and unsigned sequences.
Norms, local cycles and fixed prime identities cannot distinguish all these
positive-mean models. Complete factor information is a source specification,
not itself a signed moment estimate; sufficiently strong alternative uniform
arithmetic information might also exclude the models.

The smallest useful expansion after the full-Gram audit is a generic exact
factor-block relation-multiplier compiler, with complete product-domain and
complement certificates. Add moving prime-bank coboundaries only after those
boundaries are independently verified. Preserve every proposed coefficient,
finite failure and resource limit. The original complete actual signed defect
remains the recovery target. A successful finite certificate merely nominates
a template; proving its all-scale nonconstant identity and cofinal critical
scalar budget remains the indispensable unknown uniform step.

Primary-source context, checked separately from the derived identities above.
Tao's paper "The logarithmically averaged Chowla and Elliott conjectures for
two-point correlations" studies logarithmically weighted correlations of
multiplicative functions and uses small-prime multiplicativity and an entropy
decrement argument: https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05422 . Matomaki, Radziwill and
Tao's "An averaged form of Chowla's conjecture" proves an averaged-shift
correlation result: https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.05121 . These primary papers
motivate exposing coupled arithmetic scales rather than assuming random signs.
Their weighted/averaged conclusions are NOT imported as the complete unweighted
critical energy estimate required here. Our finite transport, coefficient
matching and counterfeit statements are derived within the repository proofs;
the proposed uniform cancellation bounds remain unknown.
