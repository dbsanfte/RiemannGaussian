Cross-divisor search design (posthoc, no scores or frozen changes)

1. Remove tautological opportunity before enlarging a search.
For complete rational weights w_a, B=sum_a w_a*x_a*sum_{d|a}x_d and c=w1. Write Z=B-c using the complete unit/inverse-row decomposition. The closure B²-c²=Z(B+c) is exact. Likewise (P+sB)²-(P+sc)²=Z(2sP+B+c). If both sides and closures are admitted, a whole-channel square is merely a shifted prefix square after reduction. It adds no unknown prime information. B-c squares are zero on the arithmetic ideal. Keep a few whole-channel cases as compiler/closure calibration, not as the main bound-search extension. Cross-divisor terms in their expansion do not by themselves defeat this redundancy.

2. Proposed independent fixed-weight channels.
Use integer powers j in{-1,0,1}. The original localizer weights (a/t)^j give c=t^-j and must remain distinguishable from the following NEW declared normalization. Prefer normalized weights a^j, so c=1 for every t: this is multiplication of the old channel by exact t^j, not a fitted parameter. a^-1 is a canonical rational with positive index denominator. Complete source domain is ALL1<=a<=t, d|a, including a1 and zero cells. No actual sign response is used.
For theta every reduced rational0<theta<1 denominator<=6, let v=floor(t^theta) by exact integer comparison. Split
 I_j=sum_a a^j*x_a*sum_{d|a,d<=v}x_d,
 O_j=sum_a a^j*x_a*sum_{d|a,d>v}x_d,
 B_j=I_j+O_j, Z_j=B_j-1.
Every source pair belongs to exactly one cell, including boundaries. Define centered contrast D_j=(I_j-O_j-1)/2. Modulo complete inverse rows, D_j=I_j-1=-O_j. This is an unknown partial channel, not the known full constant B_j=1. Its formal scalar is-1/2; its actual deterministic unit contribution has been removed. Keep both I/O and all cross products in archives. Do not replace D by its incomplete source restriction.

3. Minimal shared squares and exact relation closure.
For each fixed(theta,j), parent and child contexts sum over their COMPLETE t ranges, with ONE shared coefficient per group. Square groups: sum_t D_j², sum_t(P_t+D_j)², sum_t(P_t-D_j)². All retain cross-divisor d!=e correlations. The mixture squares have prefix/partial-channel cross terms that the previous diagonal-in-d localizers lack. No individual t, d, pair or monomial coefficient is independently fitted.
Relation groups generated from every primitive term of Z_j times U, for U in{1,P_t,D_j,Z_j}; aggregate by same relation spec but keep full source provenance. Unit primitive multiplier is (x1+1), and nonunit is a^j*x_a. Therefore Z_j*U is exact for ALL t. Optional polarization closure between j channels: Z_j*D_k and Z_j*Z_k, complete terms; enumerate fixed integer j,k by declared cost, not response-driven selection.
The direction D_j can also be represented as D_j'=I_j-1; their difference is-Z_j/2. The exact square transport is D_j²-D_j'²=-(Z_j/2)(D_j+D_j'), and prefix-mixture transport follows identically. This is a useful compiler calibration but NOT another independent search direction. Never nominate both representations as discovered different mechanisms.

4. Genuine information and honest limitation.
Modulo the inverse ideal, the proposed extension adds squares of unknown partial divisor channels and their products with the unforced prefix. A factor-small divisor and its complement interact through cross-divisor correlations. The full channel normalization fixes their SUM; it provides no separate signed bound. New squares may change the proof cone because I_j is quadratic, so D_j² is quartic and prefix*D_j is cubic. Complete inverse closures are necessary to expose these interactions without silently discarding any source term. A finite improvement is only expressivity evidence. Full-target all-scale coefficient identity plus critical scalar budget at every cofinal q remains required.

5. Scalar and growth accounting.
Use the generic FORMAL constant functional on every expanded square and relation multiplier; descriptor names never determine cost. D_j² and each(P_t+-D_j)² have scalar1/4, hence their summed scalar is X/4 parent or X/2 child, before learned nonnegative weights. Z_j has scalar-1, so Z_j*D_k has scalar1/2 and Z_j² scalar1; relations carry these exactly. All new normalized scalar charges are O(X), not cubic, for fixed coefficients. Thus old gamma0 remains the necessary cubic gate for this particular normalized dictionary; its vanishing still does NOT prove critical budget, since old prime/norm remainders persist. Cross-channel source coefficient growth for j1 is charged in polynomial bits/work and unknown signed terms; c=1 does not make partial channels bounded.
If UNNORMALIZED(a/t)^j productions are retained separately, c_-1=t and centered-square/closure constants are cubic after t summation. Apply BOTH gamma_old=0 and rational eta=0 from the supplied budget scope, under its rational/integer-power assumptions; track square as well as relation scalar coefficients. Never mix normalized and unnormalized descriptor IDs or inherit a gate by name.
