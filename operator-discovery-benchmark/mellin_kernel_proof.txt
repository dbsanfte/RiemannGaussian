Complete signed inverse tails and cofinal growth frontiers
========================================================
This is a supplied mathematical backend, not a machine-formalized proof or an
autonomously invented proof. No RH-strength growth estimate is established here.
The search has no access to this prose or to literature while selecting kernels.

1. Grammar and actual arithmetic
-------------------------------
Fix rational k>1, ell>0, b>1 and a nonzero finite rational array c[m,j], with
nonnegative integer labels m,j. Define, for y>0 and X>0,

 K(y)=sum c[m,j]*(b^j*y)^m*exp(-b^j*y),
 F_K(X)=X^k sum_{n>=1} mu(n)n^(-k) K((X/n)^ell).

mu is the unique Dirichlet inverse of the constant-one sequence. The producer
obtains each complete prefix by triangular inversion, with no prime labels.
The independent checker obtains it by prime factorization. Absolute convergence
follows from k>1, boundedness of each kernel atom and |mu(n)|<=1. All rational
root denominators are legal; finite enumeration and Taylor budgets are resources.
Common dilation and overall amplitude are normalized before a program is
evaluated. This chooses a representative up to a fixed dilation and scalar;
it does not assert equality of raw responses before this normalization.

The complete arithmetic tail is included, not set to zero. For w>1,
 B_w(N)=1/zeta(w)-sum_{n<=N}mu(n)n^(-w)=sum_{n>N}mu(n)n^(-w),
 |B_w(N)|<=N^(1-w)/(w-1).
This uses the classical absolutely convergent reciprocal Euler product, not an
estimate about prime distribution or an assumption about zeros in the strip.
For an atom (r*y)^m exp(-r*y), r=b^j, expand its tail exponential through L-1.
The resulting tail is
 (r X^ell)^m sum_{t=0}^{L-1}(-r X^ell)^t/t! * B_{k+ell(m+t)}(N).
Taylor's real remainder is at most
 (r X^ell)^(m+L)/L! * N^(1-k-ell(m+L))/(k+ell(m+L)-1).
Multiply by X^k. Native Arb arithmetic encloses every prefix, real reciprocal-
zeta moment and remainder. Precision grows with the actual cancellation needed
in B_w(N); a fixed-precision subtraction is not treated as an exact tail.
The checker changes the prefix, precision, Taylor order and summation algorithm.

2. Generic exact Mellin factor and recovery by several kernels
------------------------------------------------------------
For 1<Re(s)<k, put v=(k-s)/ell and z=b^(-v). Substitution y=(X/n)^ell gives

 H_K(s)=integral_0^infinity F_K(X) X^(-s-1)dX
       =Gamma(v) P_K(v,z)/(ell*zeta(s)),
 P_K(v,z)=sum c[m,j]*(v)_m*z^j,
 (v)_m=v(v+1)...(v+m-1), (v)_0=1.

Absolute convergence in that strip justifies exchanging integrals and sums.
No simplicity assumption on ordinary zeros is used. Gamma is nonzero and finite
when Re(v)>0. At any ordinary zero rho (0<Re(rho)<1), Re(v)>0 and z!=0.

The certificate search solves exact rational linear systems for identities
 sum_i A_i(v,z) P_i(v,z)=(v)_r z^h,
with nonnegative integers r,h. The right side cannot vanish when Re(v)>0,
z!=0. Therefore these kernels have no common numerator zero at any ordinary
zero, at any height, including multiple zeros. A bounded failed identity search
is missing a certificate, not a counterexample to a growth estimate.
A separate exact certificate handles a single moment row whose constant dilation
coefficient has absolute value at least the sum of all later absolute values:
strict |z|<1 makes its dilation polynomial nonzero even in the equality case.

If all H_i are holomorphic on eta<Re(s)<k, a zero rho with Re(rho)>eta would
force all P_i(v(rho),z(rho)) to vanish. The identity gives a contradiction.
Thus a recovering bank excludes every ordinary zero to the right of eta.

3. Bounds approaching the critical line: the quantifiers matter
--------------------------------------------------------------
Set theta_q=eta+epsilon/q^nu, epsilon>0, nu>0, rational, q>=2 integer.
The target is: FOR EVERY q THERE EXISTS a finite C_{i,q}, such that FOR EVERY
integer N>=1, |F_i(N)|<=C_{i,q} N^theta_q log(e+N)^d.
There is NO specified growth rate for C_{i,q} as q grows. A finite table of
fitted constants is an illustration of this existential target, not its proof.
Constants at a new q are calibrated only on frozen training scales, and frozen
before evaluating either validation or final scales at that q.

For eta<Re(rho)<k, theta_q<Re(rho) for every sufficiently large q. A true bound
for even one such q continues H_i past rho; simultaneously true bounds for all
members of a recovering bank contradict the common-numerator identity. The
cofinal family therefore excludes all ordinary zeros with Re(rho)>eta. eta=1/2
would imply RH by the functional equation. Finite q grids never prove this.
Pure square-root constants and logarithmic factors of prescribed degree are
not required. Nor is a polynomial growth law for C_q silently imposed.

RH also implies these bounds for every kernel in this grammar and every q at
eta=1/2. Use the named classical conditional consequence
 M(t)=sum_{n<=t}mu(n)=O_delta(t^(1/2+delta)) for every delta>0
(Titchmarsh, second edition, Theorem 14.25(C), printed p.370). For a fixed q,
choose 0<delta<theta_q-1/2 and delta<k-1/2. Partial summation with
 f(t)=t^(-k)(r X^ell/t^ell)^m exp(-r X^ell/t^ell)
has vanishing boundary terms. Its derivative obeys
 |f'(t)|<=t^(-k-1) [(k+ell*m)(r X^ell/t^ell)^m
                              +ell*(r X^ell/t^ell)^(m+1)] exp(-r X^ell/t^ell).
Substitution u=r X^ell/t^ell yields a finite gamma integral and
 X^k sum mu(n) f(n)=O_delta(X^(1/2+delta)). Sum the finitely many signed atoms.
Absorb this into X^theta_q. The M bound is imported only to identify the target
as equivalent to RH for recovering banks; it is not used to prove a new bound.
Nonrecovering kernels have an RH consequence, but no converse is claimed.
The ordinary all-height bridge above is proved here without that imported fact.

The same cofinal logic applies to the earlier fractional-remainder parameter
bridge: variable theta_q->eta and freely varying C_q still imply vanishing of
the numerators on a cofinal set. Neither its fixed-common-constant amplitude
obstruction nor its Mertens constant obstruction may reject this new target.
This is a scope extension; earlier frozen estimates and their rejections remain.

4. True weak bounds and full observer costs
------------------------------------------
For an atom, the positive continuous weight in n is unimodal. Splitting at its
maximum bounds the discrete sum by its integral over t>0 plus twice its peak.
For every real X>=1 its signed response therefore has absolute value <=W_m X,
 W_m=r^((1-k)/ell) Gamma((k-1)/ell+m)/ell
     +2 r^(-k/ell) ((k+ell*m)/ell)^((k+ell*m)/ell) exp(-(k+ell*m)/ell).
For a kernel take W=sum |c|W_m. Differentiating the absolutely convergent sum,
 X F_atom'(X)=(k+ell*m)F_atom(X)-ell F_{m+1,j}(X).
Thus |F_K'(X)|<=J=sum |c|[(k+ell*m)W_m+ell W_{m+1}], for real X>=1.
This proves the unit increment bound and extends a bound on all integers to
all reals with an additional J, without increasing the growth exponent.
For 0<X<=1, dropping the exponential gives
 |F_K(X)|<=sum |c| r^m zeta(k+ell*m) X^(k+ell*m).

At a native observer s=sigma+it, 0<sigma<k, theta_q<sigma, d>=0, each transform
norm is bounded by the full local integral, J/sigma, and the hypothesized tail
 C_{i,q} integral_1^infinity X^(theta_q-sigma-1)log(e+X)^d dX.
log(e+X)<=log(e+1)+log X; raise to integer ceiling(d) and integrate its moments
exactly using j!/(sigma-theta_q)^(j+1). This charges approaching-line costs.
For a Bezout certificate divide ell*sum |A_i|*norm_i by
 |Gamma(v)*(v)_r*z^h|. For a single dominance certificate divide ell*norm by
 |Gamma(v)*P(v,z)|. Both denominators are native lower bounds. Small response
amplitudes are never counted as improved control without this recovery cost.
The one finite observer is not an all-height quantitative bound.

The W X estimate establishes only limiting exponent >=1. When theta_q<1,
the absolute envelope / X^theta_q diverges. This proves that this particular
certificate method does not close the signed gap. It does NOT disprove the
candidate. All RH-strength uniform growth estimates remain unproved.

5. Exact signed all-scale forcing and counterfeit arithmetic
----------------------------------------------------------
Dirichlet inversion also gives an exact operator relation at every X>0:
 sum_{d>=1} F_K(X/d)=X^k K(X^ell).
Both the double arithmetic sum and its rearrangement converge absolutely:
sum_{d,n}(dn)^(-k) times a bounded kernel is finite, since k>1. The coefficient
of each product dn is sum_{n|dn}mu(n)=delta_1. This relation retains every
actual prime through the inverse sequence. It supplies a global signed equation,
but no contraction at exponent 1/2 follows merely from writing the equation.

A separate negative control adds +1 to every coefficient n>H, H=2^200.
The addition is enclosed at finite X by
 X^k sum |c|r^m X^(ell*m) H^(1-k-ell*m)/(k+ell*m-1).
It can therefore resemble actual arithmetic at every test scale. Its Dirichlet
series is 1/zeta(s)+zeta(s)-sum_{n<=H}n^(-s), with a simple pole at s=1.
A recovering bank cannot cancel this pole in every member because its exact
common-numerator identity is nonzero at v=(k-1)/ell>0. Hence a global cofinal
bound with eta<1 is false for this control, even when every finite test passes.
This counterfeit is explicitly not the constant-one inverse and is never used
as actual-prime evidence. The analytic obstruction is checked separately.

6. Independence limits
----------------------
Primitives, fitting exponents, grammar resources, certificate dictionaries and
selection rules are supplied before outcomes. Enumeration, response fitting,
moment nullspaces, promotion, bank assembly, and archive selection have no model
calls, source-paper access or expected-winner assertions. Named Riesz recovery
is a separate calibration of the grammar, not a nominated search answer. The
detector does not invent this Mellin identity or the RH equivalence theorem.
Complete tails encode actual inverse arithmetic at all scales using zeta in
its known absolute-convergence region; this is not new prime-distribution data.
Neither fresh finite success nor a proved exponent-one envelope proves RH.
