User direction and outcome
The priority is discovering structural operators that can yield signed arithmetic
control of ordinary zeta, followed by real analytical proof attempts. Historical
novelty and numerical optimizer improvements are secondary. This turn added and
ran a genuinely different structural grammar, with a proved inverse for every
admitted circuit. It produced eight selected formulas and three Sol6.1 analytical
reviews. No actual cofinal moment bound, global signed estimate or RH proof was
obtained. The overarching goal remains active and unresolved.

Why this family
Analytical continuation of the old commutator/prime/divisor potential candidates
proved useful identities and obstructions: past-only storage misses the fresh
source block; local prime laws and any finite set of correct prime signs permit
a source with positive linear mean; complete divisor-complement squares can be
bounded but do not remove their unknown core's signed drift.

A moving arithmetic-prefix average has a genuine uniform recovery theorem,
with c_m=sum_{j<=m}1/(j*d_j)+1/(m+1). However growing normalized banks become
small on high-input critical-norm tails, leaving their residual target-like.
That family was stopped BEFORE any actual arithmetic study. Its generic witness
checker has no AST/predicate/source linkage and does not certify candidate bounds.

The implemented new family instead acts DIRECTLY on the prefix sequence M(n),
through full DIVISOR-supported convolution, interleaved with arithmetic unit
signs. It includes fractional convolution orders +/-1/2,+/-1/3,+/-1/6 and exact
root/subtract-root indices. Some generated words use exact conjugation. Complete
arithmetic data/factorization are used, with no finite prime-bank truncation.
This input distinction is essential: convolving mu THEN prefixing gives a
different quotient operator and is not covered by this recovery theorem.

What analytical work actually established
The three selected-candidate reviewers read only frozen program/certificate/
obligation payloads, excluding numerical scores. Root later read their proofs
with numerical outcomes visible. All reviews and preparatory obstructions are
archived under analytical_reviews/. Their conclusions are prose mathematics,
not new full-moment admissions or a retrospective reranking.

1. Single residue kernels11/12 reduce to explicit mod5 quadratic-character
Euler factors (exceptional5 retained). They preserve the dangerous shifted-zeta
poles of the Dirichlet series of M. Phase28 has exact initial aliases; the
complete effect of a unit phase is a signed same-phase/flip-phase bank product.

2. Opposite unit prime phases at the same fractional order collapse EXACTLY
to a square-supported convolution, erasing their prime-phase information.
An intervening nonmultiplicative sign retains a precise prime-divisor defect;
the full defect square and cross term remain the cancellation obligation.

3. Interleaved42/34 admit exact Euler-core plus arithmetic-commutator splits.
The source phase at n-floor sqrt n advances through dense source values; root
boundary counts do not estimate it. Even a comparison sign with only logarithmic
adjacent jumps can have a persistent divisor commutator on arbitrarily late
inputs. This is a proved obstruction to a particular proof method, not a
counterexample to actual-Mobius cancellation.

4. Deep53/59 have exact complete divisor-path/Gram expansions. Their final
half-interval source tail has unit coefficient; at primes they retain signed
M(p) plus a bounded correction. Mod7 character defects and59's two commutator
remainders are explicit. The geometric phase in53 has a positive-density
divisor2 defect, so it also cannot be treated as sparse additive boundaries.

5. A general fractional majorant, derived independently in root/deep review,
improves all circuit norm/inverse bounds to zeta(1+epsilon)^B, B=sum|alpha|:
absolute kernel coefficients are bounded by generalized divisor d_|alpha|,
and full path convolution gives d_B. Every selected circuit has B<=1, hence
integer recovery exponent1 is valid. The original larger certificates remain
valid and unchanged; no same-formula reranking or search is performed. This
component theorem is not a signed arithmetic moment estimate.

Next structural direction, already proved but NOT yet implemented
dyadic_permutation_recovery_note.txt defines n=2^k+r and
 P(n)=2^k+[(r+2r^2) mod2^k].
It permutes each dyadic block because the difference factor1+2(x+y) is odd.
It has a constructive digit-lift inverse and preserves each complete dyadic
moment exactly. Inside a convolution circuit it changes the proper-divisor-only
dependency pattern and creates fresh within-block arithmetic interactions.
Weighted-L2 recovery stays uniform on closed dyadic horizons, with its full
explicit factor. A solitary outer permutation is an energy alias, not a lead.
Future implementation must load complete blocks, retain inverses/complements,
and generate such maps by general reversible rules rather than favor this one
example. No permutation candidate was included in this completed study.
