AUTOMATIC CUTOFF CONTROL: NONLINEAR PAYMENT AND JOINED SIGNED WORK

Status: reusable conditional proof rules verified; actual signed work OPEN.
Only this new memo was written. No source tables, numerical observations,
tests, previous-file edits, or hashes were used. The kernel and transition
identities are the reviewed mathematical primitives of Review022. The rule
does not prove the current Mobius estimate or RH.

1. Nonlinear maximum lemma, including its prefix quantifier

Let E_n>=0 be finite real numbers for n>=1 and suppose, for n>=2,
 E_n<=E_(n-1)+a_n E_(n-1)^r+b_n+R_n,
 a_n,b_n>=0, 0<=r<1. Let U be an integer and put
 M_U=max_(1<=n<=U)E_n, A_U=sum_(2<=n<=U)a_n,
 B_U=sum_(2<=n<=U)b_n.
Suppose EVERY prefix ending at V<=U satisfies
 sum_(2<=n<=V)R_n<=C U^beta, C>=0.                       (1.1)
The empty prefix is zero. No lower bound or pointwise sign on R_n is needed.
Summing the recurrence to a maximizing V gives
 M_U<=E_1+A_U M_U^r+B_U+C U^beta.
With T=E_1+B_U+C U^beta, either M_U<=2T or
 M_U/2<A_U M_U^r. Hence
 M_U<=max(2T,(2A_U)^(1/(1-r)))
     <=2T+(2A_U)^(1/(1-r)).                             (1.2)
For r=0 use the conventional M^0=1 even at zero; alternatively prove that
case directly. A_U=0 and U=1 cause no exception. The proof never uses the
desired upper bound for E_n. It is a valid nonlinear recovery/payment rule.

A bound only for the terminal sum at U is insufficient: a large intermediate
positive forcing may be canceled by a later negative forcing. The planner must
bind (1.1) for all prefixes, or supply an equivalent maximum-partial certificate.

2. The current finite-shift kernel and its exact payment

Use theta=2/3, sigma=9/20, and the complete centered kernel
 D_theta(x)=sum_(k<=x)k^(theta-1)-x^theta/theta, x>0.
The sum is zero below 1; its centering term is not. Integral comparisons give
 |D_theta(x)|<=1/theta for x>=1 and
 |D_theta(x)|=x^theta/theta for 0<x<1. Thus its global weighted squared norm
 is at most
 B=[1/(theta-sigma)+1/sigma]/(2theta^2)=100/13.
The Mellin isometry in the inherited rule uses measure dt/(2pi |s|^2),
 s=sigma+it. The complete centered kernel for zeta(s+1/3)q^-s consequently
 has full squared norm <=B q^-2sigma. This includes all lower-tail and
analytic-residue compensation; ordinary coefficient-moment convergence is
not being substituted for that centering.

For the exact head-corrected cutoff word
 F'_U=zeta(s+1/3)[A_U(s)^2 E_U(s)^3-c_U^3 sum_(d<=U)d^-s],
 q=U+1, the transition is
 F'_q-F'_U=J_0+mu(q)J_o+mu(q)^2 J_e,
 J_0=-c_U^3 zeta(s+1/3)q^-s.
Review022 supplies the complete J_o,J_e, including all finite-head terms and
residue updates. The J_0 term is present even when mu(q)=0.
With |c_U|<=2 its full squared norm is at most
 D q^-2sigma, D=4^3 B=6400/13.                           (2.1)

Let E_U be the OLD-head low-height energy of F'_U. On precisely that common
integration interval its zero-sign work is
 W_0(q)=integral [2 Re(F'_U conjugate(J_0))+|J_0|^2].
Cauchy, using only the norm in (2.1), proves
 |W_0(q)|<=2 sqrt(D)q^-sigma sqrt(E_U)+D q^-2sigma.        (2.2)
In particular its upper bound is noncircular. The rest of the exact common-
head work is the JOINED signed integral
 R_q=integral [mu(q)C_1+mu(q)^2 C_2],
 C_1=2 Re((F'_U+J_0)conjugate(J_o)+J_o conjugate(J_e)),
 C_2=2 Re((F'_U+J_0)conjugate(J_e))+|J_o|^2+|J_e|^2.
Do not independently take absolute values of these components. Their target
is a one-sided cumulative/maximum-partial upper bound, not a positivity claim.

3. All moving shells and an explicit current-scale bound

Use the exact cutoff-dependent head H_U=2 U^(45/2). The child's added shell is
 S_q=integral_(H_(q-1)<=|t|<H_q)|F'_q(s)|^2>=0,
 so exactly E_q-E_(q-1)=W_0(q)+R_q+S_q.                 (3.1)
An old-source shell bound alone cannot pay S_q. Review022's constant-factor
length extension pays the child's RAW shell by O(q^-1/16); the full centered
child head has norm O(q^11/10). Triangle therefore gives
 0<=S_q<=C_shell q^11/10, EVERY q>=2,                   (3.2)
after enlarging a fixed uniform constant. This is the corrected shell cost,
not the unchanged raw high-tail saving. All q=2 and other finite-head cases
are covered by that theorem's uniform constant, not by observations.

Apply (1.2) with r=1/2,
 a_q=2 sqrt(D)q^-sigma,
 b_q=Dq^-2sigma+C_shell q^11/10.
For U>=1, decreasing-function summation gives
 A_U<=2 sqrt(D)/(1-sigma) U^(1-sigma),
 B_U<=10D U^1/10+C_shell U^21/10.
If every joined-work prefix up to U is <=C_R U^beta, then
 M_U<=2E_1+20D U^1/10+2C_shell U^21/10+2C_R U^beta
       +16D/(1-sigma)^2 U^[2(1-sigma)].                 (3.3)
For beta=12/5 the last exponent is 11/10, and every paid exponent is strictly
smaller than beta. A valid explicit uniform conclusion is
 M_U<=[2E_1+20D+2C_shell+2C_R+6400D/121] U^12/5.         (3.4)
The head-corrected U=1 word is exactly zero by mu(1)=1, A_1=E_1(arithmetic)=delta,
so its low-energy base is zero. The abstract rule keeps E_1 for other matched
words, and a finite base can always be carried explicitly. The coefficient
state E_U(arithmetic) and the energy E_U must be distinguished in metadata.

Thus automatic recognition and kernel payment of J_0 genuinely removes a
formerly unnecessary unsigned forcing premise. Only the COMPLETE joined odd/
even work remains open, subject to the stated shell and base certificates.

4. Equivalence scope and stronger optional controls

For the current parameters, the cumulative joined-work target is equivalent,
up to uniform constants, to the original all-integer low-energy goal. One
direction is the unconditional implication (3.3). For the other, assume only
for this converse that E_V<=C_E V^beta for all integers V. The exact recurrence
and S_q>=0 give, for every V,
 sum_(q=2)^V R_q=E_V-E_1-sum W_0(q)-sum S_q
              <=E_V+sum |W_0(q)|.
By (2.2) the latter work sum is
 O(U^[beta/2+1-sigma])+O(U^[max(1-2sigma,0)](1+log U)).
When beta>=2(1-sigma)>0, it is O(U^beta); at equality the first summation
is a power sum, not a new logarithmic loss. Here beta/2+1-sigma=7/4<12/5.
For the forward direction the shell prefix must ALSO be O(U^beta), as it is
here. This parameter check is a proof gate. Outside it, do not advertise
equivalence or a paid J_0 recovery merely by naming cumulative work.

A consecutive bound R_q<=C q^(beta-1) is a sufficient but stronger option.
The global E_V bound does not imply such small consecutive increments.
Likewise, a fixed-ratio endpoint bound alone does not cover every cutoff.
A sufficient dyadic bridge is, for EVERY dyadic B and B<V<=2B,
 sum_(q=B+1)^V R_q<=C_block B^beta.                      (4.1)
Complete blocks and the last partial block give every global prefix by the
geometric series, with constant at most C_block/(1-2^-beta) when beta>0.
At beta=12/5 one may use the rational upper constant 4C_block/3. For beta=0
this argument instead pays an additional log U; it does not give a bounded
all-prefix sum. The planner must track that log-degree change. It may search
consecutive, cumulative, or maximum-partial work controls, but should not
force the strongest version as the definition of success.

The equivalence result is not an independently proved signed arithmetic gain.
It makes the missing forcing explicit and pays the J_0 sector without assuming
the target. Actual Mobius cancellation in R_q still needs a valid theorem or
an OPEN uniform lemma. Finite passes cannot supply it.

5. Safe automatic finite-polynomial kernel recognition

One sound larger class is
 J_0(s)=zeta(s+u) sum_l gamma_l c^i_l a^j_l b^k_l v^ell_l,
 a=1/q, b=q^-s, |c|<=2, q>=2,
 where all monomial exponents are nonnegative integers. Suppose v is a SCALAR
head coefficient with a proved |v(q)|<=q^(d-1), integer d>=1. Then triangle,
kernel dilation, and (2.1)'s general kernel primitive give
 ||J_0/s||<=sqrt(B) sum_l |gamma_l|2^i_l
                    q^[ell_l(d-1)-j_l-k_l sigma].       (5.1)
All residues and unit/lower-tail conventions accompany this bound. If v=d_d(q),
the complete d-fold divisor coefficient, its elementary bound d_d(q)<=q^(d-1)
is valid: freely choose the first d-1 ordered factors, each between 1 and q.
For d=0, v=d_0(q)=0 at q>=2, so terms containing v vanish. The constant monomials
without v remain. Other sequences need an independently bound source rule;
their name or formal degree is not a proof of |v|<=q^(d-1).

Let N_q be the right side of (5.1). Always use
 |W_0(q)|<=2N_q sqrt(E_(q-1))+N_q^2.
Apply the maximum lemma with A=2 sum N_q and B=sum N_q^2 plus the ACTUAL shell
bound. There is no need to choose a leading exponent if a finite sum yields a
better exact symbolic cost. If N_q<=C q^kappa is used, the nonlinear contribution
has power 2(kappa+1) when kappa>-1; at kappa=-1 it has log degree 2; when
kappa<-1 it is bounded. Sum N_q^2 has its own harmonic boundary kappa=-1/2.
The planner must compare every resulting exponent and log degree with its
generated target before declaring payment. A higher-degree head J_0=-c^m
d_d(q)zeta(s+u)q^-s, bounded in this crude way, has kappa=d-1-sigma and induced
power 2(d-sigma). It may be UNPAID even though the present d=1 rule is paid.
That is a limitation of the majorant, not a disproof of the arithmetic target.

Reject automatic use of (5.1) if v is a Dirichlet POLYNOMIAL rather than a
scalar, if J_0 retains A/E or extra unbounded zeta factors, if a denominator
depends on a possibly zero state, if exponents are negative, or if its purported
zero-sign coefficient still depends on the formal mu(q) variable. For a signed
inverse-divisor head, first expand its true dependence on mu(q) and repartition
J_0,J_o,J_e; then prove the surviving scalar bound. Cancellation of meromorphic
poles must remain complete before using a centered-kernel norm.

6. Provenance and final scope

This is an analyst-supplied GENERAL lemma and kernel recognition class. A
detector can compose it automatically with its compiled exact transition,
search control types and exponents, verify recovery and shell costs, and emit
the remaining joined-work leaf. It is not an automatic proof of that leaf,
and no preferred source candidate or fitted constant has been nominated.
The planner must retain source binding, complete analytic residue updates,
all integer/prefix quantifiers, finite base, target exponent/log degree, and
the distinction between symbolic payment and finite diagnostics.

The full original signed core remains authoritative. A paid optional component
or equivalent cutoff control is not a proof that every remaining source sector
is bounded. Whole-core coupling may be more effective than independent
component bounds. All arithmetic uniform leaves and global/RH claims stay OPEN.
