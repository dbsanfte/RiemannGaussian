INDEPENDENT PERIODIC PROFILE MASS AND FINAL COPY LEFTOVERS (060)

The admitted rules are verify_identity(cert,meter=None) and
verify_copy_bound(cert,meter=None). No discovery producer is imported, no
scientific nullspace or winning vector is enumerated, and no scientific source
table or kernel value is evaluated. Canonical rational/schema helpers and the
charge-only Meter are reused from the immutable052 independent checker.
Synthetic tests use the different prime mask [3,7], direct W expressions, and
tiny independently factored ordinary-Mobius convolution sums.

Source and finite-to-universal identity
--------------------------------------
Let p<q be registered actual primes. W(v)=floor(4v)-3floor(2v)+2floor(v).
The pair (W(v/p),W(v/q)) is in {-1,0,1}^2. The signed profile indicator vector,
ordered P,Q,D,S, has one nonzero entry: P has (A,0), entry A; Q has (0,B),
entry -B; D has (A,A), entry A; S has (A,-A), entry A. Pair (0,0) gives zero.
These are the complete actual source profiles and kernel signs, not fitted
labels. Each function is a right-continuous step function whose breakpoints
lie on the quarter grid. In coordinate z=4v their common integer period is
L=4pq. All n=0..L profiles are reconstructed by integer floors.

For canonical rational x_i and c_b on distinct positive divisors b of L,
every jump n=1..L is checked exactly:
 sum_i x_i*(f_i(n/4)-f_i((n-1)/4)) = sum_(b|n) c_b.
The right side is the jump of sum_b c_b floor(4v/b). The head value is zero.
Summing the jumps gives sum_b (L/b)c_b=0; the checker also computes this
zero drift explicitly. Thus both sides are periodic and equal on the full
quarter grid, on each intervening cell, and at every inclusive jump endpoint.
Periodicity extends the identity to every real v, not just sample values.

Finite ordinary-Mobius convolution and smooth count
---------------------------------------------------
For fixed prime mask M, put mu_M(d)=mu(d) if gcd(d,product(M))=1 and zero
otherwise. The exact local identity mu_M*One=indicator_M_smooth follows from
divisor summation: an outside prime contributes 1-1=0, whereas each registered
prime has only the unit mu_M coefficient and any nonnegative valuation is
allowed in the One factor. The integer1 is M-smooth and must be retained.

Therefore, for every integer N>=2, the signed weighted profile mass is
 sum_(d<=4N) mu_M(d)*sum_i x_i f_i(N/d)
   =sum_b c_b*sum_d mu_M(d) floor(4N/(bd))
   =sum_b c_b*Psi_M(4N/b).
This is a finite exact identity. It needs no conditional Dirichlet convergence,
analytic continuation, or cancellation theorem. The joint original source can
be extended by zero through d<=4N: if its token is nonzero then d<=4N/p, and
every term for d>4N also vanishes. Individual floor terms must NOT separately
be truncated at 4N/p; they can have a longer horizon and cancel outside the
joint token horizon. Unit d=1 and all q-range exits are included.

If k=|M|, every M-smooth integer <=Y has each prime exponent at most log2(Y).
For Y>=1, Psi_M(Y)<=(1+log2(Y))^k, while Psi_M(Y)=0 for Y<1. Since b>=1,
the mass is bounded by sum_b |c_b|*(1+log2(4N))^k, uniformly in integer N>=2.
This bound is on the signed weighted COUNT, not the total source matching cost.

Uniform token magnitude and copy conservation
---------------------------------------------
The actual normalized magnitudes are
 rgamma(1-alpha)*integral_eps^tau G_M(t)*f_profile(t)*d^-t*t^(-alpha-1) dt,
 eps=min(tau,N^-gamma), 0<alpha<1, gamma>0, 0<tau<1.
All source profile magnitude factors are nonnegative and <=2. For d>=1 and
t>0, d^-t<=1, and G_M(t)<=product(M)*t^k because 1-l^-t<=t*log(l)<=t*l.
Writing s=1-alpha in (0,1), Gamma(s)>=e^-1/s implies rgamma(s)<=e*s<3.
The complete magnitude of every token is consequently <=6*product(M)/(k-alpha).
Neither the unit nor any range exit requires an omitted exceptional magnitude.

The copy certificate requires four positive integer weights w_i<=16 and the
profile coefficients of the proved identity to equal exactly those weights.
Each token of profile i becomes w_i equal signed copies of magnitude m_i/w_i.
Every legal opposite-sign pair removes one positive and one negative copy,
so preserves the signed copy COUNT, regardless of profile or magnitude.
After a COMPLETE global opposite-sign residual scan, only one sign remains.
Its count is the absolute initial signed weighted profile mass. Each remaining
copy has magnitude <=[6*product(M)/(k-alpha)]/min_i(w_i). Hence final leftover
cost is uniformly at most
 [6*product(M)/(k-alpha)]*sum_b|c_b|/min_i(w_i)
     *(1+log2(4N))^k.
Both constants and degree are replayed exactly. Partial scans do not satisfy
this implication. Splitting into copies preserves the original signed sum.

What remains unpaid
--------------------
All already-paired absolute difference costs remain OPEN. The rule gives no
bound on their sum, no full matching-cost estimate, no original signed target
growth proof, and no RH proof. Different profile weights can worsen pairing
costs; no dominance over the unweighted policy is inferred. For example equal
opposite original magnitudes may cancel for weights1,1 but leave nonzero
paired-plus-residual cost with weights1,2. The all-N paid statement concerns
only final leftover cost under the specified complete-copy scan mechanism.
