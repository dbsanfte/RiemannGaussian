Concrete mathematical progress from Sol6.1 reviews
Three new analytical memos are archived under analytical_reviews/, with a
consolidated analytical_review.json. Reviewers used program/semantic payloads
and excluded numerical response scores; root subsequently checked their
derivations with access to the numerical report. These are mathematical prose
proofs and deductions, not formal proof-assistant certificates or another audit.

1. Selected mutation64 is the state
   U(t)=mu(t)*1_{floor(t/2)>=1}M(floor(t/4))
         -mu(max(1,floor(t/2)))*M(floor(t/2)).
   Its complete even/odd paired square has a mixed coefficient
   2[1_{n odd}mu(n)^2-mu(n(2n+1))]M(floor(n/2))M(n).
   The coprime identity exposes a precise weighted polynomial-Mobius correlation.
   All diagonal terms remain. This is a concrete signed lemma obligation;
   unweighted cancellation alone would not bound its dependent prefix weights.

2. For selected31/53, let R=floor(X^(1/3))>=2 and let P(t)=pi(R)M(floor(t^(1/3))).
   Exact cubic fiber counts prove
     X^-2 sum P(t)^2 <=12[pi(R)/R]^2 E(R).
   E(R) is covered by two smaller dyadic energies. The coefficient pi(R)/R
   tends tozero by elementary finite sieving and the harmonic product argument.
   This supplies a smaller-scale induction ingredient without assuming RH.
   Main-bank energy, covariance and full transport still need estimates.

3. For53, the square of its child mask-jump state is bounded by E(X)/2.
   Its full boundary correction retains both gained/lost coprime sets and
   every old/new prime-mask cross term. The bound supplies a parent-energy
   cost, not the negative transport credit needed to absorb it.
