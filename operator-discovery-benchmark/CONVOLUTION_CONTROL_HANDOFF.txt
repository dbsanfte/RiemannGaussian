The new language

Let c=mu*lambda, C(x)=sum_(n<=x)c(n), A_C(X)=sum_(X<=n<2X)C(n)^2,
and E_C(X)=A_C(X)/X^2. Search fixed nonnegative rational combinations V(X)
of 54 features, while keeping the complete signed innovation

  E_C(2X)+V(2X)-E_C(X)-V(X).

Six features come from COMPLETE divisor-diagonal energies: R_h=S_h-A_C or
R_diff=S_tau-S_h, normalized by X^2 ceil(X^eta), eta=0,1/6,1/3.
Here h=|c|=2^omega and tau=One*One. Forty-two features are normalized squares
of C(t)+k*C(floor(t/d_X)), with signed rational k and either a fixed divisor
or max(2,floor(X^(1/r))), r=2,3,6. Six more use three coupled prefix terms.
Every child scale recomputes its own floors, divisors and normalizers. The
underlying rational-power evaluator has broader semantics than this finite
pilot grid. Future grammars need new frozen protocols, not edits to a sealed run.

LP is a proposal mechanism for the nonnegative weights, not a certificate.
Proposed weights are rationalized and all constants refitted with exact
arithmetic. Every alias, failed proposal, missing computation and unknown
exponent constant is retained. The producer uses an explicit prime sieve;
prior coefficient prefixes overlap. No unseen-prime holdout is claimed.

Why this is a valid RH-directed language

If V is finite and nonnegative, an ALL-dyadic innovation bound

  exists q0>=1; for every integer q>=q0 there is a finite Cq>=0;
  for every dyadic X>=1: innovation <= Cq ceil(X^(1/q))

telescopes to A_C(X)=O_q(X^(2+1/q)), with the finite initial value and
2/(2^(1/q)-1) cost charged. The complete Mellin continuation of
sum c(n)n^-s=zeta(2s)/zeta(s)^2 then excludes ordinary zeta zeros to the right
of 1/2; symmetry gives RH. Finitely many q or scales do not prove the premise.
The proof text supplies this conditional argument, not a formal machine proof
or an admission of the missing inequality to the legacy theorem registry.

The mathematical audit also proves that critical C energy makes every one of
the 54 features subpower. Thus every fixed candidate's existential cofinal
innovation assertion is equivalent to critical C energy. These coordinates
may expose an easier proof; introducing them does not add prime information.
No converse RH=>this precise C-energy condition is asserted here.

Actual arithmetic facts and counterexamples

Actual coefficients satisfy c(p^j)=2(-1)^j at EVERY prime, c*h=Unit,
c*tau=IndicatorSquares, and tau=h*IndicatorSquares. The second full divisor
forcing is consequently dependent on the first. Exact square-root control of
the filtered sum is real but leaves recovery of ordinary C uncontrolled.
Complete prime-exponent boxes have signed sum +/-1, but converting the
n<=x boundary to boxes returns sum lambda(n)M(floor(x/n)); its signed boundary
still needs a bound. All intermediate remainders and forcing costs survive.

Generic h=|c|, positive inversion and sign involution do not suffice: the
one-prime sequence c(2^j)=2(-2)^j has cubic prefix energy while obeying them.
Changing one prime beyond any finite observed horizon preserves those generic
identities and matches the finite prefix, yet can introduce an off-critical
pole. Actual prime-power normalization is essential to any universal proof.
Pure root-index thinning also needs its dimensional recovery cost; retaining
the unthinned E_C prevents it from masquerading as ordinary cancellation.

New general admission rule from the analytical reviews

The separate arithmetic_filter_pole_budget.py extension defines an actual
target a=mu^{*r}*lambda^{*l}, k=r+l>=1, and a sparse fixed filter
G(x)=sum_(j=1)^m w_j A(floor(x/d_j)). Its Dirichlet series is

  P(s)*zeta(2s)^l/zeta(s)^k,  P(s)=sum_j w_j*d_j^(-s).

Merge duplicate divisors and remove zero weights first. A nonzero m-term P
has zero multiplicity at most m-1, by the derivative Vandermonde matrix on the
distinct log(d_j). Every ordinary zeta zero in Re(s)>1/2 produces a pole of
order at least k in the actual target. Thus m<=k guarantees that filtering
cannot erase it. A cofinal critical energy bound for the COMPLETE filtered
prefix G alone would imply RH, even without a controlled inverse recovering
the original A energy. The estimate is still an unproved premise.

This is a general coefficient-independent grammar rule: c=mu*lambda admits
every nonzero two-term fixed filter; mu*mu*lambda admits every three-term one.
The gate rejects zero filters, moving divisors and unsupported m>k cases. An
unsupported case is not a mathematical refutation; other proofs may admit it.
The example (1-3*4^(-s))^k has k+1 terms and a k-fold zero in 1/2<Re(s)<1,
showing why the uniform multiplicity allowance cannot simply be increased.
It does not assert an actual zeta zero at that point.

The proof has an independent analytical review, and five focused contracts
passed. Sources, tests, calibration examples and review are preserved at
convolution-control-results/pole-budget-001. This is a conditional semantic
certificate, not a formal machine proof or an automatically discovered bound.
It was added AFTER the potential pilot; it did not affect that pilot's selection.
The next frozen search can use this gate to admit more aggressive filtered
energies without keeping an unthinned base term when the gate applies. Root-
dependent moving filters still need another bridge, such as the verified W>=E
potential construction. A family varying with q requires an explicit per-q
admission and the original complete cofinal quantifiers.

Classical input: ordinary Dirichlet convolution and Mobius identities,

Research priority after the focused pilot

Alongside the fixed-filter admission rule, a useful extension is a grammar for arithmetic pairings and
their exact leftover sums: prime-power exponent moves, coprime masks, common
divisor regroupings, and scale-dependent partitions. Search for a signed
boundary or mixed-moment inequality, not merely another filtered identity.
For example, nonzero adjacent c(p^(2j-1)m) and c(p^(2j)m) terms cancel when
p does not divide m; a cutoff leaves a precisely enumerable boundary. A
program must carry that entire boundary and its cost through composition.
This pairing grammar is a proposed next extension, not implemented in this pilot.

Require a certificate of nonnegativity or exact reconstruction for each new
production, an ordinary-energy recovery statement with all costs, and a
separate unproved estimate obligation. Keep algebraic identities, conditional
bridges, observed finite successes and proved global inequalities distinct.
