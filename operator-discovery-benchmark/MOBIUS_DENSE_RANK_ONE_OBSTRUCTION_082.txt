1. Finite rank-one dual LP, directly implementable

z in[-1,1]^groups satisfying Rz=0 and F_i z=0 for every delayed i>=1, and
maximize F_0 z. This is a linear program with geometry-only coefficients.
The negative objective branch is obtained by z->-z; objective signs do not
use actual mu. A nonzero exact feasible vector supplies a certificate:

 lambda_g=z_g^2, nu_gh=2z_g z_h.

Exact feasibility follows2|z_gz_h|<=z_g^2+z_h^2. For every incidence correction

Hence the box-dual lower bound equals L(A0)=H00(F0z)^2, with no box penalty.
This remains valid for arbitrary theta, including u-dependent theta, and
any roster of nonnegative Gram features. The certificate bypasses numerical
LP dual rounding: rationally reconstruct z, check every constraint exactly,
then form lambda/nu. If reconstruction fails, retain the original numerical
proposal only. Do not round and silently tolerate moment errors; their
box penalties can dominate the purported lower bound.

Ordinary box dual and this rank-one cone are different admissions; report
both. Failure of the rank-one LP does not show the completion price is small.

2. Linear independence lemma for rational floor profiles

Let distinct positive rational kappa_1,...,kappa_J be fixed. If

then allalpha_j=0. Proof: choose a positive common denominatorB with
kappa_j=A_j/B, distinct positive integerA_j. Put t=1/(Bx), so the asserted

Duplicate kappas are first coalesced, and kappa0 profiles are omitted.

6. What this should feed back to the detector

Finite geometry-only convolution moments are not enough to price actual
signed cancellation using the independent group-box relaxation. They leave
an explicit formal covariance with orderu source mass, even though actual
Mobius satisfies all those moments. Increasing fixed profile counts,
coefficient boxes, memory or positive Gram roster does not remove the proven
fixed-roster obstruction. The LP should output a moment-null formalz and,
when feasible, a rational interval construction certificate, rather than
suggest tuning anotherC.

A genuinely different next language must impose signed arithmetic information
not represented by these fixed moments: for example growing profile rosters
with independently controlled geometry/price, genuine nonseparable divisor
incidence constraints carried jointly, or additional multiplicative/cofactor
constraints with a proved uniform strength. Adding allprefixK=1..N recovers
mu uniquely but is not itself a noncircular norm estimate. Prime-fixed rows
change the feasible covariance set and require a new audit; their presence
must be explicit, not inferred from actual-prime diagnostics.

This review proves a relaxation-price obstruction for the fixed082 grammar.
It does not refute the actual arithmetic bound, provide an RH counterexample,
or prove infeasibility of an unrestricted arithmetic/SOS proof system.
