PRIME/COFACTOR STATE DISCOVERY AND COMPLETE-PREFIX BOUND RULES
Stage035 independent analytical review. All earlier files remain unchanged.
No source values, scores, numerical experiments, old tests or hashes were used.

Status
------
The prime-jet grammar can discover globally valid arithmetic states that finite
base sections did not close. It does not inherit the radix block-sum induction
from stage034. This review gives an executable local-operator bound search,
complete prime-cutoff recurrences and recovery/cost rules. It also proves an
actual-source obstruction to uniformly paying every rough companion separately.
The original full signed core and RH remain open. No successful new arithmetic
formula is supplied as an output for the detector.

1. Authoritative source schema
------------------------------
Inputs are MULTISCALE_BOUND_DISCOVERY_HANDOFF.txt, OCCUPANCY_TRANSFER_HANDOFF.txt,
CORE_SIGNED_PRIMITIVE_SEARCH_HANDOFF.txt and the accepted moment-region parent.
The source is the ORIGINAL full core, not the old prime-power-zero a sequence:

 u=1/3, v=2/3, beta=9/2,
 nu0=K*(mu*mu), nu1=K*(mu*mu_v), nu2=K*mu_v,
 K(n)=n^-u, mu_v(n)=mu(n)n^-v,
 f=beta nu0-beta nu1-R_u nu2-beta mu.

All four modes have unit one. Thus f(1)=-beta-R_u, not zero. R_u is the
existing fixed positive source scalar beta[ zeta(4/3)/zeta(2/3)^2
 -1/zeta(2/3) ]. Source binding carries its exact definition and already
verified sign/bounds; a fitted decimal value is not a rational source identity.
No transport error or inherited opposite drift is silently deleted: this is a
direct alternative bound on f. Transfer to the accepted parent is separate.

For p prime set z=p^-1/6, a=z^2, and use t to count the p valuation. The
four local generating functions are
 F0=(1-t)^2/(1-at), F1=(1-t)(1-a^2 t)/(1-at),
 F2=(1-a^2 t)/(1-at), Fmu=1-t.                         (1.1)
These follow directly from the supplied Dirichlet-factor ASTs. They are the
source verifier's primitive semantics, not proposed discovered companions.
All denominators are nonzero for |t|<=1, since 0<a<1.

At valuation e=0 each coefficient is one. At e=1 they are
 a-2, a-1-a^2, a-a^2, -1.
At e>=2 they are respectively
 a^(e-2)(1-a)^2,
 a^(e-2)(a-1)(a-a^2),
 a^(e-1)(a-a^2), 0.                                  (1.2)
The first three coefficients obey ell_(e+1)=a ell_e only for e>=2.
The exceptional first/repeated transition cannot be replaced by powers of the
fresh-prime multiplier. Units, squares and all higher valuations are included.

The selected source normalization w=(beta nu0,-beta nu1,-R_u nu2,-beta mu)
has output row (1,1,1,1), unit (beta,-beta,-R_u,-beta). Local multiplication
is diagonal with (1.2), unchanged by the constant source weights.

2. Discoverable state and universal closure interface
-----------------------------------------------------
Start with the source output row. Generate a prime-section jet by taking a
declared coefficient of z in the row multiplied by the local valuation-e
diagonal. Rows are added only after exact independence checking. Samples may
propose a rational polynomial dependence; they never certify it for all primes
and cofactors. Record states as ('prime_jet', valuation, degree, parent), with
the full original scalar in their ancestry, rather than naming a successful
companion in the generator specification.

After a basis B is found, certify B and its inverse, target recovery and unit.
The complete valuation law is
 T(z,t)=B diag(F0,F1,F2,Fmu) B^-1.                      (2.1)
It includes the infinite tail, not merely the finitely observed jets.
The law means v(p^e m)=T_e(z)v(m) on the EXACT domain p prime, p not dividing m.
Repeated adjunction to the same prime uses the valuation history in (1.2),
or the complete generating function. A fresh-prime law cannot be applied to
p-divisible m. An AST with several distinguished primes retains distinctness,
each local valuation and its coprimality predicates.

An independent verifier recompiles the original factor ASTs and compares
rational functions after clearing denominators, with their nonzero domain.
Equality of all underlying mode-coordinate coefficients is a sufficient
universal proof even if the scalar mode basis has additional unknown relations.
Sample rank is not an arithmetic independence proof. Treat R_u symbolically,
or prove every needed specialization side condition. Do not assume it rational
or algebraically independent. Denominator cancellation is valid only with
the displayed side conditions. Coefficient equality extends to every valuation
because the rational series agree, not because an arbitrarily large finite jet
was inspected.

Suggested proof object fields: source AST and constant bindings, state DAG,
jet provenance, basis/recovery, unit, local rational matrix, fresh-prime domain,
valuation-tail recurrence, denominator nonvanishing, search limits, rejected
closures, and independent identity verdict. These are source-law certificates.
They do not set any whole-prefix or RH payment flag.

3. Exact complete-prefix and moving-cutoff semantics
----------------------------------------------------
For fixed p define H_p(x)=sum_(m<=x,p not dividing m) v(m), zero for x<1.
Then exactly, including real x and all integer endpoints,
 F(x)=sum_(e>=0)T_e(p) H_p(x/p^e).                    (3.1)
Only e<=floor(log_p x) contribute. This is not a digit partition into equal
blocks. Its e=0 term has the SAME argument x and an unpaid p-free source.

For a fixed finite prime set P, let H_P be the full prefix restricted to
numbers having no prime factor in P. Unique factorization gives
 F(x)=sum_(d<=x,d P-smooth)T(d)H_P(x/d),
 T(d)=B diag(nu0(d),nu1(d),nu2(d),mu(d))B^-1.           (3.2)
There is no requirement that two cofactors on a quadratic pair be coprime.
All common prime powers, repeated valuations, units and output crosses remain.

An executable growing-cutoff recursion is also exact. Let H(x;y) restrict
every prime factor of m to exceed y. If p is the next prime after y, then
 H(x;y)=H(x;p)+sum_(e>=1)T_e(p)H(x/p^e;p).             (3.3)
The base is H(x;y)=v(1) for x>=1 and y>=x, and zero for x<1. This is
well founded: e>=1 decreases x; the e=0 branch increases the cutoff and
reduces the remaining number of primes<=x. The initial full prefix is H(x;1).
An induction certificate must give a potential/ranking for BOTH variables.
An omitted e=0 branch, all-prime frontier, valuation tail or finite unit is
a changed target. A fixed finite P does not represent the full source beyond
the first excluded prime. The argument must state when its cutoff covers the
entire evaluated horizon.

For prefix-square energy, expand (3.1) before any magnitude:
 E_Q(F;X)=X^-2 sum_(n=X..2X-1) sum_(e,j>=0)
     H_p(n/p^e)^t T_e^t Q T_j H_p(n/p^j).              (3.4)
Both argument endpoints and EVERY inter-valuation cross occur. Replacing this
by sum_e ||T_e H_p||^2 is false without a proved comparison cost. A chosen
metric, a changing basis and a moving prime cutoff cannot erase these crosses.
Equivalent formulas hold for (3.2) with all d,d' labels. Their full Gram is PSD;
that fact alone gives no small upper bound on its target quadratic form.

4. A genuine executable operator-bound attempt
---------------------------------------------
The detector can search a rational Hermitian Q, exact target-domination cost c,
and scalar rational envelope kappa(z) for the complete transfer (2.1).
Fix sigma>0 and define ||F||_(sigma,T,Q)^2 as integral_1^T
 F(x)^*QF(x) x^(-2sigma-1) dx. Put r=p^-sigma=z^(6sigma).
Under logarithmic Fourier transform, causal dilation F(x/p^e) has symbol
r^e xi^e, |xi|=1. A sufficient all-phase operator certificate is
 T(z,r xi)^*Q T(z,r xi)<=kappa(z)Q for EVERY |xi|=1.    (4.1)
The polynomial/rational identity, real/imaginary xi constraint and denominator
positivity can be checked by exact SOS, Bernstein subdivision or a conservative
coefficient majorant. The search is on Q/envelopes and complete local matrices,
not on fitted observed prefix slopes. Clear fractional exponents with a common
denominator and state the exact positive z range.

Proof of the truncated rule: extend H_p*1_(x<=T) by zero also below one.
Its causal dilation sum agrees with F on x<=T. Global logarithmic Plancherel
and (4.1), followed by restricting the output back to x<=T, give
 ||F||_(sigma,T,Q)^2<=kappa(p)||H_p||_(sigma,T,Q)^2.    (4.2)
There is no unproved pole crossing here. The input is a finite step function.
The operator constant, target recovery and norm convention are explicit.

This is a paid COMPONENT implication, not a bound on its input H_p.
Metrics Q_p changing between prime steps require comparison Q_next<=c_p Q_old
or the corresponding exact transport of forms. Product c_p/kappa_p costs and
the final frontier norm are in the certificate. Singular Q is allowed only
with exact target recovery on the entire admitted space; dropping a target-
visible eigenmode is invalid. One can certify target recovery as a PSD block
matrix or ell ell^t<=cQ, rather than an observed-value comparison.

For step-extended integer prefixes and coefficients bounded on [1,N], take
T=N+1. On each cell [n,n+1), the weight is at least
(N+1)^(-2sigma-1). Thus sum_(n=1..N)|ell F(n)|^2
 <=c(N+1)^(2sigma+1)||F||_(sigma,N+1,Q)^2.
Divide by the declared block X^2 and retain the full unit/head. The discrete
conversion is not a free equality between the logarithmic and block norms.

Inverse removal is also searchable. For example the full-mode inverse symbols
are (1-at)/(1-t)^2, (1-at)/[(1-t)(1-a^2t)],
(1-at)/(1-a^2t), and 1/(1-t). At r=p^-sigma<1 their absolute coefficient
mass is finite. It gives a rigorous inverse bound, but the prime product of
these costs is never treated as one. Partial sums and infinite tails must be
distinguished: the prefixes below one vanish, whereas absolute norm majorants
sum the complete local series.

5. Exact failure gates for local-only contraction
-------------------------------------------------
For every positive-definite coefficient metric, fresh adjunction has the
nu0 eigenvalue a-2, tending to -2. A uniform contraction below one fails by
testing that eigenvector. This is a formal-state obstruction; it is not a
lower bound on the actual source prefix.

The stronger full-valuation norm attempt (4.1) also has an exact obstruction.
At xi=-1 the nu0 eigenvalue is
 (1+r)^2/(1+ar)>1,
since (1+r)^2-(1+ar)=r(2-a+r)>0. Thus no strictly contractive Q recovering
that visible mode can satisfy (4.1). At sigma=1/2 its squared modulus is
1+4p^-1/2+O(p^-1). In particular kappa(p)<=1+C/p for a fixed finite C is
impossible for all large p in this grammar. A weighted coefficient probability
model may instead have factors 1+O(1/p), but that is a different norm and
does not identify actual integer-prefix covariance. This distinction is the
reason occupancy spectra and finite-state block spectra cannot be copied here.

Low-phase certificates are meaningful restrictions of (4.1), not full norms.
They leave the high-frequency complement, inverse/prime-cutoff product and all
domain kernels unpaid. A metric with target-root recovery alone cannot bypass
the omitted complement. An exactly rediscovered energy potential may still
be an endpoint restatement; require an independent endpoint price.

6. Actual-source obstruction to separate rough-companion payment
---------------------------------------------------------------
There is a stronger arithmetic guard than the formal eigenvalue test.
From (1.2), retaining all four source terms,
 f(p)=-R_u p^-1/3+(beta+R_u)p^-2/3.                   (6.1)
For p>=P0=[2(beta+R_u)/R_u]^3, f(p)<=-(R_u/2)p^-1/3.
At cutoff y=sqrt(x) the rough source has ONLY its unit and primes, since any
product of two primes>sqrt(x), including a square, exceeds x. Its prefix is
 H_f(x;sqrt(x))=f(1)+sum_(sqrt(x)<p<=x)f(p).
These terms eventually all have the same negative sign.

The needed prime lower count is elementary, not an unstated short-interval PNT.
For integer n, the central binomial coefficient is at least4^n/(2n+1).
For each p<=2n its valuation is sum_j[floor(2n/p^j)-2floor(n/p^j)], a sum of
zeros and ones. Hence p^valuation<=2n and binom(2n,n)<=(2n)^pi(2n).
For n>=8 this gives pi(2n)>=n log2/log(2n). With n=floor(x/2), a safe bound
 pi(x)>=c x/log x, c=log2/8, for all real x>=16 follows by monotonicity.

For the WHOLE energy block fix ONE cutoff y_X=sqrt(2X). Then for every
n in[X,2X-1] the rough support consists only of unit and primes>y_X.
For sufficiently large X with y_X>=P0 and sqrt(2X)<=cX/(2log X), the count
is at least cX/(2log X). Formula (6.1) yields uniformly on that block
 |H_f(n;y_X)|>=c R_u X^(2/3)/(4*2^(1/3)log X),
 E(H_f;X)>=c^2 R_u^2 X^(1/3)/(16*2^(2/3)(log X)^2).  (6.2)
The negative unit cannot reduce the lower bound. All valuations are covered
by the support argument; no omitted square or composite contributes there.

Therefore an induction demanding a cofinal critical bound on EVERY rough
companion is FALSE even for the actual source. The smooth/composite complement
must cancel macroscopic rough forcing, or its countercenter must remain joint.
This does not refute a cofinal bound for the full f. A detector must reject
separate-payment rules by this structural source guard before ranking their
finite observations. It must not reject a jointly coercive storage that retains
the compensating branch and exact recovery.

7. Reusable bound providers and calibration scopes
--------------------------------------------------
Actual divisor inversion supplies a nonlocal bounded harmonic state:
c(U)=sum_(n<=U)mu(n)/n, with |c(U)|<=2, all integers U.
If P is finite, coefficientwise prime-factor removal gives exactly
 C_P(U)=sum_(n<=U,gcd(n,prod P)=1)mu(n)/n
       =sum_(d<=U,d P-smooth)c(U/d)/d.
Thus |C_P(U)|<=2 product_(p in P)(1-1/p)^-1.           (7.1)
The finite prefix truncates naturally, while the bound uses the complete
positive geometric mass. This is a source-specific cancellation provider
that a local-factor/inverse compiler can apply after discovering the relevant
observable and proving the inverse identity. It is not a square-root prefix
bound for f; moving P pays its explicit product or a separately proved estimate.
It also does not resurrect a fixed-prime frontier omitted from the source.

A generic calibration of complete prime-prefix rules is finite-prime centering.
Define a multiplicative model agreeing with the given local factors at a finite
P and having coefficient one at all other primes. Its divisor kernel g has local
symbol (1-t)F_i(t) at P and one elsewhere. Here sum_d|g(d)| is finite, by (1.1).
Let lambda=sum_d g(d)/d. Then for ALL real x>0,
 F_P(x)-lambda x=sum_d g(d)[floor(x/d)-x/d],
 |F_P(x)-lambda x|<=sum_d|g(d)|.                       (7.2)
The d>x terms are included, not dropped. The mean, unit and complete tail can
be compiled from local symbols, so a search may discover a centering/metric
and receive a genuine global bound. The finite-P constants and mean are not
constants for growing P. At P empty the complete scalar is simply
f_P(n)=-beta-R_u, with a nonzero linear prefix. This rejects any rule claiming
that finitely validated prime laws alone imply a critical full-source bound.
Calibration outcomes should be withheld and evaluated only after freeze; a
rule provider is not a successful formula supplied to the state search.

Coefficient bounds are another valid baseline: |nu0(n)|<=d2(n), while
|nu1(n)|,|nu2(n)|,|mu(n)|<=1. With the inherited R_u<5,
|f(n)|<=(3beta+5)d2(n)=(37/2)d2(n). Their absolute prefix consequences
have a positive X energy power. They are reference bounds, not signed savings.

8. A richer complete arithmetic recurrence, not a sign proof
-----------------------------------------------------------
The local compiler can also take a logarithmic derivative. Universally,
nu_i(n)log n=sum_(p^e|n)lambda_i(p^e)nu_i(n/p^e), with
 lambda0=(a^e-2)log p,
 lambda1=(a^e-1-a^(2e))log p,
 lambda2=(a^e-a^(2e))log p, lambda_mu=-log p.
Hence B_i(x)=sum_(p^e<=x)lambda_i(p^e)F_i(x/p^e).
All primes, powers and cofactors remain; no extra p-free restriction belongs
inside this divisor recurrence. In the discovered basis it is a matrix law.

Negative generator coefficients are not coercivity. The logarithmic dilation
pair <F,D_pF> has Fourier real symbol p^-sigma cos(t log p), of both signs.
So a signed work certificate must prove the complete quadratic/kernel identity
or its phase/cone restriction and price its complement. Applying magnitude to
each generator returns the known absolute envelope. A claimed negative energy
credit based only on the sign of lambda is an invalid inference.

9. Recommended executable bound-search interfaces
--------------------------------------------------
(a) discover_prime_states(source,jet_limits): proposed state DAG/basis/relations;
    verifier(source,proposal): all-prime/all-cofactor closure or exact failure.
(b) compile_valuation_operator(verified_basis): rational T and complete history.
(c) search_operator_form(T,sigma,Q_dictionary,envelope_dictionary): exact
    all-phase Q/envelope/target-domination certificate, or algebraic obstruction;
    no prefix payment unless its input/frontier is independently available.
(d) compile_rough_induction(T): equation (3.3), two-variable ranking, unit,
    finite valuation endpoints and target root. Search coupled forms/weights
    with every cross, metric conversion and rough/smooth countercenter included.
(e) inverse_mass/finite_prime_center/log_generator: generic proof primitives
    with typed norm domains, complete tails and explicit cutoff-dependent costs.
(f) recover_target(proof_DAG): original root, full integer block, all centers,
    units and inherited transfers. Reject cycles, endpoint restatements,
    unpriced prime products and unsupported whole-prefix leaves.

An exact conditional bound output is useful even when it fails: retain the
discovered source law, successful component operator estimate, precise unpaid
frontier/correlation and structural rejection witness. A local spectrum never
sets the global flag. Finite arithmetic covariance measurements can test a
generated joined hypothesis on preserved data, but cannot supply its all-scale
constant or recovery. Larger archive coverage is not proof exposure.

The next genuinely different arithmetic target is a coupled rough/smooth
recurrence that cancels the forced sector (6.2) BEFORE absolute pricing, while
keeping every valuation and the two-leg prefix kernel. The current grammar
can compile/search such certificates; neither its feasibility nor an RH bound
is asserted here. This review supplies reusable rules and failure gates, not
a nominated winning construction or an isolated omitted-complement theorem.

10. Detector-generable prime-frontier rejection certificate
----------------------------------------------------------
The source f in section6 is one application, not a hardcoded detector gate.
Input a discovered real scalar state's EXACT prime-value polynomial
 P(z)=sum_(d>=0)c_d z^d, z=p^-1/6,
its exact unit u, and its full prime-factor support semantics. The following
algorithm produces a reusable obstruction certificate for an uncompensated
rough prefix on a horizon N=2X-1:

 1. Prove each preceding coefficient zero and select the first coefficient
    c_d0 proved nonzero. Provide its sign and a rational lower bound L>0 for
    |c_d0|, plus rational upper bounds B_d for |c_d|, d>d0. Existing beta/R_u
    identities, sign domains or verified enclosures may provide these facts.
    An enclosure containing zero is NOT a nonzero certificate. If an earlier
    coefficient is unresolved, selection is unresolved. If P is identically
    zero, this gate proves no obstruction.
 2. Let r=d0/6, S=sum_(d>d0)B_d. For S>0 choose rational
    z0=min(1,L/(2S)); for S=0 use z0=1. On 0<z<=z0,
    |P(z)-c_d0 z^d0|<=S z z^d0<=L z^d0/2.
    Thus P has that constant sign and |P(p^-1/6)|>=L p^-r/2
    for p>=max(2,ceil(z0^-6)). Integer ceiling and all floors are exact.
 3. Fix the SAME cutoff y_X=sqrt(2X) for the complete energy block. For
    sufficiently large X it exceeds the sign threshold. The support rule
    proves that only unit and primes occur at every prefix n<=N. The explicit
    binomial proof in section6 gives at least cX/(2log X) such primes at n>=X,
    once sqrt(2X)<=cX/(2log X), c=log2/8. No PNT or RH is imported.
 4. If r<1 and |u|<=J, enlarge the finite head until
    J<=cL X^(1-r)/(8*2^r log X). Then uniformly for n in[X,N],
    |H(n;y_X)|>=cL X^(1-r)/(8*2^r log X),
    E(H;X)>=c^2 L^2 X^(1-2r)/(64*2^(2r)(log X)^2).     (10.1)
    If the unit has the same sign, the factor-two unit loss is unnecessary.
    The constant contains only certified parameter domains, not fitted data.

For r<1/2 this refutes any claimed cofinal subpower energy bound for this
UNCENTERED rough auxiliary. More generally it refutes a proposed power below
1-2r; at equal power logarithms must be compared carefully. At r>=1/2 it
does not refute the usual cofinal objective. For r>=1 the unit may dominate;
this recipe does not claim a diverging frontier. A unit-zero source needs no
unit head cost. Histories whose support is not prime-only at this cutoff
cannot use this gate without a separate exact support proof.

The output certificate contains the prime-value polynomial, source-state DAG,
zero/nonzero/sign proofs, L/B_d/z0, support proof, binomial count rule, unit
bound, finite-head inequalities and lower exponent 1-2r. The arithmetic
algorithm derives P from the discovered state and compiled local coefficient;
it is not given (6.1) as its desired answer. Truthful outcomes include
nonzero_lower_bound, no_obstruction and unresolved_coefficients.

Centering is explicit. Subtracting the exact prime-only profile makes this
rough branch zero, but adds that profile to the complementary branch. A
deterministic countercenter with a proved equal leading profile is also a
possible construction, with its exact residue/floor/error debts. Neither
operation pays the complete joined target. The gate does not apply to an
already compensated state without first checking its full prime/center support.

11. Infinite valuation tails and centered p-free transformation
-------------------------------------------------------------
In any verified constant basis, the full local matrix has the compact form
 T(z,t)=I+t T1(z)+t^2 T2(z)/(1-a t), a=z^2.           (11.1)
The mu entry of T2 is zero before change of basis, and T_e=a^(e-2)T2 for
every e>=2. This is a generic tail compiler result from the rational series,
not an extrapolation of observed prime powers. For a norm exponent sigma>0,
the complete absolute matrix-coefficient bound is
 1+||T1||p^-sigma+||T2||p^-2sigma/(1-a p^-sigma),
with each indicated induced norm proved in the admitted metric. A singular
metric needs kernel invariance as well. Changing basis/metric costs remain.

Let H_p^c=H_p-C_p and let C be the selected full-source center, both defined
also for x<1 according to their stated convention. The exact centered rule is
 F-C=T H_p^c+D_p, D_p=T C_p-C.                        (11.2)
Consequently (4.2) gives, for eta>0,
 ||F-C||^2 <=(1+eta)kappa ||H_p^c||^2
              +(1+1/eta)||D_p||^2.
The entire deterministic defect D_p is an output obligation. An eigenvector
or local pole cancellation does not license D_p=0 on integer cells.

For illustration of the required bookkeeping, take C_p(x)=m x^kappa for
x>=1 and zero below one, kappa>0. Let E=floor(log_p x) for x>=1. Then
 T C_p(x)=x^kappa[ T(z,p^-kappa)m
                     -sum_(e>E)T_e p^(-e kappa)m ].
For E>=1 the tail matrix is exactly
 T2 p^-2kappa (a p^-kappa)^(E-1)/(1-a p^-kappa).
For E=0 add T1 p^-kappa and use T2 p^-2kappa/(1-a p^-kappa).
Thus even matching the infinite Euler/main coefficient leaves a finite-prefix
tail, including the head x<p. If the target center is a staircase rather than
x^kappa, its floor discrepancy is an additional retained term. Moving prime
cutoffs multiply these transforms and their defects; only a verified joint
identity may cancel them. Exact infinite tail cost, finite support and original
source recovery are separate certificate fields.

Final status: the proposed search can discover actual arithmetic closure,
attempt complete operator/coupled-induction bounds, and automatically reject
false separately paid rough states. No full signed estimate is established by
this review. Source-law and local component successes remain distinct from the
OPEN joined rough/smooth covariance and from the original RH goal.
