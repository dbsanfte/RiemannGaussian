FLOOR RESIDUAL ARGUMENTS043: WHOLE NORMS, EXACT REFINEMENT, OPEN CONVERGENCE
Independent proof/admission scope. Frozen earlier sources remain unchanged.
No arithmetic coefficient archive or earlier successful suite is used.

1. Known criterion context and the exact target family

For integer n>=2 define, with zero extension beyond1,
 f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)), 0<x<=1.
On the cell (1/(k+1),1/k] its exact value is (k mod n)/n. It is bounded
between0 and(n-1)/n. A target program is a FINITE rational sum g=sum c_n f_n;
its residual is e=1-g on(0,1]. Natural-index fractional-part approximation
is established Nyman-Beurling-related territory, not a claim of a newly
invented RH criterion. Primary context: Luis Baez-Duarte,
'A strengthening of the Nyman-Beurling criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis',
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202141 . Only the following elementary one-way
zero obstruction is needed here; no converse or RH-dependent convergence
result is imported.

Indicator terms I_q=1_(x<=1/q) may appear in affine forcing programs and their
norm certificates. They are NOT freely admissible in an RH-bearing target.
Their Mellin response is q^-s/s, generally nonzero at a zeta zero. The checker
requires target.steps=[]; otherwise it verifies only a forcing/diagnostic
program, with its modified zero response explicitly exposed.

2. Mellin source guard and zero response

For Re(s)>1, Tonelli/absolute integration gives
 integral_0^1 floor(1/x)x^(s-1)dx=zeta(s)/s,
 integral_0^1 floor(1/(nx))x^(s-1)dx=n^-s*zeta(s)/s.
Thus M(f_n)(s)=(n^-1-n^-s)zeta(s)/s. The left side is holomorphic for Re(s)>0
because f_n is bounded and its log-weighted derivatives are locally dominated.
Analytic continuation extends the identity there; the apparent pole at1 is
removable because the factor vanishes. No reciprocal zeta series or ordinary
critical Möbius moment is asserted.

At an actual zero rho with sigma=Re(rho)>1/2, every finite target has
 integral e(x)x^(rho-1)dx=1/rho.
With the complex Hilbert inner product this is <e,x^(conj(rho)-1)>=1/rho.
The testing function has squared norm1/(2sigma-1). Cauchy-Schwarz proves
 ||e||_2^2 >= (2sigma-1)/|rho|^2.
Therefore ANY actual sequence of these target norms tending to0 excludes all
zeros to the right of1/2. The usual zeta zero symmetry would yield RH. This
source guard survives every finite coefficient choice: a fixed finite
projection or zero-erasing auxiliary cannot make the zero response disappear.
No finite norm improvement proves the required sequence limit.

3. Whole continuous norm admission, not a head fit

A general forcing/norm program may have
 e(x)=1-sum c_n f_n(x)-sum d_q I_q(x), q>=2.
Its whole squared norm is the positive infinite series
 sum_(k>=1)e(k)^2/[k(k+1)].
The first H cells have exact rational residues. Once H>=every step q, the
remaining pointwise absolute bound is
 M=|1-sum d_q|+sum|c_n|(n-1)/n.
The entire unobserved tail is between0 and M^2/(H+1), because the cell measures
telescope exactly. This proof uses no observed tail sign or extrapolated law.
It includes x tending to0; ignoring this part is not a global norm certificate.

Independent replay uses a minimum heap of each floor term's next integer
wrap event, not the producer's dense event sieve. It recomputes the exact
integer lattice and accumulates with at least256-bit Arb, validates the
producer's authoritative exact dyadic endpoints, and verifies inclusion
of the full analytic tail range. A finite decimal display ball must overlap
its exact interval but is diagnostic only: printing rounds its radius
independently, so it need not contain the exact endpoints. Ranking and proofs
use the verified exact endpoints, never this display text. Work is O(H+sum floor(H/n)log support),
with O(support) heap memory. A supplied head/whole zero ball is rejected unless
it truly encloses the independently priced quantities. All domain, tail,
coefficient, support and false-convergence flags are bound to the certificate.
The certificate is a theorem for ONE exact fixed program, not a theorem about
support increasing, a recipe family, or the original arithmetic source.

4. Exact dilation and the complete signed refinement

For q integer>=2, D_q g(x)=g(qx), using zero extension. Direct floor subtraction
proves, at every x including the jumps,
 D_q f_n=f_(qn)-f_q/n; D_q I_s=I_(qs).
A change of variables proves ||D_q e||_2^2=||e||_2^2/q. D_q1=I_q; it is NOT1.
The different unit/indicator term is essential to every residual refinement.

Given independently compiled target programs g_N and g_(qN), and rational b,
let h_N=1-g_(qN)-b I_q+b D_q g_N. The checker compiles its forcing program
with floor coefficients g_(qN)-b lift(g_N) and indicator coefficient b. Then
 e_(qN)=h_N+b D_q e_N,
 ||e_(qN)||^2=||h_N||^2+(b^2/q)||e_N||^2+2b<h_N,D_q e_N>.
All mixed signs and the entire global inner product are retained. No sampled
cross product pays this identity's forcing or its limit. b=0 gives h_N=e_(qN)
and is an exact endpoint restatement, explicitly labeled as such.
For b nonzero the identity is still not itself a new bound: a forcing or signed
correlation provider must be independently proved at EVERY relevant scale.

5. Conditional contraction gates and the exact missing quantifiers

Put t=b^2/q. A relative-forcing proposal is the OPEN leaf
 ||h_(N0*q^j)||^2<=tau||e_(N0*q^j)||^2, EVERY integer j>=0,
one fixed recipe, fixed q,b,tau. Finite training/evaluation records do not
license this quantifier. Triangle gives a contraction if sqrt(t)+sqrt(tau)<1.
A radical-free exact gate is
 A=1-t-tau>0 and A^2>4t*tau.
For t,tau>0 choose Young parameter y=A/(2t). The squared contraction price is
 rho=t+tau+A/2+2t*tau/A<1.
For t=0 use rho=tau; for tau=0 use rho=t. Thus the OPEN leaf implies
 ||e_(N0*q^j)||^2<=rho^j||e_N0||^2, which tends to0. It is CONDITIONAL,
not a proved global approximation. Moreover it is EXCLUDED for pure floor
recipe families with maxsupport<=N, as explained below, rather than a viable
OPEN RH route. At b=0 this leaf directly states the
endpoint contraction and is not a discovered auxiliary mechanism.

An absolute-forcing gate uses fixed y>0 and rho=(1+y)b^2/q<1. If the OPEN
all-iteration bound ||h_j||^2<=A tau^j, 0<=tau<1, were proved, then
 E_j<=rho^j E0+(1+1/y)A sum_(i=0..j-1)rho^(j-1-i)tau^i.
Both rates below1 imply convergence; at rho=tau the resonance factor j is
retained rather than erasing it. More generally rho<1 and ||h_j||->0 suffice,
by splitting the geometric convolution into a finite initial segment and
its uniformly small tail. This needs the full epsilon/eventual-scale
quantifier, not merely decreasing finite observations.

These geometric-rate proposals are negative calibrations for the root's
linear-support families. Jean-Francois Burnol, 'A lower bound in an
approximation problem involving the zeros of the Riemann zeta function',
Theorem1.3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0103058 , proves an unconditional
positive asymptotic lower bound for log(1/lambda)*D(lambda)^2. Each pure f_n
is a combination of fractional-part functions with parameters1/n and1.
Thus maxsupport<=N places g_N in B_(1/N), and its residual satisfies
liminf log(N)*E_N>0. Along N=N0*q^j this excludes geometric energy decay,
including a polynomial-in-j times geometric rate. If RH fails the distance
remains positive, so this use is not RH conditional. It does not exclude
slower convergence, or supergeometric support growth. The relative and
absolute gates retain their correct logical implication but report this
imported theorem boundary explicitly. They cannot be promoted on finite fits.

The new primary conditional leaf is instead
 ||h_N||^2<=A/L(N)^eta, L(N)=1+floor(log2 N)=N.bit_length(),
 EVERY N=N0*q^j, integer N0>=1, eta rational in(0,1], A>=0.
With t=b^2/q<1 and t>0, choose y=(1-t)/(2t). Young gives
 rho=(1+t)/2<1, B=(1+1/y)A,
 E_j<=rho^j E0+B sum_(i=0..j-1)rho^(j-1-i)/(1+i)^eta,
since q>=2 implies L(N0*q^i)>=1+i. For b=0 use rho=0 and B=A
(the exponent0 at the last convolution term means1). This tends to0 by
splitting the convolution into a finite initial segment and a small tail.
No geometric rate is claimed. eta<=1 does not contradict the imported
1/log support lower bound. At b=0 this is an endpoint requirement, not an
auxiliary cancellation discovery. A=0 would force the excluded geometric
case and cannot provide actual linear-support convergence.

The checker admits only OPEN provider status for these new leaves. It has no
rule that turns a fitted ratio, finite table, bounded-coefficient norm, or
literal caller flag into a proved global convergence result. Every output
keeps convergence_proved and RH_proved false.

6. Exact fixed-finite-stencil obstruction: three cells, not a free vector

Suppose a fixed finite head dictionary {f_n} and finitely many q>=2 give
 h=1-sum a_n f_n-sum b_q I_q, with arbitrary coefficients. Let L=lcm(head n)
(L=1 for empty head). Choose M=L*ceil(max(2,max q)/L). On the three cells
k=1,M,M+1, each f_n has values1/n,0,1/n, and each indicator has values0,1,1.
Consequently
 h(cell1)+h(cellM)-h(cellM+1)=1.
The three cell measures are1/2,1/[M(M+1)],1/[(M+1)(M+2)]. Weighted
Cauchy-Schwarz yields the exact coefficient-independent gap
 ||h||^2 >=1/[2+M(M+1)+(M+1)(M+2)]
         =1/[2(1+(M+1)^2)]>0.
This is a source-function identity, not a counterexample that forgot the
actual floor representation. It also proves1 is outside the finite span of
those head and indicator functions.

A fixed affine recurrence e_next=h+T e with bounded T and h nonzero cannot
converge to0: otherwise ||h||<=||e_next||+||T|| ||e|| would tend to0. For
coefficients varying within a fixed finite dictionary, the same positive gap
applies uniformly; a uniformly bounded operator family still cannot drive
the target residual to0. Signed cross terms cannot evade this exact identity.
The root's growing-support recipes are NOT fixed dictionaries: their M and
lower gap change with N and the lower gap may tend to0. This guard must not
be mislabeled as a refutation of growing recipes, a universal obstruction to
Nyman-Beurling approximation, or an RH refutation. Every guard output states
'growing_dictionary_refuted:false'.

7. Meaning of the detector experiment

Searching rational corrections, dilations and logarithmic/vanishing forcing
gates against whole continuous norm certificates is a finite proof attempt
on the new residual. Geometric relative/absolute gates are negative
calibrations, excluded for the chosen linear-support family by Burnol's
lower bound. A finite logarithmic-forcing pass is only evidence for the OPEN
all-scale leaf. Successful admission
requires an independently verified recipe identity and a uniform argument
for that forcing/correlation leaf. A fixed-stencil impossibility or failure
of a finite coefficient bank is scoped to that bank. It does not choose a
winning auxiliary by assumption or turn known criterion equivalence into a
proof of convergence.
