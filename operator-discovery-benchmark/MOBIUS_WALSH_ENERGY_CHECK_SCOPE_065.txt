Let chi_n(x) be the product of remaining prime signs dividing squarefree n;
chi_1=1 and chi_a chi_b=chi_(ab/gcd(a,b)^2). For each nonzero row q_j choose
any positive rational w_j; empty rows have weight exactly zero. Expand
E=sum_j q_j^2/w_j. Diagonal pairs retain their constant contribution; distinct
pairs occur twice. All rows are added before absolute values. Thus E is
nonnegative on every sign assignment, and its constant Fourier coefficient
is nonnegative. Write E=A_1+sum_(n>1) A_n chi_n, with zero terms removed.
The raw bound is L=A_1+sum_(n>1)|A_n|.

For a cycle C of at least three distinct nonconstant terms with product of
indices reduced by gcd equal to1, product_(n in C) chi_n=1. If product of
original signs sign(A_n) is -1, the preferred signed characters
sign(A_n)chi_n cannot all be +1. Their sum is at most |C|-2. Allocate positive
rational mass t_C to each member, with total allocations at n no larger than
|A_n|. Split each original coefficient into these allocations plus its
nonnegative residual capacity. Applying the cycle inequality and triangle
inequality to residual terms gives E<=L-2 sum_C t_C. Cycles may overlap;
capacity accounting uses every original coefficient, so shared terms cannot
be spent twice beyond their capacity. This is a deterministic pointwise
bound, requiring no independence or randomness of prime signs.

Weighted Cauchy gives
 (sum_j |q_j|)^2 <= (sum_j w_j)(sum_j q_j^2/w_j)
                     <= weight_mass*energy_upper.
A nonnegative rational variation_bound is admitted precisely when its square
is at least this product. All-zero rows require every aggregate and bound
zero. The checker reports both raw and cycle-improved energy bounds and
common all-free-plus/all-free-minus row-L1 witnesses. Witnesses are lower
bounds for any envelope valid for all remaining signs; their arithmetic
interpretation requires SAME-parent064 admission.

For fixed sign assignment, the parent's rows are affine in ratio r by064's
source/contraction proof. Their sum of absolute values is convex, hence its
maximum over the interval is bounded by the maximum of the two endpoint
variation bounds. We do not assert E(r) affine: squares and endpoint weight
choices need not extend as an affine energy. Endpoint convexity is applied
to the row-L1 norm itself. The parent's ratio interval, source binding and
actual Mobius interpretation remain conditional on064 admission.

A useful sufficient UNIFORM obligation would be: for a declared admitted

prove for every kappa>0 a constant C_kappa independent of required N,y such
that at BOTH endpoints

       <= C_kappa^2 N^(1+2*kappa).
Then short variation <= C_kappa N^(1/2+kappa) uniformly; combine the paid
