Target and admission boundary
The target is the complete original actual-Mobius energy E, with W=E+V and V
a sum of nonnegative weighted exact squares. The finite interpreter evaluates
each complete parent and child program in its own context, including W(1).
Its exact rational output and an independent matching output certify only the
saved finite cases. Mechanism annotations, successful finite inequalities and
selector exposure do not certify the cofinal-q universal inequality.

Conditional telescoping has a clear premise: one fixed program, some q0, and
for every integer q>=q0 a finite constant Cq independent of every dyadic X>=1
such that W(2X)-W(X)<=Cq*ceil(X^(1/q)). For X=2^K this implies
 E(X)<=W(1)+2*Cq*(X^(1/q)-1)/(2^(1/q)-1).
Thus nonnegative V and the initial W(1) must both be retained. A finite search
over q=2,3,6,12 neither establishes the cofinal quantifier nor permits choosing
a different program as X or q changes. Stored-constant failures refute the
stored finite claim; they do not rule out all future constants or programs.

What the new grammar contributes
The declared mutations change argument maps and factor placement in complete
ASTs. They can produce actual coupled signed states rather than another
coefficient fit to a fixed feature list. For example mu(floor(t/p)) inside a
prime sum is not the constant prime-domain factor mu(p)=-1. Nevertheless a
syntactic odd factor is not evidence of cancellation: coinciding arguments,
common factors, zero summands and source/domain restrictions can remove the
claimed variation. Unknown reductions must remain unknown. Top-level mechanism
labels describe syntax and proved elementary norm laws, not a solved analytic
mechanism. Finite grammar choices and mechanism quotas remain design bias.
In particular a top-level mask label can contain a coupled_sum, coupled_bank
or nonlinear core. The final selection rule uses that recognized core for its
structural slot and retains the mask annotation. Unclassified masks stay
conservative; the label never proves absence or presence of useful cancellation.

Candidate-generic analytic obligations
For U=sum_a c_a F_a, every diagonal c_a^2 F_a^2 and every pair term
2*c_a*c_b*F_a*F_b belongs to U^2. Placing a source factor inside one summand
changes these pair terms; separate diagonal estimates do not control them.
Products of unresolved states require the corresponding higher correlations
or a valid alternative estimate. Coincident Mobius indices can reduce squares
to squarefree gates, but cannot justify independence of the surviving terms.
A whole-state point factor has a norm-mask interpretation on actual Mobius
data while the other state can retain its original signed prefix correlations.

For a moving bank, use the union of the parent and child index sets. Separate
common indices with changed coefficients/arguments, added indices, and removed
indices. A nested prime bank still has old/new and new/new pair contributions;
an entry count alone does not bound them. A changed modulus generally gives
nonnested residue or coprime domains, requiring both gained and lost terms.
Source convolution or full-domain identities require their exact truncation
complements; a familiar identity with a different kernel supplies no bound.

One useful exact bookkeeping device is a parent-parameter extension Ubar_X(t)
to the entire child block, whose source/domain validity must be established.
Write K_X(t)=U_{2X}(t)-Ubar_X(t). Then the contribution of this square to DeltaV
is its frozen-parameter transport
 T_X=(4X^2)^-1 sum_child Ubar_X(t)^2-X^-2 sum_parent U_X(t)^2
plus (4X^2)^-1 sum_child [2*Ubar_X(t)*K_X(t)+K_X(t)^2].
This includes moving cutoffs, masks and argument maps; multiple states and
weights require their corresponding terms. It is an identity, not an estimate.

A prospective proof must close a signed drift estimate, for example by showing
DeltaE+T_X<=-A_X+F_q(X) and the complete transition correction<=A_X+G_q(X),
with A_X>=0 and F_q+G_q<=Cq*ceil(X^(1/q)) uniformly in dyadic X. Other valid
proof forms are possible, but an unknown energy on the right must be absorbed
with justified constants or supplied with an independent bound. Cauchy-Schwarz
against the desired target energy alone merely reintroduces the unresolved
estimate. A modulus/prime-count dependent constant is not Cq independent of X.
A fixed positive power loss is insufficient for all cofinally large q.

Standalone root-prefix squares with root exponent at most 1/2 are uniformly
bounded using |M(n)|<=n and the block normalization. Adding such bounded V
changes a cofinal DeltaE inequality only by a constant shift; that equivalence
does not prove either inequality. A root prefix mixed inside an unresolved
square retains its cross term, whose bound does not follow from bounded padding.
Pointwise ordering of potentials does not order their signed innovations.

Exposure and selection review
The fixed common validation set and separate candidate-specific diagnostics
prevent unequal diagnostic counts from changing the common failure ranking.
AST-only scale choice precedes responses. Effective exposure must respect both
parent X and fresh child 2X, and the complete t ranges in both blocks. Integer
root endpoint computations are appropriate; a clipped root may remain dormant
through several raw changes. Prime banks change at prime entry, not at every
raw integer cutoff. Coprime masks can depend only on the radical; other residue
masks need their own exact semantics. Zero or norm-zero bank terms can make a
raw bank entry ineffective. Unsupported semantics must be marked unknown,
and exhausted generation/exposure allowance must be marked unexposed.

Two extra bases can diagnose a first later regime within the declared horizon.
They do not cover every later selector transition, every prime, or cofinal X.
Even an exact effective-selector signature need not imply a different complete
candidate function or a new arithmetic estimate. Diagnostics should disclose
the syntactic/selector scope of their effectiveness claim. The separately
reported cases can inform subsequent analytic review without retrospectively
changing this run's training fits, shared ranking or final scale declaration.

Calibration quotas and exploration slots prevent small bounded or target-copy
states from occupying the entire batch. They do not establish that a selected
state beats the baseline or carries useful signed control. Baseline comparison,
unknown cases, aliases and bounded-padding status must remain visible. The
initial W(1) anchoring prevents selection from silently discarding initial cost.
Some validation/final bases are not dyadic. Their records correctly identify
this; using their failures in finite ranking is an additional design preference,
not a refutation of the exported dyadic claim.

Concrete source review
The generator contains genuine factor-in-summand, signed-pair, gcd-conditioned
and quotient-commutator mutations with full AST payloads and ordinal IDs. It
keeps the parent program intact, validates generated syntax, preserves attempts
and uses declared complexity/quota limits. The root runner evaluates original
complete E through the unchanged exact evaluator; it does not use annotations
to simplify arithmetic or drop components. Training files precede validation,
diagnostics remain outside common selection scores, and final programs and
constants are saved before final responses. All source controls are named and
the actual-Mobius scope of the semantic notes is explicit.

The independent semantic implementation uses its own factor, additive-term,
literal-common-point, prefix/root-envelope and prime-guard reconstruction. It
imports the independent unchanged validator, not producer semantic routines.
Lag identities state the divisibility domain; root envelopes are limited to
the unmasked or component square; source products and common masks preserve
mixed obligations. Metadata comparison is type-strict, so a Boolean cannot
replace an ordinal integer or false proof flag. Retained supplied metadata is
carried through reconstruction on the same meter. Agreement does not supply
an independent universal compiler or analytic proof.

Issues raised in review and resolved in root source before freeze:
- Radical signatures now apply only to an enclosing gcd(...,m)==1 predicate;
  numerical gcd values retain the full modulus.
- Exact root powers and multiplication have allocation preflights. A local
  selector-domain cap gives explicit unexposed coverage, while globally spent
  work/deadline limits propagate to the stage disposition.
- A planned regime must appear among actual diagnostic selector signatures;
  an observed context must also lie at or after its nominated transition. A
  transient skipped regime is not declared exposed from its threshold alone.
- Moving X roots outside recognized banks/masks and nested unsupported roots
  are recorded as raw or unknown maps, rather than silently disappearing.
- Recognized masked mixed cores retain their structural selection slots and
  mixed obligations instead of being reduced to calibration by the mask label.
- A complete CANDIDATE_LEADS receipt requires generation, discovery and final
  stage counters; an incomplete run retains only actually started stages.

Exposure remains a finite source-free signature plan. Prime entry and squareful
Mobius coefficients are accounted for, while further state cancellation is
explicitly unknown; a d=1 difference can still vanish. Outer max/min and exact
root endpoint ranges are evaluated. Generation/planner audit shares root code,
as declared; independent arithmetic and annotation replay do not remove that
limitation. Reviewed workflow/checker tests address these boundaries and false
component-to-whole promotions without executing them here.
