ELEMENTARY CUBIC OBSTRUCTION FOR A FIXED MU SCALAR TEMPLATE

Posthoc analytical criterion, written before reading pilot outcomes or weights.
No frozen source, certificate or selection is changed. This criterion refutes
only a particular fixed-weight critical SCALAR BUDGET, not actual RH, an actual
finite certificate, or all possible q-dependent/future templates.

1. Scope and exact cubic coefficient.
Use the exact budget extraction in grouped_arithmetic_budget_scope.txt. Fix ALL
shared structural weights and a fixed integer q>1. Put delta=1/zeta(2). Let
b_all,b_head,b_tail be the signed relation weights for norm family, multiplier
one, internal weight child_rowmass, in regions all, head n<=2X, tail n>2X.
Define the exact rational
 gamma=-(56/3)*b_all-12*b_head-(20/3)*b_tail.
Then the actual mu scalar budget satisfies
 B_q(X)=delta*gamma*X^3+o(X^3)
as X tends to infinity through integers (hence dyadics). No assertion about
nonconstant polynomial matching is needed for this scalar conclusion. It is a
necessary budget test for a uniformly sound instance of THIS fixed template.
If gamma>0, B_q is not O(X^(2+1/q)) for ANY fixed q>1. If gamma=0, this
criterion is unresolved: it supplies no next-order prime cancellation theorem.
If gamma<0 it rules out this positive cubic obstruction only; nonconstant
matching, nonnegative square weights and the actual budget bound still require
proof. No automatic global certificate is admitted in either case.

2. Head, tail and all rowmass norm sums.
Let Q(y)=sum_(n<=y)mu(n)^2=delta*y+O(sqrt y), with the explicit existing
uniform error<=3sqrt y. Define r_X(n)=sum_(t=max(2X,n))^(4X-1)t.
Exchange the COMPLETE finite sums. The all-region norm sum is
 T_all=sum_(t=2X)^(4X-1)t*Q(t)
      =delta*sum_(t=2X)^(4X-1)t^2+O(X^(5/2))
      =(56/3)*delta*X^3+O(X^(5/2)).
There are2X terms, each error at most3*t^(3/2), so the error bound follows
uniformly. The exact polynomial sum's lower-order endpoint terms are O(X^2).
For head n<=2X, all those indices appear in EVERY child prefix:
 T_head=Q(2X)*sum_(t=2X)^(4X-1)t
       =[2delta X+O(sqrt X)]*[6X^2-X]
       =12delta X^3+O(X^(5/2)).
Tail uses STRICT n>2X, hence exactly T_tail=T_all-T_head and
 T_tail=(20/3)*delta*X^3+O(X^(5/2)).
The head/tail boundary n2X is accounted for; replacing it by a different
endpoint changes lower-order terms but is not an allowed silent rewrite.
Multiplying by the signed weights and the minus sign in the budget yields
precisely delta*gamma. Overlapping all/head/tail descriptors are retained.

3. Other norm and normalizer terms are subcubic.
The mu norm weights1 andX give respectively O(X) andO(X^2). Since |J_X|<=2X,
the defect-kernel norm weight gives O(X^2); region restrictions only reduce
absolute counts. Unit constants are O(1). Square groups with zero constant do
not contribute at all. The sole constant-square weight gives
 a0 X^2 ceil(X^(1/q))=O_q(X^(2+1/q))=o(X^3) for fixed q>1.
This uses fixed finite weights. It does not assert uniformity if those weights
or q are allowed to change with X.

4. Prime-rowmass terms are o(X^3) without PNT.
For actual mu the prime_power constant is-1 at exponent1 and0 at EVERY higher
exponent. Thus only weighted actual prime sums contribute to its scalar budget.
An elementary Chebyshev estimate suffices. For integer n>=1, every prime in
(n,2n] divides binomial(2n,n); therefore
 theta(2n)-theta(n)<=log binomial(2n,n)<=2n log2.
For integer m, let n=floor(m/2). The interval(n,m] is controlled by the
binomial interval(n,2n] plus, when m is odd, at most the extra prime m.
Inductively this gives theta(m)<=4 log2*m: assuming the bound at n,
 theta(m)<=4log2*n+2n log2+log(m) for odd m,
while the even case has no extra term; log(2n+1)<=2(n+2)log2 for n>=1
leaves this below4log2*m. The initial cases m1,m2 are direct. Equivalently,
any standard elementary dyadic binomial proof of the same loose constant can
be used. Real x follows by theta(x)=theta(floor x)<=4log2*x.
For x>1, primes<=sqrt x are at mostsqrt x, while every larger prime has
log p>=(1/2)log x. Hence
 pi(x)<=sqrt x+2theta(x)/log x
       <=sqrt x+8log2*x/log x=o(x).
No prime number theorem, zero-free region, RH error or prime-sign hypothesis
is imported. Each rowmass weight is at mostsum_(t=2X)^(4X-1)t<=6X^2.
Thus any region's prime-rowmass scalar sum has absolute size
 <=6X^2*pi(4X)=O(X^(5/2)+X^3/log X)=o(X^3).
Other prime weights1,X,J similarly have absolute size at mostO(X^2) or smaller.
These estimates deliberately make no claimed signed main term or cancellation.

5. What an exact posthoc weight audit may conclude.
Once the frozen training archive is available, map each shared relation weight
to its EXACT descriptor ID, retain all programs and statuses, and compute gamma
with Fractions. Do not choose weights from final responses or discard positive-
gamma programs selectively. A finite certificate at development/final scales
can remain valid even when gamma>0: the asymptotic obstruction concerns eventual
critical scalar growth of that unchanged template, not those finite identities.
A training failure has no fixed accepted template to adjudicate. Every inferred
weight vector and its source hashes must be preserved in the posthoc record.
For gamma>0 the fixed template's all-scale critical budget is disproved, but
this does NOT refute the actual Mobius inequality or arbitrary future q-dependent
weight families. For gamma0/negative no global proof follows. In all cases an
all-scale nonconstant coefficient identity is a separate unproved obligation.
