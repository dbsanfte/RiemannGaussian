Exact completion, including all exits
-------------------------------------
For r in[p/q,1], define
 f_d(r)=1_(d<=y)*[W(N/(p*d))-r*W(N/(q*d))],
 W(v)=floor(4v)-3floor(2v)+2floor(v).
At each ratio endpoint the checker independently verifies
 f_d(r)=sum_j c_j(r)*floor(X_j/d)
for EVERY coprime integer H<d<=max(D,max_j X_j). This includes nonsquarefree
integers; there is no finite whitelist of unregistered prime factors and no
special assumption about a Mobius sign. Beyond this maximum, target and all
floors vanish identically. In particular any added horizon can introduce
nonzero rows AFTER the original token horizon D; those are checked, not lost.

For every coprime1<=d<=H, including d=1 and nonsquarefree rows with mu(d)=0,
the exact short residual is
 R_d(r)=f_d(r)-sum_j c_j(r)*floor(X_j/d).
Its COMPLETE absolute cost is priced conservatively. No arbitrary short row
is silently deleted, and H need not be at most y. The coefficient cost and
short residual cost at endpoint r are

The certificate budget must be exactly max(C_(p/q),C_1). Underpricing is
rejected, and a larger uncomputed budget is not substituted for exact replay.

------------------------
