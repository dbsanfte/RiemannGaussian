Additional exact simplification and next research direction

anchored_storage_cone_redundancy.txt proves an all-scale polynomial
reparameterization: set K'=K+G_child, G_parent'=G_parent+4G_child and G_child'=0.
All matrices remain PSD and the complete RHS and scalar charge are identical.
The initial cost changes explicitly. Thus the child PSD matrix is redundant
once the signed storage operator is present; this may improve future primal
conditioning but cannot enlarge the already separated certificate cone.
This identity is an algebraic optimization, not an arithmetic estimate.

Further fixed mixing of these same unnormalized channels will not defeat the
admitted anchored-cone separator. The next substantive investigation must
test an additional valid arithmetic constraint/localizer or a structurally
different uniformly recoverable coefficient/state law. Before expanding,
distinguish deficiencies of arithmetic closure from restrictions caused by
shared coefficient laws and scale-instance copies. Keep source completeness,
signed target recovery, exact scalar budgets and initial costs explicit.
One inexpensive, systematic diagnostic is affine completion: append the
constant unit atom to the entire regional/state bank and inspect the admitted
functional against that preregistered larger cone. A zero constant moment
forces its whole PSD row to vanish, so nonzero first atom moments would
invalidate the witness for affine completion. Missing moments must remain
unknown. This supplies no new prime-sign information by itself, and its
possible redundancy must be checked before another optimizer campaign.
Any finite survivor must then receive an analytic all-scale identity and
critical-budget proof for the required q family. That estimate remains open.
