Primitive-relation localizers against the complete affine state

This proposed language extends the publication-009 full15 affine storage
language. Publication-009 remains an exact finite separator for its declared
cone; its frozen evidence is unchanged. No result for the new cone follows
until every new column is included in an independent exact admission check.

For each positive integer t let R_f(t) be the SUM of all primitive polynomial
relations of family f in the following complete domains, in this order:
unit (the single x_1-1); norm (1<=n<=t); prime_power (actual primes p and
every exponent e>=1 with p^e<=t); squarefree_zero (every nonsquarefree n<=t);
inverse_row (2<=n<=t); coprime_product (2<=a<=b, ab<=t, gcd(a,b)=1);
gcd_product (1<=a<=b<=t, including diagonals and a=1).

No Mobius signs are inserted into these formal polynomials. Factor witnesses
certify norms and gcd indicators; actual primality certifies prime powers.
Every listed primitive relation vanishes at x_n=mu(n), hence R_f(t)=0 there
for EVERY integer t. This is exact arithmetic, not a prime-distribution
estimate or an assumed cancellation estimate.

Let U(t) be the existing complete affine state: the constant unit, the five
original linear prefix/mask coordinates, and all nine divisor contrast
coordinates. Every coordinate is retained. For each f, region r, and i=0..14
the new signed column is
    L_(f,r,i)(X) = sum_(t in r(X)) U_i(t) R_f(t),
with parent(X)=[X,2X) and child(X)=[2X,4X). There are 7*2*15=210 columns.
Each signed coefficient is one exact fixed number shared across every t and
X. Aggregating repeated primitive specs by adding their multipliers is an
exact distributive identity; pre-aggregation source coverage must still
record every t, every primitive spec, and every state multiplier. Empty
columns retain their positional IDs, and the auxiliary gamma row has 210
empty added groups.

The cancellation opportunity is concrete. The aggregate inverse relation
contains divisor-row sums, and coprime/gcd product relations connect product
moments to reduced indices. Multiplication by prefix, masks, and quadratic
divisor contrasts moves those identities into the same moment degrees as
the Gram/state cross terms. Prime-power rows also connect the actual prime
mask to the complete prime-power polynomial sum. They can impose missing
arithmetic compatibility on a separating moment functional. A signed group
requires its total dot product to vanish, not merely to have one sign.
Such a constraint can remove an earlier separator; it does not construct a
positive certificate. A missing witness coordinate is unknown, never zero.
First cancel coefficients exactly in the FULL new column; then inspect its
remaining support against the admitted witness domain.

This is a grouped finite subspace of the arithmetic relation ideal. It is
not the full ideal: each family is summed over its primitive domain and t,
and only the fifteen prescribed multipliers are allowed. Coefficients
cannot vary with t, with an individual primitive relation, or with X.
Individual arithmetic moment identities need not hold merely because
these 210 grouped identities hold. Their usefulness must be assessed by
complete exact finite admission, not by selecting favorable source cells.

The independent scalar ledger is formal coefficient extraction at the
constant monomial. The state constant vector is
    z=(1,0,0,0,0,0,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2).
This is distinct from the actual t=1 state. The aggregate relation constants
are respectively -1, -SF(t), pi(t), 0, 0, 0, 0, where SF(t) counts
squarefree integers <=t (including 1), and pi(t) counts actual primes <=t.
In particular prime-power exponents e>=2 are present but add zero constant.
Thus const L_(f,r,i)=z_i sum_(t in r(X)) const R_f(t). Product terms cannot
contribute a constant because polynomial indices are positive. An independent
ledger should also extract this coefficient directly from all expanded
relation columns, retaining original scalar groups and signed storage.

For fixed exact signed weights beta, |sum beta const L| is O_beta(X^2):
SF(t)<=t and pi(t)<=t suffice. The unit rows are O_beta(X), the four
zero-constant families contribute exactly zero, and the five zero-constant
state coordinates contribute zero for every family. No prime number
theorem, RH, or bound for a signed prime sum is needed for this scalar fact.
The old regional Gram constants and signed storage constants remain O(X),
including the exact -2X z^T K z transport term. All 366 original square
groups, 403 original relation groups, full15 regional blocks, typed signed
storage transport, and original target remain in force.

Under the OLD fixed-template scalar-growth hypotheses used for the cubic
gamma obstruction, these new O(X^2) charges cannot cancel a nonzero cubic
gamma term. The gamma=0 auxiliary constraint remains a conditional necessary
gate. This statement does not prove the old hypotheses, does not settle
the full nonconstant coefficient identity, and is not a sufficient bound.
Scale-dependent weights could invalidate the stated fixed-weight budget and
are outside this language.

Storage recovery and quantifiers are unchanged. A uniform innovation bound
still needs a single fixed admissible template (with its exact coefficients
and PSD matrices), the required estimates for all scales, a finite actual
initial storage cost, and the original q/budget quantifiers. A certificate
on a finite grid such as X in {1,2,3,5} is evidence only for those instances.
An exact separator on that grid excludes the declared shared finite cone
only. Neither outcome automatically proves a uniform bound or RH. This
file supplies arithmetic semantics and conditional scalar scope, not a
research witness, feasibility claim, witness score, or new search result.
