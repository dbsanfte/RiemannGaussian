ADMISSION RULES FOR THE GENERATED POST-MAPS

Root-written proof note. The two coarsening arguments were independently
checked in messages by the Sol6.1 transport reviewer before the run; this
file is the root's complete proof record, not that reviewer's final memo.
The separate CORE_ARITHMETIC_GROUPING_REVIEW_009.txt provides the first
frontier obstruction and the complete source conventions.

1. All17 base programs retain a singleton-prime frontier.

For n prime in(2X,5X/2], n exceeds every permitted stripping cutoff for
all X>=1024. The stripping step1or2 leaves n unchanged. Any other integer
with the same stripped key would be n times a nonunit integer and would
exceed4X-1. Thus its entire class is the singleton n. The complete source
AST combines before expansion and gives
 f(p)=-R_u p^(-1/3)+(beta+R_u)p^(-2/3), R_u>0.
The actual positivity follows also from zeta(2/3)<0,zeta(4/3)>0 and the
exact residue formula, independently of finite numerical evidence.
The complete half-step kernel on this band is at least3/(40X), for every
eta in[-2,2]. Weighted PNT therefore gives grouped absolute mass at least
c X^(1/3)/(log X)^2 for all sufficiently large X. This is incompatible
with every fixed polylogarithmic upper bound. No signed target is refuted.

The implementation derives exponent1/3 and its nonzero coefficient from
the source factor AST, not from a numerical growth fit. The proof template
would similarly reject a nonzero first-prime term with exponent lambda<1/2
under its singleton-support hypotheses, with power1-2lambda.

2. Eventual one-bucket post-maps.

Suppose the valuation step is1 and cutoff Y=floor(X^theta), theta>1/2.
After stripping, every prime factor of r(n) is>Y. For sufficiently large
X, (Y+1)^2>4X-1, so every residual on the COMPLETE support is1or a prime.
Removing its largest prime once/all, or its smallest prime once, sends
every residual to1. Thus all indices have one label and the majorant is
exactly |Delta W(X)|. This supplies no simpler arithmetic control: it is
the absolute version of the original full unresolved expression. Exclude
it from claims of a reduced grouping mechanism. It is not a refuted bound.
This argument does not apply to valuation step2.

3. Prime-only coarsening by sublinear-width interval maps.

Under the same theta>1/2, step1 assumptions, consider either
 floor(r^(1/k)), k=2,3,6, or floor(r/floor(X^a)), a=1/3,1/2.
For r comparable toX, the width of a complete key preimage is respectively
O_k(X^(1-1/k)) or O(X^a); both are o(X). Take primes in the interior band
 J_X=[9X/4,19X/8]. For sufficiently large X, every residual with the same
key as such a prime lies in(2X,5X/2). It is a prime, since the only other
possible residual is1. An original index n=d*r on that residual would
exceed4X-1 if d>=2. Thus the ENTIRE arithmetic class contains only actual
fresh primes in(2X,5X/2), not hidden smaller composites or old-head terms.

Every complete f(p) in these classes is eventually negative. Products
are positive and the child-minus-parent kernel has the lower bound above.
Summing the absolute group entries for just these classes is at least
 (3/(40X)) [sum_(p prime in J_X)|f(p)|]^2
 >= c X^(1/3)/(log X)^2, by weighted PNT. Grouping neighboring primes
together has not supplied opposite signs. These10 generated proof routes
are therefore rejected for cofinal claims before numerical scores.

The interior band is essential: classes touching2X must not be declared
prime-only. All constants here are asymptotic, independent of X, for a
fixed generated program. The result is not a prediction of finite failure
at the exploratory scales. Step2 and theta<=1/2 do not receive this rule.

4. Admitted programs remain unproved.

All other generic post-maps are retained as open constructions. Their exact
partition identity and triangle inequality neither bound their complete
grouped absolute sum nor exclude a further proof-route obstruction.
Actual core coefficients, all mode crosses, units, old-head terms and full
pair endpoints are preserved. Every selected candidate receives an explicit
uniform grouped-bound obligation with false all-scale/RH completion flags.
