1. Dirichlet units, active cutoffs and complete actual-source binding
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All products in this memo are Dirichlet convolution unless explicitly
called pointwise. The unit delta has delta(1)=1 and delta(n)=0 otherwise.
Put b0(n)=1, bv(n)=n^(-v), mu0(n)=mu(n), muv(n)=mu(n)n^(-v),
u=1/3, v=2/3, ku(n)=n^(-u). The exact complete inverse laws are
 b0*mu0=delta, bv*muv=delta.                            (1.1)
The second follows termwise from the first, since a pointwise n^(-v)
weight is a homomorphism for Dirichlet convolution. All prime powers,
common divisors and repeated valuations are included. A fake partial
source satisfying only finitely many prime laws is not a replacement
for the source binding in(1.1).

For a positive integer N and nonnegative rational cutoff exponents set
 U=floor(N^alpha), V=floor(N^gamma),
 A(n)=mu(n) if n<=U, else0,
 B(n)=mu(n)n^(-v) if n<=V, else0,
 R=delta-b0*A, S=delta-bv*B.
Nonnegative exponents ensure U,V>=1 and the unit of A and B is present.
For the stated FINITE weighted-quotient synthesis, require alpha>0
whenever the inverse power p is positive, and gamma>0 whenever q is
positive. An unused variable can be removed instead. If alpha0,p>0,
there are infinitely many retained powers r^i of weight0; the proposed
quotient is not finite-dimensional. A separate N-dependent degree cap
from support2 would then be necessary. Negative cutoffs can remove the
unit, make R(1)=1, and invalidate the inverse construction; reject them.

The full source of the current obligation is
 f=beta ku*(mu0*mu0)-beta ku*(mu0*muv)-beta mu0-R_u ku*muv,
 beta=9/2. This is the COMPLETE centered/preconditioned coefficient
source, with its unit, positive real-pole compensation and all four
modes already encoded. In particular f(1)=-beta-R_u. No mode or pole
channel is subsequently discarded. The transported source is g=f-e,
where e=f-g is the exact checked transport correction, not an estimated
or reconstructed replacement. Transport leaves the unit unchanged.

2. Strict support and exact rational-root cutoff convention
-----------------------------------------------------------
For n<=U the b0*A divisor sum includes all divisors of n, so R(n)=0
by(1.1). For n<=V the weighted inverse law similarly gives S(n)=0.
For the remaining integers one has the exact formulas
 R(n)=-sum_(d|n,d<=U)mu(d), n>U,
 S(n)=-n^(-v)sum_(d|n,d<=V)mu(d), n>V.                 (2.1)
These are signed truncated divisor sums. They must not be silently
replaced by independent nonnegative variables or their absolute bounds.

For alpha=a/b in reduced rational form, U is the unique nonnegative
integer with U^b<=N^a<(U+1)^b. Compute this using exact integer powers
and roots under the meter, rather than floating N^alpha. The analogous
rule defines V. Therefore U+1>N^alpha and V+1>N^gamma strictly, including
N1 and exact-power boundaries.

Every term of R^i*S^j has each R-factor integer at least U+1 and each
S-factor at least V+1. For i+j>0 its support is at least
 (U+1)^i(V+1)^j>N^(alpha*i+gamma*j).
Thus alpha*i+gamma*j>=1 implies support strictly GREATER than N.
The equality threshold is discarded safely. The word support means a
lower bound on every possibly nonzero coefficient; cancellations could
raise it further, but no assertion of exact first support is needed.

3. The unique finite inverse polynomial and its proof
-----------------------------------------------------
Let I be the monomial ideal spanned by r^i s^j with alpha*i+gamma*j>=1.
With positive active exponents its complement is finite. In the quotient,
r and s are nilpotent and (1-r)^p(1-s)^q is a unit. Its unique inverse is
 P_(p,q)(r,s)=sum_(alpha*i+gamma*j<1) c_p(i)c_q(j)r^i s^j,
 c_p(i)=binom(p+i-1,i) for p>=1,
 c_0(0)=1, c_0(i)=0 for i>0.                            (3.1)
For p1 the coefficients are1; for p2 they are i+1. The same definition
applies to q. An exact coefficient solver for
 (1-r)^p(1-s)^q P=1 modulo I
must return this polynomial. This explicit Taylor description is a
validation rule for generated solutions, not a seeded choice of cutoff.

From mu0*(delta-R)=A and muv*(delta-S)=B, the formal Dirichlet algebra
gives
 mu0^p*muv^q=A^p*B^q (delta-R)^(-p)(delta-S)^(-q).
Its coefficient at any fixed n is a finite sum, since R(1)=S(1)=0.
Replacing the formal inverse by(3.1) has an error supported>N by
Section2, and convolution with A^p*B^q cannot decrease support. Hence
 mu0^p*muv^q=A^p*B^q*P_(p,q)(R,S) on EVERY n<=N.        (3.2)
Equivalently multiply the finite polynomial equation by the actual
inverse powers; its ideal error remains supported>N. No convergence
of an infinite Dirichlet series at s1/2 or s2/3 is used in this proof.
All statements are finite coefficient identities with strict supports.

4. One horizon for a joined parent/child increment
-------------------------------------------------
For a coarse increment at scale X choose ONE N=4X-1, and use that same
A,B,R,S and every P_(p,q) for all parent n in[X,2X) and child n in[2X,4X).
The full prefixes, log channels and cell means involve only k<=n<=N,
so(3.2) is valid at every required coefficient. It yields the exact source
 f_N=beta ku*A^2*P_(2,0)-beta ku*A*B*P_(1,1)
                       -beta A*P_(1,0)-R_u ku*B*P_(0,1),
 f_N(n)=f(n), g(n)=f_N(n)-e(n), n<=N.                  (4.1)
At n1 every positive-degree residual monomial vanishes, A(1)=B(1)=1,
and all four units reproduce -beta-R_u. No main or unit correction is
created by the expansion. All e terms and crosses must remain before
forming the actual signed channel squares or projected means.

These COMPONENTS depend on N, even though their join equals the same
global source. For the next increment they may use another N. A proof
of a component recurrence cannot silently compare differently truncated
parent/child components or carry over a smaller-horizon bound. Either
make the component lemma uniform in the shared N or formulate it directly
for the joined exact source increment with ONE N at each scale.

6. Rigorous absolute-treatment obstructions; distinctions matter
---------------------------------------------------------------
Three different operations must not be conflated.

(a) FACTORWISE triangle treatment replaces each convolution factor by
its absolute value. Since A(1)=1, the positive majorant ku*|A|*|A|
contains ku. Its prefix is at least Z_u(n)=sum_(k<=n)k^(-u)>=n^(2/3),
since each of its n terms is at least n^(-1/3). Thus this branch has
energy at least X^(1/3), or beta^2 X^(1/3) with the source coefficient.
It cannot be paid as a polylog, regardless of cutoffs.
The same argument applies to any nonzero isolated delta-head factor
convolved with ku or b0; for b0 the analogous energy grows like X.

(b) Even if all signs inside A*A are preserved BEFORE taking coefficient
absolute values, the particular zero-residual component C=ku*A*A obeys
at every prime ell the EXACT local formula
 C(ell)=ell^(-1/3)-2 if ell<=U,
 C(ell)=ell^(-1/3) if ell>U.
Consequently |C(ell)|>=ell^(-1/3) for EVERY prime, independently of N
or U. This automatically refutes a coefficientwise-absolute polylog
payment for that component, not only the looser factorwise majorant.

No PNT import is needed for an explicit lower rate. For m=floor(x/2),
the central binomial coefficient is at least4^m/(2m+1). Its valuation
at a prime ell is a sum of at most floor(log(2m)/log ell) terms0or1,
so each prime's logarithmic contribution is at most log(2m). Therefore
 pi(x)log x>=2m log2-log(2m+1)
 >=(x-2)log2-log(x+1)>=x/4 for x>=16.
The last inequality uses log2>1/2, log17<3, and a positive derivative
thereafter; log17<3 follows, for example, from the finite series e^3>17.
Hence pi(x)>=x/(4log x), x>=16. On any block X>=16 the coefficient-
absolute prefix of C is at least X^(2/3)/(4log X), since it contains all
primes through X. Its energy is therefore at least
                        X^(1/3)/(16 log(X)^2).         (6.1)
The beta coefficient multiplies this lower bound by beta^2 if kept as
a separate absolute source component. This is an all-scale analytical
obstruction to that proof rule, not a numerical observation.

The complete join must retain known local cancellations too. At a prime
ell the actual four-mode source has
 f(ell)=-R_u ell^(-1/3)+(beta+R_u)ell^(-2/3).
The constant prime terms of the first three modes cancel exactly.
Within a row, residual components also restore the missing truncated
prime coefficient when ell>U or ell>V. Then the actual transport e may
cancel this full prime coefficient in an accepted window. None of these
identities survives separate component absolute values. This does not
prove favorable composite/cofactor cancellation, but it identifies local
cancellations the kernel compiler must preserve before posing its leaf.

(c) Taking absolute values only AFTER summing the whole signed component
PREFIX is different. Its internal A and residual signs can cancel.
A unit inside A does NOT by itself prove a power lower bound for such
a signed prefix. Neither(a)nor(b)refutes that possibility. An estimate
using it must explicitly retain the actual short-Mobius signed sum and
prove the necessary source-specific cancellation instead of citing the
divisor envelope. In particular, a local residual R is itself a signed
inverse-error coefficient and may not be treated as a positive mass.

An explicit complete-head decomposition gives a related cancellation
burden without confusing these distinctions. Split every factor into
its unit and proper-support part and combine ALL four source unit-heads
before treating them. R and S have unit0, so the resulting head is
 H=-R_u ku-beta delta, f=H+T_N exactly through N. With the actual R_u>0,
its prefix is -R_u Z_u(n)-beta and its norm on a block is at least
(R_u/2)X^(1/6). Any separate absolute payment for this head is therefore
power-unpaid. The joined complement must contain a macroscopic opposite
contribution. By the reverse norm triangle,
 ||prefix T_N-prefix e||_X>=||prefix H||_X-||G||_X.
If the target were cofinal subpower, this complement would still have
power-sized norm; it is not small padding. In the full energy identity
 ||G||^2=||Hprefix||^2+||T_Nprefix-D||^2
                       +2< Hprefix,T_Nprefix-D>,
the mixed term must cancel that macroscopic cost. Keeping it is mandatory.
This is a precise obligation, not proof that the cancellation is absent.

There is a concrete short-actual-Mobius representation, not just a new

The dyadic source-pair kernel is consequently a joined bilinear form in

No prime-power/composite sector is removed. Expanding(7.1) loses the
