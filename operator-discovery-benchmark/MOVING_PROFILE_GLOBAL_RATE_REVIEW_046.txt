MOVING PROFILE GLOBAL RATE REVIEW 046
Independent proof and executable obstruction; ordinary Mobius source only

1. Result and exact scope

Fix a>0, 1/2<theta<1, finitely many distinct positive rational rates r_j,
fixed signed rational weights w_j with sum w_j=1, an integer q>=2, and
real b with |b|<sqrt(q). Put
  L(N)=1+floor(log_2 N), beta_N=ceil(a L(N)^theta)/L(N),
  V_N(n)=sum_j w_j n^(-r_j beta_N).
Round EACH exponential downward to precision 4 ceil(log_2 N), then take
the exact signed sum, as specified by the frozen drift recipe. Write the
rounded profile V_N^R. The finite residual is formed from n=2,...,N and
c_n=-mu(n)V_N^R(n), using the actual ordinary Mobius source. There is no
additional taper, source channel or external auxiliary. Define
  h_N=e_(qN)-b D_q e_N, D_q f(x)=f(qx).

There exist constants c>0 and N_* such that, UNCONDITIONALLY,
  ||h_N||_2^2 >= c beta_N             for every integer N>=N_* .       (1)
Constants and the threshold are existential in this review and its guard;
no numerical c, zero location, failing support or effective N_* is claimed.
Consequently no finite A>0 can give
  ||h_N||_2^2 <= A L(N)^(-eta)                                       (2)
on all sufficiently large integers, or any specified unbounded geometric
chain, when eta>1-theta. Equality and slower rates remain OPEN. In
particular this does not prove eta<=1-theta attainable. Norm convergence
and RH remain OPEN: the lower bound tends to zero.

The proof is a dichotomy. If RH is false, a zero strictly right of the
critical line gives a fixed positive norm lower bound for every N. If RH
is true, a conditional theorem about M(x) pays the ACTUAL finite truncation
on a bounded frequency window, and a critical-zero boundary layer gives
the lower bound c beta_N. Combining the branches is unconditional; it
does not assume RH in order to close an upper bound.

The theta>1/2 restriction pays the finite Dirichlet tail. This proof does
not license theta<=1/2, products with old tapers, N-dependent mixtures,
critical-line Mobius series, or arbitrary sign sources. Those requests
return UNSUPPORTED. The distinction from the 044 fixed-cutoff obstruction
is deliberate: the present eventual lower bound holds on every integer,
so it also refutes the specified rates on geometric chains; the older
all-integer scalar obstruction alone did not refute individual chains.

2. Exact finite Mellin identity, including units and dilation

On 0<x<=1 let
  f_n(x)=floor(1/x)/n-floor(1/(nx)),
and set it to zero beyond 1. Let chi=1_(0,1] and
  e_N=chi-sum_(n=2)^N c_n f_n.
These finite functions are bounded near zero and belong to L^2(0,infinity).
For Re(s)>0 their Mellin transforms satisfy
  M f_n(s)=zeta(s)(1/n-n^(-s))/s,
where the apparent singularity at s=1 is removable. This follows first
where the floor integrals converge individually, then by continuation
or by the bounded floor difference. Introduce the virtual unit
  A_N(s)=sum_(n<=N) mu(n)V_N^R(n)n^(-s).
Since V_N^R(1)=sum w_j=1, the unit cancels EXACTLY in A_N(s)-A_N(1),
and the actual n>=2 basis gives
  M e_N(s)=[1-zeta(s)(A_N(s)-A_N(1))]/s.                            (3)
Dilation contributes q^(-s), so the whole signed forcing is
  M h_N(s)={1-b q^(-s)-zeta(s)[A_(qN)(s)-A_(qN)(1)
                 -b q^(-s)(A_N(s)-A_N(1))]}/s.                    (4)
The child profile, cutoff and precision are recomputed at qN. Formula
(4) retains chi, the harmonic correction, both finite cutoffs and their
signed interaction. There is no independent positive estimate of child
and parent energies in the boundary-layer argument.

3. RH-false branch: exact lower bound without tails

If RH is false, ordinary zeta zero symmetry supplies a nontrivial zero
rho with sigma=Re(rho)>1/2. Equation (4) gives, for EVERY N,
  M h_N(rho)=(1-b q^(-rho))/rho.
Both h_N terms have support in (0,1]. Cauchy-Schwarz yields
  |M h_N(rho)|^2 <= ||h_N||_2^2/(2 sigma-1),
and therefore
  ||h_N||_2^2 >= (2 sigma-1)|1-b q^(-rho)|^2/|rho|^2 >0.             (5)
Indeed |b q^(-rho)|<1 when |b|<sqrt(q). No simplicity, hypothetical
convergence, zero computation, recurrence or tail estimate enters (5).
Thus any vanishing whole forcing on an unbounded sequence would imply RH.

4. Imported conditional theorem and legitimate finite-cutoff payment

The only specialized analytic import is K. Soundararajan, Partial sums
of the Mobius function, Theorem 1, primary source
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.0723 . It proves under RH that, eventually,
  |M(x)| <= C sqrt(x) exp(sqrt(log x)(loglog x)^14).                  (6)
This conditional theorem is imported rather than discovered by the
detector. The existence of at least one ordinary critical-line zero is
also a classical imported fact. No simple-zero hypothesis is required.

Let K=N or qN and T=log K. Set E(y)=sqrt(y)(log y)^14. The quotient
E(y)/y decreases once log y>28, since its logarithmic derivative is
(-1/2+14/log y)/y. Because theta>1/2,
  r_min beta_K >= 2 E(T)/T                                         (7)
eventually: beta_K is comparable to (log K)^(theta-1), whereas E(T)/T
equals (log K)^(-1/2)(loglog K)^14. Hence for x>=K, (6)-(7) imply
  |M(x)| <= C x^(1/2+r_min beta_K/2).
For z with Re(z)=1/2+r beta_K, r>=r_min, bounded imaginary part, ordinary
partial summation now JUSTIFIES shifted-series convergence and gives
  sum_(n>K) mu(n)n^(-z)
     =-M(K)K^(-z)+z integral_K^infinity M(x)x^(-z-1) dx,
  |tail| <= C[1+2|z|/(r_min beta_K)]
                   exp(-r_min beta_K log K/2)=o(1).                (8)
The tail is uniform on the bounded scaled frequency windows used below.
The exponential beats every fixed inverse power of beta_K. Equality
with 1/zeta(z) follows from the absolutely convergent identity in Re(z)>1
and analytic continuation of the now convergent shifted series. This is
NOT an assumed infinite series on Re(s)=1/2, and (8) is NOT a global
critical-line L^2 bound on truncation or a separately integrable bias.

5. Harmonic, rounding, and moving child scale

Write C_w=sum |w_j|. The actual coefficient error obeys
  |V_N^R(n)-V_N(n)| <= C_w N^(-4),
because 2^(-4 ceil(log_2 N))<=N^(-4). On Re(s)=1/2 this gives
  |A_N^R(s)-A_N^exact(s)| <= 2 C_w N^(-7/2),                       (9)
uniformly in imaginary part; the harmonic error is at most
  C_w N^(-4)(1+log N).
At s=1 the shifted series is absolutely convergent without RH. The
elementary estimate zeta(1+kappa)>=1/kappa, kappa>0, and the integral
bound for its absolute tail give
  |A_N(1)| <= beta_N sum_j |w_j|r_j
       +sum_j |w_j|N^(-r_j beta_N)/(r_j beta_N)
       +C_w N^(-4)(1+log N)=O(beta_N).                            (10)
Only sum w=1 is needed for the virtual unit; no higher moment cancellation
is needed for (9)-(10). Actual separate floors, including their signed
errors, have therefore been paid locally rather than silently discarded.

For fixed q the integer level gap satisfies
  0<=L(qN)-L(N)<=ceil(log_2 q).
The ceiling in beta gives beta_N~a L(N)^(theta-1), with relative error
O(L^(-theta)). Consequently
  beta_(qN)/beta_N ->1.                                            (11)
This includes the level discontinuities and arbitrary integers N. It
does not require beta monotone or child beta exactly equal to parent beta.

6. Full signed boundary layer, arbitrary zero multiplicity

Assume RH and choose an ordinary critical zero rho=1/2+i gamma. Its
multiplicity d is a finite positive integer. Locally
  zeta(rho+z)=C z^d(1+O(z)), C!=0.
For s=rho+i beta_N v, v in a fixed compact real interval, (8)-(11) imply
uniformly, for K=N or qN and xi_N=beta_K/beta_N,
  zeta(s)/zeta(s+r_j beta_K) -> (iv)^d/(r_j+iv)^d.
The positive rates keep denominators uniformly separated from zero;
xi_N->1. The bounded-frequency tails and rounding vanish after
multiplication by zeta(s). The harmonic term zeta(s)A_K(1) vanishes by
(10). Inserting all terms into (4), including the signed child-parent
coupling, gives the uniform limit
  M h_N(rho+i beta_N v) -> (1-b q^(-rho)) F_d(v)/rho,
  F_d(v)=1-(iv)^d sum_j w_j/(r_j+iv)^d.                            (12)
At v=0, F_d(0)=1 for EVERY d>=1. Thus the mixture cannot remove the
central residual response at this zero. Higher Taylor cancellations of
the profile at fixed n do not make this scaled frequency window vanish.

For an explicit formal window put v_0=r_min/(4 C_w); C_w>=1. Then
  |F_d(v)-1| <= C_w (|v|/r_min)^d <=1/4, |v|<=v_0, d>=1.
Let g_0=(1-b q^(-rho))/rho; |g_0|>0 since |b|<sqrt(q). By uniform
convergence, for all sufficiently large N, |M h_N|>=|g_0|/2 throughout
this window. Mellin Plancherel on Re(s)=1/2 therefore gives
  ||h_N||_2^2=(1/(2pi)) integral_R |M h_N(1/2+it)|^2 dt
       >= [v_0 |g_0|^2/(4pi)] beta_N.                             (13)
This is a lower bound on the WHOLE residual. All signed source terms and
crosses are already in (12); no independent-block positivity assumption
or unlicensed removal of truncation has been used. The formal window is
exactly positive for all multiplicities. Its constant uses an imported
zero, and the eventual threshold is not evaluated here.

7. Unconditional conclusion and examples of source algebra

Under RH, (13) proves (1). If RH is false, (5) proves (1) eventually
because beta_N->0. This dichotomy proves (1) without resolving RH.
Since beta_N>=a L(N)^(-(1-theta)),
  A L(N)^(-eta)/beta_N
       <=(A/a)L(N)^(-(eta-(1-theta))) ->0                          (14)
for eta>1-theta. The requested uniform inequality is therefore false on
either licensed domain, for any finite A. A claimed finite passing
interval does not identify or exclude the unknown failing supports.

For the four-term bare profile in the frozen selected formulas,
  (w_1,w_2,w_3,w_4)=(4,-6,4,-1), (r_1,...,r_4)=(1,2,3,4),
the simple-zero formal layer is the exact rational identity
  1-z sum_j w_j/(z+r_j)=24/[(z+1)(z+2)(z+3)(z+4)].                 (15)
Equation (15) changes the window shape and constants, not the beta
exponent. The proof above does not need a simple zero; F_d(0)=1 is the
relevant multiplicity-independent fact. Theta=4/7 gives the forbidden
range eta>3/7; theta=3/5 gives eta>2/5. These are paper source-algebra
deductions, not applications of the executable guard to selected data.
Likewise the generic theta=5/6 bare profile has boundary power 1/6.
No external auxiliary or legacy taper is covered by those deductions.

The stable identity e_(qN)=b D_q e_N+h_N, ||D_q||=q^(-1/2), also shows
that hypothetical h_N->0 on a q-chain implies e_N->0 there, since the
fixed recurrence ratio |b|/sqrt(q)<1. This valid implication is included
in certificates, but the direct zero evaluation is stronger and avoids
using recurrence recovery as a substitute for the finite-tail proof.
