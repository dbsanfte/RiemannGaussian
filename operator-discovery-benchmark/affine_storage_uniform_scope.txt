Formal constants and the actual X=1 boundary

Before substituting any arithmetic variable, the constant state is

  z=(1,0,0,0,0,0,-1/2,...,-1/2).

For any full15 matrix M its complete formal constant is

  z^T M z = M_00 - sum_(j=6)^14 M_0j
                    + (1/4) sum_(i,j=6)^14 M_ij.

This formula contains both orientations of every divisor-divisor cross and
the complete unit-divisor cross. Unit-prefix/mask crosses have zero formal
constant but remain nonconstant columns. There are 2X child and X parent

  B_matrix(X)=2X z^T G_child z + X z^T G_parent z - 2X z^T K z.

The final term is nonpositive for PSD K and is signed transport. Regional
charges are nonnegative. The combined charge can have either sign; every
part must remain in the ledger. All of these terms are O_q(X) for fixed
finite matrices. Pure unit storage has A_K(X)=K_00 X and Delta(A_K)=-2K_00 X;
that contribution also requires its actual initial cost.

Conditional dyadic recovery and quantifiers

Put a=1/q and W=S_R/X^2. Since ceil(X^a)<=2X^a for X>=1,

  W(2X)<=W(X)+(C_q/2)X^a.

At X=2^m geometric summation, including the empty sum at X=1, gives

  W(X)<=S_R(1)+(C_q/2)(X^a-1)/(2^a-1),
  E(X)<=[S_R(1)+(C_q/2)/(2^a-1)] X^(2+a).

The denominator is positive. The recovered constant includes the actual

the same exponent with a finite q-dependent constant.

  A_Gparent(X)+A_Gchild(2X)+A_K(2X)-4A_K(X).
