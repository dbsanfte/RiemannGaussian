IMPORTED SIGNED MOBIUS CONTROL WITH AN INDEPENDENT COMPLETE FINITE PATCH

Named dependency: Ramare and Zuniga Alterman, arXiv:2408.05969v2,
Theorem 1.1 (statement on page 1, proof in section 7). For every real
x>=T=1798118 it gives |M(x)| <= (0.006688 log x-0.039)x/log(x)^2.
We use the weaker consequence |M(x)|<=c*x/log x, c=6688/1000000.
The pinned source PDF and this external theorem are explicit proof
dependencies. The external analytic proof and its prime estimates are NOT
independently re-proved by the detector. This is an imported calibration,
not discovery of that theorem or new independent prime information.

The producer infers C from |M(n)| log(e+n)/n for training n=1..1024,
rounding upward on a declared rational grid. The independent proof backend
then computes every actual mu(n) and M(n) through T and checks M(n)^2<=n.
No estimate beyond this finite prefix follows from that check alone.

Write f(x)=x/log(e+x). For n>=4, log(e+n)<=sqrt(n): it holds at four,
and the derivative of sqrt(x)-log(e+x) is positive since
e+x-2sqrt(x)=(sqrt(x)-1)^2+e-1>0. Thus the finite square-root check implies
|M(n)|<=f(n) for 4<=n<=T. Cases n=1,2,3 are checked directly; C is
required to cover them and to be >=1.

For n>=T, log(e+n)=log n+log(1+e/n)<=log n+e/n. Consequently the
imported bound is <=C f(n) whenever C>=c*(1+e/(T log T)). Native real
balls check this constant inequality. Together the two scopes cover
every integer n>=1, with no unexamined gap at the threshold.

The function f is concave on [0,infinity). Its second derivative has
the sign of N(x)=x*(2-log(e+x))-2e*log(e+x). Put y=x/e. Then
N(x)/e=y*(1-log(1+y))-2*(1+log(1+y)), whose derivative is
-log(1+y)-1/(1+y)<0 and whose value at zero is -2. Also f(0)=0.
Convexity of absolute value and concavity of f extend the integer bound
to the linear interpolation: |M_tilde(t)|<=C*t/log(e+t) for every t>=0.

For a finite signed scale polynomial R=sum_j c_j M_tilde(t/d_j),
d_j>=1, we obtain the global signed estimate
|R(t)|<=K*t/log(e+t),
K=C sum_j |c_j|*(1+log d_j)/d_j.
Indeed e+t<=d_j*(e+t/d_j), and log(e+t/d_j)>=1. The arithmetic signs
of mu were controlled by the imported M theorem before the final triangle
bound. This is not an extra cancellation estimate among filter terms.

For every positive rational q and every real X>=1,
integral_X^(2X)|R(t)|^q dt <= (2K)^q X^(q+1)/log(e+X)^q.
This universal moment certificate is below the RH exponent q/2+1.
It provides genuine all-scale signed o(t) control, with a named external
dependency, but does not prove an RH-sufficient signed estimate.

For the all-positive coefficient control M(t)=t, the logarithmic claim
fails once log(e+t)>C. For the delayed-positive control, the cumulative
sum is eventually t+constant and the same uniform claim is false.
Any source filter with nonzero sum_j c_j/d_j preserves that linear growth.
Finite matches cannot promote either control to a universal signed bound.

Historical priority is not an acceptance gate. The RH objective remains
open. The all-scale scope here relies on the imported theorem, not solely
on the independent finite computations or the source PDF's fingerprint.
