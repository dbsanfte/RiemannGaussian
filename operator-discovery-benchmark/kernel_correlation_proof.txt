COMPLETE SIGNED GRAM OPERATORS, TRUE ARITHMETIC DIAGONALS, AND THE REMAINING GAP
============================================================================
The numerical search proposes matrices and inequalities. These identities and
certificate methods are supplied, not autonomously invented or machine formalized.
No RH-strength uniform off-diagonal inequality is proved by finite observations.

1. Quadratic observables retain the complete actual arithmetic
-------------------------------------------------------------
Use the preceding kernel grammar at fixed rational k>1, ell>0, b>1:
 g_i(X/n)=(X/n)^k K_i((X/n)^ell),
 F_i(X)=sum_{n>=1}mu(n)g_i(X/n),
 K_i(y)=sum c_i[m,j](b^j y)^m exp(-b^j y).
For any fixed symmetric rational two-by-two matrix H, define
 E_H(X)=F(X)^T H F(X),
 D_H(X)=sum_{n>=1}mu(n)^2 g(X/n)^T H g(X/n),
 C_H(X)=sum_{n!=m}mu(n)mu(m) g(X/n)^T H g(X/m).
Absolute convergence of sum |mu(n)g_i(X/n)| at fixed X justifies the exact
identity E_H=D_H+C_H. This is the full infinite arithmetic pair sum. It is
not a sampled pair statistic, a random-sign model, or a diagonal approximation.

Pointwise kernel multiplication introduces general positive rational rates:
 ((r y)^m exp(-r y))*((s y)^h exp(-s y))
 =r^m s^h/(r+s)^(m+h) *((r+s)y)^(m+h) exp(-(r+s)y).
Thus the diagonal can use new rates r+s, not only powers of a fixed base.
An exact finite exponential-polynomial expansion records every coefficient.
No assumption of positivity of each expanded coefficient is made.

For w>1 the squarefree arithmetic moment is
 sum_{n>=1}mu(n)^2 n^(-w)=zeta(w)/zeta(2w).
This follows from the absolutely convergent Euler factors 1+p^(-w). Its complete
tail after N is obtained by subtracting the entire actual coefficient prefix.
The exponential Taylor remainder has the same valid absolute bound as the
inverse channel, with k replaced by 2k and the general rational rate r.
The producer uses triangular inversion and squares those coefficients.
The checker factors every index independently, changes cutoffs, Taylor order,
precision and summation, and encloses every complete infinite Gram value.

2. Squarefree information over every real scale
-----------------------------------------------
The exact square-divisor identity is
 mu(n)^2=sum_{d^2|n}mu(d).
For real x>=1 and A=floor(sqrt(x)), it gives
 Q(x)=sum_{n<=x}mu(n)^2=sum_{d<=A}mu(d)floor(x/d^2).
Since a=sum mu(d)/d^2=1/zeta(2),
 |Q(x)-a*x|<=A+x*sum_{d>A}1/d^2<=A+x/A.
Writing u=sqrt(x)/A, 1<=u<=(A+1)/A<=2, this is at most
 sqrt(x)*(u+1/u)<=(5/2)*sqrt(x).
For 0<x<1, Q=0 and a*x<=sqrt(x), since 0<a<1. Therefore

 |Q(x)-x/zeta(2)|<=(5/2)*sqrt(x) for EVERY real x>0.

This elementary actual-arithmetic estimate is proved here, using all prime
positions through square-divisor inversion, without importing a distribution
theorem. Historical novelty is not claimed. It does not prove RH.

3. A signed all-scale density remainder for every Gram diagonal
--------------------------------------------------------------
Write K(y)^T H K(y)=sum c[m,r]*(r y)^m exp(-r y), with exact rational
coefficients and rates r>0, and set
 f_X(t)=(X/t)^(2k)*K((X/t)^ell)^T H K((X/t)^ell).
Even for indefinite H, the following signed identity and bound are valid.
Stieltjes partial summation, with both boundary terms vanishing, gives
 D_H(X)=a*integral_0^infinity f_X(t)dt
           -integral_0^infinity (Q(t)-a*t) f_X'(t)dt.
Substitute t=X*u in the first integral to obtain a*X*I_H, where
 I_H=sum c[m,r]*r^((1-2k)/ell)*Gamma((2k-1)/ell+m)/ell.

For each atom, differentiation and the triangle inequality give
 integral_0^infinity sqrt(t)*|f_atom,X'(t)|dt <=sqrt(X)*U_atom,
 U_atom=r^((1/2-2k)/ell)/ell
       *[(2k+ell*m) Gamma((2k-1/2)/ell+m)
                              +ell Gamma((2k-1/2)/ell+m+1)].
Let U_H=sum |c[m,r]| U_atom. All arguments of Gamma are positive, and all
integrals converge. Thus the true signed density-correction theorem is

 |D_H(X)-a*X*I_H|<=(5/2)*U_H*sqrt(X), for EVERY real X>0.

The integral I_H retains the signed cross-kernel coefficients. Only the
error variation uses absolute coefficients. For a nonzero positive semidefinite
observable with a nonzero effective kernel, I_H>0; D_H>=0 at every scale.
This is critical-size information about the diagonal component. It is not a
bound for E_H or C_H. The missing signed cross terms are explicitly retained.

4. Singular metrics and ordinary-zero recovery
----------------------------------------------
For H=[[a,h],[h,c]], positive semidefiniteness is the exact rational condition
 a>=0, c>=0, a*c-h^2>=0. If a>0 then
 E_H=a*(F_1+h/a*F_2)^2+(c-h^2/a)*F_2^2.
If a=0, positivity forces h=0, so E_H=c*F_2^2. Only strictly positive
pivots appear. A zero effective kernel supplies no recovery information.
The active rational covectors give effective kernels L_i=sum r_ij K_j,
and |F_{L_i}(X)|<=sqrt(E_H(X)/d_i) whenever E_H is bounded above.
Consequently singular positive metrics are allowed. Indefinite metrics remain
signed observables, but an upper bound on them is not silently treated as a
bound on absolute values of the constituent responses.

The earlier exact rational Mellin bank certificates apply to these effective
kernels without renormalizing away their amplitudes or dilations. In particular
sum A_i(v,z) P_{L_i}(v,z)=(v)_r z^h prevents a common ordinary-zero numerator
zero at every height. The pivots d_i must be charged in quantitative recovery.
Small pivots or strongly cancelling effective combinations do not give control
of ordinary zeta for free. Missing scalar certificates and indefinite metrics
are not counterexamples to a numerical inequality.

5. The signed target and its quantifiers
--------------------------------------
Put theta_q=eta+epsilon/q^nu, rational eta>=1/2, epsilon>0, nu>0, q>=2.
The proposed signed inequality, for fixed H and rational rho, is

 FOR EVERY q THERE EXISTS a finite B_q>=0 such that FOR EVERY integer N>=1,
 C_H(N)<=rho*D_H(N)+B_q*N^(2 theta_q)*log(e+N)^d, d>=0.

Equivalently E_H(N)<=(1+rho)D_H(N)+B_q*N^(2 theta_q)*log(e+N)^d.
The illustrative fitted B_q values are not mandatory constants of the global
existential target. Failure of one fitted B_q at a finite N refutes that fit;
it does not refute existence of some other finite B_q. There is no prescribed
growth law in q. Finite q grids never prove this target.

For a positive observable write A=a*I_H>0, V=(5/2)*U_H, and p=max(1+rho,0).
D_H(N)<=A*N+V*sqrt(N) and D_H>=0. Therefore
 sqrt(E_H(N))<=sqrt(p*A)*N^(1/2)+sqrt(p*V)*N^(1/4)
                       +sqrt(B_q)*N^theta_q log(e+N)^(d/2).
This remains valid for rho<-1 by discarding its nonpositive diagonal term.
The active effective responses are divided by sqrt(d_i).
Their true all-scale derivative bounds extend these integer inequalities to
real X with an additional effective unit constant J_i. A supplied scalar upper
bound never controls an indefinite metric through this argument.

For sigma>1/2 and theta_q<sigma, complete Mellin norms charge:
  the local integral on (0,1),
  J_i/sigma,
  [sqrt(p*A)/(sigma-1/2)+sqrt(p*V)/(sigma-1/4)
                  +sqrt(B_q)*log_moment(sigma-theta_q,d/2)]/sqrt(d_i).
The fractional log moment is bounded using its integer ceiling, since
log(e+X)>1 for X>=1. Native Gamma, all bank multipliers, positive pivots and
denominator lower bounds enter the conditional reciprocal-zeta cost.
This is one quantitative observer, not an all-height quantitative bound.

If eta=1/2 and the effective bank has exact all-height recovery, true bounds
for every q would give holomorphy beyond every ordinary zero right of the
critical line. The bank identity gives a contradiction, so the target implies
RH. The converse follows from the already named conditional Mertens consequence
in the parent proof: under RH every F_i=O_delta(X^(1/2+delta)), while D_H=O(X),
and an arbitrary fixed rho can be absorbed into a freely varying B_q. Thus this
is an RH-equivalent target for recovering positive observables, not a fixed
pure square-root constant that silently presupposes simpler zeros or bounded
constant growth. All such signed uniform targets remain unproved here.

6. Automatic certificate attempts and controls
----------------------------------------------
The absolute bound |F_i(X)|<=W_i X proves only E_H=O(X^2), C_H=O(X^2).
It supplies unconditional energy-frontier witnesses at eta>=1, equivalent to
point growth exponent one. The diagonal density theorem has true exponent
one-half for its correction, but does not repair that signed gap. The checker
keeps the distinction between a missing proof method and a counterexample.

A separate counterfeit adds +1 to mu(n) for every n beyond a very large H0.
Its finite response perturbation is enclosed explicitly. Its Dirichlet series
has a pole at one. At least one response of a recovering effective bank has
a pole there. A cofinal positive-energy bound with eta<1 would continue all
active responses holomorphically across one, contradicting that pole.
The actual squarefree diagonal retained for this control is a supplied actual-
arithmetic comparator, not the square of the counterfeit coefficients.
Accordingly C_control is defined as E_control-D_actual; no claim about a
counterfeit pair decomposition is made. A cofinal target with eta<1 is false,
even if every finite comparison succeeds. Positive oracles and random signs
never replace the actual inverse coefficients in discovery data.

Named parent calibration imports are retained with provenance. This stage
imports no prime-distribution estimate. Backends are supplied. The final data
are drawn after the archive and new-q calibration are sealed. No mathematical
identity, diagonal-only estimate, finite fit, or weak global witness is promoted
to an RH proof.

7. A second signed target: the diagonal density correction itself
----------------------------------------------------------------
For a density observable write Z_H(X)=D_H(X)-A_H X with A_H=I_H/zeta(2).
The theorem in section 3 proves |Z_H(X)|<=V_H sqrt(X) at every real X>0.
This true bound is weaker than the new critical target

 FOR EVERY q EXISTS finite C_q, FOR EVERY integer N>=1,
 |Z_H(N)|<=C_q N^(1/4+epsilon/q^nu)log(e+N)^d.

All root denominators and freely varying constants are allowed. Finite fits
cannot prove it. This target can suffice for RH even though the uncentered
diagonal has nonnegative squarefree coefficients.

The raw diagonal Mellin transform, initially 1<Re(s)<2k, is
 W_H(s)*zeta(s)/zeta(2s),
 W_H(s)=Gamma((2k-s)/ell)/ell
          *sum c[m,r]*((2k-s)/ell)_m*r^(-(2k-s)/ell).
Its residue at one is exactly A_H. After subtracting the integral on (0,1)
and A_H/(s-1), this is the Mellin transform of Z_H on (1,infinity).
The integral on (0,1) is holomorphic for Re(s)<2k. Thus the cofinal density
target would make W_H(s)*zeta(s)/zeta(2s) holomorphic for 1/4<Re(s)<2k,
apart from its explicitly removed pole at one.

8. Numerator cancellation cannot simply be ignored
--------------------------------------------------
A zero rho of zeta with 1/2<Re(rho)<1 gives a denominator zero at s=rho/2.
The factor zeta(rho/2) could cancel it. When W_H is nonzero at every such
point, holomorphy forces zeta(rho/2)=0, with multiplicity at least that at rho.
Conjugation and the functional equation then give another ordinary zero
 rho_next=1-conjugate(rho)/2.
Its real part is again strictly greater than 1/2, and its height is halved.
Repeating yields infinitely many ordinary zeros with real parts tending to 2/3
and heights tending to zero. But zeta(2/3)!=0: the alternating eta series is
positive at every real s>0, while 1-2^(1-s)<0 for 0<s<1, so zeta(s)<0 there.
Analyticity makes a neighborhood of 2/3 zero-free. This contradicts the chain.
Therefore the centered quarter-power cofinal target implies RH. Multiplicities
and possible numerator-zero cancellations are included; no known-zero grid or
simple-zero assumption is used. At a general limiting exponent above 1/4 the
same complete chain need not follow. No blanket all-height consequence is
assigned to those above-threshold targets.

The supplied common-rate certificate writes W_H as Gamma(v)*r^(-v)*P(v)/ell.
Its domain for all ordinary denominator zeros is Re(v)>(2k-1/2)/ell. Exact
division peels roots v=-j, j>=0, which are outside that domain. If the remaining
polynomial has degree at most two, shift v=u+(2k-1/2)/ell and normalize the
leading coefficient. Nonnegative shifted coefficients imply that all roots
have Re(u)<=0: for a quadratic this follows immediately from the quadratic
formula, and for a linear polynomial from its single root. Constants have no
root. This gives an all-height certificate, including imaginary boundary roots.
Higher-degree remainders and multiple rates remain missing-certificate leads.
This bounded certificate backend does not restrict the mathematical grammar.

RH also implies the target for every finite exponential-polynomial density
kernel. The parent's named conditional Mertens bound gives 1/zeta(w)=O(|w|)
uniformly in any closed halfplane Re(w)>1/2+delta. Euler summation gives a
polynomial bound for zeta(s) on any closed vertical strip Re(s)>0 away from one.
Gamma(v) decays exponentially with height and the finite polynomial/rate factors
have at most polynomial growth. Shifting the inverse Mellin contour from Re(s)>1
to any Re(s)>1/4 crosses just the residue A_H at one. After removing it,
Z_H(X)=O_delta(X^(1/4+delta)). Choose delta smaller than epsilon/q^nu for each q.
The conditional Mertens theorem identifies equivalence; it is not used to prove
a new unconditional quarter-power estimate in this project.

Quantitative native recovery charges the density unit-increment bound, its
entire local integral, the pole subtraction, |W_H(s)| and |zeta(s)|. The latter
is needed to recover 1/zeta(2s) from a bound for zeta(s)/zeta(2s). A finite query
where zeta(s) cannot be separated from zero has unresolved quantitative cost,
not a counterexample to the qualitative reflection argument.
A rational mutation of the density center produces an uncancelled pole at one
whenever A_H!=0. It can pass finite tests if that mutation is small; the global
sublinear target is nevertheless false. These tests are separate controls.
