FLOOR RESIDUAL LOGARITHMIC RATE REVIEW 044

Scope and API
-------------
floor_residual_rate_review_044.py reviews the exact typed 044 coefficient
recipes at fixed integer indices. It never builds coefficient arrays, evaluates
an N-dependent norm, imports the search compiler, or changes frozen artifacts.
Only generate_witness from frozen floor_residual_recovery_review_043.py is
imported. Every resulting witness is independently replayed here.

audit_selected(selected, max_index=16, precision=192, meter=None) accepts
exactly {recipes: [...], rules: [...]}. Recipe fields are id, base, auxiliary;
rules must bind to a present unique recipe and contain exact q, b, and
finite_log_bound {A, eta}. Repeated family rules in distinct q/b/strata are
permitted. Each selected recipe must have a rule. Output schema is
floor-selected-log-rate-review-v1, with full per-recipe reviews under reviews.

review_recipe(recipe, rules=None, max_index=16, precision=192, meter=None,
              witnesses=None) returns floor-log-rate-review-v1. A shared
witness dictionary avoids repeated solves. It contains target_candidates,
strongest_target_rate_obstruction, rule_reviews, exact rates, full cell
witnesses, signed leading monomials, and independently checkable nonzero
licenses. A rule review's strongest_rejection selects the smallest certified
energy rate, breaking ties by index; this is a rate comparison, not a claim
that its leading constant is largest.

review_frontier(recipes, eta_values, max_index=16, precision=192, meter=None)
returns floor-log-rate-frontier-v1: per-recipe necessary TARGET rate
restrictions, without ranking, reselecting, or evaluating programs. It does
not assert that target rate restrictions alone determine forcing rates.
The publisher can instead call review_recipe with every declared rule.

Fixed-index asymptotic algebra
-----------------------------
Let L(N)=N.bit_length() and fix n>=2. Then log(N)/(L(N)*log(2))->1.
The frozen compiler's independent dyadic rounding uses at least
4*ceil(log2(N)) bits. Errors from this rounding, including positive rational
roots, decay faster than any inverse power of L at fixed n. Positive rational
roots near the log coordinate's zero have only polynomial-in-L sensitivity;
the exponentially small coordinate error therefore remains flat.

For a log taper (1-(log(n)/log(N))^alpha)^gamma, its correction from 1 is
  -gamma * log(n)^alpha * log(2)^(-alpha) * L^(-alpha)
  + o(L^(-alpha)).
A vanishing twist n^(-rate/L^level_power) has correction
  -rate * log(n) * L^(-level_power) + o(L^(-level_power)).
Unit corrections are exactly zero. Positive cutoff tapers and power
amplitudes are flat relative to every inverse log power. A moving band
eventually excludes every fixed index exactly. A log amplitude L^(-power)
times a typed weight tending to 1 has leading coefficient 1 and rate power.

Products of limit-one weights add their first corrections, because the
product of corrections is of higher order. Differences retain signed first
corrections. Exact coefficient binding is
  c_n(N)+mu(n) = -mu(n)*(base(n,N)-1)
                + auxiliary_scalar*arithmetic(n)*envelope(n,N).
Arithmetic channels are independently factored at each fixed index; no
source arrays are consulted. Exact zero arithmetic multipliers prove their
own contribution zero.

Leading constants are exact sums of monomials
  rational * log(n)^(rational) * log(2)^(rational).
At n=2 the two logarithms are exactly identified before simplification.
All terms with one common nonzero rational sign certify nonzero structurally,
since the logarithmic factors are positive. Mixed signs require an Arb
enclosure strictly excluding zero. Exact dyadic endpoints accompany every
finite Arb enclosure. No floating point sign or norm admission is used.

Cancellation is deliberately conservative. If the first-order terms cancel,
the status is UNRESOLVED and that rate becomes a cancellation barrier. An
uncompiled o(L^(-a)) remainder cannot be skipped to admit a later rate b>a.
A known term with b<=a can still dominate or survive the remainder. Neither
the cancellation of equal ASTs nor a zero-containing Arb interval is used
to infer flatness. This restricted algebra does not claim a complete formal
asymptotic expansion.

Independent witness and rate proof
----------------------------------
For f_j on cell k, f_j=(k mod j)/j. With residual e=1-sum c_j f_j-sum d_q I_q,
the witness is a rational vector alpha supported on cells 1..n satisfying
  sum alpha_k = mu(n),
  sum alpha_k*k = 0,
  sum alpha_k*(k mod j)/j = -delta(j,n), 2<=j<=n.
The second identity annihilates every higher floor column j>n, so the proof
is independent of later support size. The verifier checks all finite columns,
the constant, the tail slope, and D=sum alpha_k^2*k*(k+1). Consequently
  c_n+mu(n)-sum d_q*sum_(k>=q) alpha_k = sum alpha_k*e(cell k),
  ||e||_2^2 >= (c_n+mu(n)-sum d_q*step_response(q))^2/D.
Cell weights are exactly 1/(k*(k+1)); the inequality is weighted Cauchy.
For n=3, alpha=(-1,-1,1), D=20, and step_response(2)=0.
For n=2, alpha=(-2,1), D=14.

If the coefficient error is C*L^(-nu)+o(L^(-nu)) with C certified nonzero,
then liminf L^(2nu)*||e_N||_2^2 >= C^2/D>0. Thus a bound
||e_N||_2^2 <= A/L^eta for ANY fixed finite A is impossible when eta>2nu.
The exact energy_rate emitted is 2nu. Eta equal to or smaller than this
rate remains OPEN; no upper bound is inferred.

For h_N=e_(qN)-b*D_q e_N, a witness at n not divisible by q, with zero
indicator response, binds to the child coefficient error alone. Its parent
floor coefficient and indicator contributions vanish. Since L(qN)/L(N)->1,
the same leading rate and constant apply, independently of b. For b=0 the
forcing is simply the child residual and every witness is eligible. Nonzero
b at divisible indices is explicitly UNRESOLVED here; the necessary parent
coefficient or global sum has not been compiled. Eligible candidates whose
leading term is unresolved retain that status; aggregate rules remain OPEN
unless a certified strict rejection is present.

Logical limits
--------------
This is a generic typed-recipe rate gate, with no family identifiers encoded.
It rejects only the stated rate of the exact fixed recipe/rule. A positive
finite-index logarithmic lower rate is compatible with norm convergence to
zero. The outputs preserve convergence_status OPEN, RH_proved false,
RH_refuted false, uniform_upper_bound_proved false, and explicitly deny
additional norm/source observations. Flat fixed-index errors or unrefuted
rates do not certify convergence, a logarithmic upper bound, or RH. Other
global obstructions may apply independently.

Resources and focused verification
----------------------------------
The mathematical recipes are defined for N tending to infinity; bounded
review resources do not truncate that definition. The implementation scans
indices 2..64 (default 16), enforces AST depth 6, canonical rational bits
4096, twist level 1..8, and Arb precision 128..4096 (default 192). The
default Meter caps charged operations at 50,000,000 and elapsed time at
600 seconds; callers may provide their own shared Meter. Frontier recipe
lists are bounded at 10,000. Imported witness construction is exact rational
linear algebra; local replay and arithmetic binding do not trust its output.

Owned test_floor_residual_rate_review_044.py contains 12 focused synthetic
tests: denominators outside any nominated root bank, signed products and
differences, cancellation barriers, exact zero arithmetic multipliers,
logarithm dependence at n=2, mixed-sign Arb admission, independent full
witness replay/tampering, strict/equal/smaller rate comparisons, indicator
annihilation and divisible-index uncertainty, b0 and repeated rule strata,
frontier scope, and type/resource rejection.

The first run had one failure because the synthetic cancellation fixture's
auxiliary scalar sign made the terms add. The fixture sign was corrected;
no production algebra was changed for that failure. A separate output
clarification preserves UNRESOLVED on eligible forcing candidates. The
second focused run passed 12/12, no errors, with 39,508 charged operations.
The metered receipt preserves both invocations and 79,016 cumulative charged
operations. No old suite/study was rerun; no hashes were generated. Actual
selected/frontier application is reserved for the separately frozen parent
analytical followup.
