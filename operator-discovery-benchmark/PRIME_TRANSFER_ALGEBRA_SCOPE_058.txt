VALUATION-COMPLETE PRIME TRANSFER ALGEBRA DETECTOR 058

Disposition
===========
This producer discovers the exact rational nullspace of a declared finite
prime-transfer signature. Every returned relation has been replayed against
all signature rows. The resulting algebraic identity holds for the complete
inclusive-unit source at every x>0,t>0. No norm bound, integrated moving-middle
bound, RH conclusion, numerical candidate score or source array is supplied.
No existing/frozen file was changed. The only development matrices have one
or two primes; no three-prime scientific signature or RREF was computed.

Source and atoms
================
Fix a finite universe U of distinct actual primes and

 v_x(n)=1_(2x<n<=4x)-2*1_(x<n<=2x),
 G_m(t)=product_(p|m)(1-p^-t), G_1(t)=1,
 H_S(x,t)=sum_(n>=1)v_x(n)G_(product(S)*n)(t).

The sum includes n1 and is finite for every x>0. The atom schema is exactly

 {primes:[sorted actual subset S of U],divisor:d,attenuation:a},

where positive integers d,a have no factors outside U. Its meaning is

 a^-t H_S(x/d,t).

Repeated valuations in d and in the summation variable are fully retained.
Attenuation is a prime monomial, not a fitted scalar. All outside-prime
factors remain present through a common factor, as proved below.

Generic detector API
====================
 grammar(universe,meter=None,max_attenuation_degree=2)
 prepare_context(meter=None,max_bytes=256MiB)
 signature(universe,atoms,meter=None,context=None)
 discover(universe,atoms,meter=None,context=None)

The universe is a canonical sorted nonempty actual-prime list. Atom columns
are supplied as a distinct ordered list and retain that order in all outputs.
The default grammar enumerates every S subset, d in[1]+U, and every
attenuation prime monomial of total degree<=2. Enumeration is subset bit-mask,
then divisor order, then attenuation degree/exponent lex order. The degree
is configurable under prospective resource guards. It supplies no target
identity, winner operator, norm or anticipated source-order improvement.
For three primes the declared default grammar has8*4*10=320columns. This
is a prospective grammar count; that scientific matrix was not run here.

Complete finite signature
=========================
For an atom factor d=product p^e_p and a=product p^b_p. Pull its finite sum
back by n=d*m. The interval endpoints give exactly

 v_(x/d)(m)=v_x(n),
 a^-t H_S(x/d,t)=sum_(n>=1)v_x(n)1_(d|n)*a^-t
                                      G_(product(S)*(n/d))(t).

Put z_p=p^-t. For a valuation tuple nu_p=v_p(n), the U-dependent coefficient
is zero if any nu_p<e_p; otherwise it is the integer polynomial

 product_(p in U) z_p^b_p
   *product_(p in S OR nu_p>e_p)(1-z_p).                        (1)

Every p outside U divides n/d exactly when it divides n, since d is supported
on U. Such primes therefore supply the SAME common product outside every
atom. Multiplying (1) by that common factor retains all ordinary prime
factors. There is no independent-prime probabilistic assumption.

For each p let cap_p=max_column e_p+1. Enumerate ALL tuples
nu_p=0,...,cap_p, including the allzero unit state. The last label denotes
every true valuation>=cap_p, not merely one endpoint sample. Once a
valuation is above every divisor exponent, both presence and the predicate
nu_p>e_p are constant. Thus these finite cases exhaust all actual n>=1.
For a default three-prime grammar cap=2 at each prime, so there are27cases,
not the8squarefree cases. Arbitrary higher divisor powers are admitted
within resource limits and require their additional intermediate cases.

The producer expands (1) into exact signed integer monomials. Each row is
one (valuation tuple,monomial exponent tuple), ordered lexicographically.
Public rows are

 {valuations:[...],monomial:[...],entries:[{column:int,value:int}]}.

Only nonzero entries/rows are emitted. Their absence is exact zero, and the
metadata records the complete valuation case count and high-state semantics.
A relation is admitted by the detector only when its rational column weights
annihilate every emitted row. It then vanishes in every case of (1), including
unit and arbitrarily high powers, so the pulled-back coefficient at EACH n
is exactly zero. Summing the finite v_x(n) sum proves the identity for all
x>0,t>0. No interchange of infinite sums/integrals is needed.

Rational discovery and certificate scope
=======================================
The source code performs sparse Fraction forward elimination, exact backward
elimination to RREF, and the canonical free-column nullspace construction.
For each free column f its basis vector has coefficient1 at f,0 at every
other free column, and minus the RREF entry at each pivot. Every vector is
then independently replayed by the producer against the original integer
signature. All scalar output strings are canonical rationals.

Discovery output schema prime-transfer-discovery-v1 includes the original
universe/atoms, rank/nullity,pivot_columns/free_columns, exact rref_rows,
relations[{coefficients:[...],free_column,nonzero_count}], signature_summary,
and measured counters. signature schema prime-transfer-signature-v1 includes
the full sparse rows. grammar schema prime-transfer-grammar-v1 contains atoms
and the declared complexity metadata. Public JSON lists are newly constructed
from immutable cached tuples, avoiding mutation of cached signatures.

The basis is complete for the DECLARED finite signature and columns. It is
not claimed to enumerate all arithmetic identities, all useful combinations,
all smaller formula representations, or all improved source bounds. Root
may combine basis vectors and inspect the current d1 channel and weighted
lower channels. That classification is a separate step. The producer neither
selects a favored current channel nor discards any lower weighted term.
Prime-universe changes preserve the abstract exponent matrix for the same
ordered grammar; they change actual source interpretation, not the formal
RREF. Repeating identical abstract searches would not add algebraic evidence.

Unit convention and the unpaid integral
=======================================
This is the inclusive H source. If h_S denotes the n>=2 version, exactly

 H_S(x,t)=h_S(x,t)+v_x(1)G_(product(S))(t).

For lower arguments x/d, v_(x/d)(1) may be nonzero even when v_x(1)=0.
These lower unit corrections must be retained before transferring an
identity to the unit-removed source. No detector relation silently deletes
n1. With S empty G_1=1; an isolated unit integral against t^-alpha-1 diverges
at0, so one cannot infer integrability from a formal H relation alone.
The exact complete unit counterterms/source-order cancellation are necessary
for such an integrated application. This detector supplies only the exact
pointwise identity. Complete weighted lower terms, sign cancellations,
normalizers, finite band endpoints, and head/tail estimates remain separate
obligations. No identity is a paid all-scale signed norm bound or RH premise.

Resources and accounting
========================
Operational domains: at most4 universe primes, each<=1,000,000;
at most512 distinct atoms; atom integers<=4096bits; exponent<=16 at each
universe prime; configurable grammar attenuation degree0..8. Complete states
are capped at4096, sparse signature terms at2,000,000, rows at20,000,
intermediate rational numerator/denominator bits at8192. These are evaluator
resources, not mathematical restrictions on the identity theorem.

Default Meter permits5billion proxy operations and7200seconds. Callers may
set a larger explicitly declared work cap. Meter.operations and counts are
public; primality/factor divisions, grammar atoms, valuation-column cases,
polynomial terms, pivot checks, Fraction operations, row updates, original
signature replay terms and cache hits are counted. Fraction charges depend
on actual operand bit lengths; these are work proxies, not CPU instruction
counts. Native integer/Fraction operations are bounded by the stated bit
caps, including every partial original-row replay sum. Timing is checked at
charged operation boundaries.

Contexts cache immutable signatures keyed by the literal validated prime
universe and entire ordered atom tuple. There are no artifact hashes. The
persistent cap is256MiB and prospective working cap2GiB. Signatures are
preflighted before polynomial allocation; RREF is preflighted using a
conservative full rows*columns Fraction-cell envelope at the complete8192bit
numerator/denominator cap, plus the serialized RREF/nullspace output envelope.
Root retains aggregate
16GiB responsibility and runs no worker pool in this producer. Distinct
source-universe jobs require no hidden shared scientific arrays. The actual
three-prime pilot, if later frozen and run, can reuse one context for its
signature and discovery. No scientific RREF has occurred during development.
