Uniform divisor-complement transport: an exact rule and its remaining gate
2026-10-09. New analytical file; frozen pilot sources/results remain unchanged.

Starting point: pilot002 ID39 and the source-free algebra in
semantic_candidate_review_index_switch.txt. No numerical source table, response,
probe, test, fitted coefficient or hash is used. Statements below are direct
mathematical derivations. They bound components and identify a possible new
two-state construction; they do not certify an original-Mobius all-scale drift.

1. A general interval/quotient-cap L2 certificate
------------------------------------------------
Let A,B be integers with 1<=A<=B, I=[A,B], and length L=B-A+1. Let K be a
nonnegative integer and C,a>=0. Let f(k) be real or complex and satisfy the
FUNCTIONAL hypothesis
 |f(k)|<=C*k for every positive k<=K.
For every t in I choose an arbitrary subset D(t) of its positive divisors,
and arbitrary coefficients c(t,d) satisfying |c(t,d)|<=a. Require
 1<=t/d<=K for every admitted pair (t,d).
No nesting, multiplicativity, independence, fixed sign, or common subset among
different t is assumed. Define
 F(t)=sum_{d in D(t)}c(t,d)f(t/d).
The all-pairs absolute bound, with k=t/d, gives
 |F(t)|<=a*C*sum_{k<=K,k|t}k.
Counting multiples of lcm(k,j) in the exact inclusive interval yields
 A^-2 sum_{t=A}^B |F(t)|^2
 <=(a*C)^2/A^2 *[L*sum_{k,j<=K}gcd(k,j)+(sum_{k<=K}k)^2]
 <=(a*C)^2*[L*K^2*H_K/A^2 + K^2*(K+1)^2/(4A^2)],                 (1)
 H_K=sum_{n=1}^K1/n.
The first inequality uses count<=L/lcm(k,j)+1 and k*j/lcm=gcd. The second
uses sum gcd(k,j)<=sum_{g<=K}g*floor(K/g)^2<=K^2 H_K. Thus EVERY mixed pair
is included and bounded; this is not a diagonal-only identity. If K=0 and
the divisor subset is empty, the bound and output are bothzero.

This rule also covers arbitrary hyperbolic bands D0<d<=D1, d|t, provided
K is an exact integer upper bound for B/D0. The lower quotient constraint
coming from D1 can be kept in the state, although discarding it only enlarges
the positive bound. Strict endpoints require k<B/D0 when d>D0; a safe larger
K is permitted. In the square-root applications below an integer root of
4A or16A supplies a safe K without an irrational cutoff comparison.
To conclude a logarithmic bound uniformly, L/A and K^2/A must be bounded by
fixed constants independent of A. A general band with no such aspect bounds
only receives formula(1), not an automatically logarithmic certificate.

For implementation, H_K need not be enumerated or fitted. The elementary
dyadic grouping bound H_K<=1+ceil(log2 K) gives a purely integer upper bound
using (K-1).bit_length() for the ceiling when K>=1. The certificate data are
the exact interval, denominator A^2, coefficient envelope a, source envelope
C*k, and quotient cap K. A
sound AST rule must independently establish each hypothesis for all its
claimed contexts, keep original guards/endpoints, and not infer them from
finite responses. A fixed numerical upper bound on some saved cases is not
this uniform certificate. No component certificate changes the target E.

2. Full large-divisor complements, not just a new-cutoff strip
-------------------------------------------------------------
Write M(k)=sum_{n<=k}mu(n), M(0)=0, so |M(k)|<=k. Define R=floor(sqrt(X))
and the FULL complement on t in[X,2X-1]
 C_R(t)=sum_{d|t,d>R}mu(d)M(t/d).
Since integer d>R implies d>sqrt(X), its quotient satisfies
 k=t/d<2sqrt(X). Put K=floor(sqrt(4X)), so K<=2sqrt(X). Rule(1), a=C=1,
gives the explicit unconditional bound
 ||C_R||_X^2:=X^-2 sum_{t=X}^{2X-1}C_R(t)^2<=4H_K+9.             (2)
The constant9 follows from K+1<=3sqrt(X). The same bound holds for EVERY
t-dependent subset of this full complement and every coefficient of modulus
at most1. It does not depend on an asserted sign variation of mu(d).

The root integer is only an upper bound: if the strict quotient cutoff is
smaller, the state still keeps its exact d/t domain. Complements can include
d=t and quotient1. At X1 the actual complement is empty, though(2) remains
a harmless positive upper bound. The output is never clipped or extended to
nondivisors. Each term of the complement is a smaller prefix, but its whole
sum includes all their correlations.

The frozen parent complement also has a logarithmic bound on the CHILD
block. If its threshold remains R=floor(sqrt(X)) while t in[2X,4X-1], use
K0=floor(sqrt(16X)), A=2X,L=2X in(1), obtaining
 (4X^2)^-1 sum_child C_R(t)^2<=8H_K0+25.                         (3)
Here K0<=4sqrt(X), K0+1<=5sqrt(X). The fresh child threshold
R'=floor(sqrt(2X)) instead satisfies(2) at scale2X. Thus both complements
and every difference formed by deleting some of their divisor indices have
unconditional logarithmic normalized squares. The previously proved new-strip
bound32H_R' is another valid, more specialized estimate; it is not necessary
to restrict the full-complement rule to that strip.

3. Exact truncation exchange and the open mixed correction
----------------------------------------------------------
Let the cutoff-INDEPENDENT arithmetic function be
 H(t)=sum_{d|t}mu(d)M(t/d)=(mu*M)(t)
as a Dirichlet convolution of the function M with mu. ID39 is
 U_R(t)=H(t)-C_R(t).
The full transform is not1: sum_{d|t}H(d)=M(t), and at a prime p,
H(p)=M(p)-1. The inversion identity for an unweighted floor kernel cannot
be substituted for this M kernel.

With R' the child threshold, put Knew=C_R-C_R'. On the child block
U_R'=U_R+Knew, so the complete cutoff correction is
 2<U_R,Knew>_{2X}+||Knew||_{2X}^2
 =2<H,Knew>_{2X}+||C_R'||_{2X}^2-||C_R||_{2X}^2.                (4)
Formula(4) is the exact complement exchange, retaining every old/new and
new/new term. Its last two terms are individually logarithmically bounded
by(2),(3). The ONLY unrestricted critical factor in this decomposition is
the correlation <H,Knew>. Magnitude Cauchy-Schwarz bounds it by
||H||_{2X}*O(sqrt(log X)); an independent norm bound for H or a signed
transport absorption is still needed. The elementary norm of the old bank
only gives a positive-power bound here. Small complement norm alone never
licenses dropping its cross term with a large state.

This identifies a general certificate gate: the complement theorem can
discharge the C squares in a transport proof, while the proof must separately
discharge the signed H/Knew correlation (or absorb it by a proved negative
term from full original-energy transport). Both its coefficient and its exact
child interval remain in the gate. No unknown original energy on the right
has been replaced by a constant.

4. A new structural state can cancel the cutoff mixed term exactly
-----------------------------------------------------------------
There is a purely algebraic two-state completion. Take states
 Uminus=H-C_R (the old bank), Uplus=H+C_R=Uminus+2C_R,
with equal nonnegative weights1/2. The resulting potential is EXACTLY
 Vpair(X)=||H||_X^2+||C_R||_X^2.                                (5)
This is the parallelogram identity. All H/C mixed terms cancel between the
two complete squares; they have not been discarded inside either square.
The general compensation rule requires the same cutoff-independent H(t) in
both states and a proved quotient-cap envelope for C_X on parent and child.
If H also depends on X, its fresh-child change remains a separate unresolved
core transport; the parallelogram identity does not control that change.
If retaining the old state's weight1 is required, use weight1 for both and
the right side is2||H||^2+2||C_R||^2 instead. These are new programs, not
an asserted identity replacing ID39's original single-state potential.

The actual cutoffs, complements and queries admit finite AST expressions:
H is the sum over1<=d<=t with the divisor guard, and C is the sum over
R+1<=d<=t with that guard. M(t/d) has a positive endpoint on a true divisor
term. The source index d and prefix indices never exceed t. Empty sums retain
their original domain behavior. No implementation or research computation is
performed here; future implementation must still meet its own syntax/work caps.

For the paired program, the moving-cutoff contribution is just the difference
of C squares, logarithmically bounded without any H correlation. Hence its
full cofinal signed drift is equivalent, up to q-dependent constants, to the
cutoff-independent condition for
 E(X)+||H||_X^2.
Indeed the extra C energy is O(1+log X), and either direction of the increment
comparison loses at most its parent or child value. For every fixed q this
is bounded by a finite constant times ceil(X^(1/q)). The original E is fully
present. The construction closes cutoff compensation; it does NOT close the
remaining signed drift of E+H energy. At X1, H=1,C=0, so Vpair(1)=1 and
Wpair(1)=2 with weights1/2. The weight1 pair has Wpair(1)=3. These anchors
cannot be omitted in a later telescoping argument.

More generally, adding nonnegative squares can cancel an H/C mixed monomial
but cannot algebraically remove its unresolved H diagonal. Starting with
(H-C)^2 and adding w*(aH+bC)^2, w>=0, cancelling the cross requires wab=1;
the H diagonal is then1+w*a^2>=1. A new source relation or signed estimate,
rather than positivity alone, must control that remaining energy.

5. Exact block operator laws: where contraction is, and is not, available
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For an arbitrary input f, define its ORIGINAL exact quotient-window energy
 B_f(y)=y^-2 sum_{integer n, y<=n<2y}|f(n)|^2, y>0.
For integer y this is the usual original block energy. The real-window
notation is only explicit endpoint bookkeeping, not a transformed source.
Let D_d f(t)=1_{d|t}f(t/d). Exact integer endpoints imply
 ||D_d f||_X=d^-1*sqrt(B_f(X/d)).                               (6)
In particular its squared energy is B_f(X/d)/d^2, NOT B_f(X/d)/d.
The latter factor would belong to floor-lag repetition without a divisor guard.

Minkowski thus gives
 ||sum_d a_d D_d f||_X<=sum_d |a_d|/d *sqrt(B_f(X/d)).            (7)
Every term in(7) refers to the original input f at its explicit quotient
window. A claimed rule using only integer dyadic energies must first justify
coverage of those possibly noninteger/non-dyadic windows. A covering loss
cannot silently be omitted from a proposed contraction constant.

For the unweighted Mobius bank, the magnitude coefficient is H_R. This grows
with its cutoff, so(7) is not a cutoff-independent contraction. Furthermore
ANY full bank containing d1 fails a generic STRICT same-block contraction:
choose a formal input supported inside[X,2X-1] and zero below X. All d>=2
contributions to that output block vanish, and its output equals the input.
Thus a uniform constant<1 against that original block norm is impossible for
generic inputs. This does not rule out a signed rule specialized to actual M;
it says what extra functional information that rule must use.

Reciprocal DIVISOR weighting does give a precise generic tail contraction.
For P f=sum_{d>=2,d|t}a_d/d*f(t/d), |a_d|<=1, and the functional norm
 ||f||B=sup_{y>0}sqrt(B_f(y)) assumed finite, (7) gives
 ||P f||B<=kappa*||f||B,
 kappa=sum_{d>=2}1/d^2<=1/4+integral_2^infinity u^-2 du=3/4<1.
This contraction is on the original f quotient windows. It does not bound
||M||B without an additional bounded-forcing equation M=g+P M or another
valid source identity. No such equation is supplied by the unweighted ID39
bank. Local finite-window versions remain true but do not imply a uniform
bound as their horizon increases.

There is also an exact reciprocal normalization of convolution inversion.
Set m(t)=M(t)/t and h(t)=H(t)/t. Then
 h(t)=sum_{d|t}mu(d)/d*m(t/d),
 m(t)=sum_{d|t}1/d*h(t/d).
Their nonidentity tails satisfy the preceding contraction law, but it acts
on m,h rather than the original M,H energies. The scaling is decisive:
 E_M(X)/(4X^2)<=B_m(X)<=E_M(X)/X^2.
Recovering E_M multiplies by X^2. A contraction after this normalization is
not a uniform estimate in the critical original target units.

Reciprocal QUOTIENT weighting is a different operation:
 Q f(t)=sum_{d|t}mu(d)*f(t/d)/(t/d).
For |f(k)|<=k, |Q f(t)|<=tau(t), the full divisor count. If
J=floor(sqrt(2X-1)), divisor pairing gives tau(t)<=2*tau_J(t) on the block,
and exact multiple counts give
 ||Q f||_X^2<=4[H_J^3+2]/X=O((1+log X)^3/X).                   (8)
One uses sum_{h,j<=J}1/lcm(h,j)<=H_J^3 and J^2<=2X in the count remainder.
Thus this FULL quotient-weighted state, or any subset with bounded coefficients,
has uniformly bounded, decaying storage by a source-envelope rule alone.
It is bounded padding: adding it as its own square changes a cofinal E drift
only by a bounded error, without proving that drift. Mixing it inside an
unresolved square again retains its cross term with that square's core.

For its restricted bank1<=d<=R, an alternative exact original-energy bound is
 ||Q_R f||_X<=X^-1 sum_{d<=R}sqrt(B_f(X/d))
             <=R/X*sup_{d<=R}sqrt(B_f(X/d)).
The reciprocal quotient attenuates the original signal; it is not the ID39
operator with a new proof of contraction. It must not be substituted into
the original AST or target without explicitly creating a different state.

6. What a general full-drift certificate still needs
----------------------------------------------------
Rules(1),(2),(3),(8) are unconditional under their explicit envelope/support
hypotheses and suitable for a restricted symbolic rule library. The exact
paired-state construction(5) is a complete cutoff-compensation rule. Neither
requires fitted constants, an independent sign hypothesis or unknown E bounds.

A generic signed-drift theorem based ONLY on |f(k)|<=k, bounded divisor
coefficients and nonnegative potentials cannot close the original target.
Those magnitude hypotheses also permit the formal input f(k)=k, for which
E_f(X)>=X. Any nonnegative potential with a finite initial anchor satisfying
the cofinal drift condition would imply E_f(X)=O_q(X^(1/q)) by dyadic
telescoping, a contradiction for q>1. This is a functional obstruction,
not an experiment on another source and not a refutation for actual M.
Actual-Mobius information beyond that envelope must enter a successful theorem.

For ID39 itself the main remaining correlation is <H,Knew> in(4), together
with the signed frozen-core E/H transport. For the paired replacement the
cutoff correlation is genuinely removed, leaving exactly the signed drift
of E+||mu*M||^2. Reciprocal weights can prove different contractive or bounded
operators, but do not resolve that original critical-energy gate. All original
endpoints, complements, complete parent/child squares and initial W1 remain
part of the obligation. No RH/global/universal estimate is asserted.
