Cone metric and gradient contracts
All366 square coordinates and five slacks enter the scalar negative-part norm.
The two matrix cones are C PSD and H=P+4C PSD. P itself need not be PSD.
For half squared Frobenius cone distance, an upper-triangle offdiagonal has
coordinate derivative twice the corresponding negative-part entry. The H block
adds four times this derivative to C coordinates and once to P coordinates.
Apply the scaled adjoint D*N^T to these full projected-coordinate gradients.
The unit/constant affine state remains inside the full15 matrices.
