FINITE NORMALIZATION BRIDGES AND SOURCE-GENERATED POSITIVE KERNELS
================================================================

Scope and implementation
------------------------
Only new040 backend/test/receipt/memo files were written. No arithmetic archive,
actual full-source coefficient, new candidate observation, old study/suite or
hash was used. The source AST and mathematical synthetic fixtures generate
exact factorization and proof programs; there is no selected numerical winner.
The full signed bound and RH remain OPEN.

factor_candidates(source,meter=None,kernel_v_values=()) finds every power atom
with0<r<1. It removes exactlyone matching power atom from each covered channel;
every uncovered channel persists. Repetitions and channel weights survive.
It also enumerates shifted kernels using v from mu atoms or caller-supplied
arbitrary rationals, with v>r. It divides by mu_v exactly: delete a matching
mu_v atom when present, otherwise insert its inverse power_v. No LCM polynomial
or semantic denominator grid is required. Invalid positive-kernel pairs are
reported as inapplicable, rather than promoted to a theorem.

Factor fields include kernel_r,kernel_v (None for pure power),theta=1-r,
covered references, residual_source with exact rational atom shifts, uncovered
indices and a main_multiplier. The original source_binding remains complete.
The residual weighted source is denoted h. One obtains EXACTLY

 f=L*h+f_uncovered,

where L=K_r or L=K_r*mu_v and K_r(n)=n^(-r). The shifted equality follows from
mu_v*power_v=delta at every prime and valuation; it is not a finite jet law.

compile_bridges generates finite-cutoff programs, automatic head prices and
solved scale-nullspace templates. parameter_domain, when supplied, only prices
absolute affine weights; it does not replace exact scalar normalization.
The small numerical kernel/bridge helpers are for synthetic identity tests.
No import-time numerical source evaluation occurs.

1. The pure kernel remainder, including all subunit arguments
------------------------------------------------------------
For0<theta<1 and EVERY real y>0 define

 P_K(y)=sum_(1<=k<=floor y)k^(theta-1),
 e_K(y)=P_K(y)-y^theta/theta.

Then -1/theta<=e_K(y)<=0. If y<1 this is immediate. Otherwise m=floor y>=1;
monotonicity of t^(theta-1) gives

 ((m+1)^theta-1)/theta <=P_K(y)
 <=1+(m^theta-1)/theta <=y^theta/theta.

Since m+1>y the lower bound is at least y^theta/theta-1/theta. The kernel
statement includes integer endpoints, unit1, and every argument y=x/d<1.

2. Positive shifted kernel and complete absolute moment
--------------------------------------------------------
For0<r<1 and v>r, L=K_r*mu_v has local rational factor

 (1-p^(-v)t)/(1-p^(-r)t),
 L(p^e)=p^(-er)(1-p^(r-v))>0 for every e>=1, L(1)=1.

Let theta=1-r and a=theta+v>1. The AUXILIARY mu_v moment is absolutely
convergent:

 kappa=sum_(d>=1)mu(d)d^(-a)=1/zeta(a), 0<kappa<1.

This is not a statement that sum L(d)d^(-theta) converges: L's Dirichlet series
has the real theta pole. Nor does it license an ordinary residual h moment.
The backend explicitly labels the convergent object moment_source='mu_v'.

Define e_L(y)=P_L(y)-kappa y^theta/theta. For y>=1, writing m=floor y,

 e_L(y)=sum_(d<=y)mu(d)d^(-v)e_K(y/d)
        -(y^theta/theta)sum_(d>y)mu(d)d^(-a).

The ENTIRE second sum is retained and paid absolutely. Avoid replacing floor y
by y inside a decreasing tail integral. A uniformly valid estimate is

 sum_(d>y)d^(-a)
 <=(m+1)^(-a)+(m+1)^(1-a)/(a-1)
 <=y^(1-a)[1+1/(a-1)].

For r<v<1 this proves

 |e_L(y)|<=C y^(1-v),
 C=[1/(1-v)+1+1/(v-r)]/theta.                             (2.1)

For v=1, |e_L(y)|<=C[1+log(max(y,1))],
 C=[2+1/(1-r)]/theta.
For v>1, |e_L(y)|<=C,
 C=[v/(v-1)+1+1/(v-r)]/theta.

All statements hold ALSO for0<y<1: P_L(y)=0 and
|e_L(y)|=kappa y^theta/theta. When v<1, theta>1-v and y^theta<=y^(1-v).
For v>=1 use y^theta<=1. These constants deliberately sacrifice sharpness
to keep the all-real floor/tail proof elementary and rational.

For the source-generated pair r=1/3,v=2/3, (2.1) gives the safe constant21/2
and power1/3. The independently reviewed10 is stronger; no numeric optimization
or claim that21/2 is best is made. Source factors with arbitrary v, including
r+1/257,5/7,1 and4/3, retain exact rational shifts without rescaling the source
lattice. Constants grow as v approaches r; that debt is explicit.

3. Exact finite analytic-normalization bridge
--------------------------------------------
Let G_theta be the meromorphically continued Dirichlet value of h at theta,
when that value exists. It is a source-bound analytic scalar, NOT a presumed
ordinary coefficient sum. For ANY integer U>=0 define the finite moment

 M_U=sum_(d<=U)h(d)d^(-theta), delta_U=G_theta-M_U.

Let kappa=1 for the pure kernel and the above reciprocal zeta for shifted L.
Define complete finite expressions, for every real x>0,

 B_U(x)=sum_(d<=U)h(d)e_L(x/d),
 C_U(x)=sum_(U<d<=floor x)h(d)P_L(x/d)
          -kappa delta_U x^theta/theta.

Then exact finite reindexing gives

 F(x)=F_uncovered(x)+B_U(x)+C_U(x)
            +kappa G_theta x^theta/theta.                  (3.1)

The head sum includes ALL d<=U, even d>x; those terms have P_L(x/d)=0 but
a nonzero inner main. For U=0 the moment/head are empty and the tail retains
the unit. For U>x the raw tail is empty, yet its analytic compensation remains.
Both cases satisfy (3.1). No infinite coefficient tail is equated to delta_U.
It is the difference of a named analytic value and a genuinely finite sum.

Exact source normalization may prove G_theta=0 by analytic continuation.
For a shifted factor the analytic residual changes by zeta(theta+v), a regular
nonzero factor, so zero transfers. This still supplies no rate for delta_U,
no ordinary h(theta) convergence, and no independent bound on C_U.
Uncovered channels, especially the actual -beta mu channel, stay joined.

4. Automatically compiled finite-head price
--------------------------------------------
For a residual channel with k atoms, define growth gamma as the sum of positive
coefficient exponents from negative shifts. Absolute coefficients satisfy
|h_j(d)|<=|weight_j|d^gamma d_k(d). For k=0 the coefficient is a weighted delta.
Divisor summation gives sum_(d<=U)d_k(d)<=U(1+log U)^(k-1).

If the kernel error is C x^rho(1+log^+x)^ell, where0<=rho<1, then the needed
weighted mass is bounded by

 sum_(d<=U)d^gamma d_k(d)d^(-rho)
 <=M U^(1+gamma-rho)(1+log U)^(k-1),
 M=max(1,1/(1+gamma-rho)).

For gamma<rho this follows from Stieltjes partial summation; for gamma>=rho
pull out the increasing weight. The denominator is positive. For delta use
mass1. With U=floor(X^alpha), alpha>=0, and X<=n<2X, the complete head's
critical energy is bounded by the sum of channel terms with power

 2rho-1+2alpha(1+gamma-rho)                                (4.1)

and log degree2(k-1)+2ell. Delta channels have power2rho-1. The compiler pays
the square inequality's covered-channel count, squared absolute weight,
C^2 M^2, factor4 when a block power/log needs it, and
max(1,alpha)^(2(k-1)) for substituting log U. It never removes signed crosses
from the SOURCE identity; only this declared upper-bound route uses triangle.

Power<=0 is cofinally paid, with stated logs; power<0 decays. For the generated
r1/3,v2/3 pair and nonnegative residual shifts, (4.1) is
 -1/3+4alpha/3, hence alpha<=1/4 is paid. This is a price for a FINITE HEAD,
not for the whole signed source or complete centered tail. The detector chooses
from caller-supplied rational cutoffs and reports every unpaid term. The main
integration may instead use persistent whole moment/kernel states; their
full costs cannot be inferred from this small-head theorem.

5. Automatic scale-template selection, and the old filter limitation
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The prototype enumerates finite divisor subsets and solves their exact main
nullspace, rather than requiring a nominated coefficient row. Write
a_j=lambda_j d_j^theta; solving sumlambda_j=0 cancels the common analytic main.
Coefficients are typed rational*positive-integer^rational, not huge LCM polynomials.
For unequal cutoffs U_j, the finite difference is exactly

 delta_Uj-delta_U0 =-[M_Uj-M_U0].

Consequently sum_j a_j C_Uj(x/d_j) equals the sum of raw complete tails PLUS
kappa x^theta/theta times sum_j lambda_j[M_Uj-M_U0]. Every oriented finite band
is retained, including the case a scaled cutoff is zero. Cutoff base X and
prefix argument x are separate variables. The complete filtered original
source also includes sum_j a_j F_uncovered(x/d_j); units are not projected out.

This is an exact constructive interface, but it overlaps old fixed pole filters.
It is not promoted as a new arithmetic estimate. The inverse/recovery debt is
OPEN. In particular I-d^theta T_d has a homogeneous x^theta mode and fails the
ordinary critical absolute inverse criterion for theta>1/2. Analytic absence
of a real pole is not by itself a bounded inverse. Any alternative pole-order
RH implication must be checked from the FULL source, including every numerator,
uncovered term and possible complex filter zero. A finite sample cannot do so.

The substantive supplied new provider is the source-generated positive shifted
kernel with complete all-real remainder control, together with (3.1)'s finite
normalization accounting. It may support a later periodic/arithmetic proof
construction. Neither scalar cancellation nor nullspace selection pays the
remaining whole centered tail or -beta M companion.
