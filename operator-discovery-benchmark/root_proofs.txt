UNIVERSAL LAWS FOR THE EXPANDED GRAMMAR

These are mathematical proofs, not machine-checked Lean certificates. The
accompanying exact rational tests audit implementations of the laws. Finite
tests alone do not prove any universal statement. All arithmetic functions
below have domain the positive integers and values in Q (or C when explicitly
stated). delta means delta(1)=1 and delta(n)=0 for n>1; u means u(n)=1.

1. COEFFICIENT-LOCAL RATIONAL POWERS

Write (f*g)(n)=sum_{ij=n}f(i)g(j). This is a commutative associative Q-algebra
with identity delta: associativity follows by reindexing both parenthesizations
as the finite sum over triples ijk=n. If h(1)=0, a nonzero term in h^{*k}(n)
has all k indices at least 2, hence n>=2^k. Therefore h^{*k}(n)=0 for 2^k>n.

For f(1)=1 and a in Q define
  P_a(f)=sum_{k>=0} binom(a,k) (f-delta)^{*k}.
The preceding bound makes every coefficient a FINITE sum. It also establishes
prefix consistency and termination without analytic convergence assumptions.

Work temporarily in the finite quotient of arithmetic functions retaining
n<=N. In this quotient h=f-delta is nilpotent, h^{*k}=0 for 2^k>N. Substitution
of h into identities of formal power series over Q is therefore legitimate:
no infinite sums survive. The binomial identity
(1+x)^a(1+x)^b=(1+x)^{a+b} yields P_a(f)*P_b(f)=P_{a+b}(f).
The formal identity ((1+x)^a)^b=(1+x)^{ab}, with both powers normalized at 1,
gives P_b(P_a(f))=P_{ab}(f). One proof of these formal identities is the
coefficient recurrence k*c_k=(a-k+1)*c_{k-1} obtained from
(1+x)F'(x)=a F(x), F(0)=1; products and compositions satisfy the same
equations and constant terms. This holds for every N, hence at every n.

P_{1/q}(f)^{*q}=f for every integer q>=2. Uniqueness of the normalized root:
in coefficient n>1 of r^{*q}, exactly q factorizations have one index n and
all other indices 1; their contribution is q*r(n). All other nonzero terms
involve indices <n. Thus r(n) is forced by f(n) and earlier coefficients.
For q=2 this becomes
  r(n)=(f(n)-sum_{d|n,1<d<n}r(d)r(n/d))/2, r(1)=1.
Strong induction proves this recurrence defines the unique normalized root.
These statements fail without a normalization/constant-term choice, so the
implementation rejects f(1)!=1 rather than silently choosing another branch.

2. FORMAL LOGARITHM AND EXPONENTIAL

For f(1)=1 and h(1)=0 define
  LOG(f)=sum_{k>=1}(-1)^{k+1}(f-delta)^{*k}/k,
  EXP(h)=sum_{k>=0}h^{*k}/k!.
The same 2^k bound makes these coefficient-local. Substitution into the
formal identities exp(log(1+x))=1+x and log(exp(x))=x gives inverse laws.
Similarly LOG(f*g)=LOG(f)+LOG(g), EXP(h+k)=EXP(h)*EXP(k), and
P_a(f)=EXP(a*LOG(f)). These are classical formal arithmetic operations;
recovery by a search is not evidence of mathematical novelty.

3. ANALYTIC BRIDGE FOR ZETA ROOTS

The Euler product for zeta holds absolutely on Re(s)>1. The unique normalized
local power (1-x)^(-a) has coefficients
  c_a(e)=a(a+1)...(a+e-1)/e!, c_a(0)=1.
Take a=1/q with q>=2. Then 0<c_a(e)<=1. Define the multiplicative sequence
  b_a(n)=product_{p^e||n}c_a(e).
Consequently 0<b_a(n)<=1, so its Dirichlet series is absolutely convergent
on Re(s)>1. Finite local coefficient identities show b_a^{*q}=u. Uniqueness
from section 1 identifies b_a with P_a(u), so the grammar's root is exactly
this sequence, with no factorization assumption needed in its construction.

Absolute convergence justifies multiplying series, giving
  B_q(s)^q=zeta(s), B_q(s)=sum_n b_(1/q)(n)n^(-s), Re(s)>1.
The normalized analytic logarithm of zeta on this half-plane is the absolutely
convergent Euler logarithm, which tends to zero as Re(s) tends to infinity.
Thus B_q=exp(Log(zeta)/q) for this branch. A pointwise principal log need not
agree with it throughout the half-plane; the numerical checks use Re(s)>=4,
where |zeta(s)-1|<=zeta(4)-1<1 and the principal log agrees.

For sigma>1, the omitted series tail after N is bounded by
  sum_{n>N}|b_a(n)n^(-s)| <= sum_{n>N}n^(-sigma)
                            <= integral_N^infinity x^(-sigma)dx
                            =N^(1-sigma)/(sigma-1).
root_probe.py checks Arb ball differences against this bound, with both the
finite sum and the numerical zeta root independently evaluated by Arb.

No globally single-valued qth root across all zeta zeros is asserted. On a
region containing a zero of order m, a holomorphic qth root can exist locally
only if q divides m: orders in B^q=zeta would satisfy q*ord(B)=m. RH concerns
the locations of zeros, not this divisibility requirement. These arithmetic
root identities therefore do not by themselves imply RH.

4. DENOMINATORS REVEAL DIGITAL ARITHMETIC

For a=1/q the local coefficient is
  c_(1/q)(e)=product_{j=0}^{e-1}(q*j+1)/(q^e*e!).
If ell is a prime dividing q, every numerator factor is 1 mod ell, so
  v_ell(denominator(c_(1/q)(e)))=e*v_ell(q)+v_ell(e!).
If ell does not divide q, each block of ell^k consecutive j contains exactly
one solution to q*j+1=0 mod ell^k. Hence the numerator contains at least
floor(e/ell^k) such factors for every k. Summing over k proves its ell
valuation is at least v_ell(e!), so ell does not survive in the denominator.
Multiplicativity then gives, for EVERY positive integer n,
  D_(q,ell)(n)=v_ell(denominator(b_(1/q)(n)))
             =v_ell(q)*Omega(n)+sum_{p^e||n}v_ell(e!).
There is no cancellation against ell within individual or multiplied factors
when ell divides q; this explains why the denominator valuations add.

Legendre's formula follows by counting multiples of ell^k in e!:
  v_ell(e!)=sum_{k>=1}floor(e/ell^k)=(e-s_ell(e))/(ell-1),
where s_ell is the base-ell digit sum. The last equality follows by expanding
e=sum_j d_j*ell^j and interchanging the two finite sums. In particular,
  D_(2,2)(n)=2*Omega(n)-sum_{p^e||n}s_2(e).
The detector is permitted to fit these expressions only AFTER it observes
the exact root coefficients. The proof interprets, and is separate from,
that empirical fit. This is a consequence of classical binomial valuations,
not a claim that the formula is a newly discovered theorem.

5. CARRY-WEIGHTED CONVOLUTION AND ITS REAL LIMITATION

Let G_ell(n)=sum_{p^e||n}v_ell(e!), and set
  C_ell(i,j)=G_ell(ij)-G_ell(i)-G_ell(j)
           =sum_p v_ell(binomial(v_p(i)+v_p(j),v_p(i))).
It is a nonnegative integer. Legendre's formula expresses it as
  sum_p [s_ell(v_p(i))+s_ell(v_p(j))-s_ell(v_p(i)+v_p(j))]/(ell-1).
This counts carries, including propagated carries, in the corresponding
base-ell exponent additions. The linear Omega term in D_(q,ell) cancels,
so C_ell is also the defect D(ij)-D(i)-D(j), independently of q with ell|q.

For any rational t define
  (a *_ell,t b)(n)=sum_{ij=n}t^{C_ell(i,j)}a(i)b(j),
with t=0 interpreted by 0^0=1 and 0^k=0 for k>0. The defect telescopes:
  C(i,j)+C(ij,k)=G(ijk)-G(i)-G(j)-G(k)
                =C(j,k)+C(i,jk).
Expanding triple convolution therefore proves associativity for every n and
all inputs, including t=0. Symmetry proves commutativity, and C(1,n)=0
proves delta is the identity. These constitute a universal operator proof.

For t!=0 it is a transport of ordinary Dirichlet convolution:
  a *_ell,t b = W_t^{-1}((W_t a)*(W_t b)),
where (W_t a)(n)=t^{-G_ell(n)}a(n). Therefore this family at nonzero t is an
algebra isomorphism, not independent arithmetic algebra in the abstract.
At ell=2,t=0 the surviving factorizations have disjoint binary exponent
digits; this is classical infinitary convolution. Other prime bases similarly
retain no-carry exponent splits. Numerical recovery is interesting evidence
of structure in zeta roots, but does not establish historical novelty.

The superficially similar weight t^{Omega(gcd(i,j))} is generally NOT
associative: at i=j=2,k=4, the exponent sums on the two sides are 3 and 2.
Indeed min(1,1)+min(2,2)=3 but min(1,2)+min(1,3)=2. At t=2 the two impulse
products at n=16 have weights 8 and 4. A finite negative control checks that
the detector rejects this tempting candidate instead of assuming associativity.

NOVELTY AND PROOF STATUS

All proofs above are reusable explanations with stated domains. They certify
the defined laws mathematically, not via a theorem prover. Their programs are
also checked on unrelated rational inputs. Rational powers, formal LOG/EXP,
Legendre/Kummer valuation arithmetic and infinitary convolution all have
prior art. The carry family is reducible to a known weighted transport for
nonzero t. A candidate may be new to the detector without being new to
mathematics; none is counted as historically new on that basis.
