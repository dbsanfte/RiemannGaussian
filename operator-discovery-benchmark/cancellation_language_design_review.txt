A SMALL SIGN-PRESERVING LANGUAGE EXTENSION FOR ACTUAL MOBIUS/PRIME ARITHMETIC

Read-only design after the grouped SOS audit. Existing frozen stages already
contain complete signed Grams, gcd primitive decomposition, coprime-squarefree
density, prime-log forcing, polynomial cycles, factor-certified norm/gcd laws,
and exact finite SOS/separators. These are not proposed as new capabilities.
The accepted grouped mu critical templates collapsed to a cubic scalar budget;
this demonstrates a specific unsigned certificate route, not impossibility of
other proofs or a prediction about RH. No new numerical scores or source edits.

Recommendation: add a typed TWO-AXIS relational kernel language before adding
more fitted potentials. Its unit is a complete signed pair sum with a declared
source/domain, not a list of nonnegative scalar observables. Candidate rewrites
must produce a new pair kernel PLUS an explicit complete complement/boundary.
This adds an implementable way to search sign-preserving transformations of the
unknown mixed sum, rather than another equivalent critical correction fit.

1. Minimal productions and exact semantics.
 PairSum(f,g,Domain,K)=sum_(a,b in Domain)f(a)g(b)K(a,b).
 PartitionPair(predicate) returns disjoint signed pieces whose union is exact.
 ReindexPair(map,inverse) requires a proved finite bijection, coefficient law,
 source/product horizon, every floor endpoint and an unmatched complement.
 Polarize(U,V) keeps <U,V>=(||U+V||^2-||U-V||^2)/4 as an EXACT signed
 relation, not the upper bound from two unsigned squares. Block normalization
 X^-2 is explicit, and both own-scale child cutoffs remain declared.
 MixedDilate(p,q,K)=K(a,b)-K(pa,b)-K(a,qb)+K(pa,qb).
All coefficients are exact rationals; rational floor cutoffs and resource limits
are declared. General sparse rational linear combinations, mutation and region
refinement are allowed without passing a nominated winning kernel. Arithmetic
rewrites and mathematical proof methods are supplied assumptions, not discovered
arithmetic estimates. Unsupported relation transformations are missing, not zeros.

2. A concrete actual-prime coboundary calibration.
Fix prime p and define B_p(n)=sum_(a<=n,(a,p)=1)mu(a). Actual prime-power
normalization gives EXACTLY
 M(n)=B_p(n)-B_p(floor(n/p)).
Indeed source n has either no p factor, one p factor with opposite sign, or
at least two p factors with coefficient0. This is an all-coefficient identity,
not an approximation or an RH-strength bound for B_p.
Its COMPLETE parent energy equals
 sum_(a,b positive,p∤ab)mu(a)mu(b)L_X,p(a,b),
 L=K_X(max(a,b))-K_X(max(pa,b))-K_X(max(a,pb))+K_X(max(pa,pb))
  =max(0,min(2X,pa,pb)-max(X,a,b)).
The last equality counts the exact intersection of integer shells
 a<=n<pa and b<=n<pb inside X<=n<2X. Boundaries are exclusive at pa,pb,2X
and inclusive at a,b,X. This nonnegative kernel is still multiplied by signed
mu(a)mu(b); its positivity is not an arithmetic energy bound. The child uses
L_2X,p, and the full signed innovation is L_2X,p-4L_X,p.
The smallest actionable implementation is this MixedDilate calibration plus
independent complete small-domain pair enumeration and a mutation-capable
pair-domain AST. No search of global constants is needed to test correctness.

3. Coupled bilateral correlations, not isolated shell successes.
For disjoint declared source blocks A,B, keep
 C_X(A,B)=sum_(a in A,b in B)mu(a)mu(b)J_X(max(a,b)),
with BOTH ordered cross copies in the full target. Gcd coordinates can expose
coprime prefixes, but g1 and the complete primitive-height complement remain.
Search grouped relations between a handful of adjacent blocks and their prime-
dilated images, sharing coefficients across normalized scale regions. A negative
mixed contribution may cancel positive diagonal/block terms only after an exact
full reconstruction, not by scoring attractive pieces separately. A relation
must account for moving rational cutoffs in BOTH scales of the innovation.
Existing prime-log forcing can be an additional ReindexPair production, but
log denominators and every permitted prime power remain charged. Its zero kernel
moment does not cancel the nonconstant logarithmic-density drift automatically.

4. Actual prime-error route with the main term retained.
Lambda=mu*log gives exact prime-power provenance, but does not supply a prime
error estimate. If the target is P(n)=psi(n)-n, use increments Lambda(n)-1,
not Lambda alone. A divisor split at U retains both full sums
 psi(x)=sum_(d<=U)mu(d)log(floor(x/d)!)+sum_(U<d<=floor x)mu(d)log(floor(x/d)!).
Squaring keeps small/large-divisor cross terms. This route permits exact coupled
mu/prime forcing kernels, but its large-divisor remainder is an obligation.
PNT alone does not provide an RH-strength centered mixed estimate.

5. Finite inference and the explicit uniform gate.
Finite complete pair responses can nominate relational templates through exact
kernel fitting, sparse rational coefficients, symbolic coefficient matching and
exact dual-guided basis expansion. Share BOTH square and signed-relation weights,
then freeze the transform and transfer without refitting. Record norm/unsigned
charges separately, including any cubic scalar budget. A compression motif must
have a proved complete lattice-domain identity or recursion at every scale.
The missing analytic step remains a proved ONE-SIDED estimate for the complete
actual mixed signed innovation, with existsq0, everyq>=q0, finiteCq independentX,
everydyadicX, and all diagonal/residual/recovery costs. No generic square-root
coprime-prefix estimate, prime randomness, PNT-at-RH-strength, or the same desired
energy bound may be introduced as a new axiom. Exact expression is increased
proof-search capability, not evidence that this uniform estimate will be found.

6. Adverse cases owned by the extension.
Independently test every MixedDilate endpoint on complete tiny domains, including
p>X, X1, a=b, a or b at p boundaries and source powersp². Mutate one dilation
sign, omit one mixed cross block, change a parent cutoff at the child, omit g1,
truncate a product/source horizon, or drop the divisor complement: full identity
checking must reject each alteration. Use unsigned coefficients and squarefree
f_P counterfeits with partially normalized primes to reject claims from kernel
geometry/norms alone. A fixed-p coboundary law holds for f_P whenever p is inP,
yet that model has positive mean and cubic defect; one prime pairing is therefore
not a generic critical proof. Finite local normalization is insufficient for
these listed premises; no claim that every individual prime sign is logically
necessary in every possible successful proof is made.

Concrete handoff: implement the pair-domain AST, exact partition/bijection
certificates and the prime MixedDilate calibration first. Keep the original
full signed defect as the only recovery target. Delay another bound-fitting
stage until the language can independently certify its complete transformation,
remainder and adverse mutations. No increase in the chance of proving RH is
claimed; the benefit is that candidate sign relations become explicit, testable
and resistant to unsigned collapse.
