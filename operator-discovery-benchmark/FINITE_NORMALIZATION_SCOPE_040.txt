1. Exact common-power factorization and what normalization actually says
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Put beta=9/2, r=1/3, theta=1-r=2/3, v=2/3. Every star below is DIRICHLET
convolution. Write mu_v(n)=mu(n)n^-v, One_v(n)=n^-v and K_r(n)=n^-r.

 g=beta*mu*mu-beta*mu*mu_v-R*mu_v,
 f=K_r*g-beta*mu.

For initial Re(s)>1 the corresponding Dirichlet series identities are
absolute Euler identities. Their values at theta are analytic continuation;
ordinary convergence of sum g(n)n^-theta is NOT a premise.

 G(theta)=0 at the separately specified actual R=R_u. A source-family member
R different from R_u generally has a nonzero main coefficient. The039 pole
guard licenses exact cancellation and nonzero regular zeta constants, not an
ordinary critical series or a uniformly small finite truncation defect.

For all real y>=0 set
 e_theta(y)=sum_(k<=y)k^(theta-1)-y^theta/theta.
The decreasing integral bounds give -1/theta<=e_theta(y)<=0, including
0<=y<1. Consequently for x>=1,

 F(x)=sum_(d<=x)g(d)e_theta(x/d)-beta*M(x)
       -x^theta*tau_g(x)/theta,
 tau_g(x)=G(theta)-sum_(d<=x)g(d)d^-theta.

Tau is an ANALYTIC-MINUS-FINITE difference, not a convergent infinite tail.
This exact normalization-sensitive formula does not estimate tau or the
whole signed combination. Declaring its right hand side small is equivalent
to the original bound until an independent argument prices it.

2. A stronger source-generated shifted-kernel production
---------------------------------------------------------
The exact identity mu_v*One_v=delta instead permits

 h=beta*mu*mu*One_v-beta*mu-R*delta,
 L=K_r*mu_v,
 f=L*h-beta*mu.

The factorization is an all-coefficient Euler/convolution identity, not a
finite jet match. H(theta)=R_u-R analytically; hence the actual H(theta)=0.
The shifted inverse has an ABSOLUTELY convergent moment at theta because
theta+v>1:

 m_L=sum mu(d)d^(-theta-v)=1/zeta(theta+v).

For the actual source, m_L=1/zeta(4/3). This convergence claim concerns only
the shifted inverse, never h or the original unshifted inverse.

For general rational 0<r<v<1, theta=1-r, delta=1-v, L is positive
multiplicative with

 L(p^e)=p^(-er)*(1-p^(-(v-r))) for every e>=1.

It follows from exact local factors (1-p^-v t)/(1-p^-r t). Positivity alone
does not give a coercive inverse on arbitrary source prefixes.

Define e_L(x)=prefix(L)(x)-m_L*x^theta/theta. The following all-real rule
is independently justified:

 |e_L(x)|<=C x^delta, x>0,
 C=theta^-1[(1-v)^-1+(v-r)^-1+1].

Proof for x>=1: split prefix(K_r*mu_v), subtract the FULL absolutely convergent
moment, and use the bounded K error. The finite part is at most
theta^-1 sum_(d<=x)d^-v <=theta^-1 x^(1-v)/(1-v). For w=theta+v>1,

 sum_(d>x)d^-w <= x^(1-w)[1/(w-1)+1/x].

This includes the first unobserved integer before the decreasing tail
integral. Multiplying it by x^theta/theta supplies the remaining terms. For
0<x<1, the prefix is zero, m_L<1, and theta>delta, so the same bound holds.
There is no removed divisor with x/d<1.

Actual r=1/3,v=2/3 gives this generic C=21/2. A separately proved sharper
actual constant10 is safe: integer x has constant9, then interpolate from
floor(x), using m_L<1 and (x^theta-floor(x)^theta)/theta<=1 when x>=1.
The constant9 must not simply be asserted for all real x from the integer
tail integral. Constant tuning is not the substantive advance here.

At v=1 the finite mass has one logarithm, and at v>1 it is bounded. Those
extensions need their separate constants and scopes. At v=r the local
kernel cancels EXACTLY to delta; this is a different rule, not an absolutely
convergent inverse moment at exponent1. At v<r this positive-kernel provider
does not apply. An unrestricted rational search must reject these crossings
or use explicitly different proved rules.

3. Finite centered lifts: a proved cost, not the remaining theorem
-----------------------------------------------------------------
For any FIXED finite h_Y, including the complete unit, its centered lift is

 S_Y(x)=prefix(L*h_Y)(x)-m_L H_Y(theta)x^theta/theta
       =sum_(d<=Y)h(d)e_L(x/d).

The sum runs through ALL d<=Y, including d>x. In particular

 |S_Y(x)|<=C x^delta sum_(d<=Y)|h(d)|d^-delta.

For the actual source |h(n)|<=14 d3(n), using beta=9/2 and 1<R_u<5.
Elementary ordered factorization gives

 sum_(d<=Y)d3(d)d^-delta
 <=Y^(1-delta)(1+log Y)^2/(1-delta).

Using actual C=10 therefore gives
 |S_Y(x)|<=210 x^(1/3)Y^(2/3)(1+log Y)^2,
 E_(S_Y)(X)<=176400 Y^(4/3) X^(-1/3)(1+log Y)^4.

The latter uses E=X^-2 sum_(X<=n<2X), conservative2^2 for the x^(2/3)
factor, every integer X>=1, and the same Y throughout the window. Thus
Y<=X^a with a<1/4 pays this finite head independently. General rational
exponents are admitted by inequalities, not a nominated root list.

This convenient cutoff is also a warning: if the head energy vanishes,
F-S_Y differs from F by a vanishing norm. Searching a better finite constant
on that remainder can repeat037 padding-control behaviour; it is not evidence
of persistent cancellation. A full recurrence or signed-correlation theorem
can still be useful, but its OPEN leaf is the actual work.

4. Persistent whole moment/remainder split and exact moving boundaries
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The root's proposed persistent split is suitable for an honest next attempt:

 A_v(x)=m_L*x^theta/theta *sum_(d<=x)h_v(d)d^-theta,
 B_v(x)=prefix(L*h_v)(x)-A_v(x),
 F(x)=A_v(x)+B_v(x)-beta*M(x)

for the actual factorization; a generalized factor must retain its complete
uncovered signed terms. Analytic normalization says
A_v(x)=-m_L*x^theta*tau_h(x)/theta, but supplies no estimate on that defect.
Both components persist, and any storage must recover their original signed
sum with an independently checked PSD inequality, including every cross.

Useful exact scale identities, rather than an assumed improvement, are

 A_v(2x)-2^theta A_v(x)
   =m_L*(2x)^theta/theta sum_(x<d<=2x)h_v(d)d^-theta,

 B_v(2x)-2^theta B_v(x)
   =sum_(d<=x)h_v(d)[e_L(2x/d)-2^theta e_L(x/d)]
    +sum_(x<d<=2x)h_v(d)e_L(2x/d).

The discrete child2n+1 has its own exact ratio and extra event; it is not
replaced by child2n. A changing finite cutoff requires all new band terms
and the unit. A bound on the sum of these signed bands, or an all-scale
storage recurrence with independently paid forcing, would be an actual
argument. No such bound follows from the kernel error alone.

A machine can enumerate factor pairs and rational storage coefficients,
form the exact parent/child quadratic defect, apply source-generated global
relations and known kernel/forcing rules, and emit either an independently
replayed inequality or the explicit unpriced signed band/cross-correlation.
Finite values may rank candidates but cannot discharge this universal leaf.
The pure norm relaxation already fails on generic envelopes; that is not an
actual arithmetic refutation.

5. Another genuine finite normalization positive control: alternating inverse
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a proposed additional provider, not implemented in the current stage.
Let a(n)=(-1)^(n-1), D=delta-2delta_2. Then mu*a=D coefficientwise, so

 q=a*a*h=beta*D*D*One_v-beta*D*a-R*a*a.

For theta>1/2, a's ordinary critical moment eta(theta) exists by alternating
series. The critical moment of a*a also exists, by an exact hyperbola split;
one must prove this rather than rearrange a conditionally convergent double
series. If B_theta(x)=sum_(mn<=x)a(m)a(n)(mn)^-theta and m=floor sqrt(x),

 eta(theta)^2-B_theta(x)
  =[eta(theta)-A_theta(m)]^2
    +2 sum_(d<=m)a(d)d^-theta[eta(theta)-A_theta(x/d)].

The alternating tail yields absolute error<=6x^(1/2-theta) for all x>=1.
The finite shifted positive power factor has an absolutely convergent moment
when theta+v>1. Thus q's ordinary critical moment exists and is

 beta*D(theta)^2*zeta(theta+v)
  -beta*D(theta)*eta(theta)-R*eta(theta)^2.

Since eta(theta)=D(theta)zeta(theta), this vanishes EXACTLY at fixed R_u.
For actual theta=v=2/3, R<=5, finite shifts1,2,4 and the tail estimates give
the safe normalization-sensitive bound

 |sum_(n<=x)q(n)n^-theta|<= (411/2)x^(-1/6), x>=1.

This contrasts with the nonzero limiting constant for nonnormalized R.
It is a genuine finite bound derived without ordinary convergence of h or mu
at theta. It is a bound for a CONVOLVED source, not the original F.

Its recovery is expensive: inverting a*a requires mu*mu and D^-2. The fixed
dilation D has Dirichlet multiplier1-2^(1-s), with zeros at
s=1+2*pi*i*k/log2 and an inverse with coefficients(k+1)2^k at2^k.
No bounded critical inverse is supplied. The original actual source cannot
be paid by relabeling this convolved-source theorem. This is a useful positive
control for normalization-sensitive proof discovery and a negative control
for dropped recovery.

6. Bad shortcuts and necessary checker gates
--------------------------------------------
* Keep the separately admitted exact R_u definition. An interval R in[1,5]
  supports coefficient prices but cannot license normalized source bounds.
* Initial Euler identities require the absolute convergence domain; interior
  scalar evaluations use analytic continuation. Do not equate them with an
  ordinary coefficient series unless a separate convergence theorem proves it.
* Source factorization must preserve every channel, unit and uncovered term.
  A shifted inverse insertion requires the exact matching positive inverse.
* Kernel mass, tail and inner real-argument rules have explicit rational
  parameter domains; equality cases/logarithms and all d>x survive.
* Exact finite means H_Y(theta), analytic H(theta) and their difference are
  distinct types. Zero analytic H(theta) does not set the finite mean to zero.
* A complete source split or endpoint renaming is an identity, not a bound.
  Rank storage only after checking original-target recovery and all costs.
* Finite changes in Y,v or scale require complete transition bands and both
  child parities. All signed source and mixed storage crosses are retained.
* A dyadic filter annihilating the real theta main also has artificial complex
  zeros theta+2*pi*i*k/log2. Scalar cancellation at the real point alone does
  not supply a stable inverse, ordinary convergence, or all missing residues.
* Smoothing/Perron contours require their entire remainder and any other poles;
  local regularity at the real theta is not a global contour-shift license.
* Norm countermodels and finite-template witnesses have their declared grammar
  scope only. The original whole signed bound and RH remain OPEN.

The credible next stage is therefore a source-normalized persistent split plus
an actual complete signed recurrence/proof attempt. Kernel and finite-head
costs are proved and useful, but are insufficient alone. No all-scale original
source recurrence or whole-core estimate is established by this review.
