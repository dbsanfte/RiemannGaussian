Free-coordinate continuation versus cone-Jacobi scaling: analysis v1
2026-10-09. New memo only. Publication018/frozen sources and earlier notes
remain unchanged. No research rerun, new seed metric, solver, map replay,
source generation or heavy exact elimination was performed.

Recommendation

Prefer one preregistered EXACT-RATIONAL PRODUCER COLUMN-NORM cone-Jacobi
experiment, with norms explicitly unverified numerical calibration, from the
publication018 exact endpoint, if its explicit rational norm/bit caps complete.
It removes the known disparity in free-coordinate cone sensitivity without
materializing denseN, changing the merit, truncating support or guessing faces.
Any strictly positiveD preserves the feasible set independently of whether
the producer norms are correct. Norm correctness need not be trusted for
proposal generation or final admission. Its projected advantage over simply
running more iterations is plausible,
not guaranteed: normalization does not remove near-parallel columns, PSD
spectral approximation or a badly conditioned affine chart. A longer identity-
scaled continuation is a useful CONTROL, not the recommended main improvement.
Do not run a probe fallback or extra seed automatically if exact norms hit a cap.

Longer continuation alone

Continue the SAME exact018 endpoint, identity coordinate scaling, same merit,
80-digit sparse map/binary64 eigensolves and no new clipping or seed. The
archived curvature operator is independent of the affine center, so its
estimate/step can be reused as licensed proposal data or recomputed once
under a fixed rule. At the observed one-trial work rate,128 additional steps
would cost roughly170m proposal units and fit the nominal229.5m remaining
allocation. At three trials per step the cost can be far larger, so iteration
count alone is insufficient: an explicit charged budget and early termination
rule are necessary.

For perspective ONLY, extending the final local ratio unchanged for128 steps
would multiply merit by roughly0.155; reducing it by1e6 at that same ratio would
need about949 steps. These are illustrations of why24 more steps may merely
continue slow contraction, NOT convergence forecasts. Eigenvalue/sign active
sets can change, the ratio need not persist, and PSD penalty may remain or grow.
The old step is constrained by a large global sensitivity; more iterations
address neither that disparity nor almost dependent free directions.

Exact full-column cone-Jacobi

Write x=c+Nf in the licensed445-dimensional chart. Let B stack all366 square
and five slack coordinates, C and H=P+4C with the full symmetric Frobenius
metric. For each free coordinatej, compute the HOMOGENEOUS exact response
    v_j=N*e_j
by one descending-pivot sparse back-substitution with zeroRHS. The affine
constant must NOT be included. Do not copy/normalize the basis anew for each
column: a zeroRHS operator can skip the stored augmented target while retaining
the same licensed coefficients. No denseN or new rank decision is required.

Compute the exact rational
    g_j=||B*v_j||^2
       =sum_scalar v_i^2 + ||C(v_j)||_F^2 + ||P(v_j)+4C(v_j)||_F^2.
Use diagonal squares once and upper offdiagonal squares TWICE. Every g_j>=0.
A streaming implementation retains one1224-vector, one445-vector of norm
records, and small15x15 blocks; it can discard each response after its norm
and hash/count record. A complete column map is not needed by the search.

For g_j>0 choose the integerk_j characterized EXACTLY by
    2^(2*(k_j-1)) < g_j <= 2^(2*k_j),
and set D_j=2^(-k_j). Integer comparisons of rational numerator/denominator
suffice; no floating log or irrational square root is required. Then
    1/4 < D_j^2*g_j <=1.
D is strictly positive and rational. If g_j=0, choose D_j=1 and KEEP the
coordinate. This means its affine response is invisible to the specified
cone outputs, not that a primal coordinate or matrix direction is forcedzero.
No zero column, signed coefficient, support or possible face is discarded.

CONDITIONAL on the reported norms being the TRUE exact column norms, and
absent exponent clipping, the exact scaled curvature K_D=D*N^T*B^T*B*N*D has
trace<=445 and therefore largest eigenvalue<=445. This is a useful exact
majorant after diagonal normalization, compared with the archived approximately
2.23e10 estimate before scaling. It does not bound the smallest eigenvalue
or the condition number. Near-parallel columns can keep the search very slow.
The numerical PSD-gradient remains mixed precision; this curvature statement
is about the EXACT convex merit/operator, not exactness of a binary64 eigensolve.
Do not mark the actual scaled trace/norm bound certified unless all445 norms
have been independently verified. In the recommended proposal-only mode,
column_norms_independently_verified=False and scaled_trace_bound_certified=False.

One may bound allowed exponents in the protocol, for example|k_j|<=900.
Prefer returning UNKNOWN if a required exponent exceeds the range; clipping
keeps feasibility but invalidates the unqualified trace<=445 claim. Any clipping
policy must be declared and its changed scaled diagonal/trace recomputed exactly.

Center on the LICENSED exact018 free vectorf0, and set f=f0+D*z, initiallyz=0.

Costs and bit-growth limits

445 full-column homogeneous solves cost the multiply/add proxy
    445*2*99431 =88,493,590 units,
plus initialization,371 scalar/240 upper-triangle norm operations per column,
H assembly, hashing and bit checks. A100m scaling allocation is plausible
under that explicit meter, but not guaranteed. Exact rationals can grow:
response entries fitting4096 bits do NOT ensure their squared norm fits4096.
Declare a separate bounded norm-intermediate cap, e.g8192 bits, while keeping
source/candidate limits4096; or retain4096 throughout and accept possible
UNKNOWN. Norm accumulation can exceed even8192 through denominator growth.
Do not substitute highprecision estimates silently on rational-cap failure.
Exact proof of the scaled-diagonal/trace bound needs these exact norms.

A cheaper variant computes all445 norms in80-digit arithmetic, exports each
estimated norm and chooses D by exact integer comparisons on that finite export.
D and the coordinate change remain exact and invertible, but g_j is then only
an estimate: neither the true scaled diagonal nor trace<=445 is certified.
This variant is distinct and should have its own protocol; it is not the
recommended exact-norm experiment or an automatic fallback.

Deterministic probing alternative

For r_k in the821-dimensional stacked cone-output space, compute
    h_k=N^T*B^T*r_k,
    estimated_g_j=(1/K)*sum_k h_k,j^2.
Sixteen fixed sign probes need16 adjoint passes, approximately3,181,792 sparse
multiply/add units plus B^T/vector work, far cheaper than445 exact forward
columns. However fixed deterministic probes can miss a direction or severely
misestimate sensitivity; no probabilistic confidence follows from a published
fixed seed or incomplete Walsh/sign set. A zero estimate must use a positive
scale floor and retain the coordinate, never justify a face. Full symmetric
PSD-output probes must use the correct duplication/Frobenius adjoint factors.
Probes are useful as an explicitly approximate budget-limited alternative,
not a substitute for the exact cone-Jacobi diagnostic when that fits. No probe
responses were calculated in this analysis.

Independent checker changes

Require new protocol/source hashes; exactly the same chart, source and merit;
all445 producer norm records labeled independently unverified (or all norms
independently replayed within the SAME job if certification is requested);
strictly positiveD and declared exponent/zero policies; exact scale-coordinate
bindings; a complete018 endpoint seed license; scaled forward/adjoint calibration
and updated power identity; all trial z/f exports and Armijo quantities under
the correct coordinate metric; one exact unscaled endpoint and existing full
finite admission gates. Mark norm certification separately from numerical
merit/gradient certification; no mathematical norm bound is required or claimed
in the recommended proposal-only mode. The old checker licensed history and endpoint,
not general spectral correctness or all new diagonal-norm claims.

Parallelism is optional, not needed for the initial recommendation. Columns
are independent, but naive workers each replicating143.5m input licensing would
violate accounting. Any multiworker design needs one independently licensed
read-only map shared or a serialized verified snapshot, explicit sum-of-worker
work, shared/private memory attribution, bounded output IPC, numerical thread
limits, and one counted parent license. More workers do not increase the450m
per-job work budget. A serial streaming first experiment is easier to audit.
