Exact complete identity and the missing sign

For K_X(t)=max(0,2X-max(X,t)), J_X=K_2X-4K_X, N=4X-1,
Delta_f(X)=sum_(i,j<=N)f(i)f(j)J_X(max(i,j))=J_X(1)-2*kappa*S_X,
where kappa=1 for mu and2 for c, J_X(1)=-2X, and
S_X=sum_(ka,b<=N,k=p^e) log(p)f(a)f(b)J_X(max(ka,b))/log(kab).
All positive exponents occur for mu, only odd exponents for c. The exact
coefficient identities are mu log=-mu*Lambda and c log=-2c*Lambda_odd.
There is no extra symmetry factor inside S_X and no omitted exceptional unit.

Writing a=gu,b=gv,(u,v)=1, partition by h=max(u,v)<=B(X). The same B is used
throughout the complete J_X; child defects independently recompute their B.
S_X=S_B+R_B. Supplied uniform bounds are
 |S_B|<=8 X^2 B(1+log B)(1+log4X)                  for mu,
 |S_B|<=8 X^2 B(1+log B)^3(1+log4X)^4             for c.
B0 is exactly zero. Fixed and logarithmic B have critical envelopes; positive
power B loses that positive exponent under this proof and is not promoted.

The full remaining claim is a LOWER bound on R_B:
for all sufficiently large integerq, a finite Cq independent of dyadicX obeys
 -R_B(X)<=Cq X^2 ceil(X^(1/q)) at every dyadic X>=1.
The known prefix charge would recover the complete original defect, then the
original nonnegative block energy, and only then the ordinary-zeta pole bridge.
The full signed remainder estimate remains unproved. For these critical cuts,
the premise is equivalent in strength to the original missing energy control.

What the analysis excludes

Zero average of J does not remove a squarefree-density contribution when the
logarithmic denominator varies. Integration by parts retains both a lower
boundary and a signed drift. Actualc also has different gcd weights from mu;
the coprime-squarefree density cannot be transferred to c unchanged.

Distinct actual primes i,j above B retain both removal cores in the dense
domain. Their weights log(i)/log(ij)+log(j)/log(ij)=1 preserve the full absolute
pair contribution. This rules out a strict entrywise contraction obtained
solely from this local prime-removal operation. It does not refute cancellation
across groups of pairs or the actual RH target.

The exact identity, partition, envelope and interval method were supplied.
This numerical pilot learned only cutoff choices and stored constants; it did
not discover the prime-removal identity or a uniform cancellation estimate.

Next language and counterexample gate

The post-freeze proposal prime_pair_forcing_next_certificate_scope.txt, reviewed
independently in prime_pair_forcing_next_certificate_review.txt, introduces
polynomial multipliers of exact arithmetic relations and nonnegative squares.
Coprime cycles x_a*x_b*x_ab=x_ab^2>=0 are valid, including zeros. Their extra
product factor cannot be removed to infer positivity of a bilinear target.
Complete ordered pairs throughN may require actual products throughN^2.

For any fixed finite prime setP, f_P(n)=mu(n)^2*(-1)^omega_P(n) satisfies all
coprime cycles, complete squarefree support, correct local normalization atP,
and every inverse row divisible by a prime inP. Nevertheless its mean is
c_P*x+O_P(sqrt(x)), c_P=zeta(2)^(-1)*product_(p inP)(p-1)/(p+1)>0,
and its complete defect is(28/3)c_P^2*X^3+O_P(X^(5/2)). The source gives an
explicit finite error constant and all boundary charges. This is a counterfeit
of partial premises, not actualmu or a counterexample toRH.

Complete inverse rows already determine every actual coefficient, so nonlinear
relations add proof representations rather than logically independent arithmetic
information. A useful discovery must provide a reusable all-scale certificate,
not encode every observed coefficient separately or leave the original signed
bound as its own premise. Finite SOS feasibility alone proves no uniform law.
