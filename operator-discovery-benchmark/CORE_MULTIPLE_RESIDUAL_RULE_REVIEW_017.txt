MULTIPLE RESIDUALS, EULER-REMAINDER PAYMENT AND THE WHOLE-CORE FRONTIER

Scope and conclusion
--------------------
Only this new review is written. Previous sources, proofs, contracts and
studies are unchanged; no source evaluation, experiment, test or hash is
performed. The universal multidimensional bound and both proposed kernel
exponent rules are sound after the full tails and Euler compensator are
included. A power-unpaid certificate is a FAILURE OF THAT PROOF ROUTE,
not a disproof of the arithmetic target. No RH conclusion is claimed.

Write H(t)=1+log t for real t>=1; all logs are natural. Prefix arguments
use floor t and all sequences include their actual unit. For fixed integer
U>=1, the actual residual of016 is
 E=delta-One*A+c One, A(d)=mu(d)1_(d<=U), c=sum_(d<=U)mu(d)/d,
 |P_E(t)|<=2U, M_E(t)<=L t, L=3(1+log U).              (0.1)
These are complete all-t identities/bounds, not finite observations.

1. A universal all-t multidimensional hyperbola bound
----------------------------------------------------
More generally suppose a real sequence a has |P_a(t)|<=C and M_a(t)<=L t,
for all t>=1, with C,L>=1. For k>=1 positive convolution gives
 M_(|a|^k)(t)<=L^k t H(t)^(k-1).                       (1.1)
Induct using M_(|a|^k)(t)=sum_(d<=t)|a(d)|M_(|a|^(k-1))(t/d),
and sum_(d<=t)|a(d)|/d<=L H(t), the latter by partial summation.
This proof retains every repeated factor and valuation.

For m>=2 put y=floor(t^(1/m))>=1. Partition all integer tuples with
product<=t into the cube n_i<=y for every i and the m disjoint regions
whose FIRST index exceeding y is i. The entire cube is included, since
y^m<=t, and its exact signed sum is P_a(y)^m. Its magnitude is at most
 C(Ly)^(m-1)<=C L^(m-1)t^(1-1/m).
In region i, fixing the other factors with product d gives the exact
large-index sum P_a(t/d)-P_a(y), of magnitude<=2C. Those other products
satisfy d<=t/(y+1)<t^(1-1/m). Dropping only their restrictions for an
UPPER ABSOLUTE bound and applying(1.1) gives each region at most
 2C L^(m-1)t^(1-1/m)H(t)^(m-2).
Therefore, for every real t>=1,
 |P_(a^m)(t)|<=(2m+1)C L^(m-1)t^(1-1/m)H(t)^(m-2).    (1.2)
No unbounded C^m head remains: in the cube one factor used the prefix
bound C and the other m-1 used the mass bound Ly. This is valid also
when t<C^m or t<U^m, and when an outer region is empty.

For E this yields the explicit all-t bounds
 m1: |P_E(t)|<=2U;
 m>=2:
 |P_(E^m)(t)|<=2(2m+1)3^(m-1)U(1+log U)^(m-1)
                              *t^(1-1/m)H(t)^(m-2),
 M_(E^m)(t)<=3^m(1+log U)^m t H(t)^(m-1).              (1.3)
The requested single combined-log envelope is consequently
 C_m U t^(1-1/m)[1+log(2t)+log U]^(2m-3), m>=2,
 C_m=2(2m+1)3^(m-1).
For m1 use C1=2 and log degree0. In particular
                    |P_(E^2)(t)|<=30U(1+log U)sqrt t.  (1.4)
The exact separated log degrees in(1.3) are more informative for code
than the combined envelope; its extra log U factors are cutoff costs,
not extra powers of log t.

2. General growing-prefix hypotheses and the complete moment tail
----------------------------------------------------------------
Assume now an arbitrary real h satisfies, for all t>=1,
 |P_h(t)|<=C t^rho H(t)^ell,
 M_h(t)<=L t H(t)^k,
 0<=rho<theta<1, C,L>=1, k,ell nonnegative integers.    (2.1)
Integer log degrees give rational explicit constants. A rational degree
may instead be replaced by its ceiling in these certificate rules.
Put delta0=theta-rho and
 T_ell(delta0)=sum_(i=0)^ell binom(ell,i)i!/delta0^(i+1).
Summation by parts proves ordinary conditional convergence of
 mtheta(h)=sum_(d>=1)h(d)d^(-theta).
Its COMPLETE tail after any integer D>=1 has the exact Abel expression,
and the quantitative bound
 |sum_(d>D)h(d)d^(-theta)|
 <=C D^(rho-theta)H(D)^ell[1+theta T_ell(delta0)].       (2.2)
For the integral, substitute t=D exp(v) and use
(H(D)+v)^ell<=H(D)^ell(1+v)^ell. No d>n tail is dropped.
The same integral at D1 also bounds |mtheta(h)| by theta C T_ell(delta0).

The STRICT rho<theta hypothesis is necessary for this sufficient rule.
Equality does not prove divergence of the actual coefficient moment,
but the generic bound no longer proves convergence. For theta2/3, (1.3)
proves the m1,m2 moments; it does not prove the m>=3 moments. Analytic
continuation of E(s)^m alone is not ordinary coefficient convergence.
When theta>1-1/m, absolute convolution first in Re(s)>1 and analytic
agreement with the Abel transforms show mtheta(E^m)=mtheta(E)^m.
Conditional series cannot simply be multiplied without that justification.

3. The ordinary kernel split rule and its required stronger prefix
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Let K(n)=n^(theta-1), Jtheta(n)=n^theta-(n-1)^theta, and
 B_h(n)=P_(K*h)(n)-(mtheta(h)/theta)n^theta.
This is the prefix of the fully centered coefficient K*h-(mtheta/ theta)Jtheta.
Use the exact integer split D in[1,n] and complementary hyperbola of016.
The small-factor kernel error is at most M_h(D)/theta. Bounds(2.1)-(2.2)
on the complementary row and the full conditional moment tail give
 |B_h(n)|<=L D H(D)^k/theta
                 +A C n^theta D^(rho-theta)H(n)^ell,
 A=1/delta0+2/theta+T_ell(delta0).                     (3.1)
The complementary P_h(n/a) row gives1/delta0, its P_h(D) subtraction
gives1/theta, and the full moment tail gives1/theta+T_ell. These constants
are finite rational functions of the declared parameters.

For a certificate not optimizing C,L, choose the admissible integer
 D=ceil(n^r0)<=n,
 r0=theta/(1+theta-rho).
Then D<=2n^r0 and the exponent identity balances the two error monomials.
Consequently all integer n>=1 satisfy
 |B_h(n)|<=[2L/theta+A C]n^r0 H(n)^max(k,ell).          (3.2)
The full free-split inequality(3.1) allows the detector to choose other
cutoffs and optimize parameter costs, with its exact rounding/head checks.

At theta2/3,rho1/2, r0=4/7>1/2, so even fixed U is power-unpaid in
critical prefix energy. This is not a contradiction for E^2. The rule
would require rho<=1-theta, hence rho<=1/3 here, to reach r0<=1/2.
At equality the complete log degree remains and must be squared in energy.

4. A uniform real-quotient Euler remainder, without numerical zeta
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Write Ztheta(y)=sum_(a<=floor y)a^(theta-1), y>=1. There is a constant
 ctheta=zeta(1-theta) satisfying
 |ctheta|<=1/theta,
 |Ztheta(y)-y^theta/theta-ctheta|<=2y^(theta-1).        (4.1)
Here is an elementary proof of the bound. For integer M>=1 set
 F(M)=Ztheta(M)-M^theta/theta. Its successive differences are nonpositive
integral errors. It has a limit ctheta, since the absolute error from M
to infinity is bounded by the telescoping sum of the decreases of
t^(theta-1), hence by M^(theta-1). The bound from016,
-1/theta<=F(M)<=0, proves |ctheta|<=1/theta. For real y and M=floor y,
 F(y)-ctheta=F(M)-ctheta-int_M^y t^(theta-1)dt,
with both terms on the right in[0,M^(theta-1)] before subtraction.
Thus its magnitude is at most M^(theta-1)<=2y^(theta-1).
This includes y1 and every fractional quotient edge.

The identification with zeta needs no numerical evaluation: for a complex
exponent s with Re(s)>0, the sum-minus-integral correction converges
normally on compact sets away from s1, agrees with zeta in Re(s)>1,
and therefore gives its continuation at s=1-theta. This does not infer
ordinary convergence of any unrelated signed source series there.

5. Stronger free-split error and the retained compensating h
----------------------------------------------------------
Applying(4.1) to the small d<=D row gives the exact expansion
 B_h(n)=sum_(d<=D)h(d)[Ztheta(n/d)-(n/d)^theta/theta-ctheta]
           +complementary_row-(n^theta/theta)tail_D+ctheta P_h(D).
The small remainder is at most
 2n^(theta-1)sum_(d<=D)|h(d)|d^(1-theta)
 <=2L n^(theta-1)D^(2-theta)H(D)^k.
The same COMPLETE complementary and moment-tail bounds as before give
 |B_h(n)|<=2L n^(theta-1)D^(2-theta)H(D)^k
       +A C n^theta D^(rho-theta)H(n)^ell
                         +(C/theta)D^rho H(D)^ell.     (5.1)
No compensating source term was erased in this fully centered formula.

If the grammar explicitly SUBTRACTS ctheta*h as another operator, its
prefix is B_h(n)-ctheta P_h(n), and the last term in(5.1) is instead
bounded by(2C/theta)n^rho H(n)^ell. Recovery of the original centered
K*h then restores ctheta*h, costing a further(C/theta)n^rho H(n)^ell.
This gives a valid conservative three-part certificate. Dropping h or
pretending its coefficient is0 would change the target. Alternatively
the direct fully centered identity above keeps only ctheta P_h(D).

Balancing the TWO main error powers now gives
 d1=1/(2-rho), D=ceil(n^d1)<=n,
 r1=[theta+rho(1-theta)]/(2-rho).                      (5.2)
Since D<=2n^d1 and 2*2^(2-theta)<=8, (5.1) implies the all-n bound
 |B_h(n)|<=[8L+(A+1/theta)C]n^r1 H(n)^max(k,ell).      (5.3)
The compensator is paid: r1-rho=(theta-rho)(1-rho)/(2-rho)>0, so even
the more conservative retained n^rho term has a smaller exponent.
For the subtract-and-restore route one may instead use A+3/theta in
(5.3). Exact rational split denominators and integer ceilings require
certified comparisons, not floating endpoint guesses.

The predicted powers are therefore correct: rho0 gives r1=theta/2;
theta2/3,rho1/2 gives5/9, still above1/2. The necessary gate for this
proof rule is
 rho<=2(1-theta)/(3-2theta),
which is rho<=2/5 at theta2/3. A stronger signed prefix estimate of that
degree would be a genuinely different arithmetic input, not a change
in the kernel's local weight. Passing this gate alone does not pay any
cutoff dependence hidden in C or L.

For E^2 explicitly take C=30U(1+log U),rho1/2,ell0,
L=9(1+log U)^2,k1,delta0=1/6,A=15. The certified ordinary rule is
 [27(1+log U)^2+450U(1+log U)]n^(4/7)H(n),
and the fully centered Euler rule is
 [72(1+log U)^2+495U(1+log U)]n^(5/9)H(n).              (5.4)
Both are valid but power-unpaid. Neither proves the true E^2 estimate
cannot be sharper, nor rejects a joined cancellation with other modes.
For a concrete scope check, U1 gives E=delta and E^m=delta for every m;
its fully centered kernel prefix is bounded by1/theta, while the generic
m2 route still reports an unpaid upper exponent. Therefore an unpaid
certificate cannot be labeled an arithmetic lower-bound disproof.

5A. Scaled all-n rules and generic finite convolution costs
---------------------------------------------------------
For backward proof search the C dependence matters, not just r0,r1.
Put T0=C^(1/(1-rho))>=1. When n>=T0, the admissible integer splits are
 ordinary: D=ceil[T0(n/T0)^r0];
 Euler:    D=ceil[T0(n/T0)^(1/(2-rho))].
The quantities in brackets lie in[1,n], so their ceilings are<=n and
at most twice the unrounded quantities. The SAME free-split proofs give
 ordinary amplitude C^a0 n^r0, a0=1/(1+theta-rho);
 Euler amplitude C^a1 n^r1, a1=(2-theta)/(2-rho).
Both obey a=(1-r)/(1-rho). For the Euler retained term,
C n^rho<=C^a1 n^r1 when n>=T0, so its coefficient is not forgotten.

The n<T0 head is also paid with these same amplitudes. Use mass up to
T0 and prefix beyond it in the full Abel integral to obtain
 |mtheta(h)|<=theta[L/(1-theta)+T_ell(delta0)]
                              T0^(1-theta)H(T0)^max(k,ell).
The raw convolution is at most L(2-theta)/(1-theta)*n H(n)^k.
Since both r0,r1<theta, for n<T0 one has
 n<=T0^(1-r)n^r,
 T0^(1-theta)n^theta<=T0^(1-r)n^r=C^a n^r.
Thus define Hstar=1+log n+log T0 and s=max(k,ell). Convenient explicit
rational parameter constants giving ALL integer n>=1 are
 Kordinary=(3-theta)/(1-theta)+T_ell(delta0)+2/theta+A,
 KEuler=(3-theta)/(1-theta)+T_ell(delta0)+8+A+1/theta,
 |B_h(n)|<=Kroute L C^aroute n^rroute Hstar^s.         (5A.1)
The constants are sums rather than optimized maxima. If the operator
subtracts and then restores ctheta*h separately, use3/theta instead of
1/theta in KEuler. Log T0 is a declared cost; it cannot be erased from
the head bound. For cutoff-polynomial C its growth is only logarithmic,
but its coefficient and the cutoff powers in C^a must be propagated.

For any FINITE factor h0=A^p*B^q, the prefix hypothesis transfers with
 C replaced by C M_h0(rho),
 M_A(rho)<=U^(1-rho)/(1-rho).
The B factor has the three exact cases from its exponent2/3+rho:
 >1: mass<=1+1/(rho-1/3);
 =1: mass<=1+log V;
 <1: mass<=V^(1/3-rho)/(1/3-rho).
The mass hypothesis transfers with L multiplied by
(1+log U)^p(5/2)^q, not a new power of U. The full moments transfer
through the finite factors, and every Euler/J compensating term stays.
These bounds use all of the finite support, including products larger
than the current n. No separate-head convolution identity is assumed.

In particular suppose an auxiliary E^2 prefix certificate is
 Caux U^b t^rho times declared logs. For q0,p2 its convolution with
A^2 replaces the cutoff exponent in C by b+2(1-rho). At theta2/3 and
U comparable to X^alpha, alpha1/24, the critical energy conditions
deduced from(5A.1) are EXACTLY
 ordinary: b+10rho<=2;
 Euler:    b+13rho<=4.                                (5A.2)
Strict inequalities give power decay; equality pays the remaining
explicit log powers. These are CONDITIONAL proof-certificate regions,
not a statement that no whole-core cancellation outside them exists.

6. A concrete actual-residual reduction to divisor remainders
------------------------------------------------------------
There is a reusable source-specific next input more structured than an
unsupported assertion P_(E^2)=O(t^rho). Put the FINITE sequence
 T=c delta-A, so E=delta+One*T, and its Dirichlet value
 T_hat(1)=sum_d T(d)/d=c-sum_d A(d)/d=0.
This refers to the Dirichlet value, NOT to its unit coefficient T(1)=c-1.
Let D2(y)=sum_(ab<=y)1, zero for y<1, and let
 Delta2(y)=D2(y)-y log y-(2EulerGamma-1)y, all real y>0.
For every integer n>=1 the exact full-source identity is
 P_(E^2)(n)=1+2sum_d T(d)[floor(n/d)-n/d]
                         +sum_d(T*T)(d)Delta2(n/d).   (6.1)
All sums include the ENTIRE finite support, even d>n. The x log x and
x main terms cancel because T_hat(1)^2=0 and
(T_hat(s)^2)' at s1 equals2T_hat(1)T_hat'(1)=0. Thus no omitted fractional
tail or pole residue remains. The first correction is bounded by2||T||_1.

As a concrete reusable conditional primitive, suppose an independently
proved divisor-error certificate gives |Delta2(y)|<=C_D y^kappa for
ALL y>=1, 0<=kappa<1. Its extension to 0<y<1 is automatic with constant
 Cext=max(C_D,1+1/(1-kappa)),
since D2(y)=0 there, |2EulerGamma-1|<=1, and
y|log y|<=y^kappa/(1-kappa). It follows from(6.1) that
 |P_(E^2)(n)|<=1+2(U+2)
       +Cext n^kappa[2+U^(1-kappa)/(1-kappa)]^2.      (6.2)
This uses |c|<=2 and the full finite T weighted mass, including its unit.
For n>=1 a constant head can be absorbed into the displayed n^kappa
coefficient. This is a PROVED REDUCTION conditional on the stated
independent divisor-error theorem; no particular external exponent or
constant is imported or numerically evaluated in this review.

For kappa<=1/2, this gives the explicit cutoff exponent
 b=2(1-kappa), rho=kappa,
 |P_(E^2)(n)|<=[7+Cext[2+1/(1-kappa)]^2]U^b n^rho.     (6.3)
Indeed 1+2(U+2)<=7U<=7U^b and
2+U^(1-kappa)/(1-kappa)<=U^(1-kappa)[2+1/(1-kappa)].
For kappa>1/2 this head absorption instead costs at least exponent1;
the displayed b<1 alone is not justified by this rule. Applying the
specific region(5A.2) to(6.3) gives ordinary feasibility only at kappa0
(equality), and Euler feasibility kappa<=2/11. Thus fixed-U requirements
rho<=2/5 are NOT enough for the stated growing cutoff. This is an honest
cost frontier for the supplied route; no external divisor exponent or
full-source impossibility theorem is being asserted.

Thus a certified kappa<=2/5 would supply the Euler rule's required
stronger rho for E^2, with EXPLICIT U costs, instead of assuming a new
RH-strength prefix lemma. It would not close E^3/higher residuals or
the whole source join. More generally E^m=sum_j binom(m,j)One^j*T^j.
For rigorous arbitrary-m scope, define D_j(x)=sum_(n1...nj<=x)1, zero
for x<1. The raw complete convolution identity is
 P_(E^m)(n)=1+sum_(j=1)^m binom(m,j)sum_ALL_d T^j(d)D_j(n/d).
For ANY specified polynomial P_(j-1) of degree at most j-1,
define its COMPLETE extended remainder
 Delta_j(x)=D_j(x)-x P_(j-1)(log x), EVERY real x>0.
Then finite algebra and moment annihilation give the exact identity
 P_(E^m)(n)=1+sum_(j=1)^m binom(m,j)
                              sum_d T^j(d)Delta_j(n/d). (6.4)
To prove annihilation, the entire Dirichlet polynomial T_hat(s)^j has
a zero of order at least j at1. Its derivatives of orders0 through j-1
are the finite sums of T^j(d)d^(-1)(-log d)^ell and are0. Expanding
(n/d)P_(j-1)(log n-log d) therefore cancels EVERY main term, with ALL
d in the finite support, not only d<=n. The polynomial can be obtained
from the Laurent pole of zeta(s)^j when that proof primitive is supplied;
the cancellation identity itself does not assert a divisor asymptotic.
At j1 choose P0=1, giving the complete floor error; at j2 use the full
polynomial already present in(6.1). The initial1 retains the delta unit.

If a separate theorem bounds |Delta_j(x)|<=C_j x^kappa_j H(x)^nu_j
for x>=1, 0<=kappa_j<1, it extends to0<x<1 with the explicit additional
constant sum_ell |P_(j-1),ell|ell!/(1-kappa_j)^ell.
This follows from x|log x|^ell<=x^kappa_j ell!/(1-kappa_j)^ell.
Take C_jext as the maximum of C_j and that additional constant, and
use Hplus(x)=1+max(0,log x) on this complete extension. Consequently
(6.4) is bounded by1 plus the sum of
 binom(m,j)C_jext n^kappa_j H(n)^nu_j
                     [2+U^(1-kappa_j)/(1-kappa_j)]^j.
Thus all remainders, low quotient edges and their U costs remain explicit.
Their exponents/moments are new arithmetic leaves; analytic continuation
does not certify the associated ordinary tails. This can be compiled as
generated finite factorization plus moment-annihilation, not a nominated
estimate for E^m or a silently omitted pole complement.

Alternatively the backward search may declare a SINGLE hypothetical
all-x leaf |Delta_j(x)|<=C_j x^kappa_j(1+|log x|)^ell_j, x>0.
Then the same complete identity gives each j contribution at most
 binom(m,j)C_j n^kappa_j(1+log n+j log U)^ell_j
                      [2+U^(1-kappa_j)/(1-kappa_j)]^j.
Indeed T^j has support<=U^j and |log(n/d)|<=log n+j log U for every
finite-support d, including d>n. This is an explicit U^(j(1-kappa_j))
polylog cost with constant [2+1/(1-kappa_j)]^j for U>=1. Such a global
Delta bound is a CONDITIONAL arithmetic leaf, not a result inferred from
the identity. For j1, floor x-x is bounded for every x>0, so kappa0,
ell0,C1=1 is proved, yielding the paid-head candidate b1. The delta
unit is the separate j0 term1. A combined E^m bound must still include
every other j leaf and its cost before it can be admitted.

Finite A^p*B^q factors preserve any proved prefix bound by weighted
finite mass at rho, and preserve mass bounds by harmonic finite mass;
their complete centering moments and the J commutator rules of016 still
apply. If U grows with the study horizon, its powers in(6.2) must be
squared and added to energy exponents before admission. A fixed-U
arithmetic estimate is not uniform cofinal recovery for arbitrary cutoffs.

7. Whole-core proof-frontier interpretation
------------------------------------------
The detector can compose (1.1)-(1.3), moment/tail certificates, the free
integer split errors(3.1),(5.1), and the exact divisor-remainder reduction.
It can solve exponent balance equations, enumerate cutoffs and compare
valid proof routes. For theta2/3 its ordinary-rule residual requirement
is rho<=1/3; its Euler-remainder requirement is rho<=2/5. The universal
E^2 bound does not reach either threshold, and universal m>=3 bounds
do not even certify the required ordinary moment at that theta. These
are precise missing-rule leaves, not a theorem that such moments or
better actual estimates do not exist.

In the current inverse expansion R=E-c One. Every binomial One companion,
real-pole/main channel, actual four-mode mixed term, repeated valuation
and exact transport correction must remain. A paid centered residual is
not permission to erase the other components. Individual errors may
also cancel in the joined coarse kernel; language failure for a separate
component does not rule out that mechanism. Conversely, analytic main
cancellation is not a signed coefficient tail estimate without proof.

PROVED: universal all-t residual-power prefix/mass bounds, conditional
moment domain and full tails, Euler remainder with explicit real-floor
constant, both split inequalities/exponents, and the finite actual-T
divisor-error reduction. OPEN: stronger arithmetic input at the required
thresholds and a COMPLETE source-bound joined estimate closing the target.
No source experiment, test, hash or RH claim occurred. Only this new memo
was written; the rules are human-proved primitives for machine proof search.
