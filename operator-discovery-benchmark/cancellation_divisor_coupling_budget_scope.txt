Complete divisor-channel coupling and its scalar budget

For integer t>=1 and any complete rational coefficient program w_a(t), define
 B_w(t)=sum_(a<=t)w_a x_a sum_(d|a)x_d, c_w(t)=w_1(t).
No actual sign value is substituted. For a>=2 use rho_a=sum_(d|a)x_d; at a1 use rho1=x1−1. The exact formal identity is
 B_w−c_w=sum_(a=2)^t w_a*x_a*rho_a + c_w*(x1+1)*rho1.
The x1² source term is present. This follows directly by expansion, including c_w=0, negative coefficients, and zero-weight source pairs. Thus actual mu makes B_w=c_w. Existing independent inverse-row/unit relations suffice, but the new construction retains cross-divisor correlations that old sum-of-squares-over-d groups omit.

The smallest exact closure multiplies every displayed primitive multiplier by B_w+c_w, producing B_w²−c_w². Cross-divisor pairs d,e are complete; this is NOT sum_d(x_d P_d)². Mixed prefix squares obey
 (P+s B_w)²−(P+s c_w)²=(B_w−c_w)(2s P+B_w+c_w), s=±1.
Mixed channel products similarly follow from half the sum of two normalization relations multiplied by the other's channel-plus-constant. These are generic polynomial templates, not supplied coefficients for an RH solution. With arbitrary coefficient programs their generation must charge full source coverage, rational bit/degree/product budgets and exact grouped multiplier aggregation.

For the pilot's P_d^j(t)=sum_(a<=t,d|a)(a/t)^j x_a, B_j=sum_d x_d P_d^j and c_j=t^-j, for j=-1,0,1. The full channel is therefore an exactly computable constant, not a new signed estimate. A square of the complete channel adds no unknown arithmetic information after quotienting by the inverse ideal. Its coupling to ordinary P is a known shifted prefix; any claimed gain must survive that reduction. More promising expressivity splits divisor cells, keeping B_in+B_out=c exact and both complete sums. Their individual cancellation is unknown. A generic grammar can couple the cells through squares or an exact rational PSD Gram while retaining their common normalization relation and the full original target. Never infer a partial channel bound from the complete identity.

Formal scalar extraction changes. B_w has zero FORMAL constant, but B_w²−c_w² has formal constant−c_w². Only the unit multiplier carries that scalar: c_w(x1+1)(B_w+c_w) has formal constant c_w² and rho1 has constant−1. Thus normalization/square closure must be charged to the scalar budget, even though every actual normalization identity is0. Prefix-coupling closure has the same formal scalar−c_w². Generic extraction remains sum_nonnegative_squares lambda*P(0)² + sum_relations b*Q(0)*rho(0); a descriptor label is not a substitute for this calculation.

For unnormalized j=-1, sum_(t=X)^(2X-1)c_j²=(7/3)X³−(3/2)X²+X/6, and sum_(t=2X)^(4X-1)c_j²=(56/3)X³−6X²+X/3. j0 instead has scalar-square totals X and2X, while j1 has O(1/X). In mixed channels only j=k=-1 has a cubic constant product; j+k=-1 costs O(X²), and the remaining products are smaller. Do not omit j=-1 merely for convenience: retain it with its explicit cubic ledger. A declared additional t^j normalization would make every complete channel1 and its shell scalar cost only O(X), but this is a different fixed scale program and must be frozen as such.

Generalized cubic gate for THIS rational/integer-power family. The old mu norm-rowmass contribution is delta*gamma_old X³+O(X^(5/2)), delta=1/zeta(2). Let r_parent and r_child be the sums of fixed signed weights on all new square/prefix closures with j=-1; their scalar contribution is eta X³+O(X²), eta=−(7/3)r_parent−(56/3)r_child. More general constant-square or mixed-closure fields enter eta through the exact formal constant functional. All weights are rational at a fixed q, hence gamma_old and eta are rational. The resulting budget has leading coefficient delta*gamma_old+eta; the critical normalizer is subcubic for every fixed q>1 and does not affect it.

A cofinal critical scalar-budget theorem needs delta*gamma_old+eta=0 at EACH admitted fixed q>1. Positive leading coefficient is an unsigned cubic budget rather than a critical one. Negative leading coefficient cannot accompany an all-scale valid upper defect certificate: A(2X)<=4A(X)−cX³ eventually would make A(X)/X² negative by dyadic telescoping. This is a necessary condition for this fixed template, not a refutation of actual RH.

There is a useful exact strengthening for this family: delta is irrational, so rational gamma_old and eta satisfy delta*gamma_old+eta=0 ONLY if BOTH vanish. Irrationality of zeta(2), hence of its reciprocal, is an imported established theorem; primary reference F. Beukers, A Note on the Irrationality of zeta(2) and zeta(3), Bull. London Math. Soc.11(1979),268–272:
https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/11.3.268

Thus in this precise new family the old gamma0 gate remains necessary, but it is NOT sufficient bookkeeping: a SECOND rational cubic gate eta=0 is required. This conclusion is not generalized to arbitrary coefficient programs producing other asymptotic constants. Without importing irrationality, the necessary condition is the single real equation delta*gamma_old+eta=0.

Recovery. A whole-channel correction Q=alpha*M+sum_j beta_j*c_j has no new prime cancellation. If alpha!=0, triangle recovery charges the full deterministic bias-square energy. A nonzero unnormalized beta_-1 creates cubic cost; it must cancel identically or be normalized/explicitly charged. For partial channels, original M recovery requires a strictly positive Schur-complement/coercivity margin of the shared Gram on unknown channels, or a separately proved critical correction bound. No finite PSD certificate or complete-channel normalization supplies that missing estimate. When the lhs remains the original full defect, the original all-scale critical proof gate is unchanged; new squares and relation closures are only proof-language productions.

Adverse cases. Dropping a1, any zero-weight source pair, a divisor cell/complement, or an off-diagonal d,e term changes the identity. Treating the complete channel as new estimated prime information is tautological. Free per-index weights/multipliers can interpolate finite arithmetic and must not be advertised as uniform template discovery. All new dual columns and their full monomial domain must be checked; unsupported old-dual monomials remain unknown unless a separately declared functional extension is constructed. No numerical score or new signed-bound claim is made here.
