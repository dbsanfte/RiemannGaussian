BOUND MECHANISM SYNTHESIS: FINITE MULTISCALE CAPABILITY AND ARITHMETIC LIMIT
Design review, stage034. This is a new design record, not a modification of
the accepted moment/component certificates or an RH proof.

1. What this capability adds

The prior bound-program searches synthesized coefficients, scales and moment
allocations in supplied argument families. The autonomous system inferred
sample relations and promoted structural macros, but its global proof backend
was essentially polynomial identity checking. Neither capability demonstrated
invention of both a missing auxiliary state and a quantified induction proof.
The present bounded task can demonstrate that narrower missing capability.

Start with an anonymous scalar source x and base b. Enumerate actual sections
x(b^k n+r), initially x itself. Rational sample relations only propose closure.
If a proposed relation fails at a checked index, add the index and re-expand.
If a universally checked relation fails, its witness may trigger the same
structural expansion. The resulting state topology is discovered, not supplied
as an answer. A second search synthesizes a metric and rational prefix exponent.
This remains a specific section grammar, not invention of arbitrary mathematics.

2. Source and proof interfaces

A recursive calibration source has rational matrices M_r, initial vector v_0
and scalar output t, with v(bn+r)=M_r v(n). Require M_0 v_0=v_0 and row L1
norm at most one. Thus every state coordinate is bounded by ||v_0||_infinity
for every integer n, with no observed-value bound substituted for this proof.
The generator sees only b, a coefficient envelope B and a callable x(n).
The independent checker sees the complete hidden model. It proves proposed
section relations on its entire reachable linear space, including n=0.
Numerical fitting, validation samples and exhaustion of a finite query set
are never universal closure certificates.

After closure, write phi(bn+r)=T_r phi(n). Every phi coordinate remains a
section of x; it is not a free auxiliary trajectory. Let A=sum_r T_r and
P(n)=sum_{0<=k<n} phi(k). Under closure at n=0,
 P(bn+r)=A P(n)+sum_{j<r} T_j phi(n).
If closure is only valid for positive n, the missing unit correction is
 e=sum_{j<b}[phi(j)-T_j phi(0)],
and e must be added to this identity. It cannot be inferred to vanish.

Coefficient-state contractivity alone proves no summatory cancellation. It is
the block-sum A, or an exactly recoverable projection of A, that must be bounded.
The current implementation searches the target-observable row space R with
R A=L R, retaining the target row. It then searches rational alpha=p/q and
positive definite Q satisfying
 (L^q)^t Q L^q <= b^(2p) Q.
Exact rational PSD elimination, phase costs for powers below q, the projected
source envelope, and all digit remainders give arbitrary-integer prefixes.
No spectral radius rounded from floating point is a proof. A singular metric
would require a separate exact target-domination certificate; the current
positive-definite choice avoids that additional admission condition.

A simple alternative certificate illustrates the logic: if A^t Q A<=lambda^2 Q
with lambda>1, bounded digit forcing yields ||P(n)||_Q=O(n^log_b lambda).
All finite initial values and the geometric forcing sum are in the constant.
At lambda=1 the nonzero forcing produces a logarithm; it cannot be discarded.
Thus a checked block exponent alpha gives prefix energy O(X^(2alpha-1)),
up to a paid logarithm at zero exponent. This statement concerns all integers,
not only complete aligned blocks. The implementation's observable projection
can retain useful cancellation even if an invisible companion has linear growth.

3. Search and certificate boundary

The proof object must include section ASTs, proposed and rejected relations,
training/validation queries, universal counterexamples, exact transition rows,
observable projection, metric, exponent and finite recovery constants. Search
limits are explicit. A resource stop excludes no larger or parameter-dependent
state grammar. Sample-equivalent states cannot be universally merged until the
source checker proves their equality. CEGIS expansion is a search heuristic;
its countermodels do not turn the eventual missing relation into a theorem.

Potential generalizations are signed products through tensor closure, mixed
outputs, parameter-dependent matrix pencils and polynomial digit moments. Each
needs exact global source closure and appropriate source-growth accounting.
Fractional n weights and prime predicates typically yield n-dependent updates;
a constant-matrix fit must not be admitted for them. These are future grammar
extensions, not silently implemented features of this experiment.

4. Withheld solvable calibration

Freeze the generator, independent checker and protocol before materializing
the challenge instances. Models and expected truth labels remain private.
Provide varied bases, coupled signed channels, nontrivial fractional growth,
noncancelling sources and long transients. The model contract bounds dimensions
and rational sizes. The generator receives neither companions nor a successful
metric/exponent. Negative controls must reject false finite closures or certify
their actual linear-prefix scale rather than invent cancellation.

The benchmark author knows the grammar. Consequently successful calibration
means implementation-level auxiliary-state and global-bound synthesis in that
grammar. It is not analyst-blind novelty, independent discovery of an unknown
theorem, or an estimate of the probability of solving RH. One-state ablation
can test whether state invention, rather than a supplied scalar trick, mattered.
Challenge creation after freeze is a procedural condition enforced by the
runner; a function's frozen=True argument alone is not proof of a frozen run.

5. Original-source attempt and complete target

The actual source adapter reads only the previously verified complete archived
core f, including the unit. It supplies x(n)=f(n+1). The four signed modes are
 f=(9/2)K*(mu*mu)-(9/2)K*(mu*mu_(2/3))
   -R_u K*mu_(2/3)-(9/2)mu, K(n)=n^(-1/3).
This direct full-core route is a sufficient alternative target. It neither
numerically isolates the stage033 residual nor discards a transport correction,
paid term or opposite centering drift from an inherited decomposition. Parent
links remain candidate10_moment_region_obligation.json and its complete source.
The archived Arb enclosures are observations, not exact rational coefficients.
The source has no supplied finite recursive model. A fitted closure is OPEN
until an actual arithmetic proof is provided. Finite-dimensional constant
section closure may simply be unsuitable for primes; failure is useful evidence
about this grammar, not a disproof of any larger arithmetic mechanism.

6. Adapter implementation scope

New adapter APIs: validate_model(model,meter=None),
evaluate_model(model,n,meter=None), model_source(model,meter=None), and
load_complete_core(path,H,meter=None)->(callable,metadata). Exact recursive
model evaluations return Fraction. Archive values return finite Arb enclosures
at preserved precision; indices outside the declared horizon are rejected.
create_challenges(directory,seed,meter=None,*,frozen=True) may be called only
after the runner has frozen the proof sources. It writes model definitions and
separate posthoc truth labels, never hidden data into the generator's spec.
No challenge instance or new arithmetic observation was produced for this review.

Status: design rules supplied by analysis; state construction, closure proposals,
metrics and exponents searched by the executable. Universal closure/invariant
checking supplies the global theorem. Actual-source closure and RH remain OPEN.
