TYPED LATTICE ESTIMATE SYNTHESIS AND COMPLEMENTARY FRACTIONAL DILATIONS

Scope. This document gives supplied proof methods, not a proof of RH. The
detector chooses formulas, contour parameters, estimates and complementary
banks within a frozen bounded search. Only the unit-reference decay theorem,
the elementary weak actual bound, the sign-loss barrier, and the conditional
zero bridges are proved here. Actual critical uniform estimates remain open.
Arithmetic input types are mathematical premises, not interchangeable labels.

1. Exact arithmetic and complete evaluation

Let K>1, ell>0, r>1 and R>0 be rational, and let
 Q(y)=sum_m c_m (R y)^m exp(-R y),
with finitely many nonnegative integer m and nonzero rational coefficients.
Set g(u)=u^K Q(u^ell), G(Y)=sum_{j>=1} g(Y/j), and
 D_mu,r(X)=sum_{d,j>=1} mu(d) g(X/(j d^r)),
 D_1,r(X)=sum_{d,j>=1} g(X/(j d^r)).
These sums are absolutely convergent at every positive X: each atom is
bounded by a constant times X^(K+ell*m)(j d^r)^(-K-ell*m), and K>1, r>1.
For integral r, the actual coefficients on integer positions are the r-free
indicator sum_{d^r|n} mu(d). Fractional r gives a generalized signed measure
on the positive real positions j d^r; positivity is not asserted.

The exact prefix cutoff for r=p/q in lowest terms is j^q d^p<=N^q.
Moments at w=K+ell*h>1 are zeta(w)/zeta(r*w) for the actual model and
zeta(w)*zeta(r*w) for the unit model. The unsigned counting measure is at
most zeta(r)*t, since sum_d floor(t/d^r)<=t*zeta(r). Consequently each tail
moment in absolute value is at most zeta(r)*w*N^(1-w)/(w-1).
Taylor's theorem for exp(-z), z>=0, gives a remainder at most z^L/L!.
Combining this with the complete tail moments encloses all infinite terms;
the finite prefix is not substituted for the arithmetic object. Ball
precision must resolve cancellation in the small tail moments themselves.

2. Mellin transform, residues and the exact signed rewrite

With v=(K-s)/ell and P(v)=sum_m c_m (v)_m,
 W(s)=integral_0^infinity g(u) u^(-s-1) du
     =Gamma(v)*P(v)*R^(-v)/ell, Re(s)<K.
For 1<Re(s)<K, the Mellin transforms of D_mu,r and D_1,r are respectively
 W(s)*zeta(s)/zeta(r*s), W(s)*zeta(s)*zeta(r*s).
Let I=W(1), A=I/zeta(r) and
 B=zeta(1/r)*W(1/r)/r. The reference main residue is zeta(r)*I.
Both residues vanish exactly iff P((K-1)/ell)=P((K-1/r)/ell)=0.
For e(Y)=G(Y)-I*Y, absolute convergence gives the exact actual rewrite
 Z_mu,r(X):=D_mu,r(X)-A*X = sum_d mu(d) e(X/d^r).
The reference residual is
 R_1,r(X)=D_1,r(X)-zeta(r)*I*X-B*X^(1/r).
There is no analogous secondary residue claimed for the actual model.

3. Elementary actual all-scale bound and normalization

Write Q_r(x)=sum_d mu(d) floor(x/d^r). For x>=1, t=x^(1/r), a=floor(t),
 |Q_r(x)-x/zeta(r)| <= a+x*a^(1-r)/(r-1).
After division by t this is 1/u+u^(r-1)/(r-1), 1<=u=t/a<2.
Its derivative is (u^r-1)/u^2>=0, so a valid constant is
 C_r=1/2+2^(r-1)/(r-1).
For 0<x<1 the same bound holds because Q_r(x)=0 and x<=x^(1/r).
Partial summation against g(X/t), including both endpoints, gives
 |Z_mu,r(X)| <= C_r*U_Q,r*X^(1/r), every real X>0,
 U_Q,r=sum_m |c_m| R^((1/r-K)/ell) Gamma((K-1/r)/ell+m)
        *[(K+ell*m)+ell*((K-1/r)/ell+m)]/ell.
This is a signed true arithmetic theorem, but its ordinary-zeta exponent
is r*(1/r)=1 for every r. Merely increasing r does not strengthen it.
The RH target is density exponent (1/2+epsilon/q^nu)/r, with arbitrary
positive rational nu and independent finite C_q for every integer q>=1.
No growth law for C_q and no finite substitute for all q is imposed.

4. Automatically synthesized global negative powers: UNIT model only

For every rational a>0, every admissible Q has
 |R_1,r(X)| <= C_a X^(-a), every real X>0.
Here is an explicit certificate for C_a, including the whole height axis.
Rotate Euler's Gamma integral by theta, |theta|<pi/2. For alpha>0,
 |Gamma(alpha+it)|<=Gamma(alpha)*(cos theta)^(-alpha)*exp(-theta*|t|).
This follows by Cauchy's theorem on a sector, choosing the ray sign to
match t; the arcs vanish at infinity and at zero. No sampled height bound
is substituted for this inequality.
The zeta functional equation
 zeta(s)=2^s*pi^(s-1)*sin(pi*s/2)*Gamma(1-s)*zeta(1-s)
and |sin(pi*s/2)|<=exp(pi*|Im(s)|/2) give, for A>0 and 0<delta<pi/2,
 |zeta(-A+it)|<=Z(A,delta)*exp(delta*|t|),
 Z(A,delta)=2^(-A)*pi^(-A-1)*Gamma(1+A)
            *(sin delta)^(-1-A)*zeta(1+A).
Choose a kernel Gamma ray theta_K and a zeta complement delta such that
 kappa=theta_K/ell-(1+r)*delta>0. At Re(s)=-a, alpha=(K+a)/ell,
 |P(alpha-it/ell)|<=D(|t|),
 D(t)=sum_m |c_m| product_{h=0}^{m-1}(alpha+h+t/ell).
Writing D(t)=sum_j D_j t^j with exact nonnegative rational coefficients,
 C_a=[Gamma(alpha)*(cos theta_K)^(-alpha)*R^(-alpha)/ell]
      *Z(a,delta)*Z(r*a,delta)/pi
      *sum_j D_j*j!/kappa^(j+1).
Every supplied ray choice is valid. The detector minimizes the certified
constant over a frozen finite ray grammar; it does not fit a global bound
from finitely many values. Shift inverse Mellin from 1<c<K to -a. The only
poles crossed are 1 and 1/r. Gamma poles are at K+ell*h, on the other side
of the initial contour. Uniform exponential height decay kills the
horizontal segments (standard functional-equation polynomial bounds on
the compact intervening strip suffice). Thus the exact centered integral
has the displayed all-scale bound, including X<1.
This theorem has no RH consequence: both zeta factors are numerators.
The actual profile has 1/zeta(r*s). Unknown denominator zeros obstruct
the same contour shift. A certificate with arithmetic_model=unit cannot
be applied to arithmetic_model=inverse, even if the formulas look alike.

5. A proved obstruction to discarding the actual sign orientation

For every nonzero common-rate Q there are positive b, b_support, Y0 and
M0 such that, respectively,
 sum_d |e(X/d^r)| >= b*X^(1/r), X>=Y0,
 sum_d |mu(d)|*|e(X/d^r)| >= b_support*X^(1/r), X>=Y0*M0^r.
This is a limitation of a particular proof method, not a counterexample
to an actual signed estimate. It remains true after both unit residues
vanish and after retaining the actual squarefree support of mu.

If I!=0, choose Y0<=1 by halving until
 sum_m |c_m| R^m zeta(K+ell*m) Y0^(K+ell*m-1)<=|I|/2.
Then |e(Y)|>=|I|*Y/2 for 0<Y<=Y0; take w=1, L=|I|.
If I=0, let m0 be the first nonzero coefficient, w=K+ell*m0 and
L=|c_m0|R^m0*zeta(w)>0. Since |exp(-z)-1|<=z,
the difference from the leading lattice term is at most
 |c_m0|R^(m0+1)zeta(w+ell)Y^(w+ell)
 +sum_{m>m0}|c_m|R^m zeta(K+ell*m)Y^(K+ell*m).
Choose Y0 by halving until this divided by Y^w is <=L/2. Then
|e(Y)|>=L*Y^w/2. Both halving procedures terminate mathematically;
a finite resource exhaustion is a missing certificate, not a false bound.

Set M=ceil((X/Y0)^(1/r)); for X>=Y0, M<=2*(X/Y0)^(1/r).
The integral lower bound sum_{d>=M}d^(-r*w)>=M^(1-r*w)/(r*w-1)
gives b=L*Y0^(w-1/r)/(2^(r*w)*(r*w-1)).
For the actual support, the already proved squarefree count
 |sum_{d<=x}mu(d)^2-x/zeta(2)|<=(5/2)*sqrt(x)
implies at least M/(2*zeta(2)) squarefree d in (M,2M] once
M/(2*zeta(2))>=(5/2)*(sqrt(2*M)+sqrt(M)). A dyadic M0 suffices.
On this interval |e(X/d^r)|>=L*X^w/(2*(2*M)^(r*w)), so a valid
b_support=L*Y0^(w-1/r)/(zeta(2)*2^(2*r*w+1)).
Accordingly any backend estimating the rewritten sum by either of these
absolute norms cannot supply a power strictly below 1/r. Signed methods
that keep cancellation between the positive and negative mu(d) remain open.

6. Operator zero preservation without an unnecessary zero-free halfplane

It suffices to prove W(rho/r)!=0 for every ordinary zeta zero with
1/2<Re(rho)<1 and Im(rho)!=0, at all heights and multiplicities.
Gamma and R^(-v) are nonzero there. The finite polynomial P need only
avoid the corresponding v-values. All real roots of P are safe, wherever
their real part lies, since nontrivial ordinary zeta zeros are nonreal.
Real-rootedness is certified exactly by the Hermite-Sylvester matrix
H_ij=sum_roots lambda^(i+j), with multiplicities, being positive
semidefinite. A rational Schur-complement factorization supplies the
producer certificate; the independent checker uses exact squarefree
polynomial division and Sturm root counting instead.
For nonreal roots, an exact conjugate quadratic real-part test can exclude
the ordinary strip. Otherwise finite disjoint Rouché disks cover EVERY
distinct polynomial root. Taylor linear dominance
 |P'(c)|*h>|P(c)|+sum_{j>=2}|P^(j)(c)/j!|*h^j
proves one root per disk. A mapped rectangle rho=r*(K-ell*v) is safe if
outside the right-half strip or if a native zeta ball excludes zero on
the entire rectangle. Disk count equals the degree of the squarefree
polynomial, so no operator root is omitted. This is not a sampled zeta
zero grid. Failure to exclude an operator root is missing recovery,
not a counterexample to the growth estimate. A safe operator can still
vanish at a chosen quantitative observer, making that observer unavailable.

7. Fractional dilation constraints and automatically assembled banks

Suppose a zero-preserving actual operator satisfies the critical cofinal
target in section 3 for every integer N>=1. A true uniform unit increment
bound extends each estimate to all real X>=1. Its centered Mellin
integral is holomorphic in Re(s)>1/(2*r). The exact pole subtraction
removes only the ordinary s=1 pole. If rho is a right-half ordinary zero,
holomorphy at rho/r forces a numerator zero zeta(rho/r) of at least the
same multiplicity. When beta/r>1/2, that zero is already in the right
half. When beta/r<1/2, functional-equation symmetry gives the next zero
 rho_next=1-conjugate(rho)/r.
When beta/r=1/2, cancellation by a critical-line numerator zero is allowed.

For r>=2, the reflected branch is always used. Real parts contract to
r/(r+1) and heights to zero. Infinitely many distinct zeros would
accumulate at the nonzero real zeta value there, a contradiction.
For 1<r<2, initially divide while beta>r/2. Unless beta equals r^j/2
for some j>=1 with r^j<2, this reaches beta<r/2. The interval
(1/2,r/2) is invariant under beta ->1-beta/r, since
1-1/(2*r)<r/2 (the difference is (r-1)^2/(2*r)>0).
The same contraction contradiction follows. Thus the exceptional set is
 E_r={r^j/2:j>=1,r^j<2}; it is finite and computed exactly.
An individual r<2 gives a quasi-RH constraint, not RH. A bank whose
exact intersection of E_r is empty excludes all right-half zeros, hence
implies RH by symmetry, provided EVERY member's cofinal actual bound is
uniformly true. Completeness of each finite exception set is mandatory.
The bank is assembled by the detector; the arithmetic bounds are NOT
proved by assembling it. Resource exhaustion is not an empty set.

Conversely under RH, reciprocal zeta on any fixed right-of-critical
vertical line has polynomial height growth. For completeness, center a
disk at 2+it with radius 3/2-delta/2, for |t|>=2 and 0<delta<1.
RH excludes zeros in this disk and the pole is outside it. The branch
of log(zeta) fixed by the Euler product at the center has bounded center
value, and Re(log(zeta))=log|zeta|=O_delta(log(2+|t|)) throughout the disk,
by the standard functional-equation polynomial growth bound on its compact
real-part strip. Borel-Caratheodory on a smaller disk through
1/2+delta+it gives |log(zeta)|=O_delta(log(2+|t|)). Exponentiating bounds
1/zeta by a polynomial; the compact remaining heights cause no problem.
Further-right lines use the Euler product directly. Zeta in the numerator
also has polynomial growth. Exponential Gamma decay allows shifting to every fixed line
sigma>(1/2)/r, removing the s=1 pole, and proves the requested cofinal
bounds with free constants. The standard RH/Mertens equivalence in the
pinned Titchmarsh is additional calibration, not a new prime bound.
Hence a certified complementary bank has an RH-equivalent uniform
premise. Numerical successes establish neither direction's premise.

8. Conditional observer accounting and research status

For each positive atom, the inherited integral-plus-two-peaks estimate
bounds G(Y)/Y for Y>=1. For 0<Y<=1, the distinct bound
R^m*zeta(K+ell*m) applies. Add these constants to get an all-Y majorant.
Since the derivative is a combination of the m and m+1 atoms divided by
Y, summing its dilations with weights d^(-r) gives a global derivative
bound with the full zeta(r) cost. Add |A| for the centered response.
Omitting this local guard would incorrectly apply an undilated large-Y
bound to the small scales reached by the infinite dilation sum.

For a fixed ordinary query rho=sigma+i*t, set s=rho/r. Charge all local
integrals, the exact main pole, the actual unit increment, the true
kernel transform denominator, the numerator zeta(s), and the positive
distance from each tested exponent to Re(s). Free fitted C_q multiplies
the full logarithmic integral (ceil(d) is a conservative exponent).
Every bank member has its own premise and cost; a missing member observer
is not silently removed. One point observer is not all-height control.
