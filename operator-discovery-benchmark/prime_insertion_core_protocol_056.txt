with denominator<=12. This is a resource bound, not an exponent whitelist.

Relation grammar: form exact coefficient rows for every generator prime
and for the universal outside-prime class in the first Taylor jet of the
complete integrand. Generic rational row reduction finds relations that
annihilate the outside row and relations annihilating every row. Promote
these inferred basis vectors into reusable signed mixtures; enumerate sums
and differences of pairs of the complete-nullspace basis. No particular
winning mixture is supplied. Retain singleton controls and an ablation
with relation promotion disabled. Both branches use the same feature
family and work cap; actual work is reported separately. This ablation
shows exactly what inferred compositions enable in this restricted grammar,
not a claim about every possible alternative search or an equal-time race.

E_p andD_p generators, expanding their Boolean coefficients, and matching
