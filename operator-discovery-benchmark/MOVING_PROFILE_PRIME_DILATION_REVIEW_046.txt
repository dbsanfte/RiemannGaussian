MOVING PROFILE PRIME DILATION REVIEW 046

Result and scope
----------------
The plus-dilation has a real ordinary-prime pairing mechanism, but it does
not erase the prime-square branch, the unit/indicator, the signed harmonic
normalizers, or the critical-zero boundary layer. There is no proved
improvement to the advertised eta1 rate. The separately reviewed global
rate obstruction rejects eta1 for the two selected bare profiles: their
barriers are3/7 and2/5. Moment order4 does not bypass these whole-norm
barriers. The finite observations remain separate facts.

A useful NEW mathematical production is an exact prime-matched profile and
its predetermined source-bound dilation auxiliary. It cancels all
valuation-one coefficients in the floor-convolution source, including the
unit pairing, retains the prime-square and scalar counterterms, and is
exactly compilable with rational coefficients using the existing child
dyadic atoms. It exposes a coprime/prime-square joint discrepancy leaf. That
leaf remains OPEN, including at eta no larger than the global barrier.
This is an analytic proposal, not an implemented primitive, numerical
study, proof of convergence, or proof/refutation of RH.
Production status: DERIVED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED for prime_split,
prime_matched_parent, the bounded p-adic elimination node, and the
two-sided moment primitive. No production here carries an upper-bound claim.

Inputs read: frozen pilot-003/selected_families.json, source definitions of
the frozen044 dilation compiler, and the045 drift memo. No source arrays,
norm certificates or recovery observations were read. No existing file was
changed; no numerical norm or old suite/study was run. One exact rational
synthetic moment calculation verified the illustrative new weight vector
below. No actual candidate application or fitted coefficient is a premise.

1. Definitions, unit and exact whole-function dilation
----------------------------------------------------
Let p be an actual prime, chi=1_(0<x<=1), I_p=chi(px), D_p v(x)=v(px).
Define f_n as in043 and f_1=0. All equalities below hold on the whole
positive x-axis, with every function zero beyond its stated support.
The exact floor identity is
  D_p f_n = f_(pn) - f_p/n.
It includes x>1/p: the right side is then zero. Also
  ||D_p v||_2=p^(-1/2)||v||_2.

Use arbitrary child and parent profiles V(n), U(n), normalized V(1)=U(1)=1,
including exact signed rounded profiles. Set
  e_child=chi+sum_(2<=n<=pN) mu(n)V(n)f_n,
  e_parent=chi+sum_(2<=n<=N) mu(n)U(n)f_n,
  S_V=sum_(n<=pN) mu(n)V(n)/n,
  S_U=sum_(n<=N) mu(n)U(n)/n.
Then h=e_child+D_p e_parent is EXACTLY
  h=chi+I_p+sum_n A(n)f_n-S_U*f_p,
  A(n)=mu(n)V(n)1_(n<=pN)
       +1_(p|n)mu(n/p)U(n/p)1_(n/p<=N).
The unit A(1)=1 multiplies f_1=0, but it must still be retained in the
harmonic normalizer and in the floor-convolution identity.

The ordinary prime laws mu(pm)=-mu(m) when p does not divide m, and
mu(p^2 m)=0, give the following complete p-adic table, with p not dividing m:
  A(m)     = mu(m)V(m),                         m<=pN;
  A(pm)    = mu(m)[U(m)-V(pm)],                 m<=N;
  A(p^2 m) = -mu(m)U(pm),                      pm<=N;
  A(p^j m) = 0,                               j>=3.
The formulas also handle other prime squares through mu(m)=0. At m=1 the
valuation-one entry is A(p)=1-V(p), not an omitted or assumed zero unit.

In the signed-vector convention h=chi-sum d_n f_n-sum t_q I_q, t_p=-1,
the exceptional floor coefficient is
  d_p=V(p)+S_U-1.
For n!=p, d_n=-A(n). Thus a small A(p) never licenses dropping f_p*S_U.
The p^2 coefficients are real parent contributions; they are not zero just
because the child ordinary-Mobius coefficient at a square is zero.

2. A source-bound coprime reduction, exact even after rounding
------------------------------------------------------------
For any profile W, define
  B_M[W]=sum_(m<=M,p does not divide m) mu(m)W(m)f_m,
  T_M[W]=sum_(m<=M,p does not divide m) mu(m)W(m)/m.
The m=1 term in B is zero, while its term in T is W(1). Pairing m and pm
using the ordinary prime law and the floor identity proves
  e_M[W]=chi+B_M[W]
          -D_p B_floor(M/p)[W(p*.)]
          -f_p T_floor(M/p)[W(p*.)].
This uses no false multiplicativity of a mixture or of rounded powers.
It applies literally to the finite exact rational rounded values.

Applying it to both sides of the selected plus-dilation yields
  h = chi+I_p+B_(pN)[V]
      +D_p B_N[U-V(p*.)]
      -D_(p^2) B_floor(N/p)[U(p*.)]
      -f_p T_N[V(p*.)]
      -(D_p f_p) T_floor(N/p)[U(p*.)].                 (A)
Both scalar terms are signed actual-Mobius harmonic sums. Since
D_p f_p=f_(p^2)-f_p/p, this expression compiles back to a finite floor/step
vector with exactly the same support and all scalar signs. There is no
per-scale fitted normalizing constant.

Equation(A) is a detector-generable reduction node:
  prime_split(prime, child_profile, parent_profile, parent_support).
It emits the two coprime profile vectors, the paired profile difference,
the prime-square dilation, the two signed scalar counterterms, unit and
indicator. The coprime restriction is diagnostic; it must not replace the
ordinary all-prime target or be treated as an RH target by itself.
The factor ||D_(p^2)||=1/p is paid exactly, but this does not bound the
undilated coprime block or its signed crosses.

3. Exact divisor-product cells and a genuinely arithmetic hard leaf
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Let k=floor(1/x)>=1. The full residual is constant on cell
(1/(k+1),1/k], of mass1/[k(k+1)]. Define
  C_W(k)=sum_(d|k,d<=support) mu(d)W(d).
Changing the order of the finite floor sum gives
  sum_(n<=support) mu(n)W(n)floor(k/n)=sum_(l<=k) C_W(l).
Hence, for ALL k, with these explicitly truncated divisor sums,
  h_k=1+1_(k>=p)+k*S_V+floor(k/p)*S_U
      -sum_(l<=k) C_V(l)-sum_(l<=floor(k/p)) C_U(l).   (B)
This retains the actual signed unit and both normalizers.

For a raw mixture W_beta(n)=sum_j w_j n^(-r_j*beta), whenever k is within
the profile support, every divisor is included and Euler factorization gives
  C_W(k)=sum_j w_j product_(ell|k)(1-ell^(-r_j*beta)).  (C)
The product is over distinct actual prime divisors, so valuations>=2 are
handled automatically. For the whole forcing, formula(C) applies to BOTH
terms in(B) throughout k<=pN: child k<=pN and parent k/p<=N. Beyond that
range formula(B) remains exact, while(C) must be replaced by its truncated
version. No observed prefix licenses its tail.

Individual single-twist products in(C) are nonnegative; their signed
mixture is not generally nonnegative. For the selected order4 profile,
C_W(ell)=1-W(ell) is positive. At two distinct fixed primes ell1,ell2,
letting a=log(ell1), b=log(ell2), its leading term is
  beta^4[a^4+b^4-(a+b)^4]<0.
Thus positivity of the raw profile itself does not justify taking the
absolute value of the divisor products or discarding their covariance.
This is an analytic identity, not an additional arithmetic observation.

A concrete source leaf for proof search is a uniform BLOCK discrepancy
bound for the full signed quantity on the right of(B). If it supplies
  sum_(K<=k<2K) h_k^2 <= Q(N,K), K=1,2,4,...,
then the explicitly paid implication
  ||h||_2^2=sum_(k>=1) h_k^2/[k(k+1)]
             <=sum_(K dyadic) Q(N,K)/K^2
can close the forcing leaf if the last series is uniformly <=A/L^eta.
The implication uses only cell masses. The hard arithmetic statement must
control child/parent product correlations, harmonic normalizers and the
truncated divisor tail jointly. This is a different, inspectable source
obligation; it is not asserted true by restating the desired endpoint norm.
Absolute coefficient sums give bounds growing with support and do not
close this leaf.

4. Child beta, ceiling jumps, and exact rounding
----------------------------------------------
The selected bare raw profile is
  P_beta(n)=4n^(-beta)-6n^(-2beta)+4n^(-3beta)-n^(-4beta)
           =1-(1-n^(-beta))^4,
  beta_N=ceil(L(N)^theta)/L(N), theta=4/7 or3/5.
It satisfies0<P_beta(n)<=1 for finite n>=1. Its rounded counterpart
independently floors each atom at4ceil(log2N) bits and retains signed weights;
the absolute weight sum is15. This finite rational profile need not equal
the raw polynomial in one rounded atom.

At p=2, L(2N)=L(N)+1 exactly. Write z_L=ceil(L^theta). Since0<theta<1,
z_(L+1)-z_L is0 or1, and
  beta_(2N)-beta_N
    =-z_L/[L(L+1)]                     when there is no ceiling jump,
    =(L-z_L)/[L(L+1)]                  when there is a jump.
This jump term is O(1/L), larger than the smooth derivative size in general.
It must not be silently replaced by (theta-1)L^(theta-2). Nevertheless
beta_(2N)/beta_N->1, and the mismatch is smaller than beta_N relatively.

For the raw scalar F(u)=1-(1-exp(-u))^4, u>=0,
  sup |F'(u)|=27/64,
  sup u|F'(u)|<=4/e<=2.
Therefore the paired profile phi(n)=P_(beta_N)(n)-P_(beta_(2N))(2n)
satisfies the ALL-n bound
  |phi(n)| <= 2*|beta_(2N)-beta_N|/min(beta_N,beta_(2N))
               +(27/64)*beta_(2N)*log(2).
Replacing log(2) by1 gives an entirely rational computable scalar upper
bound from the two exact beta certificates. Separate parent/child rounding
adds epsilon_N+epsilon_(2N), epsilon_M=15*2^(-4ceil(log2M)).
This scalar bound is O(beta_N+L^-theta), hence O(beta_N) for theta>1/2.
At every fixed n, the order4 local moments give the much smaller O(beta_N^4)
gap. Neither estimate is a whole floor norm bound.

The global norm error from replacing raw profiles by the selected rounded
ones is rigorously at most
  ||h_rounded-h_raw||_2 <=pN*epsilon_(pN)+p^(-1/2)*N*epsilon_N,
because ||f_n||_2<=1 and dilation has norm p^(-1/2). It is O(N^-3).
This bound pays child precision separately from parent precision and is
negligible at any inverse-log rate. It is a norm-distance allowance; one
must use triangle inequality before squaring, not just add it to norm^2.

5. Concrete new production: prime-matched normalized parent
----------------------------------------------------------
Use the ACTUAL child profile V at support pN, with its child beta and child
rounding. Define
  lambda_N=V(p),
  Q_N(n)=V(pn)/V(p), n<=N,
provided the separately certified scalar lambda_N is nonzero. No further
rounding is needed: finite V and the quotient are exact rationals. Q_N(1)=1.
It is a source-bound profile transform, not a least-squares choice.

The new forcing is
  H_matched=e_(pN)[V]+lambda_N*D_p e_N[Q_N].
In its convolution source the coefficient at pm, p not dividing m, is
  mu(m)[lambda_N Q_N(m)-V(pm)]=0,
including the unit m=1. Its surviving prime-square coefficient is
  -mu(m)V(p^2m), p^2m<=pN.
The unit/indicator is chi+lambda_N I_p. The f_p scalar counterterm remains
and must not be confused with the vanished convolution coefficient at p.
The complete counterpart of(A) is
  H_matched=chi+lambda_N I_p+B_(pN)[V]
       -D_(p^2) B_floor(N/p)[V(p^2*.)]
       -f_p T_N[V(p*.)]
       -(D_p f_p) T_floor(N/p)[V(p^2*.)].             (D)
This identity removes the intermediate valuation-one block exactly, even
when each underlying twist atom was rounded independently.

For the selected a=1,p=2, both theta values, beta_M<=1 for all M>=2.
The raw lambda is at least P_1(2)=15/16. Since child M=2N>=4 and
epsilon_M<=15/256, its rounded value satisfies
  225/256 <=lambda_N<=271/256,
  lambda_N/sqrt(2)<=271/(256sqrt(2))<1.
The latter is licensed by the exact integer comparison271^2<2*256^2.
Thus normalization and an eventual contraction constant are not mysterious
leaves for this specific matched production. The mixed profile Q itself
may leave[0,1] at finite N; its signed coefficients remain legal.

The ORIGINAL selected forcing is recovered exactly, not replaced, by
  h=H_matched+A_prime,
  A_prime=D_p[(1-lambda_N)chi
               +sum_(2<=n<=N) mu(n)(U(n)-V(pn))f_n]. (E)
This is a predetermined arithmetic auxiliary and indicator; its coefficients
can be emitted directly from parent/child atoms. It is legal as a general
signed diagnostic vector, not as a standalone RH target with indicators.
The joint price is
  ||h||^2=||H_matched||^2+2<H_matched,A_prime>+||A_prime||^2.
All terms and crosses must remain signed. Their uniform control is OPEN.
An apparent small scalar coefficient or exact prime pairing cannot pay it.

H_matched compares two DIFFERENT target profiles. Proving its bound does not
give convergence of the original single family unless the coupled transfer
to Q_N, or the auxiliary in(E), is also controlled uniformly. A new detector
node could represent this coupled type explicitly:
  prime_matched_parent(child_profile,prime)
with obligations lambda!=0, signed scalar range, exact source pairing,
rounding provenance, profile-transfer norm, and joint forcing bound. This
is useful mathematical production even though the hard norm leaf is unpaid.
It is not permission to reinterpret frozen candidates or rerank their data.

6. Euler factors, bounded prime resolvents, and their limitations
---------------------------------------------------------------
For one raw twist at a common beta, the untruncated source has Dirichlet
series1/zeta(s+beta) in its domain of absolute convergence. Multiplication
by the plus-dilation source factor1+p^-s changes its p-local factor to
  (1+p^-s)(1-p^(-(s+beta)))
    =1+(1-p^-beta)p^-s-p^-beta p^(-2s).
It suppresses valuation one by O(beta), while valuation two persists.
For a matched scalar p^-beta in that channel the product is
  (1+p^(-(s+beta)))(1-p^(-(s+beta)))
    =1-p^(-2(s+beta)),
which cancels valuation one exactly but again retains the square channel.
For different beta values the whole source series is instead the sum of
the child reciprocal-zeta series and p^-s times the parent series; there
is no common factorization to assume. Finite supports have additional
Dirichlet tails. Mixtures are linear combinations of these source series,
not one multiplicative arithmetic function.

The standard Euler and reciprocal-Mobius identities are recorded in the
official NIST DLMF27.4 equations3 and5:
  [Euler products and Dirichlet series](https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.4#E5).
The Dirichlet convolution rule is in
  [DLMF inversion formulas](https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.5#E1).
The prime table above follows directly from ordinary factorization, rather
than importing a finite-prime countermodel or an independent-prime guess.

For a single channel alpha=p^-beta<=1, the operator
  R_(p,alpha,J)=sum_(j=0..J) alpha^j D_(p^j)
has the exact algebraic remainder
  R_(p,alpha,J)(I-alpha D_p)=I-alpha^(J+1)D_(p^(J+1)),
  ||R_(p,alpha,J)||<=1/(1-alpha/sqrt(p)),
  ||remainder||<= (alpha/sqrt(p))^(J+1).
This permits a detector to PRICE a finite p-adic elimination and its tail
without RH. Floor-basis application must retain its f_p-type scalar
counterterms and dilated indicators as in(A); source convolution alone is
not the full residual operator. Infinite prime-free Euler series still has
the same zeta denominator and does not supply a global remainder estimate.

Also I+D_p is boundedly invertible on L2, with inverse sum_j(-D_p)^j.
For the SAME residual v,
  (1-p^-1/2)||v||<=||(I+D_p)v||<=(1+p^-1/2)||v||.
Thus same-scale plus dilation cannot improve a norm exponent by itself.
The selected use of a different child support/profile is essential, and its
scale increment is precisely a new arithmetic obligation.

7. Another possible signed language: positive AND inverse moment synthesis
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local compatibility moments do not control all critical-zero responses.
For a maximal survival mixture, inverse moments are strictly positive:
  sum w_j/r_j^d =1/(d-1)! * integral_0^infinity u^(d-1)P(u)du>0.
For the selected four-rate weights, sum w_j/r_j=25/12. At an untruncated
reciprocal-zeta pole, these inverse moments do not cancel its principal part.

A general exact alternative uses s=m+d distinct positive rational rates
and solves
  sum w_j=1,
  sum w_j r_j^k=0, k=1..m-1 and k=-1..-d.
Writing y_j=w_j/r_j^d converts it to the ordinary Vandermonde system
  sum y_j r_j^l=delta(l,d), l=0..s-1.
It is therefore detector-generable by exact linear equations, with no fixed
rate bank. Its first positive moment is
  M_m=(-1)^(m+1)*e_m(r_1,...,r_s)!=0,
from the coefficient of x^d in product(x-r_j). Local order m is preserved.
Rates1..5, m=4,d=1 give the exact synthetic witness
  w=(-77/12,107/3,-117/2,122/3,-125/12),
  M_-1=0, M_0=1, M_1=M_2=M_3=0, M_4=-274,
  sum|w|=455/3.
This was verified by exact rational arithmetic, without source data.

The inverse moments cancel reciprocal-zeta Laurent principal parts through
zero multiplicity d at the exact zero, without assuming simplicity. This
is an untruncated analytic fact, not a license to approximate a finite
Dirichlet polynomial on the critical line without paying its tail. Such a
profile cannot be a nonnegative survival profile: M_-1=integral P=0 while
P(0)=1 forces negative values. The general signed range and larger absolute
sum/rounding must be paid. More importantly, this production does NOT beat
the whole beta-width norm barrier in the next section. It may change
constants or other joint arithmetic leaves; no viable faster exponent
follows from these moment constraints alone.

8. Incorporating the independently reviewed global beta-width barrier
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The parent global audit covers FIXED finite bare signed mixtures with
positive distinct rates, normalized weights, theta>1/2 and exact rounding.
The argument below explains why a prime identity cannot evade its mechanism.
It is not an assertion that an implemented guard already covers the new
coupled or scale-dependent-weight production.

For a finite pure-floor target its Mellin transform is
  E_M(s)=[1+zeta(s)*(S_M-A_M(s))]/s,
  A_M(s)=sum_(n<=M)mu(n)W_M(n)n^-s,
valid initially for Re(s)>1 and continuing to Re(s)>0 because f_n is bounded.
The full plus forcing has transform E_(pN)(s)+p^-s E_N(s), including unit
and indicator. At ANY zeta zero rho, the exact finite value is
  H_N(rho)=(1+p^-rho)/rho.
If RH is false, functional symmetry supplies a zero with Re(rho)>1/2.
Cauchy then gives the positive N-independent norm lower bound
  ||h_N||^2 >=(2Re(rho)-1)|1+p^-rho|^2/|rho|^2.
Here1+p^-rho is nonzero since |p^-rho|<1. Thus a vanishing forcing claim
cannot assume this RH-false branch away.

On the RH branch use the actual imported theorem, not a newly invented
Mertens estimate: Soundararajan, Partial sums of the Mobius function,
Theorem1, assumes RH and gives a square-root bound with an exponential
factor:
  M(x) << sqrt(x)*exp((log x)^(1/2)*(log log x)^14).
  [Primary paper, Theorem1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.0723).
For theta>1/2 the exponent sqrt(log N)*(log log N)^14 is
o(beta_N*log N). Abel summation therefore pays
the finite reciprocal-zeta tails uniformly near one fixed critical zero,
with errors faster than any inverse log power. This conditional theorem
is used only inside the RH branch of the obstruction proof.

Let rho=1/2+i*t0 have any finite multiplicity d>=1. On the shrinking Mellin
window s=1/2+i(t0+beta_N*v), the raw target response tends to
  F_d(v)/rho,
  F_d(v)=1-(iv)^d sum_j w_j/(r_j+iv)^d.
Since F_d(0)=1, it stays nonzero on some fixed v-interval, whatever positive
or inverse moments were cancelled. Child beta/parent beta tends1, so the
whole plus response tends to
  (1+p^-rho)*F_d(v)/rho.
Mellin Plancherel on that window of width proportional to beta_N gives
  ||h_N||^2 >=c*beta_N
for all sufficiently large N, for some fixed c>0. The independently paid
rounding norm error is O(N^-3) and does not change this lower rate.
The two branches together refute eta>1-theta without assuming RH true or
false as a conclusion. Selected theta4/7 allows no eta above3/7; theta3/5
allows no eta above2/5. In particular their advertised eta1 cannot hold
uniformly, even though the local order4 gate alone did not reject it.
This is a rate refutation for these fixed formulas, not an RH refutation.
The selected bare b0 control, with a=1/3,theta5/6 and rates1,2,4, has the
same argument with dilation factor omitted and barrier1/6; its eta1 is
also rejected by the separately reviewed global audit. The present prime
production analysis does not need to evaluate that control's norms.

Inverse moments may modify F_d away from v=0 but cannot change F_d(0)=1.
Prime-matched weights tend to the same fixed weights and lambda tends1;
they leave this same near-zero response mechanism in place. Extending the
automated global guard to such coupled profiles requires an explicit
bounded-weight/tail and typed-source proof; the current fixed-mixture guard
must not claim that extension merely by matching names or local limits.

9. What is usable and what remains unpaid
----------------------------------------
The usable new production is the exact prime_split / prime_matched_parent
language, with equation(A) or(D), source-generated auxiliary(E), scalar
normalization/contraction certificates and signed joint pricing. It changes
the objects offered to the detector in a principled arithmetic way. The
two-sided moment solve is another possible signed primitive, restricted
here to constant/shape calibration, not an exponent shortcut.

Before any uniform bound is claimed, proof search must pay:
  - actual all-prime coprime source and p^2 branch, not just one Euler factor;
  - child/parent beta ceilings and each distinct dyadic rounding;
  - signed harmonic normalizers, unit, indicator and exceptional f_p term;
  - source-correlated cross terms of the full emitted vectors;
  - complete head AND truncated divisor/cell tails through all scales;
  - coupled profile transfer if the matched parent differs from the target;
  - a bound exponent at or below the applicable global barrier, with equality
    still OPEN rather than admitted by necessary-condition survival.

No unconditional upper bound at eta3/7, eta2/5 or any positive eta was found
here. Such a bound for the original pure-floor forcing would still be a
substantial RH-bearing result; scalar profile estimates and finite-prime
resolvents do not constitute it. The construction can simplify prime
pairing and offer a more structured joint remainder, but it supplies no
proved exponent or scale-transfer improvement by itself. RH_proved=false,
RH_refuted=false, additional_norms_evaluated=0; original-core obligations OPEN.
