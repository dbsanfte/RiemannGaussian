STAGE037: COMPENSATED FULL-SOURCE ENERGY RECURRENCES

This protocol and the implementation are frozen before this stage reads the
arithmetic archive. Earlier experiments already used that archive. Training,
validation and final are fixed selection partitions, not claims of new data.
The original complete source f, every unit, composite and prime power remain
in F. This is a direct alternative to the accepted033 obligation, not an
isolation or replacement of its residual or144 transport bindings.

Search definition
 G_X(n)=F(n)+sum_i c_i X^(-gamma_i) A_i(floor(n/d_i);ceil(sqrt(2X))).
 A_i(t;y)=0 for t<1; otherwise h_i(1)+sum_(y<p<=t) h_i(p).
These are explicit prime profiles of the four035 generated arithmetic states.
They are not silently extended rough-prefix identities. All full-source and
helper cross terms are retained before numerical or analytical assessment.

The detector enumerates all singles and all pairs from4 states, divisors1,2,3,
coefficients -2,-1,-1/2,1/2,1,2, and16 nonnegative rational offsets in[0,1].
Offsets come from the general036 height scheduler, with explicit1/257 included.
Each exponent is the state's least already paid scale exponent plus an offset.
There is no semantic denominator ceiling; this run is one finite grammar budget.
Pairs with the same(state,divisor,exponent) are excluded as unsimplified singles.
No analyst supplies a winning compensator, sign, exponent or recurrence price.
The paid-helper restriction itself is a declared search bias, not completeness.

Goal and costs
 E_G(X)=X^-2 sum_(n=X..2X-1)G_X(n)^2.
 Search E_G(2X)<=E_G(X)+C D(X) for every dyadic integer X>=1,
 D(X)=X^-1 sum_(k=1..4X-1)f(k)^2.
 C dictionary:0,1/4096,1/1024,1/256,1/64,1/16,1/4,1,4.
These constants specify hypotheses. Finite acceptance never proves them.
The independent checker proves the helper prices, initialization, divisor
forcing O((1+logX)^3), and conditional recovery to all integer target blocks.
A uniformly proved recurrence would give E_F=O((1+logX)^4); C=0 is stronger.
The checker preserves the OPEN arithmetic lemma in the dependency graph.
An exact generic-envelope countermodel rejects that weak proof rule only.
It is explicitly not a counterexample to the actual source or its conjecture.

Data and ranking
Read the existing12-row archive once, structurally check all rows and decode
six complete-source/mode arrays through262143. Source coefficients, prefixes
and actual prime profiles are rebound. Cache13-dimensional full Gram matrices.
Blocks X=2^k,k=0..17; recurrence parents k=0..16.
Head k0..4; train5..10; validation11..14; final15..16.
Choose C from training only, without later repair. Choose candidates by zero
forcing, then term count, paid helper cost, finite C grid and required C.
Preserve candidates from overall, mixed-divisor, mixed-state, extended-fraction
and padding-control strata. Duplicates use the next eligible program.
If helper energy exceeds target energy on any training block, classify it as
padding dominated and exclude it from the four primary strata. That finite
diagnostic alone is not a mathematical impossibility claim.
Audit at most five nonempty proposals, one priced empty-program baseline, and
the evidence module's C=0 baseline using192-bit Arb on every integer of every
block. Record each positive defect as a finite arithmetic counterexample to
that exact C/program; keep failures of generic proof rules separately typed.
Do not rank using validation or final blocks. Report every chosen candidate.
