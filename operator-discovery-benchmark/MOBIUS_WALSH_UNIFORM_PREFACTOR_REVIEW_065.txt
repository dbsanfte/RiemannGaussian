WALSH ENERGY: UNIFORM PAID PREFACTOR AND ACTUAL MOMENT REVIEW 065
===============================================================

Scope and conclusion
--------------------
This source-only review reads mobius_walsh_energy_065.py,
MOBIUS_WALSH_ENERGY_CHECK_SCOPE_065.txt, the previously inspected062/063
source definitions and the frozen mathematical reviews. It reads no
scientific results arrays and executes no numerical study. It changes
none of those files, including the frozen growing-conditioning review.

The proposed coefficient-prefactor theorem is VALID for a declared
core_inverse construction, with the proposed constants and uniformity
in every allowed prefix. Upward binary weights with beta=1 and precision4
therefore have paid mass O(N polylog N) at H=floor(sqrt N). A uniform
subpolynomial bound for the ACTUAL weighted positive energy is a precise
sufficient unpaid leaf for square-root variation and the existing
conditional RH route. That energy bound is NOT proved here and is NOT
the same as the producer's universal Fourier/cycle energy upper bound.
It is a sufficient stronger normalized-moment premise, not an equivalence
to the original variation theorem.

1. Source parameters and exact coefficient norm
-----------------------------------------------
Fix p=3,q=5, registered primes {2,3,5}, integer N>=2, 1<=y<=D,
1<=H<=D, D=floor(4N/p), and r in[p/q,1]. Let L=1+log(4N),
with natural logarithms in this review. Take K=floor(y/(H+1)) and
B=max(H,K). Empty long dictionaries and K=0 are allowed.

For b=p,q, write

 w_b(d)=floor(4N/(bd))-3floor(2N/(bd))+2floor(N/(bd)),
 f(d,r)=1[d<=y]*(w_p(d)-r*w_q(d)), 1<=d<=H.

As in the source-only065 review, w_b(d) is in{-1,0,1}, so |f|<=2.
The long dictionary is obtained, for (v,c)=(4,1),(2,-3),(1,2), from
Z=floor(vN/b), 1<=i<=floor(Z/(H+1)), and
T=min(y,floor(Z/i)), retaining T>H. Add c to the threshold T and
combine duplicates. Let a_T(r)=a_(p,T)-r*a_(q,T), and K_T=floor(T/(H+1)).
The exact signed source matrix on short rows is

 G(d,ell;r)=sum_(T>H) a_T(r)*1[ell<=K_T]*floor(T/(ell*d)),
 G(u)(d)=sum_(ell<=K)G(d,ell;r)*u(ell).                     (1)

The two occurrences of u in

 c(u)=Delta D(u)P[f-G(u)]                                  (2)

refer to the same squarefree-prime-sign sequence. A squareful argument
has value zero; the unit has value one. P is core_inverse unless another
fill is explicitly declared below. After squarefree deletion, prime
parity/gcd reduction, coalescing and any actual-prime conditioning, let
q_j(x;r) be its exact remaining Walsh polynomial, and put

 A_j(r)=sum_U |coefficient_U(q_j)|, A(r)=sum_(j<=H) A_j(r).  (3)

The theorem below holds for any conditioned prime set, including none
and full conditioning. It does not require the degree-two gate.

2. A uniform absolute harmonic moment for the truncated dictionary
-----------------------------------------------------------------
For integers t>=0 define h(t)=sum_(1<=ell<=t)1/ell, h(0)=0, and

 M_abs(r)=sum_(T>H)|a_T(r)|*T*h(K_T).                      (4)

Combining signed duplicates and joining channels can only reduce this
positive expression relative to the ungrouped threshold list. On each
ungrouped entry from b,v,i, we have T<=Z/i, K_T<=4N and hence h(K_T)<=L.
Writing I=floor(Z/(H+1)), with the empty case I=0 understood,

 sum_(i<=I,T>H) T <= Z*h(I) <= Z*L.                       (5)

The first inequality even remains valid after dropping the T>H
restriction. No replacement of min(y,floor(Z/i)) by an untruncated
source has been made: it has only been upper-bounded in a positive sum.
Thus with lambda_p=1 and lambda_q=r,

 M_abs(r)
 <= L^2*sum_(b=p,q)lambda_b*sum_(v,c)|c|*floor(vN/b)
 <= 12*(1/p+r/q)*N*L^2.                                 (6)

The factor12 is 1*4+3*2+2*1. If Z=0 or I=0 that entry contributes
zero. Unit ell=1 is included whenever K_T>=1. The conservatively sized
K can include unused columns without affecting these bounds.

From (1), floor(T/(ell*d))<=T/(ell*d), so

 sum_(ell<=K)|G(d,ell;r)|<=M_abs(r)/d.                    (7)

This is a positive bound on coefficient size. It does NOT approximate
the signed source by its harmonic profile, so there is no fractional-part
error and no problematic O(N) error term in this proof.

3. Propagation through core_inverse and the Delta inverse
--------------------------------------------------------
Let rho(d) strip all2,3,5 factors, and s(d)=d/rho(d). Then

 (Pv)(d)=v(rho(d))/s(d), P(1/d)=1/d.                       (8)

The map is nonnegative and its row sums are at most one. Consequently
|Pf(d)|<=2, and by positivity, the triangle inequality and (7),

 sum_(ell<=K)|PG(d,ell;r)|
 <= P[sum_ell |G(.,ell;r)|](d)
 <= M_abs(r)*P(1/d)=M_abs(r)/d.                          (9)

Before coalescing, expanding D and then Delta bounds the coefficient
L1 by twice the absolute term count in D. Counting representations d=jm
of each short row gives

 A(r)<=2*sum_(d<=H)tau(d)
                  [|Pf(d)|+sum_(ell<=K)|PG(d,ell;r)|].    (10)

Squareful deletion cannot increase this bound. Replacing surviving
products by parity characters does not change their absolute individual
coefficients. Combining aliases, constants and diagonal terms can only
contract L1. Substituting actual signs for any prime set and coalescing
again also contracts L1. Thus (10) bounds precisely (3), not a different
polynomial with independent copies of u or independent row signs.
The Delta factor2 includes the virtual row H+1=0; it is an upper bound
also at the last row and the unit, without a missing boundary column.

Two elementary divisor estimates suffice:

 sum_(d<=H)tau(d)=sum_(a<=H)floor(H/a)<=H*(1+log H),
 sum_(d<=H)tau(d)/d
   =sum_(ab<=H)1/(ab)<=h(H)^2<=(1+log H)^2.               (11)

Combining (6),(9)-(11) proves the explicit UNIFORM theorem

 A(r)<=4H*(1+log H)
       +24*(1/p+r/q)*N*L^2*(1+log H)^2
 <=4H*(1+log H)+24*(1/p+r/q)*N*L^4.                      (12)

Here H<=D<=4N licenses the last comparison. The bound holds for every
required y, every r in[p/q,1], and every conditioning set. At p3,q5,
1/p+r/q<=8/15, hence

 A(r)<=4H*(1+log H)+(64/5)*N*L^4.                        (13)

If K=0 or y<=H, the dictionary is empty and the first term alone pays A.
If H=D, the same empty-long conclusion holds. If H=1, all harmonic and
divisor formulas remain valid with log H=0. No asymptotic finite-head
exception is necessary for (12).

4. Which other declared fills inherit a uniform profile constant?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The argument only needs nonnegative P, |Pf|<=2, and

 P(1/d)<=C_P/d with C_P independent of H.                 (14)

Then replace the second term in (12) by24*C_P*(1/p+r/q)*N*L^4.
The source063 fill definitions give the following rigorous constants:

 fill                 admissible C_P
 raw                         1
 zero                        1
 right                       1
 reciprocal_linear           1
 core_inverse                1
 left                       30
 linear                     30
 core                 no uniform C_P

For raw and zero this is immediate. Right interpolation uses the next
coprime node b>d and 1/b<=1/d; its virtual H+1 node has value zero.
For reciprocal_linear, at neighboring coprime a<d<b with an actual
right node, its weights applied to1/a and1/b give EXACTLY1/d:
[a(b-d)/(d(b-a))]/a+[b(d-a)/(d(b-a))]/b=1/d.
Replacing the virtual right-node value by zero only decreases this.

For left and linear, positivity bounds the profile by1/a, where a is
the previous coprime node. Every interval of30 consecutive integers
contains a number congruent1 modulo30, hence a>=d-29; also a>=1.
Therefore d/a<=30. This covers the terminal interval as well. These
constants are deliberately conservative; their precise optimization
is immaterial to the growth theorem.

For core, take d=2^k<=H. Its profile is1/rho(d)=1, whereas1/d=2^-k.
The needed constant is at least2^k and is unbounded as H grows. This
proves failure of this PROFILE METHOD for core. It does not establish
that core's actual coefficient norm has bad growth, or that another
argument cannot pay it.

This classification concerns declared fill branches. A finite study's
selected winner, its parent conditioning and its selected weight exponent
are separate choices. In particular no assertion is made that all36
studied selections obey (13) or the beta1 mass estimate below. Existence
of the declared core_inverse/beta1 construction is sufficient for the
conditional all-scale theorem; it need not be a finite study's winner.

5. Exact upward binary weight bound, including zero rows
------------------------------------------------------
In the producer, beta1 means exponent string '1'. With precision4,
for A_j>0 let k=floor(log2 A_j), u=2^(k-4). The exact integer-power
comparison in dyadic_power_weight chooses

 w_j=u*ceil(A_j/u).                                      (15)

The code's e is floor(log2 of the numerator/denominator power), and its
k=e//b gives the same binade for beta1 (a=b=1). Its search interval
brackets the ceiling between16 and32, including an exact binade endpoint.
No floating logarithm is used to establish (15).
Since0<=w_j-A_j<u<=A_j/16,

 A_j<=w_j<(17/16)*A_j.                                   (16)

For a zero polynomial row A_j=0, the producer sets w_j=0. That row is
omitted from divisions; c_j is zero under every sign assignment.
If A=0, W=E_mu=variation=0 with this convention. Otherwise

 W:=sum_j w_j<(17/16)*A.                                 (17)

For H=floor(sqrt N), (13),(17) prove the paid mass theorem

 W< (17/16)*[4sqrt N*(1+log N)+(64/5)*N*L^4]
   =O(N*L^4), uniformly in y and conditioning.             (18)

Other beta policies from discover do not inherit (16). The selected
candidate may use another beta. This theorem declares beta1 and binary
precision4 explicitly, without making an all-policy mass assertion.
The producer's computational capacity caps bound its finite search;
the mathematical recipe and inequality hold for every N. A theorem
using the recipe at all scales is not a claim that the current capped
software enumerates every scale.

6. A precise unpaid ACTUAL positive energy leaf
----------------------------------------------
For a declared core_inverse branch, H=floor(sqrt N), a declared
conditioning rule S(N,y), and the endpoint beta1 weights above, define

 E_mu(N,y,r)=sum_(j:A_j(r)>0) c_j(mu;r)^2/w_j(r).           (19)

The actual remaining assignment has every actual prime sign -1.
It is the SAME ordinary mu in both factors of(2). Equation(19) is an
exact nonnegative arithmetic moment with an independently paid
coefficient-mass prefactor. Weighted Cauchy gives

 sum_j |c_j(mu;r)| <=sqrt(W(r)*E_mu(N,y,r)).                (20)

A sufficient unpaid hypothesis is: for every delta>0 there exists
C_delta, independent of N and every required y, such that at BOTH
r=p/q and r=1,

 E_mu(N,y,r)<=C_delta*N^delta.                            (21)

The conditioning rule and weight recipe must be explicitly fixed;
constants may depend on that declared rule, p,q and delta. Finite-head
values may be absorbed in C_delta. No actual-energy bound is asserted
by this review.

Equations(18),(20),(21) imply, for every epsilon>0,

 sum_j |c_j(mu;r)|=O_epsilon(N^(1/2+epsilon))               (22)

uniformly in y at both endpoints: take delta=epsilon and absorb L^2
into the remaining N^(epsilon/2). The endpoint maximum also bounds
all r in[p/q,1] by convexity of the actual row-L1 norm. The squares
and r-dependent weights need not define an affine energy, and no such
affinity is used. Alternatively (21) can be required for every r;
that stronger version is unnecessary for the endpoint argument.

This reduces the proposed SUFFICIENT proof route to a normalized
positive-moment leaf after paying its coefficient prefactor. It is not
an equivalence to the original square-root variation problem. Indeed
w_j>=A_j>=|c_j(mu)| gives E_mu<=sum_j|c_j(mu)|; a square-root variation
estimate alone would only imply that weaker energy estimate, not (21).
Nor does W=O(N polylog N) itself imply (21).

7. Actual energy versus the checked universal energy envelope
-----------------------------------------------------------
The producer expands E(x)=sum_j q_j(x)^2/w_j using shared characters.
The checker supplies a pointwise upper U for EVERY free-sign assignment:
Fourier coefficient L1 minus twice the capacity-paid frustrated-cycle
mass. Thus E_mu<=U, but the distinction is material:

 * Proving (21) directly for the actual arithmetic assignment suffices
   for (22). It can retain actual arithmetic relations discarded by
   formal free signs, and does not require U to be subpolynomial.
 * Proving U=O_delta(N^delta) is a stronger sufficient universal-energy
   theorem. Combined with(18), it pays the checker-style construction.
 * Finite admissions of U, lower sign witnesses, and decreases under
   conditioning establish neither uniform theorem.

A direct proof of(21) would be a new arithmetic theorem beyond the
current finite checker scope. Its source binding still uses the exact
062/063/064 identities. One cannot relabel the actual value E_mu as a
certified universal upper, or infer its growth from cycle counts.

For perspective, the completely elementary bound |q_j(x)|<=A_j and
w_j>=A_j gives E(x)<=A for every assignment. With(13),(18) this yields
at best a linear/polylog variation scale, so it exposes rather than
pays the missing exponent.

8. Rounding changes only a uniform constant in the row-majorant budget
---------------------------------------------------------------------
For any positive row scales A_j and w_j=t_j*A_j with1<=t_j<=R,
put A=sum A_j and p_j=A_j/A. Then

 (sum w_j)*(sum A_j^2/w_j)/A^2
   =(sum p_j t_j)*(sum p_j/t_j)
   <=(R+1)^2/(4R).                                      (23)

Proof: (t-1)(R-t)>=0 implies t+R/t<=R+1. If
X=sum p_j t_j and Y=sum p_j/t_j, then X+RY<=R+1.
Consequently XY<=X(R+1-X)/R<=(R+1)^2/(4R).
Zero rows are omitted; the all-zero case is trivial.
At R=17/16 the factor is exactly1089/1088.
This proves the optional rounding claim without a distribution
assumption. It bounds the Cauchy product for the row-majorant energies;
it is not a claim that the cycle-improved universal bound equals that
product. Separately, actual energies satisfy E_mu(w)<=E_mu(A) and
W(w)<=R*A, so replacement of exact beta1 weights by their rounded
versions also loses at most R in that elementary actual product bound.
Neither statement changes an exponent.

9. Global subpower energy is stronger; partitioned moments are natural
---------------------------------------------------------------------
There is a purely algebraic counterexample to equivalence between the
desired L1 rate and a global subpower-energy premise. Let H=floor(sqrt N)
and take constant Walsh rows q_j=1 for ceil(H/2)<=j<=H, and zero rows
elsewhere. Every nonzero A_j=w_j=1 (binary rounding is exact). Then
variation=number of nonzero rows=O(sqrt N), whereas E_mu equals that
same number and is NOT O_delta(N^delta) for all delta>0. These are valid
shared-sign Walsh polynomials and positive weights, obeying the uniform
mass bound proved above. They need not arise from the actual joined
source or core_inverse, and are not a counterexample to its energy
hypothesis. They conclusively refute the abstract inference that an RH-
strength variation bound requires global subpower energy. Many actual
tail rows of this size would produce the same issue; this audit neither
asserts nor excludes their occurrence in the source.

A partition avoids imposing the large global mass on every row. For any
partition of {1,...,H} into disjoint blocks I, define

 W_I=sum_(j in I)w_j,
 E_I(mu)=sum_(j in I,A_j>0)c_j(mu)^2/w_j.

The exact block Cauchy inequality is

 sum_j |c_j(mu)| <=sum_I sqrt(W_I*E_I(mu)).                (24)

No independence between blocks or prime signs is needed. The analogous
universal energy E_I(x) can be expanded and cycle-bounded within each
block if desired. An actual arithmetic bound and a universal sign
bound remain different choices here too.

The coefficient prefactor proof can itself be restricted without paying
all divisors twice. Define

 n_I(d)=#{j in I:j divides d}
       +#{j in I:j+1<=H and j+1 divides d}.

Counting separately the two terms in each Delta row gives the exact
sufficient positive coefficient bound

 A_I:=sum_(j in I)A_j
 <=sum_(d<=H)n_I(d)*[2+M_abs/d],
 W_I<=(17/16)*A_I.                                      (25)

Thus independent improvements to the kernel or the block's incidence
count can pay a block mass. The unrestricted argument follows from
n_I<=2tau. For an interval I=[J,T] within[1,H], one can use

 sum_(d<=H)n_I(d)<=2H*sum_(j=J)^T 1/j,
 sum_(d<=H)n_I(d)/d
 <=2*[1+log(H/J)]*sum_(j=J)^T 1/j.                        (26)

These follow by summing floor(H/j), respectively h(floor(H/j))/j,
and including the shifted j+1 term only when it is within H. The
shifted term is no larger than the corresponding unshifted term.
The bounds are valid at J=H, including the last-row boundary.
They are conservative and do not themselves establish square-root
variation. In particular even a large-index block can inherit a large
M_abs contribution; improved source-bound cancellation may still be
necessary to make its mass estimate sharp.

A useful sufficient all-scale partition theorem would declare at most
polylog(N) blocks and prove block estimates

 W_I<=C*N^(alpha_I)*polylog(N),
 E_I(mu)<=C_delta*N^(beta_I+delta)*polylog(N),
 alpha_I+beta_I<=1,                                     (27)

uniformly over blocks, required prefixes and both endpoints, with
uniform constants and controlled logarithmic powers. Equation(24)
then gives O_epsilon(N^(1/2+epsilon)). More generally a directly paid
sum of sqrt(W_I E_I) at that rate suffices, with any explicitly bounded
number of blocks. This is a collection of sufficient residual leaves,
not an already established source theorem.

For the constant tail example, one block has W_I and E_I both of order
H, so alpha_I=beta_I=1/2 and the product is of order N: the correct
variation rate is recovered with no false global subpower requirement.
For the source construction, one may use exact block masses from the
conditioned coefficients or independent block estimates such as(25).
Neither this example nor(25) pays its actual block moments.

The global condition(21) remains one sufficient option, potentially
unnecessarily strong or false for the source. The partition condition
(24) with independently paid mass/moment estimates is a more flexible
next-search obligation. It must not be called equivalent to RH or
silently promoted to a necessary normalized-energy target.

10. Composition with paid long coefficients and the conditional bridge
---------------------------------------------------------------------
At H=floor(sqrt N), the062 generic long coefficient L1 remains
O(sqrt N*log(2N)) uniformly over required prefixes and parameters.
The short recipe here is fixed core_inverse and uses the SAME source
and long completion, with its original noncoprime fill and endpoint
rules. If the unpaid moment hypothesis(21), or the partition alternative
(24) with a uniform square-root budget, is proved, add that paid
long price to(22) at each common endpoint, then take the endpoint
maximum. This gives the complete coefficient budget
O_epsilon(N^(1/2+epsilon)). The common L_bridge^3 factor in the existing
prefix certificate is polylogarithmic and can be absorbed by decreasing
the epsilon used in the coefficient theorem; its original definition
and all source masks remain unchanged.

Within the already stated062 budget-to-core rule,061 paid regions and
058 conditional RH bridge, and only their original endpoint/source
scope, that uniform full budget would discharge the corresponding
arithmetic variation leaf and enable the conditional RH conclusion.
No hypothesis of that bridge is newly proved by the prefactor theorem
alone. In particular (21), the actual moment, is OPEN; the earlier
uniform-prefix/RH goal therefore remains OPEN.

Disposition: the uniform coefficient-prefactor bound (12)-(13), fill
profile classification, beta1 rounding mass and optional1089/1088
constant are proved by finite source algebra and elementary harmonic
bounds. The normalized actual positive-energy hypothesis(21), its more flexible
partition alternative(24)-(27), and the stronger universal-cycle-energy
growth hypothesis are not proved.
