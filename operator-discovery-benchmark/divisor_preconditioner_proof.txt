DIVISOR PRECONDITIONING, LOGARITHMIC ROOTS AND RETAINED SIGNED ARITHMETIC

Scope. The supplied proof methods establish true all-scale signed bounds
of ordinary exponent one for integer logarithmic jets, universal identities,
and an all-index signed support law for positive rational log roots. They do
not establish an RH-strength bound. Historical priority is not a gate.

1. Arithmetic and exact inverse preconditioning
Let u(n)=1, mu*u=delta, and L_a(n)=(log n)^a for n>1, L_a(1)=0, a>0.
Define lambda_a=mu*L_a and T_a(x)=sum_(n<=x)lambda_a(n). These finite point
sums include every term; the producer uses divisor inversion, no prime oracle.
For integer k, the logarithmic derivation Df(n)=log(n)*f(n) obeys Leibniz
under Dirichlet convolution. Consequently lambda_1 is the actual Mangoldt
sequence and lambda_(k+1)=D(lambda_k)+lambda_1*lambda_k. The Bell polynomial
is supplied by this generic differential algebra, not a nominated answer.
An anonymous feature fit may infer the same relation; numeric inference is
not universal proof. The evaluator checks prime-power support independently.

For any h(x), zero on x<1, put (u h)(x)=sum_(n<=x)h(x/n). Finite divisor
rearrangement gives mu(u h)=h for every real x>=1. If
 S_k(x):=sum_(n<=x)log^k(n)=(u h_k)(x)+R_k(x),
then EXACTLY T_k(x)=h_k(x)+sum_(d<=x)mu(d)R_k(x/d).
Only the preconditioned component collapses. The residual still carries the
actual mu signs and the ordinary-zeta denominator; zeta is not erased.

2. A general harmonic approximation grammar
Let gamma_j be Stieltjes constants, defined by
 zeta(1+w)-1/w=sum_(j>=0)(-1)^j*gamma_j*w^j/j!.
Euler summation gives, for every real x>=1,
 sum_(n<=x)log^j(n)/n = log^(j+1)(x)/(j+1)+gamma_j+e_j(x),
 |e_j(x)|<=B_j*(1+log x)^j/x,
 B_j=2*j!*sum_(h=0)^j 1/h!.
Indeed the error from the limiting Euler constant is bounded by f_j(x)
plus integral_x^infinity |f'_j(t)|dt. Expanding the integer incomplete-Gamma
integrals bounds this by 2*j!*sum_(h=0)^j log^h(x)/h!/x. The same argument
handles noninteger x and j=0.

For H_r(x)=sum_(n<=x)log^r(x/n)/n, the binomial expansion therefore yields
 H_r(x)=log^(r+1)(x)/(r+1)
       +sum_(j=0)^r (-1)^j*binom(r,j)*gamma_j*log^(r-j)(x)+E_r(x),
 |E_r(x)|<=C_r*(1+log x)^r/x, C_r=sum_(j=0)^r binom(r,j)*B_j.

The integral polynomial for S_k is
 I_k(t)=sum_(j=0)^k (-1)^(k-j)*k!/j!*t^j,
 |S_k(x)-x*I_k(log x)|<=log^k x+k!.
Choose h_k(x)=x*P_(k-1)(log x)+b for x>=1. Solve descending powers of t
so sum_r p_r[ t^(r+1)/(r+1)+sum_j (-1)^j*binom(r,j)gamma_j*t^(r-j)]+b=I_k(t).
This triangular polynomial completion works for EVERY integer k>=1 and
has leading coefficient k. It does not depend on candidate outcomes.
For example k=2 gives P(t)=2t-2(1+gamma_0); this is known Selberg calibration.
The added constant b removes the last unmatched harmonic constant; its
Mellin pole is at zero and has no effect in the right-half ordinary domain.

The exact retained residual satisfies
 |R_k(y)|<=C_R*(1+log y)^k,
 C_R>=k!+1+|b|+sum_r |p_r| C_r.
The implementation rounds upward with a strict extra guard. This proves
the bound at EVERY real y>=1, not just at fitted or checked scales.

3. A true global signed error, and its limit
Let J_k=sum_(j=0)^k binom(k,j)*j!. A decreasing-integral comparison gives
 sum_(d<=x)(1+log(x/d))^k <= (1+log x)^k+x*J_k <=2*J_k*x.
The last inequality follows termwise from log^j(x)/x<=j! for x>=1.
Applying the exact inverse BEFORE this envelope proves
 |T_k(x)-h_k(x)|<=2*J_k*C_R*x, every real x>=1.
This is a genuine signed actual-prime coupled estimate. It is NOT a bound
for the unit-reference arithmetic and cannot be transferred to that input.
It saves logarithms over an unreduced convolution estimate; its ordinary
power remains one. Reaching square-root scale would require controlling
the SIGNED residual beyond this supplied absolute-envelope method.

4. All-scale arithmetic information in fractional logarithmic roots
Factor n=prod_(i=1)^r p_i^e_i, with distinct actual primes. Write
 b=sum_i(e_i-1)log p_i>=0 and h_i=log p_i>0. Exact Mobius divisor inversion
gives lambda_a(n)=Delta_(h_1)...Delta_(h_r) t^a evaluated at b.
For every real a>0 this equals
 (a)_r * integral_[0,h_1]...integral_[0,h_r](b+u_1+...+u_r)^(a-r)du,
where (a)_r=a(a-1)...(a-r+1). For b=0 the positive integral is finite:
near the corner the volume is O(t^r) and the radial integral is O(t^a).
One may first prove the identity for Re(a)>r, then continue analytically
to Re(a)>0; the integral is locally dominated there. Integer factors that
vanish make the finite difference exactly zero. This includes all prime
powers and all distinct-prime counts, without a finite scale restriction.

Thus for integer a=k, lambda_k(n)>0 when 1<=omega(n)<=k and equals zero
when omega(n)>k. For noninteger a in (m,m+1), it is positive through
omega=m+1 and thereafter alternates with sign (-1)^(omega-m-1).
Square, cube and sixth roots (a=1/2,1/3,1/6) have alternating support over
ALL distinct-prime counts. They are not truncated almost-prime indicators.
This is signed prime-factor information, not a prime-distribution bound.
The detector fits sign signatures anonymously and checks them against this
supplied universal finite-difference certificate. Unsupported fits survive
as numerical leads. A generic sequence cannot inherit the actual-mu premise.

5. Ordinary-zeta information and the correct recovery gate
For Re(s)>1 the integer jet series is (-1)^k*zeta^(k)(s)/zeta(s), and the
summatory Mellin transform has the additional factor 1/s. The completed
h_k removes the main pole at one exactly. At k=1, the logarithmic derivative
has a nonzero pole of residue minus the multiplicity at EVERY ordinary zero.
Any proved cofinal bound with limiting exponent 1/2 for T_1-h_1 would imply
RH. The true supplied bound has exponent one and does not imply RH.
For k>=2 a numerator derivative can mask a simple zero. No generic automatic
gate may assume otherwise: y(z)=z+z^(K+1) is an explicit local counterexample
for all derivative ratios of orders 2 through K at zero. These higher coupled
prime estimates retain arithmetic information, but have missing zero recovery.

Fractional a has a branch at s=1: integral_1^infinity log^a(t)*t^-s dt
=Gamma(a+1)*(s-1)^(-a-1). Dividing by zeta leaves a fractional singularity.
A finite logarithmic polynomial center does not remove that whole branch.
The pilot does NOT pretend integer completion or a first-jet zero gate applies
to a fractional order. A full branch center and a verified ordinary-zero
bridge remain separate tasks. The coefficient sign certificates hold anyway.

6. Provenance and independent scrutiny
Source arithmetic, native constants, generic behavior fits, exact completion
and the retained remainder have separate artifacts. Discovery has no model,
literature, parent outcomes, final-data or prime-oracle access. New final scales
and generic difference inputs are evaluator-owned after archive sealing.
The independent checker factors actual integers, evaluates harmonic constants
by Cauchy contour integrals rather than native zeta Taylor series, independently
solves the triangular identities, and checks complete finite sums and rewrites.
Producer replay is a separate process. Neither finite tests nor a supplied
completion rule are represented as autonomous proof invention or an RH proof.
