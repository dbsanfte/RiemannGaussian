1. Source and exact periodic preconditioner
-------------------------------------------
Let beta=9/2, r=1/3, theta=1-r=2/3, v=2/3,
 K_r(n)=n^-r, mu_v(n)=mu(n)n^-v, nu2=K_r*mu_v.
All stars are DIRICHLET convolution, never pointwise products. The source is

 f=beta*K_r*mu*mu-beta*K_r*mu*mu_v-R*nu2-beta*mu.

For ANY integer q>=2 define
 D_q=delta-q delta_q, a_q=One*D_q.
Then a_q(n)=1-q*1_(q divides n), and its all-real prefix is
 floor(x)-q floor(x/q), in[0,q-1], for x>=0. Its unit is1. Exact complete
Mobius inversion gives mu*a_q=D_q. No prime assumption on q is required.

Preconditioning one copy therefore produces the all-coefficient identity

 a_q*f=beta*D_q*(K_r*mu)-beta*D_q*nu2
        -R*(nu2*a_q)-beta*D_q.                         (1)

Every signed term, finite unit and positive/negative dilation coefficient
survives. The transformation genuinely reduces the largest reciprocal-zeta
power from two to one, rather than replacing the source by a prime profile.

2. Moments and exact source normalization
------------------------------------------
For Re(s)>1 the source and preconditioner have absolutely convergent Euler
identities. At theta, a_q's ordinary conditional moment exists by its bounded
prefix and equals

The identity follows from analytic continuation of the initial equality and
the convergent bounded-prefix Abel integral. It is NOT ordinary convergence
of mu(theta), mu*mu(theta), or the full source's coefficient series.

The shifted inverse moment

nu2*a_q is m_v*a_q(theta), while that of nu2 is m_v. These are moments of the
INNER residual factors, not ordinary critical moments of the pole-bearing
whole kernel operators themselves. The centering coefficient of K_r*mu is
the ANALYTIC value m_mu=1/zeta(theta); ordinary convergence remains unasserted.

Write

 S_va(x)=prefix(nu2*a_q)(x)-m_v*a_q(theta)*x^theta/theta.

The source-defined scalar R_u independently satisfies

The complete main coefficient in(1) is

             -R_u*zeta(theta)/zeta(theta+v)]=0.         (2)

Cancellation must be exact algebra using the separately admitted source

Keeping R merely in[1,5] makes(2) false for a typical family member and would
leave a critical X^(1/3) energy term.

Consequently the normalization-sensitive physical reduction is

 prefix(a_q*f)(x)=beta*[J_mu(x)-q J_mu(x/q)] + Z_q(x),

The finite D_q prefix is1_(x>=1)-q*1_(x>=q). It is never silently omitted.

3. Automatic hyperbola split and the all-real uniform remainder
---------------------------------------------------------------
Let B=q-1. The periodic source has prefix in[0,B] and coefficient magnitude
at most B. Bounded-prefix Abel gives0<=a_q(theta)<=B, including the whole
infinite tail. Fix rational0<alpha<=theta<1; for x>=1 let

 =sum_(d<=U)a_q(d)prefix(K_r)(x/d)
   +sum_(m<=V)K_r(m)[prefix(a_q)(x/m)-prefix(a_q)(U)].

Extra rows with U<=x/m<U+1 are EXACTLY zero. The K main/error bound costs
B U/theta. The complete conditional-moment tail costs
2B(x/U)^theta/theta, and the rectangle costs only
B(x/U)^theta/theta since both periodic prefixes belong to[0,B]. Thus

 |S_(K_r*a_q)(x)|<=B/theta[U+3(x/U)^theta]
 <=7B/theta*x^sigma,
 sigma=max(alpha,theta*(1-alpha)).                      (4)

Here U>=x^alpha/2, 2^theta<=2, and no rounded floating split is used. The
domain alpha<=theta ensures sigma<=theta. For0<x<1 the prefix vanishes and
the full main is bounded by B*x^theta/theta<=7B*x^sigma/theta. Thus(4) holds
for EVERY real x>0, including all quotients x/d below1.

Contracting the shifted inverse is valid ONLY when v+sigma>1:

4. What is now genuinely open, and the RH bridge
------------------------------------------------
Equation(3) is useful progress: a nonlinear source has been reduced to a
linear centered inverse plus an independently paid COMPLETE error. It does
not prove J_mu, nor an all-scale original F energy theorem.

A subpower block energy for D_q*J_mu would imply holomorphy of its Abel
transform in Re(s)>1/2, after the known real-theta main and unit-cell correction
are accounted for. The relevant meromorphic part is

 D_q(s) zeta(s+r)/zeta(s)

minus an explicit real-theta pole subtraction. D_q zeros lie on Re(s)=1;
classical zeta nonvanishing on that line is needed if it is used as a general
single-q bridge. Suppose a nontrivial zero exists with real part>1/2. Let b
be the supremum of their real parts, and choose such a zero rho with
Re(rho)>max(1/2,b-r). Then zeta(rho+r) cannot vanish: its real part exceeds
b, and it is neither a trivial zero nor the real pole. D_q(rho) is nonzero.
Thus the inverse pole survives, contradicting the asserted holomorphy.

This RIGHTMOST-ZERO argument prevents an unsupported assumption that the
shifted numerator never cancels any zero. It suffices that at least one
off-half pole must survive. Real theta is not a zeta zero and its separate
source main cancellation does not affect that argument.

Physical bounded-inverse recovery from a_q is NOT supplied by this bridge.
An original-F energy proof and an RH implication are different result types.
If the root goal is reported as proved via RH, every separately required
RH-to-original-operator implication must also be admitted. In the current
stage J_mu remains OPEN, so neither route pays the persistent full core.
