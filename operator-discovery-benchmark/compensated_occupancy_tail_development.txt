Draft argument to check:

For m variables and integer j>=m, let
 A_(j,m)(q)=sum_(a_i>=1,sum a_i=j) j!/prod a_i! * prod q_i^(a_i).
This is the positive occupancy polynomial already used by the Jordan-series
proof. At sum q_i<=1 its maximum should be at q_i=1/m. A direct argument
groups terms by the total k hitting a chosen pair of variables. The pair's
factor is (q_1+q_2)^k-q_1^k-q_2^k for k>=2, times nonnegative factors from
the remaining variables. Convexity of x^k says averaging q_1,q_2 cannot
decrease this factor. Repeated pair averaging and continuity yield the
uniform maximum, including boundary points. Homogeneity moves sum q to one.

At equal shares the value is the exact rational number
 A_(j,m)=sum_(r=0..m)(-1)^r binomial(m,r)*((m-r)/m)^j.
Its interpretation as the probability that j draws visit all m equally
likely bins gives monotonicity in m: merge two bins, then apply the equal-
share maximum to the resulting m-1 probabilities. Visiting every original
bin implies visiting every merged bin. This supplies an infinite comparison,
not testing only finitely many m or j.

For signed shifts define c_j=sum_i w_i*v_i*(1-v_i)^(j-1). The full stripped
coefficient has the same Jordan multiplier degree by degree. Its absolute
value should therefore satisfy
 |B(n)| <= C_m/g, m=omega(n)>=2, g=gcd(prime exponents),
 C_m=sum_(j>=m)|c_j|*A_(j,m).
The series converges absolutely; each A_(j,m)<=1, and the c_j are finite sums
of geometric sequences with ratios strictly below one. Positivity and the
merge argument imply C_m is nonincreasing in m. The one-prime tail uses the
same series starting at j=2 because the degree-one Jordan multiplier vanishes.

For the two opposite-weight shifts 0<u<v<1, c_1>0 and c_j is eventually
negative. Its sign changes at most once: compare the increasing rational
power ((1-u)/(1-v))^(j-1) with w_v*v/(|w_u|*u). Once a strict negative
degree J is certified, every later coefficient is negative. Equalities at
one degree contribute zero and do not cause an additional sign change.
Thus the infinite sum is an exact rational closed form:
 C_m = -sum_i w_i*U_(v_i)(m)
       +2*sum_(j=m..J-1)c_j*A_(j,m).
The one-prime version subtracts the omitted degree-one coefficient.
U_v(m) is the previously proved atomic equal-share norm. A bounded search
for J must retain a lead if its budget is exhausted, rather than assume a
sign change was found.
