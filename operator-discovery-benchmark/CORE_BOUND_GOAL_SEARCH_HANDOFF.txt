What the detector does itself
----------------------------
The detector already searched supplied inequalities and had two verified
partial component controls. Analysts, including Sol6.1 subagents, supplied
much of the underlying analytic machinery. It has not independently found
the signed cancellation needed for the complete core bound.

The new stage expands the executable search. From the actual open source it
generates intermediate bound targets, enumerates finite linear factors,
tests exact harmonic moments and source matching, generates full auxiliary
identities, and solves symbolic bound requirements. It then changes the next
search task when a proposed proof route cannot meet its requested target.
No model selects a candidate during this run. Mathematical primitives and
the finite search language were supplied by us; this is not a general
analytic prover, nor discovery without design assumptions.

Sol6.1 agents proved/audited the reusable analytic rules and wrote the
independent checker. The executable searches their consequences. A request
for a strong remainder estimate remains OPEN even when the identity reducing
to it is proved. No new paid component bound or complete core control was
obtained in this stage.

Generated constructions and estimates
------------------------------------
With A=mu restricted to[1,U], c=sum_(d<=U)mu(d)/d, C=c*delta,
R=delta-One*A and E=R+c*One, the finite coefficient search finds the
source-matching zero-moment factor T=C-A. Its harmonic moment is exactly0.
This gives the complete identities
 E=delta+One*T,
 E^m=sum_(j=0..m) binom(m,j) One^j*T^j.
Products here are Dirichlet convolutions. The unit and every j remain.

The j-fold harmonic moment has a zero of order at least j. The generated
jet certificates consequently cancel EVERY monomial x*(log x)^r with
0<=r<j, after finite convolution with T^j. No specific coefficients for
an asymptotic divisor polynomial are nominated by the search.

For D_j(x)=sum_(n<=x)One^j(n), define the COMPLETE remainder
Delta_j(x)=D_j(x)-x*P_(j-1)(log x), every real x>0, where P has degree
at most j-1. Moment cancellation gives an exact reduction to
sum_ALL_d T^j(d)Delta_j(n/d). Terms d>n are essential and retained.
For j1, P0=1 and |floor x-x|<=1 give the known head bound3U. The j0
delta contribution is also retained. For j>=2, finding a suitable P and
proving a uniform remainder estimate are new OPEN search obligations.

A hypothetical complete remainder estimate with exponent kappa gives
prefix growth U^(j*(1-kappa))*n^kappa times fixed logarithms, including
the full finite weighted mass cost. This is a conditional implication,
not a theorem establishing such a kappa. Signed terms inside each weighted
remainder remain available for a stronger joint estimate.

The supplied generic E^m hyperbola bound is an unconditional baseline.
For E^2 it gives30U(1+log U)*sqrt(t), every U,t>=1. For higher powers it
gives rho=1-1/m. The machine compares ordinary and Euler-remainder kernel
routes by deriving symbolic minimax solutions from their error lines.
Whole-interval polynomial and convex-dual certificates verify optimality
within this declared error language. All small-prefix cases, conditional
infinite moment tails, finite support and Euler compensators are paid.
The moment rule requires rho<theta; equality is not licensed.

For the current alpha1/24, theta2/3 and finite factor A^2, an auxiliary
bound |P_(E^m)(t)|<=C U^b t^rho times fixed logs would meet the requested
CONSTANT-energy component target under the generated strict conditions
 ordinary: b+10rho<2;
 Euler:    b+13rho<4.
Equality gives only a fixed logarithmic loss, not constant payment here.
These are sufficient regions for this proof method, not necessary
conditions for the actual arithmetic bound. For m2 the existing baseline
produces energy powers2/7 and7/27 respectively, both unpaid.

The substitution b=j*(1-kappa) is automatic. For example the j2 Euler
leaf requires kappa<2/11 at the selected cutoff. Existence of this formal
region is not evidence that the requested divisor estimate is true or
even attainable. These requests still need proof or rejection. No external
divisor theorem or numerical evidence is silently imported.

Feedback actually changes the next task
--------------------------------------
The full signed core remains the first priority. For each generated target,
the compiler checks BOTH proved small-j heads and every conditional
remainder branch. If some portfolio has admissible exponents, it requests
proofs of those complete remainder estimates. Otherwise it redirects the
next task to JOINT WEIGHTED remainder control, retaining the entire signed
sum and actual T^j weights instead of discarding their interaction.

There are22 eligible centered multi-residual targets, powers2 through23.
Twenty have no feasible separate-remainder portfolio for the current
constant-energy target and cutoff. These20 therefore receive joint-control
tasks automatically. The two remaining conditional portfolios are requests,
not endorsements of their arithmetic plausibility. This stage emits and
checks the next tasks; it does not yet implement a general solver for new
joint arithmetic inequalities or assert those tasks have been executed.
