FLOOR CARRIES, ALL-SCALE PRIME INPUTS, AND THE MISSING SIGNED PAIR ESTIMATE

Scope and provenance
--------------------
This stage searches every integer coefficient vector (1,c2,...,c6), with
c2,...,c6 in [-3,3]. It supplies a general periodic-floor certificate template,
not an expected winning coefficient vector. The coefficient producer is the
already verified factor-free lambda=-log_D(mu). The independent evaluator uses
a prime-power sieve. All 228 parent programs are admitted independently of
their previous final outcomes. Prior final cases are development evidence.
The downstream search learns rho, gamma and B in
    C_off(X) <= rho D(X) + B X^gamma, every integer X>=1.
It does not prove that signed premise. The all-scale results proved here are
prime density and diagonal coefficient/Gram bounds, which are unsigned.
The proofs are written mathematics with independently checked exact and ball
certificates. They are not a machine-formalized proof of RH.

1. The factorial trace supplies actual-prime information
-------------------------------------------------------
The inherited universal coefficient theorem identifies lambda(p^k)=1/k and
lambda(n)=0 otherwise. Put Lambda(n)=lambda(n)log(n) and psi(x)=sum_(n<=x)Lambda(n).
Then Lambda>=0, 0<=lambda<=1, and
    sum_(d|m) Lambda(d)=log(m),
so for every integer N>=1,
    sum_(n<=N) Lambda(n) floor(N/n)=log(N!).                 (1)
This follows by exchanging two finite sums; it imports no theorem about
prime distribution. For rational c_d let
    K(u)=sum_d c_d floor(u/d).
If sum_d c_d/d=0, L=lcm(nonzero integer delays) gives K(u+L)=K(u).
For integer delays K is constant on every half-open unit cell [k,k+1).
Thus the full list K(0),...,K(L-1)>=0 is a universal certificate for K(u)>=0
for every real u>=0, including discontinuity endpoints. Normalization c_1=1
and all other delays>=2 give K(u)=1 on [1,2).
Using (1), for every integer N>=1,
    psi(N)-psi(N/2)
      <= sum_(n<=N)Lambda(n)K(N/n)
       = F_c(N):=sum_d c_d log(floor(N/d)!).                (2)

Write f(y)=y log(y)-y, f(0)=0. The elementary integral comparison for log(t)
gives, for every integer m>=1,
    1 <= log(m!)-f(m) <= 1+log(m).
For y=N/d>=1 and m=floor(y), 0<=f(y)-f(m)<=log(N).
For 0<=y<1, |f(y)|<=1 and m=0. In either case
    |log(floor(N/d)!)-f(N/d)| <= 1+log(N).
With S=sum|c_d| and H=-sum c_d log(d)/d, exact zero drift cancels the NlogN-N
terms and gives
    F_c(N) <= H N + S(1+log N).                            (3)
The native certificate verifies H>0. Iterating (2),(3) at N=2^j yields
    psi(2^k) <= H(2^(k+1)-2) + S[k+log(2)k(k+1)/2].
For 2^(k-1)<x<=2^k this implies
    psi(x)/x <= 4H+2S[k+log(2)k(k+1)/2]/2^k.               (4)
The sequences k/2^k and k(k+1)/2^k decrease for k>=2 (the second is equal at
k=2,3). Choose a frozen K>=4. The right side at k=K covers x>2^(K-1).
The complete finite prefix n<=2^(K-1) covers smaller real x because psi is
constant between successive integers and psi(x)/x decreases there.
An outward-rounded rational C_psi majorizing this finite maximum and (4)
therefore proves psi(x)<=C_psi x for EVERY real x>=1.
This is elementary prime information over all scales, recovered by the floor
certificate search. It is a Chebyshev-level density statement, not a signed
prime-error bound or evidence of historical novelty.

2. Signed discrepancy increments have a diagonal logarithmic saving
------------------------------------------------------------------
Use li(x)=Ei(log x) for x>1, with derivative 1/log x. For integer n>=2 put
    E_beta(n)=li(n)-beta sum_(m<n)lambda(m),
and E_beta(0)=E_beta(1)=0. Let U_beta be its continuous linear interpolation,
zero on [0,1]. Its increments a_1=0, a_2=li(2), and
    a_(n+1)=delta_n=r_n-beta lambda(n),
    r_n=li(n+1)-li(n), n>=2,
retain the signed arithmetic prime-power deficit. Here 0<r_n<=1/log n.
For integer N>=2, splitting at sqrt(N) gives
    sum_(2<=n<=N)lambda(n) <= sqrt(N)+2 C_psi N/log N,
    sum_(2<=n<=N)r_n^2 <= sqrt(N)/log(2)^2+4N/log(N)^2.
Use lambda^2<=lambda and (r-beta lambda)^2<=2r^2+2beta^2lambda^2. Including
a_2, and enlarging the shifted prefix n<=N-1 to n<=N, proves
    sum_(n<=N)a_n^2 <= li(2)^2
       +2sqrt(N)(1/log(2)^2+beta^2)
       +8N/log(N)^2+4beta^2 C_psi N/log N.                  (5)
After multiplying by log(e+N)/N, the majorant is
    T_beta(N)=li(2)^2 log(e+N)/N
       +2(1/log(2)^2+beta^2)log(e+N)/sqrt(N)
       +8log(e+N)/log(N)^2+4beta^2 C_psi log(e+N)/log N.
Each summand decreases for real N>=8. Indeed log(e+N)/N decreases; for the
square-root term, 2N/(e+N)<2<log(e+N) at N>=8; and
    log(e+N)/log N = 1+log(1+e/N)/log N
decreases for N>1, as does its product with 1/log N.
A complete finite prefix through a frozen N0>=8 and the single native ball
T_beta(N0) thus certify, with an outward-rounded rational C_a,
    sum_(n<=N)a_n^2 <= C_a N/log(e+N), EVERY integer N>=1.   (6)
The search can compare several proof cutoffs without using final data.
The positive-support controls are not actual-prime sequences: (4)-(6) are
never applied to those controls.

3. Exact diagonal and signed off-diagonal observables
-----------------------------------------------------
For n>=1 let phi_n(u)=clamp(u-(n-1),0,1). Then U_beta(u)=sum a_n phi_n(u),
a finite sum at every u. For a canonical filter with distinct rational delays
d>=1, c_1=1, and S=sum|c_d|, define
    T(t)=sum_d c_d U_beta(t/d),
    b_n(t)=sum_d c_d phi_n(t/d),
    A2(X)=integral_X^(2X) T(t)^2 dt,
    K_nm(X)=integral_X^(2X)b_n(t)b_m(t)dt,
    D(X)=sum_n a_n^2 K_nn(X),
    C_off(X)=A2(X)-D(X)=sum_(n!=m)a_n a_m K_nm(X).         (7)
D>=0. Off-diagonal terms keep signs from both the arithmetic increments and
the filter. In each complete window all breakpoints d*n are rational and
T is affine on each cell. Polynomial antiderivatives evaluate A2 exactly
with native ball coefficients. The independent evaluator uses Simpson's
rule on every such cell, which is exact for these quadratic polynomials.
It computes D from separate per-increment ramp cells, not from the producer's
prefix shortcut. This is complete-window integration, not sampling.

The producer shortcut uses, for p=1,2, n=floor(t/d), r=t-dn,
    integral_0^t sum_m a_m^2 phi_m(u/d)^p du
      = t sum_(m<=n)a_m^2-d sum_(m<=n)m a_m^2
        +d/(p+1)sum_(m<=n)a_m^2+a_(n+1)^2 r^(p+1)/[(p+1)d^p].
For d<e and m>=ceil(e/(e-d)), phi_m(u/d)=1 whenever phi_m(u/e)>0.
Their product is the slower ramp. A finite correction below that cutoff
therefore suffices for all cross-delay contributions to each K_mm.
Since |b_n|<=S and every supported n<=ceil(2X)<=3X when X>=1, (6) gives
    D(X) <= 3 C_a S^2 X^2/log(e+X), EVERY real X>=1.        (8)
This proof uses actual prime weights at all scales. It does not bound
C_off: positivity and coefficient density alone do not give signed
cancellation between different increments.

4. The learned signed premise and its ordinary-zeta obstruction
---------------------------------------------------------------
For fixed rational rho, gamma>=0, B>=0, the learned premise is
    C_off(X)<=rho D(X)+B X^gamma, every INTEGER X>=1.        (9)
For rho>-1, (7),(8) give A2(X)=O(X^2/log(e+X)+X^gamma).
For rho<=-1, the nonpositive diagonal contribution can be dropped, giving
A2(X)<=B X^gamma. Holder on dyadic windows makes the Mellin integral of T
holomorphic for Re(s)>eta, where
    eta=max(1/2,(gamma-1)/2) if rho>-1,
    eta=max(0,(gamma-1)/2)   if rho<=-1.                   (10)
Only integer dyadic windows are needed for that convergence; fresh irregular
integer windows remain additional necessary tests of the stated premise.

Compare U_beta with the inherited prime-error function
    R_beta(t)=li(t)-beta sum_(n<t)lambda(n), t>=2,
zero below 2. At integer t>=2 they agree. On (N,N+1), N>=2,
    R_beta(t)-U_beta(t)
      =li(t)-li(N)-(t-N)(li(N+1)-li(N))
        -beta(1-(t-N))lambda(N).
The linear-interpolation remainder of li has modulus at most
1/(8N log(N)^2)<1, since |li''(t)|=1/(t log(t)^2).
On 1<=t<2, |U_beta(t)|<=li(2)<2. Thus
    |R_beta(t)-U_beta(t)|<=2+|beta|, every real t>=1.
The corresponding filtered difference is bounded by (2+|beta|)S and its
Mellin integral is holomorphic for Re(s)>0. It cannot change the ordinary
zero obstructions in the inherited logarithmic bridge.

For completeness, the inherited Mellin formula has
    s H_R(s)/D_filter(s)+s B0(s)=-log(s-1)-beta log(zeta(s))
initially on Re(s)>1, with B0 holomorphic for Re(s)>0. A finite filter whose
unit-delay coefficient is one has an entire D_filter not identically zero.
Even where it vanishes, the quotient of two holomorphic functions is
meromorphic, and the derivative of a meromorphic function has zero residue.
The right derivative -1/(s-1)-beta zeta'/zeta has residue -beta*m at every
ordinary zero of multiplicity m, and beta-1 at the pole s=1.
Consequently beta!=1 and eta<1 is impossible due to the pole logarithm.
For beta=1 and eta<1/2 it is impossible due to the independently enclosed
actual critical-line zero. For beta=1 and eta<=1/2, a VALID UNIFORM (9)
would exclude all zeros with Re(s)>1/2; functional-equation symmetry gives RH.
Isolated filter-multiplier zeros do not need to be excluded.
An impossible stronger premise can be logically RH-sufficient by implication;
the analytic status rejects it rather than treating finite success as evidence.

5. What the search and audit establish
--------------------------------------
The selector learns rho by regression, then learns positive-residual powers,
and also explores generic rational coefficient and exponent grids. Every
candidate is frozen before discovery-validation; the archive is sealed before
fresh operating-system-random final cases. Discovery cannot read private final
files, network, or subprocesses. Rejected carries and all estimate candidates
are retained. Replay checks deterministic selection; independent mathematics
checks prime support, full period certificates, factorial identities, complete
finite proof patches, tail constants, complete windows, diagonal decomposition,
transfer domains, actual-zero and pole obstructions, and support controls.
If a final bound fails, it is retained as a counterexample; if it passes, it
remains a conjectural signed premise. The independently verified all-scale
prime and diagonal bounds are recorded separately. No statement about their
historical novelty is required. No RH-strength signed estimate, and no RH
proof, is established by finite-window success.
