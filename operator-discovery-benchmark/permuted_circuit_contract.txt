Permuted arithmetic circuit discovery v1 -- prospective new structural study
2026-10-09. Earlier studies and snapshots remain immutable.

Purpose and required gate
Extend candidate discovery beyond divisor-triangular observers by interleaving
reversible integer-coordinate maps with fractional full divisor convolutions.
The input is still the complete prefix M(n), not a transformed coefficient source.
One fixed circuit must ultimately have cofinal subpower full dyadic moments.
Its proved inverse recovers original critical weighted M energy. No finite test,
operator inverse, envelope ratio or structural novelty proves that moment gate.

Program: {"stages":[stage,...]}, at most7 stages, at most3 convolutions,3 signs,
3 permutations and2 shears. Existing convolve/sign stages have exactly the v1 semantics
and phase/index grammar in reversible_divisor_circuit_contract.txt, including
fixed original source queries in every stage and its inverse. New stage:
 {"op":"permute", "map":map_descriptor, "inverse":strict boolean}
Also admit a same-block mixing stage with exact keys:
 {"op":"shear", "coefficient":canonical rational c, "phase":old_unit_phase,
  "direction":"even_from_odd"|"odd_from_even"}
Here c is nonzero, |c|<=2, denominator<=6. For every even n>=2 the pair
(n,n+1) lies in one complete dyadic block. Evaluate phi(n) at the even n,
from the same original source. even_from_odd replaces f(n) by
f(n)+c*phi(n)*f(n+1); odd_from_even replaces f(n+1) by
f(n+1)+c*phi(n)*f(n). The other coordinate and the singleton n=1 remain.
The inverse negates c, retaining direction, phase, and fixed source. Thus both
coordinates are retained: no discarded complement or rank-deficient difference.

Dyadic index conventions
For n>=1 uniquely n=m+r, m=2^k,0<=r<m. Every map stays within this SAME block.
All evaluators require a CLOSED positive horizon H=2^K-1, H<=131071, for every
program (even baseline); values[0]=0 and values[1..H] are strict integers in
{-1,0,1}. No clipping, sparse source, or unproved zero padding. Index0 stays0.
All prime factorization and complete source oracles remain explicit.

Map descriptors (exact keys, total at most15 map nodes, depth<=3):
 {"kind":"polynomial", "coefficients":[b,a,c2,...,cJ]}
    List length2..5; each strict integer -8..8; a odd; each c_j,j>=2 even.
    r maps to sum_j c_j*r^j mod m. The integer difference factor is odd, so
    this is a bijection modulo EVERY2^k. Inverse by binary lifting or a separate
    exact complete permutation table; no floating inverse or missing residues.
 {"kind":"xor", "mask":"all"|"top"|"alternating"}
    all=m-1; top=0 if k0 else2^(k-1); alternating=sum_{0<=j<k,j even}2^j.
 {"kind":"reverse_bits"}
    Reverse exactly k low bits, including leading zero bits.
 {"kind":"rotate_bits", "shift":strict integer -4..4, nonzero}
    Rotate k bits to the LEFT by shift modulo k; k0 is the identity.
 {"kind":"compose", "maps":[map1,map2,...]}
    Length2..3, apply index map1 THEN map2, etc. Inverse reverses this order
    and inverts each component. Every component still sees the same block.
 {"kind":"choose", "phase":old_unit_phase, "positive":map1, "negative":map2}
    Evaluate phase at the ORIGINAL BLOCK BASE m, using the SAME fixed source
    values, and use that one branch on every coordinate in the block. The
    inverse uses the same branch. A coordinate-dependent branch is inadmissible.
All polynomial evaluation/modular steps, branch queries, index loops and
residency are charged. Internal map caches use exact payload equality/tuples,
not content hashes. Source-dependent choices cannot read intermediate states.

Permutation stage semantics: output[n]=input[P(n)] for forward direction,
output[n]=input[P^-1(n)] for inverse. Composition describes INDEX maps, so
it must not silently reverse this explicit pullback convention. The whole
circuit inverse reverses its stages, negates each convolution alpha, leaves
each sign unchanged, and toggles each permutation's inverse boolean.
For shear stages it negates coefficient as described above.

Recovery certificate exact schema:
 {"schema":"permuted-arithmetic-circuit-v1", "program":program,
  "fractional_order":canonical rational sum|alpha|,
  "recovery_power":ceil(sum|alpha|), "permutation_count":number_of_perm_stages,
  "shear_count":number_of_shear_stages,
  "shear_factor":canonical rational product(1+2*abs(c)),
  "domain":"closed_dyadic_horizon_weighted_l2_half_lt_sigma_le_one",
  "bound":"3^permutation_count*shear_factor*(1+1/epsilon)^recovery_power",
  "coefficient_binding":"same_fixed_source_and_block_branches_in_inverse"}
This is an UNSQUARED bound; squared energy multiplies by9^permutation_count
and shear_factor^2 and uses exponent2*recovery_power. It follows from the fractional norm of EACH
convolution and norm<=2^(1+epsilon)<3 for each permutation,0<epsilon<=1/2.
The off-diagonal part of a shear has weighted norm at most
|c|*(3/2)^(1+epsilon)<2|c| (and at most |c| in the other direction),
so both shear and inverse have norm<=1+2|c|. No fitted constant is used.
The sum-fractional-order rule survives as a PRODUCT OF NORMS even though a
single divisor-supported path kernel generally does not survive permutation.
Closed horizons exhaust all n; the previous cofinal moment/Mellin/RH bridge
therefore applies. The full finite head1..31 is retained.

Exact normalizer (proof annotations, no responses)
Normalize until unchanged using these rules in this order while scanning:
 - remove a polynomial map [0,1,0,...,0] stage (this is the only explicit
   identity descriptor recognized by this version);
 - remove adjacent opposite-direction permutations with identical descriptor;
 - remove adjacent identical unit sign stages;
 - merge adjacent convolution stages with identical prime_phase and sum alpha
   within allowed [-2,2] denominator<=6; a zero sum removes both;
 - merge adjacent shear stages with identical direction and phase and summed
   coefficient within allowed [-2,2] denominator<=6; a zero sum removes both;
 - remove terminal sign stages;
 - remove terminal permutation stages, with alias domain DYADIC MOMENTS ONLY.
Do not erase an intervening permutation/sign or assume phases multiplicative.
Normalizer result {"program":normalized, "rewrites":[{"rule":string,
"position":int,"before":[stages],"after":[stages]}],
"equivalence":"dyadic_moments"}. Original raw programs remain archived.
All finite diagnostics evaluate the normalized program; irregular blocks need
not agree with a raw word whose final permutation was removed. Independent
checker must reconstruct the normalizer; signatures are full payloads, no hash.

Numerical cancellation diagnostic, beyond a mere finite pass
Evaluate signed state starting at M. In parallel evaluate a complete absolute
PATH ENVELOPE starting at |M|: signs do nothing; permutations permute the
envelope; convolutions use absolute kernel coefficients. Thus |output|<=envelope
pointwise and every actual mixed term remains in the signed output square.
For a shear, the target envelope is incremented by |c| times the other
coordinate's envelope; both coordinates are retained. An inverse pair removed
by the normalizer supplies no artificial path-cancellation credit.
Return evaluate(program,values,meter)->{"signed":list,"absolute":list}.
moments(state,scales,meter) returns [{"X":int,"energy":canonical rational,
"absolute_energy":canonical rational}], exact full [X,2X) blocks. Energy /
absolute_energy measures retained path cancellation; if bothzero the convention
is1 (an inactive expression is not evidence). It is not a global bound or
proof of additional prime information. Normalize exact aliases BEFORE this
diagnostic, so an explicit inverse pair is not rewarded for canceling itself.
initial_cost(state,meter) uses the signed sum_{n<32}output(n)^2/n^2.

Research discipline
The prospective protocol must freeze full grammar/catalogue, generation rules,
source/scale partitions, diagnostics, selection criteria, and quotas before any
actual candidate responses. Candidate generation has no model calls, numerical
optimizer, fitted operator coefficients or access to final data. Discovery
controls may inform a declared cancellation-specificity criterion; final data
are excluded from all adaptation. Overlap with earlier research is disclosed.
All raw and normalized candidates, aliases/rejections and missing cases stay
in the archive. Preserve multiple structural families, and report baseline
comparison alongside every finite pass. A missing proof is not a refutation.
