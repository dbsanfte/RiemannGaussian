Positive diagonal metric and exact projected step
The objective remains unpenalized nonnegative least squares on the same
licensed302-row,687-column positively scaled separator equations,
  f(x)=1/2*||A*x-b||_2^2, x>=0.
Use a fixed positive diagonal D and the metric ||v||_D^2=sum_j D_j*v_j^2.
For g=A^T*(A*y-b) and L>0, minimizing
  g^T*(z-y)+(L/2)*||z-y||_D^2 over z>=0
separates by coordinate and gives
  z_j=max(0,y_j-g_j/(L*D_j)).
This is the proposed update in exact real arithmetic. Positive diagonal
weighting does not alter the componentwise projection onto the nonnegative
orthant. Extrapolated y remains unrestricted, while projected z and every
accepted x remain nonnegative. Clamping y would change the frozen method.

The transformation w=D^(1/2)*x yields the equivalent NNLS problem with
matrix B=A*D^(-1/2). Its ordinary Euclidean projected-gradient step is the
diagonal-metric step above. Exact positive D preserves strict support and
nonnegative feasibility. The proposed D_j=||A_j||_2^2 gives unit-norm
columns of B in exact arithmetic and rescales column directions without a
Gram matrix or new generator catalogue. Floating D is used only to propose
a trajectory; its entries are not exact mathematical premises of admission.

Directional line test and carried step factor
For d=z-y the unchanged quadratic identity is
  f(z)=f(y)+g^T*d+1/2*||A*d||_2^2.
Thus ||A*d||_2^2<=L*||d||_D^2 is exactly the diagonal quadratic majorization
condition along the current trial direction. In transformed coordinates it
is the033 directional test for B and D^(1/2)*d. L starts at exactly1 and
only doubles after rejected trials, carrying its accepted value forward.
Metric D stays fixed throughout; updating it per step would be a different
method and would change the interpretation of the recorded L sequence.
