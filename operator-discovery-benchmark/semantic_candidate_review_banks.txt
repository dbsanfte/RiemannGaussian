Factor-bank identities and retained complements
For every R<=t,
 C_R(t)=sum_{n<=t} sum_{d|n,d<=R}mu(d)mu(n/d).
The full bank S(t)=C_t(t) is the prefix of mu*mu, not the Mobius inverse identity
sum_d mu(d)floor(t/d)=1. Its prime-power coefficients are1,-2,1,0,... at
p^0,p^1,p^2,p^3,... . That exact multiplicative description does not bound its
signed prefix. Any full-bank rewrite retains sum_{R<d<=t}mu(d)M(N_d).

For R^2<=t, generalized Dirichlet hyperbola gives
 S(t)=2C_R(t)-M(R)^2+T_R(t),
 T_R=sum_{a>R,b>R,ab<=t}mu(a)mu(b).
Thus C_R=[S+M(R)^2-T_R]/2 exactly. The large/large complement is absent only
when R=floor(sqrt(t)); the candidates use floor(X^(1/r)), not that t-dependent
full hyperbola cutoff. Even r=2 has a generally nonempty complement across its
block. An absolute bound counts the large/large pairs and can be of order t;
it is not an all-scale subpower signed estimate.

The difference banks also have the exact rectangle-minus-hyperbola form
 M(R)M(t)-C_R(t)
 =sum_{d<=R,n<=t,d*n>t}mu(d)mu(n)
 =sum_{t/R<n<=t}mu(n)[M(R)-M(floor(t/n))].
The d=1 summand vanishes. This is a genuine complementary bilinear region, not
a theorem that its signed mass is small. The prefactor M(R) multiplying M(t)
cannot be bounded independently by assuming the desired Mertens estimate.

For ID23, fix the output parameter t and put mu_t(a)=mu(a)1_{(a,t)=1}.
Its full-domain prefix is sum_{ab<=t}mu_t(a)mu(b). At primes dividing t its
local coefficients are those of mu; at primes not dividing t they are those
of mu*mu. It is not the inverse identity. The truncated state retains its
a>R tail. Its asymmetric hyperbola identity is
 full=CoprimeBank_R+sum_{b<=R}mu(b)M_t(floor(t/b))
      -M_t(R)M(R)+sum_{a>R,b>R,ab<=t}mu_t(a)mu(b),
where M_t(u)=sum_{a<=u}mu_t(a). The second small-variable bank is different
from the first. Treating them as symmetric would drop a source-domain cost.

No multiplicativity rule collapses mu(d)mu(floor(t/d)) to mu(t) generally.
Such a rule needs d|t and gcd(d,t/d)=1, besides the exact factorization. The
gate gcd(d,t)=1 in ID23 actually excludes every divisor d>1 of t. Likewise
p not dividing t in ID32 is not a claim that p is coprime to floor(t/p).

Internal point factors: proved component bounds, unresolved interaction
For every n>=1 and 0<=theta<=1,
 M(n)-mu(n)B_theta(n)
 =sum_{floor(n^theta)<a<=n}mu(a)+(1-mu(n))M(floor(n^theta)).
For the half-root states, a positive Mobius point removes the initial root
prefix, zero leaves it unchanged, and a negative point doubles that prefix.
This is source-conditioned subtraction, not an independent random sign.
Its square contains M(n)^2-2mu(n)M(n)B_theta(n)+mu(n)^2 B_theta(n)^2.
Every pair of distinct d or p adds its corresponding full mixed product.

Using only |mu|<=1 and |M(n)|<=n, for 0<=theta<1,
 |P_{R,theta}|,|Q_{R,theta}|
 <=t^theta sum_{d<=R}d^-theta
 <=t^theta R^(1-theta)/(1-theta).
Consequently their normalized square is bounded by
 2^(2theta)/(1-theta)^2 * X^(2theta-1)R^(2-2theta).
At theta=1/2 the exact sum of t over the block improves this to6R.
At theta=1/3,r=3 this gives a constant times X^(1/9).
Thus selected29 has a root-correction envelope6floor(X^(1/6)); this is a fixed
positive-power loss, not forcing admissible for every cofinally large q.

If U=A-Q, the exact norm identity is V_U=V_A-2<A,Q>+V_Q, with block inner
product normalized by X^2. Cauchy-Schwarz gives |<A,Q>|<=sqrt(V_A V_Q), or
epsilon V_A+epsilon^-1 V_Q for twice the absolute mixed term. This consumes
main-bank energy credit; it does not create credit or establish a signed drift.
Selected29 therefore needs an estimate for the main prime bank and its signed
correlation with the quotient-point/root bank, not only a bound on the latter.

Prime-bank identities
Define kappa_R(n)=pi(min(R,floor(t/n))) for fixed t. Exact summation interchange
gives A_R(t)=sum_{n<=t}mu(n)kappa_R(n). Its divisor-convolution coefficient is
sum_{p|n,p<=R}mu(n/p). For squarefree n this is -omega_R(n)mu(n). For n=p^2 a
with a squarefree and p not dividing a, it is1_{p<=R}mu(pa); with any higher
prime exponent or two squared primes it is zero. Prime cutoff truncation and
all corresponding tails remain. Unweighted prime banks do not become a
logarithmically weighted von-Mangoldt identity by changing notation.

H_R(t)=sum_{n>=1}mu(n)[kappa_R(n)-kappa_R(n+1)]. It is not the output-step
derivative of A_R: that derivative is sum_{p<=R,p|t}mu(t/p).
In selected30, exact expansion is
 U^2=L^2 H_R^2+mu(t)^2 A_R^2-2mu(t)L H_R A_R.
All H-pair, A-pair and H/A pair correlations remain. On a dyadic parent X>=2,
V_L=E(X/2)/2; |H_R|<=pi(R) gives only
 V_U<=pi(R)^2 E(X/2)+2V_A.
The moving pi(R)^2 factor is not a uniform contraction. An induction that
silently inserts a subpower lower-scale bound and then ignores this multiplier
does not close. A signed compensation or a genuinely bounded transfer kernel
is needed. Divisibility/coprimality identities apply only on their exact cases;
off a divisor residue, the floor quotient is not a factor of t.

Selected31: exact source gap and useful lower-scale root transfer
For every admitted prime p<=R=floor(X^(1/3)) and t>=X, N_p>=floor(t^(1/3)).
Therefore its subtraction removes the common initial source segment exactly:
 U31=sum_{n>floor(t^(1/3))}mu(n)kappa_R(n).
The state is a positively weighted signed source tail. This is not a bound on
that tail or proof that its signs are independent. The original square still
equals V_A-2pi(R)<A,B_{1/3}>+pi(R)^2 V_{B_{1/3}}.

There is a noncircular component-energy estimate stronger than the trivial
positive-power envelope. For R>=2, all n=floor(t^(1/3)) in the block lie in
[R,2R). The exact fiber multiplicity is
 c_n=#{t in [X,2X):n^3<=t<(n+1)^3}<=3n^2+3n+1<=12R^2.
Since X>=R^3, the collective root component P=pi(R)B_{1/3} satisfies
 V_P<=12[pi(R)/R]^2 E(R).
No cancellation bound on M has been assumed: the right-hand side is explicitly
the smaller-scale unknown energy. If Y is the largest dyadic integer<=R,
 E(R)<=E(Y)+4E(2Y), by covering [R,2R) with [Y,2Y) and [2Y,4Y).
For the nonexceptional scales these are strictly smaller than X.

The coefficient pi(R)/R tends to zero by an elementary source-free argument.
For any fixed N let P be the product of primes<=N. Except these finitely many
primes, primes<=R occupy coprime residue classes modulo P, so
 limsup_R pi(R)/R<=phi(P)/P=product_{p<=N}(1-1/p).
The reciprocal product's geometric expansion contains every integer<=N;
hence this product<=1/(sum_{n<=N}1/n), which tends to zero. This establishes
a vanishing coefficient in the component transfer without PNT or RH.

This is a possible ingredient in a genuine smaller-scale induction. It does
not bound the main prime tail, its covariance with the root term, or the full
Delta(E+V). Those remaining terms cannot be replaced by the desired energy
estimate without an explicit closed induction and initial-scale costs.

ID32: bounded collective padding and quotient-energy increments
Its collective root component nu_R(t)B_{1/6}(t) has uniformly bounded
normalized square<=2: nu_R<=R<=X^(1/3) and B_{1/6}^2<=t^(1/3)<=2X^(1/3).
This bank-level bound is unconditional despite the growing number of primes.
The square of G_R plus this component still contains their unresolved mixed
term. A bounded component square does not make that mixed term bounded.

For every n>=1 the exact identity
 mu(n)M(n)=[M(n)^2-M(n-1)^2+mu(n)^2]/2
rewrites each quotient point/prefix component of G_R as an energy increment
plus a squarefree gate. For a fixed p and interval I=[a,b], put
 c_n=#{t in I:np+1<=t<=np+p-1}.
Then sum_{t in I,p not dividing t}mu(N_p)M(N_p)
 =1/2 sum_n c_n[M(n)^2-M(n-1)^2+mu(n)^2].
Interior c_n=p-1; the first and last weights retain the precise incomplete
residue-block endpoints. Weighted summation by parts retains both endpoint
energies and differences c_n-c_{n+1}. This gives an exact linear telescoping
identity, not a telescoping identity for V_G, which squares the coupled bank
and retains higher correlations and all prime pairs.

Selected53: prime-row cancellation, mask jumps and uniform component cost
Let G=G_m(t), B=B_{1/3}(t). When R>=2 the p=2 summand is present, and
 U53=(1-G)L+sum_{3<=p<=R}M(N_p)-pi(R)B.
On G=1 the p=2 lag cancels exactly. Its root subtraction remains: deleting a
gated prime row outright would incorrectly lose an unmatched -B contribution.
The original three-piece square is
 A_R^2+pi(R)^2 B^2+G L^2-2pi(R)A_R B-2G A_R L+2pi(R)G B L.
No term has an independent sign/correlation premise. At R=1 the bank is empty
and the actual formula is -G_2 L, not the R>=2 rearrangement. W(1)=1 must stay.

For one parent-child step let R'=floor((2X)^(1/3)), m'=max(2,R'),
 J=sum_{R<p<=R',prime}[M(N_p)-B], deltaG=G_{m'}-G_m.
On the child block, relative to the valid parent-parameter extension,
 U_child=U_old+J-deltaG L.
The complete boundary correction to its square is
 2U_old J+J^2-2deltaG U_old L-2deltaG J L+(deltaG)^2 L^2.
It includes old/new prime correlations, new-prime/mask correlations and both
gained and lost coprime sets. Equal radicals give deltaG=0. Nonnested radicals
can give both signs; a composite cutoff jump can alter the gate without adding
a prime to the bank. These selectors require separate boundary accounting.

The mask-jump component has the useful unconditional bound
 (4X^2)^-1 sum_child (deltaG)^2 M(floor(t/2))^2<=E(X)/2,
because every n in [X,2X) occurs twice. This is a uniform parent-energy cost,
not a modulus-dependent constant. Its cross terms require corresponding bank
credit; Cauchy-Schwarz cannot discharge them without consuming that credit.
The collective root term inherits the smaller-scale estimate for ID31.

Full drift and the precise unresolved gate
For every pure bank in this memo, its summand functions depend on t while X
enters through R. If U_R is extended to the entire child block using the same
parent cutoff, and J=U_{R'}-U_R, then exactly
 DeltaV=T_R+(4X^2)^-1 sum_child[2U_R J+J^2],
 T_R=(4X^2)^-1 sum_child U_R(t)^2-X^-2 sum_parent U_R(t)^2.
For53 replace J by J-deltaG L as above. Frozen-cutoff transport is not zero.
It changes quotient arguments and root-point source coordinates throughout
both child halves; controlling only new primes misses this cost.

One concrete sufficient analytic gate is to produce a nonnegative credit A_X
such that, uniformly in dyadic X, DeltaE+T_R<=-A_X+F_q(X), while the complete
boundary correction<=A_X+G_q(X), with F_q+G_q bounded by Cq ceil(X^(1/q)) for
every cofinally large q and finite Cq independent of X. A closed lower-scale
energy induction may discharge some root and mask costs using the identities
above, provided its transfer coefficients, initial W(1), endpoints and forcing
are all explicit. It must still handle the main coupled bank and signed mixed
correlations. No such closing lemma was proved in this review.

Proved ingredients: complementary convolution/hyperbola identities; source-gap
rewriting and exact gated p=2 cancellation; elementary point/root envelopes;
vanishing-coefficient lower-scale transfer for31/53 root padding; uniform
bounded root component and quotient-energy-increment identity for32; uniform
parent-energy mask-jump bound for53. These materially identify possible credit
and missing correlations. They neither certify a new all-scale function nor
close the signed target. All initial values and exceptional cutoff regimes
remain part of any eventual proof, rather than being hidden in empirical fit.
