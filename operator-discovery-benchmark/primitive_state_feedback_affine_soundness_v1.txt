Projection and cones
For each signed relation, beta=x_plus-x_minus and column_minus=-column_plus.
For the three original matrices, define C=Gc+K and P=Gp-4K. Since the storage
column is A_K=A_child-4*A_parent, the matrix contribution is exactly
A_child(C)+A_parent(P). Define H=P+4C=Gp+4Gc. If Gc,Gp,K are PSD then C,H
are PSD. Conversely C,H PSD lift as Gc=0,K=C,Gp=H. Signed coefficients lift
as x_plus=max(beta,0),x_minus=max(-beta,0). Squares and five slacks retain their
values. Thus the correct projected cone is C PSD and P+4C PSD, not P PSD.
The quotient removes representation freedoms, not forced faces or equations.
It changes storage boundary cost/objective, so a later uniform proof must check
actual initial storage cost for the chosen lift separately.

A new original-coordinate necessary row can be projected by retaining its
positive signed coefficients and its child/parent coefficients precisely when
row_minus=-row_plus and row_K=row_child-4*row_parent hold entrywise. Offdiagonal
factor2 already belongs to each original column; no additional rescaling occurs
here. The canonical lift then obeys exactly the same row. Checking these row
identities is mandatory: an arbitrary row from1957 coordinates does not define
a well-defined functional on the quotient. Its RHS is unchanged by projection.
These are coefficient identities; no square sign or PSD feasibility follows.

Exact elimination and restoration
Let old normalized equations be x_p+sum_{j>p} B_pj*x_j=b_p. Their insertion
order may differ from pivot-column order. To reduce a new equation a*x=r,
process EVERY old pivot p in ASCENDING column order, replacing
(a,r) by (a-a_p*B_p,r-a_p*b_p). Never stop when the smallest remaining index
is an old free coordinate: later old pivots can still occur. Ascending order
prevents an eliminated old pivot being reintroduced. Source insertion order
is insufficient. This operation preserves the new equation modulo old ones.
After elimination all old pivot coefficients must vanish exactly.

If all variable coefficients vanish and reduced RHS iszero, the row is
redundant. If all variable coefficients vanish and RHS isnonzero, it contradicts
the licensed old affine equations over Q. This is an affine inconsistency,
not generic cone infeasibility or an RH obstruction. Otherwise choose the
SMALLEST nonzero OLD-FREE coordinate q as the new pivot and divide the full
row and RHS by its coefficient. The augmented RHS coordinate is never a
variable or eligible pivot. Rank rises byone and old-free dimension falls
byone. The old rows need not be rewritten because the appended row has only
free coefficients and is upper triangular with pivot q.

Back-substitute the combined old and new equations in DESCENDING pivot-column
order. Retain the seed value at EVERY other old-free coordinate; recompute the
new pivot and old pivot coordinates exactly. For a redundant row the original
free coordinates are unchanged and ordinary old restoration suffices. A
contradictory row has no repaired endpoint. Missing/implicit coordinates,
truncated coefficient support and approximate row tests are forbidden.
Verify all complete original equations and the independently licensed new row
on the canonical original1957-coordinate lift, not merely the selected basis.
Exact restoration does not imply cone admission; square signs, all slacks,
