MOBIUS PREFIX SMALL-HORIZON REPAIR REVIEW 063
===========================================

Scope and disposition
---------------------
This is a source-only mathematical review of the062 handoff, report and
stopped062 review. No source table, optimizer, scientific bound, norm,
integral, candidate enumeration or test was evaluated. One saved report
JSON was decoded; the first read attempt used an unavailable python
command, then the read succeeded with python3. No old file was changed.
The finite062 certificates remain valid finite certificates. Neither
their observed costs nor their exact verification prove a uniform rate.

The review supplies an exact repair formula, complete coprime-row
parameterization, universal positive repair rules, and an actual-prime
second-difference recurrence with a paid integer-rounding boundary. The
new signed arithmetic estimates required to close it remain OPEN.

Notation
--------
Fix registered primes M, R=product(M), k=|M|>=2 and p<q in M. For integer
N>=2, integer prefix Y>=0 and 0<r<1 define

 f(d,r)=1[d<=Y]*(W(N/(pd))-r W(N/(qd))),
 W(v)=floor(4v)-3floor(2v)+2floor(v), D=floor(4N/p).

The true joined source is p^-t times sum mu_M(d)f(d,r), where
mu_M(d)=mu(d)1[gcd(d,R)=1] and r=(p/q)^t. It is harmless to clamp Y to D.
W takes values in {-1,0,1}, hence |f|<=2. Every floor and indicator is
inclusive. Source unit d=1 and outside prime factors are retained.

1. The exact small-horizon inverse
----------------------------------
Let a joined residual E(d,r) be zero for every integer d>H, H>=1. For
1<=j<=H put

 A_E(j,r)=sum_(m<=H/j) mu(m)E(jm,r), A_E(H+1,r)=0,
 b_j(r)=A_E(j,r)-A_E(j+1,r).                         (1)

Then, for EVERY positive integer d,

 E(d,r)=sum_(j<=H)b_j(r)floor(j/d).                  (2)

Proof: sum_m A_E(md)=sum_n E(nd)sum_(m|n)mu(m)=E(d).
On the other hand sum_j b_j floor(j/d)=sum_m sum_(j>=md)b_j
=sum_m A_E(md). All sums are finite, so the inversion introduces no
Dirichlet-series or interchange assumption. This is the unique repair
in the full basis floor(j/d), j=1,...,H: the matrix has unit diagonal
and is triangular when the indices are ordered from large to small.

All operations are linear in the source. An affine residual in r gives
affine b_j(r); exact independent endpoint constructions at r=p/q and1
may instead be interpolated as in062, with union horizons and complete
residuals. The repair is not a separate optimization of the two channels.

Two exact invariants retain the unit and current-scale information:

 sum_j b_j=A_E(1)=sum_(m<=H)mu(m)E(m),
 sum_j j*b_j=E(1).                                  (3)

The second identity is also simply (2) at d=1. Adding a complete repair
therefore changes the first moment of the long completion by E(1).
One may not assert that a previously zero-moment long construction
stays zero-moment after repair unless the full source unit is zero.

2. The generic indicator completion restores a full inverse
------------------------------------------------------------
For any finite target f supported d<=D define

 F(j,r)=sum_(m<=D/j)mu(m)f(jm,r), F(D+1,r)=0,
 a_j(r)=F(j,r)-F(j+1,r).                            (4)

The same proof gives f(d,r)=sum_(j<=D)a_j(r)floor(j/d).
This is a full-row identity, before any coprime mask is applied.

For an indicator 1[d<=T], F(j)=M_0(floor(T/j)), where
M_0(x)=sum_(n<=x)mu(n). Its full coefficient is

 a_j=M_0(floor(T/j))-M_0(floor(T/(j+1)))
    =sum_(T/(j+1)<m<=T/j)mu(m).                     (5)

The062 generic long completion uses
I_K(T,d)=sum_(m<=K)mu(m)floor(T/(md)),
K=floor(T/(H+1)), for each threshold T>H. Every one of these columns
has horizon floor(T/m)>H. Conversely every full indicator inverse
column with horizon>H has m<=K. Thus the generic completion is EXACTLY
the j>H part of (4). After expanding truncated W into its indicators,
this remains true under their complete signed linear combination.
More explicitly, each threshold is T=min(Y,floor(Z/m)), with its
original signed multiplicity. In the full indicator inverse,
floor(T/(ell*d))=floor(floor(T/ell)/d); retaining ell<=floor(T/(H+1))
is exactly retaining the integer floor endpoint floor(T/ell)>H.
Aggregating duplicate endpoints before or after this truncation gives
the same coefficient. There is no approximation in a grouped endpoint
or in the original prefix truncation.
Discarded thresholds T<=H only contribute to the repair; they must not
be omitted from that repair.

Consequently the short residual is

 E_H(d,r)=sum_(j<=H)a_j(r)floor(j/d),

and its exact repair is just a_j for j<=H. For j<=H its cumulative
transform is A_E(j)=F(j)-F(H+1); the constant cancels in each difference.
Small-horizon correction has not removed a Mertens remainder: it has
restored the omitted part of the ordinary-mu inverse.

Already the indicator coefficient a_1=M_0(T)-M_0(floor(T/2)) is a
CURRENT-SCALE signed interval. No unconditional square-root estimate
for it is proved here. The fact that its floor horizon is1 does not
make its arithmetic coefficient small. This is a scoped obstruction
to declaring generic repair automatically cheap, not an impossibility
theorem for other completions or for the actual joined source.

The062 paid long cost remains
O(N/(H+1)*(1+log(max(1,N/(H+1))))). For H=floor(N^eta), eta in[0,1],
it is O(N^(1-eta)log(2N)). The new unpaid cost is precisely

 V_H(N,Y,r)=sum_(j<=H)|F(j,r)-F(j+1,r)|.             (6)

A uniform square-root/polylog estimate for the COMPLETE joined (6)
would pay this repair. Separate indicator estimates followed by absolute
summation need not preserve its useful cancellations.

3. Separate the genuinely paid full channel from the prefix exit
----------------------------------------------------------------
In (4), the exact complete ordinary-mu convolution gives

 F(j,r)=C(j,r)-T(j,r),                              (7)

 C(j,r)=1[j<=4N/p]-3*1[j<=2N/p]+2*1[j<=N/p]
       -r*(1[j<=4N/q]-3*1[j<=2N/q]+2*1[j<=N/q]),

 T(j,r)=sum_(m>Y/j)mu(m)
               [W(N/(pjm))-r W(N/(qjm))].           (8)

The tail in (8) ends at floor(D/j). There is no assumption about mu
after a fixed prime cutoff. Formula (7) follows from
sum_m mu(m)floor(Z/m)=1[Z>=1] for every real Z>0. The full-channel
variation costs at most 6*(1+r)<=12, including every endpoint jump.
Therefore

 V_H<=12+sum_(j<=H)|T(j,r)-T(j+1,r)|.                (9)

If Y>=D, the tail is zero and the six original floor columns are a
universal cheap full reconstruction. For an interior prefix, the new
arithmetic leaf is the joint reciprocal EXIT variation in (9), not
an assumed bound on the whole untruncated source. The lower and upper
cofactor endpoints in every T(j)-T(j+1) must remain signed together.

An equivalent bilinear formula is

 a_j=sum_m mu(m)[f(jm,r)-f((j+1)m,r)].               (10)

If Delta f(T)=f(T,r)-f(T+1,r), then

 a_j=sum_T Delta f(T)
          [M_0(floor(T/j))-M_0(floor(T/(j+1)))].    (11)

It follows by writing f(d)=sum_(T>=d)Delta f(T); all finite thresholds,
including Y, remain. These formulas give enumerable joined kernel and
boundary primitives rather than only a numeric repair vector.

For illustration, a uniform harmonic-interval premise
|sum_(T/(j+1)<m<=T/j)mu(m)|<=C*(sqrt(T)/j+1)*polylog(T)
would pay a single indicator repair with H<=sqrt(T), by harmonic
summation. It is an UNPAID strong signed interval premise. Moreover
applying it independently to every W threshold can lose the joined
cancellation and accumulate an extra power. It is not a provider for
the actual target until the entire threshold combination is priced.

4. Coprime-only repairs have free rows, but fixed signed recovery
----------------------------------------------------------------
The062 checker requires matching only rows coprime to R. Therefore
uniqueness of the full-row repair does not imply uniqueness, optimality,
or a lower bound for every licensed LP repair.

There is an exact COMPLETE parameterization of this freedom. Fix the
actual short residual on every coprime d<=H, including d=1. Fill each
noncoprime row with an arbitrary affine value z_d(r), obtaining a full
short vector E_z. Apply (1). Conversely every small-floor repair that
matches the required coprime rows induces just such a fill, and (1)
recovers its unique coefficients. Thus the filled-row inverse covers
ALL repairs in this basis, not just the zero-fill one.

The invariant masked convolution is

 sum_(j<=H)b_j(r)Psi_M(j)
   =sum_(d<=H)mu_M(d)E_z(d,r)
   =sum_(d<=H,gcd(d,R)=1)mu(d)E_actual(d,r).        (12)

It is independent of every free fill. The unit invariant in (3) is
also fixed. Free noncoprime rows can improve coefficient cancellation;
they cannot change the actual signed remainder being recovered.

For the useful zero-fill extension of the FULL target, (4) becomes

 F_M(j,r)=1[gcd(j,R)=1]*P_M(N/j,Y/j,r),
 P_M(x,z,r)=sum_(d<=z)mu_M(d)
                      [W(x/(pd))-r W(x/(qd))].      (13)

This uses ordinary mu in the inverse but the actual masked source in
P_M. Repeated valuations and gcd overlaps are included: the displayed
zero for noncoprime j is algebraic, not a deletion of a nonzero source
term. The complete untruncated part of P_M is the six corresponding
smooth counts Psi_M. Its crude short variation is paid by
O(H*(1+log(4N))^k); its remaining masked prefix-exit variation is OPEN.

This zero-fill FULL-target inverse is a separate permissible algebraic
construction. Its long coefficients need not equal062's generic
unmasked long coefficients and do not automatically inherit their
proved coefficient cost. For a FIXED generic long completion, apply
the free-row parameterization only to its SHORT residual. The long
rows and their existing certificate stay fixed. Keeping this distinction
prevents an alternative full inverse from silently changing the paid
construction.

5. Universal repair bounds and what they actually buy
----------------------------------------------------
For an arbitrary chosen full short residual E, (1) gives

 ||b||_1<=2*sum_(d<=H)tau(d)|E(d)|
         <=2H*(1+log H)*||E||_infinity.             (14)

Indeed sum_j|A_E(j)|<=sum_d tau(d)|E(d)|, and each A_E occurs at
most twice in the variation. The elementary divisor count
sum_(d<=H)tau(d)=sum_(a<=H)floor(H/a)<=H*(1+log H)
proves the second inequality. These bounds are independent of source
sign guesses. For affine E the endpoint or direct convex price may
retain the signed p/q mixture before either bound is applied.

A quadratic alternative retaining signed crosses within E is

 ||b||_1<=2*sqrt(H)*(1+log H)^(3/2)*||E||_2.        (15)

At prime powers tau(p^e)^2=(e+1)^2<=binom(e+3,3)=d_4(p^e);
the difference is (e+1)*e*(e-1)/6>=0. Multiplicativity and
sum_(d<=H)d_4(d)<=H*(1+log H)^3 follow by counting four-factor
products and summing three harmonic factors. Cauchy-Schwarz now
proves (15). No companion RH bound enters this argument.

For H<=sqrt N, a uniform polylog sup bound for the FULL joined E
pays repair by (14). An appropriate quadratic premise can use (15).
Both are explicit source-bound leaves and neither is proved for the
generic E_H. Applying |E|<=2+long coefficient L1 merely reproduces
the bad product H*N^(1-eta) of062; no exponent is saved.

The positive rules are sufficient, not necessary. For example
E(d)=floor(H/d) has a repair consisting of the single column j=H
with coefficient1, despite a large unit and a large residual L1.
Thus a detector should also search floor-structured residual identities
or directly bound the transformed variation, rather than always impose
a small residual sup norm. This is a source-free algebraic example,
not an observation about the scientific residual.

Repair is also not automatically better than leaving the062 residual
priced in L1. Formula (14) can increase cost by a divisor weight; the
exact smooth-weighted repair price can be still larger. Only its actual
complete cost or a proved rate is a valid improvement.

6. Optional actual-prime scale innovation with paid integer transport
----------------------------------------------------------------
Choose an actual prime Q outside M. The exact law
mu_M(Qd)=-mu_M(d) for Q not dividing d and0 otherwise, including
all higher Q valuations, gives the constructive identity

 I_Q(N,Y,r):=P_M(N,Y,r)-P_M(N/Q,Y/Q,r)
 =sum_(gcd(m,QR)=1)mu(m)
       [f(m,r)-2*f(Qm,r)+f(Q^2*m,r)].               (16)

Proof: separate each P_M into valuation0 and valuation1 terms. In
the first source these are f(m)-f(Qm); in the second they are
f(Qm)-f(Q^2m). Their difference is (16). The unit m=1, new prime
Q-free companion, every cutoff Y/Q and Y/Q^2, and all W exits are
present. Valuation>=2 vanishes by the actual ordinary-mu law, not by
an approximation. No independence or prime density is assumed.

For (13), Q coprime to R implies
I_Q=F_M(1)-F_M(Q)=sum_(j=1)^(Q-1) a_j. Thus (16) is also a
concrete LOW coefficient-block innovation for that alternative full
inverse. If its long part is separately admitted and H>=Q-1, the
block lies within its short repair. It is not automatically a block
of the fixed062 generic repair, and no long-part cost is inherited.
The scale identity itself is independent of any floor construction.
It overlaps the licensed prime-transfer direction and is optional,
not the main new repair theorem or a paid arithmetic estimate.

The real arguments in (16) matter. They can be transported to integer
parents at a source-free square-root price. If x>=1, n=floor x and
z is any prefix, then

 |P_M(x,z,r)-P_M(n,floor z,r)|<=56*sqrt x.           (17)

For a single floor floor(c*x/d)-floor(c*n/d), summing its nonnegative
jumps over d counts divisors of the integers v with c*n<v<=c*x.
There are at most ceil(c) such integers and tau(v)<=2sqrt(v).
For c=4/p,2/p,1/p, the weighted jump count is at most
2*sqrt(4x/p)*(ceil(4/p)+3ceil(2/p)+2ceil(1/p))
<=14*sqrt(4x/p). The q channel has the same upper bound and r<=1;
their total is <=56sqrt x. Restricting to a prefix or to coprime d
only reduces this positive jump count. The prefix floor is exact, not
an ignored extra source row. Empty sources and x<1 can be handled in
the finite head, directly by |P_M(x,z,r)|<=2floor(4x/p).

Consequently a genuinely proved uniform innovation premise

 |I_Q(N,Y,r)|<=C*N^sigma*(1+log(4N))^A,
 every integer N>=N0, every prefix Y, every admissible r,          (18)

with integer N0>=2, sigma>=1/2 and A>=0, gives a strict normalized
scale recurrence.
Set N'=floor(N/Q), Y'=floor(Y/Q). Equations (16)-(17) imply

 |P_M(N,Y,r)|<=|P_M(N',Y',r)|
          +(C+56)*N^sigma*(1+log(4N))^A.            (19)

Iterating the floor parents yields a geometric coefficient factor
at most 1/(1-Q^-sigma), plus the source-free finite head. In
particular Q^-sigma<=1/sqrt2<3/4, so the conservative prefactor
4*(C+56)+4*N0 bounds the result at N>=2. The head estimate uses
|P_M(x,z,r)|<=4x for integer x and p>=2. This is all-integer
recovery, not just a claim along an exact geometric subsequence.

The hypothesis (18) must cover the parameter at EVERY parent. A bound
known only on t>=eps(N) cannot be silently used at N': eps(N') is
larger and leaves a missing low-t strip. One must either prove (18)
uniformly for 0<t<1, as062's algebraic certificates do at a fixed N,
or price each missing strip by the licensed endpoint theorem.

The normalized gap in (19) is valid, but the signed estimate (18)
is OPEN. Pointwise absolute values in (16) give only a linear source
count; they do not establish the square-root innovation. Applying an
assumed RH-strength bound separately to the Q-free companion would
be circular. Positive-power uniform increment control and uniform
prefix control have the same growth class: the reverse implication
follows by triangle inequality. This is therefore an exact useful
arithmetic frontier and recovery schema, not yet an advance in the
unresolved exponent. A real advance would prove (18), or a stronger
joint cancellation mechanism, from independent arithmetic structure.

7. Detector-facing grammar and honest proof obligations
-------------------------------------------------------
An enumerable argument can contain:
 * exact joined long columns with all original and added horizons;
 * a rational affine noncoprime-row fill, then inverse (1);
 * a transformed-variation or paid positive rule (14)/(15);
 * exact full-channel/exit separation (7)-(11);
 * an actual-prime innovation (16), complete cutoff masks and
   integer-parent compensation (17);
 * a uniform rate premise with all-N/all-prefix/all-parameter domains,
   finite head, and explicit scale decrease (19).

These are mechanically checkable algebraic rules or conditional
recovery rules. Their unpaid arithmetic leaves are transformed short
variation, joint reciprocal-exit variation, a short-residual quadratic
bound, or the actual Q-free second-difference estimate. They may not
be paid by the original source bound at the same scale, an assumed
Mertens/RH estimate, a disappearing gcd sector, or finite LP evidence.
Source mask changes must be recorded in the obligation graph.

For example a node invoking P_M(N,Y) to bound its own coefficient
F_M(1) creates a dependency cycle. A node invoking the Q-free
companion at current scale must expose that companion as OPEN unless
an independent source identity pays it. Merely enlarging M repeatedly
does not give a finite proof; the cost and remaining outside primes
must be controlled. Conversely use of small-index ordinary mu in the
exact floor inverse is fully legitimate and is not such an assumed
signed estimate.

A proved square-root/polylog full repair together with062's paid long
mass would provide the uniform prefix bound, then061's core recovery
and058's conditional RH bridge within their original endpoint scope.
No such repair or innovation bound has been proved here. No general
impossibility for the finite LP language or for other cheap repairs
has been proved either.

8. Factored quadratic dependency: smaller queries, current-scale cost
---------------------------------------------------------------------
This section additionally inspects the new063 producer and independent
checker source, plus the062 generic indicator-construction source. No
source array, optimizer or test is executed by this inspection. The
scope is a GENERIC ordinary-mu inverse base followed by one fixed fill
rule. An arbitrary fitted LP base is not automatically such a base.

Keep an unpruned dictionary containing every generic long endpoint.
For each threshold T in the signed truncated-W expansion and each
ell<=floor(T/(H+1)), the long coefficient has the form
mu(ell) times a source-free signed multiplicity, at X=floor(T/ell).
Duplicate columns are aggregated linearly. Pruning columns whose
coefficient becomes zero does not alter this statement: retain them
with zero coefficients in the underlying dictionary. Put

 K=max_T floor(T/(H+1))<=floor(D/(H+1)),
 g_X(r)=sum_(ell<=K)G_(X,ell)(r)*mu(ell),
 L_(d,X)=floor(X/d), 1<=d<=H.

Take K=0 if the long threshold list is empty, with an empty G matrix.
The matrix G is source-free and affine in r; it includes all threshold
multiplicities and both channels. The raw short residual vector is

 E_raw(r)=f_short(r)-L G(r)mu_(<=K).                 (20)

All eight registered rules raw, zero, left, right, linear,
reciprocal_linear, core and core_inverse are fixed linear maps P on
this vector. Their entries depend on H and registered-prime divisibility
only, not on ordinary-mu values. Every row is nonnegative with row sum
at most1, and every coprime row, including1, is the identity row.
For the reciprocal rule, between neighboring coprime indices a<d<b,
the weights are a(b-d)/(d(b-a)) and b(d-a)/(d(b-a)); they are positive
and sum1. The virtual right endpoint H+1 is zero and may reduce row
mass. The core rule copies the row with registered primes stripped;
core_inverse multiplies it by core/d<=1. Thus

 ||P E_raw||_infinity<=||E_raw||_infinity.           (21)

No hidden mu-dependent interpolation or division is present in these
rules. Other future fills must prove their own linearity and norm rule.

Define D(mu) by
 (D(mu)v)_j=sum_(m<=H/j)mu(m)v_(jm),
and Delta v_j=v_j-v_(j+1), with the virtual row H+1 zero. The exact
coefficient vector is therefore

 b(r)=Delta D(mu_(<=H)) P
                    [f_short(r)-L G(r)mu_(<=K)].    (22)

This is a factored polynomial of degree at most TWO in ordinary mu.
The first term is linear, the second is bilinear, and all coefficients
are affine in r. More explicitly, put

 T_(j,m,e)=1[jm<=H]P_(jm,e)-1[(j+1)m<=H]P_((j+1)m,e).

An out-of-range P row in this notation contributes zero.
Then
 b_j(r)=sum_m mu(m)sum_e T_(j,m,e)f_e(r)
       -sum_(m,ell)mu(m)mu(ell)
          sum_(e,X)T_(j,m,e)floor(X/e)G_(X,ell)(r). (23)

The source-free coefficient tensors may have current-scale weights.
Every mask, unit and endpoint remains inside (23). If a fill is selected
by comparing its observed costs, the winner is piecewise one of these
eight polynomials; it is not necessarily one fixed quadratic formula
valid at every N. Each fixed branch retains the exact factorization.

For H=floor(N^eta), eta in[0,1], H<=N^eta and H+1>N^eta. Consequently
K<(4/p)N^(1-eta), and all ordinary-mu dependencies are at indices

 <=max(H,K)=O(N^max(eta,1-eta)).                    (24)

The minimum exponent is1/2, attained at eta1/2. With p>=2 the bound
is at most2sqrt N there. The actual generic compiler may conservatively
generate mu through floor(D/(H+1)) rather than just the last used
threshold; the same bound applies. These are MU ARGUMENT INDICES, not
a claim that only prime indices are queried. Their prime factors are
small too, but bilinear products can be as large as H*K<D=O(N).
For a fixed branch (22) the repair uses no new current-scale source
mu lookup. This is genuine arithmetic dependency reduction.

It is not a contraction or a solved signed bound. An elementary exact
factorization makes that distinction explicit. Let L_U be the sequence
mu restricted to n<=U, with U,V nonnegative integers. Dirichlet
convolution gives

 (mu-L_U)*(mu-L_V)*One
       =mu-L_U-L_V+L_U*L_V*One.                    (25)

The left side has no coefficient at n<(U+1)(V+1), because both factors
are supported above their respective cutoffs. Therefore whenever
(U+1)(V+1)>T,

 M_0(T)=M_0(min(U,T))+M_0(min(V,T))
        -sum_(a<=U,b<=V)mu(a)mu(b)floor(T/(ab)).    (26)

This proof uses only finite convolution and mu*One=delta_1. Choosing
U=H and V=floor(T/(H+1)) meets the condition exactly. Taking the
difference of (26) at T and T/2 yields the indicator coefficient a_1
in (5), using only indices through max(H,V). Thus a current-scale
Mertens interval really can be encoded by smaller-index quadratic
arithmetic. No unknown large-prime sign is licensed to vanish; the
complete floor weights encode its contribution indirectly. This is an
identity, not an assumption about independence of the small inputs.

Nor may one freely replace mu(m)mu(ell) by mu(m*ell). Overlap sectors
are essential. With g=gcd(m,ell), the exact identity is

 mu(m)mu(ell)=mu(m)^2*mu(ell)^2*mu(m*ell/g^2).

It follows by separating squarefree and nonsquarefree inputs and then
their coprime remaining factors. It does not license deleting shared
prime factors or treating the composite-index form as a smaller query.

The concrete next analytic leaf is the COMPLETE joint variation

 sum_(j<=H)| linear_j(mu_(<=H),r)
                       - quadratic_j(mu_(<=H),mu_(<=K),r) |,

with (23)'s actual source-free tensors and either exact endpoint
control or full parameter quantification. The paid long coefficient
cost is added afterward. A detector can enumerate fills or exact
tensor rearrangements, expose gcd-overlap sectors, seek telescoping,
and attach an independently proved variation budget. It must price
the tensors, not just count their source queries. Absolute termwise
estimation can lose both bilinear cancellation and the linear-minus-
quadratic cancellation. If an argument bounds this leaf by the
current-scale Mertens term reconstructed in (26), its dependency is
still unpaid and may be circular. Smaller arithmetic queries provide
a useful new joint-quadratic proof coordinate, not by themselves a
scale-decreasing inequality, asymptotic growth estimate, or RH claim.
