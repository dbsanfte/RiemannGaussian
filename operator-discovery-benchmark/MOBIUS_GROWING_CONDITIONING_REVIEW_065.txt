GROWING PRIME CONDITIONING: JOINED SOURCE AND CORE_INVERSE AUDIT 065
=================================================================

Source-only scope and conclusion
-------------------------------
This review reads MOBIUS_QUADRATIC_CONTRACTION_HANDOFF.txt,
MOBIUS_QUADRATIC_CONTRACTION_REVIEW_064.txt,
MOBIUS_PREFIX_REPAIR_REVIEW_063.txt, mobius_prefix_repair_063.py and
mobius_prefix_completion_062.py. No scientific results array, candidate,
optimizer, sign table or numerical study was read or executed. No prior
file was changed. All identities below are finite algebraic identities;
no RH, Mertens estimate, prime-distribution asymptotic or statistical
prime-sign hypothesis is invoked.

The fixed-finite-set, raw single-indicator obstruction in064 does not
settle growing conditioning for the joined W source with core_inverse.
This audit proves a sharper reconstruction-defect identity for the
actual formal domain, and an exact source-truncated harmonic-profile
separation which core_inverse preserves. It does NOT prove that the
partially conditioned envelope has square-root growth, and does NOT
prove an all-scale lower obstruction to that growth for this source.
The exact residual lemmas required for either conclusion are given
below. In particular, an O(N) floor error cannot be used to support an
N/log(N)^2 lower witness.

1. Exact source dictionary, including the prefix and both channels
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fix integers N,y,H and p=3,q=5, registered primes R0={2,3,5}.
Write R=30, r in[p/q,1], and

 w_b(d)=floor(4N/(bd))-3floor(2N/(bd))+2floor(N/(bd)),
 f(d,r)=1[d<=y]*(w_p(d)-r*w_q(d)),  1<=d<=H.

For every d, w_b(d) is one of -1,0,1. Indeed writing N/(bd)=k+t
reduces it to floor(4t)-3floor(2t), whose four quarter-interval
values are 0,1,-1,0. Thus |f|<=1+r<=2.

For b=p,q, let a_(b,T) be the exact integer threshold dictionary:
for (v,c)=(4,1),(2,-3),(1,2), and each integer
1<=k<=floor(floor(vN/b)/(H+1)), add c to the threshold
T=min(y,floor(floor(vN/b)/k)), provided T>H.
Duplicate thresholds are combined with their signs. Set

 a_T(r)=a_(p,T)-r*a_(q,T),
 K_T=floor(T/(H+1)), K=floor(y/(H+1)), B=max(H,K).

Zero dictionary entries may be retained. The formal long expression
on each short row is EXACTLY

 G_chi(d,r)=sum_(T>H) a_T(r)
                   sum_(ell<=K_T) chi(ell)*floor(T/(ell*d)).       (1)

This is the source-free G times the SAME chi used in the inverse.
It retains source truncation, threshold multiplicities, units and the
joined p/q signs. No untruncated W channel has replaced (1).
If y<=H the long dictionary is empty; all formulas use empty sums.
The common conservative K may exceed the largest actually used K_T.

Let rho(d) be d with all factors 2,3,5 stripped, and s(d)=d/rho(d).
The declared core_inverse fill is the fixed linear map

 (Pv)(d)=v(rho(d))/s(d), 1<=d<=H.                              (2)

In particular P(1/d)=1/d EXACTLY, on every row including d=1.
Set E_chi=P(f-G_chi), A_chi(j)=sum_(m<=H/j)chi(m)E_chi(jm),
A_chi(H+1)=0, and c_chi(j)=A_chi(j)-A_chi(j+1).
This is precisely the formal evaluation of the063/064 quadratic lift.
For chi=ordinary mu it is the licensed short repair. For other chi it
is a polynomial evaluation; its floor reconstruction need not equal E.

2. What the all-free-plus assignment actually implies
----------------------------------------------------
Let C=floor(B^xi). Assume the registered primes are conditioned, either
because C>=5 or by explicitly adjoining them. Define the squarefree
multiplicative function on all positive integers

 chi_C(n)=mu(n)^2*(-1)^(number of prime divisors of n <=C),       (3)

with the evident modification if extra registered primes are adjoined.
All primes outside the conditioned set receive the COMMON sign +1.
This is a permitted assignment for every row and both occurrences of
chi. Extension beyond B is for proving identities, not a new query or
an assertion that large ordinary-mu values are available.

For a prime power p^e with e>=1, the divisor sum of chi is
1+chi(p): it is zero for conditioned p and two for free p. Therefore

 h_C(n):=(chi_C*One)(n)
       =2^omega(n)*1[n has no conditioned prime divisor].      (4)

The formula includes prime powers: no squarefree indicator belongs
on its right side. Its value at n=1 is one. This proves the hypothesis
in the task exactly, without prime counting. Actual mu instead has
mu*One=delta_1. Equation (4) describes information that the free-sign
relaxation discards, not a replacement source law for ordinary mu.

For ANY finite vector E and ANY multiplicative squarefree-sign chi,
let c=Delta D(chi)E. Telescoping the floor basis gives

 sum_(j<=H)c(j)*floor(j/d)
   =sum_(k<=H/d)A(kd)
   =sum_(n<=H/d)(chi*One)(n)*E(dn).                            (5)

Thus for the all-free-plus evaluation of the actual joined polynomial,

 F_H c_C(d)=E_C(d)+sum_(2<=n<=H/d)h_C(n)E_C(dn).              (6)

Here F_H denotes the ORIGINAL unweighted floor reconstruction. Its
extra term remains even on rows coprime to30. Formal signs do not
preserve the old exact repair admission; that admission was established
at ordinary mu. Nevertheless every universally valid free-sign upper
bound must bound c_C. There is no contradiction between these facts.

3. A stronger exact simplification at the square-root conditioning gate
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose every prime <=floor(sqrt(B)) is conditioned. Two free primes,
counted with multiplicity, have product>B. Hence on 1<=n<=B,

 h_C(n)=delta_1(n)+2*1[n is a free prime].                     (7)

In particular the reconstruction defect is now exactly

 F_H c_C(d)-E_C(d)=2*sum_(free primes P<=H/d)E_C(dP).          (8)

This is a growing-set statement, substantially sharper than treating
(4) as an unrestricted rough-number sum. Products of two free primes
can still occur at the composite scale HK; (7) cannot be used there.

Convolving (7) with actual mu gives, for every n<=B,

 chi_C(n)=mu(n)+2*v_C(n),
 v_C(n)=sum_(free primes P|n)mu(n/P).                         (9)

At most one summand survives. In fact n/P<=C when C>=floor(sqrt(B)), so that
cofactor contains only conditioned primes. This matches the direct
prime-parity expansion, including nonsquarefree n (for n containing a
conditioned square, the summand is zero). The forbidden case P^2<=B
cannot occur. Equation (9) is a short-index identity, not a current-scale
Mertens estimate.

For a general free-sign assignment x_P, the exact version is

 u_x(n)=mu(n)+sum_(free primes P|n)(x_P+1)*mu(n/P), n<=B.       (10)

Consequently h_x(n)=delta_1(n)+(x_P+1) at n=P and zero at all
other n<=B. This is an exact linear dictionary for short-index inputs;
the repair itself remains quadratic because both D and G use u_x.

Define L(u)=Delta D(u)Pf and Q(u,v)=Delta D(u)PG(v), with G
as in (1), retaining source parameters. Then

 c_C=c_mu+2L(v_C)-2Q(v_C,mu)-2Q(mu,v_C)-4Q(v_C,v_C).         (11)

No term in (11) can be dropped merely because it has degree two.
The two unequal cross terms are necessary: core_inverse and the source
matrix do not make Q symmetric. An expansion in x_P+1 from (10),
followed by x_P^2=1, gives a compact exact degree-two next-search input.
An estimate for c_mu is itself unpaid; using (11) does not pay it.

4. Harmonic-profile separation which core_inverse preserves
----------------------------------------------------------
Put S_C(x)=sum_(ell<=floor(x))chi_C(ell)/ell and define

 M_C(r)=sum_(T>H)a_T(r)*T*S_C(K_T),
 R_C(d,r)=sum_(T>H)a_T(r)
                  sum_(ell<=K_T)chi_C(ell)*{T/(ell*d)}.        (12)

Braces mean the exact fractional part. The floor identity in (1) gives

 G_chi(d,r)=M_C(r)/d-R_C(d,r),
 E_C(d,r)=Pf(d,r)-M_C(r)/d+PR_C(d,r).                        (13)

Because (2) fixes 1/d, there is NO approximation or smooth-row error
in (13). This propagates the harmonic profile through the declared fill
and includes every truncated threshold and both channels. The resulting
repair identity is

 c_C=Delta D(chi_C)Pf-M_C*t_C+Delta D(chi_C)PR_C,
 t_C(j)=S_C(H/j)/j-S_C(H/(j+1))/(j+1).                       (14)

The virtual last row gives t_C(H)=1/H. Neither t_C nor PR_C has a
known sign. The defect (4), the degree-two gate and profile invariance
alone do not bound their combined variation or prevent cancellation.

The f-only contribution IS paid, uniformly over every formal sign:

 ||Delta D(chi)Pf||_1<=2*sum_(d<=H)tau(d)|Pf(d)|
                    <=4H*(1+log H).                        (15)

This follows by counting divisors and using |chi|<=1 and |Pf|<=2.
At H of order sqrt(N), this is an admissible square-root/polylog cost.
Thus the exact unpaid all-free-plus expression has been isolated as

 Z_C(r)=Delta D(chi_C)[PR_C(r)-M_C(r)/d].                    (16)

Writing separate norms for its two terms loses the cancellation that
the audit is supposed to test.

5. Exact residual lemmas for an upper estimate or a lower witness
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let E_S(r)=sum_(j,U)|b_(j,U)(r)| denote064's fully coalesced conditioned
Fourier coefficient L1. Let c_x be the common-sign evaluation from(10).
The following are different obligations:

 (A) A Fourier-envelope theorem requires, at both r=p/q and r=1,
     sum_(j,U)|Fourier_U(Delta D(u_x)[PR_x-M_x/d])|
          =O_epsilon(N^(1/2+epsilon)).                      (17)

Here M_x,R_x are defined by (12) with u_x. The paid Pf polynomial has
Fourier L1 bounded by the SAME bound (15): each individual u_x(m) is
one conditioned sign times a single character, so termwise divisor
counting applies. Equation(17) is therefore an exact sufficient leaf
for the original envelope, with the existing paid long budget added.
It concerns Fourier coefficient norm, not merely worst-case evaluation.

 (B) An improved universal common-sign bound can instead prove
     sup_x ||Delta D(u_x)[PR_x-M_x/d]||_1
          =O_epsilon(N^(1/2+epsilon)).                      (18)

This pays a different, potentially sharper universal construction.
It does not imply (17) and must not be reported as the original
coefficient-L1 theorem without an additional comparison. Actual mu
is one of these assignments; (18) would suffice to control its repair.

 (C) To rule out ANY free-sign square-root envelope, it suffices to
     find an infinite admissible N,y sequence and one endpoint r with
     ||Z_C(r)||_1 >= c*N/log(N)^A,
     where c>0 and A is fixed, H=O(sqrt(N)*polylog(N)).        (19)

The reverse triangle inequality and(15) then prove the same lower
order for ||c_C||_1. This is an obstruction to the formal relaxation,
not a lower bound for ordinary-mu repair or a refutation of RH.
No such lower lemma is proved here.

A particularly clean coefficient test, retaining EXACT floor errors,
is for any chosen block 1<=a<=b<=H:

 sum_(j=a)^b Z_C(j)
  =sum_(m<=H/a)chi_C(m)*[PR_C(am)-M_C/(am)]
   -sum_(m<=H/(b+1))chi_C(m)*[PR_C((b+1)m)-M_C/((b+1)m)].    (20)

The second sum is empty for b=H. A lower estimate on the absolute
value in(20) directly lower-bounds ||Z_C||_1. It does not require
separately pricing every row or pretending independent row signs.
For a=1,b=H it is simply the exact signed core-filled residual inverse
at the unit. Formula(20), together with(12), is a useful source-bound
residual lemma to encode in the next search.

6. Why a leading N/log(N)^2 heuristic is currently insufficient
--------------------------------------------------------------
For a growing C the constant and error terms of064's fixed-prime-set
squarefree-density argument depend on the whole changing set. Its
positive harmonic constant is not uniform. Reusing that proof with S
silently replaced by primes<=B^xi is invalid.

For xi>=1/2, equations(9)-(10) reduce short-index harmonic sums to
actual-mu cofactor sums over primes in moving intervals. Even a valid
prime-counting asymptotic for one such sum would leave the joined
weighted floor remainder in(12), and its correlation with the other
chi factor. A large M_C is not a large c_C. Core_inverse's exact
profile invariance makes the correct candidate visible but does not
remove this remainder.

A crude explicit remainder budget illustrates the issue. Set
A_abs(r)=sum_(T>H)|a_T(r)|. Then

 A_abs(r)<=sum_(b=p,q)lambda_b*
     [floor(4N/(b(H+1)))+3floor(2N/(b(H+1)))
                                +2floor(N/(b(H+1)))],
 lambda_p=1, lambda_q=r,
 |R_C(d,r)|<=A_abs(r)*K.                                   (21)

At H,K of order sqrt(N), the last bound is O(N), already larger
than a proposed N/log(N)^2 leading term before the inverse is applied.
It cannot be labeled negligible. Source cancellation may improve it,
but that improvement needs a joint proof. Keeping the fractional parts
in(12) or the exact block in(20) avoids introducing this invalid error.
Replacing floor(T/(ell*d)) by T/(ell*d) with an unsigned O(1) per pair
has the same problem. Passing to an untruncated full channel, using
mu*One=delta for chi, or ignoring one source channel also changes the
object and does not resolve the remainder.

A simple identity also warns against treating a large harmonic moment
as a universal lower bound: if a formal residual happens to have
PR_C=M_C/d, its entire term(16) vanishes. This is a logical cancellation
example, not a claim that the actual source has that property. A lower
argument must exploit the actual exact kernels rather than profile
invariance alone.

7. Boundaries and detector-facing disposition
--------------------------------------------
If C>=B there are no free variables. Then chi_C=mu on every queried
input, and the conditioned envelope is exactly the actual finite repair
L1. Any growing-conditioning impossibility claim must exclude this
boundary. No elementary argument here proves its uniform growth.
For fixed xi<1, an all-scale theorem still needs explicit quantifiers
for H(N,y), B(N,y), every required prefix and both affine endpoints.
The empty-long case y<=H is already paid by(15), regardless of signs.
Any claimed lower obstruction must use a genuinely nonempty long part.

When extra registered primes are conditioned and C is small, use the
actual conditioned set in(3)-(10). The prime-only defect(7) requires
all primes through sqrt(B), regardless of labels. At large balanced
scales xi>=1/2 automatically includes2,3,5. Floors and strict prime
cutoffs matter: two free primes exceed floor(sqrt(B)), and their
product is strictly greater than B.

The strongest new unconditional facts are the exact formal floor
reconstruction defect(5)-(8), the short-index one-free-prime expansion
(9)-(11), and the core-preserved, fully truncated joined decomposition
(12)-(16). They establish where the actual divisor-inverse law is lost
and provide a compact residual kernel to inspect. They do not establish
that the loss is asymptotically fatal, nor that degree two restores an
RH-strength rate. The residual Fourier lemma(17), universal norm lemma
(18), and witness lemma(19)/(20) remain OPEN and have distinct scopes.

Recommended next exact search input: retain the threshold dictionary,
K_T and both endpoint weights; represent each u_x(m) by(10); apply
core_inverse as(2); coalesce constants, linear and quadratic terms
including shared primes; encode either the complete residual norm in
(17) or exact block functional(20), with(12)'s fractional parts retained.
The defect(8) is a separate consistency diagnostic, not a substitute
repair law. Do not condition additional primes merely to infer a rate
from finite contraction, and do not use current-scale source bounds to
pay the same residual circularly.

Disposition: source-only mathematical review complete. A stronger exact
defect decomposition is proved; no new general growth theorem or actual
joined-source lower witness has been established. RH and the uniform
variation leaf remain open. No scientific search or prior-file change.
