PRIME INSERTION MOVING MIDDLE: EXACT TRANSPORT AND RECOVERY REVIEW 057

Scope
=====
This new source-only review preserves all frozen files. No arithmetic source
values, numerical integrals, tests, scores, or hashes were computed. All
bounds below are unconditional algebraic/component bounds, except the
explicitly named uniform recurrence or middle-correlation premises. Those
premises remain OPEN. No square-root source estimate is claimed.

1. Source, units, and the complete finite middle
===============================================
Fix alpha=1/5. The formulas remain valid for 0<alpha<1 where stated. For a
fixed squarefree R>=2, let k=omega(R), A_R=product_{p|R}log p, and

 G_n(t)=product_{p|n}(1-p^(-t)), G_1(t)=1,
 K(n)=I_alpha^(-1) integral_0^infinity G_n(t)t^(-alpha-1)dt, n>=2,
 I_alpha=integral_0^infinity (1-exp(-u))u^(-alpha-1)du.

K(1)=0 by its original divisor definition; its integral must NOT be
replaced by the divergent integral of G_1. The inserted source is K(Rn)
for n>=2. Its prefix excludes n=1 even though K(R)>0. Define

 h_R(N,t)=sum_{2N<n<=4N}G_(Rn)(t)
                   -2 sum_{N<n<=2N}G_(Rn)(t),
 epsilon_R(N)=min(1/2,N^(-gamma_R)), tau=1/2,
 J_R(N)=(alpha I_alpha)^(-1) integral_epsilon_R(N)^tau
                                  h_R(N,t)t^(-alpha-1)dt.       (1)

Here N is an integer >=2. For R=2,3,5 use gamma=5/8; for R=6,10,15
use gamma=5/18. In both cases p_R=gamma_R(k-alpha)=1/2 exactly. The
integral is zero if its endpoints coincide. No rounded cutoff is intended.

The equivalent prefix coefficients are (2,-3,1) at (N,2N,4N). Thus the
unit correction cancels exactly, since 2-3+1=0. Prime powers and overlap
with forced primes are all retained in G_(Rn).

2. A cleaner finite signed divisor source
=========================================
Factor forced primes BEFORE expanding the remaining radical product:

 G_(Rn)(t)=G_R(t) sum_{d|n, gcd(d,R)=1}mu(d)d^(-t).

Every prime valuation in n is included: when p|R its factor appears once
in G_R, and when p does not divide R it appears once in the divisor sum.
Finite summation over n in (1) therefore gives

 h_R(N,t)=G_R(t) D_R(N,t),
 D_R(N,t)=sum_{d<=4N, gcd(d,R)=1}mu(d)W(N/d)d^(-t),
 W(y)=floor(4y)-3floor(2y)+2floor(y), |W(y)|<=1.                (2)

This is the E_R source, not the coprime-masked mixed difference D_S K.
There is no external restriction gcd(n,R)=1. The internal restriction
in (2) excludes exactly the already-forced primes. The d=1 term is zero
because W(N)=0 at integer N. No infinite mu series is used below t=1.
The finite signed mu-W polynomial, not its absolute envelope, is the
remaining source that the detector must control.

The tail t>=1/2 is bounded independently of R:

 |T_R(N)| <=128 sqrt(N),
 T_R=(alpha I_alpha)^(-1) integral_(1/2)^infinity h_R t^(-alpha-1)dt. (3)

Proof: alpha I_alpha>1/2, G_R<=1, and d=1 is exactly zero. For t>=1/2,
t^(-alpha-1)<=4 uniformly 0<alpha<1. Integrating d^(-t) gives
d^(-1/2)/log d; log d>=log2>=1/2 for d>=2. Hence the bound is at most
16 sum_{d<=4N}d^(-1/2)<=16*2*(4N)^(1/2)=64 sqrt(N).
Thus (3), the executable conservative constant 128, is valid with room
to spare. This stronger direct argument avoids the Boolean-face triangle
constant of review056. The zero d=1 term must be handled before integration.

The microscopic endpoint has the earlier unconditional bound

 |M_R(N)| <=12R/(k-alpha)*sqrt(N).                            (4)

Indeed |h_R|<=4N A_R t^k, (alpha I_alpha)^(-1)<3,
A_R<=R, and epsilon_R^(k-alpha)<=N^(-1/2). Therefore the full inserted
filter t_R=[A_R_source(4N)-3A_R_source(2N)+2A_R_source(N)]/alpha satisfies

 t_R=J_R+M_R+T_R,
 |M_R+T_R|<=[12R/(k-alpha)+128]sqrt(N).                       (5)

The symbol A_R_source denotes the prefix of K(Rn); it is distinct from
the logarithmic product A_R. Formula (5) does not recover a fixed old
constant and does not invert the insertion projection back to K.

A paid absolute envelope has a logarithmic saving but no RH-scale gain.
Put beta=k-alpha>0 and D=4N. For 0<t<=1/2,

 sum_{d<=D}d^(-t)<=D^(1-t)/(1-t)<=2D exp(-t log D),
 G_R(t)<=A_R t^k.

Consequently even the entire low interval obeys

 |J_R(N)|<=16 A_R Gamma(beta) N/[log(4N)]^beta.                (6)

The Gamma notation is only a positive elementary integral, not an imported
source theorem: integral_0^infinity exp(-v)v^(beta-1)dv. A rational
certificate can use Gamma(beta)<=1/beta+(ceil(beta)-1)!, by splitting
at v=1 and enlarging the remaining power. This bound is much larger than
sqrt(N); positivity and the forced-prime factor alone do not close the
middle. It gives a genuine, but weak, all-scale baseline.

3. Exact moving transport and paid neighboring boundary
=======================================================
For integers M>N>=2, set epsilon_N=epsilon_R(N). The complete identity is

 J_R(M)-J_R(N)
 =c_alpha integral_epsilon_N^tau [h_R(M,t)-h_R(N,t)]t^(-alpha-1)dt
  +c_alpha integral_epsilon_M^epsilon_N h_R(M,t)t^(-alpha-1)dt, (7)
 c_alpha=(alpha I_alpha)^(-1).

The last term is incoming parameter-boundary flux. Its sign is not fixed,
because h_R is a signed filter even though each G_(Rn) is positive. The
spatial difference is exactly

 h_R(M,t)-h_R(N,t)
 =2 sum_{N<n<=M}G_(Rn)(t)
 -3 sum_{2N<n<=2M}G_(Rn)(t)
   +sum_{4N<n<=4M}G_(Rn)(t).                                (8)

All endpoints in (8) are inclusive on the right and exclusive on the left;
overlapping intervals are still summed with their coefficients. These
identities cover children 2N and 2N+1 and adjacent parents N-1,N,N+1.
They must not be replaced by a transport at fixed epsilon with its flux
silently omitted.

For M=N+1, (8) has exactly the new points
2 G_(R(N+1)) -3[G_(R(2N+1))+G_(R(2N+2))]
+sum_{i=1}^4 G_(R(4N+i)), total absolute coefficient mass 12.
Its normalized integral is at most
12 [log(4N+4)]^alpha/alpha, since any partial positive integral for n>=2
is at most K(Rn)/alpha<=K(n)/alpha<=log(n)^alpha/alpha.

The moving flux in (7) is bounded, including the cap transition, by

 12(N+1)A_R/(k-alpha)
       [epsilon_N^(k-alpha)-epsilon_(N+1)^(k-alpha)]
 <=24 gamma_R A_R N^(-1/2).                                 (9)

For the inequality, x -> min(2^(-beta),x^(-p_R)) is nonincreasing and
has derivative magnitude at most p_R x^(-p_R-1), wherever differentiable.
The same integral bound crosses its corner. Thus

 |J_R(N+1)-J_R(N)|
 <=12 log(4N+4)^alpha/alpha+24 gamma_R A_R/sqrt(N).            (10)

This is a proved local regularity rule with no source observations. It
cannot be summed over N to obtain a square-root bound: its crude accumulated
cost is linear times a logarithm. Child-scale flux in (7) is of order
sqrt(N), so it is paid for the growth class but not a small fixed drift
constant or a favorable signed covariance.

4. Prime 2 gives an exact joined odd/parent pairing
==================================================
If 2|R then G_(R(2m))(t)=G_(Rm)(t), and also K(2Rm)=K(Rm). Splitting
the even and odd terms in the upper block proves, for EVERY fixed t,

 h_R(N,t)=sum_{m=N+1}^{2N}
                   [G_(R(2m-1))(t)-G_(Rm)(t)].              (11)

The corresponding full-source and common-cutoff middle identity follows
by integration. This is valid at N>=1: no unit correction appears because
both blocks are above 1. The m=N+1 odd point is 2N+1 and the last is 4N-1;
the even points exhaust 2N+2,...,4N.

Each difference in (11) compares two actual radical sets. It is not of a
fixed sign. For example at R=2, m=5 gives G_18-G_10=G_6-G_10<0,
while m=3 gives G_10-G_6>0, at every t>0. These are exact elementary factor comparisons,
not evaluated arithmetic samples. Thus radical positivity does not make
this pairing termwise contracting. It is not a prime-independence model.

For odd R, the equally complete formula is

 h_R(N,t)=sum_{m=N+1}^{2N}[G_(R(2m-1))(t)-G_(Rm)(t)]
           -sum_{m=N+1}^{2N}[G_(Rm)(t)-G_(2Rm)(t)].           (12)

The last block is nonnegative before its minus sign. It retains all m
already divisible by 2 (zero contribution) and every other prime overlap.
Dropping it might give a one-sided comparison, but cannot prove an absolute
middle bound or a squared-energy recurrence. The useful auxiliary is the
JOINED odd-parent difference with this complete compensation, not separate
positive bounds on its two terms.

Crucially (11) uses epsilon_R(N) for both legs. Substituting a separately
computed parent channel with its own epsilon_R(m), or a 2R channel with its
own k/gamma, adds exactly the boundary integral in (7). These are different
functions unless that correction is included.

5. Neighbor-parent recurrence: exact all-integer induction
=========================================================
Let z(N) consist of the middle channels with their defined moving cutoffs.
For rational lambda>=0 and fixed rational signed coefficients a_i define

 F(N)=[J_6(N)^2+lambda*(sum_i a_i z_i(N))^2]/N >=0.           (13)

More generally a fixed PSD rational Gram is allowed provided it majorizes
the original J_6 coordinate square. No omitted-cross diagonalization is
licensed. Assume the source-bound PREMISE, not merely finite evidence,

 F(2N+r)<=sum_{s=-1}^1 w_s F(N+s)+C/N^delta,
 N>=3, r in {0,1}, w_s>=0, sum_s w_s=1, C>=0, delta>=0.     (14)

Every channel, cutoff and source definition must be recomputed at its own
argument. Neither checking only even children nor a fixed chain suffices.
Let B bound ALL four bases F(2),F(3),F(4),F(5).

Then (14) implies for every integer n>=2:

 delta=0:
 F(n)<=B+C*max(0,floor(log2(n-2))-1),                        (15)

with the logarithmic expression defined to be zero at n=2;

 delta>0:
 F(n)<=B+C/(2^delta-1)<=B+2C/delta.                         (16)

Proof uses a finite weighted parent tree, not repeated maxima with a lost
branching factor. For an internal node n>=6 put d=n-2. Every allowable
parent p=floor(n/2)+s has p-2<=floor(d/2), and p>=2. It is strictly smaller
than n. The maximum path length to the bases is at most
max(0,floor(log2(d))-1). Since outgoing weights sum to one, a tree expansion
averages the base and path forcing sums. This proves (15).

For (16), read an internal path backwards from its last internal node,
which has d>=4. At j reverse steps, d>=4*2^j; its forcing denominator
floor(n/2)>=d/2>=2^(j+1). The path forcing sum is therefore at most
sum_{j>=0}2^(-(j+1)delta)=1/(2^delta-1). Finally
2^delta-1>=delta log2>=delta/2. These are valid for arbitrary positive
rational delta; no denominator restriction is mathematically needed.

For features z_i=J_(R_i) without extra rescaling, a fully source-free base
is

 B=180/alpha^2*(1+lambda*(sum_i |a_i|)^2).                   (17)

Indeed a positive partial integral at each n is bounded by log(n)^alpha/
alpha. Both the upper-block positive mass and the negative lower-block
mass are <=2N log(4N)^alpha/alpha; thus |J_R(N)| is bounded by that
quantity. For N=2,...,5, log(4N)<=log20<3, so
|J_R(N)|<=6N/alpha and F<=36N/alpha^2*(1+lambda L1^2).
The inequality log20<3 is elementary: the first nine terms of the positive
series for exp(3) already exceed 20. No finite arithmetic bases need be
computed for (17). Extra rescaled or different features require their own
explicit base factors; this base must not be transplanted to them silently.

Combining (15)-(16), F>=J_6^2/N and (5) yields a uniform full inserted-source
square-root times at most sqrt(log N) bound. The conditional RH implication
for this fixed E_6 source from review056 then applies. This does not establish
(14), does not infer it from sampled recurrence passes, and does not recover
the original fixed numerical C=2. A positive delta gives a stronger source
lemma than is required for cofinal RH-scale growth.

6. Exact work, neighbor variance, and an arithmetic proof frontier
================================================================
For a fixed PSD Gram Q, write u_n=z(n)/sqrt(n),
bar_u=sum_s w_s u_(N+s), and xi=u_(2N+r)-bar_u. Then the COMPLETE work is

 F(2N+r)-sum_s w_s F(N+s)
 =2 <bar_u,Q xi>+||xi||_Q^2
       -sum_s w_s ||u_(N+s)-bar_u||_Q^2.                    (18)

The variance term is a genuine negative algebraic credit. It is not a
free strict contraction: equal neighboring states make it zero. Dropping
the signed first cross term or estimating it as if prime factors were
independent would change the original obligation. Formula (7) supplies
an exact source-bound xi with both its spatial and cutoff-boundary terms.
All cross terms between those two components stay in ||xi||_Q^2 and in
the parent correlation. A detector can enumerate rational w and Q and
propose a SOURCE-SPECIFIC proof of their joined signed work.

Two useful enumerable leaves are consequently:
 (i) common-cutoff odd-parent joined correlations from (11)-(12), with the
     exact cutoff compensation appended;
 (ii) the signed covariance in (18) between the transported parent state
     and the complete spatial-plus-boundary innovation, allowing its
     negative neighbor variance credit.

A proof graph may pay (2), (3), (4), (7)-(12), (15)-(17), and the universal
identity (18) immediately. It may NOT pay (14) from these identities alone.
The arithmetic leaf is a uniform upper bound on the joined work in (18),
or a genuinely stronger signed interval/pairing estimate that implies it.
Taking absolute values separately in (2) proves only (6); taking them
separately in (11) is also of linear scale. The scalar growth target has
not been proved. The transported-work identity is equivalent to its exact
recurrence at that stage; progress requires a distinct arithmetic lemma
with independent dependencies, not naming xi or positivity as that lemma.

Endpoint restoration is also cross-aware. If full vector t=z+e, then

 ||t||_Q^2=||z||_Q^2+2<z,Qe>+||e||_Q^2,
 ||t||_Q^2<=(1+v)||z||_Q^2+(1+1/v)||e||_Q^2, v>0.           (19)

Paid bounds on e do not determine the sign of the cross or preserve an
old forcing constant. They do preserve the square-root/polylog growth
class. Source-feedback should retain the finite signed divisor polynomial,
its exact boundary flux, and the joined odd-parent correlation as the next
unpaid expressions. It should not claim that a fixed first-jet nullspace or
positive radical kernel has already supplied their all-scale control.

Disposition: source-only review complete; no actual uniform middle or
recurrence lemma established. All signed units, prime valuations, overlap,
prefix endpoints, moving cutoffs, both child parities, and base domains are
retained. This new review is stopped.
