CANCELLATION LANGUAGE: SIGNED COMPOSITION AND COMPLETE PRIME TRANSPORT

Status: implemented finite representation/proof calibration, 2026-10-08.
No new critical signed arithmetic estimate, global candidate or RH proof.
This is an outcome-informed extension after the grouped pilot's unsigned collapse.
It is not a fresh blind discovery experiment. Old frozen stages are unchanged.

Why this change
The grouped pilot's two accepted critical mu templates had cubic scalar budgets.
Exact padding removal reduces them to the supplied unsigned Cauchy calibration.
That is a specific failure of those templates. It motivates a language that
can preserve and transform signed cross terms, along with a scoring correction.
See GROUPED_ARITHMETIC_HANDOFF.txt and its separately sealed publication.

Implemented entry points
1. cancellation_arithmetic_language.compile_expression(expr, X, horizon, ...)
   compiles a typed expression into the existing canonical rational polynomial.
   Index variables X,N and lexical sum bindings; integer addition/subtraction,
   product, quotient/remainder, min/max/gcd; exact floor of n^(a/b); comparisons,
   divisibility, prime and squarefree predicates; rational constant/ratio weights;
   sequence variables; signed sums/products; guards; nested inclusive bounded sums.
   No signed coefficient table, analytic bound, or observed cancellation is an
   axiom. Actual-prime predicates are supplied arithmetic functionality.

   Both axes of a bilinear sum are explicit. Shifts, product indices, prime
   dilations, residue restrictions, scale-dependent prime ranges, fractional
   cutoffs and higher-degree relation multipliers compose in the same language.
   No out-of-range index is silently dropped; use an explicit guard and preserve
   its complement when claiming a decomposition. Polynomial degree/term, index
   and coefficient bits, AST depth/nodes and scalar work have explicit caps.
   Limits are computational proxies, not a full memory or bit-complexity proof.
   root(base,a,b) is floor(base^(a/b)) for base>=0,a>=0,b>=1; exponent0 returns1
   even at base0. Division/remainder use floor convention, zero divisors reject.

2. partition(expr,predicate) returns BOTH pieces under all leading sum binders.
   polarization(U,V) returns ((U+V)^2-(U-V)^2)/4 exactly. Its negative square is
   part of a signed expression, not an allowed negative SOS certificate weight.
   enumerate_mixes makes deterministic rational mixtures/products from declared
   atoms with a count cap, without numerical scores or a nominated winner.
   The grammar and its initial atoms are supplied assumptions, not bias-free.

3. cancellation_arithmetic_synthesis.synthesize uses the frozen grouped exact
   expansion/checking machinery with structural L1 costs and ZERO scalar-slack
   costs. Thus positive scalar padding cannot lower the objective by consuming
   slack. Exact reconstruction and the independent checker still determine
   admission. It claims neither LP optimality nor mathematical infeasibility.
   This is not yet an optimizer for the smallest uniform scalar budget.

4. cancellation_prime_transport.build_transport(X,p) constructs two independently
   checkable relations-only certificates for equality of the COMPLETE original
   mu energy defect and its p-free shell representation. It uses existing
   prime-power, coprime-product and squarefree-zero relations; no new axiom.

The concrete calibration and its limit
For prime p define B_p(t)=sum_(a<=t,p does not divide a)mu(a).
Every positive index has p-valuation0,1,or>=2. Actual mu at valuation1 has
opposite sign to its p-free core; valuation>=2 has coefficient0. Hence, for
EVERY t>=1, M(t)=B_p(t)-B_p(floor(t/p)). This supplied identity is proved by
the complete source partition, not learned by fitting its numerical outputs.
Squaring retains both cross terms. Its parent energy kernel on p-free a,b is
 L_X,p(a,b)=K_X(max(a,b))-K_X(max(pa,b))-K_X(max(a,pb))+K_X(max(pa,pb))
           =max(0,min(2X,pa,pb)-max(X,a,b)),
where K_X(t)=max(0,2X-max(X,t)). Every integer shell endpoint is retained.
The defect uses L_2X,p-4L_X,p, over ALL ordered pairs through N=4X-1.
The generic expression compiler reproduces this polynomial using elementary
index arithmetic; it has no dedicated winning-shell primitive.

This shell kernel still multiplies signed mu(a)mu(b). Its nonnegativity is not
a critical arithmetic bound. For any fixed finite prime set P containing p,
f_P(n)=mu(n)^2*(-1)^omega_P(n) has the same one-prime transport but positive
linear mean (see earlier prime-forcing proof). Thus the transform must not be
promoted into a signed estimate merely because its geometry simplifies.

What the calibration establishes
The full suite has 17 tests. It checks exact roots (including square/cube/sixth
roots), moving prime ranges, product horizons, complete partitions, signed
polarization, resource/type failures, zero-cost slack and padding, complete
transport certificates and endpoint enumeration, wrong-sign/complement failures,
and the partial-prime counterfeit control.
A supplied synthetic polynomial family with signed prime-dilation blocks has
unknown rational weights recovered at X1/2, independently checked without refit
at X3 for mu/c axiom systems. This is a known-family recovery calibration, not
evidence of a new actual arithmetic estimate or independent unknown-operator
discovery. A quadratic norm multiplier also eliminates the c gcd quartic term,
closing a documented proof-representation gap without adding arithmetic axioms.
The refreshed independent review covers core source hashes; the calibration
receipt records the final full test suite and dependencies separately.

Next search, not implemented or claimed complete here
A. Freeze a small balanced generator using this language to refine both axes of
   signed kernels: additive shifts, multiplicative dilations, gcd/residue masks,
   normalized mixed block products and growing prime ranges. Share structural
   weights across scales. Enumerate/mutate generally; do not seed expected
   weights or select a desired arithmetic identity as the discovery answer.
B. Archive complete target reconstruction, all complements, full scalar budget,
   signed numerical evidence, unsigned baseline, complexity, and failures as
   separate fields. A better component alone is not progress on the full bound.
C. Use exact dual obstructions and repeated subexpressions to propose additional
   multipliers/partitions, with fixed adaptation rules and a fresh final batch.
   The new compiler is a backend for that broader adaptive stage, not a claim
   that this new generator/selector has already been run.
D. For a direct prime route, explicitly center Lambda(n)-1 and retain full
   mu*log divisor decompositions, both sides of every moving cutoff, and mixed
   terms. Logarithmic weights require the existing rigorous interval backend
   or a new symbolic coefficient ring; they are not rational AST constants.
E. Prioritize evidence that complete signed bounds improve beyond the unsigned
   cubic charge, while separating numerical leads from estimates with proof.
   Preserve failures at new scales and partial-prime counterfeits. A genuinely
   RH-relevant promotion still needs a uniform full signed defect estimate for
   the cofinal family of exponents, with constants independent of X, as well as
   a complete all-scale identity. No finite calibration supplies this estimate.

Mathematical context (not imported proof assumptions)
Prime-dilation correlations and bilinear sums have established uses in Mobius
orthogonality; they justify testing this representation, not claiming RH control:
https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2011/11/21/the-bourgain-sarnak-ziegler-orthogonality-criterion/
In that criterion the auxiliary bounded sequence has small distinct-prime
correlations; do not assume those correlations are small for mu itself. The
conclusion is an orthogonality estimate with its own hypotheses/rates, not the
critical uniform estimate sought here.
